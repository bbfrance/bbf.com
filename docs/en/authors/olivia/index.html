Olivia

Restaurants writer, Canada and the US Northeast

12 articles in English

Olivia covers burger restaurants in Canada and the northeastern United States for Burger Breton: Quebec City, Ottawa and Vancouver, New York, Boston, Buffalo and Pittsburgh. She reports on the classic diners and local favorites of those cities as well as chains such as A&W Canada and Five Guys, converts every price with its currency and date, and checks addresses and opening hours against each restaurant's official pages.
