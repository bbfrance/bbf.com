Emma covers burger restaurants in the United Kingdom for Burger Breton, from London's Covent Garden and Soho to Birmingham's Digbeth, Leeds and Glasgow. She follows the British burger scene from its street-food beginnings to the chains that grew out of it, such as Honest Burgers and Five Guys UK, gives every price in pounds with the date it was checked, and notes halal options, opening hours and closures branch by branch.
