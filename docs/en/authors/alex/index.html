Alex

Restaurants editor

16 articles in English

Alex is the restaurants editor at Burger Breton: he sets the method behind every guide and ranking in the section, and covers Germany, Austria, Scandinavia and Central Europe himself, from Hamburg and Stuttgart to Vienna, Copenhagen, Stockholm and Ljubljana. Alex builds every ranking from menus, dated prices, local food press and review data, cross-checks that each venue is still open, and states the sources behind every pick so readers know exactly where each price and rating comes from.
