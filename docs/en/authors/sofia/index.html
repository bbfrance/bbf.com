Sofia covers burger restaurants in Italy, Switzerland, Belgium and the Netherlands for Burger Breton: Naples, Palermo, Lausanne, Basel, Brussels, Antwerp, Bruges and Amsterdam. She explains what each local food culture brings to the burger, from Italian bread and cured meats to Belgian fries, gives prices in euros or Swiss francs with their date, and checks every venue against its official pages before it enters a guide.
