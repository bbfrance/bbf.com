Our authors: the people behind Burger Breton

Every article on Burger Breton is signed by one named author who owns a beat: the news, a family of recipes or a part of the world. Each profile lists what the author covers, how they work and everything they have published.

Léo

Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast Asia

Léo covers burger restaurants in Australia, New Zealand, Thailand and Vietnam for Burger Breton, from the burger bars of Perth, Canberra, Auckland, Wellington and Dunedin to Bangkok, Chiang Mai and Da Nang. He follows the gourmet burger scene that New Zealand chains such as BurgerFuel helped shape, explains how each market adapts the burger to local tastes, and gives every price in local currency with the date it was checked.

Read every article by Léo

Alex

Restaurants editor

Alex is the restaurants editor at Burger Breton: he sets the method behind every guide and ranking in the section, and covers Germany, Austria, Scandinavia and Central Europe himself, from Hamburg and Stuttgart to Vienna, Copenhagen, Stockholm and Ljubljana. Alex builds every ranking from menus, dated prices, local food press and review data, cross-checks that each venue is still open, and states the sources behind every pick so readers know exactly where each price and rating comes from.

Read every article by Alex

Clara

Recipes editor

Clara runs the recipes section of Burger Breton. Trained in a Paris bakery before turning to burgers, she writes the base recipes and techniques, from brioche buns to patty doneness, and the copycats of Burger King, Hungry Jack's and MOS Burger. She tests every dough, sauce and patty at home several times, weighs everything to the gram and gives temperatures in both Celsius and Fahrenheit.

Read every article by Clara

Daniel

Restaurants writer, United States

Daniel covers burger restaurants in the American South, Midwest and West for Burger Breton: Atlanta, Miami, Dallas, Houston, Austin, St. Louis, Milwaukee and Sacramento. He knows the regional chains that define those cities, such as Whataburger in Texas and Culver's in Wisconsin, works from current menus with dated prices, addresses and opening hours checked against each restaurant's own pages, and says plainly when a branch has closed or a price could not be confirmed.

Read every article by Daniel

Julia

Recipes writer

Julia writes the copycat recipes of Burger Breton for American chains: Five Guys, Shake Shack, Whataburger, Culver's and Dairy Queen. She rebuilds each burger from the chain's published ingredient lists, nutrition data and patty weights, cooks it at home until the result matches, and gives every quantity in grams and ounces so the recipe works in any kitchen.

Read every article by Julia

Valentina

Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and Argentina

Valentina covers burger restaurants in Spain, Portugal and Argentina for Burger Breton: Barcelona, Valencia, Zaragoza, San Sebastián, Coimbra, Rosario, Mendoza and Salta. She follows Spanish chains such as Goiko and Argentina's Mostaza, reads the local press in Spanish and Portuguese, gives every price in local currency with the date it was checked, and points out the local specialties, from pintxos to the Rosario carlito, that sit next to the burger.

Maria

News editor

Maria runs the news section of Burger Breton and covers menu launches, comebacks and food trends, from new McDonald's and Burger King burgers to retro items returning to menus, as well as the history of the hamburger. She publishes a name, a price or an end date only when two sources confirm it, one of them official, such as a press release or the chain's own menu, and she breaks each story down market by market for readers in the United States, France and Spain.

Noah

Industry reporter

Noah reports on the business of burgers for Burger Breton: international expansion of the chains, franchising, value-menu price wars between McDonald's, Burger King and Wendy's, and national promotion days.
