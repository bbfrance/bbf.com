Goiko: History, Key Facts and Reviews

Goiko is a Spanish chain of gourmet burger restaurants. Andoni Goicoechea, a Venezuelan doctor training in Madrid, opened the first Goiko Grill there in 2013 with a 50,000-euro loan from his father. In October 2026 the chain's official restaurant finder lists 114 restaurants: 113 in Spain and one in Andorra. Controlled by the French investment firm Capza since late 2025, Goiko had revenue of 109 million euros in 2025.

History of Goiko

2013: a young doctor opens Goiko Grill in Madrid

Goiko started as a single burger restaurant opened in 2013 on calle de María de Molina, in Madrid, by Andoni Goicoechea. Born in Caracas to a family of Basque descent, he had moved to Madrid to train as a specialist doctor and was a geriatrics resident at La Paz hospital (The Objective). He financed the restaurant with a 50,000-euro loan from his father and wrote the first menu with his sister Daniela (Goiko). The first months were nearly empty. He left the hospital to run the business full time, and a second restaurant followed on calle del Conde de Peñalver.

2014-2018: Kevin Bacon, delivery and the L Catterton deal

Goiko grew on burgers cooked to order, table service and heavy use of social media, and it was one of the first Spanish chains to work with home delivery apps (Wikipedia). The Kevin Bacon became its best-selling burger. In June 2018 L Catterton, the investment firm backed by LVMH, bought 80% of the company in a deal that valued it at 150 million euros. Goiko then had 41 restaurants in Spain, all run by the company itself (Alimarket). Its founder kept 20%.

2019-2024: Paris, Basic by Goiko and the islands

With 78 restaurants in Spain, Goiko opened its first address abroad on rue de Richelieu, in central Paris, at the turn of 2020 (L’Hôtellerie Restauration). In 2020 it launched Basic by Goiko, a delivery and takeaway format. The chain reached the Canary Islands in 2022, then the Balearic Islands and Andorra in 2023. Revenue peaked at 138.6 million euros in 2023 (Food Retail). For the chain’s busiest cities, see our guides to Goiko in Barcelona and Goiko in Valencia.

2025-2026: losses, Capza and 114 restaurants

Goiko changed hands in 2025 after two years of growing losses. It opened a 700-square-metre flagship on Puerta del Sol in Madrid, but its planned move into Lisbon stalled. Revenue fell 11.2% to 109 million euros in 2025 and the net loss reached 36.4 million euros. Capza, the French fund that had lent to the group, took control at the end of 2025 by converting debt into equity and now holds 98% (El Debate). In October 2026 the official restaurant finder lists 114 restaurants, 37 of them in the Madrid region and none left in France.

Key dates

  • 2013: Andoni Goicoechea opens the first Goiko Grill on calle de María de Molina, Madrid.
  • 2018: L Catterton buys 80% of the chain, which has 41 restaurants in Spain.
  • 2020: the first restaurant abroad opens in Paris at the turn of the year; Basic by Goiko launches.
  • 2023: openings in the Balearic Islands and Andorra; revenue of 138.6 million euros.
  • 2025: revenue of 109 million euros; Capza takes control at year-end.
  • 2026: 114 restaurants, 113 in Spain and one in Andorra.

On Trustpilot, Goiko customers complain most about long waits, sometimes over an hour, food that reaches the table cold, fries served raw or greasy, and waiters described as rude or rushed. Coeliac diners report that often only the bun is gluten-free and that staff cannot rule out cross-contamination. Several reviewers find the prices too high for the quality. The positive reviews praise juicy burgers and attentive teams at individual restaurants, such as Collado Villalba near Madrid and Guadalajara.

How customer ratings are collected

Every chain is rated the same way, from two public sources shown side by side. Google: the ratings of the restaurants of the chain that Google Maps lists in the largest city of its home market with at least three of them, up to ten, averaged by number of reviews. Trustpilot: the rating of the brand itself.

The two are never averaged together. Google reviews judge the meal and the visit; Trustpilot mostly gathers complaints about service, orders and refunds, and many chains have no page there. A rating we could not read is left out rather than estimated, and each one shows the date we checked it.
