How we profile burger chains

Each chain page tells the brand's history from its first restaurant, then gives its size: restaurants, countries, sales and the markets where it is strongest. Every figure carries its date and links to its source, usually an annual report, the chain's own website or Wikipedia, and is updated when a newer report comes out.

Customer opinion is measured the same way for every chain. We read the Google ratings of its restaurants in the largest city of its home market with at least three of them, and its Trustpilot rating, and show the two side by side. These ratings say how customers rate the brand, not how we rate its burgers: our own verdicts are in the restaurant reviews.
