How we profile burger chains

Each chain page tells the brand's history from its first restaurant, then gives its size: restaurants, countries, sales and the markets where it is strongest. Every figure carries its date and links to its source, usually an annual report, the chain's own website or Wikipedia, and is updated when a newer report comes out.

Customer opinion is measured the same way for every chain. We read its rating on Trustpilot and the ratings of its official ordering app on the App Store and Google Play, in its home market, and average the three without weighting. The score says how customers rate the brand, not how we rate its burgers: our own verdicts are in the restaurant reviews.
