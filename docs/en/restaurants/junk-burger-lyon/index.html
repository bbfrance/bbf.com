Junk Burger Lyon: Menu, Founders and Address

Junk Burger Lyon at 22 rue Ferrandière: who founded it, the S to XXL smash burger menu, hours, halal status and Lyon rivals.

By Thomas, Restaurants writer, FrancePublished October 22, 2026

Junk Burger Lyon is the Lyon branch of Junk Smash Burgers, a Paris-born chain co-founded by Majed Mansour and Wissem Ben Ammar. You’ll find it at 22 rue Ferrandière, 69002 Lyon, on the Presqu’île between Bellecour and Cordeliers. It opened in mid-June 2023. The menu is one smash burger in five sizes, from S (one patty) to XXL (five), on a toasted brioche bun with American cheese and the house Junk sauce. And one correction up front: Majed Mansour is not the chef of Junk Lyon. He co-founded the brand, and no source puts him in the Lyon kitchen.

I cover restaurants in France for Burger Breton. On 16 October 2026 I checked the Lyon branch against Junk’s official locations page, the Lyon food press from the 2023 opening, and French and international trade press. I haven’t eaten at the Lyon counter yet. So this is a profile, not a scored review, and every fact below carries its source and date.

Junk Lyon is the only Junk restaurant in Lyon. It’s one of 20 Junk restaurants in France, according to the brand’s official locations page on 16 October 2026.

Who is behind Junk in Lyon? Majed Mansour, Wissem Ben Ammar and a correction

Junk Lyon belongs to Junk Smash Burgers, the chain founded by Franco-Tunisian friends and business partners Majed Mansour and Wissem Ben Ammar (The Caterer, May 2026; Arab News, September 2025). Vivre Lyon named both founders in its 4 July 2023 piece on the Lyon opening.

I set out to write a chef profile of Majed Mansour at Junk Lyon. The sources don’t support one. Mansour appears everywhere as a co-founder and entrepreneur, never as a chef. Only one source calls anyone a chef: the Dubai food site nogarlicnoonions (1 October 2025) describes Wissem Ben Ammar as a ‘self-taught chef’. That’s a single source, and Junk has never said publicly who does what.

No source says whether Mansour was at the Lyon opening or runs the branch day to day. None names the Lyon manager or head cook. I couldn’t confirm whether Lyon is company-owned or a franchise, either. Read this as a portrait of the founders and their brand through its Lyon outpost.

What do the founders say about Junk?

The founders pitch Junk as a burger for purists. In April 2024, Majed Mansour told the French trade magazine Zepros that Junk is aimed ‘first and foremost at smash burger purists, with few ingredients and maximum flavour’.

In September 2025 the pair told Arab News: ‘At Junk, there aren’t dozens of options: just one burger, available in different patty sizes, with fries and our signature panko.’ Then: ‘We never tried to please everyone.’ I like that line. It explains the whole menu.

From Graine couscous bar to Junk Lyon: the timeline

Before Junk, Mansour and Ben Ammar ran Graine, a couscous bar founded in 2013. During Covid they changed course and launched Junk in Paris in 2021 as a delivery-only brand (Restaurant Online, October 2024; Zepros, April 2024). The full Paris story is in my Junk Smash Burgers Paris profile.

By April 2024, Lyon was one of only four Junk branches outside Île-de-France. Radio Scoop reported in June 2023 that Lyon came right after Paris and Neuilly, which would make it Junk’s first regional branch. No second source confirms the order, so I won’t make that claim.

The group behind Junk

Junk belongs to a restaurant group that Vivre Lyon called My Belly (or InMyBelly). The group also runs Graine, Meatpacking and Puffy Cookies. In April 2024, Zepros counted 18 points of sale and 170 employees across the group, with €11.5 million in revenue for 2023 and €23 million projected for 2024. By March 2025, Zepros put Junk’s 2024 revenue at €20 million and its 2025 target at €30–32 million. I couldn’t confirm whether Graine still operates in 2026.

The Junk Lyon menu: one smash burger, S to XXL

At Junk Lyon, the size letter is the patty count: S has 1 smashed patty, XXL has 5. Every size is built the same way, with a toasted French brioche bun, premium American cheese and the house Junk sauce (official menu; Lyon Femmes, July 2023).

Veggie Meat-free patty See the vegetarian section below

Extras are jalapeños, pickles and crispy or caramelised onions. On the side: panko fried chicken and fries. Dessert is cookies from Puffy, the group’s sister brand (official site).

My take: the size ladder is the clever bit. You answer one question, how hungry you are, and the menu does the rest. What I can’t tell you yet is whether five thin patties keep their crust stacked in an XXL. That needs a visit.

The Truffle Burger

The Junk Truffle Burger is a two-patty smash burger with cheese, house-fried onions and truffle sauce. When Lyon Femmes tested Junk Lyon in July 2023, it was still a monthly special. Junk’s own site says it began as a limited edition and became a permanent item.

Is there a vegetarian burger at Junk Lyon?

Yes, Junk has a Veggie burger. In July 2023, Lyon Femmes described the Lyon version as a cauliflower patty with potatoes, emmental and panko. I couldn’t confirm the recipe is unchanged, so ask at the counter.

What beef does Junk use?

Junk says its beef comes from two French cattle breeds, Charolais and Limousin (official site, checked 16 October 2026). In Lyon that lands close to home: Charolais is the breed of southern Burgundy, and our Lyon burger hub lists it among the regional products to look for on a Lyon burger.

How much does a burger cost at Junk Lyon?

I couldn’t verify current Junk Lyon prices. Rankeat gives a band of about €10–20 per person. Several directories show €5.90 for an S and €16.90 for an XXL as Lyon prices. They aren’t: those are Paris prices from November 2022, published by Sortir à Paris before the Lyon branch even existed.

So don’t budget on them. Check the Lyon menu on click-and-collect, Deliveroo or Uber Eats before you go. I’ll add dated Lyon prices here once I’ve checked them myself.

Junk Lyon address, opening hours and delivery

Junk Lyon is at 22 rue Ferrandière, 69002 Lyon. It’s open Monday to Friday from 12:00 to 14:30 and 19:00 to 22:30, and Saturday and Sunday from 12:00 to 22:30 without a break (official site, matching Rankeat, checked 16 October 2026).

  • Where: on the Presqu’île, Lyon’s central peninsula between the Rhône and the Saône, a short walk from both Place Bellecour and Cordeliers.

  • Reviews: 4.5/5 from 650 Google reviews, via Rankeat on 16 October 2026.

Is Junk Lyon halal?

Junk Lyon’s halal status is unconfirmed. TikTok creators such as @tuviensmanger described it as halal in 2023, and third parties say the same about other branches. But Junk’s official site says nothing about halal, and I found no certifying body. If it matters to you, call +33 9 55 76 76 68 before you order.

What the Lyon press said at the 2023 opening

The Lyon press welcomed Junk in the summer of 2023. Radio Scoop announced the opening on 19 June. Lyon Femmes tasted the burgers on 3 July, a couple of weeks in. Vivre Lyon called the menu ‘minimalist’ the next day. Those impressions are now more than three years old, and I found no newer review of the Lyon branch. That gap is why this page exists.

Junk isn’t the only founder-led burger brand growing in French regional cities. For another, read my profile of Cédric Moulot and 231 East Street in Strasbourg, a city Junk has also opened in. For the longer view, see our history of the hamburger.

Junk Burger Lyon FAQ

Who founded Junk Burger, and is Majed Mansour the chef of Junk Lyon?

Junk was founded by Majed Mansour and Wissem Ben Ammar. Mansour is a co-founder and entrepreneur, not the chef of Junk Lyon, and no source ties him to the Lyon kitchen. One source, nogarlicnoonions (2025), calls Ben Ammar a self-taught chef.

Where is Junk in Lyon and what are its opening hours?

Junk Lyon is at 22 rue Ferrandière, 69002 Lyon, on the Presqu’île. It opens Monday to Friday 12:00–14:30 and 19:00–22:30, and Saturday and Sunday 12:00–22:30 (official site, 16 October 2026).

When did Junk open in Lyon?

In mid-June 2023. Radio Scoop announced the opening on 19 June 2023; the exact day hasn’t been published.

What do the sizes S, M, L, XL and XXL mean at Junk?

They give the number of smashed patties: S has 1, M 2, L 3, XL 4 and XXL 5. Every size comes on a brioche bun with American cheese and Junk sauce.

How much does a Junk burger cost in Lyon?

Current Lyon prices aren’t verified. Rankeat gives a band of about €10–20 per person. The €5.90–€16.90 figures quoted online are Paris prices from 2022.

Is Junk Lyon halal?

It’s not confirmed. Some social media creators describe it as halal, but Junk publishes no certification. Ask the restaurant.

What beef does Junk use?

French beef from Charolais and Limousin cattle, according to Junk’s official site.

Is there a vegetarian burger at Junk Lyon?

Yes, the Veggie. In 2023 it was a cauliflower patty with potatoes, emmental and panko (Lyon Femmes). The current recipe isn’t confirmed.

Does Junk Lyon deliver?

Yes. Besides dine-in and takeaway, Junk Lyon delivers through Deliveroo and Uber Eats.

Where can I eat the best smash burger in Lyon?

The names that come up most in the local press and directories are Junk, Smash Burger Joint, Smatch & Beef, Dangerous Burger and Spacefox. Smatch & Beef has the highest Rankeat score of the group (4.6/5). I haven’t tasted them side by side yet.

What is a smash burger?

A smash burger is a ball of ground beef pressed flat on a very hot griddle so it forms a thin, crisp, browned crust. Junk stacks one to five of these patties per burger.
