De Vrije Burger Stellenbosch by Bertus Basson

De Vrije Burger Stellenbosch: Bertus Basson's burger bar at 13 Drostdy Street. Free-range beef, free soft-serve, hours, rand prices and Overture's fate.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished November 5, 2026

De Vrije Burger is Bertus Basson’s burger restaurant in Stellenbosch, at 13 Drostdy Street, 7600. It is open daily from 12:00 to 20:00, according to his official site, which I checked on 31 October 2026. Looking for Overture, his fine-dining restaurant at Hidden Valley Wine Estate? It closed on 30 September 2022, and the team moved to Chorus at Waterkloof Wine Estate in Somerset West. The burger is simple: free-range beef, double cheese, a homemade bun and, by the restaurant’s own description, a free soft-serve on the side.

The burgers at De Vrije Burger: menu and prices in rand

The core of the menu is a double-cheese burger of 100% free-range beef in a homemade bun, with hand-cut chips and house sauces. The restaurant’s own line is ‘100% free range beef, double cheese, home made bun and all the trimmings’ (Visit Stellenbosch, bertusbasson.com).

The fullest priced menu I found comes from De Vrije Burger’s stall at the Time Out Market Cape Town, 19 Dock Road, V&A Waterfront (Time Out). Two caveats. The page has no date, and the stall is not among the three branches on the official site, so it may well have closed. Read these prices as a guide to the menu, not as your Stellenbosch bill.

The free soft-serve: De Vrije Roomys

Every burger comes with a free soft-serve, according to both Cape Town Magazine and Visit Stellenbosch. The restaurant calls it the ‘De Vrije Roomys’, and roomys is Afrikaans for ice cream. It’s the detail people remember. Neither source is recent, so ask at the counter if you’re counting on it.

Who is Bertus Basson?

Bertus Basson is a South African chef and restaurateur whose career is rooted in the Stellenbosch winelands. Born in Cape Town on 12 April 1979, he grew up in Polokwane, Nelspruit and Witbank. At about 19 he was cooking at Chez Bruce, a Michelin-starred restaurant in London (Wikipedia).

Back home, he founded the catering business All Things Culinary in 2005 and was named Unilever Chef of the Year in 2007. TV audiences know him as a judge on seasons 1 to 4 of The Ultimate Braai Master. Jacana Media published his two books, Homegrown (2017) and Being Bertus Basson (2018).

Overture restaurant Stellenbosch: what happened

Overture closed on 30 September 2022 after 15 years at Hidden Valley Wine Estate. Basson had opened it as chef-owner in November 2007. Its peak came at the 2019 Eat Out Awards, when Overture ranked 12th in South Africa’s Top 20 and Basson took Chef of the Year.

The ending was about the lease, not the food. Basson told Eat Out the team ‘had no success in negotiating a new lease agreement with the owners of the farm’ (Eat Out, original URL, now redirecting to News24 Eat Out). Less than three weeks later, on 18 October 2022, Chorus opened at Waterkloof Wine Estate in Somerset West, and Basson’s signature soufflé was back on the dessert menu (Dineplan).

Overture never served burgers. De Vrije Burger grew out of a different venture: according to Visit Stellenbosch, Basson and his wife Mareli created it after the success of The Deck at Hidden Valley.

Bertus Basson timeline: from Overture to De Vrije Burger

The key dates of Basson’s career, from Wikipedia, Eat Out, Dineplan and his official site:

  • 1979 (12 April): born in Cape Town.
  • About 1998: works at Chez Bruce in London, around age 19.
  • 2005: founds the catering business All Things Culinary.
  • 2007: named Unilever Chef of the Year; opens Overture at Hidden Valley Wine Estate in November.
  • 2017: opens De Vrije Burger in Stellenbosch with Mareli Basson; publishes Homegrown.
  • 2018: publishes Being Bertus Basson.
  • 2019: Eat Out Chef of the Year; Overture 12th in the Eat Out Top 20; De Vrije Burger wins the Western Cape Best Burger Award (Everyday Eatery).
  • 2022 (30 September): Overture closes.
  • 2022 (18 October): Chorus opens at Waterkloof Wine Estate, Somerset West.

Is De Vrije Burger worth it compared with RocoMamas?

On paper, De Vrije Burger is the burger I would pick in Stellenbosch. It is chef-owned, made with free-range beef, won the 2019 Western Cape best-burger award and comes with free ice cream. RocoMamas, in Eikestad Mall, is a chain smash burger with a menu running from R79 to R159 nationally (October 2026). It is the better choice if you want late opening: it trades until 22:00 or 23:00, while De Vrije Burger closes at 20:00.

FAQ: De Vrije Burger and Bertus Basson

Is Overture in Stellenbosch still open?

No. Overture closed on 30 September 2022 after 15 years at Hidden Valley Wine Estate, when the team failed to agree a new lease with the farm’s owners. They opened Chorus at Waterkloof Wine Estate on 18 October 2022.

Where is Bertus Basson cooking now?

At Chorus, Waterkloof Wine Estate, Somerset West. His group also runs Basson & Co., Spek en Bone, Kantien, Toevlug, BLOEM, Ongetem, Abundance Catering and the three De Vrije Burger branches.

Where is De Vrije Burger in Stellenbosch, and when is it open?

At 13 Drostdy Street, Stellenbosch, 7600, open daily from 12:00 to 20:00 according to the official site (checked 31 October 2026). The old 61 Plein Street address is out of date.

How much does a burger cost at De Vrije Burger?

The Time Out Market stall listed the classic De Vrije Burger at R125, or R155 with chips (undated). At launch in Stellenbosch it cost R75. Current Stellenbosch prices are not confirmed, so check in store or on a delivery app.

Do you really get free ice cream with every burger?

That’s what Cape Town Magazine and Visit Stellenbosch both say: every burger comes with a free soft-serve, the ‘De Vrije Roomys’. Neither source is recent, so ask when you order.

Does De Vrije Burger have a vegetarian burger?

The Time Out Market stall menu listed De Vrije Vegetarian, a falafel burger, at R115 (R145 with chips). Check whether the Stellenbosch branch offers it today.

Does De Vrije Burger have branches in Cape Town?

Yes, two. One is in Sea Point (7 Penarth Road, Cape Town, 8005) and the other in Bellville (Welgemoed Forum, 24 Kommissaris Street, Welgemoed, 7530). Both open from 12:00 to 21:00.
