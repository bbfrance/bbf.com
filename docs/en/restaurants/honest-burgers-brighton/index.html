Honest Burgers Brighton: Duke Street & Origins

Honest Burgers began in Brighton: a 2010 festival tent that became a UK chain of about 50 sites. What to know before eating at 8 Duke Street.

By Emma, Restaurants writer, United KingdomPublished November 18, 2026

Honest Burgers Brighton is at 8 Duke Street, Brighton BN1 1AH, just off West Street and a few minutes’ walk from the seafront. It opened on 25 February 2019, 9 years after the brand sold its very first burgers from a tent at the Brighton Food Festival. So Duke Street is a homecoming, not the original: the first permanent Honest opened in Brixton Village, London, in 2011. Co-founder Tom Barton studied at the University of Brighton, and the burger that won UK’s Best Burger in 2026 started life in his student kitchen here.

This profile comes from research, not a meal. I cross-checked trade press interviews, the company’s own pages, local listings and review platforms, all on 12 October 2026. Where sources disagree, I show both versions. Anything I could not confirm is left out, including opening hours and current menu prices.

Who founded Honest Burgers?

Honest Burgers was co-founded by Tom Barton and Philip (Phil) Eeles; The Staff Canteen also credits Dorian Waite as a third co-founder.

The sources split on Waite. The Staff Canteen names him as a co-founder, while Restaurant Online describes him only as someone Barton met who had restaurant experience. Barton and Eeles are the two names every source agrees on, so they are the two I lead with.

Tom Barton: the co-founder behind Honest Burgers

Tom Barton is the co-founder of Honest Burgers and the creator of ‘The Honest’, its award-winning burger, but he is not the company’s CEO.

Born in Bath in 1986, Barton studied Business Management and Marketing at the University of Brighton, according to a 2019 Restaurant Online profile. He met Philip Eeles while both were working at Riddle & Finns, a Brighton seafood restaurant.

He was still speaking publicly as co-founder in October 2025, at the Wimbledon opening, and again in March 2026 at the National Burger Awards. I found no source that gives his current executive or board title. The same is true for Eeles.

Who runs Honest Burgers today?

Matt Brandon is CEO of Honest Burgers: he was promoted from CFO in July 2025 to replace Kevin Styles, The Caterer reported. Earlier, Thomas Kelly, formerly of McDonald’s, had become CEO in January 2024 before moving to group chair in August 2024 (Restaurant Online).

Did Honest Burgers start in Brighton or Brixton?

Honest Burgers started in Brighton as street food and opened its first permanent restaurant in Brixton. The idea took shape in Brighton in 2009–2010, and the first burgers were sold there in 2010.

The founders’ own version is refreshingly unpolished: “After a few too many pints at The Bath Arms, we decided to go into business together” (Restaurant Online). Their first stand was a tent with a grill and a fryer at the 2010 Brighton Food Festival. They ran out of chips on day one.

The founding year depends on who you read. The Caterer gives 2009; the founders’ own account and Big Hospitality put the first sales in 2010. I give the range rather than pretend 1 date is settled.

How Honest Burgers grew into a UK chain

Honest Burgers grew from 1 Brixton restaurant in 2011 to around 50 UK sites by late 2025, and its biggest single jump came from buying Gourmet Burger Kitchen sites.

In autumn 2025, Honest bought 12 Gourmet Burger Kitchen sites. The first conversion opened in Wimbledon on 26 October 2025 and took the portfolio to 51 (The Caterer). Other counts vary: 39 in July 2025, ‘45+’ in March 2026, ‘50+’ in a Staff Canteen piece. ‘Around 50’ is the figure I would defend.

Bar chart of Honest Burgers’ growth across the UK: 1 restaurant in Brixton in 2011, 39 sites in July 2025 and 51 after the first Gourmet Burger Kitchen conversion in Wimbledon on 26 October 2025. The money backs up the expansion. For the year ending 28 January 2025, turnover rose 6%, from £56.3m to £59.8m; pre-tax profit was £1.7m and adjusted EBITDA jumped 56% to £6.2m (Hospitality & Catering News). The Honest Burgers concept: own butchery, British beef, rosemary chips

Honest serves British beef burgers made in its own butchery, Honest Butchers, with homemade rosemary salted chips. The chips, rosemary salt, veggie fritters, lemonade, pickles, relish and sauces are all made in-house (Honest Burgers).

Older third-party articles say Honest’s dry-aged beef came from The Ginger Pig. The company now says it runs its own butchery, so I treat that as history, not current supply.

Honest Burgers rosemary chips

The rosemary salted chips are Honest’s signature side, and they date back to the very first weekend: the founders sold out of chips on day 1 at the 2010 Brighton Food Festival. The rosemary salt is made in-house too.

Why Honest serves burgers pink

Honest cooks its patties medium-rare. Tom Barton says even the award-winning burger uses 2 smashed patties cooked medium-rare, which is unusual: most smash burgers are pressed thin and cooked through. I found no published policy on ordering well done, so ask when you order.

The Honest: UK’s Best Burger at the National Burger Awards 2026

‘The Honest’, created by Tom Barton, won UK’s Best Burger at the National Burger Awards 2026, the first year Honest entered. The final took place on 24 March 2026 at Big Penny Social in London, with 16 finalists (Verge Magazine, The Staff Canteen).

The build, according to Verge Magazine:

  • Dry-aged British beef, as 2 smashed patties cooked medium-rare
  • Thick-cut Wiltshire bacon
  • Homemade beef and onion relish
  • XL burger cheese
  • Honest pickles
  • Fresh white onion

The relish is the clever part. It is made from rendered dry-aged beef fat, onions, red wine vinegar and sugar, so the beef shows up twice in 1 bite. Barton told The Staff Canteen: “This burger won because it’s still a really simple bacon cheeseburger.” He traces the recipe back about 17 years, to his student kitchen in Brighton. So the UK’s best burger of 2026 was born here too.

Can you order it on Duke Street? I could not confirm that. At launch in March 2026, ‘The Honest’ was exclusive to the Smash + Grab site at Liverpool Street, London, with a limited-time nationwide rollout planned. If it is the reason for your trip, phone ahead.

The Brighton local burger at Honest

Every Honest restaurant has a local burger, and Brighton’s 2019 launch version was a beef burger with bacon, smoked Mayfield cheese, an Honest x Brighton Hot Sauce habanero and ghost-chilli sauce, chipotle slaw, rocket and pickles (Big Hospitality).

That recipe has probably moved on. TripAdvisor’s description now mentions a local burger made with Joosy, a local brewery, but I could not confirm the current build. Ask for the Brighton special when you order.

Vegan and gluten-free options at Honest Burgers Brighton

Honest Burgers Brighton has a plant-based burger, and every burger can be made gluten-free. ‘The Plant’ has a Beyond Meat patty, vegan Gouda and chipotle mayo, and there is also a homemade Southern fried fritter (Restaurants Brighton). That detail comes from a single listing, so check the current menu. With a serious allergy, tell the staff when you order.

Is Honest Burgers Brighton worth a visit?

Yes, if you want a British beef burger served pink in the city where the brand began, and especially if you want to eat outside or bring a dog.

My view: Honest is the safe pick in Brighton, not the adventurous one. The food story is the same across the chain; what Duke Street adds is the setting, with a large terrace on a pedestrianised street and a dog-friendly listing. If you only have room for one burger in town and want something you cannot get in 50 other places, I would try Salt Shed first, then come to Duke Street for the origin story. Honest Burgers Brighton FAQ

Who founded Honest Burgers?

Tom Barton and Philip (Phil) Eeles co-founded Honest Burgers. The Staff Canteen also names Dorian Waite as a co-founder. Barton and Eeles met while working at Riddle & Finns in Brighton.

Did Honest Burgers start in Brighton or Brixton?

Both, in that order. Honest first sold burgers from a tent at the Brighton Food Festival in 2010, then opened its first permanent restaurant in Brixton Village, London, in 2011.

Who is Tom Barton and what is his role at Honest Burgers today?

Tom Barton, born in Bath in 1986, is the co-founder of Honest Burgers and the creator of ‘The Honest’ burger. He was still speaking publicly as co-founder in March 2026, but his current executive title is not public. Matt Brandon has been CEO since July 2025.

Where is Honest Burgers in Brighton?

Honest Burgers Brighton is at 8 Duke Street, Brighton BN1 1AH, just off West Street and a few minutes from the seafront. The phone number is 01273 916352. Opening hours differ between listings, so call before you go.

What is the Brighton local burger at Honest Burgers?

The 2019 launch burger had bacon, smoked Mayfield cheese, an Honest x Brighton Hot Sauce habanero and ghost-chilli sauce, chipotle slaw, rocket and pickles. The current local burger may differ, so ask in the restaurant.

Why does Honest serve its burgers pink?

Honest cooks its patties medium-rare by default. Tom Barton says even the award-winning burger uses 2 smashed patties cooked medium-rare. Ask the staff about other cooking options.

What burger won UK’s Best Burger at the National Burger Awards 2026?

‘The Honest’ by Honest Burgers won on 24 March 2026, the first time Honest entered. It has dry-aged British beef, Wiltshire bacon, beef and onion relish, XL burger cheese, pickles and white onion.

Does Honest Burgers Brighton have vegan and gluten-free options?

Yes. ‘The Plant’ has a Beyond Meat patty, vegan Gouda and chipotle mayo, and every burger can be made gluten-free. Check the current menu before you visit.

Is Honest Burgers Brighton dog-friendly with outdoor seating?

Yes. The restaurant has a large outdoor seating area on Duke Street and is listed as dog-friendly.

How many Honest Burgers restaurants are there in the UK?

Around 50. The portfolio reached 51 sites in October 2025 after the first Gourmet Burger Kitchen conversion opened in Wimbledon.

What are the best burgers in Brighton besides Honest?

Salt Shed, MEATliquor, The Fountain Head and Burger & Lobster are the names local and national media mention most. Time Out ranked Salt Shed 20th among the world’s best burger restaurants in 2025.
