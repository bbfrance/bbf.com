Goiko Barcelona: All 9 Addresses, Opening Hours, Menu and Reviews

Which of Barcelona's 9 Goiko restaurants to pick, when they open, where the 4 city-only burgers are and what diners say, checked 10 October 2026.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished October 10, 2026

Goiko has 9 restaurants in the city of Barcelona and 7 more in nearby towns, according to the chain’s official province page, which I checked on 10 October 2026. If you only go to 1, make it Goiko Via Laietana 13 in the Gothic Quarter: it’s central, it stays open until midnight from Thursday to Sunday, and it’s the only Goiko that serves La Fantasma. Coeliac? Go straight to Goiko Gluten Free Sant Joan 58. The whole kitchen is gluten-free, the Associació Celíacs de Catalunya has certified it, and no Goiko in the city scores higher on Gastroranking.

Most Goiko Barcelona addresses open at noon and close at 11:30 pm Monday to Wednesday, midnight Thursday to Sunday. Gran Via 129 and Diagonal Mar open later, at 1 pm, and close earlier on most nights. Budget under €20 per person at most addresses and €20–30 at Independència 339, according to Gastroranking.

How I checked: every address, phone number, service and opening time comes from Goiko’s official restaurant pages, read on 10 October 2026. Ratings come from Gastroranking, which combines Google, Just Eat and Tripadvisor reviews. The history comes from the Spanish trade press (Alimarket), Time Out Barcelona and Metrópoli Abierta. Where I couldn’t confirm something, I say so or leave it out.

Which Goiko in Barcelona should you choose?

Pick the Goiko closest to where you’re staying. Every one serves the same national menu, and only 4 add a burger you can’t get anywhere else.

  • In the old town or Gothic Quarter: Via Laietana 13, for La Fantasma.
  • Coeliac or gluten-sensitive: Gluten Free Sant Joan 58, with no gluten anywhere in the kitchen.
  • Visiting the Sagrada Família: Independència 339, which is in the same neighbourhood.
  • Staying in the Eixample: Aribau 113, for La Montserrat.
  • Shopping or by the sea: Diagonal Mar, inside the shopping centre at the sea end of Avinguda Diagonal.
  • Want the best reviews: Sant Joan 58, rated 8.7/10 on Gastroranking and 6th of 114 burger restaurants in Barcelona.

Goiko Via Laietana 13: the Goiko for the Gothic Quarter

Goiko Via Laietana 13 (08003) is the most central Goiko in Barcelona, in the Barri Gòtic. It’s the one I’d send a first-time visitor to.

Its exclusive is La Fantasma, made with fresh cheese, crispy bacon, caramelised peppers, crispy onion and lamb’s lettuce. The official page lists free wifi, a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table, wheelchair access, table booking, delivery and takeaway.

Via Laietana street sign in Barcelona’s Gothic Quarter, home of Goiko Via Laietana 13 and its exclusive La Fantasma burger (Photo: Ardfern, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Via\_Laietana,\_Barcelona,\_December\_2014\_(01).JPG))

On Gastroranking, Via Laietana scores 8.2/10 across 4,666 reviews: 9.2 on Google (4,502 reviews) and 8.4 on Just Eat (164). It ranks 20th of 134 American restaurants in Barcelona. Reviewers praise the fast, friendly service. The recurring complaint is that quality and portions slip during promotions.

Goiko Gluten Free Sant Joan 58: a 100% gluten-free burger in Barcelona

Goiko Gluten Free Sant Joan 58 is a 100% gluten-free burger restaurant in Barcelona. Nothing on the menu contains gluten, not even in traces, and the Associació Celíacs de Catalunya has certified it (Time Out Barcelona). For a coeliac traveller, that matters more than any exclusive burger.

This address is also where Goiko started in Catalonia, back in 2017. The gluten-free conversion was in place by 2 February 2026 at the latest, but I couldn’t find the exact date. The gluten-free menu includes the Kevin Bacon, the Pigma (with a fried egg and Goiko sauce) and chicken tenders. For Free Burger Day on Wednesday 11 February 2026, the restaurant gave away 200 gluten-free Kevin Bacons.

The official page lists the longest hours in the city: noon to midnight, 7 days a week. It also lists a terrace where dogs are welcome, wifi, a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table, wheelchair access, booking, delivery and takeaway. Time Out names Girona as the nearest metro station.

Passeig de Sant Joan and the Hercules Fountain, the Eixample boulevard where Goiko Gluten Free Sant Joan 58 serves Barcelona’s 100% gluten-free burgers (nearest metro: Girona) (Photo: 1997 (Wikimedia Commons user), CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Passeig\_de\_Sant\_Joan\_Barcelona\_Catalonia.JPG))

Gastroranking gives Sant Joan 58 an 8.7/10 across 12,402 reviews: 8.8 on Google (11,365), 8.8 on Just Eat (591) and 6.6 on Tripadvisor (456). It ranks 6th of 114 burger restaurants in Barcelona, the highest of any Goiko in the city. Diners praise the gluten-free bun, the modern, clean dining room and the service. Some complain about slightly high prices and small mistakes with orders.

Goiko Independència 339: the Goiko near the Sagrada Família

Goiko Independència 339 (08026) is the Goiko for the Sagrada Família, in the Eixample neighbourhood of the same name. It’s also the second best-rated Goiko in Barcelona.

The Sagrada Família’s Passion façade: Goiko Independència 339 is in the same Eixample neighbourhood, the closest Goiko for a post-visit burger (Photo: Moyan Brenn, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Barcelona\_(5237981315).jpg))

Gastroranking scores it 8.6/10 across 8,252 reviews: 8.6 on Google (6,944), 9.2 on Just Eat (1,207) and 5.2 on Tripadvisor (101). It ranks 9th of 114 burger restaurants. It’s also the priciest address on Gastroranking, at €20–30 per person. Reviewers keep coming back to the Smash Don Vito.

The official page lists wifi, a kids’ menu, booking, delivery and takeaway. It says nothing about an exclusive burger or wheelchair access, so call +34 937 37 71 26 if access matters to you.

Goiko Aribau 113: the Eixample address

Goiko Aribau 113 (08036) is Barcelona’s second-oldest Goiko and the one to choose in the Eixample. Time Out listed it alongside Sant Joan 58 in December 2017, when they were the chain’s 20th and 21st restaurants in Spain (Time Out Barcelona).

Its exclusive is La Montserrat, made with a fried egg, a potato hash brown, fried spinach, manchego and truffle mayonnaise. The official page lists wifi, a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table, wheelchair access, booking, delivery and takeaway.

Aribau has the most reviews of any Goiko in the city. Gastroranking gives it 8.4/10 across 12,374 reviews: 8.8 on Google (11,496), 8.2 on Just Eat (558) and 6.4 on Tripadvisor (320). It ranks 15th of 114 burger restaurants. Diners praise the service, the ingredients and the value for money. They complain about the fries, small portions and deliveries that take more than 40 minutes.

The other Goiko restaurants in Barcelona

The remaining 5 sit in residential neighbourhoods or shopping centres. I wouldn’t cross town for any of them, but each is handy if you’re already nearby.

Goiko Paseo Maragall 148 (El Guinardó)

Goiko Maragall 148 (08027) has the highest Google score of any Goiko in the city, 9.4 across 5,388 reviews. Its overall Gastroranking score is 8.3/10 across 5,758 reviews, which puts it 20th of 114. The official page lists wifi, a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table, accessibility, booking, delivery and takeaway.

Goiko Gran Via 129 (La Bordeta)

Goiko Gran Via de les Corts Catalanes 129 (08014) keeps the shortest hours of all nine. It opens at 13:00 every day and closes at 23:00 from Sunday to Thursday, or 23:30 on Friday and Saturday (official page). Gastroranking gives it 8.2/10 across 6,001 reviews and ranks it 23rd of 114. The air conditioning is a frequent complaint.

Goiko Can Segalar 18

Goiko Can Segalar 18 (08014) serves La Suis, made with raclette, melted onions, a hash brown, pickles and lamb’s lettuce. The official page lists wifi, a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table, booking, delivery and takeaway.

Goiko C.C. Som Multiespai

Goiko Som Multiespai is in the shopping centre at Av. Río de Janeiro 42 (08016). Its exclusive is La Gresca, made with black garlic sauce, gouda, guacamole, crispy onion and a fried egg. It’s open until midnight from Wednesday to Sunday (official page).

Goiko C.C. Diagonal Mar

Goiko Diagonal Mar is inside the Diagonal Mar shopping centre at Avinguda Diagonal 3 (08019). It opened on 28 September 2023 in a 232 m² space (Metrópoli Abierta). The official page lists a terrace where dogs are welcome, a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table and wheelchair access.

Goiko Barcelona menu: what to order

Goiko Barcelona serves the chain’s national menu, plus an exclusive burger at 4 addresses. The official menu covers starters, burgers, smash burgers, Chicken Lovers, a kids’ menu, salads, desserts, drinks and set menus. It also has a gluten-free restaurant filter and an allergen page.

Signature burgers

On your first visit, order the Kevin Bacon. It’s the chain’s best-seller, and Time Out called it the best-selling burger in Spain back in 2017. Reviewers at Independència 339 often mention the Smash Don Vito. Time Out also recommends starting with the Venezuelan tequeños, a nod to the founder’s roots.

Vegetarian and gluten-free options

For gluten-free burgers in Barcelona, go to Sant Joan 58, where the whole menu is gluten-free and certified. At the other addresses, check the allergen page before you order. In Valencia, Goiko’s vegetarian burger is the Veggie Mercury. I couldn’t confirm that it’s on the Barcelona menu in October 2026, and I found no confirmed vegan option.

Goiko Barcelona prices

Plan on under €20 per person at Goiko in Barcelona, or €20–30 at Independència 339. Gastroranking gives the first range for 6 of the 7 listings I checked. Goiko doesn’t publish prices on its public menu: they only appear once you pick a restaurant in the online order, and they vary from 1 address to the next. That’s why I don’t quote prices for individual burgers. Time Out mentioned a weekday set menu at around €12 in 2017. 9 years on, I wouldn’t plan around that figure.

Goiko Barcelona reviews: what diners say

Diners rate Goiko Barcelona highly on Google and Just Eat, and much lower on Tripadvisor. Google scores range from 8.4 to 9.4, while Tripadvisor scores range from 5.2 to 6.6. These figures come from Gastroranking, recorded on 10 October 2026. I didn’t read the scores directly on each platform, and review totals change quickly.

  • Praise: fast, friendly service, good ingredients, value for money, and the gluten-free bun at Sant Joan 58.
  • Complaints: fries, small portions, smaller servings during promotions, deliveries over 40 minutes, and the air conditioning at Gran Via 129.

My take: Goiko is at its best at the table. Most complaints are about delivery times and promotion-day portions, so sit down and order from the regular menu if you can.

Booking, takeaway and delivery at Goiko Barcelona

Booking a table at Goiko Barcelona is optional; you can reserve online at goiko.com/es/reservas. 8 of the 9 official pages list booking, delivery and takeaway, and Diagonal Mar’s page mentions none of them. For delivery, order at goiko.com/es/delivery. None of the official pages lists payment methods or parking.

Goiko’s story in Barcelona

Goiko is a Spanish burger chain, founded in Madrid in 2013 by Andoni Goicoechea (Goikoetxea), a Venezuelan doctor of Basque descent. It arrived in Barcelona in 2017. The first restaurant opened at Passeig de Sant Joan 58, with 250 m², 15 staff and delivery through Glovo. At the time, the chain had 18 restaurants and was aiming for 26 by the end of the year (Alimarket).

Growth came fast. In April 2018, Goiko opened its fifth Barcelona restaurant, 222 m² on Plaça Ildefons Cerdà (Alimarket). The chain had 57 restaurants in Spain by February 2019, according to Alimarket. By February 2022, it had 17 restaurants in Catalonia, 89 in Spain and 2 in Paris (Alimarket). The 100th opened in Murcia in September 2022, a year before Diagonal Mar.

Goiko’s growth in Spain: 18 restaurants when Sant Joan 58 opened Barcelona’s first in 2017, 89 by February 2022 (17 in Catalonia) and 100 by September 2022. Sources: Alimarket, press reports (Chart: Burger Breton, data from Alimarket and Spanish press, rendered with QuickChart)

The chain is also changing shape. In late 2025 it moved into food stands and events (Merca2), and in February 2026 Alimarket reported that a new majority shareholder had come in. For the chain’s early years and its 1-exclusive-per-restaurant model, see my guide to Goiko in Valencia, the chain’s first city outside Madrid.

Is Goiko worth it in Barcelona?

Goiko is worth it in Barcelona if you want a reliable burger, long opening hours or a safe gluten-free meal. It isn’t the most original burger in the city, and I wouldn’t pretend otherwise. Sant Joan 58 ranks 6th of 114 burger restaurants on Gastroranking, and it’s the easiest gluten-free burger to recommend in Barcelona. My advice: try La Fantasma at Via Laietana 13 or eat gluten-free at Sant Joan 58, then go and try the independents. Our Barcelona burger page lists every Barcelona address we cover.

FAQ: Goiko Barcelona

How many Goiko restaurants are there in Barcelona? The city has 9, according to the official province page checked on 10 October 2026: Via Laietana 13, Sant Joan 58, Independència 339, Aribau 113, Paseo Maragall 148, Gran Via 129, Can Segalar 18, C.C. Som Multiespai and C.C. Diagonal Mar. 7 more are in the province.

Which Goiko is closest to the Gothic Quarter, the Sagrada Família or the sea? Via Laietana 13 is in the Barri Gòtic. Independència 339 is in the Sagrada Família neighbourhood. Diagonal Mar is in the shopping centre at the sea end of Avinguda Diagonal.

What are Goiko Barcelona’s opening hours? Most addresses are open 12:00–23:30 Monday to Wednesday and 12:00–00:00 Thursday to Sunday. Sant Joan 58 is open 12:00–00:00 every day. Gran Via 129 and Diagonal Mar open at 13:00 and close earlier on most nights.

Do I need to book at Goiko Barcelona? No, booking is optional. You can book online at goiko.com/es/reservas for 8 of the 9 addresses.

How much does a meal at Goiko Barcelona cost? Under €20 per person at most addresses, and €20–30 at Independència 339, according to Gastroranking. Goiko doesn’t publish prices on its public menu.

Which burgers are exclusive to Goiko in Barcelona? La Fantasma (Via Laietana 13), La Montserrat (Aribau 113), La Suis (Can Segalar 18) and La Gresca (Som Multiespai). For a first visit, the Kevin Bacon is the chain’s best-seller.

Is there a 100% gluten-free Goiko in Barcelona? Yes. Goiko Gluten Free Sant Joan 58 is entirely gluten-free, with no traces, and the Associació Celíacs de Catalunya has certified it. Its menu includes a gluten-free Kevin Bacon and Pigma.

Does Goiko have vegetarian options? In Valencia, Goiko’s vegetarian burger is the Veggie Mercury, but I couldn’t confirm it’s on the Barcelona menu in October 2026. Check the allergen page or ask staff.

Does Goiko deliver in Barcelona? Yes, through goiko.com/es/delivery. 8 of the 9 official pages list delivery. Some Aribau 113 reviews mention deliveries taking more than 40 minutes.

Is Goiko a Spanish chain, and who founded it? Yes. Andoni Goicoechea (Goikoetxea), a Venezuelan doctor of Basque descent, founded Goiko in Madrid in 2013.

Which Goiko in Barcelona has the best reviews? Sant Joan 58, at 8.7/10 across 12,402 reviews on Gastroranking. Next comes Independència 339 at 8.6/10.
