MOS Burger Tokyo: First Store, Menu, Prices, Hours

Where MOS Burger began in 1972, which Tokyo branches are worth the trip, what to order and what it costs after the July 2026 price rise.

By Hana, Restaurants writer, Japan and IndonesiaPublished November 11, 2026

The MOS Burger to visit in Tokyo is the chain’s first store, MOS Burger Narimasu, at 2-15-10 Narimasu, Itabashi-ku, one minute on foot from the south exit of Narimasu Station. It opens daily from 8:00 to 22:00, according to the local press and Wanderlog (checked 1 November 2026), serves breakfast until 10:30 and has a Google rating of 4.1/5 from 642 reviews. Since the price rise of 15 July 2026, the signature MOS Burger costs ¥500 and the Teriyaki Burger ¥490, tax included.

MOS Burger was born in Tokyo in 1972. On 30 September 2026 it ran 1,306 restaurants in Japan, 181 of them in the Tokyo Metropolis, so you are never far from one. This guide picks the four Tokyo addresses that matter to a burger lover, from the 1972 birthplace to the new Mosh Burger & Bar in Ginza, with dated prices, hours and what reviewers actually say. My advice up front: if you only have time for one, make it Narimasu.

How I built this MOS Burger Tokyo guide

This guide is based on official MOS data, the Tokyo local press and review platforms, not on a scored visit, so it carries no rating of ours. I checked every address, hour and price against the sources named in each section on 1 November 2026. Where sources disagree or have gone stale, I say so instead of picking the most convenient answer.

Official sources come first: the store count on mos.co.jp, the English menu on mos.jp and the official store search. Then come the Itabashi Times for Narimasu, Impress Watch for prices, SoraNews24 for Ginza, and Google, TripAdvisor, 4travel and Retty for reviews. I covered MOS Burger in Osaka, Sapporo and Hiroshima the same way.

The first MOS Burger in Narimasu: where it all began

The first MOS Burger is still open in Narimasu, Itabashi-ku, and you can eat there today. MOS started with a trial counter near the south exit of Narimasu Station in March 1972. Mos Food Services was founded on 21 July 1972 by Atsushi Sakurada (Wikipedia, The Shashi).

Don’t expect a flagship. This is a small two-floor shop on a commuter line in residential Itabashi, and that is why I would send you here before Ginza: Japan’s homegrown answer to the American burger started on this corner, and it still runs as an ordinary neighborhood MOS. The company never left Tokyo either. Its head office is in ThinkPark Tower in Osaki, Shinagawa-ku.

Address, phone and how to get to MOS Burger Narimasu

MOS Burger Narimasu sits at 2-15-10 Narimasu, Itabashi-ku, Tokyo 175-0094, phone 03-3975-1582 (+81 3-3975-1582). It is one minute on foot from the south exit of Narimasu Station on the Tobu Tojo Line, at the entrance of the Sukippu Village shopping street. Chikatetsu-Narimasu Station on the Fukutoshin and Yurakucho subway lines is about two minutes away (4travel, Retty).

There is no parking, according to Hot Pepper, so come by train. One local article writes the address as 2-5-10, but every other source gives 2-15-10, which is the one to use.

MOS Burger Narimasu opening hours and breakfast

MOS Burger Narimasu opens every day from 8:00 to 22:00, according to the Itabashi Times in 2026 and Wanderlog (checked 1 November 2026). The morning menu runs until 10:30. Hot Pepper (6:30–1:00) and Retty (7:00–midnight) still list other hours, which look out of date. The official store page could not be loaded when I checked, so confirm on the MOS store search before an early or late trip.

Inside: counter seats, the upstairs room and the 2026 mural

The ground floor has counter seats only; the upper floor has both counter seats and tables. Reviewers mention free Wi-Fi and charging outlets upstairs and describe the room as clean and bright.

On 27 February 2026, a mural covering the whole wall and windows was completed. It is by the illustrator Ebio. (えびを), first-prize winner in the arts category of MOS RECORDS, and shows the Japanese wagtail (hakusekirei), the bird of Itabashi ward (Itabashi Times).

Anniversary burgers: Narimosu Station and the Narimos Double Burger

Narimasu celebrates its MOS heritage with one-off events and store-only burgers. For the chain’s 50th anniversary in 2022, Narimasu Station was temporarily renamed なりもす駅 (Narimosu). A commemorative Narimosu Double Burger sold for ¥660, alongside 2,000 sets of souvenir tickets at ¥1,000 (Time Out Japan).

For the store’s 54th birthday, the Narimos Double Burger came back on 13 and 14 June 2026, at Narimasu only, for ¥770. The first 54 customers each day received a stationery set (Itabashi Times). Planning a pilgrimage? Watch mid-June.

Other MOS Burger branches in Tokyo for tourists

Shibuya Dogenzaka is the most practical branch for a first-time visitor in central Tokyo, Tokyo Tower is the one for its exclusive burger, and Ginza is for the new Mosh concept. With 181 stores in the Tokyo Metropolis, most neighborhoods have their own.

MOS Burger Shibuya Dogenzaka: central and open late

MOS Burger Shibuya Dogenzaka is at 2-29-8 Dogenzaka, Shibuya-ku, five minutes on foot from JR Shibuya Station, phone 03-3461-0548. Its listing gives hours of 7:00 to 1:00, varying by season, and says credit cards are not accepted (Dig Japan). That listing is undated and I could not reconfirm it in 2026, so bring cash and check the hours before a late visit.

MOS Burger Tokyo Tower and the Tokyo Tower Burger

The Tokyo Tower Burger is a 14-layer burger created for MOS Burger Tokyo Tower: two patties, pastrami, onion rings and chili sauce, plus a 15-layer cheese version. The branch opened on 13 March 2015 in the Tokyo Tower Foot Town food court, 4-2-8 Shibakoen, Minato-ku, with hours of 9:00 to 22:00. It is about six minutes on foot from Akabanebashi Station on the Toei Oedo Line (Time Out Tokyo, ASCII).

Be careful here: those sources date from 2015. The latest trace I found is a Wikimedia Commons photo of the counter taken in May 2024, the one on this page. Nothing confirms that the branch is open in 2026, that the Tokyo Tower Burger is still sold, or what it costs today. I would not build a day around it; check the official store search first.

Mosh Burger & Bar in Ginza: MOS goes upscale

Mosh Burger & Bar is MOS’s upscale bar-and-grill concept with table service, opened on 1 July 2026 on the ground floor of Ginza Nine 2 (Ginza 8-7, Chuo-ku). It opens 10:00–22:00 Monday to Thursday and Saturday, 10:00–23:00 on Friday and 10:00–21:00 on public holidays. The Mosh Cheeseburger Set costs ¥1,380 with fries or a mini Caesar salad and a drink; lemonade is +¥220 and an extra patty +¥250 (SoraNews24).

SoraNews24’s tester found the burger surprisingly big for MOS and liked the lemonade made with Setouchi lemons. The complaint was variety: seven of the eight burgers contain cheese (SoraNews24 review). À la carte prices and reservations were not confirmed when I checked. My take: at ¥1,380, the set costs almost as much as three classic MOS Burgers. Go once to see where MOS is heading, not instead of the original.

An earlier Mosh spin-off, Mosh Grab’n Go, opened on 29 November 2022 at 5-16-10 Minami-Azabu, near Hiroo Station, as a cashless cheeseburger counter with self-checkout (BCN Retail). Its current status is unknown, so do not count on it.

What to order at MOS Burger on a first visit

Order the MOS Burger and the Teriyaki Burger first: they are the two classics, and the teriyaki is a piece of Japanese burger history. MOS claims the first teriyaki burger of any Japanese chain, in May 1973. On 18 March 2026, it launched the seventh generation with a new sauce (SoraNews24). If you want to make it at home, our MOS Burger teriyaki burger recipe rebuilds it step by step.

Then try a rice burger, where pressed rice patties replace the bun; the Kaisen Kakiage with seafood tempura is ¥510. If you order one thing no Western chain sells, make it this. At Narimasu, reviewers single out the spicy double cheese MOS and the oni-pote, a mix of onion and potato fries.

Seasonal and Tokyo-only burgers

MOS rotates seasonal and regional burgers, so the menu you find depends on the month. From 14 October to the end of October 2026, Tokyo stores sold the Kanazawa Curry Katsu Burger for ¥550, in limited quantities. It was the third edition of a collaboration with Itabashi ward and Nippon Yakka Daigaku pharmaceutical university, with ¥10 per burger donated to the rebuilding of the Noto Peninsula (Impress Gourmet Watch). It is no longer on sale.

The autumn Tsukimi Focaccia with golden cheese sauce launched nationwide on 27 August 2026 at ¥630; I could not reconfirm it is still sold in November. For more 2026 launches, see our roundup of new burgers in 2026.

Vegetarian and vegan options at MOS Burger Tokyo

MOS Burger has offered a Soy Patty in place of the beef patty since 2015, and the Green Burger, made without any animal ingredients, since 2021 (Business Insider Japan, MOS press release). Availability can vary by store, so check the menu at the counter.

MOS Burger Narimasu scores 4.1/5 on Google from 642 reviews and 3.6/5 on TripAdvisor from 13, according to Wanderlog. It also has 3.34/5 on 4travel (33 reviews) and 83% recommendations on Retty (26 reviewers), all checked on 1 November 2026.

The praise is specific: tender meat, a warm, soft bun, plenty of fresh lettuce, burgers made to order. Staff get credit for being friendly and quick, and the upstairs room for being bright.

The complaints are just as consistent. The room is small, the burgers do not photograph well, the breakfast menu is short, and lunch and weekends get crowded. None of that would put me off. The 4.1 comes from 642 Google reviews, and the TripAdvisor score rests on just 13, so I trust the Google figure more.

MOS Burger vs McDonald’s: what makes the Japanese chain different

MOS Burger differs from McDonald’s mainly in that it is a Japanese chain that cooks to order and built its menu around Japanese flavors. It was founded in Tokyo in 1972, claims Japan’s first chain teriyaki burger in 1973 and sells rice burgers that replace the bun with rice. Made-to-order cooking is also why reviewers mention a wait. MOS Burger Tokyo FAQ

Where is the first MOS Burger, and can you still eat there? Yes. The first MOS Burger is MOS Burger Narimasu, 2-15-10 Narimasu, Itabashi-ku, one minute from the south exit of Narimasu Station on the Tobu Tojo Line. It opened as a trial counter in March 1972 and still serves today.

Which MOS Burger in Tokyo is best for a tourist? Shibuya Dogenzaka is the most central and has the latest hours listed, Mosh Burger & Bar in Ginza is the new upscale experience, and Narimasu is the one for burger history. Tokyo Tower’s status in 2026 is unconfirmed.

How much is a burger at MOS Burger in Tokyo in 2026? Since 15 July 2026, the MOS Burger costs ¥500, the Teriyaki Burger ¥490 and MOS Chicken ¥340, tax included. At Mosh Burger & Bar in Ginza, the Mosh Cheeseburger Set costs ¥1,380.

What time does MOS Burger open, and does it serve breakfast? MOS Burger Narimasu opens daily from 8:00 to 22:00, with a morning menu until 10:30. Hours vary by store: Shibuya Dogenzaka lists 7:00 to 1:00. Check the official store search before you go.

Is there an English menu at MOS Burger in Tokyo? MOS publishes an official English menu on mos.jp, and reviewers report one in Shibuya. I could not confirm one at every store, including Narimasu, so the online menu is a useful backup.

Can you pay by card at MOS Burger in Tokyo? It depends on the store. The Shibuya Dogenzaka listing says cards are not accepted, and no official source confirms payment methods at Narimasu. Carry cash to be safe.

What is the Tokyo Tower Burger? It is a 14-layer burger launched in 2015 at MOS Burger Tokyo Tower, with two patties, pastrami, onion rings and chili sauce; the cheese version has 15 layers. Its availability in 2026 is unconfirmed.

What is Mosh Burger & Bar in Ginza? It is MOS’s upscale bar-and-grill concept with table service, opened on 1 July 2026 at Ginza Nine 2. SoraNews24’s tester found its burgers bigger than standard MOS ones, and the Mosh Cheeseburger Set costs ¥1,380.

Does MOS Burger have vegetarian or vegan options? Yes. The Soy Patty has replaced beef on request since 2015, and the Green Burger, launched in 2021, contains no animal ingredients.

Why is the wait longer at MOS Burger? Burgers are made to order, which reviewers credit for the warm bun and fresh vegetables. At Narimasu, lunch and weekends are the busiest times.
