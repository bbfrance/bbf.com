Five Guys Leicester: Meridian Hours and Prices

Leicester's only Five Guys is now at Meridian Leisure Park: Cheapside has closed. Address, Uber Eats prices, halal facts and reviews, checked 16 Nov 2026.

By Emma, Restaurants writer, United KingdomPublished November 26, 2026

Five Guys Leicester now means one restaurant: Five Guys Leicester Meridian, at Unit A, Meridian Leisure Park, Lubbesthorpe Way, Braunstone Town, Leicester LE19 1JZ (0116 216 8861). It sits on the western edge of the city, by the M1. The old city-centre branch at 1-3 Cheapside has closed for good. I checked both facts on the official Five Guys UK locator on 16 November 2026: of the eight East Midlands restaurants it lists, Meridian is the only one in Leicester.

Plenty of directories still show Cheapside as open, and some still give it as the main Leicester address. They are wrong. Below you will find the Meridian address, delivery prices in pounds, review scores, the halal position and how to get there, so you don’t make a wasted trip into town.

Is Five Guys Cheapside in Leicester city centre closed?

Yes. Five Guys Leicester Cheapside, at 1-3 Cheapside, Leicester LE1 5EB, has closed permanently, and Leicester city centre no longer has a Five Guys.

Three sources agree. Restaurant Guru marks it “Permanently closed”, Uber Eats shows the store as closed, and it has disappeared from the official East Midlands locator. The closing date is another matter. Uber Eats gives two different dates, and I found no announcement from Five Guys or the Leicester Mercury.

It was not a quiet branch. Cheapside held 4.2/5 from 1,491 Google reviews on Restaurant Guru before it shut, and some of its last reviews complained that halal beef had vanished from the menu without warning.

Add it up: a cheeseburger, regular fries and a shake comes to £25.45 delivered. That is a lot for one person, and price is the complaint you hear most about Five Guys in the UK. Reviewers point out that a regular fries feeds two, which softens the blow if you are sharing.

What to order at Five Guys Meridian

Leicester gets the standard Five Guys UK menu: build-your-own burgers, fries cut in the restaurant and hand-spun shakes. I found nothing sold only here.

The chain claims more than 250,000 combinations from its free toppings, and the official Meridian page says there are no freezers on site and every burger is cooked to order. My advice: order the Little Cheeseburger. It has one patty instead of two, costs £2.70 less on Uber Eats, and leaves room to share a bag of fries.

Want to make it at home? My colleague Julia rebuilt the chain’s burgers from its published ingredients: try her Five Guys cheeseburger copycat recipe or the Five Guys Little Cheeseburger copycat.

Vegetarian options

The Grilled Cheese (£6.85) is the one vegetarian dish I can confirm on the Meridian delivery menu. Locaji also says the branch has vegetarian options.

The Veggie Sandwich, the Cheese Veggie Sandwich and the shake mix-ins appeared on the old Cheapside delivery menu. I could not confirm them, or their prices, at Meridian.

Peanut oil and allergies

Five Guys cooks its fries in 100% peanut oil, according to the official Meridian page. If you have a peanut allergy, tell the staff before you order.

Is Five Guys Leicester halal?

No, as far as the official record goes. On 16 November 2026, Five Guys UK’s halal information page does not list Leicester Meridian among the restaurants serving halal beef.

That page names three HCO-certified restaurants (Birmingham Fort, London Park Royal and Manchester Arndale) and 28 more that serve halal beef cooked on grills kept separate from bacon. Every other restaurant in England, Scotland and Wales is “not Halal certified and do[es] not serve Halal beef”.

Older listings muddy the picture. Zabihah still quotes the management saying halal certified beef was served at both Meridian and Cheapside, and Cheapside’s last reviews describe halal beef being withdrawn overnight. In Leicester, that question matters to a lot of diners. Ask at the counter before ordering. The nearest restaurant on the official halal list is in Birmingham.

What reviewers like

The burgers built exactly as ordered and the milkshakes come up again and again. Reviewers also mention fresh, crispy fries, the free peanuts and helpful staff. On the brand-wide Trustpilot page, one reviewer singled out an employee, Hasan, for staying polite and fast while working alone; the review does not make clear which branch it was.

What reviewers criticise

Price, first. On Locaji, some reviewers say quality has slipped: fries not always cooked to order, a shorter menu, overflowing bins. On Trustpilot, again without a confirmed branch, one customer repeated their order three times and still got the wrong food, burnt fries and missing toppings.

My reading: Meridian gets the Five Guys basics right most of the time, but you pay for them. Ask for your fries fresh, check the bag before you leave, and order click & collect rather than delivery.

Delivery: Uber Eats and Deliveroo

Five Guys Leicester Meridian delivers through Uber Eats, and the official page lists delivery and click & collect among its services.

When I checked Uber Eats on 16 November 2026, delivery was switched off at that moment, and Deliveroo would not load. Click & collect through the Five Guys website or app skips the delivery mark-up and the wait.

How to get to Meridian Leisure Park

Meridian Leisure Park is on Lubbesthorpe Way (A563) in Braunstone Town, with a free car park that includes disabled spaces. Five Guys is in Unit A.

Directories list bus stops at Meridian Leisure Park, Tiber Way and Withers Way, with line numbers I could not confirm with the operators. Check a journey planner before you travel. Honestly, this is a place you drive to.

Meridian is built for a night out: the park also has a Vue cinema, a Hollywood Bowl, Frankie & Benny’s, Nando’s and Harvester. Fancy a comparison? Burger King is in Unit E of the same park.

Wheelchair access

The sources disagree on step-free access, so call before you visit. TripAligner says the restaurant is wheelchair accessible and has high chairs. A review summarised by Wanderlog mentions “lots of steps inside without a lift”. The official page says nothing either way.

Five Guys in the UK: a short history

Five Guys was founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell and their sons. Its first UK restaurant opened on Long Acre, Covent Garden, on 4 July 2013.

The UK is now the chain’s largest market outside the United States, with 182 locations on the official UK locator in October 2026. Worldwide, Five Guys had 1,999 restaurants in 28 countries and territories in May 2026, according to Wikipedia. FAQ: Five Guys Leicester

How many Five Guys are there in Leicester? One. Five Guys Leicester Meridian, at Unit A, Meridian Leisure Park, Braunstone Town, LE19 1JZ, is the only Leicester branch on the official locator (checked 16 November 2026).

Is Five Guys on Cheapside still open? No. Five Guys Leicester Cheapside, 1-3 Cheapside, LE1 5EB, has closed permanently. Its closing date has not been published.

What are Five Guys Leicester’s opening times? Directories agree on 11:00 to 22:00 from Sunday to Thursday but disagree on Friday and Saturday (23:00 or 00:30). Ring 0116 216 8861 to confirm.

Is Five Guys Leicester halal? No halal beef is listed for Leicester Meridian on Five Guys UK’s official halal page as of 16 November 2026. Ask staff in the restaurant before ordering.

How much is a burger at Five Guys Leicester? On Uber Eats, a Hamburger costs £11.25 and a Cheeseburger £12.85 (16 November 2026). In-store prices may be lower.

Is parking free at Five Guys Meridian? Yes. Meridian Leisure Park has a free car park with disabled spaces.

Can I book a table at Five Guys Leicester? No. Five Guys Meridian does not take reservations. You can order ahead with click & collect.

Does Five Guys use peanut oil? Yes. Five Guys cooks its fries in 100% peanut oil. If you have a peanut allergy, talk to staff before ordering.
