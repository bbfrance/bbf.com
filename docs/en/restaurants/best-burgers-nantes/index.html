Best Burgers in Nantes: 10 Restaurants for 2026

Best burgers in Nantes for 2026: Dubrown, Back Bay, Le Zed, Le CookTown, MADE and 5 more, with addresses, signature burgers, prices and an FAQ.

By Thomas, Restaurants writer, FrancePublished November 19, 2026

The best burgers in Nantes are at Dubrown, Back Bay, Le Zed, Le CookTown and MADE. If you only have time for one, go to Dubrown on rue Saint-Léonard, which has baked its own buns since 2013. Back Bay on Île Feydeau is the city’s reference for an American cheeseburger. Le Zed suits a table where vegetarians and meat eaters sit together, Le CookTown makes everything from scratch, and MADE does the upscale version of the smash burger. Below are all 10 restaurants with their full address, neighbourhood and signature burger, then a summary table and an FAQ.

Most Nantes burger lists are out of date. The one press ranking I found still names Le Melting Potes, which closed for good on 27 June 2026 after 16 years. It is not in this guide. For other French cities, see my guides to the best burgers in Bordeaux, Toulouse and Lyon. For the global picture, see the world’s best burgers in 2026.

How I ranked the best burger restaurants in Nantes

I built this ranking from 4 kinds of evidence, all checked on 16 November 2026: each restaurant’s own website or Instagram, the local food press, rankeat’s November 2026 ranking (based on Google ratings and review counts), and directories such as Restoranking and HappyCow. Every Google score below is the figure rankeat reported. I have not rounded or averaged any of them. Prices are in euros, each with its source and date.

Google ratings of Nantes burger restaurants reported by rankeat in November 2026: Shake Burger 4.79/5 from 2,337 reviews, Le Zed République 4.78/5 from 672, Back Bay 4.77/5 from 482 and Le CookTown 4.75/5 from 289 (Chart: Burger Breton, data from rankeat (November 2026, https://rankeat.fr/trouver-le-meilleur-restaurant-de/france/nantes/pour/burger); rendered with QuickChart, 3200×1600. The es body gives 678 reviews for Le Zed, while en/fr and this chart give 672: align the es text.)

I left out every place whose address or opening status I could not verify. That includes Patty Finest Burgers, La Ripaille, SoFresh Burger, Petit Apeti, Le B. and Berlin 1989. I also ignored the ‘burger’ results in the big directories that are not burger restaurants at all. Rankeat’s November list puts a brunch spot first and a tacos-kebab snack bar ninth.

For some addresses, my most recent dated source is older than 2026: Roadside (2016), PNY (2023), Junk (2024) and the two Big Fernand branches. The summary table gives the date of each source. Check the restaurant’s Google listing or Instagram before you go.

The 10 best burger restaurants in Nantes

1. Dubrown: the pioneer of the quality burger in Nantes

Dubrown is my first pick in Nantes because it gets the bun right, and a bun can make or break a burger. The buns are baked every day in the restaurant’s own workshop, and the beef is French. The house motto is ‘No bullshit, just burger’. The local guide Les Tables de Nantes calls Dubrown a ‘pioneer’ of the quality burger in the city, and it has been doing it since 2013.

The signature burger is the Chelsea, served on a homemade potato bun. Set-menu (formule) prices on the official site, checked on 16 November 2026: Chelsea €16.00, Dubacon €15.95, Veggy €16.00, Ducheese €15.50 and Black Firebird €16.00. Every set menu is within 50 cents of €16. Les Tables de Nantes gives a range of €13 to €16 at lunch and €16 to €23 at dinner.

How much a burger costs in Nantes in 2026: €13–16 at lunch and €16–23 at dinner at Dubrown (Les Tables de Nantes), €15.50–16.00 for Dubrown’s set menus (official site, 16 November 2026) and €22–23 for Le Zed’s Thursday all-you-can-eat burger night (Chart: Burger Breton, data from dubrownnantes.com (16 November 2026), Les Tables de Nantes and Le Zed; rendered with QuickChart, 3200×1600)

  • Address: 12 rue Saint-Léonard, 44000 Nantes (city centre, near Bouffay)
  • Phone: +33 9 51 25 90 14
  • Website: dubrownnantes.com · Instagram
  • Opening hours: sources disagree. Les Tables de Nantes says it is open every day, but the official site lists it as closed on Sunday and Monday. Check before you go.

2. Back Bay: the American burger on Île Feydeau

Back Bay is where I would send anyone looking for a classic American cheeseburger in Nantes. On 28 April 2026, Big City Nantes called it the ‘institution of the American burger in Nantes’, even though it only opened in 2021. The founder, Tom Proux, trained at the Ferrandi school in Paris. On the menu: a Big Bacon Cheeseburger, a Pulled Pork and a Chicken BLT.

In rankeat’s November 2026 ranking, Back Bay is rated 4.77/5 from 482 Google reviews, fourth among Nantes burger restaurants. My full Back Bay Nantes review with menu and prices goes through the menu dish by dish.

  • Address: 11 quai Turenne, 44000 Nantes (Île Feydeau, Commerce tram stop)
  • Phone: +33 7 49 70 83 14
  • Website: backbay.fr · Instagram @backbay_nantes
  • Opening hours: the website lists lunch service only, Tuesday to Thursday and Saturday, closed Friday. That schedule is unusual, so call to confirm.

3. Le Zed: vegetarian and vegan burgers on Île de Nantes

Le Zed is the place for a group where half the table eats meat and half does not. It has served burgers and brunch since 2010, with plenty of vegetarian and vegan options. On Thursday evenings it runs an all-you-can-eat burger night for €22 to €23.

Rankeat’s November 2026 ranking lists ‘Le Zed République’ third, rated 4.78/5 from 672 Google reviews. Mapstr gives the address below on Île de Nantes. I could not confirm whether these are 1 restaurant or 2.

  • Address: 23 boulevard de la Prairie-au-Duc, 44200 Nantes (Île de Nantes)
  • Website: restaurant-le-zed.fr
  • Want to cook 1 at home? Try our homemade veggie burger recipe.

Tree-lined boulevard de la Prairie-au-Duc on Île de Nantes, the street where Le Zed serves vegetarian and vegan burgers (illustrative photo of the street, not of the restaurant) (Photo: Pj44300, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BoulevarddelaPrairie-au-Duc.jpg (2848×2136))

4. Le CookTown: a homemade burger in Nantes, evenings only

Le CookTown is the answer if you want a burger where everything is made in-house, the fries and chicken tenders included. It is tiny. There is no dining room, only a small terrace, so on a rainy Nantes evening plan on takeaway. Directories list it as open Tuesday to Saturday, 6:45 pm to 10 pm.

In rankeat’s November 2026 ranking, it is rated 4.75/5 from 289 Google reviews, fifth in the city. No primary source names a signature burger, so I don’t name 1 either.

  • Address: 16 rue Léon Blum, 44000 Nantes
  • Phone: +33 9 83 06 71 55
  • Website: lecooktown.fr

5. MADE: the upscale smash burger near Bouffay

MADE (Made Burger) is the upscale end of the Nantes smash burger: gorgonzola on one, Mont d’Or cheese on another. The sauces, fries and desserts come out of a test kitchen on site. According to Le Bonbon in February 2026, it is open 7 days a week.

Fanny Dubus and Jonathan Pares started MADE as a food truck in Perpignan in 2014. The Nantes restaurant replaced a former Pitaya, and its opening was announced on 21 January 2025.

  • Address: 23 rue des Carmes, 44000 Nantes (city centre, Bouffay)
  • Source: Le Bonbon Nantes, 11 February 2026

6. Glasgow Burgers: the best vegan burger in Nantes

Glasgow Burgers is the best vegan burger in Nantes, and the only address on this list where the whole menu is plant-based. Its best-seller, the ‘Glasgow’, stacks a vegan patty with onion rings and a whisky sauce. HappyCow lists it, and the blog Bruges Vegan reviewed it on 20 January 2026.

  • Address: 1 rue des Échevins, Nantes (Bouffay)
  • Website: glasgow-burgers.fr

7. Junk: the smash burger chain’s first Pays de la Loire address

Junk is the straightforward choice for a classic smash burger in Nantes. It opened on 21 November 2024 as the chain’s 18th restaurant and its first in the Pays de la Loire region. The dining room is small: 20 seats.

Junk started in Paris, and I have covered it there and in Lyon. For the menu and how the chain works, see my review of Junk’s smash burgers in Paris and my Junk Lyon guide. To make 1 at home, try our Sonic bacon smasher copycat.

  • Address: 11 rue des Vieilles Douves, 44000 Nantes (Bouffay)
  • Source: Le Bonbon Nantes, opening report

8. PNY: the Paris New York burger in Graslin

PNY (Paris New York) brought its Paris burgers to Nantes on 1 June 2023. The cheddar is English and aged for 6 months, and the team says it took 50 attempts to get the bun right. A burger cost about €13 at opening. That is a 2023 price, so check the current menu.

  • Address: 1 rue Gresset, 44000 Nantes (Graslin)
  • Sources: Le Bonbon Nantes and Yonder, 2023

Place Graslin in Nantes, with its fountain and the Théâtre Graslin: the Graslin quarter is home to PNY on rue Gresset and Big Fernand on rue Jean-Jacques-Rousseau (Photo: Wolfgang Moroder, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Place\_Graslin\_et\_Th%C3%A9%C3%A2tre\_Graslin\_a\_Nantes.jpg (6435×4912))

9. Roadside: the American diner between Commerce and Feydeau

Roadside is the American diner of the list. It opened in May 2016 as the first franchise of a concept created in Rennes by Josselin Galle. The signature burger is the Spicy El Paso, with guacamole and chipotle sauce. It ranks ninth only because my latest dated source is from 2016, so confirm it is still open before you go.

  • Address: 1 allée Flesselles, 44000 Nantes (Commerce and Feydeau)
  • Sources: Le Bonbon Nantes (2016) and Le Journal des Entreprises

10. Big Fernand: the French burger chain, twice in the city centre

Big Fernand is the practical choice rather than the exciting one. The French chain has 2 city-centre branches in Nantes and an outlet at Nantes-Atlantique airport, which makes it handy for a last burger before a flight. The Big Fernand chain profile covers its history and figures.

  • Bouffay: 5/7 rue des Halles, 44000 Nantes
  • Graslin: 8 rue Jean-Jacques Rousseau, 44000 Nantes
  • Airport: Nantes-Atlantique, public area

Halal burgers in Nantes and other honourable mentions

Black and White Burger is the address for a certified halal burger in Nantes, with beef and chicken smash burgers. The YouTubers Ibra TV and Djam founded the chain in Paris in 2018. It has 2 Nantes restaurants: 4 rue de Gorges (Commerce) and the Beaulieu shopping centre on Île de Nantes.

Shake Burger, at 140 boulevard Ernest-Dalby, is rated 4.79/5 from 2,337 Google reviews, second on rankeat’s November 2026 list. That is more reviews than the next 3 on that list combined. I still keep it in reserve: no primary source confirmed its menu or a signature burger.

Ça va Smasher serves smash burgers made with halal Black Angus beef. It opened in March 2025 at 8 rue Victor-Schœlcher, in the Food Kourt of the Atlantis area. That is in Saint-Herblain, just outside the city of Nantes, so it is not in the ranking.

Le Melting Potes has closed

Le Melting Potes, the burger restaurant on boulevard de la Prairie-au-Duc, closed for good on 27 June 2026 and was sold on 1 July 2026, according to Le Bonbon Nantes. Owned by Marc Barathon, it had opened in 2010. Any directory or top list that still names it is out of date.

FAQ: burgers in Nantes

Where can I eat the best burger in Nantes? Go to Dubrown (12 rue Saint-Léonard), the city’s quality-burger pioneer, which has baked its buns in-house since 2013. For an American cheeseburger, go to Back Bay (11 quai Turenne). Le Zed, Le CookTown and MADE complete the top 5.

What is the best smash burger in Nantes? Junk (11 rue des Vieilles Douves) serves the classic version. MADE (23 rue des Carmes) does upscale smash burgers with gorgonzola. Just outside the city, Ça va Smasher in Saint-Herblain smashes halal Black Angus beef.

Where can I find a vegetarian or vegan burger in Nantes? Glasgow Burgers (1 rue des Échevins) is 100% vegan. Le Zed on Île de Nantes has many vegetarian and vegan burgers. Dubrown also has a Veggy burger, at €16.00 as a set menu.

Where can I eat a halal burger in Nantes? Black and White Burger serves certified halal beef and chicken smash burgers at 4 rue de Gorges and in the Beaulieu shopping centre. Ça va Smasher in Saint-Herblain uses halal Black Angus beef.

How much does a burger cost in a Nantes restaurant in 2026? At Dubrown, a burger set menu costs €15.50 to €16.00 (official prices checked on 16 November 2026). Les Tables de Nantes gives €13 to €16 at lunch and €16 to €23 at dinner there. Le Zed’s Thursday all-you-can-eat burger night costs €22 to €23.

Is Le Melting Potes still open? No. Le Melting Potes closed for good on 27 June 2026 and was sold on 1 July 2026.

Which burger restaurants are open on Sunday in Nantes? MADE was reported open 7 days a week in February 2026. Le CookTown is closed on Sunday. Dubrown’s Sunday opening is unclear, so check before you go.

Where can I eat a burger near the Château des ducs de Bretagne or Bouffay? Bouffay has the densest cluster: Dubrown, MADE, Junk, Glasgow Burgers and the Big Fernand on rue des Halles. Back Bay and Roadside are a short walk away, around the Commerce tram stop.

Which burger restaurant in Nantes makes everything in-house? Le CookTown, fries and tenders included. Dubrown bakes its own buns every day, and MADE makes its sauces, fries and desserts in its on-site test kitchen.
