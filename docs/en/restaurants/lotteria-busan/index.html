Lotteria Busan: Hours, Menu and Prices by Branch

Lotteria Busan Station is open 24/7 and Nampo until 2 a.m. most nights: addresses, Korean names for Naver Map, the ₩5,100 bulgogi burger and reviews.

By Hana, Restaurants writer, Japan and IndonesiaPublished November 27, 2026

The most useful Lotteria in Busan is Lotteria Busan Station (롯데리아 부산역점), across the street from Busan Station at 201 Jungang-daero. It is open 24 hours a day, though Diningcode reports a possible break from 4 to 5 a.m. For a late night in Seomyeon, the branch at 46 Seomyeon-ro is also listed as open 24/7. Near Nampo and BIFF Square, Lotteria Busan Nampo is open until 2 a.m. from Sunday to Thursday. Since May 28, 2026, the signature Ria Bulgogi burger costs ₩5,100 on its own, the national price. I checked every address, phone number and opening time below on November 17, 2026.

Lotteria Busan at a glance: which branch to choose

Pick the branch by where you are and what time it is: Busan Station for KTX arrivals and departures, Seomyeon for nightlife, Nampo for BIFF Square and Jagalchi. Lotteria is Korea’s biggest burger chain, with 1,323 restaurants nationwide, so Busan has plenty of them. I kept only the five I could pin to a full address in at least one directory listing.

If you only remember one thing: search 롯데리아 부산역점 for the station branch. English names often fail in Korean map apps. The Korean name doesn’t.

Lotteria Busan Station: the 24-hour branch opposite the KTX station

Lotteria Busan Station sits at 201 Jungang-daero in Dong-gu, facing Busan Station, and it is the branch travelers search for most. It occupies the Haengwun Wedding Hall building (cadastral address: Choryang-dong 1208-17). The phone number is 051-466-7070, or +82 51 466 7070 from abroad, and it matches on Diningcode, Trip.com, Triple and Wanderlog.

Lotteria Busan Station opening hours

Lotteria Busan Station is open 24 hours a day, every day. Wanderlog, Trip.com and Triple all list it as 24/7. Only Diningcode mentions a break from 4 to 5 a.m., so if you step off a very early train, expect a short wait in that window.

The Lotteria inside Busan Station has closed

The Lotteria counter on the third floor inside Busan Station (206 Jungang-daero) is marked permanently closed on Wanderlog. It used to open around 5:30 a.m. and still shows up in searches, with a 3.5/5 Google rating from 26 reviews. Don’t go looking for it inside the terminal. Cross the street to 201 Jungang-daero instead.

What reviewers say about Lotteria Busan Station

Lotteria Busan Station holds 3.6/5 on Google from 796 reviews (as shown on Wanderlog), 3.7/5 on Diningcode from 10 reviews and 3.6/5 on Trip.com from 7 reviews. On Google, people like that the burgers are made to order, and they keep naming the shrimp burger, the mozzarella sticks and the value sets. The staff get a word too. The recurring complaint is slow service. Korean diners on Diningcode say the opposite about speed and add a clean dining room and low prices, but they find the patties thin and the station crowds tiring. Trip.com reviewers mention crispy fries and, again, the shrimp burger. Delivery is available; there is no parking. My read of 796 reviews: a dependable stop before a train, not a destination.

Lotteria Seomyeon: late-night burgers in Busan’s nightlife district

Lotteria Seomyeon (롯데리아 서면일번가점) is at 46 Seomyeon-ro, on the ground floor, in Busanjin-gu, and Diningcode lists it as open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. That claim rests on a single source. I’d check Naver Map before walking over at 3 a.m. The phone number, 051-817-0005, comes from the Korean directory 114.co.kr, and the cadastral address is Bujeon-dong 242-6.

Seomyeon Rotary at night, the heart of Busan’s Seomyeon nightlife district, a few minutes’ walk from the 24-hour Lotteria Seomyeon at 46 Seomyeon-ro (September 2018) (Photo: Christophe95, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Seomyeon\_Rotary.jpg)

Its Diningcode rating is 3.8/5, from just 3 reviews. They like the crispy hash browns, the fast service and the fair prices. They don’t like the small dining room or the untidy trash area. Delivery and takeout are available, with no parking.

This is also the branch where Diningcode lists the Rice Burger Jeonju Bibimbap, a set at ₩8,400 (undated price). A burger built on Jeonju bibimbap, Korea’s mixed-rice classic, is what I’d order here before any beef patty.

Lotteria Seomyeon Underground Shopping Center

A second Seomyeon branch, Lotteria Seomyeon Underground Shopping Center (롯데리아 서면지하상가점), sits in the Gayaro underground mall at B1, 777 Gaya-daero, Central Mall unit J-21. Its phone number is +82 51 802 9480. I could not confirm its opening hours, and it has no reviews on Triple, so don’t plan a late meal around it.

Lotteria Nampo: open until 2 a.m. on BIFF Square

Lotteria Busan Nampo (롯데리아 부산남포점) is at 17 BIFF gwangjang-ro in Jung-gu, right on BIFF Square in Nampo-dong. It opens at 8 a.m. every day and closes at 2 a.m. from Sunday to Thursday, but at midnight on Friday and Saturday, according to Diningcode. Autoreserve confirms the 8 a.m. to midnight schedule on Fridays and Saturdays. Yes, the weekend closes earlier: don’t count on it after midnight on a Saturday.

Its only Diningcode review gives it 5.0/5, which tells you little on its own. That reviewer liked the two ordering kiosks, which keep the wait short, the spacious dining room and the friendly staff. The phone number is 051-241-2881 (cadastral address: Bupyeong-dong 1-ga 48-1).

Close by, Lotteria Lotte Gwangbok (롯데리아 롯데광복점) is in the basement (B1) of the Aqua Mall at Lotte Department Store Gwangbok, 2 Jungang-daero, Jung-gu. I have an address for it but no verified hours.

Other Lotteria branches in Busan: what I could not confirm

Some Lotteria Busan listings that rank in search are unverified or likely closed, so I left them out of the table. Lotteria Busan Jeonpo (롯데리아 부산전포점), in Busanjin-gu about 330 m from Jeonpo station, is listed by Autoreserve as open from 8 a.m. to midnight, but I could not get its full address or phone number. Lotteria Homeplus Asiad in Yeonje-gu appears only on Autoreserve, at a time when Homeplus has announced 37 store closures in 2026.

I also could not verify a Lotteria open at Haeundae Beach. A Lotteria was listed at the Homeplus Haeundae store, but that site is slated for demolition and auction, so the branch is very likely closed. I found no confirmed Lotteria at Gimhae International Airport either.

What to order at Lotteria in Busan: the bulgogi burger first

Order the Ria Bulgogi burger first: it is Lotteria’s Korean signature, launched in 1992, and at ₩5,100 it is the cheapest way to taste what sets the chain apart. My next pick is the Ria Shrimp burger, the item Busan Station reviewers mention most on Google and Trip.com. Add seasoned potatoes (양념감자) on the side, a Korean fast-food staple.

For the premium version, the Hanwoo Bulgogi Burger set uses Korean native beef and comes to ₩12,600 at Nampo per Diningcode. That is more than double the Ria Bulgogi on its own. For something you won’t find at a Western chain, try the Rice Burger Jeonju Bibimbap at Seomyeon. To cook a Lotteria classic at home, my colleague Clara’s Lotteria double cheeseburger copycat recipe rebuilds it step by step.

Ordering, delivery and the Lotte Eatz app

Lotteria’s official app and online ordering platform is Lotte Eatz (lotteeatz.com). Diningcode reports delivery at both Busan Station and Seomyeon (46 Seomyeon-ro), plus takeout at Seomyeon. Neither branch has parking. I could not verify kiosk languages, foreign card acceptance, Kakao Pay or Samsung Pay, wheelchair access or which delivery apps cover these branches, so I’m leaving them out rather than guessing.

Lotteria’s story: a Japanese-born chain that became Korea’s burger leader

Lotteria is a Lotte group chain born in Japan and grown in Korea: Shin Kyuk-ho, the founder of Lotte, opened it in Tokyo on February 2, 1972. The first Korean restaurant opened at Sogong in Seoul on October 25, 1979, and the Ria Bulgogi followed in 1992. Today Lotte GRS runs the chain, with 1,323 restaurants in South Korea and more than 1,650 across 8 countries, according to Chowhound.

A Lotteria storefront in Changnyeong, South Gyeongsang Province, the province next to Busan, in January 2007: an example of the chain’s Korean network, now 1,323 restaurants run by Lotte GRS (illustrative photo, not taken at a Busan branch) (Photo: Visviva, public domain, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Changnyeong\_Lotteria.jpg)

Lotteria now has its sights on 19 more countries in the Middle East, South America and Oceania, including India, Saudi Arabia, Brazil, Mexico, Australia and New Zealand, through master franchises. It opened in Singapore in February 2026 and signed a partnership in Thailand in June 2026, Newsway reported on November 9, 2026. The full picture is on the Lotteria brand page and in our coverage of international burger chain expansion in 2026.

Lotteria Busan FAQ

Where is the closest Lotteria to Busan KTX station, and is it open at night? Lotteria Busan Station is at 201 Jungang-daero, directly across from Busan Station. It is open 24 hours a day, with a possible 4–5 a.m. break according to Diningcode.

Is the Lotteria inside Busan Station still open? No. The third-floor Lotteria inside the station (206 Jungang-daero) is marked permanently closed on Wanderlog. Use the branch across the street at 201 Jungang-daero.

Is there a 24-hour Lotteria in Busan? Yes. Lotteria Busan Station is listed as 24/7 by four sources. Lotteria Seomyeon at 46 Seomyeon-ro is listed as 24/7 by Diningcode alone.

How much is a bulgogi burger at Lotteria in Busan in 2026? The Ria Bulgogi has cost ₩5,100 on its own since May 28, 2026, the national price. Diningcode lists the Ria Bulgogi set at ₩8,900 at Nampo, an undated price.

What should I order at Lotteria in Korea if I’ve never been? Start with the Ria Bulgogi, Lotteria’s signature since 1992, then the Ria Shrimp and a side of seasoned potatoes (양념감자). At Seomyeon, the Rice Burger Jeonju Bibimbap is the most Korean item on the menu.

Can I order in English on Lotteria kiosks and pay with a foreign card? I could not verify kiosk languages or foreign card acceptance for the Busan branches. To be safe, have a backup way to pay.

Is there a Lotteria in Haeundae or at Gimhae Airport? I could not confirm either. The Homeplus Haeundae branch is very likely closed because the site is slated for demolition, and no Lotteria is confirmed at Gimhae International Airport.

Is Lotteria Korean or Japanese? Both. Lotte’s founder opened the chain in Tokyo in 1972, and it reached Seoul in 1979. Korea is now its largest market, with 1,323 restaurants run by Lotte GRS.

Does Lotteria have a vegetarian option in Korea? Lotteria launched the Ria Miracle Burger in 2020, with a 100% plant-based patty and bulgogi sauce. I could not confirm that it is still on the menu in 2026, so check the Lotte Eatz app before you go.

Lotteria or McDonald’s in Busan: which should I choose? Choose Lotteria for the burgers you can only get in Korea: the Ria Bulgogi, the Ria Shrimp and the rice burgers. Near Busan Station, the 24-hour opening makes it the easy call at any hour.
