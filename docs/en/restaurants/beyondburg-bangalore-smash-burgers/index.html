Beyondburg Bangalore: Smash Burgers, Founders and What to Order

Who runs Beyondburg, what to order on Palace Road and how it compares with Smash Guys, Bangalore's other smash burger name. Sourced and dated.

By AlexPublished October 8, 2026

Beyondburg is a smash burger restaurant in Prestige Trade Tower on Palace Road, Bengaluru, with a cloud kitchen in Koramangala for delivery. Chef Mohammed Anas co-founded it with Ajmal Jaseem. Order the Smash Burger first. On paper, this is the most ambitious smash burger kitchen in Bangalore: it says it bakes its brioche buns in-house every day, makes its own sauces and smokes its own brisket. Two caveats up front. I haven’t eaten there for this piece, and I couldn’t find a dated menu price or confirm that Palace Road is open this month. I checked everything below against press coverage on 8 October 2026.

Who founded Beyondburg? Chef Mohammed Anas and Ajmal Jaseem

Beyondburg Inc. was co-founded by chef Mohammed Anas and Ajmal Jaseem (Restaurant India, 16 May 2024). News reports spell his first name Mohammed. A sponsored Forbes India feature uses Muhammed. None of my sources says where he trained. I’d rather leave that gap than fill it with a guess.

The most telling detail comes from that Forbes India Brand Connect feature (sponsored, 29 July 2026): Beyondburg has taken no outside funding and puts its profits back into the business. Anas puts it this way: “We’d rather reinvest into making every restaurant better than expand faster than we’re ready for.” I like that line. It’s also the brand describing itself, and so is the feature’s count of six restaurants across South India, which I couldn’t confirm.

Where did Beyondburg start? The origin story doesn’t add up yet

Beyondburg’s first city and founding year are disputed, so I’m not stating either as fact. The Balcony Stories (December 2024) says the brand began as a Bengaluru kiosk in 2017. Restaurant India (May 2024) treats Bengaluru as an expansion after Kochi and Kozhikode. Both can’t be right. What is confirmed: Beyondburg operates in Kochi, Kozhikode and Bengaluru. In 2024 it announced Chennai, Hyderabad and Mumbai (The Balcony Stories), and I found no proof that any of those has opened yet. It has also run a two-day pop-up at Sienna in Kolkata (t2 Online).

How Beyondburg makes its smash burgers

Beyondburg says it makes nearly everything in-house: brioche baked every day, its own sauces, brisket slow-smoked in its own kitchen, and meat bought from specialty butchers across India (The Balcony Stories; Forbes India Brand Connect, sponsored). Those are the brand’s words, not my inspection. Still, they’re specific enough to be checked, and they’re the right things to claim. A bun baked that morning and brisket smoked on site are exactly what separate a craft burger bar from a frozen-patty chain.

What is a smash burger?

A smash burger is a ball of ground beef pressed hard and thin onto a very hot flat-top, so the surface caramelises into a crisp, lacy crust before the patty is flipped and topped with cheese. Thin double patties need a soft bun that squashes around them. That’s why brioche is the natural partner.

The brand also talks up organic vegetables, plastic-free operations, and Asian, vindaloo and Japanese-pickling flavours. All of that comes from the brand or from sponsored content. Treat it as a pitch until someone checks.

Beyondburg Palace Road menu: what to order

Order the Smash Burger on a first visit. It’s the signature, and the kitchen is built around it. Press coverage of the Bengaluru menu lists these items (Restaurant India; LBB):

  • Smash Burger: the signature.
  • Classic Swiss Cheese Burger
  • Nashville Fried Chicken Burger
  • Brisket Whopper: built on the slow-smoked brisket.
  • Pastrami Sandwich
  • Animal Style Fries: loaded fries with caramelised onions and minced tenderloin or chicken.
  • New York cheese bagels
  • Burnt Basque cheesecake

If you’re ordering a second dish, I’d make it the Brisket Whopper. It’s the one item that tests the smoker, and the smoker is Beyondburg’s boldest claim. Menus change, and no source gives dated rupee prices, so I’m not printing any.

Beyondburg vs Smash Guys: the smash burger in Bangalore

Beyondburg and Smash Guys are the two names that come up for smash burgers in Bangalore, and they’re built differently. Beyondburg is a multi-city South Indian brand with its own bakery and smoker. Smash Guys is a single Indiranagar diner from seasoned restaurateurs and a classically trained chef. I haven’t scored them against each other: I don’t have verified prices and dated tasting notes for both.

My take: go to Beyondburg for the whole kitchen (brisket, pastrami, house bakes). Go to Smash Guys if you want one thing done well on a night out in Indiranagar. An Oklahoma Smash, with onions pressed into the beef, is a narrower and more purist idea of the genre.

Where Beyondburg fits among the best burgers in Bangalore

Beyondburg is part of a newer wave of craft smash burger places in a city whose burger history goes back decades. The Only Place has served its Beef and Lamb Whopper since the 1960s (Explocity). Burgerman, founded by Sunil Cherian, reached Bengaluru in December 2009 (Deccan Herald). I haven’t checked that those older addresses still trade. FAQ: Beyondburg and smash burgers in Bangalore

Who founded Beyondburg and where did it start? Chef Mohammed Anas and Ajmal Jaseem co-founded Beyondburg. Sources disagree on where it started: one says a Bengaluru kiosk in 2017, another puts Kochi and Kozhikode first.

Where is Beyondburg in Bangalore and what are its timings? The dine-in restaurant is in Prestige Trade Tower on Palace Road, with a cloud kitchen in Koramangala. Reported hours are 11am–11pm Monday to Friday and 11am–1am at weekends. The listing is undated, so check before you go.

What is Beyondburg’s signature burger? The Smash Burger, served on a brioche bun the brand says it bakes in-house every day.

What is a smash burger, and where can I eat one in Bangalore? It’s a beef patty pressed thin on a hot griddle until it forms a crisp, caramelised crust. In Bangalore, the two names to know are Beyondburg on Palace Road and Smash Guys in Indiranagar.

Beyondburg or Smash Guys: which is better? It depends on what you want. Beyondburg has the bigger menu, with smoked brisket, pastrami and house-baked brioche. Smash Guys is a focused smash diner with an Oklahoma Smash on a milk bun.

How much does a burger cost at Beyondburg Bangalore? I couldn’t find a dated, verified menu price, so I’m not quoting one. Check the current menu on Zomato or at the counter.

Does Beyondburg serve beef, and are there vegetarian options? Yes, beef and other meats are on the menu: the Brisket Whopper, the pastrami and the minced-tenderloin Animal Style Fries. Beyondburg says it buys from specialty butchers. None of my sources lists a vegetarian burger, so ask the restaurant.
