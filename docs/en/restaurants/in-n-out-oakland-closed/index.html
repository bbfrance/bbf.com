In-N-Out Oakland: Closed Since 2024, Where to Go

Oakland's only In-N-Out closed on March 24, 2024. Why it shut, the nearest In-N-Outs, and the Habit Burger in Fruitvale that's still open.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 26, 2026

In-N-Out has no restaurant in Oakland anymore. Its only location, at 8300 Oakport St. near I-880 and Hegenberger Road, closed for good on Sunday, March 24, 2024. No reopening has been announced. The closest In-N-Out is across the estuary in Alameda, at 555 Willie Stargell Ave. If you want a flame-grilled burger from a California chain without leaving Oakland, go to Habit Burger & Grill at 3060 E. 9th St. in Fruitvale.

I checked every address and set of hours below on November 17, 2026, against In-N-Out’s official store pages, local press and review sites. I have not eaten at the Fruitvale Habit for this piece, so the verdict further down leans on those sources and says so. Where I could not confirm something, such as Habit Oakland’s daily hours or its phone number, I leave it out rather than guess.

Is In-N-Out in Oakland still open?

No. The In-N-Out at 8300 Oakport St., Oakland, closed permanently on March 24, 2024, and the chain has announced no return to the city. It was In-N-Out’s store No. 193 and had opened in 2005, just off Interstate 880 near Hegenberger Road (ABC7 News).

In-N-Out announced the closure in January 2024, and it was a first: the chain had never closed a restaurant in its 75-year history (KTVU, NBC). The old official page for store No. 193 now redirects to the Hayward location.

Why did In-N-Out close in Oakland?

In-N-Out closed its Oakland restaurant because of crime, according to the company. Denny Warnick, its chief operating officer, said: “Despite taking repeated steps to create safer conditions, our Customers and Associates are regularly victimized by car break-ins, property damage, theft, and armed robberies” (KTVU).

Owner Lynsi Snyder was blunter in a December 2024 interview: “gunshots went through the store, there was a stabbing” (KTVU).

In-N-Out was not alone. Along the Hegenberger Road and 98th Avenue corridor, Denny’s closed its only Oakland restaurant in January 2024, and two Starbucks stores and a Subway had already shut in late 2023 (Nation’s Restaurant News). My take: when a chain that had never closed a single store walks away, the problem was not the burgers.

What is replacing the old In-N-Out on Hegenberger Road?

The site was bought by Chandi Hospitality, a Santa Rosa company, for close to $2 million, and it is expected to become an Indian fast-food restaurant (KTVU). As of November 17, 2026, I could not confirm the restaurant’s name or an opening date, so I am not publishing either.

Where is the closest In-N-Out to Oakland?

The closest In-N-Out to Oakland is store No. 303 in Alameda, at 555 Willie Stargell Ave., with a drive-thru and a dining room. San Leandro and Hayward, both south of Oakland on the East Bay side, are the next two options.

Habit Burger Oakland: the California burger chain still open in the city

Habit Burger & Grill in Fruitvale, at 3060 E. 9th St., Suite E, is where I would send anyone in Oakland who misses In-N-Out. It opened on January 25, 2023, and it was still trading in late 2026: a Restaurantji review is dated August 2026, and the store takes orders on Uber Eats and DoorDash.

Why Habit and not one of the city’s McDonald’s or Jack in the Box branches? Because it is the closest in spirit. Like In-N-Out, Habit is a Southern California chain built around one signature burger. The difference is the grill: Habit cooks its Charburger over an open flame.

Shake Shack is no longer an option in Oakland. Its Uptown restaurant at 1954 Telegraph Ave. opened in December 2020 and closed in 2024 (ABC7 News). The chain came back to the East Bay on April 28, 2026, but in Berkeley (Berkeleyside).

Habit Burger Fruitvale: address and practical information

Habit Burger & Grill Oakland is at 3060 E. 9th St., Suite E, Oakland, CA 94601, in the Fruitvale area. When it opened, the company announced dine-in, takeout, curbside pickup and delivery, plus self-order kiosks in the dining room (press release, Jan. 18, 2023).

  • Address: 3060 E. 9th St., Suite E, Oakland, CA 94601 (Fruitvale)
  • Services: dine-in, takeout, curbside pickup, kids’ menu; no reservations
  • Ordering and delivery: Habit app, order.habitburger.com, DoorDash, Postmates and Uber Eats
  • Price range: “$” on Restaurantji
  • Hours: not confirmed. The 2023 press release gave 10:30 a.m.–10 p.m. daily; Restaurantji shows 10 a.m.–8:30 p.m. daily. Check the official Habit locator before you go.
  • Phone: not published here, because I could not confirm it on the official store page.

Can you get to Habit Oakland by BART, and is there parking?

Fruitvale has a BART station, but I could not confirm the walking distance from the station to the restaurant or which AC Transit lines stop nearby. Parking is easy, according to Restaurantji reviewers. No source mentions a drive-thru, so don’t count on one.

Habit Burger menu and prices in Oakland

Habit Oakland’s signature, the flame-grilled Charburger, is listed at $8.74 on the store’s Yelp menu page, and a Charburger Meal at $18.24. That meal price looks steep to me for a burger, fries and a drink. The page carries no date and may include a delivery markup, so treat these figures as a ceiling and check the Habit app for the Oakland store’s in-store price.

The menu goes well beyond burgers, which is why the chain renamed itself “Habit Burger & Grill” on August 28, 2024 (Restaurant Business). Besides the Charburger in single, cheese and double versions, there is the Santa Barbara Char, the Steakhouse Peppercorn Char, chicken, ahi tuna and steak sandwiches, salads, fries, sweet potato fries, onion rings, tempura green beans and shakes.

Does Habit Burger Oakland have vegetarian options?

Yes: a Veggie Burger ($10.61 on the Yelp menu), a Grilled Cheese and salads, plus meat-free sides such as fries, sweet potato fries, onion rings and tempura green beans.

Habit Burger Oakland reviews

Habit Burger & Grill Oakland holds 4.3 out of 5 on Restaurantji, from 64 reviews (checked November 17, 2026). That is the only score I could open and verify for this location; I found no Google or Tripadvisor rating I could confirm.

  • What reviewers like: crispy fries and burgers, friendly staff, a clean dining room, easy parking.
  • What they complain about: long waits, mentioned in an August 2026 review, and a patty some regulars find smaller than it used to be.

The Charburger’s national reputation rests on a 2014 Consumer Reports survey of nearly 54,000 readers, which scored it 8.1 out of 10, the top mark in the ranking (Wikipedia). That survey is twelve years old. I give the recent local reviews more weight.

My verdict: Habit Fruitvale is the best stand-in for In-N-Out inside Oakland city limits, with a flame-grilled patty and a far wider menu. It is not an In-N-Out, though. If it’s a Double-Double you’re craving, drive to Alameda; the late hours make it an easy detour.

Habit Burger & Grill vs. In-N-Out

Both are California burger chains, but Habit grills its burgers over an open flame and runs a much broader menu, with ahi tuna, steak sandwiches, salads and tempura green beans. In Oakland, the practical difference is simple: Habit is open in the city, In-N-Out is not.

FAQ: In-N-Out and burgers in Oakland

Is In-N-Out still open in Oakland?

No. Oakland’s only In-N-Out, at 8300 Oakport St., closed permanently on March 24, 2024.

Will In-N-Out come back to Oakland?

No return has been announced. The former site was sold to Chandi Hospitality and is expected to become an Indian fast-food restaurant.

Where is the closest In-N-Out to Oakland?

In Alameda, at 555 Willie Stargell Ave. It is open from 10:30 a.m. to 1 a.m. Sunday through Thursday and until 1:30 a.m. on Fridays and Saturdays. San Leandro (15575 Hesperian Blvd.) and Hayward (709 Harder Ave.) are the other nearby options.

Where can I eat a California chain burger in Oakland today?

At Habit Burger & Grill, 3060 E. 9th St., Suite E, in Fruitvale. It serves the flame-grilled Charburger and offers dine-in, takeout, curbside pickup and delivery.

How much is a Charburger at Habit in Oakland?

The store’s Yelp menu lists the Charburger at $8.74 and the Charburger Meal at $18.24. Those prices are undated and may include a delivery markup, so check the Habit app for the in-store price.

What are Habit Burger Oakland’s hours?

Sources disagree: 10:30 a.m.–10 p.m. daily in the 2023 opening announcement, 10 a.m.–8:30 p.m. daily on Restaurantji. Confirm on the official Habit locator before you go.

Does Habit Burger & Grill have vegetarian options?

Yes: a Veggie Burger, a Grilled Cheese, salads and several meat-free sides, including sweet potato fries and tempura green beans.

Is Shake Shack still open in Oakland?

No. The Uptown Oakland Shake Shack at 1954 Telegraph Ave. closed in 2024. The closest new one opened in Berkeley on April 28, 2026.
