Goiko Zaragoza: 2 Addresses, Hours, Menu, Reviews

Goiko's 2 Zaragoza restaurants compared: official hours, the 2 burgers sold only here, ratings by platform and cost per person. Checked 15 November 2026.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished November 23, 2026

Goiko has 2 restaurants in Zaragoza: San Miguel 7 in the historic centre, near Paseo de la Independencia, and Francisco Vitoria 30 (postcode 50008). For a first visit I’d pick San Miguel 7. It opens at noon every day and stays open until 11:30 pm or midnight. It’s also the only place in the world that serves La Mañosa, Goiko’s Aragonese burger. Francisco Vitoria 30 has its own exclusive, the Cinque Terre, but shorter hours: Monday to Thursday it shuts between 5 pm and 8 pm. Budget €10 to €20 per person at either one.

How I checked: every address, phone number, service and opening time below comes from Goiko’s official restaurant pages, which I read on 15 November 2026. Ratings come from Gastroranking and Restaurant Guru on the same date. The story of the chain’s arrival in Zaragoza comes from Goiko, the Spanish trade magazine Alimarket and the local site Zaragozala. Where I couldn’t confirm something, such as card payments or the nearest tram stop, I say so rather than guess.

These are the official hours as of 15 November 2026. On both pages Goiko warns that hours can change and sends you to Google for the latest. The split shift is the detail that catches people out. Spanish lunch runs late, and a 5 pm cut-off on a weekday leaves very little slack if you sit down at 3:30. For a late lunch or an early dinner on a weekday, go to San Miguel. During the Fiestas del Pilar in October, or on a public holiday, check Google Maps on the day.

Goiko San Miguel 7: the old-town Goiko and home of La Mañosa

Goiko San Miguel, at Calle San Miguel 7 (50001), is the one to choose if you’re staying in or exploring Zaragoza’s historic centre. It sits in the Casco Antiguo, near Paseo de la Independencia, and it was the first Goiko in both Zaragoza and Aragon.

The draw here is La Mañosa: Tronchón cheese, sweet potato, mushrooms, fried artichoke, smoked mayo and spinach. Goiko says it’s served only at this restaurant (official page). The name is a wink at the locals, since mañoso is the affectionate nickname for people from Aragon. With fried artichoke and spinach on top, it’s the more vegetable-heavy of Zaragoza’s 2 exclusives.

The official page lists dining in, booking, delivery, takeaway, a kids’ menu and a baby-changing table. It says nothing about step-free access. If you use a wheelchair, call ahead on +34 876 06 99 59.

Gastroranking combines 7,061 reviews into a score of 8.4/10: 8.6 on Google (5,999 reviews), 8.6 on Just Eat (729) and 6.4 on Tripadvisor (333). That ranks it 6th of Zaragoza’s 38 burger restaurants and 120th of 2,907 restaurants in the city (Gastroranking).

Goiko Francisco Vitoria 30: the accessible Goiko and home of the Cinque Terre

Goiko Francisco Vitoria, at Calle Francisco Vitoria 30 (50008), opened second. It’s the only one of the two whose official page lists facilities for wheelchair users, and on Gastroranking it ranks 1 place higher than San Miguel, with the same 8.4 score.

Here the exclusive is the Cinque Terre: mozzarella, sun-dried tomato pesto, black olives, fried aubergine and batavia lettuce, “only in this restaurant” (official page). As the name of the Ligurian coast suggests, it’s Goiko going Italian.

The Spanish version of the official page lists free wifi, a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table and accessible facilities. The English version adds booking, delivery, takeaway and online ordering (Goiko).

Gastroranking gives it 8.4/10 across 6,419 reviews and ranks it 5th of the city’s 38 burger restaurants (Gastroranking). Restaurant Guru shows 4.4/5 on Google (5,268 reviews), 2.7/5 on Tripadvisor (84 reviews), 3.8/5 on Facebook (16 reviews) and 7.2/10 on Foursquare (Restaurant Guru).

Which Goiko in Zaragoza should you choose?

Choose San Miguel 7 for its central location, longer hours and La Mañosa. Choose Francisco Vitoria 30 for step-free access or the Cinque Terre.

  • Sightseeing in the old town, or a late weekday lunch: San Miguel 7. It’s open all afternoon, every day.
  • You want the Aragonese burger: San Miguel 7, the only place serving La Mañosa.
  • You use a wheelchair or a pushchair: Francisco Vitoria 30, the only 1 listing accessible facilities.
  • You like Mediterranean flavours: Francisco Vitoria 30, for the Cinque Terre.
  • Late on a Thursday night: San Miguel 7, open until midnight.

Goiko Zaragoza menu: what to order

In Zaragoza, Goiko serves the chain’s national menu, plus La Mañosa at San Miguel 7 and the Cinque Terre at Francisco Vitoria 30. The official menu covers starters, burgers, smash burgers, Chicken Lovers, a kids’ menu (Para Peques), salads, desserts, drinks and Menús GOIKO set meals, and links to an allergen page.

The Kevin Bacon: Goiko’s best-seller

The Kevin Bacon is Goiko’s best-selling burger: ground beef mixed with chopped bacon on the griddle, topped with crispy onion and American cheddar (Zaragozala). Zaragoza once got it for free. On 10 October 2023, for the chain’s 10th birthday, Goiko gave away 10,000 Kevin Bacons across its 110 restaurants in Spain. Each Zaragoza restaurant handed out about 100 from 7 pm, in the middle of the Fiestas del Pilar.

What Zaragoza diners order

Reviews of both Zaragoza restaurants keep naming the same dishes: the Kevin Bacon, the M-30, the Triloaded starter, nachos, fries and the Goiko Cookie for dessert (Restaurant Guru, Gastroranking). At San Miguel, the chicken burgers come up often too.

My pick: Kevin Bacon or the local exclusive?

If it’s your first Goiko anywhere, order the Kevin Bacon. It’s the burger the chain was built on, and you’ll understand the hype or you won’t. If you’ve already had 1 in Madrid or Barcelona, don’t order it again here. Order La Mañosa at San Miguel 7. Tronchón is a cheese from Aragon’s border with Valencia, and a Goiko burger named after the people of Aragon is the only real reason to eat at Goiko in Zaragoza rather than any other city.

Vegetarian and gluten-free options

Goiko’s national menu has a gluten-free section, but only some restaurants offer it, and I couldn’t confirm whether either Zaragoza address is one of them. I also couldn’t confirm which vegetarian burger is on the Zaragoza menu today. Both exclusives list cheese and no meat among their toppings, but the listing doesn’t say which patty they come with. Ask the staff, and check the allergen page before you order.

Goiko doesn’t publish prices on its online menu. I found per-burger prices on unofficial menu sites, but they are neither official nor specific to Zaragoza, so I’m not quoting them. Booking, takeaway and delivery at Goiko Zaragoza

According to their official pages, both Zaragoza Goikos take bookings and offer delivery and takeaway. Book at goiko.com/es/reservas and order delivery or takeaway at goiko.com/es/delivery. Uber Eats also lists both Zaragoza restaurants. San Miguel’s page mentions paying at the table. I found no official information on accepted cards, parking or a terrace at either address.

Goiko’s story: from Madrid to Zaragoza

Goiko is a Spanish burger chain founded in Madrid in 2013 by Andoni Goicoechea. In October 2026 it had 114 restaurants, 113 in Spain and 1 in Andorra. The investment firm Capza has controlled it since late 2025, and it reported €109 million in revenue in 2025. Our Goiko brand profile has the full history and sourced figures.

Zaragoza came early. Goiko arrived in 2017, and San Miguel 7 was the first Goiko in Zaragoza and in Aragon. In May 2017, Alimarket reported that the chain “has reached cities like Valencia and Zaragoza”, with a target of 26 restaurants by the end of 2017 and 46 by the end of 2018 (Alimarket). Francisco Vitoria 30 followed about 5 months later: Zaragozala announced the second restaurant on 25 September 2017 (Zaragozala). I couldn’t confirm the exact opening day of either one.

Is Goiko Zaragoza worth it?

Yes, if you want a gourmet burger with long hours and online booking, and a burger made for Aragon. Both restaurants score 8.4/10 on Gastroranking, and each serves something you won’t find at any other Goiko. My advice: go to San Miguel 7, in the old town, for La Mañosa. Keep Francisco Vitoria 30 for weekend evenings or for when you need step-free access. And if you only have room for 1 big meal in Zaragoza, I wouldn’t spend it on a chain burger. FAQ: Goiko Zaragoza

How many Goiko restaurants are there in Zaragoza? 2, both on Goiko’s official store locator on 15 November 2026: San Miguel 7 (50001) in the historic centre and Francisco Vitoria 30 (50008).

Which Goiko should I choose in Zaragoza, San Miguel or Francisco Vitoria? San Miguel 7 for the old town, longer hours and La Mañosa. Francisco Vitoria 30 for accessible facilities and the Cinque Terre.

What are Goiko Zaragoza’s opening hours? San Miguel 7: Monday to Wednesday 12:00–23:30, Thursday to Sunday 12:00–00:00. Francisco Vitoria 30: Monday to Thursday 13:00–17:00 and 20:00–23:00, Friday and Saturday 13:00–23:30, Sunday 13:00–23:00. Goiko notes that hours may change.

Does Goiko Francisco Vitoria close in the afternoon? Yes, Monday to Thursday it closes between 17:00 and 20:00. From Friday to Sunday it serves without a break.

Can I book a table at Goiko Zaragoza? Yes. Both restaurants list booking on their official pages. Book at goiko.com/es/reservas.

Does Goiko deliver in Zaragoza? Yes. Both restaurants list delivery and takeaway, ordered through goiko.com/es/delivery. Uber Eats also lists both.

How much does a meal at Goiko Zaragoza cost? €10 to €20 per person, according to Restaurant Guru and Tripaligner. Goiko’s online menu doesn’t show prices.

What is La Mañosa at Goiko Zaragoza? A burger served only at Goiko San Miguel 7, with Tronchón cheese, sweet potato, mushrooms, fried artichoke, smoked mayo and spinach. Mañoso is the nickname for people from Aragon.

What is Goiko’s most famous burger? The Kevin Bacon: ground beef mixed with chopped bacon on the griddle, topped with crispy onion and American cheddar.

Does Goiko have vegetarian or gluten-free options in Zaragoza? Goiko’s national menu has a gluten-free section in some restaurants only, and I couldn’t confirm that either Zaragoza address is included. Ask on site and check the allergen page.

Is Goiko Zaragoza wheelchair accessible? Francisco Vitoria 30 lists accessible facilities on its official page. San Miguel 7’s page says nothing about access, so call +34 876 06 99 59 before you go.

Is Goiko the best burger in Zaragoza? Not on Gastroranking: its 2 restaurants rank 5th and 6th of 38, behind Caníbal Royal, 2 Carl’s Jr. restaurants and HAM! Fresh Burger.
