Goiko Madrid: Best Branches, Hours, Menu, Reviews

Goiko has 19 branches in Madrid, its home city. Which to pick, from the Sol flagship to the 100% gluten-free María de Molina, with hours and review scores.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished November 6, 2026

Goiko has 19 branches inside the city of Madrid, by my count from the chain’s official locator on 29 October 2026, but four central ones cover what most visitors need. Near the sights, go to Goiko Puerta del Sol (Puerta del Sol, 14): it’s the brand’s flagship and biggest restaurant, and it stays open until 2 a.m. on Fridays and Saturdays. Celiac diners should head to Goiko Gluten Free (María de Molina, 20), the chain’s first 100% gluten-free restaurant. Spending the day at the museums? Prado 15, in the Barrio de las Letras. In Malasaña, Pez 36. On the review aggregator Gastroranking, the three of these that are rated score 8.6 to 8.8 out of 10, and Gastroranking puts a meal at under €20 a head everywhere except Prado 15 (€20–30).

Goiko is a Madrid chain before anything else. It started in one tiny restaurant here in 2013, and the Madrid region still holds 37 of its 114 restaurants. I checked every address, phone number and opening hour in this guide against Goiko’s official branch pages on 29 October 2026. Review scores come from Gastroranking, which combines Google, Tripadvisor and Just Eat ratings. Anything I couldn’t trace to a source, I left out, including burger prices, which Goiko doesn’t publish online.

Which Goiko to choose in Madrid, by type of visitor

Pick Puerta del Sol if you’re sightseeing or eating late, María de Molina if you’re celiac, Prado 15 if you’re near the museums, and Pez 36 if you’re in Malasaña.

  • First-time visitor staying near Sol: Goiko Puerta del Sol. It’s on the square itself and stays open latest.
  • Celiac or gluten-free traveler: Goiko Gluten Free María de Molina 20. The whole menu is gluten-free and certified by FACE, so you aren’t picking around a shared menu.
  • Museum day (Prado, Thyssen): Goiko Prado 15, in the Barrio de las Letras, for the exclusive La Ex burger.
  • Night out in Malasaña: Goiko Pez 36, with a terrace and the exclusive La Piraña.
  • Salamanca district with a dog: Conde de Peñalver 41 or María de Molina 20. Both allow dogs on the terrace.

My own pick for a first visit isn’t the flagship. It’s Prado 15: it scores higher than Sol (8.7 against 8.6), and it has a burger you can’t order anywhere else. Sol only wins if you’re eating after midnight on a Friday or Saturday.

Goiko Puerta del Sol: the flagship and biggest Goiko

Goiko Puerta del Sol, at Puerta del Sol 14, is the brand’s first flagship and its biggest restaurant: 700 m² (about 7,500 sq ft) on two floors. It occupies the former home of Olga Ramos, the artist and violinist. The building is protected, so Madrid’s city heritage commission (CPPHAN) supervised the renovation, according to Forbes España.

Time Out Madrid announced in February 2025 that the opening was planned for Easter 2025, and Forbes España covered the opening on 21 April 2025. CEO Alejandro Hermo told Forbes that opening on Puerta del Sol was “a dream come true” (un sueño hecho realidad). The founder looked back for Time Out: “Madrid saw us born in a tiny place, barely 30 chairs and Ikea furniture.”

  • Address: Puerta del Sol, 14, Centro, 28013 Madrid
  • Phone: +34 910 45 81 42
  • Hours: Mon–Wed 11 a.m.–11:30 p.m., Thu 11 a.m.–midnight, Fri–Sat 11 a.m.–2 a.m., Sun 11 a.m.–midnight
  • Services: kids’ menu, baby-changing table, wheelchair access
  • Reviews: 8.6/10 on Gastroranking, 25th of 207 Madrid burger restaurants, under €20 per person. Diners praise the meat, the attentive staff and the fast service.

It opens at 11 a.m. every day, an hour before any other branch in this guide. If you want a Goiko burger after midnight in central Madrid, this is the only one that can help, and only on Fridays and Saturdays.

Goiko gluten-free in Madrid: María de Molina 20

Goiko Gluten Free on María de Molina 20 is the chain’s first 100% gluten-free restaurant, with a menu certified by FACE, the Federation of Celiac Associations of Spain. It switched to this format on Wednesday, 14 May 2025, according to Time Out Madrid. It’s also the best-rated branch in this guide: 8.8/10 on Gastroranking from 7,756 reviews, 5th of 207 burger restaurants in Madrid.

The gluten-free menu includes versions of the Kevin Bacon, the Pigma, the Kiki and the tequeños (Venezuelan cheese sticks). Gastroranking’s score breaks down into Google 9.0 (5,709 reviews), Tripadvisor 8.0 (1,448) and Just Eat 8.6 (599). Reviewers call it ideal for celiacs and praise the staff. Their complaints: uneven sides, small desserts, a bun that could be better, and prices some find high. A weak bun is a common gluten-free compromise, and I’d still send a celiac friend here before any other Goiko in Madrid, because the certification covers the whole menu.

The branch is on the street where Goiko was born. Time Out Madrid listed the original Goiko Grill at María de Molina 16 in 2015, near Gregorio Marañón metro (lines 7 and 10). In June 2016, Alimarket reported that Goiko had moved its first restaurant to a nearby address on the same street. María de Molina 16 no longer appears on the official locator.

Goiko Prado 15: Barrio de las Letras, near the museums

Goiko Prado 15 is the branch to pick around the Barrio de las Letras and Madrid’s museum district, and it’s the only place to get the exclusive La Ex burger. La Ex comes with spinach fondue, grilled portobello, bacon and a Grana Padano crust. Goiko opened its Barrio de las Letras restaurant in June 2016, according to Alimarket.

Calle del Prado seen from Plaza de Santa Ana in Madrid’s Barrio de las Letras, the street of Goiko Prado 15, a short walk from the Prado and Thyssen museums (street view, not the restaurant) (Photo: Malopez 21, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Calle\_del\_Prado,\_desde\_la\_Plaza\_de\_Santa\_Ana.jpg)

Prado 15 scores 8.7/10 on Gastroranking from 10,413 reviews (Google 8.8 from 9,575, Just Eat 8.2 from 400, Tripadvisor 7.6 from 446), which puts it 9th of 207. It’s also the priciest of the four, at €20 to €30 per person. Reviewers like the presentation and mention individual servers by name. The recurring criticism is cooking: meat that comes out overdone and dry, and too few staff at peak times. Say how you want your burger cooked when you order.

  • Address: Calle del Prado, 15, 28014 Madrid
  • Phone: +34 910 60 94 76
  • Hours: Mon–Wed noon–11:30 p.m., Thu–Sun noon–midnight
  • Services: Wi-Fi, kids’ menu, baby-changing table, reservations, delivery and takeaway

Goiko Pez 36: Malasaña and the La Piraña burger

Goiko Pez 36 is the Malasaña branch, with a terrace and its own exclusive burger, La Piraña. La Piraña layers wakame with crispy onion, garlic cream cheese, onion rings, bacon, cucumber and chipotle sauce. Seaweed on a beef burger is the strangest idea on any Goiko menu in Madrid, and it’s the one I’d most want to try.

Gastroranking had no score for Pez 36 in my sources, so I haven’t given one.

Goiko Conde de Peñalver 41: a Salamanca terrace

Goiko Conde de Peñalver 41 is the Salamanca-district branch with a terrace, and it ties with María de Molina 20 for the longest list of services. It offers reservations, delivery, takeaway, Wi-Fi, a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table, wheelchair access and a terrace where dogs are allowed. It scores 8.5/10 on Gastroranking from 10,142 reviews (Google 8.8 from 9,073, Tripadvisor 7.6 from 663, Just Eat 8.6 from 406) and ranks 34th of 207, with a typical spend under €20. The usual complaints are slow service at peak times and special requests that get forgotten.

  • Address: Conde de Peñalver, 41, 28006 Madrid
  • Phone: +34 910 60 96 16
  • Hours: Mon–Wed 1 p.m.–11:30 p.m., Thu–Sun noon–midnight

Goiko Madrid menu: what to order

Order the Kevin Bacon on your first visit: Goiko calls it “the true best seller of this house.” Bits of bacon are mixed into the patty before it goes on the griddle, then it’s topped with crispy onion and cheddar. According to El Español, it was created in the kitchen of the María de Molina restaurant.

The permanent burgers are the Tres Razas, Kevin Truff, Kevin Beast, Kevin Bacon, Kevin Costner, M-30, Pigma, Retro, Moza (the set lunch, or menú del día) and Veggie Mercury, the vegetarian option. The menu also has smash burgers, a Chicken Lovers range, salads, a kids’ menu, desserts and set menus (lunch, Kids, Night and Groups). The Madrid exclusives are La Ex at Prado 15 and La Piraña at Pez 36. Limited editions come and go too, like the Goiko × Mahou Kevin Reserva in our roundup of new burgers in 2026.

How much does Goiko cost in Madrid?

Gastroranking puts a meal at Goiko in central Madrid at under €20 a person, or €20 to €30 at Prado 15. Goiko.com doesn’t show prices, and delivery-app prices are often higher than in the restaurant. I couldn’t confirm a current in-restaurant menu, so I haven’t quoted prices per burger.

Goiko Madrid reviews: what diners say

Diners rate Goiko’s central Madrid branches highly, 8.5 to 8.8 out of 10 on Gastroranking, and the gluten-free María de Molina branch is 5th of 207 burger restaurants in the city. The same points keep coming up. Diners praise the meat, the attentive staff and the quick service at Sol. They complain about uneven cooking (especially at Prado 15), slow service at peak times, sides and desserts that don’t live up to the burgers, and prices some find high.

How to book a table or order delivery

You can book a table at goiko.com/es/reservas/ and order delivery or takeaway at goiko.com/es/delivery/. María de Molina 20, Prado 15 and Conde de Peñalver 41 list reservations. Goiko also runs a loyalty program, “Friends with Benefits”.

Goiko’s Madrid story: from 30 chairs to 114 restaurants

Goiko is a Madrid chain: Andoni Goicoechea founded it in the city in 2013. He was 25, a Venezuelan of Basque descent working as a geriatrics resident doctor at La Paz hospital, and he started the business with a €50,000 loan from his father, according to Goiko’s official history. Madrid is still its biggest market, with 37 of its 114 restaurants in the region in October 2026. Investment firm Capza has controlled the chain since late 2025. For the full history and figures, see our Goiko brand profile.

Goiko has since spread across Spain. For the same kind of guide in other cities, see Goiko in Barcelona, Goiko in Seville and Goiko in Valencia, or our guide to the best burgers in Valencia. To see how Goiko compares with the rest of the city, read our guide to the best burgers in Madrid.

Goiko Madrid FAQ

How many Goiko restaurants are there in Madrid?

The official locator lists 37 Goiko restaurants in the province of Madrid, and 19 of them are in the city itself (official locator, 29 October 2026).

Which Goiko is closest to Puerta del Sol?

Goiko Puerta del Sol, at Puerta del Sol 14, is right on the square. Prado 15, in the Barrio de las Letras, is the next-closest central branch.

What is the biggest Goiko?

Goiko Puerta del Sol is the brand’s biggest restaurant and its first flagship, with 700 m² on two floors.

Where was the first Goiko, and does it still exist?

The first Goiko opened on Calle de María de Molina. In 2016 it moved to a nearby address on the same street. Today the street is home to Goiko Gluten Free at number 20.

Is there a gluten-free Goiko in Madrid?

Yes. Goiko María de Molina 20 has been 100% gluten-free since 14 May 2025, with a menu certified by the Federation of Celiac Associations of Spain (FACE).

Is Goiko open late on weekends?

Goiko Puerta del Sol is open until 2 a.m. on Fridays and Saturdays. The other central branches close at midnight from Thursday to Sunday, except María de Molina 20, which closes at 11 p.m. on Sundays.

Do I need to book at Goiko in Madrid?

No, but you can book online at goiko.com/es/reservas/ at the branches that take reservations, such as María de Molina 20, Prado 15 and Conde de Peñalver 41.

Does Goiko have vegetarian options?

Yes. The Veggie Mercury is the vegetarian burger on the permanent menu.

Can I bring my dog to Goiko in Madrid?

Yes, on the terrace at María de Molina 20 and Conde de Peñalver 41.

Does Goiko deliver in Madrid?

Yes. You can order delivery or takeaway at goiko.com/es/delivery/, and Prado 15, María de Molina 20 and Conde de Peñalver 41 list both services.
