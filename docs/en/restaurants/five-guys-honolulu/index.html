Five Guys Honolulu: 3 Oʻahu Spots, Not in Waikīkī

Addresses, hours, reviews and past prices for Five Guys in Aiea, Kapolei and Mililani, plus how to reach Pearlridge, the closest to Waikīkī.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 18, 2026

There is no Five Guys in urban Honolulu: none in Waikīkī, Ala Moana, Kakaʻako or downtown. Five Guys Honolulu really means Five Guys on Oʻahu, where the chain has 3 restaurants, all inside the City and County of Honolulu: Pearlridge Center in Aiea, Ka Makana Aliʻi in Kapolei and the Town Center of Mililani. If you are staying in Waikīkī, aim for Five Guys Pearlridge (98-1005 Moanalua Road, Aiea, open 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily). I checked every listing below against the official locator and the sources cited on November 14, 2026.

Is there a Five Guys in Honolulu?

Not in the city itself: all 3 Hawaii Five Guys locations are on Oʻahu, outside urban Honolulu. The official locator at restaurants.fiveguys.com/hi lists only Aiea, Kapolei and Mililani. I found no Waikīkī, Ala Moana or Kakaʻako location there, and none in the 2025–2026 opening announcements tracked by restauranthonolulu.com.

The confusion comes from the name. The City and County of Honolulu covers the whole island of Oʻahu, so all 3 restaurants sit in Honolulu County. They are not in the Honolulu visitors picture, the strip of hotels and malls between Waikīkī and downtown. Directory sites make it worse: Wanderlog’s “Five Guys, Honolulu” listing actually points to the Aiea address.

Five Guys Pearlridge (Aiea): the closest to Waikīkī

Five Guys Pearlridge is the location to pick if you are coming from Honolulu. It is the closest to town, and its own district manager once called it the busiest of the 3. It sits at 98-1005 Moanalua Road, Aiea, HI 96701, in Pearlridge Mauka, the renovated section of the mall once called “Uptown” (Honolulu Magazine). Hours are 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day; the phone number is (808) 488-4442.

It opened as part of the Pearlridge renovation completed in April 2019, which added 11 restaurants to the center. In an August 6, 2019 Honolulu Star-Advertiser story, district manager Germaine Akahoshi called it “by far the busiest on the island.” Pearlridge Center itself opened in 1972 and is Hawaii’s second-largest mall after Ala Moana (Wikipedia).

Five Guys Kapolei (Ka Makana Aliʻi)

Five Guys Kapolei is the West Oʻahu location, inside the Ka Makana Aliʻi shopping center at 91-5431 Kapolei Parkway, Kapolei, HI 96707, phone (808) 628-4740. Its hours are the one detail I could not pin down. TripAdvisor says 11 a.m. to 10 p.m.; the mall’s own store page says 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. Call before you drive out, especially for an early lunch or a late dinner.

The mall page also lists Uber Eats ordering for this location. TripAdvisor reviewers note that it is wheelchair accessible.

Five Guys Mililani (Town Center of Mililani)

Five Guys Mililani is where the chain started in Hawaii, at 95-1249 Meheula Parkway, Mililani, HI 96789, in the Town Center of Mililani. It is open 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. Directory listings give 2 different phone numbers, 1 current and one from 2016. I can’t tell which is right, so I’ve left both out: use the number on the official locator.

Five Guys near Waikīkī: how to get to Pearlridge

The closest Five Guys to Waikīkī is Pearlridge in Aiea. Without a car, the easiest way in is the Skyline rail line, which stops at Kalauao (Pearlridge) station. The station opened on June 30, 2023, and serves the mall directly (Wikipedia).

Pearlridge comes in 2 parts: Pearlridge Mauka, where Five Guys is, and Pearlridge Wai Makai. The Skycab, Hawaii’s only monorail, links them for $1. I haven’t confirmed the exact distance or travel time from Waikīkī, so check a route planner from your hotel. Mililani has no Skyline service, and I couldn’t confirm which Skyline station or TheBus stop is closest to Ka Makana Aliʻi in Kapolei.

Five Guys Honolulu menu and Hawaii prices

The Five Guys Honolulu menu is the standard Five Guys menu. I found no Hawaii-only item. You get the usual formula: fresh beef that is never frozen, fries cut in the restaurant and cooked in peanut oil, and free toppings (Ka Makana Aliʻi, Wikipedia).

Toppings cost nothing extra. At Pearlridge, Honolulu Magazine lists jalapeños, grilled mushrooms, peppers, pickles and tomatoes among them. So the price of the base burger is the price of the burger you actually build.

Reviewers notice what they pay. Price is the most common complaint in Google reviews of Pearlridge, and 1 TripAdvisor reviewer in Kapolei paid close to $20 for 2 plain burgers. Pearlridge’s Yelp page has 253 reviews and 437 photos, but I couldn’t read its average rating, and I found no ratings for Mililani.

Peanuts and allergies at Five Guys Hawaii

Free peanuts or not, Five Guys still cooks its fries in peanut oil. Reports on the peanuts differ by location. Google reviewers say Pearlridge no longer hands them out, while TripAdvisor reviewers still mention them in Kapolei. The current policy isn’t confirmed. If you have a peanut allergy, the oil matters far more than the boxes on the counter.

When did Five Guys come to Hawaii?

The first Five Guys in Hawaii opened at the Town Center of Mililani on Tuesday, June 28, 2016, 6 years after the chain first said it was coming. The Honolulu Star-Advertiser reported more than 100 people in line and about 2,000 customers expected on opening day.

Logistics caused the wait. In July 2010, Five Guys told the Star-Advertiser it would arrive in Hawaii “in a year or so.” Getting fresh, never-frozen beef and locally baked buns onto the islands held the opening back until 2016.

The Hawaii franchisees are Ken and Amannda Schneider. In December 2015 they were planning 6 to 8 Hawaii restaurants (Star-Advertiser, Honolulu Magazine). Almost 11 years later, the official locator lists 3.

Five Guys itself dates from 1986, when Jerry and Janie Murrell and their sons founded it in Arlington, Virginia. It has been headquartered in Alexandria, Virginia, since 2023. Zagat ranked it the No. 1 burger among large chains in 2011. As of May 2026 it had 1,999 restaurants in 28 countries and territories, 1,517 of them in the United States. The full history is on our Five Guys brand profile.

Is Five Guys worth the trip from Honolulu?

Not as a destination. For a Waikīkī visitor, Five Guys is worth it only if you are already going to Pearlridge or the west side. It’s a dependable, build-your-own chain burger, but nothing on the menu is different from the mainland, and on Oʻahu you pay more for it. My advice: don’t give up an afternoon for it. If you are riding Skyline out to Pearlridge to shop anyway, get off at Kalauao and eat there. If you’re staying in town, our Honolulu burger guide covers what to eat closer to your hotel, and that is where I would spend my burger budget on the island.

If you want the Five Guys burger without the ride to Aiea, make it at home. My colleague Julia rebuilt it in our Five Guys cheeseburger copycat recipe and the Five Guys bacon burger copycat. Five Guys Honolulu FAQ

Is there a Five Guys in Waikīkī?

No. Five Guys has no restaurant in Waikīkī, Ala Moana, Kakaʻako or downtown Honolulu. The closest one is at Pearlridge Center, 98-1005 Moanalua Road, Aiea, which is served by the Skyline’s Kalauao (Pearlridge) station.

How many Five Guys are there in Hawaii?

3, all on Oʻahu: Aiea (Pearlridge Center), Kapolei (Ka Makana Aliʻi) and Mililani (Town Center of Mililani), according to the official locator checked on November 14, 2026.

What are the Five Guys Pearlridge hours?

Five Guys Pearlridge in Aiea is open 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day. The phone number is (808) 488-4442.

When did the first Five Guys open in Hawaii?

On Tuesday, June 28, 2016, at the Town Center of Mililani, with more than 100 people in line, according to the Honolulu Star-Advertiser.

Does Five Guys deliver on Oʻahu?

The Ka Makana Aliʻi page lists Uber Eats ordering for Five Guys Kapolei, and Five Guys takes online orders at order.fiveguys.com. I couldn’t confirm the delivery partners for Pearlridge and Mililani. There are no reservations: you order at the counter.

Why is Five Guys more expensive in Hawaii?

Chains price higher in high-cost regions such as Hawaii, and customers at both Pearlridge and Kapolei call the prices high. In 2016 a cheeseburger cost $10.39 at Mililani’s opening. Check today’s price at order.fiveguys.com.

Does Five Guys Hawaii have vegetarian options?

I couldn’t confirm vegetarian items such as the veggie sandwich or grilled cheese for the Hawaii locations. Check your location’s menu on order.fiveguys.com before you go.

Does Five Guys still give free peanuts in Hawaii?

It depends who you ask: Google reviewers say Pearlridge has stopped, while TripAdvisor reviewers still mention free peanuts in Kapolei. Either way, the fries are cooked in peanut oil, so people with a peanut allergy should take care.
