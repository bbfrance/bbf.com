MOS Burger Yokohama: MOS Premium, Prices, Hours

Japan has only two MOS Premium restaurants. One is by Sakuragichō Station: wagyu burgers from ¥1,880, craft beer, hours and prices checked November 2026.

By Hana, Restaurants writer, Japan and IndonesiaPublished November 26, 2026

For MOS Burger in Yokohama, go to MOS PREMIUM Sakuragichō Crossgate (モスプレミアム 桜木町クロスゲート店), one minute from JR Sakuragichō Station. Japan has only two MOS Premium restaurants, and this is one of them: wagyu burgers from ¥1,880, eight craft beers, open daily from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. (last order 9 p.m.). Just want a classic MOS Burger (¥500)? Walk five minutes from Yokohama Station to MOS Burger Yokohama Higashiguchi, open daily from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. I checked every price and opening time below on November 17, 2026.

How I put this MOS Burger Yokohama guide together

I have not eaten at these Yokohama branches myself, and I would rather say so than pretend. This guide is built from sources, like our MOS Burger guide to Nagoya. I checked every address, opening time and phone number against MOS Food Services’ press release and the official MOS Premium site, then against Tabelog, Retty and Wanderlog on November 17, 2026. Menu prices come from the Tabelog menu page updated on November 6, 2026, and from the Rurubu &more article of August 23, 2026. The ratings are Tabelog’s and Google’s (as shown on Wanderlog). MOS Premium Sakuragichō: the wagyu burger MOS you can’t find elsewhere

MOS Premium Sakuragichō is MOS Burger’s upscale format, and it is the reason a burger lover should seek out MOS in Yokohama at all. MOS Food Services calls it an “upgraded” MOS Classic. The concept started in 2015 in Sendagaya, Shibuya, Tokyo: gourmet burgers at lunch, craft beer and drinking food in the evening. Sakuragichō followed on July 19, 2019. That makes two MOS Premium restaurants in Japan, Sendagaya and Sakuragichō Crossgate, according to Rurubu &more (August 2026) and Hamoni.

MOS Premium storefront at Sendagaya, Tokyo, the concept’s first restaurant and the only other MOS Premium in Japan, with its hamburger, appetizer, beer and wine signage (March 2024; illustrative photo, not taken at Sakuragichō) (Photo: Asanagi, CC0 1.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MOS\_Premium\_Sendagaya\_branch\_2024-03-31.jpg)

Address, access and phone

MOS PREMIUM Sakuragichō Crossgate is at Crossgate 1F, 1-101-1 Sakuragichō, Naka-ku, Yokohama 231-0062 (〒231-0062 神奈川県横浜市中区桜木町1-101-1 クロスゲート1F). MOS Food Services gives these walking times:

  • 1 minute from JR Sakuragichō Station (south ticket gates, east exit)
  • 1 minute from Sakuragichō Station on the Yokohama Municipal Subway (north exit 1)
  • 3 minutes from Bashamichi Station on the Minatomirai Line (exit 1b)

JR Sakuragichō Station at night: MOS Premium Sakuragichō is a 1-minute walk from the south ticket gates and east exit (photo from 2008) (Photo: Jeremy Eades, CC BY-SA 2.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sakuragich%C5%8D\_Station,\_JR\_East\_20081005\_night.jpg)

The restaurant’s phone number is +81 45-226-3850 (045-226-3850 from inside Japan). Tabelog also lists a reservation line: 050-5597-7109. Sakuragichō is the gateway to the Minato Mirai 21 waterfront and to the Noge bar district, so the Premium slots easily into a day of sightseeing.

Opening hours of MOS Premium Sakuragichō

MOS Premium Sakuragichō is open every day from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m., with last orders at 9 p.m. It has no regular closing day and follows the Crossgate building’s calendar, according to Tabelog and Rurubu &more (August 23, 2026); Wanderlog agrees. Ignore older pages that still show the 2019 hours (until 11 p.m., Monday to Saturday).

The room and how ordering works

The dining room is a food hall shared with the tea salon Mother Leaf Tea Style. It has a 4.5-metre (15-foot) ceiling, sofas and a terrace, and the whole room is non-smoking. The official MOS Premium site gives 96 seats. When it opened in 2019, MOS Food Services described two modes: semi-self-service from 10 a.m. to 4:59 p.m., then table service with tablet ordering from 5 p.m. Recent Google reviews still mention the evening tablet, and say it is in Japanese only. If you don’t read Japanese, come at lunch or have a translation app ready.

Payment and parking

MOS Premium Sakuragichō takes Visa, Mastercard, JCB, American Express and Diners cards. It also takes IC transport cards such as Suica and PASMO, plus the PayPay and d払い QR payment apps, according to Tabelog. There is parking in the Crossgate building. I could not confirm the parking rates or whether the restaurant validates tickets.

Craft beer and drinks

MOS Premium pours eight craft beers, from ¥880 a glass. Tabelog lists a three-beer tasting flight at ¥1,980 (menu updated November 6, 2026). Red sangria and milkshakes are ¥880 each. An all-you-can-drink plan (nomihōdai) costs ¥5,500, and the MOS Premium Special Set is ¥2,980. I could not confirm which beers are on tap this month.

MOS Burger near Yokohama Station: the Higashiguchi branch

The nearest confirmed MOS Burger to Yokohama Station is MOS Burger Yokohama Higashiguchi (モスバーガー 横浜東口店). It is about a 5-minute walk from the JR station’s east side and opens daily from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. You will find it in the Sky Menor Yokohama building at 2-11-2 Takashima, Nishi-ku, next to a 7-Eleven according to a job listing for the branch. Phone: +81 45-440-1770. It opened on October 18, 2012, and serves the regular national MOS menu at the prices above. Tabelog users rate it 3.11/5 from 71 reviews.

I could not confirm payment methods, seat count, online ordering or delivery for Higashiguchi. If you need to pay by card, ask before you order.

Closed: MOS Burger Sotetsu Movil

MOS Burger Sotetsu Movil (相鉄ムービル店, 2-1-22 Minamisaiwai, Nishi-ku, 2F) is permanently closed, according to Tabelog. Some aggregator sites still list it, so don’t go there by mistake.

Other MOS Burger branches in Yokohama

Two more neighborhood MOS Burgers in Yokohama have verified addresses. Both are handy if you are staying on the Sotetsu Line side of the city.

  • MOS Burger Yokohama Tennōchō (モスバーガー 横浜天王町店): Villa d’Este KOSUGE 1F, 2-46-1 Tennōchō, Hodogaya-ku. Open daily 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. It is 68 metres (1 minute) from Tennōchō Station on the Sotetsu Line. Non-smoking, PayPay accepted (Retty).
  • MOS Burger Yokohama Asamachō (モスバーガー横浜浅間町店): 1-6-14 Asamachō, Nishi-ku. Phone +81 45-317-3566. It is 8 minutes on foot from Hiranumabashi Station (Sotetsu Line) and 9 minutes from Yokohama Station. PayPay accepted. Retty last updated this listing in March 2024 and gives no hours, so check before you go.

Directories and aggregators list other Yokohama branches, including Kannai, Saedo, AEON Yokohama Shinyoshida and Forte Kawawachō. I could not confirm that they are still open, so I left them out. MOS’s official store search is the place to check. MOS publishes no official count of its Yokohama restaurants either, so I give no number.

MOS Burger Yokohama reviews: what diners say

Diners rate MOS Premium Sakuragichō 3.9/5 on Google (927 reviews, as shown on Wanderlog in November 2026). On Tabelog it scores 3.49/5 from 442 reviews, which is solid on a platform where ratings run low. I found no dedicated TripAdvisor or Yelp page.

What reviewers praise: beefy burgers made to order, good-quality meat, a pleasant room that stays calm in the evening, efficient service and the craft beer list.

What reviewers criticize: prices three to four times those of a fast-food burger, the Japanese-only ordering tablet in the evening, small portions of fries and over-salted pasta.

Is MOS Premium Sakuragichō worth it?

Yes, if you want a sit-down wagyu burger and a beer near Minato Mirai and you know the price going in. You pay restaurant prices, not MOS prices. In return you get a burger served in only two places in Japan. If all you want is a MOS fix, the ¥500 MOS Burger at Higashiguchi does the job for a quarter of the price.

Burgers in Yokohama: why MOS Premium fits the city

Yokohama has a long Western-food tradition, which makes it a natural home for an upscale burger. The port opened to foreign trade in 1859, and the city helped shape yōshoku, including hambāgu, the Japanese hamburger steak. Further south, Yokosuka is known for the Navy Burger. Our Yokohama burger guide puts all of this in context.

MOS itself is a Tokyo story. Atsushi Sakurada founded the chain in Narimasu in 1972. It had 1,709 restaurants worldwide, 1,306 of them in Japan, as of September 30, 2026, and it is Japan’s second-largest burger chain. Our MOS Burger brand profile covers the full history and figures, and our guide to the first MOS Burger in Narimasu, Tokyo visits where it all began. Tokyo is also home to the only other MOS Premium, in Sendagaya.

Frequently asked questions about MOS Burger Yokohama

Where should I eat MOS Burger in Yokohama?

Go to MOS PREMIUM Sakuragichō Crossgate for the wagyu burgers and craft beer you can only find at two MOS restaurants in Japan. For a classic MOS Burger near Yokohama Station, use the Higashiguchi branch at 2-11-2 Takashima, Nishi-ku.

What is MOS Premium, and how is it different from a regular MOS Burger?

MOS Premium is MOS Burger’s upscale concept, launched in Sendagaya, Tokyo, in 2015. It serves wagyu burgers, craft beer and food to go with drinks in a sit-down room, at about three to four times regular MOS prices.

How much does a burger cost at MOS Premium Sakuragichō?

Wagyu burgers cost ¥1,880 to ¥1,980, ¥2,280 to ¥2,380 as doubles, and ¥2,980 for the Monster (Tabelog menu, November 6, 2026). A classic MOS Burger has cost ¥500 since July 15, 2026.

Is there a MOS Burger near Yokohama Station?

Yes. MOS Burger Yokohama Higashiguchi is about a 5-minute walk from JR Yokohama Station, in the Sky Menor Yokohama building at 2-11-2 Takashima, Nishi-ku. It is open daily from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m.

What are MOS Burger Yokohama’s opening hours? Can I go for breakfast?

MOS Premium Sakuragichō is open daily from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. (last order 9 p.m.). Higashiguchi opens earliest, at 7 a.m., and closes at 10 p.m. Tennōchō is open from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. I could not confirm a breakfast menu at any of them.

Do I need to book MOS Premium Sakuragichō? Can I go with a group or just for drinks?

No source says booking is required, and Tabelog lists a reservation line (050-5597-7109) if you want a table. With 96 seats according to the official site, a ¥5,500 nomihōdai plan and an evening drinks menu, it works for groups and for a beer after work.

Can I pay by card at MOS Burger in Yokohama?

At MOS Premium Sakuragichō, yes: Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Amex and Diners, IC transport cards, PayPay and d払い. Tennōchō and Asamachō accept PayPay. Card payment at Higashiguchi is not confirmed.

Is there an English menu? Is the ordering tablet in Japanese?

MOS Burger publishes an English menu and an English allergen list for foreign visitors on mos.jp/inbound/en. At MOS Premium, recent Google reviews say the evening ordering tablet is in Japanese only.

Does MOS Burger have vegetarian options in Yokohama?

The regular MOS network has a Soy Patty option and a Green Burger, but I could not confirm their prices since July 2026. The MOS Premium menu lists no vegetarian burger, and no vegetarian item is exclusive to Yokohama.

Can I get MOS Burger delivered or order online in Yokohama?

Not confirmed. One aggregator lists takeout and delivery for MOS Premium Sakuragichō, but no official source confirms it, and nothing is verified for Higashiguchi. Check the MOS app or your delivery app before you count on it.
