Best Burger in San Sebastián: Inside La Burguessía's La Irresistible

Two cook brothers turned lockdown delivery into Gipuzkoa's best burger of 2024. What's in La Irresistible, where to eat it and what it costs.

By AlexPublished October 7, 2026

The best burger in San Sebastián, going by verified results, is La Irresistible at La Burguessía. At the 2024 Spanish Burger Championship (Campeonato de España de Hamburguesas) it was named best burger in Gipuzkoa and finished second in the Basque Country. In 2025 it was second in the Basque Country again. It costs €16.50, and all three La Burguessía locations in the city serve it: Gros, Centro and Antiguo.

This is a sourced profile, not a tasting review: I haven’t scored La Irresistible on flavour. On 7 October 2026 I checked every fact below against La Burguessía’s official site and menu, the San Sebastián tourism board listing and Basque local press, and each source is linked where I use it. Where two sources disagree, I show you both.

Who is behind La Burguessía?

La Burguessía belongs to two brothers who are both cooks: Mikel García and his brother, whose first name appears as Ion in some sources and Jon in others. I haven’t been able to confirm which spelling is right. They began selling burgers for delivery during the pandemic lockdown, cooked in the kitchen of their own daily-menu restaurant in Gros. The first La Burguessía opened there (Muselines, Onda Vasca).

No source gives a founding date more precise than ‘during the pandemic’, which puts it in 2020 or 2021. One more detail has only one source: in 2024 Onda Vasca reported that the Gros site shared its kitchen, dining room and staff with Restaurante Capricho on Calle Zabaleta, the brothers’ original restaurant.

What is in La Irresistible burger?

La Irresistible stacks aged beef with two Basque cheeses and two kinds of pork in a potato brioche bun, for €16.50. This is the build listed on the official menu page:

  • Aged beef patty (‘chuletón madurado’)
  • Smoked Idiazabal, the Basque sheep’s milk cheese
  • Agour cheese, from the French Basque Country
  • Caramelized onion
  • Pickles
  • Crispy bacon
  • Cured Iberian pancetta
  • Mayo-beef sauce
  • Potato brioche bun

On paper, that’s a heavy burger: two cheeses and two cuts of pork on one patty. The cheese is what I’d single it out for. Idiazabal comes from this side of the border and Agour from the French side, so the burger brings both halves of the Basque Country together. Many gourmet burgers could be served in any city; this one is clearly from here.

The official page now gives the patty weight as 180 g. Onda Vasca (2024) and Muselines both reported 200 g, and Onda Vasca named Rubia Gallega beef. The recipe may have changed since, so I use the current official figure. The site mentions 30-day ageing for some preparations only, so I won’t put one ageing figure on this burger.

Gros is the original. If the lockdown-delivery story is part of why you’re going, that’s the one I’d pick. As of 2024 it still shared its premises with Restaurante Capricho. Otherwise, all three serve the same burgers, so go to whichever is closest to where you’re staying.

Opening days: call before you go

Sources disagree on La Burguessía’s closing days, so phone the location before you go. The tourism board lists Gros as closed Monday to Wednesday; the official site shows only Wednesday. I found no published booking policy either, so book on the same call.

Is La Burguessía worth it?

Yes, if you want a burger built on Basque cheeses with an award record behind it, and €16.50 doesn’t put you off. It is the only burger in the city that I found with independently reported championship results in two consecutive years. The menu lists every ingredient, so you know what you’re paying for. Staying in the Old Town and don’t want to walk far? Garua is inside the Parte Vieja, and Va Bene is on the Boulevard at its edge. Va Bene is also the choice for a plain classic burger with four decades of history.

Does Mugaritz serve a burger?

No. Andoni Luis Aduriz’s Mugaritz is an avant-garde tasting-menu restaurant, and none of the sources I checked mentions a burger on its menu or any burger project by Aduriz. That includes the official Mugaritz site, Wikipedia and the 2026 season coverage in El Español and Infobae. Mugaritz is still worth mentioning because it sets the standard here. Visitors come expecting world-class cooking, so a burger has to be good to be noticed.

What Mugaritz actually is

Mugaritz is a two-Michelin-star tasting-menu restaurant in Errenteria, about 15 minutes outside San Sebastián. Andoni Luis Aduriz founded it in 1998 and still runs it as chef-owner in 2026. He was born in San Sebastián in 1971, trained at the Gipuzkoa catering school and joined Ferran Adrià at elBulli in 1993, according to The World’s 50 Best Restaurants. In May 2023 that organisation gave him its Icon Award.

FAQ

Where is the best burger in San Sebastián?

On verified results, it’s La Irresistible at La Burguessía. It was named best burger in Gipuzkoa in 2024 and placed second in the Basque Country in both 2024 and 2025.

What is in La Irresistible and how much does it cost?

Aged beef, smoked Idiazabal, Agour cheese, caramelized onion, pickles, crispy bacon, cured Iberian pancetta and mayo-beef sauce in a potato brioche bun. It costs €16.50.

Who owns La Burguessía in Donostia?

Two brothers who are both cooks: Mikel García and his brother, whose name sources spell as Ion or Jon. They started La Burguessía as lockdown burger delivery from their daily-menu restaurant in Gros.

Which La Burguessía location is best for visitors?

Gros (Calle Zabaleta 55) is the original. Centro (Plaza Arroka 14) and Antiguo (Avenida Zarautz 4) serve the same burgers. None of the three is in the Old Town.

Is there a La Burguessía burger restaurant in San Sebastián’s Old Town?

No. For a burger in the Parte Vieja, secondary sources point to Garua and its Basque Burger with Idiazabal and Padrón peppers.

Do I need to book, and which days is it closed?

Phone the location you plan to visit. The tourism board lists Gros as closed Monday to Wednesday, while the official site shows only Wednesday closed.

Are there gluten-free or vegetarian burgers?

La Burguessía offers gluten-free bread for €2 extra. I couldn’t verify a vegetarian burger, so ask when you call.

Is there a burger at Mugaritz or anywhere run by Andoni Luis Aduriz?

No. None of the sources I checked mentions a burger on the Mugaritz menu or any burger project by Aduriz. Mugaritz serves an avant-garde tasting menu.

Is Mugaritz in San Sebastián, and how much does it cost in 2026?

No. Mugaritz is in Errenteria, outside San Sebastián’s city limits and about 15 minutes away. The 2026 season opened on 1 May, with a tasting menu at €319 per person.
