Hans im Glück Bern: Station Menu, Prices, Hours

Where to find the burger grill under Bern station, what a burger costs in CHF, when it opens, what reviewers say, and why there is no Holy Cow! in Bern.

By Sofia, Restaurants writer, Italy, Switzerland and the BeneluxPublished November 11, 2026

Hans im Glück Bern Bahnhof is the burger grill inside Bern station: level -1 of the Christoffel underpass (Christoffelunterführung 2, 3011 Bern), open daily from 9 a.m. (10 a.m. at weekends), with the signature KLASSIK burger at CHF 15.50 on the Swiss menu I checked on 5 November 2026. Looking for Holy Cow! instead? The Lausanne-born chain has no restaurant in Bern. Its nearest branches are in Biel/Bienne (Rue de la Gare 38) and Fribourg (Square des Places 3), so at Bern station, Hans im Glück is the chain burger you will find.

Hans im Glück Bern at a glance

Hans im Glück Bern Bahnhof is a sit-down burger grill that also does takeaway and delivery, tucked into the underpass beneath Bern’s main station on the western edge of the Old Town. Where is Hans im Glück in Bern station?

Hans im Glück is on level -1 of Bern station, in the Christoffel underpass (Christoffelunterführung), the passage that runs beneath the tracks at the western edge of the Old Town. The SBB station guide lists it among the station’s shops and restaurants at that location, a short walk down from the platforms for travelers between trains.

Hans im Glück Bern opening hours

Hans im Glück Bern Bahnhof opens at 9 a.m. Monday to Friday and at 10 a.m. at weekends, and closes at 11 p.m. or midnight, according to the hours the restaurant gives MySwitzerland. The official page adds that the kitchen closes at 10:30 p.m., or 11:30 p.m. on Friday and Saturday.

  • Monday to Thursday: 9:00–23:00
  • Friday: 9:00–24:00
  • Saturday: 10:00–24:00
  • Sunday: 10:00–23:00

Treat these hours with some caution. The opening-hours label on the official page contradicts itself, and the SBB station page lists much later Friday and Saturday closing times that no other source backs up. Arriving on a late train? Phone first.

Hans im Glück Bern menu and prices in CHF

A beef burger at Hans im Glück Bern costs CHF 15.50 to CHF 24.90 on the Swiss menu, and every beef burger is made with a 160 g patty of 100% Swiss beef. The prices below come from the chain’s official Swiss menu (PDF), checked on 5 November 2026. The menu is undated and not specific to Bern, so prices may vary by restaurant.

Beef burgers

The KLASSIK is the chain’s signature burger, on the menu “since 2010”, and at CHF 15.50 it is the cheapest way into the beef range.

Smash burgers, Monday to Wednesday only

Smash burgers are served only from Monday to Wednesday: CHF 12.90 for a Single, CHF 19.90 for a Double and CHF 26.90 for a Triple. The Single is the cheapest burger on the whole Swiss menu. Early in the week, that makes it the one to order for a quick bite between trains.

Swiss chicken burgers

The chicken burgers use Swiss chicken fillet and cost CHF 17.90, or CHF 18.90 for the EITLER GOCKEL. The CHF 17.90 range includes MEISTERWERK, GESELLE, ZIEGENHIRTE, HUMMUSHELD and FEUERWERK.

Sides and drinks

Golden Fries cost CHF 7.50, the same as a small salad or coleslaw. Sweet potato fries and onion rings (Ringelei) cost CHF 8.50.

Lunch and evening set menus

The lunch set adds CHF 10.90 to the price of any burger until 5 p.m. every day, and the evening set adds CHF 17.90 from 5 p.m. The lunch set brings a side, a drink and a hot drink, so a KLASSIK lunch comes to CHF 26.40. The evening set brings a side and a cocktail or aperitif, so a KLASSIK dinner comes to CHF 33.40. Swapping in sweet potato fries costs CHF 1.00 extra.

My take: at lunch, take the set. A KLASSIK with Golden Fries ordered separately already costs CHF 23.00, and for CHF 3.40 more the set adds a drink and a coffee. The evening set is harder to justify unless you were going to order the cocktail anyway.

Vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free burgers in Bern

Hans im Glück Bern is one of the easiest places in Bern to get a vegan burger: the Swiss menu lists 8 vegan and 6 vegetarian burgers, and every bun except the gluten-free one is vegan. Vegan burgers cost CHF 15.50 for the GLÜCKSGRIFF and CHF 17.50 for the others.

  • Vegan (CHF 17.50): ERBSENKÖNIGIN, ZEITGEIST, KNUSPERSCHOTE, WOLPERTINGER, PESTOPRINZ, KRÄUTERRAUCHER, WELTENBUMMLER
  • Vegan (CHF 15.50): GLÜCKSGRIFF
  • Vegetarian (CHF 17.50): FARBENSPIEL, HORNTRÄGER, BEHERZTER, SEELENHEIL, CIAO BELLA, FALAFRESH

You choose your bun: sourdough, multigrain, brioche or no bun, all vegan. A gluten-free bun costs CHF 1.50 extra, but the menu warns of possible cross-contamination, so it is not safe for people with coeliac disease. The menu also states that no dish contains palm oil or artificial flavour enhancers.

Hans im Glück Bern reviews

Hans im Glück Bern Bahnhof scores 4.4/5 on Google from 1,440 reviews and 3.3/5 on Tripadvisor from 44 reviews, according to the aggregator Wanderlog on 5 November 2026. I did not check these ratings directly on Google Maps or Tripadvisor, and I show them side by side rather than averaging them.

  • What reviewers praise: the birch-forest decor, the many vegan and gluten-free options, friendly staff and the cocktails.
  • What reviewers criticize: taste and portions that some find average.

That is a wide gap, and I think it says something about the place. Google, with more than thirty times as many reviews, paints a broadly positive picture. The small Tripadvisor sample is harsher, and harsher on the food itself. My reading: come for a dependable burger between trains and a real vegan choice, not for a burger worth crossing the city for.

Hans im Glück Bahnhof vs Gümligen: which Bern restaurant to choose

Bern has two Hans im Glück restaurants. Bern Bahnhof, in the station, opened in 2019. Gümligen, in Muri bei Bern, opened in April 2018 in the Cinedome multiplex and was the chain’s first restaurant in Switzerland. Choose Bahnhof if you are traveling by train or staying in the Old Town, and Gümligen if you are pairing dinner with a film.

The station restaurant opens two hours earlier, which counts if you want an early lunch before a train. Gümligen is bigger and stays open 30 minutes longer on Friday and Saturday nights. The official site gives Gümligen’s postcode as 3073. Some directories list 3074, the postcode of Muri village.

The Hans im Glück chain and its arrival in Bern

Hans im Glück is a German burger grill chain founded in Munich in 2010 by Thomas Hirschberger. It counted 99 restaurants in October 2026, 12 of them in Switzerland. Bern was its way into the country: its first two Swiss restaurants are both in the Bern area.

The station branch opened in 2019 in the former premises of “Viva la Vida”, as Tageskarte reported on 18 July 2019. It is run by franchisees Michael Fricker, Urs Abegglen and Martin Wallimann. What sets the Swiss menu apart from the German one is the meat: 100% Swiss beef and chicken. I found no Bern-only item on the menu.

Is there a Holy Cow! in Bern?

No: Holy Cow! has no restaurant in Bern. When I checked on 5 November 2026, the chain’s official store locator listed 21 addresses across Switzerland, in cities including Lausanne, Geneva, Zurich, Basel, Lucerne, Sion and Vevey. Bern was not one of them.

The nearest Holy Cow! branches are in Biel/Bienne, at Rue de la Gare 38, 2502 Biel/Bienne, and in Fribourg, at Square des Places 3, 1700 Fribourg. Another burger chain, The Butcher at Aarbergergasse 36 in Bern, is listed by Wanderlog as permanently closed. For the chain’s story, read our Holy Cow! brand profile, our guide to Holy Cow Basel: address, hours and menu prices and the Holy Cow burger founder story in Lausanne.

Hans im Glück Bern FAQ

Is there a Holy Cow! in Bern?

No. Holy Cow! has 21 restaurants in Switzerland but none in Bern. The nearest are in Biel/Bienne (Rue de la Gare 38) and Fribourg (Square des Places 3). Hans im Glück at Bern station is the burger chain alternative in the city.

Where is Hans im Glück in Bern station?

It is on level -1 of Bern station, in the Christoffel underpass, at Christoffelunterführung 2, 3011 Bern, on the western edge of the Old Town.

What are Hans im Glück Bern’s opening hours on Sunday and in the evening?

On Sunday it is open from 10:00 to 23:00. It closes at 23:00 Monday to Thursday and at midnight on Friday and Saturday, according to MySwitzerland. The kitchen closes at 22:30, or 23:30 on Friday and Saturday.

How much does a burger cost at Hans im Glück Bern?

Beef burgers cost CHF 15.50 to CHF 24.90 on the Swiss menu checked on 5 November 2026. The cheapest burger is the CHF 12.90 Single smash burger, served Monday to Wednesday only. A lunch set with the KLASSIK costs CHF 26.40.

Does Hans im Glück Bern have vegan or gluten-free burgers?

Yes. The menu lists 8 vegan burgers from CHF 15.50 and 6 vegetarian burgers at CHF 17.50. A gluten-free bun costs CHF 1.50 extra, with a warning about possible cross-contamination.

Can you book a table at Hans im Glück Bern?

Yes, by phone on +41 31 311 04 77 or by email at bahnhof-bern@hansimglueck-burgergrill.ch. Online booking is currently unavailable.

Does Hans im Glück Bern offer delivery or takeaway?

Yes. Bern Bahnhof offers takeaway, plus delivery and online ordering through Uber Eats.

What is the difference between Hans im Glück Bern Bahnhof and Gümligen?

Bern Bahnhof is in the station, opened in 2019 and seats about 140 indoors. Gümligen, at Feldstrasse 32, 3073 Gümligen (Muri bei Bern), is the chain’s first Swiss restaurant: it opened in April 2018 in the Cinedome multiplex and seats 270 indoors. Bahnhof opens two hours earlier; Gümligen stays open 30 minutes later on Friday and Saturday.

Where does Hans im Glück’s meat come from in Switzerland?

The Swiss menu states that every beef burger uses a 160 g patty of 100% Swiss beef and that the chicken is also 100% Swiss.
