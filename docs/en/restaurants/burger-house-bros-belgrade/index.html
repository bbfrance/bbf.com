Burger House Bros Belgrade: Menu, Prices, Address

Smash burgers in Stari Grad, Belgrade: official prices from 780 RSD, Google and TripAdvisor ratings, address near Kalemegdan. Checked 30 October 2026.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 4, 2026

Burger House Bros is at Kralja Petra 10 in Stari Grad, a few minutes’ walk from Kalemegdan Fortress, and it is the chain’s only branch where you can sit down. Burgers cost 780 to 1,090 Serbian dinars (RSD) on the official menu. According to Wanderlog’s aggregate, it holds 4.5/5 on Google from 3,810 reviews. I checked every one of these facts on 30 October 2026, from the sources listed at the end. I have not eaten there yet. For a smash burger in central Belgrade, it is still the first name I would give you, though one experienced critic disagrees, and his case is below.

Burger House Bros Belgrade: practical information

The restaurant is at Kralja Petra 10, 11000 Belgrade, and its phone number is +381 11 405 6658. The address, phone and website come from the chain’s official site and were cross-checked with two local directories, PlanPlus.rs and Gault&Millau Serbia.

Burger House Bros Belgrade address and how to get there

Burger House Bros Centar is at Kralja Petra 10, on one of the old streets of Stari Grad that run down from the pedestrian zone towards Kalemegdan Fortress. If you spend the afternoon on the ramparts, it is a natural stop on the walk back into town.

  • Public transport: the Kalemegdan stop is about 263 m away, according to PlanPlus.rs.
  • Parking: on-street parking in zone A, or the paid car park at Obilićev venac 12, about 422 m away (PlanPlus.rs).
  • Neighbourhood: Stari Grad, next to Dorćol. Both appear in our guide to the best burgers in Belgrade.

Burger House Bros Belgrade opening hours

The opening hours are not confirmed. The official site gives none, and the two directories that do list hours disagree. PlanPlus.rs says Monday 12:00–24:00 and Tuesday to Sunday 10:00–24:00. Wanderlog says 11:00–23:00 every day. Before a late visit, call +381 11 405 6658 or check the Google listing.

Signature burgers: Super Smashed and the Bros Burger

Gault&Millau Serbia singles out two burgers: the Super Smashed, for its caramelised crust, and the Bros Burger, a 220 g patty of 45-day dry-aged beef with Valcasotto cheese. The guide also mentions a Kimchi Chicken Burger. None of the three had a price on the official site when I checked, so look for them on the in-house menu or on Wolt.

Sides and drinks

According to Gault&Millau, the sides are fries, croquettes, mozzarella sticks and onion rings. Reviewers keep praising the house-made fries. The truffle fries split them: several find the truffle overpowering. TableJourney and diners also mention a short list of craft beers, and Gault&Millau notes that the kitchen runs special editions for burger festivals.

Vegetarian options

Yes, there is a vegetarian option: Gault&Millau Serbia says the restaurant serves Beyond Meat burgers. The official site listed no vegetarian item or price when I checked, so ask which one is on the menu that day.

Burger House Bros Belgrade reviews: what customers and critics say

Diners rate Burger House Bros highly: 4.5/5 on both Google (3,810 reviews) and TripAdvisor (40 reviews), according to Wanderlog’s aggregate. TableJourney gives it 4.1/5 and places it in the mid-price ($$) bracket. One detailed independent review is far harsher.

What diners praise. The double and triple smash burgers come up in review after review, followed by the four-cheese Cheesy Burger and the Triple Cheese. People mention the house-made fries, friendly staff, craft beer and a terrace with a view of a church. TableJourney’s picks are the double cheeseburger and the chicken parmesan sandwich.

What diners criticise. Some say the portions are small for the price, and the truffle fries again. At peak times the terrace fills up fast.

The dissenting critic. Burgerdudes.se, a Swedish burger review site, first visited in 2015 and called the restaurant Belgrade’s “most popular burger joint”. When it came back on 8 July 2024, it gave 2/5: gristle in the meat, too little seasoning, an under-toasted bun, and a Double Smashed Oklahoma (2 × 60 g) that was short on cheese.

Is Burger House Bros the best burger in Belgrade?

Burger House Bros is a reference address for burgers in Belgrade, but its title is contested. Gault&Millau Serbia calls it “a landmark of Belgrade’s burger culture”, and thousands of Google reviewers agree. I take the Burgerdudes review seriously because the site knew the place in 2015 and saw it slip. Here is how I read the sources: thousands of diners praise the smash burgers, and the one serious complaint is about a thick patty, so I would order a smash burger and skip the truffle fries. Before it closed, the Vračar branch had 4.7/5 from 274 Google reviews. Older guides, In Your Pocket among them, still list a “Burger House” at Nikole Spasića 3. I found nothing to confirm that address, so do not go there expecting a burger.

The story of Burger House Bros

The brand says it was founded by three brothers, Stefan, Lazar and Mirko, who grew up in Chicago and came back to Serbia to make burgers from local produce. The official site gives 2013 as the year the company was registered as “Burger House Bros”, and claims more than a million burgers sold by its tenth anniversary. Those claims come only from the brand. Independent sources, Burgerdudes.se and In Your Pocket, confirm that the business was trading as “Burger House” by 2015.

Burger House Bros Belgrade FAQ

Where is Burger House Bros in Belgrade? At Kralja Petra 10 in Stari Grad, a few minutes’ walk from Kalemegdan Fortress. The Kalemegdan public transport stop is about 263 m away.

What are Burger House Bros’ opening hours? They are not confirmed. PlanPlus.rs and Wanderlog give different hours, and the official site gives none. Call +381 11 405 6658 or check Google before you go.

How much does a burger cost at Burger House Bros? On the official site on 30 October 2026, the Crispy Chicken cost 780 RSD, the Classic Cheeseburger 950 RSD and the Super Smash Triple 1,090 RSD. Serbia uses the dinar, not the euro.

What should I order at Burger House Bros? Order a smash burger. Reviewers praise the double and triple smash burgers more than any other dish. Gault&Millau Serbia also recommends the Bros Burger: 220 g of 45-day dry-aged beef with Valcasotto cheese.

Does Burger House Bros have vegetarian options? Yes. Gault&Millau Serbia mentions Beyond Meat burgers. Ask which one is on the menu that day.

Can I get Burger House Bros delivered in Belgrade or New Belgrade? Yes, through Wolt, the delivery partner named on the official site. New Belgrade is served from a delivery address at Jurija Gagarina 19. Wolt prices may differ from the official menu.

Do I need to book a table at Burger House Bros? I could not confirm whether the restaurant takes bookings. The terrace fills up fast at peak times, so call ahead or come before the lunch and dinner rush.
