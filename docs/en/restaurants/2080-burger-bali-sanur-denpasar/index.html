2080 Burger Bali: Heru Dwi Soesilo and the Sanur Branch in Denpasar

Who owns Bali's award-winning burger brand, what the 20/80 name means, why its brisket smokes for up to 18 hours, and where to eat it in Denpasar.

By AlexPublished October 8, 2026

2080 Burger is a Bali-born burger brand owned by Heru Dwi Soesilo, and its only Denpasar branch is in Sanur, at Jl. Danau Poso No. 100. The name is the patty: 20% fat, 80% Australian beef. The brisket smokes for up to 18 hours, the rest goes over charcoal, and in October 2025 the brand won Best Western Restaurant at the Exquisite Awards 2025. For a serious burger inside Denpasar city limits, Sanur is where I would send you.

One rumour to clear up first. Some searches link chef William Wongso to a Denpasar burger spot called William’s Gourmet. I found no trace of that restaurant, or of any burger business of his. Wongso is known as an ambassador for traditional Indonesian cooking, not burgers. The Bali burger brand with a documented owner, an award and a Denpasar address is 2080 Burger, and it is the subject of this profile.

Who owns 2080 Burger?

Heru Dwi Soesilo is the owner and co-founder of 2080 Burger, according to Baliwara (April 2024) and Bisnis.com (November 2022). The sources describe him as the entrepreneur behind the business, not its chef. I could not verify what he did before 2080 Burger, so I will not guess.

In April 2024 he took his burgers to an international stage. 2080 Burger was a food provider for the gala dinner of the 57th APEC SMEWG meeting (Small and Medium Enterprises Working Group), held on 24 and 25 April 2024 in Nusa Dua. He told Baliwara: “Very happy to represent Indonesian culinary small businesses and see 2080 Burger enjoyed by the delegates” (translated from Indonesian).

The story: from Uluwatu surfers to an award

2080 Burger started in 2019 serving the surfing community in Uluwatu, then survived the pandemic on WhatsApp orders before joining GoFood in 2020. Baliwara and Bisnis.com both give 2019 as the start, and Bisnis says October 2019. The brand’s own site says it was established in 2020, so the opening date depends on how you count.

The pandemic could have ended it. Bali’s tourist trade collapsed, and a burger shop built for surfers lost its customers overnight. The team took orders by WhatsApp, then moved onto GoFood, the delivery app of Gojek, in 2020, according to Baliwara.

Recognition followed. The ESB case study says the brand is listed among the Top 80 Asia Resto & Hotels by asiatop80.com and runs the ESB point-of-sale system across its branches. Then, in an article dated 20 October 2025, Exquisite Taste Magazine named 2080 Burger the winner of Best Western Restaurant at the Exquisite Awards 2025.

The 2080 concept: 20/80 beef and 18-hour smoked brisket

The 2080 concept rests on two things: a 20% fat, 80% meat patty of Australian beef, and brisket smoked for up to 18 hours. The ESB case study and Bisnis.com both explain the ratio behind the name. Twenty percent is a classic burger-blend ratio, fatty enough to stay juicy over a hot grill, and the brand made it the name on the sign.

Smoked brisket burger in Bali

2080 Burger’s smoked brisket is cooked for up to 18 hours, per Exquisite Taste Magazine (October 2025). That brisket goes into the Black Mamba burger and the 18h Smoked Brisket Platter, both on the official menu. Eighteen hours is competition-barbecue territory. It is the reason I would order the Black Mamba here before any of the classic cheeseburgers.

Charcoal grilling

The kitchen grills beef, chicken, pork and vegetables over charcoal, according to the same Exquisite Taste article. Charcoal puts smoke into the crust. Because pork goes on the same grill, halal diners should read the FAQ below before ordering.

2080 Burger Sanur: address, hours and getting there

The Denpasar branch of 2080 Burger is 2080 Burger x Cibo Pasta House, Jl. Danau Poso No. 100, Sanur, Denpasar Selatan, Bali 80228, phone +62 822 4761 2626. Sanur is part of the city of Denpasar, on its south-east coast, so this is the only branch on the brand’s official list that lies within city limits.

Other burgers in Denpasar: Kandi Burger

Kandi Burger is the other name to know for the best burger in Denpasar: homemade burgers served in a Balinese family house with local sambals. It sits at Jl. Gunung Welirang No. 19 C, Pemecutan Klod, Denpasar Barat. The burgers come with sambal terasi, sambal matah and sambal merah, per detikBali (1 February 2025) and NomNom Bali.

FAQ

Who owns 2080 Burger in Bali?

Heru Dwi Soesilo is the owner and co-founder of 2080 Burger, according to Baliwara (April 2024) and Bisnis.com (November 2022).

What does the name 2080 Burger mean?

The name stands for the patty ratio: 20% fat and 80% meat, made from Australian beef.

Where is 2080 Burger in Denpasar, and what are its hours?

It is at Jl. Danau Poso No. 100, Sanur, Denpasar Selatan, Bali 80228 (+62 822 4761 2626). Chope lists 10:00 to 23:00 Monday to Wednesday and 10:00 to midnight Thursday to Sunday; call ahead to confirm.

How long is the brisket smoked at 2080 Burger?

Up to 18 hours, according to Exquisite Taste Magazine (October 2025). It features in the Black Mamba burger and the 18h Smoked Brisket Platter.

How much does a burger cost at 2080 Burger?

The official site lists no prices and I found no dated menu to cite. Check the GoFood listing or ask the branch for current prices.

Is 2080 Burger halal?

Halal status is not confirmed. Exquisite Taste Magazine reports that pork is grilled over charcoal alongside beef, chicken and vegetables, so do not assume it is halal.

Did William Wongso own a burger restaurant in Denpasar?

I found no evidence of it. Wongso’s documented Bali work is Indonesian cuisine: he was chef patron of Arsana at Alila Seminyak and cooked the Table of Three dinner at CasCades, Viceroy Bali, Ubud, on 2 May 2026.

Where can I eat the best burger in Denpasar?

For smoked brisket and charcoal-grilled beef, go to 2080 Burger in Sanur. For a homemade burger with Balinese sambals from IDR 35,000 (detikBali, February 2025), go to Kandi Burger in Denpasar Barat.

How many 2080 Burger branches are there, and is there one in Jakarta?

The official site lists four as of 8 October 2026: Uluwatu, Sanur, Seminyak and Jakarta Ancol, at Beach City Entertainment Center. Other sources mention Pererenan and Nusa Dua.
