Best Burgers in Brno: Centre, Veveří, Festival

The best burgers in Brno are concentrated in the compact historic centre, between náměstí Svobody, the main square, and the main railway station, and in the university districts of Veveří and Královo Pole. Menus split between thin smash burgers and thicker beef patties, usually served with Czech beer.

Brno is a student city first. Masaryk University, Brno University of Technology and Mendel University fill it with young, price-conscious diners, and that keeps casual burger kitchens busy well into the evening. The city is also a regular stop for the Burger Street Festival, which describes itself as the largest travelling street-food event in Czechia and comes to Brno twice a year. Its spring 2026 edition ran from 29 to 31 May, with free entry, in the former factory setting between the Vaňkovka shopping gallery and the Wannieck Gallery.

Burgers in Brno: key facts

  • Neighbourhoods to explore: the historic centre around náměstí Svobody and Zelný trh, the streets near the main station, Veveří and Královo Pole.
  • Festival: the Burger Street Festival sets up in Brno twice a year and brings kitchens from across Czechia.
  • Wine country next door: South Moravia, the main Czech wine region, begins just south of the city.
  • Currency: prices are in Czech koruna (CZK), the national currency.
  • Getting around: the centre is walkable, and the city’s tram network reaches Veveří, Královo Pole and the station.
