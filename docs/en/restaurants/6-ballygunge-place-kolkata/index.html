6 Ballygunge Place Kolkata: Menu, Branches, Chef

6 Ballygunge Place, Kolkata: who founded it, its 7 branches, the price for 2 (about ₹1,000) and what to order first. Checked 11 October 2026.

By Arjun, Restaurants writer, IndiaPublished October 18, 2026

6 Ballygunge Place is Kolkata’s best-known Bengali restaurant. It was founded in 2003 by 3 former colleagues from The Park Hotel: Chef Sushanta Sengupta, Swaminathan Ramani and Aninda Palit. The flagship fills a restored Raj-era bungalow at 6 Dr Amiya Bose Sarani Road, Ballygunge Place, and opens daily for lunch and dinner. Expect about ₹1,000 for two. There is no burger on the menu. Come for daab chingri and shorshe ilish instead.

Who founded 6 Ballygunge Place?

6 Ballygunge Place was founded in 2003 by Swaminathan Ramani, Aninda Palit and Chef Sushanta Sengupta, through Savourites Hospitality Pvt Ltd, the company they had set up in 1998. All 3 had worked together at The Park Hotel in Kolkata. Their starting point was simple: a visitor who wanted a proper Bengali meal had nowhere to sit down and order 1 (Wikipedia; official site).

They split the work by skill, according to Business Standard in 2011 and Wikipedia. Sengupta took the kitchen. Ramani took sales and marketing, and Palit food and beverage. No recent source confirms that Palit is still involved.

Sushanta Sengupta, the founding chef

Sushanta Sengupta is the founding director and chef of Savourites Hospitality, the group behind 6 Ballygunge Place. A 2017 Restaurant India profile says he graduated from IHM Kolkata in 1993 and trained with the Taj in Delhi. He then became Senior Sous Chef at Zen, the oriental restaurant at The Park in Kolkata (Restaurant India, 2017).

Bengali food is only half his story. The same profile credits him with launching The Wall, billed as Kolkata’s first pan-Asian restaurant serving authentic Japanese food, and with co-creating 6 Ballygunge Place Thali, presented as the first quick-service format for Bengali cuisine.

1 caveat. That 2017 profile is the latest detailed one I found, and the 2024 coverage of the Delhi opening quotes only S. Ramani. Sengupta is the founding chef. Whether he still runs the pass every night, I can’t confirm.

Chef Sushanta Sengupta’s career at a glance: IHM Kolkata graduate (1993), trained with the Taj in Delhi, Senior Sous Chef at Zen at The Park in Kolkata, co-founder of Savourites Hospitality (1998), 6 Ballygunge Place (2003) and 6 Ballygunge Place Thali (2013). Sources: Restaurant India 2017 and the official site. No licensed portrait of the chef is available. (Table: Burger Breton, from Restaurant India (2017) and 6ballygungeplace.in/about.php)

The story: a trademarked address and old Bengali cookbooks

2 things set 6 Ballygunge Place apart: its name is a registered trademark, and its menu grew out of historical research. The restaurant says it was the first retail outlet in India to register its address as a trademark. The name is literally where the first dining room stands.

The building is a former Rai Bahadur’s bungalow, restored in its Raj-era style. The menu draws on Pragya Sundari Devi’s early Bengali cookbook and on food references in Vaishnav literature (official site). You can feel it on the page. The menu is organised by river fish and household dishes, not by the butter chicken and naan of a standard Indian restaurant.

6 Ballygunge Place Kolkata: address, hours and price for 2

The Ballygunge flagship is at 6 Dr Amiya Bose Sarani Road, near Patha Bhavan School, Ballygunge Place, Kolkata 700019. It opens every day from 12:00 to 15:30 and from 19:00 to 22:30. EazyDiner puts the cost for 2 at about ₹1,000.

Branches: how many outlets does 6 Ballygunge Place have?

6 Ballygunge Place runs 7 full-service restaurants, 6 in West Bengal and 1 in New Delhi, plus 4 Thali outlets and about 15 Takeaway counters. That count comes from the official outlet list, checked on 11 October 2026.

Restaurant, Thali or Takeaway: what’s the difference?

The full-service restaurants serve the à la carte menu at the table. The Thali outlets serve fixed set plates in a quick-service format, and the Takeaway counters sell food to go. The 4 Thali outlets are in Kasba, Salt Lake Sector V, Jodhpur Park and Kamalgazi. My advice: if you have 1 meal in Kolkata, make it a long lunch at the bungalow. A Thali is the cheap, fast introduction, and Takeaway is for a hotel-room dinner.

The same group’s other restaurants

Savourites Hospitality runs 3 sister brands in Kolkata: The Bengal Lounge (6 Netaji Subhas Road, BBD Bagh), Ramanee (P569 Lake Terrace) and The Wall (31 Dr Sarat Banerjee Road), Sengupta’s pan-Asian project.

6 Ballygunge Place signature dishes: what to order

For a first visit, order daab chingri, shorshe ilish and kosha mangsho, and finish with mishti doi. The restaurant and Wikipedia both single these out as its signatures.

  • Daab chingri: prawns cooked and served inside a tender green coconut, the restaurant’s best-known plate.
  • Shorshe ilish: hilsa in mustard sauce. Hilsa is the fish Bengal argues about most, and it is full of fine bones, so eat slowly.
  • Chingri malai curry: prawns in a coconut-milk curry.
  • Kosha mangsho: slow-cooked, dry-style mutton.
  • Paturi: fish steamed in a banana leaf.
  • Mochar ghonto: banana flower cooked as a vegetable dish.
  • Fish Fry with kasundi: a breaded, deep-fried fish fillet with Bengali mustard relish.
  • Desserts: mishti doi (sweet set yogurt), payesh (rice pudding) and sandesh (fresh cheese sweet).

The menu runs from starters to mains (vegetables, prawns, crab, hilsa, katla, pabda, shutki or dried fish, mutton, pulao and breads), then desserts and mocktails. Cocktails are served only at the Ecosuite (New Town) and Chowringhee branches (official menu; EazyDiner).

Why a Bengali restaurant on a burger site?

Because you can’t understand what Kolkata wants from a burger without its fried snacks. The Fish Fry with kasundi is a breaded fillet with a sharp mustard relish, and that pairing shows up again in the city’s burger joints.

A Bengali fish fry: breaded bhetki fillet served with mustard sauce, the closest thing to a fish burger on the 6 Ballygunge Place menu (Fish Fry with kasundi). This is an illustrative photo, not taken at the restaurant (3929 × 2494 px). (Photo: Riya Chattopadhyay, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bengali\_Fish\_Fry.jpg)

I’ll be blunt, though: 6 Ballygunge Place is not part of Kolkata’s burger scene, and I won’t dress it up as one. Go for the Bengali food. In Delhi: the Malviya Nagar branch

Yes, there is a 6 Ballygunge Place in Delhi. It opened in Malviya Nagar in September 2024, the brand’s first branch outside West Bengal. The Week reported that Bank of India financed the outlet, that meat is flown in from Kolkata every 3 days and that the poppy seeds are stone-ground (The Week, 16 October 2024; Outlook Traveller). FAQ

Who founded 6 Ballygunge Place, and when?

Swaminathan Ramani, Aninda Palit and Chef Sushanta Sengupta founded 6 Ballygunge Place in Kolkata in 2003, through Savourites Hospitality Pvt Ltd. All 3 had worked together at The Park Hotel.

Who is the chef at 6 Ballygunge Place?

The founding chef is Sushanta Sengupta, an IHM Kolkata graduate (1993) who trained with the Taj and was Senior Sous Chef at Zen at The Park. His most recent detailed profile dates from 2017, so his current day-to-day role is unconfirmed.

Where is 6 Ballygunge Place, and how many branches does it have?

The flagship is at 6 Dr Amiya Bose Sarani Road, Ballygunge Place, Kolkata 700019. The official list shows 7 full-service restaurants (six in West Bengal, one in New Delhi), 4 Thali outlets and about 15 Takeaway counters.

What is the price for 2 at 6 Ballygunge Place?

About ₹1,000 for 2 at the Ballygunge flagship and about ₹1,800 at Chowringhee, according to EazyDiner (checked 11 October 2026).

What are the timings of 6 Ballygunge Place in Ballygunge?

The Ballygunge flagship opens every day from 12:00 to 15:30 and from 19:00 to 22:30.

What should I order at 6 Ballygunge Place for a first visit?

Daab chingri, shorshe ilish and kosha mangsho, with the Fish Fry with kasundi as a starter and mishti doi or sandesh for dessert.

Does 6 Ballygunge Place serve burgers?

No. No burger, bun or sandwich appears on the official menu or on EazyDiner. It is a Bengali-cuisine restaurant.

Is there a 6 Ballygunge Place in Delhi?

Yes. It opened in September 2024 on the ground floor of Eldeco Centre, Malviya Nagar, New Delhi 110017.

What is the difference between 6 Ballygunge Place, 6 Ballygunge Place Thali and the Takeaway outlets?

The restaurants serve the full à la carte menu at the table. Thali outlets serve fixed set plates in a quick-service format, and Takeaway counters sell food to go.
