How "burger near me" works

This page uses the location your browser shares to pick the closest city we cover, then opens the burger guide for that city.

  1. You tap "Find burgers near me" and your browser asks whether to share your location.
  2. Your device measures the straight-line distance between you and the center of each of the 302 cities we cover.
  3. You land on the hub of the closest city, with our burger guides and the restaurants we recommend there.

Your location stays on your device

The distance calculation runs entirely in your browser. Your coordinates are never sent to Burger Breton, never stored and never shared: the list of cities ships with the page itself.

Burger near me: frequently asked questions

How do I find the best burger near me?

Open this page, tap "Find burgers near me" and allow location access. You land on the guide to the closest city we cover, with the burger restaurants we recommend there, their addresses and their prices.

What if my town is not on the list?

You are sent to the closest city we cover, which can be a drive away if you live in a small town. We add new cities to the Restaurants section regularly.

Does Burger Breton store my location?

No. Your browser reads your position only to measure the distance to each city, on your device. It is never sent to our servers.

Why does nothing happen when I tap the button?

Your browser or phone is probably blocking location access. Allow it for this site in your settings and try again, or choose your city from the full directory.
