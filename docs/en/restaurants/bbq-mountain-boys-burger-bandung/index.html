BBQ Mountain Boys Burger Bandung: Menu & Founders

How YouTube barbecue cooks Hedi Rusdian and Gianjar Saribanon built a Bandung burger counter at Paskal Hyper Square, with its menu and 2022 prices.

By Hana, Restaurants writer, Japan and IndonesiaPublished November 24, 2026

BBQ Mountain Boys Burger (BMBB) is the Bandung burger counter of Hedi Rusdian and Gianjar Saribanon, the two cooks behind the BBQ Mountain Boys YouTube channel. It opened in December 2019 in the Paskal Hyper Square food court area, in Andir district, and it grinds its own patties from several beef cuts and bakes its own brioche buns. The signature is the BMB Cabin Style Pineapple Burger: Rp 63,500 in July 2022. A kitchen fire hit it in September 2021. In May 2026 it was still on Bandung’s best-burger lists.

Looking for Lawless Burgerbar in Bandung? It has no permanent restaurant here. The last section explains why, and our Lawless Burgerbar Jakarta profile tells the chain’s own story.

Who owns BBQ Mountain Boys Burger in Bandung?

Hedi Rusdian and Gianjar Saribanon founded BBQ Mountain Boys Burger and still run it. Both detikFood (11 July 2022) and Radar Bogor/Jawapos (10 May 2025) name them as the founders.

From a YouTube channel to a burger counter

BBQ Mountain Boys began in 2018 as a YouTube channel run by Hedi Rusdian and Gianjar Saribanon. It travelled far enough to get an English-language feature, ‘Meet Indonesia’s BBQ Mountain Boys’, republished by AOL. According to detikFood, the restaurant came next, in December 2019.

What Hedi Rusdian wanted the burger to be

Hedi Rusdian wanted a burger Indonesians would take to easily, one that didn’t leave him overstuffed the way other burgers did. He told detikFood in 2022 that this was where the menu started. To me it’s the most revealing line in the whole story. It explains why BMBB talks about juiciness and balance, and never about size.

How BBQ Mountain Boys makes its burgers

BMBB makes both its patties and its buns in-house: beef ground from several cuts, and a homemade brioche. That pairing is what Indonesian food media mean when they call it a cabin-style burger.

The patty: several cuts, 2 grinds, medium-rare

Hedi Rusdian builds each patty from several beef cuts, lean to fatty. As he described it to detikFood in July 2022, he grinds the meat both fine and coarse, coats it in a house spice rub and cooks it medium-rare. The fine grind holds the patty together. The coarse one gives it bite.

The bun: homemade brioche

The buns are brioche, made in-house. Reports differ on exactly who does the baking, so I’ll stick to the point they all agree on: the bread isn’t bought in.

In May 2023, detikJabar put the range at Rp 30,000 to Rp 70,000 per item. By May 2025, Radar Bogor’s menu listed Classic Single and Double Cheese burgers, mac and cheese, fries and chicken strips. It also mentions a dip sauce that mixes garlic, cheese and Nashville heat.

What to order first

If you only try one burger, order the BMB Cabin Style Pineapple Burger. What’s New Indonesia named it the signature in May 2026, and the pineapple is what sets it apart from the city’s many smash burgers. My own pick for a first visit would still be the Classic Single or Double Cheese. With no fruit and no extras, it’s the honest test of the two things BMBB stakes its name on, the house grind and the house brioche. At Rp 45,000 in 2022 for the Classic, it was also the cheaper way to find out.

Where to find it: Bandung burger at Paskal Hyper Square

BBQ Mountain Boys Burger is in the Paskal Hyper Square complex (The Tripark and 23 Paskal food court area), Kecamatan Andir, Bandung. The phone number published in 2022 is +62 811-2222-2802.

The last published opening hours are 10:00 to 20:00, from detikFood in 2022 and detikJabar in 2023. Nothing newer has appeared. No source gives the unit number inside the complex or says when it is quieter, so call ahead or check the brand’s Instagram before you make a special trip.

The 2021 fire at 23 Paskal and the comeback

On Saturday 25 September 2021, a fire broke out in the BBQ Mountain Boys Burger kitchen in the 23 Paskal food court. No one was killed. It was reported at 17:32 WIB and was out by 18:00 WIB. PRFM News and Urban Jabar traced it to hot cooking oil catching a spark at the stove.

The counter came back. detikFood profiled it in July 2022 and Radar Bogor in May 2025, and on 23 May 2026 What’s New Indonesia listed it among Bandung’s best burger places.

Is BBQ Mountain Boys the best burger in Bandung?

BBQ Mountain Boys Burger is one of the names that keeps turning up whenever Bandung’s best burgers are ranked: detikJabar put it on its list in 2023, and What’s New Indonesia did again in 2026. Its case rests on something few rivals in the city combine: a patty ground in-house from several cuts, on a brioche baked in-house.

On paper, I think it has earned its place on those lists. Whether it deserves the top spot is a question only a visit can settle, and I haven’t made that visit yet, so this page gives no rating. Is Lawless Burgerbar in Bandung?

No: Lawless Burgerbar has no documented permanent restaurant in Bandung. It is a Jakarta chain, and its only recorded Bandung appearance was a short pop-up at BRACI in 2026.

Lawless Burgerbar opened in 2017 at Jl. Kemang Selatan VIII No. 67H, Jakarta, as The Jakarta Post reported. Arian Arifin and Sammy Bramantyo of the band Seringai founded it with partners. In August 2022, Kompas counted 6 branches, all in Greater Jakarta: Kemang, Menteng, Bintaro, Cibubur, PIK and Blok M. Sammy Bramantyo is CEO of PT Lawless Burgerbar Asia. The full story is in our Lawless Burgerbar Jakarta profile.

The BRACI x Lawless Burgerbar pop-up ran from 25 June to 5 July 2026 at BRACI, a Western and Asian fusion restaurant at Jl. Sulanjana No. 5, Tamansari, Bandung Wetan. According to Ayobiz, it served 2 collaboration burgers and 1 Lawless signature. The Jakarta team did live cooking and took over the barbecue pit on 25 and 26 June. BRACI’s marketing strategist, Arindya Putri Devina, told Ayobiz that the previous year’s collaboration sold more than 1,000 burgers in 2 days. That figure is the company’s own claim.

1 more mix-up worth clearing up: Jl. Citarum No. 12 in Cihapit is Five Monkeys Burger, not Lawless Burgerbar.

FAQ: BBQ Mountain Boys Burger Bandung

Who owns BBQ Mountain Boys Burger in Bandung? Hedi Rusdian and Gianjar Saribanon, the duo behind the BBQ Mountain Boys YouTube channel, according to detikFood (2022) and Radar Bogor (2025).

Is BBQ Mountain Boys Burger still open after the 23 Paskal fire? Yes. No one was killed in the kitchen fire of 25 September 2021. The restaurant was profiled again in 2022 and 2025, and in May 2026 it was listed among Bandung’s best burger places.

How much does a burger cost at BBQ Mountain Boys? In July 2022, burgers cost Rp 43,500 to Rp 63,500. In 2023, detikJabar gave a range of Rp 30,000 to Rp 70,000 per item. No 2026 prices have been published.

What is the best burger to order at BBQ Mountain Boys? The BMB Cabin Style Pineapple Burger is the signature, as What’s New Indonesia noted in 2026. For a straighter test of the patty and the bun, order the Classic Single or Double Cheese.

Are the patties and buns made in-house? Yes. The patties are ground in-house from several beef cuts at 2 textures, and the brioche buns are made in-house.

What are the opening hours? The last published hours are 10:00 to 20:00 (2022 and 2023). Current hours and quieter times are unconfirmed, so call +62 811-2222-2802 or check Instagram first.

Is BBQ Mountain Boys Burger halal? I found no halal certification for BBQ Mountain Boys Burger, so I can’t say it is halal. Ask the staff directly.

Does BBQ Mountain Boys have other branches? I found none beyond Paskal Hyper Square, but no official source confirms it is the only location.

Is there a Lawless Burgerbar in Bandung? Not a permanent one. Lawless is a Jakarta chain, and its only Bandung appearance was the BRACI pop-up from 25 June to 5 July 2026.

How I researched this article

I put this profile together on 18 November 2026 from Indonesian food media (detikFood, detikJabar, Radar Bogor/Jawapos, Ayobiz, Kompas), local news coverage of the 2021 fire and What’s New Indonesia’s 2026 list. I didn’t eat at BBQ Mountain Boys Burger for this piece, which is why it carries no rating. I left out 3 unverified claims: the founders’ current ages, details from a paywalled 2021 Jakarta Post profile, and a social-media rumour about ownership that contradicts the named founders.
