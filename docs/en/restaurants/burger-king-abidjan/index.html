Burger King Abidjan: Locations, Menu Prices, Hours and Reviews (2026)

Burger King Abidjan by district: where the nine listings are, a 7,500 FCFA Whopper meal, a 2 a.m. closing in Cocody and the 2026 change of owner.

By AlexPublished October 10, 2026

Burger King Abidjan is a network of about nine restaurants in Côte d’Ivoire, most of them in Abidjan’s shopping malls, plus one at Félix-Houphouët-Boigny International Airport. On 9 October 2026, a Whopper meal cost 7,500 CFA francs (about €11.45) on Glovo delivery. Hungry after midnight? Head to Cocody Val-Doyen. It is listed as open until 2 a.m. and has more reviews than any other branch in the city. Below are the locations by district, a dated price table, the delivery options and what the ratings actually show.

How I built this Burger King Abidjan guide

I have not eaten at a Burger King in Abidjan, and this guide does not pretend I have. It is built from sources, and I checked every one of them on 9 October 2026: the 2015–2026 press coverage of the franchise, the official Cosmos Yopougon mall page, the official Burger King Côte d’Ivoire Facebook page, the BAAB city directory, Yandex Maps ratings and the Glovo menu of the “Burger King Abidjan Nord” store.

Abidjan directories contradict each other on phone numbers, opening hours and even street numbers. When two sources disagreed and I could not settle it, I left the detail out. That is why you will find no phone number here.

Burger King in Ivory Coast: from the first West African restaurant to Enko Capital

Burger King came to Ivory Coast in December 2015, when it opened its first restaurant in West Africa at the CFAO PlaYce Marcory mall in Abidjan. The mall opened on 10 December 2015. The 90 m² restaurant created about 50 jobs and was run by Servair Abidjan as exclusive franchisee (Financial Afrik, Franchising.com).

The second restaurant opened on 30 March 2016 in the public area of Félix-Houphouët-Boigny International Airport. At 294 m² with 39 staff, it was more than three times the size of the first (Jeune Afrique). In January 2018, Servair announced the transfer of three restaurants (Marcory, Port-Bouët and Plateau) to Sia Restaurant Côte d’Ivoire, a company set up in April 2017 (CCI-LCI).

Who owns Burger King Côte d’Ivoire in 2026?

Since April 2026, Burger King Côte d’Ivoire has been owned by the investment firm Enko Capital. On 23 April 2026, Enko announced it was buying Sia Restauration rapide Côte d’Ivoire, the Servair subsidiary that runs the Burger King franchise and its nine restaurants in the country. The deal goes through the FCPR Enko PME fund, which is approved by the AMF-UMOA, the regional market regulator (Forbes Afrique, Ecofin).

Enko Capital was founded in 2008 by Alain and Cyrille Nkontchou. No price was disclosed. The press has floated expansion across the WAEMU zone, but nobody has announced a single new opening, so I am not treating it as a plan.

The brand itself was founded in Miami in 1954 and belongs to Restaurant Brands International. Almost all of its restaurants are franchised, and it has more than 18,700 locations in over 100 countries, as I noted in the Burger King Cairo guide.

Burger King Plateau

Burger King Plateau is on Avenue Jean Delafosse in Abidjan’s business district. It has the best rating I found: 4.6/5 on Yandex Maps from 62 reviews. One directory puts it at number 17 and Yandex at number 20, so search for the avenue, not the street number.

Burger King Cocody: Val-Doyen, Génie 2000, Sococé and Palmeraie

Cocody has the most Burger King listings in Abidjan, and Val-Doyen is my pick for a late meal. Yandex rates it 4.4/5 from 84 reviews, the largest review count in the city, and lists it as open until 2 a.m. Génie 2000 closes much earlier, at 9:30 p.m., and its 4.2/5 rests on just 10 reviews. Sococé and Palmeraie appear in the BAAB directory. I found no second source for either.

Burger King Marcory and Cap Sud

Marcory is where Burger King first opened in West Africa, but I cannot confirm that the original PlaYce Marcory restaurant is still open. Yandex has a “Marcory” location rated 3.7/5 from 12 reviews, listed until 10 p.m., with no exact address. Two other listings (rue G20 and rue F6) may be duplicates. Cap Sud appears in the directory, with two different phone numbers depending on the source.

Burger King Yopougon Cosmos

Burger King Yopougon is in the Cosmos Yopougon mall, at unit R3 on the ground floor of the Espace Figayo. No other location in the city is as well documented: the mall’s official store page lists it as the first Burger King in Yopougon. The opening hours are another matter. The mall and a directory disagree, so check before you go.

Burger King Abidjan airport

Yes, there is a Burger King at Abidjan’s Félix-Houphouët-Boigny International Airport, in the public area before security. It opened on 30 March 2016. Yandex rates it 4.2/5 from 16 reviews and lists it as open until midnight, which is handy for a late arrival. Once you are through security, it is out of reach.

Sides and drinks

Fries cost 1,500 FCFA (about €2.30) on Glovo Abidjan Nord on 9 October 2026, and BIG fries 2,500 FCFA. Four nuggets cost 2,500 FCFA, three Tender Strips 3,500 FCFA, a 30 cl Pepsi or 7UP 1,000 FCFA and a 20 cl Pressea juice 500 FCFA. Source: Glovo, Burger King Abidjan Nord.

Best value on the Burger King Abidjan menu

The Big King meal is the best value on the Abidjan menu: at 6,000 FCFA, it costs exactly what a Whopper costs on its own. Meals are worth it in general. A Whopper, fries and a 30 cl Pepsi ordered separately come to 8,500 FCFA, against 7,500 FCFA for the Whopper meal. On a tight budget, the Hamburger (2,500 FCFA) and the Cheeseburger (3,000 FCFA) are the cheapest way in. For how these prices sit against value menus elsewhere, see my piece on the 2026 burger value wars.

Chicken, wraps and vegetarian options at Burger King Abidjan

Chicken carries a lot of the Burger King Abidjan menu. On Glovo Côte d’Ivoire, the most-ordered items include the Angry Wrap Poulet (chicken), the Long Chili Cheese Chicken and the Triple Whopper Cheese (Glovo). I could only find these on the delivery menu, not on an official Burger King menu.

Vegetarians get very little. The only meat-free item I found on the delivery menu is the Salade Fraîcheur, and I could not confirm a plant-based Whopper or any other vegetarian burger in Abidjan.

Burger King Abidjan delivery: Glovo and Yango Deli

Yes, Burger King delivers in Abidjan through Glovo, which covers Abidjan Sud, Abidjan Nord and Yopougon. The two companies announced their delivery partnership in July 2019 (Digital Business Africa). On 9 October 2026, the Abidjan Nord store showed a 300 FCFA delivery fee (about €0.45) and a 98% rating on Glovo. That fee is a fifth of the price of fries, so for most orders I would use delivery rather than cross the city.

Burger King was also one of the restaurants on Yango Deli when the service launched in Abidjan, its first African city. At launch it covered Cocody, Marcory and Plateau (Abidjan.net). I have not checked whether Burger King is still on Yango Deli.

Burger King Abidjan reviews: what the ratings say

Burger King Abidjan scores mostly well on Yandex Maps, from 3.7/5 in Marcory to 4.6/5 in Plateau. Each location has only 10 to 84 reviews, so take the scores as a rough guide. Plateau and Val-Doyen are the two I would trust first, because they pair the best scores with the most reviews. Marcory’s 3.7 rests on 12 reviews, which is too few to write the restaurant off.

Google and TripAdvisor block automated reading, so I could not collect their ratings. That also means I cannot report recurring praise or complaints about waiting times, service or burger quality, and I would rather leave a gap than invent a consensus. For TripAdvisor reviews, the listing is Burger King Abidjan on TripAdvisor.

Practical information: contact, payment and website

The only official channel I found is the Burger King Côte d’Ivoire Facebook page, which has about 10,200 followers. There is no national Burger King website for Ivory Coast. I am not publishing phone numbers either. Directories list different numbers for Cap Sud and Cosmos, and some still use the 8-digit format that Ivory Coast replaced with 10 digits in 2021.

I could not confirm payment methods (Mobile Money or cards), wheelchair access, parking or click and collect for any location. Cosmos Yopougon has an official mall page and a unit number, which makes it the easiest location to find.

Burger King Abidjan FAQ

How many Burger King restaurants are there in Abidjan?

The Burger King Côte d’Ivoire franchise runs about nine restaurants in the country, according to the April 2026 Enko Capital announcement. Directories and Yandex Maps place them in Plateau, Cocody (Val-Doyen or Vallons, Sococé, Palmeraie, Génie 2000), Marcory, Cap Sud, the airport and Cosmos Yopougon, but no official source confirms the full list.

How much is a Whopper meal at Burger King Abidjan?

A Whopper meal cost 7,500 FCFA (about €11.45) on Glovo delivery on 9 October 2026. A Whopper on its own cost 6,000 FCFA. In-store prices may differ.

Is there a Burger King open late in Abidjan?

Yes. Yandex Maps lists Burger King Cocody Val-Doyen as open until 2 a.m. and the airport location until midnight (checked 9 October 2026). The sources often disagree on hours, so confirm before you go.

Is there a Burger King at Abidjan airport?

Yes. Burger King has been in the public area of Félix-Houphouët-Boigny International Airport since 30 March 2016. It is before security, so departing passengers can use it before check-in, and arriving passengers can use it after they land.

When did Burger King open in Ivory Coast?

Burger King opened in Ivory Coast in December 2015 at the CFAO PlaYce Marcory mall in Abidjan. It was the chain’s first restaurant in West Africa.

Who owns Burger King in Ivory Coast?

Since April 2026, Burger King Côte d’Ivoire has been owned by Enko Capital, through its FCPR Enko PME fund. Enko bought Sia Restauration rapide Côte d’Ivoire, the franchise operator, from Servair. The price was not disclosed.

Does Burger King deliver in Abidjan?

Yes, through Glovo in Abidjan Sud, Abidjan Nord and Yopougon. The delivery fee shown on 9 October 2026 was 300 FCFA. Burger King was also on Yango Deli when it launched in Abidjan, but I have not checked whether it still is.

Does Burger King Abidjan have vegetarian options?

Very few. The Salade Fraîcheur is the only meat-free item I found on the Glovo menu. The chicken range is much wider, with wraps, Tender Strips, nuggets and the Long Chili Cheese Chicken.

Is Burger King in Abidjan halal?

I found no public halal certification for Burger King Côte d’Ivoire. Ask the restaurant or message the official Facebook page before you order.

Burger King or KFC in Abidjan?

Both chains are in Abidjan. Go to Burger King for flame-grilled beef such as the Whopper and the Big King, and to KFC if you want fried chicken. I have not compared their Abidjan prices yet.
