K's Pit Handa: 1950s American Diner Near Nagoya

Who founded the K's Pit burger diner, why its Nagoya branch in Ōsu no longer operates, and how to reach the Handa original from Nagoya today.

By Hana, Restaurants writer, Japan and IndonesiaPublished November 5, 2026

K’s Pit Handa is a retro 1950s–60s American diner at 5-214-18 Hakusan-chō in Handa, Aichi Prefecture, about 40 km south of Nagoya. It opened on 21 November 2000 and builds its burgers on a 150 g patty of 100% beef. As of 22 October 2026 there is no K’s Pit diner in Nagoya. The Ōsu branch opened in 2007 and no longer trades as K’s Pit. So if you want a K’s Pit burger near Nagoya, you have two options: take the Meitetsu train to Handa, or catch the K’s Pit food truck at a Nagoya-area event.

A correction before we start. This piece began as a planned profile of a chef called “Takashi Takahashi” at a K’s Pit in Nagoya. I found no source linking that name to K’s Pit, to Woods Diner or to any burger restaurant in Nagoya, so the name stays out of this article. Local media name the founder-owner as Kentaro Omura (大村憲太郎). And the original K’s Pit is in Handa, not Nagoya.

What is K’s Pit Handa?

K’s Pit Handa is an independent American-style diner on the Chita Peninsula. It serves burgers, steak, pasta, taco rice and loco moco under neon lights and checkered metal walls. The official site and the Japanese food site Kokojimo both describe a retro 1950s–60s interior with bench seats and a counter, and the venue also hosts private events and weddings (kspit.jp, Kokojimo).

It is a big room. Tabelog counts 70 seats, counter included. The restaurant is non-smoking, has a car park and does takeout, and Tabelog puts the typical budget at ¥1,000–1,999 per person (Tabelog, checked 22 October 2026).

Who founded K’s Pit? Kentaro Omura and his 1950s obsession

K’s Pit was founded by Kentaro Omura (大村憲太郎), who opened it in his twenties because he loved 1950s American culture, according to a feature by Sanshū Kawara (sanshukawara.jp). That feature is the only source that names him. I have romanized the name as Omura, though Ōmura is also possible. The founding date, 21 November 2000, is backed by two separate sources: the food-truck directory Mobimaru and the Hamburger Street blog (Mobimaru, Hamburger Street).

To me this looks like one man’s passion project, not a themed concept rolled out by a company. The 1950s setting is the reason the place exists. In the quarter-century since it opened, the name has travelled: to a shop in Nagoya, a food truck and two diners in Okinawa. Not all of them survived, and the timeline below sorts out which ones did.

The K’s Pit burger: 100% beef, no binder, house bun

The K’s Pit patty is 100% beef with no binder (つなぎなし). Two sources say so: the Nagoya Antique Market vendor page and the Japanese food guide Kotomise (Nagoya Antique Market, Kotomise). No binder means no breadcrumbs or egg holding the meat together. That sets it apart from the soft, hambāgu-style patties common in Japanese cafés.

The signature Bacon Cheese Burger

The Bacon Cheese Burger is the house signature: a 150 g patty of 100% beef with grilled bacon, tomato, lettuce, cheddar and onion on an original house bun, according to Sanshū Kawara. It’s the one I’d order first. It has no gimmick and no regional twist, so you are judging only the beef and the bun.

Chita beef burgers and the rest of the line-up

Chita beef (知多牛) is raised on the Chita Peninsula, where Handa sits, and K’s Pit puts it in two dedicated Chita Beef burgers. None of my sources says every patty is Chita beef, so don’t assume it. Retty lists ten burgers, without prices (Retty menu):

  • BBQ Chicken Burger
  • Teriyaki Chicken Burger
  • Cheeseburger
  • Turkey Burger
  • Plain Burger
  • Fish Burger
  • Beef Chili Burger
  • Bacon Cheeseburger
  • Chita Beef Burger
  • Chita Beef Cheeseburger

Kokojimo adds that the BBQ Burger comes with fresh-cut fries and pasta salad. Beyond the ten-plus burgers, the menu covers steak, pasta, taco rice and loco moco.

How much does a burger cost at K’s Pit?

Expect to spend ¥1,000–1,999 per person at K’s Pit Handa, according to Tabelog. The most recent menu prices I found come from Sanshū Kawara, which does not date them, so check on site:

Is there a K’s Pit in Nagoya? The story of the Ōsu branch

No K’s Pit diner operates in Nagoya today. The city did have one. K’s Pit Diner Ōsu opened on 8 February 2007 on the ground floor of the Oda Building, 3-44-20 Ōsu, Naka-ku, one minute from exit 8 of Kamimaezu Station. TV Aichi’s “Ōsu Tankentai” page describes a 15-seat shop open 11:00–21:00 and closed on Wednesdays (TV Aichi).

The old prices are a time capsule. On that undated, pre-closure TV Aichi page, the Bacon Cheese Burger cost ¥800, the BBQ Chicken Burger ¥700, the Oreo Shake ¥350 and onion rings ¥450. None of those prices applies anywhere now.

What happened to Woods Diner?

The Ōsu shop was later renamed Woods Diner (ウッズダイナー) at the same address, and its current status is unconfirmed. Tabelog still shows the old name, ケイズピットダイナー, and has put the listing on hold as closed, moved or unconfirmed. The latest review there dates from March 2017, with a score of 3.06 from 43 reviews (Tabelog). Foursquare marks “K’s PIT 大須店” as closed. One blogger who ate there on 16 February 2015 remembered red vinyl booths and a cheeseburger heavy on BBQ sauce (logtaka).

Who ran the Ōsu branch, and exactly when it closed or changed its name, remain unknown. Don’t plan a trip around it. For a burger in Ōsu or central Nagoya today, our Nagoya burger guide covers the city’s current options.

K’s Pit timeline: every branch, open and closed

K’s Pit started as one diner in Handa in 2000, and the name has since appeared in Nagoya and in Okinawa. Here is what I could establish for each venue as of 22 October 2026:

Are the Okinawa K’s Pit diners linked to Handa?

Both Okinawa diners have ties to Handa, but neither is confirmed as a company-owned branch. K’s Pit DINER Miyakojima opened in 2016. It is run by Nagamine, a Miyakojima native and former store manager at K’s Pit Handa, who kept the name as a tribute. Burgers there start at ¥600 (Oki-raku). Parlor K’s Pit in Uruma opened on 10 May 2022 and appears on the official kspit.jp site. It has 30 seats, opens 11:00–20:00 and closes on Wednesdays. The Plain Burger costs ¥1,200 and the BBQ Burger ¥1,300. Tabelog scores it 3.22 from 27 reviews and gives the phone number as 098-923-3891 (Tabelog).

How to get from Nagoya to K’s Pit Handa

The easiest way from Nagoya to K’s Pit is the Meitetsu train to Narawa Station (成岩), then a walk of about 15 minutes, according to Retty (Retty). Handa is about 40 km south of Nagoya, which makes the trip a comfortable half day. Drivers can use the car park. Remember the Tuesday closing day and the one irregular closing day each month, and check before you set off.

Is it worth the trip? K’s Pit is missing from the usual best-burger-in-Nagoya lists, which favour The Corner Hamburger & Saloon, FAT MAN’S MISERY in Ōsu, MEIHOKU Burger in Fushimi and Layer’s. I don’t see that as a knock on the burger: those lists stop at the city limits. My take is that K’s Pit is a day-trip pick, not a city top spot. Go for the room as much as the patty: 70 seats of 1950s America, run by its founder since 2000.

Where to find the K’s Pit food truck around Nagoya

The K’s Pit food truck is a 670 × 210 × 300 cm kitchen car that works events across Aichi, Gifu, Mie, Shiga, Shizuoka, Kyoto, Osaka, Hyogo and the Tokyo area. It has served at Nagoya Antique Market at Higashi Betsuin, in kitchen-car slot C (Nagoya Antique Market), though the vendor page gives no year. Mobimaru lists these truck prices (Mobimaru):

Food truck item Price
Real American Burger (100% beef) ¥1,200
Chita Beef Burger ¥1,200
Taco rice ¥1,000
Curly fries ¥700

I found no confirmed upcoming dates. The schedule goes up on Instagram at @kspit.foodtrucks, so check there before you go.

Where K’s Pit fits in Japan’s burger scene

K’s Pit belongs to the owner-run American burger places that grew up in Japan alongside the domestic chains. Those chains play a different game: compare the teriyaki and rice-bun tradition in my guide to MOS Burger in Nagoya, our guides to MOS Burger in Osaka and MOS Burger in Hiroshima, or the MOS Burger brand page. How I put this profile together

This is a sourced profile, not a review: I have not eaten at K’s Pit for this article. It draws on the restaurant’s official site and menu page, Tabelog, Retty, TV Aichi, the Sanshū Kawara feature, Mobimaru, Kokojimo, Oki-raku and the Hamburger Street blog, all checked on 22 October 2026. Each fact is attributed in the text, and undated prices are labelled as such. K’s Pit gets no rating from me, and I claim no awards for it because I could not verify any.

K’s Pit FAQ

Who owns K’s Pit, and who founded it? Kentaro Omura (大村憲太郎) founded K’s Pit in Handa on 21 November 2000, in his twenties, according to Sanshū Kawara. That is the only source that names him.

Is K’s Pit in Nagoya? No. The K’s Pit diner is in Handa, about 40 km south of Nagoya. Its former Nagoya branch in Ōsu no longer operates as K’s Pit.

Is K’s Pit Diner in Ōsu still open? No. It became Woods Diner at the same address. Tabelog has put that listing on hold and its latest review dates from March 2017, so treat Woods Diner’s status as unconfirmed.

How do I get from Nagoya to K’s Pit Handa? Take the Meitetsu train to Narawa Station and walk about 15 minutes. The diner also has a car park.

What is the best burger at K’s Pit, and how much does it cost? The signature is the Bacon Cheese Burger: a 150 g 100% beef patty, bacon, cheddar and a house bun. Tabelog puts the typical spend at ¥1,000–1,999 per person, and Sanshū Kawara’s undated prices include ¥2,255 for the Empire Burger.

Is the K’s Pit patty 100% beef? What is Chita beef? Yes, the patty is 100% beef with no binder. Chita beef comes from the Chita Peninsula, where Handa sits, and goes into the Chita Beef burgers. My sources do not say it is used in every patty.

Where can I find the K’s Pit food truck around Nagoya? It has served at Nagoya Antique Market at Higashi Betsuin. Check @kspit.foodtrucks on Instagram for current dates.

Are the Okinawa K’s Pit diners linked to the Handa original? Yes, though the ownership is unclear. A former K’s Pit Handa store manager runs the Miyakojima diner as a tribute, and Parlor K’s Pit in Uruma is listed on the official site.

What are K’s Pit’s opening hours? K’s Pit Handa opens 11:00–23:00 and closes on Tuesdays, plus one irregular day a month.
