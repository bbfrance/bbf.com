Chick-fil-A Atlanta: Dwarf House, Burgers, Hours

The Hapeville Dwarf House, birthplace of Chick-fil-A, is the one near Atlanta still serving burgers. Address, hours, menu and a downtown backup.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 17, 2026

The Chick-fil-A to visit in Atlanta is The Dwarf House at 461 N Central Ave in Hapeville, a separate small city next to Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport. The chain was born there in 1946, and it is one of the few Chick-fil-A restaurants that still sells hamburgers: the Dwarf Burger and the Giant Burger. It opens Monday to Saturday from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Like every Chick-fil-A, it is closed on Sunday. Staying downtown? Chick-fil-A Peachtree Center, 225 Peachtree St NE, is the practical second choice. I checked every address, phone number and opening time below against Chick-fil-A’s official pages on November 12, 2026.

Can you get a burger at Chick-fil-A? Yes, at the Dwarf House

You can order a hamburger at Chick-fil-A only at a handful of restaurants, and The Dwarf House is the best known: its menu adds the Dwarf Burger and the Giant Burger to the standard chicken lineup. That is why a burger site sends you to a chicken chain. Chick-fil-A sells chicken sandwiches first. Its birthplace still cooks beef.

Chick-fil-A does not publish how either burger is built: no official patty weight, cheese or bun. I would rather say so than copy menu descriptions from unofficial sites. 1 rule is confirmed: the Hot Brown is dine-in only, according to thedwarfhouse.com. Whether the burgers follow the same rule, I could not confirm. My advice: if a burger is the reason you are going, take a table inside rather than the drive-thru.

Dwarf House Chick-fil-A menu: what only Hapeville serves

The Dwarf House serves the full Chick-fil-A menu plus dishes a regular branch does not carry, according to Chick-fil-A and thedwarfhouse.com:

  • Dwarf Burger and Giant Burger, the two hamburgers.
  • Hot Brown: chicken with cream sauce, cheddar and bacon, dine-in only.
  • Southern sides: mac and cheese, fried okra, coleslaw and collard greens.
  • Omelets at breakfast, and a chicken salad sandwich.
  • Handmade pies: lemon, chocolate and coconut, plus fried apple and peach pies.

Here is what I would order on a first visit. A burger, to taste what Truett Cathy was selling before the chicken sandwich existed. Collard greens on the side, because no ordinary Chick-fil-A serves them. Then a slice of coconut pie. I would skip the chicken sandwich: Chick-fil-A says it was perfected here in 1964 (a boneless chicken breast on a toasted, buttered bun with 2 dill pickles), but you can eat the same sandwich at any of its 3,000-plus restaurants.

Chick-fil-A Dwarf House prices

Chick-fil-A publishes no official price for the Dwarf Burger, the Giant Burger or the pies, so I am not printing one. The figures online come from unofficial menu sites that give no source and no date. The one price you can see on site is historical: the reproduction of the original Dwarf Grill menu, set into a table, lists a hamburger of “100% choice beef” at 15 cents and a cheeseburger at 20 cents. For today’s prices, open the Chick-fil-A app before you go. Expect DoorDash and Uber Eats to charge more than the counter.

The history of the first Chick-fil-A

The Dwarf House is where Chick-fil-A started: a small diner that grew into a chain of more than 3,000 restaurants. The timeline below comes from Chick-fil-A’s own pages and Wikipedia.

  • May 23, 1946: Truett Cathy opens the Dwarf Grill in Hapeville.
  • 1964: the original chicken sandwich recipe is perfected at the Dwarf House (Chick-fil-A). Wikipedia dates the pressure-fryer discovery behind it to 1961.
  • November 24, 1967: the first mall-based Chick-fil-A opens at Greenbriar Mall in Atlanta (Wikipedia). The same year, the Dwarf House building that stood until 2021 went up.
  • March 2021: the Dwarf House closes to be rebuilt.
  • 2021: Andrew T. Cathy, Truett’s grandson, becomes CEO (Wikipedia).
  • February 17, 2022: the Dwarf House reopens with more than double the seating capacity.

What the 2022 rebuild kept

The rebuilt Dwarf House kept 16 original pieces, including the Little Red Door, the 1967 counter stools, the stained glass and the dwarf mural. New additions: an outdoor pavilion with a playground, electric vehicle chargers and a pie counter named for Zelma Calhoun. She baked the restaurant’s pies for 40 years and made more than 100 a day, according to thedwarfhouse.com, the 2022 announcement and a 2025 Chick-fil-A article. Be careful with travel blogs: most were written before the reopening, so their photos show a building that no longer exists.

Chick-fil-A near Atlanta airport

The Dwarf House is the closest historic Chick-fil-A to Hartsfield-Jackson: Hapeville borders the airport, and the official location page links a route from the airport. On a long layover with a car or a rideshare, it is the most interesting meal within easy reach of your gate.

I could not verify the nearest MARTA station or bus line, nor a reliable travel time from the terminals, so I am not giving one. Plan the trip by car or rideshare, and check the route with MARTA before relying on public transit.

Chick-fil-A Peachtree Center, downtown

Chick-fil-A Peachtree Center is the most useful downtown branch for visitors. It sits inside the Peachtree Center complex, a short walk from the Peachtree Center MARTA station. Phone (404) 577-4194. It offers pickup, delivery and catering, but it closes early: 7 p.m. on weekdays, 4 p.m. on Saturday (official page). Do not count on it for dinner after a late meeting.

Chick-fil-A at Mercedes-Benz Stadium

The stadium stand opens only on event days, for carry-out only, and stays closed on Sunday (official page). That includes Falcons home games on a Sunday. The official listing gives no usable hours, so assume it is open during events and never on a Sunday.

Truett’s Grill: the 1950s version

Truett’s Grill is a retro 1950s diner concept from the Cathy family, with table service, the full Chick-fil-A menu and Southern plates. Chick-fil-A’s Truett’s Grill page lists 3 locations: Morrow (2042 Mt. Zion Road), McDonough (785 Jonesboro Road) and Griffin (1455 North Expressway). All 3 are a drive from downtown, so check each restaurant’s own listing before you go.

Closed: Chick-fil-A Terminus 100 in Buckhead

The Chick-fil-A in the Terminus 100 building in Buckhead closed on July 31, 2026, after 18 years, with no reason given, as Atlanta News First reported. Older guides and map apps may still list it. In Buckhead, go to Peachtree & Piedmont instead.

Why is Chick-fil-A closed on Sunday?

Chick-fil-A closes every restaurant on Sunday because its founder, Truett Cathy, a devout Baptist, wanted employees to have a day of rest and worship. Atlanta gets no exception: the Dwarf House, Peachtree Center and even the Mercedes-Benz Stadium stand are all closed (Wikipedia, Chick-fil-A). The Georgia Historical Society marker outside the Dwarf House mentions the rule too. If Sunday is your only free day in Atlanta, eat your burger somewhere else.

Chick-fil-A by the numbers

Chick-fil-A is still an Atlanta-area company: its headquarters is at 5200 Buffington Road, Atlanta, GA 30349, which is in College Park. Wikipedia, which gives no update date for these figures, counts 3,488 restaurants in 48 states, Washington, D.C., and Puerto Rico, plus locations in Canada, the United Kingdom and Singapore. The same source puts 2024 systemwide sales at about $22 billion, an average of about $7.5 million per restaurant. I could not check these figures against a Chick-fil-A release.

Is the Dwarf House worth the trip?

Yes, if you come for what Hapeville alone serves. In my view the burgers, the Southern sides, the pies and the table service are the reason to make the drive, and the airport location makes it easy on a travel day. If all you want is a Chicken Sandwich and waffle fries, the trip is not worth it: any Chick-fil-A serves the same chicken menu. I am not quoting Google, Yelp or Tripadvisor scores, because I have no dated reading of them to stand behind.

Frequently asked questions about Chick-fil-A in Atlanta

Where is the first Chick-fil-A? The first Chick-fil-A is The Dwarf House, 461 N Central Ave, Hapeville, GA 30354, just south of Atlanta next to the airport. Truett Cathy opened it as the Dwarf Grill on May 23, 1946.

Can you get a burger at Chick-fil-A? Yes, at The Dwarf House in Hapeville, which serves the Dwarf Burger and the Giant Burger on top of the standard Chick-fil-A menu. Regular Chick-fil-A restaurants do not sell hamburgers.

What should I order at the Dwarf House? Order what only Hapeville serves: a Dwarf Burger or a Giant Burger, the Hot Brown (chicken, cream sauce, cheddar and bacon, dine-in only), a Southern side such as collard greens, and a slice of lemon, chocolate or coconut pie.

What are the Chick-fil-A Atlanta hours? The Dwarf House opens Monday to Saturday from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Peachtree Center opens Monday to Friday from 6:30 a.m. to 7 p.m. and Saturday from 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. Both are closed on Sunday (checked November 12, 2026).

Is Chick-fil-A open on Sunday in Atlanta? No. Every Chick-fil-A is closed on Sunday, including the Mercedes-Benz Stadium stand on game days.

Which Chick-fil-A should I pick in downtown Atlanta? Chick-fil-A Peachtree Center, 225 Peachtree St NE #B-40, near the Peachtree Center MARTA station. Go before 7 p.m. on weekdays or 4 p.m. on Saturday.

Is the Dwarf House close to Atlanta airport? Yes. Hapeville borders Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, so the Dwarf House is a short car or rideshare trip from the terminals.

What is the difference between a regular Chick-fil-A, the Dwarf House and Truett’s Grill? A regular Chick-fil-A serves the standard chicken menu at the counter and the drive-thru. The Dwarf House adds table service, burgers, Southern sides and handmade pies. Truett’s Grill is a 1950s-style diner with table service, the full Chick-fil-A menu and Southern plates.

How much does a meal at Chick-fil-A in Atlanta cost? Chick-fil-A publishes no official prices for the Dwarf House exclusives. Check the Chick-fil-A app for the current price at the restaurant you choose; delivery apps charge more.
