Five Guys Oklahoma City: Locations, Hours, Prices

The three Five Guys in OKC compared: addresses, hours, what a meal costs and what reviewers say about each one, with my pick. Checked October 29, 2026.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 5, 2026

Five Guys has three open restaurants in Oklahoma City: Quail Springs Shopping Center at 2300 W. Memorial Road, Westgate Shopping Center at 6304 SW 3rd Street, and 52nd Street at 5201 North May Avenue. I checked the official listing for each one on October 29, 2026. All three offer dine-in, takeout and delivery, and most listings give hours of 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. A meal runs from about $12 to $23 before tax. The old Five Guys on NW Expressway is gone from the official list. My pick is Quail Springs, and the reasons are below.

Five Guys Memorial Rd (Quail Springs)

Quail Springs is the Five Guys “Memorial Rd” location, and it’s the one I’d send a visitor to. It is the only OKC location whose official page lists a patio and online ordering. On Birdeye it has 1,074 reviews and a 4.3 rating, more reviews and a higher score than Westgate.

Five Guys SW 3rd Street (Westgate)

Westgate, in the Westgate Shopping Center on SW 3rd Street, scores 4.1 on Birdeye, two tenths below Quail Springs. That gap tells you little. What stands out in its reviews is staff who take allergies seriously.

Five Guys May Ave (52nd Street)

The 52nd Street restaurant is in Suite 103 at 5201 North May Avenue, the one locals call “Five Guys May Ave.” I could not find a review score for it that I could read directly, so I don’t give one. Its hours are also the least certain of the three (see below).

Is the Five Guys on NW Expressway closed?

Yes, as far as the public record shows. The former Five Guys at 6900 NW Expressway is marked “CLOSED” on Yelp, where it had 54 reviews, and it no longer appears on the official Five Guys list for Oklahoma City. I found no primary source giving a closing date. If your map app still shows it, go to one of the three addresses above.

Five Guys near Oklahoma City: Moore, Edmond and Norman

Five Guys operates in nine Oklahoma cities: Broken Arrow, Edmond, Lawton, Moore, Norman, Oklahoma City, Owasso, Stillwater and Tulsa (Five Guys Oklahoma). Edmond, Moore and Norman each have one in the metro. Five Guys Moore is at 631 SW 19th St, Moore, OK 73160, phone 405-794-5559, open 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily according to TravelOK.

Five Guys OKC menu

The Five Guys menu in Oklahoma City is the standard US menu: burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches, fries and milkshakes. I found no OKC-only item. Every burger comes with a choice of 15 free toppings (Five Guys Westgate, Wikipedia).

  • Burgers: hamburger, cheeseburger, bacon burger and bacon cheeseburger. The regular size has two patties; the Little version has one.
  • Hot dogs.
  • Sandwiches: grilled cheese, BLT and veggie sandwich.
  • Fries: Five Guys Style or Cajun.
  • Milkshakes.

The OKC store pages repeat the chain’s core promises: fresh beef that is never frozen (“there are no freezers in our restaurants”), hand-formed patties, fries cooked twice in 100% peanut oil, more than 250,000 ways to build a burger and more than 1,000 milkshake combinations (Five Guys 52nd Street).

Vegetarian options at Five Guys

Five Guys has no plant-based patty. The meat-free options are the veggie sandwich and the grilled cheese, plus the fries and shakes. The veggie sandwich is the free toppings on a bun, with no patty, so don’t order it expecting a veggie burger.

Peanut allergy warning

Five Guys is a risky choice for anyone with a peanut allergy. According to the official US nutrition and allergen guide, fries are cooked in refined peanut oil in fryers used only for fries, and peanuts are served in every restaurant. Salt and Cajun spice are the only fry seasonings.

Can you share Five Guys fries?

Yes, and you should. Quail Springs reviewers keep saying the portions are huge, and one says a regular is enough for two or three people. At $6.15 a regular, one order for the table instead of one each is the easiest saving on the menu.

Which Five Guys in Oklahoma City should you choose?

Go to Quail Springs if you can: of the two locations I could score, it has the higher rating, and it is the only one with a patio and online ordering on its official page. Westgate is the right call on the SW 3rd Street side of town, or if you’re managing an allergy other than peanuts, because its reviewers single out how staff handle allergies. Choose 52nd Street only if North May Avenue is closest, and check its hours first.

Five Guys and the Oklahoma City burger scene

Five Guys is a premium national chain in OKC, not a local legend. Jerry and Janie Murrell founded it in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, with a first restaurant in the Westmont Shopping Center. It began franchising in 2003, is still owned by the Murrell family and has been headquartered in Alexandria, Virginia, since 2023 (Wikipedia).

As of May 5, 2026, Five Guys had 1,999 restaurants in 28 countries, 1,517 of them in the United States. Our Five Guys brand profile covers the full history and figures.

Two Oklahoma names own the local chain scene: Sonic, headquartered in Oklahoma City, and Braum’s. And if you’re visiting, Five Guys is not the burger I’d cross town for. That’s the Oklahoma onion burger, and I’ve written up one of the best in town in our Nic’s Grill onion burger guide. More spots are on the Oklahoma City burger hub. Five Guys is the reliable fallback when you want a two-patty burger built exactly your way. FAQ: Five Guys Oklahoma City

How many Five Guys are there in Oklahoma City? Three: Quail Springs Shopping Center (2300 W. Memorial Road, Suite 160), Westgate Shopping Center (6304 SW 3rd Street) and 52nd Street (5201 North May Avenue, Suite 103). Edmond, Moore and Norman each have one more in the metro.

What are Five Guys Oklahoma City hours? Birdeye lists Quail Springs and Westgate as open 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. Listings for May Ave conflict, and some show an 11 p.m. close Thursday to Saturday. Confirm on the official store page before you go.

How much does a meal at Five Guys in OKC cost? About $12 to $23 before tax: $12.31 for a Little Hamburger and Little fries, $23.03 for a cheeseburger, regular fries and a shake. The figures are undated Allmenus prices for the Moore restaurant. Check the app or order.fiveguys.com for your location.

Does Five Guys Oklahoma City deliver, and can I order online? Yes. All three locations list delivery on their official pages, and you can order online from any of them at order.fiveguys.com.

Does Five Guys have a veggie burger? No. The meat-free choices are the veggie sandwich and the grilled cheese. There is no plant-based patty.

Is Five Guys safe for people with a peanut allergy? It is risky. Fries are cooked in refined peanut oil, and peanuts are served in every restaurant, according to the official US allergen guide.

Is the Five Guys on NW Expressway closed? The restaurant at 6900 NW Expressway is marked closed on Yelp and is no longer on the official Five Guys list. No official closing date has been published.
