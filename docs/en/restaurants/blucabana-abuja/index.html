BluCabana Abuja: Burger Menu, Prices and Hours

Who runs the poolside café in Mabushi, what its 10 burgers cost in naira, and whether it belongs on your Abuja burger list.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished November 26, 2026

BluCabana Abuja is a poolside restaurant and café at 1322 Shehu Yar’adua Way, Mabushi, operated by the hospitality arm of Nadeem Group, with Joseph El Hage as executive chef. Its menu lists 10 burgers at ₦16,500 to ₦22,000, each with coleslaw and French fries, next to Lebanese staples and suya-spiced sandwiches. Doors open at 9:00 a.m. every day. I checked the official site and menu on 13 November 2026, and it was still trading.

BluCabana is an all-day café with a swimming pool, a gym and a playground, not a burger specialist. Burgers are one section of a menu that also covers shawarma, wraps and steak sandwiches. Key facts, checked on 13 November 2026:

Who is behind BluCabana: Nadeem Group and the chef team

BluCabana is operated by Nadeem Group. No individual chef-founder is publicly credited with creating it. The group’s hospitality page calls it “a leading F&B venue in Abuja” (Nadeem Group) and says it was voted among the top 10% of restaurants worldwide in Tripadvisor’s Travelers’ Choice 2021. That claim is the group’s own. I could not confirm it independently.

Nadeem Group also built Sunrise Waterpark on Asokoro Hill, billed as West Africa’s biggest waterpark, which FCT Minister Nyesom Wike commissioned in March 2024 (ThisDay). The group’s chairperson is Tania Khalaf. BluCabana’s second branch is inside that waterpark.

The chefs: Joseph El Hage, Margarito Bunao and Youssef Zahlouk

BluCabana’s official site names Joseph El Hage as executive chef, working with chefs Margarito Bunao and Youssef Zahlouk (blucabana.com). That is all it says. I found no published bios, no start dates and no second source. With shawarma, taouk and falafel on the menu, the kitchen looks Lebanese-led, but that is my inference, not something the site states.

When BluCabana opened and who founded it

Neither the opening year nor a founder is on record. A Wikimedia Commons photo shows the venue in November 2021, and Nadeem Group cites a 2021 Tripadvisor award, so BluCabana has been open since at least then. Wikidata lists no founder, owner or inception date (Wikidata). Some web pages tie a named individual to the restaurant. No source I could check backs that up, so I left the name out.

Which BluCabana burger to order

The two burgers named after the restaurant, the BluCabana Burger and the BluCabana Smash Burger, are the house signatures. BluCabana does not rank its own burgers and I have not tasted them, so what follows is my reading of the menu and the prices, not a tasting verdict.

BluCabana Burger (₦19,000)

The BluCabana Burger is the most distinctive burger on the list: the cheddar sits inside the patty, not on top. Caramelized onions and mushrooms bring sweetness, and jalapeños bring the heat. If I had one order to judge the kitchen, this would be it.

BluCabana Smash Burger (₦16,500)

The Smash Burger costs exactly what the Classic costs, yet the menu gives it far more: a smashed patty, melted Emmental, sweet relish, roasted onions and house sauce on a toasted bun. At the same ₦16,500, I see no reason to order the Classic.

Wahala Burger (₦18,000)

The Wahala Burger is the loaded one, with a fried egg, crispy onion rings and turkey bacon on a homemade bun. “Wahala” is Nigerian Pidgin for trouble. With seven toppings, it earns the name on paper. The bacon is turkey, not pork, which matters if anyone at your table avoids pork.

Swiss Mushroom Burger (₦22,000)

The Swiss Mushroom Burger is the priciest on the menu and the only one served on a potato bun with crinkle-cut fries. Emmental, fresh mushrooms and a Swiss mushroom sauce make it the saucy choice. It costs ₦3,000 more than the BluCabana Burger. I would pay that only if mushrooms are the reason you came.

Beyond burgers: suya and Lebanese dishes

Suya and Lebanese dishes set BluCabana apart from Abuja’s burger specialists. The Suya Fire Steak Sandwich (₦14,500) brings suya-marinated steak, ata rodo pepper, grilled plantain and suya mayo. The Hero Beef Wrap (₦14,500) uses suya-spiced beef. Of everything on this menu, the steak sandwich is the order you would struggle to find at a burger chain.

Suya, the spiced grilled beef skewer behind BluCabana’s Suya Fire Steak Sandwich and Hero Beef Wrap. Illustrative photo taken in Kaduna, not at BluCabana. (Photo: Halima Waziri, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Suya\_in\_skewers.jpg)

The Lebanese side is the budget route. Shawarma is ₦9,000, chicken taouk ₦7,500 and falafel ₦6,500 (official menu, checked 13 November 2026). All three cost less than the cheapest burger, and the falafel costs well under half as much.

BluCabana Mabushi: address, opening hours and contact

BluCabana’s main branch is at 1322 Shehu Yar’adua Way, Mabushi, Abuja (postcode 900108), and it opens at 9:00 a.m. every day. It closes at 10:30 p.m. Monday to Thursday and at 11:00 p.m. Friday to Sunday, according to the official site, checked 13 November 2026.

Directories disagree with the official site. One lists 9 a.m. to midnight daily, another gives a 0705 number, and one search summary puts BluCabana at a Jabi address. I used only the official hours, phone numbers and the Mabushi and Asokoro addresses. Planning a late visit? Call first.

A poolside restaurant in Abuja for families

At BluCabana you eat beside a swimming pool, and that is the main reason to go. Directory listings describe the Mabushi venue with a gym, an Olympic-size pool with poolside seating and a children’s playground (Ofadaa, MyGuideNigeria). The official site adds laser tag, meetings and events, and a membership programme.

For a weekend lunch with children, a burger by the pool is a half-day outing, not a quick fast-food stop. The setting, more than any single burger, is why I would send a family here.

Is BluCabana one of the best burgers in Abuja?

BluCabana is a burger stop inside a broader day out, not Abuja’s burger destination. The usual lists of the best burgers in Abuja feature specialists such as Johnny Rockets, Smash Burger Abuja and Junk Box, and BluCabana does not appear on them. What it does have is the pool, a cheddar-filled house burger and a suya and Lebanese menu that the specialists don’t offer.

You pay for the setting. At ₦16,500 to ₦22,000, a BluCabana burger costs far more than a fast-food meal at Chicken Republic in Abuja. For more places to eat in the capital, see our guide to burgers in Abuja, or browse the restaurant reviews and city guides of the whole section.

How I put this profile together

This profile is a menu and venue analysis, not a review of a meal. I have not eaten at BluCabana, so I give no rating. Prices, hours, phone numbers and chef names come from blucabana.com and its menu page, checked on 13 November 2026. Operator details come from Nadeem Group and ThisDay, and amenities from Ofadaa and MyGuideNigeria. Where sources disagreed, I used the official one and said so. I did not use any Tripadvisor rating or ranking, because I could not check the page directly. The 2021 Travelers’ Choice mention above is quoted as Nadeem Group’s own claim.

BluCabana Abuja FAQ

Who owns BluCabana in Abuja? BluCabana is operated by Nadeem Group, which lists it among its hospitality venues. No individual founder or chef-owner is publicly credited.

Who is the chef at BluCabana? The official site names Joseph El Hage as executive chef, with chefs Margarito Bunao and Youssef Zahlouk.

Where is BluCabana, and how many branches does it have? BluCabana has two branches: the main one at 1322 Shehu Yar’adua Way, Mabushi, and one at Sunrise Waterpark, Sunrise Hills Estate, Asokoro. I could not confirm whether the Asokoro branch serves the same burger menu.

What are BluCabana’s opening hours? Monday to Thursday 9:00 a.m. to 10:30 p.m., and Friday to Sunday 9:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., per the official site on 13 November 2026.

How much does a burger cost at BluCabana? Burgers cost ₦16,500 to ₦22,000, with coleslaw and fries included. The Classic, Smash, Chicken and Avo Chicken burgers are the cheapest at ₦16,500. The Swiss Mushroom Burger is the most expensive at ₦22,000.

What is the best burger at BluCabana? BluCabana does not name a signature burger. Going by the menu, the BluCabana Burger (₦19,000), with its cheddar-filled patty, is the most distinctive pick, and the Smash Burger (₦16,500) is the best value.

Is BluCabana family-friendly? Yes. The Mabushi venue has an Olympic-size pool with poolside seating and a children’s playground, according to local directory listings.

Does BluCabana deliver or take reservations? Yes. The official site offers online ordering and table reservations, and BluCabana has apps on the App Store and Google Play.
