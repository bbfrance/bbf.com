Grégoire Berger: From Ossiano to Kraken in Dubai

Grégoire Berger left Ossiano at the end of 2024. Where the Breton chef cooks now: Kraken in Dubai and Solenn in Abu Dhabi, with menus, prices and dates.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished November 24, 2026

Grégoire Berger no longer cooks at Ossiano. The chef, born in Versailles and raised in Brittany, left the one-Michelin-star underwater restaurant at Atlantis The Palm at the end of 2024. On 17 October 2025 he opened Kraken, a seafood restaurant on Al Wasl Road in Dubai, where he is chef-founder. In August 2026 he added Solenn, a Mediterranean restaurant in Abu Dhabi. Ossiano is now Rémy Marquignon’s kitchen, and it has been temporarily paused since 20 April 2026. And the burger? None of the 3 has 1 on any menu I could find. For that, head to our guide to the best burgers in Dubai.

So why profile him here? Because Berger learned to cook in Morbihan, and a Breton who earned a Michelin star in the Gulf is our kind of story. I cover the Middle East for Burger Breton. I haven’t eaten at Kraken or Solenn yet, so everything below comes from named sources. I checked each one on 14 November 2026.

Who is Grégoire Berger?

Grégoire Berger is a French chef, trained in Brittany, who has worked in Dubai since 2014 and made his name as executive chef of Ossiano at Atlantis The Palm. According to frenchefs.com and his Gulfood speaker biography, he was born in Versailles, grew up in Morbihan and graduated from the CFA culinary school in Vannes.

He knew at 16 that he wanted to cook. He did his apprenticeship at La Closerie de Kerdrain in Auray, he told Emirates Woman in November 2023. Before Dubai came a fine-dining opening in Casablanca, then a post as first sous-chef under Frédéric Robert at La Grande Cascade in Paris.

Grégoire Berger timeline: Ossiano, Kraken and Solenn

6 dates sum up Berger’s career in the UAE: he started at Ossiano in 2014, left in January 2021, came back in March 2022, left for good at the end of 2024, opened Kraken in October 2025 and opened Solenn in August 2026. Most pages about him cover one of these moves. Plenty still call him Ossiano’s chef.

Berger at Ossiano: the Michelin years

Ossiano won its Michelin star on Berger’s watch: 1 star in the first Michelin Guide Dubai in 2022, during his second spell in the kitchen. He had left in January 2021 and returned in March 2022, as BBC Good Food Middle East reported.

His last menu there was called ‘Anchored’. It had 10 courses at AED 1,250 per person, with a wine pairing at AED 845, according to Liam Collens in November 2024. He left at the end of that year, The National confirms. Caterer Middle East, Hotelier Middle East and Gault&Millau UAE all covered the exit, but their pages wouldn’t load for me, so I can’t quote what he said when he announced it.

Who is the chef at Ossiano Dubai now?

Rémy Marquignon is Ossiano’s executive chef. He trained in Paris, has more than 15 years of experience and previously cooked at La Scène in Paris, according to Travel and Tour World (22 February 2026). Reports disagree on exactly when he started. He was in charge by early 2026 at the latest.

His 9-course tasting menu cost AED 1,250, with wine pairing from AED 1,095, Hotel & Catering reported in January 2026. That is the same price as Berger’s ‘Anchored’, for 1 course fewer, and the pairing costs AED 250 more. The room seats 54. The menu included a binchotan-grilled Blue Brittany lobster, a dish that also appeared on Ossiano’s menu in the Berger years.

Is Ossiano open?

No. Ossiano has been temporarily paused since 20 April 2026, and Atlantis has not announced a reopening date. That day Atlantis Dubai paused 7 venues, among them Ossiano, Hakkasan and Dinner by Heston Blumenthal, The National reported. The Michelin Guide listing still shows it as temporarily closed.

Atlantis The Palm resort on Palm Jumeirah, Dubai, home of Ossiano. Atlantis temporarily paused Ossiano and 6 other restaurants on 20 April 2026 and has not announced a reopening date. Photo from December 2009. (Photo: Nico Quatro, CC BY 2.0, https://www.flickr.com/photos/13748507@N08/4211465695)

1 secondary site says bookings reopen in December 2026. Atlantis hasn’t confirmed it, so I’m not treating it as fact. I also left out opening hours: the published ones conflict, and all of them predate the pause.

Ossiano’s Michelin star and awards

Ossiano holds 1 Michelin star. It kept that star in the 2026 Michelin Guide Dubai at the ceremony on 6 October 2026, where Marquignon won the Michelin Young Chef Award, according to the Michelin Guide. Michelin lists its cuisine as Creative. The dining room faces an aquarium holding 65,000 marine animals. The address is Atlantis The Palm, Crescent Road, The Palm, Dubai.

Ossiano also won the Art of Hospitality Award at MENA’s 50 Best Restaurants 2025. Its MENA 50 Best rank and Berger’s Best Chef Awards placings vary from 1 source to the next, so I’ve left both out.

Grégoire Berger at Kraken: what he cooks now

Kraken is Berger’s Dubai restaurant: a seafood place on Al Wasl Road, Jumeirah 2, that opened on Friday 17 October 2025. He created it with Badr Benryane of Monarch Hospitality Group, and Nomme describes it as a tribute to Dubai’s fishing heritage. Berger is the chef-founder. Robin Höfer is the executive chef, according to Caterer Middle East and FACT; EatGoSee spells his name Höfler.

Kraken’s own site says more than 70% of its ingredients are sourced locally. Line-caught fish are handled by ikejime, a Japanese technique that kills the fish fast and keeps the flesh firm. FACT Magazine also mentions gyotaku art (prints made from real fish) and house-made fermented kombuchas and sodas.

Kraken Dubai menu and prices

Kraken’s à la carte dishes cost AED 44 to AED 130, and its tasting menu has been reported at AED 625 or AED 695, depending on the date. EatGoSee listed these prices in February 2026:

  • Grouper karaage: AED 62
  • Dry-aged kingfish: AED 112
  • Octopus tacos: AED 120
  • Tuna steak au poivre: AED 130
  • 4-course lunch: AED 175
  • 10-course tasting menu: AED 625

In November 2025, FACT listed a ‘Taste of Kraken’ menu at AED 695. My guess is the price changed between the two reviews, but it’s only a guess. Check the current menu before you book.

My take: if you want Berger’s cooking in 2026, Kraken is where to go, and it’s the better deal. 10 courses at AED 625 is exactly half what his last Ossiano menu cost. You lose the aquarium. You keep the chef.

Kraken Dubai opening hours

Kraken’s official site gives its hours as Thursday to Sunday, 6 pm to 11 pm. The 4-course lunch EatGoSee reported doesn’t fit those hours, so call the restaurant before planning a midday visit.

Solenn: Berger’s first Abu Dhabi restaurant

Solenn is a relaxed Mediterranean restaurant at The Arcades, Al Khail Square, Abu Dhabi. Berger opened it in August 2026 with The Creative House, Adnan Alzarooni’s company, according to The National. The same article says he is planning a restaurant in Turkey. For burgers in the capital, see our best burgers in Abu Dhabi page.

Where to eat Grégoire Berger’s food in November 2026

As of November 2026, Kraken in Dubai and Solenn in Abu Dhabi are the two places to eat Berger’s food. Ossiano is paused, and it isn’t his kitchen anymore anyway.

Does Ossiano or Kraken serve a burger?

No. I found no burger on any Ossiano or Kraken menu in official or press sources. On 14 November 2026 I searched the Michelin Guide, FACT, EatGoSee, Hotel & Catering and krakendubai.com. The closest thing is Kraken’s ‘Krok Mr.’, a take on the croque-monsieur with veal ham, pecorino and truffle (FACT, November 2025). It’s a toasted sandwich, not a burger, and I couldn’t confirm its current price.

For a burger with a big-name chef behind it in Dubai, look at Dani García’s Leña, which opened there in 2023. His group now presents its Burger Bull as a Leña dish, as we explain in our piece on Dani García’s BiBo at Málaga airport. I haven’t confirmed it’s on the Dubai menu, so ask before you book. Kraken’s road has some burger history too: Slider Station once had a Dubai branch at The Galleria Mall on Al Wasl Road, as our Slider Station Kuwait guide explains. And Dubai is where Shake Shack opened its first restaurant outside the US, at Mall of the Emirates in April 2011, as told in our Shake Shack Doha guide.

FAQ: Grégoire Berger, Ossiano and Kraken

Is Grégoire Berger still the chef at Ossiano?

No. Berger left Ossiano at the end of 2024, according to The National. He is now chef-founder of Kraken in Dubai and the chef behind Solenn in Abu Dhabi.

Who is the chef at Ossiano Dubai now?

Rémy Marquignon, a Paris-trained chef who previously cooked at La Scène in Paris. He was in charge by early 2026 at the latest, and he won the Michelin Young Chef Award in Dubai in October 2026.

Is Ossiano open, and when does it reopen?

Ossiano has been temporarily paused since 20 April 2026. As of 14 November 2026, Atlantis had not announced an official reopening date.

How much does dinner at Ossiano cost?

In January 2026, before the pause, Marquignon’s 9-course tasting menu cost AED 1,250 per person, with wine pairing from AED 1,095 (Hotel & Catering). Prices may change when it reopens.

How many Michelin stars does Ossiano have?

One. Ossiano won its star in the first Michelin Guide Dubai in 2022 and kept it in the 2026 guide.

What is Kraken Dubai, and where is it?

Kraken is Grégoire Berger’s seafood restaurant on Al Wasl Road, Jumeirah 2, Dubai. It opened on 17 October 2025 and sources more than 70% of its ingredients locally.

How much is the tasting menu at Kraken Dubai?

EatGoSee reported a 10-course tasting menu at AED 625 in February 2026. FACT listed a ‘Taste of Kraken’ menu at AED 695 in November 2025. Check the current price with the restaurant.

Where is Grégoire Berger from?

He was born in Versailles and grew up in Morbihan, Brittany. He trained at the CFA in Vannes and did his apprenticeship at La Closerie de Kerdrain in Auray.

What is Solenn in Abu Dhabi?

Solenn is a relaxed Mediterranean restaurant that Berger opened in August 2026 at The Arcades, Al Khail Square, Abu Dhabi, with The Creative House.
