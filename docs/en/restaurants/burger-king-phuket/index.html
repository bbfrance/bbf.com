Burger King Phuket: Every Location, Hours, Menu

Six Burger Kings in Phuket plus the airport: addresses, hours, phones and Google ratings checked Oct 14, 2026, and which one is open 24 hours in Patong.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished October 30, 2026

Burger King has at least six active restaurants in Phuket, plus a counter at Phuket International Airport. For a late-night Whopper near Bangla Road, go to Burger King Jungceylon in Patong: the mall’s official directory lists it as open every day, 24 hours. If you’re on the northwest beaches, Burger King Boat Avenue in Bang Tao is also open around the clock, and at 4.3/5 on Google it’s the best-rated branch on the island. It’s also the one I’d pick. I checked every address, opening time, phone number and rating below on October 14, 2026.

How this Burger King Phuket guide was built

I haven’t eaten at these restaurants. This guide is built from official sources first: the Jungceylon mall directory, the Turtle Village shopping center site and Minor Food, the company that runs Burger King in Thailand. I then cross-checked every listing on Wanderlog, Restaurant Guru, Wongnai and TripAdvisor, and each rating carries its source and the date I checked it. One gap remains. Burger King Thailand’s store locator at burgerking.co.th runs on JavaScript and wouldn’t load for me, so this list may not cover every Burger King in Phuket. Branches at Central Phuket, gas stations or drive-thrus haven’t been confirmed by the brand.

Search for “Burger King Phuket” and you’ll mostly land on aggregator pages that contradict each other, and some still list branches that closed for good. Here each location is marked open, closed or unconfirmed. When sources disagree, I show both rather than pick one.

Which Burger King in Phuket should you pick?

Pick the Burger King closest to where you’re staying. Phuket is bigger than it looks on a map, and no branch is worth a cross-island taxi.

  • Staying in Patong: Jungceylon, open 24 hours inside the mall, or Patong Bangla if you’re already on the Bangla Road strip.
  • Staying in Kata or Karon: Burger King Kata on Kata Road.
  • Staying in Bang Tao, Laguna or Cherngtalay: Boat Avenue, the highest-rated on the island and open 24 hours.
  • Staying in Mai Khao or near the airport: Turtle Village, open from breakfast to 11pm.
  • Flying out: the airport counter on the 3rd floor of the domestic terminal.

My ranking, based on the ratings and on the complaints that keep coming back: Boat Avenue first, because it has the best score from more than a thousand reviews and never closes. Patong Bangla second, for the location. Jungceylon is the one to trust at 3am, because it’s the only 24-hour claim backed by an official source, even if its reviews are middling. Use the airport only if you’re stuck there.

Burger King Jungceylon: the 24-hour Burger King in Patong

Burger King Jungceylon is open every day, 24 hours, according to the official Jungceylon mall directory. That makes it the safest late-night bet in Patong. It’s inside Jungceylon, Patong’s biggest shopping mall, a short walk from Bangla Road. With roughly 1,030 to 1,057 Google reviews, it’s also the most-reviewed Burger King in Phuket.

Address: Room/Suite 1108, The Jungle Zone, Jungceylon, 181 Rat-U-Thit 200 Pee Road, Patong, Kathu, Phuket 83150. The mall lists it on the ground floor, while review platforms call it “1st floor”. In Thailand those usually mean the same level.

Phone: +66 81 927 6221 (Wanderlog and Restaurant Guru agree).

Hours: open 24 hours, daily (Jungceylon directory, checked October 14, 2026).

Burger King Jungceylon reviews

Burger King Jungceylon has 3.8/5 on Google from about 1,030 reviews, according to Wanderlog: 452 five-star and 129 one-star. Restaurant Guru counts 1,057 Google reviews and shows 3.5/5 from 81 reviews on TripAdvisor. The fans single out the Thai-only items they can’t get at home, such as a purple sweet potato pie and Yum Zing chicken, and the location next to Bangla Road. The complaints repeat: high prices, long waits, cold food, uneven service. My read is that you come here because it’s open, not because it’s the best on the island.

Burger King Patong Bangla: on the nightlife strip

Burger King Patong Bangla is the closest branch to the Bangla Road bars, and at 4.1/5 from 537 Google reviews it’s the best-rated of the three Patong locations. It stands at the corner of Bangla Road and Thaweewong Road, the beachfront road. I’m not giving a street number, because the sources give three: TripAdvisor and Wongnai list 29/1 Bangla Road, Wanderlog lists 17 Thaweewong Rd, and another Wongnai page lists 21 Thaweewong Rd.

Phone: +66 65 520 4092 (Wanderlog and Wongnai agree).

Hours: Wanderlog and Wongnai’s restaurant page say 24 hours. Wongnai’s business listing says 11am to 6am. Either way it’s open well past midnight, but check Google Maps before a 7am visit.

Services (Wongnai): dine-in, delivery, order ahead and pick up (Pick & Go). Payment by bank card and TrueMoney Wallet.

Bangla Road in Patong at night, the bar strip where Burger King Patong Bangla stands. Illustrative photo, not taken at the restaurant. (Photo: Ben Reeves, CC BY 2.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bars\_at\_Bangla\_Road,\_Patong,\_Thailand.jpg)

Skip 66/2 Thaweewong Rd. That Patong Burger King has closed permanently according to Restaurant Guru, though some listings still show it (3.8/5 from 866 Google reviews before it shut).

Burger King OTOP Patong: quieter, open late

Burger King OTOP Patong is the calmer Patong option, on the road behind the beach rather than on the strip. It’s at 237/9 Rat-U-Thit 200 Pee Road (ถ.ราษฎร์อุทิศ), Patong, Kathu, Phuket 83150, phone +66 63 228 3196, rated 3.9/5 on Google from 252 reviews (Wanderlog).

Its hours are a mess across sources. Wanderlog shows both 11am to 5am and midnight to 5am, and one result even says 24 hours. Count on late, and check before you walk over.

Reviewers mention friendly staff, a quiet room, premium burgers such as the Black Truffle and the Double Mushroom Swiss, and vegetarian options. The complaints: fry portions that vary, no restroom, a language barrier. A few reviewers report food poisoning. Those are individual user reports, not established facts.

Burger King Kata: for Kata and Karon beaches

Burger King Kata is the branch for the southwest beaches. It’s at 102/7,8 Kata Rd, Karon, Phuket, phone +66 61 404 5497, rated 4.0/5 on Google from 314 reviews (Restaurant Guru, checked October 14, 2026). Restaurant Guru puts the typical spend at 200 to 400 THB per person.

Restaurant Guru lists Sunday to Friday 10am–4am and Saturday 10am–midnight; another listing says 10am–3am. Plan on a late close, not an all-nighter.

Burger King Boat Avenue: the best-rated Burger King in Bang Tao

Burger King Boat Avenue is the highest-rated Burger King in Phuket, at 4.3/5 from 1,079 Google reviews, and it’s open 24 hours (Wanderlog). It’s the branch for Bang Tao, Laguna and Cherngtalay on the northwest coast.

Address: 49, 58 Bandon-Cherngtalay Rd, Choeng Thale, Thalang, Phuket 83110.

Phone: +66 63 228 3179.

The setting does a lot of the work here: Boat Avenue is a lively market with live music. Reviewers also praise the flame-grilled burgers and the fried chicken, and note that the staff speak English. The catch is size. The dining room has only 5 or 6 tables, service slows at peak times (one reviewer waited more than 35 minutes), and some report mistakes on the bill. Go off-peak, or take it outside.

Burger King Turtle Village, Mai Khao

Burger King Turtle Village is open 8am to 11pm every day, according to the Turtle Village shopping center. It’s in Turtle Village Phase 2, 1st floor, 889 Moo 3, Mai Khao, Thalang, Phuket 83110, phone +66 63 217 7099. The mall’s page carries no update date, so check Google Maps before you drive up.

Burger King Phuket Airport: domestic terminal, 3rd floor

Yes, there’s a Burger King at Phuket International Airport. It’s on the 3rd floor of the domestic terminal, 222 M.6, Mai Khao, Thalang 83110, open 7am to 11pm, phone +66 65 936 8348 (Wanderlog). I couldn’t confirm whether it’s before or after security, so leave a margin if you’re connecting.

At 3.1/5 from 781 Google reviews, it’s the lowest-rated Burger King on the island. People like the runway view and the fast service, and complain about cleanliness and a chaotic counter at rush hour.

The Burger King Thailand menu: what’s different in Phuket

Burger King in Thailand sells local dishes you won’t find in Europe or the US alongside the Whopper. Thaiest listed these items in November 2024. I couldn’t confirm that all of them are still on the menu in October 2026:

  • Hat Yai Chicken King, fried chicken in the style of the southern Thai city of Hat Yai
  • Tom Yum Chicken King, chicken seasoned with the flavors of the hot-and-sour soup
  • Gai Klua Kruea with sticky rice, salted fried chicken with Thai sticky rice
  • Salmon Katsu with rice. A Salmon Katsu burger is also among the Thai specialties covered by the international press (Today.com)
  • Plant-Based Whopper Jr. and Veggie Burger for meat-free orders

Reviewers in Patong also mention a purple sweet potato pie and Yum Zing chicken, and OTOP Patong reviews name two premium burgers, the Black Truffle and the Double Mushroom Swiss. If you only order one thing you can’t get at home, make it the Hat Yai chicken: it’s the item most rooted in southern Thailand, a few hours’ drive from Phuket.

The viral Real Cheeseburger (2023, no longer available)

In July 2023, Burger King Thailand launched the “Real Cheeseburger”: 20 slices of cheddar in a sesame bun, no meat at all, promoted at 109 THB instead of 380 THB (ABC7). It went viral on TikTok, and a “Real Meat Burger” followed (Today.com). Both were limited-time stunts, so don’t go looking for them in Phuket.

Burger King Phuket menu prices in Thai baht

The only spending figure I could verify is Restaurant Guru’s 200 to 400 THB per person at Burger King Kata. I’m not publishing a Whopper price. Unofficial aggregators list a Whopper at 189 THB, a Whopper with Cheese at 219 THB and a Double Whopper at 259 THB, but Thai press already reported a full-price Whopper at 259 THB in 2022. Those numbers can’t all be right, and I found no official price for October 2026. Check the Burger King Thailand app or burgerking.co.th before you order.

What the reviews say about Burger King in Phuket

Burger King Phuket reviews range from 3.1/5 at the airport to 4.3/5 at Boat Avenue, and the same themes come up at every branch:

  • Liked: Thai-only menu items, late or 24-hour opening in Patong and Bang Tao, flame-grilled burgers, a convenient location near Bangla Road and the beaches.
  • Disliked: prices that feel high for fast food, slow service at peak times, food served cold, bill mistakes, a language barrier at some branches.
  • Serious reports: some reviewers at OTOP Patong, Jungceylon and the airport describe food poisoning or credit-card fraud. These are individual user complaints, not facts confirmed by any authority or by the brand.

Who runs Burger King in Thailand

Minor Food, part of Minor International, has run Burger King in Thailand since 2000. At December 31, 2024, it reported 130 Burger King restaurants in three countries: Thailand, the Maldives and the Seychelles. Burger King itself was founded in 1954, calls itself the “Home of the Whopper”, and is described by Minor Food as the world’s second-largest fast-food hamburger chain.

Asking for directions in Thai

If a taxi or tuk-tuk driver doesn’t recognize “Burger King”, show them the Thai: เบอร์เกอร์คิง ภูเก็ต (Burger King Phuket) or เบอร์เกอร์คิง ป่าตอง (Burger King Patong). For Jungceylon, the mall’s name alone usually does it.

Burger King Phuket FAQ

How many Burger King restaurants are there in Phuket?

I confirmed six active Burger King restaurants in Phuket: Jungceylon, Patong Bangla and OTOP in Patong, plus Kata, Boat Avenue in Bang Tao and Turtle Village in Mai Khao. There’s also a counter at the airport. The official locator couldn’t be read, so other branches may exist.

Is there a 24-hour Burger King in Patong?

Yes. Burger King Jungceylon is open 24 hours a day, every day, according to the mall’s official directory. Wanderlog and Wongnai also list Patong Bangla as 24 hours, although another Wongnai listing gives 11am to 6am.

Is there a Burger King at Phuket Airport?

Yes. It’s on the 3rd floor of the domestic terminal and open 7am to 11pm. I couldn’t confirm whether it’s before or after security.

How much does a meal at Burger King Phuket cost?

Restaurant Guru puts the budget at Burger King Kata at 200 to 400 THB per person. I couldn’t verify a current official Whopper price, so check the Burger King Thailand app before you order.

Does Burger King deliver in Phuket?

Burger King Patong Bangla offers delivery and order-ahead pickup (Pick & Go), according to Wongnai. I couldn’t confirm which delivery apps, such as Grab, LINE MAN or foodpanda, cover each branch.

Can you pay by card at Burger King in Phuket?

At Burger King Patong Bangla you can pay by bank card or TrueMoney Wallet, according to Wongnai. I couldn’t confirm payment options at the other branches.

Does Burger King Phuket have vegetarian options?

Yes. The Thai menu listed a Plant-Based Whopper Jr. and a Veggie Burger in November 2024, and reviewers at OTOP Patong mention vegetarian options. I couldn’t confirm a fully vegan meal.
