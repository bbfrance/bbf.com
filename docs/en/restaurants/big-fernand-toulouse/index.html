Big Fernand Toulouse: Address, Menu, Prices and Reviews (2026)

Big Fernand Toulouse on Place Wilson: address, 2022 menu prices from €13, Google and Tripadvisor ratings, and the chain's 2026 status, checked 9 Oct 2026.

By AlexPublished October 9, 2026

Big Fernand Toulouse is the French gourmet burger chain’s only confirmed restaurant in the city: 11 allées du Président Franklin Roosevelt, 31000 Toulouse, on Place Wilson, five minutes on foot from Jean-Jaurès metro. You can eat in, take away or order delivery. The last official Toulouse menu put a burger at €13 to €13.50 on its own and €19 to €19.50 as a full meal deal. Google rates it 4.0/5, Tripadvisor 3.5/5. I checked every detail below on 9 October 2026 and dated anything that might change. One thing up front: this guide is built from the official menu, the listings and diner reviews. It is not a tasting.

What the meal deals include

The Big meal deal (“Big Formule”) gets you a burger, a side, a soft drink and a dessert. The Little meal deal drops the dessert.

For the side, you pick Fernandines, the homemade fries, or the Falade, the house salad. Swapping the soft drink for a craft beer costs €2 more. The 2022 menu also lists:

  • Kids’ menu: €10 for a beef or chicken burger, Fernandines and a drink
  • “L’Effet Big”: double meat and double cheese for €4 extra
  • Smoked bacon: €1 extra
  • Big Toasté: €6 on its own or €10 as a meal deal, with a vegetarian version
  • Desserts: €3 to €4
  • Lemonade or iced tea (33 cl): €3
  • Local craft beers (33 cl): €5, from Brasserie la Seillonne, l’Oustal, l’Excuse and la Boussole
  • AOP Cahors red wine: €5 a glass, €20 a bottle

Where the meat comes from

According to the official menu, the beef comes from French-bred cattle, the veal is French and the chicken is Label Rouge free-range.

Label Rouge is France’s official quality label for farm-raised poultry. All three origins are listed in the same 2022 PDF.

Limited editions: the truffle burger example

Big Fernand rotates limited-edition burgers across all its restaurants, and Toulouse gets them too.

In December 2022, every Big Fernand in France, Toulouse included, served Le Tartuffe: Charolais or Limousin beef, Beillevaire raw-milk Mont d’Or cheese, rocket and truffle sauce, for €14.20 on its own or €20.20 as a meal deal (Le Bonbon Toulouse, 2 December 2022). Ask at the counter which edition is on now.

Which Big Fernand burger to order

For a first visit, I’d order Le Blaise, the most southwestern burger on the menu. For the house style, get the namesake Big Fernand.

This is my reading of the menu, not a tasting score. Le Blaise is the one build that only makes sense here: Bethmale, a cow’s-milk cheese from the Ariège Pyrenees, with dried duck breast and fig jam. Salty, sweet, local. The Big Fernand is the chain’s signature, with raw-milk tomme, sun-dried tomatoes and the house Tata Fernande sauce. Want something richer? The Bartholomé, with raclette and bacon. Prefer veal and blue cheese to beef? The Victor. Le Blaise and the Victor both come from the 2022 menu, so ask whether they are still served before you settle on either.

Vegetarian options at Big Fernand Toulouse

Yes, Big Fernand Toulouse has a vegetarian burger: the Lucien, built on a Portobello mushroom with tomme, sun-dried tomatoes and caramelised onions.

On the 2022 menu, the Lucien cost the same as the beef burgers: €13 on its own, €16 or €19 as a meal deal. The Big Toasté also came in a vegetarian version.

Verdict: is Big Fernand Toulouse worth it?

Big Fernand Toulouse is worth a stop if you want a French-style gourmet burger in the city centre, close to the metro, but expect chain prices and fries that don’t always arrive hot.

How I reached this verdict: I have not eaten here for this piece, so there is no personal score. The verdict rests on the official menu, dated prices, ratings from three platforms and the praise and complaints that keep coming back in reviews. A 4.0 average across more than 1,600 Google reviews points to a steady reputation, not a cult one. Tripadvisor’s 3.5 tracks the complaints about value, and I think those complaints are fair: €19 or more for a burger, fries, drink and dessert, at 2022 prices, is a lot for a burger lunch. What earns Big Fernand its place is the sourcing. Raw-milk tomme, Bethmale, Label Rouge chicken, a Cahors red and four local craft beers: that menu was written for southwest France, not copied from a global playbook.

The chain behind it: Big Fernand’s history and its 2026 restructuring

Big Fernand is a gourmet burger chain founded in Paris in 2011. After a court-supervised restructuring in 2026, it runs 45 restaurants and is still trading.

Steve Burggraf, Alexandre Auriac and Guillaume Pagliano founded Big Fernand in 2011, and the first restaurant opened in Paris’s 9th arrondissement in January 2012 (Wikipedia). In June 2017, the London fund Bluegem Capital Partners bought 80% of Big Groupe, which then had 35 restaurants (Franchise Magazine).

On 16 February 2026, the Paris economic activities court opened safeguard proceedings (“procédure de sauvegarde”) for Big Groupe (Entrevue). Under this French procedure, a company keeps trading under court protection while it renegotiates its debts. The court approved the plan on 22 September 2026. Bluegem is putting in €2.4 million, and 85% of a €19 million debt is being written off. The network has 45 restaurants (17 company-owned, 28 franchised), made €52 million in network sales in 2025 and employs 289 people directly (Paperjam, 9 October 2026).

A Big Fernand restaurant front in Boulogne-Billancourt: the Paris-born chain, founded in 2011, now runs 45 restaurants after its safeguard plan was approved on 22 September 2026 (Photo: Guilhem Vellut, CC BY 2.0 (Wikimedia Commons, 5472×3648))

No source I found says whether the Toulouse restaurant is company-owned or franchised, or ties it to any closure plan. As of 9 October 2026, it is open.

Big Fernand Toulouse FAQ

Where is Big Fernand in Toulouse and what is the nearest metro station?

Big Fernand Toulouse is at 11 allées du Président Franklin Roosevelt, 31000 Toulouse, on Place Wilson. The nearest metro station is Jean-Jaurès (lines A and B), about five minutes’ walk away.

What are Big Fernand Toulouse’s opening hours? Is it open on Sundays?

The sources give conflicting hours and the official page lists none, so I don’t publish them. Check the Google listing or call +33 5 34 30 15 62 before a Sunday or late-evening visit.

How much does a burger cost at Big Fernand Toulouse?

On the last official Toulouse menu (2022), a burger cost €13 to €13.50 on its own, €16 to €16.50 as a Little meal deal and €19 to €19.50 as a Big meal deal. 2026 prices are likely higher.

What is a “hamburgé” and what are “Fernandines”?

“Hamburgé” is Big Fernand’s playful French name for its burgers, which the chain calls French hamburgers. “Fernandines” are its homemade fries, made fresh every morning according to the official page.

Is there a vegetarian burger at Big Fernand Toulouse?

Yes. The Lucien uses a Portobello mushroom instead of meat, with tomme, sun-dried tomatoes and caramelised onions. The Big Toasté also comes in a vegetarian version.

Can I get Big Fernand Toulouse delivered?

Yes. The official page lists delivery and takeaway. I could not confirm which apps deliver from this restaurant (Uber Eats, Deliveroo or Just Eat), so search your app for “Big Fernand Toulouse Wilson”.

Do I need to book, and is it wheelchair accessible?

I found no confirmed booking system, payment details or official accessibility information for this restaurant. Call +33 5 34 30 15 62 to ask before you go.

Is Big Fernand closing down?

No closure has been announced. A French court approved the chain’s safeguard plan on 22 September 2026, and the network continues with 45 restaurants. The Toulouse Wilson restaurant was open on 9 October 2026.

Are there other Big Fernand restaurants around Toulouse?

Toulouse Wilson is the only Big Fernand I could confirm in Toulouse. No current source confirms another restaurant elsewhere in the metropolitan area.
