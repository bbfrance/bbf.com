Harry's Charbroiled: Toronto's Smash Burger Story

How a 1968 Parkdale diner became a three-location smash-burger brand, who runs it today, and where to eat a Jane in Toronto and Prince Edward County.

By Olivia, Restaurants writer, Canada and the US NortheastPublished November 12, 2026

Harry’s Charbroiled is a Toronto smash-burger brand owned by chef and restaurateur Grant van Gameren. It grew out of a Parkdale diner that Tommy Petropoulos opened in 1968 with his brothers George and Sam. You can eat Harry’s at three addresses today: 255 Coxwell Ave. in Toronto’s east end, the Waterworks Food Hall at 50 Brant St. downtown, and 13633 Loyalist Parkway in Picton, Prince Edward County. And no, nothing is charbroiled: the patties are smashed on a griddle (Tastet).

My take: the burgers are the hook, but the story is better. A chef who made his name on Spanish small plates bought a 1968 diner, kept the prices low and the counter fast, survived a forced closure and a pandemic, and came out with three locations.

Harry’s Charbroiled Toronto at a glance

Harry’s Charbroiled is a three-location burger counter brand known for griddled smash patties, the “Jane” burger family and thick shakes. Its tagline, printed on the official site, is “Always Flippin’ Never Slippin’”. I checked every address, phone number and opening time below against that site on November 7, 2026.

Who owns Harry’s Charbroiled? Grant van Gameren

Grant van Gameren has owned Harry’s since 2016, when he took over the shuttered Parkdale diner. At the time he was best known for Bar Isabel and Bar Raval, two of Toronto’s most talked-about restaurants (NOW Toronto, August 17, 2016).

He could have turned it into a chef’s vanity project. He didn’t. Harry’s stayed a cheap, fast burger counter; what changed was what went into the food. In 2017 the kitchen brined its own peameal bacon, made its own chorizo burgers and avoided frozen, pre-portioned or precooked ingredients (The Globe and Mail, February 24, 2017). That restraint is, to me, the smartest thing he did.

Van Gameren also kept the rights to the Harry’s name when the Parkdale lease ended, which is why the brand outlived its own building. In 2024 he curated the Waterworks Food Hall, where Harry’s is one of the vendors (UrbanToronto).

Nate Young and the 2016 kitchen

Chef Nate Young ran the kitchen when Harry’s reopened under van Gameren. He had spent three and a half years at Bar Isabel before the move (NOW Toronto). Coverage from 2020 to 2022 still named him as a co-operator, alongside Robin Goodfellow in 2020. I found no 2025 or 2026 source confirming either one’s current role, so treat that as history.

Harry’s Parkdale diner history: from 1968 to the Originals Dining Lounge

The original Harry’s was a family diner at 160 Springhurst Ave., near King St. W and Jameson Ave. in Parkdale, tucked behind a No Frills (Streets of Toronto). Its full name was Harry’s Char Broil & Dining Lounge, and under Petropoulos a burger cost under C$6 (The Globe and Mail). Who was Harry? None of my sources says.

The family closed the diner in spring 2016. Van Gameren and Young reopened it that September with the same retro bones and a sharper kitchen. Prices went up: in 2017 a burger with fries cost C$11.50, taxes included and cash only, and an 8-oz hormone-free skirt steak with fries cost C$18. Those are historical prices, not today’s.

Why the Parkdale Harry’s closed in 2019

Harry’s announced in August 2019 that it would leave Springhurst Ave., citing rent increases, unexpected infrastructure costs and possible future demolition of the building. It closed in October 2019 (blogTO).

The Originals Dining Lounge: the old diner’s second life

The Originals Dining Lounge is the diner that reopened at 160 Springhurst Ave. in December 2019, without the Harry’s name. Former servers Penny Andru and Cathy ran it with an investor tied to the Petropoulos family. Longtime cook Steve Stoymbos was back on the grill, and charbroiled burgers sold for C$4.99 (Streets of Toronto, December 12, 2019; blogTO). I could not confirm whether it is still open in 2026, so call ahead.

The pandemic food truck and the College and Palmerston era

Harry’s came back in April 2020 as a bright-yellow food truck and pickup operation. It worked out of Bar Raval first, then out of the former Woodlot space at College St. and Palmerston Ave. in Little Italy, with pickup from noon to 9 p.m. daily and delivery on Uber Eats (Streets of Toronto, August 27, 2020; NOW Toronto, August 31, 2020).

Van Gameren told NOW Toronto that the pandemic had created a growing hunger for classic hamburgers, and that this demand gave Harry’s a new life. The College and Palmerston address is no longer on the official site.

Coxwell Ave.: the east-end counter

The Coxwell location sits at Gerrard St. E, in a former Coffee Time. It was announced in May 2022 as a takeout-style counter with an open kitchen and some indoor seating, designed with Solid Design Creative and Concrete Design (blogTO, May 21, 2022).

Harry’s Charbroiled Waterworks at 50 Brant St.

Harry’s Charbroiled Waterworks is the downtown stall inside the Waterworks Food Hall, near Richmond St. W and Spadina. The hall opened on July 3, 2024 with more than 20 vendors curated by van Gameren (blogTO). It keeps the latest hours of the three: until midnight on Friday and Saturday.

Picton: Harry’s in Prince Edward County

The Picton restaurant opened in June 2022, a year after a food-truck pop-up on the same stretch of the Loyalist Parkway. It is the largest of the three, with two patios, a 40-car lot and room for more than 100 people (TasteToronto). Driving out to the County wineries? Stop here for lunch. Of the three, it is the one I would plan a day around.

Plain Jane burger at Harry’s

The Plain Jane is the simplest burger at Harry’s: one four-ounce beef patty with American cheese, pickles, onion and ketchup (Streets of Toronto, May 24, 2022). Order it first. With four ingredients and a thin patty, there is nowhere for a weak sear or a stale bun to hide.

Charbroiled or smashed? The name versus the technique

Harry’s Charbroiled burgers are smashed and griddled, not cooked over a charbroiler (Tastet). The name is a carry-over from the 1968 Harry’s Char Broil & Dining Lounge. Pressing a thin patty onto a hot flat-top browns its whole surface into a crust; a grill browns only where the bars touch. I think keeping the old name was right: it is a heritage label, not a cooking claim. The irony is that the Originals Dining Lounge, which lost the name, was the one advertising charbroiled burgers in 2019.

FAQ: Harry’s Charbroiled Toronto

Who owns Harry’s Charbroiled in Toronto? Chef and restaurateur Grant van Gameren has owned Harry’s since 2016. The diner was founded in 1968 by Tommy Petropoulos with his brothers George and Sam.

Is Harry’s Charbroiled the same as the old Harry’s diner in Parkdale? It is the same brand under a new owner. Van Gameren took over the 1968 Parkdale diner in 2016, kept the name when it closed in 2019, and relaunched Harry’s Charbroiled in 2020.

What happened to the original Harry’s on Springhurst Avenue? It closed in October 2019 over rent increases, infrastructure costs and possible demolition. In December 2019 former staff reopened it as The Originals Dining Lounge; I could not confirm whether it is still open in 2026.

Are Harry’s Charbroiled burgers actually charbroiled? No. The patties are smashed and griddled; the name comes from the original Harry’s Char Broil & Dining Lounge.

What is in a Plain Jane burger? A four-ounce beef patty, American cheese, pickles, onion and ketchup. The Classic, Double and Big Jane are also on the current menu.

Where are the Harry’s Charbroiled locations and what are the hours? 255 Coxwell Ave. (11 a.m.–9 p.m. daily), 50 Brant St. at Waterworks Food Hall (Sun–Thu 11:30 a.m.–10 p.m., Fri–Sat until midnight) and 13633 Loyalist Parkway, Picton (11 a.m.–8 p.m. daily), as listed on the official site on November 7, 2026.

Is Harry’s Charbroiled still at College and Palmerston? No. Harry’s operated there in 2020, but the official site no longer lists that address.

Does Harry’s Charbroiled have a vegan burger? A Vegan Jane was on the menu in 2020. I could not confirm it on the 2026 menu, so ask at the counter.

Does Harry’s Charbroiled still run a food truck? The yellow truck launched in April 2020, but I found no official confirmation that it operates in 2026.
