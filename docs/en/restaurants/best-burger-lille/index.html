Best Burger in Lille: 10 Top Spots Ranked (2026)

Smash burgers in Vieux-Lille, maroilles burgers, halal spots in Wazemmes and a plant-based pick, with prices in euros and Google ratings dated October 2026.

By Thomas, Restaurants writer, FrancePublished November 18, 2026

The best burger in Lille is at Le Comptoir Volant, 22 place des Patiniers in Vieux-Lille. The beef is ground on site, the fries are cooked twice, and the Fatal Maroilles burger carries aged farmhouse maroilles. If you want a pure smash burger, go to Junk Lille at 80 rue Esquermoise (4.8/5 on Google). If you go by Google ratings alone, Le Bon Jeune at 36 rue des Postes in Wazemmes leads the city with 4.9/5 from 1,095 reviews. Below are my 10 picks for November 2026, with addresses, the burger to order and prices in euros.

Best burgers in Lille at a glance

Lille’s best burgers sit in 2 clusters: Vieux-Lille for gourmet and smash burgers, and the rue des Postes end of Wazemmes for cheaper, mostly halal joints. The table below gives the whole top 10 before the detail.

How I ranked Lille’s burger restaurants

I didn’t rank by Google stars alone. If I had, Le Bon Jeune would be first and Le Comptoir Volant sixth. I started from the restaurants that appear in independent Lille rankings: the 2026 list by Gourmand, the October 2026 Google top 5 by Ma Vie Lilloise and the smash burger top 5 by Le Bonbon Lille. Then I weighed what goes into the burger: beef ground in-house, named cheeses, a focused menu. Rating and review volume came after that.

There was also a filter. Older lists still rank well in search and send you to closed doors: Raptor Shack shut in March 2026, and Big Fernand no longer trades on place de Béthune. I dropped any address I could not tie to a 2026 source, and I quote a price only where a source states one.

The 10 best burger restaurants in Lille

Each entry gives the full address, the neighbourhood, the burger to order and what sets the place apart. Phone numbers come from the restaurants’ own pages or from directories.

1. Le Comptoir Volant: the best maroilles burger in Lille

Le Comptoir Volant is my number 1 because it controls the two things most burger places buy in: the beef, ground on site, and the cheese. The cheese list runs from 9-month cheddar and saint-nectaire to farmhouse maroilles and tomme d’Orchies aged in beer. That is the north of France on a bun, and no other place on this list does it. Order the Fatal Maroilles, its most popular burger. A smash burger joined the menu in summer 2025.

Maroilles, the washed-rind cheese of northern France that goes into Le Comptoir Volant’s Fatal Maroilles burger (the cheese itself, not the burger) (Photo: Gmhofmann, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maroilles-K%C3%A4se.jpg)

  • Address: 22 place des Patiniers, 59800 Lille (Vieux-Lille)
  • Phone: +33 3 20 31 49 65
  • Price: €10–20
  • Google rating: 4.6/5 from 1,708 reviews (26 October 2026)
  • Official site: comptoirvolant.fr, Instagram @le_comptoir_volant

It started as a food truck. Today it also runs a corner at the Friche Gourmande, outlets in Villeneuve d’Ascq and Marcq-en-Barœul, the taproom of the Brasserie Cambier and, still, the truck.

2. Junk Lille: the best smash burger in Vieux-Lille

Junk Lille is built around 1 smash burger, sold in 5 sizes from S (1 patty) to XXL (5 patties). The motto is ‘less is more’. You get thin, crusty patties and triple-cooked fries, plus a half-baked cookie if you have room. Google rating: 4.8/5 from 1,082 reviews (27 October 2026).

The entrance to rue Esquermoise from the Grand Place in Vieux-Lille; Junk Lille’s smash burger counter is at number 80, further along the street (2007 photo, not of the restaurant) (Photo: B.navez, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lille\_RueEsquermoise.JPG)

  • Address: 80 rue Esquermoise, 59800 Lille (Vieux-Lille)
  • Phone: +33 9 55 34 04 05
  • Opening hours: every day, 12pm to 10:30pm
  • Opened: 5 October 2023

Majed Mansour and Wissem Ben Ammar founded Junk in Paris in 2021 as a delivery-only brand. I cover the Paris branches, menu and prices in Junk Smash Burgers Paris: menu, prices and addresses.

3. Le Bon Jeune: Lille’s highest-rated burger on Google

Le Bon Jeune has the best Google rating of any burger restaurant in the Ma Vie Lilloise survey: 4.9/5 from 1,095 reviews (27 October 2026). The menu is short. There are 6 burgers, all between €9 and €11, plus hot dogs and homemade fries. The restaurant says its meat is halal. If you skip meat, order the Bon Végé with burrata. Otherwise, the Caesar burger.

  • Address: 36 rue des Postes, 59000 Lille (Moulins / Wazemmes)
  • Phone: +33 6 26 32 77 83
  • Price: €9–11
  • Official page: Le Bon Jeune on Facebook

The Halles de Wazemmes covered market, heart of the Wazemmes neighbourhood where Lille’s cheaper and halal burger joints cluster around rue des Postes (Photo: Karldupart, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wazemmes\_halles1.jpg)

4. L’Adresse: the best gourmet burger in Lille

L’Adresse is where I would book for a sit-down burger with a glass of wine. Burgers cost €16 to €27 and come with sautéed potatoes. The Rossini is topped with truffled gouda. Le Pepper stacks a poached egg, Catalan tomme and baby spinach. Google: 4.6/5 from 1,119 reviews (26 October 2026).

  • Address: 34 bis rue des Bouchers, 59800 Lille (Vieux-Lille)
  • Phone: +33 3 59 89 66 33
  • Price: €16–27

5. Soulmade: the best Oklahoma smash burger in Wazemmes

Soulmade is a smash burger and soul food joint that opens only in the evening, from 7pm. A Classic Smash costs €9.90. The OG Oklahoma, a double smash cooked on thinly sliced onions, costs €12.90 and is the one to order. The beef is Angus, the bun can be swapped for a waffle, and the restaurant says its meat is halal. Google: 4.8/5 from 385 reviews.

  • Address: 104 rue des Sarrazins, 59000 Lille (Wazemmes)
  • Phone: +33 6 95 67 47 28
  • Price: €9.90–12.90

6. French Papa Burger: a double cheeseburger with a mushroom sauce

French Papa Burger made Le Bonbon’s top 5 smash burgers in Lille. The house burger stacks 2 150 g patties, double cheddar and turkey bacon, and Le Bonbon singles out its mushroom sauce. Burgers cost €11.90 to €15.50. Google: 4.7/5 from 488 reviews.

  • Address: 2 rue Mattéotti, Lille (Saint-Maurice Pellevoisin)
  • Phone: +33 7 60 14 15 98
  • Price: €11.90–15.50

7. Le Braisé: the cheapest burger on this list

Le Braisé grills halal burgers over an open flame and charges €5.90 to €10.50, the lowest prices in this guide. The sources disagree on the fuel: Gourmand says charcoal, Le Bonbon says wood. Its 2,085 Google reviews (4.5/5, 26 October 2026) make it the most-reviewed place here.

  • Address: 107 rue de Douai, Lille (Lille-Sud / Moulins)
  • Price: €5.90–10.50

Its burger has travelled. In 2021 it ranked 36th among the 50 most-ordered dishes in the world on Deliveroo, as Le Bonbon reported in January 2022.

8. PNY Lille: a Paris–New York cheeseburger in Vieux-Lille

PNY (Paris New York) is the most expensive stop on this list, in the €20–30 band. Order the Vintage Cheeseburger with 18-month aged cheddar. Google: 4.5/5 from 1,349 reviews.

Place Louise de Bettignies in Vieux-Lille, home of PNY Lille’s Vintage Cheeseburger at number 39 (photo of the square, not of the restaurant) (Photo: Pierre André Leclercq, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lille,\_place\_louise\_de\_Bettignies.jpg)

  • Address: 39 place Louise de Bettignies, Lille (Vieux-Lille)
  • Price: €20–30

Rudy Guénaire and Graffi Rathamohan founded PNY in Paris in 2012. The Lille branch, opened in May 2021, was its first outside the capital, according to Le Bonbon Lille.

9. Fabrik Burger: molten-cheese burgers on rue des Postes

Fabrik Burger is a neighbourhood joint known for one burger: the Burger Fabrik, with a molten cheese centre. It sits in the €10–20 band and has 4.6/5 from 400 Google reviews. Le Bon Jeune is a few doors down the same street, so you can try both on 1 walk.

  • Address: 110 rue des Postes, Lille (Wazemmes)
  • Price: €10–20

10. La Clairière: the best vegan burger in Lille

La Clairière is the address for a vegan burger in Lille. Every burger is 100% plant-based, and some are gluten-free. Expect the €20–30 band. A 4.7/5 rating from 1,213 reviews puts it level with the meat places, not only ahead of other vegan spots.

  • Address: 75 boulevard de la Liberté, Lille (Lille-Centre)
  • Price: €20–30

Smash burgers in Lille: where to go

For a smash burger in Lille, go to Junk on rue Esquermoise first and Soulmade’s OG Oklahoma in Wazemmes second. Le Comptoir Volant has served a smash burger since summer 2025, and French Papa Burger also made Le Bonbon’s smash burger top 5. To try the technique at home, my colleague Alice’s Sonic All-American Bacon Smasher copycat recipe covers the press, the sear and the crust.

Where to eat a burger in Lille: Vieux-Lille or Wazemmes?

Go to Vieux-Lille for a gourmet or smash burger in old brick-and-sandstone streets, and to Wazemmes for cheaper burgers and halal options. 4 of the 10 are in Vieux-Lille: Le Comptoir Volant, Junk, L’Adresse and PNY. 3 are around rue des Postes, on the Wazemmes–Moulins edge: Le Bon Jeune, Soulmade and Fabrik Burger. Le Braisé is further south on rue de Douai.

Map of Lille’s 10 best burger restaurants: the Vieux-Lille cluster (Le Comptoir Volant, Junk, L’Adresse, PNY) near Lille-Flandres and Lille-Europe stations, and the rue des Postes cluster in Wazemmes and Moulins (Le Bon Jeune, Soulmade, Fabrik Burger), with Le Braisé further south on rue de Douai (Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL)

Arriving by Eurostar or TGV at Lille-Europe or Lille-Flandres? Aim for the Vieux-Lille cluster between trains, and check the walking time on a map before you set off. Everything else we have published on Lille is on the Lille burger restaurants page.

Big Fernand Lille: the other maroilles burger

Big Fernand lists 1 Lille restaurant, at 107 rue Esquermoise in Vieux-Lille, and its Barnabé burger is made with maroilles. 1 warning: PagesJaunes shows that address as ‘probably permanently closed’, which contradicts the chain’s own site. Call before you go. The place de Béthune branch that older lists still mention is closed. My full review is in Big Fernand Lille: address, menu, prices, and the chain’s history is on the Big Fernand brand page.

Closed and new burger restaurants in Lille in 2026

Raptor Shack closed in early March 2026 after 12 years at 18 rue d’Amiens, Le Bonbon reported. Big Fernand’s place de Béthune branch is gone too. Any list that still sends you to either is out of date.

On the arrivals side, K.O Burger, a New York chain, announced in April 2026 that its first French address would be 73 rue Masséna in Lille. From 6 to 23 August 2026, The Champions Burger festival set up on the car park of Aushopping Noyelles, in Noyelles-Godault, about 30 km south of Lille, with around 15 burger brands and a public vote.

Other Lille burger names you will see in rankings

3 more names appear often in Lille rankings. Each missed my cut for a sourcing reason. Père & Fish makes fish burgers (shrimp, salmon, octopus) and won a 2021 public vote for best burger in Lille with 3,000 participants, but I could not confirm it still trades at 66 rue Nationale in 2026. Chick’n Beef at 211 rue des Postes has 4.8/5 from 4,243 reviews, but it is a halal chain built around chicken, not beef. Kapris Pepper is the third: I could not confirm its address with a second source.

FAQ: best burgers in Lille

Where can I eat the best burger in Lille?

Le Comptoir Volant, 22 place des Patiniers in Vieux-Lille, serves the best all-round burger in Lille, with beef ground on site and aged farmhouse cheeses. For a smash burger, go to Junk Lille at 80 rue Esquermoise.

Which burger restaurant in Lille has the best Google rating?

Le Bon Jeune at 36 rue des Postes had the best Google rating for a burger restaurant in Lille: 4.9/5 from 1,095 reviews on 27 October 2026, according to Ma Vie Lilloise.

Where can I find a maroilles burger in Lille?

Order the Fatal Maroilles at Le Comptoir Volant (22 place des Patiniers). Big Fernand’s Barnabé (107 rue Esquermoise) also uses maroilles, but call first: 1 directory lists that branch as possibly closed. Both are in Vieux-Lille.

Which burger restaurants in Lille are halal?

Le Bon Jeune, Soulmade and Le Braisé say their meat is halal. I found no certification to verify, so ask the restaurant if certification matters to you.

Where can I eat a vegetarian or vegan burger in Lille?

La Clairière, 75 boulevard de la Liberté, serves only plant-based burgers and has gluten-free options. Le Bon Jeune also has a vegetarian burger, the Bon Végé with burrata.

How much does a burger cost in a good Lille restaurant?

Of the menu prices I could confirm, burgers run from €5.90 at Le Braisé to €27 at L’Adresse, and most cost between €9 and €16. PNY Lille and La Clairière sit in the €20–30 band.

Which Lille burger restaurants are open on Sundays?

Junk Lille opens every day from 12pm to 10:30pm, Sundays included. For the others, check current hours on Google Maps or Instagram before you go.

Where can I eat a burger near Lille-Flandres or Lille-Europe station?

Head to Vieux-Lille, where Le Comptoir Volant, Junk, L’Adresse and PNY Lille are close together. Check the walk from your station on a map.
