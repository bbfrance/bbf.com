Pilot Canberra: Mal Hanslow's Two-Hat Restaurant

Who runs Pilot, how it went from a neighbourhood à la carte restaurant to Canberra's only two-hatted dining room, and where to go if you came for a burger.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished November 27, 2026

Pilot Canberra is the fine-dining restaurant at the Ainslie shops, and since the Good Food Guide awards of October 2026 it is the only two-hatted dining room in the city. Chef Malcolm (Mal) Hanslow has run the kitchen since opening night, 21 September 2018. Dash Rumble and Ross McQuinn own it. The format is a seasonal tasting menu: dinner Thursday to Sunday from 6pm, lunch Saturday and Sunday from 12pm. Gourmet Traveller has named it ACT Restaurant of the Year three times. One thing it does not serve: burgers.

Who runs Pilot Canberra?

Dash Rumble and Ross McQuinn own Pilot, and chef Malcolm Hanslow has led its kitchen since it opened in 2018. Rumble and McQuinn both came from Pulp Kitchen, the restaurant that held the same site before them (HerCanberra, 2018; Region, 2024).

Early coverage names only Rumble and McQuinn as owners. Region’s December 2024 profile goes further and calls Hanslow co-owner and head chef of both Pilot and Such and Such (Region). I call him “chef and co-owner” on the strength of that report.

The official site lists a small team: Hanslow in the kitchen, Tom Opie as head of drinks. A 2021 report also named Barry Burger as sous chef. That is a person, not a dish.

Malcolm Hanslow: from CIT student to two-hatted chef

Malcolm Hanslow trained in Canberra, ran his first kitchen at 24 and came home from Sydney to open Pilot. Region gave his age as 36 in December 2024. It dates his studies at CIT, Canberra’s public technical institute, to about 19 years earlier.

eightysix in Braddon

Hanslow’s first head-chef job was at eightysix in Braddon, where he ran the kitchen at 24 (Region).

Sydney: Oscillate Wildly, Ester and Automata

Before Pilot, Hanslow cooked in Sydney at Oscillate Wildly, Ester and Automata. He then returned to Canberra to run Pilot’s kitchen (HerCanberra; Canberra Times).

Such and Such in Civic

Since January 2023, Hanslow has also been executive chef of Such and Such, the owners’ second venue at 8/220 London Circuit in Canberra City (Broadsheet; Canberra Times). It holds one hat in the October 2026 Good Food Guide awards.

The story of Pilot Ainslie: from à la carte to degustation

Pilot opened as an à la carte restaurant in 2018 and switched to a degustation format during the COVID lockdowns. It still runs that way today.

It took over the former Pulp Kitchen site at the back of the Ainslie shops. Pulp Kitchen closed in August 2018, and Pilot opened a month later, on Friday 21 September (HerCanberra).

The first menu listed 15 starters and mains. The most expensive main in 2018 was the rump, at A$47. HerCanberra counted 36 seats at opening. Today the official site describes a 24-seat dining room and a small outdoor area that doubles as a gallery for local artists.

Those two numbers tell the story better than any award citation. Pilot cut a third of its seats and dropped the menu that let you order one main and leave. That is a bet on depth over volume, unusual for a suburban shopping strip, and the awards that followed suggest it paid off.

Dash Rumble summed up the ambition in 2021: “We wanted to open a restaurant that would be good for Canberra, but also a good restaurant for Australia.” His shorthand was “excellence without pomposity” (Region).

Pilot restaurant Ainslie degustation: what to expect

Pilot serves a tasting menu that changes with the seasons, so there is no fixed dish list to plan around. Book ahead: 24 seats, dinner four nights a week and lunch only on weekends means few tables.

I could not find the current degustation price on the official site on 17 November 2026, so I am not publishing one. Check the booking page on pilotrestaurant.com for today’s price and dietary options.

Is Pilot among the best restaurants in Canberra?

Yes. On awards alone, Pilot sits at the top of any list of the best restaurants in Canberra. Gourmet Traveller named it the ACT’s best three times between 2021 and 2024, and the Good Food Guide’s October 2026 awards left it the only two-hatted room in the city. Gourmet Traveller has also reviewed it at length.

My view: the hats grab the headlines, but the Gourmet Traveller run says more. A 24-seat room behind a suburban shopping strip beat the city’s bigger names three times in four years, and the 2025 loss to Lunetta did not stop it taking two hats a year later. My one complaint is practical. A tasting menu with no published price is hard to plan a trip around, and Pilot should post it.

Pilot and Canberra’s burger scene

Pilot is not a burger restaurant, and no source I checked lists a burger on its menu. It is on Burger Breton because it shapes how Canberra eats out, and readers planning a food trip to the capital keep asking about it.

FAQ about Pilot Canberra

Who is the chef at Pilot in Canberra?

Malcolm (Mal) Hanslow has been Pilot’s chef since it opened in 2018. He trained at CIT in Canberra, ran eightysix in Braddon, and cooked at Oscillate Wildly, Ester and Automata in Sydney.

Who owns Pilot restaurant in Ainslie?

Dash Rumble and Ross McQuinn own Pilot. Region described chef Malcolm Hanslow as co-owner in December 2024.

Is Pilot in Ainslie still open, and what are its opening hours?

Yes. As of 17 November 2026, Pilot serves dinner Thursday to Sunday from 6pm and lunch Saturday and Sunday from 12pm, at Shop 5/6, 1 Wakefield Gardens, Ainslie ACT 2602.

Does Pilot Canberra only serve a degustation menu, and how much does it cost?

Pilot serves a seasonal degustation menu, a format it adopted during the COVID lockdowns. The current price was not published on the official site when I checked; confirm it when you book.

How many hats does Pilot have in the Good Food Guide?

Two. In the Good Food Guide awards announced in October 2026, Pilot was Canberra’s only two-hatted restaurant.

Has Pilot won Gourmet Traveller’s ACT Restaurant of the Year?

Yes, three times: 2021, 2022 and 2024. Lunetta won the title in 2025.

What is Such and Such, and how is it linked to Pilot?

Such and Such is the second venue from Pilot’s owners, opened in January 2023 at 8/220 London Circuit, Canberra City, with Malcolm Hanslow as executive chef. It holds one Good Food Guide hat.

Does Pilot serve burgers?

No. Pilot is a fine-dining tasting-menu restaurant. For burgers, see our Canberra burger guide.
