La Prensa Restaurant Zaragoza: Marisa Barberán and David Pérez

How a 1970 bulk-wine shop became one of Zaragoza's 2 Michelin-starred restaurants, and why La Prensa is closed for refurbishment in October 2026.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished October 14, 2026

La Prensa in Zaragoza is chef Marisa Barberán’s restaurant. She has run the kitchen since 1985, and her husband David Pérez, the owner and sommelier, is the son of the man who opened the business as a bulk wine shop in 1970. It holds 1 Michelin star, first awarded in November 2012 and renewed in the Michelin Guide Spain 2026. Don’t book a trip around it yet, though. When I checked the official website on 9 October 2026, it said the restaurant was “closed for refurbishment” and that a reopening date would be announced. And no, there are no burgers here: La Prensa serves tasting menus only.

From 1970 wine shop to Michelin star: the La Prensa timeline

La Prensa took 42 years to go from a neighborhood wine shop to a Michelin star, and the restaurant or the major guides document every step.

David Pérez’s father opened the business in 1970 as a bulk wine shop. A bar came later, then a small dining room for tastings and homemade food, according to the official site and Pérez’s interview with GoAragon. No source I found gives the founder’s name, so I have left it out rather than guess.

Marisa Barberán, the chef of La Prensa

Marisa Barberán has been La Prensa’s head chef since 1985, and she is the person who turned a wine-shop dining room into a starred kitchen.

She started cooking there 15 years before La Prensa became a full restaurant, and 41 years later she is still in the same kitchen. To me, that is the most telling fact in the whole story. The star did not come from a chef passing through on the way to a bigger city. It came from someone who built the kitchen up from a tasting room. She and David Pérez are married, and the work splits cleanly: she cooks, he handles the wine and the dining room.

Michelin describes her cooking as “Contemporary”, with “an honest approach, the use of the best seasonal ingredients”. Guía Repsol names her caldereta de gamba roja, a red prawn stew, as a signature dish, and says the restaurant keeps its own vegetable garden. Both details come from Repsol alone. I cannot confirm that either will survive the refurbishment.

David Pérez, owner and sommelier

David Pérez is La Prensa’s owner, sommelier and front-of-house manager, and the son of the man who founded the business in 1970.

The wine shop is still in the restaurant’s DNA. In his December 2024 interview with GoAragon, Pérez says the goal is for the customer to have a “complete hedonistic experience”. In practice, that means he pairs the wine with Barberán’s cooking and runs the room himself.

Is La Prensa open? Refurbishment status in October 2026

No. La Prensa is closed for refurbishment according to its official website (checked 9 October 2026), and no reopening date has been announced.

I found no press coverage of the closure: no start date, no scope of works, no reopening date. The Michelin Guide page still lists lunch and dinner service days, which I would treat as outdated until the restaurant confirms them. Before you plan a trip around La Prensa, check its website or call +34 976 381 637.

Michelin star restaurants in Zaragoza in 2026

Zaragoza has 2 Michelin-starred restaurants in the 2026 guide: La Prensa and Gente Rara, with 1 star each.

If you read somewhere that La Prensa is “Zaragoza’s only Michelin-starred restaurant”, the information is out of date. Gente Rara also holds a star in the Michelin Guide Spain 2026, published on 25 November 2025. Cancook, another Zaragoza restaurant, lost its star in that edition. Across the whole Aragón region, 11 restaurants hold a star, according to Zaragozala and HoyAragón.

Restaurant City Michelin 2026

While La Prensa is closed, Gente Rara is the city’s other starred table. Check that it is taking bookings before you count on it. Will La Prensa keep its star in the 2027 guide while its doors are shut? Nobody has said, and I won’t guess.

Does La Prensa serve burgers? Zaragoza’s burger scene in context

La Prensa does not serve burgers. Its official site, Michelin, Repsol and GoAragon all describe tasting-menu fine dining and nothing else.

If you came to Zaragoza for burgers, the city’s story starts across town. According to Aragón Digital, Zaragoza’s first burger was served at Nevada, on Plaza San Francisco 11. Nevada opened in 1957 as an ice-cream shop, and the burger reportedly arrived through contact with the nearby US air base.

Nevada’s burger is thin and elongated, because round buns were not available back then, and it comes with homemade tomato sauce. The “first burger” claim rests on 1 press article and a listing, so I read it as local lore rather than settled history. It is still the burger I would put first on a Zaragoza list, for the story alone.

FAQ about La Prensa restaurant in Zaragoza

Who is the chef at La Prensa in Zaragoza?

Marisa Barberán has been head chef at La Prensa since 1985. Her husband, David Pérez, is the owner, sommelier and dining room manager.

Does La Prensa in Zaragoza have a Michelin star?

Yes. La Prensa has held 1 Michelin star since November 2012, and the star was renewed in the Michelin Guide Spain 2026. It has also held 1 Guía Repsol Sol since 2013.

Is La Prensa open?

No. As of 9 October 2026, the official website says La Prensa is closed for refurbishment and that the reopening date will be announced. Opening hours on third-party sites may be outdated.

How much is the tasting menu at La Prensa?

The Tasting Menu (snacks, 6 courses, 2 desserts) costs €95, and the Gourmet Menu (snacks, 8 courses, 3 desserts) costs €130, VAT included, according to the official site. Prices may change after the reopening.

How many Michelin-starred restaurants does Zaragoza have in 2026?

2: La Prensa and Gente Rara, with 1 star each. Cancook lost its star in the 2026 guide. Aragón as a whole has 11 starred restaurants.

Where is La Prensa in Zaragoza?

La Prensa is at Calle José Nebra 3, 50007 Zaragoza, Spain. The phone number is +34 976 381 637.

Does La Prensa serve burgers?

No. La Prensa serves tasting menus only. For burgers, see our Zaragoza city guide.

Where was the first burger served in Zaragoza?

According to Aragón Digital, Zaragoza’s first burger was served at Nevada, on Plaza San Francisco 11. Nevada opened in 1957 as an ice-cream shop and still serves a thin, elongated burger with homemade tomato sauce.
