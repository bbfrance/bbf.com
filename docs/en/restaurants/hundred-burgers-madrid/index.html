Hundred Burgers Madrid: Menu, Prices, Locations

Ranked No. 1 by Burgerdudes 3 years running: which Madrid branch to pick, what to order, what it costs and whether to book.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished November 18, 2026

Hundred Burgers in Madrid is worth the trip if you want to taste the burger ranked No. 1 in the world. On April 29, 2026, Burgerdudes put it at the top of The World’s Best Burgers for the third year in a row. Madrid has 2 sit-down restaurants, in Chueca (Plaza de Pedro Zerolo 1) and Chamberí (Calle de Eloy Gonzalo 12), plus a takeaway and delivery kitchen in Alcobendas. Expect €10 to €15 per burger and about €20 per person before drinks. Book ahead at Chamberí if you can.

Hundred Burgers in 6 key figures

6 numbers explain why Hundred is the burger name everyone in Madrid is talking about.

  1. 3 consecutive No. 1 titles in Burgerdudes’ The World’s Best Burgers (2024, 2025 and 2026).
  2. 2020: the year Hundred opened in Valencia.
  3. 3 locations in Madrid: Chueca, Chamberí and a takeaway kitchen in Alcobendas.
  4. €10 to €15: the price of a burger quoted by the press in 2026.
  5. About €20: the average spend per person before drinks, according to Macarfi.
  6. 1,346 burger joints in 65 countries assessed for the 2026 ranking.

3 No. 1 titles: is Hundred really the world’s best burger?

Hundred Burgers is the world’s best burger according to 1 list: The World’s Best Burgers, published every year by the Swedish burger review site Burgerdudes. In 2024 Hundred became the first restaurant outside the United States to take the No. 1 spot. It kept the title in 2025, as Time Out Madrid reported on April 7, 2025.

Then came April 29, 2026. Burgerdudes released The World’s 25 Best Burgers 2026, and Hundred was No. 1 again, the first restaurant to hold the top spot 3 years running. Holy Burger in São Paulo came second and Au Cheval (Chicago and New York) third, according to Burgerdudes and El Español.

Why the Burgerdudes ranking matters

The ranking carries weight because of its scale, not because it is an official guide. Spanish headlines like to call it the “Michelin guide of burgers”. It isn’t: Burgerdudes is an independent review site, and its 2026 list was built from 1,346 burger joints in 65 countries, according to El Español and Madrid Secreto. My view: 3 wins in a row from a panel that wide is hard to dismiss, but it is still 1 panel’s taste. Treat it as a strong reason to go, not a final word.

Madrid did well beyond Hundred. 2 other local spots made the 2026 top 25: Briochef at No. 15 and BDP at No. 21.

Founded in 2020: is Hundred Burgers from Madrid or Valencia?

Hundred Burgers is from Valencia, not Madrid. Álex González-Urbón and Ezequiel Maldjian founded it there in 2020, according to Wikipedia and Time Out Madrid.

The groundwork took 3 years. From 2017 to 2020, starting in New York, the two founders traveled through 22 countries and tasted more than 300 burgers. Madrid only came in 2023. For the Valencia side of the story, read my colleague Alex’s guide to the best burgers in Valencia, where Hundred ranks first.

In October 2025 the ownership changed. Buenavista Equity Partners bought 80% of Hundred Burgers, while the founders kept 20% and stayed in charge, as Wikipedia and Europa Press via Bolsamanía reported.

Hundred Chueca (Plaza de Pedro Zerolo)

Hundred Chueca is the most central option, a sit-down restaurant with table service on Plaza de Pedro Zerolo, in the heart of Chueca. It was already open by August 2024, when the salmonella closure hit both Madrid restaurants. On Google it scores 9.4/10 from 9,398 reviews, as aggregated by Gastroranking. That is a third-party figure, not our rating.

Hundred Chamberí (Calle de Eloy Gonzalo)

Hundred Chamberí was the brand’s first sit-down restaurant in Madrid, announced for late September 2023. It sits at Calle de Eloy Gonzalo 12, has a terrace according to Macarfi, and is the only Madrid branch whose phone number I could confirm: +34 919 99 27 18.

Hundred Alcobendas (takeaway and delivery)

Alcobendas is where Hundred started in the Madrid area, on Monday, August 21, 2023. The site at Calle de San José Artesano 8 is a production kitchen and dark kitchen. No tables, no bookings: only takeaway and delivery.

Hundred Burgers menu: what to order

Order the Singular on a first visit, and the Loser if you like a little heat. Those are the two signature burgers the Spanish press describes most often.

  • Singular: Galician blonde beef (rubia gallega), cheddar, “mom’s BBQ” sauce, bacon, caramelized onion and camembert cream.
  • Loser: rubia gallega beef, cheddar, caramelized onion and a mildly spicy gochujang sauce.
  • Satisfaction (double) and Paul Finch (limited edition): both singled out by the Burgerdudes jury in 2026, alongside the Loser.

What makes the Hundred burger different

2 things set Hundred apart: the bun and the beef. The bun is a semi-brioche, a hybrid of brioche and Japanese milk bread, baked in-house every morning. The dry-aged rubia gallega beef is ground on site every day, according to Burgerdudes and Time Out Madrid.

How long the beef is aged is less clear. Sources quote 45, 60 or 120 days depending on the burger and the year, so I won’t give you a single figure until Hundred confirms one.

Gluten-free, vegetarian and vegan options

Hundred offers gluten-free buns as well as vegetarian and vegan options, according to Macarfi. Ask staff which burgers can be adapted when you order.

A burger at Hundred costs €10 to €15 in 2026, and a meal comes to about €20 per person before drinks. The press ranges differ slightly:

Booking a table at Hundred Burgers Madrid

Book ahead for Chamberí: Macarfi says “booking is essential” at Calle de Eloy Gonzalo 12, and the number is +34 919 99 27 18. Time Out Madrid, though, has noted that the restaurant struggles to take bookings. Have a backup plan.

For Chueca, I could not confirm either the booking policy or a phone number, so check the official website or Google Maps. Alcobendas takes no bookings at all.

Opening hours

I could not confirm 2026 opening hours for any of the 3 sites, so check each branch on Google Maps or the official website before you travel. Remember that Madrid eats late: lunch rarely starts before 2 pm, dinner rarely before 9 pm.

Hundred Burgers delivery in Madrid

Yes, Hundred delivers in Madrid. In 2023 Time Out Madrid reported delivery through Glovo in Madrid and Valencia, as well as through Hundred’s own app and website. That is what the Alcobendas kitchen was built for.

The August 2024 salmonella outbreak

In August 2024, Hundred briefly closed both Madrid sit-down restaurants after a salmonella outbreak. The Eloy Gonzalo and Pedro Zerolo branches shut on August 4 and 5, 2024, after a batch of truffle and egg yolk sauce was linked to the illness. Time Out counted 16 people affected; Wikipedia says 17. Both restaurants reopened on August 16, 2024, according to Time Out Madrid, and the chain kept its No. 1 title in 2025 and 2026.

My verdict: is Hundred Burgers Madrid worth it?

Yes, if you love burgers and you are already in Madrid. At €10 to €15, this is the cheapest way I know to judge a world No. 1 for yourself. I would pick Chueca: it is central, it has the strongest Google score and you can walk there from Gran Vía. Choose Chamberí only if you manage to book and want the terrace. And skip Alcobendas unless you are ordering delivery.

How I built this guide

I have not given Hundred a Burger Breton rating, because this guide has no first-hand visit behind it and our rating is reserved for restaurants we have eaten at. I built it from the Burgerdudes ranking, the Spanish and English press (Time Out Madrid, El Español, Madrid Secreto, Valencia Secreta), Macarfi and Gastroranking, all checked on November 3, 2026, and linked each source where I use it. Third-party ratings are quoted as such and are not our verdict.

Hundred Burgers Madrid FAQ

Is Hundred Burgers really the best burger in the world?

According to Burgerdudes’ The World’s Best Burgers, yes. Hundred was ranked No. 1 in 2024, 2025 and 2026, the first restaurant ever to top the list 3 years in a row. The 2026 edition assessed 1,346 burger joints in 65 countries.

Where are the Hundred Burgers restaurants in Madrid?

There are 3: Plaza de Pedro Zerolo 1 in Chueca and Calle de Eloy Gonzalo 12 in Chamberí, both sit-down restaurants, plus a takeaway and delivery kitchen at Calle de San José Artesano 8 in Alcobendas.

How much does a burger cost at Hundred Burgers?

The 2026 press puts a burger at €10 to €15. Macarfi estimates the average spend at about €20 per person before drinks, and Gastroranking at €20 to €30.

Do I need to book at Hundred Burgers Madrid?

At Chamberí, Macarfi says booking is essential: call +34 919 99 27 18. The Chueca booking policy is not confirmed, and Alcobendas takes no bookings.

Which burger should I order at Hundred?

Start with the Singular (rubia gallega beef, cheddar, bacon, caramelized onion, camembert cream, BBQ sauce) or the Loser with gochujang. The Burgerdudes jury also praised the double Satisfaction and the limited-edition Paul Finch.

Does Hundred Burgers have gluten-free or vegan options?

Yes. Hundred offers gluten-free buns and vegetarian and vegan options, according to Macarfi.
