BurgerFuel Christchurch: 5 Stores, Hours, Prices

All 5 BurgerFuel restaurants in Christchurch: addresses, official hours, the one near the airport, late weekend openings, NZ$ prices and Google ratings.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished November 20, 2026

BurgerFuel has 5 restaurants in Christchurch: Bush Inn (Riccarton), Spitfire Square (by the airport), Papanui, Ferrymead and Sydenham. All 5 open at 10am. Bush Inn and Spitfire Square close at 10pm every day; Papanui, Ferrymead and Sydenham stay open until 11pm on Fridays and Saturdays. An American Muscle costs about NZ$15.00, the Bastard about NZ$20.90. I checked every address, phone number and opening hour against BurgerFuel’s official store locator on 5 November 2026. My pick, if you only have time for one: Spitfire Square, for its ratings and its location.

BurgerFuel Christchurch at a glance

BurgerFuel Christchurch is 5 restaurants in 5 suburbs, and the right 1 depends on where you are and when you’re hungry.

  • Nearest the airport: Spitfire Square, 544 Memorial Avenue.
  • Oldest: Bush Inn, Riccarton, open since 15 January 2014, and the biggest BurgerFuel in New Zealand when it opened.
  • Latest on weekends: Papanui, Ferrymead and Sydenham, until 11pm on Friday and Saturday.
  • Highest Google rating found: Spitfire Square, 4.7/5 from 1,567 reviews.
  • City centre: no longer served. The Hereford Street restaurant closed in April 2024.
  • Budget: about NZ$15 to NZ$21 for a burger, NZ$6.90 for fries with aioli (unofficial prices, July 2026).

Which BurgerFuel in Christchurch should you choose?

Pick Spitfire Square if you’re flying, Bush Inn if you’re in Riccarton or with a group, and Papanui, Ferrymead or Sydenham for a late weekend burger.

BurgerFuel Spitfire Square, the one near Christchurch Airport

BurgerFuel Spitfire Square is the closest BurgerFuel to Christchurch International Airport, on Memorial Avenue at the corner of Peter Leeming Road. It sits in the Spitfire Square Shopping Centre and opens daily from 10am to 10pm. That makes it the obvious stop for a first or last meal in Christchurch.

Christchurch Airport’s domestic terminal, almost finished in October 2012. BurgerFuel Spitfire Square, the closest BurgerFuel to the airport, is a few minutes’ drive east along Memorial Avenue. It’s also the best-rated store I could verify: 4.7/5 on Google from 1,567 reviews and 4.5/5 on TripAdvisor from 82. Reviewers praise the New Zealand beef, the fries, the staff and the clean tables. They complain about loud music and sticky tables at peak times. The detail that matters if you have a plane to catch: expect around 20 minutes’ wait when it’s busy. Leave yourself a margin.

BurgerFuel Bush Inn (Riccarton), the first in the South Island

BurgerFuel Bush Inn is the chain’s original Christchurch restaurant, in the Bush Inn Shopping Centre at Riccarton Road and Waimairi Road. It opened on 15 January 2014 and is open daily from 10am to 10pm.

Don’t be thrown by the name. The official site says Bush Inn; TripAdvisor and Localista call it BurgerFuel Riccarton. When it opened it had about 100 seats inside and out, the largest capacity of any BurgerFuel in New Zealand at the time, according to the chain. That figure is from 2014, but it’s still why I’d send a group here first. I couldn’t find a verifiable, current Google rating for this store.

BurgerFuel Papanui, the late-night option on the north side

BurgerFuel Papanui, at Shop 5/485 Papanui Road, is the north-side option, open until 11pm on Fridays and Saturdays. BurgerFuel announced it on 18 August 2015.

It scores 4.4/5 on Google (1,274 reviews) and 4.3/5 on TripAdvisor (42 reviews). The praise is specific: very friendly staff, a roomy dining room, banana thickshakes, a free birthday burger. So are the complaints, mainly delays for delivery drivers and takeaway portions some find a little small. The official page lists dine-in and takeaway. If you want to sit down rather than grab and go, this is the store I’d choose.

BurgerFuel Ferrymead, for the eastern suburbs and gluten-free buns

BurgerFuel Ferrymead, at Unit 15, 1005 Ferry Road, serves the eastern suburbs and stays open until 11pm on Friday and Saturday nights. Its official page says it opened in April 2016.

Reviews collected by the gluten-free aggregator atly single out its gluten-free buns. They also mention a relaxed vibe, cars on show out front and plenty of parking nearby, plus delivery and takeaway. Coeliacs should start here. I couldn’t find a verifiable Google rating for Ferrymead.

BurgerFuel Sydenham, the nearest to the central city

BurgerFuel Sydenham, at 290 Colombo Street, is the closest store to the central city now that Hereford Street has closed. It opened on Monday 7 November 2016 as the sixth BurgerFuel in Christchurch, and closes at 11pm on Fridays and Saturdays.

The dining room is compact, with about 30 seats. It holds 4.4/5 on Google (964 reviews) but only 3.8/5 on TripAdvisor, from just 10 reviews. People single out the service, the Bacon BBQ Roadster and the American Muscle Double. The weak spot is customised orders: delays and the odd mistake. Changed a burger? Check your bag before you leave.

Just outside the city: BurgerFuel Rolleston

BurgerFuel also has a restaurant in Rolleston, at 2/4 Rōhutu Way in the Selwyn District, just outside Christchurch. It opened in October 2022 and isn’t counted among the 5 city stores. Handy if you’re heading south on State Highway 1.

BurgerFuel Hereford Street has closed

The BurgerFuel on Hereford Street, in the BNZ Centre in central Christchurch, closed in April 2024. Burger Fuel Group confirmed the closure in its FY24 results on the NZX. In its FY25 results, management was blunt: “This site never recovered from ongoing roadworks, and a new food precinct nearby didn’t help”.

TripAdvisor, Yelp and HappyCow still list the store. Ignore them. From the central city, Sydenham on Colombo Street is the nearest BurgerFuel.

BurgerFuel Christchurch menu and prices in NZ$

BurgerFuel’s menu in Christchurch is the national New Zealand menu: smashed grass-fed beef burgers, free-range chicken, plant-based options and thick-cut fries. The chain promises “100% pure grass fed NZ beef, smashed juicy fresh to order”, free-range chicken, and beetroot pickled in organic cider vinegar and stout.

The official NZ menu page lists these burgers: American Muscle (single and double), BioFuel, T-Bird, Ford Freakout, C N Cheese, Bacon Backfire, Bacon BBQ Roadster and the Bastard. Sides include thick-cut potato fries and Motobites, plant-based crumbed mushroom bites.

What to order at BurgerFuel on a first visit

For a first visit, order the American Muscle: it’s BurgerFuel’s flagship smashed beef burger and the cheapest way in at about NZ$15. Hungrier? The American Muscle Double and the Bacon BBQ Roadster are the two burgers Sydenham reviewers name. Add fries with aioli. At Papanui, add a banana thickshake too.

Vegan, gluten-free and halal options at BurgerFuel Christchurch

BurgerFuel advertises vegan, vegetarian, halal, low-carb and dairy-free options, plus gluten-friendly buns, on its official New Zealand menu. The plant-based Motobites are 1 vegan-friendly side.

Gluten-free buns get specific praise at Ferrymead. Halal is less clear-cut. The brand lists halal options, and Wanderlog’s review summary mentions halal preparation on request at Spitfire Square, but no official source confirms it store by store. If halal matters to you, ring the restaurant before you go.

Ordering, delivery and booking

Every Christchurch store takes online orders through eat.burgerfuel.com and the BurgerFuel app, and the official pages also list catering and the VIB Club loyalty programme. Papanui and Sydenham explicitly list dine-in and takeaway.

Delivery exists, but no official source says which platform serves which store. An unofficial national list mentions Uber Eats and Delivereasy, and atly reports delivery from Ferrymead. Your delivery app will tell you what reaches your address. No official store page mentions table booking.

BurgerFuel doesn’t publish payment methods, wheelchair access or bus routes for its Christchurch stores. Bush Inn and Spitfire Square sit in shopping centres, and third parties describe ample parking at Ferrymead.

BurgerFuel in Christchurch: a short history

Christchurch was the first South Island city to get a BurgerFuel: the Bush Inn restaurant opened in Riccarton on 15 January 2014. BurgerFuel announced it on 2 October 2013, after fans campaigned for it on Facebook. The original franchisees were Stephan Schaefer and Malte Herzhoff.

The opening broke BurgerFuel’s New Zealand record for opening-day sales, as Scoop reported in January 2014. “Local excitement and anticipation around the opening has been huge,” Herzhoff said at the time. In a May 2020 article, BurgerFuel described him as one of its first South Island franchisees, running Spitfire Square and Bush Inn.

Papanui followed in August 2015, Ferrymead in April 2016 and Sydenham in November 2016. Papanui and Sydenham were opened by Cantabrians Daniel and Natasha Chima, who also ran Hereford Street at the time. These owner details are historical; BurgerFuel doesn’t publish its current franchisees.

BurgerFuel, a New Zealand chain

BurgerFuel is a New Zealand burger chain founded by Chris Mason on Ponsonby Road, Auckland, in October 1995. It has been listed on the NZX as BFG since 2007. At 31 March 2026 it had 62 restaurants in New Zealand and 3 in the Middle East, and it posted a record net profit of NZ$1.97 million for the 2026 financial year. BurgerFuel Christchurch FAQ

How many BurgerFuel restaurants are there in Christchurch?

5: Bush Inn (Riccarton), Spitfire Square, Papanui, Ferrymead and Sydenham, according to the official store locator on 5 November 2026. A sixth, in Rolleston, sits just outside the city.

Which BurgerFuel is closest to Christchurch Airport?

Spitfire Square, at Shop 4, 544 Memorial Avenue (corner of Peter Leeming Road). It’s open daily from 10am to 10pm.

How late is BurgerFuel Christchurch open on Friday and Saturday?

Papanui, Ferrymead and Sydenham stay open until 11pm on Fridays and Saturdays. Bush Inn and Spitfire Square close at 10pm every day.

Is the BurgerFuel on Hereford Street still open?

No. The Hereford Street store in the BNZ Centre closed in April 2024. The nearest BurgerFuel to the central city is now Sydenham, at 290 Colombo Street.

What was the first BurgerFuel in Christchurch?

Bush Inn, in Riccarton, opened on 15 January 2014. It was the first BurgerFuel in the South Island and broke the chain’s New Zealand opening-day sales record.

How much does a BurgerFuel burger cost in New Zealand?

About NZ$15.00 for an American Muscle, NZ$21.00 for the double and NZ$20.90 for the Bastard, according to an unofficial price list from 15 July 2026. Check the final price on eat.burgerfuel.com.

Does BurgerFuel Christchurch have vegan, gluten-free or halal burgers?

BurgerFuel advertises vegan, vegetarian, halal, low-carb and dairy-free options and gluten-friendly buns. Halal isn’t confirmed store by store, so call ahead.

Does BurgerFuel Christchurch deliver?

Yes, delivery is reported, but BurgerFuel doesn’t say which platforms serve each Christchurch store. You can also order online for pickup through eat.burgerfuel.com or the app.

Can you book a table at BurgerFuel Christchurch?

None of the official store pages offers table booking. They list dine-in, takeaway, online ordering and catering.

Which BurgerFuel in Christchurch has the best Google rating?

Spitfire Square, with 4.7/5 from 1,567 Google reviews. Papanui and Sydenham both score 4.4/5. I couldn’t verify ratings for Bush Inn and Ferrymead.

Is BurgerFuel a New Zealand chain?

Yes. Chris Mason founded BurgerFuel on Ponsonby Road in Auckland in October 1995, and the company has been listed on the NZX since 2007.
