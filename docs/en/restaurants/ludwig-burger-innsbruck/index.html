Ludwig Burger Innsbruck: Menu, Prices and Hours

Ludwig burger Innsbruck at Museumstraße 3: organic Austrian beef, 2026 menu prices in euros (€11.90–€17.80), opening hours, lunch deal and tram access.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 25, 2026

Ludwig – Das Burger Restaurant at Museumstraße 3, 6020 Innsbruck, is the burger restaurant to book in Innsbruck. The patties are 100% organic Austrian Simmental beef, minced in-house every day, and a beef burger on the regular menu costs €11.90 to €17.80. It opens Monday to Saturday 11:30–22:00 and Sunday 17:00–22:00. Innsbruck Tourism calls it an almost cult address, and it has held that reputation since 2014. If you have time for one burger in Innsbruck, I’d make it this one. I checked prices and hours on Ludwig’s official website on 17 November 2026.

Looking for Rinderwahn in Innsbruck? There isn’t one. Rinderwahn’s official website lists two addresses, both in Vienna: Weihburggasse 3 (1st district) and Naschmarkt 1 (6th district). Heading to the capital? See our guide to the best burgers in Vienna. In Tyrol, Ludwig is the burger restaurant to look for.

Ludwig Innsbruck opening hours

Ludwig Innsbruck opens Monday to Saturday from 11:30 to 22:00 and on Sunday from 17:00 to 22:00, so there is no Sunday lunch. Those are the hours on the official website.

Tourist listings such as tirol.at and tyrol.com still show 11:00–22:00 on weekdays. I trust the restaurant’s own site over a tourism database. For an early lunch, though, a quick call to +43 512 319222 settles it.

Where Ludwig is and how to get there by tram

Ludwig is at Museumstraße 3 in central Innsbruck, a short walk from the old town. The closest stop is the IVB tram stop Museumstraße, on line 2. Line 1 also stops there on its Marktplatz–Museumstraße section.

I found no verified information on parking at the restaurant. Driving in? Leave the car in one of the public car parks in the centre and walk.

On paper, my pick is the Ludwig. Brisket smoked for 16 hours over applewood, on a patty minced that morning: no global chain puts that on a bun. It’s also the dearest burger on the regular menu, and still the one I’d order first.

Chicken burgers

Ludwig serves two chicken burgers, both made with Tyrolean organic chicken: the Crispy Chicken at €14.90 and the BBQ Chicken at €15.90. The bird is a Tiroler Bio-Alpenhendl. Ludwig uses the thigh meat, marinated in organic buttermilk, and it comes from the Stipplerhofl organic mountain farm in the Ötztal valley.

Extras: gluten-free bun, low carb and a double patty

Any burger can be adapted. A gluten-free bun costs €2.00 extra, and the low-carb option (lettuce instead of the bun) is €1.50. An extra 160 g patty is €5.80. Organic bacon, an organic egg, avocado or guacamole are €2.00 each.

You can also swap in a 120 g Bio-Angus extra old patty on any burger. It is wet-aged organic beef from Goha’s in Vienna.

Drinks and desserts

Ludwig pours organic and regional drinks, not the usual soft-drink lineup. A 0.5 l milkshake (vanilla, chocolate or mango) is €7.20, and the homemade lemonade (0.4 l) is €4.80. Beer drinkers get a 0.5 l organic Trumer Original for €5.50, or cans from the Tyrolean craft brewery Bierol, such as The Padawan at €6.80. An espresso made with organic Dinzler coffee costs €2.40.

Dessert is short: a warm brownie at €6.50 or a lemon cheesecake at €5.90.

The Ludwig lunch deal

Ludwig’s lunch deal is a burger and fries for €14.90, Monday to Friday from 11:00 to 15:00, except bank holidays. You choose the Cheese, a veggie burger or the Crispy Chicken. Swapping the fries for handmade fries, sweet potato fries or a small salad costs €1.00 more.

Take the Cheese. With fries it would cost €18.80 à la carte (€13.90 + €4.90), so the deal saves €3.90. One catch: the menu says the deal starts at 11:00, but the restaurant’s official opening time is 11:30. Arrive after 11:30.

Is Ludwig an organic burger restaurant?

Yes, largely. Ludwig is a BIO AUSTRIA partner at Bronze level, which is a partial organic certification, and its stated philosophy is “organic and regional where it makes sense” (BIO AUSTRIA, 1 December 2025).

The menu lists the beef, chicken, bacon, the potatoes for the handmade fries, the Bergkäse cheese, the milk and the eggs as organic. It also names the butcher, the chicken farm and the baker. Most burger menus name none of them.

Where Ludwig Innsbruck’s ingredients come from, on a map of Austria: organic beef from the Sonnberg butcher in Unterweißenbach (Upper Austria), organic chicken from the Stipplerhofl farm in the Ötztal valley (Tyrol), Bio-Angus extra old from Goha’s in Vienna, and buns from the Kröll bakery in Innsbruck (marker). Where Ludwig’s beef comes from

Ludwig’s beef is 100% organic Simmental from small-scale organic farms in Austria, supplied by the Sonnberg organic butcher in Unterweißenbach, Upper Austria. It is minced on site every day, never frozen, and grilled over a flame.

Some tourist listings call the beef “Tyrolean”. The official menu names an Upper Austrian butcher, so “Austrian organic beef” is the accurate description.

The buns: baked daily by Kröll in the old town

The brioche and potato buns are baked fresh every day. For Innsbruck, the menu names Kröll, a bakery in the old town. On a menu shared across three cities, it’s the one truly local touch. Want to bake a brioche bun at home? Our homemade burger buns recipe walks through it step by step.

Is the beef at Ludwig halal?

Only the Bio-Angus extra old patty is labelled halal on the menu: the Goha’s beef is certified both BIO AUSTRIA and halal. The menu says nothing about the standard beef, so if halal matters to you, order the Bio-Angus patty and confirm with the staff. They can also answer allergen questions.

The history of Ludwig: a small Austrian chain born in Innsbruck

Ludwig was founded in Innsbruck in 2014 by Angela and Erich Hauser, who had moved back to Tyrol from London, according to BIO AUSTRIA. Museumstraße 3 was the first restaurant.

The chain now has three restaurants: Innsbruck, Salzburg (Linzergasse 39) and the SCS shopping centre in Vösendorf, south of Vienna. I covered the Salzburg branch in our review of Ludwig burger in Salzburg.

Innsbruck Tourism’s blog calls Ludwig an almost “cult” burger address, popular with families and vegetarians, with a kids’ menu and plenty of meat-free burgers (innsbruck.info). Tirol Werbung describes a modern loft-style room with a relaxed atmosphere and a menu in several languages (tyrol.com).

In August 2018, news.at ranked Ludwig Innsbruck first among Austria’s best burger restaurants, based on TripAdvisor reviews (news.at, 7 August 2018). The ranking is old. What it shows is how long the reputation has lasted.

Ludwig Innsbruck reviews: what diners say

Reviews of Ludwig Innsbruck praise the burgers and the sides and complain mostly about price and portion size. That’s the pattern across the TripAdvisor review excerpts I read, many of them several years old.

  • Praised: the burgers, the sweet potato fries, the homemade ketchup, the milkshakes and the lemonade.
  • Criticised: prices seen as high, and portions some diners found small.

You won’t find a star rating or a review count here. I couldn’t check TripAdvisor and Google directly when writing this guide, and I only print ratings I can date and verify. On price, the critics have a point: €17.80 for the house burger plus €6.80 for handmade fries comes to €24.60 before a drink. For organic beef minced that morning in a city-centre restaurant, I think that’s fair. Still, the lunch deal is the smarter way in.

Ludwig Innsbruck FAQ

Does Rinderwahn have a restaurant in Innsbruck? No. Rinderwahn has only two restaurants, both in Vienna: Weihburggasse 3 and Naschmarkt 1. Its official website lists no address in Tyrol.

Where is Ludwig in Innsbruck? Ludwig is at Museumstraße 3, 6020 Innsbruck. Take the tram to the Museumstraße stop (line 2, or line 1 on the Marktplatz–Museumstraße section).

Is Ludwig Innsbruck open on Sunday lunchtime? No. On Sundays Ludwig opens at 17:00 and closes at 22:00. Monday to Saturday it is open 11:30–22:00.

How much does a burger cost at Ludwig Innsbruck? Beef burgers on the regular menu cost €11.90 (Classic) to €17.80 (Ludwig), and the current Double Cheese XO special is €17.90. Chicken burgers are €14.90–€15.90, meat-free burgers €12.90–€13.90. Prices checked on 17 November 2026.

Does Ludwig have vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free burgers? Yes. There are two vegetarian burgers (Brokkoli & Käse, Halloumi), one vegan burger (Süßkartoffel) and a vegan special (Smashing Pumpkin). A gluten-free bun costs €2.00 extra.

Is Ludwig’s beef organic and halal? The beef is 100% organic Austrian Simmental from the Sonnberg butcher in Upper Austria. Only the optional Bio-Angus extra old patty is labelled halal on the menu.

Do I need to book at Ludwig Innsbruck? Can I get delivery? You can book a table online on ludwig-burger.at or by email at book.ibk@ludwig-burger.at. Delivery is available through Foodora and Wolt.

Is there a lunch menu at Ludwig Innsbruck? Yes. A burger and fries cost €14.90, Monday to Friday 11:00–15:00, except bank holidays. The restaurant officially opens at 11:30.

Is Ludwig Innsbruck good for kids? Yes. Innsbruck Tourism describes it as popular with families and mentions a kids’ menu.

Which is the best burger in Innsbruck? For organic beef, Ludwig. For a fully vegan burger, Swing Kitchen in the old town. Le Burger at the DEZ shopping centre is the chain alternative.
