Mostaza Buenos Aires: Locations, Prices, Hours

Which Mostaza to pick in Buenos Aires: Obelisco late nights, Microcentro lunch, Belgrano hours, plus 2026 combo prices in ARS and an honest read of reviews.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished November 27, 2026

Mostaza is Argentina’s own burger chain and the country’s number two in fast food after McDonald’s. It had more than 40 branches inside Buenos Aires city limits (CABA) in July 2026, so the red-and-yellow sign is hard to miss. Three of them are the useful ones for most visitors: Mostaza Express at Avenida Corrientes 970 near the Obelisco for a late bite, Mostaza Diagonal at Avenida Roque Sáenz Peña 572 in the Microcentro, and Mostaza Cabildo at Avenida Cabildo 2150 in Belgrano. On October 9, 2026, a combo cost ARS 18,500 to 23,500 on Rappi. Google reviews put all three at around 4 out of 5.

I wrote this guide from the chain’s official site, Argentine business press and review aggregators, and I checked every source on November 15, 2026. Where sources contradict each other, or rest on a single aggregator, I say so. Anything I could not confirm is out, including phone numbers and 24-hour claims. For the chain’s full story, from a first food court in 1998 to more than 200 branches, see our history of the Mostaza chain.

Mostaza Buenos Aires at a glance: key facts

Mostaza is an Argentine fast-food chain founded in 1998 in Greater Buenos Aires. It sells burgers, sandwiches, desserts and coffee in Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay and Bolivia.

Which Mostaza to choose in Buenos Aires

Pick Mostaza Express on Corrientes for late nights near the theaters, Mostaza Diagonal for a quick lunch in the Microcentro, and Mostaza Cabildo if you are in Belgrano and want the fullest published hours. Traveling with kids or a group? Head for the newer large branches in Constitución, Villa del Parque and Flores: more seats, and self-order kiosks.

Mostaza Express, Avenida Corrientes 970: late-night food near the Obelisco

Mostaza Express at Avenida Corrientes 970 in San Nicolás is the closest branch to the Obelisco and the one to know after a show on Corrientes. Some aggregators file it under Recoleta. They are wrong: it sits in San Nicolás, a few steps from the 9 de Julio.

Aerial night view looking straight down on the Obelisco, where Avenida Corrientes crosses Avenida 9 de Julio (October 2025). Mostaza Express at Corrientes 970 is a few steps away. (Photo: ProtoplasmaKid, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vista\_a%C3%A9rea\_nocturna\_de\_Avenida\_9\_de\_julio\_y\_Avenida\_Corrientes\_08.jpg)

On Google it holds 4.0/5 from 4,070 reviews. Tripadvisor gives 3.5/5 from 27 reviews, and Restaurant Guru 4.1/5 from 2,727 reviews (figures via Sluurpy). It offers dine-in, takeout and delivery, serves alcohol and is listed as wheelchair accessible.

Reviewers keep praising the speed. They also mention a cramped dining room and a security guard at the door at night.

Is it open 24 hours? I could not confirm it. Sluurpy lists 7 a.m. to 3 a.m. on most days, round-the-clock opening on Thursday and Friday, and a midnight close on Wednesday. Those are aggregator hours, so check Google Maps or the Mostaza App before you walk over at 2 a.m.

Mostaza Diagonal, Avenida Roque Sáenz Peña 572: the Microcentro workhorse

Mostaza Diagonal, at Avenida Roque Sáenz Peña 572 on Diagonal Norte (C1016), is the most reviewed downtown branch and the easiest lunch stop between the Plaza de Mayo and the Obelisco. It holds 3.9/5 on Google from 9,430 reviews and 4.0/5 on Tripadvisor from 392 reviews, according to Wanderlog.

Avenida Roque Sáenz Peña (Diagonal Norte) in the Microcentro, seen from the Bencich building (2016). Mostaza Diagonal is at number 572 on this avenue. (Photo: Prensargenta, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Diagonal\_Norte\_al\_Obelisco.jpg)

Regulars praise well-salted fries, a clean dining room and friendly staff. The complaints repeat too: burgers that feel generic, wrong orders, and meat quality that varies from branch to branch. Wanderlog also lists a vegan option here, the “Vegano Sin Carne”.

Its listed opening hours don’t add up. Wanderlog shows a 6:30 p.m. opening on weekdays, almost certainly a typo for 6:30 a.m., and I am not publishing them until the chain confirms. Check the official locator first.

Mostaza Cabildo, Avenida Cabildo 2150: Belgrano’s busiest branch

Mostaza Cabildo, at Avenida Cabildo 2150 near Juramento in Belgrano, has the most Google reviews of the three: 4.0/5 from 14,338. It is also the only one with full weekly hours listed, by Restaurant Guru.

That spend range already looks dated: the cheapest Rappi combo below costs ARS 18,500. One aggregator, so treat all of it as a guide, not a promise.

The new large-format branches: Constitución, Villa del Parque and Flores

Mostaza’s newest CABA branches are bigger, with more than 100 seats, self-order kiosks and separate dessert counters. For families and groups, they beat the cramped downtown branches.

  • Constitución, corner of Brasil and Lima, facing Plaza Constitución. Opened in December 2024 as the chain’s 42nd branch in CABA and 192nd in Argentina. It has 132 seats, a coffee island and a street-facing dessert window, in an area that sees more than 600,000 people a day (Infogastronómica, December 20, 2024).
  • Villa del Parque, Cuenca 3070. Opened on July 15, 2025, as a franchise: 235 m², 110 seats, 4 ordering kiosks and 40 staff (Mercado).
  • Flores, Avenida Rivadavia 7299 at General José Gervasio Artigas. More than 100 seats, 35 jobs, ordering kiosks and a standalone dessert kiosk. It brought the chain to 217 branches (iProfesional, July 16, 2026).

The menu is built around the Mega burgers, sold alone or as combos. For a first visit I would order the Mega Cuarto combo. It is the cheapest on the list, and going double costs only ARS 4,000 to 5,000 more.

PriceListo, a price aggregator, lists 160 items from ARS 2,024.62 in its “menú 2026”. Its collection date is unknown, so read these as rough guides only:

  • Sundae: ARS 3,200

Ignore the US dollar menus on American price sites. Some list Mostaza burgers at USD 12 to 16, and those figures are wrong. To compare a Mostaza combo with a Big Mac in Argentina, see our Big Mac Index 2026 by country.

Limited editions: La Cuarta burgers

On May 11, 2026, Mostaza launched two La Cuarta burgers for its pre-World Cup campaign, “Hambre de la Cuarta”. Both come on a brioche bun. The patty blends two Argentine cuts: roast beef, which in Argentina means a cut from the chuck, and tapa de asado, the rib cap. The slogan: “Carne de verdad, 100% argentina” (Mercado).

  • La Cuarta Provolone y Bacon: crispy bacon, chimichurri, grilled onions and provolone.
  • La Cuarta Ahumada: smoky sauce and smoked cheese.

Chimichurri and grilled provolone are parrilla staples. That makes the first one the most Argentine burger I know of on a chain menu. Both were limited editions, though, and I could not confirm whether CABA branches still sell them. The same goes for two earlier limited editions, the Mega Dibu and the Mega Homo Argentum. Check the app.

Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options

Mostaza’s meat-free range is the Mega NotBurger. Its pea-protein patty is made with Chilean food-tech company NotCo, and it launched in more than 150 branches in August 2021 (Vegconomist). The versions include Deluxe, Classic, Cuarto and Vegan, the last on a vegan bun.

For gluten-free (sin TACC) eating, directories such as Find Me Gluten Free report a dedicated gluten-free menu, including at the Cabildo branch. I found no official certification, so do not count on a certified gluten-free kitchen. If you have celiac disease, ask staff about cross-contact before you order.

Mostaza Buenos Aires reviews: is it better than McDonald’s?

The three branches in this guide score around 4 out of 5 on Google, and the verdict is fair fast food rather than a destination. Wanderlog reviewers often rate it above McDonald’s. My take: the fries and the Argentine limited editions are the reasons to choose it. The core burgers are not.

Ordering, delivery and payment at Mostaza

You can order at the counter, at self-order kiosks in the newer branches, in the Mostaza App (iOS and Android), or for delivery through Rappi and PedidosYa. The official site also runs MostaClub, a loyalty program, and lists bank promotions (Promos Bancarias).

Mostaza is fast food, so there are no reservations. Restaurant Guru lists credit cards at the Cabildo branch, but I found no chain-wide policy on foreign cards. Carry some pesos as a backup. Aggregators also report security guards at most Greater Buenos Aires branches.

Frequently asked questions about Mostaza Buenos Aires

Where is the closest Mostaza to the Obelisco, and is it open 24 hours? The closest is Mostaza Express at Avenida Corrientes 970, a few steps from the Obelisco. Sluurpy lists it open until 3 a.m. most days and round the clock on Thursday and Friday, but 24-hour opening is not confirmed. Check Google Maps or the app.

How much is a Mostaza combo in Buenos Aires in 2026? A combo cost ARS 18,500 (Mega Cuarto) to ARS 23,500 (Doble Mega Cuarto a Fondo) on Rappi Argentina on October 9, 2026. Branch prices can differ, and they change quickly with inflation.

How many Mostaza branches are there in Buenos Aires? More than 40 inside CABA as of July 2026, out of 217 nationwide. The exact city count varies by source: the chain announced about 45 in July 2025.

Is Mostaza better than McDonald’s in Argentina? Many Wanderlog reviewers think so, mainly for the fries. Reviews also flag generic burgers and uneven quality between branches, so expect solid fast food, not a gourmet burger.

Does Mostaza have gluten-free and vegetarian options? Yes. The Mega NotBurger range is meat-free, and one version comes on a vegan bun. Gluten-free directories report a sin TACC menu, but I found no official certification.

Can I get Mostaza delivered in Buenos Aires? Yes, through Rappi, PedidosYa and the Mostaza App, depending on the branch.

Can I pay with a foreign card? Credit cards are listed at the Cabildo branch. No chain-wide policy on foreign cards is published, so keep pesos on hand.

What are Cajita Mostaza and MostaClub? Cajita Mostaza is the chain’s boxed meal, listed at ARS 13,900 to 14,500 by PriceListo (collection date unknown). MostaClub is the loyalty program on the official site and app.

Which limited-edition burgers does Mostaza sell right now? The latest I could confirm are the La Cuarta Provolone y Bacon and La Cuarta Ahumada, launched on May 11, 2026. Check the app for what CABA branches serve today.
