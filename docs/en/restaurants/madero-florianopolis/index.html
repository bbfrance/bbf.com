Madero Florianópolis: Addresses, Hours and Prices

Madero's 3 Florianópolis restaurants compared: Beiramar, Villa Romana and Container, with addresses, hours, ratings and the R$54 Cheeseburger.

By Lucas, Restaurants writer, BrazilPublished October 11, 2026

Madero runs 3 burger restaurants in Florianópolis: a Steak House in Beiramar Shopping (Centro), a Steak House in Villa Romana Shopping (Santa Mônica) and the Container Primavera Garden on the SC-401 in Saco Grande. First visit? Go to the Beiramar. It is the most reviewed of the 3, with 4.4/5 on Google from 4,758 reviews, and it opens at 11:45am every day. Driving to the northern beaches? Stop at the Container instead. It is cheaper and quicker. Wherever you sit, budget about R$54 for the signature Cheeseburger, Madero’s national reference price.

1 warning about search results: this guide covers the burger chain, not the Hotel Porto Madero in Canasvieiras. On 11 October 2026 I checked every address, phone number and opening hour against Madero’s official restaurant pages, and took ratings from Restaurant Guru and TripAdvisor the same day. This is a desk check, not a visit report. Where a figure does not come from Florianópolis itself, such as the burger price, I say so and name the source.

Map of the 3 Madero restaurants in Florianópolis: Beiramar Shopping (R. Bocaiúva, 2468, Centro), Villa Romana (Av. Madre Benvenuta, 687, Santa Mônica) and Container Primavera Garden (SC-401, km 4 Norte, Saco Grande, on the road to the northern beaches) (Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL)

Which Madero in Florianópolis should you choose?

Pick the Beiramar for the full Madero experience in central Floripa, the Container Primavera for a fast, cheaper burger on the road north, and the Villa Romana if you want the newest dining room or the Italian Legno menu.

Madero Steak House Beiramar Shopping (Centro)

Madero Beiramar is the one to choose in Florianópolis. It is the most reviewed location, ranked #113 of 1,955 restaurants in the city on TripAdvisor, and the easiest to reach from downtown. You will find it on the Sambaqui floor of Beiramar Shopping, a mall of about 210 stores with covered parking.

  • Address: R. Bocaiúva, 2468, Centro, Florianópolis – SC, 88015-902
  • Phone: (48) 3112-0766
  • Dine-in and takeout: Sunday to Thursday 11:45am–10:30pm; Friday and Saturday 11:45am–11pm
  • Delivery: every day 11am–9:45pm
  • Services: dine-in, takeout, delivery, reservations
  • Signature dishes on the official page: Cheeseburger, L’Entrecôte, Petit Gâteau de Doce de Leite

Source: official Madero Beiramar page, checked 11 October 2026.

TripAdvisor still lists this location under its old name, “Madero Burger & Grill”. Same restaurant. Restaurant Guru also shows 8.1/10 on Foursquare from 778 ratings. Most reviews praise the modern room and the burgers. The one recent complaint I read was about the service, not the food.

Madero Steak House Villa Romana (Santa Mônica)

Madero Villa Romana is the newest Madero in Florianópolis. It opened on 19 August 2023 as the chain’s third location in the city, and it is the only one that also serves the Legno Ristorante Italiano pasta menu. It sits inside Villa Romana Shopping, the mall formerly known as Shopping Iguatemi Florianópolis. An old guide sending you to “Madero Iguatemi” means this one.

  • Address: Av. Madre Benvenuta, 687, Santa Mônica, Florianópolis – SC, 88035-000
  • Phone: (48) 3112-1991
  • Dine-in: Sunday to Thursday 11:45am–10pm; Friday and Saturday 11:45am–11pm
  • Delivery: Sunday to Thursday 11:45am–9:45pm; Friday and Saturday 11:45am–10:30pm
  • Services: dine-in, takeout, delivery, reservations

Source: official Madero Villa Romana page, checked 11 October 2026.

Architect Kethlen Ribas Durski designed the dining room. As at every Madero, the food comes from the group’s central kitchen in Ponta Grossa, Paraná (Acontecendo Aqui, 15 August 2023). I found no Google or TripAdvisor rating for this location, so I give none. 1 practical point: go by the restaurant’s hours, not the mall’s. The food court keeps different times.

Madero Container Primavera Garden (Saco Grande, SC-401)

Madero Container Primavera is the casual, lower-priced Madero in Florianópolis, open every day from 11:30am to 11pm on the SC-401 highway toward the northern beaches. It opened in August 2015 next to Primavera Garden, in a 2-level container building of about 240 m² (2,580 sq ft) with 85 seats (Acontecendo Aqui, August 2015). Some local sources call the road Rodovia José Carlos Daux. It is the same road.

  • Address: SC-401, km 4 Norte, Saco Grande, Florianópolis – SC, 88032-005
  • Phone: (48) 3371-7667
  • Dine-in and takeout: every day 11:30am–11pm
  • Delivery: every day 11:30am–10:30pm
  • Reservations: not mentioned on the official page
  • Featured on the official page: the Madero Burger and brigadeiro de colher (spoonable brigadeiro)

Source: official Madero Container Primavera page, checked 11 October 2026. The 11am–10pm hours in the 2015 opening coverage are out of date.

Restaurant Guru shows 4.4/5 on Google from 2,419 reviews, 4/5 on TripAdvisor from 78 reviews and 8.2/10 on Foursquare. Reviewers mention a calm room, friendly staff and an easy stop on the way north. 2 orders keep coming up: the Duplo Bacon and the Italian soda. There is terrace seating.

Madero Florianópolis menu: what to order

The Madero Florianópolis menu is built around the Cheeseburger: a 180 g (6.3 oz) beef patty, organic lettuce and tomato, cheddar-style cheese and house-made mayonnaise. The Steak House locations add steaks such as L’Entrecôte. The Villa Romana adds Italian pasta from the Legno menu.

Madero publishes a PDF menu for each format (Steakhouse, Container, Prime, Madero + Jeronimo), in Portuguese and English, with a nutrition and allergen table. Ask for the cardápio if you are ordering in Portuguese. The national menu has a vegetarian and vegan section, but I could not confirm the name or price of the meat-free burger served in Florianópolis. Check the official Madero menus before you go.

For dessert, the Beiramar page features the Petit Gâteau de Doce de Leite, and the Container page features the brigadeiro de colher.

Madero Florianópolis prices

Budget about R$54 for a Madero Cheeseburger. Per person, count R$40–60 at the Container or R$60–140 at the Beiramar. The R$54 figure is Madero’s national reference price, not one I recorded on a Florianópolis menu.

In March 2026, a Forbes Brasil ranking built with the Brazilian Franchising Association (ABF) named the Madero Cheeseburger the most expensive chain burger in Brazil, at R$54 (Forbes Brasil, March 2026). That matches the price in our Madero Curitiba guide. In Fortaleza, our Madero Fortaleza guide found R$57 on Rappi, so expect delivery to cost a little more. The per-person ranges come from Restaurant Guru, checked 11 October 2026.

Madero Florianópolis reviews: is it worth it?

Yes, if you want a dependable premium burger in a comfortable room. Both rated locations hold 4.4/5 on Google from thousands of reviews, and the Beiramar sits in the top 6% of Florianópolis restaurants on TripAdvisor. The catch is the bill: no chain in Brazil charges more for its cheeseburger.

My take: Madero is the safe pick, not the adventurous one. Every restaurant is supplied by the same central kitchen in Ponta Grossa, so a Cheeseburger in Floripa should taste like 1 in Curitiba. That is the point, and also the limit. At R$54 you are paying for consistency and a nice room, not for anything you can only eat on the island. With a week in Florianópolis, I would eat at the Container once, on a beach day, and spend the other burger nights at independent hamburguerias. We add them to our Florianópolis burger hub as we verify them.

How to get there: parking and access

Both Steak House locations are inside malls with parking, and the Container sits on the SC-401 highway. A car or a ride-hailing app is the easiest way in. Beiramar Shopping has covered parking (NSC Total). Villa Romana Shopping announces 1,200 parking spaces, a figure reported by QuintoAndar.

Madero’s official pages say nothing about parking or wheelchair access. Restaurant Guru lists both for the Beiramar and the Container, but those listings are self-reported and I could not verify them. Nor could I verify bus lines, so I list none.

Common mix-ups: Balneário Camboriú, Hotel Porto Madero and Iguatemi

3 mix-ups keep showing up in online listings for Madero Florianópolis.

  • Av. Atlântica, 3180 is not in Florianópolis. That Madero, phone (47) 3367-8009, is in Balneário Camboriú, about 80 km (50 miles) north. Some guides, including Guia da Semana, file it under Florianópolis.
  • Hotel Porto Madero is a hotel in Canasvieiras. It has no link to the burger chain.
  • Madero Iguatemi is now Madero Villa Romana. The mall was renamed; the Madero there is the Villa Romana location.

What is Madero? The Brazilian premium burger chain

Madero is a Brazilian premium burger and steakhouse chain founded by chef Junior Durski, who opened the first restaurant in Curitiba on 7 June 2005. As of 31 March 2026, Grupo Madero ran 355 restaurants under the Madero Steak House, Madero Burger, Jeronimo and Madero & Jeronimo brands (Grupo Madero investor relations).

Downtown Curitiba at golden hour (June 2026). Chef Junior Durski opened the first Madero here on 7 June 2005, some 300 km by road north of Florianópolis. (Photo: Wilfredor (Wikimedia Commons, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Curitiba\_Skyline\_2.jpg), CC0)

In 2025 the group reported gross revenue of R$2.3 billion and net income of R$103.2 million (NeoFeed). For the full story, read our guide to Madero in Curitiba, where the chain was born. For more burger cities in Brazil, see our best burgers in São Paulo and the Burger Breton restaurants hub.

Madero Florianópolis FAQ

How many Madero restaurants are there in Florianópolis?

3, according to Madero’s official site on 11 October 2026: Madero Steak House Beiramar Shopping (Centro), Madero Steak House Villa Romana (Santa Mônica) and Madero Container Primavera Garden (Saco Grande).

What are Madero’s opening hours in Florianópolis?

The Beiramar is open 11:45am–10:30pm Sunday to Thursday and 11:45am–11pm Friday and Saturday. The Villa Romana is open 11:45am–10pm Sunday to Thursday and 11:45am–11pm Friday and Saturday. The Container Primavera is open every day 11:30am–11pm.

How much does a burger cost at Madero in Florianópolis?

Madero’s Cheeseburger has a national reference price of R$54 (Forbes Brasil, March 2026). I found no price recorded on a Florianópolis menu, so check the official menu or your delivery app before ordering.

Can you book a table at Madero in Florianópolis?

Yes, at the Beiramar and the Villa Romana: both official pages list reservations. The Container Primavera page does not mention reservations either way.

Does Madero deliver in Florianópolis?

Yes. All 3 official pages list delivery. Delivery runs from 11am to 9:45pm at the Beiramar, until 9:45pm or 10:30pm at the Villa Romana depending on the day, and until 10:30pm at the Container.

Does Madero have vegetarian or vegan options?

Madero’s national menu includes a vegetarian and vegan section. I could not confirm which meat-free burger is served in Florianópolis, so check the official menu.

What is the difference between Madero Steak House and Madero Container?

The Steak House is the full restaurant format, with steaks such as L’Entrecôte alongside burgers and reservations available. The Container is a smaller, casual format: in Floripa, a 2-level building with 85 seats and a terrace, at R$40–60 per person according to Restaurant Guru.

Is the Madero on Avenida Atlântica in Florianópolis?

No. The Madero at Av. Atlântica, 3180 is in Balneário Camboriú, about 80 km (50 miles) north of Florianópolis.

Is there parking at Madero in Florianópolis?

The Beiramar and Villa Romana locations are inside malls with parking: covered parking at Beiramar Shopping, and 1,200 spaces announced by Villa Romana Shopping. Madero’s official pages say nothing about parking.
