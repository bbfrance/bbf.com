MOS Burger Sapporo: Best Branch, Menu Prices, Hours and Reviews

MOS Burger Sapporo guide: the Yonbangai branch near Susukino, July 2026 yen prices (¥500 MOS Burger), what to order, hours, payment and reviews.

By AlexPublished October 10, 2026

Yes, MOS Burger is in Sapporo, and the branch to head for is MOS Burger Sapporo Yonbangai (モスバーガー札幌四番街店) at Minami 3-jō Nishi 4-chōme 1, Chūō-ku. It is about a minute’s walk from both Tanukikōji and Susukino stations. Since the nationwide price rise of July 15, 2026, a classic MOS Burger costs ¥500 and a Teriyaki Burger ¥490, tax included. The city has at least a dozen MOS addresses, from Hokkaidō University to the Sapporo Dome area, so one is rarely far away.

This guide is built from checked listings, not from a tasting visit. I pulled MOS Burger’s own store data as published on NAVITIME and cross-checked it against Japanese directories (Hot Pepper, Retty, Jalan, Mapion), review aggregators and the Japanese business press. I verified every fact on October 8, 2026. When two sources disagree, I show you both. A tidy number I made up would be worse than no number.

MOS Burger Susukino: the Yonbangai branch in detail

The Yonbangai branch is the MOS Burger Susukino travelers are looking for. It sits between the Tanukikōji shopping arcade and the Susukino nightlife district. I pick it over the other Sapporo branches for its location. If you are staying in central Sapporo, you can walk there, and its three floors of seating give you the best odds of a free table when the arcade is busy.

Address and how to get there

Show this address to a taxi driver: 北海道札幌市中央区南3条西4丁目1. By subway, get off at Susukino on the Namboku line and walk about 96 m, or walk about 64 m from Tanukikōji station. There is no parking and no drive-through. Come on foot.

One detail I could not confirm: Retty places the store on the first floor of the Silver Building (シルバービル), but no second source repeats it. Look for the MOS sign, not the building name.

MOS Burger Sapporo opening hours

There is no single reliable set of hours for the Yonbangai branch. Four listings, three answers. NAVITIME, which publishes data supplied by MOS, and Hot Pepper both say 9:00 to 22:00 every day. Retty says 9:00 to 1:00 the next morning every day. Jalan says the store runs 24 hours from Tuesday to Saturday.

My advice: count on 9:00 to 22:00, the narrowest window, which also comes from MOS’s own data. Before a late-night visit, check the official MOS store locator at mos.jp/shop or the store’s Google listing. Hours are subject to change and were checked on October 8, 2026.

Delivery, takeout and payment

Yonbangai offers takeout and delivery, and its delivery is listed on Demae-can (出前館), one of Japan’s largest food delivery platforms. I could not load its Demae-can store page on October 8, 2026, and I could not verify MOS online ordering, Uber Eats or Wolt for this branch.

Payment is unclear, so carry some yen in cash. NAVITIME says credit cards are not accepted, while Retty mentions PayPay, a Japanese QR payment app. MOS has been rolling out card and cashless payment across its network, but no official listing I found confirms foreign cards at this branch. Wheelchair access and Wi-Fi are also unverified.

What to order at MOS Burger

The MOS Burger is the chain’s signature, and the Teriyaki Burger is the most Japanese thing on the standard menu. Both are made to order, which is what MOS is built on. If I could order only one, it would be the Teriyaki: it is ¥10 cheaper than the MOS Burger since July, and it gives you a flavor most Western chains do not have. For a side, MOS Chicken costs ¥340. The corn soup is the better deal: still ¥380, its price before the July rise.

Retty reviewers at the Yonbangai branch mention triple cheese burgers, the low-carb menu, spicy limited-time burgers and milkshakes. To cook the teriyaki at home after your trip, follow our MOS Burger teriyaki burger recipe step by step.

MOS Burger rice burger

A rice burger replaces the bun with two pressed patties of rice. MOS launched its first one, the Tsukune Rice Burger, in 1987. Travelers rarely find anything like it at home. That is reason enough to order one, even before the MOS Burger. Its price after July 2026 is not verified.

A MOS rice burger, with two pressed rice patties instead of a bun. MOS invented the format in 1987 (2011 Kaisen Kakiage edition) (Photo: 221.20, public domain (Wikimedia Commons, 1422×1314))

MOS Burger vegetarian and plant-based options

MOS has a plant-based line built around the MOS PLANT-BASED GREEN BURGER, which contains no animal ingredients and none of the five gokun alliums (the pungent vegetables avoided in some Buddhist diets). It launched on March 26, 2020, at ¥538. A teriyaki version with a soy patty followed on September 22, 2021, at ¥580, according to MOS press releases.

Those are launch prices, not today’s prices. I could not confirm that either burger is on the menu in Sapporo in October 2026, so ask at the counter or check mos.jp before you go.

Hokkaidō sourcing and limited items

MOS uses Hokkaidō produce for regional limited menus, and the clearest recent example is the Hokkaidō Tomato Festival. From July 17 to 26, 2026, it sold two limited burgers at ¥920 each: the Tobikiri Ōmori Tomato Mos Vegetable Cheese Burger and the Tobikiri Ōmori Tomato Chili Burger.

The fresh tomatoes came from four Hokkaidō suppliers: Mos Farm Mukawa, JA Date, Air Water Chitose farm and JA Biratori, according to ASCII. That festival is over. I found no Hokkaidō-limited item confirmed for October 2026, so if you come across a regional special, consider it a lucky find.

MOS Burger Sapporo reviews

Reviews of MOS Burger in Sapporo are positive, but there are few of them for the central branch. On Retty, the Yonbangai branch has 6 reviews and an 81% recommendation rate (page updated April 7, 2026). That is too small a sample to mean much. I found no Google or Tabelog rating for it that I could verify, so I am not publishing one.

The best-reviewed branch I found is Toyohira: 3.8/5 on Google from 569 reviews and 4.5/5 on Tripadvisor from 8 reviews, as shown by the Wanderlog aggregator. Reviewers praise the staff, who are welcoming to foreign visitors too, a recently renovated dining room, burgers that look like the menu photos and meals around ¥1,000.

My method: I report a rating only with its platform, review count and check date, and I never give one branch’s score to another.

MOS Burger vs McDonald’s in Japan

MOS Burger differs from McDonald’s in two ways: it cooks burgers to order instead of preparing them ahead, and it is a Japanese chain built on Japanese flavors. You see it on the menu: teriyaki, rice burgers, Hokkaidō tomatoes in summer. The wait is a little longer, since your burger is assembled after you order. My view: if you only have room for one fast-food meal in Sapporo, make it MOS. The rice burger alone is worth the trip.

A short history of MOS Burger

Atsushi Sakurada founded MOS Burger. The first, experimental store opened in 1972 in front of Narimasu station on the Tōbu Tōjō line, in Itabashi, Tokyo, and MOS Food Services was incorporated in July 1972. The chain expanded abroad from 1991, starting with Taiwan, then Singapore, Hong Kong, Thailand, South Korea and the Philippines.

Stock-market data sites, checked on October 8, 2026, put the network at about 1,310 restaurants in Japan and 411 abroad. Hokkaidō alone had 49 MOS Burger restaurants in July 2026, special outlets excluded, according to the chain’s Tomato Festival announcement. For where MOS fits in the wider story, read our history of the hamburger.

Practical tips for your visit

Here is how I would plan a MOS Burger stop in Sapporo:

  • Go to Yonbangai if you are in Susukino or Tanukikōji, and to Ōdōri Nishi 14-chōme if you are at the west end of Ōdōri Park.
  • Check the official locator at mos.jp/shop for hours before a late visit.
  • Carry yen in cash in case cards are refused.
  • Budget ¥1,130 for a MOS Burger, medium fries and a medium soft drink at July 2026 prices. Toyohira reviewers put a meal at around ¥1,000.
  • Ask about Hokkaidō-limited items: they come and go with the seasons.

FAQ: MOS Burger Sapporo

Is there a MOS Burger in Sapporo? Yes. Sapporo has at least a dozen MOS Burger branches. The most central is Sapporo Yonbangai, at Minami 3-jō Nishi 4-chōme 1, Chūō-ku, a minute’s walk from Tanukikōji and Susukino stations.

Is MOS Burger in Susukino open late? It depends on which listing you trust. NAVITIME (MOS data) and Hot Pepper give 9:00 to 22:00 daily, Retty gives 9:00 to 1:00, and Jalan lists 24-hour service Tuesday to Saturday. Check mos.jp or Google on the day.

How much does a burger cost at MOS Burger in Japan in 2026? Since July 15, 2026, a MOS Burger costs ¥500 and a Teriyaki Burger ¥490, tax included. MOS Chicken is ¥340 and medium fries ¥340.

Does MOS Burger have vegetarian or vegan options? MOS launched the MOS PLANT-BASED GREEN BURGER in 2020, with no animal ingredients and none of the five gokun alliums, then a soy-patty teriyaki version in 2021. Availability in Sapporo in October 2026 is not confirmed.

Does MOS Burger have Hokkaidō-only burgers? Yes, seasonally. In July 2026, the Hokkaidō Tomato Festival offered two ¥920 burgers made with Hokkaidō tomatoes. The event ended on July 26, 2026, and no current regional item is confirmed.

Can I pay with a foreign credit card at MOS Burger Sapporo? Not confirmed for the Yonbangai branch. NAVITIME says cards are not accepted, while Retty mentions PayPay. Bring yen in cash to be safe.

Can I get MOS Burger delivered in Sapporo? Yes, the Yonbangai branch lists delivery through Demae-can. MOS online ordering and other apps were not verified for this branch.

What does MOS stand for? MOS officially stands for “Mountain, Ocean, Sun”. Wikipedia describes this as a backronym of “Merchandising Organizing System”.
