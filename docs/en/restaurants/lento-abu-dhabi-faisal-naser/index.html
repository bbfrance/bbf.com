LENTO Abu Dhabi: Faisal Naser's Smash Burgers

Emirati chef Faisal Naser's LENTO: wagyu smash burgers, the 2022 and 2024 menus, the Al Danah address, and why Ossiano isn't an Abu Dhabi burger spot.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished November 27, 2026

LENTO is the smash-burger counter that Emirati chef and restaurateur Faisal Naser opened in Abu Dhabi in 2020 (Spinneys, List Magazine). Grass-fed wagyu patties smashed thin, locally sourced free-range chicken, buns baked for the kitchen, and about 90% of the ingredients made from scratch. Of the burger brands born in the capital, it is the only one built around a chef, and it is the one I would send you to first. One warning: the last published address dates from November 2024, so check the branch and the hours on LENTO’s official links page, linktr.ee/lento.ae, before you go.

If you landed here looking for Grégoire Berger or Ossiano, neither belongs in a guide to Abu Dhabi burgers. Ossiano is in Dubai. It has never served burgers, and Berger left it at the end of 2024. The details are further down.

Who is Faisal Naser, the chef behind LENTO?

Faisal Naser is the Emirati chef and restaurateur who founded LENTO in Abu Dhabi in 2020 and still fronts the brand today.

He sums up his philosophy in one sentence he gave Spinneys: “My ethos is quality over quantity and therefore artisanal over commercial… At LENTO I use the same concept in the fast-food frame” (Spinneys, “Slow & steady”). That is the whole idea of the place. The format is a burger counter. The prep is closer to a restaurant kitchen.

He is still cooking in public in 2026. Gulf News and Caterer Middle East list Naser among the chefs taking part in Taste of Abu Dhabi 2026, held at Gateway Park North on Yas Island from 13 to 15 November, with LENTO in the pop-up line-up under “Burgers” (Gulf News, Caterer Middle East).

What he did before LENTO is harder to pin down. I found no source I could verify on his training or earlier kitchens, so I have left that out rather than guess.

What does “Lento” mean?

“Lento” is the Spanish word for “slow”, and Naser chose it to signal his slow-preparation approach to burgers.

It is a jab at the speed that defines most burger chains. The patty is still smashed hot and fast on the griddle. What is slow is everything around it: the sauces, the buns, the sourcing.

What makes LENTO’s burgers different?

LENTO’s burgers stand out because almost everything on the tray is made in-house: about 90% of ingredients are prepared from scratch, around grass-fed wagyu smashed patties and free-range local chicken.

  • The beef. Grass-fed wagyu, smashed thin so the edges crisp and caramelise (BBC Good Food Middle East).
  • The chicken. Locally sourced free-range birds for the fried chicken burgers.
  • The bun. BBC Good Food Middle East described “homemade buns” in 2022. Curly Tales described “freshly baked sourdough buns” in 2024 (Curly Tales). Both point to bread baked for the restaurant rather than bought in.
  • The sauces. Porcini aioli, fermented honey-butter emulsion, umami sauce and green salsa all appear on the dated menus below.

Most “best burgers in Abu Dhabi” lists miss this. They name LENTO, then describe the toppings. The real difference is technique and sourcing.

LENTO’s signature burgers

LENTO’s best-known burgers are the Mushroom & Swiss, the Honey Butter Fried Chicken and the Picante Umami, as listed in June 2022. Later coverage shows a different line-up, so treat both lists as snapshots.

The 2022 signatures

BBC Good Food Middle East listed these three when LENTO announced its Dubai branch on 27 June 2022:

Burger What’s in it
Mushroom & Swiss Grass-fed wagyu patties, Swiss gruyère, caramelised onions, brown butter, porcini aioli
Honey Butter Fried Chicken Fried chicken, fermented honey-butter emulsion, ranch coleslaw, cheese
Picante Umami Double grass-fed wagyu patties, American cheese, green salsa, umami sauce, jalapeños

On paper, the Honey Butter Fried Chicken is the one I would order first. Fermenting the honey butter should give it a tang that a plain sweet glaze never has, and the ranch coleslaw adds the crunch. The Mushroom & Swiss is LENTO’s take on an American fast-food classic. To compare it with the chain original, try our Hardee’s Mushroom and Swiss copycat recipe, which rebuilds that burger at home.

The 2024 menu at LENTO Street Eatery

In November 2024, Curly Tales listed a different menu at LENTO Street Eatery in Abu Dhabi:

  • OG Double Cheeseburger
  • Smashed Cheeseburger
  • Chipotle burger
  • Korean burger
  • Fried chicken burger
  • Fish tacos and shrimp tacos

All of them came on freshly baked sourdough buns, according to the same article. I could not confirm the November 2026 menu, and I found no verified prices in dirhams, so I am not quoting any. The current menu is on LENTO’s official links page.

Where is LENTO in Abu Dhabi?

The most recent published address for LENTO in Abu Dhabi is LENTO Street Eatery, 913 Hazza Bin Zayed The First St, Al Danah, Zone 1, open from 1pm to 11pm, as reported by Curly Tales in November 2024.

That address and those hours come from a single article, now two years old. Coverage from 2020 to 2022 may describe a different first site. Check the location pinned on LENTO’s official channels or Google Maps before you make the trip.

Does LENTO have a branch in Dubai?

Yes. LENTO announced its second UAE branch in 2022, at 2 Al Wasl Road in the Umm Al Sheif area of Dubai (BBC Good Food Middle East, Architizer).

I could not confirm that it is still open in November 2026. For other burger options in that city, see our Dubai burger guide.

Grégoire Berger and Ossiano: not an Abu Dhabi burger story

Ossiano is a seafood fine-dining restaurant at Atlantis The Palm in Dubai, not an Abu Dhabi burger venue, and its former chef Grégoire Berger runs no burger restaurant in Abu Dhabi.

Berger joined Atlantis The Palm as executive chef of Ossiano in 2014 (Hospitality Net). The restaurant sits about 10 m below the Ambassador Lagoon aquarium on Palm Jumeirah and won one Michelin star in the first Michelin Guide Dubai in 2022 (Kerzner, The National). Berger left at the end of 2024, after about ten years. His final “Anchored” ten-course menu cost AED 1,250 per person (Liam Collens, Fact Magazine).

Atlantis The Palm on Palm Jumeirah, Dubai, photographed in June 2017. In 2025 he opened Kraken, a seafood restaurant with no alcohol licence in a villa on Al Wasl Road in Dubai, and Solenn, a Mediterranean concept that started at Dubai Festival City. I followed that move in Grégoire Berger after Ossiano: Kraken in Dubai.

Solenn’s Abu Dhabi branch opened in August 2026 at The Arcades, Al Khail Square, overlooking the Abu Dhabi Equestrian Club. It serves sharing plates, its signature dish is “Illusion of a Tomato”, and there is no burger on the menu (The National, tbreak). Go for the tomato, not for a burger.

Other homegrown burger restaurants in Abu Dhabi

LENTO is not Abu Dhabi’s only homegrown burger brand: Just Burger dates from 2014 and Burger28 from 2017, and Dubai-born SLAW has since expanded to the capital.

How I researched this profile

I built this profile from published sources only, each dated and linked above. I did not eat at LENTO for this article. Facts confirmed by two sources are stated plainly. Facts from a single source, such as the Al Danah address and the 1pm to 11pm hours, are attributed and dated. Where no source confirmed something (current prices, halal certification, Naser’s earlier career), I left it out.

FAQ: LENTO Abu Dhabi

Who is the chef behind LENTO in Abu Dhabi?

Emirati chef and restaurateur Faisal Naser founded LENTO in Abu Dhabi in 2020 and was listed among the chefs at Taste of Abu Dhabi in November 2026.

What does “Lento” mean?

“Lento” means “slow” in Spanish. The name reflects Naser’s slow-preparation ethos, which he describes as artisanal quality applied to a fast-food format.

What is the best burger at LENTO?

My pick on paper is the Honey Butter Fried Chicken, with a fermented honey-butter emulsion and ranch coleslaw; I have not tasted it. For beef, the 2022 signatures were the Mushroom & Swiss and the double-patty Picante Umami, and the 2024 menu led with the OG Double Cheeseburger.

Where is LENTO in Abu Dhabi and what are its opening hours?

In November 2024, Curly Tales placed LENTO Street Eatery at 913 Hazza Bin Zayed The First St, Al Danah, Zone 1, open 1pm to 11pm. I could not verify either for 2026, so confirm both on LENTO’s official channels before visiting.

Is LENTO halal?

Meat served in UAE restaurants is generally halal, but no source I checked states LENTO’s status explicitly. Ask the restaurant directly or check its official channels.

Does LENTO have a branch in Dubai?

LENTO announced a Dubai branch at 2 Al Wasl Road, Umm Al Sheif, in 2022. Whether it is still open in November 2026 is unconfirmed.

Is Ossiano in Abu Dhabi?

No. Ossiano is an underwater seafood fine-dining restaurant at Atlantis The Palm, Palm Jumeirah, in Dubai. It is not a burger restaurant.

Does Grégoire Berger have a restaurant in Abu Dhabi?

Yes: Solenn, a Mediterranean sharing-plates restaurant at The Arcades, Al Khail Square, which opened in August 2026. It does not serve burgers.

What are the best chef-led or homegrown burger restaurants in Abu Dhabi?

LENTO is the clearest chef-led brand born in the city. Other homegrown names include Burger28 (2017) and Just Burger (2014). SLAW, founded in Dubai by Emirati chef Ali Yazdi, has also expanded to Abu Dhabi.
