Five Guys Baltimore: 6 Locations, Menu, Prices

Five Guys Baltimore: all 6 locations, from the new Inner Harbor store on Pratt Street to top-rated Brewers Hill in Canton, with menu, prices and reviews.

By Olivia, Restaurants writer, Canada and the US NortheastPublished November 25, 2026

Five Guys lists 6 restaurants in Baltimore, MD, in its official directory, and five of them were confirmed open on November 1, 2026: Inner Harbor (600 East Pratt Street), Canton’s Shoppes at Brewers Hill (3600 Boston St.), York Road, Security Boulevard and Eastern Avenue (509 Bayview Boulevard). The sixth, in Charles Village (3220 St. Paul Street), is listed but unconfirmed. Visiting the harbor? Go to Five Guys Inner Harbor at Lockwood Place, 600 East Pratt Street, Baltimore, MD 21202 (410-601-3888), open since late June 2026. If reviews decide it for you, head to Brewers Hill in Canton: 4.4/5 on Birdeye from 893 reviews, the best score of any Baltimore Five Guys I could check. I verified every address and phone number on November 1, 2026.

Five Guys Inner Harbor: the Lockwood Place restaurant

The Five Guys closest to the Inner Harbor is the one at Lockwood Place, 600 East Pratt Street, which opened in late June 2026, according to What Now Baltimore. Its official page lists dine-in, takeout and delivery, with online ordering on order.fiveguys.com and in the Five Guys app (official page).

Baltimore’s Inner Harbor facing east: the Pratt Street Power Plant (left), the lightship Chesapeake, the National Aquarium and the USS Torsk submarine. Five Guys Inner Harbor at Lockwood Place is across Pratt Street from the Power Plant, just out of frame. Photo from April 2011, taken before the restaurant opened (3218 × 1593) (Photo: AgnosticPreachersKid, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Inner\_Harbor\_-\_facing\_east.jpg)

This is a homecoming. On November 7, 2024, WMAR 2 News reported that Five Guys had signed a lease at Lockwood Place, alongside Fogo de Chão and Burlington (WMAR 2 News). The chain had left the waterfront several years earlier, when its old Harborplace restaurant closed. Harborplace itself has largely emptied out, and the Gallery at Harborplace has been closed since January 2022 (Wikipedia).

If you are walking the harbor promenade, this is the one I would pick. Same menu as the other five, and no car needed. I found no official information on parking, the nearest transit stops, accessibility or payment methods, so call 410-601-3888 if any of those matter to you.

Five Guys Canton: Shoppes at Brewers Hill

Five Guys Shoppes at Brewers Hill, at 3600 Boston St. in Canton, is the best-reviewed Five Guys in Baltimore among the sources I could verify. It offers dine-in, takeout, delivery and online ordering (official page).

Birdeye gives it 4.4/5 from 893 reviews, and Restaurantji gives it 4.2/5 from 163 reviews, with 4.5 for food, atmosphere and service. Reviewers keep mentioning the same things: burgers and fries made to order in under 7 minutes, and polite staff. If you live east of downtown or are already in Canton, this is my pick for the best Five Guys experience in the city.

York Road, Eastern Avenue and Security Boulevard

The other three open Five Guys in Baltimore are neighborhood stops, handy if you live nearby or are driving. All three list dine-in, takeout and delivery on their official pages.

  • York Road (6302 York Road, MD 21212): north Baltimore, serving lunch and dinner. Birdeye rates it 4.2/5 from 445 reviews, and one reviewer called it the “Best service of any five guys on the planet” (official page).
  • Eastern Avenue (509 Bayview Boulevard, Suite 401, MD 21224): east Baltimore. The official page mentions free peanuts while you wait (official page).
  • Security Boulevard (7091 Security Blvd, MD 21244): west side, the only Baltimore location that lists outdoor seating. Order online at order.fiveguys.com/location/baltimore-security-blvd/menu (official page).

Five Guys Charles Village and Reisterstown Road Plaza: what’s coming

The Charles Village Five Guys at 3220 St. Paul Street is in the official directory, but I could not confirm that it is open: its store page returned an error on November 1, 2026. According to What Now Baltimore, it takes over the former Trek Bicycle space, covers about 3,000 square feet with about 70 seats and about 50 staff, and was targeting an opening before the end of 2026.

A second project is under construction at Reisterstown Road Plaza: about 2,400 square feet and about 50 jobs, with work started in spring 2026 and an opening planned for early in the first quarter of 2027, per What Now Baltimore. No exact street address has been published yet. The Baltimore Business Journal summed up the push: Five Guys opened downtown and plans two more city locations by early 2027 (via Segall Group).

Who is behind it? The Inner Harbor store is run by franchisee Rockham 5G, with Thomas Russell as director of operations in Maryland. Rockham 5G describes itself as the largest Five Guys franchisee on the East Coast and one of the top 3 in the country, and it is also behind the Charles Village and Reisterstown Road Plaza projects.

Five Guys Baltimore hours

I could not verify Five Guys Baltimore hours, so call the restaurant or check the Five Guys app before you go. The official store pages load their hours with JavaScript, and the third-party listings contradict each other.

For Brewers Hill, Restaurantji shows 10 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily while Birdeye shows 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. Birdeye lists York Road at 11 a.m. to 10 p.m., and one press snippet gives Lockwood Place 10 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily. None of these is official, so treat them as a rough guide only.

Five Guys Baltimore menu: what to order

Baltimore serves the standard US Five Guys menu, with no Baltimore-only items. According to the official menu, you can order:

  • Burgers: fresh patties formed by hand, with as many free toppings as you want.
  • Fries: boardwalk-style, cooked in 100% refined peanut oil, Five Guys style or Cajun style.
  • Hot dogs: all-beef, split and grilled.
  • Sandwiches: including a BLT and a grilled cheese.
  • Milkshakes: a vanilla base with mix-ins, including a Reese’s Peanut Butter Cup shake.
  • Drinks: Coca-Cola and Dr Pepper.

The Baltimore store pages add the chain’s claims: more than 250,000 ways to build your burger, more than 1,000 milkshake combinations, fresh ground beef with no freezers, and fries cooked twice in 100% peanut oil (Inner Harbor page).

At Brewers Hill, Restaurantji lists the most popular orders as the Double Cheeseburger “All the Way” with fries, the Little Bacon Cheeseburger, the bacon mushroom cheeseburger, large Cajun fries and the kosher-style hot dog. My order would be the Little Bacon Cheeseburger with Cajun fries: one patty is plenty once the toppings pile up. Want it at home? For context, the median hamburger in the US cost $14.64 in February 2026, up 2.7% in a year, according to Toast’s price index (FOX 35 Orlando). The meal is another story: close to $30 for a burger, fries and a soda is not a cheap lunch, and the regular fries alone cost almost as much as the shake. Five Guys and peanut allergies

If you have a peanut allergy, Five Guys is a risky choice. The fries are cooked in 100% peanut oil, the Eastern Avenue page advertises free peanuts while you wait, and the menu features a Reese’s Peanut Butter Cup shake. Talk to the staff and read the chain’s US nutrition and allergen guide before you order.

Five Guys vs a Baltimore burger

Five Guys is a reliable, build-your-own chain burger, but nothing on the menu tells you that you’re in Baltimore. For local flavor, think Old Bay on your fries and pit beef, both covered in our Baltimore burger guide.

My take: Five Guys is the fast, familiar option after a walk around the Inner Harbor. Save one meal for something only Baltimore does. And if your trip takes you down to the capital, about an hour away, read my colleague Daniel’s Lucky Buns guide in Washington, DC.

Five Guys: a quick history

Five Guys was founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell as a takeout counter, named after their five sons, and it is still a family business (FOX 35 Orlando). The chain turned 40 in 2026 and opened its 2,000th restaurant on September 25, 2026, at Universal CityWalk in Orlando. The full story, figures and reviews are on our Five Guys brand profile.

FAQ: Five Guys Baltimore

How many Five Guys are there in Baltimore?

The official directory lists 6 Five Guys in Baltimore: Inner Harbor (600 East Pratt Street), Shoppes at Brewers Hill (3600 Boston St.), York Road (6302 York Road), Security Boulevard (7091 Security Blvd), Eastern Avenue (509 Bayview Boulevard) and St. Paul Street (3220 St. Paul Street). The St. Paul Street opening was not confirmed on November 1, 2026.

Which Five Guys is closest to the Inner Harbor?

Five Guys at Lockwood Place, 600 East Pratt Street, Suite 101, Baltimore, MD 21202 (410-601-3888). It opened in late June 2026, according to What Now Baltimore.

What are Five Guys Baltimore’s hours?

The official hours could not be verified. Listings range from 10 a.m.–11 p.m. to 11 a.m.–10 p.m. daily depending on the source, so call your location or check the Five Guys app before going.

How much is a meal at Five Guys in Baltimore?

About $28.65 for a cheeseburger, regular fries and a bottled Coke, based on undated Allmenus prices for York Road that look like delivery prices. Check the Five Guys app for the current price at your location, before tax and tip.

Does Five Guys deliver in Baltimore?

Yes. The official directory lists delivery and curbside pickup for its Baltimore locations, though the St. Paul Street store was not confirmed open on November 1, 2026. You can also order on order.fiveguys.com or in the Five Guys app.

Is the Charles Village Five Guys on St. Paul Street open?

Not confirmed. It appears in the official directory, but its store page returned an error on November 1, 2026. What Now Baltimore reported an opening targeted before the end of 2026.

Which Five Guys in Baltimore has the best reviews?

Shoppes at Brewers Hill in Canton: 4.4/5 from 893 reviews on Birdeye and 4.2/5 from 163 reviews on Restaurantji. York Road follows with 4.2/5 from 445 Birdeye reviews.

Does Five Guys have vegetarian options?

The official US menu includes a grilled cheese, fries and milkshakes, none of which contain meat, and you can ask for a bun with toppings and no patty. The fries are cooked in peanut oil, so ask staff about ingredients if you have other dietary needs.

Can I eat at Five Guys with a peanut allergy?

It is risky. Fries are cooked in 100% peanut oil, free peanuts are offered at some Baltimore locations, and the menu includes a peanut butter shake. Speak to staff and read the Five Guys allergen guide before ordering.

Do I need a reservation at Five Guys?

No. You order at the counter, online or in the app, and the official directory lists curbside pickup for its Baltimore locations.

Why is Five Guys more expensive than other burger chains?

Five Guys says it uses fresh beef with no freezers, prepares ingredients by hand and fries in 100% peanut oil, and toppings are free. Its York Road burgers on Allmenus start at $13.19, close to the $14.64 median US hamburger price that Toast reported in February 2026.
