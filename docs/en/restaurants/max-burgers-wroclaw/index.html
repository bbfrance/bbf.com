MAX Burgers Wrocław: Addresses, Hours and Prices

MAX Burgers Wrocław: both addresses (Galeria Dominikańska, Wroclavia), opening hours, prices in zł, vegan menu and reviews, now that Bobby Burger has gone.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 24, 2026

MAX Premium Burgers is the burger chain to pick in Wrocław now that Bobby Burger is gone. It has 2 addresses: Galeria Dominikańska at plac Dominikański 3 in the Old Town, and Westfield Wroclavia at ul. Sucha 1, opposite Wrocław Główny station. Wrocław is where the Swedish chain opened its first restaurant in Poland, on 1 September 2017. I checked the menu, prices, opening hours and reviews below against official and platform sources on 1 November 2026. 1 warning up front: the hours don’t match from 1 source to the next. Confirm them before you go.

Is Bobby Burger still open in Wrocław?

No. Bobby Burger is no longer an option in Wrocław, and the company behind it is being wound up. Old TripAdvisor, Yelp and Restaurant Guru listings still show it at ul. Szewska 27, 50-139 Wrocław, with phone numbers and hours that contradict each other. None of them mentions the liquidation.

Why Bobby Burger dropped out

  • Bobby Burger started in 2012 as a food truck in Warsaw, led by Bogumił Jankiewicz (Horecatrends).
  • The company entered restructuring: its arrangement hearing was published in the KRZ register on 17 August 2023 (Continuum).
  • In December 2024 the shareholders voted to dissolve the company and open its liquidation. By March 2025 the chain had 13 restaurants, down from nearly 50 at its peak (Franczyza w Polsce, 19 March 2025).
  • The cities still listed in early 2025 were Warsaw, Kraków, Sopot, Bydgoszcz, Rzeszów, Toruń, Radom and Łódź. Wrocław was not among them.
  • On 1 November 2026, bobbyburger.pl showed “Ta restauracja jest czasowo niedostępna” (this restaurant is temporarily unavailable) and listed no locations.

No primary source gives a closing date for the Szewska 27 branch. What we can say is that it no longer appears among the chain’s restaurants.

Where are the MAX restaurants in Wrocław?

MAX has 2 restaurants in Wrocław, both on the ground floor (level 0) of a shopping centre. The Old Town one is in Galeria Dominikańska. The other is in Westfield Wroclavia, opposite the main station, with its own street entrance on ul. Sucha.

MAX Galeria Dominikańska, the Old Town address

MAX Galeria Dominikańska is the original: the first MAX in Poland, opened on 1 September 2017 (Wiadomości Handlowe). It is on the ground floor of the Galeria Dominikańska mall at plac Dominikański 3. The mall and Trip.com give another phone number, +48 71 346 83 00. The table above uses the official one.

MAX Wroclavia, opposite the main station

MAX Wroclavia is the practical stop if you are travelling by train or coach. It is on ul. Sucha, across the street from Wrocław Główny rail station, and the coach station (PKS) is in the same Wroclavia building. The opening was announced on 23 March 2021, when country director Jerzy Jakubiak called Wrocław MAX’s first Polish city (Eurobuild CEE). On 1 November 2026, Westfield’s page showed it as “temporarily closed”, while the MAX site still gave opening hours. Call +48 71 733 75 85 before you make the trip.

MAX Wrocław opening hours

According to the official MAX site, both Wrocław restaurants stay open well past midnight. Other sources disagree, sometimes by several hours, so check the MAX app or phone the restaurant before a late-night visit.

The Galeria Dominikańska tenant page gives much shorter hours: 9:30–21:00 Monday to Saturday and 10:00–20:00 on Sunday. Trip.com says 10:00 to midnight. My guess is that the mall’s hours apply to the indoor entrance only, but no source confirms it. If you want a burger after 21:00, phone first.

What to order at MAX in Wrocław

MAX serves beef and chicken burgers, a plant-based range, breakfast and desserts. The Westfield and Galeria Dominikańska listings both mention plant-based “chicken” and “beef” in the vegetarian and vegan range. On Trip.com, the dish reviewers mention most is the crispy sweet potato fries with their dips.

  • The Original: the chain’s classic burger, listed at 27.95 zł on delivery platforms in 2026.
  • Chili Cheese: breaded spicy cheese with jalapeños, listed at 17.45 zł.
  • Sweet potato fries: the side reviewers mention most, served with dipping sauces.
  • Plant-based burgers: meat-free versions of the chicken and beef burgers, clearly marked on the menu according to Spinach reviewers.
  • Breakfast: both Wrocław restaurants list breakfast (“śniadania”) as a service.

I could not check the full current Polish menu on an official source: the MAX Poland menu page returned a 404 error on 1 November 2026. That is why other burgers, the drinks and the kids’ menu are not listed here.

How much does a burger cost at MAX in Poland?

Expect around 27–28 zł for a burger and 30–42 zł for a meal at MAX in Poland. On 16 December 2025, the average item price on the Polish menu was 27.19 zł and meals ranged from 30 to 42 zł (Pricelisto).

Vegan burger in Wrocław: the MAX plant-based menu

For a vegan or vegetarian fast-food burger in Wrocław, MAX is the easy choice. Spinach reviewers praise how many vegan options there are and how clearly they are labelled. Trip.com reviewers are cooler and call them decent. MAX presents the plant-based range as part of its climate strategy: the chain says it has been carbon neutral since 2008, and that the UN recognised its climate action in 2019.

Gluten-free buns and burgers are reported at MAX in Poland by the specialist apps Gluto and Find Me Gluten Free. MAX itself does not confirm this, so coeliac diners should ask staff at the counter.

Delivery, ordering and services

Both Wrocław restaurants offer dine-in, an express till (“kasa ekspresowa”), breakfast, MAX’s own delivery, and ordering through the MAX app or order.maxpremiumburgers.pl. Neither has a drive-through. The app is on the App Store and Google Play.

MAX Premium Burgers Wrocław reviews

Online reviews of MAX in Wrocław are positive but few: 4.1/5 on Trip.com from only 7 reviews, and 3.6/5 on Spinach. Diners praise the speed and the vegan choice. The complaints, which come from across the chain, are about portion size and delivery mistakes.

On Trustpilot, reviewers of the chain as a whole complain that burgers have got smaller while prices went up, and about burnt buns, forgotten sauces and incomplete deliveries. None of those reviews is specific to Wrocław. I have no Google or TripAdvisor rating for either Wrocław restaurant: the pages would not load when I checked.

Who runs MAX, and how it differs from McDonald’s

MAX is a Swedish, family-owned chain, not a US brand. Curt Bergfors and Britta Fredriksson founded it in 1968 in Gällivare, in Swedish Lapland, and the Bergfors family still owns it. Compared with the American giants in Wrocław, it has 2 clear differences: a much wider meat-free menu, and a climate position it has promoted since 2008.

Poland is MAX’s second market. On 15 September 2025, MAX opened its 200th restaurant, in Gislaved, Sweden. At that point it had 160 restaurants in Sweden, 26 in Poland, 8 in Norway and 6 in Denmark (MAX press release). On 1 November 2026 its website listed 207 restaurants. For the chain’s full history, figures and ratings, see our MAX Burgers brand profile.

My take: which MAX, and when

If I had 1 evening in Wrocław, I would use MAX as a fallback, not as the destination. Galeria Dominikańska is the branch to pick: it is the original, it is a few minutes’ walk from the Rynek, and on paper it has the latest hours in the Old Town. Wroclavia makes sense only if you have a train or coach to catch, and only after a phone call, given Westfield’s “temporarily closed” notice. For a burger that tells you something about the city, I would go to a local chain like Pasibus first.

FAQ

Is Bobby Burger still open in Wrocław? No. Wrocław was missing from Bobby Burger’s list of cities in early 2025. The company has been in liquidation since December 2024, and its website listed no restaurants on 1 November 2026. The old address, ul. Szewska 27, survives only in outdated directories.

Where is MAX in Wrocław? At plac Dominikański 3 (Galeria Dominikańska, level 0) and ul. Sucha 1 (Westfield Wroclavia, level 0, opposite Wrocław Główny station). Westfield showed the Wroclavia branch as temporarily closed on 1 November 2026.

Is MAX in Wrocław open late at night? The official site lists Galeria Dominikańska as open until 04:00 Monday to Thursday, 05:00 on Friday and Saturday and 03:00 on Sunday. The mall lists much shorter hours, so call +48 71 661 29 48 before a late visit.

How much is a burger at MAX in Poland? The Original was listed at 27.95 zł and the Chili Cheese at 17.45 zł on delivery platforms in 2026. Meals cost 30–42 zł in December 2025.

Does MAX have vegan or gluten-free burgers in Wrocław? Vegan and vegetarian, yes: MAX has a large plant-based range, including meat-free chicken and beef. Gluten-free buns are reported by specialist apps but not confirmed by MAX.

Does MAX serve breakfast in Wrocław? Yes. Both Wrocław restaurants list breakfast (“śniadania”) among their services on the official MAX site.

Can I get MAX delivered in Wrocław? Yes, through MAX’s own delivery, the MAX app or order.maxpremiumburgers.pl. In 2023 both branches were also on Bolt Food, Glovo, Pyszne.pl, Uber Eats and Wolt.

How do I get to MAX Wroclavia from Wrocław Główny station? Cross ul. Sucha. MAX Wroclavia faces the rail station and has its own outside entrance on that street. The coach station is in the same Wroclavia building.

How I put this guide together

I built this guide on 1 November 2026 from MAX Poland’s official restaurant pages, the two malls’ tenant pages, dated trade press, delivery platforms and review sites, and I link each source where I use it. Where sources disagree, as they do on hours and phone numbers, I give the official figure and say where the others differ.
