Hamburger America NYC: Menu, Prices and Burgers

Who the burger scholar is, what to order at his SoHo lunch counter, what it costs in November 2026 and why New York critics took it so seriously.

By Olivia, Restaurants writer, Canada and the US NortheastPublished November 11, 2026

Hamburger America is George Motz’s burger counter at 155 West Houston Street, on the corner of MacDougal Street in SoHo, Manhattan. It opened in November 2023 and is open seven days a week. On November 4, 2026, a single burger cost $7.75 and a double $12.50. Order the Fried Onion Burger, Motz’s version of the Oklahoma onion burger, and the Classic Smash Burger, which Eater called “probably the best smash burger in the city”. My take: if you want to know what an American burger tasted like before brioche buns and truffle mayo, this is the cheapest history lesson in Manhattan.

Who is George Motz, the burger scholar?

George Motz is an American filmmaker, author and TV host who has documented regional hamburgers for more than twenty years, and he is the owner and head chef of Hamburger America. His publisher’s bio quotes The New York Times calling him the “foremost authority on hamburgers”. He lives in Brooklyn.

He built that reputation on screen and in print, long before he had a flat-top of his own. His 2004 documentary Hamburger America profiles eight burger joints. The guidebook of the same name followed in 2008, then The Great American Burger Book in 2016 (Wikipedia).

Television did the rest. Motz hosted Burger Land on the Travel Channel from 2012 to 2013. Since 2018 he has worked with First We Feast, and its YouTube series Burger Scholar Sessions is where most fans picked up the nickname.

He also founded The Food Film Festival in New York in 2007, won three regional Emmy Awards (one in 2000, two in 2006) and studied at the Catholic University of America (Catholic University magazine).

He turns up across our own coverage. He is one of the voices in our history of the hamburger, his 2018 remarks on butter burgers appear in our piece on Solly’s Grille and the Milwaukee butter burger, and he was the guest at an event in our profile of Rose’s Burgers in Rosario, Argentina. That background is the whole premise of the restaurant: it serves the kind of burgers he spent two decades filming.

Where is Hamburger America in New York?

Hamburger America is on the corner of West Houston Street and MacDougal Street in SoHo, and the entrance is on Houston. The official site gives the address as 155 West Houston St. The Infatuation and Time Out New York list 51 MacDougal St, NY 10012.

Same corner, two addresses. I use 155 West Houston St because that is what the restaurant publishes, but either one will take you to the counter in a map app.

What to order at Hamburger America: the signature burgers

Hamburger America has two signature burgers, George Motz’s Fried Onion Burger and the Classic Smash Burger, both $7.75 single and $12.50 double on the November 2026 menu. The idea, as Wikipedia sums it up, is to use historically accurate ingredients and methods to recreate “what hamburgers used to taste like”.

Wikipedia adds that the patties are smashed with Motz’s own “Smashula” press, custom-made in Argentina. I found no second source for that, so treat it as Wikipedia’s claim, not ours.

George Motz’s Fried Onion Burger

The Fried Onion Burger is Motz’s take on the Oklahoma onion burger: beef smashed onto paper-thin onions, with American cheese and no condiments (Tasting Table, The Infatuation). The onions fry into the patty on the griddle and season it, which is why nothing else goes on. This is the burger I’d send anyone to order first. The Oklahoma style is hard to find in New York, and here it comes from someone who has spent his career documenting regional burgers. To see the style in its home state, read my colleague Daniel’s profile of Nic’s Grill and its Oklahoma City onion burger.

The Classic Smash Burger

The Classic Smash Burger comes with mustard, diced onion, dill pickle and cheese. Eater called it “probably the best smash burger in the city” in November 2023, a bold claim in a city full of smash burgers. On a first visit, order both as singles: $15.50 for the pair, before tax and tip.

Hamburger America’s monthly regional burger specials

Since April 2024, Hamburger America has served a different regional American burger as a monthly special. In November 2026 it’s the Original Triple Cheezborger from Chicago’s Billy Goat Tavern, at $16: three 2-oz patties, American cheese, dill pickle chips, raw onion, mustard and ketchup on a toasted Kaiser roll (official menu, checked November 4, 2026).

Past specials include the olive burger from Lansing, Michigan, the Wisconsin butter burger and the No. 5 from Keller’s Drive-In in Dallas, Texas (Wikipedia). I think the specials are the smartest idea on the menu. They turn Motz’s books into something you can order at the counter, one city a month, without booking a flight. The special changes every month, so check the site before you plan a visit around one.

From documentary to lunch counter: a Hamburger America timeline

Hamburger America was a film in 2004 and a restaurant in November 2023. Here is how one led to the other.

  • 2000: Motz wins his first regional Emmy Award.
  • 2004: He directs the documentary Hamburger America, profiling eight burger joints.
  • 2006: Two more regional Emmy Awards.
  • 2007: He founds The Food Film Festival in New York.
  • 2008: First edition of the Hamburger America guidebook.
  • 2012–2013: He hosts Burger Land on the Travel Channel.
  • 2016: The Great American Burger Book comes out.
  • 2018: He begins working with First We Feast, including Burger Scholar Sessions.
  • November 2023: Hamburger America opens in SoHo, developed with Jonathan and Andrew Schnipper. Opening-day guests include Chris Rock, Sean Evans, Christy Turlington and Michael Bloomberg.
  • November 2023: Eater calls the classic smash “probably the best smash burger in the city”.
  • January 2024: The New Yorker calls it “fantastic, strong and correct”.
  • April 2024: The monthly regional burger specials begin. Pete Wells gives the restaurant 2 stars in The New York Times, and it makes the NYT 100 best NYC restaurants list.
  • September 2024: The Infatuation rates it 8.1/10.
  • April 14, 2026: Hachette publishes Hamburger America, Revised and Expanded Fourth Edition, covering 220 burger joints, 38 of them new.
  • November 2026: The monthly special is Billy Goat Tavern’s Original Triple Cheezborger.

Is Hamburger America worth it?

Yes, if you want to understand the American burger rather than just eat a big one. A single costs under $8, the Oklahoma onion burger is hard to find anywhere else in the city, and the special changes every month.

My advice: order the Fried Onion Burger and the Classic Smash side by side, and check the monthly special before you go. It’s counter service, so don’t plan a long sit-down lunch. Hamburger America FAQ

Who is George Motz and why is he called the burger scholar?

George Motz is a filmmaker, author and TV host who directed the 2004 documentary Hamburger America, wrote the Hamburger America guidebook and hosted Burger Land. The nickname comes from his First We Feast series Burger Scholar Sessions, and The New York Times has called him the “foremost authority on hamburgers”.

What is the exact address of Hamburger America?

The official site gives 155 West Houston St, SoHo, New York. Many reviews list 51 MacDougal St, NY 10012. Both are the same corner of Houston and MacDougal, and the entrance is on Houston Street.

How much does a burger cost at Hamburger America?

On November 4, 2026, a single burger cost $7.75 and a double $12.50, for both the Fried Onion Burger and the Classic Smash Burger. The November 2026 monthly special costs $16.

Should I order the Fried Onion Burger or the Classic Smash?

Order the Fried Onion Burger for the Oklahoma style: onions smashed into the patty, American cheese and no condiments. Order the Classic Smash for mustard, diced onion, pickle and cheese. Two singles cost $15.50, so you can easily try both.

What is an Oklahoma fried onion burger?

An Oklahoma fried onion burger is a beef patty smashed onto a pile of paper-thin onions on the griddle, so the onions fry into the meat. At Hamburger America it comes with American cheese and no condiments.

What is the monthly burger special at Hamburger America?

In November 2026 it is the Original Triple Cheezborger from Chicago’s Billy Goat Tavern, at $16: three 2-oz patties, American cheese, pickle chips, raw onion, mustard and ketchup on a toasted Kaiser roll. The special has changed every month since April 2024.

What did the New York Times say about Hamburger America?

Pete Wells gave it 2 stars in his April 16, 2024 review, “Hamburger America Is Deliriously Messy”. The same month, the restaurant made the NYT 100 best NYC restaurants list, which called it “the geeked-out burger stand New York didn’t know it needed”.

Does George Motz still cook at Hamburger America?

Motz is the owner and head chef. In September 2024, The Infatuation reported that he was often behind the counter flipping patties, but I couldn’t confirm how often he works the grill in 2026.

What are Hamburger America’s opening hours?

The official site says the restaurant is open seven days a week but doesn’t publish detailed hours. Older listings disagree with each other, so check hamburgeramerica.com on the day you go.

Is there more than one Hamburger America location?

No. As of November 4, 2026, the SoHo counter at 155 West Houston St is the only location I could find.
