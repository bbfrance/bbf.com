HUGGYS Liège: Burgers, Address and Founders

HUGGYS Liège: Passage Lemonnier address, hours, what to order and how two HEC-ULiège friends grew one 2012 bar into 13 Belgian burger restaurants.

By Sofia, Restaurants writer, Italy, Switzerland and the BeneluxPublished November 11, 2026

Liège’s own burger brand is HUGGYS, and in the city itself there is one place to eat it: HUGGYS Liège 2, Passage Lemonnier 22, 4000 Liège. It is open every day from 11:45 to 22:00, public holidays included. Thomas Mémurlin and Roberto Navarro started the brand in September 2012 as The Huggy’s Bar. Fourteen years later the beef still comes from a Liège butcher, fresh Belgian Blue delivered daily. The eight house beers are brewed with Brasserie C, a Liège brewery. The brand now counts 13 restaurants across Wallonia and Brussels.

Who founded HUGGYS? The Liège founders’ story

HUGGYS was founded by Thomas Mémurlin and Roberto Navarro, two friends from HEC-ULiège, the University of Liège’s business school. They opened The Huggy’s Bar on Boulevard d’Avroy in September 2012, according to the brand’s own history and L’Avenir.

It started with a trip to the United States. The founders came back wanting “a typical US restaurant mixing sports bar and burgers”, in their own words. Hence the first name. Hence, too, the bar-first feel the brand held on to for more than a decade.

By 2017 they had a burger recipe book, THBook. The brand’s blog tells how the book came about. A year later L’Avenir profiled the pair under a headline Liège readers will have enjoyed: “Les kings du burger sont liégeois” (“The burger kings are from Liège”).

Why The Huggy’s Bar became HUGGYS

The Huggy’s Bar became HUGGYS in July 2024, with a new brand identity to go with the shorter name. Paris Match Belgique broke it on 22 July 2024 (“Le Huggy’s Bar, c’est fini !”), and the brand gave its reasons on its own blog. My read: dropping “Bar” fits what HUGGYS has become, a restaurant brand that happens to pour its own beer.

The 13 HUGGYS restaurants today

HUGGYS lists 13 restaurants on its concept page: Soumagne, Bruxelles 2 Louise, Nandrin, Gosselies, Liège 2, Awans, Namur, Gerpinnes, Nivelles, Bruxelles 1 Legrand, Sprimont, Braine-l’Alleud and Waterloo. Only Liège 2 is in the city of Liège. Four more (Soumagne, Nandrin, Awans and Sprimont) are in the province of Liège. Namur is the next stop along the Meuse.

Is the original Huggy’s Bar on Boulevard d’Avroy still open?

No longer, as far as HUGGYS is concerned: the Boulevard d’Avroy site is not among the 13 restaurants, and its old page on huggys.be now shows Liège 2. I found no statement from the brand or the local press giving a closure date or a reason, so I won’t guess. In Liège today, head to Passage Lemonnier.

What makes a HUGGYS burger different: Belgian Blue beef and house beer

A HUGGYS burger is built on Liège suppliers. The beef is Belgian Blue from a Liège butcher, the beer comes from a Liège brewery, and the burger is served with twice-fried Belgian fries.

  • Beef. HUGGYS says it uses “fresh Belgian Blue beef, delivered daily by our master butcher from Liège”. Belgian Blue (blanc-bleu belge) is the country’s own heavily muscled breed, and its meat is lean.
  • Made to order. Every burger is assembled when you order it, with house-made sauces.
  • Two buns. A classic bun and an American-style sesame bun, both house recipes.
  • Fries. Belgian fries, fried twice. Anything else would not survive a Liège table.
  • Beer. Eight house beers developed with Brasserie C.

Start with Le Magic Tom. HUGGYS pushes it in Liège and in Brussels alike, and a beef signature is the fairest test of a Belgian Blue patty. Le Liégeois is the one I wanted to recommend in its home town, given the name. But the Liège 2 page does not list it, and I could not confirm its current recipe. Ask before you count on it.

HUGGYS does not publish burger prices on the pages I checked on 3 November 2026. Check the live menu or a delivery app for the current price in euros before you go.

Is HUGGYS the best burger in Liège?

HUGGYS is the most established burger name in Liège: it was born in the city, still buys its beef and beer there, and is the only Liège burger restaurant that grew into a 13-restaurant Belgian brand.

That is a rare path for the region: two local business graduates, one American-style bar in 2012, and a chain built from it in under fifteen years. On roots and reach, no one in Liège comes close. Whether it is the best burger in the city has to be settled at the table, and this profile does not claim to settle it.

Where to eat a burger in Liège: practical tips

For a burger in central Liège, head to the Passage Lemonnier arcade, off Vinâve d’Île in the shopping district: HUGGYS Liège 2 serves non-stop from 11:45 to 22:00.

  • Timing. The kitchen does not close between services, so a 15:00 or 16:00 burger is possible when most Liège kitchens are shut.
  • Holidays. Open on public holidays too.
  • Access. Wheelchair accessible, with a terrace.
  • At home. Takeaway, or delivery through Uber Eats, Deliveroo and Takeaway.com.
  • Phone. +32 4 223 29 70.
  • Brussels next? HUGGYS is number five in our guide to the best burgers in Brussels, with two restaurants near Avenue Louise.

HUGGYS Liège FAQ

Who founded HUGGYS, and when? Thomas Mémurlin and Roberto Navarro founded HUGGYS, then called The Huggy’s Bar, in Liège in September 2012. They met at HEC-ULiège.

Where is HUGGYS in Liège, and what are its opening hours? HUGGYS Liège 2 is at Passage Lemonnier 22, 4000 Liège. It is open every day from 11:45 to 22:00, public holidays included.

Is the original Huggy’s Bar on Boulevard d’Avroy still open? HUGGYS no longer lists it among its 13 restaurants, and its old web page now points to Liège 2. Neither the brand nor the local press has given a closure date.

Why did The Huggy’s Bar change its name? The brand became HUGGYS in July 2024 with a new identity, as reported by Paris Match Belgique on 22 July 2024 and announced on the HUGGYS blog.

What beef does HUGGYS use? Fresh Belgian Blue beef, delivered daily by a master butcher from Liège, according to HUGGYS.

Where do HUGGYS beers come from? HUGGYS serves eight house beers developed with Brasserie C, a brewery in Liège.

Does HUGGYS have vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free burgers? Yes. The Liège 2 menu includes vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options.

Does chef Arabelle Meirlaen serve burgers? No. Arabelle Meirlaen runs a vegetable-led, organic restaurant in Marchin, about 30 km from Liège, with no burger on the menu.
