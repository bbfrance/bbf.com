Cook Out Charlotte: Locations, Menu, Hours (2026)

Cook Out Charlotte guide: 13 listed locations, what's in a Cook Out Tray, late-night hours and Yelp reviews by store, checked on November 15, 2026.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 24, 2026

Cook Out Charlotte is less a single restaurant than a citywide habit. On November 15, 2026, I found at least 13 Cook Out addresses listed in Charlotte, from Albemarle Road in East Charlotte to North Tryon Street in University City. People come for a cheap combo called the Cook Out Tray, more than 40 milkshake flavors and a drive-thru that stays open long after midnight. Three of the Charlotte stores showed a Yelp score that day, and they ranged from 2.0 to 3.5 stars. Reviewers love the prices and the shakes. They hate the drive-thru waits and the wrong orders.

What is Cook Out? A North Carolina burger chain born in Greensboro

Cook Out is a family-owned drive-thru chain that Morris Reaves founded in Greensboro, North Carolina, in 1989. The first restaurant opened on Randleman Road. Before that, the Reaves family ran more than a dozen Wendy’s franchises in the Piedmont Triad and sold them back to Wendy’s in 1979, according to Wikipedia.

Morris Reaves still owns the company. His son, Jeremy Reaves, is CEO, and headquarters are in Thomasville, North Carolina. For two decades Cook Out never left the state. Its first store outside North Carolina opened in Spartanburg, South Carolina, on July 30, 2010, and it reached Florida in July 2025.

How big is it today? In August 2024, WhatNow Charlotte counted more than 350 restaurants in nine states, over 130 of them in North Carolina. Cook Out publishes no official count and other reports give different totals, so treat that number as an estimate.

The brand is famously quiet. As Jeremy Markovich reported in NC Rabbit Hole on August 1, 2024, Cook Out rarely talks to the press, barely posts on social media and prints Bible verses on its cups. Its loudest marketing is NASCAR. It has sponsored races since 2020, including the Southern 500 and events at Richmond and Martinsville.

New and planned Cook Out stores in Charlotte

Cook Out grows in Charlotte by taking over old fast-food buildings. On August 5, 2024, WhatNow Charlotte reported a planned store of about 2,828 sq ft at 3704 W W.T. Harris Blvd (28269). It would go into a former Jack in the Box and get a double drive-thru and a dining room.

WhatNow Charlotte also announced two more conversions: 715 Charlottetowne Ave, a 3,451 sq ft former Wendy’s, and 10525 Mallard Creek Rd, another former Jack in the Box. I could not confirm whether these two, or the store at 10645 Park Rd, are open today.

Then there’s the taco experiment. Cook Out filed a permit for Taco Roo’s, a spin-off of about 1,820 sq ft at 9605 N Tryon St in University City, as WSOC-TV and Axios Charlotte reported on August 3, 2023. The first Taco Roo’s opened in Wilmington in 2020 and has since closed. Nobody has confirmed that the Charlotte one ever opened.

Drive-thru only? How a Cook Out store works

A classic Cook Out has no dining room. You order at one of two drive-thru lanes or at a walk-up window. Many stores, including the University City one in the photo above, add a few outdoor picnic tables. Some newer buildings, like the planned W.T. Harris Blvd store, add an indoor dining room with counter service. On a cold or rainy night, expect to eat in the car.

Cook Out Charlotte menu: burgers, Carolina barbecue and the Cook Out Tray

The Cook Out menu covers hamburgers, hot dogs, chicken sandwiches and Carolina-style barbecue, built around a value combo called the Cook Out Tray. In Charlotte reviews, the barbecue, hush puppies, fries and hot dogs get as many mentions as the burgers.

What is in a Cook Out Tray?

A Cook Out Tray is one main item, two sides and a drink, and it is the chain’s signature order. Pick a main (a burger, a hot dog, a chicken sandwich or barbecue), then two sides such as fries or hush puppies.

Cook Out milkshake flavors

Cook Out offers more than 40 milkshake flavors, according to Wikipedia and NC Rabbit Hole. At the Charlotte stores, reviewers praise the shakes more often than anything else on the menu. The flavor list changes, so check the menu board or cookout.com on the day you go.

Cook Out Charlotte hours: open late

Cook Out is a late-night stop, but the hours below come from an unofficial site and are not confirmed store by store. That site lists most locations from 10:30 a.m. to 3 a.m. Sunday through Thursday, and until 4 a.m. on Friday and Saturday. Before a late run, check your store’s hours on Google Maps or cookout.com.

Cook Out Charlotte reviews: cheap and fast, inconsistent service

Charlotte’s Cook Out stores score between 2.0 and 3.5 stars on Yelp, and across the city the complaints and the compliments repeat. I read the Yelp, Wanderlog and MapQuest review excerpts available on November 15, 2026 and grouped the recurring points. I have not rated a store myself, so this guide carries no Burger Breton rating.

Cook Out Charlotte reviews: Yelp ratings on November 15, 2026. University City on N Tryon St scores 3.5 (about 115 reviews), Freedom Dr 3.0 (about 107) and S Tryon St 2.0 (about 39). (Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0, data from Yelp search excerpts read on November 15, 2026)

What reviewers like:

  • Low prices and the value of the Tray
  • The milkshakes
  • Barbecue, hush puppies, fries and hot dogs
  • Speed when the line is short

What reviewers criticize:

  • Long waits at the drive-thru
  • Wrong or incomplete orders
  • Unfriendly staff

My take: go for the price and the shake, and don’t expect a polished experience. A two-star average on South Tryon against 3.5 in University City tells me the store matters more than the brand here. If I had one Charlotte Cook Out to try first, it would be the N Tryon St store. Wherever you go, check your bag before you leave the window. Wrong orders are the single most common complaint.

Cook Out and the Charlotte burger scene

Cook Out is the cheap, late-night end of Charlotte’s burger map. Our Charlotte burger restaurants hub collects everything we publish on the city. Cook Out belongs to the same Carolina fast-food culture that produced Hardee’s, founded in Greenville, NC, in 1960. You’ll find it all over the state, including in Raleigh, where Poole’s Diner serves one of the city’s best-known burgers.

Cook Out Charlotte FAQ

How many Cook Out restaurants are there in Charlotte?

At least 13 Cook Out addresses are listed in Charlotte. Seven of them come only from an unofficial site, and some planned stores may not be open yet. Confirm on cookout.com or Google Maps.

Which Cook Out in Charlotte has the best reviews?

Of the stores I could check, the University City location on N Tryon St had the highest Yelp rating on November 15, 2026: 3.5 stars on about 115 reviews.

Is Cook Out open late in Charlotte?

Yes. An unofficial site lists most Charlotte stores from 10:30 a.m. to 3 a.m. Sunday through Thursday, and until 4 a.m. on Friday and Saturday, Sundays included. Hours are not confirmed store by store.

Does Cook Out have indoor seating?

Most stores are drive-thru and walk-up only, often with a few outdoor picnic tables. Some newer stores have an indoor dining room with counter service.

Who owns Cook Out?

Founder Morris Reaves owns Cook Out, and his son Jeremy Reaves is CEO. The company is based in Thomasville, North Carolina.

Does Cook Out take cards, deliver or have vegetarian options?

I could not confirm payment methods, delivery through apps such as DoorDash or Uber Eats, or vegetarian options from an official source. Ask at the window or check cookout.com.
