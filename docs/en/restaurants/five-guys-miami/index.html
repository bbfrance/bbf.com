Five Guys Miami: Locations, Prices and Reviews

All 5 Five Guys in Miami plus South Beach: addresses, phones, menu prices checked Nov. 1, 2026, review scores and what to order at each location.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 4, 2026

Five Guys has five restaurants inside Miami city limits, according to the chain’s official store list checked on November 1, 2026, and a sixth just across the causeway in South Beach. My pick is Five Guys Shoppes at Midtown Miami, 3256 Buena Vista Blvd., which has the best review scores of the group by a clear margin. Downtown or in Brickell? Go to Bayside Marketplace at 401 Biscayne Blvd., and order ahead. Staying on the beach? Use 1500 Washington Ave. Budget about $28 for a cheeseburger, regular fries and a drink at online-listed prices, before Florida sales tax and tip.

How many Five Guys are there in Miami?

The city of Miami has five Five Guys restaurants: University Center North, Shoppes at Midtown Miami, Biscayne Blvd (Bayside), London Square and The Falls. That is the official list at restaurants.fiveguys.com/fl/miami, and all five show “In-store Dining, Curbside and Delivery.”

Your map app will probably show more. Five Guys’ Florida directory also has restaurants in Miami Beach, North Miami, Coral Gables, Doral and Hialeah. Those are separate cities, but aggregator sites tag them “Miami” anyway, so the counts never match.

Five Guys Midtown Miami: the best pick

Five Guys Shoppes at Midtown Miami is the one I’d send you to: it has the strongest review scores in the city and sits a few minutes from Wynwood. It’s at 3256 Buena Vista Blvd., Miami, FL 33127, and the phone number is 305-571-8345.

The official store page lists dine-in, takeout, delivery and outdoor seating at lunch and dinner. You can order ahead at order.fiveguys.com. One quirk: the official Miami list links this store’s online menu under the name “North Miami Ave.” It’s the same restaurant.

The numbers back it up. Wanderlog, checked November 1, 2026, gives Midtown 4.3/5 on Google from 931 reviews and 4.5/5 on TripAdvisor from 546. Reviewers keep naming the Bacon Cheeseburger and the fries, and they mention friendly staff. Their complaints are waits at peak hours and service that varies from visit to visit. Ignore the Wanderlog page title, which calls this location “Miami Beach.” It’s on the mainland, in Midtown.

Five Guys Bayside: the closest to Downtown and Brickell

Five Guys Biscayne Blvd is the Downtown Miami location, inside Bayside Marketplace at 401 Biscayne Blvd, Miami, FL 33132. That’s the waterfront mall on Biscayne Bay at the northeast edge of Downtown. Phone: 305-373-3506.

Brickell has no Five Guys of its own, so this is your nearest option there. The official page lists dine-in, takeout and delivery. You can order through the Five Guys website and app, DoorDash or Uber Eats. Sources disagree on the unit number inside the mall. Head for Bayside Marketplace and you’ll find it.

Know this before you go: Bayside has the most mixed reviews of the Miami locations. Aggregator pos.do, checked November 1, 2026, reported 2.5/5 on Yelp (46 reviews), 4.0/5 on TripAdvisor (198) and 4.5/5 on Uber Eats (about 7,000 ratings). The same complaints keep coming up: uneven service, rude cashiers, waits of 20 to more than 35 minutes, a broken milkshake machine, flat sodas and dirty tables. The burgers, fries, portions and customization still get praise.

The gap between a 4.5 on Uber Eats and a 2.5 on Yelp tells the story. The kitchen is fine and the counter is the problem. So order ahead in the app, pick up, and eat on the bayfront.

Five Guys South Beach (Miami Beach)

Yes, South Beach has a Five Guys, at 1500 Washington Ave, Miami Beach, FL 33139, phone 305-538-3807. If your hotel is on the beach, this is the one: no causeway, no traffic.

The official page lists dine-in, takeout, delivery and outdoor seating. Online orders go through order.fiveguys.com/location/five-guys-south-beach/menu. None of my sources gave me verified review scores for this location, so I’m not quoting any.

Five Guys in Kendall and West Miami

Three of Miami’s five locations serve the residential south and west of the county, far from the tourist core. Unless you live nearby, you won’t stumble on them.

  • University Center North: 1433 SW 107th Ave, Miami, FL 33174.
  • London Square: 13440 SW 120th St. Bay 610, Miami, FL 33186, in Kendall.
  • The Falls: 8741 SW 136th St., Miami, FL 33176, phone 786-723-5796. The official page lists dine-in, takeout and delivery.

Closer to Coral Gables? There’s a Five Guys at 1540 S. Dixie Highway (US 1), Coral Gables, FL 33146. That’s a separate city, even though aggregators often tag it “Miami.”

Five Guys Miami opening hours

I couldn’t confirm the opening hours for any Five Guys in Miami, so I’m not publishing them. The official store pages load hours through a widget that didn’t display when I checked. Third-party listings contradict each other: for Midtown alone, one says 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily, and another says 10 a.m. to 11 p.m. Sunday to Wednesday and until midnight Thursday to Saturday.

Check the location’s page at restaurants.fiveguys.com or call the store before you go, especially for a late-night burger. Until the chain confirms its hours, I won’t say any Miami location stays open late.

How much does a meal at Five Guys Miami cost?

A cheeseburger, regular fries and the priciest drink ($4.55) come to $28.41 at these listed prices, before Florida sales tax and tip. Add a milkshake and you’re at $36.80. Delivery apps charge fees on top. That’s a lot for a chain burger, so I’d share the fries.

How Miami compares with Tampa

Miami comes out slightly cheaper than Tampa. The same cheeseburger, regular fries and drink cost $29.73 at Five Guys Kennedy Blvd in Tampa ($15.35 + $9.59 + $4.79, Allmenus, checked October 24, 2026), $1.32 more than in Miami. What to order at Five Guys Miami

Get the Bacon Cheeseburger and share one order of fries. That’s the combination Midtown reviewers recommend most, and I agree: the bacon is what sets the burger apart. The chain says its fries are cut fresh and cooked twice in 100% peanut oil.

Sacks of potatoes stacked below the “Fresh Cut Potatoes, Cooked in 100% Peanut Oil” sign at Five Guys Bayside in Miami, the source of the fries reviewers recommend sharing. Photo: Phillip Pessar, CC BY 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Five\_Guys\_Bayside\_Marketplace\_Downtown\_Miami\_-\_54836616115.jpg (Photo: Phillip Pessar, CC BY 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Five\_Guys\_Bayside\_Marketplace\_Downtown\_Miami\_-\_54836616115.jpg)

  • Burger size: a regular burger has two patties. The “Little” versions have one and cost $4.20 less at the listed prices. For most people, a Little is enough.
  • Toppings: free, and Five Guys claims “over 250,000” ways to build a burger. Ask for grilled onions, grilled mushrooms and jalapeños.
  • Fries: a regular feeds two people and a Little feeds one. Ordering a large for yourself is a waste of money.
  • Milkshake: the chain advertises “over 1,000” combinations. Some Bayside reviewers found the machine out of service.

Five Guys says on its Miami pages that the beef is fresh and that its restaurants have no freezers. To make it at home, see our copycat recipes for the Five Guys Bacon Cheeseburger and the Five Guys Cheeseburger.

Five Guys or an independent burger in Miami?

Five Guys is a dependable, customizable chain burger, but it isn’t the best burger in Miami. If you only have one burger meal in the city, spend it at one of the independents in our guide to the best burgers in Miami. Every guide is also listed on our Miami burger hub.

Five Guys was founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell and their sons, and started franchising in 2002. As of May 2026 it had 1,999 restaurants in 28 countries and territories, 1,517 of them in the United States. The full history and sourced figures are on our Five Guys brand profile.

FAQ

How many Five Guys are there in Miami, and which one should I choose?

The city of Miami has five: University Center North, Shoppes at Midtown Miami, Biscayne Blvd (Bayside), London Square and The Falls. Midtown (3256 Buena Vista Blvd.) has the best review scores. Bayside (401 Biscayne Blvd) is the most convenient downtown. South Beach (1500 Washington Ave) is in the separate city of Miami Beach.

What are Five Guys Miami opening hours?

I couldn’t confirm them. The official pages show hours through a widget, and third-party sites contradict each other. Check the location’s page at restaurants.fiveguys.com or call the store before you go.

How much is a burger at Five Guys in Miami?

Online listings checked November 1, 2026 show a hamburger at $13.43, a cheeseburger at $14.75 and a bacon cheeseburger at $17.15, before tax. These are probably delivery prices and may be higher than in the store.

Is there a Five Guys in South Beach?

Yes. It’s at 1500 Washington Ave, Miami Beach, FL 33139, phone 305-538-3807, with dine-in, takeout, delivery and a patio.

Is there a Five Guys in Brickell?

No. The closest is the Bayside Marketplace location at 401 Biscayne Blvd in Downtown Miami.

Can I get Five Guys delivery or curbside pickup in Miami?

Yes. All five Miami locations list in-store dining, curbside and delivery. You can order on the Five Guys website and app, and Bayside is also on DoorDash and Uber Eats.

Does Five Guys Miami have vegetarian options?

Yes. The Veggie Sandwich and the Grilled Cheese are the vegetarian items. The fries are meat-free too, cooked in peanut oil.

Can I eat at Five Guys with a peanut allergy?

Be very careful. Five Guys cooks its fries in 100% peanut oil, and the Bayside counter stacks boxes of it in plain view. One Bayside review notes there were no free peanuts on offer, but that doesn’t make the store peanut-free. Talk to the staff before ordering anything.
