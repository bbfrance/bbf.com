Junk Smash Burgers Toulouse: Address, Hours, Menu

Junk Smash Burgers Toulouse: 41 rue des Filatiers, open daily 12:00–22:30. The S-to-XXL menu, the founders and how it compares with other Toulouse burgers.

By Thomas, Restaurants writer, FrancePublished November 24, 2026

Junk Smash Burgers Toulouse is at 41 rue des Filatiers, 31000 Toulouse, in the historic center, a short walk from Les Carmes. It is open every day from 12:00 to 22:30, with no afternoon break. This is the only Toulouse branch of Junk, the smash-burger chain Majed Mansour and Wissem Ben Ammar started in Paris in 2021. The menu is the same short ladder as in Paris: S to XXL, one to five smashed beef patties on a brioche bun with American cheese and Junk sauce. I checked the address, hours and menu against Junk’s official pages on 20 October 2026. I have not eaten there yet, so this is a practical guide, not a review.

Where is Junk in Toulouse? Rue des Filatiers, near Les Carmes

Junk’s only Toulouse address is 41 rue des Filatiers, 31000 Toulouse, in the historic center. The street runs toward Les Carmes, so Junk is an easy stop if you are already at the Carmes market or walking down from the center. The official site, PagesJaunes and the Hôtel Albert 1er guide all give the same address.

Opening hours and how to order

Junk Toulouse is open every day of the week from 12:00 to 22:30 and never closes between lunch and dinner. To me, that is the strongest practical argument for the place. Many restaurants in central Toulouse stop serving in the afternoon. Here, a 16:00 burger is not a problem.

Junk Toulouse has listings on Deliveroo and Uber Eats, but neither page would load for me, so I cannot confirm the delivery menu or its prices. I could not find out how big the dining room is either, and I won’t describe a room I haven’t seen.

Who founded Junk? Majed Mansour and Wissem Ben Ammar

Junk was co-founded by Majed Mansour and Wissem Ben Ammar, two Franco-Tunisian entrepreneurs. They built the chain. They are not the chefs of the Toulouse branch, and no source I found names a head chef or manager there, so I am not naming one either.

Online, Mansour is sometimes described as the chef or owner of a single Toulouse burger joint. That is wrong. He is one of the two people behind the whole brand, and Toulouse is one of its 20 French restaurants.

From fast couscous to dark kitchens

Before burgers, the pair launched Graine in Paris in 2013, an early “fast couscous” concept. When Covid hit, they moved into dark kitchens and ran several delivery-only brands. Junk came out of that period, built around a single smash-burger recipe (Business of Bouffe, episode #81, aired 6 July 2026).

Process comes first, and they say so plainly on the podcast: “Nous, c’est processé, on ne rigole pas. Tout est au millimètre” (“With us, everything is a process, we don’t mess around. It’s all down to the millimeter”). In a chain with one recipe and five sizes, that discipline is the product.

From one delivery brand to London, Dubai and New York

Junk launched in 2021 as a delivery-only brand. Its first counter opened in 2022 at 114 rue Montmartre in Paris’s 2nd arrondissement; the full story is in our guide to Junk Smash Burgers in Paris. Zepros, a French trade publication, dates the launch to late 2022 instead.

The growth since then, by date and source:

  • Around 2024: the company, then called My Belly Group and now Junk Group, reported €11.5 million in 2023 revenue across 7 restaurants. Toulouse was not one of them (Zepros).
  • June 2024: Toulouse was still only a planned opening, alongside Nice and Nantes (Restaurant Online).
  • 2024 and 2025: Junk opened in London (2024) and Dubai (2025) (Business of Bouffe).
  • March 2025: €20 million in 2024 revenue, 14 restaurants open and 4 under construction (Zepros, 18 March 2025).
  • June 2026: a 10-year lease on 2,150 sq ft at 452 West Broadway in SoHo, New York, the brand’s first US site. No opening date has been announced (Commercial Observer).
  • 20 October 2026: 20 Junk restaurants in France across 9 cities, 12 of them in Paris. The group counts 24 Junk restaurants open worldwide, 2 under construction and 10 Puffy coffee shops (Junk, Junk Group).

When did Junk open in Toulouse?

Nobody has confirmed the Toulouse opening date, and the sources disagree. Mapstr says 2023. PagesJaunes gives 25 July 2022, which looks like the registration date of the legal entity rather than the day the doors opened.

The trade press points later. Restaurant Online still listed Toulouse as a planned opening in June 2024, and a Zepros piece from around 2024 counted 7 restaurants, none in Toulouse. My best estimate is mid-2024 to 2025. I won’t print a year until Junk or the local press confirms one.

The Junk menu: what S, M, L, XL and XXL mean

At Junk, the size letter tells you the number of patties: an S has one smashed beef patty, an XXL has five. Every size comes on a brioche bun with premium American cheese and Junk sauce, according to the official menu.

Extras are pickles, crispy onions and jalapeños. Sides are panko-fried chicken and double-cooked fries. Dessert is one of four Puffy cookies, from the group’s own cookie-and-coffee brand.

What beef does Junk use?

Junk uses beef from Limousin and Charolais cattle, bought through short supply chains, according to Zepros. Both are French breeds. Zepros reported this for the whole chain around 2024, not for Toulouse specifically.

What the burger is like, according to a Toulouse hotel guide

On 15 October 2026, the Toulouse hotel Hôtel Albert 1er named Junk the best smash burger in the city on its blog. It describes a thin crust on the beef, cheese that melts through the whole burger and a brioche bun that lets the patty lead. It singles out the fries and calls the Puffy cookies “quite pricey”.

Treat that with caution: it is a hotel recommending places to its guests, not independent food criticism. I haven’t eaten at Junk Toulouse yet, so there is no rating here.

How much does a burger cost at Junk Toulouse?

I could not confirm current Toulouse prices. The official menu lists none, and the Deliveroo and Uber Eats pages would not load. That is my main complaint about Junk online: a chain this obsessed with process could easily publish its prices.

The only verified figure covers the whole chain. Around 2024, Zepros reported an average spend of €15 per order in the restaurant and €26 for delivery. Use it as a rough guide, not as a Toulouse price list.

Average spend per order across the Junk chain, around 2024: €15 when eating in and €26 for delivery (Zepros). These are chain-wide figures, not a Toulouse price list. (Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0, data from Zepros (https://resto.zepros.fr/snacking/burger-poulet/gros-appetit-junk))

FAQ: Junk Smash Burgers Toulouse

Who founded Junk, and is Majed Mansour a chef? Majed Mansour and Wissem Ben Ammar co-founded Junk. Both are entrepreneurs who had already launched Graine in Paris in 2013. No source names Mansour as the chef of Junk Toulouse, and no head chef for the Toulouse branch is publicly named.

Where is Junk in Toulouse, and what are its hours? It is at 41 rue des Filatiers, 31000 Toulouse, near Les Carmes. It is open Monday to Sunday from 12:00 to 22:30 with no afternoon break (official site, checked 20 October 2026).

When did Junk open in Toulouse? The date is unconfirmed. Sources give 2022, 2023 or later. Trade press still listed Toulouse as planned in June 2024, so mid-2024 to 2025 is the most likely window.

What do S, M, L, XL and XXL mean at Junk? They give the number of smashed beef patties: one for an S, up to five for an XXL. Each comes with American cheese and Junk sauce on a brioche bun.

How much does a Junk burger cost in Toulouse? Current Toulouse prices could not be verified. Chain-wide, Zepros reported an average spend of €15 per order in the restaurant and €26 for delivery, around 2024.

Is Junk Toulouse halal? The official Junk site does not mention halal, and I found no certification. The only claims come from third-party sites about other branches. Ask the restaurant directly before you order.

Can you eat in at Junk Toulouse? It is a burger counter, but I could not confirm how much seating there is. Call ahead before coming as a group.

Does Junk Toulouse deliver? Junk Toulouse has listings on Deliveroo and Uber Eats, but I couldn’t open them to check the delivery menu. Junk has an exclusive delivery partnership with Deliveroo in London, Paris and Dubai, according to Zepros (March 2025).

Is Junk a franchise? No. The founders say every Junk restaurant is company-owned (Business of Bouffe, July 2026).

What beef does Junk use? Limousin and Charolais beef from short supply chains, according to Zepros (chain-wide, around 2024).

Is there a vegetarian burger at Junk? Yes. The menu includes a Veggie burger with a plant-based patty.

What are the best smash burgers in Toulouse besides Junk? Aggregators recommend Superette (7 place de l’Estrapade) and Black and White Burger. Our Toulouse burger guide lists only addresses we have verified.
