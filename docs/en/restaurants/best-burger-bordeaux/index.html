Best Burger in Bordeaux: Top 10 Spots for 2026

Petit Mignon, Kokomo, Funky Burger and 7 more: the best burger in Bordeaux, France for 2026, with addresses, hours, dated prices and halal or veggie picks.

By Thomas, Restaurants writer, FrancePublished October 1, 2026Updated November 11, 2026

The best burger in Bordeaux in 2026 is at Petit Mignon, 33 rue Saint-Rémi in the Saint-Pierre quarter of the old town. This small burger-and-tartare bistro rates 4.8/5 on Google from 2,053 reviews, as shown on Wanderlog on 11 November 2026: the top score in my list, on a large review base. After it come Kokomo on place Fernand Lafargue, Funky Burger on rue du Loup, a pioneer since 2009, and Edmond Pure Burger near Gambetta.

I checked all 10 addresses on 11 November 2026 against their own websites, their menus and the Bordeaux press. Where a fact is missing or shaky, I say so: I have no reliable price for Junk, PNY, Black and White or Five Guys. This guide is part of our Bordeaux burger page and our burger city guides in the Restaurants section.

How I ranked Bordeaux’s burger restaurants

This ranking is built from official websites, menus, local press and dated Google ratings, not from tastings. I did not eat at these 10 places for this guide, and I won’t pretend I did. Every fact links to its source, and I rechecked everything on 11 November 2026.

Four things push a place up. First, it must be open and verifiable in November 2026, on its own site or a source dated this year. Second, a high Google rating on a large number of reviews. Third, what goes into the bun: beef origin, house-made buns, cheeses. Fourth, when two places are close, an independent ranks above a chain. Every chain is labelled as one.

I ignored sponsored entries in other lists. The Quoi faire à Bordeaux top of 16 June 2026, for example, has 2 sponsored entries out of 6. Edmond’s Gault&Millau page is sponsored content too, not an award. As far as I could find, no Bordeaux burger venue holds a Michelin, Fooding, Petit Futé or Gault&Millau distinction.

One last limit: Google Maps, Tripadvisor and Yelp blocked my research tools, so Google ratings come from Wanderlog, seen on 11 November 2026 unless stated otherwise. They are customer ratings, not my verdict.

1. Petit Mignon, Saint-Pierre: the top-rated burger and tartare bistro

Petit Mignon is the highest-rated burger restaurant in my Bordeaux list: 4.8/5 on Google from 2,053 reviews (Wanderlog, seen 11 November 2026). TheFork gives it 9.3/10 from 746 reviews, according to Mappy.

On Instagram the restaurant calls itself a “spécialiste du tartare et burger sur Bordeaux”, a tartare and burger specialist. The room is small with an open kitchen, and reviewers advise booking, which you can do on TheFork.

The menu shown on Sluurpy is neither official nor dated. It lists the Burger Végé and the Kokorico at €14, the Burger Gourmand at €16, the Tout cheddar at €16–18, Le Nordique and the Burger du Chef at €18, and Le délice at €22. Budget about €25 a head, according to Mappy and TheFork. Sources disagree on opening hours, so call before you go.

  • Address: 33 rue Saint-Rémi, 33000 Bordeaux (Saint-Pierre)
  • Phone: +33 5 56 81 06 83
  • Hours: not confirmed (lunch and dinner per Wanderlog, lunch only per Sluurpy)
  • Burgers: from €14 (Burger Végé, Kokorico) to €22 (Le délice), Sluurpy menu, undated
  • Veggie: Burger Végé on the menu
  • Booking: advised, via TheFork
  • Link: no website; Facebook page

2. Kokomo, Saint-Pierre: The Beast with aged Aubrac beef

Kokomo is the independent with the most Google reviews in my list: 4.4/5 from 3,158 reviews (Wanderlog, seen 11 November 2026). It is open every day from 12:00 to 23:00, handy if you land late.

Its flagship, The Beast, costs €13: aged Aubrac beef, cheddar, an onion ring, caramelised smoked bacon and sriracha mayo (Quoi faire à Bordeaux, 16 June 2026). In January 2025, Le Bonbon reported that Deliveroo named The Beast its most-ordered burger outside Paris. I pass on the platform’s claim as reported; I could not check it.

For vegetarians, the Champ O also costs €13, and any burger can be made vegetarian for €2 more. The kids’ menu is €10.90. The kokomoburgers.com website did not load when I checked.

  • Address: 9 place Fernand Lafargue, 33000 Bordeaux (Saint-Pierre)
  • Phone: +33 5 57 30 28 85
  • Hours: daily, 12:00–23:00 (Quoi faire à Bordeaux, June 2026, and Wanderlog)
  • Signature burger: The Beast, €13 (June 2026)
  • Veggie: Champ O, €13; any burger vegetarian for +€2

3. Funky Burger, rue du Loup: Bordeaux’s burger pioneer since 2009

Funky Burger opened in 2009, which makes it one of Bordeaux’s first burger restaurants, according to its official website. It uses certified French beef, artisan bread and fresh fries.

The bestseller is the Corleone: a thick patty, aged cheddar and bacon. The names are playful: the Pikachu, the Fourmidable with Fourme d’Ambert blue cheese, and “Elle est où la poulette ?”, a joke from the French TV series Kaamelott. The first combo cost €14 in January 2026, in a 46-seat room (Le Bonbon, 23 January 2026). On Google it holds 4.4/5 from 1,520 reviews (Wanderlog, seen 11 November 2026).

Plan around the hours: Funky is closed on Sundays and Mondays.

  • Address: 5 rue du Loup, 33000 Bordeaux (city centre)
  • Phone: +33 5 56 81 55 50
  • Hours (official site): Tuesday to Saturday, 12:00–14:00 and 19:00–22:00; closed Sunday and Monday
  • Signature burger: Corleone; combo from €14 (January 2026)
  • Veggie: vegetarian dishes on the menu; no halal claim

4. Edmond Pure Burger, Gambetta: house buns and Espelette pepper

Edmond Pure Burger bakes its own buns and promises “viande 100 % française”, 100% French meat, according to its official menu, seen on 11 November 2026 (the restaurant does not date its prices). It is the only menu in Bordeaux proper where I found a south-west touch.

L’Edmond costs €13: beef, raclette, caramelised onion and smoked pork belly. L’Edith, at the same price, pairs sheep’s-milk tomme with cherry jam and an Espelette pepper sauce, the Basque chilli. Around them: L’Edgard with Saint-Nectaire and pepper sauce, L’Edimbourg, L’Edwin and the chicken Edward, all €13. The Cheese Burger is €10.50, the Bacon Cheese €11.50, fries €4.

Edmond took part in the Burger Lovers Festival in Bègles, 9–13 September 2026. I could not find the results. Google rating: 4.1/5 from 782 reviews (Wanderlog, seen 11 November 2026). The old cours Victor Hugo branch is permanently closed, so head to Gambetta.

  • Address: 34 rue du Palais Gallien, 33000 Bordeaux (Gambetta)
  • Phone: +33 5 56 81 77 93
  • Hours (official site): Monday–Friday 11:30–14:20 and 18:30–22:30; Saturday 11:30–23:00; Sunday 11:30–14:30 and 18:30–22:20
  • Signature burger: L’Edmond, €13 (official menu, seen 11 Nov 2026)
  • Veggie: L’Edenté, €13

5. Les Burgers de Colette, Chartrons, Victoire and Grand Théâtre: a veggie twin for every burger

Founded in 2019, the French chain Les Burgers de Colette now runs in 9 cities and has 3 addresses in Bordeaux (coletteburgers.fr). Its menu is the only one in this top 10 that carries a date: “© Colette 2026”.

Prices run from €8 for the Smashy Suzanne, 2 smashed 60 g patties with American cheese, to €13. The Original Colette costs €12: 110 g of beef, cheddar, raclette and bacon. The Marco, the burger of the moment at €13, brings stracciatella, pistachio pesto and mortadella. The Peppery Claudine and the Classic Ginette are €10, the Cheesy Brigitte €13, fries from €2 and the kids’ menu €8.

Its real strength: every burger comes in a vegetarian version at the same price, made with Happyvore plant-based patties. The Grand Théâtre branch serves until 02:00 from Thursday to Saturday, the latest burger in the city centre I found. Google rating for the Victoire branch: 4.5/5 from 1,949 reviews (Wanderlog, seen 11 November 2026).

  • Chartrons: 23 cours Portal, 33000 Bordeaux; Monday–Thursday 11:45–14:15 and 18:30–22:00, Friday–Saturday 11:45–22:30, Sunday 11:45–22:00 (official page)
  • Victoire: 276 rue Sainte-Catherine, 33000 Bordeaux; +33 5 57 67 97 32; Monday–Wednesday and Sunday 11:30–23:00, Thursday–Saturday 11:30–23:30 (official page)
  • Grand Théâtre: 10 rue des Piliers de Tutelle, 33000 Bordeaux; Monday–Wednesday and Sunday 11:30–22:00, Thursday–Saturday 11:30–02:00 (official page)
  • Signature burger: Original Colette, €12 (official 2026 menu)
  • Veggie: Happyvore version of every burger, same price

6. Junk Bordeaux, Gambetta: Paris smash burgers on rue Judaïque

Junk, the Paris smash burger chain, took over 7 rue Judaïque, the former Upper Burger address (junkburgers.com). It serves lunch and dinner every day.

The brand uses French Charolais and Limousin beef, a French brioche bun and a house sauce. Its bestsellers are the Truffle and the M. I could not verify prices at the Bordeaux branch, so I quote none. For the menu and the chain’s story, read my guide to Junk smash burgers in Paris and my Junk Burger Lyon guide.

  • Address: 7 rue Judaïque, 33000 Bordeaux (Gambetta)
  • Hours (official site): daily, 12:00–14:30 and 19:00–22:30
  • Bestsellers: Truffle and M (smash burgers)
  • Price: not verified; no halal claim

7. Big Fernand Bordeaux, Sainte-Catherine: the French chain open every day

You’ll find Big Fernand at 5 rue Guiraude, inside the Promenade Sainte-Catherine shopping district, open daily from 12:00 to 22:30. It is the only Big Fernand in the city, opened on 26 April 2018.

The chain serves its national menu of “hamburgés” made with French beef and raw-milk cheeses, with Le Bartholomé as its star. Budget €10–20 a head, according to Sluurpy and Restaurant Guru. Google rating: 4.5/5 from 3,606 reviews, as shown on Restaurant Guru on 31 October 2026. Everything else is in my guide to Big Fernand Bordeaux: address, hours and menu.

  • Address: 5 rue Guiraude, 33000 Bordeaux (Promenade Sainte-Catherine)
  • Phone: +33 5 57 14 94 77
  • Hours: daily, 12:00–22:30; closed 25 December, 1 January and 1 May
  • Signature burger: Le Bartholomé; €10–20 a head
  • Veggie: Le Gabin and Le Lucien

8. PNY Chartrons: a Paris burger on the Chartrons market square

PNY is a Paris chain that opened on place du Marché des Chartrons in early September 2022, its 12th restaurant, with 140 seats (Le Bonbon, 2022).

The brand puts The Return of the Cowboy forward: a flame-grilled patty, old-fashioned cheddar, an onion fritter, smoked bacon and “Sunday” barbecue sauce. It says it uses a Somerset cheddar aged 6 to 18 months (pnyburger.com). The price of about €13 quoted at the 2022 opening is not a current price.

I have no reliable opening hours. PNY’s site shows none for Bordeaux, and the only recent source, the Quoi faire à Bordeaux top of June 2026, places PNY right under a “Sponsorisé” (sponsored) label, so I don’t repeat its hours.

  • Address: 10 place du Marché des Chartrons, 33000 Bordeaux (Chartrons)
  • Signature burger: The Return of the Cowboy
  • Price and hours: not verified for 2026

9. Black and White Burger, Mériadeck: the certified halal burger

Black and White Burger is the only place in this top 10 whose brand states that all its beef and chicken are halal-certified, with no pork (bnwburger.com). The chain’s Bordeaux branch is on rue Claude Bonnier, near the Mériadeck stop on tram line A.

The only prices and hours I found for this branch come from a sponsored entry in another list, so I don’t repeat them. Check the menu on site.

  • Address: 16 rue Claude Bonnier, 33000 Bordeaux (Mériadeck, tram A)
  • Halal: yes, stated by the brand for all beef and chicken, no pork
  • Prices and hours: not verified

10. Five Guys Bordeaux Saint-Rémi, Saint-Pierre: the only Five Guys in Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Five Guys Bordeaux Saint-Rémi is the American chain’s only restaurant in the Nouvelle-Aquitaine region (Five Guys France). It opened on 31 October 2025 at 43 rue Saint-Rémi, in the former Ganache premises.

It spreads over 2 floors and 380 m², with 114 seats (Bordeaux Secret, 20 October 2025). It shares the street with Petit Mignon, so you can compare a bistro and a chain 10 doors apart. I did not verify its hours, prices or rating. Want to make one at home? My colleague Julia’s Five Guys cheeseburger copycat recipe shows how, and the Five Guys brand profile covers the chain’s history.

  • Address: 43 rue Saint-Rémi, 33000 Bordeaux (Saint-Pierre)
  • Opened: 31 October 2025
  • Hours, prices, rating: not verified

Worth the trip outside Bordeaux: two burgers beyond the city limits

Two places outside the city of Bordeaux are worth the trip but stay out of the ranking: Le Bistrot du Lac in Bruges, by Bordeaux Lac, and Sabora Burgers in Libourne.

Le Bistrot du Lac by Flow is at the Bordeaux Lac campsite, boulevard Jacques Chaban-Delmas, 33520 Bruges. Its menu has the most local references I found. Le Gavé Bordelais costs €21: pulled Gascon pork, onion confit with grape must, mayonnaise made with Fine Bordeaux brandy, and Saint-Nectaire AOP cheese. Every burger can be made halal on request, and the restaurant opens Tuesday to Saturday, 12:15–13:45 and 19:30–21:45 (Quoi faire à Bordeaux, 16 June 2026).

Sabora Burgers is at 7 rue Gambetta, 33500 Libourne. It rates 4.8/5 on Google from 604 reviews (Wanderlog, seen 11 November 2026).

New in autumn 2026: Bill’s Burger near Gare Saint-Jean

Bill’s Burger opened in October 2026 at 13 place Casablanca, 33800 Bordeaux, in the Belcier quarter by Gare Saint-Jean station (Quoi faire à Bordeaux, 14 October 2026). It is the easiest answer to “a burger straight off the train”.

The Bill’s Tasty, a smash burger with fries and a topping, costs €17.90. The menu also lists the Original, the Smoke and the Frenchy with raclette (bills-burger.fr, Instagram @billsburgerfr). I don’t know its hours yet. It is too new to rank, and I will look at it again in the next update.

The year also brought two big burger festivals. The Champions Burger ran at Aushopping Bordeaux Lac from 18 June to 5 July 2026, and its European title went to Godeo’s “Umami Gold”, which is not a Bordeaux burger (Le Bonbon, 17 June 2026). At the Burger Lovers Festival in Bègles, 9–13 September 2026, every burger cost €12.95 (Quoi faire à Bordeaux).

Best smash, halal and veggie burgers in Bordeaux

For a smash burger, go to Junk on rue Judaïque or order Colette’s €8 Smashy Suzanne. For halal, Black and White Burger in Mériadeck is the only brand that states it. For vegetarians, Colette makes every burger in a plant-based version at the same price.

  • Smash: Junk (Truffle, M), Colette’s Smashy Suzanne (€8, 2026 menu), the Bill’s Tasty near the station (€17.90 with fries). Smash Smash, 277 rue Sainte-Catherine, was registered in November 2022 according to a franchise directory; aggregators call it halal, which I could not confirm, and its €6.50–10.50 prices date from 2023.
  • Halal: Black and White Burger (brand-stated); Le Bistrot du Lac in Bruges, on request.
  • Veggie: Colette (Happyvore version of every burger), Edmond’s L’Edenté (€13), Kokomo’s Champ O (€13, or any burger vegetarian for +€2), Funky Burger’s vegetarian dishes, Big Fernand’s Le Gabin and Le Lucien.

How much does a burger cost in Bordeaux in 2026?

In late 2026 a burger on its own costs €8–13 on Bordeaux menus, a burger-and-fries combo €14–18, and a bistro or out-of-town burger €14–22.

Burger and fries combo €14–18 Funky €14 (January 2026), Bill’s Tasty €17.90 (October 2026)

FAQ: best burger in Bordeaux

Where is the best burger in Bordeaux?

At Petit Mignon, 33 rue Saint-Rémi, in the Saint-Pierre quarter. This burger and tartare bistro rates 4.8/5 on Google from 2,053 reviews (Wanderlog, 11 November 2026). Book ahead: the room is small.

What is the best smash burger in Bordeaux?

Junk, 7 rue Judaïque near Gambetta, is the only smash burger specialist chain in the ranking. On a budget, Colette’s Smashy Suzanne costs €8 (2026 menu). Near the station, Bill’s Burger serves the Bill’s Tasty at €17.90 with fries.

Where can I eat a halal burger in Bordeaux?

At Black and White Burger, 16 rue Claude Bonnier in Mériadeck: the brand states that all its beef and chicken are halal-certified. Aggregators call Smash Smash on rue Sainte-Catherine halal, without confirmation. Outside the city, Le Bistrot du Lac in Bruges makes its burgers halal on request.

Where can vegans and vegetarians eat a burger in Bordeaux?

At Les Burgers de Colette, which makes every burger in a Happyvore plant-based version at the same price. Edmond serves L’Edenté and Kokomo the Champ O, at €13 each. Wild Note Vegan Burger, often listed online, is permanently closed.

Which Bordeaux burger places are open on Sunday or late at night?

Colette Grand Théâtre serves until 02:00 from Thursday to Saturday. Colette Victoire serves until 23:30 from Thursday to Saturday. Kokomo is open daily until 23:00 and Big Fernand daily until 22:30. Edmond opens on Sunday evening. Funky Burger is closed on Sunday and Monday.

How much does a burger cost in Bordeaux in 2026?

Expect €8–13 for a burger on its own, €14–18 for a combo with fries, and €14–22 in a bistro or outside the city. These ranges come from official menus, 2026 press and, for Petit Mignon, an unofficial, undated menu.

Where can I eat a burger near Gare Saint-Jean?

At Bill’s Burger, 13 place Casablanca, which opened in October 2026 in the Belcier quarter next to the station. Its hours are not confirmed yet.

Where can I eat a burger in Chartrons?

At Les Burgers de Colette, 23 cours Portal, or PNY, 10 place du Marché des Chartrons. Colette Chartrons opens every day: lunch and dinner Monday to Thursday, all day Friday to Sunday. I have no reliable hours for PNY.

Is there a burger place at Bordeaux Lac?

Le Bistrot du Lac by Flow, at the Bordeaux Lac campsite, is in the town of Bruges, outside Bordeaux. Its Gavé Bordelais costs €21. It opens Tuesday to Saturday, lunch and dinner.

Is there a Five Guys in Bordeaux?

Yes, at 43 rue Saint-Rémi, opened on 31 October 2025. It is the only Five Guys in Nouvelle-Aquitaine, with 114 seats over 2 floors.
