Burger King Goa: Porvorim and Calangute Timings

Both Burger King outlets in Goa, Porvorim and Calangute: addresses, timings, phone numbers, rupee prices and the beef-free menu, checked 18 November 2026.

By Arjun, Restaurants writer, IndiaPublished November 27, 2026

Burger King Goa has two restaurants, both in Bardez taluka, North Goa. The Porvorim outlet sits on the NH66 near the Mall De Goa and is open 7 am to 11:59 pm every day. The second is at Casa Sofiana on the Candolim–Calangute–Baga beach strip and is open 10 am to 11:45 pm every day. There is no official outlet in Panaji, Margao or Vasco. I checked every address, timing and phone number below against Burger King India’s official store pages on 18 November 2026. My advice in one line: stay on the coast, eat at Calangute; need breakfast or a highway stop, Porvorim.

How many Burger King outlets are there in Goa?

There are two Burger King outlets in Goa, both in North Goa: Porvorim (NH66, near the Mall De Goa) and Candolim–Calangute–Baga (Casa Sofiana). That is the full list on the official Burger King India locator for Goa, checked on 18 November 2026.

Both sit on roads most visitors already use. Porvorim is on the highway between Panaji and the northern beaches; the Calangute outlet is right in the busiest beach belt. For the rest of the state’s burger scene, from beach shacks to choris pao, see our guide to the best burgers in Goa.

Burger King Porvorim, near Mall De Goa

Burger King Porvorim is a roadside outlet on the NH66 at Alto Porvorim, not a food-court counter inside the mall. It is about a minute’s walk from the Hotel Grand Kadamba, which is itself some five minutes on foot from the Mall De Goa. Magicpin gives the same address as “Oslinda by Pinto Rosario, 70/6 National Highway 66, Pilerne, Mall De Goa” (Magicpin, Expedia).

Trust the official page over Magicpin here. Magicpin lists another phone number and opening hours of 4:15 am to 6 am, which cannot be right. Call +91 88798 08617 instead. The official hours run from 7 am to 11:59 pm, seven days a week. That makes Porvorim the only Burger King in Goa for an early breakfast. The official page also gives the postcode 403501, while most Porvorim addresses use 403521, so I have printed it as published.

Burger King Calangute, Candolim and Baga

Burger King Candolim–Calangute–Baga is on the ground floor of Casa Sofiana, in the beach belt that joins the three villages. It opens daily from 10 am to 11:45 pm and has dine-in, according to its official store page. If your hotel is on the coast, this is the one to walk to.

Burger King Goa menu: what you can order

The Burger King Goa menu is the Indian national menu: burgers and wraps, Whoppers, premium burgers, chicken wings, sides, desserts and BK Cafe drinks, with a large vegetarian range. Both official Goa store pages list the same categories:

  • Burgers & Wraps
  • Whopper
  • King Premium Burger
  • Chicken Wings
  • Sides & Beverages
  • Desserts
  • BK Cafe

The offer highlighted on both official pages in November 2026 was the Crispy Veg Meal at ₹99.

Is there beef at Burger King in Goa?

No. Burger King India has served a beef-free menu since it launched, and nothing on the official Goa store pages points to a beef burger. That catches some visitors out, because Goa’s Catholic food tradition puts beef on far more local menus than elsewhere in India. When Burger King opened its first Indian restaurant on 9 November 2014, at Select Citywalk in Saket, New Delhi, the menu had a Veg Whopper, a Chicken Whopper, a Mutton Whopper and the King’s Paneer Melt, made on separate vegetarian and non-vegetarian production lines (Ahram Online, Food Engineering, Ausfoodnews).

The Indian Whopper keeps the flame-grilled build of the original and swaps the beef for chicken or a vegetarian patty. For the classic beef version, my colleague Clara takes it apart layer by layer in her Whopper ingredients and copycat recipe.

Vegetarian options at Burger King Goa

Vegetarians get a full menu at Burger King Goa, not a token option: every price I could source for Porvorim is for a veg item, from the ₹55 Crispy Veg Burger to the Veg Makhani Burst Burger and the BK Veg Pizza Puff. The Crispy Veg range follows the Indian aloo tikki style, with a spiced potato patty.

Payment, parking and delivery

Both Burger King outlets in Goa take cards and have free on-site parking. Porvorim also accepts UPI and has street parking.

The official Goa pages say nothing about delivery or takeaway, so I cannot confirm whether Swiggy or Zomato deliver from either outlet, or where. Open the app with your hotel address and see whether Burger King appears. I found nothing either on wheelchair access, a drive-thru or table booking.

Which Burger King is closest to the beaches?

The Casa Sofiana outlet in Candolim–Calangute–Baga is the only Burger King in Goa on the beach strip itself. Porvorim is inland on the NH66: the better stop if you are driving between Panaji and the northern beaches, or shopping at the Mall De Goa.

Burger King in India: the chain behind the Goa outlets

Burger King’s two Goa outlets belong to a network of more than 600 restaurants in India, run by Restaurant Brands Asia. The 600th opened at GTB Nagar in Delhi in September 2026 (Restaurant India). The company reported 607 restaurants in 144 cities at the end of Q2 of fiscal year 2027, and 619 by the date of its earnings call (Multibagg). Two of them in a state that draws millions of visitors a year is a thin presence.

Burger King’s growth in India: the first restaurant at Select Citywalk, Saket (New Delhi) on 9 November 2014, the 600th at GTB Nagar (Delhi) in September 2026, 607 restaurants in 144 cities at the end of Q2 FY2027, and 619 by the earnings call. Goa has 2 of them. (Chart: Burger Breton, data from Ahram Online, Restaurant India and Multibagg)

Since 7 July 2026, Restaurant Brands Asia has been controlled by Inspira Global through Lenexis Foodworks, which holds 41.78%. India became Burger King’s 100th country in 2014, through a joint venture between BK AsiaPac and Everstone Capital. Burger King Goa FAQ

How many Burger King restaurants are there in Goa?

There are two: Porvorim, on the NH66 near the Mall De Goa, and Candolim–Calangute–Baga, at Casa Sofiana. Both are in Bardez, North Goa, according to the official locator checked on 18 November 2026.

Is there a Burger King in Panaji (Panjim) or Margao?

No. The official Burger King India locator lists no outlet in Panaji, Margao or Vasco. A “Bus Depot Panjim” listing still shows on Justdial, but it is not on the official locator, so treat it as closed or unconfirmed.

What are the Burger King Porvorim timings?

Burger King Porvorim is open Monday to Sunday from 7 am to 11:59 pm. Burger King Candolim–Calangute–Baga is open Monday to Sunday from 10 am to 11:45 pm. Both were checked on the official store pages on 18 November 2026.

Does Burger King in Goa serve beef?

No beef burger appears on the official Goa store pages. Burger King India has had a beef-free menu since it opened in 2014, with chicken, vegetarian and paneer options instead.

How much does a meal at Burger King Goa cost?

Magicpin gives an average cost for two of ₹400 at Burger King Porvorim. On 18 November 2026, veg burgers were on offer from ₹55, and the official Crispy Veg Meal deal was ₹99.

Does Burger King deliver in Goa on Swiggy or Zomato?

I could not confirm it. The official Goa store pages do not mention delivery, so check Swiggy or Zomato with your exact address before ordering.

Can I pay by card or UPI at Burger King Goa?

Yes. Porvorim takes cash, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, debit cards, UPI and online payment. Candolim–Calangute–Baga takes cash, credit and debit cards and online payment.

Is there parking at Burger King Goa?

Yes. Both outlets have free on-site parking, and Porvorim also has street parking.
