Burger King New Delhi: Outlets, Hours and Prices

Burger King New Delhi: addresses, phones and official hours for Janpath, Inner Circle E8, Rajiv Chowk metro and Saket, plus menu prices and reviews.

By Arjun, Restaurants writer, IndiaPublished November 5, 2026

Burger King New Delhi has five outlets worth knowing. Four are in and around Connaught Place: Janpath (open 8 a.m. to 1 a.m.), Inner Circle E8, and two counters at Rajiv Chowk metro station. The fifth is at Select Citywalk in Saket, the mall where the chain opened its first Indian restaurant in 2014. There is no beef and no pork on the menu. Whoppers come with chicken or a vegetarian patty, and a Chicken Whopper was listed at ₹219 on Zomato when I checked on 22 October 2026. Addresses, phone numbers and official hours come first. The menu, prices and what reviewers say follow.

Burger King Connaught Place: Janpath, the late-night pick

Burger King Janpath is the Connaught Place outlet to choose for a burger after dinner. It is open every day from 8 a.m. to 1 a.m., two hours later than the metro counters.

  • Address: No 68, Ground Floor, Janpath, Connaught Place, New Delhi 110001
  • Phone: +91 96532 70228
  • Hours: daily, 8 a.m. – 1 a.m.
  • Services: dine-in, takeaway and delivery through the BK app
  • Payment: cash, credit or debit card, online payment
  • Rating on the official page: 4.4/5

Janpath sits on the main shopping street south of the Connaught Place circles, which makes it an easy stop after an afternoon at the market stalls. Source: official Janpath outlet page, checked 22 October 2026.

Burger King Inner Circle E8: the sit-down outlet in CP

Burger King Inner Circle E8 is the Connaught Place outlet with room to sit, spread over a ground floor and a mezzanine. Yappe puts it about 130 metres from Rajiv Chowk metro station.

  • Address: No E8, Ground & Mezzanine Floor, Rajiv Chowk, Inner Circle, Connaught Place, New Delhi 110001
  • Phone: +91 11 4350 9889
  • Hours: opens at 9 a.m.
  • Payment: cash, debit card, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UPI and online payment
  • Cost for two: about ₹500 (EazyDiner) or about ₹400 (Zomato)

The closing time is the problem here. The official page lists a 9 a.m. opening, and its text also mentions a late slot until 2 a.m. Yappe gives 9 a.m. to 1 a.m., while EazyDiner says 9 a.m. to 11 p.m. Three sources, three answers. Ring +91 11 4350 9889 before you go late at night. EazyDiner also says there is no parking, so come by metro.

Ratings for E8 are consistent across sources. The official page shows 4.2/5. Google shows 4.2/5 from 8,369 reviews, as aggregated by Yappe. EazyDiner shows 4.2/5 from 43 reviews. Source for address and hours: official E8 page, checked 22 October 2026.

Burger King Rajiv Chowk metro: two quick counters

Burger King has two counters at Rajiv Chowk metro station. RCK 4 is inside the station and opens earliest, at 7 a.m. RCK 01 is just outside it and opens at 8 a.m. Both close at 11 p.m.

Rajiv Chowk RCK 4 (inside the station)

  • Address: Unit No RCK 4, Metro Station, Rajiv Chowk, New Delhi 110001
  • Phone: +91 86558 85630
  • Hours: Monday to Sunday, 7 a.m. – 11 p.m.
  • Payment: cash, card, UPI and online payment
  • Rating on the official page: 4.1/5

Source: official RCK 4 page, checked 22 October 2026.

Rajiv Chowk RCK 01 (near the station)

  • Address: Shop No RCK 01, near Rajiv Chowk Metro Station, Rajiv Chowk, New Delhi 110001
  • Phone: +91 11 2341 5042
  • Hours: Monday to Sunday, 8 a.m. – 11 p.m.
  • Rating on the official page: 4.2/5
  • Google rating: 4.2/5 from 13,890 reviews (Yappe)

Yappe puts RCK 01 about 150 metres from the station, with a different phone number and a 7 a.m. opening. I have kept the figures from the official RCK 01 page. Treat both counters as takeaway first: reviewers describe very little seating and a cramped kiosk at peak hours.

Burger King Saket Select Citywalk: where Burger King India began

Burger King Saket is on the second floor of Select Citywalk, the mall where Burger King opened its first restaurant in India on 9 November 2014. I could not confirm whether today’s outlet is the same unit as the 2014 original, so treat it as the same mall, not necessarily the same shop.

  • Address: No 16, S01, 2nd Floor, Select City Walk, Saket, New Delhi 110017 (Swiggy Dineout lists it as Food Stall No. 16, My Square, S-01, Second Floor, Select Citywalk, A-3, District Centre, Saket)
  • Phone: +91 79770 39081
  • Hours: Monday to Sunday, 11 a.m. – 11 p.m.
  • Payment: cash, debit card, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UPI and online payment
  • Parking: mall parking and valet, per Swiggy Dineout
  • Cost for two: about ₹500 (Swiggy Dineout)

Its official page shows a 4.6/5 rating. Swiggy Dineout shows 4.2/5 from about 4,000 ratings. Swiggy also lists the outlet as open until 2 a.m., but the official Saket page says 11 p.m., and I have kept the official time. Popular orders on Swiggy are the Chicken Whopper, Veg Whopper, Crispy Veg, Chicken Keema Burger, Keema Fries and Strawberry Sundae.

How many Burger King outlets are there in New Delhi?

Burger King has more than 100 restaurants across Delhi NCR, according to its operator, Restaurant Brands Asia, in September 2026. The official locator shows about 60 listings under “New Delhi” across 10 pages. Some are duplicates, and the total takes in addresses across the whole of Delhi, such as Rohini, Model Town and Laxmi Nagar, so I am not quoting an exact figure for the city.

The 100-plus milestone came on 10 September 2026, when Restaurant Brands Asia opened India’s 600th Burger King at GTB Nagar, Delhi. The chain is now in more than 140 Indian cities and is aiming for 700 restaurants by December 2026 (Restaurant India, Sahi).

Burger King New Delhi menu: a Whopper without beef

Burger King in New Delhi serves no beef and no pork. The menu is built on chicken, vegetarian patties and paneer. Cow slaughter is banned in Delhi, so the beef Whopper the rest of the world knows has never been on the menu here.

Every official New Delhi page I checked splits the menu into the same seven categories: Burgers & Wraps, Whopper, King Premium Burger, Chicken Wings, Sides & Beverages, BK Cafe and Desserts. Dishes that come up again and again on delivery platforms include:

  • Whoppers: Chicken Whopper, Veg Whopper, Peri Peri Chicken Whopper, Chicken Whopper Jr.
  • Chicken burgers: Chicken Royale, Chicken Keema Burger, Keema Crunch
  • Vegetarian burgers: Crispy Veg, Veg Makhani Burst
  • Sides and sweets: fries, Peri Peri fries, Keema Fries, Chocolate Thick Shake, Strawberry Sundae

magicpin also lists a Mutton Whopper for the Rajiv Chowk outlet, but I could not confirm on an official source that it is on the menu today. For how the Indian Whopper compares with the beef original, my colleague Clara’s Whopper ingredients and copycat recipe breaks down the classic build.

The 2014 launch menu

Burger King launched in India with a Veg Whopper, a Mutton Whopper, a Chicken Whopper, the Chicken Tandoor Grill and the King’s Paneer Melt. There was no beef on the menu. India became the brand’s 100th country through a joint venture between BK AsiaPac and Everstone Capital (Ausfoodnews, Ahram Online).

Vegetarian options and separate kitchens

Burger King’s New Delhi menu offers several vegetarian burgers, including the Veg Whopper, Crispy Veg and Veg Makhani Burst. When the chain launched in 2014, it ran separate vegetarian and non-vegetarian production lines with separate staff (Inside Retail). I found no recent official statement that this separation is still in place, so ask at the counter if it matters to you.

What reviewers like: low prices, a good vegetarian choice, friendly staff, self-order kiosks and the handy location near the metro.

What reviewers dislike: crowds and waits at peak times, especially at weekends. They also mention little seating, a cramped metro kiosk, cleanliness that could be better, delivery orders arriving cold or incomplete, and air conditioning that does not keep up.

My verdict: use the metro counters for a takeaway between trains, Inner Circle E8 if you want a seat in CP, and Janpath once everything else has shut. Saket has the history, but its official page shows the same menu as the CP outlets, so I would not cross the city just for it. I found no usable TripAdvisor or Yelp pages for these outlets. Review sources: Yappe (E8, RCK 01), magicpin, and the official outlet pages, checked 22 October 2026.

Who runs Burger King in India?

Burger King India is run by Restaurant Brands Asia, which has been controlled by Inspira Global since 7 July 2026. Through Lenexis Foodworks, Inspira bought a 41.78% stake for about ₹2,235 crore, and Madhusudan Agrawal became chairman (Restaurant India). In the first quarter of FY2027, the company reported consolidated revenue of ₹822.6 crore, up 17.9% year on year, with same-store sales growth of 12.6%. It says digital accounts for more than 90% of its ordering channels (Sahi).

FAQ: Burger King New Delhi

How many Burger King outlets are there in New Delhi?

Burger King has more than 100 restaurants in Delhi NCR, according to Restaurant Brands Asia (September 2026). The official locator lists about 60 entries under “New Delhi”, including some duplicates.

Where was the first Burger King in India, and is it still open?

The first Burger King in India opened on 9 November 2014 at Select Citywalk, Saket. A Burger King still operates on the mall’s second floor, at No 16, S01, but I could not confirm that it is the exact same unit.

Which Burger King in New Delhi stays open the latest?

Janpath in Connaught Place and the Akshardham Metro Station outlet at Parsvnath Mall are both open until 1 a.m., according to the official locator. Janpath is open every day from 8 a.m.

Is there a Burger King at Connaught Place or Rajiv Chowk metro?

Yes. Connaught Place has outlets at No 68 Janpath and at E8 Inner Circle. Rajiv Chowk has two counters: RCK 4 inside the metro station (7 a.m. – 11 p.m.) and RCK 01 just outside it (8 a.m. – 11 p.m.).

Does Burger King serve beef in New Delhi? What is in an Indian Whopper?

No. Burger King India serves neither beef nor pork. The Whopper comes with a chicken patty or a vegetarian patty, and there are also Peri Peri Chicken and Whopper Jr. versions.

How much is a Whopper in New Delhi?

A Chicken Whopper was listed at ₹219 on Zomato for the Connaught Place E8 outlet on 22 October 2026, and a Chicken Whopper Jr. at ₹199. These are delivery prices, and in-store prices may differ.

What vegetarian options does Burger King India have?

Vegetarian burgers include the Veg Whopper, Crispy Veg and Veg Makhani Burst. Separate vegetarian and non-vegetarian lines were documented at the 2014 launch, but I found no recent official confirmation that they are still used.

Can I get Burger King delivered in New Delhi?

Yes. Janpath delivers through the BK app, and Burger King outlets are listed on Zomato, Swiggy and magicpin. Restaurant Brands Asia says digital accounts for more than 90% of its ordering channels.

Does Burger King New Delhi accept UPI and cards?

Yes. UPI, cards and cash are accepted at the outlets checked. Saket and E8 list Visa, Mastercard and American Express. I could not confirm whether foreign-issued cards work, so carry UPI or cash as a backup.
