Huggys Liège: Menu Prices, Hours and Address

Where to eat a HUGGYS burger in Liège city centre, what to order, what it costs in euros and when to go, checked on 2 November 2026.

By Sofia, Restaurants writer, Italy, Switzerland and the BeneluxPublished November 23, 2026

HUGGYS has one restaurant in the city of Liège: HUGGYS Liège 2, at Passage Lemonnier 22, 4000 Liège, inside the covered arcade a few steps from the Place Saint-Lambert. It opens every day from 11:45am to 10pm, bank holidays included, and its signature burgers cost €18.00 to €20.00 on the online ordering site. Wanderlog puts it at 4.5/5 on Google from 3,949 reviews. Looking for Manhattn’s Burgers in Liège? There isn’t one. HUGGYS, the chain that started here in 2012 as Huggy’s Bar, is the local burger to try.

HUGGYS Liège 2 is the only HUGGYS inside the city of Liège. Every detail below comes from the chain’s official pages, checked on 2 November 2026.

How to get to Huggys Liège by tram

Take tram T1 to the Opéra or Place Saint-Lambert stop; both are a short walk from the Passage Lemonnier.

Liège’s T1 line has run since 28 April 2025, covering 11.7 km and 23 stops between Sclessin and Coronmeuse (source). The restaurant is inside the Passage Lemonnier, a covered shopping arcade built in 1836–1838 by the architects Louis-Désiré Lemonnier and Henri-Victor Beaulieu, which runs from the Vinâve d’Île to the rue de l’Université (Belgique Insolite). On delivery apps, search for “Huggys Passage Lemonnier”: that is the name they use.

Huggys Liège opening hours: is it open on Sunday?

Yes. HUGGYS Liège 2 opens seven days a week, 11:45am to 10pm, Sundays and bank holidays included.

The chain closes five days a year, around Christmas and New Year plus one staff day (FAQ). Other sources disagree slightly: the general FAQ says “from noon to 10pm” and Wanderlog shows 12pm–10pm. I went with 11:45am, the time on the restaurant’s own page and the time the lunch deal starts.

Huggys Liège menu and prices

A classic burger at Huggys Liège costs €13.90 to €21.50 and a signature burger €18.00 to €20.00, fries and drinks extra.

Those are the prices on the restaurant’s online ordering site, checked on 2 November 2026. The dining room may charge differently: I found no dated official menu, and the chain’s menu page currently returns an error. Do the sums before you sit down. A Magic Tom with fries comes to €21.50 before a drink. That is sit-down restaurant money, not fast food, and the Belgian beef and the house beer are what you are paying for.

Vegetarian, vegan and kids’ burgers

Le Veggie costs €14.00 and Le Vegan €13.00, which makes them cheaper than any beef burger on the menu.

Kids get the Kid’s Burger or the Kid’s Veggie at €9.00 each. The official FAQ also mentions a gluten-free menu and a kids’ menu. The chain has its own vegan and vegetarian pages for Liège. Vegetarians are not an afterthought here, which is rarer than it should be in a city built on boulets.

Sauces cost €1.30 to €2.40, and the Liège sauce is the dearest at €2.40. Soft drinks run €2.30 to €3.00. One aggregator says the coleslaw is unlimited too. The official site only confirms the fries, so don’t count on it.

The HUGGYS Lunch deal

The HUGGYS Lunch is a smash burger, fries and a soft drink, served every day from 11:45am to 2:30pm while stocks last, to eat in or take away.

Swap the soft drink for a HUGGYS beer and it becomes the “Power Lunch” (source). The official page gives no price and I could not find one, so ask at the counter.

Why Huggys tastes like Liège: Belgian Blue beef and Brasserie C beers

HUGGYS is built on Liège suppliers: fresh Belgian Blue beef delivered daily by a Liège master butcher, and eight house beers brewed with Brasserie C, a Liège brewery.

Belgian Blue is the lean cattle breed raised across Wallonia. The buns, a Classic or a sesame-topped American Style, are made for the chain (concept). Brasserie C, at Impasse des Ursulines 14 in the Saint-Léonard area, is best known for its Curtius beer. HUGGYS developed its beers with the brewery for beer and burger pairing, and staff will suggest one for each burger. The same beer goes into the sauce on Le Liégeois. That makes sense in a city that already pours sweet-sour sauce lapin over its boulets à la liégeoise.

Huggys Liège reviews: what diners say

According to Wanderlog, Huggys Liège 2 holds 4.5/5 on Google from 3,949 reviews and 4.0/5 on TripAdvisor from 205.

Wanderlog does not say when it collected these figures, and TripAdvisor and the delivery apps blocked direct access when I checked, so read them as an indication (Wanderlog). They are third-party ratings, not ours: I have not rated this restaurant.

The reviews Wanderlog summarises keep coming back to the burgers themselves, staff who know their beer pairings, and the vegetarian and vegan choices. Several diners also like the self-service sauces and find the prices fair. The complaints are just as consistent: crowds at weekends and the odd billing mistake. Book for Friday or Saturday evening, and read your bill before you pay.

How to book a table at Huggys Liège

Book online for groups of up to 22 people; larger groups have to contact the restaurant directly.

A terrace booking comes with a fallback table inside in case the weather turns, which in Liège is worth having. The chain also offers a birthday package (FAQ).

The Huggys Challenge Burger

The HUGGYS Challenge Burger weighs more than 2 kg. Finish it within one hour and the meal is free, plus a T-shirt.

The challenge is in the official FAQ. Skip the fries if you are going to try it.

Is the original Huggy’s Bar on boulevard d’Avroy closed?

The first Huggy’s Bar, at boulevard d’Avroy 122, is not on the official list of HUGGYS restaurants, checked on 2 November 2026.

It opened in September 2012, the chain’s starting point. Restaurant Guru marks it “permanently closed”. Yelp and TripAdvisor still have pages for it, and I found no press article confirming a closure or a date. Because those old pages are still online, review sites often mix up the two addresses, so check which one a review is about. If you want a HUGGYS in central Liège, go to the Passage Lemonnier.

HUGGYS history: from Huggy’s Bar to a 13-restaurant Belgian chain

HUGGYS started in Liège in September 2012 as “The Huggy’s Bar” and now runs 13 restaurants across Belgium.

  • 2008: Thomas Mémurlin and his co-founder Roberto meet at HEC Liège, the University of Liège’s management school (official story).
  • September 2012: the first Huggy’s Bar opens at boulevard d’Avroy 122, Liège.
  • 2015: openings in Nandrin and Soumagne.
  • 2016: a second Liège restaurant opens in the Passage Lemonnier, plus Awans, where the head office moves.
  • 13 January 2024: HUGGYS opens in Gosselies, in the Parenthèse shopping centre (DH).
  • 22 July 2024: Huggy’s Bar becomes HUGGYS, with a new visual identity by the agency COAST (Paris Match Belgique).
  • 10 October 2026: the 13th restaurant opens in Waterloo, chaussée de Bruxelles 196, with 300 m² and about 100 seats. The chain employs about 300 people, and Thomas Mémurlin is co-founder and CEO (L’Express Franchise).

Is there a Manhattn’s Burgers in Liège?

No. Manhattn’s Burgers has no restaurant in Liège or anywhere in Wallonia; the nearest are in Leuven and Brussels.

The official list, checked on 2 November 2026, counts 13 restaurants: three in Paris and ten in Belgium, in Brussels, Ixelles, Etterbeek, Watermael-Boitsfort, Antwerp, Wijnegem, Ghent, Leuven and Bruges (Manhattn’s). The brothers Jérôme and Philippe Vandermeulen founded the chain in Brussels in 2014, with a first restaurant on the avenue Louise. Heading to Flanders? Read our review of Manhattn’s Burgers in Bruges. In Liège, I would not miss it: HUGGYS is the chain that started here.

Huggys Liège FAQ

Where is Huggys in Liège?

HUGGYS Liège 2 is at Passage Lemonnier 22, 4000 Liège, in the covered arcade between the Vinâve d’Île and the rue de l’Université. The nearest tram stops are Opéra and Place Saint-Lambert on line T1.

What are the opening hours of Huggys Liège?

HUGGYS Liège 2 is open every day from 11:45am to 10pm, Sundays and bank holidays included. The chain closes five days a year, around Christmas and New Year plus one staff day.

How much does a burger cost at Huggys Liège?

Online, a Cheeseburger costs €13.90, the Magic Tom €18.00 and Le Liégeois €19.00, checked on 2 November 2026. Fries are €3.50. Dine-in prices may differ.

What is the signature burger at Huggys?

The Magic Tom, served since 2012: beef patty, Irish Cheddar, a cheese croquette, double bacon, house pepper sauce, red onion and pickles. Le Liégeois, with its house Liège sauce made with beer, is the local pick.

Are the fries unlimited at Huggys?

The official concept page says the double-fried Belgian fries are served unlimited. Online, a portion costs €3.50.

Does Huggys Liège have vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free burgers?

Yes. Le Veggie costs €14.00 and Le Vegan €13.00 online, and the official FAQ mentions a gluten-free menu.

Do I need to book at Huggys Liège?

At weekends, yes: reviewers report crowds. Book online for up to 22 people; for larger groups, contact the restaurant.

Can I get Huggys delivered in Liège?

Yes. HUGGYS Liège 2 delivers through Uber Eats, Deliveroo and Takeaway.com, and also does takeaway.

Is the Huggy’s Bar on boulevard d’Avroy still open?

It is no longer on the official list of HUGGYS restaurants (checked 2 November 2026), and no press report gives a closure date. Go to the Passage Lemonnier instead.

What is the Huggys Challenge Burger?

A burger of more than 2 kg. Finish it in one hour and your meal is free, plus a T-shirt.
