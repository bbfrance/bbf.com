K'u'uk Mérida: Pedro Evia's Yucatecan Restaurant

Address, hours, chef Pedro Evia, the menu formats and where K'u'uk stands after the Michelin Guide's first Yucatán edition in 2026.

By Carmen, Restaurants writer, MexicoPublished October 31, 2026

K’u’uk is a Yucatecan fine-dining restaurant in Mérida, not Cancún. Chef Pedro Evia and his partner Eduardo Rukos opened it in 2012 at Avenida Rómulo Rozo 488, Colonia Itzimná, and it serves tasting menus built on ancestral Yucatec Maya cooking techniques. No burgers. I checked every current fact below against the official site, kuukrestaurant.com, on 15 October 2026.

Not to be confused. If you searched for K’u’uk in Cancún, or for Roberto Alcocer’s K’u’uk, you have hit two mix-ups at once. K’u’uk has never been in Cancún, and its founding chef is Pedro Evia. Alcocer is a respected chef with no documented link to K’u’uk: he cooks at Malva in Valle de Guadalupe, Baja California, and at Valle in Oceanside, California (El Restaurante, 26 March 2026). Valle earned a Michelin star in 2023 and kept it in 2024, and Alcocer was a James Beard Best Chef: California semifinalist in 2025 (Mission Pacific Hotel).

K’u’uk Mérida at a glance

K’u’uk is a 42-seat tasting-menu restaurant in a 19th-century Mérida mansion, still run by its two founders. The key facts as of 15 October 2026:

K’u’uk
City Mérida, Yucatán, Mexico
Address Avenida Rómulo Rozo 488 (between calles 27 and 27A), Colonia Itzimná, 97100 Mérida, near the Monumento a la Patria
Building Villa Donata, a 19th-century mansion
Size 16 tables, 42 covers
Opened 2012
Founders Pedro Evia (chef), Eduardo Rukos (director and head of research)
Format Experiential tasting menus, plus traditional Yucatecan dishes for sharing
Hours Tue–Sat 1:30 pm–11:00 pm; Sun 1:30 pm–4:30 pm; closed Monday
Burgers None on the menu

Sources: kuukrestaurant.com and tablejourney.com for the address, building, size and hours. The 2012 date comes from MID City Beat and joinpearl.co; the official site only says “over a decade ago”. Hours change. Confirm them before you travel.

Who is Pedro Evia, the chef behind K’u’uk?

Pedro Evia is a self-taught Yucatecan chef who co-founded K’u’uk in 2012 and is still listed as its chef. He learned on the job under chef Marcos Meneses and made four study trips to Italy, according to a 2020 profile in MID City Beat.

His partner, Eduardo Rukos, is named on the official site as director and head of research. The site also lists Miguel Benítez and pastry chef Karla Fernández on the team; Benítez’s exact role is not stated.

Evia’s work now reaches beyond Yucatán. In 2022 he opened Q78 Gastromezcal at Calle Villalar 6 in Madrid: about 240 mezcals, and dishes such as cochinita tortas (El Universal, May 2022, updated March 2023). How much of 2026 he spends at the K’u’uk stove, no source says.

Pedro Evia’s other restaurants

Evia has also been linked to several Mérida venues. These mentions date from 2020, and I have not confirmed that each one is still open:

  • Kiwiik, a breakfast spot, with Eduardo Rukos
  • La Piñuela, in downtown Mérida, with Eduardo Rukos
  • Sensei, Time and Cosa Nostra (Cosa Nostra opened in 2010)
  • Q78 Gastromezcal, Madrid (opened 2022; current status not checked)

What K’u’uk serves: contemporary Yucatecan cuisine

K’u’uk serves contemporary Yucatecan cuisine rooted in Maya technique. The restaurant calls itself a sanctuary for researching, celebrating and reviving ancestral Yucatec Maya cooking, and it means the research part: one of its two founders carries the title head of research.

There are two ways to eat here. The main one is the experiential tasting menu. Alongside it, K’u’uk serves traditional Yucatecan dishes meant for sharing at the table.

How many courses is the K’u’uk tasting menu, and how much does it cost?

Neither figure is public. The official site publishes no prices, and the “11 courses” some aggregators quote has no primary source behind it. Ask for the price in Mexican pesos when you book.

Does K’u’uk have a Michelin star?

No. K’u’uk is not among the Yucatán restaurants awarded a Michelin star in 2026. The Michelin Guide Mexico 2026 covered Yucatán for the first time, at a ceremony in Guadalajara on 20 May 2026. The region’s three one-star restaurants are Huniik and La Barra de Huniik, both by chef Roberto Solís in Mérida, and Ixi’im at Chablé in Chocholá (Diario de Yucatán; Yucatán Magazine).

One loose end. K’u’uk’s own website mentions Michelin recognition that does not match the published 2026 results, and El Universal calls K’u’uk “present in the Michelin Guide” without naming a distinction. Until the guide itself confirms something, I state none here: no star, no Bib Gourmand, no “recommended” listing.

Earlier awards for K’u’uk

K’u’uk’s best-documented awards date from its first years, and all of them come from a single secondary source, MID City Beat (2020). According to it, K’u’uk was named No. 1 new restaurant in Mexico at the Travel + Leisure Gourmet Awards 2013 and made Food & Wine’s “100 restaurants worth a pilgrimage” list. It was nominated for best tasting menu in Mexico in 2014. In 2015, Evia was invited to cook a Marché du Film dinner in Cannes.

Best fine dining in Mérida, Yucatán: K’u’uk vs Huniik vs Ixi’im

Huniik holds Mérida’s Michelin star; K’u’uk has been the city’s reference for research-led Yucatecan cooking since 2012. Here is what the record shows:

K’u’uk Huniik / La Barra de Huniik Ixi’im
Location Colonia Itzimná, Mérida Mérida Chablé, Chocholá
Chef Pedro Evia (founder) Roberto Solís Not confirmed
Michelin 2026 Not among the one-star restaurants One star One star

My take: if the star is what you are chasing, book Huniik. If you want the kitchen that put ancestral Maya technique on Mérida’s fine-dining map more than a decade ago, and is still run by the two people who opened it, book K’u’uk. Ixi’im means a drive out to Chocholá, so plan it as its own outing.

Is K’u’uk in Cancún?

No. K’u’uk has one location, in Mérida, and I found no branch in Cancún. Both cities are on the Yucatán Peninsula, but Cancún is on the Caribbean coast and Mérida is far to the west, so from a Cancún hotel this is an overnight trip or a very long day. I have not verified an exact road distance, so I do not quote one.

Should K’u’uk be on a burger lover’s Yucatán itinerary?

Yes, as the fine-dining night of the trip, not as a burger stop. Neither the official site nor any press I found lists a burger. Go for Yucatecan flavours you will not find on a bun, then let our guides handle the burgers.

How I built this profile

This profile is built from documents, not from a meal at K’u’uk. On 15 October 2026 I checked the official site for the address, hours, team and concept. The 2026 Michelin results come from the Diario de Yucatán and Yucatán Magazine, Evia’s background and early awards from MID City Beat, and Q78 in Madrid from El Universal. Anything I could not trace to a primary or reputable press source is left out: the tasting-menu price and course count, a Two Knives award at the Best Chef Awards 2024, a World’s 50 Best Discovery listing, an OAD 2025 ranking and a World’s Best Wine Lists rating.

K’u’uk Mérida FAQ

Who is the chef at K’u’uk in Mérida?

Pedro Evia, a self-taught Yucatecan chef, is K’u’uk’s founding chef. The official site lists him alongside co-founder Eduardo Rukos (director and head of research), as checked on 15 October 2026.

Is K’u’uk in Cancún or Mérida?

K’u’uk is in Mérida, at Avenida Rómulo Rozo 488, Colonia Itzimná. It has no Cancún location.

Does K’u’uk have a Michelin star?

No. Yucatán’s one-star restaurants in the Michelin Guide Mexico 2026 are Huniik, La Barra de Huniik and Ixi’im. Any other Michelin distinction for K’u’uk is unconfirmed.

How much does the K’u’uk tasting menu cost?

K’u’uk does not publish its prices, and I found no verified current price. Ask for the price in Mexican pesos when you book.

Do you need a reservation at K’u’uk?

Yes, book ahead. The dining room has only 16 tables and 42 covers. K’u’uk is closed on Mondays and serves only lunch on Sundays.

Does K’u’uk serve burgers?

No. No burger appears on K’u’uk’s menu in any source I checked.

What other restaurants does Pedro Evia run?

Evia has been linked to Kiwiik, La Piñuela, Sensei, Time and Cosa Nostra in Mérida, and he opened Q78 Gastromezcal in Madrid in 2022. Their current status is not verified.

Is Roberto Alcocer connected to K’u’uk?

No. Roberto Alcocer runs Malva in Valle de Guadalupe, Baja California, and Valle in Oceanside, California. No source links him to K’u’uk or to Cancún.
