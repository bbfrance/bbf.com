Sikwa in San José, Costa Rica, Has Closed

Pablo Bonilla's Indigenous-cuisine restaurant, named Costa Rica's best three years running, announced its closure in late October 2026. What we know.

By Carmen, Restaurants writer, MexicoPublished November 12, 2026

Sikwa in San José, Costa Rica, has closed. In late October 2026, eight years after it opened, the restaurant announced on social media that its Los Yoses dining room was shutting. Its founding chef, Pablo Bonilla, had already said in early September that he was leaving. Sikwa never served burgers. It was a fine-dining kitchen built on the Indigenous cooking of the Bribri and Cabécar peoples, and Latin America’s 50 Best Restaurants named it The Best Restaurant in Costa Rica in 2023, 2024 and 2025. It belongs on a burger site anyway: no restaurant did more to put San José on the region’s dining map, and its closure changes what a food trip to the city looks like. This is the Costa Rican capital, by the way, not San José, California.

Is Sikwa in San José, Costa Rica, still open?

No. As of November 4, 2026, Sikwa’s Los Yoses dining room is closed and no reopening date has been announced. The restaurant’s message read: “Sikwa no termina, se transforma. Estamos explorando cuál será su próximo capítulo” (“Sikwa isn’t ending, it’s transforming. We are exploring what its next chapter will be”), according to La República and La Nación.

Costa Rican outlets don’t agree on the day of the announcement. La Nación’s report is dated October 21, 2026, and La Fatfluencer gives the same date; La República and La Teja say October 22. Nobody gives a last day of service. So I’m sticking to “late October 2026”. La Teja calls the closure temporary, while Sikwa itself only talks about a transformation, with no new address, format or date.

Planning a trip? Don’t try to book. The last address 50 Best lists, C. 41 in Los Yoses, no longer takes guests.

Map of Sikwa’s two San José addresses: Barrio Escalante (first location, Calle 33 between Avenidas 5 and 7) and Los Yoses (last location, C. 41, closed in October 2026). The marker shows the centre of Los Yoses, not the exact door. (Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL)

Why did Sikwa close?

Sikwa has not given a reason for the closure. La Fatfluencer, which covered it on October 29, 2026, confirms that no specific reason was given. The closure came about seven weeks after Pablo Bonilla announced his departure. It’s tempting to link the two. No source does, and neither will I.

Sikwa’s story runs from a 2018 opening in Barrio Escalante to the closure of its Los Yoses location in October 2026. These are the dates the sources confirm.

If you know San José’s dining district, Sikwa’s first address will ring a bell: Calle 33 in Barrio Escalante is the street our San José, Costa Rica city page points burger hunters to. The date of the move to Los Yoses was never published. I treat Escalante as the first location and Los Yoses as the last.

Who is Pablo Bonilla, the chef behind Sikwa?

Pablo Bonilla is the Costa Rican chef who founded Sikwa in 2018 and ran its kitchen until early September 2026. He built the restaurant around Costa Rica’s Indigenous food heritage. He told AFP that about 80% of the products came from Indigenous sources, bought from Indigenous and artisanal-fishing communities (Tico Times, February 23, 2023).

Sikwa’s sourcing: about 80% of its products came from Indigenous sources, bought from Indigenous and artisanal-fishing communities (Pablo Bonilla to AFP, 2023). (Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0; data: Tico Times / AFP, February 23, 2023)

In 2025, 50 Best listed him at the head of the kitchen with Yerling Avilés, and Álvaro Piñar as pastry chef. When Bonilla announced his departure on Facebook, he summed up his years at Sikwa in one line quoted by La Nación: “Aquí entendí que la cocina empieza mucho antes de encender un fuego” (“Here I understood that cooking starts long before you light a fire”).

Where is Pablo Bonilla cooking now?

As of November 4, 2026, no official source has said where Pablo Bonilla will cook next. I’ll update this page when he or a new project announces it.

What did Sikwa serve? Indigenous cuisine, not burgers

Sikwa served contemporary dishes rooted in the Indigenous cuisine of Costa Rica’s Bribri and Cabécar peoples. There were no burgers. Latin America’s 50 Best described it as “a research centre for Costa Rican gastronomic education” (50 Best, Sikwa).

These are the dishes 50 Best listed. They belong to a past menu:

  • Shrimp tartare with pico de gallo ponzu and a green-plantain hash brown
  • Hen empanadas made with purple corn and green plantain
  • Green curry with the catch of the day
  • Charcoal-grilled beef with white-bean purée and cacao mole
  • Pork flank with green-papaya kimchi

Green plantain, purple corn, cacao, fish from artisanal fishers. Read that menu and you’re reading a map of where Costa Rica’s food comes from. That’s why Sikwa belongs in a portrait of the city’s dining scene, not in a burger ranking.

What does the name “Sikwa” mean?

“Sikwa” is a Bribri and Cabécar word for a foreigner or a non-Indigenous person, according to the EFE wire published by El Financiero. I read the name as a deliberate wink: a restaurant named after the outsider, cooking Indigenous food for diners in the capital.

Sikwa on Latin America’s 50 Best Restaurants

Sikwa was ranked by Latin America’s 50 Best Restaurants three years in a row and was named The Best Restaurant in Costa Rica each time. Its best result was No. 25, in 2024.

Sikwa on Latin America’s 50 Best Restaurants: No. 47 in 2023, No. 25 in 2024 and No. 43 in 2025, named The Best Restaurant in Costa Rica each year. A lower rank is better. (Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0; data: La República and La Fatfluencer, checked November 4, 2026)

These ranks come from La República and La Fatfluencer, checked on November 4, 2026. My take: three national titles in a row is a rare run, and Sikwa earned it with Costa Rica’s own ingredients rather than imported fine-dining codes. That is what made it matter beyond the country.

Where to eat burgers in San José, Costa Rica, instead

For burgers in the Costa Rican capital, head to Barrio Escalante and Calle 33, then San Pedro de Montes de Oca, Escazú and Santa Ana. Our San José, Costa Rica burger page covers those neighbourhoods. For a sense of the scene and its prices: Costa Rica Burger Weeks 2026 ran from April 10 to May 3, 2026, with 27 restaurants, including Burga, Deep Dip Burger, Smashburger, UFO Burgers and Tres Cuartos. Specials cost ₡8,500 to ₡10,500 for Visa cardholders, plus the 10% service charge (Tico Times).

For another chef-led restaurant at a turning point, read our profile of La Prensa in Zaragoza and chef Marisa Barberán, or browse every city in our burger restaurant guides.

How this article was checked

I put this portrait together from Costa Rican press coverage of the closure (La Nación, La República, La Teja, La Fatfluencer), the Latin America’s 50 Best page for Sikwa, and the AFP and EFE profiles published in 2023. Every source was checked on November 4, 2026. Where the sources disagree, as they do on the announcement date, I give the range rather than pick one. I left out biographical details that only appear on aggregator sites. Sikwa gets no rating here: it was a fine-dining restaurant, not a burger restaurant, and it is now closed.

Sikwa FAQ

Is Sikwa still open in San José?

No. Sikwa announced in late October 2026 that its Los Yoses location was closing. As of November 4, 2026, no reopening date, new address or new format has been announced.

Why did Sikwa close?

The restaurant has not given a reason. It said only that “Sikwa no termina, se transforma” and that it is exploring its next chapter.

Will Sikwa reopen?

Nothing is confirmed. La Teja calls the closure temporary and Sikwa talks about a transformation, but no reopening, location or date has been announced.

Who founded Sikwa?

Chef Pablo Bonilla founded Sikwa in 2018. He announced that he was leaving the restaurant in early September 2026.

Did Sikwa serve burgers?

No. Sikwa served contemporary dishes based on Bribri and Cabécar Indigenous cuisine, such as charcoal-grilled beef with cacao mole and purple-corn empanadas. None of the dishes 50 Best listed was a burger.

Where was Sikwa located?

Sikwa opened in Barrio Escalante, on Calle 33 between Avenidas 5 and 7. Its latest address was C. 41 in Los Yoses, San José, which closed in October 2026.

What was Sikwa’s best ranking on Latin America’s 50 Best?

No. 25, in 2024. It was also No. 47 in 2023 and No. 43 in 2025, and was named The Best Restaurant in Costa Rica in all three editions.

Where can I find the best burgers in San José, Costa Rica?

In Barrio Escalante along Calle 33, in San Pedro near the university, and in Escazú and Santa Ana. See our San José, Costa Rica burger page.
