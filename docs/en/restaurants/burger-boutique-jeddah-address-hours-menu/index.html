Burger Boutique Jeddah: Address, Hours and Menu

Burger Boutique Jeddah at City Yard, Ar Rawdah: address, phone, conflicting opening hours, slider and smash menu, HungerStation delivery. Checked Oct 2026.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished November 27, 2026

Burger Boutique Jeddah is the only Jeddah branch of a gourmet burger brand founded in Kuwait in 2005. It sits at City Yard on Abdul Maqsud Khojah Street, in the Ar Rawdah district, and its official phone number is +966 53 505 5201. The menu turns on one choice: slider or smash burger, on a classic, red or black bun, with loaded fries, chicken bites and wings on the side. Go for that menu and for a slice of Gulf burger history. But call first. The two published opening schedules disagree, and on 28 October 2026 I could not verify a single price in Saudi riyals (SAR).

I have not eaten at the City Yard branch yet, so this is a sourced guide, not a review: every fact below carries its source and the date I checked it. In Arabic, look for برجر بوتيك جدة on maps and delivery apps.

Burger Boutique Jeddah address: City Yard, Ar Rawdah

Burger Boutique Jeddah is at City Yard on Abdul Maqsud Khojah Street, in the Ar Rawdah district. DestinationKSA gives that address in its August 2025 guide. Foursquare lists the same place as “Burger Boutique - Al-Rawdah - Abdulmaqsud Khuja Street”, so two independent listings agree.

Map of the Ar Rawdah district of Jeddah, where Burger Boutique sits at City Yard on Abdul Maqsud Khojah Street; the pin marks the district, not the exact entrance, since no street number or Saudi national address was found (Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL)

One caveat. Gastronomica, the group that owns the brand, now writes only “Jeddah” on its official page, without the City Yard label. I found no street number, postal code or Saudi national address. Search “Burger Boutique City Yard” in your maps app and check the pin before you set off.

Gastronomica lists one Jeddah branch only. If you see a second Jeddah location on a delivery app, read the delivery section below before you drive across town.

My advice: plan for dinner, when both sources agree the restaurant is open, and call +966 53 505 5201 if you want lunch or a table after midnight.

Burger Boutique Jeddah menu: sliders, smash burgers and three buns

At Burger Boutique Jeddah you pick a burger format, then a bun. DestinationKSA describes burgers served either as sliders or as smash burgers, on a classic, red or black bun. That choice is the most distinctive thing on the menu, and none of the delivery listings I found explains it.

Classic, red or black bun: how to choose

Burger Boutique offers three buns: classic, red and black. No source I found says what gives the red and black buns their colour or whether they taste different, so ask at the counter. List Magazine pictures the brand’s best-known burger in the red bun. If you only go once, that is where I would start.

Solar Gravity or Lunar Gravity? The signature burger

Burger Boutique’s signature burger goes by two names depending on the source, and I could not settle which one is right. Francorp ME, which markets the brand’s franchise, calls it the Solar Gravity Burger. List Magazine, a Jeddah publication, highlights a “Lunar Gravity Burger”: a patty with beef bacon and cheddar in a red bun.

These may be two different burgers on the same menu, or one burger with a misprinted name. Neither source shows the official menu. Ask for both names at the counter. If the staff know only one, order that one.

Other signature dishes and sides

Beyond the burgers, Francorp ME names the Sriracha Honey Shrimp and the Commando Fries as signature dishes. For the Jeddah branch, DestinationKSA adds:

  • Southern Fried Chicken Bites
  • Sticky Maple Buffalo Wings
  • Several loaded fries

Francorp ME also says Burger Boutique was one of the first restaurants to bring sriracha and truffle to diners in the region. That is the franchise seller’s own claim, so read it as marketing. I found no confirmed list of drinks or vegetarian dishes.

Grass-fed beef: a claim, not a confirmed fact

Burger Boutique has advertised organic, grass-fed beef, but no current official source confirms it for Jeddah. List Magazine describes grass-fed organic beef with no antibiotics or hormones. Gulf News reported the same organic grass-fed beef on 18 May 2020, along with a lighter “Zap it” menu cooked with less oil and 100% recycled paper packaging. That report was about the Riyadh branches, six years ago. If the beef matters to you, ask the branch.

Burger Boutique Jeddah prices in SAR

I could not verify any Burger Boutique Jeddah price in Saudi riyals as of 28 October 2026. The HungerStation menu page returned an error when I tried to open it, and the brand publishes no price list. I would rather leave this blank than print a number I cannot date. Open the HungerStation app and set your delivery address to Ar Rawdah to see today’s prices.

Does Burger Boutique Jeddah deliver?

Yes: Burger Boutique Jeddah delivers through HungerStation, which lists it under two Jeddah zones, Ar Rawdah and Al Bawadi. Both listings carry the same restaurant ID (83749). That points to one kitchen serving two delivery zones, not two restaurants. Al Bawadi is a delivery area, so do not head there to eat in.

I could not confirm a Burger Boutique listing on Jahez, Talabat or The Chefz. Online ordering beyond HungerStation, table reservations, payment methods, wheelchair access and parking at City Yard were not stated by any source I found.

Burger Boutique Jeddah reviews: what the data does and does not show

I found no Google, TripAdvisor or Yelp rating for Burger Boutique Jeddah that I could verify with a review count on 28 October 2026. Google and Foursquare hid the details behind a login. So I am not publishing a score, and I am not summarising praise or complaints I could not read myself.

For scale, a local rival gives you a benchmark. Section B, at 3362 Hira Street in Ash Shati, shows 4.3 out of 5 on 13,792 Google reviews, according to Wanderlog (checked 28 October 2026). When you look up Burger Boutique on Google Maps, compare its score and its number of reviews against that.

What the press says is consistent on style. DestinationKSA calls the dining room “chic”. List Magazine describes an industrial-chic interior and counts Burger Boutique as part of Jeddah’s “modern burger revolution”.

Who owns Burger Boutique? A Kuwaiti brand from 2005

Burger Boutique is a Kuwaiti brand, not a Saudi one. Basil Al Salem, founder and CEO of the Kuwaiti restaurant group Gastronomica, opened it in 2005 as “B+F Burger Boutique”, according to his 2017 interview with Entrepreneur Middle East. He had founded Gastronomica in Kuwait two years earlier, in 2003.

Francorp ME confirms the 2005 date and calls Burger Boutique Gastronomica’s first concept. Forbes Middle East put Al Salem on its 2024 list of “20 Leaders Behind Luxury Dining in the Middle East”. Gastronomica’s home page sums up his approach as the “Total Dining Experience”.

Gastronomica later built a second burger concept in Kuwait, the conveyor-belt Slider Station, another Gastronomica brand, which I wrote about earlier this month. For where the burger itself comes from, long before it reached the Gulf, read my colleague Maria’s history of the hamburger.

Best burger in Jeddah: where Burger Boutique fits

Burger Boutique is the veteran of Jeddah’s gourmet burger scene, not its undisputed champion. Its claim is age: it was serving gourmet burgers in Kuwait in 2005. DestinationKSA’s August 2025 roundup lists it alongside Century Burger, Flat Burger, Lou Burger, MMMM Burger, NORA Burger, Score Eatery, Section B, SMPL Burger and WK Burger.

I have not tasted that list side by side, so I will not crown a winner. Here is my position: Burger Boutique earns its place on a Jeddah burger list for its history and for a menu built around a real choice, slider or smash, three buns. I will judge the patty only after I have eaten one. Until then, go for the coloured buns and the format, and treat it as a reference point for how the Gulf’s gourmet burger scene started. Burger Boutique Jeddah FAQ

Where is Burger Boutique in Jeddah, and is there more than one branch? Burger Boutique Jeddah is at City Yard, Abdul Maqsud Khojah Street, in Ar Rawdah. Gastronomica lists only one Jeddah branch; the Al Bawadi listing on HungerStation is a delivery zone of the same kitchen.

What are Burger Boutique Jeddah’s opening hours on Friday and Saturday? Gastronomica gives 12 PM–2 AM on weekends for all Saudi branches. DestinationKSA (August 2025) gives 5 PM–1 AM from Thursday to Saturday. Call +966 53 505 5201 to confirm.

How much does a burger cost at Burger Boutique Jeddah? I could not verify any price in SAR as of 28 October 2026. Check the current menu in the HungerStation app or at the counter.

What is the Solar Gravity Burger, and is it the same as the Lunar Gravity? Francorp ME names the Solar Gravity as a signature burger; List Magazine describes a Lunar Gravity with beef bacon and cheddar in a red bun. They may be two burgers or one name misprinted; ask at the branch.

Is Burger Boutique Saudi or Kuwaiti, and who founded it? It is Kuwaiti. Basil Al Salem, founder and CEO of Gastronomica, opened it in Kuwait in 2005 as B+F Burger Boutique.

Does Burger Boutique Jeddah deliver? Yes, through HungerStation in the Ar Rawdah and Al Bawadi zones. I could not confirm a listing on Jahez, Talabat or The Chefz.

Is Burger Boutique halal? None of the sources I checked mentions an explicit halal certification. Ask the branch or write to info@burgerboutique.com.

Are there vegetarian options at Burger Boutique? No source I checked lists a vegetarian burger or dish. Ask the staff before ordering.

Do I need to book at Burger Boutique Jeddah? No source mentions reservations. Call +966 53 505 5201 if you are coming as a group.

Which other burger restaurants should I try in Jeddah? DestinationKSA’s 2025 list names Century Burger, Flat Burger, Lou Burger, MMMM Burger, NORA Burger, Score Eatery, Section B, SMPL Burger and WK Burger. Our Jeddah burger guide puts them on the map.
