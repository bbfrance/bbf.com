Best Burgers in Milan: 10 Restaurants for 2026

Where to eat the best burger in Milan in 2026: Al Mercato, Dumbo, Meat Crew, Chuck's and 6 more, with addresses, prices in euros and recent closures.

By Sofia, Restaurants writer, Italy, Switzerland and the BeneluxPublished October 22, 2026

The best burger in Milan for a sit-down gourmet meal is still at Al Mercato Burger Bar, Via Sant’Eufemia 16, which chefs Eugenio Roncoroni and Beniamino Nespor opened in 2010. For a smash burger, go to Dumbo (Mercato Comunale Isola or Via Achille Maiocchi 12), Meat Crew or Chuck’s. They are the best-placed addresses inside the city in Scatti di Gusto’s June 2026 tasting. Expect about €10 to €16 for the burger, and €25 to €40 per person once fries and a drink are added. Below are my 10 picks, checked against sources on 20 October 2026, plus the addresses older lists still give that have since closed.

How I ranked Milan’s burger restaurants

This ranking comes from published sources, not from a single tasting. I worked from the most recent Italian food press, the restaurants’ own pages and closure reports. The backbone is Scatti di Gusto’s smash burger tasting by Emanuele Bonati (29 June 2026). Its older top 20 of Milan hamburgers (updated 2022) is used only for history. I also used Dissapore, Puntarella Rossa, MilanoToday, Il Giorno and Milano Segreta. Every price carries the date of its source. When I could not find a 2026 source showing a place is open, the entry says so. You will find no star ratings and no Google scores here: a score I haven’t earned at the table would mislead you.

The gourmet institutions come first, because Milan’s burger scene grew out of them. Then come the smash burger bars the press ranked highest in 2026. The list ends with the kosher and vegan options that most English-language guides skip.

The 10 best burger restaurants in Milan

Each entry gives the address, the burger to order and the source behind every price.

1. Al Mercato Burger Bar, Sant’Eufemia

Al Mercato is the restaurant that put gourmet burgers on Milan’s map. Chefs Eugenio Roncoroni and Beniamino Nespor opened it in 2010. Nespor died in November 2016, aged 34 (Il Fatto Quotidiano). Marcello Rizza has owned the group since 2022.

  • Address: Via Sant’Eufemia 16, Milan (Sant’Eufemia / Missori)
  • Why it stands out: it was cooking chef-level burgers in 2010, years before the smash bars arrived.
  • Good to know: the group closed its Porta Nuova branch in summer 2025. On 26 November 2025 it also closed Al Mercato Steaks&Burgers in Porta Venezia, to focus on Via Sant’Eufemia (Scatti di Gusto). Only Via Sant’Eufemia is still open.
  • Prices: older lists give €11–15. Those figures predate 2026, so check the current menu.
  • Listing: italia.it

My take: shrinking back to one address is good news for the burger. One kitchen, the original one, is easier to keep consistent than three.

2. Dumbo, Isola and Porta Venezia

Dumbo, a smash burger chain from Paris, came second in Scatti di Gusto’s June 2026 tasting, the best result for any address inside Milan. Charles Ganem and Samuel Nataf founded it in Paris in 2019, and Milan is its third European city after Paris and London. It started with a pop-up at Mercato Isola, and the second Milan branch opened in Porta Venezia on 8 October 2026 (MilanoToday).

  • Addresses: Mercato Comunale Isola, Piazzale Lagosta 7, 20159 Milan; Via Achille Maiocchi 12, 20129 Milan (Porta Venezia)
  • Hours: Monday to Sunday, 12:00–14:30 and 19:00–22:30
  • Signature burger: the Classic Cheeseburger: two-week aged beef from La Granda, American cheddar, pickles, onion, ketchup and mustard in a potato bun. Vegetarians get the Earth Burger.
  • Price: €11–13 (Scatti di Gusto, 29 June 2026)
  • Official site: dumboparis.com/locations/milano

Mercato Comunale Isola, the covered market at Piazzale Lagosta 7 where Dumbo serves its Paris-born smash burgers in Milan (photo taken before Dumbo opened) (Photo: Saggittarius A, CC BY 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mercato\_coperto\_Isola\_1.jpg)

3. Meat Crew, Porta Venezia, Zara and Bocconi

Meat Crew is the smash burger chain of YouTuber Mocho (Massimo Novati), ranked third in Milan by Scatti di Gusto in June 2026, with three branches across the city. It is also the priciest smash burger on this list, €3.50 to €4.50 more than Chuck’s for the same style of burger.

  • Addresses: Via Melzo 24 (Porta Venezia); Viale Restelli 3 (Zara); Viale Bligny 18 (Bocconi)
  • Prices: burgers €14.50–15.50, meal deals €21.90–22.40 (Scatti di Gusto, 29 June 2026). Dissapore puts a full meal at about €25.
  • Source: Dissapore

The Porta Venezia toll gates in Milan, gateway to the city’s densest burger cluster: Dumbo at Via Achille Maiocchi 12 and Meat Crew at Via Melzo 24 (Photo: Leonhard Lenz, CC0 1.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Porta\_Venezia\_Milano\_2022-09-28\_01.jpg)

4. Chuck’s, Porta Vittoria

Chuck’s serves the most New York-style smash burger in Milan, and you can’t change a thing on it. Chuck George, a Filipino-American entrepreneur from New York, opened it in 2022 inside the Sidewalk Kitchens food hall.

  • Address: Sidewalk Kitchens, Via Bonvesin de la Riva 3, Milan (Porta Vittoria)
  • Signature burger: a smash patty on a Martin’s potato roll with cheddar, pickles, white onion and the house sauce. ‘No modifications’ means no swaps.
  • Price: €10–12 (Scatti di Gusto, 29 June 2026)
  • Source: Puntarella Rossa

If I had room for one smash burger in Milan, I would start here. It is the cheapest of the city’s top three, and a kitchen that refuses substitutions is telling you it trusts its recipe.

5. Ham Holy Burger, Brera and De Angeli

Ham Holy Burger is the place for a burger made with Italian beef in Milan: Piedmontese Fassona patties and bread baked every day. Scatti di Gusto ranked it third in Milan in 2022.

  • Addresses: Via Palermo 15 (Brera / Moscova); Via Marghera 34 (De Angeli)
  • Good to know: you order on an iPad at the table.
  • Price: from €9 (ESN Milano Bocconi), not reconfirmed for 2026.
  • Check before you go: I found no 2026 source confirming both branches are open. Check the official Instagram or Google listing.

6. Joe’s American Smashburger, Mercato Centrale

Joe’s American Smashburger is Joe Bastianich’s smash burger counter in the food hall of Milano Centrale station. It opened in August 2026 on the ground floor of Mercato Centrale Milano (Scatti di Gusto).

  • Location: ground floor of Mercato Centrale Milano, at Milano Centrale station
  • Signature burger: a 100% Black Angus smash burger with black truffle
  • Good to know: it’s the easiest stop on this list if you are catching a train. I could not confirm current prices from an official source.

Milano Centrale station, home to the Mercato Centrale food hall where Joe Bastianich’s Joe’s American Smashburger opened in August 2026, an easy burger stop between trains (Photo: Leonhard Lenz, CC0 1.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Milano\_Centrale\_2022-09-28\_01.jpg)

7. Fonzo’s NY, Garibaldi

Fonzo’s NY is a New York-style smash burger bar between Garibaldi and Brera, founded by Giacomo Cavalli, a model, Olympic sailor and artist.

  • Address: Via Anfiteatro 2, Milan (Garibaldi / Brera)
  • Signature burger: the Classic double with cheddar, onion and pickles, €12 (Scatti di Gusto, 29 June 2026)
  • Check before you go: the June 2026 tasting is the latest source I found. Check the official Instagram for current hours.

8. Street Smash Burgers, Porta Venezia

Street Smash Burgers is a smash burger chain founded in Lisbon in 2024. It now runs several outlets in Milan, including one in Porta Venezia.

  • Address: Via Lazzaro Spallanzani 6, Milan (Porta Venezia)
  • Price: €11.90–13.90 (Scatti di Gusto, 29 June 2026)
  • Source: Scatti di Gusto menu and prices

9. Denzel, near Piazza Piemonte

Denzel is Milan’s kosher burger restaurant. You choose a beef, lamb or mixed patty in 150 g, 180 g or 230 g.

  • Address: Via Giorgio Washington 9, Milan (near Piazza Piemonte)
  • Good to know: closed on Saturdays for Shabbat.
  • Check before you go: I found no 2026 source confirming it is open (zero.eu).

10. Flower Burger, Italy’s first vegan burger chain

Flower Burger is the best-known vegan burger in Milan. Matteo Toto founded it in the city in 2015 as Italy’s first vegan burger fast-food chain, and its buns are coloured with turmeric, plant charcoal or cherry extract.

  • Address: I could not confirm a current Milan address, so check the store list on the official site before you go.
  • Official site: flowerburger.it

Best smash burger in Milan

The best smash burgers in Milan are at Dumbo, Meat Crew and Chuck’s, the top three addresses inside the city in Scatti di Gusto’s June 2026 tasting of 15 smash burgers. First place went to JB Burger, just outside the city limits (see below). Inside Milan, a smash burger costs €10 to €15.50.

To make one at home, our copycat of Sonic’s All-American Bacon Smasher shows how to smash a patty on a hot griddle. More home versions are in our burger recipes section.

Worth the trip: JB Burger in Rozzano

JB Burger is the best smash burger in the Milan metro area, according to Scatti di Gusto, which ranked it first in 2026. It is missing from my top 10 only because it is in Rozzano, outside the city of Milan. Joe Bastianich opened it on 7 April 2026 with Bun Burgers and Gioia Group, in the Fiordaliso shopping centre at Via Eugenio Curiel 25. The patties are 90–100 g and smashed, and the New York Cheeseburger costs €7.90–13.90 (Scatti di Gusto). Branches have been announced in Brera, Turin and Rome.

Where to eat burgers in Milan by neighbourhood

Porta Venezia is where I would base a burger crawl: Dumbo, Meat Crew and Street Smash Burgers are a short walk apart. Isola and Brera come next.

  • Porta Venezia: Dumbo (Via Achille Maiocchi 12), Meat Crew (Via Melzo 24) and Street Smash Burgers (Via Lazzaro Spallanzani 6)
  • Isola: Dumbo at Mercato Comunale Isola
  • Brera and Garibaldi: Ham Holy Burger (Via Palermo 15) and Fonzo’s NY (Via Anfiteatro 2)
  • City centre: Al Mercato (Via Sant’Eufemia 16)
  • Centrale station: Joe’s American Smashburger
  • Porta Vittoria: Chuck’s
  • Piazza Piemonte and De Angeli: Denzel (Via Giorgio Washington 9) and Ham Holy Burger (Via Marghera 34)
  • Bocconi and Zara: Meat Crew

Closed burger restaurants in Milan to skip

Several burger places that older Milan guides still list are closed or no longer operating normally.

  • Quintalino, Via Terraggio 9: opened on 22 November 2023 by Alessandro Cattelan, Francesco Panella and Vittorio De Rosa, and has since closed (Dissapore).
  • Trussardi, Piazza della Scala: the restaurant whose burger appeared in 2022 rankings closed for good around September 2023 (Scatti di Gusto).
  • Al Mercato Porta Venezia and Porta Nuova: closed in 2025. Only Via Sant’Eufemia remains.
  • Burgez: the Milan chain Simone Ciaruffoli founded in 2015 had about 20 outlets. It went into liquidation in 2025 and was taken over by the Obicà group (Il Giorno). I could not confirm which outlets are still open.

How much does a burger cost in Milan?

A burger in a Milan burger restaurant costs about €10 to €16 in 2026, and a full meal with a drink €25 to €40 per person. Smash burgers run from €10–12 at Chuck’s to €14.50–15.50 at Meat Crew (Scatti di Gusto, June 2026). The meal range comes from Milano Segreta, and Dissapore puts a meal at Meat Crew at about €25. Treat these as guide figures: menus change.

FAQ: burgers in Milan

Where can I eat the best burger in Milan? For a gourmet burger, go to Al Mercato Burger Bar at Via Sant’Eufemia 16, open since 2010. For a smash burger, go to Dumbo in Isola or Porta Venezia, Meat Crew, or Chuck’s in Porta Vittoria.

What is the best smash burger in Milan? Inside the city, Dumbo and Meat Crew ranked second and third in Scatti di Gusto’s June 2026 tasting. First place went to JB Burger, in the Fiordaliso shopping centre in Rozzano, just outside Milan.

Where can I get a vegan or vegetarian burger in Milan? Flower Burger, founded in Milan in 2015, is Italy’s first vegan burger chain. Dumbo also serves a vegetarian Earth Burger at both of its Milan branches.

Is there a kosher burger in Milan? Yes. Denzel, at Via Giorgio Washington 9 near Piazza Piemonte, serves kosher beef, lamb or mixed burgers and is closed on Saturdays. Check that it is open before you go: I found no 2026 confirmation.

Is there a Wendy’s in Milan? No. As of 9 October 2026, Wendy’s had not opened in Milan. The Milan project has been delayed, and Rome will come first. See our 2026 report on burger chain expansion.

Which Milan burgers use Italian ingredients? Ham Holy Burger uses Piedmontese Fassona beef and bakes its bread every day. Dumbo’s Milan patties use two-week aged beef from La Granda.

Is any Milan restaurant in the World’s 101 Best Burger Places 2026? No. The only Italian restaurant on the 2026 list is Reburger in Florence, at number 27 (La Nazione).
