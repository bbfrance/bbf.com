Big Fernand Bordeaux: Address, Hours, Menu

Big Fernand Bordeaux, 5 rue Guiraude in the Promenade Sainte-Catherine: opening hours, menu, prices and Google reviews, checked on 31 October 2026.

By Thomas, Restaurants writer, FrancePublished November 1, 2026

Big Fernand Bordeaux is at 5 rue Guiraude, 33000 Bordeaux, inside the open-air Promenade Sainte-Catherine shopping district, about 350 m south of the Grand Théâtre. It is the only Big Fernand in Bordeaux. The chain serves French gourmet burgers, which it calls “hamburgés”, for dine-in, takeaway and delivery, and you should budget €10 to €20 per person. The shopping centre lists it as open every day, public holidays included, from 12:00 to 22:30. Google rates it 4.5 out of 5, on 1,585 to 3,606 reviews depending on which aggregator you trust. I checked every fact below on 31 October 2026.

A word on method first. I have not eaten at this branch for this guide. It is built from the chain’s official pages, the Promenade Sainte-Catherine listing, the local press and the review platforms, each cited and dated. Where they disagree, I show you the disagreement instead of quietly picking a winner. The format mirrors my Big Fernand Toulouse guide, so you can set the 2 South-West branches side by side.

Where is Big Fernand in Bordeaux? Address and location

Big Fernand Bordeaux is at 5 rue Guiraude, 33000 Bordeaux, inside the Promenade Sainte-Catherine shopping district. Mappy, Sluurpy, Restaurant Guru, Le Bonbon and Justacote all give that address (Mappy, Sluurpy), and the Promenade Sainte-Catherine lists Big Fernand among its shops on its official brand page.

The chain’s own page is vaguer. It names the branch “Big Fernand Bordeaux Sainte-Catherine” and places it on rue Sainte-Catherine, Bordeaux’s long pedestrian shopping street, close to the Grand Théâtre and place de la Comédie (official page). No street number, no phone, no hours. That is why the directories fill the gap. If you are shopping or sightseeing in the old town and want a burger near rue Sainte-Catherine, this one is right on your route.

Big Fernand Bordeaux opening hours

According to the Promenade Sainte-Catherine, Big Fernand Bordeaux is open every day, public holidays included, from 12:00 to 22:30, and closed on 25 December, 1 January and 1 May. It is the most local source available. The review aggregators disagree with it, and the chain’s official page shows no hours at all.

Big Fernand Bordeaux menu and prices

Big Fernand Bordeaux serves the chain’s national menu: around 10 signature hamburgés, smash burgers, salads, homemade fries and desserts, for €10 to €20 per person. I found no burger exclusive to Bordeaux. The menu below comes from the official Big Fernand menu, which carries no prices.

The hamburgés: beef, chicken, fish and veggie

The signature burgers cover beef, chicken, fish and vegetarian, plus a build-your-own option.

  • Le Big Fernand: the house burger that gives the chain its name.
  • Le Bartholomé: billed by the chain as its star burger.
  • Le Victor: a beef burger.
  • Le Benoit Saint Denis: the newest addition to the menu.
  • Le Steve: the fish burger.
  • Le Paolo, Le Philibert and Le Paulin: the chicken burgers.
  • Le Gabin and Le Lucien: the 2 vegetarian burgers.
  • Build your own: you pick the fillings.

Smash burgers, salads and toasties

Beyond the hamburgés, the menu has 3 smash burgers (Smash Poitrine, Double Smash and Smash Fernand), a Steak-Frites, Big Falades salads (chicken, vegetarian or salmon) and Big Toastés toasted sandwiches.

Sides, desserts and drinks

The sides are Fernandines (homemade fries), Gargandines, Tendres (chicken pieces) and a side salad called the Falade. For dessert: Häagen-Dazs ice cream, a Brookie, a cookie, the Fondant Baulois, an artisan flan and a dessert cream. To drink, homemade peach iced tea and lemonade, plus wine, beer and soft drinks.

How much does Big Fernand Bordeaux cost?

Expect to spend €10 to €20 per person at Big Fernand Bordeaux, the range both Sluurpy and Restaurant Guru give. I could not get burger-by-burger prices for this branch: the delivery menus were not readable, and delivery prices are often marked up anyway. The closest reference point is the 2022 official menu quoted in my Toulouse guide, which put a burger at €13–13.50 and the Big Formule meal at €19–19.50. 4 years on, Bordeaux prices are unlikely to be lower, so check the board at the counter.

Vegetarian options at Big Fernand Bordeaux

Yes, there are vegetarian options: the national menu lists 2 vegetarian burgers, Le Gabin and Le Lucien, plus a vegetarian Big Falade salad. Sluurpy also lists vegetarian options for the Bordeaux branch.

What is a “hamburgé”? How Big Fernand differs from fast food

A “hamburgé” is Big Fernand’s own name for its burgers, built on baker’s bread, 100% French beef and raw-milk cheeses from a cheesemonger (official menu, Promenade Sainte-Catherine). The burgers carry old-fashioned French first names: Bartholomé, Victor, Gabin.

That sourcing is the real difference from a fast-food counter, and it explains why a meal here costs more than at the global giants. The seasonal specials push the same idea further. In December 2023, every Big Fernand in France served the Tartuffe: Charolais or Limousin beef, raw-milk Mont d’Or from cheesemonger Beillevaire, rocket and a truffle Frangine sauce, for that month only (Le Bonbon Bordeaux, 14 December 2023).

Customers praise the friendly, fast service and the generous burgers, and complain most about prices and peak-time waits. Restaurant Guru also ranks the branch 39th of 1,093 fast-food restaurants in Bordeaux. My reading: the Google score is the one to trust here. It sits on more than 1,500 reviews whichever count you take, while the Tripadvisor dip rests on about 200, many of them from tourists comparing it with a sit-down restaurant.

What reviewers like

Across Sluurpy, Restaurant Guru and Custplace, the same strengths keep coming back:

  • a welcoming team and quick service;
  • generous, juicy burgers;
  • homemade fries and sides;
  • the homemade ketchup, singled out by several reviewers.

What reviewers criticize

The complaints are just as consistent:

  • prices seen as high for a burger meal;
  • waiting at peak times;
  • quality that varies from 1 visit to the next.

1 isolated review also raised hygiene and how the dishes were finished. A single comment is not a pattern, but it is there.

Is Big Fernand Bordeaux worth it for a visitor?

Yes, if you want a French-sourced burger in the middle of a shopping or sightseeing day and do not want to gamble on an unknown address. The sourcing story is real, the location could hardly be more central and the Google score is high on a large sample. It is not the place for a cheap bite, and it is not where you will find Bordeaux’s most original burger. For independent burger bars across the city, start with my colleague Alex’s best burger in Bordeaux guide.

Delivery, takeaway and other services

Big Fernand Bordeaux offers dine-in, takeaway and delivery, according to its official page. For delivery, it is listed on Deliveroo and on Uber Eats as “Big Fernand - Bordeaux”.

Sluurpy and Restaurant Guru also report a terrace, wheelchair access and card payment, and Sluurpy adds that the branch takes French meal vouchers (titres-restaurant). These come from aggregators only, so call ahead if any of them matters to you. I found no source on table reservations, and the restaurant is not on TheFork.

History of Big Fernand and its Bordeaux branch

Big Fernand was founded in Paris in 2011 by Steve Burggraf, Alexandre Auriac and Guillaume Pagliano, and opened in Bordeaux on 26 April 2018. When Le Journal des Entreprises announced the opening, the network counted around 40 restaurants in France and already had a Gironde address in Arcachon, opened in 2016 (Le Journal des Entreprises, 16 April 2018).

Today the chain is owned by Bluegem Capital Partners. It had 45 restaurants as of 22 September 2026, 44 in France and 1 in Luxembourg, and network sales of €52 million in 2025 (Paperjam). For the full timeline and the figures behind it, read our Big Fernand brand profile.
