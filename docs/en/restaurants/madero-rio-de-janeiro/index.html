Madero Rio de Janeiro: Locations, Hours and Menu

Every Madero Steak House in Rio, from Botafogo and Leblon to Barra da Tijuca and Santos Dumont airport, with opening hours checked on October 17, 2026.

By Lucas, Restaurants writer, BrazilPublished November 11, 2026

Madero has around fifteen locations in Rio de Janeiro. That count comes from the official restaurant pages I went through on October 17, 2026, plus the venue that opened at Shopping Metropolitano Barra in August. Staying in Copacabana, Ipanema or Leblon? Go to Madero Rio Sul in Botafogo or Madero Rio Design Leblon. Both open daily at 11:45 am and close at 10:30 pm Sunday to Thursday, and at 11 pm on Friday and Saturday. For a table facing the sea, Madero Praia do Pepê in Barra da Tijuca serves until 11 pm every day. And if you are flying out of Santos Dumont, there is a Madero Steak House inside the airport.

Madero is a sit-down steak house chain from Curitiba, and the cheeseburger is the dish it built its name on. Do not walk in expecting fast food. You get table service, and at peak times a wait to match. Below: which location to pick for your neighbourhood, every address and phone number, opening hours, the menu, what reviewers say and how to book.

Which Madero in Rio de Janeiro should you choose?

For most visitors, the best Madero in Rio de Janeiro is Madero Rio Sul in Botafogo. It is a short hop from Copacabana, and it has more Google reviews than any other Rio location I could check. Where you sleep decides the rest.

  • Copacabana, Botafogo or Flamengo: Madero Steak House Shopping Rio Sul, Rua Lauro Müller, 116, Botafogo.
  • Ipanema or Leblon: Madero Steak House Rio Design Leblon, Av. Ataulfo de Paiva, 270, Leblon, in the basement of the Rio Design Leblon mall according to Riotur.
  • Barra da Tijuca, by the beach: Madero Steak House Praia do Pepê, Av. do Pepê, 700.
  • Barra da Tijuca, shopping day: Madero Steak House NYCC/BarraShopping, Av. das Américas, 5000.
  • Centro or before a flight: Madero Steak House Aeroporto Santos Dumont, Praça Senador Salgado Filho, Centro.

Madero Rio Sul in Botafogo: the most-reviewed Madero in Rio

Madero Rio Sul is the nearest Madero to Copacabana and Botafogo, with a 4.5/5 Google rating from 7,348 reviews. It is in Shopping Rio Sul at Rua Lauro Müller, 116, Botafogo, Rio de Janeiro – RJ, 22290-160, phone +55 21 3512-0024.

That review count is why it is my default recommendation. A 4.5 that holds across more than seven thousand diners tells you more than a perfect score from a few hundred. TripAdvisor is harsher, at 3.6/5, and I read the gap as good burgers with uneven service.

The dining room and takeaway are open Sunday to Thursday from 11:45 am to 10:30 pm, and Friday and Saturday from 11:45 am to 11 pm. Delivery runs every day from 11:45 am to 9:45 pm. The official page says it takes reservations and groups, and Restaurant Guru lists it as wheelchair accessible.

Madero Leblon: the pick for Ipanema and Leblon

If you are staying in Ipanema or Leblon, Madero Rio Design Leblon is the one to choose. It is at Av. Ataulfo de Paiva, 270, Leblon, Rio de Janeiro – RJ, 22440-033, phone +55 21 3512-0242. Riotur says it was the second Madero to open in Rio and has 140 seats.

The dining room and takeaway are open Sunday to Thursday from 11:45 am to 10:30 pm, and Friday and Saturday from 11:45 am to 11 pm. Delivery stops at 10 pm Sunday to Thursday and at 10:30 pm on Friday and Saturday. Riotur gives other hours and another phone number. I have gone with the official Madero page.

Madero Praia do Pepê: the beachfront Madero in Barra da Tijuca

Madero Praia do Pepê is the only Madero in Rio I found facing the beach, and it is the one I would pick for a weekend lunch. It is at Av. do Pepê, 700, Barra da Tijuca, Rio de Janeiro – RJ, 22620-170, phone +55 21 3197-1790. The dining room is open every day from 11:45 am to 11 pm. Delivery runs daily until 10 pm.

Sopa Cultural reported that it opened on Sunday, April 28, 2024. It covers 397 m² (about 4,270 sq ft), seats more than 150 people and was designed by the architect Kethlen Ribas Durski.

Madero Santos Dumont Airport: a burger before your flight

Yes, there is a Madero Steak House at Santos Dumont Airport, Rio’s domestic airport in Centro. The address is Praça Senador Salgado Filho, s/n, Centro, Rio de Janeiro – RJ, 20021-340, phone +55 21 3512-0708.

The dining room and takeaway open at 11:45 am every day, so this one is no help on an early morning departure. They close at 10:30 pm Sunday to Thursday and at 11 pm on Friday and Saturday. The official sitemap also has a separate Madero & Jeronimo Burger page at Santos Dumont.

Madero NYCC/BarraShopping: the big mall in Barra

Madero NYCC/BarraShopping is the obvious stop on a shopping day in Barra da Tijuca, with a 4.5/5 Google rating from 4,029 reviews. It is at Av. das Américas, 5000, Barra da Tijuca, Rio de Janeiro – RJ, 22640-102, phone +55 21 3512-0038.

Watch the hours here. The dining room and delivery run Monday to Thursday from 11:45 am to 10:30 pm, Friday and Saturday from 11:45 am to 11 pm, and Sunday from noon to 9:45 pm. That is the earliest Sunday close of any Madero in Rio. Takeaway runs Monday to Saturday from 11:45 am to 10 pm, and Sunday from noon to 9:45 pm.

The new Madero at Shopping Metropolitano Barra

Grupo Madero opened a triple venue at Shopping Metropolitano Barra on Monday, August 31, 2026, according to Diário do Rio and Correio da Manhã. A Madero Steak House, a Jeronimo Burger and an Empório Madero share the site at Av. Embaixador Abelardo Bueno, 1300, Barra da Tijuca.

On the menu: the Madero cheeseburger, chicken and vegetarian versions, Jeronimo smash burgers on brioche buns, fries and milkshakes. Its opening hours, phone number and seat count were not in the press coverage or on the official site when I looked.

Madero Rio de Janeiro menu: burgers, steaks and dessert

The Madero menu in Rio is built around the cheeseburger, with steaks, a vegetarian and vegan section and desserts. The official location pages highlight three dishes: the Cheeseburger, L’Entrecôte and the Petit Gâteau de Doce de Leite.

Riotur describes the Madero Cheeseburger as selected beef on a crusty bun baked on site, with iceberg lettuce, tomato, grilled onion, cheddar and house-made mayonnaise. I could not reconfirm the patty weight for Rio. The official Madero menu has the current figure.

Vegetarian and vegan options at Madero

Yes, Madero has vegetarian options. The menu has a vegetarian and vegan section, with nutrition and allergen information. When I checked, I could not confirm the current name or price of the vegetarian burger in Rio, so ask your server.

Madero Rio de Janeiro prices

Budget about R$60 to R$160 per person at Madero Rio Sul and R$60 to R$120 at Madero NYCC, according to Restaurant Guru’s estimates checked on October 17, 2026. I could not confirm individual burger prices at any Rio location, so I have not printed any. Before you go, open the Rappi menu for your location, which shows what each dish costs today.

Is there a happy hour at Madero in Rio?

Madero Praia do Pepê ran a weekday happy hour when it opened in April 2024, according to Sopa Cultural. From Monday to Friday, 5 pm to 8 pm, it took 50% off draught beer, caipirinhas, cocktails and wine by the glass, and 10% off the menu. Heineken or Amstel on draught started at R$7.45. Those are 2024 prices. Ask whether the offer still runs, and where.

Reservations, delivery and payment at Madero Rio

The official Madero pages for Rio list dine-in, reservations, takeaway, delivery and groups. That makes a family table easy to arrange, and Rio Sul and Leblon are the easiest of all.

Madero takes Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Diners Club, Elo and Maestro. It also takes the Brazilian meal vouchers Alelo, Sodexo, Ticket and VR. Delivery goes through Rappi, which lists Madero menus for Leblon, Centro, Tijuca, Barra and Del Castilho.

Madero: the Curitiba steak house chain behind the burger

Madero is a Brazilian steak house chain founded by the chef Junior Durski. The first Madero Steak House opened in Curitiba on June 7, 2005. Our guide to Madero in Curitiba, where it all began covers the home city.

By March 31, 2026, Grupo Madero ran 355 restaurants across 283 multi-brand sites, according to its investor relations profile. Its brands are Madero Steak House, Madero Burger, Jeronimo, Madero & Jeronimo, Madero Café and Empório Madero. Since 2015 a central kitchen in Ponta Grossa, Paraná, has supplied the chain. It covers 32,408 m² and produces or buys more than 96% of the group’s food. In 2026 the group opened its first restaurants outside Brazil, in Ciudad del Este, Paraguay.

Madero Steak House is the sit-down brand with table service. Jeronimo is the group’s smash burger brand, on brioche buns. In Rio the two sometimes share a site: the Metropolitano Barra venue, and the Madero & Jeronimo pages at Santos Dumont and Jacarepaguá.

Rio has its own chapter in Brazilian burger history: Bob’s, Brazil’s first fast-food chain, opened in Copacabana in 1952. For the rest of the city’s burger scene, head to our Rio de Janeiro burger hub.

Madero Rio de Janeiro FAQ

How many Madero restaurants are there in Rio de Janeiro? Around fifteen. The official sitemap lists 14 location pages in the city when checked on October 17, 2026, and a new venue opened at Shopping Metropolitano Barra on August 31, 2026.

Which Madero is closest to Copacabana, Ipanema or Leblon? From Copacabana, go to Madero Rio Sul in Botafogo, at Rua Lauro Müller, 116. From Ipanema or Leblon, go to Madero Rio Design Leblon, at Av. Ataulfo de Paiva, 270.

What are Madero’s opening hours in Rio? Most locations open at 11:45 am and close at 10:30 pm Sunday to Thursday and 11 pm on Friday and Saturday. Praia do Pepê is open every day until 11 pm, and NYCC/BarraShopping closes at 9:45 pm on Sunday.

Is there a Madero at Santos Dumont Airport? Yes. Madero Steak House Aeroporto Santos Dumont opens at 11:45 am every day and closes at 10:30 pm Sunday to Thursday and 11 pm on Friday and Saturday.

Can I book a table at Madero in Rio? Yes. The official pages for the Rio locations list reservations and groups.

Is there a beachfront Madero in Rio? Yes. Madero Praia do Pepê, at Av. do Pepê, 700 in Barra da Tijuca, faces the beach and has been open since April 28, 2024.

Which cards does Madero accept in Rio? Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Diners Club, Maestro and the Brazilian card Elo, plus the Alelo, Sodexo, Ticket and VR meal vouchers.

Is Madero fast food? No. Madero Steak House is a sit-down restaurant with table service. Jeronimo, from the same group, is its smash burger brand.
