Madero Brasília: Addresses, Hours, Prices and Reviews

All 12 Madero restaurants in the Federal District, compared: which one to pick, when each one opens, what a burger costs in reais and what diners say.

By Lucas, Restaurants writer, BrazilPublished October 13, 2026

Madero lists 12 restaurants in Brasília and the wider Federal District (DF) on its official website, checked on 2026-10-10. If you only have time for one, make it Madero Steak House Pátio Brasil in Asa Sul. It was the capital’s first Madero, opened in June 2015, and it has by far the most reviews: 4.6/5 on Google from 5,377 of them. Doors open at 11:45 a.m. every day. Closing is 10 p.m. Monday to Saturday, but 8 p.m. on Sundays and holidays, which catches people out. The Cheeseburger Madero showed up at R$59 in a Rappi search snippet for Asa Norte. I could not confirm that price on the live page.

Madero runs five full-service Steak Houses in Brasília’s big malls and a sixth at Brasília International Airport (BSB). All six serve the same national menu. What changes is the neighborhood, the closing time and the number you call to book.

Madero Pátio Brasil (Asa Sul): the address I recommend

Madero Steak House Pátio Brasil, in Asa Sul, is the Madero I would pick in Brasília. It is the oldest in the capital and has the most reviews behind its rating. If you are staying in the hotel sector, it is also the one you can walk to.

It opened in June 2015 inside Pátio Brasil Shopping as the first Madero in the DF, Metrópoles reported at the time. The mall itself dates from 1997. It has about 180 stores and 2,000 parking spaces, and it sits right next to the Setor Hoteleiro Sul, the hotel sector south of the Monumental Axis, according to Pátio Brasil Shopping.

The official address is Setor Comercial Sul Q 6 Bloco A, Asa Sul, Brasília – DF, 70307-902. You will see others. The mall gives SCS Quadra 07 Bloco A with the same postal code, Wanderlog lists SCS Q. 6 Lj 619 and TripAdvisor says SCS Quadra 7. Don’t worry: they all lead to the same mall.

The catch is the clock. Pátio Brasil closes earlier than the other Maderos in town, at 10 p.m. Monday to Saturday and 8 p.m. on Sundays and holidays, in step with the mall’s food court (10 a.m.–10 p.m. Monday to Saturday, noon–8 p.m. on Sunday). On a Sunday evening, Shopping ID, Park Shopping or Iguatemi buy you two and a half more hours.

The official page lists dine-in, takeout, delivery and table reservations. To book, call (61) 3041-7005. Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Elo and Diners are among the cards accepted.

The other Madero Steak Houses in Brasília

Shopping ID, Park Shopping and Iguatemi stay open later than Pátio Brasil: until 10:30 p.m. Sunday to Thursday and 11 p.m. on Friday and Saturday. Pick whichever is closest to you. Taguatinga’s hours are not published on a page I could verify.

Madero Shopping ID (Asa Norte)

Madero Shopping ID was the second Madero in the DF. It opened on August 3, 2016, with a charity dinner for the Lar da Criança Padre Cícero children’s home, according to Finíssimo. At opening it measured 595 m² with 182 seats, which makes it the biggest room for which I have figures. It is at Setor Comercial Norte Quadra 6 Conjunto A, Asa Norte, phone (61) 3246-1727, and it takes reservations and groups.

Madero Park Shopping (Guará)

Madero Park Shopping opened on April 26, 2017, as the fourth Madero in the DF, with 130 seats, Metrópoles reported. Its opening was also a charity dinner, at R$125 a head, for Abrace. It is at SMAS Trecho 1, 6580, Guará, phone (61) 3046-6746, and takes reservations, including large groups. Restaurant Guru shows different hours for this branch; go by the official page.

Madero Iguatemi Brasília (Lago Norte)

Madero Iguatemi is at SHIN CA 4, Lago Norte, phone (61) 3246-1752. In April 2019 Metrópoles announced it as the DF’s fifth Madero, due in the second half of that year. I could not confirm the actual opening date.

Madero Taguatinga Shopping

Madero Taguatinga Shopping is on the mall’s third floor and covers 548 m², according to the Correio Braziliense. Its menu puts the Cheeseburger Madero, L’Entrecôte and the petit gâteau de doce de leite up front. I could not verify its phone number, hours or opening date, so check the official site before you make the trip.

Madero at Brasília Airport (BSB): Gates 45–48

Yes, there is a Madero inside Brasília International Airport. Madero Steak House Aeroporto is between Gates 45 and 48 and opens every day from 11:40 a.m. to 9 p.m.

On a layover, it is the easiest sit-down burger at BSB, and you don’t have to leave the terminal. Watch the closing time, though. Evening departures after 9 p.m. are common, and the kitchen will be shut by then, so eat early. The phone number is (61) 3364-9815. It accepts Alelo, American Express, Diners Club, Elo, Maestro, Mastercard, Sodexo, Ticket, Visa and VR.

Madero Container and Madero & Jeronimo in Brasília

Beyond the six Steak Houses, Madero runs two smaller formats in the DF: Madero Container and Madero & Jeronimo. The official listing names three Containers and four Madero & Jeronimo sites. Taquari, in Lago Norte, appears under both, which is why the names add up to 13 while the site counts 12. I could not confirm whether Taquari is one restaurant or two.

Madero Container serves a shorter menu. The brand says it is made without preservatives or colorings, and it includes vegetarian and vegan options. The Águas Claras Container is at Av. das Castanheiras, corner of Rua 26 Norte, Águas Claras, 70297-400, phone (61) 3246-3062, per its official page. It serves dine-in and takeout daily from 11:30 a.m. to 11 p.m., the latest closing of any Madero in town, and delivers until 10:30 p.m. It also takes reservations and meal vouchers. For a late burger on a weeknight, this is the one.

Madero & Jeronimo is the group’s hybrid format: Madero under the same roof as Jeronimo, its smash burger brand. These sites are on the official list as of 2026-10-10, but I found no independent confirmation that all of them are open.

Madero Brasília reviews: what diners say about Pátio Brasil

Diners rate Madero Pátio Brasil 4.6/5 on Google from 5,377 reviews and 4.2/5 on TripAdvisor from 1,160, according to Wanderlog on 2026-10-10. People come back for the burger and the room. The pasta and the starters get the complaints.

  • What diners praise: the quality of the burgers, the decor, and an easy place to go with family or friends.
  • What diners criticize: pasta they find expensive and bland (the filet mignon with gorgonzola), forgettable starters, and a minority who call the burger “average” or “good but far from the best”.

That half-point gap between Google and TripAdvisor is telling. TripAdvisor leans toward travelers comparing Madero with burgers they ate elsewhere, and they are harder to impress than locals who treat it as their reliable mall dinner.

How Madero grew in Brasília: from 1 restaurant in 2015 to 12

Madero went from one restaurant in Brasília in 2015 to 12 listed across the Federal District in 2026. The Steak Houses came first, in the big malls. The smaller formats followed.

  • June 2015: the first Madero in the DF opens at Pátio Brasil, Asa Sul.
  • August 3, 2016: the second, Shopping ID in Asa Norte.
  • April 26, 2017: the fourth, Park Shopping in Guará.
  • April 2019: the DF has four Maderos (Shopping ID, Pátio Brasil, Park Shopping, Águas Claras), and Iguatemi Lago Norte is announced as the fifth.
  • October 2026: 12 locations listed on the official site, across three formats.

What is Madero? The Brazilian burger chain explained

Madero is a Brazilian steak house chain with table service, best known for its cheeseburger. It is not a fast-food counter. Chef Junior Durski founded it, and the first Madero Steak House opened in Curitiba on June 7, 2005. I tell that story in my Madero Curitiba guide.

According to its investor relations profile, Grupo Madero ran 355 restaurants in 283 multi-brand operations as of March 31, 2026. Its brands are Madero, Jeronimo, Madero & Jeronimo, Madero Café and Empório Madero. Much of the food is prepared in a central kitchen in Ponta Grossa, Paraná, and finished in each restaurant.

If you have never been, picture a casual steak house inside a shopping mall. There are waiters and a wine list, and the menu is built around beef. Still, the burger is what everyone orders.

Is Madero Brasília worth it for burger lovers?

Yes, if you want a dependable Brazilian chain burger in a comfortable dining room. Order the Cheeseburger Madero, skip the pasta, and budget for a full meal rather than a quick bite.

My pick is Pátio Brasil if you are in Asa Sul or the hotel sector, or if you want the branch with the most reviews behind it. On a Sunday night, go to Shopping ID, Park Shopping or Iguatemi instead, since they close later. For a late weeknight burger, the Container in Águas Claras serves until 11 p.m. At the airport, Gates 45–48 is the obvious layover stop, as long as you are there before 9 p.m.

A Madero in Brasília serves the same menu as one in Fortaleza or Curitiba. That consistency is both its strength and its limit: you know exactly what you will get, and you won’t be surprised. If you have only one burger night in the capital, I would rather you try a local spot from our Brasília burger city guide. For the same chain elsewhere, read Madero Fortaleza. Practical information: reservations, delivery, payment and parking

Every Madero Steak House with an official page in Brasília offers dine-in, takeout and delivery, and most take reservations. Card and meal-voucher acceptance is broad.

  • Reservations: confirmed at Pátio Brasil, Shopping ID, Park Shopping (including large groups) and Container Águas Claras. Call the branch.
  • Delivery: offered by Pátio Brasil, Shopping ID, Park Shopping, Iguatemi, the airport and Container Águas Claras. Madero Steak House Asa Norte is listed on Rappi.
  • Payment: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Elo and Diners at Pátio Brasil. Iguatemi, Park Shopping and the airport also take Maestro and the Alelo, Sodexo, Ticket and VR meal vouchers. Container Águas Claras takes meal vouchers.
  • Parking: Pátio Brasil Shopping has about 2,000 spaces. I could not confirm parking prices.
  • Public transport and accessibility: I could not verify the nearest metro station or wheelchair access for any branch. Check with the mall before you go.

Madero Brasília FAQ

How many Madero restaurants are there in Brasília? Twelve across the Federal District, according to the official site on 2026-10-10. The list includes six Madero Steak Houses (Pátio Brasil, Shopping ID, Park Shopping, Iguatemi, Taguatinga Shopping and the airport), three Madero Containers and four Madero & Jeronimo sites. Taquari, in Lago Norte, appears under both of the smaller formats.

Which Madero should I choose in Brasília? Pátio Brasil in Asa Sul. It was the first in the city, in June 2015, and holds 4.6/5 on Google from 5,377 reviews. On Sunday evenings, pick Shopping ID, Park Shopping or Iguatemi, which close later.

What are Madero Brasília’s opening hours on Sunday? Pátio Brasil is open 11:45 a.m.–8 p.m. on Sundays and holidays. Shopping ID, Park Shopping and Iguatemi are open 11:45 a.m.–10:30 p.m. The airport branch is open 11:40 a.m.–9 p.m. every day.

How much does a burger cost at Madero in Brasília? A Rappi search snippet for Asa Norte showed the Cheeseburger Madero at R$59 and burgers from R$54 to R$83. I could not confirm those prices on the live page, and dining-room prices may differ.

Is there a Madero at Brasília airport? Yes. Madero Steak House is between Gates 45 and 48 at Brasília International Airport, open daily from 11:40 a.m. to 9 p.m. Phone: (61) 3364-9815.

Can I book a table at Madero in Brasília? Yes. Call Pátio Brasil on (61) 3041-7005, Shopping ID on (61) 3246-1727 or Park Shopping on (61) 3046-6746.

Does Madero Brasília deliver? Yes. The Steak Houses with an official page and Container Águas Claras deliver, and Madero Steak House Asa Norte is listed on Rappi.

What is the difference between Madero Steak House, Madero Container and Madero & Jeronimo? The Steak House is the full-service restaurant with the complete menu. The Container serves a shorter menu with vegetarian and vegan options. Madero & Jeronimo is a hybrid of Madero and Jeronimo, the group’s smash burger brand.

Does Madero have vegetarian or vegan options? Yes. Madero Container Águas Claras lists vegetarian and vegan options. I could not confirm the name or price of a meat-free burger in Brasília.

Does Madero Brasília have a happy hour? In 2015 Pátio Brasil offered draft beer at R$5 and half-price appetizers, 6–8 p.m. on weekdays. I could not confirm that the offer still runs.

What is the best burger on the Madero menu? The Cheeseburger Madero: a 180 g patty on a crusty bun with organic lettuce and tomato, cheddar-style cheese and house-made mayonnaise.
