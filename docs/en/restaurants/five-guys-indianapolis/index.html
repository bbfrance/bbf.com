Five Guys Indianapolis: Locations, Hours, Prices

All 6 Five Guys in Indianapolis: addresses, phones, hours, prices with the 9% meal tax and review scores, plus the downtown pick near Monument Circle.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 25, 2026

Five Guys has six restaurants inside Indianapolis city limits, according to the chain’s official store locator, which I checked on November 19, 2026. If you’re downtown for a convention, a Pacers game or the sights, go to Five Guys Lincoln at 48 E Washington St, a short walk from Monument Circle. Review directories list most of the stores as open 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily; Five Guys itself doesn’t post hours. Budget about $15 for a cheeseburger and regular fries after Indy’s 9% meal tax. That figure uses national reference prices, because no Indianapolis menu was available online.

Indianapolis has six official Five Guys: Lincoln downtown, Castleton, Pyramid Place, Cherry Tree Crossing, Raceway Market Shops and Greenwood Place. The locator lists in-store dining, curbside and delivery for all six. Which Five Guys in Indianapolis should you pick?

For a visitor, the best pick is Five Guys Lincoln at 48 E Washington St, the only downtown store and the closest to Monument Circle. The other five sit in suburban-style shopping centers, so choose by where you’re staying or driving. Don’t cross town for a rating: Pyramid Place and Raceway Market Shops lead Birdeye at 4.4, the rest sit at 4.3, and a tenth of a point across 1,500 reviews is noise.

  • Downtown, conventions, sightseeing: Lincoln, 48 E Washington St.
  • North side, Castleton or 82nd Street: Castleton Shopping Center, 5317 East 82nd Street.
  • Northwest, along West 86th Street: Pyramid Place Shoppes, 3454 W 86th Street.
  • East side, along East Washington Street: Cherry Tree Crossing, 9808 East Washington Street.
  • West side, Rockville Road: Raceway Market Shops, 9210 Rockville Road Unit 1.
  • South side, US 31: Greenwood Place, 7815 S. US 31.

Five Guys downtown Indianapolis: the Lincoln store

Five Guys Lincoln is at 48 E Washington St, Indianapolis, IN 46204, phone 317-353-3422. Its official page lists dine-in, takeout and delivery. Restaurantji describes it as wheelchair accessible, with sidewalks and ramps. That’s the only accessibility information I found, so call ahead if it matters to you.

Parking near the downtown Five Guys

The nearest garage is City Park Garage at 120 E Washington St, which takes automated payment. Circle Centre Mall’s garage at 48 W Maryland St is the cheaper bet, listed at $2 for up to three hours. Both come from a local guide by IndyToday, not from Five Guys, so check the rates at the gate.

Five Guys Indianapolis hours

Review directories list most Indianapolis Five Guys as open 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day, Sunday included. Treat those as directory hours: the official Five Guys store pages showed no hours when I checked on November 19, 2026.

Birdeye shows 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily for Lincoln, Castleton, Cherry Tree Crossing, Raceway Market Shops and Pyramid Place. Downtown is where it gets murky. Restaurantji says 10 a.m. to 10 p.m., another directory says 10 a.m. to 9:30 p.m. Call before a late or early visit there. I found no hours at all for Greenwood Place.

Five Guys Indianapolis menu

The Indianapolis stores serve the standard national Five Guys menu; I found no Indy-only item. That means burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches, fries, milkshakes and drinks, with 15 free toppings on any burger.

  • Burgers: Hamburger, Cheeseburger, Bacon Burger and Bacon Cheeseburger. Each comes as a regular two-patty burger or a one-patty Little version.
  • Hot dogs and sandwiches: hot dogs, the Veggie Sandwich and the Grilled Cheese.
  • Fries: Five Guys style or Cajun style.
  • Milkshakes and drinks.

The Indianapolis store pages repeat the brand’s promises: more than 250,000 ways to build a burger, more than 1,000 milkshake combinations, fresh beef that’s never frozen, fries cooked in 100% peanut oil and ingredients prepped by hand every day.

Want to recreate it at home? Julia’s Five Guys cheeseburger copycat recipe rebuilds the two-patty classic. Her Little Cheeseburger copycat and Bacon Burger copycat cover the rest of the menu.

Vegetarian options, gluten and peanut oil

Vegetarians get two choices: the Veggie Sandwich and the Grilled Cheese. Fries are cooked in peanut oil, which matters if you have a peanut allergy. I couldn’t find a reliable gluten-free answer for these stores, so check the official allergen list on fiveguys.com before you order.

Five Guys Indianapolis prices

There’s no official Indianapolis price list I could confirm. The online ordering site and the delivery apps either blocked access or showed incomplete menus when I checked. The prices below are national reference prices, with their source and date, and your store may differ.

Sources don’t agree. ThePricer updated its page on September 1, 2026, with much higher numbers: a Hamburger at $13.19–$13.26, a Cheeseburger at $14.52–$14.99 and regular fries at $8.99. My read is that those are delivery-app prices, which run well above the counter, so I use the February list for the math below. For the downtown store, Restaurantji gives $22 as an example full meal.

How much is a burger and fries with tax in Indianapolis?

A cheeseburger with regular fries comes to about $15.13 after tax at February 2026 national prices. The math: $8.69 + $5.19 = $13.88, plus 9% tax ($1.25). A Hamburger with regular fries works out to about $14.37.

What a Five Guys meal costs in Indianapolis with tax (7% Indiana sales tax + 2% Marion County food and beverage tax): Cheeseburger + regular fries $13.88 → $15.13; Hamburger + regular fries $13.18 → $14.37; Little Cheeseburger + regular fries $11.88 → $12.95. February 2026 national prices, drink and tip not included. (Table: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0, data: ThePricer (February 2026) and Indiana Department of Revenue, checked November 19, 2026)

That 9% stacks two taxes on prepared food in Marion County. Indiana’s 7% state sales tax applies, with no local sales tax on top, and Marion County adds a 2% food and beverage tax. Sources: Indiana Department of Revenue and Kintsugi’s Indianapolis sales tax guide, checked November 19, 2026.

Five Guys Indianapolis reviews

Customers rate the Indianapolis Five Guys 4.3 or 4.4 out of 5 on Birdeye, each on roughly 1,480 to 1,790 reviews (checked November 19, 2026). Scores from different platforms are given separately below.

  • Lincoln (downtown): 4.3/5 on Birdeye (1,789 reviews). 4/5 on Restaurantji (296 reviews), with sub-scores of 4.4 for food, 4.3 for service and 4.0 for atmosphere.
  • Castleton: 4.3/5 on Birdeye (1,701 reviews).
  • Cherry Tree Crossing: 4.3/5 on Birdeye (1,721 reviews).
  • Raceway Market Shops: 4.4/5 on Birdeye (1,477 reviews). The store hasn’t claimed this listing.
  • Pyramid Place: 4.4/5 on Birdeye (1,511 reviews).
  • Greenwood Place: no verifiable rating found.

What customers praise at the downtown store

Restaurantji reviewers of the downtown store come back to the same points: courteous staff, ingredients that taste fresh, juicy burgers and fry portions that are hard to finish. A few also note the vegetarian option. That’s the brand’s pitch delivered as promised: a made-to-order burger with a pile of toppings and a bag of fries big enough to share.

What customers complain about

Price tops the list. After that come smaller frustrations at the downtown store:

  • Fries that are too salty.
  • Cheese that isn’t melted.
  • Grill smoke drifting into the dining room.
  • Not many seats.
  • A restroom that opens only with a code printed on your receipt.
  • Waits and order mistakes, reported by some customers.

My take: if seats and smoke bother you, order ahead and take your food out. And split the regular fries. Reviewers keep saying how big that bag is.

Ordering online, delivery and curbside pickup

Every Indianapolis Five Guys takes online orders through order.fiveguys.com and the Five Guys app, and all six offer delivery. Uber Eats and DoorDash both deliver from the downtown store.

Curbside is less clear. The city locator lists it for all six stores, but only the Castleton and Pyramid Place pages mention it, so confirm it when you place your order. Five Guys doesn’t take reservations as far as I could find; ordering ahead is the closest thing.

No Five Guys at Indianapolis International Airport

Indianapolis International Airport (IND) has no Five Guys. Past security, your burger options are Bub’s Burgers in Concourse A and Shake Shack in Concourse B, according to Indianapolis Monthly.

Five Guys history: from Arlington to Indiana

Five Guys was founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell and their sons. It began franchising in 2002. By May 2026 it had 1,999 restaurants in 28 countries and territories, including 1,517 in the United States. Sources: the Five Guys 2026 media fact sheet and Wikipedia. The full story is on our Five Guys brand profile.

A local burger to try instead of a chain

Honestly, if you only have one burger meal in Indianapolis, make it a local one. The Workingman’s Friend in Haughville opened in 1918 and is known for a double cheeseburger smashed very thin. You’ll find it and the rest of the city’s best burgers in our Indianapolis burger guide.

Five Guys Indianapolis FAQ

How many Five Guys are there in Indianapolis?

The official locator lists six Five Guys inside Indianapolis city limits: Lincoln (downtown), Castleton Shopping Center, Pyramid Place Shoppes, Cherry Tree Crossing, Raceway Market Shops and Greenwood Place. Carmel, Fishers, Greenwood, Plainfield, Brownsburg, Noblesville and Zionsville have more nearby.

Where is the Five Guys in downtown Indianapolis?

The downtown Five Guys, called Lincoln, is at 48 E Washington St, Indianapolis, IN 46204, near Monument Circle. Its phone number is 317-353-3422.

Is Five Guys in Indianapolis open on Sunday?

Yes. Review directories list most Indianapolis stores as open seven days a week, 11 a.m. to 10 p.m., Sunday included. Five Guys doesn’t publish these hours on its store pages, so call ahead to confirm.

How much is a Five Guys burger and fries in Indianapolis with tax?

At national February 2026 prices, a cheeseburger ($8.69) with regular fries ($5.19) comes to about $15.13 after Indianapolis’s 9% meal tax. That’s 7% Indiana sales tax plus 2% Marion County food and beverage tax. Delivery apps charge more.

Can I order online or get curbside pickup?

Yes for online ordering: use order.fiveguys.com or the Five Guys app. Delivery is listed for all six stores, and Uber Eats and DoorDash deliver from downtown. Curbside is confirmed on the Castleton and Pyramid Place pages; check for the other stores when you order.

Where can I park near the downtown Five Guys?

City Park Garage at 120 E Washington St is the closest. Circle Centre Mall’s garage at 48 W Maryland St is listed at $2 for up to three hours.

Is there a Five Guys at Indianapolis airport?

No. Indianapolis International Airport has no Five Guys. Your burger options are Bub’s Burgers in Concourse A and Shake Shack in Concourse B.

Does Five Guys have vegetarian options, and are the fries cooked in peanut oil?

Yes. The menu has a Veggie Sandwich and a Grilled Cheese, and fries are cooked in 100% peanut oil. For gluten and other allergens, check the official allergen list on fiveguys.com.
