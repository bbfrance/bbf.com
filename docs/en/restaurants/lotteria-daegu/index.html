Lotteria Daegu: Addresses, Hours, Menu, Reviews

Where to find Korea's biggest burger chain in Daegu, what to order for about ₩5,100, and what the few published reviews say, checked on October 15, 2026.

By Hana, Restaurants writer, Japan and IndonesiaPublished October 24, 2026

The most central Lotteria in Daegu is Lotteria Daegu Dongseong-ro (롯데리아 대구동성로점), at 39 Dongseong-ro, Jung-gu, on the city’s main shopping street. Its directory listing says it is open 24 hours a day, every day, with a one-hour break from 4:00 to 5:00 a.m. Order the Ria Bulgogi, Lotteria’s signature since 1992: ₩5,100 nationwide since May 28, 2026. My take: Lotteria is a fast-food chain, not a destination. It is still the cheapest way to taste how Korea rewrote the burger, and the bulgogi burger is the one item you won’t find at a chain back home.

How this guide was built: I have not eaten at the Daegu branches yet. Everything below comes from Korean directory listings, the Korean business press and Lotteria’s own channels, all checked on October 15, 2026, and every address, hour and rating names its source. One thing is missing: how many Lotteria branches Daegu has. I found no source that counts them, so I give no figure.

Lotteria Daegu Dongseong-ro: the late-night pick downtown

Lotteria Daegu Dongseong-ro is the branch to choose downtown, because it is listed as open around the clock except for one hour at dawn. According to Diningcode, you order at kiosks downstairs and eat in a dining room upstairs. The listing flags it as a good spot to eat alone, and it delivers. Phone: 053-600-1100.

Lotteria Daegu Jung-dong in Suseong-gu

Lotteria Daegu Jung-dong is a daytime branch on Myeongdeok-ro in Suseong-gu, open from 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. according to Diningcode. Its only review on that site describes a renovated, clean dining room. Phone: 053-767-4849.

Lotteria at Daegu International Airport

There is a Lotteria at Daegu International Airport, on the second floor of the terminal at 221 Gonghang-ro, Dong-gu. Its Triple listing gives no hours, no phone number and no reviews. Don’t count on it for a pre-flight meal without checking the airport directory first.

Other Lotteria branches in Daegu

Directory listings also exist for Lotteria Myeongdeok Negeori (롯데리아 명덕네거리점) and Lotteria Daegu Station (롯데리아 대구역사점), so the chain has more branches in the city center. Sources: Triple and Autoreserve. Trip.com also lists Lotteria restaurants in Seo-gu and Dong-gu, with no reviews. I could not verify the addresses or hours of these branches, so they stay out of the table.

Lotteria Daegu menu and prices: what to order

Order the Ria Bulgogi (리아 불고기), Lotteria’s bulgogi burger, at ₩5,100 for the burger alone. The Ria Shrimp (리아 새우) costs the same. Both are national prices, set on May 28, 2026, when Lotteria raised 22 single burgers by an average of 2.9%, or ₩100 to ₩300 each (CEO Score Daily, May 21, 2026).

The Lotteria bulgogi burger: Korean fast food explained

The Lotteria bulgogi burger is a burger flavored like bulgogi, the sweet soy-marinated Korean grilled beef. Lotteria launched it in 1992 and marked its 30th anniversary in 2022. Korean press puts cumulative sales above one billion. Treat that as a press figure: Lotteria has published no official, dated count (iNews24, Dailian). The shrimp burger is the other Korean classic, but if you order one thing, make it the bulgogi: it is the burger Lotteria built its name on.

Ria Bulgogi vs Hanwoo Bulgogi Burger

The difference is the beef. The Hanwoo Bulgogi Burger, launched in 2004, uses hanwoo, Korea’s native premium cattle. The Ria Bulgogi is the everyday version, and the cheaper one (Hankyung).

Lotteria Daegu reviews

Too few people have reviewed Lotteria in Daegu online to support a verdict. On Diningcode, Dongseong-ro scores 3.7 out of 5 from just 6 reviews, and Jung-dong scores 5.0 out of 5 from a single one (checked October 15, 2026). TripAdvisor, Yelp and Trip.com show nothing. I did not record Google, Naver or Kakao ratings, so I don’t quote them.

Is Lotteria Korean or Japanese?

Lotteria started in Japan and is now run from Korea. Shin Kyuk-ho of the Lotte group founded it in Tokyo on February 2, 1972. The first Korean branch, Lotteria Sogong in Seoul, opened on October 25, 1979 (iNews24). In 2023 Lotte sold the Japanese business to Zensho Holdings, which planned to rename the Japanese restaurants Zetteria by March 2026. That leaves Korea as the brand’s home (Wikipedia).

Lotteria in Korea in 2026: key facts

  • Operator: Lotte GRS, which calls Lotteria Korea’s first burger franchise (Smart Today, March 20, 2026).
  • Size: about 1,300 restaurants in South Korea, more than any other burger chain there (Sateconomy). Chowhound gives 1,323 in Korea and more than 1,650 across 8 countries.
  • Brand ranking: No. 1 in fast food on the 2026 Korea Brand Power Index (K-BPI) by KMAC, for the 28th year in a row.
  • Finances: Lotte GRS reported 2025 consolidated revenue of ₩1,118.9 billion and operating profit of ₩51 billion, up 30.6%.
  • New markets: Fullerton, California in August 2025, Malaysia in December 2025 and Singapore in February 2026.

How to order at Lotteria without speaking Korean

At Lotteria Daegu Dongseong-ro you order at a self-service kiosk downstairs, according to Diningcode. I could not confirm whether the kiosks have an English option, or which foreign cards and mobile wallets they accept, so carry a backup way to pay. Save the Korean name of your burger (리아 불고기) on your phone and show it if you get stuck. Online ordering runs through Lotteria’s official site and app, Lotte Eatz. Customer service answers on 080-550-0005, weekdays from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., according to a search summary of the Lotte Eatz contact page.

Frequently asked questions about Lotteria in Daegu

Where is the most central Lotteria in Daegu, and is it open at night? Lotteria Daegu Dongseong-ro, at 39 Dongseong-ro in Jung-gu, is the most central branch. Diningcode lists it as open 24 hours, every day, except for a break from 4:00 to 5:00 a.m.

How much is a bulgogi burger at Lotteria in Korea in 2026? The Ria Bulgogi costs ₩5,100 for the burger alone, the national price since May 28, 2026.

Is there a Lotteria at Daegu Airport or Daegu Station? Yes, there is one at Daegu International Airport, on the 2nd floor of the terminal at 221 Gonghang-ro. A Daegu Station branch (대구역사점) appears in directories, but I could not verify its details. I found no confirmed Lotteria at Dongdaegu Station.

Is Lotteria better or cheaper than McDonald’s in Korea? I had no dated McDonald’s Korea prices to compare like for like, so I give no price verdict. Lotteria is Korea’s biggest burger chain by restaurant count and has topped the K-BPI fast-food ranking for 28 years running. Where it beats McDonald’s, in my view, is on Korean flavors like bulgogi and shrimp.

Does Lotteria have vegetarian options in Korea? I could not verify vegetarian options at Lotteria Korea. Ask at the counter or check the menu on Lotte Eatz before you go.

Can I get Lotteria delivered in Daegu? Yes. Diningcode lists delivery from the Dongseong-ro branch, and Lotteria takes orders through its Lotte Eatz app. I did not verify whether Baemin or Coupang Eats cover Daegu branches.
