Burger King Chennai: All 15 Locations, Hours, Menu and Reviews

All 15 Burger King restaurants in Chennai with addresses, phones and hours from the official locator, plus the best outlet, the beef-free menu and budget.

By AlexPublished October 8, 2026

Burger King Chennai has 15 restaurants on the official Burger King India store locator, which I checked on 8 October 2026. My pick for most visitors is Burger King Royapettah, on the 3rd floor of Express Avenue mall on Whites Road: open 10 am to 11 pm daily, rated 4.1/5 across 2,371 aggregated Google reviews, and under 500 m from LIC metro station. Hungry late? Kovilambakkam serves until 1 am, the latest in the city, and nine other outlets, the airport among them, stay open until 11:59 pm. One thing to know before you order: like every Burger King in India, the Chennai menu has no beef and no pork. The Whopper comes in chicken, mutton or vegetarian versions.

Burger King Chennai at a glance

Burger King runs 15 listed restaurants in Chennai, spread from Madhavaram in the north to Guduvanchery on GST Road in the south. I built this guide from the official store locator and each official store page, then checked it against Yappe, EazyDiner, Zomato and Swiggy. Where sources disagree, I publish only the official figure and say so.

  • Number of restaurants: 15 listed on stores.burgerking.in (8 October 2026). An earlier ScrapeHero count from February 2025 found 12, so the network has grown. I could not confirm that all 15 are trading today.
  • Best overall: Royapettah (Express Avenue mall), central and the most reviewed.
  • Best for south Chennai: Velachery Road (Phoenix Market City), rated 4.2/5 on its official page.
  • Latest closing: Kovilambakkam, 10 am to 1 am.
  • At the airport: Aerohub West Block, Meenambakkam, open until 11:59 pm.
  • Menu: no beef, no pork; chicken, mutton and a large vegetarian range.
  • Budget: about ₹500 for two people, according to EazyDiner.

Which Burger King in Chennai should you choose?

Choose your Burger King in Chennai by neighbourhood first and closing time second. Every outlet serves the same national menu, so crossing the city for a particular branch makes no sense.

  • City centre and Mount Road: Royapettah at Express Avenue, under 500 m from LIC metro.
  • South Chennai and Velachery: Velachery Road at Phoenix Market City.
  • IT corridor (OMR): Thoraipakkam, Semmenchery or Egattur (Marina Mall).
  • East Coast Road: Neelankarai.
  • West Chennai: Vadapalani (Forum Vijaya Mall), Ashok Nagar or Mogappair.
  • Flying out: the airport outlet in Meenambakkam.
  • Late at night: Kovilambakkam, open until 1 am, or one of the street outlets open until 11:59 pm.

Burger King Express Avenue, Royapettah: the flagship pick

Burger King Royapettah is the best-documented and most-reviewed Burger King in Chennai. It sits in Shop No 7 on the 3rd floor of Express Avenue, No 49/50L, Whites Road, Royapettah, Chennai 600107. The official phone number is +91 86579 73371.

  • Hours: 10 am to 11 pm, every day, per the official page. Hours may vary with the mall’s own opening times.
  • Services: dine-in, takeaway and delivery through the BK app.
  • Payment: cash, debit cards, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UPI and online payment.
  • Parking: free parking on site.
  • Getting there: LIC metro station is about 460 m away.
  • Ratings: 4.1/5 on the official page and 4.10/5 across 2,371 Google reviews aggregated by Yappe.
  • Budget: about ₹500 for two people, per EazyDiner.

The hours for this outlet are a mess online. Yappe says 7 am to 11 pm; EazyDiner says 11 am to 8 pm. I publish only the official 10 am to 11 pm, and if you are cutting it close, ring the store first. Yappe also shows a different phone number. Use the official one above.

Burger King Phoenix Market City, Velachery: best for south Chennai

Burger King Velachery Road is the best pick for south Chennai and has the highest official rating in the city, 4.2/5. It is at PN S/40, 2nd Floor, Phoenix Market City, Velachery Road, Chennai 600042, phone +91 74001 33407. Hours run 11 am to 11 pm, Monday to Sunday. Payment options match Royapettah, parking is free, and EazyDiner puts the average spend at about ₹500 for two.

Burger King Chennai menu: what you can order

The Burger King Chennai menu follows the national Indian menu, with no beef and no pork. The official Chennai store page groups it into seven categories: Burgers & Wraps, Chicken Wings, Whopper, King Premium Burger, Sides & Beverages, BK Cafe and Desserts. I found no item exclusive to Chennai or Tamil Nadu.

What is in a Whopper in India?

In India the Whopper is made with chicken, mutton or a vegetarian patty, never beef. Burger King launched in India in 2014 with mutton and chicken Whoppers and vegetarian options such as a paneer melt, according to NPR. To compare with the beef original, Clara’s Whopper ingredients and copycat recipe breaks down the classic build.

Table comparing the Whopper in the US with the Whopper in India. US: flame-grilled beef patty. India: chicken, mutton or vegetarian patty, no beef or pork, made on separate vegetarian and non-vegetarian lines with separate staff. (Table: Burger Breton, sources NPR (2014) and Ahram Online)

Vegetarian burgers at Burger King Chennai

Vegetarian burgers take up a large share of the menu at Burger King Chennai. They are not an afterthought. At the 2014 launch, NPR and Ahram Online reported that Burger King India ran two separate production lines, one vegetarian and one non-vegetarian, each with its own staff. Those reports are from 2014, and I found no newer official statement on the setup. In a city where many diners are strictly vegetarian, it is worth asking at the counter.

Burger King Chennai menu prices

The only Burger King Chennai price I could confirm from an official source is a current deal: two Crispy Veg Burgers for ₹79, via the BK app or in restaurant. The average bill is about ₹500 for two people at Royapettah and Phoenix Market City, according to EazyDiner.

I am not publishing a Whopper price. The official burgerking.in site returned an error when I checked, and unofficial price sites contradict each other: one puts the Veg Whopper anywhere from ₹139 to ₹313, and another lists a Bacon King and a beef Whopper that are not sold in India. Check the BK app, Swiggy or Zomato for the live price at your outlet. The ₹79 deal is a good example of the low-price push I covered in the 2026 burger value wars.

Delivery, payment and practical tips

You can get Burger King delivered in Chennai through the BK app, and the restaurants are also listed on Swiggy and Zomato. The Royapettah and Velachery outlets take cash, debit cards, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UPI and online payment.

  • Foreign cards: the official pages list Visa, Mastercard and Amex, but they do not say whether foreign-issued cards work. Carry some cash as a backup.
  • Mall outlets: Express Avenue, Phoenix Market City, VR Chennai, Forum Vijaya Mall and Marina Mall outlets follow mall hours.
  • Not confirmed: wheelchair access, drive-thru, reservations and breakfast service. None appear on the official pages, so I do not claim them.

Burger King in India and Chennai: a short history

Burger King opened its first Indian restaurant on 9 November 2014 at Select Citywalk in Saket, New Delhi, in a joint venture with Everstone. In 2015 the company named Chennai as its next southern city after Bengaluru and Hyderabad, and it has been in Chennai since the mid-2010s. I could not confirm the exact date or address of the first Chennai outlet.

A Burger King restaurant in New Delhi, the city where the chain opened its first Indian outlet at Select Citywalk, Saket, on 9 November 2014. (Photo: PrakharPachaury, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Burger\_king\_DELHI.jpg))

The Indian operator, once called Burger King India Limited, is now Restaurant Brands Asia (RBA). Inspira Global took control on 7 July 2026 with a 41.78% stake, investing about ₹2,235 crore. The 600th Indian Burger King opened on 10 September 2026 at GTB Nagar in Delhi; the chain is in more than 140 cities and targets 700 restaurants by December 2026. In Q1 of fiscal 2027, RBA’s consolidated revenue rose 17.9% year on year to ₹822.6 crore, and Burger King India’s same-store sales rose 12.6%. Frequently asked questions about Burger King Chennai

How many Burger King restaurants are there in Chennai?

The official Burger King India store locator lists 15 restaurants in Chennai as of 8 October 2026, up from 12 counted by ScrapeHero in February 2025.

Which is the best Burger King in Chennai?

Burger King Royapettah at Express Avenue is the best all-round pick: the most central, under 500 m from LIC metro, and rated 4.1/5 across 2,371 Google reviews. Velachery Road at Phoenix Market City is the best choice in south Chennai, rated 4.2/5.

Does Burger King in Chennai serve beef?

No. Burger King India serves no beef and no pork. Whoppers come with chicken, mutton or a vegetarian patty.

What are Burger King Chennai opening hours?

Royapettah opens 10 am to 11 pm and Velachery Road 11 am to 11 pm. Most street outlets close at 11:59 pm, mall outlets at 11 pm, and Kovilambakkam is open 10 am to 1 am, the latest in the city.

Is there a Burger King at Chennai Airport?

Yes. Burger King Chennai Airport is at Aerohub West Block, Meenambakkam, Chennai 600016, phone +91 72009 59452, open until 11:59 pm.

How much does a Whopper cost in Chennai?

No official Whopper price was available when I checked on 8 October 2026, and unofficial price sites contradict each other. The confirmed deal is two Crispy Veg Burgers for ₹79, and a meal for two averages about ₹500.

Are vegetarian options cooked separately?

At its 2014 launch, Burger King India reported separate vegetarian and non-vegetarian production lines with separate staff, according to NPR and Ahram Online. I found no newer official statement, so ask at the counter if it matters to you.

Can I get Burger King delivered in Chennai?

Yes, through the BK app, and the restaurants are also listed on Swiggy and Zomato.

Does Burger King Chennai accept UPI and cards?

Yes. Royapettah and Velachery Road accept cash, debit cards, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UPI and online payment.

Burger King or McDonald’s in Chennai?

Both chains are well established in the city; McDonald’s in south India is run by the franchisee Westlife. I would pick Burger King for the flame-grilled Whopper range and for late hours, with Kovilambakkam open until 1 am.
