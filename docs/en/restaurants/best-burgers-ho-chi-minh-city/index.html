Best Burgers in Ho Chi Minh City: 10 Saigon Picks

Where to eat the best burger in Ho Chi Minh City: 10 Saigon spots ranked, from the 2025 Burger Festival winner to Wagyu smash, with addresses and VND prices.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished November 26, 2026

The best burgers in Ho Chi Minh City are at Chuck’s Burgers in Thanh My Tay, the Judges’ Choice winner of the 2025 Saigon Burger Festival, and at Bunz Smash Burgers, the Wagyu smash burger bar that opened in 2025. Behind them come Marcel Gourmet Burger for a French take, Soul Burger for a chef-driven monthly special and MAD ROOSTA for flame-grilled Angus. Expect to pay 150,000 to 260,000 VND for a good burger in Saigon in November 2026.

I built this ranking from the Saigon Burger Festival hall of fame, the local food press and each restaurant’s own menu prices, all checked on 24 November 2026. Every address uses the new ward names introduced when Vietnam abolished districts in July 2025, with the old district in brackets, because that is what you will still hear from taxi drivers.

How I ranked the best burgers in Ho Chi Minh City

I ranked each restaurant on festival results, agreement between local guides, and the depth of what is documented about its burgers. I weighted the Saigon Burger Festival hall of fame most, because it is the only judged competition in the city. I then cross-checked District 0’s Best of Saigon: Burgers (31 May 2026), Saigon Scene’s best burgers (updated 17 November 2026) and best smash burgers (24 October 2026) lists, and each restaurant’s official page where one exists.

I did not copy any Google or Tripadvisor star rating, because I could not record a dated figure for each venue. I also could not confirm November 2026 opening hours directly for Soul Burger, Relish & Sons, Avenue Saigon and The Smash Saigon. Check their pages before you cross town.

1. Chuck’s Burgers: the 2025 Saigon Burger Festival winner

The judges of the 2025 Saigon Burger Festival gave Chuck’s Burgers their Judges’ Choice award, and that is why it tops this list. It is a small Californian-style joint that grinds its own beef.

  • Address: 46 Phạm Viết Chánh, Thanh My Tay ward (formerly Binh Thanh). Some guides list No. 47.
  • Style: Californian, beef ground on site.
  • Order: the mushroom bacon ranch, the house signature.
  • Price: cheeseburger from 160,000 VND, double at 260,000 VND (District 0 and Saigon Scene, 2026).

The founder, Charlie Ta, left Vietnam with his family at the age of two and came back from the United States in 2011, Tuoi Tre News reported in December 2023. He opened around 2013 in District 1, then moved to Pham Viet Chanh street, into a room of about 40 seats. One detail I love: Chuck’s has given free hot dogs to schoolkids who bring home good grades.

Chuck’s has no official website or Instagram account that I could verify. Its older branches at 71 Pasteur and 196 Le Lai are not confirmed as still open, so head to Pham Viet Chanh.

2. Bunz Smash Burgers: the best smash burger in Saigon

For a smash burger in Saigon, go to Bunz: Australian Wagyu smashed on the plancha and served on a brioche bun baked in-house. The motto on its site is “Smash First. Ask Later.”

  • Addresses: 7 Street No. 17, Zone B, An Phu An Khanh (formerly District 2), Tuesday to Sunday 11am–10pm, tel. 0586 454 454; Rooftop, 3B Lý Tự Trọng, Ben Nghe (formerly District 1), daily 11am–10pm, tel. 0587 454 454; plus a kiosk in Thu Dau Mot (Binh Duong).
  • Style: Wagyu smash burger.
  • Order: the West Coast Smash, with caramelized onions.
  • Price: from 160,000 VND (District 0); Large Smash Burger at 200,000 VND (Saigon Scene).
  • Official site: bunzburger.com

Bunz opened in 2025. Its founder is Adrian Chong Yen, the restaurateur behind Sol and Taco del Sol, The Dot Magazine reported. The first room in An Phu seats 28. Everything is made in-house, down to the pickles and the buns. That is rare at this price in the city, and it is why Bunz ranks this high after barely a year.

3. Marcel Gourmet Burger: the French burger in Saigon

Marcel Gourmet Burger pairs Australian beef with French cheeses and sauces. Its oddest creation, Le Phở, puts the flavors of Vietnam’s national soup in a bun. Saigon Outcast’s hall of fame lists Marcel as the 2019 Saigon Burger Festival winner.

  • Addresses: 115 Calmette, Ben Thanh (formerly District 1); 19A Ngo Quang Huy, An Khanh (formerly Thao Dien); 158 Hai Ba Trung, Tan Dinh (formerly District 1). Source: marcel.vn outlets.
  • Style: French gourmet burger.
  • Order: Le Phở, at 195,000 VND.
  • Price: 105,000 to 210,000 VND before VAT.

Frenchman Alexandre Halbers founded Marcel in 2017 with Charles Postel, and Openasia Group has owned it since late 2024. I break down the full menu, every price and the story behind Le Phở in my Marcel Gourmet Burger Saigon menu and prices guide.

4. Soul Burger: a chef’s new burger every month

Soul Burger is the chef-driven pick: American chef Gabe Boyer creates a new burger every month, and the restaurant won the Saigon Burger Festival in 2020.

  • Address: 35 Vo Truong Toan, An Khanh (formerly Thao Dien, Thu Duc).
  • Style: chef’s burgers named after 1970s soul legends.
  • Order: the James Brown, or the burger of the month.
  • Price: 110,000 to 480,000 VND (Vietnam Airlines guide).

Boyer trained at the Culinary Institute of America and cooked at Tru and L2o in Chicago before founding Soul Burger in 2015, he told Vietcetera. I could not confirm from a primary source that he still runs the kitchen in 2026. At the top of its range, 480,000 VND, it is the most expensive burger on this list. The monthly special is the better reason to cross the river.

5. MAD ROOSTA: flame-grilled Angus in District 1 and Thao Dien

MAD ROOSTA cooks Australian Angus over a flame while half the city is smashing patties on a plancha. It is also one of the few spots on this list with branches both downtown and in Thao Dien.

  • Addresses: 2nd floor, 95 Hai Ba Trung (formerly District 1); 5 Street No. 10, An Khanh (formerly Thao Dien).
  • Style: flame-grilled Australian Angus.
  • Order: the Double Bomb (315,000 VND), or the Chicken Cheddar (175,000 VND) if you want chicken.

Some guides place MAD ROOSTA near Pham Viet Chanh street. That is wrong: the city-center address is 95 Hai Ba Trung. Do not confuse it with Mad Burger, the 2018 festival winner; I found no link between the two.

6. Avenue Saigon: the two-time festival champion

Avenue Saigon, a gastropub in Thao Dien, is the only place to win the Saigon Burger Festival twice, in 2022 and 2023.

  • Address: 18 Trần Ngọc Diện, An Khanh (formerly Thao Dien).
  • Style: gastropub burgers.
  • Order: the Wagyu Cheeseburger, or the Jerk Chicken Burger.
  • Price: 150,000 to 200,000 VND (District 0, 2026).

Its last title dates from 2023. Neither the 2024 nor the 2025 festival went its way.

7. Relish & Sons: a smashed Iberico burger and a vegan patty

Relish & Sons smashes Iberico pork into a burger on a potato bun and makes everything in-house “right down to the dill pickles”, according to Saigon Scene. It is also the only spot on this list with a documented vegan burger, a mushroom patty.

  • Address: 2 Dang Huu Pho, An Khanh (formerly Thao Dien), 11am–10pm. A second address, 105-107 Xuan Thuy, also circulates; I could not confirm which one is current.
  • Style: smashed Iberico on a potato bun.
  • Order: the Iberico smash burger, or the vegan mushroom patty.

8. Eddie’s New York Deli & Diner: the burger breakfast option

Eddie’s opens at 8am. That makes it the one place on this list where a burger works as a late breakfast. Its Double Smash Cheeseburger comes with fries for 179,000 VND before VAT and service.

  • Addresses: 73 Pasteur (city center); 71 Thảo Điền, An Khanh (formerly Thao Dien); 408 Phạm Thái Bường, Phu My Hung (formerly District 7).
  • Style: New York diner.
  • Order: the Double Smash Cheeseburger, or the Classic Diner Steakburger (149,000 to 199,000 VND).

One guide credits Eddie’s with a Reuben Burger. I could not confirm it: the Reuben at Eddie’s appears to be a sandwich.

9. The Wagon Wheel: Southern burgers next to Ben Thanh Market

The Wagon Wheel is the burger to eat near Ben Thanh Market: a Southern US kitchen two minutes from the market halls.

  • Address: 200 Lê Thánh Tôn, Ben Thanh (formerly District 1).
  • Style: cooking from the American South.
  • Order: the Lone Star Burger, a blend of Australian Black Angus with jalapeño cheddar, at 260,000 VND.

10. The Smash Saigon: burgers inspired by Vietnamese dishes

The Smash Saigon turns Vietnamese classics into burgers: a Chả Cá Lã Vọng Burger based on Hanoi’s turmeric fish with dill, an Ốc Burger with snails and a bò lá lốt burger. Founders Nguyễn Diệu Hương Trang and Nguyễn Minh Đức want the burger to become an everyday meal “like bánh mì”, Saigoneer reported in 2023.

  • Address: 127 Quốc Hương, An Khanh (formerly Thao Dien).
  • Style: Vietnamese fusion.
  • Order: the Chả Cá Lã Vọng Burger.

Saigon Burger Festival winners from 2018 to 2025

The Saigon Burger Festival is the city’s judged burger competition, and its winners are the most reliable shortlist of the best burgers in Ho Chi Minh City. The 2025 edition took place on 20 and 21 December 2025 at Nexus Square (formerly District 1). Saigon Outcast, founded by Linh Nguyen, organized it and combined a structured jury with a public vote, Vietcetera reported.

Smash burgers in Saigon: where to go

The best smash burgers in Saigon are at Bunz, Eddie’s and Relish & Sons. Bunz smashes Australian Wagyu on brioche, Eddie’s serves a Double Smash Cheeseburger with fries at 179,000 VND, and Relish & Sons smashes Iberico pork on a potato bun. Ducky’s Smash Burger is a good backup. Want to make one at home? Alice’s Sonic All-American Smasher copycat recipe walks you through the technique.

Best burgers in District 1 Saigon, near Ben Thanh

If you stay in old District 1, five places on this list are a short ride away. The Wagon Wheel sits at 200 Lê Thánh Tôn by Ben Thanh Market. Marcel is at 115 Calmette, MAD ROOSTA at 95 Hai Ba Trung, Eddie’s at 73 Pasteur, and the Bunz Rooftop at 3B Lý Tự Trọng.

Best burgers in Thao Dien

Thao Dien, now part of An Khanh ward, has the densest burger scene in Saigon. Seven spots from this list are there: Soul Burger, Avenue Saigon, Relish & Sons, The Smash Saigon, Marcel (19A Ngo Quang Huy), MAD ROOSTA (5 Street No. 10) and Eddie’s (71 Thảo Điền). Bunz’s first room is close by in An Phu.

Vietnamese fusion burgers: phở, chả cá and bún chả

Yes, Saigon has burgers built on Vietnamese dishes. They are the most original thing you can eat here, and the one burger you will not find at home. Marcel serves Le Phở (195,000 VND). The Smash Saigon makes a Chả Cá Lã Vọng Burger. Grilldog, on Ngô Đức Kế street, serves a bún chả burger with grilled pork, pickled green papaya, herbs and a nước mắm mayonnaise, Vietcetera reported in June 2026.

Three more burger spots worth the trip

These three missed the top 10, but each offers something the list does not: a blue-cheese burger, a late-night canal-side alley and a 25,000 VND entry price.

  • Brick & Barrel, 56 Võ Thị Sáu, Tan Dinh: moved from Calmette street; Danablue and honey-mustard burger from 250,000 VND.
  • Eat Me Saigon, 81/51 Nguyen Cuu Van (formerly Binh Thanh): an alley by the Nhieu Loc canal, 4pm to midnight, run by former Survivor contestant Cao Boi.
  • Back’s Burger, No. 81, Street No. 6, Tan Phong (formerly District 7, Phu My Hung): Korean-run, Back to the LA burger, 25,000 to 185,000 VND.

FAQ: burgers in Ho Chi Minh City

Where can I eat the best burger in Ho Chi Minh City?

Chuck’s Burgers, at 46 Phạm Viết Chánh in Thanh My Tay, won the Judges’ Choice at the 2025 Saigon Burger Festival. Bunz Smash Burgers is the best smash burger, and Marcel Gourmet Burger the best French-style burger.

Who won the Saigon Burger Festival 2025?

Chuck’s Burgers won the Judges’ Choice, and BON won the People’s Choice. The festival was held on 20 and 21 December 2025 at Nexus Square.

How much does a burger cost in Saigon?

A good burger in Saigon costs 150,000 to 260,000 VND in November 2026. Prices range from 105,000 VND before VAT at Marcel to 480,000 VND for the most expensive burger at Soul Burger. Some places add VAT and a service charge to menu prices.

Where can I find a smash burger in Saigon?

Go to Bunz Smash Burgers in An Phu or on the Ly Tu Trong rooftop, Eddie’s New York Deli & Diner, or Relish & Sons in Thao Dien.

What are the best burgers near Ben Thanh Market?

The Wagon Wheel, at 200 Lê Thánh Tôn, is closest to Ben Thanh Market. Marcel at 115 Calmette, Eddie’s at 73 Pasteur, MAD ROOSTA at 95 Hai Ba Trung and the Bunz Rooftop at 3B Lý Tự Trọng are all in old District 1.

What are the best burgers in Thao Dien?

Soul Burger, Avenue Saigon, Relish & Sons and The Smash Saigon are the top picks in Thao Dien. Marcel, MAD ROOSTA and Eddie’s also have branches there.

Are there Vietnamese-inspired burgers in Saigon?

Yes. Try Le Phở at Marcel, the Chả Cá Lã Vọng Burger at The Smash Saigon and the bún chả burger at Grilldog on Ngô Đức Kế street.

Are there vegetarian or vegan burgers in Saigon?

Yes. Relish & Sons makes a vegan mushroom patty in Thao Dien.
