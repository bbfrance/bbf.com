Hamburgerei Munich: Menu, Prices and Founders

Who started Munich's "burgers on a world tour" restaurant in 2013, what each burger costs, and where to find its 2 Munich branches.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 18, 2026

Hamburgerei Munich is an independent gourmet burger restaurant founded in 2013 by Jörn Grimmer and Karsten Grünberg. They opened the first branch on Brienner Straße in Maxvorstadt, a short walk from Königsplatz. In November 2026 it runs 2 Munich restaurants, in Maxvorstadt and Haidhausen, plus 1 in Augsburg. Every burger is named after a nationality and comes with a beef, vegetarian or vegan patty. Prices run from €12.90 to €19.90 (menu dated 9 July 2026). My position: it still belongs on any shortlist of the best burgers in Munich. The patties are grilled fresh on lava stone, the chutneys are made in-house, and vegans get the whole menu, not a token option. The Maxvorstadt branch holds 4.7/5 on Google (checked 14 November 2026).

Who founded Hamburgerei in Munich?

Hamburgerei was founded by Jörn Grimmer and Karsten Grünberg, who opened their first restaurant on Brienner Straße in 2013. The Bayerische Staatszeitung profiled them as young entrepreneurs in May 2015. Hamburgerei was started by 2 business owners. It is not a restaurant built around a celebrity chef.

The company file backs this up. Hamburgerei Einstein GmbH was registered at the Munich district court on 2 January 2013, under the name Hamburgerei Verwaltungs-GmbH. As of December 2023 it lists both men as managing directors (North Data). A second company, Hamburgerei Brienner GmbH, was founded on 3 August 2015 with the same 2 directors. It was still active in November 2026.

The Münchner Gründerpreis 2015

In 2015 Grimmer and Grünberg won the Münchner Gründerpreis, the Munich founders’ award, in the StartUp category. The prize came from the Stadtsparkasse München, with €5,000 attached. By then the business already had 2 restaurants, on Brienner Straße and Einsteinstraße. According to the Bayerische Staatszeitung, it employed 20 full-time and 20 part-time staff and used no flavour enhancers or additives.

Part of Munich’s gourmet burger wave

Hamburgerei arrived 3 years after another Munich burger name. Hans im Glück was born in the city in 2010 and grew into a national chain. Hamburgerei went the other way. It stayed small: 2 restaurants in Munich and 1 in Augsburg, with the founders still listed as directors. Of the two stories, I find that one more interesting.

The name is a play on words. Hamburgerei sounds like a German craft workshop, as in Bäckerei (bakery) or Metzgerei (butcher’s shop). It is a fitting nod to the German roots of the dish, which you can read about in our history of the hamburger.

What makes Hamburgerei’s burgers different?

3 things set Hamburgerei’s burgers apart: country-themed recipes, house-made chutneys and a lava-stone grill. Every burger comes with a beef, vegetarian or vegan patty, and with fries, sweet potato sticks or salad on the side (in-muenchen.de, 24 October 2026).

Burgers on a world tour

The menu travels: Der Argentinier, Der Perser, Der Franzose, Der Mexikaner and so on, each burger named after a nationality. The idea is as old as the restaurant. An Arts in Munich review from September 2013 already mentions a Bavarian burger with Obatzda, a Mexican one with tortilla chips and a Japanese one with bean sprouts.

Sourcing: regional beef, family-bakery buns, lava-stone grill

Hamburgerei uses regional beef in steak quality, grilled fresh (never frozen) on a lava-stone grill, according to its About us and Philosophy pages. The buns come from a Munich family bakery. Sauces and chutneys are made in-house, and the menu rotates. What the restaurant does not say is just as telling: it does not name the bakery, the farm or the butcher, and it gives no patty weight.

Hamburgerei Munich menu and prices

At Hamburgerei Brienner Straße, burgers cost €12.90 to €19.90. The classic Der Hamburger is the cheapest and the double-patty Der Amerikaner the most expensive. Which burgers to order first

Order Der Franzose first. It pairs a fig-mustard chutney with apricot and nuts under grilled goat cheese, for €15.90. My second pick is Der Perser at €13.90. Both are built around a house-made chutney, and the chutneys are what separate Hamburgerei from an ordinary cheeseburger counter. I would leave Der Amerikaner for last. It costs €19.90, and on paper 3 cheeses and 2 sauces leave little room for the beef the restaurant works so hard to source. The city’s official portal, muenchen.de, singles out the spicy Texan and the delicate French burger.

The Bavarian burger

When the Bavarian burger is on the menu, it comes in a Laugensemmel, the Bavarian pretzel roll. According to muenchen.de, it is topped with Obatzda (a spiced cheese spread), coleslaw and radish. It was not on the July 2026 menu, so ask at the counter whether it is back.

Lunch deals

Hamburgerei offers lunch discounts from 11:30 to 15:00, according to muenchen.de. The portal does not give the discount amount, so check it in the restaurant.

Vegan and vegetarian burgers at Hamburgerei

Every burger at Hamburgerei comes in a vegan or vegetarian version, so plant-based diners get the whole menu, not 1 token option. Muenchen.de also mentions low-carb options, and HappyCow lists the restaurant. In 2013 the vegan patties were falafel-based, according to Arts in Munich. None of the sources I checked describes today’s vegan patty.

Hamburgerei Munich locations and opening hours

Hamburgerei has 2 Munich restaurants, in Maxvorstadt and Haidhausen, and they keep the same hours. Both open at 11:30 every day and close at 22:00 from Sunday to Thursday, or 23:00 on Fridays, Saturdays and public holidays. A third branch, in Augsburg, has its own website at hamburgerei-augsburg.de.

Maxvorstadt: the flagship near Königsplatz

The original Hamburgerei sits at Brienner Straße 49, between Königsplatz and Stiglmaierplatz. After a morning in the museums of the Kunstareal, it is the obvious lunch stop. The 4.7/5 Google rating shown on the official site belongs to this branch, and aggregators count 2,987 reviews behind it (checked 14 November 2026). In 2013, Arts in Munich described ordering at the counter.

Haidhausen: courtyard terrace

Hamburgerei Haidhausen, at Einsteinstraße 106, has 65 seats inside and a large courtyard terrace. On a summer evening, the terrace makes it the better of the two.

What happened to Hamburgerei Schwabing?

On 27 May 2025, Hamburgerei’s Schwabing restaurant on Feilitzschstraße became a hybrid. According to the Abendzeitung München, it serves Café Buur brunch from 09:00 to 17:00, then Hamburgerei burgers until 23:00. Constanze Grimmer and Karsten Grünberg had run the site since 2018. They converted the space in 4 days.

Constanze Grimmer explained the move to Tageskarte in June 2025: “Alle Kosten sind explodiert und man braucht einfach mehr Umsätze” (“all costs have exploded and you simply need more revenue”). In November 2026 the official site no longer lists Schwabing among its restaurants. I could not confirm that the evening burger service still runs, so call ahead before you go.

Is Hamburgerei worth visiting in 2026?

Yes. 13 years in, Hamburgerei is still run by the two men who founded it, and it still does what got it noticed in 2013. The patties are grilled fresh on lava stone, the chutneys come from its own kitchen, and every burger has a vegan version. The Maxvorstadt branch holds 4.7/5 on Google (checked 14 November 2026). My 1 reservation is the price. At €12.90 to €19.90 these are not cheap burgers, and at that price I would like to know how much beef I am getting. The restaurant does not say.

The quotes on its press page are the restaurant’s own picks. They include Feinschmecker (“Fast food on the highest level”), the Münchner Merkur (“There is real craftwork involved”) and the Süddeutsche Zeitung (“Burgers – these please”). The independent signals point the same way: muenchen.de includes Hamburgerei in its guide to burgers in Munich, and in-muenchen.de profiled it in October 2026.

Hamburgerei Munich FAQ

Who founded Hamburgerei in Munich, and when?

Jörn Grimmer and Karsten Grünberg founded Hamburgerei and opened the first restaurant in 2013 on Brienner Straße in Maxvorstadt. They won the Münchner Gründerpreis in 2015.

How much does a burger cost at Hamburgerei?

Burgers cost €12.90 to €19.90 at Brienner Straße (menu dated 9 July 2026), from Der Hamburger to the double-patty Der Amerikaner. Sides of fries, sweet potato sticks or salad are available.

Does Hamburgerei have vegan and vegetarian burgers?

Yes. Every burger on the menu can be ordered with a beef, vegetarian or vegan patty.

Where does Hamburgerei’s beef and bread come from?

The restaurant says it uses regional beef in steak quality and buns from a Munich family bakery. It does not name the farm, the butcher or the bakery.

Is Hamburgerei a chain?

No. Hamburgerei is a small group, not a national chain. The official site lists 3 restaurants: Maxvorstadt and Haidhausen in Munich, and Augsburg.

Can you order Hamburgerei for delivery?

Hamburgerei appears on the delivery platforms Wolt and Lieferando. Check which branch delivers to your address in the app.

What is the best burger restaurant in Munich?

Hamburgerei is a strong pick for a gourmet burger in Munich, with a 4.7/5 Google rating at Maxvorstadt (checked 14 November 2026). For a wider comparison, see our Munich city hub and the Restaurants section.
