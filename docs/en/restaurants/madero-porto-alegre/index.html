Madero Porto Alegre: Locations, Prices and Hours

Madero Porto Alegre: all 8 locations compared, with hours, phone numbers, dated menu prices (cheeseburger from R$51) and Google ratings of 4.5 to 4.6.

By Lucas, Restaurants writer, BrazilPublished November 26, 2026

Madero Porto Alegre has 8 location pages on the chain’s official website, at 7 street addresses, as of November 13, 2026. For a sit-down dinner with a reservation, my pick is Madero Steak House Iguatemi (Av. João Wallig, 1800, Passo d’Areia), open until 10:30 p.m. Sunday to Thursday and 11:00 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays. For a quicker or later burger, go to a Madero Container or a Madero & Jeronimo: both formats stay open daily until 11:00 p.m. On the official national menus I checked the same day, the Madero cheeseburger costs R$61 with fries at the Steak House and R$51 on its own at the Container. The 4 locations I could find ratings for score 4.5 to 4.6 out of 5 on Google.

Which Madero to choose in Porto Alegre

Choose the Steak House Iguatemi for a booked sit-down meal, the Container at Boulevard Laçador for a fast burger near the airport road, and Madero & Jeronimo Assis Brasil if you want the location with the best Google rating I could verify. On price alone, I’d send most people to a Container. The R$64 combo adds a drink to the burger and fries that cost R$61 at the Steak House, and you skip the wait for table service.

Madero Steak House Iguatemi: the best Madero for a sit-down dinner

Madero Steak House Iguatemi is the most complete Madero in Porto Alegre: table service, online reservations, delivery and mall parking. Its official name is “Madero Steak House Shopping Iguatemi Passo D’Areia,” inside Shopping Iguatemi Porto Alegre at Av. João Wallig, 1800, Passo d’Areia, 91340-000.

The dining room opens Sunday to Thursday from 11:45 a.m. to 10:30 p.m., and Friday and Saturday until 11:00 p.m. Delivery runs every day from 11:45 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. The phone number is +55 51 2312-1508 (official page).

It takes Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Elo, plus the Alelo, Sodexo, Ticket and VR meal vouchers. It is also the Madero where Spinach.guide lists a vegan burger in regular and junior sizes (Spinach.guide).

Madero Steak House Praia de Belas: the Steak House near the Guaíba

Madero Steak House Praia de Belas is the second full-service Madero in the city, inside Praia de Belas Shopping at Av. Praia de Belas, 1181, stores 2008/2012, 90110-001. The phone number is +55 51 2312-1770 (official page).

It closes earlier than Iguatemi. The dining room and takeout counter open every day from 11:45 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., and delivery stops at 9:40 p.m. It offers reservations, takeout and delivery, and takes Alelo, American Express, Diners Club, Elo, Maestro, Mastercard, Sodexo, Ticket, Visa and VR.

Madero Container: Boulevard Laçador, PUC and Anita Garibaldi

Madero Container is the chain’s compact, counter-style format, with a shorter menu and lower prices than the Steak House. All 3 Porto Alegre Containers open every day from 11:30 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., with delivery until 10:30 p.m.

  • Container Boulevard Laçador (“Madero Container Boulevard Laçador São João”): Av. dos Estados, 111, São João, 90200-000. Phone +55 51 2312-1324. Dine-in and takeout (official page).
  • Container PUC (“Madero Container PUC Jardim Carvalho”): Av. Ipiranga, 6111, Jardim Carvalho, 90160-092. Phone +55 51 2312-1323 (official page). Status not confirmed, see above.
  • Container Anita Garibaldi (“Madero Container Anita Garibaldi Boa Vista”): R. Anita Garibaldi, 2301, Boa Vista, 90480-201. Phone +55 51 2312-1002 (official page).

Madero & Jeronimo: Assis Brasil, Iguatemi and Bela Vista

Madero & Jeronimo serves Madero’s classic cheeseburgers and Jeronimo’s smash burgers under one roof, with one bill for both brands. Jeronimo is the group’s fast-casual smash burger brand, launched in 2017. All 3 Porto Alegre locations open daily from 11:30 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.

  • Madero & Jeronimo Assis Brasil (“Madero & Jeronimo Burger Assis Brasil São Sebastião”): Av. Assis Brasil, 6032, São Sebastião, 91110-000. Phone +55 51 2312-5356. Delivery until 10:30 p.m. (official page). Some aggregators file it under Sarandi with a wrong postal code; use the official address.
  • Madero & Jeronimo Shopping Iguatemi: Av. João Wallig, 1800, Passo d’Areia, 91340-000, in the same mall as the Steak House. Phone +55 51 2312-2505. Delivery from 11:00 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. Self-service ordering kiosks (official page).
  • Madero & Jeronimo Bela Vista (“Madero & Jeronimo Burger Bela Vista”): R. Casemiro de Abreu, 1224, Bela Vista, 90420-000. Phone +55 51 2312-2151. Delivery from 11:00 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. (official page).

Porto Alegre was the second city Madero chose for Jeronimo, according to Jornal do Comércio in March 2018.

Madero Porto Alegre menu and prices

On Madero’s official national menus, checked on November 13, 2026, a cheeseburger costs R$61 with fries at the Steak House and R$51 on its own at the Container. The PDF files carry a “_25” suffix, which suggests a 2025 edition. They are not confirmed for Porto Alegre, and a given location or delivery app may charge differently.

Every Madero burger comes on a crusty bun baked to order, with organic lettuce and tomato, cheddar-style cheese and house mayonnaise (Steak House menu). Madero publishes separate menus for each format, in Portuguese and English, on its menu page. Download the English PDF before you go if you don’t read Portuguese.

Why some sites say the Madero cheeseburger costs R$54

R$54 is a national reference price, not a Porto Alegre menu price. A Forbes Brasil ranking built with data from ABF, the Brazilian Franchising Association, named the Cheeseburger Madero at R$54.00 the most expensive burger among Brazil’s 10 largest fast-food chains in March 2026 (Portal Tela). The method differs from a menu reading, which is why it sits between the R$51 Container price and the R$61 Steak House price with fries.

Madero happy hour in Porto Alegre

Madero runs a happy hour Monday to Friday from 5:00 to 8:00 p.m., public holidays excluded. Both official menus list it (Container, Steak House).

  • Container: draft beer and Heineken long necks at half price.
  • Steak House: draft beer, caipirinhas, cocktails and wine at half price.
  • Both: 4 mini cheeseburgers with fries for R$59, and buy one empanada, get the second free.

Vegetarian and vegan options

Madero has meat-free burgers, but what you get depends on the format. The Container menu lists the Falafel Vegetariano, a 100 g chickpea patty, at R$46 alone or R$59 as a combo. Spinach.guide reports a clearly marked vegan burger in regular and junior sizes at the Steak House Iguatemi, among 2 to 3 vegan dishes (Spinach.guide). I could not find it on the Steak House menu pages I checked, so I can’t give its name or price.

Is Madero really “the best burger in the world”?

No independent award backs it: “the best burger in the world” is Madero’s own slogan. The slogan is marketing; the ratings are not. 4 Porto Alegre locations hold 4.5/5 or more on Google across more than 14,000 reviews in total, which is hard to fake. My take: a reliable, well-made chain cheeseburger, priced like a sit-down meal. Just don’t book a Steak House when you’re in a hurry, because slow table service is the complaint that comes back most.

Madero vs the xis: which burger to eat in Porto Alegre

Eat both: Madero is a thick steak-house cheeseburger, while the xis is Porto Alegre’s own pressed sandwich. The xis is about 18 cm (7 in) wide, filled with a beef patty, cheese, corn, peas and mayonnaise, and its local signature is the xis coração with grilled chicken hearts. Our Porto Alegre burger guide covers the neighborhoods where lancherias serve it, from Cidade Baixa to Bom Fim.

Madero’s founder, Junior Durski, was openly wary of the city’s meat culture. “A gente demorou muito para vir para Porto Alegre. Tínhamos um certo receio de vir para cá com o Madero, porque é um restaurante de carnes, e isso é o que o gaúcho sabe fazer como ninguém. Mas quando a gente veio foi uma coisa sensacional,” Durski told Jornal do Comércio in March 2018. In English: “We took a long time to come to Porto Alegre. We were wary of coming here with Madero, because it’s a meat restaurant, and that’s what gaúchos do better than anyone. But when we came, it was sensational.”

A short history of Madero, the Brazilian burger chain

Madero is a Brazilian burger and steak chain founded by chef Junior Durski, who opened the first Madero Steak House in Curitiba on June 7, 2005. The Container format followed in 2014, the central kitchen in Ponta Grossa in 2015 and the Jeronimo brand in 2017. The group ran 369 restaurants on June 30, 2026 (Grupo Madero). The full story, with key dates and figures, is on our Madero brand profile.

I could not confirm with a primary source when the first Madero opened in Porto Alegre, so I don’t give a date.

Practical information: payment, reservations and delivery

Every Porto Alegre Madero I checked delivers, and the 2 Steak Houses take reservations.

  • Reservations: online at the Steak House Iguatemi. Praia de Belas also lists reservations but doesn’t say how to book, so call. No booking is listed for the Container or Madero & Jeronimo locations.
  • Delivery: from every location, ending between 9:40 p.m. (Praia de Belas) and 10:30 p.m. (Containers and Madero & Jeronimo). I could not confirm which apps handle it in Porto Alegre, iFood or Madero’s own app.
  • Payment: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Elo and the Alelo, Sodexo, Ticket and VR meal vouchers at the Steak Houses. The Container menu adds PIX, Mercado Pago, debit cards and cash, and the Banrisul cards Banricompras and Banricard, accepted only in Santa Catarina and Rio Grande do Sul.
  • Parking: the Steak House Iguatemi lists the Shopping Iguatemi car park. Parking rates, accessibility and nearby public transport are not on Madero’s pages; check the mall websites or call ahead.

FAQ: Madero Porto Alegre

How many Madero restaurants are there in Porto Alegre? Madero’s official site has 8 Porto Alegre location pages at 7 street addresses as of November 13, 2026: 2 Steak Houses, 3 Containers and 3 Madero & Jeronimo. Its sitemap lists only 5 addresses, so Container PUC, Madero & Jeronimo Iguatemi and Madero & Jeronimo Bela Vista should be confirmed by phone.

Which Madero should I choose in Porto Alegre? Book the Steak House Iguatemi for a sit-down dinner. Pick a Container or a Madero & Jeronimo for a quick burger until 11:00 p.m. Madero & Jeronimo Assis Brasil has the best Google rating I could verify, 4.6/5 from 3,029 reviews.

How much does a burger cost at Madero in Porto Alegre? On the official national menus checked on November 13, 2026, the Madero cheeseburger costs R$61 with fries at the Steak House and R$51 alone (R$64 as a combo) at the Container. Local and delivery-app prices may differ.

What are the opening hours of Madero at Shopping Iguatemi? The Steak House Iguatemi opens Sunday to Thursday from 11:45 a.m. to 10:30 p.m., and Friday and Saturday until 11:00 p.m., with delivery daily until 10:00 p.m. The Madero & Jeronimo in the same mall opens daily from 11:30 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.

Can I book a table at Madero in Porto Alegre? Yes, at the 2 Steak Houses. The Steak House Iguatemi takes online reservations, and Praia de Belas also takes bookings.

Does Madero deliver in Porto Alegre, and until what time? Yes, every location lists delivery. It stops at 9:40 p.m. at Praia de Belas, 10:00 p.m. at the Steak House Iguatemi and 10:30 p.m. at the Containers and Madero & Jeronimo.

Does Madero have vegetarian or vegan options? Yes. The Container menu has the Falafel Vegetariano, a chickpea burger at R$46. Spinach.guide reports a vegan burger in 2 sizes at the Steak House Iguatemi, but I could not confirm its name or price on the official menu.

What is the difference between Madero and Jeronimo? Madero serves a thick cheeseburger with a 180 g patty, plus steaks. Jeronimo, launched in 2017, is the group’s fast-casual smash burger brand. Madero & Jeronimo serves both, with one bill.

Is there a happy hour at Madero? Yes, Monday to Friday from 5:00 to 8:00 p.m., except public holidays. Drinks are half price, and 4 mini cheeseburgers with fries cost R$59.

Should I try a Madero burger or a xis in Porto Alegre? Try both. Madero is a premium Brazilian chain cheeseburger; the xis is the oversized pressed sandwich of Rio Grande do Sul, best ordered as a xis coração in a late-night lancheria.
