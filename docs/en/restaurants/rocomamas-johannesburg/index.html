RocoMamas Johannesburg: Best Branch, Menu and Prices

Which of the city's many RocoMamas branches to pick, what the smash burgers cost in rand, and what diners say, all checked on 14 October 2026.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished October 15, 2026

For RocoMamas in Johannesburg, go to RocoMamas Rosebank at The Zone @ Rosebank on Cradock Avenue. It is the branch I would pick for most visitors: you can walk there from the Rosebank Gautrain station, and it opens at 9am every day. It also holds 4.3/5 from about 1,860 Google reviews. A smash burger on the national menu costs R79 to R159, and the Old Skool at R79 is the cheapest. Need halal? Head to the certified branches at Nelson Mandela Square in Sandton or at Melrose Arch. I checked every address, opening time and price below on the official RocoMamas website on 14 October 2026.

RocoMamas in Johannesburg: the short answer

RocoMamas is a South African smash burger chain that was founded in Johannesburg in July 2013 and now lists 48 restaurants across Gauteng province. Branches in the greater Johannesburg area include Rosebank, Sandton (Nelson Mandela Square and Grayston Drive), Melrose Arch, Randburg, Rivonia, Auckland Park, Southgate, The Glen and Fourways. Most sit inside shopping centres. Great if you drive. If you rely on the Gautrain, only a handful make sense.

  • Best for visitors without a car: RocoMamas Rosebank, walking distance from the Gautrain station.
  • Best for halal diners: RocoMamas Nelson Mandela Square – Halaal (Sandton) or RocoMamas Melrose Arch – Halaal.
  • For families north of the city: RocoMamas Randburg, on the same road as the very first RocoMamas, and listed as child-friendly.
  • Price of a burger: R79 to R159 on the national menu, fries extra from R37.

Featured branch: RocoMamas Rosebank address and opening hours

RocoMamas Rosebank is at Shop CR01 & CR010S, The Zone @ Rosebank, Cradock Avenue, Rosebank, Johannesburg 2196, and it opens daily from 9am. I made it the featured branch for one reason: no RocoMamas in Johannesburg is easier to reach without a car. The Zone @ Rosebank (177 Oxford Road) sits between Rosebank Mall and the Rosebank Gautrain station, so you can walk over from the train (In Your Pocket).

RocoMamas Randburg: on the road where the brand began

RocoMamas Randburg sits in Appletons Village on Malibongwe Drive, the same road as the very first RocoMamas. BizNews photographed the founders “at the original restaurant in Malibongwe Drive” in 2019. I could not confirm that today’s Randburg branch occupies the same premises. Call it the brand’s home turf, not the original shop.

Milkshakes, drinks and desserts

A G-Shake milkshake costs R50 whatever the flavour: Oreo, Butter Toffee, Espresso, Bubblegum, Strawberry, Blueberry, Belgian Chocolate or Peanut Butter. Waffles run from R49 to R89. Soft drinks cost R27 to R35, a Red Bull R45 and a 500 ml water R27.

Kids’ menu: Lil’ Rock

Yes, RocoMamas has a kids’ menu called Lil’ Rock, for children under 12, and every item comes with fries. The Roadster Burger costs R72, the Cheese Burger R79, Chicken Strips R57 and 4 wings R69.

Vegetarian options at RocoMamas

RocoMamas has no vegetarian or plant-based burger on its national South African menu as of 14 October 2026. The only meat-free dishes I could confirm are the Chopped Green Salad at R40 and the Spicy Slaw at R45; fries, mac’n’cheese and waffles also look meat-free from their menu descriptions. A vegetarian will eat. They will not eat well.

What a RocoMamas burger costs: a sample order

A RocoMamas meal for one costs about R145 to R215 at national menu prices, before the tip. My go-to order on paper would be a Rockstar (R109), shoestring fries (R37) and a G-Shake (R50): R196. Swap the Rockstar for a Bacon Cheeze Guac (R129) and you reach R216. On a budget? The Old Skool (R79) with fries and a soft drink (R27) comes to R143. Add the tip at the table, as is usual in South Africa.

Is RocoMamas halal in Johannesburg?

Not every RocoMamas is halal: in Johannesburg, the branches marked “Halaal” in the official list are RocoMamas Nelson Mandela Square (Sandton), RocoMamas Melrose Arch and RocoMamas Lenasia. The official Nelson Mandela Square page states that it is halal certified. Halal branches have their own PDF menu, separate from the national menu, so prices and dishes may differ. The regular menu includes bacon and pork ribs. Never assume a branch without “Halaal” in its name is halal.

A Johannesburg brand: the history of RocoMamas

RocoMamas was founded in Johannesburg in July 2013 by Brian Altriche, and Spur Corporation, listed on the JSE, now owns the majority of it. The first restaurant opened on Malibongwe Drive in Randburg. BizNews names Brian Altriche and Paul Dempsey as the 2 founders (BizNews, 20 May 2019).

Spur did not wait long. On 21 January 2015, Spur Corporation announced it was buying 51% of the brand, which then had 4 restaurants in greater Johannesburg and 3 more planned (News24). Today the Gauteng list alone runs to 48, and Spur opened 5 more RocoMamas in South Africa in the half-year ended December 2025 (TimesLIVE).

RocoMamas timeline: July 2013, founded in Johannesburg, first restaurant on Malibongwe Drive, Randburg. 21 January 2015, Spur Corporation buys 51% (4 restaurants, 3 planned). H2 2025, 5 new South African openings. 2026, 48 branches in Gauteng. (Table: Burger Breton, sources News24 (2015), BizNews (2019), TimesLIVE (2026), rocomamas.com)

The brand sells itself as “not normal”: funky decor, rock ‘n’ roll music, open kitchens, smash burgers, Mofo wings and ribs (Simbisa Brands). The official branch pages also say each restaurant shows work by local artists. So if you have eaten at RocoMamas in Stellenbosch, you already know a brand that started here in Johannesburg.

RocoMamas Johannesburg FAQ

Where is RocoMamas in Johannesburg, and which branch is best without a car?

RocoMamas has many branches across Johannesburg, mostly in shopping centres. Without a car, choose RocoMamas Rosebank at The Zone @ Rosebank, Cradock Avenue, which you can walk to from the Rosebank Gautrain station.

What are the opening hours of RocoMamas Rosebank and RocoMamas Sandton?

RocoMamas Rosebank opens Monday to Thursday 9am – 10pm, Friday and Saturday 9am – 11pm, and Sunday 9am – 9pm. RocoMamas Nelson Mandela Square in Sandton opens every day from 10am to 10pm. No public holiday hours are published.

How much is a burger at RocoMamas?

On the national South African menu checked on 14 October 2026, smash burgers cost R79 (Old Skool) to R159 (Slacker). Limited editions started at R69. Fries cost from R37 and milkshakes R50.

Is RocoMamas halal in Johannesburg?

Only the branches marked “Halaal” are halal: in Johannesburg these are Nelson Mandela Square (Sandton), Melrose Arch and Lenasia. The rest of the chain serves bacon and pork ribs.

Does RocoMamas have vegetarian options?

There is no vegetarian or plant-based burger on the national menu. Meat-free choices are the Chopped Green Salad (R40), Spicy Slaw (R45), and, from their descriptions, fries, mac’n’cheese and waffles.

Does RocoMamas deliver in Johannesburg?

The official pages for Rosebank, Nelson Mandela Square and Randburg list online ordering. RocoMamas does not confirm delivery through Uber Eats or Mr D on those pages, so check the apps for your address.

Can you book a table at RocoMamas?

The official branch pages do not mention reservations. Call the branch directly, for example 011 268 2462 for Rosebank, to ask.

Who founded RocoMamas, and who owns it?

Brian Altriche founded RocoMamas in Johannesburg in July 2013, and BizNews also names Paul Dempsey as a founder. Spur Corporation bought 51% of the brand in 2015 and holds the majority.

What is the best burger at RocoMamas?

I have not tasted the Johannesburg menu, so I won’t crown a winner. Diners at Rosebank praise how consistent the burgers are. On price, the Old Skool (R79) is the cheapest way in, and the Slacker (R159, three 100 g patties) gives you the most beef for your rand.

Does RocoMamas have a kids’ menu?

Yes. The Lil’ Rock menu is for children under 12 and includes fries: Roadster Burger R72, Cheese Burger R79, Chicken Strips R57 and 4 wings R69. The Randburg branch is listed as child-friendly.

What is a smash burger?

A smash burger is a ball of minced beef pressed flat onto a very hot griddle, so it gets a thin patty with crisp, browned edges. At RocoMamas, a standard smash patty weighs 150 g, and the double and triple burgers use 100 g patties.
