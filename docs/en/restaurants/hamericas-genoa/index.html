Hamerica's Genoa: Address, Hours and Menu Prices

Ham Holy Burger has left Genoa. Hamerica's now trades at Via San Lorenzo 77: hours, burgers from €9.90, delivery and reviews, checked 29 Oct 2026.

By Sofia, Restaurants writer, Italy, Switzerland and the BeneluxPublished November 23, 2026

Hamerica’s Genoa is at Via San Lorenzo 77, a short walk from Genoa Cathedral (San Lorenzo). Lunch runs daily from 12:00 to 15:30. Dinner runs from 19:00 to 23:00 Monday to Thursday, and until 23:30 Friday to Sunday. Burgers with fries cost €9.90 to €16.90 on the Hamerica’s menu. If you came looking for Ham Holy Burger Genoa, it is gone: the chain’s official site no longer lists a Genoa restaurant, and Hamerica’s now runs the same address. I checked all of this on 29 October 2026.

Ham Holy Burger Genoa: what happened to Via San Lorenzo 77

Ham Holy Burger no longer operates in Genoa, and Hamerica’s, a different chain, now trades at the same address, Via San Lorenzo 77.

Ham Holy Burger opened at Via San Lorenzo 77 on Thursday, 11 December 2014, after Milan and Rome, as Scatti di Gusto reported at the time. By July 2017 the chain, then owned by Gruppo Sebeto, had 10 restaurants, Genoa among them (Ristorazione Italiana Magazine).

On 27 March 2020, Rossopomodoro founders Franco Manna and Giuseppe “Pippo” Montella took over Ham Holy Burger from the Sebeto group. It still had 10 outlets, one of them in Genoa (Scatti di Gusto). Today the official Ham Holy Burger site lists 4 restaurants. None is in Genoa.

When did the Genoa branch close, and why? I found no dated, reliable report. Nor did I find evidence that Hamerica’s bought the business: all I can say for certain is that Hamerica’s now trades at the same address. Some directories, including TheFork and Sluurpy, still show “Ham Holy Burger Genova” as open. Those listings look stale, so ignore them, and the old phone number they carry.

What Hamerica’s is: the Milan chain behind the Genoa burger

Hamerica’s is an Italian chain of American-style restaurants, founded in Milan in 2007 by Ivan and Francesca Totaro under the name 212 Hamburger.

In 2017, 212 Hamburger, 212 Rotisserie and Carolina’s merged under the Hamerica’s brand, short for “House of America”. The chain had 30 restaurants in 2022 (Wikipedia), with its head office at Via Carlo Vittadini 11 in Milan. The menu covers burgers, tacos and burritos, meats smoked for 6 hours and New York-style desserts. The chain says its meat is fresh and Italian (hamericas.com).

Is there a Hamerica’s at Genoa Waterfront?

Hamerica’s lists a second Genoa restaurant, Genova Waterfront, but I could not confirm that it is open, so check before you go.

The official store locator gives P.le John Fitzgerald Kennedy 1, c/o Centro Commerciale Waterfront, 16129 Genova, phone +39 010 407 1307, open 11:30–22:00 Sunday to Thursday and 11:30–23:00 Friday and Saturday. Yet when the Waterfront Mall at the Palasport opened on Friday, 27 March 2026, Mentelocale’s list of its 19 bars and restaurants did not include Hamerica’s.

The mall has about 70 shops and 750 parking spaces on 2 levels, and its food court includes McDonald’s and Popeyes, according to Mentelocale. Parking is free for the first 3 hours with a minimum purchase at one of the Palasport’s shops (Mentelocale). For the chain itself, see our McDonald’s brand profile.

A burger near Genoa Cathedral: is Hamerica’s worth the stop?

Hamerica’s at Via San Lorenzo 77 is the chain burger in Genoa’s historic centre: reliable if you want something familiar, but no reason on its own to come to this street.

My view: I would send cruise passengers and day visitors here when they want a known burger, a vegan option and prices fixed in advance, steps from the cathedral. I would not send anyone looking for a taste of Genoa. I found no Ligurian special on the Hamerica’s menu. The pesto burger that once tied this address to the city was a Ham Holy Burger exclusive in 2014, made with Pesto Rossi and served with Maltus Faber beers from Genoa. That local touch went with the old tenant.

FAQ: Hamerica’s Genoa and Ham Holy Burger Genoa

Does Ham Holy Burger still exist in Genoa?

No. The official Ham Holy Burger site lists 4 restaurants, in Milan, Settimo Torinese and Serravalle Designer Outlet, and none in Genoa (checked 29 October 2026).

What is at the old Ham Holy Burger address on Via San Lorenzo?

Hamerica’s Genova now trades at Via San Lorenzo 77, the address where Ham Holy Burger opened in December 2014. I found no evidence of a takeover, only the same address.

Are Hamerica’s and Ham Holy Burger the same chain?

No. Hamerica’s was founded in Milan in 2007 as 212 Hamburger. Ham Holy Burger has been owned since 2020 by Franco Manna and Pippo Montella, the founders of Rossopomodoro.

What are Hamerica’s Genoa opening hours?

Via San Lorenzo is open 12:00–15:30 every day, then 19:00–23:00 Monday to Thursday and 19:00–23:30 Friday to Sunday, according to the official store locator (checked 29 October 2026).

How much is a burger at Hamerica’s Genoa?

On the national menu, burgers with fries cost €9.90 to €16.90. A Classic is €11.50 and the signature 212 is €13.90. Prices can vary by restaurant and on delivery apps.

Does Hamerica’s Genoa have vegetarian or vegan burgers?

Yes. The menu lists a Veggie Burger at €12.60 and 3 plant-based burgers from €13.90 to €14.50, and any burger can be made plant-based for €1.50 extra.

Does Hamerica’s Genoa deliver?

Yes. The Via San Lorenzo restaurant delivers through Glovo, Just Eat and Deliveroo.

Do I need to book at Hamerica’s Genoa?

I could not confirm whether the restaurant takes reservations. Call +39 010 092 1430 before you go, especially with a group.

Does Hamerica’s Genoa have gluten-free options?

The Hamerica’s menu flags no gluten-free option. Ask the restaurant directly before ordering.

Where is the nearest Ham Holy Burger to Genoa?

Of the 4 restaurants on the official site, the closest to Genoa is at Serravalle Designer Outlet. The others are at Torino Outlet Village and on Via Palermo and Via Marghera in Milan.
