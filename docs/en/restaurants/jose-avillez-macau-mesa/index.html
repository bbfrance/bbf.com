José Avillez in Macau: Mesa, Not Lisboeta

José Avillez's only Macau restaurant is Mesa at Grand Lisboa Palace, Cotai. No burger there: his are in Lisbon, and Lisboeta's Honbo is listed closed.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished November 26, 2026

There is no José Avillez burger in Macau, and the chef has nothing to do with Lisboeta. His one restaurant in the city is Mesa by José Avillez, a Portuguese dining room on Level 3 of The Karl Lagerfeld Macau, inside Grand Lisboa Palace Resort Macau in Cotai. None of the sources I checked puts a burger on Mesa’s menu. For an Avillez burger, you need a flight to Lisbon, where Mini Bar and Cantinho do Avillez both serve one. As for Lisboeta, its only documented burger shop, Honbo, shows as closed on OpenRice as of 11 November 2026.

Why the mix-up? Grand Lisboa Palace and Lisboeta are neighbouring Cotai resorts with almost the same name, and plenty of pages pin Avillez to the wrong one. I found no source giving him any role at Lisboeta. Mesa is at Grand Lisboa Palace, run by SJM. Lisboeta belongs to a different company, Macau Theme Park and Resort (MTPR). Checked 11 November 2026.

Mesa by José Avillez: the chef’s Macau restaurant

Mesa by José Avillez is the chef’s only restaurant in Macau and, according to his official site, his first in the Far East. You’ll find it on the third floor of The Karl Lagerfeld Macau, a hotel inside SJM’s Grand Lisboa Palace Resort on Rua do Tiro, Cotai.

Why Macau? Avillez gave his reason to Macao News in September 2023: “Mesa is a special restaurant due to the historical relationship between Portugal and Macao. I hope it will increase interest in Portuguese culture and in Portugal as a destination.”

When did Mesa open?

Mesa was operating in 2023 and relaunched on 15 January 2025 after a few weeks closed for renovation. Its first opening date is murky. Pages online give 2019, 2021 or 2023. 2019 can’t be right: SJM’s own stock exchange announcement set Grand Lisboa Palace’s opening for 30 July 2021, and The Karl Lagerfeld Macau was announced for a June or July 2023 opening. Until the resort’s press office gives a date, I’m sticking to what is documented.

Who cooks at Mesa every day?

Mesa’s head chef is Herlander Fernandes, not José Avillez. SCMP’s 100 Top Tables says Fernandes joined in 2023, and Tatler Asia reported in May 2024 that he was 31 and had already spent seven years cooking in Macau. Avillez signs the concept and the menu from Lisbon. That’s normal for a chef with restaurants on three continents. Still, it’s worth knowing before you book: what reaches your table is Fernandes’s execution of Avillez’s ideas.

What is on Mesa’s menu?

Mesa serves modern Portuguese dishes, and no source lists a burger among them. SCMP and Sassy Hong Kong name these signatures:

  • Seafood rice with caviar, a take on the Portuguese arroz de marisco
  • Crispy suckling pig
  • Codfish à Brás with “explosive olives”, the spherified olives Avillez is known for
  • “One of the Three Little Pigs”, a dessert of acorn biscuits, cured-ham ice cream and bone-marrow caramel

Cod with José Avillez’s signature “explosive olives”, served at Cantinho do Avillez in Lisbon in November 2021. Illustrative photo, not taken at Mesa Macau. (Photo: Bex Walton, CC BY 2.0, https://www.flickr.com/photos/7831824@N04/51705884032)

On paper, the dessert is the one I’d book for. Cured ham in an ice cream, bone marrow in a caramel: that’s the savoury-sweet wit Belcanto built its name on, and nothing else in Macau sounds quite like it.

Is Mesa by José Avillez Michelin-starred?

I could not confirm any Michelin distinction for Mesa. Some aggregator sites call it “two-Michelin-starred”. They’re wrong. Those two stars belong to Belcanto, Avillez’s flagship in Lisbon. One search summary mentions a 2026 MICHELIN Guide Hong Kong Macau listing, and others mention Forbes Travel Guide and Trip.Gourmet awards. I have not verified any of them on the guides’ own sites, so I won’t repeat them.

Booking, hours and prices

To book Mesa, call +853 8881 1800 or message @mesa.macau on Instagram. Sources disagree on the opening hours, and SCMP’s price per head comes without a currency. I’m printing neither until the restaurant confirms them, so check with Mesa before you go.

Who is José Avillez?

José Avillez is the Portuguese chef behind Belcanto in Lisbon, which holds two Michelin stars. The milestones of his group, as reported by Wikipedia and his own Mini Bar menu:

  • September 2011: Cantinho do Avillez opens in Chiado, Lisbon. Sites at Parque das Nações, Cascais and Porto follow.
  • 2012: Belcanto opens in Lisbon and wins its first Michelin star.
  • 2014: Belcanto wins its second Michelin star.
  • Dubai: Tasca, at the Mandarin Oriental Jumeira, becomes his first international restaurant and holds one Michelin star.
  • By 2023: Mesa opens in Macau, his first restaurant in the Far East.
  • 15 January 2025: Mesa relaunches after renovation.

Where to eat a José Avillez burger: Lisbon, not Macau

José Avillez’s burgers are served in Lisbon, at Mini Bar and Cantinho do Avillez, not at Mesa. If his name brought you here looking for a burger, that’s where to go.

Mini Bar: the Barrosã PDO Burger

Mini Bar, at Rua António Maria Cardoso 58 in Chiado and part of the chef’s Bairro do Avillez cluster, makes its burger with Barrosã PDO beef from a protected native breed of northern Portugal. The Mini Bar online menu lists the Barrosã PDO Burger with fries at €29 and the Barrosã PDO Cheese Burger with fries at €31. The menu PDF is undated, so take these as the prices shown online when I checked on 11 November 2026.

€29 is a lot for a burger in Portugal. You pay for Chiado, the Avillez name and beef you can trace to its pastures. My colleague Valentina follows that burger from its mini-plate days in our José Avillez Porto and Mini Bar burger story.

Cantinho do Avillez: the Cantinho Burger

Cantinho do Avillez serves a Cantinho Burger with homemade fries, according to Smart Travelling. I could not confirm its current price, or that it is still on the menu, so ask before you order.

Lisboeta Macau restaurants: the Honbo burger episode

Lisboeta’s only documented burger restaurant was Honbo, a Hong Kong brand that OpenRice now lists as closed. Lisboeta Macau is a Cotai resort operated by Macau Theme Park and Resort (MTPR). Its first attractions opened on 17 July 2021: GoAirborne indoor skydiving, the 388-metre ZIPCITY zip line and the Night Market. The shopping mall’s official name is H853 Fun Factory.

Lisboeta Macau in Cotai, the MTPR leisure resort with no link to José Avillez, seen in August 2022. The building in the photo is its replica of the Macau Palace floating casino. HONBO’s burger shop opened in the resort’s H853 Fun Factory mall that same month. (Photo: Hugo F.H.C, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:LISBOETA\_MACAU\_1.jpg)

Honbo Lisboeta: what opened and what is left

Honbo opened at Lisboeta in August 2022, at F05, 1st Floor, H853 Fun Factory, according to The Beat Asia. Michael Chan, a former doctor, founded the brand in 2017 in Wan Chai, Hong Kong. The Beat Asia listed the Honbo burger at MOP 98, built on USDA beef and a potato milk bun, and gave the phone number +853 2870 0765.

Is Honbo at Lisboeta Macau still open?

Honbo Lisboeta is listed as closed on OpenRice as of 11 November 2026, and the closure date is unknown. The Beat Asia’s burger guide, last updated on 14 April 2025, still lists the store. A 2023 Taiwanese travel site gives a different address inside Lisboeta, so the shop may have moved before it closed. I’m not publishing any address as current. For the brand’s full story and its Hong Kong shops, read our Honbo Hong Kong smash burger guide.

FAQ: José Avillez and Mesa in Macau

Does José Avillez have a restaurant at Lisboeta Macau? No. No source shows any role for José Avillez at Lisboeta Macau. His Macau restaurant, Mesa, is at Grand Lisboa Palace, a different resort in Cotai.

Where is José Avillez’s restaurant in Macau, and what is it called? It is called Mesa by José Avillez. It is on Level 3 of The Karl Lagerfeld Macau, Grand Lisboa Palace Resort Macau, Rua do Tiro, Cotai.

Is Mesa by José Avillez Michelin-starred? No Michelin distinction for Mesa could be confirmed. The two Michelin stars often attached to it belong to Belcanto in Lisbon.

Who is the head chef at Mesa Macau? Herlander Fernandes, who joined in 2023 according to SCMP’s 100 Top Tables.

Does Mesa by José Avillez serve a burger? No source lists a burger on Mesa’s menu. It serves Portuguese dishes such as seafood rice with caviar, crispy suckling pig and codfish à Brás.

Where can I eat a José Avillez burger? In Lisbon. Mini Bar lists a Barrosã PDO Burger with fries at €29 and a cheeseburger version at €31 on its online menu, and Cantinho do Avillez serves a Cantinho Burger with homemade fries.

Is Honbo at Lisboeta Macau still open? OpenRice lists it as closed as of 11 November 2026. The closure date is unknown.

How much does dinner at Mesa Macau cost? No confirmed figure exists. SCMP gives a price per head without a currency, so call +853 8881 1800 for current prices.
