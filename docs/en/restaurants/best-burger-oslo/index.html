Best Burger in Oslo: The 10 Top Spots for 2026

Best burger in Oslo for 2026: 10 sourced picks, from World's 101 Best Burgers entries to Grünerløkka smash joints, with addresses and NOK prices.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 18, 2026

The best burger in Oslo in 2026 is at Down South Deli in St. Hanshaugen. It sits at No. 86 on the World’s 101 Best Burgers 2026, the higher of only 2 Oslo entries; Doug’s is the other, at No. 97. For a smash burger, I’d go to Render first, then Doug’s. Grünerløkka has the most burger bars. Budget 199 to 220 NOK for a signature burger at a sit-down burger bar (2025 prices), against 78 NOK for a Big Mac (July 2026).

I’ll be upfront: this guide was built at a desk, not on a tasting tour. I’m Alex, Burger Breton’s restaurants editor for Scandinavia, and I put this ranking together on 14 November 2026 from dated critics’ lists, local guides and official listings. Why another list? Because most Oslo burger guides that rank in search are crowd-sourced with no reasoning, or blog rankings frozen in 2021. Here you get 10 addresses, each with its district, its signature burger and a dated price where one exists, then a neighbourhood guide and an FAQ.

How I ranked Oslo’s burger restaurants

I ranked each restaurant on independent recognition I could date and source, starting with the 2026 global list. Places on the World’s 101 Best Burgers 2026 come first, then the dated 2025 reviews by the Swedish burger critics at Burgerdudes. From No. 4 down, the evidence thins out to Anders Husa’s Oslo food guides, Visit Oslo, Visit Norway and delivery listings. There the order is my judgement: kitchens with a clear point of view (a Josper charcoal oven, a smash-only menu, everything made by hand) rank above places I could only document by address.

I didn’t use Google or Tripadvisor scores. They can’t be recorded in a dated, verifiable way, and a third-party rating is not our verdict. Prices come from 2025 reviews and carry their date. Expect them to be higher now.

I checked every address against at least 1 listed source. For Down South Deli, Doug’s, Render and Smash by Ghost Town, I found sources dated 2025–2026. St. Lars rests on its own website. For Illegal Burger, Troys, Kverneriet, Burger Joint, Døgnvill and Flamme Burger, the only sign they are still trading is their current delivery and tourist-board listings, so check their latest posts before you cross town.

1. Down South Deli: the best burger in Oslo, according to the World’s 101 list

Down South Deli is Oslo’s highest-ranked burger restaurant, at No. 86 on the World’s 101 Best Burgers 2026. It opened on 1 February 2023 as the sister deli of Way Down South, the barbecue restaurant at Østre Elvebakke 7, which opened in 2016 and turns 10 this year (Way Down South).

Burgers are only part of the menu, next to fried chicken and pastrami-reuben sandwiches, and the curing, pickling and smoking all happen in-house (Anders Husa). The global list puts it first. The kitchen is why I’m happy to leave it there: a smash burger from a team that cures and smokes its own meat is rare in Oslo.

Address: Waldemar Thranes gate 8, 0171 Oslo (St. Hanshaugen, near Bislett). Phone: +47 48 02 10 71. Link: En Primeur Club listing. En Primeur Club says the deli takes walk-ins. I couldn’t confirm its opening hours from an official source, so check before you go.

2. Doug’s Hamburgers: Oslo’s smash burger diner

Doug’s is an Oslo-born, American-style smash burger diner, at No. 97 on the World’s 101 Best Burgers 2026. It has 3 restaurants in the city, at Mathallen, Aker Brygge and Bjørvika. Only the Mathallen address could be verified, so it’s the one I give below.

When Burgerdudes reviewed the Bjørvika branch in June 2025, the Double Royale cost 199 NOK: 2 thin patties, cheddar, fried onions, pickles, mustard and ketchup. The Oklahoma Chili Cheese cost the same, and rosemary fries were 55 NOK (Burgerdudes). The Oklahoma name nods to the fried-onion burger of the American Plains, which my colleague Daniel covers in his guide to Nic’s Grill and the Oklahoma City onion burger.

Doug’s has looked abroad, too. In December 2024, Taste of Manchester reported that it would open at Circle Square on Oxford Road in Manchester, with a “Happier Meal” of burger, fries and a martini.

Address: Mathallen, Vulkan 5, 0178 Oslo (Vulkan, next to Grünerløkka), inside Oslo’s food hall. Link: En Primeur Club listing.

3. Render Burger: the Oslohoma smash

Render makes my pick for the best onion smash burger in Oslo. Burgerdudes gave it 4.5 out of 5 in May 2025 and called it “the best burgers we have had in Oslo”. 6 Australians founded it, and it started with just 4 burgers on the menu.

The Oslohoma Smash is an Oklahoma-style onion smash with double cheddar and Render sauce on a sesame milk bun. It cost 210 NOK in May 2025, and The Baconator cost 220 NOK (Burgerdudes).

Address: Toftes gate 19B, 0556 Oslo (between Torshov and Grünerløkka). I couldn’t find an official website or Instagram account. Ignore the Operagata address some search results give: Toftes gate 19B is the only address with a source.

4. Illegal Burger: the Josper pioneer since 2010

Illegal Burger opened in 2010 with 1 stated aim: to serve the best burger in Oslo (In Your Pocket). Patties are cooked in a Josper charcoal oven and come with oven-roasted potatoes. The Gourmet adds truffle mayo, mushrooms and cheese. Anders Husa lists it among the best cheap eats in Oslo, which makes it the one to remember on a budget. Vegetarians and pescatarians can swap the beef for a vegetarian or fish patty.

Addresses: Møllergata 23, 0179 Oslo (Sentrum, near Youngstorget) and Olaf Ryes plass 4 (Grünerløkka). On 14 November 2026, its official website showed a hosting placeholder, so I link to its In Your Pocket listing instead.

5. Troys: the truffle mayo burger

Troys grills its burgers in a Josper oven, and the truffle mayo burger is its most-ordered dish (Anders Husa). It and Illegal Burger are the two charcoal kitchens in this guide: my pick if you’d rather taste smoke than a griddle smash. 1 branch is in Majorstuen and 1 in Grünerløkka, so there’s usually 1 near you.

Addresses: Hegdehaugsveien 36 (Majorstuen/Homansbyen) and Helgesens gate 16 (Grünerløkka). Link: Burgerdudes on Troys.

6. Smash by Ghost Town: a late-night smash burger in Grünerløkka

Smash by Ghost Town serves smash burgers until 23:30 on Fridays and Saturdays, the latest closing time I could confirm in this guide. The menu sticks to 4 smashes: the Ghost Town Smash Burger, the Retro Smash, the Chris P. Bacon-Blue and the Herregårds Smash.

Address: Thorvald Meyers gate 73, 0552 Oslo (Grünerløkka). Hours: Monday to Thursday 15:00–22:00, Friday and Saturday 12:00–23:30, Sunday 12:00–21:00 (Wolt listing, checked 14 November 2026).

7. Kverneriet Majorstua: handmade since 2013

Kverneriet has been “flipping burgers since 2013” and makes its patties, buns and fries by hand (Visit Norway). If you’re shopping on Bogstadveien, Majorstuen’s main street, this is the burger break I’d take.

Address: Kirkeveien 64B, Oslo (Majorstuen).

8. Burger Joint Aker Brygge: burgers from a 1960s Airstream

Burger Joint is the burger stop on the Aker Brygge waterfront. It serves from a 1960s Airstream caravan, with beef from Strøm-Larsen, an Oslo butcher (Visit Oslo).

Address: Holmens gate 3, 0250 Oslo (Aker Brygge). Phone: +47 21 08 22 75. Website: burgerjoint.no.

9. Døgnvill Burger: by the Opera and on the Tjuvholmen waterfront

Døgnvill’s Bjørvika branch is the easy choice near Oslo Central Station (Oslo S): it sits next to the Opera House, a 5-minute walk from the station (Visit Oslo). The second branch is on the Tjuvholmen waterfront (Tjuvholmen).

Addresses: Operagata 6, 0150 Oslo (Bjørvika) and Lille Stranden 10, 0252 Oslo (Tjuvholmen).

10. Flamme Burger: on Thorvald Meyers gate since 2021

Flamme Burger opened its first restaurant in Grünerløkka in early 2021, and Wolt lists a second branch in Frogner (Wolt). With Smash by Ghost Town at No. 73 on the same street, Thorvald Meyers gate gives you 2 burger options a few minutes’ walk apart.

Address: Thorvald Meyers gate 87, 0550 Oslo (Grünerløkka).

Worth knowing: St. Lars, the grill bar that topped Oslo’s old burger ranking

St. Lars is a charcoal grill bar in Bislett. In Anders Husa’s Oslo burger ranking, last updated in 2021, its burger came first with 9.6/10. The St. Lars Burger was a smashed, charcoal-grilled patty with bacon on a brioche bun, and it cost 239 NOK at the time (Anders Husa).

St. Lars opened in 2011. It has an open charcoal grill, a ham slicer and about 50 seats. The kitchen is open Monday to Saturday, 16:30–22:00, and closed on Sundays and public holidays (St. Lars). I left it out of the top 10 because I couldn’t confirm the burger is still on the menu. St. Lars is a grill bar first, not a burger specialist.

Address: Thereses gate 45, 0354 Oslo (Bislett).

Where to eat a burger in Oslo, by neighbourhood

Grünerløkka and neighbouring Vulkan have the most burger restaurants in Oslo. For visitors, the harbour front from Aker Brygge to Bjørvika is the easiest area.

Grünerløkka and Vulkan: Oslo’s burger district

Grünerløkka is home to 5 of the burger bars in this guide: Smash by Ghost Town, Flamme Burger, Troys, Illegal Burger and Render, the last at the Torshov end. Doug’s is a short walk away in Mathallen, at Vulkan. Thorvald Meyers gate alone has 2 of them.

City centre and the harbour: Aker Brygge, Tjuvholmen and Bjørvika

For a burger in Oslo city centre or by the harbour, go to Burger Joint at Aker Brygge, Døgnvill at Tjuvholmen or by the Opera, or Doug’s in Aker Brygge or Bjørvika. Away from the water, Illegal Burger on Møllergata is the central option.

St. Hanshaugen, Bislett and Majorstuen

West of the centre, Down South Deli and St. Lars are a few streets apart around Bislett. Kverneriet and Troys cover Majorstuen and Homansbyen.

How much does a burger cost in Oslo?

A signature burger at an Oslo burger bar cost 199 to 220 NOK in 2025 reviews (roughly $20 to $23). A Big Mac costs 78 NOK: that’s $8.05, or 29.5% more than in the United States, according to The Economist’s July 2026 Big Mac index (our Big Mac index 2026 report). The dollar figures use the exchange rate implied by that index.

How much a burger costs in Oslo, in NOK: Big Mac 78 (July 2026), Doug’s Double Royale 199 (June 2025), Render Oslohoma Smash 210 (May 2025), Render Baconator 220 (May 2025), St. Lars Burger 239 (2021); rosemary fries at Doug’s add 55. That’s roughly $20 to $23 for a signature burger. (Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0, data from Burgerdudes (2025), Anders Husa (2021) and The Economist Big Mac index (July 2026))

Sides add up: rosemary fries were 55 NOK at Doug’s in June 2025. On a budget, Anders Husa lists Illegal Burger among Oslo’s best cheap eats, though I found no current price to compare. Mind the clock, too. Norwegians eat middag, their main hot meal, early, around 5 pm, and St. Lars opens its kitchen at 16:30.

FAQ: the best burger in Oslo

Where can I eat the best burger in Oslo?

Down South Deli at Waldemar Thranes gate 8 is the best burger in Oslo by the most recent dated ranking: No. 86 on the World’s 101 Best Burgers 2026. Doug’s (No. 97) and Render (4.5/5 from Burgerdudes) follow.

What is the best smash burger in Oslo?

Render’s Oslohoma Smash, an Oklahoma-style onion smash at 210 NOK in May 2025, is the best-reviewed smash burger in Oslo. Doug’s, whose Double Royale cost 199 NOK in June 2025, is the smash diner on the World’s 101 Best Burgers 2026.

How much does a burger cost at a restaurant in Oslo?

Expect 199 to 220 NOK for a signature burger at a burger bar, based on 2025 prices, plus about 55 NOK for fries. A Big Mac costs 78 NOK (July 2026).

Which Oslo burgers are on the World’s 101 Best Burgers 2026 list?

2: Down South Deli at No. 86 and Doug’s at No. 97.

Which Oslo neighbourhood has the most burger restaurants?

Grünerløkka, with 5 of the burger bars in this guide, plus Doug’s at Mathallen in neighbouring Vulkan.

Where can I eat a burger near Oslo Central Station or the Opera House?

Døgnvill Burger at Operagata 6, Bjørvika, is next to the Opera House and a 5-minute walk from Oslo S. Doug’s also has a branch in Bjørvika.

Are there vegetarian or gluten-free burgers in Oslo?

Yes for vegetarians: Illegal Burger offers vegetarian and fish patties. I couldn’t confirm a gluten-free bun at any of these 10 restaurants, so ask when you order.

Do I need to book a table for a burger in Oslo?

Not at Down South Deli, which takes walk-ins according to En Primeur Club. I couldn’t confirm booking policies for the others, so check their pages before peak evening hours.
