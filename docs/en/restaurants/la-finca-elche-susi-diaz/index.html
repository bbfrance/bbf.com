La Finca Elche: Susi Díaz, Michelin Star, Hours

Who Susi Díaz is, how her Elche restaurant earned a Michelin star and, in 2025, 3 Repsol Soles, when to book a table, and why you won't find a burger there.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished November 26, 2026

La Finca is the only Michelin-starred restaurant in Elche (Elx), in Spain’s Alicante province, and it does not serve burgers. Chef Susi Díaz opened it with her husband, José María García, in 1984, in a century-old farmhouse in the Perleta district. The Michelin Guide files it under contemporary cuisine at its top €€€€ price level. In 2025 the Guía Repsol raised it to 3 Soles, the guide’s highest rating. If you came to Alicante for a burger, our Alicante restaurants page is where to start.

La Finca Elche at a glance: Michelin star, address and hours

La Finca is a contemporary fine-dining restaurant at Partida (Camino) de Perleta 1-7 in Elche, with one Michelin star held since 2006 and 3 Repsol Soles since 2025. I checked every detail below against the official site (lafinca.es) and the Michelin Guide on 11 November 2026.

Is La Finca in Alicante or in Elche?

La Finca is in Elche, not in Alicante city. Elche is a separate city in the province of Alicante, and the restaurant’s postcode, 03295, belongs to Elche. People search for it under “Alicante” because the province, the airport and the coast all carry that name.

So plan the evening around the drive. Take a taxi or a car rather than counting on a walk from an Alicante hotel, and check the route and travel time before you book.

Who is Susi Díaz? Chef biography

Susi Díaz is a self-taught Spanish chef, born in Elche on 2 April 1956, who learned to cook from her two grandmothers and has run La Finca since 1984. Her biography on Reporter Gourmet and her interviews in El Español Alicante tell the same story: no culinary school, family recipes first, technique later.

Chef Susi Díaz of La Finca in Elche, in chef’s whites and a Canal Cocina apron, photographed in 2010 for the Spanish TV cooking channel (Photo: Gastronomus, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SusiDiazCanalCocina.jpg)

Television made her famous. She sat on the judging panel of Top Chef España on TVE for five seasons, from 2013 to 2017, according to Reporter Gourmet and Directo al Paladar. Reporter Gourmet also records a recipe book, Sentidos, published in 2011.

La Voz de Lanzarote has reported her as president of Saborea España, the Spanish gastronomic tourism association. I found no source confirming that she still holds the post in 2026.

Her cooking philosophy

Susi Díaz cooks from memory: she takes traditional Alicante recipes and rebuilds them with modern technique. “Hacer un plato con el recuerdo es más importante que pensar en el futuro,” she told Alicante Plaza in March 2025, meaning “making a dish from memory matters more than thinking about the future.” That one sentence explains La Finca better than its list of awards.

Her own example is her grandmother’s mackerel escabeche, reworked with contemporary technique. The Michelin Guide describes the same approach: fish and seafood from Santa Pola, organic produce, and citrus, herbs and edible flowers from the restaurant’s own kitchen garden.

Michelin star and Repsol Soles: La Finca’s awards

La Finca holds one Michelin star, first awarded in 2006, and earned 3 Repsol Soles, the guide’s top rating, in the Guía Repsol 2025. Plenty of pages online still show the old 2 Soles, so here is where each rating stands.

One Michelin star since 2006

The Michelin Guide has given La Finca one star every year since 2006, according to El Español Alicante. When I checked the guide’s website on 11 November 2026, the star was still there. I found no dated press coverage of the 2026 Spain and Portugal guide, so the website is my source.

3 Repsol Soles in 2025

La Finca moved from 2 to 3 Repsol Soles at the Guía Repsol 2025 gala in Tenerife, in March 2025. Only one other restaurant in Spain reached 3 Soles that year, Bagá in Jaén, according to Alicante Plaza and Directo al Paladar. Whether La Finca kept the third Sol in the 2026 guide is still unconfirmed.

Sustainability and career awards

In 2023 the Guía Repsol also gave La Finca its Sol Sostenible, a sustainability award. Díaz has collected honors of her own: the Premio Nacional de Hostelería in 2019 for 35 years at La Finca, and in 2020 a public garden in Elche named after her.

What La Finca serves: menus and signature dishes

La Finca serves contemporary Alicante cuisine in three formats: à la carte, custom menus, and the Uttopía tasting menu in L and XL versions (Michelin Guide). Seafood from Santa Pola and vegetables from the kitchen garden drive the cooking.

Signature dishes reported by the press

Directo al Paladar has named these dishes as signatures of the kitchen. Read the list as what La Finca is known for, not as tonight’s menu:

  • Asparagus ice cream, reported as on the menu for about 20 years
  • Tear-drop peas with sea cucumber
  • Caviar with Iberian pork jowl
  • Squid stew with chickpeas and squash
  • Creamy rice with mullet

Prices: what I could and could not confirm

You won’t find a tasting-menu price here. The Uttopía L and XL figures that circulate online are old, and I would rather print nothing than a stale number. Ask for the current price when you book.

The official site does list group and event menus at €99, €109 and €119 per person, each with a cocktail, a starter, a main course and drinks. La Finca also hosts events with a dance floor and a DJ.

Opening hours and booking

According to the official site, La Finca opens for lunch Wednesday to Sunday, 13:30 to 15:30, and for dinner on Friday and Saturday, 20:30 to 22:30, from September to mid-July. From mid-July to August it serves lunch and dinner Tuesday to Saturday.

Other listings disagree: the Michelin Guide and older press articles each show a different schedule. I am publishing only the official hours, checked on 11 November 2026. Confirm by phone (+34 965 45 60 07) or email (info@lafinca.es). Outside summer, my advice is to book lunch. It runs five days a week against two for dinner, and you see the garden in daylight.

Does La Finca serve burgers?

No. None of La Finca’s official menus has a burger, and Susi Díaz has made it clear she wants nothing to do with fast food. Asked by El Español in 2022 what she would eat in a hurry, she said she would pick “un bocata bien hecho, de calidad”, a well-made quality sandwich, “y nunca me iría a la cocina preparada o fast food, jamás”: never ready meals or fast food.

I like that answer. It is the instinct behind her cooking: good bread, good product, nothing industrial. It also means a burger lover should not drive to Elche expecting one. We have profiled other starred chefs who keep burgers off the menu, such as Mario Lohninger in Frankfurt and Maximilian Lorenz in Cologne. The same rule applies here: come for the cooking, not for a patty.

Where to eat a burger in Alicante instead

For burgers, stay in Alicante city: it has its own smash-burger scene and a yearly burger festival. Our Alicante restaurants page gathers our coverage of the city.

The local smash-burger story starts with Big Twins, presented by Alicante Plaza as Spain’s smash-burger pioneers. Three brothers, Agustín, Fernando and Nacho, founded it in Alicante in June 2020 with about €10,000. By October 2023 it had six locations: three of its own in Alicante (Benalúa, Calle Tucumán and Playa de San Juan) and franchises in Elche, Murcia and Córdoba. Those counts are from 2023, so check the brand before you go.

The Champions Burger, the touring burger contest, set up at Playa de San Juan (Avenida Costa Blanca 164) from 10 to 21 April 2025, its third year in a row in Alicante. Heading north? Our ranking of the best burgers in Valencia covers the regional capital.

Is La Finca worth the trip from Alicante?

Yes, if fine dining is the point of the evening. La Finca is Elche’s only Michelin-starred restaurant, and in 2025 it was one of just two restaurants in Spain promoted to 3 Repsol Soles. A star kept for two decades and a fresh top Repsol rating tell me the kitchen is still moving, not coasting on its name.

It is not worth the trip for a quick or cheap meal, and it is the wrong address for burgers. Book ahead, ask for the tasting-menu price, and sort out transport to Elche before you sit down. For more chefs in the same league, browse our restaurants section or our profile of AnnaLena in Vancouver.

FAQ: Susi Díaz and La Finca

Who is Susi Díaz and which restaurant does she run?

Susi Díaz is a self-taught chef born in Elche in 1956. She co-founded La Finca in Elche in 1984 with her husband José María García and is still its chef and owner.

Where is La Finca: in Alicante or in Elche?

La Finca is in Elche (Elx), at Partida (Camino) de Perleta 1-7, 03295. Elche is a separate city in the province of Alicante, not part of Alicante city.

Does La Finca have a Michelin star, and since when?

Yes. La Finca has held one Michelin star since 2006, and the Michelin Guide still showed it in November 2026. It is Elche’s only Michelin-starred restaurant.

How many Repsol Soles does La Finca have?

La Finca was rated 3 Repsol Soles, the top rating, in the Guía Repsol 2025, up from 2. Its 2026 rating was not verified when this article was published.

How much does the tasting menu at La Finca cost?

I could not confirm a current price for the Uttopía L and XL tasting menus, so ask when you book. Group and event menus on the official site cost €99, €109 and €119 per person.

What days is La Finca open, and do you need to book?

From September to mid-July, the official site lists lunch Wednesday to Sunday (13:30–15:30) and dinner Friday and Saturday (20:30–22:30). In summer it opens for lunch and dinner Tuesday to Saturday. Listings disagree, so book and confirm by phone on +34 965 45 60 07.

How do you get to La Finca from Alicante?

Take a taxi or a car to Elche, a separate city in the same province. Check your route and travel time before booking, as the restaurant is not in Alicante city.

Was Susi Díaz a judge on Top Chef España?

Yes. She was a judge on Top Chef España on TVE for five seasons, from 2013 to 2017.

What kind of food does La Finca serve? Does it serve burgers?

La Finca serves contemporary cuisine built on traditional Alicante recipes, with Santa Pola seafood and produce from its own garden. It serves no burgers.

What are Susi Díaz’s signature dishes?

The press has reported asparagus ice cream, tear-drop peas with sea cucumber, caviar with Iberian pork jowl, squid stew with chickpeas and squash, and creamy rice with mullet. The current menu may differ.
