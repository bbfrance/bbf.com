RocoMamas Stellenbosch: Address, Hours, Menu Prices and Reviews

RocoMamas Stellenbosch at Eikestad Mall: official hours, smash burgers from R79 to R159 on the national menu (Oct 2026) and what Google reviewers say.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished October 10, 2026

RocoMamas Stellenbosch is the town’s only branch of the South African smash burger chain, at Shop 41 & 42, Eikestad Mall, Andringa Street, in Stellenbosch Central. It opens at 9 a.m. every day and closes at 10 p.m. Sunday to Thursday, 11 p.m. on Friday and Saturday. On the chain’s national menu, which I checked on October 9, 2026, smash burgers run from R79 for the Old Skool Burger to R159 for the triple-patty Slacker. Aggregators put its Google rating at about 4.3/5 from roughly 1,380 reviews. Diners like the burgers and the staff. Their main complaint is slow service at peak times, so my advice is simple: go before or after the dinner rush.

RocoMamas Stellenbosch address: where to find it in Eikestad Mall

RocoMamas Stellenbosch is in Shop 41 & 42 of Eikestad Mall, on Andringa Street, in the heart of Stellenbosch Central.

Map of RocoMamas Stellenbosch at Eikestad Mall, Andringa Street, in Stellenbosch Central, about 50 km (31 miles) from Cape Town. (Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL)

Eikestad Mall’s street address is 43 Andringa Street. According to LekkeSlaap, it has more than 100 shops, including Woolworths, Checkers and Game, plus covered parking and 24-hour security. The mall’s own line is +27 21 886 6267.

The name comes from the town itself. Stellenbosch, founded in 1679, is known as Eikestad, the “city of oaks”. It is also where pinotage was born in 1925, and it sits about 50 km (31 miles) from Cape Town.

Drinks and kids’ menu

Milkshakes, called G-Shakes, cost R50. A 300 ml soda is R27 to R35, juices R33 to R35, a Red Bull R45 and water R27. The “Kid Rock” menu for under-12s comes with fries and costs R57 to R79.

Vegetarian and halal options

The only vegetarian dishes I could confirm on the 2026 South African menu are the Chopped Green Salad (R40) and the Spicy Slaw (R45).

That is thin. The old Eat Out listing mentioned a veggie patty, but I could not find its current name or price on the national menu. RocoMamas does run a halaal menu at brand level, yet nothing I found confirms that the Stellenbosch branch is halaal certified, so ask before you order.

What reviewers praise

Four things come up again and again: friendly, responsive staff, the smash burgers, the spicy Mofo wings and the ribs. Many reviewers also mention good value, helped by promotions such as Triple Treat.

What reviewers criticise

The complaints are just as consistent. Some dishes come out too salty, service drags at peak times, and orders or bills are sometimes wrong. A few reviewers also mention poor ventilation.

My read: the food earns the 4.3, not the service. Order the smash burgers and the wings, skip the dinner rush if you are short on time, and check your bill before you pay.

Services: delivery, takeout, reservations and payment

Aggregators list delivery, takeout, card payments, outdoor seating, Wi-Fi, reservations, wheelchair access and beer at RocoMamas Stellenbosch, but no official source confirms them.

Restaurant Guru and the old Eat Out listing report these services, and Eat Out named Amex, Mastercard and Visa. The official page mentions online ordering without details for this branch. I could not confirm that the restaurant is on Uber Eats or Mr D today.

Stellenbosch does have RocoMamas delivery history. In December 2017, an Uber Eats campaign had rapper Jack Parow deliver RocoMamas orders in town for two hours, according to Ads of the World. That proves the branch was open in 2017, nothing more. To book a table or check delivery, call 021 883 2272.

RocoMamas in South Africa: the chain that brought the smash burger home

RocoMamas is a South African burger chain founded in July 2013 in Johannesburg by Brian Altriche, and it built its name on the smash burger.

Altriche had run a Spur franchise for 12 years and was the first Ocean Basket franchisee in 1996. He lived in Los Angeles in the late 1980s and early 1990s and brought the smash burger idea back from the US, according to News24 and BusinessLIVE.

On January 21, 2015, JSE-listed Spur Corporation announced it was buying 51% of RocoMamas, which then had four restaurants in greater Johannesburg and three in the pipeline, as News24 reported. Today the brand says it has more than 100 restaurants. Spur opened five more in South Africa in the half-year to December 2025, according to TimesLIVE.

The brand calls itself “not normal”. Simbisa Brands describes funky decor, a rock’n’roll mood, an open kitchen, customisable burgers, smashed patties, Mofo wings and ribs.

Is RocoMamas Stellenbosch worth it?

Yes, if you want a quick, filling smash burger in central Stellenbosch for under R130 and don’t mind mall-chain atmosphere.

It is open from 9 a.m. until late, the mall has covered parking, and an Old Skool Burger at R79 is an easy budget meal between wine farms. If you are after an independent burger joint with a story, this is not it. I’ll add more verified addresses to our Stellenbosch burger guide as I check them.

RocoMamas Stellenbosch FAQ

Where is RocoMamas in Stellenbosch?

RocoMamas Stellenbosch is at Shop 41 & 42, Eikestad Mall, Andringa Street, Stellenbosch Central, Western Cape, 7600. It is the only RocoMamas listed in Stellenbosch on the official site.

What are RocoMamas Stellenbosch opening hours?

It opens at 9 a.m. every day. It closes at 10 p.m. Sunday to Thursday and at 11 p.m. on Friday and Saturday, per the official page checked on October 9, 2026.

How much is a burger at RocoMamas in South Africa?

On the national menu checked on October 9, 2026, burgers cost R79 (Old Skool) to R159 (Slacker). Prices at a given branch may differ.

What is a RocoMamas smash burger?

It is a beef patty pressed flat on the grill so the edges crisp. Most RocoMamas burgers use one 150 g smashed patty; the Slacker uses three 100 g patties.

Does RocoMamas Stellenbosch deliver?

Aggregators say it offers delivery and takeout, and the official page mentions online ordering. I could not confirm a current Uber Eats or Mr D listing, so check the apps or call 021 883 2272.

Are there vegetarian or halal options?

The confirmed vegetarian dishes are the Chopped Green Salad (R40) and the Spicy Slaw (R45). RocoMamas has a halaal menu at brand level, but halaal certification of the Stellenbosch branch is not confirmed.

Can you book a table at RocoMamas Stellenbosch?

Restaurant Guru lists reservations, but no official source confirms it. Call 021 883 2272 to book.

Does RocoMamas Stellenbosch serve breakfast?

It opens at 9 a.m., but the official menu shows no breakfast offer, so don’t count on one.

Who owns RocoMamas, and is it South African?

Yes, it is South African. Brian Altriche founded it in Johannesburg in July 2013, and Spur Corporation bought 51% in 2015.

What is the most popular burger at RocoMamas?

RocoMamas does not publish a best-seller. Reviewers praise the smash burgers in general, and the Old Skool Burger (R79) is the simplest way in.
