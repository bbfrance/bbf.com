Five Guys Salt Lake City: Address, Hours, Menu

The only Five Guys in Salt Lake City is at 3167 E. 3300 S., in Millcreek's Canyon Rim Center. Hours, bus route, delivery prices and reviews.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 17, 2026

The only Five Guys in Salt Lake City is at 3167 E. 3300 S., Salt Lake City, UT 84109, phone (801) 484-2480. It sits in the Canyon Rim Center, which is inside the city of Millcreek even though the mailing address says Salt Lake City. There is no Five Guys downtown. You can eat in, take out, use curbside pickup or get delivery, and UTA bus 33 runs along 3300 South right past it. The hours are the weak spot: sources disagree, so check the official store page before you go. I checked the address, phone and services on Five Guys’ own pages on November 13, 2026.

Five Guys Salt Lake City address: Canyon Rim, not downtown

Five Guys Salt Lake City is at 3167 E. 3300 S., in the Canyon Rim Center, on the east side of the valley. The official Salt Lake City store list shows only this one restaurant. Staying downtown? Plan on a drive or a bus ride.

The shopping center is in Millcreek, which became an incorporated city on December 28, 2016. Canyon Rim is one of Millcreek’s 4 community councils, along with East Mill Creek, Millcreek and Mount Olympus (City of Millcreek, Wikipedia). The Canyon Rim Shopping Center, at 3215 East 3300 South, is listed in Millcreek, UT 84109 (CommercialSearch).

So “Five Guys 3300 South”, “Five Guys Canyon Rim” and “Five Guys Millcreek” are all the same restaurant. The online ordering site adds a fourth name, Olympus, which is the one you’ll pick when you order ahead.

According to storeopeninghours.com, its neighbors in the center are Jersey Mike’s Subs, Savers and PetSmart, about 1 minute by car from exit 3 of I-215. That’s a secondary source, so check it on your map app.

How to get to Five Guys Canyon Rim by bus or car

Without a car, take UTA bus 33, which runs along 3300 South past Five Guys Canyon Rim. The line links Olympus Cove P+R to Millcreek TRAX station, about 6.4 miles end to end (Transit app). Storeopeninghours.com lists a stop called 3300 South @ 3174 East in front of the center.

From downtown, ride TRAX south to Millcreek station, then bus 33 east along 3300 South. Check the current UTA schedule before you go.

By car, it’s about a minute from exit 3 of I-215. A shopping center this size usually has a lot out front. Still, no official source confirms parking, wheelchair access or accepted payment methods, so call ahead if any of these matter to you.

Ordering online, curbside and delivery

Five Guys Salt Lake City takes orders at the counter, online for pickup or curbside, and for delivery. The official page lists delivery both direct and through DoorDash and Uber.

1 catch. When I checked Postmates on November 13, 2026, it showed “Delivery currently unavailable”, even though the official page lists delivery. Look at the app before you count on it.

Online orders go through order.fiveguys.com, location “olympus”. That page blocked my checks, so I couldn’t read the menu or prices there.

Five Guys Salt Lake City menu: what to order

The menu at Five Guys Salt Lake City is the standard Five Guys lineup: burgers, Little burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches, fries and milkshakes. Nothing on it is exclusive to Utah.

The store page says the beef is fresh, with no freezers in its restaurants, and claims more than 250,000 ways to customize a burger and more than 1,000 milkshake combinations.

  • Burgers: Hamburger, Cheeseburger, Bacon Burger and Bacon Cheeseburger, all with 2 patties. My colleague Julia rebuilt the classic at home in her Five Guys cheeseburger copycat recipe.
  • Little burgers: the same builds with 1 patty. Julia’s Five Guys Little Bacon Cheeseburger copycat shows how they’re stacked.
  • Hot dogs: Hot Dog and Cheese Dog.
  • Sandwiches: Grilled Cheese and BLT. The Grilled Cheese is the obvious meat-free pick.
  • Fries: Little, Regular and Large.
  • Milkshakes: 1 size, with mix-ins.

What I’d order: a Little Cheeseburger with the toppings you actually want, and 1 order of fries to share. 2 patties is more burger than most people need. As for the fries, 1 TripAdvisor reviewer writes that “a small order will feed 4”. Believe them.

I didn’t go through Five Guys’ allergen and nutrition pages for this guide, so I’m not making claims about peanut oil, gluten or vegetarian options. Check the official allergen information on fiveguys.com, and tell the staff about any allergy.

Does Five Guys serve fry sauce in Utah?

Fry sauce at Five Guys Salt Lake City is not confirmed, and 1 TripAdvisor review describes a dispute with staff over it. Fry sauce, ketchup mixed with mayonnaise, is Utah’s signature condiment. Five Guys runs a national menu, and I couldn’t read this location’s own menu online.

A cup of fry sauce, Utah’s ketchup-and-mayo dip, from a Salt Lake City burger chain in 2008. It’s not on Five Guys’ standard menu. Illustrative photo, not taken at Five Guys. (Photo: Authalic, CC BY 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Frysauce.JPG)

Ask at the counter, or mix your own from the ketchup and mayo on the toppings list. Honestly, if fry sauce is the point of your trip, you’re at the wrong counter: Utah’s burger stands do it better.

Is Five Guys worth it over Salt Lake City’s local burgers?

Five Guys Salt Lake City is a reliable fast-casual stop if you’re already on the east side. It isn’t a Salt Lake institution, and I wouldn’t cross the valley for it. The local scene belongs to places like Crown Burgers, a Salt Lake City chain founded in 1978 that popularized the pastrami burger.

If you came to eat burgers, start with our Salt Lake City burger guide. For a homegrown pick with a story, read my review of Lucky 13, Salt Lake City’s firefighters’ burger bar. Keep Five Guys for the nights you want a burger built exactly your way, fast, near I-215.

How Five Guys came to Utah

Five Guys reached the Salt Lake area in 2008. By December 2008 it had 2 restaurants there, on Fort Union Boulevard and South Sandy Parkway, and franchisee Burgermeister, LLC had signed a deal to open 50 restaurants in Utah, Idaho and Oklahoma over 7 years (KSL, December 1, 2008). Gastronomic SLC reported a Five Guys opening near South Towne in Sandy in July 2008, one of the first in the valley (Gastronomic SLC).

When the Canyon Rim restaurant opened, I couldn’t find out, so there’s no date here.

A short history of Five Guys

Five Guys is a family-owned burger chain founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell and their sons. It’s still family-run and based in Alexandria, Virginia. In May 2026 it had 1,999 restaurants in 28 countries and territories, 1,517 of them in the United States (Wikipedia).

Five Guys Salt Lake City FAQ

Where is Five Guys in Salt Lake City?

At 3167 E. 3300 S., Salt Lake City, UT 84109, in the Canyon Rim Center. The shopping center is in the city of Millcreek, though the mailing address says Salt Lake City.

Is there a Five Guys in downtown Salt Lake City?

No. The official store list shows only 1 Salt Lake City location, on 3300 South in Canyon Rim, on the east side of the valley.

What are the Five Guys Salt Lake City hours?

Sources disagree: 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. on TripAdvisor, 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. on Birdeye and storeopeninghours.com, and 10 a.m. to 9:30 p.m. for delivery on Postmates. Call (801) 484-2480 or check the official store page before an early or late visit.

How much does a burger cost at Five Guys Salt Lake City?
