Maison des Cariatides Dijon: Chefs, Menus and Angelo Ferrigno

The Michelin-listed table on rue Chaudronnerie: chefs from Thomas Collomb to the Gomis couple, Angelo Ferrigno's star years, and a burger tip.

By Thomas, Restaurants writer, FrancePublished October 12, 2026

La Maison des Cariatides in Dijon is a fine-dining restaurant at 28 rue Chaudronnerie, run today by owner-chefs Marie-Cécile and Vincent Gomis. Angelo Ferrigno is not its current chef, owner or founder. He cooked there from 2012 to 2018, became head chef in 2015 and has run his own Michelin-starred restaurant, CIBO, at 24 rue Jeannin since 2020. The restaurant was founded by Thomas Collomb. It serves modern tasting menus from €59 at lunch to €139 at dinner, and it has no burger on the menu.

I wrote this because the same mistake keeps turning up. Search for the restaurant and many pages still credit Angelo Ferrigno, who left in 2018, and none of them puts the chefs in order. So I cross-checked the Guide Michelin and Gault&Millau listings, the restaurant’s official site, Le Fooding, Gilles Pudlowski’s blog and Dijon’s local press, all on 10 October 2026. This is a researched profile, not a meal review. Where the sources disagree, I say so rather than pick a side.

Who runs La Maison des Cariatides today?

Marie-Cécile and Vincent Gomis are the current owner-chefs of La Maison des Cariatides, according to the Guide Michelin and the restaurant’s own website. They took over from chef Sylvain Gauthey. Gault&Millau’s 2026 review says the change happened “two years ago”, which places it around 2024.

Who does what in the kitchen is less clear. Gault&Millau names Marie-Cécile Gomis as chef; Michelin and the official site present the couple as leading the restaurant together. I couldn’t confirm the split, so I list them as joint owner-chefs.

Sylvain Gauthey hasn’t gone far. He now runs La Table d’Eole in Brochon, a village on the Côte de Nuits wine road south of Dijon.

What the food is like: modern Burgundian cooking with Mediterranean notes

The cooking at La Maison des Cariatides is refined contemporary French cuisine built on Burgundian roots with Mediterranean influences. The restaurant’s own phrase is “modern and indulgent gastronomic cuisine, between roots and influences”. The Guide Michelin points to a recurring use of sweet spices and citrus, and singles out the seafood dishes.

Gault&Millau cites 3 dishes that show the style:

  • Milk-fed veal tartare with a lemon-kombu sorbet and a tuna emulsion
  • Confit lamb shoulder with artichoke barigoule
  • Red-fruit panna cotta with verbena

In good weather there is a terrace at the back of the building, away from the street.

Maison des Cariatides menu and prices

A meal at La Maison des Cariatides costs €59 to €79 per person at lunch and €119 to €139 at dinner, drinks excluded. Those are Gault&Millau’s 2026 figures. There is no à la carte: you pick a tasting menu, and the number of courses sets the price.

If I were booking, I’d take the 5-course lunch at €59. Same kitchen, same style, €60 less than the shortest dinner menu.

Opening hours and booking

La Maison des Cariatides is open Wednesday to Sunday and closed Monday and Tuesday. Lunch runs from noon to 1:30 pm according to the Guide Michelin. The sources disagree on the rest:

Angelo Ferrigno: from Cariatides head chef to CIBO

Angelo Ferrigno is a Burgundian chef, born in 1992, who won a Michelin star as head chef of La Maison des Cariatides in 2016 and has run his own starred restaurant, CIBO, in Dijon since 2020. His is the best-known name tied to the address, which is exactly why so many pages still put him there.

Training in Burgundy

Ferrigno was born in Semur-en-Auxois to a Portuguese mother and an Italian father. He started training at 14 at CFA La Noue in Dijon, then worked at L’Hostellerie d’Aussois in Semur-en-Auxois and L’Hostellerie de Levernois near Beaune (Gault&Millau).

The 2016 Michelin star at La Maison des Cariatides

In 2016, a year after becoming head chef, Ferrigno earned La Maison des Cariatides a Michelin star. Le Fooding described him as the youngest starred chef in France at the time. His age is reported inconsistently: Le Fooding says 23, Pudlowski says 24. With a 1992 birth year, either fits depending on his birthday.

CIBO, 24 rue Jeannin

CIBO is Ferrigno’s own restaurant, opened on 11 June 2020 at 24 rue Jeannin in Dijon. He decided to go solo in 2018, aged 26, and the works took 2 years. The star came fast: CIBO entered the 2021 guide with one, about 8 months after opening (macommune.info). The Guide Michelin still lists it with 1 star.

The cooking at CIBO is locavore, with ingredients sourced within 200 km (about 125 miles). It leans on Scandinavian influences, fermentation and freshwater fish. CIBO opens Tuesday to Friday and closes Saturday, Sunday and Monday. Phone: +33 3 80 28 80 76, website cibo.restaurant.

Gault&Millau France 2022 gave Ferrigno 3 toques and named him a “Grand de Demain”, its label for rising chefs.

Cave, 29 rue Jeannin

In November 2024 Ferrigno opened Cave at 29 rue Jeannin, directly across the street from CIBO, with 2 longtime collaborators. At opening, lunch was a 3-course menu at €30 and dinner was shared plates (Le Bonbon Dijon, 12 November 2024). I could not confirm that Cave is still open in October 2026, so check before you go.

La Maison des Cariatides vs CIBO vs Cave

The 3 tables differ most on style, price and opening days. If it’s Ferrigno’s cooking you’re after, go to CIBO, not the Cariatides. If you want the cheapest way into Dijon’s chef-led cooking, the €30 Cave lunch (if it’s still running) and the €59 Cariatides lunch are the 2 doors.

The building: why it’s called the Maison des Cariatides

The restaurant is named after its building, the Maison des Cariatides, also called Maison Pouffier: a listed historic monument (Mérimée record PA00112406) whose façade is carved with caryatids and atlantes. Caryatids are columns sculpted as female figures; atlantes are their male counterparts.

Carved caryatid and atlas on the Renaissance façade of the Maison des Cariatides, Dijon: columns sculpted as female and male figures. (Photo: GO69, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dijon\_(21)\_Maison\_des\_Cariatides\_-\_01.jpg))

The house was built for the Pouffier family, a dynasty of Dijon merchants. Its sculpted façade is in the Burgundian Renaissance style associated with Hugues Sambin (Monumentum, Lonely Planet). Sources put the construction anywhere from the early 16th to the early 17th century, so I leave the date open until the official record can be checked.

The full listed façade of the Maison des Cariatides (Maison Pouffier), 28 rue Chaudronnerie, Dijon, historic monument PA00112406. (Photo: William Jexpire, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maison\_des\_Cariatides\_(Dijon).jpg))

That façade is why travel guides such as Lonely Planet list the address. They send visitors to look at the stonework; the restaurant just happens to be inside.

Sculpted detail of the Maison des Cariatides façade on rue Chaudronnerie, the reason guides like Lonely Planet send visitors to this Dijon address. (Photo: Christophe.Finot, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dijon\_-\_Maison\_des\_Cariatides\_7.jpg))

Is La Maison des Cariatides part of Dijon’s burger scene?

No. La Maison des Cariatides does not serve burgers and plays no part in Dijon’s burger scene. None of the menus, guide listings or reviews I checked mention a burger, and the restaurant never appears in local “best burger in Dijon” rankings.

If you came for a burger, the name Dijon keeps coming back to is Foodies, at 12 rue François Rude. Its founder, Julien Chauvenet, won the World Burger Contest at Sirha Lyon on 20 January 2023 with his assistant Candice Salvan. They swept it: first place, the public prize, best traditional burger and best vegetarian burger (macommune.info). 1 caveat: Chauvenet is the 2023 champion, not the current one. The 2025 contest went to Louis-Gautier Lepers of Papa Renard in Morzine (Lyon Capitale).

FAQ: La Maison des Cariatides Dijon

Who is the chef at La Maison des Cariatides in Dijon today?

Marie-Cécile and Vincent Gomis are the owner-chefs. They took over from Sylvain Gauthey around 2024, according to Gault&Millau. Gault&Millau names Marie-Cécile Gomis as chef, while Michelin and the restaurant present the couple as co-leaders.

Is Angelo Ferrigno still at La Maison des Cariatides?

No. Angelo Ferrigno worked there from 2012 to 2018 and was head chef from 2015. Since June 2020 he has run his own restaurant, CIBO, at 24 rue Jeannin in Dijon.

Did Angelo Ferrigno found La Maison des Cariatides?

No. The restaurant was founded by Thomas Collomb. Ferrigno joined the kitchen team in 2012 under Collomb and Lucie Brunet.

Does La Maison des Cariatides have a Michelin star?

It won a Michelin star in 2016 under Angelo Ferrigno. Today it is listed in the Guide Michelin as Modern Cuisine at the €€€€ price level. Sources disagree on whether it holds a star in 2026, so check the live Michelin page before you book on that basis.

How much does a meal at La Maison des Cariatides cost?

Lunch menus cost €59 for 5 courses or €79 for 6 courses. Dinner menus cost €119 for 7 courses or €139 for 8 courses. Drinks are extra.

What days is La Maison des Cariatides open?

Wednesday to Sunday; it is closed Monday and Tuesday. Michelin and Gault&Millau give different dinner times and disagree on Sunday dinner, so call +33 3 80 45 59 25 to confirm and book.

Where is CIBO, and when did it get its Michelin star?

CIBO is at 24 rue Jeannin, Dijon. It opened on 11 June 2020 and won 1 Michelin star in the 2021 guide, about 8 months later. It is open Tuesday to Friday.

Why is it called the Maison des Cariatides?

The building’s façade is carved with caryatids, columns shaped as female figures, along with male atlantes. Also known as Maison Pouffier, it is a listed historic monument built for a family of Dijon merchants.

Does La Maison des Cariatides serve burgers?

No. It serves gastronomic tasting menus only. For a burger in Dijon, look at Foodies, 12 rue François Rude, whose founder Julien Chauvenet won the 2023 World Burger Contest.

Where can you eat the best burger in Dijon?

Foodies is the most recognized name, thanks to its 2023 World Burger Contest win. See our Dijon burger page for the city overview.
