Best Burgers in Vancouver, BC: Top 10 for 2026

The 10 best burgers in Vancouver, BC in 2026: Golden Plates winners, every address checked, prices in CA$ and the closed spots to skip.

By Olivia, Restaurants writer, Canada and the US NortheastPublished November 17, 2026

The best burger in Vancouver, BC (British Columbia, Canada) in 2026 is the smash burger at Between 2 Buns in Chinatown. Georgia Straight readers voted it Best Burger at the Golden Plates 2026, ahead of Black+Blue and Bin 4 Burger Lounge. My top 10 adds Pourhouse in Gastown, Feenie’s at the new Time Out Market, Burger Crush, Juicy Joe’s, Hawksworth, Alibi Room and Blaze Gourmet Burgers. I checked every address on November 15, 2026. Anything that has closed is out.

1 thing first: this guide covers Vancouver, British Columbia, not Vancouver, Washington. Search from the US and most review sites will send you to the Washington city.

These are the 10 best burger restaurants in Vancouver, BC in 2026, with their neighbourhood and signature burger.

How I ranked Vancouver’s best burger restaurants

I started from the one verified 2026 burger award in the city, then checked menus, local press and each restaurant’s own pages, and I dropped every place that isn’t open in 2026.

That award is the Best Burger category of the Georgia Straight’s Golden Plates 2026, published June 4, 2026. Nothing else comes close. The Vancouver Magazine Restaurant Awards 2026, out on May 4, have no burger category, from the judges or the readers.

I kept the readers’ winner at number one and Bin 4 at number 3. Black+Blue, their runner-up, drops to fourth, and that’s deliberate. I couldn’t find its burger’s name, toppings or price anywhere, and I won’t rank a burger I can’t describe above Pourhouse’s dry-aged chuck. From fourth place down, the order is mine. I weighed what each kitchen does that nobody else in town does: the beef, the technique, the chef, the room.

Each entry cites its sources: Daily Hive, Time Out, Noms Magazine, the Georgia Straight and the restaurants’ own sites. You won’t find Google ratings here. I couldn’t record them on a fixed date, and an undated star count tells you less than nothing. Prices are in Canadian dollars (CA$), with the year they were reported, before the 5% GST and the tip.

1. Between 2 Buns: Golden Plates 2026 winner and Vancouver’s best smash burger

Between 2 Buns is the best burger in Vancouver, BC in 2026 according to Georgia Straight readers, and the address to start with if you want a smash burger.

It began as a roaming food truck that parked at Container Brewing, The Boxcar and Main St Brewing. Chinatown came next: the Georgia Straight says it has had a permanent home there since 2020. Regulars order the Double Cheeser or the Jalapeño Bacon Cheeser.

The kitchen likes to experiment. In 2026 it ran a limited-edition Bicol Express Smash Burger, reported by Daily Hive: 2 smashed patties, American cheese, lettuce, pickles, chicharon crumbs, fried onions, house-made Bicol jam and coconut mayo. A classic cheeseburger loaded with Filipino flavours. That kind of idea is why I’d put this kitchen first even without the award.

A second location soft-launched on March 26, 2026 at 4245 Fraser Street in Fraserhood. It shares the space with Egg & Co.: brunch in the daytime, Between 2 Buns in the evening. Ray Loy is named as a partner in the Fraser Street location.

  • Address: 105 East Pender Street, Vancouver, BC V6A 1T6 (Chinatown); second location at 4245 Fraser Street (evenings)
  • Order: Double Cheeser or Jalapeño Bacon Cheeser
  • Good to know: the old domain between2bunsburgers.com no longer resolves. Check current hours on Instagram @between2bunsburgers: neither location has published its 2026 hours.

2. Pourhouse: the dry-aged burger of Gastown

Pourhouse serves the most indulgent burger in Gastown: dry-aged beef chuck, aged cheddar and pork belly, with foie gras as an add-on.

The Pourhouse Burger uses chuck that Noms Magazine says is dry-aged for 40 days, and that Daily Hive says comes from Cache Creek. Add foie gras, a fried egg or crispy chicken skin if you want more. Local chefs singled it out in BC Living back in 2015.

Half the appeal is the room. Pourhouse occupies a 1910 building, the former Leckie Boot Company, and pours drinks across a 38-foot bar of reclaimed Douglas fir that’s 120 years old.

  • Address: 162 Water Street, Gastown, Vancouver, BC V6B 2M9
  • Phone: 604-568-7022
  • Website: pourhousevancouver.com
  • Price: no confirmed 2026 price; check the menu online

3. Bin 4 Burger Lounge: the best-value smash burger in Vancouver

Bin 4 Burger Lounge in South Granville came third in Best Burger at the Golden Plates 2026, and its happy-hour smash burger costs CA$9.

Bin 4 is a BC chain born in Victoria, with other locations in Burnaby, Kelowna and Nanaimo. It uses what it calls “premium BC beef”. The Bin 700 “Smashville” is CA$18 at lunch and CA$9 at happy hour. Same burger, half the price: on a budget, go at happy hour. There’s also a burger of the month.

  • Address: 2996 Granville Street (at W 14th Ave), Vancouver, BC V6H 3J7
  • Phone: 604-428-7649
  • Website: bin4burgerlounge.com, Instagram @bin4burgerlounge
  • Order: the Bin 700 “Smashville” at happy hour

4. Black+Blue: the steakhouse burger that took Golden Plates silver

Black+Blue, a downtown steakhouse, came second in Best Burger at the Golden Plates 2026, the only steak kitchen on the readers’ podium.

The Glowbal Restaurant Group opened it in fall 2011 on Alberni Street, the stretch locals call Vancouver’s “Rodeo Drive”. The beef ages in a room lined with Himalayan salt, and there’s a rooftop terrace called The Roof.

Here’s why it sits fourth and not second. The restaurant’s website doesn’t publish its menu as text, and I couldn’t confirm the burger’s name, toppings or price anywhere else. Readers clearly love it. I’d still like to know what I’m recommending. Sources also disagree on the street number (1028 or 1032 Alberni Street), so call before you go.

  • Address: Alberni Street, downtown Vancouver (1028 or 1032, depending on the source)
  • Phone: 604-637-0777

5. Feenie’s: an Iron Chef’s smashed wagyu burger at Time Out Market Vancouver

Feenie’s is the new burger counter of 2026: chef Rob Feenie serves a smashed wagyu burger at Time Out Market Vancouver, which opened on May 28, 2026.

Rob Feenie was the first Canadian to win on Iron Chef America. He beat Masaharu Morimoto in an episode that aired on February 20, 2005, with crab as the secret ingredient. Before that, he ran the kitchen at Lumière.

The Feenie’s Burger is a quarter pound of smashed wagyu with maple-smoked bacon, Canadian cheese, mushrooms, lettuce, onion, tomato, his RF signature sauce and Hobbs pickles on a toasted potato bun. Local press reported it at CA$18.99. The market also has a Time Out Market Burger, made with wagyu ground in-house and Gruyère and inspired by the burger at Maison du Crocodile.

  • Address: Time Out Market Vancouver, Oakridge Park, 650 West 41st Avenue (Cambie and 41st)
  • Order: the Feenie’s Burger

6. Burger Crush: downtown smash burgers from a Victoria food truck

Burger Crush is one of the smash burger names that come up most in Vancouver, with a 40-seat restaurant on Nelson Street downtown.

The chain started as a food truck in Victoria. Its Vancouver restaurant opened on January 11, 2023 in the former Johnny Rockets space. Menu, prices and other locations are in our full guide to Burger Crush.

  • Address: 755 Nelson Street, downtown Vancouver
  • Seats: 40

7. Juicy Joe’s Burgers: a halal burger in Vancouver

Juicy Joe’s is where I’d send anyone looking for a halal burger in Vancouver: the restaurant is fully halal-certified, and its smash burgers use grass-fed Canadian beef that’s never frozen.

Juicy Joe’s used to cook out of the shared kitchen at Paul’s Omelettery (2211 Granville Street). It’s now listed in Kitsilano, but some older listings still show the Granville Street address. Check before you go.

  • Address: 2004 West 4th Avenue, Kitsilano, Vancouver
  • Good to know: fully halal-certified, grass-fed Canadian beef, smash style

8. Hawksworth: the fine-dining burger at the Rosewood Hotel Georgia

Hawksworth Restaurant, inside the Rosewood Hotel Georgia, is the fine-dining burger on this list, and it’s still open in 2026.

The Hawksworth Burger has house-ground beef, a secret house sauce, applewood-smoked bacon and aged Canadian cheddar. It cost CA$23 in 2019, according to Daily Hive, already the highest price in this guide. I couldn’t confirm what it costs now. You’re paying for the hotel dining room as much as the burger, and some nights that’s exactly what you want.

  • Address: Rosewood Hotel Georgia, 801 West Georgia Street, downtown Vancouver

The Rosewood Hotel Georgia at 801 West Georgia Street in downtown Vancouver, home of Hawksworth Restaurant and its Hawksworth Burger (photo from 2018). (Photo: Wpcpey, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rosewood\_Hotel\_Georgia\_2018.jpg)

9. Alibi Room: craft beer and a Pemberton beef burger in Railtown

Alibi Room is the burger to have with a craft beer: 50 taps, and a burger made with natural beef from Pemberton farms.

The bar opened in 2006 on the edge of Gastown. Its burger comes with smoked onion mayo and zucchini pickles; it was CA$16 in 2019. Reviews from 2026 mention a change in management, so check the current menu online before you go for the burger.

  • Address: 157 Alexander Street, Vancouver, BC V6A 1B8 (Railtown)

The bar and its row of craft beer taps at Alibi Room, 157 Alexander Street in Railtown, Vancouver, where the Pemberton beef burger comes with a beer from one of 50 taps (photo from 2011). (Photo: Charles Thompson, CC BY 2.0, https://www.flickr.com/photos/86541980@N00/6189760618)

10. Blaze Gourmet Burgers: beef, chicken and vegan burgers in Mount Pleasant

Blaze Gourmet Burgers is my pick for a group with mixed diets: it serves beef, chicken and vegan burgers.

Blaze started as a ghost kitchen in 2019 and is still expanding in 2026, with a new location in Port Moody at Moody Yards.

  • Address: 43 East 5th Avenue, Mount Pleasant, Vancouver, BC V5T 1G7
  • Phone: 604-568-5486

New and worth knowing: Four Bite Burger and Cheese Slap

2 newer burger spots didn’t make the 2026 top 10 but are worth a detour: Four Bite Burger for sliders at lunch and Cheese Slap for a halal smash burger late at night.

Four Bite Burger had its grand opening on September 24, 2026 in the Bentall Centre food court (105-505 Burrard Street). It sells beef-and-onion sliders on steamed potato buns, Monday to Friday from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. The founders, Jeremy Lau and Peter Yau, also run Bovine Rice Bowls. Too new to rank, for now.

Cheese Slap has served halal smash burgers and pizza late into the night at 925 Granville Street, across from the Vogue Theatre, since June 2024.

Closed Vancouver burger restaurants older lists still recommend

Plenty of “best burgers in Vancouver” lists still send readers to places that have shut. Skip these.

  • Hundy (Yaletown): closed in 2026 after 8 years; the space was sold to the Key Collection (Daily Hive)
  • Upstairs at Campagnolo and its Dirty Burger: closed in 2020
  • Moderne Burger (865 W Broadway): closed in February 2023, now a Popeyes
  • Save On Meats diner: closed
  • Burgoo (downtown): closed in May 2026
  • Odd Burger: closed July 12, 2026
  • DownLow Burgers: its Instagram says “closed for now”. In 2026 it only ran as a summer pop-up at the Trout Lake concession (3300 Victoria Drive)

Burgers by neighbourhood: Chinatown, Gastown, downtown and beyond

Staying in 1 part of town? Here’s the nearest good burger on this list.

  • Chinatown: Between 2 Buns (105 East Pender Street)
  • Gastown and Railtown: Pourhouse (162 Water Street) and Alibi Room (157 Alexander Street)
  • Downtown: Black+Blue, Burger Crush, Hawksworth, Cheese Slap and Four Bite Burger
  • South Granville and Kitsilano: Bin 4 Burger Lounge and Juicy Joe’s
  • Mount Pleasant and Fraserhood: Blaze Gourmet Burgers and the second Between 2 Buns
  • Oakridge: Feenie’s at Time Out Market Vancouver

FAQ: best burgers in Vancouver, BC

Where can I get the best burger in Vancouver in 2026? The best burger in Vancouver, BC in 2026 is at Between 2 Buns, 105 East Pender Street in Chinatown. It won Best Burger at the Georgia Straight’s Golden Plates 2026, ahead of Black+Blue and Bin 4 Burger Lounge.

Which burger won Best Burger at the Golden Plates 2026? Between 2 Buns won Best Burger at the Georgia Straight’s Golden Plates 2026, published June 4, 2026. Black+Blue came second and Bin 4 Burger Lounge third.

Where can I find the best smash burger in Vancouver? For smash burgers, go to Between 2 Buns (Chinatown and Fraser Street), Juicy Joe’s (Kitsilano) or Burger Crush (755 Nelson Street). For the best price, Bin 4’s Bin 700 “Smashville” costs CA$9 at happy hour.

Where can I get a halal burger in Vancouver? Juicy Joe’s Burgers at 2004 West 4th Avenue in Kitsilano is fully halal-certified and uses grass-fed Canadian beef. Cheese Slap at 925 Granville Street also serves halal smash burgers, and it’s open late.

How much does a burger cost at a Vancouver restaurant? The two 2026 restaurant burger prices I could confirm are CA$18 for Bin 4’s Smashville at lunch and CA$18.99 for the Feenie’s Burger, before the 5% GST and the tip. Bin 4 drops to CA$9 at happy hour. Older prices from 2019: CA$16 at Alibi Room and CA$23 for the Hawksworth Burger.

Prices are before the 5% GST and the tip. (Chart: Burger Breton, original work, CC BY 4.0 (data: Bin 4 Burger Lounge, Time Out, Daily Hive))

Which Vancouver burger restaurants have closed? Hundy in Yaletown closed in 2026 and Upstairs at Campagnolo, home of the Dirty Burger, closed in 2020. Moderne Burger closed in February 2023, Burgoo’s downtown location in May 2026 and Odd Burger on July 12, 2026. The Save On Meats diner is also closed.

Where can I get a vegan or vegetarian burger in Vancouver? Blaze Gourmet Burgers at 43 East 5th Avenue in Mount Pleasant serves vegan burgers alongside its beef and chicken burgers.

What’s the difference between Vancouver, BC and Vancouver, Washington? Vancouver, British Columbia is the Canadian city covered in this guide. Vancouver, Washington is a smaller US city across the border near Portland, Oregon. Searches for “best burgers Vancouver” often return Tripadvisor and Yelp pages for the Washington city, so add “BC” to your search.

What is White Spot’s Triple O sauce? Triple O sauce is the signature burger sauce of White Spot, a British Columbia chain founded in 1928 as a drive-in at Granville Street and 67th Avenue.

A White Spot restaurant in Richmond, BC, the British Columbia chain founded in 1928 and known for its Triple O burger sauce. This location is in Richmond, not Vancouver (photo from June 2008). (Photo: Ken Mist, CC BY 2.0, https://www.flickr.com/photos/37996606796@N01/2597375997)

Where can I get a burger near Gastown, Chinatown or Granville Island? In Gastown, go to Pourhouse (162 Water Street) or Alibi Room (157 Alexander Street) in nearby Railtown. In Chinatown, Between 2 Buns is at 105 East Pender Street. On Granville Island, Popina Canteen (1691 Johnston Street) served a grass-fed BC beef cheeseburger from shipping containers, but I couldn’t confirm it’s open in 2026, so check before you go.
