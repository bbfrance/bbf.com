The Standard Covington: Burgers Near Cincinnati

Who owns The Standard, which burgers it has served at Burger Week, and why it's worth the short trip across the Ohio River from Cincinnati.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 24, 2026

The Standard Covington is a burger bar in a converted 1930s filling station at 434 Main St., Covington, Kentucky, a few minutes across the Ohio River from downtown Cincinnati. Paul Weckman and Emily Wolff own it. They’re the MainStrasse Village couple behind Otto’s, Frida 602, Larry’s and Mama’s on Main. It opened in July 2020 and was still hosting events in 2026. Go for the building’s story and the house burger, but call first: I couldn’t confirm a regular menu price.

Who owns The Standard Covington: Paul Weckman and Emily Wolff

Paul Weckman and Emily Wolff own The Standard. The married couple opened it in 2020 and run it alongside their other restaurants in MainStrasse Village. They met in their first week at the University of Kentucky (City of Covington).

Weckman studied agricultural economics and finance, and he handles the kitchen and the business side. Wolff is an artist and the group’s creative director. She designed The Standard’s original wallpaper herself (NKY Tribune). In 2025, Covington gave the couple its Authenti-CITY award.

The 5 MainStrasse Village restaurants of Weckman and Wolff

Weckman and Wolff have opened 5 restaurants on Main Street in Covington since 2003, and The Standard was the fourth. The NKY Tribune gives this timeline:

  • Otto’s: opened March 23, 2003, when the owners were 22.
  • Frida 602: opened around 2015.
  • Larry’s: a dive bar, opened in 2019.
  • The Standard: opened in July 2020.
  • Mama’s on Main: 621 Main St., opened March 4, 2022.

The restaurants now operate as Otto’s Hospitality Group. Molly Costello started at Otto’s as a server in 2008 and worked her way up to chef. She became vice president in 2022 and partner in September 2025 (LINK nky). No source I found names a head chef for The Standard itself, so I won’t guess.

From Dick’s Standard Service to burger bar

Before it served burgers, the building was Dick’s Standard Service, a garage and filling station that the Ostendorf family ran for 84 years, starting in the 1930s. Butch Ostendorf closed it in 2015. It then sat empty for about 5 years, until Weckman and Wolff took it on (NKY Tribune; Michael Monks, The River City News, July 2020). Some sources spell the family name Ostendorff.

The restaurant wears that history openly. The name comes from the station. A mural honors Butch. The bourbon slushee follows the recipe of his late wife, Marge (CityBeat, July 24, 2020). To me, that’s the case for crossing the river. Plenty of bars around Cincinnati sell a cheeseburger. Very few of them sit inside a garage that one family kept running for 8 decades.

What The Standard served when it opened in 2020

The opening menu in July 2020 was short and built around one burger. The River City News listed the burger, a watermelon salad, steak on flatbread, onion rings, fries and slaw. The drinks were a garden stand lemonade, a mango cart margarita, a Main Street mule and a Standard mojito.

The Standard Covington burger: what to order

Order The Standard Burger: a single patty with American cheese, lettuce, onion, tomato and Thousand Island. It carries the restaurant’s name, and it’s the build CityBeat listed for Cincinnati Burger Week 2023.

I couldn’t confirm the year-round burger menu or its regular prices, because the online ordering and reservation pages blocked my checks. The Standard at Cincinnati Burger Week

The Standard has taken part in Cincinnati Burger Week several times, most recently the 2026 edition, held July 13 to 19. The main event runs in July, and a “Halfway to Burger Week” edition comes around in February. Planning a summer trip? Burger Week is the cheapest time to try The Standard’s burger.

Is The Standard in Cincinnati or Covington?

The Standard is in Covington, Kentucky, not Cincinnati. Covington sits directly across the Ohio River from downtown Cincinnati. The address is 434 Main St., Covington, KY 41011, on the corner of Fifth and Main. That puts it in the MainStrasse Village restaurant district, a short walk from the couple’s other places. Is The Standard one of the best burgers in Covington, KY?

The Standard deserves a place on any Covington burger list, but most published lists are too old to include it. Visitcincy’s “Top 10 Burgers in Covington” and CityBeat’s “32 best places” list both came out in 2019, a year before it opened. My case for it rests on what I could check. It’s owner-run, by a team that has cooked on Main Street since 2003. The building carries 8 decades of local history. The kitchen has also shown up for Burger Week since 2023. I didn’t eat there for this piece, so there’s no rating here. You’ll find more city guides on our Restaurants hub.

FAQ: The Standard Covington

Who owns The Standard in Covington, KY? Paul Weckman and Emily Wolff. They’re the married couple behind Otto’s, Frida 602, Larry’s and Mama’s on Main, which now operate as Otto’s Hospitality Group.

Is The Standard in Cincinnati or Covington? Covington, Kentucky, at 434 Main St., on the corner of Fifth and Main. Covington is just across the Ohio River from downtown Cincinnati.

What burger should I order at The Standard Covington? Start with The Standard Burger: a single patty with American cheese, lettuce, onion, tomato and Thousand Island. Check the current menu for regular prices. The $7 to $9 prices quoted for Burger Week were promotional.

What was the building before it became The Standard? Dick’s Standard Service, a garage and filling station that the Ostendorf family ran for 84 years, starting in the 1930s. It closed in 2015.

Does The Standard Covington serve brunch? I couldn’t confirm a current brunch menu. Third-party listings show the restaurant opening at 10 a.m. on weekends, so call ahead.

Does The Standard take part in Cincinnati Burger Week? Yes. It served The Standard Burger in 2023 and French Onion Sliders in 2025, and it took part again in July 2026.

What other restaurants do the owners of The Standard run in MainStrasse Village? Otto’s, Frida 602, Larry’s and Mama’s on Main, at 621 Main St.

Was chef Ryan Santos involved with The Standard? No. I found no source linking him to it. Santos was the chef-founder of Please, a fine-dining restaurant in Over-the-Rhine that Cincinnati Magazine ranked No. 1 in the city. Please has since closed for good (WCPO).
