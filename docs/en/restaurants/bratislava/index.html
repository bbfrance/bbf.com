Best Burgers in Bratislava: Old Town and Danube

The best burgers in Bratislava are concentrated in the Old Town (Staré Mesto), the compact pedestrian centre below the castle, and along the newer Danube riverfront developments to its east. Across the river, Petržalka, the largest housing estate in Slovakia, and the residential districts of Ružinov and Nové Mesto serve a mostly local crowd.

Bratislava sits about 60 km from Vienna and borders both Austria and Hungary, so its food mixes Central European habits. The local ingredient to know is bryndza, a soft, salty sheep’s cheese best known in bryndzové halušky, the national dish of small potato dumplings. Slovak lager, or white wine from the Small Carpathians, a wine region that starts at the city’s northern edge, usually goes with the meal.

Burgers in Bratislava: key facts

  • Neighbourhoods to explore: the Old Town, the Danube riverfront, Petržalka, Ružinov and Nové Mesto.
  • Regional ingredient: Slovenská bryndza, a crumbly sheep’s cheese from the mountains of central Slovakia, protected by an EU geographical indication.
  • Day-trippers: many visitors come from Vienna by train or by boat on the Danube and eat in the Old Town.
  • Currency: the euro (EUR), used in Slovakia since 2009.
  • Getting around: the Old Town is best on foot, and trams and buses cross the Danube to Petržalka.
