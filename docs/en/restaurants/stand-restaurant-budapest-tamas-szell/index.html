Stand Restaurant Budapest: Széll's Two-Star Table

Tamás Széll's two-Michelin-star Budapest restaurant: address, hours, menus, awards, and why there's no burger, plus where Széll's burger recipe lives.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 20, 2026

Stand is a two-Michelin-star restaurant at Székely Mihály utca 2 in Budapest’s District VI, run by chef-owner Tamás Széll. It serves modern Hungarian cuisine as tasting menus, à la carte and a lunch menu (Michelin Guide and standrestaurant.hu, checked 2026-11-01). It does not serve a burger. I went through the official site, the Michelin listing, Hungarian food media and the menu of Stand25, the sister bistro, and found no burger in any of them. The one documented link between Széll and burgers is a homemade-burger video series he presented for Lidl Hungary’s cooking school. My take: if you came to Budapest for a burger, Stand is the wrong door. If you want to see how far Hungarian cooking has come since the 2010s, it is the first place I would book.

One thing doesn’t add up. The official site mentions a Saturday lunch “from October” without a year, and Michelin’s hours leave it out. Phone or email before planning a lunchtime visit. The site publishes no prices either, so ask for the current tasting-menu price when you book.

Who is Tamás Széll, the chef behind Stand?

Tamás Széll is the Hungarian chef who won gold at the Bocuse d’Or Europe 2016 and then built Stand into a two-Michelin-star restaurant. He won that European final in Budapest in May 2016, while he was chef at Onyx, which already held a Michelin star (We Love Budapest, The Caterer).

The competition hall of the Bocuse d’Or Europe 2016 at Hungexpo, Budapest, on 10 May 2016: chefs’ kitchen boxes below giant screens and fans waving flags, the contest Tamás Széll won for Hungary (Photo: Elekes Andor, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bocuse\_d%27Or\_Europe\_Budapest\_2016.JPG)

Eight months later came the world final in Lyon. Széll’s Hungarian team finished fourth, one place ahead of France, and took the special prizes for best meat plate and best poster (We Love Budapest, Daily News Hungary). Beating the host nation in Lyon gave him the standing to open his own places. He started small, with a market kiosk.

Tamás Széll and Lidl Hungary

For about ten years Széll was Lidl Hungary’s partner chef. The partnership ended in April 2024, when Jani Mizsei of MÁK took over (Pénzcentrum, Trade Magazin). The Lidl years are when home cooks across the country got to know him, and they are where his burger lessons come from.

How Stand opened in 2018

Stand opened in July 2018, founded by Tamás Széll and Szabina Szulló, described as his spouse, with the kitchen open to the room behind glass walls (We Love Budapest). Prices at opening: 7,500–9,500 HUF for a three- or four-course lunch, 19,500 HUF for four courses at dinner, 29,500 HUF for the eight-course chef’s menu. Those are 2018 figures. Don’t budget with them.

A two-Michelin-star restaurant in Budapest: stars and awards

Stand holds two Michelin stars: the first since March 2019, the second since 2022, both kept in the Michelin Guide Hungary 2025. The guide still listed two stars on 2026-11-01. I found no primary source for a 2026 Michelin result, so I report none.

Stand restaurant Budapest’s Michelin stars by year: opened July 2018, first star March 2019, second star 2022, still two stars in the Michelin Guide on 1 November 2026 (Chart: Burger Breton, original work (data: Michelin Guide, We Love Budapest), all rights reserved)

At home, the recognition is even louder. Hungary’s Dining Guide named Stand Restaurant of the Year and “Best of the Best” in September 2026, its fifth Restaurant of the Year title according to Telex and Dining Guide. I could match three of those years to primary reports: 2018, 2019 and 2026. The other two remain unconfirmed.

What to eat at Stand: tasting menus and Hungarian classics

Stand serves reworked Hungarian classics. Michelin’s inspectors single out the gulyás soup, the venison and the somlói galuska, the sponge-cake trifle with chocolate sauce and whipped cream that never leaves the menu. The wine list is mostly Hungarian, with sweet Tokaji among it (Michelin Guide, checked 2026-11-01).

Somlói galuska, the Hungarian sponge dessert with chocolate sauce and whipped cream that Michelin singles out at Stand restaurant Budapest (illustrative photo, not taken at Stand) (Photo: Toben, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Soml%C3%B3i\_galuska.jpg)

Four ways in: the Chef’s tasting menu, a vegetarian Chef’s tasting menu, à la carte or the lunch menu (standrestaurant.hu). The vegetarian menu is listed as a menu of its own, not as swaps on the meat one. Few fine-dining kitchens in Central Europe bother with that, and I think it says something about how seriously Stand takes vegetables.

Who runs Stand’s kitchen today?

Benjamin Morris has been Stand’s head chef since October 2025, running the restaurant alongside Tamás Széll. He joined the team in 2020. The same month, co-founder and co-owner Szabina Szulló stepped back from daily kitchen work because she was expecting a child (Telex, Dining Guide). Those reports gave no return date. She remains a co-owner.

Stand vs Stand25 Bisztró: which one to book

Book Stand for a two-star tasting menu in Pest. Book Stand25 for Széll’s cooking at bistro prices in Buda: dishes priced individually and a 19,500 HUF three-course menu. For a first visit on a budget, I would start with Stand25.

Tamás Széll’s burger recipe: the Lidl cooking-school series

Széll’s only documented burger is one he taught, not one he serves: a three-part “Házi hamburger” (homemade burger) series for the Lidl Hungary cooking school. It predates the end of his Lidl partnership in April 2024. The exact air dates are not given.

  1. The bun. Part one makes a burger bun by hand (video).
  2. The sauce and toppings. Part two covers the burger sauce, the vegetables and the toppings (video, Lidl recipe page).
  3. The patty and assembly. Part three covers the patty and how a professional stacks the burger (video).

The videos are in Hungarian. Notice the order: a Bocuse d’Or chef starts with the bun, not the meat. For a home cook, that is the most useful lesson in this whole profile. No restaurant menu carries a “Széll burger”, so this series is as close as you will get to one.

Where to eat a burger in Budapest instead

For an actual burger in Budapest, skip Stand and head for the burger bars. Tuning Burger is one of the best documented. Róbert Erdélyi founded it in 2014 at Akácfa utca 45 in District VII, a short walk from Stand. It later opened at Bálna, Római-part, Time Out Market and Városliget, the last of these on 30 April 2025 (Time Out Budapest, We Love Budapest). How I researched this profile

This is a profile, not a meal review, so Stand gets no rating from me. Every fact comes from Stand’s and Stand25’s official sites, the Michelin Guide, or Hungarian and international food media: We Love Budapest, Telex, Dining Guide, HVG, Pénzcentrum. I checked all of them on 2026-11-01. Where a fact rests only on a third-party guide or on the venue’s own word, I say so. Where I could not pin it down at all, I left it out, which is why you won’t find a current tasting-menu price here.

FAQ about Stand and Tamás Széll

Does Stand in Budapest serve burgers?

No. Stand serves modern Hungarian tasting menus, à la carte and a lunch menu, and neither Stand nor its sister bistro Stand25 lists a burger. Széll’s only burger link is a past Lidl Hungary cooking-school video series.

How many Michelin stars does Stand have, and since when?

Two. Stand won its first star in March 2019 and its second in 2022, and kept both in the Michelin Guide Hungary 2025. Michelin still listed two stars on 2026-11-01.

Did Tamás Széll win the Bocuse d’Or?

He won the Bocuse d’Or Europe in Budapest in May 2016. At the world final in Lyon in January 2017 his team finished fourth, ahead of France, and won the prizes for best meat plate and best poster.

How much does the tasting menu at Stand cost?

Stand does not publish its prices. Ask when you book by phone (+36 30 785 9139) or email (info@standrestaurant.hu). In 2018 the eight-course chef’s menu cost 29,500 HUF, which tells you little about today’s price.

Where is Stand and when is it open?

Stand is at Székely Mihály utca 2, 1061 Budapest, in District VI (Terézváros). Michelin lists it as open Tuesday to Saturday from 6 pm to midnight, closed Sunday and Monday.

Does Stand offer a vegetarian menu?

Yes. Stand serves a vegetarian Chef’s tasting menu alongside the standard one (standrestaurant.hu, checked 2026-11-01).

Who runs Stand’s kitchen today?

Benjamin Morris, on the team since 2020, became head chef in October 2025 and runs Stand with Tamás Széll. Co-owner Szabina Szulló stepped back from daily cooking that month.

What is the difference between Stand and Stand25?

Stand is the two-star tasting-menu restaurant in Pest. Stand25 is the casual bistro at Attila út 10 in Buda, with goulash at 5,900 HUF and a three-course menu at 19,500 HUF (stand25.hu, checked 2026-11-01).

Where can you eat the best burger in Budapest?

At the city’s burger bars rather than its fine-dining rooms. Tuning Burger has been at Akácfa utca 45 since 2014. More addresses are on our Budapest burger city hub.
