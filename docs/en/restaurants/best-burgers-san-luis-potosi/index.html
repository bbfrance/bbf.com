Best Burgers in San Luis Potosí: Zato's (1987)

Francisco Javier Aranda Guerra's charcoal-grill burger house on Avenida Carranza: its history, the house burger, checked prices and both branches.

By Carmen, Restaurants writer, MexicoPublished November 18, 2026

Zato’s Hamburguesas, on Avenida Venustiano Carranza, is the San Luis Potosí burger house with the longest history I could verify. Francisco Javier Aranda Guerra opened it in 1987, and it still grills over charcoal. The house burger comes with melted asadero cheese and a cold-cut topping Zato’s keeps to itself. When I checked Uber Eats on 13 November 2026, the Hamburguesa Zato’s cost MX$110. That is cheap for this city: the gourmet burger bars were charging more than twice as much in 2025.

Who founded Zato’s? Francisco Javier Aranda Guerra

Francisco Javier Aranda Guerra founded Zato’s in 1987 and is still described as its owner. He began with a rented stand on Avenida Venustiano Carranza and only later bought premises of his own, according to a 2021 profile in the local magazine La Orquesta. Why burgers? Out of necessity, he told the magazine.

The name is personal. “Zato’s” was his childhood nickname, according to the same profile. What I could not find is whether he still works the grill himself in 2026, so I won’t claim he does.

The Hamburguesa Zato’s: what is in the signature burger

The Hamburguesa Zato’s is a beef patty with asadero cheese, lettuce, tomato, ketchup, mayonnaise, the house cold cuts and house pickled chiles. That is the description on the Carranza branch’s Uber Eats menu, which calls the cold-cut topping a “secret recipe”.

Asadero is a soft, mild Mexican cheese made for melting, which is why it works on a hot grill. La Orquesta adds that the “secret” cold cuts include ham and bacon, served with grilled onion. Beyond that, Zato’s has never published the recipe, and I am not going to guess.

Charcoal-grilled burgers in San Luis Potosí: how Zato’s cooks them

Zato’s cooks its burgers over charcoal, not on a flat-top griddle. La Orquesta describes charcoal-grilled beef under melted asadero, with ham, bacon and grilled onion piled on top. If you want a charcoal burger in San Luis Potosí, this is the address with the longest track record I could find.

That sets it apart. The newer places in the price comparison below lead with a pineapple burger or a bacon jam. Zato’s still leads with the grill.

Zato’s branches: addresses and opening hours

Zato’s has 2 verified branches: the original on Avenida Venustiano Carranza and a second on Coronel Romero, opened in 1993.

  • Carranza branch. Rappi and Uber Eats list it at Calle Río Usumacinta 210, Lomas los Filtros, at the corner with Av. Venustiano Carranza, near the UASLP Faculty of Medicine. Rappi gives postcode 78210, Uber Eats 78219. Yelp lists Av. Venustiano Carranza 2425. These are most likely the same corner site.
  • Coronel Romero branch. Rappi lists it at Prol. Cnel. Romero, Issste, 78369 San Luis Potosí.

The only hours published anywhere I looked are delivery hours: 7:00 PM to 11:00 PM for the Carranza branch on Uber Eats. No source gives in-store hours or a phone number, so message the official Facebook page before you make a special trip. 1 search summary mentions a third branch near Himno Nacional. Nothing else backs it up, so I have left it out.

How Zato’s compares with other burgers in San Luis Potosí

Zato’s sits at the low end of the city’s burger prices: its MX$110 house burger costs less than half the MX$233 to MX$249 that Tunns, Jhonny Fries and Sarten charged in May 2025. It is not the cheapest, though. Geras and El Crustáceo Cascarudo listed burgers under MX$100. Those prices come from the lifestyle site LíderLife, published on 27 May 2025, and I have not rechecked them.

My take: go to the gourmet bars for novelty toppings. If you want a burger with almost 4 decades of local history behind it, go to Zato’s. I have not eaten there yet, so there is no rating here until I do. Chains trade in the city too, including Carl’s Jr., whose Mexican branches I have covered in Puebla and Mérida. None of them can offer what Zato’s does: a local founder’s charcoal grill, on the same avenue since 1987.

Why Avenida Carranza is San Luis Potosí’s burger street

Avenida Venustiano Carranza is the city’s main burger strip, where family burger houses, gourmet newcomers and chains share 1 long avenue west of the historic centre. Our San Luis Potosí burger guide notes that some Carranza burger houses have been trading since the 1980s. Zato’s, founded in 1987, is the one I could verify. The avenue is long, so check the street number before you go.

Looking for Nicos? It is in Mexico City, not San Luis Potosí

Nicos, the restaurant of chef Gerardo Vázquez Lugo, is in Mexico City, and it does not serve burgers. It opened in 1957 as a small soda fountain in Clavería, Azcapotzalco, founded by María Elena Lugo and Raymundo Vázquez (Chilango). Their son Gerardo now runs the kitchen. The Michelin Guide lists it at Av. Cuitláhuac 3102 for cecina tacos, tamarind shrimp and mole enchiladas. For a burger in the capital, start with our guide to the best burgers in Mexico City.

FAQ: Zato’s and burgers in San Luis Potosí

Where are the best burgers in San Luis Potosí?

Start on Avenida Venustiano Carranza, the city’s burger strip. Zato’s Hamburguesas, founded there in 1987, is the longest-running burger house on the avenue that I could verify. Its charcoal-grilled house burger cost MX$110 on Uber Eats in November 2026.

When was Zato’s founded and who founded it?

Zato’s was founded in 1987 by Francisco Javier Aranda Guerra, who started from a rented stand on Avenida Carranza. The name comes from his childhood nickname.

How much does a burger cost at Zato’s?

On Uber Eats, on 13 November 2026, the Hamburguesa Zato’s cost MX$110, the double MX$150 and the hot dog MX$85. In-store prices are not published and may differ.

What is on the Zato’s burger?

A beef patty, asadero cheese, lettuce, tomato, ketchup, mayonnaise, house pickled chiles and a “secret recipe” cold-cut topping that includes ham and bacon. It is cooked over charcoal.

What are Zato’s opening hours?

The Carranza branch delivers on Uber Eats from 7:00 PM to 11:00 PM. In-store hours are not published, so check the official Facebook page before you go.

Where can I find charcoal-grilled burgers in San Luis Potosí?

At Zato’s Hamburguesas, which grills its burgers over charcoal. Its 2 branches are on Avenida Carranza (corner of Río Usumacinta) and on Coronel Romero.

Is Nicos restaurant in San Luis Potosí?

No. Nicos is a traditional Mexican restaurant in Clavería, Azcapotzalco, Mexico City, at Av. Cuitláhuac 3102. Chef Gerardo Vázquez Lugo runs its kitchen, and the Michelin Guide lists no burger on its menu.
