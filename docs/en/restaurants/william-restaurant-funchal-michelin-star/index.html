William Restaurant Funchal: Michelin Star & Chefs

William restaurant in Funchal: the one-Michelin-star kitchen at Reid's Palace, chef José Diogo Costa, hours, menus and the hotel's €28 Wagyu burger.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished November 27, 2026

William restaurant in Funchal is the one-Michelin-star dining room of Reid’s Palace, A Belmond Hotel, at Estrada Monumental 139. Since 2023 its kitchen has been run by Madeiran chef José Diogo Costa, winner of the 2025 MICHELIN Young Chef Award and now executive chef of the whole hotel. William serves contemporary Madeiran tasting menus for dinner only, Tuesday to Saturday. It does not serve burgers. Reid’s does, though: the hotel’s Pool Terrace menu lists a Wagyu burger, and that’s where this article ends up.

First, a common error to clear up. Plenty of pages call Luís Pestana William’s “chef, owner and founder”. He was none of the last two. Pestana was the hotel’s executive chef, an employee who led the kitchen when it won and renewed its star. Belmond operates the restaurant, and the hotel itself was founded in 1891 by William Reid.

William is a contemporary fine-dining restaurant with one Michelin star, inside Reid’s Palace on Funchal’s Estrada Monumental. Here are the facts I verified on 20 November 2026 against the Michelin Guide and Visit Madeira.

Exact service times conflict between sources. The Michelin Guide’s English page says 19:00–21:30, its French page says 19:30–22:00, and Time Out (2021) says 19:00–23:00 with last orders at 21:30. Three sources, three answers. Confirm your table time with reservations.

Who is the chef at William today?

José Diogo Costa has led William since 2023 and is also executive chef of Reid’s Palace, according to the Michelin Guide’s French listing and Michelin Guide Portugal.

Costa trained at the hotel schools of Coimbra and Porto and holds WSET Level 2 in wine. His CV runs from Norway, Thailand and Macau to Agrícola Macatho in Chile and Gut Oggau in Austria. He then ran the kitchen at Azores Wine Company on Pico for two years before coming home to Madeira (Michelin Guide Portugal, 21 March 2025).

Sources disagree on his birthplace. Madeira Island News says Seixal on the north coast; Reporter Gourmet says Funchal. So I’ll stick to what both agree on: he’s Madeiran.

José Diogo Costa and the 2025 MICHELIN Young Chef Award

José Diogo Costa won the MICHELIN Young Chef Award at the Michelin Guide Portugal 2025 gala, held on 25 February 2025 at the Alfândega Congress Center in Porto. He couldn’t attend, so Reid’s general manager Eduardo Peregrino accepted the award for him (Madeira Island News; Michelin Guide Portugal).

How Costa cooks: local, emotional and modern

Costa describes his cooking as “local, emotional and modern”, and he backs it with a supply chain of more than 40 local producers. He brings back forgotten Madeiran ingredients such as chícharo and brigalhó. Signature dishes include local fish with textures of fennel, and lamb smoked over oak from Madeira wine casks (Reporter Gourmet). That last one is the dish I’d book for.

Who was Luís Pestana, chef of Madeira’s first star?

Luís Pestana is the Madeiran chef who led William to its first Michelin star and renewed it in 2022. The Michelin Guide credits him with William’s gastronomic refinement. Belmond says he won the star about 16 months after the restaurant opened.

Pestana spent his career at Reid’s. His bio for the Fine Wines & Food Fair says he started cooking with his family, trained from age 16 and finished top of his class. He rose to sous-chef, then executive chef of Belmond Reid’s Palace, overseeing William. Along the way he did internships, including one with three-star chef Marc Meneau.

That same bio calls him “the first and only Madeiran chef to achieve a Michelin star”. Read that line with care. It’s a self-reported, undated event bio, and the star is now held by Costa, who is Madeiran too.

Time Out Madeira (5 April 2021) credits the star to Luís Pestana and Joachim Koerper together. I couldn’t confirm Koerper’s exact role at William or how long he was involved.

What does Luís Pestana do now?

I don’t know, and I won’t guess. His LinkedIn headline still reads “Chefe executivo – Reid’s Palace”, but the Michelin Guide and Belmond both name Costa as executive chef. No source I found explains when or why Pestana left the role, or what he does today.

William Reid’s Palace Michelin star: the timeline

William has held one Michelin star since 2016/2017, kept it under two chefs and still holds it in the current guide. Here is the timeline in one place, because most pages online get part of it wrong.

Why 2016 or 2017? Most likely because the star appeared in the Michelin Guide Spain & Portugal 2017, which was announced in late 2016. I haven’t checked the 2017 results list myself, so I’m giving both years rather than picking one.

What’s on the menu at William?

William serves two tasting menus, “William” and “Discovery”, built on Madeiran and Atlantic produce. The Michelin Guide mentions Atlantic fish with celeriac and fig, lamb with mushrooms and sweet potato, and São Tomé chocolate. Belmond’s menu page adds Seixal trout, lobster bisque with seaweed, and produce from the hotel’s own garden. Sommelier Alberto Luz runs the wine list.

How much does the tasting menu at William cost?

I couldn’t verify current tasting-menu prices. The Michelin Guide places William in its top €€€€ price band, but Belmond’s official menu PDF wouldn’t open when I checked, and no other source gave a dated price. Ask reservations before you book.

Is William still worth booking?

On the record, yes, if you want Madeira on a plate rather than a generic luxury dinner. I haven’t eaten there yet, so this is a judgement on the evidence, not on a meal. But the evidence is strong: the kitchen kept its star through a change of chef, and Costa’s 40-plus local producers and revived island ingredients give William something no other address in Funchal copies. Just don’t come looking for a burger. William doesn’t serve one.

Where to eat a burger at Reid’s Palace

The burger at Reid’s is on the Pool Terrace menu, not at William. The hotel runs several outlets: William, Brisa do Mar (chef Zélia Santos), the 1891 Bar, Ristorante Villa Cipriani, Gelateria San Giorgio, the Pool Terrace, Afternoon Tea and the Tea Library (Belmond).

Two details caught my eye. São Jorge is an Azorean cheese, sharp and buttery, an odd but logical choice on an Atlantic island. And a fried egg on top makes this a knife-and-fork burger. Don’t expect to eat it with one hand by the pool. One thing I won’t do is credit it to Costa: no source ties the Pool Terrace burger to him or to William’s brigade.

How the €28 Wagyu burger compares

At €28, the Reid’s Wagyu burger costs about five times a Portuguese Big Mac. Our Big Mac index 2026 puts the Big Mac at €5.65 in Portugal. Our guide to McDonald’s in Funchal, with locations, hours and prices quotes a Numbeo estimate of about €12 per person for a full McDonald’s meal in the city.

Best burger in Funchal beyond Reid’s

For a casual burger in Funchal, the city’s independent burger joints and its McDonald’s sit at the opposite end of the price scale from Reid’s. Aggregator lists (Restaurantji, Wanderlog, Foursquare) keep naming the same four places:

  • Castelo dos Hamburguers, Rua da Carreira 304
  • Hamburgueria do Mercado, Rua da Carreira 75A, founded in 2020
  • Deli Burger D’mar
  • The Red Car, Estrada Monumental

I haven’t verified their prices, hours or rankings, so this is a starting list, not a recommendation. For the addresses we have checked, see our Funchal burger restaurants hub and the full Restaurants section.

FAQ

Who is the chef at William restaurant in Funchal today?

José Diogo Costa, a Madeiran chef who took over William in 2023. He is also executive chef of Reid’s Palace and won the 2025 MICHELIN Young Chef Award.

Does William at Reid’s Palace still have a Michelin star?

Yes. The Michelin Guide lists William with one star, “Contemporary” cuisine and a €€€€ price band (checked 20 November 2026).

When did William earn its Michelin star?

In 2016 or 2017, depending on the source. Belmond and the Michelin Guide’s French page say 2016; Reporter Gourmet says 2017. The likely explanation is the Michelin Guide Spain & Portugal 2017, announced in late 2016.

Who was Luís Pestana?

The Madeiran executive chef of Reid’s Palace who led William to its first star and renewed it in 2022. He was an employee, never the owner or founder. His current role is unconfirmed.

What are William’s opening hours?

Tuesday to Saturday, dinner only; closed Sunday and Monday. Published service times conflict (19:00–21:30, 19:30–22:00 or 19:00–23:00), so confirm when you book on +351 291 717 030 or reservations.rds@belmond.com.

How much does the tasting menu at William cost?

No current price could be verified. Michelin rates it in its highest €€€€ band; ask reservations for the price.

Does Reid’s Palace serve burgers?
