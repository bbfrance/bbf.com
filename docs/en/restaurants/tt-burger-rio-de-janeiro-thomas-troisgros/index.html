T.T. Burger Rio de Janeiro by Thomas Troisgros

Thomas Troisgros's T.T. Burger opened in Arpoador in 2013. The signature burger, Rio locations, the closure of Olympe and Oseille's Michelin star.

By Lucas, Restaurants writer, BrazilPublished November 19, 2026

T.T. Burger is the burger chain chef Thomas Troisgros started in Arpoador, Rio de Janeiro, in 2013. Olympe, the name many people search alongside his, was never a burger place: it was his father Claude Troisgros’s Michelin-starred restaurant, and it closed in 2020, according to NeoFeed. Today Thomas runs T.T. Burger, its sister brands and Oseille, a 16-seat counter in Ipanema with 1 Michelin star. For a burger in Rio with a starred chef behind the menu, T.T. Burger is the name to start with.

I checked every fact below on 6 November 2026 against T.T. Burger’s official site, the MICHELIN Guide and the Brazilian food and business press. Where sources disagree, I say so. Where I could not confirm something, I left it out, which is why this piece has no street address for the Arpoador branch.

Who is Thomas Troisgros?

Thomas Troisgros is a Rio-born chef and the fourth generation of the Troisgros chef family: son of Claude Troisgros and grandson of Pierre Troisgros, according to Prazeres da Mesa and Time Out Rio.

Pierre Troisgros, Thomas Troisgros’s grandfather, photographed in 2006. His 1963 salmon in sorrel sauce gave Thomas’s Michelin-starred Ipanema counter, Oseille, its name. (Photo: Arnaud 25, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pierre\_Troisgros.jpg)

Food lovers in Europe know the name from the family restaurant in Roanne. Claude took it to Brazil, and Thomas grew up in his kitchens. His Prazeres da Mesa biography says he trained at the Culinary Institute of America, then worked with Daniel Boulud in New York and at Mugaritz and Arzak in San Sebastián. I found no second source for that training, so read it as his published bio.

What became of Olympe in Rio de Janeiro?

Olympe is closed. It held 1 Michelin star and shut after the pandemic: NeoFeed (23 July 2022) says Thomas closed it in 2020 to focus on growing the group, and Brazil Journal (7 April 2024) says Claude and Thomas chose to focus on the group’s other brands.

Olympe was Claude Troisgros’s restaurant at Rua Custódio Serrão 62, on the Lagoa and Jardim Botânico side of the city, and the flagship of his group. Thomas ran its kitchen for about 10 years before opening Oseille, according to Time Out Rio and NeoFeed. Old TripAdvisor pages and directory listings still show Olympe as open. They are wrong.

Sources disagree on Olympe’s founding year (1983 or 1984) and on how long it stood on Custódio Serrão, so I don’t give either figure here.

Olympe vs Madame Olympe

Madame Olympe is a new restaurant, not a reopening. Claude Troisgros opened it in August 2025 at Rua Conde de Bernadotte 26, Leblon, with chef Jéssica Trindade in the kitchen, according to Time Out Rio and Brazil Journal. The tasting menu costs R$ 480 for 8 courses or R$ 380 for 4. Neither source names Thomas as part of it.

T.T. Burger: the Troisgros burger in Rio

T.T. Burger opened in 2013 in Arpoador as Reserva T.T. Burger, founded by the Meisler and Troisgros families with Thomas Troisgros as chef, according to the official site and Diário do Rio.

NeoFeed reported that the project started with an investment of R$ 600,000, was created for the Arpoador location of the Reserva fashion group, and sold 900 burgers a day in its early period. André Meisler, a former BTG banker and Reserva board member, joined as a partner in 2016. NeoFeed is the single source for these figures.

No source I checked explains what ‘T.T.’ stands for. Thomas Troisgros’s initials are the obvious guess, but it remains a guess.

The signature T.T. Burger and the guava ketchup

The signature T.T. Burger is a 180 g Angus-blend patty with meia-cura (semi-aged) cheese, organic lettuce and tomato, sweet-and-sour red onion pickles and the house sauce, per the official site and Diário do Rio.

The brand builds its ‘Brazilian burger’ identity on 3 ingredients: a sweet potato bun, guava ketchup made from goiabada (Brazil’s firm guava paste) and domestic beef. That is how T.T. Burger presents itself. I could not confirm that the sweet potato bun and guava ketchup come on every standard burger today, so check the menu at your branch.

A slice of goiabada, Brazil’s firm guava paste and the base of T.T. Burger’s guava ketchup. Illustrative photo, not taken at T.T. Burger. (Photo: Voton, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Goiabada\_slice.jpg)

I could not confirm a current menu price for the signature burger either. NeoFeed’s ‘about R$ 90’ figure from 2022 is an average check per customer, not the price of one burger.

How the recipe changed since 2013

The burger has changed since launch. According to Prazeres da Mesa, the 2013 version had 200 g of beef, semi-aged cheese, tomato, romaine, crunchy chayote pickles and a sweet potato bun made in the Complexo do Alemão community. The official site now describes the chayote pickles as a former ingredient, and the patty is 180 g today.

My read: losing 20 g of beef and swapping chayote, a very Brazilian vegetable, for red onion has made the burger more conventional. Its Brazilian character now rests on the bun and the guava ketchup. If your branch serves both, order them; without them, you are paying for a well-made but ordinary Angus burger.

A past limited edition: X T.êT.udo

For its 13th anniversary, T.T. Burger sold the X T.êT.udo: a 180 g Angus blend with egg, crispy bacon, shoestring potatoes, onion, lettuce and tomato, at R$ 62.90 or R$ 72.90 as a combo. The offer ran until 20 September 2026 and has ended.

Put that price next to Oseille’s R$ 490 5-course menu and the burger cost about an eighth as much. That gap is the whole case for T.T. Burger: it is the cheapest way to eat a Thomas Troisgros menu in Rio.

Where to eat T.T. Burger in Rio de Janeiro

In August 2026 Diário do Rio listed T.T. Burger restaurants in Arpoador, Barra da Tijuca, Botafogo (recently reopened in a new format), Leblon, Macaé, Recreio dos Bandeirantes, Tijuca and Vogue Square Barra. The official site also lists Niterói, across the bay.

Arpoador is the original location. I have not given a street address or opening hours because I could not confirm them on an official source. Check ttburger.com.br before you go.

For the wider picture, start with our Rio de Janeiro burger hub. Rio’s burger history did not start with chefs: Bob’s, Brazil’s first fast-food burger chain, was founded in Copacabana in 1952.

T.T. Burger outside Rio

T.T. Burger also operates in São Paulo (Itaim Bibi and Pinheiros), Campinas, Brasília, Belo Horizonte, Vila da Serra (in Nova Lima, next to Belo Horizonte), Goiânia, Curitiba, Porto Alegre and Vitória, according to the official site.

The Campinas restaurant in Cambuí opened on 10 January 2026, per Campinas.com.br. Curitiba runs in partnership with chef Manu Buffara. Porto Alegre opened in 2024, per Jornal do Comércio. Sources give different total counts (13, 14, 16 or 17), so the safest figure is more than a dozen restaurants.

T.T. Burger vs Madero

Both are Brazilian premium burger brands, but T.T. Burger is built around a chef from a famous restaurant family, while Madero, the national premium-burger chain, grew as a large chain under its own brand; I list its branches in Madero’s Rio de Janeiro locations and hours. T.T. Burger sells its chef’s name and its Brazilian ingredients. It even has its own cookbook, T.T. Burger: Hambúrguer de Verdade, published in 2024 according to Time Out Rio.

The business behind the burger

T.T. Burger is a sizable business, not a side project. Time Out Rio reported in May 2025 about R$ 1 million in monthly sales, about R$ 800,000 of it from digital orders, with 6 units in Rio state and 8 outside it.

T.T. Burger’s monthly sales in May 2025: about R$ 1 million, roughly R$ 800,000 (80%) of it from digital orders (Time Out Rio). At the time the chain had 6 restaurants in Rio de Janeiro state and 8 outside it. (Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0; data: Time Out Rio (14 May 2025); rendered with QuickChart, 1600×900)

Earlier, NeoFeed reported Grupo T.T. revenue of R$ 52 million in 2022, R$ 22 million of it from franchises. Thomas then ran 10 brands across 11 venues in the T.T., Azedinha and Oseille groups. Those are 2022 figures.

Grupo T.T. revenue in 2022: R$ 52 million, of which R$ 22 million came from franchises and the remaining R$ 30 million from other sources (NeoFeed, July 2022). (Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0; data: NeoFeed (23 July 2022); rendered with QuickChart, 1600×900)

The group’s sister brands, per the official site:

  • Três Gordos (2020): ultra smash burgers, a thinner, crisper style than the thick T.T. patty.
  • Tom Ticken (2021): chicken.
  • Marola: fish sandwiches.

The brand also runs MuT.T.irão, a volunteer social program launched in 2014. T.T. Burger says it has more than 5,000 volunteers. That figure is the brand’s own claim.

Oseille: Thomas Troisgros’s Michelin star

Yes, Thomas Troisgros has a Michelin star: his Ipanema restaurant Oseille holds one, according to the MICHELIN Guide.

Oseille is a 16-seat counter at Rua Joana Angélica 155, Ipanema, 22420-030, open Wednesday to Saturday, tel. +55 21 3854-1819. Its name honors Pierre Troisgros’s 1963 saumon à l’oseille, the salmon in sorrel sauce that made the family famous. Time Out Rio listed tasting menus at R$ 490 for 5 courses and R$ 650 for 7, wine not included, in May 2025. Prices may have changed since.

FAQ

Is Olympe in Rio de Janeiro still open?

No. Olympe closed after the pandemic; NeoFeed dates the closure to 2020. Listings that still show it open are outdated.

What is the difference between Olympe and Madame Olympe?

Olympe was Claude Troisgros’s Michelin-starred flagship on Rua Custódio Serrão, closed since 2020. Madame Olympe is a new tasting-menu restaurant he opened in Leblon in August 2025, with chef Jéssica Trindade.

Who founded T.T. Burger and what does T.T. stand for?

T.T. Burger was founded in 2013 by the Meisler and Troisgros families, with Thomas Troisgros as chef. No source I checked confirms what ‘T.T.’ stands for.

What is on the signature T.T. Burger and how much does it cost?

A 180 g Angus-blend patty, meia-cura cheese, organic lettuce and tomato, sweet-and-sour red onion pickles and house sauce. I could not confirm a current menu price; check the official site or delivery apps.

What is guava ketchup and why does T.T. Burger use it?

It is a ketchup made with goiabada, Brazil’s guava paste. T.T. Burger uses it, along with a sweet potato bun and domestic beef, to give its burger a Brazilian identity.

Where is the original T.T. Burger in Arpoador?

The first T.T. Burger opened in Arpoador in 2013, at the Reserva fashion group’s location. I could not confirm the exact current address and hours on an official source, so check ttburger.com.br before visiting.

Does Thomas Troisgros have a Michelin star?

Yes. His Ipanema counter Oseille holds 1 Michelin star. Olympe, where he cooked for about 10 years, also held 1 star before it closed.

How is Thomas Troisgros related to the Troisgros family of Roanne?

He is the fourth generation of the family: son of Claude Troisgros and grandson of Pierre Troisgros.

What is Três Gordos and how does it differ from T.T. Burger?

Três Gordos is Grupo T.T.’s ultra-smash-burger brand, launched in 2020. It serves thin smashed patties, while T.T. Burger serves a thicker 180 g Angus-blend patty.
