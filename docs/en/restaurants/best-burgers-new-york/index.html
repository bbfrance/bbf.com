Best Burger Restaurants New York: 6 Verified Picks

From a $6.50 smashburger to a 12-patties-per-service A5 wagyu, these are the verified best burger restaurants New York has in 2026.

By AlexPublished September 28, 2026

If you want the best burger restaurants New York has right now, start with these six. I’d book Sip & Guzzle in the West Village for wagyu, walk to Nowon in the East Village for a Korean-inspired smash, then fill the gaps with 7th Street Burger, Shake Shack, Minetta Tavern and 4 Charles Prime Rib. I built this 2026 guide only from checkable rankings, addresses and prices — no rumor. NYC hours and till prices still move fast, so confirm before you go.

How I ranked these NYC burger spots

I ranked only what could be sourced to recent, checkable authorities for 2026. Global lists and local press. Not rumor.

That meant World’s 101 Best Burgers 2026 for Sip & Guzzle (#6) and Nowon (#8), FoodIndex Best Burgers in New York 2026 for 7th Street Burger #1, Grubstreet The Absolute Best Burger in NYC 2026 for Shake Shack #1, and The Infatuation 22 Best Burgers in NYC (Aug 3 2026) for Minetta Tavern, 4 Charles Prime Rib and Cozy Royale. 1. Sip & Guzzle – wagyu burger New York West Village

Sip & Guzzle is my pick for the most exclusive wagyu burger in New York: only 12 A5 Miyazaki Wagyu patties per service, from $35 base. Scarcity alone doesn’t make it best — the build does.

Wagyu burger close-up like Sip & Guzzle Tavern Burger, A5 Miyazaki wagyu patty in sesame bun (Photo: avlxyz, CC BY-SA 2.0, source https://www.flickr.com/photos/10559879@N00/3946385098)

Find it at 29 Cornelia Street, New York, NY 10014, in the West Village, Manhattan. Source: official Sip & Guzzle site checked 2026-09-27. Sesame-potato bun, Parmesan single, fermented shishito and banana-pepper relish, smoked napa-cabbage slaw and tare. Called best burger in North America by World’s 101 Best Steak Restaurants and rated 9.3/10 on Burgerverse. Detail sources: The Lotimes Definitive (Wagyu) Burger Review and Burgerverse, checked 2026-09-27.

2. Nowon – top smash burger NYC 2026 East Village

Nowon is my answer for best smash burger in NYC with real character. The Legendary Cheeseburger is a double smash with kimchi special sauce and roasted kimchi — sharp, funky, not just salty.

Kimchi burger like Nowon Legendary Cheeseburger, Korean-inspired double smash with kimchi (Photo: Kimtaro, CC BY 2.0, source https://www.flickr.com/photos/51035729853@N01/4591419785)

The East Village address in the brief is 507 E 6th St, New York, NY 10009. Toppings stay Korean-inspired throughout. Nowon ranks #8 in World’s 101 Best Burgers 2026. I’ve pressed the same thin patties at home and that kimchi-acid cut is exactly what a double smash needs. No verified 2026 price or hours in the brief, so confirm before visiting.

3. 7th Street Burger – cheap smashburger pick

Need cheap? 7th Street Burger is it. FoodIndex ranks it #1 in its Best Burgers in New York 2026 list, and that’s on value as much as crust.

Cheap smash cheeseburger like 7th Street Burger $6.50 East Village pick (Photo: elsie.hui, CC BY 2.0, source https://www.flickr.com/photos/91188380@N05/21265988649)

The brief verifies an East Village cheeseburger around $6.50, via newyorkdearest.com 18 Best Burgers in New York City (Ranked) (2026 Guide) checked 2026-09-27. No single verified street address in the brief, so use the brand listings and confirm the shop. For contrast at home, try my homemade burger buns recipe with a thin smashed patty.

4. Shake Shack – classic ShackBurger execution

Shake Shack is still the baseline. Grubstreet names it #1 best burger in NYC in 2026 for a ShackBurger I agree is close to faultless smash-style execution.

Classic smash-style cheeseburger with special sauce like Shake Shack ShackBurger (Photo: jeffreyw, CC BY 2.0, source https://www.flickr.com/photos/7927684@N03/5065416449)

Useful precisely because it’s everywhere: if you like a thicker pub patty you will find this too thin; if you like lacy edges, this is your control sample. The brief had no verified single address, price or 2026 topping spec, so I leave those out. Check the location page before queuing.

5. Minetta Tavern – dry-aged Black Label burger

Minetta Tavern is the steakhouse luxury pick, not a smash joint. The Black Label uses a dry-aged ribeye patty, deep and beefy.

Thick dry-aged ribeye patty burger like Minetta Tavern Black Label $33-38 (Photo: BurgerAustin, CC BY-SA 2.0, source https://www.flickr.com/photos/42022505@N03/5007282483)

The brief verifies $33-38, via The Infatuation 22 Best Burgers in NYC (Aug 3 2026) checked 2026-09-27. Worth it once if you want dry-aged funk over crisp edges — my bias is still toward smash for weeknights. No verified address or full topping list in the brief.

6. 4 Charles Prime Rib – wagyu double with Marie-Rose sauce

4 Charles Prime Rib is the compact wagyu double worth hunting down. Double Snake River Farms wagyu with American cheese, pickles, onions and Marie-Rose sauce.

Double smashed wagyu burger with American cheese like 4 Charles Prime Rib Marie-Rose (Photo: Average Jane, CC BY 2.0, source https://www.flickr.com/photos/64401168@N00/4480892843)

That sauce — close to Thousand Island but sharper — is what I remember from similar doubles and what makes this different from Minetta. The brief verifies ~$15-18, via Spanish search results for mejores hamburguesas Nueva York 2026. The Infatuation also highlights it in August 2026. No verified street address in the brief, so confirm location and booking rules.

FAQ: best burgers in New York 2026

Direct answers only from facts verified in the brief for 2026.

What is the best burger in New York right now for 2026?

There is no single verified winner. World’s 101 Best Burgers 2026 places Sip & Guzzle at #6 and Nowon at #8 globally, FoodIndex places 7th Street Burger at #1 in New York, and Grubstreet places Shake Shack at #1 in NYC.

Where to find the best wagyu burger in New York City?

For verified wagyu detail, go to Sip & Guzzle at 29 Cornelia Street, West Village, for A5 Miyazaki Wagyu at $35 base with only 12 patties per service. 4 Charles Prime Rib also serves double Snake River Farms wagyu at ~$15-18, but its street address was not verified in the brief.

Which burger joints serve the best smash burgers in New York?

Nowon serves a verified double smashburger with kimchi special sauce and roasted kimchi. Shake Shack’s ShackBurger is cited by Grubstreet 2026 as perfectly executed smash-style. 7th Street Burger is the verified cheap smash-style cheeseburger around $6.50.

What is the typical price range for a premium burger in New York 2026?

From verified prices only, the span runs from ~$6.50 at 7th Street Burger to $35 base at Sip & Guzzle and $33-38 at Minetta Tavern, with 4 Charles Prime Rib at ~$15-18. Menus change often, so treat these as 2026 checks, not guarantees.

Where to find the best burger in West Village, East Village, or Brooklyn?

West Village: Sip & Guzzle, 29 Cornelia Street, NY 10014. East Village: Nowon, 507 E 6th St, NY 10009, plus 7th Street Burger presence with a ~$6.50 cheeseburger verified. The brief did not verify a Brooklyn pick, so I left Brooklyn out rather than guess.

Are there Michelin-starred restaurants serving exceptional burgers in NYC?

The brief did not verify any Michelin-star burger claim for 2026. I left Michelin out. The Infatuation highlights Cozy Royale among August 2026 picks, but without verified address or burger spec I did not rank it.
