Burger King Bandung: Locations, Opening Hours, Menu and Reviews

Four Burger King restaurants are open in Bandung in October 2026. The 24-hour BIP branch on Jl. Merdeka is the best bet; the PVJ branch has closed.

By AlexPublished October 7, 2026

As of 7 October 2026, Burger King Indonesia’s official site (bkdelivery.co.id) lists four Burger King restaurants in Bandung: Bandung Indah Plaza (BIP), Buah Batu, Cihampelas Walk and Paskal 23. A fifth sits just over the city line in Cimahi. If you only go to one, make it Burger King Bandung Indah Plaza on Jl. Merdeka: it is listed as open 24 hours and holds a Google rating of 4.7/5 from 5,398 reviews. And ignore the Paris Van Java (PVJ) branch that still turns up in search results. It has closed for good.

I have not eaten at these branches, and I won’t pretend otherwise. This guide is built from Burger King Indonesia’s official restaurant list, Indonesian press, Wikipedia, and review data from Google (via Wanderlog) and TripAdvisor, all checked on 7 October 2026. Where no official source confirms a detail, I say so rather than guess. That happens a lot here: Burger King Indonesia publishes addresses, and almost nothing else, branch by branch.

Burger King’s official list for Bandung has four restaurants, three of them inside malls: Bandung Indah Plaza, Buah Batu, Cihampelas Walk and Paskal 23. Here is each one with its official address and what I could confirm.

Burger King Bandung Indah Plaza (BIP): the 24-hour branch

Burger King BIP is the branch to pick after dark. Wanderlog lists it as open 24 hours a day, every day, on the ground floor of the Bandung Indah Plaza mall at Jl. Merdeka 56. It is also the most-reviewed Burger King in Bandung by a wide margin: 4.7/5 on Google from 5,398 reviews. Both the rating and the 24-hour listing come via Wanderlog, not from Burger King itself, so confirm them on Google Maps on the day.

Burger King Cihampelas Walk (Ciwalk)

Burger King Ciwalk sits on the ground floor of the Cihampelas Walk mall at Jl. Cihampelas No. 160, in the Cipaganti area of Coblong. It opened in 2017, which makes it, with Paskal 23, the oldest Burger King still trading in Bandung. TripAdvisor lists the mall’s hours as 10 am to 11 pm every day (TripAdvisor). The restaurant’s own hours are not confirmed.

Cihampelas Walk mall on Jl. Cihampelas No. 160, where Burger King Ciwalk has traded on the ground floor since 2017; the mall opens 10 am–11 pm (Photo: Vebra (Panoramio), Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0)

Burger King Paskal 23

Burger King Paskal 23 is on level 3 of the Paskal 23 mall at Jl. Pasir Kaliki 25-27, in Kebon Jeruk, Andir. Like Ciwalk, it opened in 2017. The branch runs its own Instagram account (@burgerkingpaskal23_bandung), the most useful place I found for local news. One aggregator gives 10 am to 10 pm daily; nothing official backs that up.

Paskal 23 mall on Jl. Pasir Kaliki 25-27 in Andir, Bandung: Burger King Paskal 23 is on level 3 (Photo: Baqotun0023, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Burger King Buah Batu

Burger King Buah Batu is the odd one out: the only official Bandung branch outside a mall, on a standalone site at Jl. Buah Batu No. 165 in Turangga, Lengkong. No official source confirms its hours or whether it has a drive-thru. Check Google Maps or call the national line before you drive over.

Burger King Bandung opening hours

Burger King Bandung Indah Plaza is the only Bandung branch listed as open 24 hours. For the other three, no official source gives day-by-day hours. Mall branches usually follow mall hours, but I could not confirm that branch by branch. Late at night, BIP is the only safe bet.

  • BIP: 24 hours, every day (Wanderlog, checked 7 October 2026).
  • Cihampelas Walk: the mall opens 10 am–11 pm daily. The restaurant’s hours are not confirmed.
  • Paskal 23: 10 am–10 pm daily according to one aggregator. Not confirmed.
  • Buah Batu: not confirmed.

If you search in Indonesian, “jam buka Burger King BIP” and “Burger King 24 jam Bandung” both lead to the BIP branch.

Burger King Bandung menu and prices in IDR

The Bandung menu is Burger King Indonesia’s national menu. Online it is grouped into What’s New, Burgers, Chicken, Snacks, King Deals, Desserts, Beverages, King Jr and a dedicated Cheese Whopper range (bkdelivery.co.id, checked 7 October 2026). The Whopper is the anchor: a flame-grilled beef patty in a sesame bun. For exactly what goes into one, read Clara’s breakdown of Whopper ingredients and a copycat recipe.

Vegetarian options

No vegetarian or plant-based burger is confirmed on Burger King Indonesia’s current menu, and reviewers at the old PVJ branch complained about the gap. Check the online menu before you go. Fries, desserts and drinks are the safe meat-free choices.

Burger King Bandung reviews: what customers say

Burger King BIP has the best review record in Bandung: 4.7/5 on Google from 5,398 reviews. Its TripAdvisor score is lower, at 3.3/5, but that rests on just 3 reviews. I could not find verifiable ratings for the other three branches.

What BIP reviewers praise:

  • Plenty of seating, from sofas to high chairs
  • A large terrace with a smoking area
  • Several tills open at once, so the wait is short

What they criticise:

  • Air conditioning that is uneven inside
  • A noisy dining room at peak hours

Source: Wanderlog, Burger King Bandung Indah Plaza, checked 7 October 2026.

Older TripAdvisor reviews of the closed PVJ branch (4/5, 21 reviews) tell a similar story about the chain in the city. Reviewers liked the juicy flame-grilled beef, a “very big” Whopper and quick, friendly staff. They grumbled about weekend crowds and the lack of a vegetarian burger and onion rings (TripAdvisor). That is a trend, not a verdict on today’s branches.

My verdict: Burger King in Bandung is a dependable international fallback, not a reason to visit. Local editorial guides to the city’s fast food, such as What’s New Indonesia’s May 2025 list, leave Burger King out entirely and point to Five Monkeys, BMB Burger and Brother Jonn & Sons instead. I’d do the same. Go to BK for a Whopper you already know at 2 am; go local for anything with a sense of place. The Bandung burger guide covers the wider scene.

Delivery, ordering and contact

You can order Burger King delivery in Bandung through the official site bkdelivery.co.id or the national number 021-30030025, the only phone number I could confirm. The site also runs a loyalty programme called BK Member (bkdelivery.co.id, checked 7 October 2026).

No official source publishes branch phone numbers. The +62 22 2060800 number still shown on TripAdvisor belonged to the closed PVJ branch. Availability on GrabFood, GoFood and ShopeeFood, payment methods (QRIS or card), parking and wheelchair access are not confirmed for any Bandung branch.

Getting there from Jakarta by Whoosh

If you come from Jakarta on the Whoosh high-speed train, which has run since October 2023, the ride from Halim to Tegalluar takes about 40 minutes. A feeder train also links Padalarang to Bandung station. If you arrive late, BIP is the one branch listed as open 24 hours. I could not confirm walking distances or transfer times from either station to any Burger King, so plan the last leg with a ride-hailing app. For burgers at the other end of the line, see the Jakarta burger guide. Burger King in Indonesia and Bandung: a short history

Burger King first came to Indonesia in 1986 with a restaurant on Jl. MT Haryono in South Jakarta. The 1998 Asian financial crisis closed it. The chain returned on 26 April 2007 at Senayan City mall in Jakarta, run by Mitra Adiperkasa (MAP) through PT Sari Burger Indonesia. Expansion outside Jakarta began in Bali in December 2010, and Bandung followed: the city’s first Burger King opened at Paris Van Java mall in December 2011. That branch has since closed (Wikipedia Indonesia; CNBC Indonesia).

Burger King at Cibubur Square in greater Jakarta in November 2010, from the expansion years just before the chain reached Bandung (Photo: Nur Cholis (Panoramio), Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)

Burger King Indonesia now belongs to the Indian group Restaurant Brands Asia (RBA), formerly Burger King India. On 30 June 2026 it ran 137 Burger King and 25 Popeyes restaurants in Indonesia, after closing 42. No Indonesian openings are planned for the current financial year, and RBA’s management calls the subsidiary a turnaround (Multibagg, RBA Q1 FY27). Inspira Global has agreed to acquire control of RBA at 70 Indian rupees per share (exchange4media).

Timeline of Burger King in Indonesia: 1986 first Jakarta restaurant, 1998 exit, 2007 return at Senayan City, 2010 Bali, December 2011 Bandung PVJ, 2017 Paskal 23 and Ciwalk, June 2026 137 restaurants after 42 closures (Chart: Burger Breton, data from Wikipedia Indonesia (CC BY-SA 4.0), CNBC Indonesia and Multibagg (RBA Q1 FY27))

Burger King itself was founded in Miami in 1954. For how the chain fares in another large emerging-market city, see my guide to Burger King in Cairo.

FAQ: Burger King Bandung

How many Burger King restaurants are there in Bandung?

Burger King Indonesia officially lists four in Bandung: Bandung Indah Plaza, Buah Batu, Cihampelas Walk and Paskal 23. A fifth is in neighbouring Cimahi on Jl. Raya Cibabat. The list was checked on 7 October 2026.

Is Burger King Bandung Indah Plaza really open 24 hours?

Wanderlog lists Burger King BIP as open 24 hours every day as of 7 October 2026. That listing comes from a third-party aggregator, so confirm on Google Maps before a late-night visit.

How much is a Whopper in Bandung?

Third-party price lists put an à la carte Whopper between IDR 49,545 and IDR 63,636. The two sources disagree and neither is official, so check bkdelivery.co.id for the current price and whether tax is included.

Is Burger King in Indonesia halal?

I could not find a verified halal certification record for Burger King Indonesia from BPJPH or LPPOM MUI. If halal status matters to you, search the official register at bpjph.halal.go.id or ask the brand directly.

Can I get Burger King delivered in Bandung?

Yes. Burger King Indonesia takes delivery orders through bkdelivery.co.id and its national number, 021-30030025. Coverage on GrabFood, GoFood and ShopeeFood in Bandung is not confirmed.

Does Burger King Indonesia have vegetarian options?

No vegetarian burger is confirmed on the current Indonesian menu, and reviewers have noted that one is missing. Fries, desserts and drinks are the meat-free options.

Is the Burger King at Paris Van Java (PVJ) closed?

Yes. Burger King PVJ Mall on Jl. Sukajadi is listed as permanently closed. Its TripAdvisor page does not say so, which is why it still confuses visitors. The nearest open alternative on the city’s north side is Cihampelas Walk.

What Burger King items are unique to Indonesia?

The Cheese Whopper range, Gold Whopper Steakhouse BBQ, Quattro Whopper, Mozzarella Cheeseburger XL, Vanilla Latte Super Float, Choco Pie and Banana Chocolate Pie all appear on Indonesian menus and reviews.
