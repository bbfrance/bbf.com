Burger King Cairo: Locations, Menu and Tips

Burger King is present in Cairo, built around the flame-grilled Whopper, but check the official Egypt channels for current locations, menu and EGP prices.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished October 6, 2026

Looking for Burger King in Cairo? Yes — Burger King lists restaurants in Greater Cairo. Short answer done. The catch: I verified country presence on 2026-10-04, not each open store, so check the official Egypt finder and the live Google Maps listing for address, hours and EGP prices before you go.

Burger King Cairo locations: how to find an open restaurant

Skip rumour lists. Use the official Egypt finder, then Google Maps for live open status. That 2-step check is the only one I trust in Cairo.

I cross-checked corporate sources on 2026-10-04: American chain born in Miami in 1954 (Insta-Burger King 1953), now an independently operated subsidiary of Restaurant Brands International with more than 18,700 outlets in 100-plus countries. For Egypt, Burger King confirms restaurants in Cairo — mostly the big Greater Cairo malls — with ordering and delivery through official channels and local aggregators. I have no dated, store-by-store address, phone or per-day hours I can stand behind, so I am not listing any as confirmed.

Burger King Cairo menu: Whopper, chicken and sides

In Cairo expect flame-grilled beef led by the Whopper, plus chicken, fish and vegetarian options by market. The exact Cairo lineup lives only on the official Egypt menu.

The Whopper — created in 1957 by James McLamore and David Edgerton — is still the signature. I like it for 1 reason: flame-grill char and cool crunch from lettuce, tomato, onion and pickles. The Middle East and Egypt add halal products and chicken, fish and vegetarian options by market. I had no dated Cairo menu extract with sides, desserts, drinks or local exclusives, so no Cairo-specific item list here. Check https://burgerking.com.eg/ for the current menu and allergen and halal statements.

For what makes a Whopper a Whopper, I rebuilt it at home — double patties, sesame bun, dripping sauce — in my ultimate double Whopper copycat recipe. That test is why I say: order the Whopper plain if you want to taste the grill.
