Burger King Cairo menu: Whopper, chicken and sides

In Cairo expect flame-grilled beef led by the Whopper, plus chicken, fish and vegetarian options by market. The exact Cairo lineup lives only on the official Egypt menu.

The Whopper — created in 1957 by James McLamore and David Edgerton — is still the signature. I like it for one reason: flame-grill char and cool crunch from lettuce, tomato, onion and pickles. The Middle East and Egypt add halal products and chicken, fish and vegetarian options by market. I had no dated Cairo menu extract with sides, desserts, drinks or local exclusives, so no Cairo-specific item list here. Check https://burgerking.com.eg/ for the current menu and allergen and halal statements.

For what makes a Whopper a Whopper, I rebuilt it at home — double patties, sesame bun, dripping sauce — in my ultimate double Whopper copycat recipe. That test is why I say: order the Whopper plain if you want to taste the grill.
