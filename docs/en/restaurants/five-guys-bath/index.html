Five Guys Bath: Address, Hours, Menu and Reviews

Five Guys Bath at 8 Saw Close, by the Theatre Royal: opening hours to 11.30pm, menu, prices, parking and ratings, checked on 4 November 2026.

By Emma, Restaurants writer, United KingdomPublished November 24, 2026

Five Guys Bath is the city’s only Five Guys, at Unit 3, 8 Saw Close, Bath BA1 1EY, on the square in front of the Theatre Royal. 3 listings that agree give the same hours: 11am to 11pm from Sunday to Thursday, 11am to 11.30pm on Friday and Saturday. Food hygiene inspectors gave it the top rating of 5. Tripadvisor is harsher: 3.3/5 from 53 reviews. Five Guys UK publishes just 1 price, the kids’ meal from £8. My take: it’s the most practical late burger in Bath’s theatre quarter, but reviewers say quality swings from 1 visit to the next. I checked every fact below on 4 November 2026.

Where is Five Guys in Bath? Address and how to find Saw Close

Five Guys Bath is at Unit 3, 8 Saw Close, Bath BA1 1EY, on the square in front of the Theatre Royal. That’s the address on the official Five Guys Bath page, and Apple Maps and Trustpilot list the same one.

The Theatre Royal faces Saw Close (spelled Sawclose on some records), between Barton Street and Monmouth Street. Kingsmead Square is just round the corner. Searching for ‘Five Guys Saw Close’? Same restaurant: Bath has no other branch.

Map of central Bath with Five Guys at 8 Saw Close, the Theatre Royal next door, Kingsmead Square, the Charlotte Street car park by Queen Square and Bath Spa station, about a 10-minute walk away. (Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)

Why 2 postcodes: BA1 1EY or BA1 3EY?

Use BA1 1EY, the postcode on the official Five Guys page and on Apple Maps. The Food Standards Agency’s register lists the same restaurant as ‘Five Guys, 8 Sawclose, BA1 3EY’ (FSA ID 1199467). Both records point to the same site. If your sat nav struggles, search for the Theatre Royal Bath instead.

Is Five Guys Bath far from Bath Spa station?

No. The Theatre Royal Bath says it is about a 10-minute walk from Bath Spa station and the bus station, and Five Guys is on the same square. That makes it an easy stop on a day trip from London or Bristol.

Parking near Five Guys Bath

The closest car park is Charlotte Street, near Queen Square, about a 5-minute walk away. Kingsmead Square car park is reached via James Street West. Both tips come from the Theatre Royal’s getting-here page.

Five Guys Bath opening hours and opening times

Five Guys Bath opens at 11am every day. It closes at 11pm from Sunday to Thursday and at 11.30pm on Friday and Saturday. The official store page shows no hours, so these come from 3 listings that agree: Apple Maps, Welcome to Bath and opening-times.co.uk.

On bank holidays, or if you’re cutting it fine after a show, ring 01225 430107 first. 1 aggregator lists different hours and a different phone number. I left both out because no other source backs them up.

A burger near the Theatre Royal Bath, before or after the show

Five Guys shares Saw Close with the Theatre Royal Bath, which makes it the obvious pre-show or post-show burger. It stays open until 11pm on weeknights and 11.30pm at weekends, so you can usually still order once the curtain falls. On a busy show night, I’d place a Click & Collect order at the interval rather than join the queue afterwards.

Five Guys Bath menu: burgers, fries, shakes and toppings

The Five Guys Bath menu is the standard UK menu: burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches, breakfast, fries, vanilla milkshakes with mix-ins, Coca-Cola and Dr Pepper drinks, kids’ meals and free toppings, according to the official Five Guys UK menu. When I checked, an M&M’s milkshake was on as a limited edition.

The Bath page claims more than 250,000 possible topping combinations across burgers, hot dogs and sandwiches. It also says the fries are cut fresh on site every day, with no freezers. The ‘high-quality Scottish and Irish beef’ line is Five Guys’ own wording, repeated on Apple Maps. I couldn’t find it on the official menu.

Five Guys Bath prices

The only price Five Guys UK publishes is the kids’ meal, from £8, with a hamburger, hot dog or grilled cheese. Unofficial UK price lists disagree with each other, and prices vary between branches, so I’m not passing them off as Bath prices. For the exact bill, check the Five Guys app or the Bath listing on Deliveroo or Uber Eats before you go.

Vegetarian options and the peanut oil warning

Apple Maps lists vegetarian options at Five Guys Bath, and the UK menu includes a grilled cheese. If you have a peanut allergy, note that the UK menu says the fries are cooked in 100% refined peanut oil. Talk to staff before ordering.

Services: delivery, Click & Collect and payment

Five Guys Bath offers dine-in, takeaway, Click & Collect, outdoor seating and delivery through Just Eat, Deliveroo and Uber Eats. It takes cash, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Both lists come from the official store page, which also mentions the Five Guys Rewards loyalty programme.

Apple Maps describes the restaurant as wheelchair accessible and family friendly, and says it takes contactless payments. The official page says nothing about accessibility, so call ahead if step-free access matters to you.

What customers praise and criticise

The complaints are about execution, not the concept. Reviewers mention burnt bacon, fries that come out underdone or too salty, and patchy service. Some still single out the burgers for praise.

Food hygiene rating

Five Guys Bath holds the top food hygiene rating of 5, with ‘Very good’ for food handling, for cleanliness and condition of the premises, and for management of food safety. Bath and North East Somerset Council carried out the inspection on 19 May 2026 (FSA record).

Is Five Guys Bath worth it? My verdict

Yes, if what you want is a late burger by the theatre and you go in knowing the reviews call it inconsistent. The location, the late hours and the top hygiene score are real strengths. The 3.3 on Tripadvisor is not. For a chain that sells itself on fresh beef and fries cut on site, that’s middling. Treat it as a convenient post-show stop, not a destination meal. How I judged it: I read the official store page, the UK menu, the FSA register and the Tripadvisor and Trustpilot figures available on 4 November 2026. I haven’t eaten at this branch yet, so I’ve given no score.

The Five Guys story and how it came to Saw Close

Jerry and Janie Murrell and their sons founded Five Guys in Arlington, Virginia, in 1986. The chain now claims nearly 2,000 restaurants worldwide (Five Guys). Our history of the hamburger covers what came before.

Five Guys came to the UK on 4 July 2013, at 1-3 Long Acre. Our guide to the first UK Five Guys in Covent Garden tells that story. In January 2020, Five Guys took over the Saw Close unit first let to Byron, as part of its 2020 openings (AGG, 6 January 2020). I couldn’t confirm the month the Bath branch actually opened.

The unit is part of the Saw Close regeneration led by Deeley Freed. The scheme brought Bath’s first casino, a Z Hotel and 2 restaurants, Ask and Byron (Intergame). Today Five Guys Bath belongs to the chain’s South West region, which lists 14 branches, including Bristol, Bristol Clifton, Cheltenham, Exeter and Swindon Designer Outlet.

Where else to eat a burger in Bath

The best-documented alternative to Five Guys in Bath is Smashburger, at 9 Southgate Street, BA1 1AQ. When it opened on Thursday 25 May 2023, its 150 covers made it the chain’s largest UK restaurant (Bath Echo). For more options, see our page on burger restaurants in Bath.

Heading elsewhere in England? I’ve also covered Five Guys Oxford, Five Guys Cambridge and Five Guys Leeds. The Five Guys brand profile covers the chain’s history and figures.

Five Guys Bath FAQ

Where is Five Guys in Bath? At Unit 3, 8 Saw Close, Bath BA1 1EY, on the square in front of the Theatre Royal and about a 10-minute walk from Bath Spa station.

What are Five Guys Bath’s opening hours? 11am to 11pm from Sunday to Thursday and 11am to 11.30pm on Friday and Saturday, according to Apple Maps and 2 directories. The official page shows no hours, so call 01225 430107 to confirm.

How much does a meal at Five Guys Bath cost? The only official price is the kids’ meal, from £8. Check the Five Guys app or a delivery app for current Bath prices.

Does Five Guys Bath deliver? Yes, through Just Eat, Deliveroo and Uber Eats. Click & Collect and takeaway are also available.

Can you book a table at Five Guys Bath? The official page doesn’t list reservations among its services. Call 01225 430107 if you need to check for a group.

Is Five Guys Bath halal? I couldn’t verify the halal status of the Bath branch. Ask the restaurant directly before you order.

Are there vegetarian options at Five Guys? Yes. Apple Maps lists vegetarian options, and the UK menu includes a grilled cheese.

Do Five Guys fries contain peanuts? The UK menu states the fries are cooked in 100% refined peanut oil. Anyone with a peanut allergy should speak to staff first.

Where can I park near Five Guys Bath? Charlotte Street car park, near Queen Square, is about a 5-minute walk away. Kingsmead Square car park is reached via James Street West.

Is Five Guys Bath wheelchair accessible? Apple Maps says it is. The official page doesn’t say, so call ahead if you need step-free access.
