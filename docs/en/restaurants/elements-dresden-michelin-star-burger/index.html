Elements Dresden: Michelin Star and €19 Burger

Who runs Elements in Dresden, how the starred restaurant and its Bib Gourmand DELI differ, and whether the Boulevard Burger is worth ordering.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 24, 2026

Elements Dresden is a Michelin-starred restaurant run by chef Stephan Mießner, with his wife Martina Starovičová-Mießner front of house. It sits in a former turbine factory at Königsbrücker Straße 96, in Dresden-Neustadt. Under the same roof is the DELI, a casual Bib Gourmand dining room. Its Boulevard Burger cost €19 when I checked the menu on 12 November 2026. Elements is not a burger restaurant. But if you want a starred chef’s take on a burger, for a lot less than the €130 Chef’s Menu, the DELI is the place to order it.

Elements Dresden at a glance: address, hours and prices

Elements is at Königsbrücker Straße 96, Haus 25–26, 01099 Dresden. The fine-dining restaurant opens Wednesday to Saturday for dinner, and the DELI opens Tuesday to Saturday from noon. I checked the details below on the official site, the Michelin Guide and the Dresden tourism listing on 12 November 2026.

Who is the chef at Elements Dresden? Stephan Mießner

Stephan Mießner is the chef and co-owner of Elements. His wife, Martina Starovičová-Mießner, runs the front of house and looks after guests. The Michelin Guide names both as the couple behind the restaurant and the DELI.

Mießner cooked in various places in Europe and South Africa before he came back to Dresden, according to Dresden Magazin. None of the sources I could check names the kitchens he worked in, so I won’t guess.

The couple play the star down. They told Dresden Magazin it was never the goal: they “simply wanted to create a place to meet and enjoy good food.” Plenty of chefs say that. Here the floor plan backs it up, because the house is built around two rooms, and the cheaper one is open all day.

Elements’ Michelin star: how long and how it ranks in Dresden

Elements holds one Michelin star in the Michelin Guide Germany 2026, presented in Frankfurt on 23 June 2026. It has kept the star for more than a decade. It is one of three one-star restaurants in Dresden, not the only one, whatever some listing sites still say.

The other two are Genuss-Atelier, run by chef Marcus Blonkowski at Bautzner Straße 149, and Heiderand (Tageskarte, Michelin Guide Germany 2026). If you’re comparing Michelin star restaurants in Dresden, Elements stands out for two reasons. It’s the one with a casual all-day dining room on the same site, and it’s set in an industrial building.

The official site also shows a Gault&Millau rating of 16 points. The site doesn’t give an edition year, and I couldn’t confirm it on Gault&Millau’s own pages.

Elements vs the DELI: two venues under one roof

Elements is the evening fine-dining restaurant, and the DELI is the relaxed dining room and lounge that serves warm food from noon to 22:00. Both share a kitchen team led by Mießner and the same address.

  • Elements (restaurant): classic French cooking with a modern twist, a €130 four-course Chef’s Menu, dinner only.
  • DELI and Lounge: regional high cuisine with a cosmopolitan twist. In March 2025 the Michelin Guide listed starters at €6–15, mains at €16–26 and desserts at €8. The menu changes completely four times a year.
  • Shared: 100 indoor seats across the site, plus a beer garden and a terrace on the Zeitenströmung grounds.

The Michelin Guide made the DELI Germany’s “Bib des Monats” (Bib of the Month) on 3 March 2025. So one address holds a star for dinner and a Bib Gourmand for lunch. If you only have a few hours in Dresden, that’s the real reason to know about Elements.

The Boulevard Burger at the Elements DELI

The Boulevard Burger is the DELI’s burger. It’s a 180 g beef patty topped with pulled pork, BBQ sauce and red cabbage slaw (Rotkrautslaw), and it cost €19 on 12 November 2026. Both the current menu on the official site and the Michelin Guide’s March 2025 Bib article list it.

What makes it interesting is the double meat. Beef plus slow-cooked pork is a lot of burger, and red cabbage slaw is a very German way to cut the richness. Neither the menu nor the Michelin article says where the beef comes from or what breed it is, so I can’t tell you that.

Is it worth ordering? For €19 you’re eating from a kitchen with a Michelin star, at a fraction of the €130 menu price. That’s the case for it. But no source I found puts it among Dresden’s best burgers, and I wouldn’t cross town for it alone. My advice: book a weekday lunch at the DELI, order the burger, and treat it as the cheapest way into a starred kitchen. Don’t expect the city’s top burger. The menu changes every quarter, so check it’s still on before you go.

The setting: a turbine factory in Dresden-Neustadt

Elements sits in the Zeitenströmung complex in the north of Dresden-Neustadt. The dining room used to be the staff area of a former turbine factory. Architects Zander Architekten converted the complex, which was completed in 2008 (Wicona).

The Michelin Guide describes floor-to-ceiling arched windows, floorboards, a high open ceiling and industrial loft architecture. In summer there’s a terrace on the grounds. It doesn’t feel like a classic starred dining room, and that fits a couple who say they wanted a place to meet more than a place to collect awards.

How to get to Elements Dresden

To get to Elements, take tram 7 or 8 to the Heeresbäckerei stop on Königsbrücker Straße. If you drive, there’s free parking on site, according to the Dresden tourism listing.

Elements Dresden FAQ

Who is the chef at Elements in Dresden?

Stephan Mießner runs the kitchen and co-owns Elements with his wife, Martina Starovičová-Mießner, who runs the front of house.

Does Elements Dresden have a Michelin star, and since when?

Yes. Elements holds one Michelin star in the Michelin Guide Germany 2026 and has kept it for more than a decade.

How much does the tasting menu at Elements Dresden cost?

The Chef’s Menu costs €130 for four courses (official site, checked 12 November 2026).

What is the difference between Elements and the DELI?

Elements is the dinner-only fine-dining restaurant, with classic French cooking given a modern twist. The DELI is the casual Bib Gourmand dining room on the same site. It serves regional cooking with a cosmopolitan twist from noon, with dishes from €10 to €26.

Does Elements Dresden serve burgers?

The fine-dining restaurant doesn’t. The DELI serves the Boulevard Burger, with a 180 g beef patty, pulled pork, BBQ sauce and red cabbage slaw, for €19 (checked 12 November 2026).

How many Michelin-starred restaurants are there in Dresden?

There are three, all with one star: Elements, Genuss-Atelier and Heiderand (Michelin Guide Germany 2026).

Is Elements Dresden open for lunch?

The DELI is. It opens Tuesday to Saturday, 12:00–22:00, with warm food all day. The fine-dining restaurant opens only for dinner, Wednesday to Saturday, 18:00–22:00, according to the official site.

How do you get to Elements Dresden?

Take tram 7 or 8 to the Heeresbäckerei stop, near Königsbrücker Straße 96 in the Zeitenströmung complex. Free parking is available on site.

Do you need a reservation at Elements Dresden?

None of the sources I checked states a booking rule. For the €130 dinner menu, call +49 351 2721696 or use the official site before you go.
