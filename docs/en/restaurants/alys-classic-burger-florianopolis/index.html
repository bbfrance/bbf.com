Aly's Classic Burger Florianópolis: Alysson Müller's Bar

Chef Alysson Müller's burger bar in Florianópolis Centro: house-made cheese and buns, smash burgers, address, hours and R$60–80 per person.

By Lucas, Restaurants writer, BrazilPublished October 15, 2026

Aly’s Classic Burger is chef Alysson Müller’s burger bar in Florianópolis Centro, at R. Des. Arno Hoeschl, 279. It serves classic burgers, smash burgers and hot dogs. Almost everything is made on site: long-fermentation buns, sauces, bacon, caramelized onions, even the American cheese, from a recipe the official site credits to São Paulo chef Fábio Moon. Restaurant Guru puts the typical spend at R$60–80 (Brazilian reais) per person, and it rates 4.7/5 there from 653 Google reviews. My take: if you want a chef’s burger in downtown Floripa rather than a chain, start here.

Who is chef Alysson Müller?

Alysson Müller is a French-trained Santa Catarina chef. Floripa knows him as the ‘Rei do Polvo’ (King of Octopus), after his seafood restaurant Rosso. His group’s site says he was born in Biguaçu, on the mainland just north of the island, into a family of restaurant owners. He did apprenticeships in São Paulo kitchens, then opened Rosso in Santo Antônio de Lisboa in 2010 (Grupo Alysson Müller).

Grilled octopus, the dish behind chef Alysson Müller’s nickname ‘Rei do Polvo’ (King of Octopus) from his seafood restaurant Rosso, opened in 2010 in Santo Antônio de Lisboa (illustrative photo, not taken at Rosso) (Photo: pelican via Flickr (https://www.flickr.com/photos/85936780@N00/7770285890), CC BY-SA 2.0)

He has also appeared on TV food shows and on Rádio Itapema FM. That matters here. Aly’s isn’t an anonymous franchise: it’s the burger side project of a chef the city already trusts with seafood.

Alysson Müller’s restaurants in Florianópolis

Grupo Alysson Müller runs 5 venues in Florianópolis, and Aly’s Classic Burger is the burger one. The group lists them as follows (Grupo Alysson Müller):

  • Rosso: seafood, Santo Antônio de Lisboa, opened 2010
  • Artusi: Italian, Centro
  • El Señor: Iberian, Centro
  • Aly’s Classic Burger: burgers, Centro
  • Karabã: Asian street food, Mercadoteca

Largo da Alfândega in Florianópolis Centro, the downtown district where Aly’s Classic Burger and chef Alysson Müller’s Artusi and El Señor are located (Photo: Francisco Anzola via Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?curid=32183158), CC BY 2.0)

El Señor Asador de Brasa opened to the public on 6 October 2021 as the chef’s fifth restaurant. By then, Aly’s Classic Burger was already cooking on natural gas (Noticenter, 2021).

When did Aly’s Classic Burger open?

Aly’s Classic Burger opened during the COVID-19 pandemic, according to the chef’s group. Its Foursquare page dates from late November 2020. I found no primary source with an exact opening day. The operating company, Dop Gastronomia Ltda, was registered in 2017, but that is the company’s date, not the burger bar’s.

What makes Aly’s burgers different: house-made everything

At Aly’s Classic Burger, nearly every component is made in-house, from the long-fermentation buns to the bacon and the cheese. The official site lists breads, sauces, bacon, caramelized onions and cheese as house-made (alysburger.com.br). It also mentions ‘controlled fat ratios’ for the patties without publishing the blend. I won’t guess at cuts or percentages.

The house American cheese and its São Paulo roots

Aly’s makes its own American cheese from a recipe the official site credits to chef Fábio Moon of Fat Cow in São Paulo. The site calls it fresh and creamy, with no additives. To me it’s the most telling line on the menu. A burger bar that bothers to make its own American cheese is showing you where its priorities lie. It also ties Floripa’s burger scene to São Paulo’s; for that context, see our guide to the best burgers in São Paulo.

What to order at Aly’s Classic Burger

Order the Classic Cheese Burger first: a 160 g patty with house American cheese, pickles, mayonnaise and ketchup, served with fries. It’s the signature on the official menu. These are the items the site describes (alysburger.com.br):

Item What’s in it Order it if…
Classic Cheese Burger 160 g patty, house American cheese, pickles, mayo, ketchup, fries You want the house signature
Hot dog Caramelized onion, Berna bacon, American cheese, Dijon mustard You want the house bacon and onions in another format
Cheese French Fries 200 g fries, house cheese, ‘kryptonite’ mayo (maionese criptonita), house ketchup You want to taste the house cheese on its own

I couldn’t verify prices for individual items: the iFood and Cardápio do Chef listings wouldn’t load. That’s why I give the per-person range and no price per burger.

Is Aly’s the best burger in Florianópolis?

On paper, Aly’s Classic Burger is the strongest chef-driven contender for the best burger in Florianópolis. It makes almost everything in-house and holds a 4.7/5 Google rating from 653 reviews. It has won no awards that I could find, so I won’t call it award-winning. Methodology: I built this profile from the official site, the chef’s group site, local press, the company registry and aggregated review data, and checked every source on 14 October 2026. It is not based on a tasting visit, and no venue paid to be included.

A note on Black Sheep: a sushi bar, not a burger spot

Black Sheep in Florianópolis was never a burger restaurant. It was a Japanese fusion sushi bar. This profile started out as a piece on Black Sheep, and that idea didn’t survive the first round of checks. Chef Emerson Kim ran it at Av. das Rendeiras, 1956, Lagoa da Conceição. In 2012 it offered a R$65 tasting menu and à la carte dishes from R$7 to R$20, with no burger on the menu (Comideria, 2012). Lonely Planet later listed Black Sheep inside The Roof at the Majestic Palace Hotel, Av. Beira-Mar 2746, with a R$125 omakase (Lonely Planet). Foursquare lists the Lagoa sushi bar as closed. Kim left for Makoto in São Paulo about 6 years after arriving in Florianópolis, and later led Spicy Fish in Ipanema, Rio de Janeiro (CNN Brasil, 2022).

FAQ: Aly’s Classic Burger and burgers in Floripa

Short answers to the questions people ask most about Aly’s Classic Burger, its chef and burgers in Florianópolis.

Who is chef Alysson Müller, and which restaurants does he own in Florianópolis?

Alysson Müller is a French-trained chef from Biguaçu, known locally as the ‘Rei do Polvo’. His group runs Rosso, Artusi, El Señor, Aly’s Classic Burger and Karabã.

Where is Aly’s Classic Burger, and what are its opening hours?

It’s at R. Des. Arno Hoeschl, 279, Centro, Florianópolis. Official hours are Tuesday to Sunday 11:30am–4pm and 6pm–11:20pm, and Monday 6pm–11:20pm. Check @alysclassicburger on Instagram before a lunch visit.

How much does a burger cost at Aly’s Classic Burger?

Restaurant Guru puts the typical spend at R$60–80 per person. I couldn’t verify prices for individual burgers.

Does Aly’s Classic Burger make smash burgers?

Yes. The official site lists smash burgers next to its classic burgers and hot dogs.

What makes its American cheese special?

It’s made in-house from a recipe credited to Fábio Moon of Fat Cow in São Paulo. The site describes it as fresh and creamy, with no additives.

Does Aly’s Classic Burger deliver on iFood?

Aly’s Classic Burger shows up in iFood search results, but I couldn’t load the listing to confirm the delivery area or prices. Check the iFood app with your address.

Does Aly’s have a second location in Sambaqui?

Not that I could confirm. The operating company has a second registered address in Sambaqui, but I found no evidence of a burger outlet there. The Centro address is the only one I list.

Was Black Sheep in Florianópolis ever a burger restaurant?

No. Black Sheep was a Japanese fusion sushi bar run by chef Emerson Kim, first at Lagoa da Conceição and later inside The Roof at the Majestic Palace Hotel. Foursquare lists the Lagoa sushi bar as closed. I couldn’t confirm whether the Majestic Palace location still operates.
