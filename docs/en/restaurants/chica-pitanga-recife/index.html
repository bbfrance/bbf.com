Chica Pitanga Recife: Per-Kilo Lunch, No Burgers

Who really runs Chica Pitanga, what you eat there and where to go in Recife when you want a burger.

By Lucas, Restaurants writer, BrazilPublished November 26, 2026

Chica Pitanga is not a burger restaurant, and no chef named Claudia Lima works there. It is a family-run Brazilian-Portuguese restaurant at Rua Petrolina, 19, in Boa Viagem, Recife, founded in 1995: a per-kilo self-service buffet at lunch and an à la carte dinner signed by consulting chef Claudemir Barros. If it’s a burger you’re after, head to our Recife burger page instead.

How I built this profile: from the restaurant’s own channels, dated Pernambuco food press from 2022 to 2025 and travel guides, all checked on 12 November 2026. I have not eaten there for this piece, so there is no rating. Where sources disagree or are old, I say so.

Is Chica Pitanga a burger restaurant?

No burger appears on any Chica Pitanga menu reported between 2022 and 2025, at lunch or at dinner.

I read through the menus described by Folha de Pernambuco (2022 and 2023), Revista Mensch (2024), Rede Food Service (2024) and the Melhores Destinos guide. Not one burger. A search for “Chica Pitanga” with hambúrguer and burger turned up nothing either. My view: Chica Pitanga is worth a lunch if you are staying in Boa Viagem and want a tour of Pernambuco cooking on one plate. It has no place in a burger round-up, and anyone who put it there was guessing.

Who owns Chica Pitanga? The family behind the restaurant

Chica Pitanga was founded by Dona Conchita with the siblings Paula Aquino and João Amorim, and Filipe Aquino, co-founder and partner, runs it today.

Revista Mensch tells the family story: Dona Conchita, Paula and João “transformed Portuguese culture into the flavours of Pernambuco” (Revista Mensch, January 2024). Rede Food Service names Paula Aquino and João Amorim as the founding siblings and Filipe Aquino as a partner (Rede Food Service, February 2024). None of the sources I found gives Dona Conchita’s full name.

Receita da Boa calls Filipe Aquino co-founder and current operator; Revista Mensch calls him managing partner. How he is related to Paula Aquino or Dona Conchita, no source says, and I won’t guess. His own summary of the house approach: “O nosso diferencial sempre foi priorizar ingredientes de alta qualidade e estar atento ao que o cliente do restaurante demanda.” In English: “What sets us apart has always been putting high-quality ingredients first and paying attention to what our customers ask for.”

Is Claudia Lima the chef at Chica Pitanga?

No source links anyone named Claudia Lima to Chica Pitanga, as chef, owner or founder.

The people the sources do name are the founding family, Filipe Aquino and consulting chef Claudemir Barros. The nearest name in Recife’s food press is chef Claudia Luna, who ran the restaurant Seu Luna in Ipsep for 20 years before leaving it (Folha de Pernambuco). Different person, different restaurant, no connection to Chica Pitanga.

Claudemir Barros: the chef who signs the dinner menu

Claudemir Barros is the consulting chef behind Chica Pitanga’s à la carte dinner: Brazilian cooking with a Portuguese accent, built on local ingredients.

Barros was named Chef of the Year (“Chef do Ano”) at the 2012 Melhores do Ano de Pernambuco awards run by Prazeres da Mesa magazine (Receita da Boa, Revista Mensch). Before Chica Pitanga, he made his name at Wiella Bistrô and Oleiro. Local press ties him to “Nova Cozinha Nordestina”, a new northeastern cooking that pairs technique with regional produce (Folha de Pernambuco, 2023). Rede Food Service also credits him with a 2018 book, Sonhos e Sabores.

A caveat. The most recent dated source naming him at Chica Pitanga is from January 2025, and I could not confirm he still holds the role in November 2026.

What is a per-kilo restaurant? How lunch works at Chica Pitanga

A per-kilo restaurant (comida a quilo) is a Brazilian self-service buffet where you fill your plate, weigh it at the counter and pay for the weight.

Chica Pitanga runs lunch this way, with Portuguese-Pernambucan and regional dishes on the buffet (Folha de Pernambuco). Lonely Planet lists it as a “top choice” and calls it “as near as por kilo restaurants get to gourmet” (Lonely Planet). If you have never eaten a quilo:

  • Take a plate and serve yourself from the buffet.
  • Weigh the plate at the scale; staff note the weight.
  • Drinks and desserts usually go on the same bill.
  • Pay at the till on the way out.

Per-kilo prices do circulate online. I left them out: they come from an undated listing that still describes a per-kilo dinner the restaurant dropped years ago. The only dated figure is an average spend of about R$ 60 per person in February 2024 (Rede Food Service).

What to order at dinner at Chica Pitanga

Dinner is à la carte, and the press has reported Portuguese-leaning dishes built on northeastern ingredients such as queijo coalho, cashew and pitanga.

Dinner switched from buffet to à la carte after a renovation (Folha de Pernambuco). The dishes below appeared on menus reported from 2022 to 2024. Read them as a guide to the style, not as tonight’s menu:

With one dinner there, I would order the queijo coalho carpaccio and the cartola, and skip the cod. You can eat good bacalhau anywhere in Brazil. Those two dishes put Pernambuco at the centre of the plate. Cartola is the local dessert of fried banana under melted cheese; here it comes with cinnamon ice cream. And look for the stylized pitanga tree in the middle of the dining room: it gives the place its name (TripExpert).

Chica Pitanga awards: VEJA best self-service and Lonely Planet

Chica Pitanga has won VEJA Recife Comer & Beber’s best self-service award five times and holds a 2012 TripAdvisor Certificate of Excellence.

  • VEJA Recife Comer & Beber, “O Melhor Self-Service”: 2000-01, 2001-02, 2002-03, 2009 and 2012 (Revista Mensch, Receita da Boa).
  • TripAdvisor Certificate of Excellence: 2012 (Revista Mensch, Receita da Boa).
  • Lonely Planet: listed as a “top choice” in Recife.
  • Chef award: Claudemir Barros, Chef do Ano 2012, Prazeres da Mesa Melhores do Ano de Pernambuco.

Most of those awards are more than a decade old. They tell you the buffet built its reputation early. They don’t tell you how it eats today.

Is Chica Pitanga still open? Hours and status in 2026

I found no dated 2026 source confirming that Chica Pitanga is open, so check its Instagram or WhatsApp before you go.

In January 2025, Receita da Boa reported that the restaurant would reach 30 years of uninterrupted service in December 2025. I found no coverage of the anniversary itself. Hours are just as murky: one online listing gives dinner on Friday and Saturday, while Rede Food Service (2024) gives lunch every day and dinner Monday to Thursday only. I won’t publish hours I can’t confirm. Message the restaurant on WhatsApp at (81) 99125-7788 or check @chicapitangarecife before you plan a meal.

Where to eat burgers in Recife instead

For a burger in Recife, skip Chica Pitanga and start with our Recife burger page.

Our Recife burger page maps the neighbourhoods to explore (Boa Viagem, Recife Antigo, Graças, Espinheiro and Casa Forte) and the northeastern ingredients worth ordering, queijo coalho and carne de sol first. The scene is real. The Recife Burger Gourmet by Restaurant Week reached its fifth edition with 30 hamburguerias and restaurants from Greater Recife (Folha de Pernambuco). In Boa Viagem itself, Boi Bread Burguer, at Rua Tenente Domingos de Brito, 1145, made the top 10 of the 2023 Festival Recife Love Burger (Diario de Pernambuco). That result is from 2023, so check it is still open first.

I ran into the same mix-up in Ceará: my profile of Lia Quinderé and Sucré in Fortaleza explains why that patisserie is not a burger stop either. Travelling further in Brazil? See the best burgers in São Paulo, the Madero Fortaleza addresses, hours and prices, or all our burger restaurant guides.

It was founded by Dona Conchita with the siblings Paula Aquino and João Amorim. Filipe Aquino is co-founder and current operator. Consulting chef Claudemir Barros signs the dinner menu.

No. No source connects anyone named Claudia Lima to the restaurant. Chef Claudia Luna, formerly of Seu Luna in Ipsep, is a different person with no link to Chica Pitanga.
