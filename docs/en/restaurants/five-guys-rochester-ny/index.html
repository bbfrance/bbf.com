Five Guys Rochester NY: All 6 Locations, Prices

Where to find Five Guys in Greater Rochester, which location to pick, what a meal cost in 2024 and how it compares with Bill Gray's and Tom Wahl's.

By Olivia, Restaurants writer, Canada and the US NortheastPublished October 29, 2026

Five Guys has two restaurants with a Rochester, NY address: Henrietta Plaza at 1100 Jefferson Road and Brighton at 1881 Monroe Ave. Four more sit in the suburbs, in Greece, Webster, Penfield and Victor. Go to Henrietta Plaza. It is the best-rated of the three locations reviewed on Restaurantji (4.3/5 from 303 reviews), and the only one whose official page lists outdoor seating. In March 2024, a burger cost $7.19 to $11.29 in the Rochester area, according to the Democrat and Chronicle.

First, check that you have the right Rochester. Searches for “Five Guys Rochester” also bring up Rochester, Minnesota (2800 41st St. NW) and Rochester Hills, Michigan (969 S. Rochester Rd.). This guide covers Rochester, New York, and its suburbs only.

Five Guys Henrietta Plaza (Jefferson Road): the best pick

If I had one Five Guys stop in Rochester, it would be Henrietta Plaza at 1100 Jefferson Road. It has the highest rating, outdoor seating and the easiest highway access.

The restaurant sits near the corner of Jefferson Road and East Henrietta Road, in south Rochester. The listing site storeopeninghours puts it about a minute from I-390 (the Genesee Expressway). The official page lists takeout, dine-in, delivery, outdoor seating, lunch and dinner. For anything else, call 585-272-1811.

Five Guys Henrietta reviews

Henrietta Plaza scores 4.3/5 on Restaurantji from 303 reviews: 4.4 for food, 4.4 for service, 4.3 for atmosphere. The split is wider than the average suggests. 62% of reviewers give five stars, yet 17% give one.

The praise is specific: food cooked to order, the fries, the staff, the strawberry milkshake. The orders reviewers name most are the bacon double cheeseburger with grilled jalapeños, Five Guys Style fries, the bacon cheeseburger with Cajun fries and the Little Bacon Cheeseburger. Restaurantji also lists vegetarian options, a kid-friendly setting and dogs allowed on the patio. Yelp shows 79 reviews, but its rating did not load for me.

Five Guys Brighton (Monroe Ave)

Five Guys Brighton, at 1881 Monroe Ave., Rochester, NY 14618, is the weaker of the two city locations: 3.7/5 on Restaurantji against 4.3 for Henrietta.

The official page lists takeout, dine-in, delivery, lunch and dinner, but no outdoor seating. The phone number is 585-385-4778. One quirk when you order online: the ordering link for this store uses the slug “pittsford”. You have not picked the wrong restaurant.

Five Guys Brighton reviews

Brighton’s 3.7/5 on Restaurantji comes from 173 reviews (food 4.1, service 4.1, atmosphere 3.8). 54% of reviewers give five stars, but 16% give two and 14% give one. That is nearly a third of reviewers leaving unhappy. Search snippets show 2.7/5 from 19 reviews on Yelp and 4.0/5 from 188 ratings on TripAdvisor; neither page would open for me to confirm.

Fans mention fresh fries, the Oreo milkshake, clean tables and staff who take dietary restrictions seriously. The most-cited orders are the Little Bacon Cheeseburger, the cheeseburger with Cajun fries, the grilled cheese, the kosher-style hot dog and the bacon cheese dog.

Five Guys in the Rochester suburbs: Greece, Webster, Penfield, Victor

If you live north, east or southeast of the city, a suburban Five Guys is closer than either Rochester address. All four offer dine-in, curbside and delivery.

  • Greece (Ridgecrest Plaza): 1948 Ridge Road West, Greece, NY 14626. Rated 4.1/5 on Restaurantji from 288 reviews (food 4.1, atmosphere 4.0, service 4.0). Reviewers like the friendly staff, accurate orders and clean dining room; they complain about rising prices and occasional waits. The Veggie Sandwich and Cajun fries come up often.
  • Webster: 927 Holt Rd. Suite 200, Webster, NY 14580.
  • Penfield: 2067 Fairport Nine Mile Point Rd., Penfield, NY 14526. Rochester First reported that it is “tucked behind the Starbucks”, in a renovated 11,000-square-foot building it shares with Jersey Mike’s.
  • Victor: 405 Commerce Drive, Victor, NY 14564.

Five Guys Rochester hours

No official source confirms Five Guys Rochester hours, so call before a late visit.

The official store pages load their hours with JavaScript, and the listing sites disagree. Restaurantji shows 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily for Henrietta and Brighton, and 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. for Greece. Storeopeninghours shows 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. for Henrietta. Treat all of these as rough guides. A call to Henrietta (585-272-1811) or Brighton (585-385-4778) settles it.

Five Guys Rochester menu

Five Guys in Rochester serves the standard US menu, with nothing exclusive to the area, according to the official store pages.

  • Burgers: fresh beef patties formed by hand, with unlimited free toppings. The chain claims more than 250,000 possible combinations.
  • Fries: boardwalk-style, cooked twice in 100% refined peanut oil, served Five Guys Style or Cajun.
  • Shakes: a vanilla base with mix-ins, more than 1,000 combinations by the chain’s count.
  • Hot dogs: all-beef, split and grilled.
  • Sandwiches: including a BLT.
  • Drinks: Coca-Cola and Dr Pepper products.

The official pages also say the restaurants have no freezers for the meat and give out free peanuts while you wait. Want to rebuild the order at home? My colleague Julia’s Five Guys cheeseburger copycat recipe and her Little Bacon Cheeseburger copycat cover the two burgers Rochester reviewers name most.

Those prices are more than two years old. Read them as a floor, not as today’s bill. Five Guys prices each location separately, and the ordering site would not load for me, so pick your store in the Five Guys app or on order.fiveguys.com to see the real total before you pay.

The Democrat and Chronicle published those prices after a Five Guys receipt went viral on X on March 1, 2024. It showed $24.10 for a $12.49 bacon cheeseburger, a $2.89 soda and $5.19 small fries, plus tax and tip. As WROC’s Rochester First pointed out, that receipt was not from Rochester. Here the same bacon cheeseburger was $1.20 cheaper. For prices in another market, see our guide to Five Guys in London’s Covent Garden.

Delivery, curbside and ordering

Every Five Guys in Greater Rochester offers delivery and curbside pickup. You can order in the Five Guys app or on order.fiveguys.com.

Delivery runs through DoorDash and Uber, according to the official store pages. I found nothing official on parking, RTS bus stops, wheelchair access or payment methods. If any of those matter, call the store.

Five Guys vs Bill Gray’s and Tom Wahl’s

Five Guys is the national chain here. Rochester’s own burger names are Bill Gray’s and Tom Wahl’s, both decades older and rooted in the region.

Chain Founded Where Restaurants Known for
Five Guys 1986 Arlington, Virginia 6 in Greater Rochester Customizable burgers, peanut-oil fries
Bill Gray’s 1938 Webster, NY 14 in the Rochester area Started as a hot dog stand
Tom Wahl’s March 19, 1955 Avon, NY 7 in 2026 Ground steak sandwiches, house root beer

Bill and Alberta Gray founded Bill Gray’s, which bought Tom Wahl’s in 1986, so the two local favorites now belong to the same company. Tom Wahl’s has locations in Brighton, Fairport and Greece. It also once owned the “Wahlburger” name, which it licensed to the Wahlberg family in 2011 (source: Wikipedia).

The original Tom Wahl’s drive-in in Avon, NY, about 20 miles south of Rochester. The chain opened on March 19, 1955, and has belonged to Bill Gray’s since 1986 (2576x1932). (Photo: DanielPenfield, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tom\_Wahl%27s\_original\_location.JPG)

My take: if you are in Rochester for one burger, make it a local one, a Tom Wahl’s ground steak sandwich with a root beer. Five Guys earns its place when you want the order you already know, built your way, or when a craving hits near Jefferson Road at 9 p.m.

Five Guys history and its place in Rochester

Jerry and Janie Murrell founded Five Guys in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia. The chain opened its 2,000th restaurant at Universal CityWalk in Orlando on September 25, 2026.

I could not find when the first Rochester Five Guys opened. Of the six, Penfield is the most recent I could trace, from Rochester First’s coverage of its opening. FAQ: Five Guys Rochester NY

How many Five Guys are there in Rochester, NY?

There are 2 Five Guys with a Rochester, NY address: Henrietta Plaza (1100 Jefferson Road) and Brighton (1881 Monroe Ave.). Greater Rochester adds 4 more in Greece, Webster, Penfield and Victor, for 6 in total.

Which Five Guys should I choose in Rochester: Henrietta or Brighton?

Choose Henrietta Plaza on Jefferson Road. It rates 4.3/5 on Restaurantji against 3.7/5 for Brighton, and it is the only Rochester location whose official page lists outdoor seating.

What are Five Guys Rochester’s opening hours?

No official source confirmed the hours on October 26, 2026. Listing sites show 10 a.m. or 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily, but they contradict each other, so call 585-272-1811 (Henrietta) or 585-385-4778 (Brighton) before you go.

How much does a meal at Five Guys Rochester cost?

In March 2024, a burger cost $7.19 to $11.29 in the Rochester area, fries $5.19 to $7.39 and a drink $2.49 to $3.49, according to the Democrat and Chronicle. Prices have probably risen since, so check the app for your store.

Does Five Guys deliver in Rochester?

Yes. All 6 Greater Rochester locations deliver through DoorDash and Uber, and offer curbside pickup and ordering in the Five Guys app or on order.fiveguys.com.

Does Five Guys Rochester have outdoor seating?

Henrietta Plaza does: its official page lists outdoor seating, and Restaurantji notes that dogs are allowed on the patio. The official pages for the other locations do not mention it.

Are there vegetarian options at Five Guys Rochester?

Yes. On Restaurantji, customers mention a Veggie Sandwich at Greece and a grilled cheese at Brighton, though I did not find those names on the official menu page. Restaurantji also lists vegetarian options for Henrietta.

Can I eat at Five Guys with a peanut allergy?

Be careful. Five Guys cooks its fries in 100% refined peanut oil and gives out free peanuts in its restaurants, according to its official pages. If you have a peanut allergy, talk to the staff before ordering.

Is Five Guys better than Bill Gray’s or Tom Wahl’s?

For customization, yes: Five Guys lets you build your burger exactly your way. For a Rochester meal, I would pick Tom Wahl’s (since 1955) or Bill Gray’s (since 1938), the local originals known for hot dogs, ground steak sandwiches and house root beer.

How do I find the right Five Guys Rochester: NY, MN or MI?

Check the ZIP code and street. Rochester, NY locations are at 1100 Jefferson Road (14623) and 1881 Monroe Ave. (14618); Rochester, Minnesota is at 2800 41st St. NW, and Rochester Hills, Michigan is at 969 S. Rochester Rd.
