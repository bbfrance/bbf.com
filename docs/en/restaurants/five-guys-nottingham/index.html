Five Guys Nottingham: Long Row vs Showcase

Nottingham has two Five Guys: Long Row on Old Market Square (halal beef) and Showcase by the cinema on Redfield Way. Addresses, hours, prices and reviews.

By Emma, Restaurants writer, United KingdomPublished November 25, 2026

Nottingham has two Five Guys. The city-centre one, Five Guys Burgers and Fries Nottingham, is at 35 Long Row, NG1 2DR, on Old Market Square (0115 671 3462). The other, Five Guys Nottingham Showcase, sits on Redfield Way, NG7 2UW, beside the Showcase Cinema de Lux in Lenton (0115 671 4410). My short answer: go to Long Row for halal beef, a central location and the best chance of a late meal. Go to Showcase if you are driving or seeing a film. Both hold the top 5/5 food hygiene rating and score above 4/5 on Google.

The two Nottingham branches share the same menu and services and differ on three points: halal status, location and parking. I built this table from the official Five Guys UK pages, the Food Standards Agency (FSA) and review aggregators, all checked on 17 November 2026.

Five Guys Long Row, Old Market Square: address and services

Five Guys Long Row is the city-centre branch, at 35 Long Row, Nottingham NG1 2DR, on the edge of Old Market Square. The official page gives its phone number as 0115 671 3462 (+44 115 671 3462). This is the branch I would send most visitors to: it is on the square everyone already knows, a short walk from the Council House and the main tram stops.

Long Row seen from Old Market Square in September 2026: the Five Guys lettering is partly hidden behind the trees on the left, a short walk from the Council House and Nottingham’s main tram stops. (Photo: Aethonatic, CC0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shops\_along\_Long\_Row\_-\_24\_September\_2026.jpg)

You can eat in, take away, order Click & Collect or get delivery through Just Eat, Deliveroo or Uber Eats, and there is outdoor seating. The restaurant takes cash, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

For families, Family Friendly UK lists high chairs, a kids’ menu and pushchair access. The toilet code is printed on your receipt. No bookings: you walk in and queue.

A small address puzzle. Five Guys lists the restaurant at 35 Long Row, the FSA hygiene record says 34 Long Row, and a 2024 licence application says 34–35 Long Row. It is the same restaurant; I use the official address.

Five Guys Nottingham Showcase, Redfield Way: address and services

Five Guys Nottingham Showcase is on Redfield Way, Nottingham NG7 2UW, next to the Showcase Cinema de Lux in Lenton. Its official phone number is 0115 671 4410 (+44 115 671 4410).

Five Guys Nottingham Showcase on Redfield Way, NG7 2UW, next to the Showcase Cinema de Lux car park, just off the A52 Clifton Boulevard in Lenton. (Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)

The services match Long Row: dine-in, takeaway, Click & Collect, delivery through Just Eat, Deliveroo and Uber Eats, and outdoor seating. It takes the same cards and phone payments.

The cinema next door has a free car park with Blue Badge spaces, according to Showcase Cinemas and Euan’s Guide. Reviewers say they leave the car there for Five Guys too. Neither Five Guys nor the cinema says the car park is meant for restaurant customers, though, so read the signs before you walk off.

Some directory sites list a different phone number for this branch. It does not match the official page: use 0115 671 4410.

Five Guys Nottingham opening hours: what is confirmed

Five Guys shows no opening hours in the text of its official pages for either Nottingham branch, so the Five Guys app or a phone call is the only reliable check. Third-party sites disagree with each other, and I could not confirm any of their figures.

For Long Row, Wanderlog and Family Friendly UK list roughly 10am to 1am Monday to Wednesday, 10am to 3am Thursday to Saturday, and 9am or 10am to 1am on Sunday. Tripaligner gives 10am to 12:30am on Tuesday. Some of these listings may mix delivery hours with dining-room hours.

The 3am figure has a paper trail. In November 2024, Five Guys JV Ltd applied to Nottingham City Council to vary the licence for 34–35 Long Row, asking to close and serve late-night refreshment until 3am every day. Comments were accepted until 18 December 2024. I found no public record of whether the change was granted.

For Showcase, Wanderlog lists 10am to midnight Monday to Saturday and 10am to 11:30pm on Sunday. An older Localmint listing gives 11am to 10:30pm Sunday to Thursday and 11am to 11:30pm on Friday and Saturday.

My advice for a late night in town: call Long Row on 0115 671 3462 or open the app before you walk over. It is the better bet for late food in Nottingham city centre, but I would not trust any closing time after midnight until the branch confirms it.

Is Five Guys Nottingham halal?

Five Guys Long Row serves halal beef, according to the official Five Guys UK halal list. Pork bacon is cooked on separate grills with separate utensils. The restaurant is not fully certified by the Halal Certification Organisation (HCO), and it sells Hawkstone beers, which some halal diners will want to know before they go.

Five Guys Nottingham Showcase does not appear on the official halal list. That leaves its status unconfirmed, which is not the same as non-halal. If halal matters to you, Long Row is the safe choice; otherwise ask the Showcase team directly.

Source: Five Guys UK halal information, checked on 17 November 2026.

Five Guys Nottingham menu

Both Nottingham branches serve the standard Five Guys UK menu: burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches, breakfast, fries, milkshakes and soft drinks. I found nothing sold only in Nottingham.

  • Burgers: the core of the menu, with free toppings. Five Guys says there are more than 250,000 possible combinations.
  • Hot dogs and sandwiches: including a BLT, a grilled cheese and a veggie sandwich.
  • Breakfast: listed on the UK menu.
  • Fries: boardwalk-style, plain or Cajun, cut fresh every day and cooked only in refined peanut oil.
  • Milkshakes: with mix-ins. Five Guys claims more than 1,000 combinations.
  • Drinks: Coca-Cola and Dr Pepper ranges. Reviewers at Showcase mention free refills.

When I checked, the UK limited editions were an M&M’S Shake and a coffee mix-in for shakes. Five Guys also says its restaurants have no freezers, and that staff often throw an extra scoop of fries into the bag.

Vegetarians get the veggie sandwich and the grilled cheese, plus fries and shakes. If you have a peanut allergy, take this one seriously: every portion of fries is cooked in peanut oil.

Source: Five Guys UK menu and the Long Row official page, checked on 17 November 2026.

Five Guys Nottingham prices

Five Guys UK publishes a single price on its official menu: the kids’ meal, from £8. Adult prices for the Nottingham branches are not published anywhere official.

The closest I got was a Nottingham Deliveroo listing, seen in search results on 17 November 2026: Cheeseburger £12.45, Bacon Cheeseburger £12.95, Little Cheeseburger £9.85. These are delivery prices and may be higher than at the counter. The Five Guys app shows the exact in-store price.

The best way to trim the bill comes from Showcase reviewers: share a large fries rather than ordering one each. The portions are generous before the extra scoop even goes in.

Five Guys Nottingham reviews

Both Nottingham branches do well on Google and only middling on Tripadvisor. Long Row has 4.2/5 from about 3,057 Google reviews and 3.5/5 from about 406 Tripadvisor reviews. Showcase has 4.3/5 from about 1,860 Google reviews and 3.5/5 from about 54 Tripadvisor reviews.

Five Guys Nottingham reviews compared: Long Row scores 4.2/5 and Showcase 4.3/5 on Google, and both score 3.5/5 on Tripadvisor (Wanderlog, 17 November 2026). (Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0, data from Wanderlog checked on 17 November 2026)

I have not rated either branch myself: these are customer scores, collected through Wanderlog and Family Friendly UK on 17 November 2026, and I read them for recurring themes rather than single comments. I give the Google figures more weight. Thousands of reviews say more than 54, and Showcase’s Tripadvisor score rests on very few.

Number of reviews behind each rating: about 3,057 Google and 406 Tripadvisor reviews for Long Row, compared with 1,860 and 54 for Showcase. Showcase’s Tripadvisor score rests on a small sample. (Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0, data from Wanderlog checked on 17 November 2026)

What customers say about Five Guys Long Row

Long Row reviewers keep coming back to the same strengths: consistent burgers, good beef, unlimited free toppings, quick and friendly service, and the milkshakes, with Oreo, Reese’s and Lotus Biscoff named most often. The complaints repeat too: a noisy ground floor, waits, bacon running out and cleanliness. Several reviewers say the upstairs floor is quieter. If you want a conversation with your burger, head up the stairs.

Trustpilot has only six reviews for Long Row, rated 3.4/5 in a search snippet. Too few to mean anything.

What customers say about Five Guys Showcase

Showcase reviewers mention fresh burgers, free toppings, service that holds up at busy times, clean tables, free refills, easy parking nearby and the outdoor seating. Their main tip is the one above: share a large fries.

Which Five Guys in Nottingham should you choose?

Choose Long Row for halal beef, a city-centre location and late-night food; choose Showcase for parking and a meal before or after a film.

  • Students and night owls: Long Row. It is on Old Market Square and is the branch most likely to stay open late, though hours after midnight are unconfirmed.
  • Halal diners: Long Row. It is the only Nottingham branch on the official halal list.
  • Drivers and cinema-goers: Showcase. The Showcase Cinema de Lux next door has a free car park.
  • Families: both work. Long Row has documented high chairs, a kids’ menu and pushchair access.
  • A quieter meal: Showcase gets fewer complaints about noise, and at Long Row reviewers recommend sitting upstairs.

History of Five Guys in Nottingham

Long Row came first, and Showcase followed. On 13 April 2017, Five Guys submitted plans for a new 2,500 sq ft building near the Showcase cinema, designed by AHR Architects and expected to create 40 jobs, according to TheBusinessDesk. Planning permission was granted on 14 June 2017.

The Showcase cinema off Clifton Boulevard, Nottingham, photographed in August 2008, before Five Guys built its restaurant beside it in 2017. (Photo: David Lally, CC BY-SA 2.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The\_Nottingham\_Showcase\_Cinema\_-\_geograph.org.uk\_-\_930973.jpg)

Neither opening date is confirmed. Long Row was already trading before April 2017, but I found no reliable date for its launch.

During the 2020 lockdown, Long Row stayed open for delivery and Click & Collect from 12pm to 10pm. The Showcase branch reopened for delivery and Click & Collect on Tuesday 19 May 2020, according to Nottinghamshire Live.

Long Row closed on Saturday 28 December 2024 after a ceiling collapse, which the management called a maintenance issue. Nottinghamshire Live reported that it was due to reopen on Friday 3 January 2025.

Five Guys itself was founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell and their sons. Its first restaurant outside North America opened in London on 4 July 2013, and the chain had 1,999 restaurants in 28 countries and territories in May 2026. Five Guys Nottingham FAQ

How many Five Guys are there in Nottingham?

There are two: Five Guys Burgers and Fries Nottingham at 35 Long Row, NG1 2DR, and Five Guys Nottingham Showcase on Redfield Way, NG7 2UW. They are among the eight restaurants in the official Five Guys East Midlands directory.

What are Five Guys Nottingham’s opening hours?

Five Guys does not show opening hours on its official pages for either Nottingham branch. Third-party sites list Long Row as open until 1am, or 3am Thursday to Saturday, and Showcase until about midnight, but these figures are unconfirmed. Check the Five Guys app or call the branch.

Is Five Guys Nottingham open late?

Long Row is the branch to try for late-night food. Five Guys applied in November 2024 to extend its licence to 3am every day, but I found no record that the application was granted. Call 0115 671 3462 to check.

Is Five Guys Nottingham halal?

Five Guys Long Row serves halal beef and cooks pork bacon on separate grills with separate utensils, but it is not fully HCO-certified and serves beer. Five Guys Showcase is not on the official halal list, so its status is unconfirmed.

Does Five Guys Nottingham deliver?

Yes. Both branches deliver through Just Eat, Deliveroo and Uber Eats, and both offer Click & Collect.

Can you park near Five Guys Nottingham?

At Showcase, the cinema next door has a free car park with Blue Badge spaces, which reviewers say they use. Long Row is on Old Market Square in the city centre, so use a city-centre car park or public transport.

Are there vegetarian options at Five Guys Nottingham?

Yes. The UK menu includes a veggie sandwich and a grilled cheese, plus fries and milkshakes.

Are Five Guys fries cooked in peanut oil?

Yes. Five Guys UK cooks all its fries in 100% refined peanut oil. People with a peanut allergy should take this into account before ordering.

Do you need to book at Five Guys Nottingham?

No. Five Guys does not take bookings: both branches are walk-in only.

What is Five Guys Nottingham’s hygiene rating?

Both branches have the top FSA food hygiene rating of 5/5. Long Row was inspected on 19 June 2025 and Showcase on 23 January 2026.

Does Five Guys Nottingham serve alcohol?

Long Row serves Hawkstone beers, according to the Five Guys UK halal information page. I found no information about alcohol at Showcase.
