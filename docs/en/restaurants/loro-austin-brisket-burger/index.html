Loro Austin: Franklin and Cole's Brisket Burger

Who founded Loro, how its brisket-jam cheeseburger ties it to Franklin Barbecue, and which of its three Austin burgers to order, with November 2026 prices.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 27, 2026

Loro Austin is a counter-service Asian smokehouse that pitmaster Aaron Franklin (Franklin Barbecue) and chef Tyson Cole (Uchi) opened on April 4, 2018, at 2115 S. Lamar Blvd. The burger to order first is the Loro Cheeseburger ($14.95): beef topped with red onion-brisket jam, muenster, lettuce and loro burger sauce. No other burger on the menu ties Franklin’s brisket this directly to Cole’s kitchen, and it is sold at both Austin locations, South Lamar and Domain NORTHSIDE.

I checked both Austin menus and location pages on November 15, 2026. Every price below is a pre-tax menu price in US dollars. Texas sales tax is added at the register.

Loro Austin at a glance

Loro is a Texas smokehouse that pairs oak-smoked and grilled meats with Southeast Asian and Japanese-inspired sauces and sides. You order at the bar. There are no reservations, and the patio is dog-friendly.

Who owns Loro: Aaron Franklin and Tyson Cole

Loro is a two-founder partnership, not Aaron Franklin’s own restaurant. Franklin co-founded it in 2018 with Tyson Cole, and Cole’s company, Hai Hospitality, the parent of Uchi, launches and operates the brand.

If you are planning a barbecue trip, that matters. Franklin’s own restaurant is Franklin Barbecue, at 900 E. 11th St. Loro’s official site still credits Franklin and Cole as its creators, but no public source documents a day-to-day role for Franklin in the Loro kitchen today. Think of him as co-founder and partner, not as the man running the pits.

Aaron Franklin, the pitmaster

Aaron Franklin is the Austin pitmaster behind Franklin Barbecue and the first pitmaster to win a James Beard chef award. He took Best Chef: Southwest in 2015.

Aaron and Stacy Franklin started Franklin Barbecue as a trailer in Austin in 2009 and moved into the brick-and-mortar building on East 11th Street in 2011. Today it opens Tuesday to Sunday from 11am until the meat sells out. Mondays, it is closed.

Franklin was inducted into the American Royal Barbecue Hall of Fame in 2020. He co-authored three books (Franklin Barbecue: A Meat-Smoking Manifesto, Franklin Steak and Franklin Smoke), co-created the PBS series BBQ with Franklin, which started in 2013, and teaches on MasterClass.

Aaron Franklin, the Franklin Barbecue pitmaster who co-founded Loro Austin with Tyson Cole, at the 2019 Texas Book Festival in Austin. (Photo: Larry D. Moore, CC BY 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aaron\_Franklin\_2019\_Texas\_Book\_Festival.jpg)

Tyson Cole, the chef behind Uchi

Tyson Cole is the Austin chef who opened Uchi in May 2003. He was named a Food & Wine Best New Chef in 2005 and won the James Beard Award for Best Chef: Southwest in 2011.

So Loro has an unusual founding pair: two Best Chef: Southwest winners, one from Texas barbecue, one from Japanese cooking. In November 2020, an affiliate of KSL Capital Partners invested in Hai Hospitality, the company that runs Loro, according to Nation’s Restaurant News.

Aaron Franklin’s restaurants and ventures

Franklin owns or co-owns three Austin restaurants: Franklin Barbecue, Loro (with Tyson Cole) and Uptown Sports Club, which Franklin Barbecue lists as a sister restaurant. His other listed ventures are Hot Luck Fest, co-founded with James Moody and Mike Thelin; Franklin Backyard, a private-events space at 2027 Anchor Lane; and Franklin Barbecue Pits, co-launched in 2020.

The Loro Cheeseburger: a brisket burger in Austin

The Loro Cheeseburger is a brisket burger: its main topping is a red onion-brisket jam, with muenster cheese, lettuce and loro burger sauce. It costs $14.95 at both Austin locations.

The jam is where Franklin’s craft reaches the plate. Brisket made his name. Here it becomes a topping instead of a side. Loro’s meats are smoked overnight, starting at midnight, and most proteins are finished over post oak on an open-fire grill, according to the restaurant’s Our Story page.

Critics agree it is rich. The Infatuation, in its Austin review dated April 7, 2025 (score 8.2), calls it “a cheeseburger topped with a mound of brisket. It’s heavy, rich, and decadent.” Back in the opening season, John T. Edge described it in Garden & Gun as “brisket-forward” with red onion jam.

What is in the Loro burger patty?

Loro’s official menu does not say what goes into the patty. The most detailed public description comes from D Magazine in February 2022, about the Dallas location: chuck and brisket trims, grilled over a Grillworks grill on post oak coals. I treat that as a 2022 Dallas description, not a confirmed 2026 Austin recipe.

About the sauce

The current official menu calls it “loro burger sauce.” Earlier coverage and third-party menus called it yuzu 1000 island. Yuzu 1000 island still appears by name on Loro’s other two Austin burgers. On the house cheeseburger, the menu no longer says so.

Loro Austin serves three dressed burgers in November 2026, and only the Loro Cheeseburger is on both menus. The Caramelized Onion-Cheddar is South Lamar only, and the Double Smash is Domain NORTHSIDE only.

My pick: the Loro Cheeseburger first. It is the only burger here that carries the restaurant’s brisket. If you want a second burger, choose by location. South Lamar has the Onion-Cheddar, with pickles and arugula to cut the richness. The Domain has the Double Smash, the closest thing to a classic Texas cheeseburger on a Loro menu.

If an older Austin burger list quotes the Loro burger at $14, trust the menu instead: it is $14.95 today.

Loro Asian Smokehouse menu: brisket beyond the burger

If you came for brisket, the Loro menu has three more ways to eat it. These are the South Lamar prices on November 15, 2026:

  • Oak-Smoked Brisket, with chili gastrique and Thai herbs: $19.95, served daily after 4pm.
  • Oak Smoked Beef Brisket Sandwich, with papaya salad, peanuts, chili aioli and Thai herbs: $15.95.
  • Chopped Smoked Brisket Bowl: $18.75.

Loro is also built for lingering: boozy slushees, a Lil’ Loro kids menu, and dogs welcome on the patio.

Loro South Lamar vs. Loro Domain NORTHSIDE

Both Austin locations keep the same hours and sell the same house cheeseburger. They differ on the second burger and the happy hour: South Lamar runs happy hour from 2pm to 5pm on weekdays, and the Domain stretches it to 6pm.

Loro South Lamar is the original, opened on April 4, 2018, at 2115 South Lamar Blvd. Loro Domain NORTHSIDE opened on December 21, 2024, on the ground floor of the Aloft Austin at Domain NORTHSIDE, according to Community Impact and Tribeza. If you can only go once, go to South Lamar: it is the original, and the Onion-Cheddar is the more interesting second burger on paper.

Loro vs. Franklin Barbecue: which one to visit

Franklin Barbecue is a traditional Central Texas barbecue joint that stays open until it sells out. Loro is an all-day Asian smokehouse with burgers, open until 10pm or 11pm. They share a founder and a love of post oak, not a menu.

Loro timeline: from Franklin’s trailer to six Texas locations

Loro grew from a single South Lamar restaurant in 2018 to six Texas locations by 2026. The first Franklin Barbecue dates show where its brisket comes from.

  • 2009: Aaron and Stacy Franklin open Franklin Barbecue as a trailer in Austin.
  • 2011: Franklin Barbecue moves to 900 E. 11th St.
  • 2015: Franklin wins the James Beard Award for Best Chef: Southwest.
  • August 2017: A smokehouse fire cuts Franklin Barbecue’s capacity. It reopens in November 2017 and is back to full operation in March 2018, according to Wikipedia’s history of the restaurant.
  • March 29, 2018: A Loro preview party benefiting No Kid Hungry is planned, per Community Impact.
  • April 4, 2018: Loro opens at 2115 S. Lamar Blvd. At opening, pitmaster Bramwell Tripp ran two Oyler rotisserie smokers fueled by post oak, as Garden & Gun reported. That describes 2018–2019, not today’s kitchen.
  • November 2020: A KSL Capital Partners affiliate invests in Hai Hospitality.
  • July 5, 2021: Loro East Dallas opens.
  • Fall 2022: Loro Addison, at 14999 Montfort Drive, is announced.
  • December 21, 2024: Loro Domain NORTHSIDE opens as the second Austin location.
  • November 2026: Loro lists six Texas locations: Austin South Lamar, Austin Domain North Side, Dallas East, Dallas Addison, Houston Heights and Houston Kirby. Houston Heights occupies a 1940s church at 1001 W. 11th St.

Is Loro Austin worth it?

Yes, if you want a brisket burger in Austin without Franklin Barbecue’s sell-out hours. The Loro Cheeseburger is the reason to go: Franklin’s signature meat on a cheeseburger, for $14.95, at a counter where nobody rushes you.

My recommendation rests on the official menus, dated prices and published reviews, not on a scored visit, so I give no rating here. The evidence points one way: The Infatuation scores Loro 8.2 and singles out the cheeseburger, and both critics I cite describe a rich, brisket-heavy burger. Pair it with something bright from the Southeast Asian side of the menu.

Loro Austin FAQ

Who owns Loro in Austin: is it Aaron Franklin’s restaurant?

No. Loro was co-founded in 2018 by Aaron Franklin and Tyson Cole, and it is operated by Cole’s company, Hai Hospitality. Franklin’s own restaurant is Franklin Barbecue, at 900 E. 11th St.

What is on the Loro Cheeseburger and how much does it cost?

The Loro Cheeseburger comes with red onion-brisket jam, muenster cheese, lettuce and loro burger sauce. It costs $14.95 before tax at both Austin locations (checked November 15, 2026).

Is the Loro burger made with brisket?

The brisket is in the topping: a red onion-brisket jam. The official menu does not describe the patty blend. D Magazine reported in 2022 that the Dallas location used chuck and brisket trims, but that is not confirmed for Austin today.

What is the difference between Loro and Franklin Barbecue?

Franklin Barbecue is Aaron and Stacy Franklin’s traditional Texas barbecue restaurant, open Tuesday to Sunday until sold out. Loro is an Asian smokehouse co-founded by Franklin and Tyson Cole, open daily into the evening, with burgers and Southeast Asian and Japanese-inspired dishes.

Does Loro take reservations?

No. Loro is counter service: you order at the bar, and it does not take reservations.

Do you need to wait in line at Loro like at Franklin Barbecue?

No sell-out line to plan around. Loro does not close when the meat runs out, and it stays open until 10pm or 11pm, so you can come in the evening and order at the counter.

Which Loro location has the smash burger?

The Double Smash Burger ($13.95) is on the Domain NORTHSIDE menu, at 11601 Domain Dr., Ste. 200. It is not on the South Lamar menu.

Where are the Loro locations in Austin, and what are their hours?

Loro South Lamar is at 2115 South Lamar Blvd, Austin, TX 78704. Loro Domain NORTHSIDE is at 11601 Domain Dr., Ste. 200, Austin, TX 78758. Both open Sunday to Thursday from 11am to 10pm and Friday to Saturday from 11am to 11pm.

What kind of food is Loro: Asian, barbecue or both?

Both. Loro smokes its meats overnight and finishes most proteins over post oak on an open-fire grill, then serves them with Southeast Asian and Japanese-inspired sauces and sides.

Is Loro dog-friendly and kid-friendly?

Yes. Loro has a dog-friendly patio and a kids menu called Lil’ Loro.

Is Loro worth it, and what should you order first?

Yes, for a brisket burger fan. Order the Loro Cheeseburger first, then add the Oak-Smoked Brisket ($19.95, after 4pm) if you want brisket on its own.
