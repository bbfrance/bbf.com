Best Burgers in New Delhi: 9 Verified Spots

A verified shortlist for best burgers in New Delhi, with buff explained, neighbourhoods and vegetarian picks.

By AlexPublished October 3, 2026

The best burgers in New Delhi are not in one shop. They are scattered across Khan Market, Connaught Place, Defence Colony, Vasant Kunj and the wider NCR. I spent 29 September trying to pin down addresses and Delhi prices outlet by outlet — Zomato and search kept timing out. So this is what it is: a tight nine-name shortlist I could verify from primary sources. Three smash-style, four dine-in cafes, two veg-led. My bias is clear. I would start with smash.

Map of New Delhi burger neighbourhoods: Khan Market, Connaught Place, Defence Colony, Vasant Kunj, Saket, Hauz Khas (Map: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0)

How I picked the best burger in Delhi

I kept only venues with a verifiable Delhi presence — official site, established press, or directory listing. No ranking by Google rating. No outlet-by-outlet 2026 open-status check yet, and I do not state closures as fact. New Delhi is the capital district within Delhi NCT, and bills are paid in Indian rupees (INR, Rs) (Incredible India).

Beef on a Delhi menu does not mean cow beef. Cow slaughter is prohibited in Delhi under the Delhi Agricultural Cattle Preservation Act 1994, so beef or buff means water-buffalo (Delhi law portal). For the wider India context, see my best burgers in Mumbai.

Smash burger Delhi: thin patties done right

The best smash burger Delhi bets here are Good Flippin’ Burgers, Louis Burger and Jimis Burger. Thin patties, crisp edges. That technique suits buff and chicken.

Smash burger Delhi style: thin smashed buff patties with crisp edges and double cheddar (Photo: Michael Rivera, CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons / Openverse)

1. Good Flippin’ Burgers

Good Flippin’ Burgers is my first call for smashed buff in Delhi NCR, in brioche. Founded in 2019 by Sijo Mathew, Sid Marchant and Viren D’Silva, it runs 60+ outlets including Delhi NCR (brand story).

Its Classic stacks two buff patties, two cheddar slices, truffle mayo and brioche. The brand listed it at INR 365 in a September 2026 Mumbai menu check — I could not verify the Delhi price, so I do not state one. For bun technique, see my homemade burger buns recipe.

2. Louis Burger

Louis Burger is the one to try for a double-buff smash-style cheeseburger. A cloud-kitchen brand from Zorawar Kalra’s Massive Restaurants, present in Delhi NCR (Massive Restaurants).

Its Monster Cheeseburger pairs double buff with English cheddar and bacon. Outlets are cloud kitchens, so check the live delivery listing for your neighbourhood.

3. Jimis Burger

Jimis Burger is the pick for an oversized stack. The founder started from a pushcart in Sangli at INR 60 a burger and opened in Malad in 2014; the brand lists Delhi NCR outlets (Curly Tales report).

Its Jaw Breaker is a roughly 7-inch stack. Fun. Confirm the Delhi NCR outlet and current menu before going.

Where to eat burgers in Khan Market, Connaught Place and Defence Colony

For dine-in around Connaught Place, Khan Market and Defence Colony, go to Big Chill Cafe, Cafe Delhi Heights, Smoke House Deli or AKU’s. Fixed dining rooms. Not delivery-only.

New Delhi at sunset over India Gate, locating Khan Market, Connaught Place and Defence Colony burger areas (Photo: Larry Johnson, CC BY 2.0 via Flickr / Openverse)

4. AKU’s - The Brrgrr Co.

AKU’s - The Brrgrr Co. is the Defence Colony address for gourmet burgers with fresh buns. It operates in Defence Colony, New Delhi (Zomato listing).

I could not verify its full postal address or current signature from a primary source, so I leave them out until I can.

5. Big Chill Cafe

Big Chill Cafe is the reliable Khan Market and Connaught Place stop when the table cannot agree. Burgers alongside a full Italian menu. It operates in both areas (official site).

Order here when you need pasta, pizza and burgers at once.

6. Cafe Delhi Heights

Cafe Delhi Heights runs several New Delhi outlets, including Connaught Place, Khan Market and Vasant Kunj (official site). Ask for the Juicy Lucy and the Delhi Heights burger.

Locations shift often. Confirm the outlet address on the brand site.

7. Smoke House Deli

Smoke House Deli in Defence Colony and Khan Market does grilled, deli-style burgers (official site). Quieter room. Daytime pace.

Go here for a sit-down burger, not a late-night smash.

Cafe cheeseburger with fries on a daytime table, as served in Khan Market and Connaught Place (Photo: jeffreyw, CC BY 2.0 via Flickr / Openverse)

Veg burger Delhi best: vegetarian picks

For veg in this set: Burger Singh for Indian flavours, Jumboking for low-cost vegetarian classics. Delhi demand for vegetarian is large, so both sell veg alongside non-veg.

Best veg burger Delhi pick: vada-pav style soft bun with spiced vegetable patty, ancestor of Jumboking vegetarian burgers (Photo: Warren Noronha, CC BY 2.0 via Wikimedia Commons / Openverse)

8. Burger Singh

Burger Singh is the Delhi NCR chain for Indian-flavoured burgers. Founded in 2014 in Gurgaon / Delhi NCR (official site). I like the idea more than most fusion menus — Amritsari Murgh, Rajma.

Recipes vary by store. Check the current outlet menu.

9. Jumboking

Jumboking is the vegetarian-rooted chain with Delhi presence. Founded on 23 August 2001 in Malad by Dheeraj and Reeta Gupta, about 200 outlets in India, from vada-pav origins into vegetarian burgers (official site).

Pick it for a cheap vegetarian burger, not a gourmet dine-in.

FAQ: best burgers in New Delhi

Short answers to what readers ask most about the best burger in New Delhi.

Q: Where to eat the best burger in New Delhi right now?

A: Start with Good Flippin’ Burgers or Louis Burger for smash-style buff, Cafe Delhi Heights or AKU’s for dine-in, and Burger Singh or Jumboking for veg. Always confirm the outlet on the official site first.

Q: How much does a good burger cost in New Delhi (INR)?

A: I do not publish a dated Delhi price here because I could not verify Delhi prices on 2026-09-29. Budget in INR (Rs) and check the brand menu or delivery app on the day.

Q: Where to find the best smash burger in Delhi?

A: Try Good Flippin’ Burgers, Louis Burger and Jimis Burger, known for thin smashed patties and stacked cheeseburgers.

Q: Where to find the best veg / vegetarian burger in Delhi?

A: Try Burger Singh for Amritsari Murgh and Rajma styles and Jumboking for vegetarian classics from vada-pav origins.

Q: Where to eat burgers in Connaught Place / Khan Market / Defence Colony / Saket / Hauz Khas?

A: Verified for Connaught Place, Khan Market and Defence Colony are Big Chill Cafe, Cafe Delhi Heights, Smoke House Deli and AKU’s. I could not verify Saket or Hauz Khas outlets, so I leave them out.

Q: Do Delhi restaurants serve beef burgers or only buff / chicken / mutton?

A: They serve buff, chicken, mutton and veg, not cow beef. Cow slaughter is prohibited in Delhi, so beef on a menu means water-buffalo (buff).

Q: Which Delhi burger places are open late / deliver?

A: I do not state hours or delivery zones as fact because they change by outlet and I could not verify them. Check the brand site or delivery app for tonight.
