MOS Burger Taichung: 29 Branches and Hours

MOS Burger Taichung: all 29 branches with addresses, phones and hours, from the HSR station counter to the 24-hour Xueshi store, plus rice burgers to try.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished November 18, 2026

MOS Burger Taichung has 29 branches across the city: that is how many I counted in MOS Burger Taiwan’s official store locator on 4 November 2026. 5 of them matter to most visitors. The Taichung HSR station branch opens 06:00–22:00, Xueshi in North District never closes, and Wenxin in Xitun runs until 02:00. Henan Fengjia sits by Feng Chia Night Market, and Gongyi serves the Nantun stretch of Gongyi Road. Every address, phone number and opening time below comes from the official locator (mos.com.tw), checked on 4 November 2026.

MOS Burger Taichung at a glance

MOS Burger is a Japanese burger chain that cooks to order, and Taichung is one of its densest cities in Taiwan, with 29 branches in the official locator.

  • Branches in Taichung: 29 in the official locator on 4 November 2026, including 3 with restricted access (a TSMC site, a hospital and a highway service area).
  • Earliest openings: most branches open at 06:00 or 06:30, because breakfast is a core part of MOS in Taiwan.
  • Late night: Xueshi (24 hours), Wenxin (until 02:00), Gongyi (until 23:00).
  • For travellers: the Taichung HSR station branch, 06:00–22:00.
  • Signature item: the rice burger, called 珍珠堡 (“pearl burger”) in Taiwan.
  • Ordering: at the counter, by kiosk where installed, or through the MOS Order app.
  • Local search terms: 摩斯漢堡 台中 (MOS Burger Taichung), 摩斯漢堡 台中 門市 (Taichung branches), 高鐵台中 摩斯 (MOS at Taichung HSR).

Which MOS Burger in Taichung should you choose?

Pick the HSR branch if you are catching a train, Xueshi or Wenxin if you are hungry after midnight, and Henan Fengjia or Gongyi if you are already out eating in Xitun or Nantun.

My own shortlist is shorter still. If you only pass through Taichung, the HSR counter is the one worth planning around: it opens before the first southbound trains, and a cooked-to-order burger beats a station bento you have to eat cold. Everyone else should pick by the clock, not the district.

MOS Burger Taichung HSR: eating at the high-speed rail station

The MOS Burger at Taichung HSR station sits at No. 8, Zhanqu 2nd Road in Wuri District and opens daily from 06:00 to 22:00, according to the official locator.

On a local map, search for 高鐵台中 摩斯. Those hours cover the first southbound trains and most evening arrivals. What I could not confirm is whether the counter sits before or after the ticket gates, or on which floor. Allow a few spare minutes and check the station map when you arrive.

MOS Burger Taichung open 24 hours: the Xueshi branch

The Xueshi branch at No. 300, Xueshi Road in North District is the only MOS Burger in Taichung that the official locator lists as open 24 hours.

It sits near China Medical University, in the student quarter that also takes in Yizhong Street. Some third-party listings call other branches “always open”. The official hours on 4 November 2026 did not back that up. Trust the locator.

Late-night MOS Burger in Xitun: the Wenxin branch

The Wenxin branch at No. 310, Section 3, Wenxin Road in Xitun stays open until 02:00, the latest closing time in the city after Xueshi.

United Daily News reported on 28 April 2026 that MOS had finished renovating a branch called 台中文心店 in the first half of the year, and that is the name the locator gives this one. But the article gave no address, and a second branch stands on the same road (市政文心店, Section 2). I can’t say for sure which one got the makeover.

Near Feng Chia Night Market and Gongyi Road

Henan Fengjia, at No. 270, Section 2, Henan Road, is the branch closest to Feng Chia University and its night market, open 06:30–22:00.

Gongyi, at No. 497, Section 2, Gongyi Road in Nantun, closes at 23:00. That one extra hour over most branches is handy if you are eating your way down Gongyi Road and want something to finish on.

The MOS Burger Taiwan menu: rice burgers and breakfast

The MOS Burger menu in Taiwan is built around 2 things you won’t find at McDonald’s: rice burgers (珍珠堡) and a full breakfast service that starts at 06:00 in most Taichung branches.

What is a MOS rice burger (珍珠堡)?

A MOS rice burger swaps the bread bun for 2 discs of pressed rice mixed with barley and millet, an idea MOS launched in Japan in December 1987.

Taiwan sells the range as 珍珠堡, “pearl burger”, made with a 超級大麥 (“super barley”) rice blend, according to the travel blog Sekainomys. The numbers are big: MOS Burger Taiwan told United Daily News in April 2026 that it had sold more than 100 million rice burgers in the country. Fillings run from beef to seafood and ginger pork. The summer 2026 addition was an eel rice burger (蒲燒鰻珍珠堡), launched on 10 June, according to UDN. Check the app before you go: seasonal items come and go.

If you order 1 thing here that you can’t get in Europe, make it a rice burger. A regular MOS burger is good, but you can find something like it elsewhere. The pressed-rice bun is the reason to choose MOS over the McDonald’s next door.

Breakfast at MOS Burger Taiwan

Breakfast matters at MOS in Taiwan: the outlet Friday News reports that it brings in about 30% of the chain’s sales there. That explains why almost every Taichung branch opens at 06:00 or 06:30.

This is the biggest gap with Japan. In Taiwan, MOS fights for the morning trade against the breakfast shops on every corner. In Japan it is mostly a lunch and dinner stop, as our guides to MOS Burger in Osaka, Sapporo, Nagoya and Hiroshima show.

MOS Burger Taichung prices

I am not publishing MOS Burger Taichung prices in NT$ yet. I could not read them on an official source on 4 November 2026, and the blogs I found contradict each other.

Vegetarian and vegan options at MOS Burger Taiwan

The king oyster mushroom rice burgers (杏鮑菇珍珠堡) are the vegan choice at MOS Burger in Taiwan, according to Taiwan News. The pea-protein burger is not vegan.

According to Taiwan News, the mushroom rice burgers, the 超級大麥 version included, suit strict vegans. The plant-based burger with a pea-protein patty (摩力樂活蔬食堡) is another story. Its sauce contains egg and the 5 pungent vegetables (五辛) that Buddhist vegetarians avoid, and it goes on the same griddle as the meat. The article is not recent, so ask at the counter whether both are still served.

How to order at MOS Burger in Taichung: app, kiosks, delivery and payment

You can order at MOS Burger Taichung at the counter, on a self-service kiosk where one is installed, or in the MOS Order app. Delivery is available through Uber Eats from at least the Daye branch.

  • App: MOS Taiwan launched its MOS Order app in 2012 (UDN).
  • Kiosks: MOS says it introduced fast food’s first Taiwan-made ordering kiosk in 2016, and kiosks now run in 209 restaurants nationwide (UDN). I could not confirm which Taichung branches have one.
  • Delivery: the Daye branch (No. 182, Daye Road, Nantun) is listed on Uber Eats. I did not check foodpanda or Uber Eats branch by branch.
  • Payment: the chain says it accepts credit cards and several other payment methods at all its restaurants (UDN). I could not confirm whether EasyCard or LINE Pay work at each branch.
  • Reservations: none that I could find. This is counter-service fast food.

MOS Burger Taichung reviews: what customers praise and criticise

Customers of MOS Burger in Taiwan tend to praise the freshly made burgers and the calm, clean dining rooms. The complaints are just as consistent: the wait, and prices above McDonald’s.

These themes come from aggregated listings and general press, not from a branch-by-branch reading of recent Taichung reviews. Read them as the chain’s reputation, not as a verdict on any one address.

  • Praised: burgers made to order, fresh ingredients, clean dining rooms, friendly staff, seats with power outlets and no time limit, the black tea, and the originality of the rice burgers.
  • Criticised: waiting times caused by cooking to order, prices above McDonald’s, some burgers found too salty, and service that varies from 1 visit to the next.

I am not quoting Google or Uber Eats ratings for individual branches. The figures I found came from search summaries, and at least 1 belonged to the wrong branch (Miaoli HSR instead of Taichung HSR). Dated ratings will go in once I have read them directly on Google Maps and TripAdvisor.

MOS Burger in Taiwan: a short history

MOS Burger opened its first restaurant in Taiwan in February 1991, on Xinsheng South Road in Taipei, through An Shin Food Services, a company set up in November 1990 with the TECO group.

Some English sources, Wikipedia among them, give 1989. Taiwanese sources (United Daily News, Stockfeel) and our MOS Burger brand profile give 1991. In 2003, according to the Taipei Times, TECO Electric & Machinery held 65% of the Taiwanese joint venture with MOS Food Services. The chain marked 35 years in Taiwan in April 2026.

MOS Food Services’ figure makes Taiwan its largest market outside Japan. An Shin, listed under stock code 1259, reported 2025 consolidated revenue of NT$6.062 billion (up 0.27%), with an operating loss of NT$13 million and a net profit attributable to the group of NT$79 million (Commercial Times, 22 May 2026). The company plans to install MOS vending machines by the end of 2026 and to renovate restaurants that have been open for more than 15 years first.

MOS Burger or McDonald’s in Taichung?

Choose MOS Burger in Taichung if you want a rice burger, a quieter seat or a Japanese-style burger cooked to order. Choose McDonald’s if speed and price come first.

The reviews spell out the trade-off: you wait longer and pay more, and you get a burger assembled after you order it. I think that’s a fair deal at breakfast or late at night, when a calm seat and a plug for your phone matter as much as the food. With a train to catch in 10 minutes, it isn’t. FAQ: MOS Burger Taichung

How many MOS Burger restaurants are there in Taichung?

I counted 29 in the official MOS Burger Taiwan locator on 4 November 2026: 26 open to the public and 3 with restricted access (a TSMC site, Taichung Veterans General Hospital and the Tai’an North highway service area).

Is there a MOS Burger at Taichung HSR station?

Yes. The Taichung HSR branch (高鐵台中店) is at No. 8, Zhanqu 2nd Road, Wuri District, tel. (04) 3600-9039. It is open from 06:00 to 22:00.

Which MOS Burger in Taichung is open 24 hours?

The Xueshi branch at No. 300, Xueshi Road, North District, is the only 1 listed as open 24 hours. The Wenxin branch in Xitun is open until 02:00, and the Gongyi branch in Nantun until 23:00.

How much does a meal at MOS Burger in Taiwan cost?

I could not verify current prices on an official source on 4 November 2026, so I am not quoting any. Check the MOS Order app or the menu board in the restaurant for exact NT$ prices.

What is a 珍珠堡 rice burger?

It is a MOS burger whose bun is pressed rice mixed with barley and millet instead of bread. MOS created it in Japan in 1987, and in Taiwan it is sold as 珍珠堡 with a “super barley” rice blend.

Is the MOS Burger menu in Taiwan different from Japan?

Yes. Taiwan has a breakfast service that reportedly brings in about 30% of sales, and its rice burgers are sold under the 珍珠堡 name. I found no item that only Taichung sells.

Does MOS Burger Taichung have vegan options?

The king oyster mushroom rice burgers (杏鮑菇珍珠堡) are vegan, according to Taiwan News. The pea-protein burger (摩力樂活蔬食堡) contains egg and the 5 pungent vegetables, and it shares a griddle with meat. Ask in store whether both are still on the menu.

Can I get MOS Burger delivered in Taichung?

Yes. The Daye branch in Nantun is on Uber Eats, and the MOS Order app takes orders. I did not check delivery for every branch.

Can I pay by card at MOS Burger?

Yes. MOS Burger Taiwan says it accepts credit cards and several other payment methods. I could not confirm EasyCard or LINE Pay branch by branch.

Why is MOS Burger slower than McDonald’s?

MOS assembles each burger when you order it instead of keeping burgers ready, so you wait longer. This is the complaint that comes up most often in reviews.
