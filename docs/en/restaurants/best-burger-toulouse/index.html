Best Burger in Toulouse, France: Top 10 for 2026

Best burger in Toulouse for 2026: Superette in Saint-Cyprien, L'Antidòt by the Capitole, Junk in Carmes. 10 spots, dated prices, halal and veggie picks.

By Thomas, Restaurants writer, FrancePublished October 2, 2026Updated November 11, 2026

The best burger in Toulouse, France, in 2026 is at Superette, 7 place de l’Estrapade in Saint-Cyprien. It is the only Toulouse address that the French food guide Le Fooding files under burgers, with a Classic made from beef ground by the butcher across the street and burgers from €8 to €12. After Superette, I rank L’Antidòt Burger on rue du Taur, an organic burger bar rated 4.9/5 on Google from 801 reviews (via Restaurant Guru, September 2026), then Junk in Carmes for smash burgers. All 10 picks are below, with hours, signature burgers, dated prices and halal or vegetarian options.

This ranking replaces my October 2026 version, which listed a single address. It sits on our Burgers in Toulouse city page and with our other burger city guides in the Restaurants section.

How I ranked these Toulouse burger restaurants

This ranking is built from official websites, guides (Le Fooding, Time Out), menus and dated ratings. I did not eat at these 10 places for this guide, and nothing below suggests otherwise.

I sorted the places on 4 criteria, in this order. First, recognition by an independent guide or by several local rankings. Second, activity confirmed in 2026 on the official site, the menu or a recent listing. Third, a dated price. Last, the Google rating and its review count. A place with no verified price can stay in the top 10, but it drops down.

The Google ratings come from Restaurant Guru or Sluurpy, which relay Google: I did not read them on Google directly. I give them with the date I saw them, as a guide, never as my verdict. Prices taken from Deliveroo or Uber Eats may be higher than at the counter. TripAdvisor, the local paper La Dépêche and actu.fr blocked my research tools, so nothing here comes from them.

There is no trophy to settle it. I found no Toulouse winner of France’s burger championships (the Championnat or the Coupe de France du burger) or of the World Burger Contest.

1. Superette, Saint-Cyprien: the burger Le Fooding picks

Superette serves the best burger in Toulouse on the evidence I found: it is the only Toulouse address under the “Burgers” filter of Le Fooding, whose listing went up on 24 May 2025 and was updated on 18 March 2026.

Vincent Louw, who worked at La Crique in Biarritz and La Pente Douce in Toulouse, runs it. The menu is by Jeff Claudio, formerly of Yardbird in Hong Kong. Le Fooding describes The Classic as a medium-rare patty, using beef from the butcher across the street, with a sauce built on Kewpie, the Japanese mayonnaise. There is also a fried-chicken burger.

Superette opened in 2022 and added a second shop in Paris, on rue Jean-Pierre-Timbaud in the 11th arrondissement, in summer 2026, according to Time Out Paris (19 August 2026). Time Out’s prices are for the Paris shop: a double smash with cheddar at €10, fries at €4, with 2 beef recipes (smashed or not), fried chicken and an egg-based vegetarian option. I have not checked that the Toulouse menu is identical; for Toulouse, the figure to go by is Le Fooding’s €8–12 per burger.

Why first? No other place in this ranking has a national guide behind it in the burger category, and its prices are dated. That a Toulouse shop is now exporting itself to Paris doesn’t hurt.

  • Address: 7 place de l’Estrapade, 31300 Toulouse (Saint-Cyprien, on the left bank of the Garonne)
  • Hours: daily 12:00–14:30 and 19:00–22:30 per the official site; Le Fooding says lunch ends at 14:00 on weekdays
  • Signature burger and price: The Classic; burgers €8–12, fries €3 (Le Fooding, March 2026)
  • Halal / veggie: halal not stated; egg-based vegetarian option listed in Paris, not verified in Toulouse
  • Rating: 4.5/5 on Google (480 reviews), seen on 11 November 2026 via Restaurant Guru
  • Official links: superette-burger.com and Instagram @superette.burger; no phone number published

2. L’Antidòt Burger, rue du Taur: organic burgers by the Capitole

For an organic, locally sourced burger in central Toulouse, go to L’Antidòt Burger at 10 rue du Taur. Its Google rating is the highest in this list, level with Les Filoches but on more than twice the reviews: 4.9/5 from 801 (via Restaurant Guru, page updated 20 September 2026).

The official site stresses local and organic produce, homemade cooking, a vegetarian range and student menus. Local rankings have put it near the top since at least 2023: “the most organic” for Le Bonbon in 2023, number 1 at BURG’UP, and number 2 in the Hotel Albert 1er guide published on 15 October 2026.

Why second and not first? I found no verified burger price for 2026, only the €10–20 range Toulouseforyou gave in 2024. And Le Fooding does not list it among its Toulouse burgers.

  • Address: 10 rue du Taur, 31000 Toulouse, a short walk up from Place du Capitole
  • Hours: every day except Wednesday, 11:30–14:15 and 18:30–21:15, until 21:45 on Saturday evening (official site, checked 11 November 2026)
  • Price: €10–20 per Toulouseforyou (2024); no verified 2026 burger price
  • Halal / veggie: vegetarian range; halal not stated
  • Phone: +33 6 87 80 65 66
  • Official links: antidot-burger.fr and Instagram @antidotburger

3. Junk Toulouse, Carmes: smash burgers made with French beef

In Carmes, the smash burger to get is at Junk, 41 rue des Filatiers, open daily from 12:00 to 22:30. It is a French chain with 20 restaurants according to its website, and the number 1 in the Hotel Albert 1er guide.

You order by size, from S (1 patty) to XXL (5 patties), made with French Charolais and Limousin beef. There is also a Truffle burger and a Veggie. Deliveroo prices on 11 November 2026, which may be higher than at the counter: S €7.20, M €10.90, L €14.50, XL €17.90, XXL €19.90, Truffle €13.90, Veggie €10.90.

Ratings: 4.8/5 on Google from 948 reviews (Restaurant Guru, 27 October 2026). I put it behind Superette and L’Antidòt because it is a chain: you get the same burger in Paris and Lyon. My guides to Junk’s smash burgers in Paris and Junk Burger Lyon, menu and address cover the menu in detail.

  • Address: 41 rue des Filatiers, 31000 Toulouse (Carmes)
  • Hours: daily 12:00–22:30
  • Signature burger and price: smash M, €10.90 (Deliveroo, November 2026)
  • Halal / veggie: Veggie at €10.90; halal not stated
  • Official link: junkburgers.com; no phone number published

4. Les Filoches, Saint-Michel: pulled-meat burgers

Les Filoches makes burgers with slow-cooked shredded meat, at 1 rue Saint-Joseph near the Palais de Justice. It is the most distinctive place on this list, rated 4.9/5 on Google from 357 reviews (Restaurant Guru, seen on 11 November 2026).

The menu reads like a tour of French braises. On Uber Eats on 11 November 2026: Bœuf Bourguignon at €13 (shredded slow-cooked beef), Cantalou at €14 (beer-braised pork, Cantal cheese), Manigod at €14 (reblochon), Poulet at €13 (halal chicken) and Végétarien Avocado at €14 (sweet potato and chickpea). Delivery prices may be higher than at the counter.

Only the chicken burger is halal; the restaurant as a whole is not. The company was registered in 2023 and its president is Pierre Bouissou, according to Pages Jaunes, the French business directory. The Petit Futé guide lists it too.

  • Address: 1 rue Saint-Joseph, 31400 Toulouse
  • Hours: Tuesday to Saturday, 11:30–14:00 and 19:00–22:00 (Restaurant Guru)
  • Signature burger and price: Bœuf Bourguignon, €13 (Uber Eats, November 2026)
  • Halal / veggie: halal chicken burger; Végétarien Avocado
  • Phone: +33 5 62 83 24 77
  • Instagram: @lesfiloches

5. Novo Burger, Honoré-Serres and États-Unis: late-night halal burgers

Need a halal burger after midnight? Novo Burger is the best-documented halal option in this ranking, and the latest-opening: on avenue Honoré-Serres it serves until 02:00 Monday to Saturday and until 03:00 on Sunday (official site).

It is a small local chain that states halal on both of its websites. At Honoré-Serres on 11 November 2026, Le Classique cost €11.90, or €13.90 as a meal deal, and burgers ran from €11.90 to €17.90. The Menu Foie gras, at €22.90, is the only southwest French specialty I found on a 2026 Toulouse burger menu.

The second branch, at 50 avenue des États-Unis, also states halal on its website and is rated 4.7/5 on Google from 802 reviews (Restaurant Guru, 2 October 2026). A third branch reported near the Capitole could not be confirmed, so I do not list it.

  • Addresses: 6 avenue Honoré Serres, 31000 Toulouse; 50 avenue des États-Unis, 31200 Toulouse
  • Hours (Honoré-Serres): Monday to Saturday 11:00–02:00, Sunday 11:00–03:00
  • Signature burger and price: Le Classique, €11.90 on its own, €13.90 as a meal deal (official site, November 2026)
  • Halal / veggie: halal stated; no vegetarian option stated
  • Phones: +33 5 81 60 40 44 (Honoré-Serres); +33 9 54 84 66 69 (États-Unis)
  • Instagram: @novoburger31

6. Toulouse Burger, rue Gabriel-Péri: build your own

At Toulouse Burger, 29 rue Gabriel Péri, you build your own, and it is one of the cheapest eats in this top 10: €8.50 a burger and €9 for the burger of the moment on Deliveroo, as relayed by Sluurpy (page updated 13 June 2026).

In 2024, Toulouseforyou quoted a €9–14.50 range, beef from an Aveyron breed, a Roquefort sauce and a vegan option. Those are the only nods to the Aveyron I saw on a Toulouse burger menu, and they date from 2024. Rating: 4.5/5 on Google from 435 reviews (via Sluurpy, page of 13 June 2026).

  • Address: 29 rue Gabriel Péri, 31000 Toulouse
  • Hours: not verified; check the Google listing before you go
  • Signature burger and price: build-your-own, €8.50 (Deliveroo via Sluurpy, June 2026)
  • Halal / veggie: vegan option reported in 2024; halal not stated
  • Phone: +33 5 61 13 11 31
  • Official link: none found

7. Burger’n’Co, Saint-Aubin and Compans: rock-themed burgers

At Burger’n’Co, the burgers are named after rock songs. The small chain has 2 Toulouse addresses, Saint-Aubin and Compans, and one in Montpellier, according to its official site.

The menu plays on the titles: Stairway to Heaven, Come as You Are, Highway to Hell, and the Antisocial, with Morteau sausage and camembert. I found no reliable price: the figures online come from user comments. Yelp rates it 4.5/5 from 113 reviews (seen on 11 November 2026).

The Saint-Aubin address comes from Grizette (2019) and mapstr. For Compans, sources disagree on the street number on the esplanade Compans-Caffarelli, so I give none.

  • Addresses: 20 rue Pierre-Paul Riquet, 31000 Toulouse (Saint-Aubin); esplanade Compans-Caffarelli (number unconfirmed)
  • Signature burger: the Antisocial (Morteau sausage, camembert)
  • Price: not verified
  • Halal / veggie: not stated

8. Big Fernand Toulouse Wilson: the French gourmet burger chain

Big Fernand, the French gourmet burger chain, has its Toulouse restaurant by Place Wilson, at 11 allées du Président Franklin Roosevelt. Google rates it 4.0/5 from 1,607 reviews (relayed by Sluurpy, 9 October 2026).

Its official page is live, but its only official prices date from 2022: €13 to €13.50 for a burger on its own. Le Blaise, with Bethmale cheese, dried duck breast and fig, appears only on that 2022 menu. Big Fernand ranks eighth for those old prices and for the lowest Google rating I recorded in this top 10.

My full profile, Big Fernand Toulouse: address, menu, prices and reviews, has the details. The chain’s history is on the Big Fernand brand page.

  • Address: 11 allées du Président Franklin Roosevelt, 31000 Toulouse (Place Wilson)
  • Signature burger and price: Le Big Fernand, €13 on its own (official menu, 2022)
  • Halal / veggie: Lucien vegetarian burger on the 2022 menu; halal not stated
  • Phone: +33 5 34 30 15 62
  • Metro: Jean-Jaurès (lines A and B), about 400 m away

9. Black and White Burger, rue du Taur: the certified halal chain

At 63 rue du Taur, Black and White Burger serves certified halal beef and chicken and no pork, according to the chain’s official site.

The national menu runs from €7.90 to €12.90, but prices vary by restaurant. I have no rating or price for the Toulouse branch itself. Hence ninth, and Novo Burger documents its halal offer better.

  • Address: 63 rue du Taur, 31000 Toulouse
  • Price: €7.90–12.90 on the national menu (seen on 11 November 2026), varies by restaurant
  • Halal / veggie: certified halal beef and chicken, no pork; no vegetarian option stated

10. Les Mecs au Camion, food truck: burgers on the move, with a caveat

Les Mecs au Camion has no fixed address: this burger food truck tours the Toulouse area, and its official site names the allées Jules-Guesde, Montaudran, Colomiers and La Friche Gramont, with no exact address.

To be straight with you, it is tenth for want of a better-verified option. I wanted Le Coyote Burgers on place du Peyrou here, but I could not confirm it is open in 2026, and one directory gives a different address for it. Les Mecs au Camion at least has a live official site. I have not verified its schedule, menu or prices, so check its Instagram @lesmecsaucamion before you head out.

  • Where: allées Jules-Guesde, Montaudran, Colomiers, La Friche Gramont (pitches from the official site, days not verified)
  • Signature burger and price: not verified
  • Halal / veggie: not stated

Where to eat a burger in Toulouse by area: Capitole, Carmes, Saint-Cyprien, Compans and the north

Most of the best burgers in Toulouse sit in the centre, between rue du Taur, Carmes and Saint-Cyprien. For a late-night halal burger, head north. The areas below are my reading of the map, not official boundaries.

  • Capitole, rue du Taur and Wilson: L’Antidòt Burger (no. 10) and Black and White Burger (no. 63) on rue du Taur; Big Fernand on Place Wilson. Five Guys, the American chain, has an address at 24 rue d’Alsace-Lorraine according to a directory: see the Five Guys brand page.
  • Carmes and Saint-Michel: Junk at 41 rue des Filatiers, and Bioburger, an organic burger chain, at no. 36 on the same street according to Tourisme Occitanie. Further south, Les Filoches on rue Saint-Joseph.
  • Saint-Cyprien: Superette on place de l’Estrapade, across the Garonne on the left bank.
  • Compans, Saint-Aubin and Matabiau: Burger’n’Co, Toulouse Burger and Novo Burger on avenue Honoré-Serres. Big Smash is at 68 rue de Bayard, near Matabiau station, with a second address in the Montaudran Aerospace district. Les Burgers de Colette is at 19 boulevard de Strasbourg.
  • North (31200): Novo Burger on avenue des États-Unis, and Memphis at no. 347 on the same avenue. BĀGGĀ, on rue de l’Abbé-Gabriel-Latour, served a raclette-and-rösti double smash called the “Winter Lover” in January 2025, according to a Le Bonbon article that reads as sponsored; I could not confirm it is open in 2026.
  • Outside Toulouse: Twilsons opened at Espaces Fenouillet on 28 October 2026, according to Le Bonbon.

Smash, halal or vegetarian: which Toulouse burger to pick

For a smash burger, go to Junk in Carmes; for halal, Novo Burger or Black and White Burger; for a vegetarian burger, L’Antidòt Burger or Les Filoches.

  • Smash burger Toulouse: Junk, from €7.20 for an S to €19.90 for an XXL (Deliveroo, November 2026). Superette serves a €10 double smash at its Paris shop (Time Out Paris, August 2026); in Toulouse, Le Fooding describes a medium-rare Classic. Valentino Smash House, 100% halal, is listed as temporarily closed.
  • Halal burger Toulouse: Novo Burger, halal stated, open until 02:00 or 03:00; Black and White Burger, certified halal beef and chicken, no pork. At Les Filoches, only the chicken burger is halal.
  • Vegetarian burger: a vegetarian range at L’Antidòt Burger; Végétarien Avocado at €14 at Les Filoches (Uber Eats); Veggie at €10.90 at Junk (Deliveroo); a vegan option at Toulouse Burger (2024); the Lucien at Big Fernand (2022 menu).

FAQ: where to eat a burger in Toulouse, France

Where is the best burger in Toulouse?

At Superette, 7 place de l’Estrapade in Saint-Cyprien, the only Toulouse burger address listed by Le Fooding. The guide describes The Classic there: a medium-rare patty made with beef from the butcher across the street. Burgers there cost €8 to €12 (March 2026).

Where can I find a halal burger in Toulouse?

At Novo Burger, 6 avenue Honoré Serres or 50 avenue des États-Unis, which state halal on their official sites. Another option is Black and White Burger, 63 rue du Taur, with certified halal beef and chicken and no pork.

Where can I get a smash burger in Toulouse?

At Junk, 41 rue des Filatiers in Carmes, open daily from 12:00 to 22:30. The smash M cost €10.90 on Deliveroo on 11 November 2026, made with French Charolais and Limousin beef.

Where can I find a vegetarian burger in Toulouse?

L’Antidòt Burger, 10 rue du Taur, has a vegetarian range. Les Filoches serves a Végétarien Avocado with sweet potato and chickpea (€14 on Uber Eats), and Junk a Veggie at €10.90 on Deliveroo.

How much does a burger cost in Toulouse in 2026?

Expect €8 to €14 for a burger on its own and €14 to €23 for a meal deal. Dated examples: €8–12 at Superette, €10.90 for Junk’s smash M, €11.90 for Le Classique at Novo Burger.

Where are the cheap eats for a burger in Toulouse?

The lowest prices I found: Junk’s S at €7.20 on Deliveroo, Black and White Burger from €7.90 on its national menu, and Toulouse Burger at €8.50. Delivery prices may be higher than at the counter.

Where can I eat a burger late at night in Toulouse?

At Novo Burger, 6 avenue Honoré Serres: open until 02:00 Monday to Saturday and until 03:00 on Sunday. Junk and Superette close at 22:30.

Where can I eat a burger near Place du Capitole?

On rue du Taur, which runs from Place du Capitole: L’Antidòt Burger at no. 10 and Black and White Burger at no. 63. Big Fernand is a few streets away, at 11 allées du Président Franklin Roosevelt.

Is there an award-winning burger in Toulouse?

I found no Toulouse winner of France’s burger championships or of the World Burger Contest. In 2024, Memphis on avenue des États-Unis sold the Grillin’ Burger by Joannes Richard, the 2023 World Food Championships burger winner in Dallas, for €17.95. It is no longer on the menu.
