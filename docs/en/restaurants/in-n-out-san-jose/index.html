In-N-Out San Jose: 4 Locations, Hours and Menu

San Jose has 4 official In-N-Out restaurants. Compare their hours, find the one closest to SJC airport, and see what to order.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 20, 2026

In-N-Out San Jose means 4 official restaurants: 550 Newhall Drive near the airport (store #240), 2950 E. Capitol Expressway in East San Jose (#158), and 2 in South San Jose, 5611 Santa Teresa Boulevard (#142) and 5590 Cottle Road (#342). All 4 have a drive-thru and a dining room, and all 4 open at 10:30 a.m. 3 serve until 1 a.m. Sunday through Thursday and 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday (at Capitol Expressway, only the drive-thru on weekends). Santa Teresa closes at midnight every night. Flying in or out of San Jose International Airport (SJC)? Go to Newhall Drive. I checked every address and opening time against In-N-Out’s official store pages on November 16, 2026.

How many In-N-Out restaurants are in San Jose?

San Jose has 4 In-N-Out Burger restaurants, listed on the chain’s official locator as stores #142, #158, #240 and #342. They run from the airport district in the north down to the neighborhoods around Oakridge Mall in the south. Not one is downtown. All 4 hug a freeway, which tells you who In-N-Out builds for: people in cars. For the rest of the city’s burgers, see our San Jose burger guide.

In-N-Out near San Jose airport: 550 Newhall Drive (#240)

The In-N-Out closest to San Jose International Airport is store #240 at 550 Newhall Drive. In-N-Out’s own directions put it “on Coleman W 880 & 87 Fwys; SW of San Jose Int’l Airport”. If I could keep only one of the 4, it would be this one. It’s the easiest to reach from the terminals and the freeways, and it serves until 1 a.m. on weeknights and 1:30 a.m. on weekends, late enough for the last arrivals. 1 warning: some travel aggregators list it at “530 Newhall” with 8 a.m.–10 p.m. hours. Both are wrong. The official page gives 550 Newhall Drive and the later hours in the table above.

East San Jose: 2950 E. Capitol Expressway (#158)

Store #158 at 2950 E. Capitol Expressway serves East San Jose, south of Tully Road and east of Highway 101. Its hours split by service. Sunday through Thursday, the dining room and the drive-thru both run 10:30 a.m. to 1 a.m. On Friday and Saturday, the official page has the dining room closing at midnight while the drive-thru stays open until 1:30 a.m. So the dining room closes earlier on weekends than on weeknights. That’s backwards from most fast food, and it will catch out anyone hoping for a table after a Saturday movie. Check the store page first.

South San Jose: 5611 Santa Teresa Boulevard (#142)

Store #142 at 5611 Santa Teresa Boulevard is the only In-N-Out in San Jose that closes at midnight every night. It sits south of Blossom Hill Road, below Oakridge Mall and the 85 freeway. Fine for lunch or dinner in South San Jose. After midnight, don’t bother: drive a few minutes to Cottle Road.

South San Jose: 5590 Cottle Road (#342)

Store #342 is at 5590 Cottle Road, on the northeast corner of Cottle Road and Poughkeepsie Road. It shares the 95123 ZIP code with Santa Teresa but not its bedtime. Cottle Road serves until 1 a.m. Sunday through Thursday and 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday, which makes it South San Jose’s late-night option.

In-N-Out San Jose hours: which one is open latest?

Newhall Drive, Cottle Road and the Capitol Expressway drive-thru are open latest, until 1 a.m. Sunday through Thursday and 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday. All 4 stores open at 10:30 a.m. every day. Santa Teresa closes at midnight, and so does the Capitol Expressway dining room on Friday and Saturday. Hours change, so I’ve dated mine: these come from the official store pages as of November 16, 2026.

In-N-Out San Jose menu: what to order

In San Jose you get In-N-Out’s standard menu, and it’s short: hamburger, cheeseburger and the Double-Double (2 beef patties, 2 slices of cheese), plus fries, soft drinks and milkshakes. According to the chain’s history on Wikipedia, lemonade arrived in 2003 and hot cocoa in January 2018. The short list is deliberate. The official store pages promise “only the freshest ingredients with no heat lamps, freezers or microwaves”. In Northern California, the stores are supplied through the company’s Lathrop distribution center, which opened in 2006.

The In-N-Out secret menu: Animal Style and more

The In-N-Out “secret menu” isn’t secret. The chain publishes it on its own website as the “Not So Secret Menu”, and you can order any of it by name at the counter or the drive-thru:

  • Animal Style: the patty is cooked with mustard, then topped with pickles, extra spread and grilled onions.
  • Protein Style: the burger is wrapped in lettuce instead of a bun.
  • Double-Meat: 2 patties, no cheese.
  • 3x3 (Triple Triple): 3 patties, 3 slices of cheese.
  • 4x4 (Quad Quad): 4 patties, 4 slices of cheese.
  • Grilled Cheese: 2 slices of American cheese, lettuce, tomato and spread, no meat.

On a first visit, order a Double-Double Animal Style and leave the 4x4 to the people filming themselves. 2 thin griddled patties keep the meat, cheese, bun and spread in balance. 4 just bury it. The Double-Double is also the burger Newhall Drive’s Yelp reviewers mention most, usually next to Animal Style fries.

Vegetarian options at In-N-Out

In-N-Out has no veggie patty. The meat-free burger is the Grilled Cheese from the Not So Secret Menu: 2 slices of American cheese, lettuce, tomato and spread on a bun. Add fries and a shake for a vegetarian meal. Don’t confuse it with Protein Style, which swaps the bun for lettuce but keeps the beef. That one is for low-carb eaters, not vegetarians.

In-N-Out San Jose menu prices

In-N-Out doesn’t publish prices on its website, and prices vary from store to store. Menu-price aggregator sites quote figures, but they aren’t official or tied to a San Jose store, so I won’t repeat them. For the real price at a given San Jose location, read the menu board at the drive-thru. In-N-Out San Jose reviews

The In-N-Out at 550 Newhall Drive is rated 4.0 out of 5 on Yelp from 738 reviews (Yelp, as relayed by search results on November 16, 2026). The praise keeps coming back to the same points: fast, friendly service even when the line wraps around the lot, the Double-Double, Animal Style fries and clean dining rooms. I couldn’t verify scores for the Capitol Expressway, Santa Teresa or Cottle Road stores, so I’ve left them out rather than guess. Delivery, ordering and payment

All 4 In-N-Out San Jose restaurants offer drive-thru and dine-in service, according to their official store pages. In-N-Out doesn’t run its own delivery. Delivered orders go through third-party apps such as DoorDash, according to secondary sources, so open the app to see whether it covers your San Jose address. There are no reservations, as you’d expect at a fast-food counter, and the official pages list no phone number for any of the 4 stores.

New In-N-Out locations proposed in San Jose

2 more In-N-Out restaurants have been proposed in San Jose. Neither is approved, and neither has an opening date. On McKee Road, In-N-Out filed a pre-application with the city for 2641 McKee Road in East San Jose, near the I-680 interchange. The site now holds a hair salon and a dry cleaner, and the plan includes a drive-thru, a covered patio and parking (Funcheap SF; BizToc). On Stevens Creek Boulevard, a plan for 3150–3158 Stevens Creek Boulevard, near Westfield Valley Fair and Santana Row, describes a 3,860 sq ft restaurant with a 29-car drive-thru and 84 indoor seats. I wouldn’t count on that one. The city pointed out that the Stevens Creek Urban Village rules prohibit drive-thrus, and the last report I found, from KRON4 in September 2022, had the project still in the development application process. I’ll update this guide if either project gets a permit or an opening date.

In-N-Out’s history: from Baldwin Park to San Jose

In-N-Out Burger was founded on October 22, 1948, in Baldwin Park, California, by Harry and Esther Snyder. It was California’s first drive-thru hamburger stand: customers ordered through a 2-way speaker. The company is now based in Irvine. It has never franchised and is still privately owned, led by Lynsi Snyder. As of August 19, 2026, it had 442 restaurants in 10 states, 289 of them in California (ScrapeHero). Since the 1980s, under president Rich Snyder, it has printed Bible references on its cups and wrappers. To see In-N-Out on its Southern California home turf, read our Los Angeles burger guide.

In-N-Out San Jose FAQ

How many In-N-Out restaurants are there in San Jose?

There are 4: 550 Newhall Drive (#240), 2950 E. Capitol Expressway (#158), 5611 Santa Teresa Boulevard (#142) and 5590 Cottle Road (#342). All 4 have a drive-thru and a dining room.

Which In-N-Out is closest to San Jose airport (SJC)?

The In-N-Out at 550 Newhall Drive (#240) is closest. It sits on Coleman Avenue by the 880 and 87 freeways, southwest of San Jose International Airport.

What time does In-N-Out close in San Jose?

Newhall Drive and Cottle Road close at 1 a.m. Sunday through Thursday and 1:30 a.m. on Friday and Saturday. Capitol Expressway’s drive-thru keeps the same hours, but its dining room closes at midnight on Friday and Saturday. Santa Teresa closes at midnight every night.

What is Animal Style at In-N-Out?

Animal Style is an option on In-N-Out’s official Not So Secret Menu. The patty is cooked with mustard, then topped with pickles, extra spread and grilled onions. You can order fries Animal Style too.

Does In-N-Out have vegetarian options?

Yes. Order the Grilled Cheese: 2 slices of American cheese, lettuce, tomato and spread, with no meat. Add fries and a shake for a full meal. There’s no veggie patty.

Does In-N-Out deliver in San Jose?

In-N-Out doesn’t deliver itself. Delivery goes through third-party apps such as DoorDash, so check the app for your San Jose address.

Is a new In-N-Out opening in San Jose?

2 sites have been proposed: 2641 McKee Road and 3150–3158 Stevens Creek Boulevard. Neither is approved, and neither has an opening date.
