Five Guys Tampa: 5 Locations, Menu and Prices

Which of Tampa's five Five Guys to pick, what to order, what the listed prices look like and what diners say, checked on October 24, 2026.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished October 30, 2026

Five Guys has 5 restaurants in the city of Tampa, FL: Collier Center on W Kennedy Blvd, Carrollwood on N Dale Mabry Hwy, University Collection Mall on E Fowler Ave, The Fairgrounds on Elm Fair Blvd and Falkenburg Commons on S Falkenburg Rd. Visiting? Go to Collier Center, 3841 West Kennedy Blvd., Tampa, FL 33609 (813-879-0300), the most central of the five. All five offer dine-in and delivery, and Falkenburg and E Fowler share the best Restaurantji score, 4.2/5. One warning: the New Tampa store on County Line Rd. closed on March 15, 2026.

Five Guys Kennedy Blvd: Collier Center, the pick for visitors

For most visitors, the best Five Guys in Tampa is Collier Center, at 3841 West Kennedy Blvd. It’s the most central of the five, and its official page lists dine-in, takeout, delivery and curbside pickup (Five Guys Collier Center).

Five Guys Tampa: West Kennedy Boulevard (State Road 60) crossing the Kennedy Bridge into downtown Tampa, Florida, under a clear sky. Five Guys Collier Center is on the same boulevard further west, at 3841 West Kennedy Blvd. This photo shows the boulevard, not the restaurant. (Photo: DanTD, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kennedy\_Bridge;\_Tampa-1.JPG (5152×3864))

It also has the lowest Restaurantji score in town, 3.8/5. Why do I still send visitors there? Because 0.4 of a point on a few hundred reviews matters less than half an hour in Tampa traffic. You can order ahead online and skip the line. On Yelp it is the most reviewed Five Guys in Tampa, with 92 reviews and 65 photos (Yelp), but the rating itself wouldn’t load for me, so I’m not quoting one.

Searching for “Five Guys Kennedy Blvd” or “Five Guys Collier Center”? This is the place, even though the official listing calls it “Tampa- Collier Center”.

Five Guys Carrollwood, University Collection Mall, Fairgrounds and Falkenburg

The other four Tampa locations are neighborhood stops, and the right one is usually the closest one. All four list dine-in, takeout and delivery on their official pages.

  • Carrollwood (14398 N. Dale Mabry Hwy, Suite 106): curbside pickup, plus delivery direct from Five Guys or through DoorDash and Uber Eats (official page).
  • University Collection Mall (2702 E. Fowler Ave, Unit 1A): the Five Guys near USF. It has curbside pickup and shares the top Restaurantji score in town (official page).
  • The Fairgrounds (8606 Elm Fair Blvd): the only Tampa location that lists outdoor seating, and reviewers keep mentioning how clean it is (official page).
  • Falkenburg Commons (2530 S. Falkenburg Rd.): the most reviewed on Restaurantji, at 4.2/5 from 251 reviews. Diners credit the crew with keeping up during the rush (official page).

If I lived in Tampa and wanted to sit down and eat, I’d pick Falkenburg. It has the best score on the largest number of reviews, and that’s the most reliable signal in this data. Parking, HART bus access, wheelchair access and payment methods are another matter: no official source covers them at any of the five, so I won’t guess. If one of them matters to you, call ahead.

Five Guys New Tampa closed: County Line Rd. and nearby options

The Five Guys at 6431 E. County Line Rd. in New Tampa closed on March 15, 2026, so don’t drive there. Neighborhood News reported that it was a corporate-owned store. A neighboring business owner told the paper it closed after a performance review, and a shawarma restaurant is due to take its place (Neighborhood News).

Up north, two franchised Five Guys are still open outside the Tampa city limits, according to the same source. One is at 25599 Sierra Center Blvd., across from Tampa Premium Outlets on S.R. 56. The other is at 28894 Wesley Chapel Blvd./S.R. 54 in Wesley Chapel.

Five Guys Tampa hours

I couldn’t verify the opening hours of any Five Guys in Tampa, so check the official store page or call before you go. The official pages showed no hours I could read, and the other listings don’t agree.

The gap is wide. Foursquare gives 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day for four of the restaurants. Another listing has Kennedy open 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. Sunday to Wednesday and until midnight Thursday to Saturday. The delivery apps say 11 a.m. to 9:30 p.m. On a Saturday night, that’s a 2.5-hour spread. I’d rather send you to the official pages linked above than print a schedule that leaves you outside a dark restaurant at 10:15.

Five Guys Tampa menu: what to order

Tampa serves the standard US Five Guys menu: hand-formed burgers with free toppings, hot dogs, sandwiches, fries and shakes. None of the official pages lists a Tampa-only item.

From the official US menu (fiveguys.com/menu):

  • Burgers: fresh patties formed by hand, with as many toppings as you like at no extra charge.
  • Hot dogs: all-beef, split lengthwise and grilled.
  • Sandwiches: including a BLT and a grilled cheese.
  • Fries: boardwalk-style, Five Guys style or Cajun style, in several sizes.
  • Shakes: a vanilla base with the mix-ins of your choice.
  • Drinks: Coca-Cola and Dr Pepper sodas.

The Tampa store pages repeat the chain’s usual claims (Collier Center page): fresh beef and no freezers, and fries cooked twice in 100% refined peanut oil. They also promise more than 250,000 burger combinations and over 1,000 shake combinations, plus free peanuts while you wait. Five Guys says customers eat nearly 300,000 of those peanuts a week.

My order: a bacon cheeseburger, Cajun fries and a shake. The Cajun fries come up in Tampa reviews more than anything else on the menu, and Kennedy’s shakes get singled out. At those prices, a cheeseburger, regular fries and a bottled Coke come to $29.73 before Florida sales tax and tip. That’s steep for a counter burger. Five Guys Tampa reviews: what diners say

Reviews of Five Guys in Tampa are solid, not glowing. On Restaurantji they range from 3.8/5 at Kennedy to 4.2/5 at Falkenburg and E Fowler. Diners praise the fresh toppings, the Cajun fries and the staff, and complain about prices and orders that come out wrong. Here’s the breakdown as of October 24, 2026.

Five Guys Tampa reviews: Restaurantji ratings for all 5 locations as of October 24, 2026. Falkenburg Commons 4.2/5 (251 reviews), University Collection Mall on E Fowler Ave 4.2/5 (138), Carrollwood on N Dale Mabry Hwy 3.9/5 (90), The Fairgrounds on Elm Fair Blvd 3.9/5 (179) and Collier Center on W Kennedy Blvd 3.8/5 (176). (Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0, data from Restaurantji (October 24, 2026), rendered with QuickChart)

What reviewers like (Restaurantji, Foursquare, Wanderlog):

  • Burgers piled with fresh toppings.
  • Cajun fries and big portions. “A regular order of fries is enough for 2 or 3 people,” one Foursquare reviewer wrote.
  • Friendly staff, a clean dining room at The Fairgrounds, a crew that keeps up during the rush at Falkenburg, and good shakes at Kennedy.

What they complain about:

  • Waits at peak times at Falkenburg.
  • High prices, and a sulfur smell from the soda fountain at Carrollwood (Dale Mabry).
  • Undercooked fries, overcooked burgers or missing toppings on some orders.

My take: the five are too close to justify driving across town for a 0.4-point gap. If you’re torn between two, Falkenburg and University Collection Mall have the best scores on the most reviews. Whichever one you choose, check your bag before you leave: missing toppings are the most common complaint, and they’re easy to catch at the counter.

Five Guys and peanut allergies

With a peanut allergy, Five Guys is a risky choice. According to the official menu and store pages, the fries are cooked in 100% refined peanut oil and free peanuts sit out in the dining room. Talk to the staff and read the chain’s US allergen information before you order.

Five Guys: a quick history

Five Guys was founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell and their sons, and started franchising in 2002. In May 2026 it had 1,999 restaurants in 28 countries and territories, 1,517 of them in the United States (Wikipedia). The full story, with sourced figures and customer ratings, is on our Five Guys brand profile. For where the burger itself came from, read our history of the hamburger.

FAQ: Five Guys Tampa

How many Five Guys are there in Tampa?

There are 5 Five Guys restaurants in the city of Tampa, according to the official locator: Collier Center (3841 West Kennedy Blvd.), Carrollwood (14398 N. Dale Mabry Hwy), University Collection Mall (2702 E. Fowler Ave), The Fairgrounds (8606 Elm Fair Blvd) and Falkenburg Commons (2530 S. Falkenburg Rd.).

Which Five Guys in Tampa should I pick?

If you’re visiting, pick Collier Center at 3841 West Kennedy Blvd., the most central location. If you’re choosing on reviews, Falkenburg Commons and University Collection Mall share the top Restaurantji rating, 4.2/5.

What are Five Guys Tampa’s hours?

They couldn’t be verified. Depending on the source, listings open at 10 a.m. or 11 a.m. and close at 9:30 p.m., 10 p.m. or midnight. Check the official store page or call the restaurant before you go.

How much is a meal at Five Guys in Tampa?

Counter prices couldn’t be confirmed. For Kennedy, Allmenus lists a cheeseburger at $15.35, regular fries at $9.59 and a bottled Coke at $4.79, but those are likely delivery prices. Check the Five Guys app for your location.

Does Five Guys Tampa offer delivery and curbside pickup?

Yes. All 5 Tampa locations list delivery. Curbside pickup is listed at Collier Center, Carrollwood, University Collection Mall and The Fairgrounds, but the Falkenburg Commons store page doesn’t mention it.

Is there a Five Guys at Tampa International Airport?

None of the 5 Tampa locations on the official Five Guys locator is at the airport, and I couldn’t confirm one inside TPA. Check the airport’s dining directory before your flight.

Is the Five Guys in New Tampa on County Line Rd. closed?

Yes. The Five Guys at 6431 E. County Line Rd. closed on March 15, 2026, according to Neighborhood News. The nearest options up north are at 25599 Sierra Center Blvd. and 28894 Wesley Chapel Blvd.

Can I eat at Five Guys with a peanut allergy?

It’s risky. The fries are cooked in 100% refined peanut oil, and free peanuts are served in the dining room. Speak to the staff and read Five Guys’ allergen information before ordering.

Is one order of Five Guys fries enough for two?

Usually, yes. Reviewers keep mentioning the portion size, and one Foursquare reviewer says a regular order feeds 2 or 3 people.

Does Five Guys have vegetarian options?

The official US menu includes a grilled cheese, plus fries and shakes. I couldn’t confirm that the veggie sandwich is on the Tampa menu, so check the app or ask at the counter.

Do I need a reservation at Five Guys in Tampa?

No. None of the Tampa store pages lists reservations: they list dine-in, takeout, delivery and curbside. To skip the line, order ahead online or in the app.
