Big Fernand Marseille: Address, Hours and Menu

Big Fernand Marseille is at 48 La Canebière, 5 minutes from the Old Port: hours, menu, average spend and the old addresses to ignore.

By Thomas, Restaurants writer, FrancePublished November 24, 2026

Big Fernand Marseille is at 48 La Canebière, 13001 Marseille, on the ground floor of the Mercure Marseille Canebière Vieux-Port hotel, 5 minutes’ walk from the Old Port. It opened in March 2026 and offers dine-in, takeaway and delivery. TheFork scores it 8.6/10 from 305 reviews, with an average spend of €18 per person. Ignore the Docks de la Joliette and Prado addresses that directories still show: neither operates as an active restaurant. Outside the city centre, the chain also runs a counter at Marseille Provence Airport, in Terminal 1. I checked every fact below on 5 November 2026.

Big Fernand Marseille address: on La Canebière, near the Old Port

Big Fernand Marseille is at 48 La Canebière, 13001, in the hotel building that once housed the Le Capucin bar-brasserie. Accor puts its 90-room Mercure 5 minutes on foot from the Old Port (Vieux-Port) and 10 minutes from Saint-Charles railway station. The chain’s own page adds the Opéra and the Centre Bourse shopping centre as neighbours.

The pedestrianised lower Canebière, with the Palais de la Bourse on the left (July 2023). Centre Bourse is right behind it, and Big Fernand Marseille is a short walk further up the avenue. (Photo: Chabe01, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Canebi%C3%A8re\_-\_Marseille\_I\_(FR13)\_-\_2023-07-22\_-\_1.jpg)

Big Fernand calls this a “new format”: a restaurant inside a hotel, with its own door onto the street. You don’t need to cross the hotel lobby. Franchise trade site Toute-la-franchise dated the opening to the week of 17 March 2026.

In my view, this is the first Big Fernand in Marseille that sits where people actually walk. The first branch was tucked inside the converted Docks warehouses at La Joliette, and the second was in a shopping centre in the 8th arrondissement. La Canebière is the city’s main avenue. If I had an hour to kill between trains at Saint-Charles, this is where I would go: straight down the avenue, no transfers.

I could not confirm the nearest metro or tram stop from an official source, so I have left it out.

Big Fernand Marseille opening hours

Big Fernand Marseille La Canebière is listed as open every day from 11am to 11pm, with continuous service, but the chain has not published those hours itself. PagesJaunes gives 11am–11pm, Monday to Sunday. Mappy says it is “open until 11pm”, serves all day, and stays open on Mondays and through August.

Not every listing agrees. 1 directory has it opening at noon, and the official restaurant page shows no hours at all. For a late-morning visit, check the Google or TheFork listing on the day.

No phone number either. Directories give 2, one of them a Paris line, and the chain’s page shows neither.

Big Fernand Marseille menu: what to order

Big Fernand Marseille serves the chain’s national menu: 10 signature burgers, a build-your-own option, 3 smash burgers and a steak-frites. Nothing on it is exclusive to Marseille. The chain calls its burgers hamburgés and gives each one a first name.

Its own tagline is “the burger recipe rethought around good produce from our regions”: bakery bread, beef from French cattle breeds and raw-milk cheeses.

The 10 signature hamburgés

The official menu sorts the 10 hamburgés by main filling:

  • Beef: Big Fernand, Bartholomé (billed as the star burger) and Victor
  • Chicken: Paolo, Philibert and Paulin
  • Fish: Steve
  • Vegetarian: Gabin and Lucien
  • New: Benoît Saint Denis

Smash burgers, salads and the rest of the menu

Besides the hamburgés, there is a build-your-own burger, 3 smash burgers (Smash Poitrine, Double Smash and Smash Fernand) and a Steak-Frites.

  • Big Falades: salads with chicken, vegetarian or salmon
  • Big Toastés: toasted sandwiches with tomme cheese and caramelised onions
  • Les Tendres and Gargandines
  • Fernandines: the chain’s homemade fries
  • Desserts: ice cream, brookie, cookie, fondant baulois, flan and crème dessert
  • Drinks: homemade iced tea, lemonade, sodas, craft lemonades, wine and beer

Which burger to choose at Big Fernand

For a first visit, order the Bartholomé. The chain calls it its star burger. On the 2022 Toulouse menu, it was beef, raclette, smoked bacon and caramelised onions, a heavy, cheesy burger built for a cold evening rather than a hot Marseille lunch. In summer, I would rather take a smash: a thinner, crisper patty, and less of it. Gabin and Lucien are the vegetarian options. The Benoît Saint Denis is the newest name on the board.

Big Fernand Marseille prices

Expect to spend around €18 per person at Big Fernand Marseille: that is TheFork’s average spend for the La Canebière branch. I could not confirm individual burger prices. The official online menu shows none, TheFork’s menu was not accessible, and I could not find the branch on Uber Eats or Deliveroo.

For a rough scale, the last official prices we have are in our Big Fernand Toulouse guide. In 2022, a hamburgé cost €13–13.50 there and the Big Formule meal deal €19–19.50. Those are 4-year-old prices from another city, not the Marseille menu. You may also come across €9.50 on Privateaser: that figure belongs to the closed Prado branch.

Big Fernand Marseille reviews

Big Fernand Marseille La Canebière scores 8.6/10 on TheFork from 305 reviews, as shown on Mappy on 5 November 2026. For a branch this young, the number moves fast: another reading showed 8.7/10 from 161 reviews. Any score you see is a snapshot.

I have not eaten at the La Canebière branch yet, so Burger Breton gives it no rating of its own, and we never pass off a third-party score as our verdict. I could not check its Google rating either, and I found no Tripadvisor or Yelp listing for the address. I have not summarised what reviewers like or dislike, because I could not read the review texts.

How I checked: this guide draws on the chain’s official pages, its online menu, local and trade press, and the TheFork, Mappy and PagesJaunes listings, all checked on 5 November 2026.

Can you book or get delivery at Big Fernand Marseille?

Yes: Big Fernand Marseille takes table bookings on TheFork and delivers in central Marseille. The official page lists dine-in, takeaway and delivery, and the chain takes online orders at commande.bigfernand.com. Which delivery apps carry the branch, I could not confirm.

A burger near the Old Port in Marseille

Big Fernand on La Canebière is a 5-minute walk from the Old Port. If you come down on foot from Saint-Charles station, you can’t miss it.

For other burger options around the Vieux-Port, Noailles, Cours Julien and La Joliette, start from our Marseille burger hub, where every guide we publish on the city is listed.

Big Fernand at Marseille Provence Airport

Big Fernand also has a restaurant in Terminal 1 of Marseille Provence Airport (MRS), opened on 6 May 2025. It shares a new 500 m² food court with Starbucks and Il Gusto Di Napoli. The airport is in Marignane, not in Marseille itself.

The history of Big Fernand in Marseille

La Canebière marks Big Fernand’s return to central Marseille, after the Docks and Prado branches dropped off. Marseillecentre.fr reports that the chain had “scaled back” at Docks Village in recent years and calls La Canebière its comeback to a busy location.

About Big Fernand

Big Fernand is a French gourmet burger chain founded in Paris in 2011 by Steve Burggraf, Alexandre Auriac and Guillaume Pagliano. Its first restaurant opened in January 2012. The chain is owned by Bluegem Capital Partners and had 45 restaurants as of 22 September 2026. The full history, figures and customer ratings are on our Big Fernand brand profile.

Big Fernand Marseille FAQ

Where is Big Fernand in Marseille?

Big Fernand Marseille is at 48 La Canebière, 13001 Marseille, on the ground floor of the Mercure Marseille Canebière Vieux-Port hotel. It is about 5 minutes on foot from the Old Port and 10 minutes from Saint-Charles station.

What are Big Fernand Marseille’s opening hours?

PagesJaunes lists Big Fernand Marseille La Canebière as open from 11am to 11pm, Monday to Sunday, with continuous service, including Mondays and August. The chain has not published these hours itself, so check before you go.

Is Big Fernand at the Docks de la Joliette closed?

The Docks de la Joliette branch, opened in 2015 at 1 rue des Docks, no longer appears as an active restaurant. The chain’s city-centre branch is now at 48 La Canebière. The Prado branch at 41 boulevard Michelet is permanently closed.

How much does a meal at Big Fernand Marseille cost?

TheFork lists an average spend of €18 per person at Big Fernand Marseille La Canebière. Individual burger prices for the branch were not on the official menu when I checked on 5 November 2026.

Can you book a table at Big Fernand Marseille?

Yes. Big Fernand Marseille La Canebière takes bookings online through TheFork.

Does Big Fernand Marseille deliver?

Yes. The restaurant delivers in central Marseille, and Big Fernand takes online orders at commande.bigfernand.com. I could not confirm which delivery apps serve the branch.

Are there vegetarian burgers at Big Fernand Marseille?

Yes. The menu has 2 vegetarian hamburgés, Gabin and Lucien, plus a vegetarian Big Falade salad. Pescatarians also have the Steve, a fish burger.

Is Big Fernand Marseille halal?

I found no verified information on halal meat at Big Fernand Marseille. Ask the restaurant directly before ordering.

Where is Big Fernand at Marseille Provence Airport?

Big Fernand is in the Terminal 1 food court at Marseille Provence Airport, alongside Starbucks and Il Gusto Di Napoli, and opened on 6 May 2025. The airport has not confirmed whether it is before or after security.

Which burger should I order at Big Fernand?

The Bartholomé, the chain’s star burger, is the safest first order. Pick Gabin or Lucien for a vegetarian option, or one of the 3 smash burgers for a thinner, crisper patty.
