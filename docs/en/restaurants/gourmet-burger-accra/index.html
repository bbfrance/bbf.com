Gourmet Burger in Accra: Where to Eat 1 Now

Burger & Relish, billed as Ghana's first gourmet burger bar, has closed. Junkies Burgers has 3 Accra branches and burgers from GH₵85 to GH₵200.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished November 19, 2026

For a gourmet burger in Accra today, start with Junkies Burgers: 3 branches, open every day until 11:45pm, burgers from GH₵85 to GH₵200 on its official menu. The restaurant that started the genre, Burger & Relish, opened in Osu in November 2014 as “Ghana’s first gourmet burger restaurant and cocktail bar”. Both of its branches are now listed as permanently closed. I checked every fact below on 17 November 2026.

1 correction up front. Selassie Atadika’s Midunu keeps turning up in Accra burger searches. It is a fine-dining company, not a burger restaurant.

Where to eat a gourmet burger in Accra now: Junkies Burgers

Junkies Burgers is the open, founder-led burger bar I would check first in Accra: 3 branches, prices posted online, open daily until 11:45pm. It began as a home-made project by “3 friends”, “born in the heart of the COVID-19 pandemic”, and now trades under the parent company HBO Holdings Company (official site). Its tagline is “Accra’s Best Burgers”. That is its own claim, not my verdict.

I have not eaten there yet, so this is not a rated review and there is no score. The site does not name the 3 founders, and I won’t guess. I could not verify its Google or TripAdvisor ratings either.

Junkies Burgers’ burgers cost GH₵85 to GH₵200 on its official menu, checked on 17 November 2026. A plain beef burger is GH₵100, and the top-end Fully Loaded XL is GH₵200.

This is a crowd-pleasing menu rather than a chef’s 1: beef, cheese, bacon, egg, 1 fish and 1 chicken option, and nothing as local as shito. Dirty sodas cost GH₵75 to GH₵100. Prices are as listed online and can change in store.

Is Burger & Relish in Osu still open?

No. Both Burger & Relish branches are closed. Wanderlog lists the Osu branch on Dadebu Road as “Permanently closed”, with a Google rating of 4.2/5 from 1,184 reviews, and the East Legon branch at A&C Shopping Centre the same way, with 3.9/5 from 23 reviews.

I could not find a closure date for either 1. The clearest sign is in Osu itself. According to a City Life Accra listing, Bôndai, a Mediterranean-Japanese restaurant from an independent, family-run group, now occupies the glass-fronted building at 14th Lane and Dadebu Street. I found nothing linking Bôndai to the Burger & Relish founders.

The Counter, the US custom-burger franchise that had an outlet at Accra Mall, is gone too: Time Out Accra now marks The Counter as closed. Several top search results for a gourmet burger in Accra still send you to both. Don’t trust them.

Burger & Relish Accra: the story of Ghana’s first gourmet burger restaurant

Burger & Relish opened on Monday 24 November 2014 in central Osu, billed as “Ghana’s first gourmet burger restaurant and cocktail bar”, according to Time Out Accra. It brought chef-style burgers, house sauces and a rum-focused bar to a city where a burger mostly meant fast food. By 2016 it was on Time Out’s list of the best burgers in town.

Oxford Street, Osu’s busy main avenue in Accra, photographed in 2006 at the Papaye fast-food restaurant: Osu is the district where Burger & Relish opened Ghana’s first gourmet burger restaurant in 2014 (Photo: Guido Sohne, CC BY-SA 2.0, https://www.flickr.com/photos/82552702@N00/226898152)

Who founded Burger & Relish?

Time Out Accra names George Moffet and Marcus Morgan-Etty as the co-founders of Burger & Relish. Its 2015 “My Accra” interview presents Marcus Etty as the owner.

What was on the Burger & Relish menu?

The launch menu crossed American smokehouse burgers with Asian flavours. Time Out Accra listed the Smokin Joe (secret house BBQ marinade, cheese sauce, crispy smoked bacon, homemade pickles and rosemary salt fries), the Seoul Burger (soy-glazed beef patty, gochujang mayonnaise and Asian coleslaw) and a Thai Chicken Burger with ginger, coriander, chilli and lime mayo.

By January 2016, Time Out’s best burgers in Accra list spelled it Smokin’ Jo: a “quality beef patty with smoked crispy bacon, cheddar, the 14-spice house barbecue sauce and mayo”. Vegetarians had a chickpea-and-coriander burger or a mushroom burger. In January 2018, Eat.Drink.Lagos reported the Frenchie (beef patty, onions, truffled mushrooms and raclette) at GH₵45.

The Independence Burger: jollof rice balls and shito mayo

The Independence Burger was Burger & Relish’s Ghanaian-fusion signature: a burger served with jollof rice balls and shito mayo, according to Time Out Accra’s 2017 review. Shito is Ghana’s dark, spicy pepper sauce; jollof is its best-known rice dish. Of everything on the old menus, this is the burger I would have ordered. It is also the one idea none of today’s Accra burger menus I checked has picked up.

Shito, Ghana’s dark, spicy black pepper sauce, photographed in Accra in 2025: Burger & Relish mixed it into the mayo on its Independence Burger. Illustrative photo, not taken at Burger & Relish (Photo: Umuhiera, CC0 1.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ghanaian\_shito.jpg)

The same review lists 3 Little Piggies, a chorizo and beef burger with crispy smoked bacon, bacon jam, homemade BBQ sauce and crispy onions. Mains cost GH₵35 to GH₵70 in 2017, at the corner of 14th Lane and Dadebu Street, open noon to 11pm Sunday to Wednesday and noon to 1am Thursday to Saturday.

East Legon expansion in 2016

Burger & Relish opened a second branch on 25 August 2016 at A&C Mall in East Legon, according to Time Out Accra. That was the brand at its peak: 1 site in Osu’s nightlife district, 1 in residential East Legon.

Is Midunu a burger restaurant? A correction about Selassie Atadika

No. Midunu is Selassie Atadika’s private and nomadic dining company and chocolate brand, built on “New African Cuisine”, and none of the sources I checked mention a burger. Some pages and story pitches tie her to Accra’s burger scene. They are wrong.

Atadika founded Midunu in Accra in 2014. She holds an International Affairs degree from Columbia University, worked for the United Nations for about a decade, then graduated from the Culinary Institute of America (Basque Culinary World Prize, Food Forever).

Midunu’s official site offers private dining, nomadic dining, events and a chocolate line, all by appointment or reservation, and gives no public address. Its nomadic dinners are 4-course meals served at different places across Accra (Plant Forward Kitchen, MyJoyOnline).

In April 2025, TIME named her one of 6 honorees of its 2025 Earth Awards; the Ghana News Agency calls her the first Ghanaian to receive it. Yale lists her as a MacMillan Fellow. Book Midunu for a serious take on African cooking. Not for a burger.

FAQ: gourmet burgers in Accra

What is the best burger in Accra right now?

For a founder-led burger bar that is open now, Junkies Burgers has 3 branches and posts its prices online; it calls itself “Accra’s Best Burgers”. Our Accra burger page lists more addresses.

How much does a burger cost in Accra in cedis?

At Junkies Burgers, burgers cost GH₵85 to GH₵200 on the official menu checked on 17 November 2026, with a plain beef burger at GH₵100. Gourmet mains at Burger & Relish cost GH₵35 to GH₵70 in 2017.

Where are the Junkies Burgers branches in Accra?

Junkies Burgers has 3 branches: East Legon (next to Pinocchio), Labone (3 Peter Ala Adjetey Ave) and North Legon (New Lucky Electricals car park, after Wisconsin University). All open daily from 10am to 11:45pm.

Is Burger & Relish in Osu still open?

No. Wanderlog lists both the Osu and the East Legon branches as permanently closed, and the Bôndai restaurant now occupies the Osu building. No closure date has been published.

Who founded Burger & Relish?

Time Out Accra names George Moffet and Marcus Morgan-Etty as the co-founders. The restaurant opened in Osu on 24 November 2014, billed as Ghana’s first gourmet burger restaurant and cocktail bar.

What was the Independence Burger?

The Independence Burger was Burger & Relish’s Ghanaian-fusion signature, served with jollof rice balls and shito mayo, according to Time Out Accra’s 2017 review.

Is Midunu in Accra a burger restaurant?

No. Midunu is Selassie Atadika’s private and nomadic dining company and chocolate brand, focused on New African Cuisine. It works by reservation only and does not serve burgers, according to its official site.
