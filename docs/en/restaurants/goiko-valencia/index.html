Goiko Valencia: Addresses, Opening Hours, Menu and Reviews (2026)

Which of Valencia's Goiko restaurants to pick, when they open, what to order and what diners say, with every fact checked on 8 October 2026.

By AlexPublished October 8, 2026

Goiko has three confirmed restaurants in Valencia: Calle de la Paz 9 and Martínez Cubells 4 in the old town (Ciutat Vella), and Avenida d’Aragó 26 in El Pla del Real. My pick for most visitors is Goiko La Paz 9. It’s the most central, it’s the only one whose official page lists a terrace, and it has more reviews than Aragó, the other rated address. All three open at midday (Aragó at 1 pm Monday to Wednesday) and close at 11:30 pm early in the week, midnight Thursday to Sunday. Each serves a burger you won’t find at any other Goiko.

How I checked: every address, phone number and opening time below comes from Goiko’s own restaurant pages, read on 8 October 2026. Opening dates come from the Spanish trade and local press that covered each launch, and ratings from Gastroranking, which pulls in Google, Just Eat and Tripadvisor. Where I couldn’t confirm something, I say so.

Goiko La Paz 9: the best Goiko in central Valencia

Goiko La Paz, at Calle de la Paz 9 in the La Xerea part of Ciutat Vella, is the Goiko I’d send you to in central Valencia. It opened in early 2017 as the chain’s second restaurant in the city (Valencia Plaza).

Calle de la Paz in Valencia’s Ciutat Vella. Goiko La Paz is at number 9 on this 19th-century street, a short walk from the cathedral. 1200×1600 px. (Photo: Zarateman, CC0 1.0, via Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Valencia\_-\_Calle\_de\_la\_Paz.jpg))

No Goiko in the city lists more services. The official page has dine-in, takeaway, delivery, online booking, a pet-friendly terrace, free wifi, a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table and a wheelchair-accessible entrance.

Its exclusive is La Coqueta: oregano provolone, caramelised tomatoes, fried aubergine, red onion and batavia lettuce. Of the three, it’s the one I’d order first. It’s built on vegetables rather than more meat and more sauce, which suits a city that still eats from its huerta.

Gastroranking combines 9,640 reviews for La Paz into a score of 8.8/10: 9.0 on Google (8,424 reviews), 7.8 on Just Eat (715) and 7.4 on Tripadvisor (501). It ranks 4th of 53 burger restaurants in Valencia on that site (Gastroranking).

Goiko Martínez Cubells 4: the original Goiko in Valencia

Goiko Martínez Cubells, at Martínez Cubells 4 (46002), is where Goiko started in Valencia, and the second old-town option. It opened on 18 January 2017 with 160 m² and 70 seats. Valencia was the chain’s first city outside Madrid (Alimarket).

The exclusive here is La Senyoreta: ñora pepper aioli, Roncal cheese, a sweet potato and bacon hash brown, mushrooms and a fried green pepper. Ñora, the small dried pepper that goes into rice dishes across Valencia, Alicante and Murcia, gives the aioli its regional accent. The Catalan-Valencian name helps too.

The official page lists free wifi, a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table and step-free access. There’s no terrace listed, so don’t plan on eating outside.

Goiko Aragó 26: the pick away from the old town

Goiko Aragó, at Avenida d’Aragó 26 in El Pla del Real, makes sense if you’re staying east of the old town or heading to a match at Mestalla. It opened on Saturday 13 May 2017 as Valencia’s third Goiko (Official Press).

Its exclusive is La Explotada: a Monterey Jack and leek croquette, confit sun-dried tomatoes, fried leek, lamb’s lettuce, house mayonnaise and crispy bacon. Services listed on the official page are wifi, a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table and step-free access.

On Gastroranking, Aragó 26 scores 8.8/10 across 9,056 reviews: 9.0 on Google (7,837), 8.4 on Just Eat (976) and 7.4 on Tripadvisor (244) (Gastroranking).

Goiko Valencia menu: what to order

The Goiko menu in Valencia is the chain’s national menu plus one exclusive burger per restaurant. The official menu covers starters, burgers, smash burgers, Chicken Lovers, a kids’ menu, salads, desserts, drinks and set menus, with a gluten-free section and an allergen page.

Signature Goiko burgers

The national line-up includes the Tres Razas, Kevin Truff, Kevin Beast, Kevin Bacon, Kevin Costner, M-30, Pigma, Retro, Moza and Veggie Mercury. The Kevin Bacon is the chain’s best-seller: bacon is minced into the beef patty, then topped with crispy onion and cheese (Tendance Hôtellerie). First time at Goiko? Order that one.

Vegetarian and gluten-free options

Vegetarians get the Veggie Mercury. Coeliacs get a dedicated gluten-free section and an allergen page. Read the allergen page before ordering; burger names won’t tell you what’s in the bun.

Goiko Valencia prices

Budget roughly €10–20 per person at Goiko in Valencia. Gastroranking puts the average at under €20 a head at Aragó 26, and Restaurant Guru gives €10–20. Goiko’s online menu shows no prices, and I couldn’t find an official Valencia price list, so I’m not quoting per-burger prices here. Goiko Valencia reviews: what diners say

Diners rate Goiko well in Valencia, but it depends where you look. Google is the most generous: both rated addresses, La Paz 9 and Aragó 26, score 9.0/10. Tripadvisor is cooler at 7.4. Just Eat, which mostly reflects delivery, lands in between.

Gastroranking’s summary for Aragó 26 lists the recurring themes:

  • Praise: generous gourmet burgers, attentive service, quick turnaround, clean dining room.
  • Complaints: slow delivery (around an hour reported), burgers that are hard to eat neatly, and the temperature in the dining room.

My reading: the complaints are mostly about delivery. Eat in if you can. Review totals also differ from one aggregator to the next, so treat them as orders of magnitude rather than exact counts.

Booking, takeaway and delivery

Book a table at Goiko Valencia online at goiko.com/es/reservas. Delivery and takeaway orders go through goiko.com/es/delivery. La Paz 9 officially offers all three. For the other two addresses, the booking page shows what’s available.

Goiko’s story: from Madrid to Valencia

Goiko is a Spanish burger chain founded in Madrid in 2013 by Andoni Goicoechea (Goikoetxea), a Venezuelan doctor of Basque descent (Viernes Gastronómicos). Valencia, in January 2017, was its first city outside Madrid.

Growth came fast. By February 2019 Goiko had 57 restaurants, and private equity firm L Catterton owned 80% of the company (Alimarket). The 100th opened in September 2022 at the Nueva Condomina centre in Murcia (Alimarket). Lyon followed in February 2023 (Tendance Hôtellerie).

Is Goiko worth it compared with Valencia’s independent burger joints?

Yes, as a reliable choice. It’s not the most original burger in the city. Goiko gives you long hours, online booking, steady 9/10 Google scores and one burger per address you can’t get anywhere else. Gastroranking ranks La Paz 4th of 53 burger restaurants in Valencia. My advice: go to La Paz for La Coqueta, or to Aragó if you’re near Mestalla, and keep an evening for the independent bars in Russafa or El Carmen. FAQ: Goiko Valencia

How many Goiko restaurants are there in Valencia? Three are confirmed on goiko.com: La Paz 9, Martínez Cubells 4 and Aragó 26. Press reports mention a fourth near the City of Arts and Sciences (2019) and a fifth in Alfafar (2023), but I could not confirm either was open on 8 October 2026.

Which Goiko is closest to Valencia’s old town? Goiko La Paz, at Calle de la Paz 9 (46003), is in La Xerea in Ciutat Vella. Goiko Martínez Cubells 4 (46002) is also in Ciutat Vella.

What are Goiko Valencia’s opening hours? La Paz and Martínez Cubells open Monday to Wednesday 12:00–23:30 and Thursday to Sunday 12:00–00:00. Aragó 26 opens at 13:00 Monday to Wednesday and closes at the same times. Goiko notes that hours can vary.

Do I need to book at Goiko Valencia? Booking is optional but available online at goiko.com/es/reservas. La Paz 9 officially offers online booking and has more reviews than Aragó 26, the other rated address. Book there for weekend evenings.

What is Goiko’s most famous burger? The Kevin Bacon, the chain’s best-seller: a beef patty with bacon minced into it, topped with crispy onion and cheese.

Which burgers are exclusive to Goiko in Valencia? La Coqueta (La Paz 9), La Senyoreta (Martínez Cubells 4) and La Explotada (Aragó 26) are confirmed. La Torrá and La Alfa Romero come from earlier press reports.

Does Goiko have vegetarian and gluten-free options? Yes. The Veggie Mercury is the vegetarian burger, and the menu has a gluten-free section plus an allergen page.

Does Goiko deliver in Valencia? Yes, through goiko.com/es/delivery, and La Paz 9 lists delivery among its services. Reviews for Aragó 26 mention delivery times of around an hour.

How much does a meal at Goiko Valencia cost? Roughly €10–20 per person according to Gastroranking and Restaurant Guru. Goiko’s online menu doesn’t publish prices.
