McDonald's Rabat: Addresses, Menu Prices, Hours

3 verified McDonald's addresses in Rabat, Glovo prices in dirhams (Big Mac 55 MAD), conflicting late-night hours and what 3,069 Google reviews say.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished November 19, 2026

McDonald’s has several restaurants in Rabat, and the one to know is McDonald’s Rabat Agdal at 105 Avenue Fal Ould Oumeir (phone +212 537-670171). It is the most-reviewed branch in the city, at 3.6/5 from 3,069 Google reviews. 2 more have addresses you can trust: 1 at Rabat-Agdal high-speed train station and 1 in the city centre at 3 Avenue Amir Moulay Abdallah. When I checked Glovo on 13 November 2026, a Big Mac cost 55 MAD and a Best Of Big Mac meal 68 MAD. The meat is halal and there is no pork on the menu.

McDonald’s Rabat at a glance

McDonald’s is the largest burger chain in Morocco, with 80 restaurants as of December 2025. Finding the right 1 in Rabat is harder than it should be. Directories copy each other, the official store pages would not load for me, and 3 sources give 3 closing times for the same branch. So I checked every fact below on 13 November 2026, and where sources disagree, I say so.

McDonald’s Rabat Agdal: the flagship branch

McDonald’s Rabat Agdal, at 105 Avenue Fal Ould Oumeir, is the best-documented and most-reviewed McDonald’s in Rabat. 3 sources give the same address and phone number: the McDonald’s Maroc store page, RabatCity and Restaurant Guru. Agdal is the university and office district south-west of the centre. The Rabat-Salé tramway, running since 2011, links it to downtown in a few stops.

A Rabat-Salé tram running through Agdal, the easiest way to reach McDonald’s Rabat Agdal from the city centre without a car (photo from 2012). 4288×2412, loading lazy. (Photo: simon_music, CC BY-SA 2.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tramway,\_Rabat\_(7001556526).jpg)

Restaurant Guru, a review aggregator, ranks the branch 21st of 478 fast-food places in Rabat and reports card payment, a terrace and wheelchair access. Kiosks, a McCafé, a play area, Wi-Fi, parking? I could not confirm any of them for this branch, so they are not in this guide.

McDonald’s Rabat opening hours: is it open late?

Yes, the Agdal branch stays open past midnight, but nobody agrees on exactly when it closes. The McDonald’s Maroc page shows 10:00 to 01:30 in search results. Restaurant Guru says 09:30 to 02:00. A third directory claims 10:00 to 03:00. The official page blocked my access, so call 05 37 67 01 71 before you head over at 1 a.m.

McDonald’s at Rabat-Agdal train station

There is a McDonald’s at Rabat-Agdal, the Al Boraq high-speed stop, but its exact address and hours are unconfirmed. Directories put it on Avenue Mohamed Triki or on Avenue El Hadj Ahmed Charkaoui. The only listing with hours (10:00 to 20:00 daily, plus a drive-thru) would not open for me. Catching the 7 a.m. train to Casablanca? Bring breakfast.

McDonald’s Rabat city centre

The city-centre branch is at 3 Avenue Amir Moulay Abdallah, phone 05 37 20 23 09. Telecontact files it as “Rabat Medina”, and lists its operator as a limited company founded in 2008. I found no recent ratings or opening hours I could tie to this address.

A Big Mac costs 55 MAD and a Best Of Big Mac meal 68 MAD on Glovo in Rabat, as of 13 November 2026. These are delivery prices from a Glovo store simply called “McDonald’s Rabat”, which does not say which branch fills the orders, and counter prices may be lower. 1 price-tracking site has shown a Big Mac at 39 MAD and at 55 MAD on different dates. In Casablanca, guide-casa.ma put it at 59 MAD in July 2026, as quoted in our best burgers in Casablanca guide. Read the tables below as a ceiling, not a receipt.

Sides, drinks and desserts

Sides start at 25 MAD, drinks at 13 MAD and desserts at 18 MAD. McShaker Fries in chili or BBQ flavour cost 25 MAD, McFlavor Fries 27 MAD. The cheapest drink is local bottled water: Oulmès at 13 MAD, Sidi Ali at 15 MAD. Desserts run from a donut at 18 MAD to a brownie at 23 MAD and a McFlurry at 30 MAD. 2 salads, the Caesar (42 MAD) and the Pêcheur (39 MAD), round out the menu. Feeding a group? The 21-piece Chicken Box is 116 MAD.

What is only on the McDonald’s Morocco menu

McDonald’s Morocco sells several items you won’t find in the US, and the most local of them come with sauce algérienne. It is a spicy, slightly tangy mayonnaise found in snack bars across France, Belgium and the Maghreb. If I had 1 order to place here, it would be McFlavor Fries with that sauce, not another Big Mac. A Big Mac is the same everywhere; the fries are not.

  • Grand McWrap Chicken with sauce algérienne: 58 MAD.
  • Grand McWrap Beef with Tasty sauce: 59 MAD.
  • McFlavor Fries, topped with cheddar and crispy onions or with sauce algérienne: 27 MAD.
  • McFizz, a sparkling drink in Mojito, Piña Colada, Passion fruit or Grapefruit flavour: 22 MAD.
  • Chicken Mythic: 70 MAD, one of the priciest burgers on the menu.

Is McDonald’s halal in Morocco?

Yes. McDonald’s Morocco serves only halal meat and has no pork on its menu. That covers the beef patties, the chicken and the fish sandwiches, and the trays carry the IMANOR halal seal. The Royal Cheese you order in Rabat is the halal version of a burger sold around the world.

Vegetarian options at McDonald’s Rabat

There is no vegetarian burger on the McDonald’s Rabat menu on Glovo as of 13 November 2026. Meat-free options are fries, desserts, drinks and possibly the salads, depending on how they are made. Pescatarians have the Filet-O-Fish (49 MAD) and the Double Filet-O-Fish (55 MAD).

According to Restaurant Guru, the Agdal branch scores 3.6/5 from 3,069 Google reviews, 3.8/5 from 207 Facebook reviews and 6.1/10 from 90 Foursquare reviews. My verdict: if you want a McDonald’s in Rabat, make it Agdal. It has the best scores, the clearest address and the latest hours. Going by 3 1,000 reviews, order a burger and a McFlurry there and get your coffee somewhere else. At lunchtime, when the students arrive, take it away.

McDonald’s Rabat delivery: Glovo and McDelivery

McDonald’s delivers in Rabat through Glovo, for a 9 MAD delivery fee, and its store page showed a 92% satisfaction score on 13 November 2026. The station branch is also listed with McDelivery, the chain’s own delivery service. Expect Glovo menu prices to run higher than at the counter.

McDonald’s in Morocco and Rabat: a short history

McDonald’s opened its first Moroccan restaurant in December 1992 on the Aïn Diab corniche in Casablanca, at the initiative of Abderrahmane Belghiti. La Vie éco and Bladi.net describe Morocco as the first African and Arab country to get the chain. An older La Vie éco article says Rabat got its own McDonald’s “2 years after” Casablanca, so around 1994, and that the network was then run as a master franchise by First Rest International.

Most of the growth is recent. 30 of today’s restaurants opened in the last 5 years. The 80th opened on 18 December 2025 in Terminal 1 of Casablanca’s Mohammed V Airport, with 95 jobs. According to Challenge.ma and Morocco World News, McDonald’s Morocco employs 5,500 people and aims to create 3,500 more jobs by 2030. Driss El Alami was CEO at the time of that opening.

McDonald’s Rabat FAQ

How many McDonald’s are there in Rabat?

Rabat has at least 3 McDonald’s with a verifiable address: Agdal, Rabat-Agdal station and the city centre. Restaurant Guru lists 6 more, but no official list could be checked in November 2026, so I do not give a total.

What time does McDonald’s Rabat Agdal close?

McDonald’s Rabat Agdal closes after midnight. Sources give 01:30, 02:00 or 03:00, so call 05 37 67 01 71 before a late visit.

How much is a Big Mac in Morocco?

A Big Mac costs 55 MAD on Glovo in Rabat, and a Best Of Big Mac meal costs 68 MAD (checked on 13 November 2026). Counter prices may be lower.

Does McDonald’s deliver in Rabat?

Yes. McDonald’s delivers in Rabat through Glovo, with a 9 MAD delivery fee as of 13 November 2026. The station branch is also listed with McDelivery.

Is there a McDonald’s at Rabat-Agdal train station?

Yes, a McDonald’s is listed at Rabat-Agdal high-speed train station. Its exact address and its 10:00–20:00 hours are not confirmed, so do not count on it for an early or late train.

Is there a McDonald’s drive-thru in Rabat?

The Rabat-Agdal station branch is listed with a drive-thru (McDrive), but only in a directory listing I could not open. Check McDonald’s Maroc’s drive-thru page before you go.

Is there a vegetarian burger at McDonald’s Rabat?

No. The Rabat menu on Glovo has no vegetarian burger as of 13 November 2026. Fries, desserts and drinks are meat-free, and the Filet-O-Fish suits pescatarians.

Since when has McDonald’s been in Morocco?

McDonald’s has been in Morocco since December 1992, when it opened on the Aïn Diab corniche in Casablanca. Rabat followed about 2 years later, according to La Vie éco.
