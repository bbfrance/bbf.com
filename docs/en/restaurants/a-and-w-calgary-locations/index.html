A&W Calgary: Locations, 24-Hour Spots and Menu

Which Calgary A&W to pick by quadrant and time of night, the 24-hour list, what to order in October 2026 and what it costs with Alberta's 5% GST.

By Olivia, Restaurants writer, Canada and the US NortheastPublished October 29, 2026

A&W Calgary is not one restaurant but dozens: the first page of Yellow Pages alone lists 32 A&W locations across the city (checked October 24, 2026). For a central pick, go to the A&W at 507 17th Avenue SW in the Beltline. For a late-night burger, head to 516 16th Avenue NW instead: Yellow Pages lists it as open 24 hours, and it holds 4.6/5 on more than 4,000 Uber Eats ratings. Order the Teen Burger, or the limited-time Hot Honey BBQ Teen Burger launched across Canada on October 8, 2026. In Alberta, the only tax added to the menu price is the 5% federal GST.

How this A&W Calgary guide was built

This guide comes from directories, delivery platforms and A&W’s own press releases, not from a visit. I did not eat at these Calgary locations, so this article carries no Burger Breton rating. I would rather say that plainly than fake a verdict. A&W Canada’s official store locator (web.aw.ca) did not load when I checked on October 24, 2026, so A&W itself confirms none of the addresses, hours or services below. Every location comes from Yellow Pages, Canpages, circulaire-en-ligne.ca or shopping-canada.com, and I flag where those directories disagree. Call the restaurant or check the A&W app before you go. I used the same method in my A&W Montreal locations guide and my A&W Ottawa Rideau Centre guide.

How many A&W restaurants are there in Calgary?

Calgary has dozens of A&W restaurants, but there is no official total. The first Yellow Pages results page for “A&W Calgary AB” lists 32 detailed A&W entries; its headline count of 126 results includes other businesses. For scale, A&W Canada had 1,113 restaurants nationwide on September 6, 2026, according to its third-quarter results.

Best A&W in Calgary: 507 17th Avenue SW, Beltline

The A&W at 507 17th Avenue SW (Calgary, AB T2S 0A9, 587-351-3450) is the most central pick for anyone spending an evening on 17th Avenue. It sits in the Beltline, on the strip of bars and restaurants that our Calgary burger page sends readers to.

Its hours are the weak spot. Yellow Pages lists the phone number but no hours. Only circulaire-en-ligne.ca says it is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and I could read that claim only in a search summary because the page itself was blocked. After midnight, I would not bet on it. Call first, or go to 16th Avenue NW.

A&W 16th Avenue NW: the best-documented 24-hour location

The A&W at 516 16th Avenue NW (Calgary, AB T2M 0J4, 403-220-9027) is the safest late-night bet in Calgary. Yellow Pages lists it as open 24 hours, and its Uber Eats store (“A&W 16th Ave NW”) shows 4.6/5 on more than 4,000 ratings. That is a delivery rating, not a Google one. I read it from a search summary on October 24, 2026, because the Uber Eats page returned an error. Still, no other Calgary A&W comes close to that much supporting data, and it is where I would send a friend at 2 a.m.

A&W in downtown Calgary

Downtown Calgary has two A&W counters listed by Yellow Pages: 520 3rd Avenue SW (T2P 0R3, 403-266-0785) and 605 5th Avenue SW (T2P 3H5, 403-269-2762). Both showed “closed now” when I checked on Saturday, October 24, which points to office-tower hours. No directory gave their weekday hours. On a weekend, walk to 17th Avenue instead. Shopping-canada.com lists the A&W in the Scotia Centre (T2P 2W2) as closed.

A&W Chinook Centre and Macleod Trail

The A&W at Chinook Centre is at 14F-6455 Macleod Trail SW (Calgary, AB T2H 0K8, 403-640-4127). Directories showed it open until 9 p.m. on the day I checked. Canpages and Restaurant Guru also list an A&W nearby at 6666 Macleod Trail SW. I could not confirm whether that is a second restaurant or the same one listed twice, so I keep them separate.

Is there an A&W at Calgary airport (YYC)?

Probably, but I could not confirm where. A&W was among the 18 food counters announced for Calgary International Airport’s new international terminal, according to Daily Hive in December 2017. Yellow Pages lists an A&W at 2000 Airport Road NE (T2E 6W5, 403-291-1999), which is the airport’s postal address. The exact spot (zone, gate, before or after security) and whether it is open today are both unconfirmed, so check the YYC dining directory before your flight.

Hot Honey BBQ Teen Burger (limited time)

The Hot Honey BBQ Teen Burger is a limited-time Teen Burger launched at every A&W in Canada on Thursday, October 8, 2026. It stacks a grass-fed beef patty, cheddar, bacon, lettuce, onion and tomato under a hot honey BBQ sauce made with Bull’s-Eye, according to A&W’s October 6 release and Daily Hive. No end date has been announced. I have not tasted it. On paper it is a Teen Burger with a sweeter, hotter sauce, so if you already like the original, it is worth one order before it goes.

The Teen Burger and the Burger Family

The Teen Burger is A&W Canada’s signature burger. It belongs to the Burger Family the chain launched in 1963, alongside the Papa, Mama and Baby Burgers. If you order only one thing, make it this one: it is the burger the chain built its name on. To rebuild it at home, my colleague Clara tested an A&W Teen Burger copycat recipe with Teen Sauce.

Is A&W’s Smash Burger still on the menu?

I cannot confirm that the Smash Burger is still on the menu in Calgary. A&W launched its first-ever smash burger across Canada on May 25, 2026, for a limited time, as a single or a double with cheddar, pickles, onions, sauce and a brioche bun. No official source I could read says when it ended, so check the app.

Vegetarian options: the Plant-Based Burger

A&W’s vegetarian option is the Plant-Based Burger. Its pea and soy protein patty replaced the Beyond Meat Burger on the permanent menu across Canada on September 22, 2025. The standard build includes mayonnaise, so ask to hold it if you avoid eggs.

Is A&W beef really hormone-free?

Yes, by the chain’s own standard: since 2013, A&W Canada has served beef from cattle raised without added hormones or steroids, as CTV News reported at the time.

A&W Canada vs A&W in the US

A&W Canada and A&W in the United States are separate companies. A&W Canada opened its first restaurant on Portage Avenue in Winnipeg in 1956. It became independent of the American chain in 1972, when Unilever bought the Canadian business, and a group of Canadian managers and franchisees bought it in 1995. Today it is headquartered in North Vancouver, trades on the Toronto Stock Exchange under the symbol AW, and is Canada’s second-largest burger chain after McDonald’s (2022 data).

The chain is still growing. It counted 1,113 restaurants on September 6, 2026, up from 1,094 on December 28, 2025, with system sales of CAD 1.92 billion in 2025. On October 16, 2026, it announced a new strategy built on premium beef burgers, chicken, plant-based food, global flavours, new formats and the expansion of Pret A Manger. Our A&W Canada brand profile has the full history and figures.

Practical tips before you go

A&W has not confirmed the practical details for any Calgary location: drive-thru, delivery, online ordering, payment methods, wheelchair access and parking. Here is what you can do with what is known.

  • Check hours in the A&W app or by phone. Directory hours are third-party and may describe only the drive-thru.
  • For late nights, start with 516 16th Avenue NW. It is the 24-hour listing with the most supporting data.
  • Downtown on a weekend, skip the office-tower counters. Both 3rd Avenue SW and 5th Avenue SW showed closed on a Saturday.
  • Budget with the 5% GST only. Alberta has no provincial sales tax.
  • Compare delivery and in-app prices. Uber Eats lists the 16th Avenue NW store, and delivery menus can cost more than the counter.

A&W Calgary FAQ

How many A&W restaurants are in Calgary? There is no official count. Yellow Pages lists 32 detailed A&W entries on its first results page for Calgary, spread across all four quadrants.

Which A&W is open 24 hours in Calgary? Yellow Pages lists nine locations as open 24 hours, including 516 16th Avenue NW, 5120 Shaganappi Trail NW, 80 Crowfoot Way NW and 80 Glendeer Circle SE. Circulaire-en-ligne.ca adds five more. Neither directory says whether this covers the dining room or only the drive-thru.

Is there an A&W on 17th Avenue SW? Yes, at 507 17th Avenue SW (T2S 0A9, 587-351-3450) in the Beltline. One directory lists it as open 24 hours, but that is not confirmed.

Is there an A&W at Calgary airport? A&W was announced for YYC’s international terminal in 2017, and Yellow Pages lists an A&W at 2000 Airport Road NE (403-291-1999). Its exact location and current status are not confirmed.

How much is a Teen Burger at A&W in Calgary? No official Calgary price is published. As a national benchmark, Narcity recorded $8.79 for a Teen Burger and $14.09 for a Teen Burger combo in August 2025, before the 5% GST.

What is the Hot Honey BBQ Teen Burger? A limited-time Teen Burger launched across Canada on October 8, 2026, with a grass-fed beef patty, cheddar, bacon, lettuce, onion, tomato and a hot honey BBQ sauce made with Bull’s-Eye. No end date has been announced.

Does A&W Calgary have vegetarian burgers? Yes. The Plant-Based Burger, made with pea and soy protein, has been on the permanent menu across Canada since September 22, 2025. It comes with mayonnaise by default.

Can I get A&W delivered in Calgary? Yes, at least from the 16th Avenue NW location, which has an Uber Eats store. Check the delivery apps and the A&W app for your address.

Are A&W Canada and A&W USA the same company? No. A&W Canada has been independent from the American chain since 1972 and Canadian-owned since 1995.
