Burger on 16 Kuala Lumpur: Menu, Prices, Branches

Who is behind the halal-friendly Imbi burger bar, what to order, how prices have moved since 2017, and both addresses checked in November 2026.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished November 24, 2026

Burger on 16 is an independent gourmet burger restaurant in Kuala Lumpur, known for Australian beef, house-made sauces and one signature dish: the California Cheese Skirt. It is still open. When I checked its official site on 6 November 2026, it listed two branches. The original is at 16, Lorong 1/77a in Imbi, behind Berjaya Times Square on the edge of Bukit Bintang; the second is in Kota Damansara, Petaling Jaya. Three partners founded it, and the Malaysian press names one of them: Mohanad Mudhafer. My take, before the details: it is not the best burger in Kuala Lumpur, but it is one of the most dependable addresses near Bukit Bintang.

Who owns Burger on 16? The three founders

Burger on 16 was founded by three partners, and Mohanad Mudhafer is the only one named in published sources. The official site calls it the work of “three burger enthusiasts” with a “shared passion”. Older listings are plainer: the shop was “born from a simple vision of 3 individuals from the love of Burgers and Coffees”.

After the 2019 Asia ranking, the magazine Malaysian Business wrote that Mohanad Mudhafer established Burger on 16 “with two other partners”. Yelp lists a “Mohanad M.” as the business owner. That is thin: one editorial source and one platform listing. Worse, the Malaysian Business page now returns an error, so I could only read it through a search snippet. I found no names for the other two partners, and nothing that describes any of the three as a chef.

A note on Chef Wan

Chef Wan has no link to Burger on 16 in any source I found. Chef Wan is the Malaysian celebrity chef Datuk Redzuawan Ismail. His Kuala Lumpur restaurant is De.Wan 1958, which serves Malaysian food at Level 2-2, The LINC KL, 360 Jalan Tun Razak, and is listed in the MICHELIN Guide. It does not serve burgers. If you searched for “Chef Wan burger”, Burger on 16 is not his.

From an Imbi coffee shop to Asia’s top 50

Burger on 16 started as a small burger-and-coffee shop in Imbi and became one of four Malaysian names on Big Seven Travel’s 2019 list of Asia’s 50 best burgers. Its founding year is unclear. Yelp says 2016, but the earliest dated review I could find, by EatDrinkKL, is from June 2017. So: open since at least 2017.

In August 2019, Big Seven Travel ranked Burger on 16 no. 43 in its 50 Best Burgers in Asia, as reported by The Star. The jury praised its “classic American style food with really good shakes and some proper tasty desserts”. Three other Malaysian venues ranked higher: myBurgerLab at no. 2, Killer Gourmet Burgers at no. 15 and Buns Burger Bar at no. 29 (TallyPress, September 2019).

What to order: the Burger on 16 menu

Order the California Cheese Skirt first. It is the burger Burger on 16 is known for: a house-made Aussie beef patty “blanketed with a skirt of shredded cheese” that crisps around the meat. The rest of the menu covers beef, chicken and vegetarian burgers, fries, snacks, desserts and milkshakes. In October 2023, The Peak counted more than 20 burgers, all with house-made sauces and buns.

Signature beef burgers

The beef burgers are the core of the menu, all built on Australian beef. Apart from the California Cheese Skirt, reviewers between 2017 and 2023 singled out these:

  • Vampire Hates Garlic: a garlic-forward burger, RM18.90 in June 2017 (EatDrinkKL).
  • Cheese Loaders: a beef patty stuffed with mozzarella, plus cheddar, RM22.90 in January 2023 (Hype.my).
  • Chk New York Swag: a double smashed beef patty with crunchy mozzarella, caramelised onions, onion rings, cheddar and honey BBQ sauce, RM22.90 in January 2023 (Hype.my).
  • Inferno Beef, Mushroom Swiss and Philadelphia Cheesesteak: cited by The Peak in October 2023.

Chicken, vegetarian and vegan burgers

Burger on 16 also serves chicken burgers, a vegetarian black bean burger and, as of 2023, three vegan options. The Inferno chicken burger cost RM17.90 in both 2017 and 2020; in 2017 a fried egg added RM2. In March 2020 the Crunchy Fillet chicken burger was RM17.50 (Malaysian Foodie). The Peak lists a Black Bean Burger, and Hype.my counted three vegan options in January 2023.

Fries, sides and milkshakes

Among the sides, the ones to know are the Seven Wonders Fries, the Cowboy Fries and the milkshakes Big Seven Travel praised. The Seven Wonders Fries come with a seasoning of seven herbs and spices and cost RM8.50 in 2017. Cowboy Fries were RM13.50 in 2020. The Peak also lists onion rings and buffalo wings. Shakes: Hype.my counted six in 2023. The Nutella and Peanut Butter shakes cost RM12.90 each in 2020, and the Nutella was still RM12.90 three years later. The Imbi shop also serves coffee, including lattes, as it has since the burger-and-coffee days.

Burger on 16 prices: what a burger costs

In March 2020, burgers at Burger on 16 cost RM17.50 to RM25.90. The latest dated price, from January 2023, is RM22.90 for the Cheese Loaders and the Chk New York Swag. I could not confirm 2026 prices. Every menu published by a reviewer is three to nine years old, so read the table as price history and expect to pay more today. What the history shows is restraint: the California Cheese Skirt rose only RM2 between 2017 and 2020, and the Nutella shake did not move at all between 2020 and 2023.

Getting to Burger on 16 from Bukit Bintang

If you are staying in Bukit Bintang, the Imbi branch is an easy walk: it is on a quiet street behind Berjaya Times Square. Driving is harder. Street parking there is scarce, so reviewers suggest parking at Berjaya Times Square and walking round to Lorong 1/77a.

Opening hours: check before you go

Sources disagree on the opening hours of the Imbi branch, so call it or check its listing on the day. In 2017 it opened from 11:30 am to 12:30 am. In 2020, Malaysian Foodie gave 11 am to 1:30 am Monday to Thursday and 11 am to 2 am Friday to Sunday, the same hours TripAdvisor shows. In 2023, Hype.my reported 11 am to 11:30 pm daily. The only hours reported for Kota Damansara are noon to 11:30 pm. All the sources agree on one point: it stays open late, which makes it a good late dinner after Bukit Bintang.

Is Burger on 16 halal?

Burger on 16 is described as halal and Muslim-friendly, but I found no official JAKIM halal certificate. TripAdvisor tags the Imbi branch Halal, Vegetarian friendly and Vegan options, and the food blog TheFoodBunny called it halal in November 2021. If certification matters to you, ask the staff to show the certificate before you order. Our Kuala Lumpur burger hub covers the city’s halal burger culture, from the Ramly street burger to the JAKIM logo on restaurant doors.

Burger on 16 in the Kuala Lumpur burger scene

Burger on 16 belongs to Kuala Lumpur’s gourmet burger tier, alongside myBurgerLab, Killer Gourmet Burgers (KGB) and Woodfire, while the Ramly street burger remains the local benchmark. Local guides such as kualalumpurcity.my name those restaurants most often when they list the best burgers in Kuala Lumpur.

Its rivals compete on concept. Burger on 16 competes on consistency: a coffee-shop format, Australian beef and a cheese-skirt signature. Critics clearly prefer myBurgerLab, which was no. 2 in the 2019 Big Seven Travel ranking to Burger on 16’s no. 43. Diners have been kinder. On 6 November 2026, TripAdvisor showed 4.7 out of 5 from 459 reviews, no. 3 of 158 quick-bite places in Kuala Lumpur. Keeping a rating that high for nine years is harder than winning one list.

Is Burger on 16 worth it?

Yes, if you are staying near Bukit Bintang and want a halal-friendly gourmet burger with a proper milkshake. If you have one burger meal in the city and want the critics’ pick, go to myBurgerLab instead. At Burger on 16, order the California Cheese Skirt and Cowboy Fries, and check hours and prices first, because the published ones are out of date. I have not given it a Burger Breton rating: this profile is built from the restaurant’s own pages and dated reviews, not from a visit of mine.

If you are travelling around Southeast Asia, compare it with the independent burger bars I have covered elsewhere: the smash burgers of Prime Burger in Phuket, the founder-run Lawless Burgerbar in Jakarta and our full guide to the best burgers in Jakarta. Every city we cover is listed on the Restaurants hub.

How I researched this profile

My sources are the restaurant’s official site (burgeron16.com), dated reviews from 2017, 2020 and 2023, the 2019 Big Seven Travel ranking as reported by The Star and TallyPress, The Peak’s October 2023 guide and the TripAdvisor listing, all checked on 6 November 2026. Where they conflict, on opening hours or the founding year, I give each version with its date. I left out two claims I could not verify: a monthly sales figure said to follow the 2019 ranking, and undated prices from a 2022 listicle.

Burger on 16 FAQ

Who owns Burger on 16 in Kuala Lumpur? Burger on 16 was founded by three partners. Malaysian Business names one of them, Mohanad Mudhafer, as having established the restaurant “with two other partners”. The other two partners have not been named publicly.

Is Chef Wan involved with Burger on 16? No. I found no source linking Chef Wan (Datuk Redzuawan Ismail) to Burger on 16. His Kuala Lumpur restaurant is De.Wan 1958, a Malaysian-cuisine restaurant at The LINC KL.

Is Burger on 16 halal? TripAdvisor and food bloggers describe it as halal, but I found no JAKIM halal certificate. Ask the staff if certification matters to you.

Where is Burger on 16 and how do I get there from Bukit Bintang? The original branch is at 16, Lorong 1/77a, Imbi, on a quiet street behind Berjaya Times Square at the edge of Bukit Bintang. If you drive, park at Berjaya Times Square, because street parking is scarce.

What is the best burger to order at Burger on 16? The California Cheese Skirt, an Australian beef patty wrapped in a crisp skirt of shredded cheese. It is the restaurant’s best-known burger.

How much does a burger cost at Burger on 16? In March 2020, burgers cost RM17.50 to RM25.90, and the California Cheese Skirt was RM25.90. The latest dated price, from January 2023, is RM22.90. No 2026 prices have been published, so expect to pay more.

Is Burger on 16 open late at night? Yes, it has always stayed open into the evening, but the reported closing time at Imbi ranges from 11:30 pm to 2 am, depending on the source and the year. Check with the branch before a late visit.

Does Burger on 16 have vegetarian or vegan burgers? Yes. The menu includes a Black Bean Burger, and Hype.my counted three vegan options in January 2023. TripAdvisor tags the Imbi branch Vegetarian friendly and Vegan options.

How many branches does Burger on 16 have? Two: the original in Imbi, Kuala Lumpur, and a second at Dataran Sunway, Kota Damansara, in Petaling Jaya, Selangor.

Why was Burger on 16 named one of the best burgers in Asia? Big Seven Travel ranked it no. 43 in its 50 Best Burgers in Asia in August 2019, praising its “classic American style food with really good shakes and some proper tasty desserts”.
