Best Burgers in Manchester 2026: Top 10 Spots

Where to eat the best burgers in Manchester in 2026: 10 picks in the city and Salford, with addresses, award records and 1 closure to skip.

By Emma, Restaurants writer, United KingdomPublished November 18, 2026

The best burgers in Manchester in 2026 are at Burgerism on Piccadilly Gardens, So Famous on Blackett Street for halal smash burgers until 4am, Honest Burgers on Bridge Street and Mollie’s Diner in St John’s. Burger & Beyond, Doug’s Hamburgers, That Burger Place, Hawksmoor, Elnecot and Slap & Pickle complete my top 10. I checked every name against sources on 14 November 2026. 1 name you won’t find: Almost Famous, the chain that started Manchester’s burger wave, closed in January 2025.

The Manchester burger hub covers the city’s burger scene as a whole. This guide is the ranked shortlist.

How I ranked Manchester’s burger restaurants

I ranked these 10 burger restaurants on 4 things: national award records in 2026, the signature burger as local critics describe it, consistency across independent guides, and proof that the place is still trading in 2026.

I started from the guides that rank for this search, including Manchester’s Finest (updated 5 June 2026), Secret Manchester (25 July 2025) and Confidentials. Then I cross-checked every name against the 2026 finalist lists of the National Burger Awards and the British Burger Awards, and against the local press.

Awards count most, but not blindly. Honest Burgers won the biggest prize of the year and still sits third, because I could not confirm that its winning burger is served in Manchester. I dropped any address I could only find in 1 older guide. Google ratings are out too: I didn’t record them with a date, and an undated star rating tells you nothing. Prices appear only where a source dates them.

1. Burgerism, Piccadilly Gardens: the best smash burger in Manchester

Burgerism is my No. 1 because it is the one Manchester-born brand on this list that reached a 2026 national final, and it now has a dining room in the middle of town.

It started as a dark kitchen on an industrial estate in Salford, under co-founder and CEO Mark Murphy. On 11 February 2026 it opened its first eat-in restaurant at One Piccadilly on Piccadilly Gardens, according to The Manc. It is the brand’s fifth site.

Order The Cheesed: 2 smashed patties, cheese, lettuce, tomato, pickle, onion and house sauce in a seeded brioche bun. It’s a classic build with no gimmicks, which is exactly why I rank it first. 22 seats and no bookings: expect a queue at peak times.

  • Address: One Piccadilly, Piccadilly Gardens, Manchester
  • Distinction: finalist, Best of Northwest England, British Burger Awards 2026 (listed as Burgerism, Salford)
  • Good to know: in September 2026 Burgerism opened in Meanwood, Leeds, its first permanent site outside Greater Manchester (Restaurant Online)

2. So Famous: the best halal and late-night burgers in Manchester

So Famous is the place for a halal burger in Manchester, and the only pick on this list that stays open until 4am every night.

The whole menu is halal: smash burgers and buttermilk fried chicken from certified suppliers, with a fryer kept just for the fries, as the So Famous website explains. If you need one burger after midnight in Manchester, this is where I’d send you.

So Famous is a double finalist at the British Burger Awards 2026, in Burger Restaurant of the Year and King Burger of the Year. The ceremony took place in Wolverhampton on 9 November 2026. The results were not published when I checked on 14 November, so for now it is a finalist.

  • Address: Unit D, Aldow Industrial Park, Blackett Street, Manchester M12 6EJ
  • Hours: daily, 11:45am to 4am
  • Halal: 100% halal menu

3. Honest Burgers, Bridge Street: the brand behind the UK’s best burger of 2026

Honest Burgers created The Honest, the burger that won the National Burger Awards 2026, and its Manchester branch is on Bridge Street, between Spinningfields and Deansgate.

The Honest is a smashed dry-aged beef patty with thick-cut smoked bacon, cheese, beef and onion relish, onion and pickles, developed by co-founder Tom Barton. It won on 24 March 2026 at Big Penny Social in London, the first year the brand entered (Time Out).

The catch: I could not confirm that The Honest is on the Manchester menu, and that is the only reason a national champion sits third here and not first. Ask before you order. For the chain’s history and figures, read our Honest Burgers brand profile.

  • Address: 36 Bridge Street, Manchester M3 3BT
  • Area: Spinningfields / Deansgate

4. Mollie’s Diner, Old Granada Studios: the best burger in St John’s

Mollie’s is a motel and American-style diner from the Soho House group, and its double cheeseburger made the 2026 national final.

Mollie’s Manchester opened on 5 December 2025 in the Old Granada Studios, in the St John’s district (The Manc). The diner seats 110.

The Mollie’s Double Cheeseburger stacks 2 aged-beef patties with American cheese, burger sauce, house relish and pickles. It was a National Burger Awards 2026 finalist. Time Out files that entry under Oxford and Secret Manchester under Manchester, so I credit the Mollie’s brand rather than this branch.

  • Address: Old Granada Studios, 4 Atherton Street, Manchester M3 3GS
  • Area: St John’s

5. Burger & Beyond, House of Social: the London cult burger’s first northern site

Burger & Beyond brought its award-finalist Bacon Butter Burger to Manchester, its first site outside London, inside the House of Social food hall.

Tom Stock and Craig Povoas started the brand as a food truck, then opened their first permanent restaurant in Shoreditch in 2018. Their Manchester counter is in House of Social, which opened on 19 August 2025 (The Manc).

The Bacon Butter Burger is dry-aged beef with double American cheese, crispy pancetta, brown butter mayo and onion, and it was a National Burger Awards 2026 finalist. Sources disagree on the street address of House of Social, and food-hall line-ups change, so look at the brand’s own channels first.

6. Doug’s Hamburgers, Circle Square: Norwegian smash burgers on Oxford Road

Doug’s Hamburgers is an Oslo burger brand whose first UK site sits at Circle Square on Oxford Road, and its signature is the Oklahoma smash.

Doug’s has 3 restaurants in Norway. The Manchester branch is run by the team behind Madre and Medlock Canteen. The Oklahoma smash uses aged beef with onion pressed into the patty as it cooks.

The order with a sense of humour is the Happier Meal: burger, fries and a martini. In February 2025, Confidentials paid £6 for the Classic and £9.95 extra for the Happier Meal, and scored the restaurant 14.5/20 (Confidentials). Treat those prices as a 2025 baseline.

  • Address: 1 Circle Square, 3 Symphony Park, Manchester M1 7FS
  • Area: Oxford Road

7. That Burger Place, Salford: the local smash-burger favourite

That Burger Place is a Salford-born smash-burger bar that has reached the National Burger Awards final twice.

Ryan and Megan McDermott founded it in Swinton in 2017 and cook Los Angeles-style smash burgers with grass-fed beef from a British butcher. In July 2025 they closed their Walkden and Bury sites to focus on 1 restaurant in Salford (Salford Now). Shrinking to 1 site to get the burgers right is a choice I respect.

The brand was a National Burger Awards finalist in 2022, then again in 2024 with the Golden Gate Smash. To try the technique at home, my colleague Alice’s bacon smash burger copycat recipe walks through the press.

  • Address: 327 Bolton Road, Salford M6 7GU

8. Hawksmoor, Deansgate: the best steakhouse burger in Manchester

Hawksmoor is where to go for a steakhouse burger in Manchester: grass-fed beef and bone marrow, served in a former Victorian courthouse on Deansgate.

1 source tops the Hawksmoor Hamburger with Ogleshield, another with Stichelton. The prices differ too, and I could not confirm whether it is a lunch-only dish. Read the current menu on the Hawksmoor website before you book a table for it.

  • Address: 184-186 Deansgate, Manchester M3 3WB

9. Elnecot, Ancoats: a Welsh wagyu cheeseburger

Elnecot is the Ancoats pick, a neighbourhood restaurant on Cutting Room Square known for its Welsh wagyu cheeseburger.

The burger comes with cheddar, onion, pickle and an umami glaze, according to Manchester’s Finest and Confidentials. I could not confirm it is on the 2026 menu.

  • Address: Cutting Room Square, 41 Blossom Street, Ancoats, Manchester M4 6AJ

10. Slap & Pickle, Society: Yorkshire smash burgers by the Bridgewater Hall

Slap & Pickle serves smash burgers made with heritage-breed Swaledale beef from a counter in the Society food hall, a short walk from the Bridgewater Hall.

The brand was born in Leeds in 2017, and the burger to try is the Personal Cheesus. Food-hall counters come and go, and I found no 2026 source confirming this one, so check Slap & Pickle’s official channels before a concert night.

  • Address: Society, Basement, 100 Barbirolli Square, Manchester M2 3BD

Closed: Almost Famous is no longer open in Manchester

Almost Famous is closed: the chain shut all its sites in Manchester, Liverpool and Leeds and announced its liquidation on 27 January 2025.

It opened in the Northern Quarter in 2012 and is often credited with starting Manchester’s burger wave (The Caterer). Some guides still list it in 2026. Don’t make the trip.

Which area of Manchester to choose for burgers

For burgers, Deansgate and Spinningfields have the most picks on this list, and Ancoats, Piccadilly and Salford are each worth a dedicated trip.

  • Deansgate and Spinningfields: Honest Burgers and Hawksmoor, with Slap & Pickle at Barbirolli Square nearby.
  • St John’s: Mollie’s in the Old Granada Studios.
  • Piccadilly and Oxford Road: Burgerism on Piccadilly Gardens and Doug’s at Circle Square.
  • Ancoats: Elnecot on Cutting Room Square.
  • Salford: That Burger Place on Bolton Road, and Burgerism’s roots.
  • Northern Quarter: the old home of Almost Famous. None of my 2026 top 10 is there.

Also worth knowing

Local guides often list 3 more names, but I couldn’t confirm they are still trading in 2026, so they are not ranked.

  • What’s Your Beef, Kargo MKT, Central Bay, Unit 32, Quayside MediaCity, Salford M50 3AG. 2 friends from Rochdale run it. The Stateside Deluxe is a 5 oz grass-fed, aged beef patty with bacon.
  • Tender Cow at Mackie Mayor, 1 Eagle Street, Manchester M4 5BU. An undated review recommends its Mackie cheeseburger and chips; I left the price out because I couldn’t date it.

FAQ: best burgers in Manchester

Where can I eat the best burger in Manchester? In 2026, the strongest picks are Burgerism on Piccadilly Gardens, So Famous on Blackett Street, Honest Burgers at 36 Bridge Street and Mollie’s Diner in the Old Granada Studios. All 4 belong to brands that reached or won a national award final in 2026.

What is the best smash burger in Manchester? Burgerism’s The Cheesed is my pick for the best smash burger in Manchester. Strong alternatives are So Famous, the Oklahoma smash at Doug’s Hamburgers and the LA-style smash burgers at That Burger Place in Salford.

Where can I find a halal burger in Manchester? So Famous, Unit D, Aldow Industrial Park, Blackett Street, M12 6EJ, serves a 100% halal menu of smash burgers and buttermilk fried chicken, with certified suppliers and a fryer used only for fries.

Where can I get a burger late at night in Manchester? So Famous is open every day from 11:45am to 4am, which makes it the best late-night burger in Manchester on this list.

How much does a burger cost in Manchester? Few Manchester burger prices are published with a date. In February 2025, Confidentials paid £6 for the Classic at Doug’s Hamburgers. Check each restaurant’s current menu for today’s prices.

Is Almost Famous still open in Manchester? No. Almost Famous closed all its restaurants in Manchester, Liverpool and Leeds and announced its liquidation on 27 January 2025.

Which Manchester burger restaurants have won awards? Honest Burgers won the National Burger Awards 2026 with The Honest. Burger & Beyond and Mollie’s were 2026 finalists. So Famous and Burgerism were British Burger Awards 2026 finalists; the results of the 9 November 2026 ceremony were not yet published when I checked.

Do I need to book for a burger in Manchester? Not at Burgerism, which takes no reservations at its 22-cover Piccadilly Gardens site. I could not confirm booking policies for the others, so check each restaurant’s own website.

Which area of Manchester is best for burgers? Deansgate and Spinningfields have the most options on this list, with Honest Burgers and Hawksmoor. Ancoats has Elnecot, and Piccadilly has Burgerism.
