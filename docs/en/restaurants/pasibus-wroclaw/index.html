Pasibus Wrocław: From Food Truck to Burger Chain

Who founded Pasibus, how 1 old Fiat Ducato became a chain of more than 30 restaurants across Poland, and what to order when you visit Wrocław.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished October 19, 2026

Pasibus is the burger chain that started in a Wrocław food truck. Dawid Bojarojć, Piotr Staszczyński and Jan Kulisiewicz are named as its founders. Their first truck, an old Fiat Ducato called ‘Dziadzio’, served its first burgers on 21 August 2013. By April 2025 Pasibus had 36 restaurants in 16 Polish cities, and the last full branch list, from November 2022, put 10 of them in Wrocław. No other city had as many.

It is a chain built by its founders, not a chef’s restaurant. That is what makes it worth a profile: 12 years from 1 second-hand van to dozens of branches.

Pasibus Wrocław key facts

Pasibus is a Polish burger chain from Wrocław, owned by the company Pasibus sp. z o.o. and grown from a single food truck launched in August 2013.

Who founded Pasibus?

Pasibus was founded in Wrocław in 2013 by Dawid Bojarojć, Piotr Staszczyński and Jan Kulisiewicz, according to the Polish Wikipedia entry on Pasibus and Horecatrends.

Dawid Bojarojć came first. Polish Wikipedia credits him with the first Pasibus burger recipes, developed with help from Piotr Arasymowicz.

The other 2 arrived as customers. In a 2018 interview with Horecatrends, Piotr Staszczyński said Dawid launched the truck in mid-2013 and prepared the burgers every night in a rented kitchen. Staszczyński and his brother-in-law kept coming back to eat, and about a year later they joined as partners.

What each founder does today, I could not confirm. A 2025 interview in Wiadomości Handlowe names Alicja Staszczyńska as a member of the Pasibus management board.

The Wrocław burger food truck that started it all

Pasibus began with ‘Dziadzio’ (‘Grandpa’), an old Fiat Ducato that started serving burgers on 21 August 2013 and was one of Wrocław’s first burger food trucks.

That date comes from Polish Wikipedia. Horecatrends and the city portal wroclaw.pl also describe the truck. The name stuck: in the November 2022 list of Wrocław branches, the restaurant at Powstańców Śląskich 5 was still called ‘Dziadzio’.

More trucks followed, according to Polish Wikipedia:

  • Pasibus Junior, from 21 September 2014
  • Pasiczepa, from 17 May 2015
  • Pasibydlak, an 8-metre vehicle for big events, from 1 May 2016

The first Pasibus restaurant on Skłodowskiej-Curie

The first fixed Pasibus, ‘Przystanek Pasibus’ (‘Pasibus Stop’), opened in early 2015 on ul. Skłodowskiej-Curie in Wrocław, opposite the Arkady Wrocławskie shopping centre.

The sources disagree on the exact date. Wrocław.pl announced on 15 January 2015 that it would open in mid-January. Polish Wikipedia gives 10 February 2015. In his 2018 interview, Staszczyński remembered it as ‘early 2014’. I go with 2015, since 2 separate sources agree on the year.

The Agora investment and the 2024 exit

In 2020 the media group Agora backed the growth of Pasibus, then left the deal in October 2024.

In late January 2020, Agora group companies Helios and Step Inside signed an investment agreement with some shareholders of Food for Nation, the company behind Pasibus. Helios put about PLN 5 million into Step Inside, and 6 Pasibus restaurants were open at the time, Wirtualnemedia reported.

4 years later Agora sold out. On 7 October 2024, Helios sold its 83.1% stake in Step Inside, a Pasibus franchisee, to a company called Step Outside for PLN 17.4 million, paid in instalments from August 2025 to August 2030, according to Wirtualnemedia and Agora’s own filing. The brand itself belongs to Pasibus sp. z o.o.

The first Pasibus drive-thru, near Wrocław

Pasibus announced its first drive-thru in August 2025: about 300 m² of dine-in and drive-thru space on ul. Czekoladowa at Aleja Bielany, in Bielany Wrocławskie, just south-west of the city.

Wrocław.pl and Wiadomości Handlowe presented it as the chain’s 39th location, due to open around late September or early October 2025. Mateusz Kilanowski spoke for the brand. I found no primary source confirming the opening date, so check pasibus.pl before you drive out.

Pasibus Wrocław addresses

Wrocław has more Pasibus restaurants than any other city: 10 of the chain’s 28 were in Wrocław when wroclaw.pl published its list on 3 November 2022.

What to order at Pasibus

At Pasibus, order a beef burger. Beef is the core of the menu, alongside fried-cheese, breaded-chicken and vegetarian or vegan options, a monthly special and, since June 2020, breakfast.

These named burgers appear in the sources I could check. Names and recipes change, so compare them with the current menu.

  • Bebek: the chain’s best-known name. Pasibus sold it for PLN 1 at the Warsaw Promenada opening in April 2025, per Horecatrends.
  • Boczuś Włap: the breakfast burger, with egg, bacon, cheddar and mushrooms.
  • The Italian-style burger: aged cheese and a tomato-basil sauce. Wrocław.pl called it ‘Włoski Pasterz’ in 2023, while other listings spell it ‘Włoski Pastuch’.
  • Gonzales: beef, pink sauce, rocket, red curry paste, cheddar, jalapeños and nachos, according to In Your Pocket.
  • Burger of the Month: a rotating special that has run since 1 January 2015.

The names are half the fun. ‘Pasibus’ itself plays on ‘pasibrzuch’, Polish for a glutton, and ‘bus’, the truck it started in. Many burgers carry the same kind of playful Polish nickname.

For a first visit I would order the Bebek. The reason is simple: when Pasibus opened in Warsaw and wanted a crowd, the Bebek is the burger it sold for PLN 1. That tells you which recipe the chain treats as its calling card. If you like heat, go for the Gonzales: red curry paste and nachos on the same bun is not an order you find at most chains.

How much does a burger cost at Pasibus?

I could not confirm current prices at Pasibus, so I do not give any here. The figures on delivery apps and nutrition sites are undated and may include delivery markups. The only price I can source is the PLN 1 Bebek, an opening offer at the Warsaw Promenada branch in April 2025. Check the menu board or pasibus.pl for today’s prices.

Is Pasibus the best burger in Wrocław?

Pasibus is Wrocław’s best-known home-grown burger brand, covered by both the city portal wroclaw.pl and the In Your Pocket guide. Whether it is the best is another question.

I would not crown it without tasting it next to the city’s independent burger bars, and this profile is not that test. Judge it as a chain. Its strengths are the story and the reach: 10 branches in Wrocław alone in 2022, so there is usually 1 nearby. Its weak point, for a guide like ours, is how little it says about its beef, patties and buns. For more places, see our guide to the best burgers in Wrocław.

Pasibus across Poland

Pasibus is no longer just a Wrocław story: by April 2025 it ran 36 restaurants in 16 Polish cities, including Warsaw and Gliwice.

The count moves fast. There were 28 restaurants in 14 cities in October 2022 and 36 in 16 cities in April 2025, and the drive-thru was announced as the 39th location in August 2025. If you are travelling on, see our hubs for burgers in Warsaw and burgers in Poznań.

How I put this profile together

I built this profile from dated, published sources only: Polish Wikipedia, interviews with the founders and the board in Horecatrends and Wiadomości Handlowe, Wrocław’s city portal, Agora’s stock exchange filing and Wirtualnemedia. I checked every source on 19 October 2026. When only 1 source supports a fact, the text names it.

I have not eaten at Pasibus for this article, and I say so plainly. I left out what I could not verify: beef sourcing, patty weight, bun supplier, cooking technique, current prices and current ownership. Pasibus has no rating here because this is a profile, not a review.

Pasibus FAQ

Who founded Pasibus and when?

Dawid Bojarojć, Piotr Staszczyński and Jan Kulisiewicz founded Pasibus in Wrocław in 2013. Bojarojć started the first food truck that summer, and Staszczyński joined as a partner about a year later.

Is Pasibus from Wrocław?

Yes. Pasibus started in Wrocław with a food truck in August 2013, opened its first restaurant there in 2015, and still has its registered office at ul. Parafialna 32/34, Wrocław.

Where is the original Pasibus food truck in Wrocław?

The first truck, ‘Pasibus Dziadzio’, was an old Fiat Ducato that started serving on 21 August 2013. None of the sources I read say where it is today. The Pasibus at Powstańców Śląskich 5 carries its name.

What is the best burger at Pasibus?

The Bebek is the burger Pasibus itself features most, and it was the PLN 1 offer at its Warsaw opening in 2025. Fans of spice can try the Gonzales, with jalapeños and red curry paste.

How many Pasibus restaurants are there in Poland?

Pasibus had 36 restaurants in 16 Polish cities in April 2025, and announced its drive-thru as the 39th location in August 2025. Check pasibus.pl for the current count.

Who owns Pasibus today?

The brand belongs to Pasibus sp. z o.o. (KRS 0000961192), formerly Food for Nation. I could not confirm its current shareholders. Agora’s Helios sold its stake in the franchisee Step Inside in October 2024.

Does Pasibus have vegetarian or vegan burgers?

Yes. Next to its beef burgers, the Pasibus menu includes vegetarian and vegan options, as well as fried-cheese and breaded-chicken burgers.

How much does a burger cost at Pasibus?

I could not confirm current in-restaurant prices. Delivery-app prices are undated and may include markups, so check the menu at the restaurant or on pasibus.pl.

Where is the Pasibus drive-thru near Wrocław?

The first Pasibus drive-thru was announced for ul. Czekoladowa at Aleja Bielany, in Bielany Wrocławskie just south-west of Wrocław, with an opening planned for late September or early October 2025. Check pasibus.pl to confirm it is open.

Is Pobite Gary in Wrocław a burger restaurant?

No. Kuchnia Pobite Gary at Redycka 24 in Wrocław served traditional Polish food and is listed as closed. The Pobite Gary that serves burgers is in Gdańsk-Wrzeszcz, at ul. Słowackiego 21.
