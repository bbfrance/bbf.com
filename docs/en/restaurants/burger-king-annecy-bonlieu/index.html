Burger King Annecy Bonlieu: Hours, Menu, Reviews

The town-centre Burger King near the lake: address, opening hours, delivery, prices and what diners say, plus the short answer on Big Fernand in Annecy.

By Thomas, Restaurants writer, FrancePublished November 26, 2026

Burger King Annecy Bonlieu is in the Centre Bonlieu, 1 rue Jean Jaurès, 74000 Annecy, a short walk from the lake. Its official page, which I checked on 2 November 2026, gives dine-in hours of 10:00 to 23:00, every day. There is no drive-thru. You can order Click & Collect, or get delivery through King Delivery, Deliveroo or Uber Eats. And if you came here looking for Big Fernand: there isn’t one in Annecy.

Burger King Annecy at a glance

Burger King has one restaurant in central Annecy, Bonlieu, and a second on avenue du Pont Neuf in Cran-Gevrier. The table below covers the town-centre branch. Every line comes from the official Burger King France page as I found it on 2 November 2026.

Where is Burger King in Annecy town centre?

Burger King Annecy Bonlieu is inside the Centre Bonlieu at 1 rue Jean Jaurès, 74000 Annecy. Burger King’s own restaurant page and its TripAdvisor listing give the same address.

Map of central Annecy. The marker shows Burger King Annecy Bonlieu, Centre Bonlieu, 1 rue Jean Jaurès, 74000 Annecy, between the lakefront to the east and the old town along the Thiou. Burger King Pont Neuf, 26 avenue du Pont Neuf in Cran-Gevrier, is at the western edge of the map. (Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)

That puts it in the modern centre, between the lakefront and the Vieille Ville. If you are spending the day by the water or in the old town, no other Burger King in Annecy is closer.

I could not verify parking or bus connections for the Centre Bonlieu, so they are not here. Check the City of Annecy and Sibra, the local bus network, before you travel.

Burger King Annecy opening hours

Burger King Annecy Bonlieu opens seven days a week, 10:00 to 23:00 for dine-in, according to its official page on 2 November 2026. The other channels stop a little earlier.

  • Dine-in: 10:00–23:00, every day
  • Click & Collect: 10:15–22:30, every day
  • King Delivery: 10:15–22:15, every day

One caution. An earlier search snippet of the same official page showed later hours, with dine-in until 00:30 and Click & Collect until midnight. The hours probably shift with the season. Planning a late visit? Call +33 4 50 77 72 21 first.

Delivery, Click & Collect and drive-thru

Burger King Annecy Bonlieu offers dine-in, Click & Collect and delivery, but no drive-thru: the official page lists it as unavailable.

Delivery comes through three services: Burger King’s own King Delivery, Deliveroo and Uber Eats. Prices on the apps are not always the in-store prices, so compare before you order.

Menu and prices at Burger King Annecy

Bonlieu serves the standard Burger King France menu, and the cheapest way in is the Menu Bon et Pas Cher range at €4, €5 or €6. The restaurant’s page was promoting that range when I checked it on 2 November 2026.

The same page also pushed raclette products and King Tortillas. Raclette in Haute-Savoie is an easy sell, but nothing says these items are exclusive to Annecy. Treat them as a national or limited-time offer.

I could not confirm the branch’s own prices for the Whopper, the full meals or the drinks. They vary by restaurant and by channel, so the kiosk or the Burger King app has the day’s figure. Restaurant Guru puts the typical spend at €10–20 per person.

My view: Bonlieu is a useful stop if you want something fast and cheap a few minutes from the lake, or a late dinner after the old town has closed its kitchens. If you came to Annecy to eat, spend the same €15 on a burger with melted reblochon. This creamy AOP cheese comes from the Aravis mountains and the Thônes valley, and our Annecy burger page explains where it turns up.

Restaurant Guru’s summary of the reviews comes back to the same few points:

  • vegetarian and vegan burgers that are well prepared
  • smiling staff
  • fast service
  • the ice cream

Reviewers on the same platform also mention wheelchair access. Burger King’s official page says nothing about accessibility, so call ahead if step-free access matters to you.

How many Burger Kings are there in Annecy?

At least two Burger King restaurants serve Annecy: Bonlieu in the town centre and Pont Neuf in Cran-Gevrier, a delegated commune of Annecy west of the centre.

According to the Cylex and Sluurpy directories, the second one is at 26 avenue du Pont Neuf, Cran-Gevrier, 74960 Annecy. I could not confirm its hours, phone number or services on burgerking.fr, and the directory data contradicted itself, so I am not publishing them.

Restaurant Guru hints at more branches nearby, but I could only pin down these two. The official Burger King France restaurant locator has the full list.

Is there a Big Fernand in Annecy?

No. Big Fernand has no restaurant in Annecy as of 2 November 2026. An aggregator page for Big Fernand Annecy says no restaurant is currently available in the city and lists its address as “awaiting confirmation”. Neither Wikipedia’s Big Fernand entry nor the chain’s own sites mention Annecy.

Big Fernand is a French gourmet burger chain founded in Paris in 2011 by Steve Burggraf, Alexandre Auriac and Guillaume Pagliano. It is now owned by the fund Bluegem Capital Partners. The full story is on our Big Fernand brand page.

I could not confirm which Big Fernand is nearest to Annecy, so I won’t guess. I have covered the chain elsewhere in France: see Big Fernand in Toulouse and Big Fernand in Bordeaux. Closer to Annecy, in the same Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region, our guide to the best burgers in Lyon covers the independent scene.

Burger King in France: the background

Burger King was founded in Jacksonville, Florida, in 1953 and now belongs to Restaurant Brands International. According to RBI’s annual report, it had about 19,900 restaurants in 126 countries at the end of 2025.

France had 617 of them on 31 December 2025, according to L’Officiel de la Franchise. For the chain’s history and how customers rate it across France, see our Burger King brand page.
