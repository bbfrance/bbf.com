Canalla Bistro Valencia: Ricard Camarena's Burger

Who runs it, what's in the burger, what it costs, and how this Ruzafa bistro became a two-Michelin-star chef's way into Valencia's burger scene.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished November 28, 2026

Canalla Bistro is chef Ricard Camarena’s casual restaurant at Calle Maestro José Serrano 5, on the edge of Ruzafa in Valencia, Spain. Its best-known dish is a burger of Galician beef, griddled onion and cheddar, listed at €18 on the restaurant’s Glovo delivery menu on 19 November 2026. Camarena owns the bistro. His fine-dining restaurant at Bombas Gens held two Michelin stars in the 2025 guide. VisitValencia puts the average bill at about €35 a head. My advice: book ahead, and order the burger with the bluefin tuna tartare. For the rest of the city, start with our Valencia burger city guide.

Before you read on: this is a desk-research profile. I haven’t eaten at Canalla Bistro yet, so it has no Burger Breton rating. Every fact below comes from the restaurant’s official site, Ricard Camarena’s official biography, VisitValencia, the Michelin Guide, Glovo and two Valencia papers, Valencia Plaza and El Español. I checked them all on 19 November 2026 and link each one where I use it.

Who is Ricard Camarena, the chef behind Canalla Bistro?

Ricard Camarena is a Valencian chef from Barx and the owner of Canalla Bistro. His fine-dining Ricard Camarena Restaurant, inside the Bombas Gens Centre d’Art, kept its two Michelin stars in 2025. The bistro’s full name, “Canalla Bistro by Ricard Camarena”, leaves no doubt about who is in charge, and his official biography still lists it among his current restaurants.

In January 2026 Camarena also received the Mercado de Colón gastronomy prize. The market’s traders voted for him unanimously (El Español, 10 January 2026).

Ricard Camarena timeline: from Barx to two Michelin stars

Camarena took 17 years to go from a pool restaurant in his home village to two Michelin stars in Valencia. These dates come from his official biography unless another source is named:

  • 2001–2004: runs a pool restaurant in Barx, where he was born and raised.
  • 1 October 2004: opens Arrop in Gandia with his wife, Mª Carmen Bañuls.
  • 2007: Arrop wins a Michelin star.
  • 2009: Arrop moves to the Palacio Marqués de Caro in Valencia.
  • July 2012: Ricard Camarena Restaurant opens in Ruzafa.
  • October 2012: Canalla Bistro opens (Valencia Plaza, 2014).
  • June 2014: leaves his job as gastronomic director of Ramsés in Madrid to focus on growing Canalla Bistro (same source).
  • 15 June 2017: Ricard Camarena Restaurant moves to Bombas Gens Centre d’Art.
  • November 2018: second Michelin star.
  • 2025: keeps both stars (El Español).
  • January 2026: Mercado de Colón gastronomy prize (same source).

Ricard Camarena’s restaurants in Valencia

In November 2026, Camarena’s official site lists six Valencia addresses: Ricard Camarena Restaurant (Bombas Gens), Canalla Bistro, Habitual, Bar X, Central Bar and NOBAR. Canalla Bistro is the one to pick if you want his cooking without committing to a tasting menu. Other sources give a slightly different line-up, so check his site before you plan around one of the bars.

What is Canalla Bistro? Ricard Camarena’s casual restaurant

Canalla Bistro calls itself “the shameless bistro of Ricard Camarena”. The cooking borrows from Asia, Latin America and the Mediterranean, and starts from local seasonal produce, with plenty of vegetables (canallabistro.com).

White fish served on crushed potatoes with a creamy sauce, shredded leek and chives at Canalla Bistro Valencia, April 2018: proof that the menu reaches well beyond the burger. Photo: Heidi Donat (StellarD), CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Canalla\_Bistro\_5.jpg (Photo: Heidi Donat (StellarD), CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Canalla\_Bistro\_5.jpg)

The Michelin Guide describes the room as “fun and informal”, with an open-view kitchen and “crates once used to transport oranges” (Michelin Guide). VisitValencia gives a capacity of up to 170 guests. Both details may describe an older version of the room, and I couldn’t match them against recent photos.

In its first year, the bistro shared a kitchen back to back with Camarena’s gastronomic restaurant, then also in Ruzafa (Valencia Plaza, 2013). That arrangement ended in 2017, when the fine-dining restaurant moved to Bombas Gens.

Is Canalla Bistro a Michelin-starred restaurant?

No. Canalla Bistro is listed in the Michelin Guide at the €€ price level, but it has no star. Both stars belong to Ricard Camarena Restaurant at Bombas Gens. The bistro does hold 1 Sun from Spain’s Guía Repsol, according to VisitValencia. It also appears in the We’re Smart Green Guide, which ranks restaurants on how they use vegetables. One caveat: on 19 November 2026 the Michelin listing showed up in search results, but the page returned an error when I opened it directly.

The Canalla Bistro burger: what’s in it and what it costs

The Canalla Bistro burger is made with Galician beef (vaca gallega), griddled onion, cheddar, tomato, lettuce and a mustard sauce. It was €18.00 on the restaurant’s Glovo delivery menu on 19 November 2026 (Glovo). The menu calls it “Hamburguesa de vaca gallega, cebolla a la plancha y cheddar” and lists it twice: under the bestsellers and under “Nuestros Hits de Canalla Bistro”, the house hits.

That €18 is the delivery price. I couldn’t check the dine-in price on the restaurant’s own menu, so ask when you book. The current menu doesn’t name the beef supplier, the patty weight, the cooking method or the bun, and I’d rather leave those out than guess.

The burger has been on the menu since at least 2013. It started with Comté and now comes with cheddar. Here’s what each source describes:

The Comté and the Luismi Garayar beef belong to the 2013–2017 era. Don’t expect them on your plate today.

Is it the best burger in Valencia from a Michelin chef?

It is Valencia’s clearest example of a burger from a two-Michelin-star chef, and local critics have rated it since at least 2013. In 2017, Valencia Plaza asked a panel of local experts to rank the city’s burgers: Canalla Bistró came 2nd, behind Burger Beer, with Mediterránea de Hamburguesas 3rd (Valencia Plaza, 9 February 2017).

Best burgers in Valencia according to Valencia Plaza’s 2017 expert panel: 1. Burger Beer, 2. Canalla Bistró, 3. Mediterránea de Hamburguesas. (Table: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0, data from Valencia Plaza, 9 February 2017)

Camarena gave the same article a line I like for its nerve: “No como hamburguesas fuera de mis restaurantes” (“I don’t eat burgers outside my restaurants”). He recommended only Canalla Bistro and Habitual. That ranking is nine years old now, and the cheese has changed since. For a current comparison, read our ranking of the best burgers in Valencia. For a chain-versus-chef contrast, see our Goiko Valencia guide.

My take: if you want to taste Camarena’s cooking without paying for a tasting menu, and you like burgers, this is the table to book. If the burger is all you care about, I’d look at the dedicated burger bars first. Canalla’s strength is the whole menu, not one dish.

What else to order at Canalla Bistro

Start with the bluefin tuna tartare, which both the restaurant and VisitValencia list among the house signatures. The full list:

  • Bluefin tuna tartare
  • Crispy cone with salmon and avocado tartare
  • Seasonal vegetable tempura
  • Canalla-style pastrami sandwich

The menu marks allergens and has gluten-free options (canallabistro.com). VisitValencia puts the average spend at about €35 per person. If the dine-in burger costs close to the €18 delivery price, that leaves roughly €17 for a shared starter and a drink.

Where to eat in Ruzafa, Valencia: finding Canalla Bistro

Canalla Bistro is at Calle Maestro José Serrano 5, 46005 València, where Ruzafa meets L’Eixample. Street signs and maps may show the street under its Valencian name, Carrer del Mestre Josep Serrano, or simply as Maestro Serrano. Booking, opening hours and practical information

Book Canalla Bistro through its website, by email at reservas@canallabistro.com, on TheFork or OpenTable, or by phone on +34 963 740 509. VisitValencia lists opening hours of 13:30–15:30 and 20:00–23:30, Monday to Sunday. The official site shows no hours, so call before you go.

  • Delivery: through Glovo.
  • Private hire and gift vouchers: available.
  • Accessibility: wheelchair access.
  • Dogs: welcome on a leash if you tell the restaurant in advance.
  • Dietary needs: allergens marked on the menu, gluten-free options.

Source: canallabistro.com, checked 19 November 2026.

Is there a Canalla Bistro in Madrid or Mexico City?

No Canalla Bistro is open in Madrid today. In November 2026, Valencia is the only branch I could confirm. A Canalla Bistro used to operate inside Platea Madrid, at Calle Goya 5–7, until Camarena left without renewing his contract: “We want to focus our efforts on something that we control” (Neo2). I couldn’t confirm when the Madrid bistro opened or closed.

The now-closed Canalla Bistro in Madrid, on an upper balcony of the Platea food hall on Calle Goya, with its illuminated sign above the dining tables (October 2017). In 2014 Camarena announced a Mexico City opening (Valencia Plaza). I found no source showing that it ever opened, or that it operates today.

Canalla Bistro Valencia FAQ

Who owns Canalla Bistro in Valencia?

Chef Ricard Camarena owns Canalla Bistro and opened it in 2012. His official biography still lists it among his current restaurants.

Does Canalla Bistro have a Michelin star?

No. Canalla Bistro is listed in the Michelin Guide at the €€ price level, without a star. The two Michelin stars belong to Ricard Camarena Restaurant at Bombas Gens. The bistro holds 1 Repsol Sun.

How much is the burger at Canalla Bistro?

The Galician beef, griddled onion and cheddar burger cost €18.00 on Canalla Bistro’s Glovo delivery menu on 19 November 2026. The dine-in price wasn’t verified.

What is in the Canalla Bistro burger?

Galician beef, griddled onion, cheddar, tomato, lettuce and a mustard sauce. Earlier versions, from 2013 to 2017, used Comté cheese.

Do I need a reservation at Canalla Bistro?

Booking is wise. You can reserve on the website, by email at reservas@canallabistro.com, on TheFork or OpenTable, or by calling +34 963 740 509.

Where is Canalla Bistro and which neighbourhood is it in?

At Calle Maestro José Serrano 5, 46005 València, on the border between Ruzafa and L’Eixample.

What else should I order at Canalla Bistro besides the burger?

The bluefin tuna tartare, the crispy cone with salmon and avocado tartare, the seasonal vegetable tempura and the Canalla-style pastrami sandwich.

What are Ricard Camarena’s restaurants in Valencia?

In November 2026 his official site lists Ricard Camarena Restaurant (Bombas Gens), Canalla Bistro, Habitual, Bar X, Central Bar and NOBAR. Other sources differ, so check his site before you go.

Is there still a Canalla Bistro in Madrid?

No. The Canalla Bistro inside Platea Madrid closed after Camarena chose not to renew his contract. The Valencia restaurant is the only branch I could confirm open in November 2026.

How much does a meal at Canalla Bistro cost per person?

About €35 per person on average, according to VisitValencia. The Michelin Guide places it at the €€ level.
