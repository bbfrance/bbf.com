Five Guys Naples, Italy: Address, Hours, Menu

The only Five Guys in Campania is under Napoli Centrale station: address, hours, menu, first reviews, and why there is no Ham Holy Burger in Naples.

By Sofia, Restaurants writer, Italy, Switzerland and the BeneluxPublished November 17, 2026

Five Guys in Naples, Italy, is inside Napoli Centrale, the city’s main train station, in the underground Galleria Commerciale Ipogea on Piazza Giuseppe Garibaldi, 80142 Napoli. It opens every day at 11:00. Closing is 22:00 from Sunday to Thursday and 22:30 on Friday and Saturday. It opened on Monday, 10 November 2025, and it is still the chain’s only restaurant in Campania. If you came here looking for Ham Holy Burger in Naples, there isn’t one: its 4 branches are in Milan, Settimo Torinese and Serravalle, all in the north.

My view, before the details: this is a sensible stop if you have a train to catch. It is not a reason to cross Naples. The city has burger bars that cook things only Naples would make, and I point you to them at the end.

Where is Five Guys at Naples train station?

Five Guys is in the Galleria Commerciale Ipogea, the underground shopping gallery of Napoli Centrale beneath Piazza Garibaldi. Search for the gallery, not a street number: the official listing gives none. Apple Maps shows “Piazza Giuseppe Garibaldi 98–112”, but the chain does not confirm it.

Five Guys got the space through an agreement with Grandi Stazioni Retail, the company that runs the shops in Italy’s big stations. Napolike and Grande Napoli both put it at 355 m², with 60 seats inside and 44 on a terrace. Neither says where that terrace is, inside the gallery or up on the square, and I couldn’t confirm it. I don’t have a verified walking route from the platforms or the metro either, so I won’t invent one.

Watch out for Five Guys Naples, Florida

Type “five guys naples” into an English search engine and you’ll mostly get the branch in Naples, Florida, at 1410 Pine Ridge Rd. Add “Italy” or “Napoli”. Locals search “Five Guys Napoli” or “Five Guys piazza Garibaldi”, and both go straight to the station restaurant.

When did Five Guys open in Naples?

Five Guys opened in Naples on Monday, 10 November 2025, almost 2 months late. The original date was 15 September 2025, and the Neapolitan site Napolike reported the postponement as “slittata al 10 novembre 2025”.

It was the 11th Five Guys in Italy and the first in Campania. That count dates from November 2025, and I couldn’t confirm how many Italian branches there are now. The opening had 1 local touch I liked: the first 50 customers received a limited-edition deck of carte napoletane, the Neapolitan playing cards, in Five Guys colours. Giuseppe Greco, director of operations at Five Guys Italy, said: “Portare Five Guys a Napoli rappresenta un passo importante per noi” (“Bringing Five Guys to Naples is an important step for us”).

What’s on the Five Guys Naples menu?

Five Guys Naples serves the chain’s standard Italian menu, and nothing on the official menu is made for Naples. In a city that puts genovese sauce in burgers, that’s a missed chance. The upside is that you know exactly what you’ll get.

  • Burgers and hot dogs, built with the toppings you choose.
  • Sandwiches: a BLT and a vegetarian Grilled Cheese.
  • Boardwalk fries: cooked in 100% refined peanut oil, plain or Cajun.
  • Milkshakes: vanilla base with mix-ins you add.
  • Drinks: Coca-Cola and Dr Pepper.
  • Free peanuts while you wait.

The limited-edition shake listed in November 2026 is the Peach Crumble, made with peach and Biscoff biscuit crumbs. At the opening in November 2025, the winter special was a Cinnamon Bun Shake. Want to rebuild the burgers at home? My colleague Julia tested a Five Guys cheeseburger copycat recipe, a bacon cheeseburger version and the smaller Little Cheeseburger.

Vegetarian options and peanut allergy

The vegetarian dish at Five Guys Italy is the Grilled Cheese sandwich, and Apple Maps also lists vegetarian options for this branch. With a peanut allergy, be careful here: the fries are cooked in peanut oil and free peanuts are handed out in the restaurant.

How much does Five Guys cost in Naples?

I could not confirm Five Guys Naples prices as of 3 November 2026. The official Italian menu shows none, and the figures on third-party sites are undated and unsourced, so I’m not repeating them. Station restaurants can also charge more than city-centre branches. To see today’s prices in euros, open the Naples store on order.fiveguys.it before you go.

Takeaway, delivery and accessibility

You can order Five Guys Naples for collection through Click & Collect on the official ordering site. Apple Maps also lists takeaway, delivery (including contactless delivery), Apple Pay, contactless payment, wheelchair access, accessible toilets and high chairs. That list comes from a single secondary source, and I haven’t confirmed which delivery apps carry the branch. If access or a payment method matters for your visit, call +39 081 461 3608.

Five Guys: the chain behind the Naples branch

Five Guys was founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell and their sons. By May 2026 it counted 1,999 restaurants in 28 countries and territories. Its history, sourced figures and customer ratings are on our Five Guys brand profile.

Better burgers in Naples than a station chain

If you have an hour, leave the station. For a burger that tastes of Naples, head into the historic centre or Chiaia. Puok Burger Store, the Neapolitan chain run by Egidio Cerrone, is at Piazzetta Nilo 9 and Via Cilea 104, with an Oink Lab in Sant’Antimo. It was named Miglior Paninoteca dell’Anno 2026 at Bargiornale’s Barawards. Smashtag opened its first Naples branch on Piazza San Pasquale in Chiaia on 29 November 2025.

I rank both, along with the rest of the city’s burger bars, in my guide to the best burgers in Naples. More addresses are on the Naples burger hub, and every city we cover is on the Restaurants hub.

FAQ: Five Guys Naples, Italy

Is there a Ham Holy Burger in Naples?

No. Ham Holy Burger has no restaurant in Naples or anywhere in Campania. Its official site lists 4 branches: Milano Brera (Via Palermo 15), Milano Marghera (Via Marghera 34), Settimo Torinese (Torino Outlet Village) and Serravalle (Serravalle Designer Outlet). The Naples confusion probably comes from its owners. In March 2020, Franco Manna and Giuseppe “Pippo” Montella, the restaurateurs behind Rossopomodoro, bought the chain, but it has never opened in Naples. For its Milan branches, see my guide to the best burgers in Milan. I ran into the same question in Florence, which I cover in Five Guys Florence: address, hours and prices.

Where is Five Guys in Naples?

Five Guys Naples is in the Galleria Commerciale Ipogea, the underground shopping gallery of Napoli Centrale station, Piazza Giuseppe Garibaldi, 80142 Napoli. It is the only Five Guys in Campania.

Is Five Guys Naples open on Sunday?

Yes. Five Guys Naples opens on Sunday from 11:00 to 22:00, the same as Monday to Thursday. On Friday and Saturday it stays open until 22:30.

When did Five Guys open in Naples?

Five Guys Naples opened on Monday, 10 November 2025. It had first been planned for 15 September 2025.

How much is a burger at Five Guys Naples?

I could not confirm Five Guys Naples prices as of 3 November 2026. Check the Naples store on order.fiveguys.it for current prices in euros, and remember that station branches can charge more than city-centre ones.

Does Five Guys Naples deliver?

Apple Maps lists delivery for Five Guys Naples, but I could not confirm which delivery apps carry it. Click & Collect through the official Five Guys Italy ordering site is confirmed.

Is Five Guys safe for a peanut allergy?

Be careful if you have a peanut allergy. Five Guys Italy fries its potatoes in 100% refined peanut oil and gives out free peanuts in the restaurant. Ask the staff before you order.

Does Five Guys Naples have vegetarian food?

Yes. The Five Guys Italy menu has a vegetarian Grilled Cheese sandwich, and Apple Maps lists vegetarian options for the Naples branch.
