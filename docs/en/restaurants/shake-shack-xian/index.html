Shake Shack Xi'an: None Yet, Where to Eat Burgers

No Shake Shack in Xi'an as of October 2026: the nearest Shacks, whether one is coming, and where to eat a burger in the city instead.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished October 31, 2026

As of 24 October 2026, there is no Shake Shack in Xi’an. I found no official listing, press report or local announcement of a Shack in the city. The most recent full count, from September 2024, puts Shake Shack in 16 cities of mainland China. Xi’an is not one of them. For a burger in Xi’an, your realistic options are Tastien, the Chinese chain whose bun takes its cue from Shaanxi’s own rou jia mo, and the Burger King listed at Xi’an Xianyang International Airport.

Shake Shack in Xi’an: no restaurant as of 24 October 2026. Searching 西安 Shake Shack turns up nothing official. The chain’s China store pages could not be loaded on that date (shake-shack.cn returned a 404 and shakeshack.com/locations a 403). Before you travel, search the official Shake Shack WeChat mini-program, Dianping or Amap in case a Shack opened very recently.

Is there a Shake Shack in Xi’an?

No: as of 24 October 2026, no verifiable source shows a Shake Shack restaurant in Xi’an. I went through the chain’s China expansion record, the Chinese business press and the travel platforms that list restaurants in the city. Every result for “Shake Shack Xi’an” led somewhere else: Shanghai, Beijing, a 2021 expansion announcement, or a 2023 forum thread on justxa.com with no address.

My advice is blunt. Don’t plan a Xi’an day around a ShackBurger. This is a city where people have been eating meat stuffed into flatbread for centuries, and that is the better lunch. Below are the dated facts behind the “not yet”, then the burger chains you can actually find.

The nearest Shake Shack to Xi’an

The closest cities to Xi’an on that list are Chengdu (2 Shacks) and Chongqing (1 Shack), both to the south-west. If a ShackBurger is the point of your trip, plan it for one of those stops, and check the branch is still open first: the chain closed some Chinese locations in 2024.

Map of Xi’an (marked) and the nearest Shake Shack cities, Chengdu and Chongqing, roughly 600 to 700 km to the south-west. Chengdu had 2 Shacks and Chongqing 1 in September 2024. (Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL)

Those closures included Taikoo Hui in Guangzhou, which opened in August 2023 and shut on 8 September 2024, and Tandu in Wuhan. Sina Finance reported that the year’s announced openings and closures cancelled out, 3 of each (Sina Finance, 14 September 2024).

Will Shake Shack open in Xi’an or Shaanxi?

No opening has been announced, although Shaanxi, the province of Xi’an, has been on Shake Shack’s China plan since June 2021. That month, Shake Shack and its Hong Kong franchise partner Maxim’s Caterers announced an expanded deal to open Shacks in new provinces, Shaanxi among them. The target reported at the time was 79 restaurants in China by 2031 (Inside Retail Asia, 23 June 2021).

5 years on, nothing in Xi’an. The chain’s China story still runs through the big coastal cities. Maxim’s opened the Hong Kong Shacks in 2018, and the first mainland Shack opened on 24 January 2019 at Xintiandi in Shanghai (Rachel Gouk, Jiemian). In September 2025, Tencent News reported that Shake Shack was testing a delivery and counter format in China (Tencent News, 21 September 2025). A smaller format could make a city like Xi’an easier to enter. Nothing confirms it yet, and I would not bet on a Xi’an Shack in 2027.

For the wider picture of who is opening where, see our report on burger chains’ international expansion in 2026, and the Shake Shack brand profile for the chain’s history.

Tastien: the Chinese burger with Xi’an roots

Tastien (塔斯汀) is the burger chain most worth knowing in Xi’an, because its “Chinese burger” is built on a bun inspired by rou jia mo, the classic Shaanxi street snack. The meat filling draws on rou jia mo too, according to the chain’s Wikipedia page. In Xi’an that makes Tastien less a foreign import than a fast-food echo of what the city already eats, and to me that is a more interesting thing to try than another ShackBurger.

What is Tastien?

Tastien is a Chinese fast-food chain founded in 2012 and headquartered in Fuzhou, Fujian province. It began with pizza, switched to burgers in 2017 and launched its “Chinese burger” concept in 2019 (Wikipedia). On its official site, the brand calls itself a Chinese-burger specialist with buns “rolled by hand and baked to order” (手擀现烤).

How big is Tastien?

Tastien had 11,447 restaurants at the end of 2025, in 29 provinces and 328 cities, according to the annual report of restaurant data firm 窄门餐眼 published on 12 March 2026 (Sohu). That is 2,453 more, net, than a year earlier: a 27.27% rise. The chain crossed 10,000 restaurants in October 2025, with 10,160 counted on 13 October by GeoQ (Tencent News).

For scale: roughly 300 Tastiens for every Shake Shack in mainland China, going by the 2024 Shack count.

How much does a Tastien burger cost?

A Tastien burger costs between 12 and 25 CNY, the price range of its products reported in October 2025 (Tencent News). The average spend per customer in 2025 was 18.03 CNY (窄门餐眼 via Sohu). Shake Shack in China sits in another bracket: its average ticket has historically been above 80 CNY (Tencent News, September 2025). 4 Tastien meals for 1 Shack visit.

Where to find Tastien in Xi’an

I could not verify a specific Tastien branch in Xi’an, with an address, hours and phone number I could stand behind, as of 24 October 2026. Branch names in the Baqiao district surfaced online, but without a primary source, so I am not listing them. With more than 11,000 restaurants in 328 cities, finding one should not be hard: search 塔斯汀 西安 on Amap, Dianping or Meituan once you are in the city.

Burger King at Xi’an Xianyang Airport

Burger King (汉堡王) is the one Western burger chain I found a dated listing for at Xi’an Xianyang International Airport, useful if you are flying in or out. Its Trip.com page, checked on 24 October 2026, lists the restaurant as 汉堡王(咸阳机场T2出发店), open 06:00 to 23:00, phone +86 29 8879 0091, with a 4.7/5 rating from 334 Trip.com reviews (Trip.com).

Treat the location with care. The listing’s name says Terminal 2 departures; its address says the domestic departures area of Terminal 3, first floor. The airport also opened a new Terminal 5 in 2025, which may have moved some outlets. Check 汉堡王 咸阳机场 in a map app or ask at the information desk before you count on it. A Whopper before a flight, fine. I would not ride out to the airport for one.

FAQ: Shake Shack in Xi’an

Is there a Shake Shack in Xi’an?

No. As of 24 October 2026, no verifiable source shows a Shake Shack open in Xi’an, and the city was not among the chain’s 16 mainland cities in September 2024.

Where is the nearest Shake Shack to Xi’an?

The nearest cities with a Shake Shack are Chengdu (2 restaurants) and Chongqing (1 restaurant), based on the September 2024 count. Check the branch is still open before you go.

Is Shake Shack opening in Xi’an?

No opening has been announced. Shaanxi, Xi’an’s province, was named in Shake Shack’s June 2021 expansion deal with Maxim’s, but no Xi’an Shack has followed.

What is Tastien?

Tastien (塔斯汀) is a Chinese fast-food chain founded in 2012 in Fuzhou that sells “Chinese burgers” with buns rolled by hand and baked to order. It had 11,447 restaurants at the end of 2025.

What is the link between Tastien and rou jia mo?

Tastien’s bun and minced-meat filling are inspired by rou jia mo, the traditional street snack of Shaanxi, the province of Xi’an.

How much is a Tastien burger in China?

Tastien products cost between 12 and 25 CNY (October 2025), and the average spend per customer was 18.03 CNY in 2025.

Is there a Burger King at Xi’an airport?

Trip.com lists a Burger King (汉堡王) at Xi’an Xianyang International Airport, open 06:00 to 23:00, as of 24 October 2026. The listing gives conflicting terminals (T2 and T3), and the airport opened Terminal 5 in 2025, so confirm the location on arrival.
