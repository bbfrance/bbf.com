Shake Shack Guangzhou: Addresses, Menu and Prices

Both Shake Shacks in Guangzhou in November 2026: addresses in Chinese for taxis, metro, the salted egg yolk menu, yuan prices and Dianping ratings.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished November 24, 2026

Shake Shack Guangzhou has 2 restaurants open as of November 17, 2026, both in Tianhe district: the original at Parc Central (天环广场), open since April 6, 2022, and a new 1 at Link Plaza Tianhe (天河领展广场), which opened on November 11, 2026. Budget about ¥70–75 per person (the 2024 average), or from ¥45 with the new Freestyle Combo. Both branches are also running a limited Cantonese edition built around salted egg yolk (金沙), and that, more than the ShackBurger, is the reason to go. I checked every address, phone number and price below against the cited sources on November 17, 2026.

How many Shake Shacks are there in Guangzhou?

There are 2 Shake Shacks in Guangzhou, both in Tianhe district: Shake Shack Parc Central and Shake Shack Link Plaza Tianhe. A third branch at Taikoo Hui (太古汇) opened in July 2023 and closed in early September 2024. Ignore any map listing that still shows it.

Shake Shack Parc Central: the first Shake Shack in Guangzhou

Shake Shack Parc Central is the city’s original branch, in shop B166 on level B1 of the Parc Central mall on Tianhe Road. It opened at 11 a.m. on April 6, 2022, according to the trade site Winshang. Apple Maps gives a full street number, “No. 218 Tianhe Road, Parc Central East Tower, B1 B166”, but no other source repeats it. For a taxi, I’d show the Chinese address from the table instead.

Parc Central (天环广场, Tianhuan Plaza) is a transit-oriented mall that opened in March 2016. Most of it is underground: 2 levels above ground, 3 below, 24 m tall in all, with a direct connection to the metro. The practical upshot is simple. You can reach the Shack without stepping into Guangzhou’s summer heat or rain.

Metro: how to get to Shake Shack Parc Central

The nearest station listed for Shake Shack Parc Central is Tianhe South (天河南) on the APM line, exit A, about 110 m on foot, according to Dianping. A link to Tiyu Xilu station (Lines 1 and 3) is likely given where the mall sits, but I could not confirm it, so plan your route via the APM line.

Shake Shack Link Plaza Tianhe: the new branch on Machang Road

Shake Shack Link Plaza Tianhe opened on November 11, 2026, inside the Link Plaza Tianhe mall at 36 Machang Road, east of Zhujiang New Town. Guangzhou’s Xin Kuai Bao and Sina covered the opening, themed “揾Shack新花臣” (a Cantonese play on finding a new Shack). The mall used to be called Taiyang Xintiandi (太阳新天地购物中心). Its owner, Link REIT, lists 998 parking spaces, so if you drive, this is your branch.

As of November 17, 2026, the restaurant’s unit number, floor, phone number, opening hours and nearest metro station had not been published. I’ll add them here once the chain or Dianping lists them.

Shake Shack Guangzhou opening hours

No reliable opening hours are published for Shake Shack Guangzhou: the listings for Parc Central contradict each other. Apple Maps says 7 a.m.–10:30 p.m. daily; Trip.com says 10 a.m.–10 p.m. The early start would match Shanghai, where the iapm and Grand Gateway 66 branches open at 7 a.m. (see my Shake Shack Shanghai guide). Nobody has confirmed breakfast service in Guangzhou, though. Call +86 20 8117 0668 or check the store on Dianping before an early visit.

Shake Shack Guangzhou menu: Cantonese exclusives

Shake Shack Guangzhou serves the chain’s core burgers, fries and shakes, plus a limited Cantonese edition built around salted egg yolk (金沙) that launched in both branches on November 11, 2026. Xin Kuai Bao’s opening report repeats the chain’s claim that the beef is 100% imported grain-fed Angus. For the classic patty at home, Julia’s ShackBurger copycat recipe rebuilds the burger that anchors the menu.

The 金沙 salted egg yolk edition (November 2026)

The limited “金沙” edition is Shake Shack’s Cantonese take on salted egg yolk, a flavor Guangzhou diners already know from dim sum and Cantonese seafood. According to Xin Kuai Bao and Sina, it includes:

  • A 金沙 chicken and pineapple burger
  • 金沙 salted egg yolk fries
  • A limited-edition sparkling salted lemon tea
  • Kapok-flower merchandise (a mug and a tote bag), the kapok being Guangzhou’s city flower

Shake Shack has not said how long the edition will last. If it’s on the board when you walk in, order from it: this is the only part of the menu you can’t get at any other Shack.

Desserts and beers created for the 2022 opening

For the Parc Central opening in 2022, Shake Shack created a set of Guangzhou-only desserts and drinks, according to Winshang. I could not confirm whether any of them are still on the menu:

  • Guangzhou Elixir (羊之甘露), a shake inspired by the mango, pomelo and sago dessert
  • Parc-fect Partner (天之骄仔), a frozen custard inspired by the local “chicken cake” biscuit, with black sesame and banana
  • Canton Love Story (西关小姐东山少爷), a frozen custard inspired by cheung fun rice rolls, with waffle and strawberry
  • 2 beers brewed with BRAVO BREWING CO.: a lychee ale and a Kölsch-style beer
  • The Shack Attack brownie, made by the local bakery 贝甲烘焙

Vegetarian options

Vegetarian options at Shake Shack Guangzhou are not confirmed for 2026. A mushroom-based burger, the Shroom Burger, was mentioned in 2022, but I could not verify that it is still sold. Check the menu on the WeChat mini-program or on Meituan before you go.

Shake Shack Guangzhou prices in yuan

A meal at Shake Shack Guangzhou costs about ¥70–75 per person, more than twice the roughly ¥30 spent at a nearby KFC or McDonald’s, according to 2024 figures reported by Canyin88. Dianping showed ¥72 per person at Parc Central back in 2022. Canyin88 also cited a sample bill of ¥143 for a burger, a shake and cheese fries.

The new Freestyle Combo, a first for Shake Shack in Guangzhou, lowers the entry price: a burger, fries and a drink of your choice from ¥45, according to Xin Kuai Bao. During the opening days at Link Plaza Tianhe (November 11–13, 2026), the first 100 customers who spent at least ¥68 got a free tote bag, and the merchandise could be added for ¥6 with the chicken and pineapple burger.

Shake Shack Guangzhou reviews

Shake Shack Parc Central holds 4.1 out of 5 from 10,066 Dianping reviews, as relayed by Apple Maps on November 17, 2026. Google, TripAdvisor and Yelp carry little weight in mainland China; Dianping is the rating that matters locally. Trip.com shows 4.3 out of 5, but from only 3 reviews, all from 2022, which praise the service, the fries, the free frozen custard tasting and the buzz of opening week.

The recurring criticism, reported by Canyin88 and Sina, is value for money. Diners rate the service, taste and quality as fine, just too expensive next to KFC and McDonald’s. Link Plaza Tianhe has no rating yet: it only opened on November 11, 2026.

Is Shake Shack Guangzhou worth it?

Shake Shack Guangzhou is worth it for a Cantonese-flavored treat you can’t find at any other Shack, less so as an everyday lunch. At more than twice the price of a KFC meal, the bill only makes sense when you order what is unique to the city, starting with the 金沙 salted egg yolk burger and fries. For a first visit, my pick is the ¥45 Freestyle Combo: it covers the core menu for about a third of the ¥143 burger, shake and cheese fries bill Canyin88 reported.

Why Shake Shack Taikoo Hui closed

Shake Shack Taikoo Hui closed in early September 2024, about a year after it opened in July 2023 on level B2/M of the Taikoo Hui mall. The Chinese trade press, Canyin88 and Sina, pointed to 2 problems. Diners judged the prices too high for what they got compared with KFC and McDonald’s. And the branch sat only about 800 m from Parc Central, so the two Shacks fought over the same customers. Shake Shack did not open another Guangzhou branch until November 2026, at Link Plaza Tianhe, with a ¥45 combo that reads to me as a direct answer to that price complaint.

Shake Shack in Guangzhou and South China: a short history

Shake Shack is a New York burger chain founded in 2004 by Danny Meyer, with 703 restaurants as of July 1, 2026 and a presence in 20 countries at the end of 2025, according to its shareholder letters. Its arrival in Guangzhou goes back to June 30, 2020, when Shake Shack and its licensee Maxim’s Caterers announced at least 15 restaurants in South China by 2030, in cities including Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Fuzhou and Xiamen (Business Wire).

The Guangzhou branches appear to be run under license by the Hong Kong group Maxim’s: that follows from the 2020 agreement, and Winshang named the local partner as Meixin Foods Limited (美心食品), the Chinese name of Maxim’s. The same licensee runs the branches I cover in my Shake Shack Hong Kong guide. For the chain’s full history, figures and customer ratings, see the Shake Shack brand profile.
