Best Burgers in Auckland: 10 Spots Ranked (2026)

Ten Auckland burger joints ranked for 2026, from Baby G's Avondale smash burgers to Burger Nation winners, with addresses and sources.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished November 11, 2026

The best burgers in Auckland in 2026 come out of small independent kitchens, and most of them are smash burgers. My number one is Baby G Burger in Avondale, a 15-seat shop built around the Oklahoma Dreaming Double. Second: Burgernaut on Fort Street, winner of the plant-based category at Burger Nation 2026. Third is Fat Puku’s on Queen Street, the only place whose menu prices I could verify, with doubles at NZ$20.90 to NZ$22.90. New Zealand’s best burger of the year came from further out, though. Smashies, a food truck from Pukekohe in the south of the Auckland region, took the Burger Nation 2026 supreme title.

Ten Auckland burger joints make this 2026 ranking, and seven of them are smash burger specialists. The table gives each one’s neighbourhood and the signature burger named by the restaurant or the local press.

How I ranked Auckland’s burger restaurants

This is an editorial ranking built from sources, not a tasting panel. My starting point was The Denizen’s list of Auckland’s best burgers right now, published on 8 October 2026. It names 28 places and ranks none of them, so the ordering is mine.

I cross-checked those names against the Burger Nation 2026 results reported by Hospitality Business, then against Broadsheet, Metro, Concrete Playground and Urban List. A place climbs when several sources single out the same burger, or when it has a 2026 award behind it. It drops when I could not pin down a signature burger or an address. Every address below comes from the restaurant’s own site or from two press sources, all checked on 24 October 2026.

Some things are left out on purpose. There are no Google ratings, because I could not record and date them for every venue. There are no opening hours, because the sources contradict each other. And prices appear only where an official menu shows them, which in October 2026 meant Fat Puku’s and nobody else. Check hours on each restaurant’s own channels before you go.

1. Baby G Burger, Avondale: the best smash burger in Auckland

Baby G Burger is the Auckland smash burger the local press keeps calling legendary, and it tops my list. American founder Cam Blanchard, a former mechanic, ran it as a pop-up for two years before opening the permanent shop on Sunday 8 October 2023, according to The Denizen. The G is for his partner, Greta.

Order the Oklahoma Dreaming Double. Smashed patties with onion, American cheddar, mustard mayo, hot sauce, maple syrup, pickles and diced onion. Maple and hot sauce on a double cheeseburger is a bold call. It is also why Baby G sits first here: nobody else in Auckland has a signature this distinctive, and the press has backed it since the pop-up days.

  • Address: 1879 Great North Road, Avondale, Auckland (confirmed by Broadsheet)
  • Seats: about 15
  • Official page: Instagram @babygburger

2. Burgernaut, Fort Street: best burger in Auckland CBD and best vegan burger

Burgernaut is the best burger in Auckland CBD on this list, and the only city-centre spot with a 2026 Burger Nation title. It is the burger brand of Urbanaut Brewing Co., whose taproom is at 597 New North Road in Kingsland. The Fort Street site brings the same burgers a short walk from the waterfront.

The everyday order is the Classic Smash: a seared patty, cheddar, pickles, diced onion, secret sauce and a potato bun. The award went to something stranger, the Smashed Hash Smashburger with Cheese Dipper Cup. It is a smashed hash brown and an Impossible patty with Angel Food vegan cheese, spiced apple chutney and roasted capsicum, all in a potato bun. It reads like a dare. The judges took it seriously, and that is why Burgernaut beats Fat Puku’s for second place.

  • Address: 63 Fort Street, Auckland CBD 1010
  • Phone: +64 9 972 4723
  • Official site: urbanautbeer.com/pages/burgernaut

3. Fat Puku’s Smashed Burgers, Queen Street and Wynyard Quarter

Fat Puku’s is the only burger joint on this list with prices I could verify, with doubles from NZ$20.90 on its official menu. Childhood friends Vetearii and Gary started it in 2020, after the pandemic pushed them out of the travel industry. The name joins their Persian cat, Puku, with the Māori word puku, belly.

The best-seller is the Bernie Mac: two patties, American cheddar, lettuce and B.Mac sauce, for NZ$20.90. The Big Poppa adds bacon, smoked cheese, house ranch and jalapeños to a double beef patty, for NZ$22.90. The Notorious V.E.G, on an Impossible patty, is NZ$19.90. The site shows “4.7 from 6000+ reviews”, but that is a self-reported figure mixing Google and delivery apps, so I gave it no weight.

  • Addresses: 1/492 Queen Street, Auckland CBD 1010; Shop 1/12 Jellicoe Street, Wynyard Quarter, Auckland 1010
  • Prices: checked on fatpukus.co.nz on 24 October 2026

4. Parade, Ponsonby Road: the best burger in Ponsonby

Parade is the burger to eat on Ponsonby Road, and every one comes on a pretzel bun baked in-house. Bryan Anderson, Nick Fury and Sean Bone, the trio behind Mutual Friends café in Newmarket, opened it in April 2022, as The Denizen reported.

Order the Double Smash: smashed beef, cheese, secret sauce and pickles in that pretzel bun. The house bun is what lifts Parade above a dozen other smash burgers in town. Parade also took part in PonsonBurger 2026, held from 25 May to 7 June, so it was trading this year.

  • Address: 252 Ponsonby Road, Ponsonby, Auckland 1011
  • Phone: +64 9 666 0027

5. Big J’s Takeaways, Mount Wellington: a classic Kiwi takeaway burger

Big J’s Takeaways is the suburban takeaway on this list, with patties hand-made on site. The Classic Cheese is a house patty with sautéed onions, house relish and cheddar. No smash, no brioche, and I like it for that. A new Burger of the Week lands every Thursday, according to Concrete Playground.

  • Address: Shop 4, 1 Melton Road, Mount Wellington, Auckland 1060
  • Phone: +64 9 579 0340
  • Before you go: sources disagree on the closing day, so check before you make the trip.

6. Lowbrow, K Road: the best chicken burger near Karangahape Road

Lowbrow is the K Road pick, and the one for fried chicken in a bun. It serves seasonal “contemporary fast food” (fried chicken, burgers and beer) out of St Kevins Arcade. The Denizen’s 2026 list singles out the Old Bae: a free-range chicken thigh in a cornflake crust, with Emmental, Old Bay mayo and pickled courgette.

  • Address: St Kevins Arcade, 18/183 Karangahape Road, Auckland 1010
  • Phone: +64 9 300 6410
  • Official site: lowbrow.co.nz
  • Before you go: the menu is seasonal, so the Old Bae may rotate off.

7. Real Burger, Birkenhead: the best smash burger on the North Shore

Real Burger is the North Shore smash burger, and it keeps things simple. Grass-fed New Zealand beef, smashed, finished with Real Sauce on a potato bun baked daily, according to Metro. Nashville hot chicken is on the menu too. If you live north of the bridge, there is no reason to cross it for a smash burger.

  • Address: 30 Birkenhead Avenue, Birkenhead, Auckland
  • Official site: realburger.co.nz

8. Burger Burger, Commercial Bay: burgers with a harbour view

Burger Burger at Commercial Bay is the burger with a view, looking out over the Waitematā Harbour. Mimi Gilmour (Buckley) co-founded the chain, and the Commercial Bay restaurant opened in June 2020 as its sixth site. The official locations page now lists three: Commercial Bay, Torbay and The Mount.

It ranks eighth for a plain reason: I could not confirm a signature burger. You come here for the setting. It is the easiest sit-down burger near the Viaduct and the ferry terminal.

9. Ralph’s Bar and Eatery, Mount Eden: an American diner on Dominion Road

Ralph’s is the American diner of this guide, serving a brioche-bun cheeseburger, curly fries and fried pickles on Dominion Road. It has merged with Vinyl Coffee Shop, according to Urban List. The Denizen’s 2026 pick is the Classic Double Cheeseburger.

  • Address: 225B Dominion Road, Mt Eden, Auckland 1024
  • Phone: +64 9 214 9369

10. Burgout, Onehunga: a prawn burger with Korean influence

Burgout in Onehunga closes the top 10 with the most unusual menu here: The Denizen highlights a prawn burger and Korean flavours. I could not verify its street address, so search for it by name before heading to Onehunga.

Special mention: Smashies, Burger Nation 2026 winner

Smashies made the best burger in New Zealand in 2026, according to Burger Nation. It is a food truck from Pukekohe, in the Franklin area of the Auckland region, about 50 km south of the city centre. Owners Dave and Rachel Tomlinson won the supreme title with the Classic Smashie Double: two quarter-pound smashed patties, American cheddar, secret sauce and McClure’s pickles on a brioche bun.

It sits outside the numbered ranking only because a truck has no fixed address I could verify. Follow its own channels to find where it is parked.

Burger Nation 2026: New Zealand’s burger awards

Burger Nation is New Zealand’s national burger competition. Cook & Nelson, the company co-founded by Nick Brown, runs it with McClure’s Pickles. The 2026 edition drew more than 300 burgers, more than 700 votes and more than 7,000 online ratings, per Hospitality Business and the official Burger Nation site.

Auckland did well in 2025 too. The supreme title went to Peake’s Kitchen in Papatowai, in the Catlins, but two Auckland venues took categories: 507 Eatery for plant and Soul Batter for seafood, per Hospitality Business.

Best smash burgers in Auckland

The best smash burgers in Auckland are at Baby G, Burgernaut, Fat Puku’s and Parade, with Real Burger on the North Shore. That is five of my top seven, and the 2026 supreme winner is a smash burger too. To try the technique at home, my colleague Alice breaks it down in her Sonic bacon smash burger copycat recipe.

Best burgers near Auckland CBD, the Viaduct and Wynyard Quarter

Four burger stops sit in the city centre or on the waterfront, the easiest picks if you are staying downtown or catching a ferry.

  • Burgernaut, 63 Fort Street: Classic Smash and the award-winning plant burger.
  • Fat Puku’s, 1/492 Queen Street and 1/12 Jellicoe Street in Wynyard Quarter: doubles from NZ$20.90.
  • Burger Burger, Commercial Bay: harbour views.
  • Wise Boys, Commercial Bay: fully vegan, named in The Denizen’s 2026 list.

Vegan burgers in Auckland

The best vegan burger in Auckland is Burgernaut’s Smashed Hash Smashburger, winner of the Burger Nation 2026 plant category. Fat Puku’s Notorious V.E.G, on an Impossible patty, costs NZ$19.90. Wise Boys in Commercial Bay runs a fully vegan menu.

Auckland burgers FAQ

Where can I eat the best burger in Auckland?

Baby G Burger at 1879 Great North Road, Avondale, tops this 2026 ranking with its Oklahoma Dreaming Double. Burgernaut at 63 Fort Street is second and the best pick in the CBD.

What is the best burger in New Zealand in 2026?

The Classic Smashie Double from Smashies, a Pukekohe food truck, won the Burger Nation 2026 supreme title. More than 300 burgers competed.

Where can I find a good smash burger in Auckland?

Baby G in Avondale, Burgernaut in the CBD, Fat Puku’s on Queen Street and in Wynyard Quarter, Parade in Ponsonby and Real Burger in Birkenhead all specialise in smash burgers.

How much does a burger cost in Auckland?

At Fat Puku’s, a double smash burger costs NZ$20.90 to NZ$22.90 and the Impossible burger NZ$19.90, per the official menu checked on 24 October 2026. I could not verify current prices for the other venues.

Where can I get a vegan burger in Auckland?

Burgernaut on Fort Street won the Burger Nation 2026 plant category. Fat Puku’s sells the Impossible-based Notorious V.E.G, and Wise Boys in Commercial Bay is fully vegan.

Which burger places are near the Viaduct and Commercial Bay?

Burger Burger and Wise Boys are in Commercial Bay. Fat Puku’s has a shop on Jellicoe Street in Wynyard Quarter, and Burgernaut is on Fort Street in the CBD.

What is a Kiwi burger?

A Kiwi burger is a beef burger topped with beetroot and a fried egg, usually with lettuce, tomato and cheese.

Is BurgerFuel the best burger in Auckland?

BurgerFuel is the New Zealand chain most visitors know, but it is not in this top 10. This ranking favours the independent burger joints that 2026 local press and Burger Nation put forward.
