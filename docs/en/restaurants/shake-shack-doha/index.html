Shake Shack Doha: 5 Locations, Menu and Hours

All 5 Shake Shack branches in Doha with addresses, phones and hours checked on 3 Nov 2026, plus the halal menu and why Villaggio is the one to pick.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished November 12, 2026

Shake Shack has five restaurants in and around Doha, and every one of them is inside a shopping mall: Villaggio, Mall of Qatar, City Centre Doha, Gulf Mall and Doha Festival City. If you only go to one, make it Villaggio. It was the first Shake Shack in Qatar, in late 2012, it keeps the longest hours (09:00 to 02:00 every day), and the Gold Line metro stops across the road. All meat on the Qatar menu is halal. I checked every address, phone number and opening time below against the official locator of Alshaya, the company that runs the chain in Qatar, on 3 November 2026.

Shake Shack in Qatar: 5 locations at a glance

Shake Shack Qatar runs five restaurants: two in Doha proper (City Centre Doha and Gulf Mall), one in Baaya (Villaggio), one in Al Rayyan (Mall of Qatar) and one in Umm Salal Mohammed (Doha Festival City). Alshaya Group operates all five under licence from the New York brand.

Call ahead before a late-night or Friday-midday visit.

Shake Shack Villaggio: the first and the best-placed in Doha

Shake Shack Villaggio is the branch I would send anyone to first. It is the oldest in Qatar, the first to open in the morning and the last to close at night, and the only one with a metro station on its doorstep. It sits in Villaggio mall on Al Waab Street in Baaya; the phone number is +974 4452 9668.

The Peninsula reported its opening on 9 December 2012 (The Peninsula, 2012). That made Villaggio the first Shake Shack in Qatar, about 20 months after the chain’s first restaurant outside the United States opened at Mall of the Emirates in Dubai in April 2011.

How to get to Shake Shack Villaggio by metro

Take the Gold Line of the Doha Metro to Al Aziziyah, its western terminus. The station sits directly opposite Villaggio on Al Waab Street and has been open since 21 November 2019 (Wikipedia).

Parking at Villaggio

Villaggio has a large car park and a valet service. Qatar Rail handed 210 parking spaces back to the mall once the station works were finished (Gulf Times).

Shake Shack Doha opening hours

Every Shake Shack in Doha opens at 09:00 or 10:00 and closes at midnight or later. Villaggio holds out until 02:00 every night. After 01:00, it is the only Shake Shack in Qatar still serving.

  • Latest closing: Villaggio, 02:00 every day.
  • Earliest opening: Villaggio, Gulf Mall and Doha Festival City, 09:00.
  • Shortest hours: City Centre Doha, which closes at midnight from Sunday to Thursday.
  • Weekend: Qatar’s weekend is Friday and Saturday, when City Centre Doha stays open until 01:00.

Shake Shack Doha opening hours by branch: Villaggio opens 09:00 to 02:00, the longest day; City Centre Doha closes at midnight Sunday to Thursday (01:00 on Friday and Saturday). Source: Alshaya locator, 3 November 2026. (Chart: Burger Breton, own work from Alshaya locator data (https://locations.alshaya.com/shake-shack/qa), CC BY 4.0)

All hours come from Alshaya’s store pages, checked on 3 November 2026. Third-party listings disagree: Wanderlog shows 12:00 to 02:00 on some days at Villaggio. I trust the official locator over aggregators. Hours shift during Ramadan and on public holidays, so check it again before those.

Shake Shack Qatar menu: burgers, chicken, fries and shakes

The Shake Shack Qatar menu has seven sections: Burgers, Chicken, Crinkle Cut Fries, Flat-Top Hot Dogs, Shakes & Shack Ice Cream, Cold Drinks and Hot Drinks (official Qatar menu). It is the New York formula with halal meat: smashed patties, crinkle-cut fries and frozen custard.

When Villaggio opened in 2012, The Peninsula described a menu led by the ShackBurger, made with halal US Black Angus beef raised without hormones or antibiotics. Alongside it came flat-top hot dogs, crinkle-cut Yukon fries, milkshakes and frozen custard, the egg-rich ice cream the chain is known for. Want to try the burger at home first? Our copycat ShackBurger recipe breaks down the patty, the bun and the ShackSauce.

Bestsellers on Talabat Qatar

The most ordered items on Talabat Qatar are the Shackburger Box and the Chick’n Shack Box (both Game-day combos), the BBQ Shack Meister Burger and the Shack Burger Single (Talabat, checked on 3 November 2026). The BBQ Shack Meister is a limited edition, so check it is still listed before you set your heart on it. For a first order I would skip the boxes and take the Shack Burger Single: it is the burger the chain was built on, and the fairest one to judge it by.

Is Shake Shack halal in Qatar?

Yes. The official Shake Shack Middle East FAQ for Qatar states: ‘At Shake Shack, we only use Halal meat’ (FAQ). That covers the beef burgers, the chicken and the hot dogs. For more halal burgers across the city, see our guide to where to eat a burger in Doha.

Vegetarian options at Shake Shack Doha

Shake Shack Doha has five vegetarian options, according to the official Qatar FAQ: the Shroom Burger, the Falafel Shack, the Grilled Cheese, plain fries and cheese fries. If you have an allergy, filter the menu by allergen in the online ordering system.

Shake Shack Doha menu prices in riyals

I could not confirm a single Shake Shack price in Qatari riyals (QAR) from an official source, so this guide prints none. Talabat and Snoonu only show prices once you enter a delivery address, and the riyal figures circulating online have no source I could verify. Prices in Saudi riyals (SAR) that turn up in search results are from Saudi Arabia, not Qatar. For current prices, use the Shake Shack Middle East app or read the menu board at the counter.

Shake Shack Doha reviews: what diners say

Diners rate Shake Shack in Doha highly: Villaggio scores 4.8 out of 5 on Google, as shown on Wanderlog, and the chain scores 4.8 out of 5 on Talabat. People praise the patties and the fries. They complain about portion size and salt.

Google reviews quoted on Wanderlog praise well-caramelised smashed patties, crisp crinkle-cut fries and friendly staff. The complaints repeat too: portions some diners find small, and food that is sometimes too salty. I have not scored any branch myself. This guide is built from official sources and review data, not from a rated visit, and I would rather give no rating than a borrowed one. I could not access Tripadvisor scores for the Doha branches, and I found no Yelp listings.

Shake Shack delivery, online ordering and Click & Collect in Doha

You can get Shake Shack delivered in Doha through two third-party apps, Talabat and Snoonu. If a delivery goes wrong, the official FAQ sends you to the customer service of the app you ordered through, not to Shake Shack.

Alshaya lists online ordering and Click & Collect at City Centre Doha, Gulf Mall and Doha Festival City: order ahead, then pick up at the counter. I could not verify payment methods, wheelchair access, Wi-Fi or self-order kiosks at any branch.

Which Shake Shack in Doha should you choose?

Go to Villaggio for a late meal or a metro trip. Otherwise, eat at the branch in the mall you are already in: all five serve the same menu, and I found no Qatar-only item.

  • Villaggio (Baaya): best overall. First in Qatar, open 09:00 to 02:00, Gold Line metro opposite, large car park and valet.
  • Mall of Qatar (Al Rayyan): handy for the west of the city, open 10:00 to 01:00.
  • City Centre Doha: central, with Click & Collect, but closes at midnight Sunday to Thursday.
  • Gulf Mall: opens at 09:00, with Click & Collect.
  • Doha Festival City (Umm Salal Mohammed): on the Ground Floor, for the north of the city. Call first, as its hours vary between sources.

Shake Shack in the Middle East: the brand behind the Doha restaurants

Shake Shack is a New York fast-casual burger chain with 703 restaurants worldwide as of 1 July 2026 (Shake Shack Q2 2026 shareholder letter). Its growth abroad started in the Gulf: Dubai in April 2011, then Doha in late 2012. In 2012 The Peninsula credited the Qatar launch to Union Square Hospitality Group; today Alshaya runs the Qatar restaurants. Shake Shack Doha FAQ

How many Shake Shacks are there in Doha and Qatar?

There are five Shake Shack restaurants in Qatar: Villaggio (Baaya), Mall of Qatar (Al Rayyan), City Centre Doha, Gulf Mall (Doha) and Doha Festival City (Umm Salal Mohammed), according to Alshaya’s locator on 3 November 2026.

When did Shake Shack open in Qatar?

Shake Shack opened its first Qatar restaurant at Villaggio mall in Doha in late 2012. The Peninsula reported the opening on 9 December 2012.

Is Shake Shack halal in Doha?

Yes. The official Shake Shack Middle East FAQ for Qatar says the chain only uses halal meat.

What are Shake Shack Villaggio’s opening hours?

Shake Shack Villaggio is open every day from 09:00 to 02:00, according to Alshaya’s store page checked on 3 November 2026.

How do I get to Shake Shack Villaggio by metro?

Take the Gold Line to Al Aziziyah, its western terminus, which sits opposite Villaggio mall on Al Waab Street.

How much does a ShackBurger cost in Doha?

I could not verify a current ShackBurger price in Qatari riyals (QAR) from an official source. Check the Shake Shack Middle East app or the menu board in the restaurant for the current price.

Can I get Shake Shack delivered in Doha?

Yes, through Talabat and Snoonu. City Centre Doha, Gulf Mall and Doha Festival City also offer online ordering with Click & Collect.

Does Shake Shack Doha have vegetarian options?

Yes: the Shroom Burger, the Falafel Shack, the Grilled Cheese, fries and cheese fries, according to the official Qatar FAQ.

Is the Shake Shack menu in Doha different from New York?

The Doha menu has the same core sections as the chain’s home menu (burgers, chicken, crinkle-cut fries, flat-top hot dogs, shakes and frozen custard), but all meat in Qatar is halal. I found no confirmed Qatar-only item.
