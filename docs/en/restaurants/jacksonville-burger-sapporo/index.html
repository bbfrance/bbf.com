Jacksonville Burger Sapporo: Owner, Menu, Prices

Jacksonville burger Sapporo: founder Seiichi Kanada's Oregon story, the No.1 Western BBQ Burger (¥1,900), menu prices and all four branch addresses.

By Hana, Restaurants writer, Japan and IndonesiaPublished November 27, 2026

Jacksonville is an American-style gourmet burger restaurant in Sapporo, founded in 2008 by Seiichi Kanada (金田清一) and run by his company, ENT WILL co., ltd. The name comes from Jacksonville, Oregon, the small town where Kanada ate his first real gourmet burger. Order the Western BBQ Burger. It is the menu’s No.1 best seller, ¥1,900 tax included, stacked with a 100% Hokkaido beef patty, bacon, cheese and Hokkaido onion rings. All four branches are in Sapporo, and local media call the Kita 24-jo branch the flagship.

Who owns Jacksonville burger in Sapporo?

Jacksonville is owned by its founder, Seiichi Kanada, through his company ENT WILL co., ltd. (株式会社エンウィル), according to the restaurant’s official website. The owner’s message on that site gives 2008 as the founding year, and the cover of the current menu reads “Since 2008”.

The Rogue Valley Times in southern Oregon also names Kanada as the owner. In a December 2023 profile, reporter Buffy Pollock wrote that he had moved to Sapporo about 15 years earlier. That fits.

Why is a Sapporo burger restaurant called Jacksonville?

The restaurant is named after Jacksonville, Oregon, a small historic town near Medford, not Jacksonville, Florida. In his message on the official site, Kanada writes that he ate his first real gourmet burger in the village of Jacksonville, Oregon, and was moved by how good it was.

Several older English-language expat guides to Sapporo say the owner lived in Jacksonville, Florida. They are wrong: both the owner’s own words and the Rogue Valley Times point to Oregon.

From Medford exchange student to Sapporo burger founder

Kanada came to the United States as an exchange student. According to the Rogue Valley Times (December 1, 2023), he was hosted by Larry and Linda Smith of Jacksonville, Oregon. He attended St. Mary’s High School in Medford for three years and graduated from South Medford High in 1994.

Map of Jacksonville, Oregon, a few miles west of Medford. Seiichi Kanada lived here with his host family before he graduated from South Medford High in 1994. He opened his Sapporo burger restaurant in 2008 and named it after the town. (Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL)

The same article has my favorite detail in the whole story. For his senior project, Kanada ran a hot dog cart at Alba Park during the 1994 Pear Blossom Festival in Medford. A teenager selling sausages in buns at a small-town festival. It reads like a rehearsal.

The sources do not agree on how long he stayed in America. The Rogue Valley Times says about four years in southern Oregon. Kanada’s own message on the official site says he lived in the United States for 10 years and ate burgers “north, south, east and west” across the country. The year he returned to Japan is not on record.

The first years in Sapporo

Jacksonville opened in Sapporo in 2008, and the Rogue Valley Times reports that it took “three hard years” to gain traction. It survived. Today it runs four branches and still posts a new monthly burger on its website: a moon-viewing Avocado Burger in September 2026, a Halloween Pumpkin Burger in October and an Autumn Mushroom and Cheese Sauce Burger on November 2, 2026.

What is the best burger to order at Jacksonville Sapporo?

Order the Western BBQ Burger: it is marked 人気No.1 (most popular) on the April 2025 menu, and the Rogue Valley Times also names it the top seller. It costs ¥1,900 tax included (¥1,727 before tax).

The menu says it recreates “the taste of the American West”. It stacks mayonnaise, lettuce, tomato, bacon, grilled onion, cheese, the beef patty, Hokkaido onion rings, BBQ sauce and burger sauce. No other burger on the menu sums up Kanada’s idea as well: an American build, made with Hokkaido produce.

Jacksonville menu and prices

A burger on Jacksonville’s grand menu costs between ¥1,650 and ¥2,200 tax included, according to the official menu dated April 2025. Every burger comes with homemade pickles and potatoes. The menu is split into American Gourmet Burgers and Hokkaido Gourmet Burgers, plus one Monthly Burger.

Jacksonville Sapporo burger prices, tax included, from the April 2025 menu. They range from ¥1,650 (Teriyaki Egg Cheese, Hawaiian Teriyaki) to ¥2,200 (Double Cheese), and five burgers cost ¥1,750. Every burger comes with fries or wedges and homemade pickles. (Chart: Burger Breton, own work, data from the Jacksonville official menu (April 2025), rendered with QuickChart)

Older guides quote around ¥900 to ¥1,600. Those figures come from earlier menus. A revision after April 2025 is possible, so treat the figures below as the latest official ones I could check, on November 12, 2026.

How to customize your order

Jacksonville lets you adjust almost everything, and the menu prices each change:

  • Just Burger, without sides: −¥200.
  • Hokkaido onion rings instead of fries: +¥150.
  • Toppings: from +¥100 (pickles, sauces) to +¥550 (an extra 100% beef patty).
  • Set drinks: from +¥250.
  • Doneness and ingredients: staff will adjust the cooking or remove an ingredient on request.

Does Jacksonville use Hokkaido beef?

Yes: the April 2025 menu states that the patty is 100% Hokkaido beef (道産牛使用の牛肉100%), raised free-range on Sugiyama-san’s farm in Sōya, in the far north of Hokkaido. The fresh beef is hand-chopped and bound with nothing else. The travel site Chikyū no Arukikata also confirms the 100% beef, no-binder patty.

That was not always the whole story. The sourcing page of the official site still mentions both American and Hokkaido beef, and the Rogue Valley Times wrote in 2023 that the restaurant used Japanese and imported US beef. Either the sourcing changed or the website has not caught up with the menu. The menu is the more recent document, and it says 100% Hokkaido beef.

Hokkaido buns, vegetables and potatoes

The rest of the burger is local too, according to the official site. Buns are made from Hokkaido wheat by a baker with 30 years’ experience. Tomatoes and lettuce come from Mukawa, onions from Niseko, and the potatoes from Hokkaido. You choose between Hokkaido wedges and American shoestring fries.

Which branch is the original?

No source I found confirms which branch opened first in 2008, or when the others opened. Local media call the Kita 24-jo branch the flagship (本店), and that is as far as the record goes.

Getting to the flagship

The Kita 24-jo flagship is a two- to three-minute walk from Kita-24-jō Station on the Namboku subway line, in a nightlife district locals nickname “mini Susukino”. If your schedule is tight, go to Moyuku Sapporo instead: it is in the basement of a shopping building downtown and closes only at New Year.

Is Jacksonville the best burger in Sapporo?

Jacksonville is the strongest case I know for an independent American burger restaurant in Sapporo. The founder has a real American story, the menu names its Hokkaido farms and cheesemakers, and the business has lasted 18 years. The English-language guides that rank for “best burger in Sapporo” already point to it.

It is not a cheap meal. At ¥1,650 to ¥2,200 per burger with sides, it costs more than a chain burger. I think the premium is justified by what the menu documents: hand-chopped beef from a named farm, local cheese and fries or wedges included. For the Japanese-chain counterpoint, read my guide to MOS Burger in Sapporo, and browse every pick in our Sapporo burger guide.

If you are traveling across Japan, compare Jacksonville with two other American-style burger restaurants we profiled: Munch’s Burger Shack in Tokyo and K’s Pit Handa, an American diner near Nagoya. All our city guides are in the Restaurants section.

Jacksonville Sapporo FAQ

Who owns Jacksonville burger restaurant in Sapporo?

Seiichi Kanada (金田清一) founded Jacksonville in 2008 and owns it through his company, ENT WILL co., ltd., according to the official site.

Is Jacksonville Sapporo named after Jacksonville, Oregon or Florida?

Oregon. The owner writes that he ate his first gourmet burger in Jacksonville, Oregon, a small town near Medford where he was hosted as an exchange student. The Florida version found in some expat guides is wrong.

What should I order at Jacksonville Sapporo?

The Western BBQ Burger at ¥1,900 tax included, the menu’s No.1 best seller. For a Hokkaido twist, try the Niseko Cheese or Asahikawa Cheese Burger at ¥1,750.

How much does a burger cost at Jacksonville Sapporo?

Between ¥1,650 and ¥2,200 tax included, with homemade pickles and fries or wedges, according to the official menu dated April 2025. Ordering a Just Burger without sides takes ¥200 off.

Does Jacksonville Sapporo use Hokkaido beef?

Yes. The current menu states a 100% Hokkaido beef patty from Sugiyama-san’s farm in Sōya, hand-chopped with no binder. Older sources mention imported US beef, so the sourcing may have changed over time.

Does Jacksonville Sapporo offer takeout or delivery?

Yes. The Maruyama branch is a dedicated takeout stand, and the Kita 24-jo branch has a listing on Uber Eats. Whether an English menu is available is not confirmed by the official site, so ask staff on arrival.

What is the difference between a hamburger and hamburg steak in Japan?

A hamburger (ハンバーガー, hanbāgā) is a patty served in a bun, which is what Jacksonville makes. Hamburg steak (ハンバーグ, hanbāgu) is a seasoned minced-meat steak served on a plate, usually with rice and sauce. Search in Japanese with ハンバーガー to find burger restaurants.
