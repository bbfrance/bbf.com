Prime Burger Phuket: Smash Burgers and Prices

Prime Burger Phuket: what to order, THB prices, halal and vegan options, and where the Patong, Central Phuket, Porto de Phuket and Rawai branches are.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished October 17, 2026

Prime Burger is a Phuket smash burger chain founded in 2019 by the Swede Tobias Garneij. Its signature burger, the Stockholm, is named after Garneij’s hometown and won Best Burger at the island’s annual burger competition. Most signature burgers cost about 199–329 THB, according to the official FAQ. Looking for the best smash burger in Phuket? Order the Stockholm as a double, then come back for the Phuket, which is topped with grilled pineapple. Time Out names four of the island’s eight branches: Patong, Central Phuket, Porto de Phuket and Rawai.

Who founded Prime Burger in Phuket?

Tobias Garneij, a Swedish national, founded Prime Burger in Phuket in 2019. The year comes from the company’s own About page (Prime Burger, About). Some aggregators give 2018. I trust the company on its own birthday. Time Out Phuket and the food blog Zoy To The World both name Garneij as the founder (Time Out Phuket, Zoy To The World).

The Swedish thread runs through the menu. The Stockholm carries the name of Garneij’s hometown, and the other burgers are also named after cities: Paris, Bangkok, San Francisco, New York and Phuket itself. For the burgers of the real Stockholm, see the guide to the best burgers in Stockholm.

From one island to 25 restaurants across Thailand

Prime Burger now runs 25 restaurants in Thailand and has moved to a franchise model, according to its official FAQ (Prime Burger, FAQ). Phuket still has the most branches, 8, one more than Bangkok.

What to order: Prime Burger’s signature burgers

Start with the Stockholm, the burger that made Prime Burger’s name in Phuket. After that, it depends on what you want. The Paris brings bacon and truffle mayo. The Bangkok and the Phuket bring chili heat. The San Francisco is the plant-based one.

The Stockholm burger, ingredient by ingredient

The Prime Burger Stockholm is a smash burger with cheese, pickled red onions, pickles, tomato, lettuce and the house Prime Sauce. That’s how Zoy To The World described it in November 2023, and later press summaries match. Two sour toppings on one burger is a deliberate choice: against a seared, fatty patty, the acid does the work that ketchup usually does.

Single, double or triple: patty sizes

A single has one 130 g patty. A double has two 95 g patties, and a triple has three (Prime Burger, Zoy To The World). My pick is the double. It has only 60 g more beef than the single, but two seared surfaces instead of one, and the crust is the reason to order a smash burger.

Where to find Prime Burger in Phuket, including Patong

Prime Burger has eight branches in Phuket. Time Out Phuket (June 2026) names four of them: Porto de Phuket, Central Phuket, Patong and Rawai.

  • Prime Burger Patong: Central Patong, 198/9 Rat-U-Thit 200 Pi Rd, Pa Tong, Kathu, Phuket 83150 (address from Tripadvisor; confirm the house number on the official locator).
  • Central Phuket: inside the Central Phuket mall, near Phuket Town.
  • Porto de Phuket: the lifestyle mall in Cherngtalay, on the north-west coast.
  • Rawai: in the far south of the island.

Prime Burger’s awards: what won, and when

The Stockholm won Best Burger at Phuket’s burger competition. The sources give different years, so here is each version with its source.

  • Official site: Best Vegan Burger 2019 (San Francisco); Best Burger 2020 (Stockholm) and Best Vegan Burger 2020 (San Francisco) at Phuket Burger Fest; Best Beef Burger 2024 (New York) (Prime Burger, About).
  • The Phuket News and Zoy To The World: the Stockholm won Phuket’s Best Burger 2019, and a halloumi burger came second in the vegetarian/vegan category (The Phuket News).

Until the organizers confirm it, I won’t pin the Stockholm’s win to a single year. I also couldn’t find out who organized the 2024 Best Beef Burger award. Honestly, ‘award-winning’ on the menu tells you less than the patty weights do.

How Prime Burger makes its smash burger

A smash burger is a ball of ground beef pressed thin onto a very hot griddle, so the outside browns into a crust while the inside stays juicy. Prime Burger smashes each patty to order.

According to The Phuket News, Garneij had a custom stainless-steel smasher made: a round press with ridges in its base. (I could only read the search snippet; the article page was blocked when I checked on 14 October 2026.)

The official site says the beef is 100% Australian, imported and ground fresh every day, and the buns are baked by hand daily. One press summary describes an Australian grass-fed Angus blend of 70% chuck and 30% brisket. The official site doesn’t say that, so treat it as reported, not confirmed. For other smash burger specialists in Asia, compare Bikini Bottom Express in Da Nang and Beyondburg in Bangalore.

Halal, vegan and delivery

Prime Burger is halal-certified, including its beef and chicken suppliers, per the official FAQ. That matters in Phuket, which has a large Muslim community and many visitors from the Middle East and Malaysia.

Vegans get the San Francisco: a Beyond Meat patty, vegan cheese and vegan chili mayo. Most locations deliver through Grab (GrabFood), also per the FAQ.

Best burgers in Phuket besides Prime Burger

Prime Burger is one of seven places on Time Out Phuket’s best burger list, updated in June 2026 by Joanna Matlub. The other six are Millionaire Burgers, OOO Burger, Pan Burger, Treasure Burger, EAT. Burgers & Beers and Madcow Burger. The best burgers in Phuket city page brings together all our coverage of the island, and the Restaurants hub lists every city guide.

Methodology and sources

This profile is based on Prime Burger’s official About, FAQ and home pages, Time Out Phuket’s best burger list (June 2026), The Phuket News’s 2019 coverage and Zoy To The World’s November 2023 visit. Every fact was checked on 14 October 2026. For the founding year, I follow the company’s own date. Where sources disagree on award years and the beef blend, I give each claim with its source. Prices are the official range, not a 2023 menu.

FAQ

Who founded Prime Burger in Phuket?

Tobias Garneij, a Swede, founded Prime Burger in Phuket in 2019. The Stockholm burger is named after his hometown.

Which Prime Burger burger won Phuket’s Best Burger, and in what year?

The Stockholm. The official site lists Best Burger 2020 at Phuket Burger Fest, while The Phuket News and Zoy To The World report Phuket’s Best Burger 2019.

How much does a burger cost at Prime Burger Phuket?

Most signature burgers cost about 199–329 THB, according to the official FAQ (checked 14 October 2026).

Where are Prime Burger’s branches in Phuket?

There are eight branches in Phuket. Time Out names Patong, Central Phuket, Porto de Phuket and Rawai. The Patong branch is at Central Patong, 198/9 Rat-U-Thit 200 Pi Rd.

What beef does Prime Burger use, and is it halal?

100% Australian beef, imported, ground fresh every day and smashed to order. The chain is halal-certified, including its beef and chicken suppliers.

Does Prime Burger have vegan or vegetarian burgers?

Yes. The San Francisco has a Beyond Meat patty, vegan cheese and vegan chili mayo. The official site says it won Best Vegan Burger in 2019 and 2020.

What is a smash burger, and how does Prime Burger make it?

A smash burger is a ball of beef pressed thin on a hot griddle so it forms a browned crust. According to The Phuket News, Prime Burger uses a custom stainless-steel smasher with a ridged base.

Does Prime Burger deliver in Phuket?

Yes. Most locations deliver through Grab (GrabFood), per the official FAQ.
