Saint Restaurant Sandton and Chef David Higgs

Who the chef behind Saint is, what the restaurant serves, how to book, and why burger-seekers should head to Marble instead.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished November 27, 2026

Saint restaurant in Sandton is a “twisted Italian” pizza, pasta and steakhouse restaurant at The Marc, on the corner of Rivonia Road and Maude Street. Chef David Higgs and Gary Kyriacou, the duo behind Marble, opened it in August 2018, and its official site still names Higgs as head chef. Before you book, know this: Saint is not a burger restaurant. I went through every one of its online menus on 17 November 2026 and found no burger. If a burger is what you want, the only Higgs burger is the Beef Sliders bar snack at Marble in Rosebank. My view: put Saint on your Sandton dinner list, not your burger list.

Who is David Higgs? The chef behind Saint

David Higgs is a South African chef, born in Walvis Bay, Namibia. He made his name in Cape Winelands fine dining, then moved to Johannesburg. According to Daily Maverick, he is a founding partner of Saint, Marble and Zioux. He also owns Pantry, a gourmet store on a petrol-station forecourt, and wrote the book Mile 8.

From Walvis Bay to a Cape Town hotel kitchen

Higgs started out cooking hotel breakfasts. He lived in Walvis Bay until he was about 10, then his family moved to Cape Town. In 1989 he started as breakfast chef at the Tulbagh Protea Hotel on the Cape Town Foreshore, as Tony Jackman reported in Daily Maverick in April 2024. That is about as far from a Josper grill as a kitchen gets.

Rust en Vrede and the 2010 Eat Out sweep

Rust en Vrede made Higgs one of South Africa’s most decorated chefs. In 2007 he left Meerendal to open a fine-dining restaurant with Jean Engelbrecht at the Rust en Vrede wine estate near Stellenbosch. Three years later he swept the board. Rust en Vrede won Eat Out Restaurant of the Year in 2010, and Higgs took Chef of the Year and the Service Excellence award (Eat Out). Earlier, in 2003, Eat Out reports he was named Young Chef of South Africa by the Chaîne des Rôtisseurs.

Stellenbosch has a chef-led burger story of its own. My own profile of De Vrije Burger Stellenbosch by Bertus Basson shows what happens when a fine-dining chef turns to burgers. That is exactly what Higgs has not done at Saint.

Five Hundred at The Saxon, Sandton

Five Hundred brought Higgs to Sandton. He won Eat Out S.Pellegrino Chef of the Year 2013 for Five Hundred at The Saxon Hotel (Eat Out), then took it to No. 2 in the Eat Out Top 10. Five Hundred has since closed.

Marble, Saint and Marble Cape Town

With Gary Kyriacou, Higgs left hotel fine dining and opened restaurants of his own. The risk was personal: Higgs sold his house to fund Marble, and Kyriacou also runs the Century 21 South Africa property business (Wanted Online, November 2018). Marble opened in Rosebank on 4 July 2016, built around a wood-fired grill imported from Grill Works in Michigan. Saint followed in August 2018, and by the end of that year the pair employed about 300 people across the two. Marble Cape Town, at Castle Union House on the V&A Waterfront with head chef Matt van Niekerk, was open by December 2024 (Time Out Cape Town).

What is Saint Sandton? “Italian, but twisted”

Saint is an Italian restaurant with a steakhouse inside it. Higgs calls the food “Italian, but twisted” (Eat Out, 2018). At launch it was pitched as “Pazzo Italiano”, a pizza and Champagne destination (In Your Pocket). In Your Pocket also reports that some of Saint’s pizza chefs trained at Gennaro’s in Milan, a pizzeria operating since 1935.

Launch-era write-ups disagree on where Saint’s pizza ovens came from. The restaurant’s own site does not settle the question, so I have left it out.

Saint Sandton menu: pizza, pasta and the steakhouse

The Saint Sandton menu has eight sections: Antipasti, Salads, Pasta, Pizza, Steakhouse, Seafood, Dessert and a Bar menu of snacks, cocktails and wine. For me the Steakhouse is the part that tells you most about Saint. Its three sub-menus are named after the heat source, Josper, Wood-Fired Oven and Grill, and that is Marble’s live-fire thinking brought into an Italian dining room.

The steakhouse cuts listed on saint.restaurant on 17 November 2026:

  • 340 g Prime strip steak
  • 340 g Prime boneless ribeye
  • 460 g bone-in fillet
  • 630 g dry-aged American Wagyu tomahawk

Saint Sandton steakhouse cuts by weight: 340 g Prime strip, 340 g Prime boneless ribeye, 460 g bone-in fillet, 630 g dry-aged American Wagyu tomahawk (official menu, 17 November 2026; no prices published). (Chart: Burger Breton (original), data from saint.restaurant, checked 17 November 2026)

Each comes with fries, salad or vegetables. Saint publishes no prices online, so I can’t tell you what a meal costs today. The only price I could find is from February 2019, when Daily Maverick’s Food Mole paid R265 for the prawn tagliatelle. That figure is almost eight years old, so treat it as history, not a guide to today’s bill.

Is there a burger at Saint Sandton?

No. On 17 November 2026 I checked the main menu, the steakhouse, the grill and the bar snacks pages: no burger, no slider, no sandwich. I could not confirm whether Saint ever ran a burger as a special or at launch, so I won’t claim one. For a kitchen this serious about beef that is a surprise, but it suits the room. Saint is a pizza-and-steak place.

Where David Higgs does serve a burger: Marble’s Beef Sliders

The only burger-format dish I found in the Higgs-Kyriacou group is at Marble in Rosebank. Its bar menu lists Beef Sliders: “butter bun, beef patty, red onion, truffled mayonnaise, gherkin” (marble.restaurant). No price is shown online. It’s a bar snack, not a full burger. Still, if you want to taste beef from Higgs in a bun, this is the only place in Johannesburg to order it.

Saint vs Marble: what is the difference?

Saint and Marble share founders but not a menu. Marble is the live-fire restaurant; Saint is the Italian one with a steakhouse attached.

How to book Saint Sandton

Book Saint online through Dineplan, by phone on 010 594 5888 or by email at info@saint.restaurant. Watch the kitchen break: the kitchen closes from 4pm to 5pm, and lunch tables must be handed back by 4:30pm, so a late lunch here is a short one. Saint also hosts private events, listed on its private-events page.

How I put this profile together

Every fact here comes from Saint’s and Marble’s official sites, their booking page and dated South African food press, all checked on 17 November 2026. I did not eat at Saint for this piece, so it carries no rating. Where sources conflict or rest on a single report, I either say so or leave the detail out. My verdict on paper: go to Saint for steak or pizza in Sandton, and go to Marble’s bar for a Higgs burger. For more venues, see the Restaurants section.

FAQ: Saint restaurant Sandton and David Higgs

Who owns Saint restaurant in Sandton?

Saint was founded in August 2018 by chef David Higgs and Gary Kyriacou, the same partners behind Marble in Rosebank.

Is David Higgs still the chef at Saint?

Yes. As of 17 November 2026, Saint’s official site names David Higgs as head chef. I could not confirm whether he cooks there every service or oversees the kitchen at group level.

Where is Saint in Sandton, and when is it open?

Saint is at Upper Retail Shop, The Marc, corner Rivonia Road and Maude Street (129 Rivonia Road, Sandown). It is open every day for lunch from 12pm to 4pm and dinner from 5pm to 10pm.

Does Saint Sandton serve burgers?

No. Its current online menus list no burger, slider or sandwich. Marble in Rosebank, also run by Higgs, serves Beef Sliders at the bar.

What kind of food does Saint serve?

Italian with a twist: antipasti, salads, pasta, wood-fired pizza and seafood, plus a steakhouse of Josper, wood-fired and grilled cuts such as a 630 g dry-aged American Wagyu tomahawk.

What awards has David Higgs won?

In 2010 he won Eat Out Chef of the Year and the Service Excellence award at Rust en Vrede, which was named Restaurant of the Year. In 2013 he won Eat Out S.Pellegrino Chef of the Year for Five Hundred. Eat Out also reports he was the Chaîne des Rôtisseurs’ Young Chef of South Africa in 2003.

Is Saint Sandton expensive, and do I need to book?

Saint publishes no prices online, so I cannot quote a current bill. Its menu is built around prime and Wagyu steaks, which suggests a special-occasion bill. Bookings are taken on Dineplan and by phone, and booking ahead is the safe choice.
