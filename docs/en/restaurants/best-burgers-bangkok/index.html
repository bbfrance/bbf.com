Best Burgers in Bangkok: 3 Verified Spots to Start

Best burgers in Bangkok: 3 verified spots led by a 24/7 Silom classic, checked September 2026.

By AlexPublished September 29, 2026

If you want a burger in Bangkok tonight, start here. I checked primary sources on 2026-09-29 and could only verify three names — led by 25 Degrees Bangkok in Silom, open 24/7 with a weekday Signature Burger lunch set. I held back a full top-10 because seven other candidates had no verifiable address or menu.

How I verified these best burgers in Bangkok

I list only what I could trace to an official site, social page, or listing checked on 2026-09-29. No Google ratings. No undated prices. No chef names or awards without a licensed source.

1. 25 Degrees Bangkok – Silom, open 24/7

The late-night pick. If I land late in Silom, this is where I go first. Address: Level G, Pullman Bangkok Hotel G, 188 Silom Road, Bangkok. The official site states “WE’RE OPEN 24/7” and lists a weekday “Signature Burger, Fries & Drink” set at THB 380 net mid-size and THB 495 net up-size, 11h–15h (source). Same page claims “LSA BEST BURGER IN BANGKOK” and lists +66 2352 4192. My call: order the Signature. It is the only order here I can price with a date.

2. Daniel Thaiger – Bangkok, American gourmet burger since 2013

The history pick for smash-burger fans. Its Facebook page bills it as “American Gourmet Burger est. 2013” (source), and its Instagram was live when I checked on 2026-09-29. I am not giving an address, menu pick, or price — I found none on a primary source that day.

Daniel Thaiger style: smash burger close-up showing thin smashed patty, alt: Bangkok smash burger smashed patty close-up (Photo: Michael Rivera, CC BY-SA 4.0)

3. Easy Burger – Bangkok, official site active in 2026

The one to watch. The official site at https://www.easyburgerbkk.com/ keeps an active Menu page, last modified 2026-01-14 when I checked on 2026-09-29. That freshness is my only 2026 signal. No address or priced dish to publish yet.

Best burgers in Bangkok FAQ

Where can I eat the best burger in Bangkok?

Start with 25 Degrees Bangkok in Silom for a verified 24/7 option, then check Daniel Thaiger and Easy Burger on their official pages for current locations and hours.

What is the best smash burger in Bangkok?

No verified winner on 2026-09-29. Daniel Thaiger and Easy Burger fit the smash-burger intent, but without verified specs or prices I will not rank them.

How much does a good burger cost in Bangkok?

Only one dated price verified: THB 380 net (mid-size) and THB 495 net (up-size) for the 25 Degrees weekday Signature Burger, Fries & Drink set, 11h–15h, checked 2026-09-29.

Which burger spots in Bangkok are open late or 24/7?

25 Degrees Bangkok states “WE’RE OPEN 24/7” on its official site, checked 2026-09-29. I found no verified late-night hours for the other two.

Do I need a reservation, and what about halal or vegetarian options?

I found no verified reservation, halal, or vegetarian policy from a primary source for these three spots on 2026-09-29. Check the official site or Instagram before you go and ask staff directly.

What I could not verify yet

Seven other addresses, all Google ratings, undated THB prices, Sukhumvit / Thonglor / Ekkamai / Sathorn rankings, founder names, and awards remain unverified. I will expand this starter into a full guide once addresses and menus are confirmed on primary sources.
