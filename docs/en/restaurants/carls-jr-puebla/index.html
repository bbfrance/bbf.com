Carl's Jr. Puebla: Locations, Hours and Prices

Five official Carl's Jr. branches in Puebla, from the historic center to Angelópolis: addresses, hours, menu prices in pesos and reviews.

By Carmen, Restaurants writer, MexicoPublished November 11, 2026

Carl’s Jr. has at least five restaurants in the Puebla metro area. For a visitor, the easiest is Carl’s Jr. Centro Puebla at 16 de Septiembre #107, in the historic center, which Rappi lists as open daily from 8:30 a.m. to 9:45 p.m. A Famous Star with cheese costs 104 MXN there on Rappi. The most reviewed branch is Galerías Serdán: 4.3/5 from 3,465 Google reviews. I checked every address, price and rating below on November 4, 2026, and where the chain’s official site and a directory disagree, I went with the official site.

Carl’s Jr. Puebla at a glance

Carl’s Jr. has five branches in the Puebla area listed on carlsjr.com.mx: Centro, Galerías Serdán, Zavaleta, Triángulo and Plaza Palmas Life, which technically sits in San Andrés Cholula. When a sixth restaurant in Puebla state opened in Tehuacán in September 2025, the local outlet Posta counted five in the city itself.

  • Best for visitors: Centro Puebla, a short walk from the zócalo.
  • Most reviewed: Galerías Serdán, inside a shopping mall with parking and wheelchair access.
  • Late night: a branch on Vía Atlixcáyotl was reported open 24 hours Thursday to Saturday in July 2025.
  • Budget: 200 to 300 MXN per person at Galerías Serdán, according to Restaurant Guru.

Carl’s Jr. Centro Puebla: the branch in the historic center

Carl’s Jr. Centro Puebla is at 16 de Septiembre #107, Col. Centro, 72000 Puebla, phone (222) 246 0776. It’s the branch I’d send a first-time visitor to. You’re already downtown for the cathedral, so there’s no taxi to pay.

The official branch page gives the address and phone number but no opening hours. Rappi fills that gap: every day, 8:30 a.m. to 9:45 p.m. Rappi also puts the restaurant at number 101 rather than 107. I went with the official 107.

On Rappi, Centro Puebla scores 4.1/5 from 228 ratings, and delivery takes about 35 minutes. I couldn’t find Google, TripAdvisor or Yelp ratings for this branch, so I’m not quoting any.

Galerías Serdán: the most reviewed Carl’s Jr. in Puebla

Galerías Serdán has more reviews than any other Carl’s Jr. in Puebla: 4.3/5 on Google from 3,465 reviews and 7.9/10 on Foursquare from 58, according to Restaurant Guru on November 4, 2026. It’s inside the Galerías Serdán mall on Blvd. Hermanos Serdán.

Carl’s Jr. Puebla ratings: Galerías Serdán 4.3/5 on Google (3,465 reviews) and 7.9/10 on Foursquare (58, shown as 3.95/5); Centro 4.1/5 on Rappi (228 ratings), as of November 4, 2026. (Chart: Burger Breton, CC0, data from Restaurant Guru and Rappi (2026-11-04), rendered with QuickChart (800×400 PNG; rebuild labels per locale))

Restaurant Guru lists dine-in, takeout and delivery through Uber Eats and Rappi, plus parking, wheelchair access and card payments. There are no reservations. It’s the only Puebla branch where I could confirm parking and accessibility, which makes it the default if you’re driving or using a wheelchair.

Triángulo: the Club Puebla branch

Triángulo, in Las Ánimas, is the branch Carl’s Jr. México chose for a Club Puebla autograph session. On Tuesday, February 24, 2026, at 4 p.m., players Iker Moreno, Angelo Araos, Emiliano Gómez and Lucas Cavallini signed for fans who brought a purchase receipt, according to the chain’s official post of February 16, 2026.

Plaza Palmas Life: near Angelópolis, in San Andrés Cholula

Plaza Palmas Life is the official branch closest to Angelópolis and Cholula, at Av. Zeta del Cochero 423 in the Reserva Territorial Atlixcáyotl. The chain files it under Puebla, but it’s in the municipality of San Andrés Cholula. That’s a common trap with “Puebla” addresses on this side of the metro area.

Is there a 24-hour Carl’s Jr. in Puebla?

Maybe. A Carl’s Jr. on Vía Atlixcáyotl was reported open 24 hours Thursday to Saturday in July 2025. The only source is InformaBTL, which cited a Diario Cambio video, and I found nothing confirming the schedule in November 2026. Call before a late-night trip.

This Atlixcáyotl branch opened in May 2022 across from the BUAP’s Complejo Cultural Universitario, in the Angelópolis area, and Carl’s Jr. México announced its “Gran Apertura” on June 9, 2022. I couldn’t find its street address, and nothing shows it’s the same restaurant as Plaza Palmas Life, so I list them separately.

Vegetarian option at Carl’s Jr. Puebla

The vegetarian option at Carl’s Jr. Puebla is the Veggie Star Burger, 152 MXN on Rappi at the Centro branch. Carl’s Jr. warns that some products depend on availability by restaurant or city, so check the app for your branch.

Is there a Puebla-only burger?

No. I found no Puebla-exclusive burger at Carl’s Jr. One reviewer at Galerías Serdán mentions a “Diablo burger,” but it isn’t on the official menu. For a sandwich with a real local accent, leave the chain and order a cemita poblana.

Deals: Martes Todos Comen Carl’s and the 35th anniversary

The official Puebla branch pages promote two national deals: “Martes Todos Comen Carl’s” on Tuesdays and the chain’s 35 years in Mexico. For the anniversary, a Famous Star con Queso sold for 35 MXN on Wednesday, September 30, 2026, combos excluded, according to Crónica. The excluded locations were airports, Mérida (see our Carl’s Jr. Mérida guide) and Galerías Campeche, none of them in Puebla.

Carl’s Jr. Puebla reviews: what diners say

Diners at Galerías Serdán praise the bacon, the guacamole, the onion rings and the friendly staff, according to reviews summarized by Restaurant Guru. The complaints come back just as often: prices called “excesivamente caros” (way too expensive), uneven service and inattentive cashiers at peak times.

My take: order the Guacamole Bacon Burger, the item reviewers keep praising, and stay away from the mall at lunchtime if you’re in a hurry. If price matters, the Famous Star at 104 MXN is the best-value burger on this menu, and a Tuesday promotion makes it cheaper still. Downtown, walk into Centro rather than ordering through Rappi. The app’s prices are the delivery prices, and the branch is a few steps away.

Carl’s Jr. in Puebla: openings and history

Carl’s Jr. came to Mexico in 1991. Its first restaurant outside the United States opened in San Pedro Garza García, Nuevo León. The chain opened its 400th Mexican restaurant in Tampico in December 2024, according to PR Newswire. The brand itself goes back to 1941, when Carl Karcher bought a hot dog cart in Los Angeles. The full story is on our Carl’s Jr. brand page.

Puebla’s growth spurt came in 2022. After Atlixcáyotl in May, Pabellón Serdán in Villa San Alejandro opened on November 15, 2022, and its first 50 customers got a booklet of free burgers good for 365 days, Telediario reported. Pabellón Serdán has no official branch page today, so I can’t confirm it’s still open.

On Wednesday, September 3, 2025, Carl’s Jr. opened in Tehuacán, at the El Paseo mall next to the City Express hotel, open 10 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily. Locals camped out on Avenida Adolfo López Mateos for a free burger, Posta reported.

Carl’s Jr. timeline from Los Angeles to Puebla: 1941 hot dog cart, 1956 first restaurants in Anaheim and Brea, 1991 first Mexican restaurant in San Pedro Garza García, May 2022 Atlixcáyotl, November 2022 Pabellón Serdán, December 2024 400th Mexican restaurant in Tampico, September 2025 Tehuacán, September 30, 2026 35th-anniversary deal. Which Carl’s Jr. in Puebla should you choose?

Pick the branch by where you’re staying: Centro if you’re sightseeing downtown, Galerías Serdán if you need parking, and Plaza Palmas Life if you’re in Angelópolis or Cholula.

  • Historic center: Centro Puebla, the earliest confirmed opening in the city (8:30 a.m. on Rappi).
  • Driving, or need step-free access: Galerías Serdán, with mall parking and wheelchair access.
  • Angelópolis, Cholula, BUAP campus: Plaza Palmas Life, or the Vía Atlixcáyotl branch for a possible late-night stop.
  • Las Ánimas: Triángulo.

FAQ: Carl’s Jr. Puebla

How many Carl’s Jr. restaurants are there in Puebla? At least five have official pages: Centro, Galerías Serdán, Zavaleta, Triángulo and Plaza Palmas Life (San Andrés Cholula). A sixth in Puebla state opened in Tehuacán in September 2025.

Which Carl’s Jr. in Puebla is open 24 hours? The branch on Vía Atlixcáyotl was reported open 24 hours Thursday to Saturday in July 2025. That’s unconfirmed for November 2026, so call ahead.

What are the hours of the Carl’s Jr. in Puebla’s historic center? Rappi lists Centro Puebla as open daily from 8:30 a.m. to 9:45 p.m. The official page gives no hours.

How much is a Famous Star at Carl’s Jr. in Puebla? On Rappi, a Famous Star con Queso costs 104 MXN and the combo 240 MXN at the Centro branch (November 4, 2026). In-store prices may be lower.

Does Carl’s Jr. Puebla deliver? Yes. Centro Puebla delivers on Rappi in about 35 minutes, and Galerías Serdán delivers through Uber Eats and Rappi. I couldn’t confirm DiDi Food.

Is there a Carl’s Jr. drive-thru in Puebla? No source confirms a drive-thru at any Puebla branch. Check with the restaurant before you go.

Does Carl’s Jr. Puebla have vegetarian burgers? Yes: the Veggie Star Burger, 152 MXN on Rappi at Centro Puebla.

Is the Carl’s Jr. menu in Mexico different from the US menu? The core burgers are the same: flame-grilled Famous Star, Super Star, Western Bacon and Big Carl, plus the Big Angus range, breakfasts, chicken, salads and shakes. Availability varies by restaurant and city, and Puebla has no known local exclusive.

Is there a Carl’s Jr. near Angelópolis or Cholula? Yes: Plaza Palmas Life, at Av. Zeta del Cochero 423 in San Andrés Cholula, and the Vía Atlixcáyotl branch across from the BUAP’s Complejo Cultural Universitario.

When is breakfast served at Carl’s Jr. in Mexico? The official Mexican menu includes breakfasts, but I couldn’t find official breakfast hours for the Puebla branches. Centro Puebla opens at 8:30 a.m. on Rappi.
