MOS Burger Fukuoka: Hakata, Tenjin, Nakasu (2026)

Which branch to pick near Hakata Station, in Tenjin or for a late night in Nakasu, with addresses, opening hours, 2026 prices in yen and what reviewers say.

By Hana, Restaurants writer, Japan and IndonesiaPublished November 24, 2026

MOS Burger has several branches in Fukuoka, and which one you want depends on where you are. Getting off the Shinkansen? MOS Burger Hakata Chikushi-guchi is on the 2nd floor of the JR Chikushi Building, a 1-minute walk from JR Hakata Station. It opens at 7 a.m. For a late meal, MOS Burger Hakata Nakasu is listed as open until 2 a.m. For a Tenjin shopping day, go to Tenjin-Kita, 3 minutes from Tenjin Station exit 6. Under national prices in effect since July 15, 2026, a classic MOS Burger costs ¥500 and a Teriyaki Burger ¥490, tax included.

I haven’t eaten at the Fukuoka branches yet, so this guide has no rating of my own. I built it from published listings and reviews (Tabelog, Retty, Hot Pepper, Jalan, Gurunavi, Wanderlog, Spinach.guide), MOS’s official menu, visitor page and press releases, and the Japanese press. I checked every address, opening time and score on November 3, 2026, and each one names its source. 1 thing to know before you read the scores: Tabelog rates much harder than Google, and a score around 3.0 there is ordinary.

Which MOS Burger in Fukuoka should you choose?

Pick the MOS Burger Fukuoka branch closest to where you’ll be. The menu barely changes from 1 branch to the next. Hours, payment options and location change a lot.

  • Arriving or leaving by Shinkansen: Hakata Chikushi-guchi, 41 m from JR Hakata Station, from 7 a.m.
  • Shopping in Tenjin: Tenjin-Kita, 220 m from Tenjin Station (exit 6).
  • Late night in Nakasu: Hakata Nakasu, listed as open until 2 a.m., with an English menu and the best review scores in the city.
  • Staying near Ropponmatsu and the student area: Ropponmatsu, 53 m from Ropponmatsu Station on the Nanakuma subway line, 7 a.m. to midnight. Bring cash.
  • Driving, or staying in Higashi-ku: Fukuoka Doi, 7 a.m. to 1 a.m., with parking.
  • Staying south of Hakata Station on a budget: Minoshima, inside the Mr Max store, about 1.2 km from the station.

If I could only pick 1 MOS meal in Fukuoka, I’d go to Nakasu. It has the highest scores in the city on both Google and TripAdvisor, a large sample of reviews and the latest closing time. Reviewers also say the staff speak English.

MOS Burger at Hakata Station: Chikushi exit

The MOS Burger at Hakata Station is on the Chikushi exit side (the Shinkansen side), on the 2nd floor of the JR Chikushi Building at 1-1 Hakata-eki Chūōgai. Retty puts it 1 minute (41 m) from JR Hakata Station and 9 minutes (680 m) from Gion subway station.

The branch name is confusing. Retty calls it Hakata Chikushi-guchi. Hot Pepper and Jalan list a “MOS BURGER Excite Hakata” (モスバーガーエキサイド博多店) at the same address, and Jalan gives the same phone number, 092-432-2815. It’s almost certainly the same restaurant under 2 names. The closing time is the one thing that differs: midnight for Retty, 11 p.m. for Hot Pepper and Gurunavi. If you’re arriving on a late train, plan on 11 p.m.

Hot Pepper counts 76 seats. Retty says the dining room is fully non-smoking. On payment, Retty lists cards (American Express is named) and PayPay, and Hot Pepper adds e-money and QR codes. That makes it the easiest MOS in Fukuoka to pay at without cash.

2 other station names come up in searches. Gurunavi mentions a Deitos Annex branch. A MOS Burger Hakata Bus Terminal also appears, listed at 2-1 Hakata-eki Chūōgai with 83 seats. But the official Hakata Bus Terminal floor guide showed no MOS when I checked on November 3, 2026. Treat it as possibly closed and head to the Chikushi exit instead.

MOS Burger Nakasu: late-night burgers until 2 a.m.

MOS Burger Hakata Nakasu, at 3-7-18 Nakasu, is the MOS Burger to know for a late night in Fukuoka. Spinach.guide and Wanderlog both list it as open daily from 9 a.m. to 2 a.m. Neither one is an official source, so call +81 92-263-8868 before you count on it after midnight.

It also has the best reviews of any MOS in Fukuoka. Wanderlog shows 4.0/5 on Google from 591 reviews and 4.1/5 on TripAdvisor from 32 (checked November 3, 2026). Reviewers keep coming back to a clean dining room and staff who speak English, and say the food comes out hot and fast. The Wagyu Beef Burger gets mentioned by name. The complaints: fries that are too salty, and a Green Burger that divides people.

Spinach.guide says the branch takes mobile payment and has an English menu. It’s the easy choice if you don’t read Japanese.

MOS Burger Tenjin-Kita: the Tenjin shopping stop

MOS Burger Tenjin-Kita, at 3-16-1 Tenjin in Chūō-ku, is the MOS for a shopping day in Tenjin. It’s 220 m from Tenjin Station. Gurunavi says that’s 3 minutes from exit 6.

Its hours are the most disputed in the city. Tabelog and Gurunavi say 7 a.m. to 1 a.m. every day. Wanderlog says 9 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. The official page didn’t load when I checked, so I can’t tell you which is right. If you’re going early or late, call 092-771-3377 first.

It scores 3.8/5 on Google (656 reviews, via Wanderlog), 3.6/5 on TripAdvisor (28 reviews) and 3.04/5 on Tabelog (43 reviews), as of November 3, 2026. Reviewers like the quiet dining room and say the food is less greasy than at a typical fast-food chain. They name the onion rings and fries, the rice burgers and the shrimp croquette burger, and mention an English menu. The criticisms: the burgers vary from 1 visit to the next, the ketchup is stingy and the restrooms aren’t always clean. Tabelog notes a dedicated smoking area and a budget under ¥999.

Ropponmatsu, Doi and Minoshima: neighborhood MOS Burgers

These 3 MOS Burger Fukuoka branches are for people staying outside the center: students and residents in Ropponmatsu, drivers in Higashi-ku, and budget travelers south of Hakata Station.

A MOS Burger in a converted traditional Japanese house in Kitakyushu, also in Fukuoka Prefecture. Illustrative photo from 2007, not one of the Fukuoka city branches in this guide. (Photo: Nissy-KITAQ, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mos\_burger\_Uomachi\_minami.JPG)

Ropponmatsu is 53 m from Ropponmatsu Station on the Nanakuma subway line. Tabelog lists it as open daily from 7 a.m. to midnight, with a dedicated smoking area. It scores 3.01/5 on Tabelog from 12 reviews. The catch: Tabelog says it takes cash only.

Fukuoka Doi is 368 m from JR Doi Station in Higashi-ku and opens daily from 7 a.m. to 1 a.m. Tabelog lists parking, takeout and a dedicated smoking area, and scores it 3.01/5 from 12 reviews. It’s the one to pick if you’re driving.

Minoshima is inside the Mr Max store at 2-5-6 Minoshima, about 1.2 km from Hakata Station. Wanderlog lists it as open from 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. and shows 3.7/5 on Google from 295 reviews. Reviewers say a meal with a drink comes in under ¥1,000 and the staff are patient. There’s no English menu, so have the official English menu open on your phone.

MOS warns that some branches charge different prices, and I didn’t verify any Fukuoka-specific price, so check the menu board before you order.

What to order at MOS Burger

Start with the classic MOS Burger, with its meat sauce, and the Teriyaki Burger, which dates from 1973. Then try something you won’t find back home: a rice burger, a MOS invention from 1987 that swaps the bun for pressed grilled rice. The ¥510 seafood kakiage version is on the official English menu, and Tenjin-Kita reviewers name the rice burgers among their favorites. To make the teriyaki at home, my colleague Clara has a copycat recipe for the MOS teriyaki burger.

Vegetarian and vegan options at MOS Burger

MOS Burger’s fully plant-based option is the Green Burger, launched in 2021 with no animal-derived ingredients. Since 2015, the Soy Patty has been available as a meat-free swap. Spinach.guide confirms the Green Burger at the Nakasu branch and calls it vegan, but suggests asking about the sauces because of the risk of cross-contamination. MOS publishes an allergen list in English on its page for international visitors.

Fukuoka’s own MOS item: the Amaou strawberry shake

MOS’s Kyushu exclusive is a strawberry shake, not a burger. On July 19, 2023, MOS launched the “Mazeru Shake Amaou” in its Kyushu restaurants, with a few exceptions. It’s a vanilla shake mixed with a sauce made from Amaou strawberries from Fukuoka Prefecture, with 3.3% fruit pieces, according to MOS’s press release.

I can only confirm it for 2023. A March 2024 press release also shows up, but I found nothing proving it’s on sale in November 2026, and I won’t quote a price. Think of it as a seasonal Kyushu special. If you spot it on the menu board, order it.

How to pay at MOS Burger in Fukuoka

Officially, MOS accepts cash, credit cards and e-money, including prepaid transit cards. MOS adds 2 caveats on its visitor page: electronic payment may not be available at some restaurants, and some don’t have the multilingual menu.

Branch by branch, here’s what I could confirm: cards and PayPay at Hakata Chikushi-guchi (Retty), mobile payment at Nakasu (Spinach.guide), and cash only at Ropponmatsu (Tabelog). I couldn’t confirm Hayakaken, Fukuoka’s transit card, or Suica at any specific branch. Carry a few thousand yen in cash, just in case.

MOS Burger or Hakata ramen at a yatai?

Eat Hakata ramen at a yatai first. The open-air food stalls are what Fukuoka is famous for, and no chain burger replaces that night out. MOS does a different job. It covers a 7 a.m. breakfast before a train at Hakata, a fast lunch in Tenjin, or a meal in Nakasu at 1 a.m. that still comes in around ¥1,000. The teriyaki and rice burgers also make it more than a fallback. For independent burger bars in the city, start from the Fukuoka burger hub.

A short history of MOS Burger

Atsushi Sakurada founded MOS Burger. The first test stand opened on March 12, 1972, near Narimasu Station in Itabashi, Tokyo, and Mos Food Services was incorporated on July 21, 1972. The name was later read as “Mountain, Ocean, Sun”. At the end of June 2026, MOS had 1,308 restaurants in Japan, down from a peak of 1,321 in March 2025. Ryutsuu News reports sales of ¥24.636 billion for April–June 2026, up 3.6%. For the chain’s full profile, see the MOS Burger brand page. For the bigger story, read the history of the hamburger.

MOS Burger Fukuoka FAQ

Is there a MOS Burger at Hakata Station, and at which exit? Yes. It’s on the Chikushi exit (Shinkansen) side, on the 2nd floor of the JR Chikushi Building at 1-1 Hakata-eki Chūōgai, a 1-minute walk from the station. Depending on the site, it’s listed as Hakata Chikushi-guchi or Excite Hakata, with the same phone number, 092-432-2815.

Which MOS Burger in Fukuoka is open late? Hakata Nakasu is listed as open until 2 a.m. by Spinach.guide and Wanderlog. Fukuoka Doi is open until 1 a.m. and Ropponmatsu until midnight, according to Tabelog. Tenjin-Kita may close at 1 a.m. or 10:30 p.m., depending on the source, so call first.

What time do MOS Burger branches in Fukuoka open for breakfast? Hakata Chikushi-guchi, Ropponmatsu and Fukuoka Doi open at 7 a.m. every day. Nakasu and Minoshima open at 9 a.m., according to aggregator listings.

How much is a MOS Burger meal in Fukuoka in 2026? A MOS Burger costs ¥500, a Teriyaki Burger ¥490, medium fries ¥340 and a medium soft drink ¥290, tax included, under national prices in effect since July 15, 2026. Ordered separately, burger, fries and drink come to ¥1,130. Some branches charge different prices.

Can I pay by card, PayPay or transit card? Hakata Chikushi-guchi takes cards and PayPay, and Nakasu takes mobile payment. Ropponmatsu is cash only, according to Tabelog. MOS says it accepts transit cards in general, but I couldn’t confirm Hayakaken or Suica at any specific Fukuoka branch.

Does MOS Burger sell anything exclusive to Fukuoka or Kyushu? In July 2023, MOS launched the Mazeru Shake Amaou in Kyushu, a vanilla shake with a sauce made from Fukuoka’s Amaou strawberries. I couldn’t confirm that it’s on sale in November 2026.

Is there an English menu and a vegan option? Nakasu and Tenjin-Kita have English menus, according to reviewers and Spinach.guide. Minoshima doesn’t. The Green Burger contains no animal-derived ingredients. Spinach.guide confirms it at Nakasu, but ask about the sauces.

How is MOS Burger different from McDonald’s in Japan? MOS is Japan’s own burger chain, founded in Tokyo in 1972. It’s known for the Teriyaki Burger (1973) and the rice burger (1987), and Fukuoka reviewers describe its food as less greasy than typical fast food.
