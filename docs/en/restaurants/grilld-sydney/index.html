Grill'd Sydney: 25 Restaurants, Menu and Prices

All 25 Grill'd restaurants in Sydney: addresses, Saturday hours, menu prices in A$, diner ratings and which CBD branch to pick.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished October 17, 2026

Grill’d has 25 restaurants in Greater Sydney, by my count from the chain’s official list of 37 New South Wales sites, checked on 9 October 2026. If you only try 1, make it Grill’d World Square at 58/680 George St in the CBD. It is confirmed open, it serves from 10:30 am to 10 pm on Saturdays, and its 1,122 Google reviews (4.2/5) are the biggest sample of the 2 Sydney branches whose ratings I could verify. Want a table you can book? Go to Grill’d Barangaroo, the newest CBD branch. A burger costs about A$14.50 to A$18.90, based on national average prices.

This guide is part of our Sydney burger city guide, filed under the Burger Breton restaurants section. If you are also heading to the capital, I covered the same chain in Grill’d Canberra: all 5 restaurants, menu and prices.

How I checked this guide

Every address, phone number and opening time below comes from the official Grill’d restaurant pages, checked on 9 October 2026. Ratings come from Wanderlog, which aggregates Google and TripAdvisor scores, because I could not open TripAdvisor or Google Maps directly. Prices come from menuprices.com.au, updated 9 August 2026. They are national averages, not prices I paid in Sydney. Where a source was missing or contradicted another, I left the fact out and said so.

Grill’d in the Sydney CBD: World Square, Barangaroo and Galeries

3 Grill’d restaurants sit in the Sydney CBD: World Square and Galeries on George St, and Barangaroo on the harbour side. My advice is simple. Go to World Square for a quick meal and Barangaroo when you want a guaranteed table, and phone Galeries before walking there.

Grill’d World Square: the most reviewed CBD branch

Grill’d World Square is at World Square Shopping Centre, 58/680 George St, Sydney NSW 2000, phone +61 2 9261 4900. It opens 10:30 am to 10 pm on Saturdays. The official page lists Pick Up, delivery and catering, with catering orders placed before 5 pm for pick-up the next day (official page).

It scores 4.2/5 on Google from 1,122 reviews and 4.0/5 on TripAdvisor from 273 reviews (Wanderlog). Of the 2 Sydney branches with ratings I could verify, that is the larger sample by far. Diners come back to the same points: the original burgers, the sweet potato chips, friendly staff, a clean dining room. The complaints repeat too. Service is slow at peak times, and the gluten-free buns come out over-toasted.

Grill’d Barangaroo: the newest CBD branch, with table bookings

Grill’d Barangaroo, officially “Grill’d Burgers Barangaroo”, is at 6/25-31 Barangaroo Ave, Barangaroo NSW 2000, phone +61 2 7262 0250. It opens 10:30 am to 10 pm on Saturdays. It is one of the few Sydney branches where you can book a dine-in table online, and the only one in the CBD. It also takes group bookings and offers Pick Up, delivery and catering (official page).

It opened on Thursday 2 October 2025. The first 754 customers got a free burger, and burgers were A$10 on the opening Thursday and Friday. Founder Simon Crowe called it an addition to “our Sydney CBD restaurant stable” (realcommercial.com.au).

Grill’d Galeries: call before you go

Grill’d Galeries is at The Galeries, Level 1, 500 George St, Sydney NSW 2000, phone +61 2 9264 9566. It is the one CBD branch I cannot vouch for. On 9 October 2026 the official Grill’d page announced a reopening on “Thursday, October 8th”, with no year. 8 October fell on a Thursday in 2026, so the branch has most likely just reopened, but the Galeries centre website still listed it as “Temporarily Closed”. When open, the centre gives usual hours of 11 am to 10 pm every day, with dine-in, Pick Up, delivery, catering and group bookings (official page, The Galeries). A 1-minute call to +61 2 9264 9566 saves you the walk.

Which Grill’d to choose by Sydney neighbourhood

Pick World Square or Barangaroo in the CBD, Bondi Beach after a swim, Darlinghurst Crown St if you are on Oxford Street, Westfield Parramatta in the west and Crows Nest on the North Shore. Here is why, plus a few others worth knowing.

  • Bondi Beach: Shop 1 & 2, 89-93 Hall St, on the corner of Hall St and “450m from Bondi Beach”, per the official page. It was announced as the chain’s 166th restaurant in Australia, and 3 local lifeguards created signature burgers for charities: Live, Learn & Survive, Ocean Heroes and the Waves of Wellness Foundation (QSR Media, Visit Bondi Beach).
  • Darlinghurst Crown St: 241 Crown St, “right around the corner from Oxford Street”. It scores 3.9/5 on Google (750 reviews) and 4.5/5 on TripAdvisor (160 reviews). Fans point to fresh ingredients and the Healthy Fried Chicken burger. Critics mention uneven service and incomplete delivery orders, and 1 diner reported a 30-minute wait and a cold order (Wanderlog). My take: eat in rather than order delivery here.
  • Westfield Parramatta: Shop 4069, 159-175 Church St, “only six minutes from Parramatta train station”. The official page lists online ordering, Pick Up, delivery, catering and group bookings.
  • Crows Nest: 57 Willoughby Rd. The official page calls it “Our first restaurant in NSW”, and it takes dine-in table bookings.
  • Bondi Junction: 9-13 Bronte Rd, recently renovated according to the official page, open until 10:30 pm on Saturdays.
  • Broadway: inside Broadway Shopping Centre, a short hop from the CBD, with dine-in and group bookings.
  • Blacktown: in May 2023 it became the first halal-certified Grill’d (see the halal question below).
  • Top Ryde: the official page mentions outdoor seating on La Piazza.

Grill’d Sydney opening hours

Most Grill’d restaurants in Sydney open at 10:30 am and close at 10 pm on Saturdays. Rouse Hill and Leichhardt stay open until 11 pm. Balgowlah keeps the shortest day, 11:30 am to 9 pm. The official pages only display the current day’s hours, so Saturday is the only day I could verify. Check the restaurant’s page on grilld.com.au for weekday hours before you go.

What is on the Grill’d menu

The Grill’d menu is built around “Hi Fibre Lo GI” buns, 100% Australian lamb and wagyu specialty burgers, and chicken tenders. The official menu also lists the High Performance range, whose Protein Pro packs 81 g of protein per serving, plus sliders, salads and a kids’ menu (grilld.com.au/menu).

  • Healthy Fried Chicken: tenders made with 100% RSPCA-approved chicken, cooked in extra virgin olive oil.
  • Wraps, until 5 pm: The Classic, Bacon Club, Cali Green Goddess and Golden Caesar.
  • Grill’d x Twisties: a collaboration with the Australian snack brand, with a cheese option for an extra A$2.
  • Sides: Famous Grill’d Chips and sweet potato chips, the side World Square reviewers mention most.

There is no Sydney-only item. The menu is the same across Australia. If you order 1 side, make it the sweet potato chips: at about A$8.90 they cost A$1.40 more than the regular chips, and they are the side diners mention most.

Vegetarian and gluten-free burgers in Sydney

Grill’d is a practical choice for a vegetarian or gluten-free burger in Sydney. Vegetarians can order the Garden Goodness (about A$14.90) or a burger from the Beyond Meat range, such as the Beyond Simply Grill’d (about A$15.90). Gluten-free buns are available, but some World Square reviewers find them over-toasted. Ask for yours lightly toasted.

Closed or outdated Grill’d addresses in Sydney

Grill’d Darling Harbour, Surry Hills and Westfield Sydney are not on the official list, even though Time Out Sydney still shows them. Darling Harbour was at Harbourside, 2-10 Darling Drive, a centre closed for redevelopment since 2022. Surry Hills did not close so much as change its name: its address, 241 Crown St, is now the Darlinghurst Crown St restaurant. I found no closing dates for any of them (Time Out Darling Harbour, Time Out Surry Hills, Wikipedia, Darling Harbour).

Grill’d history: the Australian healthy burger chain

Grill’d is an Australian burger chain founded by Simon Crowe on 2 March 2004 in Hawthorn, Melbourne, and sold on the promise of “healthy burgers”. It now runs more than 170 restaurants in Australia, plus 1 in Seminyak, Bali. Its beef and lamb have been grass-fed and free-range since 2010 and its chicken RSPCA-approved since 2016. Since August 2025 its patties have also been sold in Coles supermarkets (Wikipedia, Dynamic Business).

In Sydney, the chain started in Crows Nest, which it calls its first NSW restaurant, and it now counts 25 sites across the metro area. On 16 June 2026, the ACCC took Grill’d to the Federal Court over its “Tree Day Tuesday” campaign, run with Greenfleet from January 2021 to April 2024. The ACCC alleges that only 17% of sales qualified for a donation. The case is ongoing (The New Daily, Mediaweek).

Grill’d Sydney FAQ

How many Grill’d restaurants are there in Sydney?

There are 25 Grill’d restaurants in Greater Sydney, out of 37 in New South Wales, by my count from the official list checked on 9 October 2026. The CBD Galeries branch was announced as reopening on 8 October but was still listed as closed by its shopping centre.

Which Grill’d is closest to the Sydney CBD?

Grill’d World Square at 58/680 George St is the most central branch confirmed open. Grill’d Barangaroo at 6/25-31 Barangaroo Ave is the other CBD option. Phone Galeries at 500 George St before you go.

What are Grill’d opening hours in Sydney?

On Saturdays, most Sydney branches open from 10:30 am to 10 pm. Rouse Hill and Leichhardt close at 11 pm, and Balgowlah opens 11:30 am to 9 pm. Each official page only shows weekday hours on the day itself.

How much does a burger cost at Grill’d?

A Grill’d burger costs between A$14.50 (Simply Grill’d) and A$18.90 (Wagyu Wunder), based on national average prices from August 2026. Regular chips add about A$7.50, and delivery costs 15 to 30% more.

Is Grill’d halal?

Since May 2024, Grill’d has served halal meat, except bacon, across all its restaurants, according to Wikipedia and Inside Retail, without certification at each restaurant. Blacktown became the first halal-certified Grill’d in May 2023, with no bacon and no alcohol, but its official page no longer mentions certification. Ask the restaurant if certification matters to you (Inside Retail).

Does Grill’d have gluten-free, vegetarian or vegan burgers?

Yes. Grill’d offers gluten-free buns, the vegetarian Garden Goodness and a plant-based Beyond Meat range, including the Beyond Simply Grill’d. Some World Square reviewers find the gluten-free buns over-toasted.

Can you book a table at Grill’d in Sydney?

Yes, at some branches. Barangaroo and Crows Nest take online dine-in table bookings, and Neutral Bay lists dine-in bookings. Several others, including Darlinghurst, Broadway and Bondi Beach, take group bookings.

Is there a Grill’d near Bondi Beach or Darling Harbour?

Grill’d Bondi Beach is at 89-93 Hall St, 450 m from the beach. The Darling Harbour branch at Harbourside is no longer on the official list. The nearest options are Barangaroo and World Square in the CBD.

Does Grill’d deliver in Sydney?

Yes. The official pages of the Sydney restaurants list delivery and Pick Up, and Westfield Parramatta also lists online ordering. Delivery prices run 15 to 30% higher than in-store prices.

Is Grill’d healthier than regular fast food?

Grill’d bases its “healthy burgers” claim on Hi Fibre Lo GI buns, grass-fed free-range beef and lamb, RSPCA-approved chicken and tenders cooked in extra virgin olive oil. It also sells a high-protein range, with 81 g of protein in the Protein Pro. Even so, a wagyu burger with chips and a shake is still a heavy meal. How healthy it is depends on what you order, not on the logo.
