Bascook Bilbao: Aitor Elizegi, Menu and Prices

Who the chef behind Bascook is, what his Abando restaurant serves for €20 to €72, and why it is not the burger bar some directories claim.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished November 27, 2026

Bascook is Aitor Elizegi’s restaurant in Bilbao. It opened on 7 June 2010 in a former salt warehouse at C. Barroeta Aldamar 8, in the Abando district, and serves what it calls “urban & Basque, green & local” cooking. Lunch starts at €20 for the dish of the day; set menus run from €35 on weekdays to €72 for the tasting menu with drinks (official menu, checked 20 November 2026). One thing first, because several booking sites get it wrong: Bascook is not a burger restaurant. There is no burger on its menu.

Aitor Elizegi is also the man who presided over Athletic Club from 2018 to 2022, and most English-language results stop there. This profile covers the other half of his career: the cook, his restaurants from 1995 to today, and what you will actually find at Bascook if you book a table.

Who is Aitor Elizegi, the chef behind Bascook Bilbao?

Aitor Elizegi Alberdi is a Bilbao chef and restaurateur, born on 27 June 1966 in the Santutxu neighbourhood, who trained at the Galdakao catering school and later served as president of Athletic Club. Sources: Wikipedia and BAO, La Revista de Bilbao.

Aitor Elizegi, the chef behind Bascook Bilbao, with Bilbao councillor Koldo Narbaiza and Jon Arrugaeta of Siadeco at the launch of the Biribilko service in Bilbao, 27 September 2017. (Photo: Bizkaie, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Biribilko.jpg)

In the Basque Country he is known as the “Rey del Bacalao”, the king of cod, for his salt-cod cooking (Muselines). Bascook’s own website goes further and calls him the world’s “número uno” in cod cuisine. That is the restaurant talking, not an independent ranking.

Aitor Elizegi at Athletic Club

Elizegi was president of Athletic Club from 27 December 2018 to 24 June 2022. He beat Alberto Uribe-Echevarría by 9,264 votes to 9,179. Eighty-five votes. He did not run again and was succeeded by Jon Uriarte (Wikipedia).

Athletic Club presidential election, 27 December 2018: Aitor Elizegi won 9,264 votes to Alberto Uribe-Echevarría’s 9,179, a margin of 85 votes (source: Wikipedia). (Chart: Burger Breton, data from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aitor\_Elizegi)

Other public roles in Bilbao

Elizegi was the pregonero, the official opening speaker, of Aste Nagusia, Bilbao’s big summer festival, in 2008. From 2017 he also chaired Bilbao Dendak, the city’s merchants’ association. Both facts come from a single source, Muselines.

What Bascook serves: urban Basque cooking in a salt warehouse

Bascook cooks seasonal Basque produce from short supply chains, linked to the Slow Food movement, with international and especially Japanese techniques. The menu is split into vegetarian, classic and international sections (Bascook; Directo al Paladar).

When I went through the official menu on 20 November 2026, two dishes summed up the range. A tuna tartare brioche with guacamole and togarashi totopos (corn chips), €22.50. And a sliced grilled txuleta, the Basque bone-in rib steak, with potatoes panadera and piperrada, €32. The txuleta is the one I would order first: €32 for the steak Bilbao is proudest of, in a chef’s dining room, is a fair price for the city centre.

My pick is the €35 weekday lunch menu. It costs €23 less than the cheapest evening set menu, and since dinner only runs three nights a week, a Tuesday or Wednesday lunch is the easiest way to get a table.

Bascook opening hours, address and booking

Bascook is open for lunch Tuesday to Saturday, 13:00–15:30, and for dinner Thursday to Saturday, 20:30–23:00. It is closed on Sunday and Monday (official site, checked 20 November 2026).

Basquery, Elizegi’s other address in Abando

Basquery, at Ibáñez de Bilbao 8 near the Jardines de Albia, is Elizegi’s brewery-restaurant a few minutes’ walk from Bascook in the same 48001 postcode (salir.com; Muselines). Abando, across the river from the Old Town, is Bilbao’s central district. I could not confirm on an official source that Basquery is still open in November 2026, so call ahead.

Does Bascook serve burgers? No, and here is why the myth exists

Bascook does not serve burgers: its official menu, checked on 20 November 2026, has none. The closest dish is the tuna tartare brioche, a bun with a filling, but not a burger.

The confusion comes from booking and directory pages. Some carry generic, machine-written blurbs; one claims Bascook “takes the hamburger experience to the next level”. Nothing on the restaurant’s own menu supports that. Some pages also mix Elizegi up with Aitor Rauleaga, a different Bilbao chef whose restaurant is in the Michelin Guide.

Elizegi’s group may still have a burger link. Aggregator sites list a sirloin burger with homemade fries at Basquery. Basquery’s own site returned no content when I checked, so treat it as unconfirmed and phone +34 944 072 712 before going for it.

Where to eat a good burger in Bilbao

For burgers in Bilbao, look beyond Bascook. Our Bilbao burger page collects the city’s burger addresses as we verify them.

The aggregator Gastroranking currently lists HDH, Itxas Bide, Malditas Deusto, Berty’s Burger, Tipula Casco Viejo and Bilbo Burger among Bilbao’s burger restaurants. Itxas Bide stands out for beef aged 40 to 60 days. These are aggregator ratings, not our own verdicts.

The most decorated burger cook near Bilbao works outside the city. Imanol (Manuel Andrés Pérez) of Bediako Batzoki Berria in Bedia, about 20 km away, won Burger Combat 2025 at Salón Gourmets with “La Llama de Arratia”. It stacks a matured-cow chuck patty, a blend of Idiazábal, Ronkari cheddar and Ardibei cheeses, a crispy-coated runny yolk and a mushroom and onion stew, finished with oak smoke (Directo al Paladar, 7 April 2025). If you have a car and one burger to eat in Biscay, that is the one I would drive for.

Heading east along the coast? San Sebastián is about 100 km from Bilbao, and our best burgers in San Sebastián guide covers the Basque Country’s other burger city. For more cities, browse the Restaurants section.

Bascook Bilbao: FAQ

Who is Aitor Elizegi: a chef or the former Athletic Club president? Both. Aitor Elizegi is a Bilbao chef and restaurateur who opened his first restaurant, Gaminiz, in 1995, and he was president of Athletic Club from December 2018 to June 2022.

Who owns Bascook in Bilbao? Bascook is Aitor Elizegi’s restaurant: the official site presents it as “la propuesta del chef Aitor Elizegi”. His day-to-day role in the kitchen since leaving Athletic is not publicly documented.

Does Bascook serve burgers? No. The official menu, checked on 20 November 2026, has no burger. The closest dish is a tuna tartare brioche at €22.50.

How much does it cost to eat at Bascook, and is there a menú del día? Yes. A single dish of the day costs €20 (€26 with dessert), the weekday lunch menu (Tuesday–Friday) costs €35, and the set menus cost €58 to €60, or €70 to €72 with drinks.

What are Bascook’s opening hours, and do you need to book? Lunch runs Tuesday to Saturday, 13:00–15:30, and dinner Thursday to Saturday, 20:30–23:00; it is closed Sunday and Monday. Booking by phone, WhatsApp (+34 688 895 765) or email (reserva@bascook.com) is the safest option, especially for the three dinner services.

What kind of food does Bascook serve? Bascook serves seasonal Basque produce cooked with international, especially Japanese, techniques. The menu has vegetarian, classic and international sections.

Which other restaurants does Aitor Elizegi run in Bilbao? Besides Bascook, he opened Txocook, a Basque tavern on Uribitarte (2014), and Basquery, a brewery, bakery, deli and restaurant on Ibáñez de Bilbao (2017). Their current status was not confirmed on official sources in November 2026.

Is Bascook a Michelin-starred restaurant? No. Bascook holds no Michelin star. Do not confuse Aitor Elizegi with Aitor Rauleaga, a different Bilbao chef whose restaurant is in the Michelin Guide.

Where can you eat a good burger in Bilbao? Not at Bascook. Start with our Bilbao city page, and if you have a car, Bediako Batzoki Berria in Bedia, home of the Burger Combat 2025 winner, is about 20 km away.

How I put this profile together

I built this profile from Bascook’s official website and menu, the BAO and Muselines profiles of Elizegi, Wikipedia and Directo al Paladar, all checked on 20 November 2026. I did not eat at Bascook for this piece, so it carries no rating. Facts with a single source are attributed in the text.
