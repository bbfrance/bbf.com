Whataburger Houston: Best Location, Menu, Hours and Reviews

Which of Houston's 56 Whataburgers to choose, what to order, what it costs and when to go, with every address and hour checked on October 8, 2026.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished October 10, 2026

The Whataburger I would send a visitor to in Houston is No. 266 Westheimer & Timmons, at 3639 Westheimer Rd near Uptown and the Galleria. Its dining room and drive-thru never close. The building began life as a Herfy’s and was rebuilt in the early 1990s, so it has more history than most fast-food boxes in the city. The official locator lists 56 Whataburgers inside Houston city limits, so another one is rarely far away. First visit? Get the classic Whataburger or the Patty Melt and ask for Spicy Ketchup. After 11 p.m., the Honey Butter Chicken Biscuit joins the menu. I checked every address, phone number and hour below against the official Whataburger locator on October 8, 2026.

Best Whataburger in Houston: No. 266 Westheimer & Timmons

No. 266 at 3639 Westheimer Rd is my pick for 3 reasons. It never closes. It sits on Houston’s busiest bus corridor. And it has the best backstory of any Whataburger in town. Uptown and the Galleria are a few minutes away, which makes it the obvious stop after an afternoon of shopping.

Address, phone and hours of No. 266

Whataburger No. 266 is at 3639 Westheimer Rd, Houston, TX 77027, phone (713) 627-8602, and its dining room and drive-thru are open 24/7. The official location page lists these services:

  • Dining room: open 24 hours, 7 days a week.
  • Drive-thru: open 24 hours, 7 days a week.
  • Curbside pickup: 6 a.m. to 2 a.m. every day.
  • Delivery: 24 hours.
  • Online ordering: resumes at 6 a.m.; you can schedule an order outside those hours.
  • Holidays: hours vary, so check the location page before you go.

Source: official No. 266 location page, checked October 8, 2026. The page says nothing about accessibility, parking or Wi-Fi, so neither do I.

Getting to Westheimer & Timmons by bus

METRO’s 82 Westheimer bus runs the Westheimer corridor between Downtown and Highway 6, passing Greenway Plaza and Uptown. METRO calls it the busiest bus route in Texas, with more than 12,000 boardings a day. I could not confirm which stop is closest to Timmons Lane; METRO’s trip planner will tell you.

A former Herfy’s with Houston history

No. 266 started out as a Herfy’s, one of 11 Houston burger restaurants Whataburger bought in 1975. According to Houston Historic Retail, the Westheimer building was rebuilt in the early 1990s with design touches that recall the original Herfy’s architecture.

Whataburger Houston menu: what to order

For a first Whataburger in Houston, order the classic Whataburger or the Patty Melt, and after 11 p.m. the Honey Butter Chicken Biscuit. Ask for Spicy Ketchup: staff will not hand it out unless you do.

The classic Whataburger

The Whataburger is a single 100% American beef patty with mustard, lettuce, tomatoes, pickles and diced onions on a toasted 5-inch white bun. Mustard, not mayo, is the default, and that is the Texas signature: it sharpens the beef where mayonnaise would smother it. Patty Melt

The Patty Melt is 2 beef patties, Monterey Jack, grilled onions and creamy pepper sauce on Texas Toast. It is the order I would push on anyone who already knows the classic. The Texas Toast makes it heavier, and the pepper sauce is the part you will not get from a diner patty melt.

Honey Butter Chicken Biscuit and breakfast hours

Whataburger serves breakfast from 11 p.m. to 11 a.m., and the Honey Butter Chicken Biscuit is only available in that window. It is crispy chicken strips with honey butter in a biscuit. At a 24/7 location like No. 266, it is the late-night order.

Spicy Ketchup, Jalapeño Ranch and customizing

Spicy Ketchup and Jalapeño Ranch are Whataburger’s own condiments, and you have to ask for both. The chain says a burger can be built 36,864 ways, so swapping onions for jalapeños or adding a second patty is routine at the counter. Payment, app and rewards

Whataburger has taken Apple Pay in the dining room and drive-thru since late 2017. The app accepts saved debit and credit cards, Apple Pay, PayPal, Paze and Whataburger gift cards, and its rewards program gives 10 points per dollar spent.

Whataburger Houston reviews

The best-documented reviews of a Houston Whataburger are for No. 196 on the West Loop. Customers praise its fast drive-thru and hot food, and complain about cold fries and waits. I found no verifiable Google, Yelp or TripAdvisor rating for No. 266, so I give it no score.

What customers say at No. 196 Loop 610 & 18th

No. 196 at 1718 West Loop N holds 4.2/5 on Google from 1,897 reviews and 3.9/5 across all platforms from 2,494 reviews, as aggregated by Sluurpy on October 8, 2026.

  • Praised: a fast 2-lane drive-thru, food served hot, courteous staff.
  • Criticized: cold fries, Dr Pepper syrup running out, waits at the drive-thru.

The critics’ view

Not everyone in Houston buys the legend. For ‘The Blind Cult of Whataburger’ (PaperCity, February 15, 2016), James Brock ate burgers from 23 locations and came away unimpressed: soft bun, mediocre lettuce and tomatoes, overcooked beef, uneven quality. The one thing he praised was the courteous service.

My verdict

Whataburger is worth it in Houston for 1 thing: a customizable, mustard-forward Texas burger at any hour. I would not cross town for it when the city still has family institutions like Lankford’s, and the uneven quality Brock described in 2016 still turns up in reviews as cold fries and long waits. But at 2 a.m. on Westheimer, with a Patty Melt and a cup of Spicy Ketchup, it is the right call. Whataburger in Houston: a short history

Whataburger reached Houston in 1957, 7 years after the first restaurant opened in Corpus Christi. The chain grew in the city in these steps:

  • August 8, 1950: Harmon Dobson, with Paul Burton, opens the first Whataburger on Ayers Street in Corpus Christi.
  • 1957: the first Houston Whataburger opens on Old Spanish Trail. It is a rectangular building with a V-shaped canopy, not the later A-frame. Its exact address has not been documented.
  • Around 1960: the Houston franchisee’s contract ends.
  • 1961: the striped orange-and-white A-frame appears, with the first prototype in Odessa, Texas.
  • 1963: Houston has 4 Whataburgers.
  • 1975: Whataburger buys Houston’s 11 Herfy’s restaurants, built between 1971 and 1975 and owned by Campbell’s Soup since 1971. They close briefly, then reopen as Whataburgers.
  • 2019: the Dobson family sells a majority stake to BDT Capital Partners of Chicago and keeps a minority share.

FAQ: Whataburger Houston

Is Whataburger open 24 hours in Houston? Some are. No. 266 at 3639 Westheimer Rd keeps its dining room and drive-thru open 24/7. No. 271 on S Shepherd runs a 24/7 drive-thru but closes its dining room at midnight Sunday to Thursday. Hours vary by location and on holidays, so check the locator.

What time does Whataburger serve breakfast? Whataburger serves breakfast from 11 p.m. to 11 a.m., including the Honey Butter Chicken Biscuit.

How much does a Whataburger cost in 2026? A Whataburger cost $7.43 in San Antonio in July 2026, with combos from about $11.89 to $15.98. Houston prices were not verified and vary by location, so check the app.

What should I order at Whataburger for the first time? Order the classic Whataburger or the Patty Melt, and ask for Spicy Ketchup. After 11 p.m., add a Honey Butter Chicken Biscuit.

Whataburger or In-N-Out: which is better? For a Texas-style burger in the middle of the night, Whataburger. Its menu is larger and more customizable, and breakfast runs through the night; ABC13 made the Houston case in ‘7 ways Whataburger wallops In-N-Out Burger’. Critics like PaperCity’s James Brock find Whataburger overrated.

Where was the first Whataburger in Houston? The first Houston Whataburger opened in 1957 on Old Spanish Trail, in a rectangular building with a V-shaped canopy. Its exact address has not been documented.

Is there a Whataburger at Houston’s IAH airport? Yes. No. 1049 is in Terminal B at gate B-21, 3100 N Terminal Rd, phone (281) 233-3347.

Can I pay with Apple Pay at Whataburger? Yes. Apple Pay works in the dining room and drive-thru, and the Whataburger app accepts cards, Apple Pay, PayPal, Paze and gift cards.

Are there vegetarian options at Whataburger? I could not confirm the current vegetarian items on October 8, 2026. Check the Whataburger app before ordering.
