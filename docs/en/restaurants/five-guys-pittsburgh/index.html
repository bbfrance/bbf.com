Five Guys Pittsburgh: All 5 Locations, Menu, Reviews and Prices

Which of the five Pittsburgh restaurants to pick, what to order, what a meal costs and what diners really think, checked on October 9, 2026.

By AlexPublished October 11, 2026

Five Guys has five restaurants with a Pittsburgh, PA address: Market Square (3 PPG Place, downtown), Oakland, Bloomfield, Fox Chapel and North Fayette. Visiting the city? Go to Five Guys Market Square at 3 PPG Place, Pittsburgh, PA 15222 (412-227-0206). It is the most central of the five, and it has outdoor seating. A burger, fries and a drink come to about $21 (US dollars), going by Restaurantji’s estimate. Reviews split hard: diners like the fresh, hot food and the big portions of fries, and complain about the price and the service.

Five Guys downtown Pittsburgh: Market Square at PPG Place

For a visitor, the best Five Guys in Pittsburgh is Market Square, at 3 PPG Place, right inside the glass PPG Place complex downtown. Its official page lists takeout, dine-in, delivery and outdoor seating, for lunch and dinner (Five Guys Market Square). Restaurantji confirms the address and phone number.

PPG Place in downtown Pittsburgh, the glass complex that houses Five Guys Market Square at 3 PPG Place, the best pick for visitors. (Photo: Roy Luck, CC BY 2.0 (via Wikimedia Commons))

Five Guys Market Square and Five Guys PPG Place are one and the same restaurant. If you are already downtown for sightseeing, a game or work, this is the one I would pick. No need to drive out to the suburbs for the same menu.

Parking, transit stops, accessibility and accepted payment methods: I found no official source for any of them. If one matters to you, call 412-227-0206 first.

Five Guys Oakland, near Pitt and CMU

Five Guys Oakland, at 117 S. Bouquet St., is the student location, a short walk for University of Pittsburgh and Carnegie Mellon crowds. It offers takeout, dine-in and delivery (official page).

It also has a documented local history. On October 17, 2005, The Pitt News reported that Five Guys was taking over the vacant Bouquet Street building, a former KFC, Taco Bell and Pizza Hut combo (The Pitt News). Local owner Raji Sankar planned to open “in the first week of November” and to accept Panther Funds, Pitt’s student card.

Five Guys Oakland at 117 S. Bouquet St., near Pitt and Carnegie Mellon, in the former KFC, Taco Bell and Pizza Hut building (photo from March 2008). (Photo: Hepcat748, CC BY-SA 3.0 (Wikimedia Commons))

That is all the record shows. The exact opening date, whether this was the first Five Guys in Pittsburgh and whether Panther Funds still work today all remain unconfirmed. Ask at the counter before you pay with your student card.

Bloomfield, Fox Chapel and North Fayette

The other three Five Guys in Pittsburgh are neighborhood and suburban stops, useful if you live nearby or are already in the car. All three list takeout, dine-in and delivery.

  • Bloomfield (5207 Liberty Ave, Suite 101): you can order on the Five Guys website, in the app, on DoorDash and on Uber Eats (official page).
  • Fox Chapel (1029 Freeport Rd.): adds outdoor seating (official page).
  • North Fayette (324 McHolme Dr.): adds outdoor seating (official page).

Our Pittsburgh burger hub lists Oakland, Bloomfield and Market Square among the neighborhoods worth exploring. Three of the five Five Guys sit in exactly those places.

Five Guys Pittsburgh menu: what to order

Pittsburgh serves the standard US Five Guys menu: burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches, fries and shakes, all built to order. The official pages show no Pittsburgh-only items.

According to the official US menu (fiveguys.com/menu), you can order:

  • Burgers: fresh patties formed by hand, with as many free toppings as you want.
  • Hot dogs: all-beef, split and grilled.
  • Sandwiches: including a BLT.
  • Fries: boardwalk-style, cooked in 100% refined peanut oil, in Five Guys style or Cajun style.
  • Shakes: a vanilla base with mix-ins of your choice.
  • Drinks: Coca-Cola and Dr Pepper.

The Pittsburgh store pages go further (Market Square page). The chain claims “more than 250,000 ways to customize your burger and more than 1,000 milkshake combinations”. It also says the beef is fresh, there are no freezers on site, the fries are cooked twice in 100% peanut oil and the ingredients are prepped by hand every day. Free peanuts are there while you wait: nearly 300,000 a week across its customers, according to Five Guys.

What I would order: a bacon cheeseburger, Cajun fries and a shake. That also happens to be what Market Square diners order most, according to Sluurpy. Reviewers keep mentioning the size of the fries, so one order between two is usually enough. Want to try it at home first? Five Guys and peanut allergies

If you have a peanut allergy, Five Guys is a risky choice. The fries are cooked in 100% peanut oil, and free peanuts sit out in the dining room while you wait (per the official menu and store pages). Talk to the staff before ordering, and read the chain’s US allergen information.

Free peanuts in a Five Guys dining room, a serious risk for anyone with a peanut allergy, along with fries cooked in 100% peanut oil. (Photo: Ser Amantio di Nicolao, CC0 1.0 (Wikimedia Commons))

Five Guys vs Burgatory and Primanti Bros.

Five Guys is a reliable, customizable chain burger, but it is not the Pittsburgh burger experience. Local press rankings of the city’s best burgers, such as Pittsburgh Magazine’s, skip Five Guys and pick local names instead.

My take: Five Guys is the fast, familiar option when you are downtown or near campus and short on time. If you want a burger chain with Pittsburgh roots, Burgatory is the like-for-like comparison. And go to Primanti Bros. at least once, because nothing on the Five Guys menu tells you where you are. Pittsburgh also gave the world the Big Mac, created in 1967 by local McDonald’s franchisee Jim Delligatti, a story told in our history of the hamburger. The ketchup on your tray has local roots too: Henry J. Heinz launched his tomato ketchup here in 1876.

Five Guys: a quick history

Five Guys was founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell as a small takeout counter (Five Guys blog). Forty years later, on September 25, 2026, it opened its 2,000th restaurant at Universal CityWalk in Orlando (QSR Magazine). The chain now operates in 30 countries.

FAQ: Five Guys Pittsburgh

How many Five Guys are there in Pittsburgh?

There are five Five Guys restaurants with a Pittsburgh, PA address: Market Square (3 PPG Place), Oakland (117 S. Bouquet St.), Bloomfield (5207 Liberty Ave), Fox Chapel (1029 Freeport Rd.) and North Fayette (324 McHolme Dr.).

Which Five Guys is best for visiting downtown Pittsburgh?

Market Square, at 3 PPG Place, Pittsburgh, PA 15222. It is the downtown location and offers dine-in, takeout, delivery and outdoor seating.

What are Five Guys Pittsburgh’s hours?

They are not published in a form I could verify. Aggregators list Market Square at 10 a.m. or 11 a.m. to 9 p.m., and Oakland at 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. Check the official store page or call the restaurant before you go.

How much is a meal at Five Guys in Pittsburgh?

About $21 for a burger, fries and a drink, according to Restaurantji’s estimate for Market Square. Check the Five Guys app for current prices at your location.

Does Five Guys deliver in Pittsburgh?

Yes. All five Pittsburgh locations list delivery and curbside pickup. You can order on the Five Guys website or app, and Bloomfield also lists DoorDash and Uber Eats.

Does Five Guys have vegetarian options?

The official US menu lists burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches (including a BLT), fries and shakes. I could not confirm the names of meat-free sandwiches on the official page, so ask in store. Fries and shakes contain no meat, but the fries are cooked in peanut oil.

Can I eat at Five Guys with a peanut allergy?

It is risky. Fries are cooked in 100% peanut oil and free peanuts are served in the dining room. Speak to staff and read Five Guys’ US allergen information before ordering.

How big is an order of fries at Five Guys?

Big. Reviewers at Market Square regularly mention generous portions of fries, so one order can easily be shared between two.

Does Five Guys Oakland accept Panther Funds?

It planned to when the restaurant was announced in 2005, according to The Pitt News. Whether it still does is unconfirmed, so ask before paying.

Is Five Guys better than Burgatory or Primanti Bros.?

They do different things. Five Guys is a customizable national chain. Burgatory is a Pittsburgh burger chain, and Primanti Bros. serves the city’s signature sandwich with fries inside. For a local experience, try Primanti Bros. at least once.
