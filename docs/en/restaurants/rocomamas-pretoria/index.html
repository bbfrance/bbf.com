RocoMamas Pretoria: Best Branch, Hours and Prices

All 10 RocoMamas branches in Pretoria and Centurion compared: addresses, hours, halaal, drive-thru and smashburgers from R69 (checked Nov 2026).

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished November 26, 2026

RocoMamas Pretoria has 10 branches across Pretoria and Centurion on the chain’s official Gauteng list. If you only try one, make it RocoMamas Menlyn, at Shop LF99 & 100, Menlyn Park, Atterbury Road. It opens 10:00–21:00 Monday to Thursday, 10:00–22:00 on Friday, 9:00–22:00 on Saturday and 9:00–21:00 on Sunday. Smashburgers cost R69 to R165 (South African rand) on the national menu. Menlyn also has the biggest review base I found in the city: 4.8/5 from 5,939 Google reviews, as shown on Wanderlog. I checked every address, phone number, hour and price below against the official RocoMamas pages on 6 November 2026.

RocoMamas Menlyn: address, hours and reviews

RocoMamas Menlyn is inside Menlyn Park at Shop LF99 & 100, Atterbury Road, Menlyn, Pretoria, 0181. Phone +27 10 447 9788 (010 447 9788 locally). It gets my vote for three reasons: a central spot in the east of the city, weekend hours as long as any mall branch, and the largest pool of reviews of the branches I checked.

RocoMamas Menlyn opening hours

RocoMamas Menlyn opens Monday to Thursday 10:00–21:00, Friday 10:00–22:00, Saturday 9:00–22:00 and Sunday 9:00–21:00, according to its official page (checked 6 November 2026).

RocoMamas Menlyn reviews

RocoMamas Menlyn scores 4.8/5 on Google from 5,939 reviews and 4.0/5 on Tripadvisor from 24, according to Wanderlog on 6 November 2026. For a chain restaurant, that Google average is high. It is high enough that I’d read it with some caution, and I took it from Wanderlog rather than from Google Maps itself. Reviewers keep coming back to the burgers and wings, the milkshakes, the sharing platters, the outdoor seating and friendly staff. The complaint that recurs is that some burgers lack flavour. At a smashburger place, where the seared crust is the whole point, I take that one seriously.

Google review counts for the three most-reviewed RocoMamas in Pretoria: Menlyn 5,939 (4.8/5), Brooklyn Mall 2,566 (4.5/5) and Loftus 1,751 (4.5/5), as shown on Wanderlog on 6 November 2026. (Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0, data from Wanderlog (Google aggregates), checked 6 November 2026)

The alternatives: Brooklyn Mall, Loftus and Queenswood Drive Thru

Three other branches each beat Menlyn on one count: Brooklyn Mall on online ordering, Loftus on public transport, Queenswood on the drive-thru and the early start.

RocoMamas Brooklyn Mall

RocoMamas Brooklyn Mall is at Shop 28A, Brooklyn Mall, Veale Street, Nieuw Muckleneuk, Pretoria, 0181, phone 010 447 9789. Its hours are the same as Menlyn’s. The official page takes online orders, and so does the RocoMamas app. On Wanderlog it shows 4.5/5 on Google from 2,566 reviews and 4.0/5 on Tripadvisor from 42. Reviewers mention friendly staff, speed and clean tables. Ignore the aggregator that lists it at “Shop 4 Brooklyn Piazza”: the official address is Shop 28A, Brooklyn Mall.

RocoMamas Loftus: the branch you can reach without a car

RocoMamas Loftus is at Shop A02, Loftus Park, Kirkness Street, Arcadia, Pretoria, 0007, phone 066 477 1003. Loftus Park sits next to Loftus Versfeld Stadium and officially opened on 3 May 2018. A Gautrain bus stop beside the precinct links it to Hatfield station, according to Property24. The official hours are Monday to Thursday 10:00–21:00, Friday 9:00–22:00, and Saturday and Sunday 9:00–21:00. Wanderlog lists different hours, so phone before a match day or a late visit. On Wanderlog, Google reviews average 4.5/5 (1,751 reviews) and Tripadvisor 4.0/5 (16). The reviews are split. Some praise the wings, the outdoor area and the family feel, while others report slow service, wrong orders and dry food. Loftus is the branch I’d choose for convenience, not for the burger.

RocoMamas Queenswood Drive Thru

RocoMamas Queenswood Drive Thru, at 210 Stead Avenue, Queenswood, Pretoria, 0186, is the only Pretoria branch that opens at 8:00 every day of the week. Phone 063 689 6904. It opens Monday to Saturday 8:00–22:00 and Sunday 8:00–21:00, according to its official page. On 4 July 2022, Spur Corporation announced the first RocoMamas drive-thru at Little Falls in Johannesburg and said a second was “planned in Queenswood in Pretoria later this year” (Bizcommunity reported it the same day). I could not confirm when Queenswood actually opened, or whether the drive-thru serves its own menu.

RocoMamas halaal in Pretoria and Centurion

RocoMamas Eldo Square in Centurion is the only branch in the Pretoria area marked “Halaal” on the official Gauteng list (checked 6 November 2026). None of the other nine carries the label. I could not verify Eldo Square’s hours, whether it has a separate menu, or its prices, so phone the brand line on 0860 888 772 before you make the trip.

RocoMamas Pretoria menu and prices (November 2026)

A RocoMamas smashburger costs R69 to R165 on the national South African menu checked on 6 November 2026. The NuSkool at R69 is the entry point, the Old Skool at R85 is the classic, and the Slacker at R165 tops the range. The brand does not say whether Pretoria franchisees charge exactly the national prices, so treat these as a guide.

Limited editions

Three limited-edition burgers were on the menu on 6 November 2026. The King Pin is R119, a double 100 g smash with gouda on brioche. The Head Honcho is R82 (fried chicken, cheddar and mushroom sauce), and The Top Don is R69 (fried chicken and coleslaw). The Slawdaddy has gone.

Sides, wings, ribs and shakes

Shoestring fries start at R37 (R56 large). Crinkle fries are R45, and Cheesy or Chilli Cheese Fries R57. Mac’n’Cheese costs R49. Nachos Classico are R135, or R79 for a half portion, and bombs are R45 for three or R84 for six. Wings run R59 for 4, R115 for 8 and R160 for 12. Pork ribs are R130 for 250 g, R227 for 500 g and R320 for 750 g. A G-Shake costs R55, waffles R52 to R65, the Chicken Caesar Salad R120, the Chopped Green Salad R40 and the Spicy Slaw R45.

Kids’ menu

The Lil’ Rock menu is for children under 12 and comes with fries: the Roadster at R75, a Cheese Burger at R82, Chicken Strips at R59 and 4 wings at R72.

Prices went up in late October 2026

RocoMamas raised several prices between 14 October and 6 November 2026. The Old Skool went from R79 to R85, a jump of about 8%. The Slacker rose from R159 to R165 and the G-Shake from R50 to R55. The R69 NuSkool arrived at the same time, and I read it as the chain keeping a burger under R70 while the classic crept up. If you see the R65 Old Skool still quoted on briefly.co.za or fastfoodsmenu.com, that is a 2024 price.

Vegetarian options at RocoMamas

The national RocoMamas menu had no vegetarian or plant-based burger on 6 November 2026. The only meat-free items were the Chopped Green Salad (R40) and the Spicy Slaw (R45), which is thin for a chain of more than 100 restaurants. Some Brooklyn Mall reviews mention a “Veg OldSkool” with a vegetable patty, but it is not on the current menu, so ask the branch before you count on it.

RocoMamas myths to ignore

Several aggregator sites repeat claims that the official sources contradict. RocoMamas was not “established in 2010”. The first restaurant opened in July 2013 on Malibongwe Drive in Randburg, founded by Brian Altriche and Paul Dempsey. The menu has no “Big Daddy Burger” and no “Big Ribs”, and it lists no vegan or gluten-free options.

A short history of RocoMamas

RocoMamas is a South African smashburger chain that opened its first restaurant in Randburg in July 2013. Spur Corporation bought 51% of it in January 2015 (News24). By 30 June 2026 it had 115 restaurants, 92 of them in South Africa, according to Spur Corporation’s 2026 annual financial statements. The full timeline is on our RocoMamas brand profile.

How I put this guide together

I did not visit these branches for this guide, so it gives no rating and no tasting notes. My pick of Menlyn rests on location, opening hours and review volume, not on a meal. Addresses, phone numbers and hours come from each branch’s official page on rocomamas.com, and prices from the national menu, all checked on 6 November 2026. Review scores come from Wanderlog, not directly from Google or Tripadvisor, and are quoted with their review counts. I left out anything I could not confirm on an official or named source.

RocoMamas Pretoria FAQ

How many RocoMamas are there in Pretoria, and which one should I choose? The official Gauteng list names 10 branches in Pretoria and Centurion (checked 6 November 2026). Menlyn, in Menlyn Park, is the best all-round choice and has the largest Google review count I found (5,939, via Wanderlog).

What are RocoMamas Menlyn’s opening hours? Monday to Thursday 10:00–21:00, Friday 10:00–22:00, Saturday 9:00–22:00 and Sunday 9:00–21:00, according to the official page.

Where is RocoMamas Menlyn in Menlyn Park? Shop LF99 & 100, Menlyn Park, Atterbury Road, Menlyn, Pretoria, 0181. Phone +27 10 447 9788.

How much does a RocoMamas burger cost in 2026? Smashburgers cost R69 (NuSkool) to R165 (Slacker) on the national menu checked on 6 November 2026. The Old Skool costs R85.

Is there a halaal RocoMamas in Pretoria? Yes, one: RocoMamas Eldo Square – Halaal, at Shop 6-8, Eldo Square Shopping Centre, Volga Street, Eldo Glen, Centurion. It is the only branch in the area labelled halaal.

Is there a RocoMamas drive-thru in Pretoria? Yes. RocoMamas Queenswood Drive Thru is at 210 Stead Avenue, Queenswood, and opens Monday to Saturday 8:00–22:00 and Sunday 8:00–21:00.

Which RocoMamas opens earliest in Pretoria? Queenswood Drive Thru opens at 8:00 every day. Castle Gate also opens at 8:00 from Monday to Saturday, but at 9:00 on Sunday.

Can I get to RocoMamas in Pretoria without a car? Yes. Go to RocoMamas Loftus at Loftus Park, Arcadia. A Gautrain bus stop beside the precinct connects it to Hatfield station.

Does RocoMamas deliver in Pretoria? The Garsfontein branch lists delivery and contactless delivery, and the Brooklyn Mall page takes online orders through the RocoMamas app. I could not confirm delivery for the other branches.

Is there a vegetarian burger at RocoMamas? Not on the national menu checked on 6 November 2026. The meat-free items are the Chopped Green Salad and the Spicy Slaw.

Can I book a table at RocoMamas? No Pretoria branch page confirms table bookings. Call the branch directly or the brand line on 0860 888 772 (howzit@rocomamas.co.za).

Does RocoMamas have a kids’ menu? Yes. The Lil’ Rock menu is for under-12s and comes with fries: the Roadster at R75, a Cheese Burger at R82, Chicken Strips at R59 and 4 wings at R72.
