Burger Van Bucharest: Menu, Prices and Address

Where to find the smash burger brand that began as Bucharest's first food truck in 2014, what to order, what it costs in lei, and what diners say about it.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 20, 2026

The easiest place to eat Burger Van in Bucharest is Burger Van Bistro, Str. George Vraca 4, a small burger bar on a quiet street between Cișmigiu Park and Calea Victoriei, open every day from 12:00 to 22:00. The Smash Burger Menu (a smash burger with fries and coleslaw) costs 48 lei in the online shop. That is the same price as any signature burger on its own, which makes it the best-value order on the menu. On Google the bistro holds 4.3/5 from about 1,820 reviews: diners praise the fresh burgers and the fries, and grumble about prices and a cramped room. I checked every fact below on 3 November 2026.

Burger Van Bucharest at a glance

Burger Van is a homegrown Bucharest street-food brand built on smash burgers. It started as a food truck in 2014. Today it runs several outlets across the city, and the George Vraca bistro is the flagship.

Burger Van Bistro address: George Vraca 4

Burger Van Bistro is at Str. George Vraca 4, in central Bucharest. It is the only Burger Van outlet whose full street address the brand publishes and every listing confirms.

Map of Burger Van Bistro, Str. George Vraca 4, 010146 Bucharest (marker), between Cișmigiu Park to the west and Calea Victoriei to the east, in Sector 1. Open daily 12:00–22:00. (Map: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)

The bistro sits on a calm side street between Cișmigiu Park and Calea Victoriei, the city’s grand old avenue. A walk round the park lake, lunch here, then the museums on Victoriei: it fits neatly into a day in the centre.

Some older listings give a second address at Str. Speranței 6. I found nothing recent to confirm it still trades, so it is not in this guide.

Burger Van opening hours

Burger Van Bistro is open every day of the week from 12:00 to 22:00, according to the brand’s official location page, checked on 3 November 2026.

Third-party listings disagree. In Your Pocket still shows 12:30–22:30 from Tuesday to Sunday and 12:00–21:00 on Monday, which looks out of date; Wanderlog matches the official hours. Planning a late dinner? Call the bistro or check the Burger Van Facebook page first.

Vegetarian and vegan burgers at Burger Van

Burger Van has 1 vegetarian burger and 1 vegan burger. The Veggie Burger costs 44 lei and the Beyond Burger costs 50 lei in the online shop.

  • Veggie Burger (280 g), 44 lei: halloumi, aubergine, courgette and pesto. Vegetarian, not vegan, because of the halloumi.
  • Beyond Burger (280 g), 50 lei (was 65 lei): a plant-based patty with vegan cheese and vegan sauce. This is the one to order if you came looking for a vegan burger in Bucharest.

Spinach Guide, a vegan-focused directory, also lists the bistro.

What to order at Burger Van

On a first visit, order the Smash Burger Menu at 48 lei. It is the burger the brand was built on, and the fries and coleslaw cost nothing extra compared with a signature burger alone. No other line on the menu is better value.

Burger Van says its beef is never frozen and its patties are smashed on the griddle in front of the customer for a browned Maillard crust (Burger Van, About us). Zile și Nopți reported the same never-frozen meat and fresh buns in 2019. Prefer a thicker patty? The Honest Burger uses Black Angus beef, and at 44 lei it is the cheapest burger on the menu. For something no big chain sells, the French Connection pairs camembert with caramelised bacon and Dijon. On the side, reviewers keep praising the sweet potato fries, but I did not find them in the online shop on 3 November 2026. The aioli and parsley fries (22 lei) are listed.

Burger Van Bucharest reviews: what diners say

Diners rate Burger Van Bistro 4.3/5 on Google and 4.0/5 on Tripadvisor. They like the fresh ingredients and friendly staff, but criticise the prices, the small dining room and uneven portions.

I took these ratings from the aggregators Wanderlog and Spinach Guide on 3 November 2026, not directly from Google or Tripadvisor. 1 search snippet showed 4.1 on Tripadvisor, so expect small differences when you check.

What diners like

  • Burgers made with fresh ingredients.
  • Sweet potato fries and the aioli sauce.
  • Friendly staff.
  • A warm, relaxed atmosphere.

Of roughly 1,820 Google reviews, 1,176 give 5 stars (Wanderlog).

How Burger Van Bistro’s roughly 1,820 Google reviews break down: 1,176 5-star, 141 one-star and about 503 in between. Average 4.3/5, versus 4.0/5 from 179 reviews on Tripadvisor (via Wanderlog, 3 November 2026). (Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0, data from Wanderlog (https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1541528/burger-van-bistro))

What diners criticise

  • Prices that feel a little high.
  • Only about 3 tables inside.
  • Self-service: you order and collect at the counter.
  • Burger quality and portion sizes that vary from visit to visit.

About 141 Google reviews give 1 star. The harshest critique I found is old: in January 2016, the blog No Garlic No Onions published “Burger Van Bucharest: this is not a burger you want to try”. The page returned a 404 error when I checked it in November 2026. Read it as a snapshot of 2016, not of today.

The atmosphere at George Vraca

In a 2022 post, Eva Poteaca of Live the World described a colourful room, a large blackboard where customers write their reviews, and free water flavoured with cucumber or fruit. She also mentioned a second room across the street. I could not confirm that it is still in use.

How I researched this Burger Van guide

I have not given Burger Van a Burger Breton rating: this guide rests on sources, not on a visit, and we only rate places we have eaten at. The address, hours, phone number and prices come from Burger Van’s own website. I checked them against In Your Pocket, Spinach Guide, Wanderlog and bucharest.ro, and I give every third-party rating with its source and date. Where the sources disagree, I say so.

Burger Van delivery in Bucharest

Burger Van delivers anywhere in Bucharest through its own website, Glovo and Tazz. It does not deliver to Ilfov County, the area around the city, and refunds orders placed there.

Prices on Glovo and Tazz may differ from the online shop’s.

Burger Van history: from food truck to bistro

Burger Van launched on 4 July 2014 as a food truck, billed as “the first real food truck in Bucharest”. It opened its George Vraca bistro in 2015.

Vice Romania announced the launch 2 days earlier: an evening event at 19:00 on the terrace of Club Control, with 5 burgers on the menu and the first 50 burgers given away free. The concept took its cue from the street-food scenes of London, Paris and Berlin (Vice Romania, July 2014).

Burger Van’s own timeline adds a second van in 2015, the George Vraca bistro the same year, and Burger Van Home by 2017. Then it stops. I could not confirm whether a Burger Van truck still drives around the city today.

Burger Van Bucharest FAQ

Where is Burger Van in Bucharest? The flagship Burger Van Bistro is at Str. George Vraca 4, 010146 București, near Cișmigiu Park and Calea Victoriei. It is the best choice if you are visiting the city centre.

What are Burger Van Bistro’s opening hours? The bistro is open every day from 12:00 to 22:00, according to Burger Van’s official website on 3 November 2026.

How much does a burger cost at Burger Van? In the online shop, burgers cost 44 to 60 lei, and a Smash Burger meal with fries and coleslaw costs 48 lei (prices on 3 November 2026). Prices in the bistro and on delivery apps may differ.

What is the most popular burger at Burger Van? Burger Van does not publish a best-seller. The brand is built on the smash burger, and the Smash Burger Menu at 48 lei is the dish to start with.

Does Burger Van have vegetarian or vegan burgers? Yes. The Veggie Burger with halloumi, aubergine, courgette and pesto is vegetarian at 44 lei. The Beyond Burger with vegan cheese and vegan sauce is fully vegan at 50 lei.

Can I get Burger Van delivered in Bucharest? Yes. Burger Van delivers anywhere in Bucharest through its website, Glovo and Tazz, but not to Ilfov County.

Was Burger Van really Bucharest’s first food truck, and does the van still run? Burger Van was billed as “the first real food truck in Bucharest” when it launched on 4 July 2014. The brand’s timeline stops in 2017, so I could not confirm whether the van still runs.

Do I need to book at Burger Van Bistro? The brand does not mention reservations. The bistro is self-service with about 3 tables inside and a terrace, so expect to queue or eat outside at busy times.

Can I pay by card at Burger Van? Yes. Burger Van Bistro accepts bank cards and mobile payment, according to Spinach Guide and In Your Pocket.
