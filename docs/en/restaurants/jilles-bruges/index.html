Jilles Bruges: Menu, Prices, Beer and Founder

Jilles Bruges: burgers from €17.50 to €21.50, each paired with a Belgian beer, by the Fish Market. Hours, booking, founder and menu checked in November 2026.

By Sofia, Restaurants writer, Italy, Switzerland and the BeneluxPublished November 19, 2026

Jilles Bruges is Jilles Beer & Burgers at Braambergstraat 10, a short walk from the Vismarkt (Fish Market). It belongs to a Flemish group founded by Jilles D’Hulster, the “red-haired Jilles” of the brand’s own site, who opened the first Jilles in Ghent with his late father, Jack. Burgers cost €17.50 to €21.50, each with a suggested Belgian beer, and you can book online. I checked the menu, prices and hours against the official site on 12 November 2026; every price and opening time below dates from that check.

Jilles Bruges at a glance: address, hours and prices

Jilles Bruges serves dinner every evening and lunch on Wednesday and from Friday to Sunday. A burger costs €17.50 to €21.50 (official prices, checked 12 November 2026).

Plan for an early dinner. Lunch lasts 2 hours, and evening service stops at 21:00 except on Friday and Saturday, when it runs to 21:30. If you want a canal walk after dark and a burger afterwards, it won’t work here: book for around 19:00 instead.

Who is behind Jilles? The founder, Jilles D’Hulster

Jilles D’Hulster is the founder and owner of Jilles Beer & Burgers. According to a 2017 article by the Belgian property firm Dewaele, he opened the first Jilles in Ghent with his father, Jack D’Hulster, who later died. By 2017 the group ran restaurants in Ghent, Bruges and Ostend.

The brand doesn’t hide its founder. The official site calls him “the red-haired Jilles, the man behind the burger restaurants”, and the house beer is named after him: Rostekop is Flemish slang for “redhead”.

Dates are fuzzier. The Bruges page says “Established in 2010”, so by its own account the brand dates from about 2010. The Bruges company, Jilles Brugge SRL (BE 0629.956.404), was registered on 12 May 2015 and is still active, with accounts last filed on 4 September 2026 (Companyweb). I found no recent interview with him, so I can’t tell you how involved he is day to day in Bruges.

Jilles and Jack: 2 burger concepts, 1 family

The difference is the format. Jilles is a sit-down restaurant serving burgers on plates with paired beers. Jack, named after the founder’s late father, serves burgers in bags. The first Jack opened at Grote Markt 21 in Kortrijk in May 2016.

In 2017 Jilles D’Hulster told Dewaele: “Within 3 years I want Jack in every big Belgian city.” I couldn’t confirm whether that happened, or whether Jack is still trading. Read it as a 2017 ambition, not a result.

Where else to find Jilles in Belgium

The official site lists 5 Jilles restaurants: Bruges (Braambergstraat 10), Ghent (Tentoonstellingslaan 165), Ostend (Albert I-Promenade 60), Kortrijk (Grote Markt) and Brussels. No Brussels address was published when I checked.

For a first visit, my pick is the Hugo Classic. It is the cheapest burger on the list and the plainest test of the beef and the bun. Add the gluten-free Jilles Fries and a 33 cl Rostekop and you pay €27.50 per person at November 2026 prices. 2 people who share a 75 cl bottle pay €9.50 for the beer instead of €11. That is a lot for a burger, fries and 1 beer. What you are really paying for is the beer list, and that is the point of the place.

Where the ingredients come from

Jilles says it uses “everything from Belgian soil… from the buns to the sauces and the burgers”, working with local entrepreneurs and farmers. That is the restaurant’s own claim. Its official pages name no breed, butcher or bakery, so I can’t verify it further.

Beer and burger pairing in Bruges: how Jilles does it

At Jilles, every burger has a matching Belgian beer chosen by beer sommelier Sofie Vanrafelghem. The group even published a Beer & Burgers book with 48 combinations (Jilles stories, Visit Gent).

That is the real reason to come here rather than to any other burger bar in town. Bruges is a beer city, and Jilles treats the beer as half the meal, not an afterthought. I couldn’t confirm whether Vanrafelghem still works on the pairings in 2026, so ask your server which beer the menu suggests for your burger.

If you’re unsure, order the Rostekop. An amber is maltier than a blonde and lighter than a dark ale, so it won’t overpower a plain beef burger.

The room: 1920s style near the Fish Market

Jilles Bruges has a 1920s-style interior of solid wood, cold-rolled steel and handcrafted, recycled materials. In summer there is a terrace, and groups are welcome.

Location is the other draw. Braambergstraat is a quiet street by the Vismarkt, a few minutes from the Burg and the canals, so it fits easily into a day on foot in the old town.

Jilles vs. Simon Stevinplein: choosing a burger in Bruges

Jilles is the choice for a sit-down burger with beer pairings near the Fish Market. The other well-known cluster is Simon Stevinplein, home of Manhattn’s Burgers Bruges and Ellis.

Jilles, Ellis, Bohemian Burgers, Manhattn’s, Mosh and Paul’s Boutique are the names that keep coming up on “best burger in Bruges” lists. For context: no Bruges restaurant made the Belgian top 10 of the Best Burger Benelux 2024 contest, won by Bar Jérôme in Leuven, Burger Boutique in Antwerp and Kitsune in Brussels (Paris Match Belgique).

A correction: Filip Claeys and De Jonkman don’t serve burgers

Filip Claeys, one of Bruges’s best-known chefs, runs De Jonkman, a 2-Michelin-star seafood restaurant. He does not cook burgers. Some lists pair his name with Bruges burgers, but the Michelin Guide, his restaurant’s own site and the press I checked show no burger on any menu.

De Jonkman sits in a former hunting lodge of Male Castle, at Maalsesteenweg 438 in Sint-Kruis. Claeys is a fisherman’s grandson who trained under Sergio Herman at Oud Sluis (see our piece on Sergio Herman and Le Pristine in Antwerp). In 2011 he founded NorthSeaChefs to promote lesser-known and bycatch fish. His motto: “We must learn to eat what the fisherman catches” (Michelin Guide, De Jonkman, The50).

Book De Jonkman for North Sea crab, not a cheeseburger. It opens Wednesday to Saturday, for lunch and dinner.

FAQ: Jilles Bruges

Who owns Jilles Beer & Burgers in Bruges?

Jilles D’Hulster founded and owns Jilles. He opened the first restaurant in Ghent with his late father, Jack. The Bruges branch is run by the company Jilles Brugge SRL, registered in 2015.

Where is Jilles in Bruges and when is it open?

It is at Braambergstraat 10, 8000 Brugge, near the Vismarkt. Dinner starts at 18:00 every day and ends at 21:30 on Friday and Saturday, 21:00 on other days. Lunch, 12:00–14:00, is served on Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday.

How much does a burger cost at Jilles Bruges?

On 12 November 2026 the burgers cost €17.50 (Hugo Classic) to €21.50 (Eddy Carnibaal, a double). Fries are €4.50, or €5.50 for sweet potato fries. Takeaway is 10% cheaper.

Can you book a table at Jilles Bruges?

Yes. Book online at jilles.be/en/book-now/bruges or call +32 50 70 55 55. Groups are welcome.

Does Jilles Bruges have vegan or gluten-free options?

The Jilles Fries are gluten-free. The official menu I checked did not confirm a vegan or gluten-free burger, so ask when you book.

What is Rostekop?

Rostekop is the Jilles house beer, an amber named after the red-haired founder. It costs €5.50 for 33 cl or €9.50 for 75 cl.

What is the difference between Jilles and Jack?

Jilles is the sit-down beer-and-burger restaurant. Jack, named after the founder’s late father, serves burgers in bags; it opened in Kortrijk in May 2016. Jack’s current status is unconfirmed.

Does De Jonkman in Bruges serve burgers?

No. De Jonkman is Filip Claeys’s 2-Michelin-star seafood restaurant in Sint-Kruis. Its menu has no burgers.

What is the best burger in Bruges?

No Bruges restaurant made the Belgian top 10 of Best Burger Benelux 2024. Jilles stands out for its beer pairings, and Manhattn’s and Ellis on Simon Stevinplein are the central picks. Compare them in our Bruges burger guide.
