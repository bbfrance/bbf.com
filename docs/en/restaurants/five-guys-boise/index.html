Five Guys Boise: Locations, Hours, Menu, Reviews

Boise has two Five Guys: BoDo downtown and Fairview in west Boise. Addresses, hours, menu prices, Google ratings and the Idaho potato connection.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 27, 2026

Boise has two Five Guys. Five Guys BoDo is downtown at 321 South 8th Street (208-429-8776), and Five Guys Fairview is in west Boise at 1587 N. Milwaukee St. (208-658-4930). Third-party listings give BoDo’s hours as 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day. A Hamburger is listed at about $10.57 and a Cheeseburger at $11.72. Both branches score above 4 out of 5 on Google. I checked every fact here on November 24, 2026, and I flag anything Five Guys itself doesn’t confirm.

How many Five Guys are in Boise?

There are two Five Guys restaurants inside Boise city limits, BoDo and Fairview, according to the chain’s official Boise city page. I found no closure notices for either. Both do dine-in, takeout, delivery and curbside pickup.

Five Guys downtown Boise (BoDo): address, hours and patio

Five Guys BoDo is at 321 South 8th Street, in the BoDo district of downtown Boise. If you’re already downtown, go to this one. The Downtown Boise Partnership places it near the Warehouse Food Hall and mentions an outdoor patio.

The phone number, 208-429-8776, appears on both the Five Guys page and the Partnership’s listing. The Partnership and Birdeye give the hours as 11 a.m. to 10 p.m., seven days a week. Five Guys’ own store page didn’t show daily hours when I looked, which is odd for a chain this size. Call or check Google Maps before a late visit.

Order ahead at order.fiveguys.com/location/bodo/menu. The official store page lists delivery through the Five Guys app, DoorDash and Uber Eats.

Five Guys Milwaukee St, Boise (Fairview): address and hours

Five Guys Fairview is at 1587 N. Milwaukee St., Boise, ID 83704, in west Boise. The chain calls this branch “Fairview.” Its phone number, 208-658-4930, is on the official page and on Wanderlog.

Nobody agrees on when Fairview opens. Wanderlog says 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily, and Birdeye says 11 a.m. The official page lists no daily hours at all. If you’re aiming for an early lunch, call first.

Online orders go through order.fiveguys.com/location/boise-fairview/menu. Dine-in, takeout, delivery and curbside pickup are all offered.

Five Guys Boise menu and prices

Boise gets the standard Five Guys menu: burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches, fresh-cut fries and milkshakes. The chain says it uses fresh ground beef and keeps no freezers in its restaurants. Because every topping is free, it claims a burger can be built “more than 250,000 ways” and a shake more than 1,000 ways.

Reviewers say again and again that one regular order feeds two.

Are Five Guys fries made with Idaho potatoes?

Mostly, yes. Co-founder Jerry Murrell told Food Republic and The Takeout that Five Guys fries are made from Idaho Burbank potatoes, except for about two months a year when the chain switches to Norkotahs from Washington State. He said the potatoes come mainly from Idaho, grown north of the 42nd parallel. Five Guys’ own website only says “up north” and never names Idaho.

“The Way They Dig Potatoes in Idaho,” a 1907 tall-tale postcard by M.L. Oakes showing giant potatoes hauled by horses. Idaho’s potato pride goes back more than a century, and Five Guys still sources most of its fries from the state. 1024×647, loading=lazy, decoding=async (Photo: M.L. So in Boise, the fries in your bag were quite possibly grown in the same state. Five Guys cuts them in the restaurant and fries them twice in 100% refined peanut oil, Five Guys Style or Cajun. The chain lists 530 to 1,100 calories depending on the size. To me, the fries are the best reason to choose Five Guys in Idaho, more than the burger.

Five Guys Boise reviews: what customers say

Google reviewers like both Boise branches. Birdeye shows Fairview at 4.3/5 from 1,673 reviews (1,658 of them on Google) and BoDo at 4.2/5 from 836 reviews (829 on Google). TripAdvisor is less kind: Wanderlog lists Fairview at 3.5/5 there, but from only 19 reviews.

Five Guys Boise reviews: Fairview has 1,673 reviews averaging 4.3/5, about twice as many as BoDo, which has 836 reviews averaging 4.2/5. Figures from Birdeye, nearly all of them Google reviews, checked November 24, 2026. Bar chart, 800×400, loading=lazy (Chart: Burger Breton, data from Birdeye (https://reviews.birdeye.com/five-guys-173505037210810, https://reviews.birdeye.com/five-guys-173505024695373), seen 2026-11-24)

These are the things reviewers praise at both branches:

  • Fresh beef that has never been frozen
  • Free toppings, so you build the burger you want
  • Huge fry portions, with the Cajun fries singled out
  • Milkshakes worth the extra spend
  • Friendly staff

These complaints keep coming up:

  • High prices for fast food
  • Loud music in the dining room
  • Burgers so loaded that toppings fall out
  • Waits at peak hours
  • Uneven cleanliness from one visit to the next
  • A cold dining room

My read: Five Guys in Boise delivers what it promises, a messy, fully loaded burger and too many fries. The complaints are about price and atmosphere, not about the food. If value is what you care about, this isn’t your spot. If you do go, skip the large fries. The regular already covers two people.

BoDo or Fairview: which Five Guys in Boise should you pick?

Pick BoDo if you’re downtown and want to sit outside. Pick Fairview if you’re in west Boise. The menu is identical, so location decides.

If you’re torn, I’d lean toward Fairview. It has twice as many Google reviews as BoDo and a slightly higher average, 4.3 against 4.2, which tells me the score holds up under heavier traffic. BoDo makes more sense as part of a downtown evening, with the Warehouse Food Hall a short walk away. Both branches offer curbside pickup.

I couldn’t confirm parking, wheelchair access, payment methods or Valley Regional Transit bus routes for either branch, so I’ve left them out rather than guess.

Five Guys Boise FAQ

How many Five Guys are there in Boise?

There are two: Five Guys BoDo at 321 South 8th Street downtown, and Five Guys Fairview at 1587 N. Milwaukee St. in west Boise, according to the chain’s official Boise page.

What are Five Guys Boise’s hours? Is it open on Sunday?

The Downtown Boise Partnership and Birdeye list BoDo as open 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day, Sunday included. For Fairview, sources disagree on the opening time (10 a.m. or 11 a.m.) but agree on a 10 p.m. close. Five Guys’ official pages didn’t show daily hours when I checked, so confirm on Google Maps before you go.

How much is a burger at Five Guys in Boise?

Allmenus lists a Hamburger at $10.57, a Cheeseburger at $11.72 and a Bacon Cheeseburger at $12.64 for the BoDo location, as seen on November 24, 2026. These prices are undated and may include a delivery markup. Check the live prices on order.fiveguys.com.

Does Five Guys use Idaho potatoes for its fries?

Mostly. Co-founder Jerry Murrell told Food Republic and The Takeout that the chain uses Idaho Burbank potatoes, switching to Washington Norkotahs for about two months a year.

Can I order Five Guys online or get delivery in Boise?

Yes. Both branches take online orders on order.fiveguys.com, and the official city page lists curbside pickup at both. Delivery runs through the Five Guys app, DoorDash and Uber Eats.

Is Five Guys safe for people with a peanut allergy?

Five Guys cooks its fries in 100% refined peanut oil, according to its official menu. I couldn’t confirm the full allergen details for the Boise branches, so anyone with a peanut allergy should talk to the staff before ordering.

Does Five Guys Boise have vegetarian options?

Allmenus lists a Grilled Cheese at $6.89 at BoDo. I couldn’t confirm the full vegetarian and gluten-free lineup on the official Boise menu, so check order.fiveguys.com for the current list.

Is there a Five Guys in Meridian or Nampa?

Secondary listings show Five Guys in Meridian and Nampa, outside Boise city limits. I couldn’t confirm those addresses on the official Five Guys locator, so check restaurants.fiveguys.com before you drive out.
