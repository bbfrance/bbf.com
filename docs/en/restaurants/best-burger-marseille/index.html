Best Burger in Marseille: Top 10 for 2026

Howard, Holymelt, Moon Burger and seven more: where to find Marseille's best burgers, what to order and what it costs, with every source dated.

By Thomas, Restaurants writer, FrancePublished November 26, 2026

The best burger in Marseille is at Howard, 20 rue Haxo, near the Old Port. Time Out calls its founders “the pioneers of the smash burger” in the city, and the details back it up: grass-fed Angus beef raised in the Camargue, potato buns baked on site. Holymelt (smash burgers made from scratch in the 2nd arrondissement) and Moon Burger (the most inventive menu in town, on a house bun in Saint-Victor) complete my top three. All ten places follow, with the address, what to order and what it cost when each source was published.

How I ranked the best burgers in Marseille

This ranking is built on independent evidence, and I rechecked every source on 2 November 2026. To make the list, a place had to appear in recent burger guides or local press: Le Grand Pastis (top 10, 18 May 2026), News of Marseille (top 10, 28 February 2025), Time Out Marseille, Marseille Tourisme, Les Cachotteries de Marseille, Le Bonbon, Made in Marseille, Gault&Millau and Gilles Pudlowski. Recent coverage counted for more. So did hard facts: where the beef comes from, who bakes the bread, who runs the kitchen. A named beef supplier beats a high Google score every time.

Two limits up front. First, I could not confirm each place’s 2026 status on its Google Business Profile. The newest evidence is the May 2026 press, so call ahead or check Google Maps before you go. Second, most prices come from articles published between 2022 and 2025, and each one is given with its source and date. Star ratings need a visit, and this guide is built on sources, so you won’t find any here. Nor will you find a Google or TripAdvisor score passed off as our verdict.

1. Howard: the best smash burger in Marseille

Howard is the best smash burger in Marseille. Time Out credits its founders, Marie-Laure Oddone and Damien Hirel, with bringing the style to the city. Before Howard, the couple ran a pizzeria near Toulon (Time Out, 12 May 2024).

Howard’s official site describes Angus beef raised in the Camargue and fed on fresh grass. The potato buns are baked on site from a house recipe, and the sauces and fries are made in-house too. That is why Howard is my number one. The beef has a named origin, the buns never leave the building, and this team was smashing patties in Marseille before anyone else. The menu lists the Petit Cheeseburger, Classic Cheeseburger, Green Chili, Smoky Joe and Bunfury. Order the Smoky Joe, which Time Out describes as smoked bacon, Texan sauce, pickles and caramelized onions.

  • Address: 20 rue Haxo, 13001 Marseille, near Vieux-Port metro station
  • Price: burgers €11.50 to €15.40, meals €16.50 to €20.40 (Time Out, 12 May 2024; Time Out rated it 4/5)
  • Hours (official site): Monday to Thursday 11:45 am–3 pm and 6:45–10:15 pm; Friday 11:45 am–3 pm and 6:45–10:30 pm; Saturday 11:45 am–10:30 pm; Sunday 6:30–10:30 pm
  • Links: howardburger.fr, Instagram @howard_marseille

Want to try the technique at home? Alice’s Sonic Bacon Smasher copycat recipe uses the same press-and-sear method.

2. Holymelt: smash burgers and coffee made from scratch

Holymelt makes nearly everything itself: buns from Label Rouge flour, house sauces and coffee ground on site. Marseille Tourisme, Le Grand Pastis and Les Cachotteries all describe that set-up, plus a vegetarian option.

Les Cachotteries (2022) lists the Vintage Pulled Beef, the Chick’n Fire and the Smoky Blue Cheese, with burgers from €10.90. News of Marseille (2025) prices the Holy Cheese at €13. That makes it the cheapest signature burger among the 2025 prices in this guide, and my value pick.

  • Address: 42 rue Mazenod, 13002 Marseille, between La Joliette and Les Docks
  • Phone: +33 9 71 36 99 90 (Le Grand Pastis)
  • Hours: sources disagree, including on which days it closes, so check before you go

3. Moon Burger: the most creative burgers in Marseille

Moon Burger serves Marseille’s most unusual burgers on a house-made bun. If a classic cheeseburger bores you, go here. Made in Marseille, Le Grand Pastis, Les Cachotteries and News of Marseille all cover it.

The Smoked Fig combines smoked provola, fig jam and caramelized onions. The Gravity puts a duck patty under honey sauce, and at €18 (News of Marseille, 2025) it is the priciest burger in this guide. The press also mentions the London Express with eggplant, a burger with cooked pineapple and a five-patty stack. Vegetarians get a halloumi version.

  • Address: 29 boulevard de la Corderie, 13007 Marseille (Saint-Victor). Most sources give number 29, but the Marseille Tourisme blog gives 27.
  • Phone: +33 4 91 01 74 86
  • Note: sources disagree on its opening year, so I don’t give one

4. Burger’s Banquet: bistro burgers behind the Opera

Burger’s Banquet is a daytime bistro behind the Opera, known for AOC Charolles beef and bread from a local baker. Gilles Pudlowski (2014) and Le Bonbon both describe the Charolles beef. The chicken is free-range Label Rouge.

Order the Blue Burger with Bleu d’Auvergne cheese, then the “Infiniment vanille” cheesecake. In 2014, Pudlowski named Damien Modena as the chef, an alumnus of Spoon London and L’Oustau de Baumanière. I could not confirm that he is still in the kitchen in 2026.

  • Address: 9 rue Molière, 13001 Marseille
  • Hours: Monday to Saturday, 8:30 am–4 pm, daytime only (Le Grand Pastis, 18 May 2026)
  • Phone: +33 4 91 93 32 40

5. Le Bon Burger: a Provençal burger by the Old Port

Le Bon Burger puts Provence in a bun. Its Provençal burger comes with tapenade (€14, News of Marseille, 2025). Made in Marseille says the Old Port restaurant opened in August 2014, with bread from a Marseille baker and house-made potatoes and coleslaw instead of fries.

A second location on avenue du Prado has a Gault&Millau listing. The guide says Julien Perez took it over, that it uses only fresh French beef with 15% fat, and that its buns come from La Fournée d’Eugène. Gault&Millau lists only the Prado address. I could not confirm that the Old Port location is still open, or that both have the same owner.

  • Old Port: 24 place aux Huiles, 13001 Marseille
  • Prado: 275 avenue du Prado, 13008 Marseille, +33 4 91 30 87 44, Instagram @lebonburgerprado

6. Le Bar Bû: burgers and cocktails near the Old Port

Le Bar Bû serves burgers, fish and chips and cocktails a few steps from the cours d’Estienne d’Orves. If you want a cocktail with your burger, this is my pick. Every burger is named after a famous bearded man. The ZZ Top comes with an egg and barbecue sauce, and the Tryphon Tournesol (Professor Calculus in the English Tintin books) comes with foie gras. Le Bonbon gave a price range of €15 to €20 in 2022.

  • Address: 8 rue Euthymènes, 13001 Marseille
  • Website: bar-bu-restaurant-marseille.com

7. Le Panier à Burgers: seasonal burgers in Cinq-Avenues

Le Panier à Burgers changes its burgers with the seasons. News of Marseille (2025) priced L’Automnal, with game meat and mushroom sauce, at €17. Restoranking reports a Google score of 9.4/10 from 483 reviews. That figure comes from a third-party site, and I give it only as a sign of popularity.

  • Address: 35 avenue du Maréchal-Foch, 13004 Marseille, according to News of Marseille and Restoranking. Le Grand Pastis places it on boulevard Sakakini, so check before you go.
  • Don’t confuse it with Le Bistro du Panier, a different restaurant at 58 rue Caisserie

8. La Folie du Burger: the Marseille-style aioli burger

La Folie du Burger serves the most Marseille burger on this list: Le Marseillais, with house-made aioli (€15 as a meal, News of Marseille, 2025). Made in Marseille also mentions a truffle burger. There are two locations.

  • City center: 144 rue de Rome, 13006 Marseille
  • East side: 8 route des Trois-Lucs, 13012 Marseille

9. Flower Burger: the best vegan burger in Marseille

Flower Burger is the place for a vegan burger in Marseille: every burger is 100% plant-based. The concept comes from Milan, and the colored buns are made with beet, turmeric or charcoal. News of Marseille (2025) priced the Flower Cheddar at €14.

  • Address: 64 avenue Robert Schuman, 13002 Marseille, in La Joliette near Les Terrasses du Port
  • Phone: +33 9 82 24 70 69 (Le Grand Pastis)

10. L’Après M: social fast food in the northern districts

L’Après M is a “fast social food” restaurant in a former McDonald’s that closed at the end of 2019. Former staff and local residents took it over. Marseille Tourisme says the menu includes “burgers by Gérald Passedat”, the three-Michelin-star chef of Le Petit Nice.

I could not confirm the name, ingredients or price of the Passedat burger, or that it is still on the menu in 2026. Its website, apresm.org, mostly describes a mutual-aid organization. Go for the story and the cause, and check the menu before you make the trip.

  • Address: 214 chemin de Sainte-Marthe, 13014 Marseille
  • Hours: open all year, closed on Sundays
  • Links: apresm.org, Instagram @lapres.m

Burgers near the Old Port in Marseille

Four places on this list are a short walk from the Old Port: Howard (20 rue Haxo), Le Bon Burger (24 place aux Huiles), Le Bar Bû (8 rue Euthymènes) and Burger’s Banquet (9 rue Molière). Howard is the closest to Vieux-Port metro station. Burger’s Banquet only serves until 4 pm, so go there for lunch.

Other Marseille spots that didn’t make the top 10

These four places came up in my research but didn’t meet the criteria for this ranking.

  • Le Bistro du Panier (58 rue Caisserie, 13002): Restoranking reports a Google score of 9.6/10 from 790 reviews, but I found no press guide and no signature burger.
  • Cornbread: a soul food spot founded by Ilias, known for fried chicken and chicken & waffles. It isn’t a burger restaurant as such. Time Out (8 May 2026) gives 15 rue des Trois Rois, 13006, while older sources say 32 rue des Trois Mages.
  • Marius Burger: local flavors such as aioli, panisse and cod. Sources disagree on where it is (a restaurant in Allauch, outside Marseille, or a food truck in the 12th), and I couldn’t confirm it’s still open in 2026.
  • Black and White Burger (Quai du Lazaret): says on bnwburger.com that it is halal (see the FAQ).

FAQ: burgers in Marseille

Where can I get the best burger in Marseille?

Howard, at 20 rue Haxo near the Old Port, tops this ranking. It is the city’s pioneering smash burger spot, with Camargue Angus beef and potato buns baked on site. Holymelt (2nd) and Moon Burger (7th) are the next two picks.

Is Marseille good for burgers?

Yes. Marseille has smash burger specialists, bistros that use AOC Charolles beef, a 100% plant-based chain and local twists with aioli or tapenade. Recent guides by Le Grand Pastis (May 2026) and Time Out cover more than ten serious burger places.

Which burger places are near the Old Port?

Howard, Le Bon Burger, Le Bar Bû and Burger’s Banquet are all in the 1st arrondissement, a short walk from the Old Port. Howard is the closest to Vieux-Port metro station.

What is the best smash burger in Marseille?

Howard is the best-known smash burger in Marseille, and Time Out calls its founders the city’s smash burger pioneers. Holymelt, on rue Mazenod, is the other place to try. It makes everything on site, including the buns.

Are there halal burgers in Marseille?

None of the ten places in this guide says it is halal in the sources I checked. Black and White Burger, on Quai du Lazaret, says on its own website (bnwburger.com) that it is halal. Ask any restaurant about its certification before you order.

Where can I find a vegan or vegetarian burger in Marseille?

Flower Burger, at 64 avenue Robert Schuman, is 100% plant-based. Holymelt has a vegetarian option, and Moon Burger serves a halloumi version and the London Express with eggplant.

How much does a burger cost in Marseille?

A restaurant burger in Marseille usually costs about €11 to €18. Prices in the sources range from €10.90 at Holymelt (2022) and €11.50 to €15.40 at Howard (2024) up to €18 for Moon Burger’s Gravity (2025). A meal at Howard cost €16.50 to €20.40 in 2024.

Which burger places are open on Sundays in Marseille?

Howard’s official site lists Sunday hours of 6:30 to 10:30 pm. Burger’s Banquet opens Monday to Saturday only, and L’Après M closes on Sundays. Check Google Maps for the other places.

Is there a Marseille-style burger?

Yes. La Folie du Burger makes Le Marseillais with house-made aioli, and Le Bon Burger tops its Provençal burger with tapenade. Marius Burger serves panisse and aioli burgers, but I could not confirm its address or that it is still open.
