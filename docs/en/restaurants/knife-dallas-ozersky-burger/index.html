Knife Dallas Closed: Where to Get the Ozersky Now

Knife Dallas closed in 2025, but John Tesar's Ozersky burger, No. 6 on Thrillist's 2017 list, is still on the lunch menu at Knife Steakhouse Plano.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 24, 2026

Knife Dallas is closed, but the Ozersky burger is not gone. John Tesar’s steakhouse at The Highland Dallas hotel closed on August 31, 2025, and the burger that made it famous is now on the lunch menu at Knife Steakhouse Plano (6121 W Park Blvd, Suite RS9, The District at Willow Bend), daily from 11 AM to 3 PM. Plano is the last Knife left in North Texas: Knife Italian Steak at The Ritz-Carlton Dallas, Las Colinas closed after service on October 6, 2026. Tesar has moved on too. In October 2026 he was named corporate executive chef of Palladino’s Steak & Seafood in Dallas and New York.

I checked everything below on November 3, 2026, against Knife Plano’s official site, Visit Plano, Bravo’s bio of Tesar and the Dallas food press that covered each opening and closing. Each source link sits next to the fact it supports. I have not eaten at the Plano location for this piece, so nothing here is a tasting note. Where sources disagree, or where no official source confirms a detail, I say so.

Is Knife Dallas closed?

Yes. Knife Dallas closed for good on August 31, 2025, after more than a decade at The Highland Dallas, 5300 E. Mockingbird Ln. at US-75. Tesar and the hotel ended their partnership as of March 28, 2025, according to the Dallas Morning News. The restaurant kept serving through the summer.

The hotel did not leave the space empty for long. On September 29, 2025, CultureMap Dallas reported that The Reserve, a modern American grill led by chef Andrew Bell, had replaced Knife. Drive to Mockingbird Lane for an Ozersky today and you will find a different chef, a different menu and no Ozersky.

In 2025 Tesar announced a new Knife in Uptown Dallas for that fall. The Dallas press also described a three-concept project: a Knife steakhouse, Pocket Knife and Switchblade. As of November 2026, none of them has a confirmed address or opening date. Until one does, I treat them as plans, not places.

Where to eat the Ozersky burger in 2026: Knife Steakhouse Plano

Knife Steakhouse Plano is the only restaurant that lists the Ozersky burger in 2026, and only at lunch. The official Knife Plano site puts the Ozersky Burger on the lunch menu next to a 100% Tajima Wagyu Burger. Neither burger appears on the dinner or bar menus.

My advice: go for lunch and order before 3 PM. I could not confirm whether the kitchen will make an Ozersky at dinner, because the menu only shows it at lunch. If you want an evening burger, call first.

Knife Plano opened at The Shops at Willow Bend in October 2018, along with a Knife Burger counter (Local Profile). Sources disagree on whether that counter is still running, so don’t count on it. The steakhouse is the sure bet. It runs a dry-aging program from 45 days to one year, with beef from 44 Farms and Heartbrand in Texas and Creekstone in Kansas (Visit Plano). In October 2026, CraveDFW reported that Tesar is a partner and operator in Plano with restaurateur George Stergios.

What is on the Ozersky burger?

The Ozersky is a deliberately plain cheeseburger: a 44 Farms Texas beef patty, American cheese and thin-sliced red onion on a soft white bun, with house-made pickles. That is the build described by PaperCity and Orlando Weekly.

  • Beef: a 44 Farms patty from Texas. Several media reports describe it as 80/20 sirloin, but no official menu confirms the blend, the patty weight or whether it is a single or a double.
  • Cheese: American, melted over the patty.
  • Onion: red onion, sliced thin.
  • Bun: soft white bun.
  • Pickles: made in-house, served with the burger.

What strikes me is what’s missing. No bacon, no aioli, no truffle. A steakhouse that aged rib-eyes for months put its name on a burger a roadside diner could serve. I think that restraint is the whole argument: with nothing on it to hide behind, the beef has to carry the burger, and only a kitchen sure of its beef supply would take that bet.

Why the Ozersky burger is named after Josh Ozersky

The Ozersky is named for Tesar’s friend Josh Ozersky, the late food writer and founder of the Meatopia festival. CraveDFW and Orlando Weekly both report the dedication. For a writer who built a festival around meat, a bare American-cheese burger feels like a better tribute than a wagyu showpiece would have been. That’s my reading, not a reason Tesar has given.

Was the Ozersky really the best burger in America? The Thrillist ranking

Thrillist ranked the Ozersky No. 6 on its list of the 100 best burgers in America in May 2017. The buzz started months earlier. On October 14, 2016, KERA News reported that Thrillist writer Kevin Alexander, midway through a national burger tour, said the Ozersky might be the best burger he had eaten.

On the final list, Stanich’s in Portland took No. 1, according to Food Republic. Closer to home, the Dallas Observer put the Ozersky at No. 23 of its 100 Favorite Dishes.

So does the Thrillist ranking still make the Ozersky the best burger in Dallas? Not on its own. It was a national top-10 burger in 2017, but that ranking is nine years old and the Dallas restaurant behind it has closed. I would call it a chef’s burger with a real pedigree that you can still order, not the city’s best by default. For current picks, see our best burgers in Dallas guide for 2026.

Knife Dallas: dry-aging and national praise (2014–2025)

Knife Dallas made its name with extreme dry-aging, including a 240-day dry-aged rib-eye, reported by the Dallas Observer and CraveDFW. In its first year it was the only Dallas finalist in Esquire’s Best New Restaurants in America (CultureMap Dallas). Sources put the opening in late April or in May 2014, so I give it as spring 2014.

What happened to Knife Burger?

Knife Burger, the counter-service spin-off built around the Ozersky, closed in both of its confirmed food-hall locations. The Legacy Hall unit in Plano opened in November 2017 and closed after a dispute with Front Burner Group (CultureMap Dallas). The Crockett Row unit in Fort Worth lasted barely seven months, from December 2018 to July 2019 (CultureMap Fort Worth).

Legacy Hall food hall at Legacy West in Plano, Texas, next to Haywire (photo taken July 2026). The Knife Burger counter opened here in November 2017 and sold the Ozersky for $5.95. It closed after a dispute with Front Burner Group. (Photo: Ranch9613, CC0 1.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Legacy\_West\_Foodhall\_Plano,\_Texas.jpg)

In those years, QSR Magazine reported that Knife Burger in Plano sold the Ozersky for $5.95. That price is history. Knife Plano does not publish a current price online, so I can’t give you one for 2026.

Knife Italian Steak in Las Colinas (2024–2026)

Knife Italian opened at The Ritz-Carlton Dallas, Las Colinas, 4150 N. MacArthur Blvd. in Irving, on March 19, 2024. It became Knife Italian Steak in September 2025 and closed after service on October 6, 2026 (CraveDFW, CultureMap Dallas). That closure left Plano as the last Knife in North Texas.

Who is John Tesar?

John Tesar is a Paris-trained chef, a Top Chef Season 10 contestant and the founder of Knife, and he has cooked in Dallas since 2007. According to his Bravo bio, he trained at La Varenne École de Cuisine in Paris and cooked in Westhampton, New York, and in Las Vegas before moving to Dallas.

On TV, Tesar competed on Top Chef Season 10, set in Seattle, and won the first season of Food Network’s Extreme Chef. Some trade press credits him with more than one Top Chef season, but Bravo’s own bio names only Season 10. His cookbook Knife: Texas Steakhouse Meals at Home (Flatiron Books) went on sale in May 2017 (D Magazine).

The Knife name has traveled beyond Texas. Knife & Spoon at The Ritz-Carlton Orlando, Grande Lakes, earned a Michelin star in 2022 and 2023, according to FSR Magazine. Sources differ on whether Tesar licenses the restaurant or is a partner in it.

John Tesar at Palladino’s: where he cooks now

Since October 2026, John Tesar has been corporate executive chef of Palladino’s Steak & Seafood, replacing opening chef Sam Hazen. Nation’s Restaurant News reported the Palladino Group’s announcement on October 29, 2026. The role covers the Dallas restaurant near Preston Hollow, at Preston Road and Royal Lane, and the Palladino’s at Grand Central Terminal in New York (FSR Magazine).

How much time he still spends in the Plano kitchen is not public. CraveDFW lists him as Knife Plano’s partner and operator, while NRN only says Knife Plano remains open.

FAQ: Knife Dallas and the Ozersky burger

Is Knife in Dallas closed? Yes. Knife Dallas at The Highland Dallas closed on August 31, 2025. The Reserve, led by chef Andrew Bell, replaced it.

Where can I still get the Ozersky burger in 2026? At Knife Steakhouse Plano, 6121 W Park Blvd, Suite RS9, Plano, TX 75093. It is on the lunch menu, served daily from 11 AM to 3 PM.

Does Knife Plano serve burgers at dinner, or only at lunch? The official site lists the Ozersky and the Tajima Wagyu burger only on the lunch menu. Call ahead if you want one at dinner or at the bar.

What is on the Ozersky burger at Knife? A 44 Farms Texas beef patty, American cheese and thin-sliced red onion on a soft white bun, with house-made pickles. Reports of an 80/20 sirloin blend are not confirmed on an official menu.

Why is the Ozersky burger named after Josh Ozersky? It honors Tesar’s friend Josh Ozersky, the late food writer and founder of Meatopia.

Is the Ozersky really the best burger in Dallas, according to Thrillist? Thrillist’s Kevin Alexander called it possibly the best burger of his national tour in 2016, and Thrillist ranked it No. 6 in America in May 2017. Stanich’s in Portland was No. 1.

What happened to Knife Burger? The Legacy Hall unit in Plano (opened November 2017) closed after a dispute with Front Burner Group. The Crockett Row unit in Fort Worth opened in December 2018 and closed in July 2019. The status of the Willow Bend counter is unclear.

What replaced Knife at The Highland Dallas? The Reserve, a modern American grill led by chef Andrew Bell, reported by CultureMap Dallas on September 29, 2025.

Who is John Tesar, and was he on Top Chef? Tesar is a La Varenne-trained chef based in Dallas since 2007. He competed on Top Chef Season 10 in Seattle and won the first season of Food Network’s Extreme Chef.

Where is John Tesar cooking now? He is corporate executive chef of Palladino’s Steak & Seafood in Dallas and New York, as reported on October 29, 2026. CraveDFW also lists him as partner and operator of Knife Steakhouse Plano.
