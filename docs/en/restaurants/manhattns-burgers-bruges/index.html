Manhattn's Burgers Bruges: Menu, Prices, Reviews and Opening Hours

Manhattn's Burgers Bruges on Simon Stevinplein: hours, 2024 menu prices (€12.50–€14.30), reviews and how it compares with Ellis on the same square.

By AlexPublished October 11, 2026

Manhattn’s Burgers in Bruges is at Simon Stevinplein 2, 8000 Brugge, and opens every day from 11:30 am to 10 pm. I checked those hours against the chain’s official restaurant list on 10 October 2026. The menu is flame-grilled Irish Angus burgers on brioche buns, with Belgian fries and Belgian beer. On the last official Belgian menu (August 2024), burgers cost €12.50 to €14.30. You can’t book. Deliveroo delivers, and Google reviewers give it 4.6/5. My short verdict: this is a dependable chain burger on a pretty old-town square, not a reason to travel to Bruges. On Google, though, it is the better-rated of the two burger places on Simon Stevinplein. The other is Ellis Gourmet Burger, a few doors along.

Where is Manhattn’s Burgers in Bruges?

Manhattn’s Burgers is at Simon Stevinplein 2, 8000 Brugge, in the historic centre of Bruges. The chain’s Bruges page and its restaurant list give that address, and so do student.be and Wanderlog.

Manhattn’s Bruges opening hours

Manhattn’s Burgers Bruges is open seven days a week, Sunday included, from 11:30 am to 10 pm. That comes from the chain’s official restaurant list, checked on 10 October 2026.

One caveat. The generic footer on the Bruges page says 11:30 am to 10:30 pm. I trusted the restaurant list, because it gives hours location by location. Holiday hours aren’t published, so if you’re planning a late dinner, check the official page on the day.

Manhattn’s Bruges menu and prices

The Manhattn’s Bruges menu is built around flame-grilled burgers made with grass-fed Irish Angus beef, served on a brioche bun with house sauces and fresh fries. On the most recent official Belgian menu (August 2024), burgers cost €12.50 to €14.30.

Those prices are two years old. The chain’s current online menu lists the dishes but no prices, so the figures below come from the official Belgian menu PDF dated August 2024. Expect to pay the same or more in Bruges today, and more again on delivery apps.

The Wall Street Deal is not much of a deal. Bought separately, an Empire and a portion of Godfather Fries already come to €16.80, so you’re paying €7.10 for the iced tea and the cookie, which together cost €9.40 on their own.

Drinks, milkshakes and Bruges beer

The beer list is where Manhattn’s feels most like Bruges. The 2024 menu flagged Brugge Tripel as a “Popular pick!”, at €6.00 for 33 cl or €8.50 for 50 cl. Alongside it were Steenbrugge Blond, Rodenbach Fruitage, Brussels Beer Project’s Delta IPA and BBP Pico Bello (0.3%). Milkshakes (vanilla, speculoos, Oreo) cost €6.50 to €7.00, house iced tea €4.50 and a New York cookie €4.90. Seasonal beers rotate, so I can’t promise the Tripel is still on.

Manhattn’s Bruges reviews: what diners say

Manhattn’s Bruges reviews are mostly positive on Google, which rates it 4.6/5 from 1,542 reviews. TripAdvisor is cooler at 3.5/5, but from just 17 reviews. Both figures come from Wanderlog, consulted on 10 October 2026. Wanderlog doesn’t say when it pulled them, and TripAdvisor blocked direct access, so treat them as a snapshot.

Another aggregator, intravel.net, shows 4.5/5 from 700 reviews. Each site captured its figures at a different time, which explains the mismatch. The pattern holds across all three, though: a large, happy Google crowd and a small, more critical TripAdvisor sample.

Manhattn’s Burgers Bruges ratings by platform: Google 4.6/5 (1,542 reviews), intravel.net 4.5/5 (700) and TripAdvisor 3.5/5 (17). Figures come from aggregators and were consulted on 10 October 2026. (Chart: Burger Breton, data from Wanderlog and intravel.net (10 October 2026) — SVG 800×400, src/assets/restaurants-bruges-manhattn-s-burgers/manhattns-bruges-ratings-chart.svg)

  • What reviewers praise: the Gatsby above all, then friendly staff and quick self-service ordering.
  • What they criticise: some diners say the fries taste “pre-made”. That’s a pointed complaint, because the chain says its fries are fresh and BRUZZ reports they are cooked twice.
  • Value: intravel.net puts a typical spend at €20 to €30 per person. That matches a burger, fries and a drink at the 2024 prices.

Wanderlog and intravel.net also mention a terrace, an upstairs dining room, wheelchair access and paid parking nearby. The chain doesn’t confirm any of it, so I’m passing it on as unverified.

The story behind Manhattn’s Burgers

Manhattn’s is a Belgian burger chain founded by the brothers Jérôme and Philippe Vandermeulen. They opened the first restaurant on 22 October 2014 at 164 avenue Louise in Brussels, according to the “Our story” section of the official menu.

The idea came from New York. In 2011, Jérôme was working on a waffle and coffee food truck near Central Park, and he decided to bring that Manhattan feel back to Europe. The chain’s tagline today: “Belgian roots, New York soul”. When BRUZZ covered the chain’s tenth anniversary, it singled out the flame-grilled Irish Angus, the twice-cooked fries, the house sauces and the Belgian specialty beers.

Bruges has a personal link to the brand. Philippe, the chef of the two brothers, trained at the Spermalie hotel school in Bruges, as AC Franchise reported on 30 September 2026.

Where else you’ll find Manhattn’s

The official site lists 13 restaurants as of October 2026: 10 in Belgium and 3 in Paris. The Belgian ones are five in Brussels (Bourse, Merode, Louise, Boondael and Fox Food Market), Antwerp Groenplaats, Wijnegem Shopping Center, Ghent Graslei, Leuven Tiensestraat and Bruges. The newest Paris branch opened on Place Saint-Michel on 2 October 2026. Next, the chain is aiming for 10 to 15 more openings by 2029 across Belgium, France, the Netherlands and Germany.

Manhattn’s Burgers Bruges FAQ

Where is Manhattn’s Burgers in Bruges? At Simon Stevinplein 2, 8000 Brugge, Belgium, in the old town. It is the chain’s only restaurant in Bruges.

What are Manhattn’s Bruges opening hours? Is it open on Sunday? Yes. It opens every day, Sunday included, from 11:30 am to 10 pm, according to the official restaurant list (checked 10 October 2026).

Can I book a table at Manhattn’s Burgers Bruges? No. The official Bruges page says “No reservations”, and you order at the counter.

How much is a burger at Manhattn’s in Bruges? On the latest official Belgian menu (August 2024), burgers cost €12.50 (Empire Cheeseburger) to €14.30 (Gatsby). The set meal with fries, iced tea and a cookie was €23.90. No 2026 in-restaurant prices are published.

Does Manhattn’s Bruges deliver or do takeaway? Delivery runs through Deliveroo, linked from the official Bruges page, and intravel.net lists takeaway. The official page does not mention Uber Eats for Bruges.

Are there vegetarian options? Yes: the Brooklyn Falafel burger and the Soho Veggie salad. If you have allergies, the menu asks you to tell the staff.

What’s the best burger at Manhattn’s? The Gatsby, with Emmental AOP, sautéed mushrooms, bacon and black truffle mayo, is the one reviewers mention most.

Can I pay cash at Manhattn’s Burgers Bruges? Cards are accepted, according to intravel.net. Some summaries say it is cashless, but I couldn’t confirm that, so bring a card.

Where does Manhattn’s beef come from? The chain uses grass-fed Irish Angus beef, grilled over a flame.

What is the best burger in Bruges? There is no reliable, recent ranking of burgers in Bruges yet. Of the two burger places on Simon Stevinplein, Manhattn’s has the higher Google rating.
