Best Burger in Salta: Burgerteca's La Segunda

Two 24-year-old friends from Salta started in a home kitchen and won the expert-jury prize at Burgerpalusa 2026, Argentina's national burger contest.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished November 2, 2026

The best burger in Salta, Argentina, is La Segunda at Burgerteca, Rivadavia 747. On 2–3 October 2026 it took the expert-jury prize at the 5th Burgerpalusa in Buenos Aires, Argentina’s national burger contest. Burgerteca belongs to Pablo Agüero and Pablo Nickler, two 24-year-old friends from Salta. Three years ago they were cooking on a small griddle in a home kitchen. I haven’t eaten there yet, so this is a profile built from the local press, not a review.

Who founded Burgerteca? Pablo Agüero and Pablo Nickler

Burgerteca was founded by Pablo Agüero and Pablo Nickler, two Salta locals who have been friends since secondary school. Both are 24.

They told Informate Salta they started “sin saber nada”: knowing nothing. They quit their jobs, put their savings in and leaned on their families. That’s the whole business plan, and it worked.

From a home griddle to Rivadavia 747

Burgerteca grew in three steps: a home kitchen, then a delivery and takeaway shop, then a sit-down restaurant. According to El Tribuno and Salta Zoom, the pair first cooked on a small griddle with a fryer and sold to friends and family.

Next came a delivery and takeaway counter at the corner of Santiago del Estero and Güemes. They stayed about a year, then moved to Rivadavia 747, a dining room with about 80 seats.

The founding date depends on who you read. El Tribuno gives 31 October 2023. Informate Salta mentions a start on 10 April at Nickler’s home. Other outlets just say “about three years”. Every source agrees on 2023, and that’s what I’m using.

What is in La Segunda, Burgerpalusa’s 2026 jury winner?

La Segunda is a double beef burger with provolone, bacon, mushroom cream, onion, blue cheese and aioli on a classic bun. Infogastronómica, El Tribuno and Salta Zoom all list the same build.

  • Double beef patty
  • Provolone
  • Bacon
  • Mushroom cream made with button, portobello and pine mushrooms
  • Onion
  • Blue cheese
  • Aioli (roasted-garlic aioli, according to El Tribuno)
  • Classic bun

On paper, it is a maximalist burger. Two cheeses, a three-mushroom cream and garlic aioli all push in the same direction, and my first reaction to the list was that something had to give. Nickler’s answer, to Salta Zoom: “Es como una bomba dentro de una hamburguesa, pero están todos los ingredientes equilibrados”. Like a bomb inside a burger, but every ingredient in balance. An international jury agreed. I’d still like to taste it myself: blue cheese next to provolone is the kind of pairing that wins on a judging table and can turn heavy by the last bite.

Burgerpalusa 2026: who won the best burger in Argentina?

Burgerteca’s La Segunda won the expert-jury prize at the 5th Burgerpalusa, and Burger Shoppe of Mendoza won the public vote. The festival ran on 2–3 October 2026 at Tribuna Plaza, at the Hipódromo de Palermo racecourse in Buenos Aires.

It was Burgerteca’s first Burgerpalusa. First entry, first title. Salta Zoom reports a jury with members from Sweden, Peru and Argentina, and El Tribuno adds Ecuador.

Local press puts the field at some 200 applicants, with 24 preselected. Outlets disagree on how many reached the final (8 or 12), so I’m not giving a number.

Burgerteca of Salta won the expert-jury prize. (Chart: Burger Breton (original work), CC BY 4.0, data from El Tribuno and Salta Zoom (October 2026), rendered with QuickChart (800x400))

The prize had three parts, according to Infogastronómica and El Tribuno:

  • A trophy
  • ARS 2,500,000 in products and supplies
  • Direct places at the Montevideo Burger Show in Uruguay and at Perú Burger Fest

When I checked on 23 October 2026, nobody had yet reported whether Burgerteca had competed in Montevideo, or how it placed.

How the 2026 result compares with 2025

For the second year running, both Burgerpalusa titles went to burger bars outside Buenos Aires. At the 4th edition in September 2025, the jury chose Pretextos of Mendoza and the public chose Roses in Rosario, the Burgerpalusa public-vote winner I profiled. My view: the most interesting burgers in Argentina are now being made in the provinces, and the capital’s festival is where they come to prove it.

Where to eat in Salta beyond burgers

Salta’s reputation rests on empanadas salteñas, lomitos and llama, not on burgers. Burgerteca’s win doesn’t change that, but it does earn a burger a spot on the list. On a northwest Argentina route, slot it in between two empanada stops.

El Baqueano and Fernando Rivarola: what is current and what is history

El Baqueano, Salta’s best-known chef’s restaurant, is not a burger place, and it has left Cerro San Bernardo. Many travel guides still send diners up the hill, so here is where things stand. It is a fine-dining restaurant of native Argentine cuisine, founded by chef Fernando Rivarola and sommelier Gabriela Lafuente.

History

  • El Baqueano started at Chile 499 in San Telmo, Buenos Aires (sources disagree on the year: Bichos de Campo says 2008, La Nación says 2007).
  • It ranked No. 21 on Latin America’s 50 Best Restaurants 2021.
  • After Rivarola won a municipal tender in 2019, the restaurant moved to the summit of Cerro San Bernardo in Salta in April 2022. You reached it by cable car (about 10 minutes) or by road.
  • In early 2022 the San Telmo site became Trashumante, a more casual restaurant.
  • On the hill, El Baqueano ran as a restaurant, research and training venue with a free public library, the Biblioteca Gastronómica Dolli Irigoyen, and a producer network, Proyecto Milpa.
  • Its signature dishes were llama crudo or carpaccio and a pacú choripán. No burger was on the menu.

Current status

In early August 2026, El Baqueano announced it was leaving Cerro San Bernardo after four years. It called the move “una decisión dolorosa que excede nuestra voluntad”, a painful decision beyond its control (InSalta, 4 August 2026). No reasons were given. The brand continues, but no new location had been announced by 23 October 2026.

Who is Fernando Rivarola?

Fernando Rivarola is the Argentine chef who co-founded El Baqueano. According to La Nación and Bichos de Campo, he was born in San Cayetano in Buenos Aires province and grew up near Necochea. He trained in Mar del Plata and studied game cookery in Spain under Luis Lera.
