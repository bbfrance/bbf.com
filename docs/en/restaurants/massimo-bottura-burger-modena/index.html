Massimo Bottura Burger: Where to Eat It in Modena

Bottura's Emilia Burger costs €19 at Franceschetta58 in Modena. What goes into it, who cooks it now, and how it began at Shake Shack in 2014.

By Sofia, Restaurants writer, Italy, Switzerland and the BeneluxPublished November 18, 2026

Massimo Bottura’s burger is served at Franceschetta58, his bistro at Via Vignolese 58 in Modena, where the menu lists it as “Emilia Burger by Massimo Bottura”. Not in Bologna, despite what half the travel lists say. On the official menu I checked on 4 November 2026 it costs €19 à la carte, and it is included in the €68 “Tradition in Evolution” tasting menu. Bottura founded and owns the place. The cooking is done by Francesco Vincenzi, head chef since 2017. And the burger itself was born in New York, as a one-day special at Shake Shack in October 2014.

Is Franceschetta58 in Bologna? No, it is in Modena

Franceschetta58 is in Modena, and Massimo Bottura has no verified restaurant or burger in Bologna. The mix-up is easy to see: Bologna is the regional capital and where most visitors to Emilia-Romagna sleep, so search results and travel lists file Bottura under it. They are wrong. The bistro sits in a former tyre shop just outside Modena’s historic centre, which makes it a day trip from Bologna, not a dinner round the corner. Staying in Bologna and want a burger tonight? Where to eat the Massimo Bottura burger in Italy

Franceschetta58 in Modena is the one place in Italy where the Emilia Burger appears on a current official menu. I looked for others. A few search snippets claim it is also served at Gucci Osteria, but not one of them was dated or came from Bottura’s group, so I am not repeating the claim here.

Franceschetta58 Modena: the bistro of the Francescana Family

Franceschetta58 is Massimo Bottura’s casual restaurant in Modena. The Italian food site Scatti di Gusto calls it the bistro of his “Francescana Family” and the “younger sister” of the 3-Michelin-star Osteria Francescana (Scatti di Gusto). The building was a gommista, a tyre shop, on Via Vignolese, a few minutes’ walk outside the historic centre. It serves lunch and dinner from Monday to Saturday. Sunday, it is shut.

How Franceschetta58 started in 2011

Franceschetta58 opened on 26 November 2011 as a deli-style “gastrobistrot”, with Bottura working alongside Marta Pulini of Bibendum Catering. Dissapore covered the opening night. Its headline promised everything at €6; the article itself reported €7 a dish (Dissapore, 26 November 2011). Bottura said he wanted people to “spend an evening together without paying exaggerated sums”, and he named Momofuku Ssäm Bar in New York and L’Astrance in Paris as his models.

In December 2024, Food and Wine Italia celebrated “10 years” of Franceschetta58 (Food and Wine Italia). The maths only works if the bistro was relaunched around 2014, and the restaurant has never confirmed that. I am sticking with the opening year on record: 2011.

Who cooks at Franceschetta58: head chef Francesco Vincenzi

Francesco Vincenzi has been head chef of Franceschetta58 since 2017. Bottura founded and owns the restaurant, but none of the pages I checked says he cooks there day to day, so don’t book expecting to see him at the pass. Vincenzi was born in 1992 in Formigine, just south of Modena. He spent 7 years in the kitchen at Osteria Francescana, from 2010 to 2017, before taking over the bistro (Scatti di Gusto, Food and Wine Italia).

His most unusual supplier is a prison. Since 2022, Vincenzi has run a vegetable garden at Modena’s casa circondariale, the city jail, and its Brussels sprouts and turnip tops end up on the restaurant’s plates. Of everything I found in my research, this is the detail that tells you most about how the bistro thinks.

What is in the Emilia Burger?

The Emilia Burger puts Emilia-Romagna’s best-known flavours in 1 bun: cotechino sausage, Parmigiano Reggiano, salsa verde and balsamic vinegar. Franceschetta58’s official menu gives only the name and the price, so the exact 2026 recipe is not published. The two versions that have been described in print are not the same.

The regional ingredients behind the burger

Most of the Emilia Burger comes from the food valley around Modena. Cotechino, a soft, fatty pork sausage, is a Modena speciality. Parmigiano Reggiano aged 24 months brings salt and those small crunchy crystals. The balsamic in the 2014 mayonnaise is Modena’s best-known export. The exception is the beef: Chianina is a Tuscan breed from the Val di Chiana, not an Emilian one, a small break from the burger’s regional story. At the New York launch, Bottura said he wanted “to re-interpret Emilia with all of the best ingredients in our food valley” and to strike “the perfect balance of acidity, sweetness and bitterness” (Identità Golose). On paper, the salsa verde and balsamic carry the acidity, and the cotechino carries the richness.

How the price has changed

The Emilia Burger has risen from €15 to €19. Scatti di Gusto’s review, first published in 2023 and updated on 29 July 2024, priced it at €15, with the two tasting menus at €59 and €74. That is a 27% jump in about 2 years. Even so, my verdict is that €19 is fair: it is less than the tortellini on the same menu, and it is the cheapest way to eat a dish with Bottura’s name on it.

The Emilia Burger’s backstory: Shake Shack, New York, 2014

The Emilia Burger began as a one-day special at Shake Shack. It was served at the chain’s original Madison Square Park kiosk in New York on Monday 13 October 2014, Columbus Day. Shake Shack made 1,000 burgers at $8.95 each (Time Out New York). Bottura developed it with Mark Rosati, Shake Shack’s culinary director (The Caterer).

The original Shake Shack kiosk in Madison Square Park, New York, in March 2008. The Emilia Burger launched here as a one-day special on 13 October 2014: 1,000 burgers at $8.95. (Photo: Ehedaya, Public Domain, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The\_Shake\_Shack.JPG)

According to Identità Golose, the first people were queueing at 8 a.m. and all 1,000 burgers were gone within a few hours. Shake Shack never brought it back. It is not on the chain’s regular menu, so Modena is now the only place to order it.

Who is Massimo Bottura?

Massimo Bottura is the Modena chef who founded Osteria Francescana. He was born in the city on 30 September 1962 and opened Osteria Francescana on 19 March 1995. It holds 3 Michelin stars and a green star (2020), and it was No. 1 on The World’s 50 Best Restaurants in 2016 and 2018. In 2015 he co-founded the non-profit Food for Soul. His other venues include Gucci Osteria in Florence (2018), Beverly Hills (2020), Tokyo (2021) and Seoul (2022), and the Casa Maria Luigia guest house, which opened in May 2019 (Wikipedia).

Almost everything on that list costs serious money. The burger does not, and I think that is why it matters: it is Bottura’s cooking in a form anyone can order at lunch. For another chef who builds a menu around their own region, read our profile of K’u’uk in Mérida and chef Pedro Evia.

Massimo Bottura’s casual restaurant in Modena vs Osteria Francescana

Franceschetta58 is the affordable way into Bottura’s world: €19 for the burger, €64 for the cheapest tasting menu. That was the plan in 2011, when he set it up so people could eat together without fine-dining prices. I have not printed Osteria Francescana’s tasting-menu price because I could not confirm it on the official site, so check it there before you compare.

Day trip from Bologna to eat Bottura’s burger in Modena

Franceschetta58 is an easy day trip from Bologna, about 40 km away. Modena is the next major stop on the railway line, and regional trains link the two cities. I have not verified current journey times or fares, so check Trenitalia on the day. From Modena station, walk through the historic centre and out along Via Vignolese to the restaurant.

Day trip from Bologna: map of the Bologna–Modena railway corridor, with Franceschetta58 marked in Modena, about 40 km north-west of Bologna. (Map: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)

It is closed on Sunday, so go between Monday and Saturday. My suggestion: lunch at Franceschetta58, then the cathedral and the Ghirlandina tower in the centre before the train back. To ask about booking, email info@franceschetta58.it or call +39 059 3091008. FAQ: Massimo Bottura burger and Franceschetta58

Where can I eat Massimo Bottura’s Emilia Burger in Italy? At Franceschetta58, Via Vignolese 58, 41124 Modena. The burger is on the official à la carte menu as “Emilia Burger by Massimo Bottura”.

How much does the Emilia Burger cost at Franceschetta58? €19 à la carte, according to the official menu checked on 4 November 2026. It cost €15 in Scatti di Gusto’s 2023–2024 review.

What is in the Emilia Burger? The 2014 Shake Shack version had a beef patty ground with Parmigiano Reggiano, plus cotechino, salsa verde and balsamic mayonnaise. In 2023–2024, Scatti di Gusto described the Modena version as a Chianina and cotechino patty with 24-month Parmigiano Reggiano and salsa verde. The current menu does not list the ingredients.

Is Franceschetta58 in Bologna or Modena? Modena. From Bologna, take a regional train to Modena and check times with Trenitalia.

Does Massimo Bottura cook at Franceschetta58? Bottura founded the restaurant and still owns it, but Francesco Vincenzi has been head chef since 2017. None of the sources I checked says Bottura cooks there day to day.

Is Franceschetta58 cheaper than Osteria Francescana? Yes. It was set up as the affordable option: tasting menus cost €64 to €82, and the burger costs €19 à la carte. Check Osteria Francescana’s current price on its official site.

Do you need a reservation at Franceschetta58? The restaurant does not publish its booking policy. Contact it at +39 059 3091008 or info@franceschetta58.it before you go.

Can you order the Emilia Burger on its own? Yes. It is on the à la carte menu at €19, and it can also be added to the “I ❤ Modena” tasting menu for €9.

Was the Emilia Burger created for Shake Shack, and can you still get it there? It was first served at Shake Shack Madison Square Park on 13 October 2014, as a one-day run of 1,000 burgers at $8.95. It was a limited edition and is not on Shake Shack’s regular menu.

Can I make the Emilia Burger at home? Yes. The recipe was published in the Shake Shack cookbook, and Bottura’s MasterClass has a chapter on the Emilia Burger (MasterClass).
