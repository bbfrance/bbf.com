Jilles Beer & Burgers Ghent: Prices and Founder

Jilles Beer & Burgers in Ghent: beef burgers from €17.50 to €21.50, a Belgian beer for each, vegan options and the founder's story. Checked Nov 2026.

By Sofia, Restaurants writer, Italy, Switzerland and the BeneluxPublished November 28, 2026

Jilles Beer & Burgers, at Tentoonstellingslaan 165 near Citadelpark, is the Ghent-born burger brand to know. Beef burgers cost €17.50 to €21.50, and a beer sommelier has picked a Belgian beer to go with each one. Founder Jilles D’Hulster started the business with his father, Jack. I checked every price and fact on 16 November 2026.

One correction first. This profile began as a search for a burger by chef Michaël Vrijmoed, and there isn’t one: his two-star Ghent restaurant cooks vegetables, not burgers. More on that further down.

Jilles Beer & Burgers Ghent: key facts

Jilles Beer & Burgers Ghent is a gourmet burger restaurant at Tentoonstellingslaan 165, 9000 Gent, that pairs every burger with a Belgian beer. It is one of three Jilles restaurants; the other two are in Bruges and Ostend.

Who founded Jilles Beer & Burgers?

Jilles D’Hulster founded Jilles Beer & Burgers and opened his first restaurant in Ghent with his father, Jack. The house beer is named after him too: Rostekop, an amber beer for “ginger Jilles, the guy behind the burger restaurants”.

His motto came from his father: “Do a simple thing well, and do it properly.” The Belgian property firm Dewaele quoted that line in a 2017 article, and it fits the menu: a short list of burgers, each with its own beer.

The founding date is murkier. Jilles’ own site says the brand was “established in 2010”. According to Companyweb, the company Jilles Gent SRL (no. 0632.812.954) was registered on 23 June 2015. I could not confirm which year the first restaurant opened, or whether it was the Tentoonstellingslaan site.

What is the difference between Jilles and Jack?

Jilles is the sit-down beer-and-burger restaurant. Jack was a takeaway burger concept named after Jilles’ father. Jack also started in Ghent, and Dewaele reports that a Jack opened at Grote Markt 21 in Kortrijk in May 2016.

That is my only source on Jack, and it dates from 2017. I could not verify that Jack still operates in Ghent or Kortrijk in November 2026, so read it as brand history, not as an address to visit.

Prices are from jilles.be, checked on 16 November 2026. €17.50 for the plainest burger on the menu is not cheap. Jilles does not say who supplies its beef or its buns, or how it cooks its patties, so I can’t tell you the meat alone justifies that price. My view: you are paying for the beer list and the pairing as much as for the burger. If you want the double, order the Eddy Carnibaal.

Burger and beer pairing in Ghent: how Jilles does it

At Jilles, beer sommelier Sofie Vanrafelghem picks a specific Belgian beer for each burger on the menu. That is what sets the brand apart in Ghent, where most burger places treat beer as a side order rather than a pairing.

The house beer is Rostekop, an amber named after the red-haired founder. Skip the default lager and ask for the beer listed next to your burger. In a country with this many beer styles, that pairing is the reason to come here rather than somewhere cheaper.

Vegan burgers in Ghent at Jilles

Yes, Jilles Ghent serves vegetarian and vegan burgers alongside beef and chicken. Visit Gent describes the menu as “gourmet and vegan burgers, with a pure taste and deliciously different”.

That counts in Ghent, a city where Thursday Veggie Day has made plant-based eating a local habit. Gluten-free and lactose-free options are also on the menu, so a group with mixed diets can all order here.

Where else to find the best burger in Ghent

Besides Jilles, this guide covers two more Ghent burger addresses: Uncle Babe’s Burger Bar and Ellis Gourmet Burger. Only one of them was born in Ghent.

  • Uncle Babe’s Burger Bar, Sluizekenstraat 2, 9000 Gent. Its operating company was founded on 10 October 2012, and Le Fooding lists it.
  • Ellis Gourmet Burger has a Ghent restaurant but is a Brussels brand. Five friends, including Thierry Canetta and Amaury Hendrickx, founded Ellis in 2011, and the first restaurant opened on Place Sainte-Catherine in Brussels (La Libre, 2018). Ellis uses Rouge de Flandre beef from butcher Hendrik Dierendonck. The official site does not give its Ghent street address, so check it before you go.

For the Brussels side of that story, see our guide to the best burgers in Brussels. For every Ghent address we cover, start from our Ghent burger city page.

Does Vrijmoed in Ghent serve burgers?

No. Vrijmoed is a two-Michelin-star restaurant with no burger on any of its menus, and nothing links its chef to burgers. The Michelin Guide lists it as Creative and Modern Cuisine in the €€€€ price band, at Vlaanderenstraat 22, 9000 Gent.

Vrijmoed, Vlaanderenstraat 22 in Ghent: the listed townhouse that now holds the two-Michelin-star vegetable restaurant, seen in a 1977 black-and-white heritage photo when it was a hair salon (‘Coiffure’ in the window). (Photo: Vlaamse Gemeenschap (Onroerend Erfgoed), CC BY 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gent\_Vlaanderenstraat\_22\_-\_208723\_-\_onroerenderfgoed.jpg)

Michelin describes chef Michaël Vrijmoed as passionate about vegetables. He pairs classic French technique with traditional fermentation and influences from further afield, and offers a vegetarian menu called “Purs”. According to Visit Gent, he trained under Peter Goossens at the three-star Hof van Cleve.

On 16 November 2026 I went through the Michelin Guide, Visit Gent and vrijmoed.be. None of them mentions a burger, and a search for “Michaël Vrijmoed burger” turns up nothing relevant. The idea for this article came from a mix-up. I would rather say so than invent a burger that does not exist.

If you want Vrijmoed for what it is, it opens Tuesday to Friday, 12:00 to 13:30 and 19:00 to 21:30, and closes Saturday, Sunday and Monday. Book on +32 9 279 99 77 or at info@vrijmoed.be. FAQ

How much does a burger cost at Jilles in Ghent?

Beef burgers cost €17.50 to €21.50, from the Hugo Classic to the Eddy Carnibaal double burger. Takeaway orders get 10% off. Prices checked on 16 November 2026.

Who founded Jilles Beer & Burgers?

Jilles D’Hulster founded it and opened his first restaurant in Ghent with his father, Jack. The house amber beer, Rostekop, is named after him.

Which Belgian beer goes with a burger at Jilles?

Each burger has its own suggested Belgian beer, chosen by beer sommelier Sofie Vanrafelghem. The house beer is Rostekop, an amber beer.

Are there vegan burgers in Ghent?

Yes. Jilles Ghent serves vegetarian and vegan burgers, plus gluten-free and lactose-free options.

Do I need to book a table at Jilles Ghent?

Jilles takes bookings online on its Ghent booking page and by phone on +32 9 330 36 23. It seats groups of up to 90, so book ahead if you are a group.

Where is the best burger in Ghent?

This guide covers three Ghent burger addresses: Jilles Beer & Burgers, Uncle Babe’s Burger Bar and Ellis Gourmet Burger. Jilles is the one with Belgian beer pairings and a Ghent-born founder.

What is the difference between Jilles and Jack burgers?

Jilles is the sit-down beer-and-burger restaurant. Jack was a takeaway concept named after the founder’s father. It was last documented in 2017, and its current status is unverified.

Does Vrijmoed in Ghent serve burgers?

No. Vrijmoed is a two-Michelin-star restaurant at Vlaanderenstraat 22 focused on vegetables and creative cuisine. No official source mentions a burger on its menus.

Who is Michaël Vrijmoed and how many Michelin stars does Vrijmoed have?
