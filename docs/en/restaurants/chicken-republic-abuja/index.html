Chicken Republic Abuja: Branches, Menu and Prices

Chicken Republic Abuja: the Aminu Kano drive-thru in Wuse 2, five more branches, hours, the Chief Burger menu and prices in naira, and what customers say.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished November 2, 2026

The Chicken Republic to choose in Abuja is Chicken Republic - Aminu Kano, at 2 Aminu Kano Crescent, Wuse 2. It is the only Abuja listing that mentions a drive-thru. It has free, wheelchair-accessible parking, and it is listed as open from 8am to 10pm every day, with a Google rating of 4.1/5 from about 1,100 reviews. Other listings cover Garki (Area 8), Life Camp, Gwarinpa, Wuse Zone 6 and a second Wuse 2 address on Adetokunbo Ademola Crescent. Order the Chief Burger Meal, a fried chicken burger, or a Spicy ChickWizz wrap. Expect to pay roughly ₦3,800 to ₦4,800 for a Chief Burger. That range comes from unofficial aggregators I could not confirm, so check the price in the delivery app before you go.

Chicken Republic in Abuja: the everyday chicken burger

Chicken Republic is a Nigerian fast-food chain built on fried chicken and jollof rice. Burgers are a sideline. It is no burger specialist like Burger King or Johnny Rockets, but it is far easier to find in Abuja than either. For a lot of people in the capital, a Chief Burger is simply the nearest burger.

The chain was founded in 2004 by Deji Akinyanju, and its first restaurant opened in Apapa, Lagos (Wikipedia; MarketingEdge). If you are heading south too, my guide to the best burgers in Lagos covers the city where the brand began, and our Chicken Republic Lagos guide to the menu, prices and branches covers the chain’s restaurants there.

Chicken Republic belongs to Food Concepts Plc, a Lagos company that also owns PieXpress and The Chop Box (Food Concepts). For its 20th anniversary, the brand announced more than 300 outlets across Nigeria and Ghana (MarketingEdge).

A Chicken Republic restaurant with a PieXpress kiosk in front of it. Both brands belong to Food Concepts Plc. This outlet is in northern Nigeria, not in Abuja. (Photo: Anasskoko, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chicken\_Republic\_2.jpg)

Food Concepts’ revenue grew to ₦95.3 billion in 2024, up 44% from ₦66.2 billion in 2023, and most of that growth came from higher menu prices (MoneyCentral). The practical upshot: ignore any price list from before 2024.

Food Concepts, Chicken Republic’s owner, grew its revenue from ₦66.2 billion in 2023 to ₦95.3 billion in 2024 (+44%), mostly through higher menu prices (MoneyCentral). (Chart: Burger Breton (original), CC BY 4.0; data: MoneyCentral (2024 results))

The chain has no official store locator. The old chicken-republic.com domain now redirects to the Food Concepts website, which lists neither branches nor prices. Of the Abuja branches with listed hours, Aminu Kano in Wuse 2 and Area 8 in Garki close latest, both at 10pm. Area 8 and Adetokunbo Ademola open earliest, at 7am. Life Camp keeps the shortest day, from 10am to 9pm.

Chicken Republic Wuse 2 drive-thru: the branch to choose

The Aminu Kano branch at 2 Aminu Kano Crescent is the best Chicken Republic in Abuja for most visitors, because no other listing offers as many services. Aminu Kano Crescent is also Wuse 2’s restaurant strip, so the branch is easy to find and you have other options next door if the queue is long.

Panorama of Wuse 2, the Abuja district that is home to Aminu Kano Crescent, its restaurant strip and the Chicken Republic drive-thru. (Photo: Biggiepopa, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Panorama\_view\_of\_wuse\_2,\_Abuja.jpg)

According to its Google listing, as summarised by Top-rated.online, the branch offers:

  • Ways to eat: dine-in, takeaway, drive-thru and no-contact delivery
  • Meals: breakfast, plus catering for events
  • Accessibility: wheelchair-accessible entrance, toilets and car park
  • Parking: free
  • Payment: credit cards, debit cards and NFC mobile payments

That list is why I would send you here and not to another branch. Abuja is a city you cross by car, and a drive-thru with free parking saves you a hunt for a space. It is also the only branch whose listing documents step-free access in full: entrance, toilets and car park.

Chicken Republic Abuja menu: burgers, wraps and sides

The Chicken Republic menu in Abuja is mostly chicken and rice, with a small burger and wrap range built around the Chief Burger and the ChickWizz. The full menu covers fried chicken, rotisserie chicken, jollof rice, spaghetti, wraps, burgers and sandwiches (Wikipedia).

Chicken Republic burgers and wraps

These are the burgers and wraps on the menu, according to unofficial price lists (Nigerianprice, Pricelisto):

  • Chief Burger: the chain’s main burger. The Chief Burger Meal is a fried chicken burger with lettuce, cheese and house sauce, served with chips and a drink.
  • ChickWizz: a chicken wrap in Original, Spicy and Double versions.
  • Wrapstar: a second wrap, listed without a description.
  • Big Boyz: a larger meal, also listed without a description.

If you want a burger, order the Chief Burger Meal. If you want heat, go for the Spicy ChickWizz. Don’t come here for beef: every burger I found on the menu is chicken.

Sides and drinks

Chicken Republic’s sides are coleslaw, pasta salad, chicken salad, chips, fried rice, jollof rice and spaghetti. Drinks include 60cl bottles of Coca-Cola, Fanta and Sprite, plus Monster energy drinks (Nigerianprice, August 2023 list). In the Aminu Kano reviews, jollof rice is the side that keeps coming up as a positive. It is what I would add to a Chief Burger instead of chips.

Takeaway tray of Nigerian jollof rice with fried meat and a tomato and onion side, the kind of side Chicken Republic regulars praise most. Illustrative photo, not taken at Chicken Republic. (Photo: Flixtey, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Packed\_Jollof.jpg)

Vegetarian options

Chicken Republic has no confirmed vegetarian burger in Abuja. Vegetarians are limited to sides such as chips, rice and coleslaw, and I could not confirm what goes into the rice dishes.

Chicken Republic prices in Abuja (naira)

A Chief Burger at Chicken Republic costs roughly ₦3,800 to ₦4,800, and a Citizens meal roughly ₦4,600 to ₦5,800. Treat both ranges as indicative: they come from price aggregators that give no date and no source, and I found no official 2026 price list. Some sites still quote ₦2,000 for the Chief Burger. That figure dates from August 2023, before the price rises behind Food Concepts’ 2024 revenue jump.

The most reliable way to see the current price is the branch’s menu on Chowdeck or Heyfood at the moment you order. On 29 October 2026, Chowdeck’s Wuse Zone 6 listing was running three promotions: Citizens BOGOF (buy one, get one free), BigWhizz and BigWhizz Reloaded.

Chicken Republic Abuja delivery and online ordering

Chicken Republic delivers in Abuja through third-party apps, not through its own website. Chowdeck announced an official partnership with the chain (Chowdeck blog), and its Wuse Zone 6 listing quoted 20 to 30 minutes for delivery. Heyfood lists the Wuse, Utako and Apo branches (Heyfood).

Chicken Republic Abuja reviews: what customers say

The two Abuja branches with large review counts both score 4.1/5: Aminu Kano on Google and Wuse Zone 6 on Chowdeck. Customers praise the chicken, the jollof rice and the value, and complain about queues at peak times.

Branch Platform Rating Reviews Checked
Aminu Kano, Wuse 2 Google (via Top-rated.online) 4.1/5 about 1,100 29 Oct 2026
Wuse Zone 6 Chowdeck 4.1/5 2,200+ 29 Oct 2026

At the Aminu Kano branch, reviews often mention:

  • Positives: tasty fried chicken, good jollof rice, affordable prices and value meals, a clean air-conditioned dining room and friendly staff.
  • Complaints: crowds at peak hours, long waits, and staff who can get impatient with large orders.

I found no TripAdvisor or Yelp reviews for Chicken Republic’s Abuja branches. The Google figure is second-hand, taken from an aggregator of the Google listing, so the current score on Google Maps may differ slightly.

My reading: two branches on two different platforms landing on the same 4.1/5 points to a consistent chain, not a destination. Go to Aminu Kano outside the lunch and early-evening rush and most of the complaints disappear.

How I built this guide

I checked every fact in this guide on 29 October 2026. Chicken Republic publishes no official branch pages, so I used Waze, Google listing data collected by Top-rated.online, the ConnectCiti, Ofadaa and Finelib directories, and the Chowdeck and Heyfood delivery listings. Every address, phone number, opening time and rating comes with its source. I left out anything I could not source: the contents of the Citizens meal, which sources describe differently, and public transport, table bookings and play areas, which no source documents. This guide carries no Burger Breton rating, because the scores above are customer scores, not my own verdict.

Chicken Republic Abuja FAQ

Where is Chicken Republic in Abuja?

The best-equipped branch is Chicken Republic - Aminu Kano, at 2 Aminu Kano Crescent, Wuse 2, Abuja 904101. Other listings include 115 Adetokunbo Ademola Crescent in Wuse 2, 673 Ogbomosho St in Area 8, Garki, Life Camp, 3 Damaturu Street in Gwarinpa and Wuse Zone 6, plus Utako and Apo on Heyfood.

What are Chicken Republic’s opening hours in Abuja?

The Aminu Kano branch in Wuse 2 is listed as open daily from 8am to 10pm, and Area 8 in Garki from 7am to 10pm. Life Camp is listed from 10am to 9pm. These hours come from directories, so confirm them on Google Maps.

Is there a Chicken Republic drive-thru in Abuja?

Yes. The Aminu Kano branch at 2 Aminu Kano Crescent, Wuse 2, has a drive-thru, and it also offers dine-in, takeaway and delivery.

How much is a Chief Burger at Chicken Republic?

Aggregators list the Chief Burger at roughly ₦3,800 to ₦4,800, with no date or source, so treat that range as indicative. The ₦2,000 price still found online dates from August 2023 and is out of date. Check Chowdeck or Heyfood for the current price.

Does Chicken Republic deliver in Abuja?

Yes, through Chowdeck, an official partner, and through Heyfood, which lists the Wuse, Utako and Apo branches. Chowdeck quoted 20 to 30 minutes for the Wuse Zone 6 branch.

Does Chicken Republic serve breakfast?

The Aminu Kano branch in Wuse 2 lists breakfast among its services. Area 8 in Garki opens at 7am, according to its directory listing.

Can I pay by card at Chicken Republic in Abuja?

Yes. The Aminu Kano branch accepts credit cards, debit cards and NFC mobile payments, and the Adetokunbo Ademola branch is listed with a card terminal on Ofadaa.

Does Chicken Republic have vegetarian options?

No vegetarian burger is confirmed. Vegetarians can order sides such as chips, rice and coleslaw.
