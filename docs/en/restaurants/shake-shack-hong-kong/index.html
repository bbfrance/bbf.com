Shake Shack Hong Kong: 10 Locations, Menu, Prices

Which of the city's 10 Shacks to pick for your route, what the Hong Kong-only shakes are, what a ShackBurger costs in HK$, and when each branch opens.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished November 15, 2026

Shake Shack Hong Kong has 10 restaurants, according to the chain’s official locator, checked on 6 November 2026: ifc mall, Pacific Place, Times Square, The Peak Tower, Harbour City (Ocean Terminal), ELEMENTS, Langham Place, Avenue of Stars, New Town Plaza and Citygate Outlets. If you only go to one, make it the first. The flagship sits on Podium Level 4 of ifc mall in Central, with a terrace facing Victoria Harbour, and it is open daily from 10:30am to 10:30pm. A single ShackBurger costs HK$62 on the ifc menu published by OpenRice (roughly US$8).

This guide is part of our Hong Kong burger guide. The chain’s history, from its New York hot dog cart onward, is in our Shake Shack brand profile. This is desk research, not a tasting tour. I built it from the official Hong Kong locator and menu, OpenRice and TripAdvisor listings, and the local food press. Every address, phone number and opening time below comes from shakeshack.com.hk, checked on 6 November 2026.

Shake Shack in Hong Kong: when it opened and who runs it

Shake Shack opened its first Hong Kong restaurant on 1 May 2018, on the 4th floor of ifc mall in Central, as reported by QSR Magazine and Time Out Hong Kong. A pop-up came first, on 14 April 2018.

Customers queue at the order counter of Shake Shack ifc mall, Central, in October 2018, 5 months after it opened. The menu boards on the right already have a “Hong Kong Exclusive” section. (Photo: N509FZ, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Counter\_at\_Shake\_Shack\_Hong\_Kong\_(20181005114007).jpg)

The local franchise partner is Maxim’s Caterers Limited. Shake Shack announced the deal in July 2017 with a blog post titled “Lei Ho 你好, Hong Kong!”, also covered by Hong Kong Free Press. Maxim’s also runs the chain in mainland China, which I cover in our guide to Shake Shack Shanghai locations, menu and prices.

Hong Kong was not Shake Shack’s first market in East Asia: South Korea and Japan both came first, in 2016. It was, however, the chain’s first address in Greater China.

The network grew from there. Pacific Place in Admiralty followed in October 2018, according to the food blog Ordinary Patrons. Citygate Outlets in Tung Chung opened in 2022, according to Hypebeast. The newest is Langham Place in Mong Kok. Shake Shack HK announced it for early 2026 on Instagram on 14 December 2025, as reported by A Foodie World. It is now on the official locator, but I could not confirm the exact opening date. For the wider picture of who opened where this year, see our 2026 burger chain expansion report.

Which Shake Shack in Hong Kong should you choose?

Pick ifc mall for the harbour view, The Peak Tower or Avenue of Stars if you are sightseeing, Citygate Outlets if you are near the airport, and Langham Place if you want to eat late. Here is how each one fits into a visitor’s day.

Shake Shack ifc mall: the Victoria Harbour terrace

Shake Shack ifc mall is the flagship, and according to OpenRice its outdoor terrace on Podium Level 4 looks straight across Victoria Harbour to Kowloon. It has 64 seats and serves alcohol. It also has step-free access, Wi-Fi and parking in the ifc car park. From Exit B2 of Hong Kong MTR station, it is about a 6-minute walk. This is the branch I would send a first-time visitor to. The burger is the same as at the other 9; the view is not.

The shop reopened on 26 April 2023 after a renovation marking 5 years of Shake Shack in Hong Kong, according to Marketech APAC. Inside, a mural by the artist Nisar links the chain’s original New York hot dog cart to Victoria Harbour, The Peak and Ocean Park.

1 warning on the address: TripAdvisor lists it as “8 Harbour View Street” with a different phone number. The official site and OpenRice both give 8 Finance Street and (852) 2522 5608, and that is what I use here.

Shake Shack Peak Tower: a burger at the top of the tram

Shake Shack Peak Tower is on the ground floor of The Peak Tower at 128 Peak Road, the building where the Peak Tram stops. It is open daily from 9:30am to 10pm, so you can fit it in before or after the view from Victoria Peak.

Shake Shack Harbour City and Avenue of Stars: Tsim Sha Tsui

Tsim Sha Tsui has 2 Shacks on the Kowloon waterfront. The Avenue of Stars branch is right on the Tsim Sha Tsui Promenade, open 10am to 10:30pm. The Harbour City one is on the ground floor of Ocean Terminal at 3 Canton Road, open 10:30am to 10:30pm. Either works before or after the evening light show on the harbour.

Shake Shack Citygate: the closest to Hong Kong airport

Shake Shack Citygate Outlets in Tung Chung, on Lantau, is the closest branch to Hong Kong International Airport. It opens at 8am on weekdays and 8:30am at weekends. When it opened in 2022, it launched with its own exclusive concrete (frozen custard blended with toppings), according to Hypebeast.

Early and late: Pacific Place, ELEMENTS and Langham Place

5 Shacks open at 8am on weekdays: Pacific Place, ELEMENTS, Langham Place, New Town Plaza and Citygate. Those are the ones for an early meal. Don’t expect a breakfast menu, though: the official site does not publish one. For late eating, Langham Place in Mong Kok is the only branch open until 11pm every day.

Shake Shack Hong Kong menu: burgers and sides

The Hong Kong menu is built on the chain’s US classics, made with 100% Angus beef, according to the official Hong Kong menu. The beef burgers are the ShackBurger, SmokeShack, Avocado Bacon Burger, Shack Stack and Hamburger. There are 2 vegetarian options, the Veggie Shack and the ’Shroom Burger.

On OpenRice, the same orders keep coming up from ifc reviewers: a Double ShackBurger, cheese fries and a chocolate shake. That is a fair first visit. If you want to rebuild the core burger at home, my colleague Julia’s ShackBurger recipe and her Shake Shack Avocado Bacon Burger copycat start from the chain’s own ingredient lists.

Vegetarian options at Shake Shack Hong Kong

Shake Shack Hong Kong has 2 meat-free burgers: the ’Shroom Burger, a fried portobello mushroom, and the Veggie Shack, built on 11 vegetables, according to the official menu. Add fries or onion rings and the whole order is vegetarian.

Shake Shack Hong Kong prices in HK$

A single ShackBurger costs HK$62 and a double HK$87 on the ifc menu published on OpenRice, checked on 6 November 2026. OpenRice does not date its menu, so treat these as indicative. Delivery costs more: a search snippet of the foodpanda listing for Times Square showed HK$69 for a single and HK$94 for a double. The Hong Kong dollar is pegged to the US dollar at about HK$7.8 to US$1, which puts the single at roughly US$8.

Hong Kong-only shakes and local collaborations

Shake Shack Hong Kong sells 3 shakes you will not find in the US, according to the OpenRice menu: the Yuen Yeung Shake (HK$56), the Mango Bubble Shake (HK$58) and the Pistachio Mochi Shake (HK$68). They are the most Hong Kong thing on the menu. They are also why I would spend my money on a shake here rather than on a ShackBurger, which the chain serves in more than a dozen countries.

  • Yuen Yeung Shake (HK$56): inspired by yuenyeung, the cha chaan teng drink that mixes coffee and milk tea.
  • Mango Bubble Shake (HK$58): vanilla frozen custard with mango, coconut cream, pomelo and boba pearls, modelled on the dessert mango pomelo sago. It launched in October 2023, according to Time Out Hong Kong.
  • Pistachio Mochi Shake (HK$68): made with the local mochi maker Man Fung Food, according to Marketing-Interactive.

(Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0, data from OpenRice (https://www.openrice.com/en/hongkong/menu/568104/all))

The first Hong Kong exclusive was the Milk Tea Shake, inspired by Hong Kong-style milk tea. Time Out mentions it as the predecessor of the Mango Bubble Shake.

Short local collaborations come and go. In October 2025 there was a foie gras burger with the Regent Hong Kong, reported by Time Out Hong Kong. There were also yuzu hot wings with the Hong Kong sauce brand Flagrant, reported by Marketing-Interactive. Both were limited runs and are no longer on sale, so check the official menu for this winter’s seasonal item before you go.

Shake Shack Hong Kong reviews

Reviews of Shake Shack Hong Kong are good but not glowing. On TripAdvisor, the ifc mall branch scores 3.9 out of 5 from 278 reviews and ranks No. 16 of 319 Quick Bites in Hong Kong (TripAdvisor, checked 6 November 2026).

  • What reviewers praise: the harbour view, juicy burgers, crisp fries and fast service.
  • What they criticise: uneven quality (dry or over-salted burgers), a packed dining room at weekends, high prices for fast food and a short menu.

On OpenRice, Hong Kong’s main local review platform, the ifc branch has 725 reviews and 395 likes. It carries the Food Wise Eateries label and is marked “BYOW permitted”. Shake Shack also won Best Burger at the Foodie Forks Awards in September 2025, according to A Foodie World.

When the chain arrived in 2018, the South China Morning Post’s verdict was well-priced American burgers but not worth a long wait. The launch queues are long gone, so the second half of that verdict matters less today.

Delivery, payment and practical tips

Shake Shack Hong Kong delivers through foodpanda, with listings for at least Times Square and New Town Plaza. Expect higher prices than at the counter. At ifc mall, OpenRice lists Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UnionPay, JCB, Octopus, Apple Pay, Google Pay, WeChat Pay, Alipay, PayMe and cash.

Some details I could not confirm: table reservations, an official online ordering service, listings on Deliveroo or Keeta, and accessibility or parking at branches other than ifc. I have left them out rather than guess.

Shake Shack or a local burger in Hong Kong?

My advice: go to Shake Shack for the ifc terrace and the Hong Kong-only shakes, not for the burger alone. A ShackBurger in Central follows the same recipe as 1 in New York. For a burger you can only eat here, I would pick Honbo, the local smash burger brand. Its stores and menu are in our guide to Honbo Hong Kong smash burgers.

FAQ: Shake Shack Hong Kong

How many Shake Shacks are there in Hong Kong? There are 10, according to the official locator checked on 6 November 2026: ifc mall, Pacific Place, Times Square, The Peak Tower, Harbour City, ELEMENTS, Langham Place, Avenue of Stars, New Town Plaza and Citygate Outlets. An 11th is in Macau, at The Londoner Macao.

Which Shake Shack in Hong Kong has a harbour view? Shake Shack ifc mall, at Shop 4018, Podium Level 4, 8 Finance Street, Central, has an outdoor terrace facing Victoria Harbour. On the Kowloon side, the Avenue of Stars branch sits on the Tsim Sha Tsui Promenade.

When did Shake Shack open in Hong Kong, and who runs it? The first Hong Kong Shake Shack opened at ifc mall on 1 May 2018, after a pop-up on 14 April 2018. Maxim’s Caterers runs it under a franchise announced in July 2017.

How much is a ShackBurger in Hong Kong? HK$62 for a single and HK$87 for a double on the ifc menu on OpenRice, checked on 6 November 2026. Delivery on foodpanda showed HK$69 and HK$94.

What time does Shake Shack Hong Kong open? It depends on the branch. Pacific Place, ELEMENTS, Langham Place, New Town Plaza and Citygate open at 8am on weekdays. ifc mall and Harbour City open at 10:30am daily. Langham Place closes latest, at 11pm.

Is there a Shake Shack near Hong Kong airport? Yes. Shake Shack Citygate Outlets in Tung Chung, on Lantau, is the branch closest to the airport. It opens Monday to Friday 8am–10:30pm, and weekends and public holidays 8:30am–10:30pm.

Does Shake Shack Hong Kong have vegetarian burgers? Yes, 2: the ’Shroom Burger, a fried portobello mushroom (HK$69), and the Veggie Shack, built on 11 vegetables (HK$68).

Can I pay with Octopus at Shake Shack Hong Kong? Yes, at ifc mall, according to OpenRice. Cards, cash and mobile wallets including Apple Pay, Alipay, WeChat Pay and PayMe are also accepted there.
