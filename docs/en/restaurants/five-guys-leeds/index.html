Five Guys Leeds: Trinity, White Rose and Cardigan Fields Compared

All three Five Guys restaurants in Leeds compared: addresses, late hours, halal beef at Trinity and the Duncan Street closure, checked 10 October 2026.

By Emma, Restaurants writer, United KingdomPublished October 12, 2026

Five Guys has three restaurants in Leeds: Leeds Trinity on Albion Street in the city centre, Leeds Cardigan Fields on Kirkstall Road in Burley, and Leeds White Rose in the White Rose shopping centre off Dewsbury Road. If you only remember one, make it Trinity. It’s the most central, it’s the only one serving halal beef, and from Wednesday to Saturday it’s listed as open until 00:30. The old Five Guys on Duncan Street has closed. I checked every address, phone number and service below against the official Five Guys restaurant locator on 10 October 2026.

Five Guys Leeds Trinity: the city-centre restaurant

Five Guys Leeds Trinity, officially Five Guys Burgers and Fries Leeds Trinity, is at Unit R2, Albion Street, Leeds LS1 5AY, inside the Trinity Leeds shopping centre. It opened on 29 April 2024 on the top floor of Trinity, in the unit that used to be Giraffe, according to The Leeds List and The Hoot Leeds. Trinity Leeds confirmed the opening on X the same week.

This is the Five Guys I’d send most readers to. It shares a building with Trinity Kitchen, the street-food hall whose independent traders change several times a year (more on our Leeds burger page). One trip, and you can set a chain burger against an independent one.

  • Official name: Five Guys Burgers and Fries Leeds Trinity
  • Address: Unit R2, Albion Street, Leeds LS1 5AY
  • Phone: 0113 322 8242 (+44 113 322 8242)
  • GPS: 53.796656, -1.544518
  • Services: dine-in, takeaway, Click & Collect, terrace; delivery via Just Eat, Deliveroo and Uber Eats
  • Payment: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash
  • Accessibility: wheelchair-accessible entrance, toilets and seating (Welcome to Yorkshire)
  • Shopping centre: Trinity Leeds, 27 Albion St, Leeds LS1 5AT, switchboard 0113 394 2415

Five Guys Leeds Trinity opening hours

Five Guys Leeds Trinity is listed as open from 11:00 to 23:00 on Monday, Tuesday and Sunday, and from 11:00 to 00:30 from Wednesday to Saturday. These hours come from Welcome to Yorkshire, cross-checked with an hours directory, as of 10 October 2026. The official locator loads its hours dynamically and I couldn’t read them outside a browser, so confirm on the official Trinity page before heading out after 23:00.

Five Guys Leeds closing times by day: Trinity closes at 23:00 Sunday to Tuesday and at 00:30 Wednesday to Saturday (shown as 24.5). White Rose closes at 22:00, or 22:30 on Friday and Saturday. Cardigan Fields is not shown because its hours are unconfirmed. Listed hours from Welcome to Yorkshire as of 10 October 2026 (800×450). Five Guys halal in Leeds: only Trinity

Five Guys Leeds Trinity is the only Five Guys in Leeds on the chain’s official halal list. It serves halal beef, but the restaurant isn’t fully certified by HCO, the halal certifier named on the Five Guys page. Pork bacon is still on the menu here, cooked on a separate grill with separate utensils. All of that comes from the Five Guys UK halal information page. Cardigan Fields and White Rose aren’t on the list.

The Albion Street entrance of Trinity Leeds, the shopping centre where Five Guys Leeds Trinity has traded on the top floor since 29 April 2024. Photographed on the centre’s opening day, 21 March 2013 (2304×1536). (Photo: EG Focus (Flickr, via Wikimedia Commons), CC BY 2.0)

Five Guys White Rose: shopping and the IMAX

Five Guys White Rose sits in the leisure extension of the White Rose shopping centre, at Unit R13 Leisure Extension, White Rose, Dewsbury Rd, Leeds LS11 8LU. It’s the obvious choice before or after a film. The extension, known as The Village, cost £25m, covers 65,000 sq ft and opened in 2017 with an 11-screen Cineworld IMAX and a row of restaurants including Five Guys, according to Wikipedia.

The Cineworld IMAX in The Village, the leisure extension of the White Rose Centre in Leeds that opened in 2017 and houses Five Guys Leeds White Rose (5152×3864). (Photo: Mtaylor848 (Wikimedia Commons), CC BY-SA 4.0)

It’s also the easiest Five Guys in Leeds to reach by car. The centre has several car parks, and Welcome to Yorkshire lists wheelchair-accessible parking, entrance, toilets and seating. No car? A bus station at the north end of the centre connects to Leeds city centre.

  • Official name: Five Guys Burgers and Fries Leeds White Rose
  • Address: Unit R13 Leisure Extension, White Rose, Dewsbury Rd, Leeds LS11 8LU
  • Phone: 0113 467 6152
  • GPS: 53.758093, -1.575878
  • Services: dine-in, takeaway, Click & Collect, terrace; delivery via Just Eat, Deliveroo and Uber Eats
  • Listed hours: Monday to Thursday 11:00–22:00, Friday and Saturday 11:00–22:30, Sunday 11:00–22:00 (Welcome to Yorkshire, as of 10 October 2026)
  • Cash: the official page lists cash, but Welcome to Yorkshire says cash isn’t accepted, so bring a card

Five Guys Cardigan Fields: Kirkstall Road

Five Guys Cardigan Fields is at Unit 11b, Cardigan Field Leisure Park, Kirkstall Rd, Leeds LS4 2DG, in Burley, west of the city centre. It holds the top food hygiene rating of 5 out of 5 from the Food Standards Agency, after an inspection on 21 August 2025.

I couldn’t confirm its opening hours. The only source I found was a third-party directory, and the official page didn’t display them in a readable form, so I’ve left them out. Same with parking: it’s a leisure park, but no official source confirms the car park, so check before you drive.

  • Official name: Five Guys Burgers and Fries Leeds Cardigan Fields
  • Address: Unit 11b, Cardigan Field Leisure Park, Kirkstall Rd, Leeds LS4 2DG
  • Phone: 0113 467 6144
  • GPS: 53.804772, -1.583255
  • Services: dine-in, takeaway, Click & Collect, terrace; delivery via Just Eat, Deliveroo and Uber Eats
  • Payment: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash
  • Hygiene: FSA rating 5/5, inspected 21 August 2025
  • Hours: check the official Cardigan Fields page

Five Guys Duncan Street: closed and moved to Trinity

The Five Guys on Duncan Street in Leeds is closed. It used to trade at 9/11 Duncan St, near the Corn Exchange, and the chain’s city-centre restaurant is now on the top floor of Trinity Leeds. Several websites and map listings still show Duncan Street as open, so don’t walk there.

The former Five Guys on Duncan Street, Leeds, photographed on 20 October 2016. This Grade II-listed site near the Corn Exchange has closed, and Urban Fresh Burger plans to open there next (5152×3864). (Photo: Mtaylor848 (Wikimedia Commons), CC BY-SA 4.0)

The old site has a new tenant lined up. Urban Fresh Burger, a chain founded in Doncaster in 2017, plans to open its sixth restaurant there, The Hoot Leeds reported on 28 September 2026. The building is Grade II listed. Leeds City Council granted listed building consent for the new burger restaurant on 24 September 2026, according to the Yorkshire Evening Post. No opening date has been announced.

Five Guys Leeds menu and prices

The Five Guys Leeds menu is the standard UK menu: burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches, breakfast, fries, milkshakes, drinks, toppings and kids’ meals. The only price Five Guys publishes is the kids’ meal, from £7.50, with a choice of hamburger, hot dog or grilled cheese, per the official Five Guys UK menu on 10 October 2026.

A Five Guys bacon cheeseburger with lettuce, ketchup, mustard and grilled onions, one of the topping combinations on the standard UK menu served in Leeds (1024×683). (Photo: Jay Reed (j.reed, Flickr), CC BY-SA 2.0)

I won’t print burger prices for Leeds. Delivery apps blocked my checks, and the unofficial menu sites I found contradict each other by more than a pound on the same burger. For the real price at your restaurant, open the order page at order.fiveguys.co.uk and pick Trinity, Cardigan Fields or White Rose.

What the Leeds pages do confirm is the concept. Five Guys advertises more than 250,000 possible topping combinations, fries hand-cut every day and cooked in 100% peanut oil, no freezers, and everything cooked to order. The Five Guys Rewards loyalty programme runs through Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

Five Guys Leeds reviews: what the ratings say

Five Guys White Rose holds 4.3 out of 5 on Google from 1,985 reviews, and Five Guys Leeds Trinity holds 4.2 out of 5 from 63 reviews, as shown on Welcome to Yorkshire when I checked on 10 October 2026. The listings don’t say when those figures were captured, so treat them as a snapshot.

I left out the other numbers floating around. I couldn’t reach the TripAdvisor and Yelp pages, and the only Cardigan Fields rating I found came from a search summary, not the platform itself. The Trustpilot page labelled “Leeds White Rose” shows 2.3/5, but that score appears to cover the whole Five Guys UK brand, not this restaurant.

My methodology: I only quote a rating when I can name its source and the date I saw it. I don’t average scores across platforms, and I don’t attribute a brand-wide score to a single restaurant.

A short history of Five Guys, from Virginia to Leeds

Five Guys was founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell and their sons. The chain now has close to 2,000 restaurants around the world, according to its Our Story page. Its first UK restaurant opened on 4 July 2013 at 1-3 Long Acre in Covent Garden, which I covered in our guide to Five Guys London Covent Garden.

Staff preparing burgers at Five Guys Covent Garden, London, the chain’s first UK restaurant, which opened on 4 July 2013. Photographed on 10 October 2014 (2052×1533). (Photo: Tjeerd Wiersma (Flickr, via Wikimedia Commons), CC BY 2.0)

In Leeds, the most recent change is the Trinity opening in April 2024, which took over from Duncan Street as the chain’s city-centre address. I couldn’t find opening dates for Cardigan Fields or Duncan Street, so I can’t tell you which was the first Five Guys in Leeds.

Frequently asked questions about Five Guys Leeds

How many Five Guys are there in Leeds?

There are three Five Guys restaurants in Leeds: Leeds Trinity (Unit R2, Albion Street, LS1 5AY), Leeds Cardigan Fields (Unit 11b, Cardigan Field Leisure Park, Kirkstall Rd, LS4 2DG) and Leeds White Rose (Unit R13 Leisure Extension, White Rose, Dewsbury Rd, LS11 8LU), per the official locator on 10 October 2026.

Is Five Guys on Duncan Street in Leeds closed?

Yes. The Duncan Street restaurant has closed, and the city-centre Five Guys is now on the top floor of Trinity Leeds. Urban Fresh Burger plans to open in the old unit, with no date announced as of 28 September 2026.

Which Five Guys in Leeds is open latest?

Five Guys Leeds Trinity is open latest, listed until 00:30 from Wednesday to Saturday and until 23:00 on other days, as of 10 October 2026. White Rose is listed as closing at 22:00, or 22:30 on Friday and Saturday.

Is Five Guys Leeds halal?

Only Five Guys Leeds Trinity serves halal beef. It isn’t fully HCO-certified, and pork bacon is cooked on a separate grill with separate utensils. Cardigan Fields and White Rose aren’t on the official halal list.

How much does a meal at Five Guys Leeds cost?

The only official price is the kids’ meal, from £7.50. Five Guys doesn’t publish per-restaurant burger prices, so check the live menu on order.fiveguys.co.uk for the Leeds restaurant you choose.

Can I get Five Guys delivered in Leeds?

Yes. All three Leeds restaurants deliver through Just Eat, Deliveroo and Uber Eats, and all three offer Click & Collect.

Is there parking at Five Guys White Rose or Cardigan Fields?

White Rose has several car parks and wheelchair-accessible parking, per Wikipedia and Welcome to Yorkshire. No official source confirms parking at Cardigan Fields, so check before you drive.

Does Five Guys take cash in Leeds?

Trinity and Cardigan Fields accept cash according to their official pages. For White Rose, the official page says yes but Welcome to Yorkshire says no, so bring a card.

Can I book a table at Five Guys Leeds?

No official Five Guys page for Leeds lists table booking. Click & Collect is the way to skip the wait.

Is Five Guys safe for people with a peanut allergy?

Five Guys cooks its fries in 100% peanut oil, according to the official Leeds pages. If you have a peanut allergy, read the official Five Guys allergen guide and speak to staff before you order.

Does Five Guys Leeds have vegetarian options?

The UK menu includes a grilled cheese, offered in the kids’ meal, but I couldn’t confirm the full vegetarian range for Leeds. Check the official allergen and menu guides before you go.

How many free toppings can you add at Five Guys?

Five Guys advertises more than 250,000 possible topping combinations. I couldn’t confirm the exact number of free toppings from an official source on 10 October 2026, so ask at the counter.
