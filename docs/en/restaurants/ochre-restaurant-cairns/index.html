Ochre Restaurant Cairns: Burgers and Bush Tucker

Ochre on the Cairns marina: chef Craig Squire's bush tucker menu, its two lunch burgers (AUD 30–32), hours, address and the Nick Holloway mix-up.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished October 29, 2026

Ochre, on the Cairns marina, is a native-produce restaurant first and a burger stop second. Chef Craig Squire opened it in 1994 as Red Ochre Grill and still owns it. Today it sits on the Harbour Lights boardwalk at 6/1 Marlin Parade, open Monday to Saturday for lunch, tapas and dinner. Burgers are lunch only: on the Winter 2026 menu, the Ultimate Aussie Burger costs AUD 32 and the FNQ Burger, made with barramundi, AUD 30. I checked every price, address and hour below against Ochre’s own website and menus on 21 October 2026.

One correction before going further, because it keeps circulating online: Nick Holloway does not cook at Ochre. He runs Nu Nu in Palm Cove. Ochre is Craig Squire’s.

Who owns Ochre? Chef Craig Squire

Ochre was founded by chef Craig Squire, and he still owns it more than 30 years later. His official bio traces a career that runs from South Australia to London and Austria before Cairns.

Squire finished a four-year apprenticeship at the Adelaide Festival Centre (Regency Park) in 1984. A year later, aged 21, he opened Gaby’s Restaurant on Kangaroo Island. Then came London, at Kensington Place among others, and Austria.

Back home, he opened Red Ochre Grill in Adelaide in 1993, then Red Ochre Grill in Cairns in 1994. Sources disagree on where he was born. They all agree that he came from South Australia. Source: Ochre, About Chef Craig.

The team behind Ochre today

Craig Squire shares the business with Carley Elsum, whom Ochre’s website names as Managing Director since 2010 and Cairns Local News describes as an owner. In its November 2024 report, Cairns Local News also named head chef Harrison Wall, sous chef Mikayla Stirling and restaurant manager Jamille Roberts. That line-up is two years old, and I could not confirm it is unchanged. Source: Cairns Local News, 7 November 2024.

From Red Ochre Grill to the marina: Ochre’s timeline

Ochre has had two names and two addresses: it opened as Red Ochre Grill on Shields Street in 1994, became Ochre Restaurant in 2008 and moved to the Harbour Lights boardwalk in July 2016.

  • 1984: Craig Squire completes his apprenticeship at the Adelaide Festival Centre.
  • 1985: he opens Gaby’s Restaurant on Kangaroo Island.
  • 1993: Red Ochre Grill opens in Adelaide.
  • 1994: Red Ochre Grill opens in Cairns, on Shields Street.
  • 2008: the Cairns restaurant is renamed Ochre Restaurant.
  • 2010: Carley Elsum becomes Managing Director, per Ochre’s website.
  • July 2016: after 22 years on Shields Street, Ochre moves to a purpose-built venue on the Harbour Lights boardwalk.

Bush tucker restaurant in Cairns: what Ochre cooks

Ochre is the Cairns restaurant to book if you want bush tucker, the native foods of Australia, on a modern plate. Its menus are headed ‘Guaranteed Local Produce’, and TripExpert credits it with using more than 30 native foods.

Bush tucker meats in Australia: a home-cooked plate of emu, crocodile, kangaroo, buffalo and beef. Illustrative photo, not taken at Ochre. (Photo: Terrazzo, CC BY-SA 2.0, https://www.flickr.com/photos/8249050@N02/6286224689)

The native signatures on the Winter 2026 menus are hard to miss. Kangaroo appears twice. Crocodile goes into a laksa. Wattleseed, quandong, finger lime, pepper leaf, macadamia and Ooray plum run through the sauces and desserts. Seafood is marked A (Australian), M (mixed) or I (imported), so you know exactly where your fish comes from.

The Ultimate Aussie Burger

The Ultimate Aussie Burger is Ochre’s beef burger, and the beetroot is what makes it Australian. It stacks premium beef mince, bacon, cheddar, pickles and salad in a milk bun, with a pepper leaf aioli as the native touch. Onion rings and spiced fries come on the side. Ochre does not say where the patty’s beef comes from, so I won’t either: the ‘Local Tablelands Beef’ heading on the menu covers the steaks only.

The FNQ Burger: a barramundi burger in Cairns

The FNQ Burger is a barramundi burger named after Far North Queensland, and it is the one I would order. My reason is simple: of the two burgers, it is the only one whose main ingredient has its origin printed on the menu. The fish is marked A for Australian, and you choose it grilled or in tempura. It comes on a brioche bun with Asian slaw, cucumber and a finger lime mayo. Finger lime is the native citrus whose pulp bursts like tiny beads of lime. The burger is dairy-free and, at AUD 30, AUD 2 cheaper than the beef one.

Is Ochre one of the best burgers in Cairns?

No, not in the sense a burger ranking means it. Ochre is a native-produce restaurant with two well-built lunch burgers. Go for a burger that tastes of the region, not for the cheapest or the biggest in town.

If the burger itself is the goal, Cairns has a national award winner. In 2024, Edge Hill Butchery won ‘best burger’ at the Australian Meat Industry Council national awards with a lamb and halloumi burger, against more than 140 entries. Source: Cairns Local News, March 2024. For more venues across the city, see our Cairns burger guide.

Waterfront restaurant at the Cairns marina: awards and reputation

Ochre’s reputation rests on its wine list and its tourism awards as much as on its food. Its official list includes Gold at the Queensland Tourism Awards (Tourism Restaurants and Catering Services, 2016) and Silver in 2015.

  • Wine List of the Year Glass Winner: 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024.
  • Three Restaurant and Catering Wine List awards: 2018.
  • TripExpert Expert’s Choice: 2024.
  • Tripadvisor Travellers’ Choice: 2023, 2024 and 2025.

Source: Ochre, Awards and Achievements; the 2024 awards are confirmed by Cairns Local News. TripExpert gives Ochre a score of 92 from four expert reviewers, including Frommer’s, Lonely Planet and Insight Guides, and places it in the top 1% of Cairns restaurants. Source: TripExpert.

Nick Holloway and Nu Nu: the Palm Cove correction

Nick Holloway has no verified link to Ochre: he is executive chef and co-owner of Nu Nu Restaurant in Palm Cove, a beach village north of Cairns. Nu Nu’s team page does not mention Ochre.

Nu Nu opened in 2004, and Holloway co-owns it with Jason Rowbottom. He also runs Numi Ice Creamery on the Palm Cove Esplanade and Nu Nu Events and Catering. He trained at Stella and Blake’s, spent four years at Pearl in Richmond, Melbourne, and has twice been a guest chef on MasterChef Australia. Source: Nu Nu team page.

Nu Nu was Gourmet Traveller’s Regional Restaurant of the Year in 2008. It is a separate restaurant with its own story, and worth the drive if you are staying up the coast.

FAQ: Ochre restaurant Cairns

Who owns Ochre restaurant in Cairns?

Chef Craig Squire founded Ochre in 1994 as Red Ochre Grill and still owns it. Cairns Local News also names Carley Elsum as an owner; Ochre’s site lists her as Managing Director since 2010.

Is Nick Holloway the chef at Ochre?

No. Nick Holloway is executive chef and co-owner of Nu Nu in Palm Cove. Ochre is Craig Squire’s restaurant.

Does Ochre Cairns serve burgers, and how much do they cost?

Yes, at lunch only. On the Winter 2026 menu, the Ultimate Aussie Burger is AUD 32 and the FNQ Burger is AUD 30, both with fries. The dinner menu has no burger.

What is the FNQ Burger at Ochre?

It is a barramundi burger: Australian barramundi, grilled or tempura, on a brioche bun with Asian slaw, cucumber, finger lime mayo and spiced fries. It is dairy-free.

Where is Ochre restaurant, and is it open on Sundays?

Ochre is at 6/1 Marlin Parade, Cairns QLD 4870, on the Harbour Lights boardwalk at the marina. It opens Monday to Saturday and is closed on Sundays.

Can you eat kangaroo and crocodile at Ochre?

Yes. The Winter 2026 menus list kangaroo satays with macadamia sauce (AUD 18), chargrilled kangaroo sirloin with quandong chilli sauce (AUD 49) and crocodile laksa (AUD 29).

What is bush tucker, and which native ingredients does Ochre use?

Bush tucker means the native foods of Australia. Ochre’s current menus use wattleseed, quandong, finger lime, pepper leaf, macadamia and Ooray plum, alongside kangaroo and crocodile.

Is there a lunch deal at Ochre?

Yes. The Lunch 2 Course with a glass and the Lunch Share Platter (three dishes, fries and a drink, minimum two guests) each cost AUD 52 per person. Ochre takes bookings through its contact page.

Is Ochre still on Shields Street?

No. Ochre spent 22 years on Shields Street, then moved to the Harbour Lights boardwalk in July 2016.
