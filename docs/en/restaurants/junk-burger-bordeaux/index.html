Junk Burger Bordeaux: Menu, Founders and Address

Who is behind the smash burger chain at 7 rue Judaïque near Gambetta, what it serves, and where it fits in Bordeaux's burger scene.

By Thomas, Restaurants writer, FrancePublished November 26, 2026

Junk Burger Bordeaux is the Bordeaux branch of Junk, the French smash burger chain, at 7 rue Judaïque, 33000 Bordeaux, a short walk from Place Gambetta. It opens every day from 12:00 to 14:30 and from 19:00 to 22:30 (official locations page, checked 16 November 2026). The menu is one burger in five sizes, from S (one patty) to XXL (five patties), plus a Truffle and a Veggie. Junk was founded in Paris by Majed Mansour and Wissem Ben Ammar. Mansour is a co-founder, not the chef here: no source puts him in the Bordeaux kitchen.

I haven’t eaten at Junk Bordeaux yet, so there is no score on this page. It is a profile built from the brand’s own pages, the French trade press, directories and review aggregators, all checked on 16 November 2026, with each source named next to the fact it supports. Junk sits at number 6 in my best burgers in Bordeaux ranking, and this page tells the story behind that pick. My view, from the menu alone: one burger in five sizes is the right call for a smash specialist, and the M, with two patties, is where I’d start.

Who founded Junk, and is Majed Mansour the chef?

Junk was founded by two Franco-Tunisian friends and business partners, Majed Mansour and Wissem Ben Ammar (Arab News, 28 September 2025; Restaurant Online, 4 October 2024). Majed Mansour is a co-founder of the brand, not the chef or the manager of the Bordeaux branch.

I set out to write a chef profile of Majed Mansour at Junk Bordeaux. The sources won’t support one. Mansour appears everywhere as a co-founder and entrepreneur, never as a chef. Only one source calls anyone a chef: the Dubai food site nogarlicnoonions (1 October 2025) describes Wissem Ben Ammar as a ‘self-taught chef’ who drives the recipes and flavour. That’s one source, and Junk has never said publicly who does what in the kitchen.

I found no source that places Mansour in Bordeaux, at the opening or in the day-to-day running of the branch. I also found no name for the person who cooks or manages Junk Bordeaux, so I give none.

Majed Mansour’s smash burger philosophy

Majed Mansour sums up the brand in one line: ‘Junk is aimed first and foremost at smash burger purists, with few ingredients and maximum flavour’ (Zepros, 10 April 2024, my translation). The two founders put it even more simply to Arab News in 2025: ‘At Junk, there aren’t dozens of options: just one burger, available in different patty sizes, with fries and our signature panko.’

From couscous bar to smash burger chain

Before Junk, Mansour and Ben Ammar ran Graine, a couscous bar brand in Paris founded around 2013. They changed course during Covid and launched Junk in Paris in 2021 as a delivery-only brand. The first restaurant opened in October 2022 at 114 rue Montmartre (Restaurant Online, 2024; Zepros, 2024). I tell the Paris side of the story in full in my guide to Junk smash burgers in Paris.

When did Junk open in Bordeaux, and what was there before?

Junk Bordeaux opened by early October 2023, at the address that directories still list for Upper Burger. No press article gives an exact opening date, so I can only bracket it with the two sources I have.

  • About 4 October 2023: Bordeaux food creator @bordeauxbymargot posts on TikTok about a ‘new smash burger restaurant in Bordeaux, JUNK’, at 7 rue Judaïque, ‘next to Gambetta and the Sherlock’. The post is marked as an invitation. It is a single social media source.
  • 10 April 2024: Zepros counts seven Junk restaurants, three in the Paris region (Beaugrenelle, rue Montmartre, Neuilly) and four in the regions: Lille, Lyon, Bordeaux and Strasbourg.

Before Junk, the address belonged to Upper Burger. Mapstr and Sluurpy describe Upper Burger at 7 rue Judaïque as founded in 2020 and serving smash and truffle burgers, and both pages still list it there. I could not find out whether Upper Burger closed, moved or handed over its lease, or when. What is certain is that Junk now occupies 7 rue Judaïque.

Junk Bordeaux menu: what S, M, L, XL and XXL mean

At Junk, the letter tells you how many smashed beef patties are in the burger: S has 1, M has 2, L has 3, XL has 4 and XXL has 5 (official menu, checked 16 November 2026). Every size comes with the same toppings, so you only choose how much beef you want.

Truffle, Veggie and extras

Besides the five sizes, Junk serves two other burgers. The Truffle has two patties, house-fried onions and a house truffle sauce. It began as a limited edition and became a permanent bestseller, according to the brand. The Veggie uses a plant-based smashed patty.

  • Extras: pickles, crispy onions, jalapeños
  • Sides: panko fried chicken, double-cooked fries
  • Desserts: Puffy cookies in four flavours: milk chocolate; white chocolate and macadamia; double chocolate and peanut butter; double chocolate and toasted hazelnut

Junk burger sizes explained: S has 1 smashed beef patty, M 2, L 3, XL 4 and XXL 5, all with brioche, American cheese and Junk sauce (official menu, 16 Nov 2026) (Chart: Burger Breton, data from junkburgers.com/menu (checked 16 Nov 2026))

What beef does Junk use?

Junk uses French beef from Charolais and Limousin cattle, smashed and seared to order, in a French brioche bun toasted to order with premium American cheese (junkburgers.com, checked 16 November 2026). One Dubai article mentions ‘premium wagyu beef’, but that contradicts the brand’s own site, so I stick to the official sourcing.

What is a smash burger?

A smash burger is a ball of ground beef pressed flat on a very hot griddle, so the thin patty browns hard and gets crisp edges. Junk stacks those thin patties rather than serving one thick one. To try the technique at home, follow Julia’s Shackburger copycat recipe or Alice’s Sonic All-American Bacon Smasher copycat.

How much does a burger cost at Junk Bordeaux?

I could not verify current prices at Junk Bordeaux, because the official menu shows none. The estimates I found are undated or not specific to Bordeaux, so read them as rough guides only.

  • Restaurant Guru: €10–20 per person (checked 16 November 2026).
  • Bordeaux Travel Guide: the M costs under €10 (undated).
  • La Reco guide: about €15 per meal on average (undated).
  • Zepros, nationwide: the average ticket across the chain was €15 dine-in and €26 for delivery (10 April 2024).

For a firm price, check the click-and-collect or delivery app before you go. I don’t carry over the 2022 Paris prices, which may not match Bordeaux today.

Junk’s place in Bordeaux’s burger scene

Junk Bordeaux is the only smash burger specialist chain in my Bordeaux top 10. Google gives it 4.8/5 from 779 reviews, as relayed by Restaurant Guru on 16 November 2026, and Restaurant Guru ranks it #180 of 2,846 Bordeaux restaurants. Those are aggregator figures, not my verdict.

Junk is still missing from the local editorial lists. Le Bonbon Bordeaux’s top burgers list (28 October 2024) names Kokomo, Edmond, Les Burgers de Colette, Big Fernand and Le Camion qui fume, but not Junk. I found no Sud Ouest, Le Bonbon or Bordeaux Secret article about the branch. With 779 Google reviews behind it, I read that as a gap in the local press, not a sign that Bordeaux hasn’t found the place.

How big is the Junk chain?

On 16 November 2026, Junk’s official site lists 24 restaurants: 20 in France, 3 in the United Kingdom and 1 in the United Arab Emirates. In France, 12 are in Paris, with the others in Bordeaux, Lille, Lyon, Nantes, Neuilly-sur-Seine, Nice, Strasbourg and Toulouse.

Junk Burger Bordeaux FAQ

Who founded Junk Burger, and is Majed Mansour the chef of Junk Bordeaux?

Majed Mansour and Wissem Ben Ammar founded Junk. Mansour is a co-founder, not the chef of Junk Bordeaux, and no source ties him to its kitchen. Only one source, nogarlicnoonions (2025), calls Ben Ammar a self-taught chef.

Where is Junk in Bordeaux, and what are its opening hours?

Junk is at 7 rue Judaïque, 33000 Bordeaux, near Place Gambetta. It opens Monday to Sunday from 12:00 to 14:30 and from 19:00 to 22:30 (official site, checked 16 November 2026).

When did Junk open in Bordeaux, and what was there before?

Junk Bordeaux was open by early October 2023, according to a local creator’s TikTok post. It occupies 7 rue Judaïque, the address that directories still list for Upper Burger, a smash and truffle burger spot founded in 2020.

What do the S, M, L, XL and XXL sizes mean at Junk?

The letter gives the number of beef patties: S has 1, M has 2, L has 3, XL has 4 and XXL has 5. Every size has a brioche bun, American cheese and the Junk sauce.

How much does a burger cost at Junk Bordeaux?

Current Bordeaux prices are not verified. Restaurant Guru gives €10–20 per person, and Bordeaux Travel Guide says the M costs under €10, but that figure is undated. Check the app or delivery platform before you go.

Is Junk Bordeaux halal?

It’s not confirmed. Junk’s official site says nothing about halal, and I found no certifying body, so ask the restaurant directly.

Is there a vegetarian burger at Junk Bordeaux?

Yes. The Veggie uses a plant-based smashed patty, according to the official menu. I could not confirm that the whole burger is vegan, so ask about the cheese and sauce if that matters to you.

Does Junk Bordeaux deliver?

Yes. Junk Bordeaux offers dine-in, takeaway and delivery, and Deliveroo lists it as ‘JUNK’ for Bordeaux hypercentre.

Where can I eat the best smash burger in Bordeaux?

Junk at 7 rue Judaïque is the only smash burger specialist chain in my Bordeaux top 10. For the full ranking, with prices and other smash options, read my best burger in Bordeaux guide.
