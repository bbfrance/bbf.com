Whataburger Dallas: 24-Hour Spots, Airports, Menu

All 21 Dallas Whataburgers checked on the official locator: which one keeps its dining room open at 3 a.m., and where to eat at Love Field or DFW.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished October 24, 2026

If you want a Whataburger in Dallas at any hour, go to No. 728 Haskell & Capitol, 2428 N Haskell Ave. It is the only central Dallas location where the dining room and the drive-thru both stay open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The official locator lists 21 Whataburgers under Dallas, two of them in the DFW airport ZIP code. Flying out? Love Field has a dine-in Whataburger inside the terminal, and DFW has one in Terminal D at gate D17. I checked every address, phone number and hour below against the official Whataburger locator on October 20, 2026.

Best Whataburger in Dallas: No. 728 Haskell & Capitol

No. 728 at 2428 N Haskell Ave is my pick for one reason: you can sit down inside at any hour. Greenville Ave and Lemmon Ave, the two other central locations I compared, close their dining rooms at midnight and serve only through the drive-thru until 6 a.m. Haskell keeps both open around the clock. That matters more than it sounds. Arrive by rideshare after a red-eye, or on foot after a night out, and you have no car window to order from. At 3 a.m., a dining room that is still open is the only way you get a burger.

Address, phone and hours of No. 728

Whataburger No. 728 is at 2428 N Haskell Ave, Dallas, TX 75204, phone (214) 821-0293, and its dining room, drive-thru and delivery run 24/7. The official location page lists these services:

  • Dining room: open 24 hours, 7 days a week.
  • Drive-thru: open 24 hours, 7 days a week.
  • Delivery: 24 hours.
  • Curbside pickup: 7 a.m. to midnight every day.
  • Online ordering: resumes at 6 a.m.; you can schedule an order outside those hours.

Source: official No. 728 location page, checked October 20, 2026. The page says nothing about parking, accessibility or the nearest DART stop, and I won’t guess. Aggregators put it at the edge of Uptown and Old East Dallas, but I found no map source that settles the neighborhood.

What the reviews say about Haskell

Haskell is one of the most reviewed Whataburgers in Dallas: 259 reviews on Yelp as of July 2026. Yelp blocked my access to the star rating itself, and I am not going to print a number I could not read. If speed of service matters to you, skim the latest Yelp reviews before you go.

Alternatives: Greenville Ave and Lemmon Ave

No. 559 on Greenville Ave and No. 176 on Lemmon Ave are my second choices. Their drive-thrus run 24/7, but their dining rooms close at midnight. After 12 a.m. you eat in the car, or you drive to Haskell.

No. 559 Greenville & Caruth Haven

No. 559 is on Greenville Avenue at Caruth Haven Lane, well north of the Lower Greenville bar strip, so it is not a walk from those bars.

Yelp shows 156 reviews for this location (August 2026). Source for hours: official No. 559 location page, checked October 20, 2026.

No. 176 Lemmon & Versailles

No. 176 at 5500 Lemmon Ave is the closest of the three to Love Field, and its hours match Greenville’s. It is the one I would stop at on the way to a flight.

Whataburger at Dallas airports: Love Field and DFW

Yes, you can eat Whataburger before a flight in Dallas. There is one inside the Love Field terminal and one at DFW Terminal D, gate D17. Both are inside the terminals, so neither has a drive-thru, and the D17 counter sits at a gate, past security.

Whataburger Dallas Love Field (No. 1039)

The Love Field Whataburger is dine-in only, inside the airport at 8008 Cedar Springs Road, Dallas, TX 75235, phone (214) 654-0914. Its official store page says 5 a.m. to 9 p.m., Monday through Sunday. The summary on the locator’s Dallas list, though, says ‘closed weekends’. One of the two is wrong and I could not tell which, so call before a Saturday or Sunday flight.

The location closed in January 2025 during terminal works and reopened on September 9, 2025, in the main food court, with ordering kiosks. Gilbert Aranza of Star Concessions told People Newspapers that travelers can now order and head to their gate ‘in under eight minutes’, compared with about 15 minutes before. It is also the most reviewed Dallas Whataburger on Yelp, with 298 reviews as of October 2026. Sources: official No. 1039 page and People Newspapers, September 10, 2025, checked October 20, 2026.

Whataburger at DFW Airport: Terminal D, gate D17 (No. 1235)

At DFW International Airport, the Whataburger listed under Dallas is in Terminal D, the international terminal, at gate D17, phone (469) 457-0690. It opened on April 23, 2022, run by franchisee Gilbert Aranza, according to CultureMap Dallas and Fox 4. The locator raises the same weekend doubt as at Love Field, so I am not publishing its hours. Call the store. Yelp lists 36 reviews (June 2026).

Terminal E and Terminal B at DFW

DFW has had a Whataburger in Terminal E since 2019, according to the same 2022 reports. It does not appear on the Dallas locator, and I could not confirm its current status. A third DFW location was planned for Terminal B, at 2141 South International Parkway: 3,102 sq ft, operated by Star Concessions, with works scheduled from December 8, 2025, to June 5, 2026, per a state filing reported by WhatNow Dallas. I could not confirm that it has opened.

No. 786 Hwy 114 & Freeport, near DFW

No. 786 at 2900 N. Airfield Drive, Dallas, TX 75261, phone (972) 574-2235, is in the airport area but outside the terminals. It is a regular roadside Whataburger with a drive-thru, so it works on the drive in, not once you are past security.

  • Drive-thru: Monday to Thursday 5 a.m. to 10 p.m., Friday and Saturday 5 a.m. to midnight, Sunday 9 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Dining room: Monday to Saturday 5 a.m. to 10 p.m., Sunday 9 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Curbside and delivery: closed

Source: official No. 786 location page, checked October 20, 2026. Yelp shows 60 reviews (August 2026).

Whataburger Dallas menu: what to order

For a first visit, order the classic Whataburger: lettuce, three tomato slices, four pickle slices, chopped onions and mustard. Mustard, not mayonnaise, is the default, and I would try it as built before customizing anything. Founder Harmon Dobson wanted a burger so big it took two hands to hold, and the classic still follows that idea. The menu also lists the Whataburger Jr., the Triple Meat, the Bacon & Cheese Whataburger, the Justaburger, the Whatachick’n and breakfast biscuits (source: Wikipedia).

The Haskell and Greenville store pages say they serve breakfast, lunch and dinner, with burgers, chicken sandwiches, biscuits and pancakes, and that you earn loyalty points through the Whataburger app or website. No official source gave me breakfast hours for Dallas, so I am not quoting any.

Whataburger history: why Texas loves it

Whataburger is a homegrown Texas chain that stays open late, and the state legislature has gone as far as honoring it. In 2001, the Texas House of Representatives declared Whataburger a ‘Texas Treasure’ in H.R. 723.

  • 1950: Harmon Dobson opens the first stand on Ayers Street in Corpus Christi on August 8.
  • 1961: the first orange-and-white A-frame opens in Odessa (No. 24).
  • 1982: the first restaurants open 24 hours a day.
  • 2009: headquarters move to San Antonio.
  • 2013: Fancy Ketchup goes on sale in bottles.
  • 2019: BDT Capital Partners becomes majority owner.
  • 2024: the 1,000th restaurant opens, in Atlanta.
  • 2025: Debbie Stroud becomes CEO on January 1, succeeding Ed Nelson (KBTX).

The official history counted ‘more than 1,130’ restaurants in 17 states in May 2025; Wikipedia gives 1,211 in 17 states as of 2026. I found no reliable source for when the first Whataburger opened in Dallas. For the bigger picture, read our history of the hamburger.

Whataburger Dallas FAQ

How many Whataburger locations are in Dallas? The official locator lists 21 Whataburgers under Dallas, TX, including two in the DFW airport ZIP code 75261 (checked October 20, 2026). Tasting Table’s April 2026 count also gives 21.

Which Whataburger in Dallas has a dining room open 24 hours? No. 728 Haskell & Capitol, 2428 N Haskell Ave, keeps both its dining room and its drive-thru open 24/7. It is the only central Dallas location that does. Greenville Ave and Lemmon Ave close their dining rooms at midnight but keep the drive-thru open all night.

Is there a Whataburger at Dallas Love Field or DFW Airport? Yes. Love Field has a dine-in Whataburger in the main food court (No. 1039), listed at 5 a.m. to 9 p.m.; call before a weekend flight. DFW has one in Terminal D at gate D17 (No. 1235). A Terminal E location has operated since 2019, and a Terminal B location was planned for 2026, but I could not confirm the current status of either.

What should I order at Whataburger the first time? The classic Whataburger: lettuce, three tomato slices, four pickles, chopped onions and mustard. Add bacon and cheese if you want the Bacon & Cheese Whataburger.

How much does a Whataburger meal cost in Dallas in 2026? I could not verify a Dallas menu price. The national Whatadeal menu launched in January 2026 offers a Bacon & Cheese Whataburger Jr. for $5, plus $2.49 for small fries and a small drink, at participating locations.

Does Whataburger deliver in Dallas? Can I order online? Yes, at many locations. Haskell, Greenville and Lemmon deliver 24/7. Online ordering at Haskell resumes at 6 a.m., and you can schedule orders outside those hours. Love Field (No. 1039) and N Airfield Dr (No. 786) do not deliver.

Does Whataburger serve breakfast in Dallas? Yes. The Dallas store pages list breakfast with biscuits and pancakes, but I could not confirm breakfast hours from an official source, so check the Whataburger app for your location.

Does Whataburger have vegetarian options? The official Dallas store pages do not document vegetarian options or allergens. Ask at the counter or check the Whataburger website before ordering.

How I checked this guide

Every address, phone number and hour in this guide comes from Whataburger’s official locator and store pages, checked on October 20, 2026. I did not rank the locations on taste. My pick rests on hours, services and location, because those facts can be checked. History, prices and airport details are cited to the official Whataburger history, Wikipedia, Newsweek, People Newspapers, CultureMap Dallas, Fox 4 and WhatNow Dallas. I left out anything I could not source, including parking, accessibility, DART stops and most star ratings. Holiday hours can differ, so check the store page before you go.
