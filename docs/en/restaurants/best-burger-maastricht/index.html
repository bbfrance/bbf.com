Best Burger in Maastricht: Le Smash, Not Brut172

Where to eat a burger in Maastricht in 2026: Le Smash on Koestraat, with dated prices. Brut172, Hans van Wolde's 2-star table, serves no burger.

By Sofia, Restaurants writer, Italy, Switzerland and the BeneluxPublished November 17, 2026

The best burger in Maastricht that I can verify as open in November 2026 is at Le Smash, Koestraat 8, in the Jekerkwartier. Its smash burgers start at €13.95 on Uber Eats (checked 11 November 2026), the beef is halal and there are Impossible vegan patties. If you came looking for Hans van Wolde’s Brut172, I have to disappoint you. It is not a burger restaurant, and it is not in Maastricht. It is a 2-Michelin-star fine-dining restaurant in Reijmerstok, a village east of the city.

I clear up that mix-up first. Then come the people behind Maastricht’s burgers, the venues from the old lists that are still open, and the reason chains have such a hard time in this city. Every price and address has a source and the date I checked it. For the wider picture, start at our Maastricht burger hub.

Best burger in Maastricht: the short answer

Le Smash on Koestraat is the Maastricht burger restaurant with the most verified current information: a confirmed address, dated prices, and halal and vegan options. Patty ’n Bun on Sint Pieterstraat is the backup. Burgerlijk, which many lists still recommend, is marked closed on HappyCow.

Is Hans van Wolde’s Brut172 a burger restaurant in Maastricht?

No. Brut172 is a creative, seasonal fine-dining restaurant with 2 Michelin stars, and neither its Michelin listing nor its press coverage mentions a burger. It is at Reijmerstokkerdorpsstraat 143 in Reijmerstok, a village in the municipality of Gulpen-Wittem, outside Maastricht (Michelin Guide, checked 11 November 2026).

The confusion makes sense. Van Wolde is the best-known chef with a Maastricht story, and Brut172 is close enough to feel like part of a weekend in the city. But if you want a burger tonight, do not drive out to Reijmerstok. Book Brut172 for a special occasion, and get your burger in town.

Brut172: 2 Michelin stars in a Limburg farmhouse

Brut172 opened in March 2020 in a converted farmhouse with 22 seats in the dining room and a 6-seat kitchen table. Set menus run from 6 to 10 courses. The site also has a butcher shop, a wine bar, a patisserie, and lodge and mansion rooms (FavorFlav; Michelin).

The red-brick Limburg farmhouse of Brut172, Hans van Wolde’s 2-Michelin-star restaurant at Reijmerstokkerdorpsstraat 143 in Reijmerstok, about 10 minutes outside Maastricht and with no burger on the menu (October 2026) (Photo: Kleon3, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Reijmerstokkerdorpsstraat\_143,\_Brut172\_(03).jpg (1945×1434))

Michelin’s example of a signature dish is seared langoustine on a roasted onion tarte Tatin, with som tam salad and tom yum sauce. Thai flavours on French pastry. That tells you the register.

2 stars in its first guide

Brut172 won 2 Michelin stars in the 2021 guide, its first, and was the only new 2-star restaurant in the Netherlands that year. The result leaked on 27 March 2021, and Van Wolde said he had counted on 1 star (NOS). Some online summaries say ‘2 stars since 2024’. That is wrong; I follow NOS.

Hans van Wolde’s real Maastricht link: Beluga

Hans van Wolde’s Maastricht restaurant was Beluga, which he founded in 1997. It was never a burger bar. Beluga started on Havenstraat and moved in 2001 to a Jo Coenen building on the Maas at Plein 1992 12. Under Van Wolde it held 1 Michelin star from 1998 to 2004 and 2 from 2005 to 2019 (Wikipedia).

Beluga, the Maastricht restaurant Hans van Wolde founded in 1997: the white Jo Coenen building on the Maas at Plein 1992 (left), next to a medieval tower in the Céramique district, photographed in 2012 when it held 2 Michelin stars (Photo: Kleon3, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beluga,\_C%C3%A9ramique,\_Maastricht.jpg (610×416))

Van Wolde left Beluga in 2019 after legal disputes with his successor (FavorFlav). The restaurant carried on without him. I have not yet verified its current chef and stars in the guide, so I leave them out. Van Wolde now holds the title of culinary ambassador of Limburg (The Best Chef Awards).

Smash burger in Maastricht: Le Smash on Koestraat

Le Smash is a Dutch smash burger chain with a Maastricht branch at Koestraat 8, 6211 HR, in the Jekerkwartier. Visit Maastricht describes the method as smashing and flipping the patty on the grill, a technique of American origin. Pressing the beef flat on a hot plate gives a thin patty with a dark, crisp crust, and that crust is the whole point of the style (Visit Maastricht).

De Limburger announced the opening in July 2024, and Visit Maastricht featured it as its ‘new kid in town’ in December 2024. I found no exact opening day.

Le Smash Maastricht prices and hours

On Uber Eats, a burger at Le Smash Maastricht costs €13.95 and the Le Special menu €21.95 (checked 11 November 2026). Delivery prices are often higher than counter prices, so treat these as a ceiling.

Is €13.95 a fair price? For a delivered smash burger in a Dutch city, it is in line with what I paid at the Rotterdam branches. I would still want the counter price before calling it good value. Uber Eats gives the hours as 12:00 to 21:30, but those are delivery hours. The dining room may keep different ones.

Halal and vegan burgers at Le Smash

Le Smash Maastricht says all its burgers are 100% halal, and it serves alcohol (Zabihah). For a vegan burger in Maastricht, there are Impossible plant-based patties (Uber Eats). No other address on this list can seat a halal eater, a vegan and a beer drinker at the same table, and for a group that settles it.

Who is behind Le Smash Maastricht

Graeme Koopman and Bram Smit founded Le Smash in Amsterdam, and within about a year it had opened in Rotterdam and Amsterdam-West (Entree Magazine). On 5 July 2024, De Limburger reported that Bram Smit, then 42, and Quirin van Engelshoven, then 39, were bringing the brand to Maastricht as its fourth location (De Limburger via Drimble).

Both men are Limburgers. Smit comes from the province, and Van Engelshoven was born in Maastricht and founded Frites uit Zuyd. Who runs the branch day to day, I don’t know yet. What matters is that a local name opened the door. In this city, that counts for a lot.

If you know Le Smash from Rotterdam, I covered its Witte de Withstraat and Markthal branches in our guide to the best burgers in Rotterdam.

Burgers in the Jekerkwartier and the old centre

Le Smash sits in the Jekerkwartier, Maastricht’s old quarter along the Jeker south of the Vrijthof, and the centre’s other burger listing is a short walk away. Patty ’n Bun, at Sint Pieterstraat 50, 6211 JP, serves burgers, loaded fries, hot wings and shakes (Visit Maastricht). Without dated prices or diet details, it stays out of my verdict for now.

Is Burgerlijk in Maastricht still open?

Probably not. HappyCow lists Burgerlijk as closed in a business update dated 5 October 2026, but no second source confirms it yet. Burgerlijk, at Rechtstraat 37 in Wyck, was called ‘the first real burger bar of the city’ in 2015 (Your Little Black Book). In 2019, iamexpat put it among the 10 best burger joints in the Netherlands for its wagyu burger (iamexpat).

That is my problem with most ‘best burger Maastricht’ results: they still send you to Burgerlijk. Until Google Maps or the Chamber of Commerce confirms its status, don’t plan an evening around it (HappyCow).

Why burger chains struggle in Maastricht

Chains often fail in Maastricht because locals stick with their own places. Entree Magazine names Schnitzelparadies, Kippenhok and Bavet among the chains that didn’t last. Yves Mullenders of De Poshoorn put it bluntly: “The Maastrichter is loyal, chauvinist and proud of their own environment” (Entree Magazine).

I think that explains how Le Smash came in: a chain, yes, but with Limburg partners and a local restaurateur’s name in the local paper. Whether it lasts is the test this city sets every newcomer. I would give it better odds than most.

Maastricht burger trivia: the first lab-grown burger

Maastricht’s best-known contribution to burger history came from a lab, not a restaurant. Mark Post of Maastricht University presented the first cultured-meat burger, tasted in London on 5 August 2013. My colleague Maria tells the full story in our history of the hamburger.

FAQ: burgers, Brut172 and Hans van Wolde in Maastricht

Is Brut172 in Maastricht? No. Brut172 is at Reijmerstokkerdorpsstraat 143 in Reijmerstok, a village in Gulpen-Wittem east of Maastricht.

Does Brut172 or Hans van Wolde serve burgers? No. Brut172 serves creative, seasonal set menus of 6 to 10 courses, and neither Michelin nor the press mentions a burger.

How many Michelin stars does Brut172 have? 2. It won both in the 2021 guide, its first, and the Michelin listing still shows 2 stars on 11 November 2026.

What happened to Beluga after Hans van Wolde left? Van Wolde left Beluga in 2019 after legal disputes with his successor, and the restaurant continued without him. Check the Michelin Guide for its current star status.

Where can I get the best burger in Maastricht? Le Smash, Koestraat 8 in the Jekerkwartier, is the open burger restaurant with the most verified details: smash burgers from €13.95 on Uber Eats (11 November 2026), halal beef and vegan patties.

Is Burgerlijk in Maastricht still open? HappyCow has listed it as closed since an update on 5 October 2026. No second source has confirmed it yet.

Where is there a smash burger in Maastricht, and how much does it cost? At Le Smash, Koestraat 8. On Uber Eats a burger costs €13.95, fries €4.50 and the Le Special menu €21.95 (checked 11 November 2026).

Are there halal or vegan burgers in Maastricht? Yes. Le Smash says all its burgers are 100% halal, and it offers Impossible vegan patties. It also serves alcohol.
