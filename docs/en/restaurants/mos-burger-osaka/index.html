MOS Burger Osaka: Best Location, Menu, Prices, Hours and Reviews

Where to eat Japan's homegrown burger chain in Osaka, what to order, what it costs after the July 2026 price rise, and when each branch opens.

By AlexPublished October 9, 2026

MOS Burger Nishi-Shinsaibashi, at 1-9-28 Nishishinsaibashi in Chuo-ku, is the MOS Burger in Osaka I’d send most visitors to. It’s central. It’s open every day from 7am to 10pm. And its reviews hold up: 4.0/5 on Google from 591 reviews. Since the price rise of July 15, 2026, the signature MOS Burger costs ¥500 and the Teriyaki Burger ¥490, tax included. Below are four more useful branches (Universal CityWalk, Abenobashi, Utsubo-Honmachi, Showa-cho), a dated menu with prices, what reviewers say, and answers to the questions travelers ask most.

How I put this MOS Burger Osaka guide together

This guide draws on official MOS sources, Japanese business press and review platforms, all checked on October 9, 2026. Let me be upfront: I haven’t eaten at the Osaka branches listed here, so the branch verdicts rest on published reviews and listings, not on my own visit. I took prices from the July 2026 price list as reported by Impress Watch and Jiji Press, because MOS’s own press release for it now returns an error. Addresses, phone numbers and opening hours come from Tabelog, Retty and Wanderlog. Every rating has a source. Anything I couldn’t verify is out, including seat counts, Wi-Fi and payment options at Nishi-Shinsaibashi.

Hours and prices change. Check the official MOS Burger store search for Osaka before you go. MOS itself notes that prices can differ at some branches (一部店舗では価格が異なります).

What is MOS Burger? A short history of Japan’s own burger chain

MOS Burger is a Japanese burger chain founded by Atsushi Sakurada. It is known for cooking burgers to order and for Japanese flavors such as teriyaki and the rice burger. A test stand opened in March 1972 near Narimasu Station in Tokyo, and Mos Food Services was incorporated on July 21, 1972 (Wikipedia, The Shashi).

MOS Burger Narimasu in Itabashi, Tokyo, near the spot where the chain’s first test stand opened in March 1972 (photo April 29, 2025). (Photo: Asanagi (Wikimedia Commons), CC0 1.0)

  • What MOS stands for: “Mountain, Ocean, Sun”. The name was fitted to the acronym after the fact: the company grew out of an earlier firm called “Merchandising Organizing System”.
  • Teriyaki Burger: MOS says it launched its teriyaki burger in 1973. MOS also calls it the first teriyaki burger, but I found no independent source to confirm that, so treat it as the chain’s own claim.
  • Rice burger: launched in 1987. The bun is replaced with two grilled rice patties.
  • Abroad: expansion began in Taiwan in 1989. MOS now operates in East Asia, Southeast Asia and Oceania, and it closed its Australian restaurants in August 2024.
  • Size in Japan: 1,308 restaurants at the end of June 2026, down from a peak of 1,321 at the end of March 2025. In its first quarter to June 2026, MOS opened 4 restaurants and closed 8. Sales rose 3.6% to ¥24.636 billion and operating profit fell 52.9% to ¥811 million (Ryutsuu News, August 13, 2026).

Burgers came to Japan from America and changed along the way. My history of the hamburger traces where the original came from.

MOS Burger vs McDonald’s in Japan: what’s different

MOS Burger cooks your food after you order, and its menu carries Japanese flavors that McDonald’s doesn’t. Fans say that’s worth the short wait. MOS also buys its raw vegetables from partner farms in Japan and logs every pesticide type and fertilizer amount they use, according to its listings on Ekiten and Tabelog.

MOS Burger at a glance
Cooking Made after you order, not held in a warming chute
Vegetables Raw vegetables from partner farms in Japan, with pesticide and fertilizer use logged
Japanese specialties Teriyaki Burger (MOS dates it to 1973), rice burger (since 1987)
Meat-free Soy Patty swap since 2015, plant-based Green Burger
Signature burger price ¥500 for the MOS Burger (from July 15, 2026)

My take: nobody goes to MOS for a bargain. You go for a burger built to order with a Japanese accent: the meat sauce, the teriyaki glaze, a bun made of rice. The McDonald’s next door has none of them.

Best MOS Burger in Osaka: Nishi-Shinsaibashi review

MOS Burger Nishi-Shinsaibashi is the MOS to pick for a first visit to Osaka. It has the deepest pool of reviews of any branch in this guide, and it sits in Minami, the shopping district where most visitors spend their days. Amerikamura, Osaka’s “American Village”, is close by, and the Midosuji subway line links the area to Umeda, Namba and Tennoji.

The green sign of a MOS Burger storefront in Chuo-ku, Osaka, the central ward that is also home to the Nishi-Shinsaibashi branch (photo April 2016, not the Nishi-Shinsaibashi branch itself). (Photo: Tjeerd Wiersma (Flickr, via Wikimedia Commons), CC BY 2.0)

MOS Burger Nishi-Shinsaibashi reviews

Reviewers rate MOS Burger Nishi-Shinsaibashi 4.0/5 on Google (591 reviews) and 4.0/5 on TripAdvisor (41 reviews), according to Wanderlog. They keep coming back to the same points:

  • Very fresh ingredients
  • Seats with charging outlets, handy on a long day of walking around Shinsaibashi
  • Attentive staff
  • An English menu
  • The matcha shake and the lemon-vanilla shake

Reviewers put a typical visit at about 25 minutes, which is slower than grab-and-go fast food. That’s the trade for a burger cooked to order.

Verdict: for a fresh, cooked-to-order stop in central Osaka, especially before the shops open, this is the MOS I’d pick.

MOS Burger near Universal Studios Japan

Yes, there is a MOS Burger right by Universal Studios Japan. It is on the first floor of Universal CityWalk Osaka, 171 m from Universal City Station, and opens every day from 7:30am to 10pm. It takes credit cards, and the average spend on Tabelog is under ¥999. The branch closed for renovation from January 29 to March 9, 2025, and was due to reopen on March 10, 2025. Its post-renovation hours come only from Tabelog, so check them on mos.jp before a park day.

MOS Burger at Universal CityWalk Osaka, the branch next to Universal Studios Japan, 171 m from Universal City Station (photo 2006, before the 2025 renovation). It opens daily at 7:30am. (Photo: Terence Ong (Wikimedia Commons), CC BY 2.5)

MOS Burger in Tennoji: Kintetsu Abenobashi

MOS Burger Kintetsu Abenobashi opens earliest of the Osaka branches I checked, at 6:45am, and closes at 10:45pm every day. You’ll find it inside the station’s east exit. It has 67 seats and a separate smoking area, and it accepts JCB, AMEX and Diners cards but not e-money. Tabelog reviewers single out the teriyaki chicken burger, the fish burger sets and the seasonal sweet potato shake. A second branch nearby is on the 3rd floor of Abeno Q’s Mall (1-6-1 Abenosuji, 06-6633-6155).

What to order at MOS Burger

On a first visit, order the MOS Burger (¥500), the Teriyaki Burger (¥490) and a rice burger. Between them, they cover everything MOS does differently.

  • MOS Burger: the signature, topped with a chili-style meat sauce. Add the ¥100 chili dip if you like heat.
  • Teriyaki Burger: the burger MOS dates to 1973. To cook one at home, try my colleague Clara’s copycat recipe for the MOS Tobikiri teriyaki burger, the egg-and-cheese version.
  • Rice burger: grilled rice patties instead of a bun, on the menu since 1987.
  • MOS Chicken (¥340): the fried chicken on the side.
  • Shakes: reviewers in Osaka recommend the matcha and lemon-vanilla shakes, and the seasonal sweet potato one.

Limited-time MOS Burger menu items

As of October 9, 2026, the official MOS menu page lists three seasonal items: Tsukimi Focaccia with golden cheese sauce (¥630), the Menchi-katsu Cheese Burger with demi-glace sauce (¥510) and the Mazeru Mitsu-imo sweet potato shake (S ¥410 / M ¥470). They rotate, so check mos.jp/menu to see what’s on when you visit.

MOS Burger vegetarian and vegan options

MOS Burger is one of the easier fast-food chains in Japan for meat-free eaters. Since 2015 you’ve been able to swap the beef patty for a Soy Patty, and the Green Burger contains no animal ingredients at all (Business Insider Japan, MOS press release). The Green Burger launched in 2021 at ¥538, with a teriyaki version at ¥580. Those prices are out of date, and I couldn’t confirm the current ones. A word of caution: a Soy Patty swap doesn’t make a burger vegan, because the sauces and bun may still contain animal products. Only the Green Burger is fully plant-based.

The MOS Green Burger Teriyaki set, the plant-based burger with no animal ingredients, photographed in October 2021, the year it launched (¥580 then; current price unconfirmed). (Photo: Tokumeigakarinoaoshima (Wikimedia Commons), CC BY-SA 4.0)

MOS Burger opening hours in Osaka: breakfast and late night

Four of the five branches in my table open by 7:30am, which makes MOS an easy breakfast in Osaka. Kintetsu Abenobashi is first, at 6:45am. Nishi-Shinsaibashi and Utsubo-Honmachi follow at 7am, Universal CityWalk at 7:30am. For a late bite, Showa-cho stays open until midnight and Utsubo-Honmachi until 11pm. One Tabelog review of Utsubo-Honmachi mentions a Morning Dog Drink Set at ¥440, but that price predates July 2026.

MOS Burger Osaka FAQ

Where is the most convenient MOS Burger in Osaka for tourists? In Shinsaibashi, go to Nishi-Shinsaibashi (1-9-28 Nishishinsaibashi, open 7am–10pm). For Universal Studios Japan, the Universal CityWalk branch is 171 m from Universal City Station. Near Tennoji, use Kintetsu Abenobashi in the station. In Umeda, there is a branch in the Osaka Ekimae Dai-2 Building.

How much does a burger cost at MOS Burger in 2026? Since July 15, 2026, the MOS Burger costs ¥500, the Teriyaki Burger ¥490 and MOS Chicken ¥340, tax included. Adding fries M and a drink M as a set costs about ¥450 more, according to a secondary source. Prices can vary by branch.

What is the difference between MOS Burger and McDonald’s in Japan? MOS cooks after you order, uses raw vegetables from partner farms in Japan, and serves Japanese specialties like the teriyaki burger and the rice burger. Its signature MOS Burger has cost ¥500 since July 15, 2026.

Why does MOS Burger take longer than other fast-food chains? Because your food is made after you order instead of being cooked in advance. It’s the price of a fresher burger.

Does MOS Burger have an English menu? Yes, reviewers of the Nishi-Shinsaibashi branch mention an English menu. I couldn’t confirm that every branch has one.

Can I pay by card at MOS Burger in Osaka? It depends on the branch. Universal CityWalk takes credit cards. Kintetsu Abenobashi takes JCB, AMEX and Diners but not e-money, and Showa-cho accepts PayPay. I couldn’t confirm the payment options at Nishi-Shinsaibashi.

Is there a MOS Burger near Universal Studios Japan? Yes. MOS Burger Universal CityWalk Osaka is on the first floor of Universal CityWalk (6-2-61 Shimaya, Konohana-ku), 171 m from Universal City Station, and opens daily from 7:30am to 10pm.

What does MOS stand for in MOS Burger? MOS stands for “Mountain, Ocean, Sun”. The name was matched to the acronym after the fact, because the company grew out of an earlier firm called “Merchandising Organizing System”.

Does MOS Burger have vegan options? Yes. The Green Burger contains no animal ingredients. You can also swap the beef for a Soy Patty, which MOS has offered since 2015, though that alone doesn’t make a burger vegan.

What time does MOS Burger open in Osaka? Most branches open between 6:45am and 7:30am: Abenobashi at 6:45am, Nishi-Shinsaibashi and Utsubo-Honmachi at 7am, Universal CityWalk at 7:30am. Showa-cho opens at 9am and stays open until midnight.
