Danny's Burgers Bristol: UK's Best Burger 2022

Who runs Danny's Burgers, where to find its weekend smash burger pop-up in St Werburgh's, and how a lockdown sideline won the UK's Best Burger in 2022.

By Emma, Restaurants writer, United KingdomPublished November 18, 2026

Danny’s Burgers is Danny Hawke’s smash burger business, and its usual home is a weekend pop-up inside Fierce & Noble Brewery, 25 Mina Road, St Werburgh’s, Bristol BS2 9TA. Hawke was named National Burger Chef of the Year in 2021. 6 months later, his Beef Chucky 2.0 won UK’s Best Burger at the National Burger Awards. Few Bristol burger businesses have a record like that.

I built this profile from the National Burger Awards results, Bristol’s local food press and venue listings, all checked on 12 November 2026. I have not yet eaten at the pop-up. Where no source confirms a detail, such as current prices or kitchen hours, I say so.

Who is behind Danny’s Burgers? Founder Danny Hawke

Danny Hawke founded Danny’s Burgers and still owns it. Every source I checked agrees: NCASS (the body that runs the National Burger Awards), Secret Bristol, Bristol24/7, 365bristol, Bristol Express and the Bristol Post.

From Grillstock to Bristol Drum Smokers

Danny’s Burgers began as a passion project after Hawke won Grillstock in 2017, according to 365bristol and Bristol24/7. Grillstock is a barbecue festival. The sources do not say which category he won.

Barbecue was his trade at the time. He ran Bristol Drum Smokers, a barbecue equipment business, and the drum smoker ended up at the heart of his most famous burger.

A lockdown sideline that grew

Danny’s Burgers launched during the Covid-19 lockdowns as a sideline to Bristol Drum Smokers. In 2021, just before Hawke took a national title, the Bristol Post described him as a Bristol BBQ enthusiast who sold burgers for fun in lockdown. No source I checked gives the launch month.

2021: National Burger Chef of the Year

Hawke was named National Burger Chef of the Year at the 2021 grand final in London on 28 September. In the same final, Danny’s Burgers finished second for best burger, behind a Glasgow business (Bristol Post, 4 October 2021; NCASS).

2022: UK’s Best Burger with the Beef Chucky 2.0

Danny’s Burgers won UK’s Best Burger at the live cook-off on 20 April 2022, and NCASS published the result the next day. It was a hard test. Each finalist cooked 3 burgers: a Signature burger, a Technical burger with set ingredients and a Meat-Free burger. The field included Bleecker, Burger & Lobster, Dirty Bones, MEATliquor and Squeezed, another Bristol business.

Some sources online credit Hawke with a 2022 chef title, but that is wrong. Burger Chef of the Year 2022 went to Oz Harborth of Zephyr Burgers in Plymouth. Hawke’s chef title is the 2021 one.

2026: back in the final with The Frenchie

Danny’s Burgers was back in the National Burger Awards final in London on 24 March 2026 with a burger called The Frenchie. It was one of 16 finalists, and one of 4 from Bristol alongside Dodo Pub Co, Mollie’s and Oowee Diner (Time Out, 13 February 2026). Honest Burgers took the 2026 title with The Honest (Verge Magazine); I cover it in my guide to the best burgers in London. Nobody has published what goes into The Frenchie yet.

What is in the Beef Chucky 2.0 burger?

The Beef Chucky 2.0 is a 50-day dry-aged beef burger topped with smoked, braised ex-dairy chuck, American cheese, beer cheese sauce, pickles, mustard, candied jalapeños and raw white onion, served in a seeded brioche bun.

Secret Bristol published the full build on 21 April 2022:

  • a 50-day dry-aged beef blend
  • dry-aged ex-dairy chuck roll, smoked for 6 hours over charcoal and hickory in a drum smoker, then braised with bone marrow and coffee stout
  • Kerrymaid American cheese
  • beer cheese sauce
  • Kühne dill pickles
  • mustard
  • candied jalapeños
  • fresh white onions
  • a seeded brioche bun

This is why I rate it among the most original award burgers of recent years. It is really 2 dishes stacked together: a fast, crusty smash patty and a slow barbecue chuck that takes hours before service. The raw onion, pickles and candied jalapeños are there to cut through all that fat and smoke. It is the 2022 winner, though, and no source confirms it is on today’s menu.

What is a Cali-style smash burger at Danny’s?

Danny’s calls its burgers Cali-style or LA-style smash burgers. The patties are thin, pressed hard onto a hot surface and made from native-bred, free-range dry-aged beef from local independent suppliers (365bristol, Bristol Express).

Visit Bristol names a bacon cheeseburger among the classics. For non-meat eaters, it lists a plant-based burger on a Moving Mountains patty with American cheese, dill pickles, fresh onion and house sauce. The weekend pop-up adds rotating weekly specials, so the board changes from 1 visit to the next.

The current core menu is not confirmed. A FirstTable listing shows a classic cheeseburger, a smoked bacon cheeseburger with Sandridge Farm bacon, a crispy chilli cheeseburger and an Oklahoma fried onion burger, but it may be out of date. Check the board or Danny’s own channels before you go.

Where to eat Danny’s Burgers in Bristol now

Danny’s Burgers usually trades inside Fierce & Noble Brewery, 25 Mina Road, St Werburgh’s, Bristol BS2 9TA, as a weekend pop-up.

Bristol24/7 gave that address in 2026, and so did 365bristol and a February 2026 Yahoo News UK report. According to Visit Bristol, Danny’s also turns up at markets and events around the city. In July 2026 it teamed up with Big Nath’s on an exclusive menu for the EatDrink24/7 launch party.

Danny’s Burgers smash burger masterclass

According to 365bristol, Danny’s Burgers runs a smash burger masterclass at Fierce & Noble on the last Sunday of each month, from 12:00 to 15:00.

You cook hands-on and hear how the business started, and 2 Fierce & Noble beers or soft drinks are included. The price is not published, and I could not confirm that the class still runs in late 2026. Book through Danny’s own channels.

Danny’s Burgers and the best burger in Bristol

Danny’s Burgers is in Bristol’s top tier, alongside Squeezed, the 2019 National Burger Awards winner at Wapping Wharf, and Burger Theory, founded in 2012 (Bristol Express).

Bristol has 2 national best-burger winners: Squeezed in 2019 and Danny’s in 2022. In my view, Danny’s is the one to pick if you want barbecue technique, because no other Bristol smash burger I know of builds a 6-hour smoke into the patty stack. For a city-wide shortlist, see our Bristol burger guide.

Hawke also works with other food businesses. With Pieminister he made a one-off pie-burger hybrid, the Victory Bap, which put Pieminister’s UK champion Mooless Moo pie together with the Beef Chucky 2.0 (Bristol24/7). He has also crowdfunded for a food truck to cater events and weddings and to tour the country, while keeping the Fierce & Noble pop-up. Whether the truck is on the road yet is not confirmed.

Danny’s is part of a wider wave of British independents that made their names on the awards circuit. Compare it with Beefy Boys in Oxford, the dry-aged burgers of Bread Meats Bread in Glasgow and the best burgers in Birmingham, including Original Patty Men. Danny’s Burgers Bristol FAQ

Who owns Danny’s Burgers in Bristol?

Danny Hawke founded Danny’s Burgers and owns it. He started it during the Covid-19 lockdowns as a sideline to his barbecue equipment business, Bristol Drum Smokers.

Where is Danny’s Burgers in Bristol and when is it open?

Danny’s usually runs a weekend pop-up inside Fierce & Noble Brewery, 25 Mina Road, St Werburgh’s, Bristol BS2 9TA. Its kitchen hours are not published. The taproom is closed on Monday and Tuesday and open Wednesday to Sunday.

Did Danny’s Burgers win the UK’s best burger?

Yes. Danny’s Burgers won UK’s Best Burger at the National Burger Awards on 20 April 2022 with the Beef Chucky 2.0. Danny Hawke was also National Burger Chef of the Year in 2021.

Does Danny’s Burgers have a permanent restaurant?

Not that I could confirm. Its usual base is the weekend pop-up at Fierce & Noble. Older listings at the Love Inn on Stokes Croft and New Bristol Brewery on Wilson Street are unverified, so treat them as past sites.

Does Danny’s Burgers have vegan or vegetarian options?

Yes. Visit Bristol lists a plant-based burger with a Moving Mountains patty, American cheese, dill pickles, fresh onion and house sauce. If you eat vegan, ask whether the cheese is plant-based.

How much does a burger at Danny’s Burgers cost?

I could not verify current prices in pounds, so none are printed here. Check the board at Fierce & Noble or Danny’s own channels for today’s prices.

Can I take a smash burger masterclass in Bristol?

Yes. Danny’s Burgers runs a masterclass on the last Sunday of each month, 12:00–15:00, with 2 drinks included. The price is not published, and I could not confirm that the class still runs in late 2026.

Is Danny’s Burgers on Deliveroo?

Deliveroo has listed Danny’s at 25 Mina Road and at the Love Inn, but I could not confirm that either listing is live in November 2026. Check the app to see what delivers to your postcode.

Sources and method

The facts in this profile come from NCASS and Secret Bristol (21 April 2022), the Bristol Post (4 October 2021), Bristol24/7, 365bristol, Visit Bristol, Bristol Express, Time Out and Yahoo News UK (February 2026), Verge Magazine and CAMRA, all checked on 12 November 2026. Danny’s official website was down when I checked. This is a profile, not a rated review: I have not scored the burgers.
