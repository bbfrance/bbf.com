Jollibee Manila: Best Branch, Menu, Prices, Hours

Jollibee Rizal Park, the 24-hour branches, the ₱185 Super Meal A and what diners complain about: a dated, sourced guide to Jollibee in Manila.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished October 21, 2026

For most visitors, the best Jollibee in Manila is Jollibee Rizal Park, on the corner of T.M. Kalaw and Ma. Orosa Street in Ermita, at the southern edge of Rizal Park. It is also listed as Jollibee Kalaw. Google shows it open daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m., rated 4.0/5 from 966 reviews. If you need food at 3 a.m., go to one of the 24-hour branches in Binondo and Tondo instead. The only official 2026 price is ₱185 for the Super Meal A: Chickenjoy, half a Jolly Spaghetti, a Yumburger, rice and a drink. Every fact below was checked on October 15, 2026, and every fact has a source.

How I built this Jollibee Manila guide

This is a desk-researched guide, not a tasting report, and I would rather say so up front. Addresses and the 24-hour branches come from Jollibee’s official store finder. Prices come from Jollibee’s own announcements, and older figures carry their year. The rating and the review themes come from the branch’s Google profile as shown on Wanderlog. If only one source confirms a phone number or opening hours, I say so in the text. Checked on October 15, 2026.

Jollibee Philippines in brief: the country’s national fast-food chain

Jollibee is the biggest fast-food chain in the Philippines, and it started in Metro Manila. In 1975, Tony Tan Caktiong’s family opened two Magnolia ice cream parlors, one in Cubao, Quezon City, and one in Quiapo, Manila (Wikipedia, FilipiKnow). In 1978, on the advice of consultant Manuel C. Lumba, the Cubao parlor became the first Jollibee.

It grew fast. By the end of 1978 there were seven restaurants in Metro Manila. The first franchise opened in 1979 on Ronquillo Street in Santa Cruz, Manila. The bee mascot came in 1980, meant to stand for Filipino optimism. Chickenjoy arrived in the 1980s, and Burger Steak followed in 1995 (Wikipedia).

Jollibee is now the flagship of a much larger group. In the second quarter of 2026, Jollibee Group ran 10,767 restaurants in 33 countries, 3,516 of them in the Philippines. Those counts include Chowking, Mang Inasal, Greenwich and the group’s other brands, not just Jollibee. Jollibee’s system-wide sales in the Philippines grew 6.6% over the quarter (Jollibee Group Q2 2026 results, September 8, 2026). The chain is big abroad too: Vietnam alone had 213 Jollibee restaurants in 2025, as our Lotteria and Burger King guide to Da Nang shows. The full figures are on our Jollibee brand profile.

Jollibee Rizal Park: the best Jollibee in Manila for visitors

Jollibee Rizal Park is my pick for visitors because of where it is and what it looks like. TheSmartLocal ranks it among the most beautiful Jollibee branches in the world: a restaurant “built like a house” with a red roof, next to the landmarks of Rizal Park (TheSmartLocal, December 24, 2021). Most Jollibees are mall counters, and this one isn’t.

The location is what sells it. Kalaw Avenue runs along the southern edge of Rizal Park, also called Luneta, and it also serves the National Museum of Natural History, Museo Pambata and the National Library of the Philippines (Wikipedia). Google puts a typical visit at about 20 minutes. That’s enough for a tray of Chickenjoy before you walk to Intramuros.

Jollibee 24 hours in Manila: branches open all night

Yes, the official store finder lists three Jollibee branches in the City of Manila as open 24 hours (jollibee.com.ph/stores).

  • 168 Phase 5: Level 1, 168 Shopping Mall Phase 5, Soler St., Barangay 293 (Binondo area).
  • Abad Santos Avenue: Barangay 204.
  • Abad Santos cor. Solis St.: Barangay 244.

The store finder filters by service: curbside pickup, delivery, dine-in, drive-thru and takeout. Watch for one trap. The “5th Avenue” and “9th Avenue” branches are in Caloocan, not the City of Manila, and the 9th Avenue cor. Rizal Ave branch closes at 8:59 p.m.

Jollibee Manila menu: what to order on a first visit

On a first visit, order what Filipinos grew up on: Chickenjoy, a Yumburger, Jolly Spaghetti and a Peach Mango Pie. The official menu has seven categories: Breakfast, Chickenjoy, Burgers, Burger Steak & Chicken Fillet, Jolly Spaghetti & Palabok, Desserts & Sweet Pies, and Beverages (jollibee.com.ph/menu).

Yumburger: the Jollibee burger

The Yumburger is Jollibee’s signature burger: a beef patty with a mayonnaise dressing, one of the chain’s first products (Wikipedia). Jollibee advertises it as “100% pure beef” (Malaya). It’s a small, sweet-leaning burger, and that’s the point. Our Manila burger guide explains how it became a national habit.

Chickenjoy: is Jollibee a burger chain or a fried chicken chain?

Both, and the chicken arguably wins. Jollibee started with burgers, but Chickenjoy, added in the 1980s, is now just as central to the menu. The Super Meal A puts chicken, rice, spaghetti and a burger on one tray. To a visitor that looks excessive. In Manila it’s a normal order.

Burger Steak, Jolly Spaghetti and the Champ

Burger Steak, a patty over rice with gravy, has been on the menu since 1995. Jolly Spaghetti shares a menu category with palabok, a Filipino noodle dish. The Champ, the bigger premium burger, came back in 2020 alongside the Bacon Champ and the Amazing Aloha Champ (SunStar). I could not confirm that it is sold in Manila branches today.

Vegetarian options at Jollibee

Vegetarians will struggle. The official menu flags no vegetarian dish, breakfast included, and I could not confirm that any side, pasta or dessert is meat-free. Ask at the counter.

Jollibee Yumburger price in the Philippines

Jollibee has not published a 2026 price for a single Yumburger. Menu aggregator sites contradict each other and are often out of date, so I left them out. The latest verified figure dates from June 2024: ₱105 for a Yumburger with fries and a drink, or ₱95 during the Jolly Meal Savers promotion. Expect to pay more today. These value meals are one front in a wider price fight that my colleague Noah tracks in his burger value wars 2026 analysis.

Jollibee delivery in Manila and how to pay

Jollibee delivers across Manila. You can call the central express line #8-7000, order on order.jollibee.com, or use GrabFood or foodpanda (Globe, Jollibee).

Online orders can be paid by GCash, credit or debit card, or cash on delivery (Jollibee Help Center). I could not confirm which payment methods each branch takes at the counter.

Is Jollibee in Manila worth it?

Yes, but go for the culture, not for a better burger than the one at home. AFAR describes Jollibee as a destination for Filipinos, not just a fast-food stop (AFAR), and that matches the evidence: a chain that serves spaghetti and rice next to its burgers and has outgrown every foreign rival at home. The Rizal Park branch adds a striking building and a central location. Go off-peak and order the Super Meal A, which gets you four classics for ₱185, then walk to the museums on Kalaw Avenue.

FAQ: Jollibee Manila

Where is the most convenient Jollibee for tourists in Manila? Jollibee Rizal Park (Kalaw), at T.M. Kalaw cor. Ma. Orosa St. in Ermita. It is on the southern edge of Rizal Park, close to the National Museum of Natural History and the National Library.

What are the opening hours of Jollibee Rizal Park? Google lists it as open daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. (checked October 15, 2026). No official source confirms the day-by-day hours.

Is there a Jollibee open 24 hours in Manila? Yes. The official store finder lists 168 Phase 5 on Soler St. and two branches on Abad Santos Avenue as open 24 hours.

How much does a Jollibee meal cost in 2026? The Super Meal A costs ₱185 at every branch, according to Jollibee’s announcement of March 16, 2026. No other official 2026 price has been published.

What should I order at Jollibee for the first time? Chickenjoy, a Yumburger and Jolly Spaghetti, which all come together in the Super Meal A, plus a Peach Mango Pie for dessert.

Where did the first Jollibee open, and can I visit it? The first Jollibee opened in 1978 in Cubao, Quezon City, in a former Magnolia ice cream parlor. The first franchise opened in 1979 on Ronquillo Street in Santa Cruz, Manila. I could not confirm whether either site still operates as a Jollibee.

Can I get Jollibee delivered in Manila? Yes. Call #8-7000, order on order.jollibee.com, or use GrabFood or foodpanda. Online orders accept GCash, cards and cash on delivery.
