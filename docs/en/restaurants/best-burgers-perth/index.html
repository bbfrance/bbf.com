Best Burgers Perth: 8 Verified Spots to Try

From Fremantle diners to Leederville smash burgers, these are the verified names to shortlist for the best burgers Perth has right now.

By AlexPublished October 1, 2026

If you want the best burgers Perth has right now, start with Short Order Burger Co in Fremantle and Perth CBD, Hoodburger in Northbridge, and Bad Love and Jus Burgers in Leederville. I built this shortlist only from verifiable 2024-2026 sources, led by the Broadsheet Perth burger guide updated 19 June 2024. If I had one lunch, I would start at Short Order in Fremantle.

The 8 picks, suburb by suburb

All eight trace to the June 2024 Broadsheet guide and September 2026 operator checks. The first six have a clear reason to go; the last two I list without inventing detail.

1. Short Order Burger Co — Fremantle and Perth CBD

Short Order Burger Co is my first stop for the best burger Perth CBD and Fremantle visitors can compare side by side. Founder Simon Kony started flipping patties in 2015 at Fremantle food hall Sunshine Harvester Works, then called The Mantle, before opening the permanent Fremantle site with a liquor licence.

The Fremantle shop works as a 50s American diner and lists a Nashville fried chicken sandwich. The Perth CBD outpost centres on a cheeseburger on a seeded milk bun, with coffee and thickshakes. If you bake at home, our homemade burger buns explains what a seeded milk bun should do.

2. Hoodburger — Northbridge, Inglewood, Ardross and Mandurah

Hoodburger is the pop-up turned local chain on this list. Brothers Matt and Tom Shaw started it as an 80s/90s fast-food love-letter pop-up.

It now lists venues in Northbridge, Inglewood, Ardross and Mandurah, plus a wine bar at Hoodburger 848. Verify your chosen store on the official site: hoodburger.com.au with Menu, Store Locator, Rewards and Catering, and official Instagram @itshoodburger.

3. Bad Love Burger Co — Leederville

Bad Love Burger Co is my answer for best smash burger Perth seekers in Leederville. It is the bricks-and-mortar store from the Bayswater Bowls Club Smashburger BBQ pop-up team.

The focus is smashed beef burgers, plus fried chicken and Philly cheesesteaks. I rank it here for technique intent: smashed, thin patties, not thick pub patties.

4. Jus Burgers — Leederville

Jus Burgers in Leederville is the local-ingredient pick. The Leederville menu features Margaret River Wagyu beef, free-range Swan Valley chicken and Amelia Park lamb in Turkish or brioche buns.

Order Wagyu if you want richness, chicken if you want lighter. The Turkish-versus-brioche bun choice changes the bite more than most toppings.

5. Royal’s Chicken & Burgers — East Victoria Park

Royal’s Chicken & Burgers is listed in East Victoria Park, with further venues in Wembley and Northbridge. The positioning is burgers plus fried chicken plus hip-hop plus local beer and boozy shakes.

Pick East Victoria Park for the original listing, Northbridge if you want the best late-night burger in Perth / Northbridge option from this set. Confirm late hours on Google before going, I could not verify 2026 hours.

6. Alfred’s Kitchen — Guildford

Alfred’s Kitchen in Guildford is claimed to be Perth’s longest-running roadside burger kitchen, in business since 1946. It serves tuckshop-style burgers with a fire-pit out front.

I include it as the historic counterpoint to the smash-burger shops. Expect simple, roadside format, not Fremantle diner or Northbridge late-night.

7. Johnny’s — Perth

Johnny’s makes the Broadsheet Best Burgers in Perth guide updated 19 June 2024, so it makes this shortlist. I found no primary-source suburb, signature, founder or price in the research brief, so I leave those blank rather than guess. Check the Broadsheet guide and the venue’s official channels before you go.

8. Varsity — Perth

Varsity is also named in that same 19 June 2024 Broadsheet guide. Without a primary source for suburb, menu standout or price, I keep it as a name to verify, not a recommendation yet. Check the Broadsheet guide and the venue’s official channels before you go.

FAQ: eating burgers in Perth

Where is the best burger in Perth right now?

For a first-time visitor, start with Short Order Burger Co in Fremantle or Perth CBD for classic cheeseburgers, then Hoodburger in Northbridge and Bad Love in Leederville for smash-style beef. They are all named in the June 2024 Broadsheet guide and traceable to operator sources in September 2026.

Which Perth burger joint has the best smash burger?

Bad Love Burger Co in Leederville is the clearest smash burger Perth match in verified sources, as the bricks-and-mortar store from the Bayswater Bowls Club Smashburger BBQ pop-up team focused on smashed beef. I make no wider best-smash claim without tasting data and 2026 reviews.

Where can I get the best burger in Fremantle?

In Fremantle, the verified pick in this guide is Short Order Burger Co, founded by Simon Kony after starting in 2015 at Sunshine Harvester Works. It runs a 50s American diner format with a Nashville fried chicken sandwich alongside burgers.

What is the best late-night burger in Perth / Northbridge?

From verified locations only, Hoodburger in Northbridge and Royal’s in Northbridge are the two Northbridge options in this guide. I could not verify 2026 late-night hours, so check the Hoodburger store locator and Google Business Profile before going.

How much does a burger cost in Perth in 2026?

I do not publish a 2026 cheapest burger Perth price here because the brief contains no dated AUD price snapshot. Perth prices are in Australian dollars (A$) with metric units. Record the menu price, date and source at visit time.

Which Perth burger restaurants are halal / vegetarian / vegan-friendly?

I do not state halal, vegetarian or vegan status for any venue here because the brief contains no primary-source claim. Ask the venue directly and check its current menu.
