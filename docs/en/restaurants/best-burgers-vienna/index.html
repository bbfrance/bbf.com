Best Burgers Vienna: 4 Verified Spots for 2026

Four Vienna burger addresses I could verify as open in 2026, with full addresses, hours and 2026 prices.

By AlexPublished October 4, 2026

Where to eat the best burger in Vienna in 2026? I verified four spots as open in 2026 on their official sites: Rinderwahn, Le Burger Mariahilfer Strasse, Die Burgermacher and Flatschers. Full address, hours and a current menu. That was my cut.

How I picked these Vienna burgers

I only kept addresses with a working official site checked on 1 October 2026, a full street address and 2026 hours. So this English edition lists four verified picks, not ten. I left out Google ratings, Burgerista, Addicted to Rock, HANS DAS GLUECK or Tom’s Burger Joint prices without verification. See my method in the best burgers in New York guide and the wider Vienna burger guide.

1. Rinderwahn

Rinderwahn runs two Vienna counters — centre and market. My default when someone wants one safe lunch first. I verified both addresses on its official site on 1 October 2026. Addresses: Weihburggasse 3, 1010 Wien and Naschmarkt 1, 1060 Wien. Weihburggasse hours are Monday to Sunday 12:00-22:30, phone +43 1 5120996. Its weekday dirty lunch deal is 14.90 EUR, Monday to Friday 12:00-15:00 excluding public holidays. Official site: Rinderwahn official site with addresses and hours.

Rinderwahn Vienna: cheeseburger with fries — best burger near Naschmarkt and city centre, weekday lunch deal (Photo: jeffreyw, CC BY 2.0)

2. Le Burger Mariahilfer Strasse

Le Burger Mariahilfer Strasse is my pick for classic thick-patty beef in Neubau. I verified its address, hours and beef claim on its official location page. Address: Mariahilfer Strasse 114, 1070 Wien, phone 01 90 59 615. It opens Sunday to Thursday 11:00-23:00 and Friday to Saturday 11:00-23:30, kitchen until 22:30/23:00. The beef is Austrian AMA beef, never frozen, ground fresh each day and hand-shaped. It scored 91 points in the Falstaff Streetfood Guide 2025. Sources: Le Burger Mariahilfer Strasse location page and Falstaff Streetfood Guide listing for Le Burger.

Le Burger Vienna Mariahilfer Strasse: thick Austrian AMA beef burger, hand-shaped fresh patty (Photo: kurmanstaff, CC BY 2.0)

3. Die Burgermacher

Die Burgermacher calls itself Vienna’s first specialised burger restaurant, opened in 2008. I kept it because its history, menu and prices are all on its official site — rare and useful. Address: Burggasse 12, 1070 Wien, phone 0699 11 58 95 99, with 30 seats. It opens Tuesday to Friday 17:00-23:00, Saturday 12:00-23:00, Sunday 12:00-22:00. Classic Beef Burger is 11.90 EUR and Fleischeslust Burger is 12.90 EUR, served on organic buns from bakery Schrott. Source: Die Burgermacher official site with history, menu and prices. Pair it with homemade burger buns if you bake the Schrott-style bun at home.

Die Burgermacher Vienna: Classic Beef Burger on organic bun — pioneer burger restaurant since 2008 (Photo: Rex Roof, CC BY 2.0)

4. Flatschers Restaurant and Bar

Flatschers is my pick for steakhouse-style burgers cooked to order. I verified its lava-grill method on its official site. Address: Kaiserstrasse 113-115, 1070 Wien, open Monday to Sunday 17:00-24:00, phone +43 1 5234268. Steaks and burgers cook on a lava-stone grill with a choice of rare, medium-rare, medium or well-done. Source: Flatschers official site with address and lava-grill concept.

Flatschers Vienna: burger patties on lava-stone grill — steakhouse cooking from rare to well-done (Photo: Valters Krontals, CC BY 2.0)

Vienna burger guide Naschmarkt: where to go

For a burger near Naschmarkt, go to Rinderwahn at Naschmarkt 1, 1060 Wien. It is the only verified pick in this guide located directly at the market.

How much does a burger cost in Vienna in 2026?

Expect about 11.90 EUR to 14.90 EUR for a verified burger in Vienna in 2026. Die Burgermacher lists 11.90 EUR and 12.90 EUR, and Rinderwahn lists a 14.90 EUR weekday lunch deal. My take: under 13.00 EUR, Die Burgermacher is the value baseline. I left out other 2026 prices I could not verify.
