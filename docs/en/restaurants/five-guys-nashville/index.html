Five Guys Nashville: 4 Locations, Prices, Menu

West End, Bellevue, Belle Meade and Melrose compared, with dated prices, what you'll really pay after Nashville's 9.25% tax, and what reviewers keep saying.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 12, 2026

Five Guys has 4 restaurants with a Nashville address: West End (2018 West End Ave.), Bellevue (7026 Highway 70 South), Belle Meade (21 White Bridge Rd) and Melrose (810 Gale Lane, Suite 102). Visiting? Go to West End. It’s the most central of the 4, on the West End Avenue corridor between Midtown and Vanderbilt, and it has by far the most reviews. A cheeseburger, regular fries and a soda there come to up to about $31 with tax, going by the menu prices listed online. I checked every address, phone number and rating below on November 2, 2026.

Five Guys is the burger chain founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, and Nashville gets the same menu as the rest of the US. I cover burger restaurants in the American South for Burger Breton, and I built this guide out of frustration. The directories that rank for “Five Guys Nashville” give you an address and a star rating. That’s it. They don’t compare the locations, they don’t date their prices, and they can’t agree on the hours. What follows is what held up when I checked it against the official store pages. For the full story of the chain, see our Five Guys brand profile.

Five Guys West End: the pick near Vanderbilt

Five Guys West End, at 2018 West End Ave., Nashville, TN 37203, is the location I’d send a visitor to. It’s the closest of the 4 to the center of town and to Vanderbilt, and no other Nashville location comes near its review count. Call 615-320-3678, or order ahead on the official store page, in the Five Guys app, or through DoorDash and Uber Eats. One warning from reviewers: parking is tight, especially if you’re driving something large.

Five Guys Bellevue (Highway 70 South)

Five Guys Bellevue, at 7026 Highway 70 South, Nashville, TN 37221, is the only Nashville location whose store page lists outdoor seating. Phone: 615-673-0568. Bring a sweater. “Always freezing” is how more than one reviewer describes the dining room, though they also say the portions make up for prices that feel a little high. WKRN’s “Neil’s Dining Dash” segment cites a health inspection score of 98 for this location.

Five Guys Belle Meade (White Bridge Rd)

Five Guys Belle Meade is at 21 White Bridge Rd, Nashville, TN 37205, phone 615-678-6025. It’s the one people search for as “Five Guys White Bridge Rd”. According to MapQuest, it takes American Express, Visa, Mastercard, Discover, Apple Pay, Android Pay and cash. It’s the only Nashville location where I could find a payment list; for the other 3, no source confirms one.

Five Guys Melrose (Gale Lane)

Five Guys Melrose, at 810 Gale Lane, Suite 102, Nashville, TN 37204, serves the Melrose area south of downtown. Phone: 615-891-2403. It took over the former Pie Five space, and the Nashville Post’s July 2021 “Permit Patrol” column put the build-out permit at $340,000. When it opened, I can’t tell you: no source I trust gives a date.

Hermitage and Madison: the rest of Davidson County

Two more Five Guys restaurants serve Davidson County outside the “Nashville” mailing address: Hermitage (4013 Lebanon Pike, Hermitage, TN 37076) and Madison (2220 Gallatin Pike N, Suite 300, Madison, TN 37115). Both list dine-in, curbside and delivery. Across Tennessee, Five Guys is in 27 cities, including Brentwood, Franklin, Hendersonville, Mt. Juliet, Murfreesboro and Smyrna around Nashville.

Five Guys Nashville hours: check before you go

Five Guys Nashville opens between 10 and 11 a.m. and closes around 10 p.m., but the exact hours vary by source and I couldn’t confirm them day by day. The official store pages load their hours in JavaScript, and the directories disagree. Birdeye and MapQuest list West End and Bellevue at 11 a.m.–10 p.m. every day, and Belle Meade at 10 a.m.–10 p.m. Wanderlog lists West End at 10 a.m.–10 p.m. Sunday to Wednesday and 10 a.m.–11 p.m. Thursday to Saturday. Going late? Check the Google listing or the store page first.

Five Guys Nashville menu: what to order

The Nashville menu is the standard US Five Guys menu: burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches, fries, shakes and drinks. I found no item exclusive to Nashville.

  • Burgers: Hamburger, Cheeseburger, Bacon Burger and Bacon Cheeseburger, each with 2 patties, plus “Little” versions with 1 patty.
  • Hot dogs: Hot Dog, Cheese Dog, Bacon Dog, Bacon Cheese Dog.
  • Sandwiches: Veggie Sandwich, Veggie Cheese, Grilled Cheese, BLT, Patty Melt.
  • Fries: Five Guys Style or Cajun, in Little, Regular or Large.
  • Shakes: a vanilla base with mix-ins.
  • Drinks: sodas and bottled water.

The store pages make the same promises in Nashville as everywhere else. The beef is fresh and never frozen, the fries are cooked twice in 100% peanut oil, the toppings are prepped by hand every morning, and you get free peanuts while you wait. The chain counts “more than 250,000 ways” to build a burger and “more than 1,000” shake combinations.

The 15 free toppings

Every topping at Five Guys is free, with no limit. There are 15: mayo, lettuce, pickles, tomatoes, grilled onions, raw onions, grilled mushrooms, jalapeño peppers, green peppers, ketchup, mustard, relish, BBQ sauce, hot sauce and A1 sauce. Burgers come plain unless you ask. Order one “all the way” and you get 8 of them: mayo, lettuce, pickles, tomatoes, grilled onions, grilled mushrooms, ketchup and mustard. The rest you name one by one (Five Guys toppings list).

Vegetarian options at Five Guys Nashville

Vegetarians have 3 choices at Five Guys Nashville: the Veggie Sandwich, the Veggie Cheese Sandwich and the Grilled Cheese. The veggie sandwiches are grilled toppings in a bun with no patty. There’s no plant-based burger on the menu.

What a meal really costs with Nashville sales tax

Nashville adds a 9.25% sales tax on restaurant food: 7% for the state of Tennessee and 2.25% for Davidson County. The menu price never includes it. Here’s what 2 typical orders come to at the West End prices above:

Order Before tax Tax (9.25%) Total
Cheeseburger, regular fries, soda $28.41 $2.63 $31.04
Little Hamburger, little fries, Dasani $19.41 $1.80 $21.21

Neither total includes a tip. Going Little on the burger and the fries and switching to water saves about $10. Or share the fries: a regular order is big, and reviewers keep calling the portions generous.

Five Guys Nashville reviews: what customers say

Five Guys Nashville scores about 4.3 out of 5 on Google at every location with enough reviews. West End leads with 4.3/5 from 886 Google reviews and 4.5/5 from 76 Tripadvisor reviews, according to Wanderlog on November 2, 2026. My method: I gathered the ratings and counts on aggregator sites, then grouped the comments that keep coming up. I didn’t average the platforms together.

Five Guys Nashville reviews: West End, Bellevue and Belle Meade all rate about 4.3/5. West End has 886 Google reviews, Bellevue 795 to 813 depending on the aggregator, and Belle Meade about 382. Melrose has no reliable figure. Data from Wanderlog and Birdeye, checked November 2, 2026 (Chart: Burger Breton, CC BY 4.0, data from wanderlog.com and reviews.birdeye.com (checked 2 November 2026))

What reviewers like:

  • Burgers they call tasty, and a fry portion most describe as generous.
  • Friendly staff and a clean dining room (West End).
  • Fast service.
  • Portions that justify the price (Bellevue).

What they criticize:

  • Burgers some find underseasoned.
  • Fries that disappoint some customers.
  • Prices that feel high for a counter burger.
  • Limited parking at West End, especially for large vehicles.
  • A dining room kept very cold at Bellevue.

My verdict: Five Guys in Nashville is Five Guys, no better and no worse. You get a consistent, generous, expensive counter burger that you build yourself. After a day on Broadway or around Vanderbilt, West End is a safe bet. But at close to $31 for one meal, I’d spend that money on a local burger. If you came to Nashville for something you can’t get at home, keep reading.

A short history of Five Guys

Jerry and Janie Murrell and their sons founded Five Guys in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia. The chain started franchising in 2002–2003. As of May 2026, it had 1,999 restaurants in 28 countries and territories, 1,517 of them in the US, according to the Five Guys 2026 media fact sheet. I couldn’t find when the first Nashville location opened. More in our Five Guys brand profile.

Five Guys Nashville FAQ

How many Five Guys are there in Nashville?

There are 4 Five Guys restaurants with a Nashville address: West End, Bellevue, Belle Meade and Melrose. Two more are in Davidson County, in Hermitage and Madison.

Which Five Guys is closest to Vanderbilt?

Five Guys West End, at 2018 West End Ave., Nashville, TN 37203, is the closest to Vanderbilt and Midtown. Phone: 615-320-3678.

Is Five Guys Nashville open late?

Not very late. Most listings show closing at 10 p.m., and Wanderlog lists West End until 11 p.m. Thursday to Saturday. Sources disagree, so check Google or the store page before you go.

How much is a meal at Five Guys in Nashville?

A cheeseburger, regular fries and a soda at West End come to $28.41 before tax and $31.04 with Nashville’s 9.25% sales tax, based on menu prices listed online on November 2, 2026, which may include a delivery markup. A Little Hamburger meal with little fries and water comes to $21.21 with tax.

Are the toppings really free at Five Guys?

Yes. All 15 toppings are free, and you can add as many as you want, from grilled onions and mushrooms to jalapeños and A1 sauce. Ordering “all the way” gets you 8 of them in one go.

Does Five Guys Nashville deliver or offer curbside pickup?

All 4 Nashville locations offer delivery and takeout. West End and Belle Meade also take orders online, in the Five Guys app, and on DoorDash and Uber Eats. The city page mentions curbside for all 4, but the individual store pages don’t, so call ahead if you’re counting on it.

Does Five Guys have vegetarian options?

Yes: the Veggie Sandwich, the Veggie Cheese Sandwich and the Grilled Cheese. None of them has a meat-free patty. They’re grilled toppings and cheese in a bun.

Is Five Guys safe for people with a peanut allergy?

Be careful. Five Guys cooks its fries in refined peanut oil and hands out free peanuts in its restaurants. If you have a peanut allergy, talk to the staff before you order.
