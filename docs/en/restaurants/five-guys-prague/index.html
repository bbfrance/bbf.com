Five Guys Prague: Address, Hours, Menu, Prices

Five Guys Prague opened on 8 December 2025 in the Máj on Národní. Address, late-night hours, menu, prices in CZK and what reviewers think.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 14, 2026

Five Guys Prague is at Národní 63/26, Nové Město, 110 00 Praha 1, on the 2nd floor of the Máj – House of Fun building. It opened on Monday 8 December 2025 and is still the only Five Guys in Czechia. Opening hours were 11:00–23:00 Monday to Thursday and 11:00–03:00 on Friday and Saturday. Bring a London-sized budget: a cheeseburger cost 275 CZK in March 2026, and a full meal about 500 CZK. I checked every fact below against the official restaurant page and the Czech press on 27 October 2026.

Five Guys Prague address: Národní 63/26, inside the Máj

Five Guys Prague is a 250 m² restaurant on the 2nd floor of the Máj – House of Fun, Národní 63/26, in Prague 1. It sits in the building’s food court, which has about 600 shared seats, according to CzechCrunch (4 December 2025). Locals search for it as “Five Guys Máj” or “Five Guys Praha”.

The Máj used to be a department store. Today it is a leisure centre of more than 17,000 m², with games, minigolf, simulators, restaurants on the 1st and 2nd floors and the Fly Vista ride 40 metres up, according to Expats.cz. In other words, Five Guys is 1 stop in a building designed to keep you there all afternoon.

I could not confirm public transport stops, parking or step-free access from a verified source. Check your route on the Prague transit planner at pid.cz before you go, and call the restaurant if you need lift access.

Five Guys Prague opening hours: late nights on Friday and Saturday

Five Guys Prague opened with hours of 11:00–23:00 Monday to Thursday and 11:00–03:00 on Friday and Saturday, according to e15.cz. Apetit gave “every day 11:00–23:00, Friday and Saturday until 03:00”, which suggests Sunday also closes at 23:00.

Five Guys Prague menu: burgers, Cajun fries and custom shakes

The Prague menu is the standard Five Guys lineup: hamburgers, cheeseburgers, bacon burgers and 1-patty “Little” versions, plus hot dogs, sandwiches, fries and build-your-own milkshakes. The official Czech page says the fries are hand-cut daily and cooked in 100% peanut oil, plain or Cajun-style. It also says nothing is frozen and claims over 250,000 topping combinations.

Prague Daily News reported an open kitchen with no freezers and fresh ingredients delivered every day. In October 2026 the official page also listed a limited-edition shake, the “Cherry Cherry Boom Boom”, made with amarena cherries and vanilla.

Five Guys Prague reviews: great fries, American prices

Reviewers of Five Guys Prague praise the fries and burgers but complain about the prices and the cramped space. The most detailed review is from Newstream.cz, published in March 2026. I have not recorded dated Google or Tripadvisor ratings, so this guide quotes no star score.

What reviewers liked

  • 2 thin patties with a caramelised crust.
  • A soft, slightly sweet bun.
  • Fries cut from fresh potatoes, in generous portions.
  • Fast service: about a 10-minute wait on a Sunday afternoon.

What reviewers criticised

  • American-level prices, while Czech purchasing power is about half as high.
  • A cramped space at peak hours.
  • No restroom in the restaurant itself: the food court toilets cost 20 CZK.
  • Potatoes imported from Germany.

My verdict: worth it for fans, not for budget travellers

Five Guys Prague is worth a stop if you already love the chain’s fries, or want a burger after midnight on a Saturday. It is not a cheap meal by Prague standards. About 500 CZK for a cheeseburger, small fries and a drink is a premium price in Czechia, and the complaints about price and space are consistent across reviews. Demand hasn’t suffered. Five Guys says the branch served more than 10,000 customers in its first week, and Forbes.cz reported that it ranked among the chain’s 10 highest-grossing restaurants worldwide, ahead of branches in central London, Milan, Paris and Dubai. My advice: go mid-afternoon on a weekday, not on a Friday evening. I give no rating because I have not yet reviewed it in person against our usual scoring.

How Five Guys came to Prague

Five Guys reached Prague after almost 2 years of delays. The opening was planned for 2024, then spring 2025, then September 2025, held back by construction work and the chain’s quality requirements, according to Expats.cz and Deník. It finally opened on 8 December 2025.

The franchisee is FG Czech s.r.o., owned by brothers Martin and Václav Klán of the family-run FLKR Group, with Martin Levec as operations director (e15.cz). In May 2026 the operator was aiming for 2 to 3 restaurants in Czechia by late 2026 or early 2027, including 1 in Brno, and 8 to 12 in the long run (Tradeinfo.cz). Those branches are announced, not open.

The chain itself was founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell and runs about 1,999 restaurants in 28 countries. Read its full history, figures and reviews on our Five Guys brand profile.

Five Guys Prague FAQ

Where is Five Guys in Prague? Five Guys Prague is at Národní 63/26, 110 00 Praha 1, on the 2nd floor of the Máj – House of Fun food court in Nové Město.

Is Five Guys Prague open late at weekends? Yes. At opening it served until 03:00 on Friday and Saturday, and until 23:00 Monday to Thursday. Sunday hours are not confirmed, so call +420 727 932 396 before you go.

How much is a burger at Five Guys Prague? The cheapest burger was 175 CZK at opening. A cheeseburger cost 275 CZK on 7 March 2026, and a cheeseburger meal with fries and a drink came to about 500 CZK.

Is Five Guys more expensive than McDonald’s in Prague? Yes. At opening, e15.cz reported that Five Guys prices would top both McDonald’s and KFC in Czechia.

Is there more than 1 Five Guys in Prague or Czechia? No. As of October 2026, Národní 63/26 is the only Five Guys in Czechia. More branches, including 1 in Brno, are announced for late 2026 or early 2027 but none is confirmed open.

Does Five Guys Prague deliver? Not officially yet. The official page lists delivery as “coming soon” and offers dine-in, takeaway and Click & Collect.

Can I pay in euros at Five Guys Prague? The official page lists American Express, Maestro, Mastercard and Visa and does not mention euros. Plan to pay by card in Czech koruna.

Does Five Guys Prague have vegetarian options? Not confirmed. The chain sells sandwiches and fries, but I could not verify a vegetarian burger or grilled cheese on the Czech menu.

Are Five Guys fries cooked in peanut oil? Yes. The official Czech page says the fries are cooked in 100% peanut oil. If you have a peanut allergy, ask staff before ordering.

Is there a Beef Brothers burger restaurant in Prague? No. The only BeefBrothers s.r.o. in Prague is an advertising and video production agency founded in 2008. The German Beef Brothers burger chain, in Cologne and Düsseldorf, was taken over by Ruff’s Burger and rebranded.
