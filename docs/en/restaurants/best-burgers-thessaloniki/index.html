Best Burgers in Thessaloniki: 10 Picks for 2026

Thessaloniki's 10 best burger restaurants, ranked: award-winning Pax, Black Angus in Ladadika, smash burgers and a seftalia burger with feta.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 11, 2026

My pick for the best burger in Thessaloniki in 2026 is Pax Homemade Burgers (El. Venizelou & Kalapothaki 8, city centre). It won Burger Fest’s Best Burger House title in 2023 and Best Burger in 2024, and it cooks only fresh Greek beef. Tarantino Sandwiches & Fries in Ano Ladadika (Black Angus and slow-smoked brisket) and Brothers in Law on Pavlou Mela (more than 30 burgers in a Brooklyn-style room) round out my top 3. For a smash burger, go to Kenedi, Paul’s Burger or Balboa. For a burger that tastes of Greece, head to Mano à Mano in Neo Rysio for its seftalia burger with feta and Florina pepper jam.

How I ranked Thessaloniki’s burger restaurants

I ranked these places first by how strongly recent, independent sources agree on them, then by awards, then by how recently each one was covered. I built the list on 22 October 2026 from the Greek press and city guides: emakedonia’s Best of Street Food features of February 2025 and July 2026, Athens Voice (including its January 2026 profile of Pax), Nikana’s fast-food guide updated on 9 September 2026, Greeka, the Greek Gastronomy Guide and Pillowfights (February 2025). Maxmag’s 2020 list served only as background. Several of its addresses have since closed or moved.

I have not yet logged a dated visit to each counter, so this ranking reflects the sources, not my own tasting notes. A 2026 source confirms that Pax, Tarantino, Black Dagger and Mano à Mano are still open. For the other six, my most recent source dates from 2025, is undated, or is a delivery listing, and I flag it in each entry. Check the restaurant’s own page before you cross town.

There are no prices and no Google or Tripadvisor scores here. I could not find a dated 2026 price on any official menu, and the ranges in older guides are stale. As for the Tripadvisor and Restaurant Guru lists that top English search results: they are generated automatically, with no verified addresses and not a word about the burger itself.

The 10 best burger restaurants in Thessaloniki

1. Pax Homemade Burgers: the best burger in Thessaloniki

Pax is the most decorated burger house in Thessaloniki: it won Burger Fest’s Best Burger House award in 2023 and its Best Burger award in 2024 (Athens Voice, January 2026). It opened in November 2013 (Maxmag), so the awards came a decade in. That staying power is why it tops my list.

The patties are 100% fresh Greek beef, the sauces are made in house and the fries are cut by hand. Order the Epic Smash, a double with bacon, or the BBQ Burger with smoked chipotle. There is also a Habanero Honey Chicken, and Greeka lists beef, chicken and 100% buffalo burgers, served from an open kitchen in an industrial room modelled on New York burger houses (Greeka).

Very hungry? The Pax burger bowl piles a beef patty, a fried egg, tater tots, bacon and onion rings into one dish. Pax also has a second address in Athens, at Aiolou 34.

  • Address: El. Venizelou & Kalapothaki 8, Thessaloniki (city centre)
  • Phone: +30 231 022 1852
  • Hours: Sunday to Thursday 12:00–00:00, Friday and Saturday 12:00–00:30 (Nikana, updated 9 September 2026)
  • Style: fresh Greek beef, smash and classic burgers, buffalo and chicken

2. Tarantino Sandwiches & Fries: the best Ladadika burger

In Ladadika, the old warehouse quarter by the port, Tarantino is the burger to look for. It opened in April 2018 on Ernestou Emprar in Ano Ladadika, the upper edge of the district, and it cooks 100% Black Angus burgers (Athens Voice, 2018).

The menu goes beyond the patty: slow-smoked brisket, pulled pork and a vegetarian burger built on a portobello mushroom. Get the sweet potato fries with truffle mayonnaise on the side. Nikana still lists Tarantino in its guide updated on 9 September 2026.

There is a second Tarantino counter in Modiano Market, the covered market in the centre. It is handy if you are already shopping there (Pillowfights).

  • Address: Ernestou Emprar 5, Ano Ladadika, Thessaloniki (Athens Voice gives 3-5)
  • Phone: +30 2310 530 020
  • Style: Black Angus burgers, smoked brisket, pulled pork, portobello veggie burger

3. Brothers in Law: the widest burger menu in Thessaloniki

Brothers in Law has the biggest menu on this list: more than 30 burgers and wraps, served since 2012 in an industrial, Brooklyn-style room on Pavlou Mela (Spotted by Locals). Another source dates the opening to 2013.

The brioche buns are made by hand, and so is the beef mince, every day (Greeka). The famous order is the Elvis: peanut butter, cheddar, strawberry jam, bacon and fried banana. It reads like a dare. That is exactly why people order it.

Greeka lists other branches on Komninon (Komninon 3), Lambraki and Vasilissis Olgas. Sources disagree on which address is now the main one and which branches are still open, so check brothersinlaw.gr before you go.

  • Address: Pavlou Mela 30, Thessaloniki (city centre)
  • Phone: +30 2310 270707
  • Style: hand-minced beef on handmade brioche, 30+ burgers and wraps
  • Status: most recent source from 2025; confirm opening before visiting

4. Black Dagger: the Brooklyn Burger in Stavroupoli

Black Dagger is the best reason to leave the centre for Stavroupoli, in the west of the city. It opened in 2019 where Mpes sto Psito (2010–2019) used to be, and every burger arrives pierced by a black dagger (emakedonia, July 2026).

Order the Brooklyn Burger, which got noticed at the Athens Burger Fest in September 2019. Emakedonia picked Black Dagger for its Best of Street Food 2026 feature, so it is confirmed open this year.

  • Address: M. Alexandrou 16, Stavroupoli, Thessaloniki
  • Phone: +30 2310 602220
  • Style: loaded burgers served with a black dagger

5. Burgler: the best burger in Kalamaria

Burgler is my pick in Kalamaria, the seafront district in the south-east. It was founded in 2017 and later moved to its current address on Metamorfoseos. The name mashes up ‘burglar’ and ‘burger’ (emakedonia, February 2025).

The burger I would order is the Yorker: blue cheese, caramelised onions, bacon and a whiskey sauce (Maxmag). Salads and vegetarian options are on the menu too, so it works for a mixed group.

  • Address: Metamorfoseos 5, 551 31 Kalamaria, Thessaloniki
  • Phone: +30 2314 007722
  • Style: gourmet burgers, salads, vegetarian options
  • Status: most recent source from February 2025; confirm opening before visiting

Aretsou marina on the Kalamaria seafront, south-east Thessaloniki, the district where Burgler serves its Yorker burger on Metamorfoseos (photo of the neighbourhood, not of the restaurant) (Photo: Tilemahos Efthimiadis, CC BY-SA 2.0, https://www.flickr.com/photos/64379474@N00/2778488371)

6. Funky Burger: Greek buffalo and local beef in Toumpa

Funky Burger puts northern Greece on the bun. It sources antibiotic-free meat from Lake Kerkini, Veria and Aridaia, and its signature is a Kerkini buffalo burger (Greek Gastronomy Guide).

It opened in summer 2016 and won first prize at a burger competition in Athens a year later. The guide does not name the contest. One detail I like: Funky Burger donates its unsold bread every day to meals for homeless people.

  • Address: Markou Mpotsari 139 (corner of Anatolikis Thrakis), Toumpa, Thessaloniki
  • Phone: +30 2310 945925
  • Style: local, antibiotic-free beef and buffalo
  • Status: the Greek Gastronomy Guide entry is undated, and a 2020 guide listed Egnatia 144; confirm the current address and opening before visiting

7. Kenedi Burgers & Fries: a classic smash burger in the centre

For a smash burger right in the centre, Kenedi is the address. Thin patties are pressed onto a cast-iron plate until the edges crisp, American style (emakedonia, February 2025).

Want to try the technique at home? Our Sonic Bacon Smasher copycat recipe shows how to press the patty on a very hot surface.

  • Address: Desperai 5, Thessaloniki (city centre)
  • Phone: +30 2312 312437
  • Style: American smash burgers on cast iron
  • Status: most recent source from February 2025; confirm opening before visiting

8. Mano à Mano: the Greek seftalia burger with feta

Mano à Mano serves the most Greek burger on this list: a seftalia burger with feta and Florina pepper jam. Seftalia is a caul-wrapped sausage from the Cypriot tradition, and Florina’s sweet red peppers come from western Macedonia (emakedonia, July 2026).

Seftalia, the caul-wrapped Cypriot sausage that Mano à Mano turns into a Greek burger with feta and Florina pepper jam, served here the traditional way with cabbage, tomato and lemon (illustrative photo, not taken at Mano à Mano) (Photo: Pampos1997, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cypriot\_traditional\_plate\_with\_seftalia.jpg)

Chef Lamprini opened it in 2019 with Manos and Katerina. The other burger to try is the Masterpiece: truffle mayonnaise, mushroom duxelles, caramelised onion and truffle salami. It is in Neo Rysio, outside the city, and it has a garden. Go by car on a summer evening.

  • Address: S. Vezyrtzi 11, Neo Rysio, Thessaloniki
  • Phone: +30 23921 15300
  • Style: chef-driven burgers with Greek ingredients, garden seating

9. Paul’s Burger: a family-run smash burger in east Thessaloniki

Paul’s Burger is a small smash burger shop that a mother and her daughter have run since March 2023 (emakedonia, February 2025). The formula is short: smashed minced beef, cheddar and pickles they make themselves.

House pickles are a detail I always look for. They tell you the kitchen cares about what goes around the patty, not just the patty.

  • Address: Georgiou Papandreou 38, Thessaloniki (east of the city)
  • Phone: +30 2310 421322
  • Style: smash burgers, house pickles
  • Status: most recent source from February 2025; confirm opening before visiting

10. Balboa: smash burgers in an American diner setting

Balboa is a smash burger diner in the centre, with film posters on the walls (Pillowfights). The menu lists a Smashed Bacon Cheeseburger, a Double Oklahoma Cheeseburger and buttermilk chicken burgers (Wolt).

None of the other nine places here does the Oklahoma style, where thin-sliced onions are pressed into the patty on the griddle. That makes it the burger I would pick at Balboa. Balboa is listed on Wolt Thessaloniki, but I could not find its street address, so I list it under its area only.

  • Area: Kentro (city centre), Thessaloniki
  • Style: smash burgers, Oklahoma onion burger, buttermilk chicken
  • Address: not confirmed; check the Wolt listing

Also worth a look: Juicy Lucy (Zefxidos 6) made a black pepper burger with cheddar and bacon in 2020, and a 2026 mention points to an ‘Onassis burger’ with fried feta. I could not confirm it is still open, so it stays off the ranking.

Smash burger in Thessaloniki: where to go

The best places for a smash burger in Thessaloniki are Kenedi, Paul’s Burger, Balboa and Pax’s Epic Smash. Kenedi, Balboa and Pax are in the centre, and Paul’s Burger is in the east of the city.

Burgers by neighbourhood: Ladadika, centre, Kalamaria and beyond

Most of Thessaloniki’s best burgers are in the city centre and Ano Ladadika, close enough to walk between. Pax, Brothers in Law and Kenedi are in the centre. Tarantino is on the edge of Ladadika, near the port and the bars.

Outside the centre, Burgler covers Kalamaria in the south-east, Funky Burger is in Toumpa and Black Dagger is in Stavroupoli to the west. Mano à Mano is the furthest out, in Neo Rysio beyond the city limits. For more on the city’s neighbourhoods, see our Thessaloniki burger page.

Burger Fest: Thessaloniki’s burger festival and awards

Thessaloniki has its own burger festival. Its first Thessaloniki edition of Burger Fest ran from 31 May to 2 June 2019 at the Thessaloniki International Fair grounds, with more than 12 burgers and pop-ups and a €5 entry ticket (iefimerida).

Burger Fest awards are the closest thing Greece has to an official burger ranking. Pax took Best Burger House in 2023 and Best Burger in 2024, and Black Dagger’s Brooklyn Burger got noticed at the Athens edition in September 2019.

Thessaloniki burgers: FAQ

Where can I eat the best burger in Thessaloniki? At Pax Homemade Burgers, El. Venizelou & Kalapothaki 8 in the city centre, winner of Burger Fest’s Best Burger House (2023) and Best Burger (2024). Tarantino in Ano Ladadika and Brothers in Law on Pavlou Mela are the next best options.

What is the most famous burger restaurant in Thessaloniki: Tarantino, Pax or Brothers in Law? Pax has the strongest awards record, with two Burger Fest titles in 2023 and 2024. Tarantino is best known for Black Angus and smoked brisket. Brothers in Law, open since 2012 (2013 according to one source), is known for its 30-plus burgers and the peanut-butter-and-banana Elvis.

Where can I find a smash burger in Thessaloniki? Go to Kenedi Burgers & Fries (Desperai 5), Balboa in the centre, Paul’s Burger (Georgiou Papandreou 38) or Pax for its Epic Smash.

How much does a burger cost in Thessaloniki in 2026? I could not verify 2026 prices on any official menu, so I do not quote any. The ranges published in 2020 are out of date. Check the restaurant’s menu or its Wolt listing for current prices in euros.

Are there vegetarian burgers in Thessaloniki? Yes. Tarantino serves a vegetarian portobello mushroom burger, and Burgler has vegetarian options and salads. None of my sources confirms a fully vegan burger.

Which neighbourhood is best for a burger in Thessaloniki? The city centre and Ano Ladadika, where Pax, Brothers in Law, Kenedi, Balboa and Tarantino are within walking distance. Kalamaria (Burgler) and Toumpa (Funky Burger) are worth the trip if you are staying nearby.

Is there a Greek burger with feta or local meat in Thessaloniki? Yes. Mano à Mano in Neo Rysio serves a seftalia burger with feta and Florina pepper jam, Funky Burger makes a burger with Kerkini buffalo, and Pax uses 100% fresh Greek beef.

Can I get a burger late at night in Thessaloniki? Yes. Pax Homemade Burgers stays open until midnight from Sunday to Thursday and until 00:30 on Friday and Saturday.
