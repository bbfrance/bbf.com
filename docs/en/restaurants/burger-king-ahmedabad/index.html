Burger King Ahmedabad: Outlets, Hours and Prices

Burger King Ahmedabad: all 7 official outlets, which stay open until 5 a.m., menu prices in rupees and what customers say.

By Arjun, Restaurants writer, IndiaPublished November 25, 2026

Burger King Ahmedabad has at least seven restaurants on the chain’s official store locator, and five of them stay open well past midnight. CG Road and Prahlad Nagar run from 9 a.m. to 5 a.m., Memnagar (Himalaya Mall) from 10 a.m. to 5 a.m., Tapovan Circle on SP Ring Road until 4 a.m. and Nikol until 3 a.m. There is no beef or pork on the menu. Vegetarian burgers started at ₹55 on magicpin when I checked on 5 November 2026, and a meal for two costs about ₹400 to ₹500. My short answer: go to CG Road if you are central and hungry after midnight, and to Nikol or Prahlad Nagar if you are driving.

Late-night Burger King in Ahmedabad: which outlets stay open

No Burger King in Ahmedabad is officially open 24 hours, but three close at 5 a.m.: CG Road, Prahlad Nagar and Memnagar. Two more, Tapovan Circle and Nikol, stay open until 4 a.m. and 3 a.m. For a night owl or a traveller with an early flight, that is the real reason to know these addresses.

  • Until 5 a.m.: CG Road (opens 9 a.m.), Prahlad Nagar (opens 9 a.m.), Memnagar, Himalaya Mall (opens 10 a.m.)
  • Until 4 a.m.: Tapovan Circle, SP Ring Road
  • Until 3 a.m.: Nikol, Hilltown Plaza
  • Mall hours: One Mall until 11:59 p.m., Palladium Mall until 11 p.m.

How many Burger King restaurants are there in Ahmedabad?

The official Burger King India locator lists at least seven restaurants in Ahmedabad: six on its first page and at least one on the second. I won’t give a firm total. EazyDiner and local directories also mention outlets in Gurukul, Satellite, Chandkheda, Navrangpura and on SG Highway, and I could not match any of them to an official page.

Across India, Burger King had 607 restaurants in 144 cities on 30 September 2026, 74 more than a year earlier. In the quarter to September (Q2 FY2027), it opened 17 restaurants, grew same-store sales by 14.0% and posted its first quarterly profit, ₹15.6 crore, according to results published on 29 October 2026 (Restaurant India, Franchise India). I found no date for the first Burger King in Ahmedabad.

Burger King Ahmedabad menu: no beef, plenty of vegetarian

Burger King in Ahmedabad serves no beef and no pork, like every Burger King in India. The menu runs on chicken and vegetarian patties, which suits a city where many diners eat vegetarian.

The official Ahmedabad pages split the menu into seven sections: Whopper, King Premium Burger, Burgers & Wraps, Chicken Wings, Sides & Beverages, Desserts and BK Cafe. The only vegetarian burgers I found with an Ahmedabad price are the Crispy Veg and the Veg Makhani Burst. I could not confirm any Gujarat-only item, or a Jain menu without onion and garlic. If you need one, ask at the counter before ordering.

What reviewers like: the burgers themselves, especially for the price, and polite delivery riders.

What reviewers dislike: food that arrives cold or stale, missing or wrong items, undercooked fries, and buns and patties that vary from one order to the next.

My verdict: Burger King in Ahmedabad is a cheap late-night stop, not a destination. The pattern in the complaints points one way: eat at the counter rather than order delivery, because cold fries and missing items are what drag the scores down. CG Road is the most central, Prahlad Nagar and Nikol have free parking, and the two malls only make sense if you are already shopping there. I could not verify Google or TripAdvisor ratings for these outlets, so I left them out. Reviews from magicpin and EazyDiner, checked 5 November 2026.

Who runs Burger King in India?

Burger King India is run by Restaurant Brands Asia, controlled since July 2026 by Inspira Global, which bought a 41.78% stake for about ₹2,235 crore. The 600th Indian restaurant opened on 10 September 2026 at GTB Nagar, Delhi, and the stated target is 700 restaurants by December 2026 (Sahi). The chain’s history and figures are on our Burger King brand profile.

FAQ: Burger King Ahmedabad

How many Burger King restaurants are there in Ahmedabad, and which should I choose?

The official locator lists at least seven: CG Road, Prahlad Nagar, Memnagar (Himalaya Mall), SP Ring Road (Tapovan Circle), Nikol, One Mall and Palladium Mall. Choose CG Road for a central late-night meal, Prahlad Nagar or Nikol if you need parking, and the malls if you are already shopping there.

Which Burger King in Ahmedabad is open late at night?

CG Road, Prahlad Nagar and Memnagar (Himalaya Mall) are open until 5 a.m., Tapovan Circle on SP Ring Road until 4 a.m. and Nikol until 3 a.m., according to the official pages checked on 5 November 2026. None is officially open 24 hours.

Does Burger King in Ahmedabad serve beef?

No. Burger King India serves neither beef nor pork. Whoppers and other burgers are made with chicken or vegetarian patties.

What vegetarian burgers does Burger King Ahmedabad have?

The Crispy Veg and Veg Makhani Burst were listed for the CG Road outlet on magicpin on 5 November 2026, at ₹55 and ₹59. I could not confirm a Jain or Gujarat-specific menu.

How much does a meal at Burger King Ahmedabad cost?

Budget about ₹400 for two according to magicpin (CG Road) or about ₹500 according to EazyDiner (Prahlad Nagar), checked on 5 November 2026. Vegetarian burgers started at ₹55.

Does Burger King deliver in Ahmedabad?

Burger King takes online orders through burgerking.in, and its Ahmedabad outlets appear on magicpin, Zomato and Swiggy. The official outlet pages do not confirm delivery outlet by outlet, so check the app for your address.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Cash, debit cards, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UPI and online payment, according to the official pages of CG Road, Prahlad Nagar, Memnagar, One Mall and Nikol. The Palladium Mall and SP Ring Road pages list no payment methods.

Is there parking at Burger King in Ahmedabad?

Yes, at Prahlad Nagar (free on-site and street parking) and Nikol (free parking), according to their official pages. I found no parking details for the other outlets.

Is there a Burger King at Ahmedabad airport?

I could not verify whether Burger King has an outlet at Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport. None of the seven official Ahmedabad listings is at the airport.
