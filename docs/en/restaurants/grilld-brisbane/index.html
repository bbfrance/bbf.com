Grill'd Brisbane: Stores, Menu, Prices, Reviews

Wintergarden, South Bank, Emporium and the Mt Ommaney drive-thru: checked addresses, hours, AUD prices and ratings for Grill'd in Brisbane.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished November 3, 2026

If you’re in the Brisbane CBD, the Grill’d to head for is Grill’d Wintergarden, inside the Wintergarden Shopping Centre at L105/171 Queen St, on Queen Street Mall. It opens at 10:30am, takes table bookings, and rates 4.1/5 on Google across 962 reviews. Burgers run from about A$14.50 to A$18.90 on indicative national prices. Grill’d has more than 20 restaurants across Greater Brisbane. For visitors, the other three worth knowing are South Bank, Emporium in Fortitude Valley and Mt Ommaney, the chain’s first-ever drive-thru.

This is a checked guide, not a tasting review. On 11 October 2026 I went through the official grilld.com.au page for each restaurant, the official menu, press coverage and review aggregators, and I dated every fact. Where I couldn’t verify something, I say so rather than guess.

Grill’d Brisbane locations: the 4 stores visitors need

Grill’d Brisbane covers the whole metro area, but four stores matter most to visitors: Wintergarden in the CBD, South Bank on Grey Street, Emporium in Fortitude Valley and the Mt Ommaney drive-thru in the western suburbs. The official restaurant list shows more than 20 Grill’d stores in Greater Brisbane, from Chermside and North Lakes to Carindale, Indooroopilly and Springfield.

Grill’d Wintergarden (Brisbane CBD)

Grill’d Wintergarden is the closest Grill’d to the centre of Brisbane, at L105/171 Queen St inside the Wintergarden Shopping Centre. Third-party listings put it on the level above Queen Street Mall, with a dining room that looks out over the centre. It’s also the only one of the four whose official page offers table bookings, not just group ones. Staying or working in the CBD? This is the one I’d pick.

Grill’d South Bank

Grill’d South Bank is at Shop 3, 167 Grey St, South Brisbane, the handy choice after a day at South Bank Parklands. When I checked, it had the latest Sunday close of the four: 11pm. It takes group bookings too, which helps if you’re feeding a crowd after an afternoon by the river.

Grill’d Emporium (Fortitude Valley)

Grill’d Emporium is at T3 Emporium, 1000 Ann St, Fortitude Valley. Staying in the Valley, or heading there for the night? This is your stop. Services match South Bank: dine-in, delivery, pick up, catering and group bookings.

Grill’d Mt Ommaney: the first Grill’d drive-thru

Grill’d Mt Ommaney, at 171 Dandenong Road in Mount Ommaney, is the chain’s first drive-thru. It opened in late June 2023 on a former Hungry Jack’s site in front of the Mt Ommaney shopping centre, with more than 40 options on the menu, according to Concrete Playground. SmartCompany reported it as the chain’s 165th restaurant.

Founder Simon Crowe drew a careful line at the launch: “Grill’d drive-thru is a quick way to get your burger fix, but it’s not fast food” (SmartCompany, 29 June 2023). Its official page lists pick up and delivery only. Treat it as a drive-thru, not a sit-down stop.

Bookings, delivery and catering

You can book a table at Grill’d Wintergarden, and Wintergarden, South Bank and Emporium all take group bookings, according to their official pages. All four stores offer pick up and delivery. Catering orders at Wintergarden, South Bank and Emporium must go in before 5pm for pick-up the next day.

For Wintergarden, aggregator viabrisbane also lists a wheelchair-accessible entrance and seating, high chairs, and paid parking. That’s not from Grill’d itself, so call +61 7 3003 1500 if access matters to you. I found no official information on payment methods, parking or public transport for any of the four stores.

What’s on the Grill’d menu

The official Grill’d menu runs from classic beef burgers to specialty burgers made with Australian lamb and wagyu. A “High Performance” range of double-patty burgers includes the Protein Pro, which Grill’d says delivers 81 g of protein. There are also sliders, wraps until 5pm, salads, a kids’ Mini Me Pack, and RSPCA-certified chicken tenders cooked in extra virgin olive oil, in 3 or 5 pieces. Buns include a “Hi Fibre Lo GI” option.

I found no Queensland-only item on the official menu. Some recent reviews mention a “Big Queenslander”, but nutrition site fatsecret marks it as archived and it isn’t on the official menu. Don’t count on finding it.

Vegetarian burgers at Grill’d Brisbane

Vegetarians get two real options. The Garden Goodness veggie burger (about A$14.90) is one of the most praised items in Wintergarden reviews, and the Beyond Simply Grill’d (about A$15.90) is a plant-based take on the classic. Vegan or gluten-free? Ask staff which items fit. I couldn’t confirm either from the official menu.

Grill’d Brisbane reviews: what diners say about Wintergarden

Grill’d Wintergarden rates 4.1/5 on Google from 962 reviews and 4.2/5 on TripAdvisor from 111 reviews, according to Wanderlog, checked 11 October 2026. Viabrisbane confirms the 4.1 on Google.

  • What diners praise: juicy burgers, the sweet potato chips, friendly staff, a big dining room overlooking the shopping centre, and the Garden Goodness veggie burger.
  • What they criticise: slow service at times, wrong orders and uneven quality.

For a chain that sells itself on quality, inconsistency is the complaint I’d take most seriously. At a busy CBD lunch, check your bag before you walk off.

I couldn’t find dated, sourced ratings for South Bank, Emporium or Mt Ommaney. Rather than print numbers I can’t stand behind, I’d point you to each store’s Google Maps listing.

Wednesday deals and limited-time offers

Some diners mention a Wednesday “Burger Love” deal and a limited-time Grill’d x Twisties offer. I couldn’t confirm the terms or dates for Brisbane. Check the Grill’d app or ask in store before planning around either.

What is Grill’d? The Australian healthy burger chain

Grill’d is an Australian burger chain founded by Simon Crowe in Hawthorn, Melbourne, on 2 March 2004. It still has its head office there and now runs more than 170 restaurants. It built its name on healthier burgers, and sourcing is what sets it apart: grass-fed, free-range beef and lamb since 2010, and RSPCA-certified chicken since 2016, according to Wikipedia.

Grill’d patties have been sold at Coles since August 2025, and its sauces joined them in May 2026. Outside Australia, there is just one Grill’d, in Seminyak, Bali, which opened in December 2019. If Bali is on your itinerary, our guide to 2080 Burger, the Sanur burger spot in Denpasar covers a local alternative.

The brand is also in court. On 16 June 2026, the ACCC sued Grill’d in the Federal Court over its “Tree Day Tuesday” campaign, according to The New Daily. The case is ongoing and nothing has been proven. For a chain that sells itself on doing the right thing, it’s a case I’ll be watching.

Which Grill’d in Brisbane should you choose?

Choose by where you are: Wintergarden for the CBD, South Bank for the Parklands, Emporium for Fortitude Valley and Mt Ommaney if you want to stay in the car. The menu is the national one everywhere, so location and services decide it.

  • CBD, Queen Street Mall: Grill’d Wintergarden, the only one of the four with table bookings.
  • South Bank Parklands: Grill’d South Bank on Grey St, open until 11pm on Sunday.
  • Fortitude Valley: Grill’d Emporium on Ann St.
  • Driving through the western suburbs: Grill’d Mt Ommaney, the first Grill’d drive-thru.
  • Group dinner: Wintergarden, South Bank or Emporium, all listing group bookings.

Grill’d Brisbane FAQ

How many Grill’d restaurants are there in Brisbane?

More than 20 across Greater Brisbane, according to the official grilld.com.au restaurant list in October 2026. They include Wintergarden, South Bank, Emporium, Mt Ommaney, Chermside, Carindale, Indooroopilly, Toowong and North Lakes.

Which Grill’d is closest to the Brisbane CBD?

Grill’d Wintergarden, in the Wintergarden Shopping Centre at L105/171 Queen St, Brisbane City QLD 4000, on Queen Street Mall. Phone +61 7 3003 1500.

What are Grill’d Wintergarden’s opening hours?

It opens at 10:30am daily. It closes at 10pm Sunday to Thursday and 10:30pm Friday and Saturday, according to viabrisbane. The official page confirmed 10:30am to 10pm on Sunday 11 October 2026.

How much is a burger at Grill’d in Brisbane?

About A$14.50 (Simply Grill’d) to A$18.90 (Wagyu Wunder), based on national prices from menuprices.com.au in August 2026. Delivery apps charge 15 to 30% more.

Where is the Grill’d drive-thru in Brisbane?

At 171 Dandenong Road, Mount Ommaney QLD 4074, in front of the Mt Ommaney shopping centre. It opened in late June 2023 as the first Grill’d drive-thru.

Can you book a table at Grill’d in Brisbane?

Yes. Grill’d Wintergarden takes table and group bookings, and South Bank and Emporium take group bookings, according to their official pages.

Does Grill’d have vegetarian options?

Yes. The Garden Goodness veggie burger and the plant-based Beyond Simply Grill’d are on the menu. For vegan or gluten-free needs, check with staff before ordering.

Is there a Grill’d near South Bank or Fortitude Valley?

Yes. Grill’d South Bank is at Shop 3, 167 Grey St, South Brisbane, and Grill’d Emporium is at T3 Emporium, 1000 Ann St, Fortitude Valley.

Can I get Grill’d delivered in Brisbane?

Yes. Wintergarden, South Bank, Emporium and Mt Ommaney all list delivery on their official pages. Expect higher prices on delivery apps than in store.

Is Grill’d healthier than regular fast food?

Grill’d positions itself as a healthier burger chain, with grass-fed, free-range beef and lamb, RSPCA-certified chicken and tenders cooked in extra virgin olive oil. How healthy your meal is still depends on what you order.
