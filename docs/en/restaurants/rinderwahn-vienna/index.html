Rinderwahn Vienna: Menu, Prices, Hours, Reviews

Rinderwahn Vienna's two addresses, 2026 menu prices from €17.90, the €14.90 lunch deal, hours, booking and ratings, checked on 19 November 2026.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 24, 2026

Rinderwahn is a Viennese premium burger brand with two addresses in Vienna: the restaurant at Weihburggasse 3 in the 1st district, a few streets from Stephansplatz, and the takeaway kiosk Rinderwahn am Markt at Naschmarkt 1 in the 6th district. A Rinderwahn Cheeseburger costs €18.90, most signature burgers €19.90, and the weekday Dirty Lunch Deal €14.90. Weihburggasse is open every day from 12:00 to 22:30 according to the official site, takes table bookings online and holds 4.4/5 on Google (4,794 reviews, as relayed by Wanderlog). My short verdict: a solid sit-down burger near the cathedral, steep at dinner, fair at weekday lunch. I checked every price, address and opening hour below against Rinderwahn’s own pages on 19 November 2026.

Rinderwahn Vienna at a glance: two addresses, two formats

Rinderwahn Vienna runs two very different places: a sit-down burger restaurant on Weihburggasse and a street-food kiosk at the Naschmarkt. Want a table, a garden terrace or a booking? Go to Weihburggasse, the flagship. Already walking the market stalls and just want something in hand? The kiosk.

Rinderwahn Weihburggasse: address, opening hours and booking

Rinderwahn Weihburggasse is at Weihburggasse 3, 1010 Vienna, and the official site lists it as open Monday to Sunday from 12:00 to 22:30, including 24 and 25 December. It sits in the Innere Stadt, the 1st district, a short walk from St. Stephen’s Cathedral and the Stephansplatz U1/U3 station. Searching for a burger near Stephansplatz? This is it.

  • Address: Weihburggasse 3, 1010 Wien
  • Phone: +43 1 512 0996
  • Email: weihburggasse@rinderwahn.at
  • Hours (official site): Monday to Sunday, 12:00–22:30; also open on 24 and 25 December
  • Service: table service, dining room and outdoor garden terrace
  • Booking: online table reservation on rinderwahn.at
  • Delivery: online ordering and delivery through Foodora, linked from the official site

One caution on hours. Google Maps, as relayed by Wanderlog, shows shorter evenings: 12:00–22:00 from Sunday to Thursday and 12:00–22:30 on Friday and Saturday. I have used the official hours above. For a late dinner, call or book online first.

The dining room fills up at peak times, and reviewers say so again and again. Weekend evening with a group? Book on the official site instead of walking in.

Rinderwahn Naschmarkt: the takeaway kiosk

Rinderwahn am Markt is the Naschmarkt takeaway kiosk at Naschmarkt 1, 1060 Vienna, open Monday to Friday 11:30–21:00 and Saturday 08:00–21:00 according to the official site. It opened in spring 2018 in the stall formerly occupied by Cityfish, between the U4 stations Karlsplatz and Kettenbrückengasse.

Rinderwahn Vienna menu and prices (2026)

Most burgers on the Rinderwahn Vienna menu cost €18.90 to €19.90, with a triple-patty burger at €21.90 and a wagyu burger at €29.90. Burgers come on brioche buns, and fries are extra, from €5.50. These prices come from the official menu, checked on 19 November 2026.

Rinderwahn Vienna burger prices in 2026: €17.90 for vegetarian burgers, €18.90 for the Rinderwahn Cheeseburger, €19.90 for most signature burgers, €21.90 for the triple-patty Der Rinderwahn and €29.90 for the Kobe Burger Deluxe. Official menu checked on 19 November 2026. (Chart: Burger Breton editors, CC0 1.0, data from the official menu at https://www.rinderwahn.at/de/speisekarte.html)

Watch out for older prices online: some aggregators still show the cheeseburger at €13.90. The current official menu says €18.90. That is a city-centre price, and I won’t pretend otherwise. Die Burgermacher lists its Classic Beef at €11.90, as I noted in our guide to the best burgers in Vienna. You pay for the address, the table service and the terrace.

What does a meal at Rinderwahn cost?

A cheeseburger, fries and a house lemonade at Rinderwahn cost €30.30 at official menu prices, so budget about €30 per person for dinner. That is €18.90 for the Rinderwahn Cheeseburger, €5.50 for French Fries and €5.90 for a half-liter lemonade. A house sauce for the fries adds €3.00. The fries are what tip the bill past €30: they are never included.

What a meal at Rinderwahn costs: a dinner of Rinderwahn Cheeseburger (€18.90), French Fries (€5.50) and a half-liter house lemonade (€5.90) comes to €30.30, while the weekday Dirty Lunch Deal (soup or salad plus a burger) is €14.90. The Dirty Lunch Deal: the cheapest way to eat at Rinderwahn

The Dirty Lunch Deal costs €14.90 and runs Monday to Friday from 12:00 to 15:00, except on public holidays. It combines a salad or a soup with one burger from a set list: Scharfe Resi, Dicke Berta, Bella Luisa, Bacon Charlie, Pulled Pork, Eitler Gockel or a veggie option.

That is €3 less than the cheapest burger on its own, and you get a soup or salad on top. It is the best value on the menu by a distance. Rinderwahn’s own pages disagree on whether a drink is included, so assume it is not and ask when you order. I treat the deal as a Weihburggasse offer, because the official site does not say whether the Naschmarkt kiosk serves it.

What to order at Rinderwahn

For a first visit, order the Rinderwahn Cheeseburger with fries and house mayo. It is the cheapest beef burger on the menu and the plainest expression of the concept: a 180 g beef patty on a brioche bun. These picks are based on the menu and on what reviewers repeat most, not on a tasting of every burger.

  • For a first visit: Rinderwahn Cheeseburger (€18.90) with French Fries (€5.50) and Rinderwahn Mayo (€3.00).
  • For something only Rinderwahn does: Dicke Berta (€19.90), with crispy rösti and Emmental, or Bella Luisa (€19.90), with mountain cheese and mushrooms.
  • For big appetites: Der Rinderwahn (€21.90), with three 100 g patties.
  • For a splurge: Kobe Burger Deluxe (€29.90), with wagyu, egg and sweet potato fries.
  • For vegetarians: Ziegen Peter (€17.90), with grilled aged goat’s cheese, or a Beyond Meat version of the Dicke Berta (€19.90).

Is Rinderwahn worth it?

Yes, if you want a sit-down gourmet burger in central Vienna and accept city-centre prices. At dinner, it is hard to love a €30 bill for a burger, fries and a lemonade. At weekday lunch, the €14.90 deal changes the maths completely, and that is when I would go. Thousands of reviews sitting at 4.4 on Google is a strong signal, and the menu genuinely covers vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free diners. Already at the Naschmarkt? Use the kiosk and skip the walk.

The history of Rinderwahn in Vienna

Rinderwahn was founded by the Huth restaurant family, known in Vienna for Huth Gastwirtschaft, Da Moritz and Da Max, and opened its first restaurant near Stephansplatz in 2016. The name means “mad cow”. The decor leans into it: large cow figures and cheeky wall slogans such as “Ich will ein Rind von dir”. The food has stayed simple: beef burgers in brioche buns, fries and house mayo.

  • 2016: the first Rinderwahn opens on Weihburggasse, near Stephansplatz (Hogapage, vienna.at).
  • Spring 2018: Rinderwahn am Markt opens at the Naschmarkt (Leadersnet, 15 April 2018).
  • February 2019: a third restaurant with 120 seats opens at Praterstraße 31 in Leopoldstadt (vienna.at, 4 February 2019).
  • July 2022: Gabriele and Robert Huth hand both Rinderwahn restaurants over to Sarah & Werner Gastronomiebetriebs GmbH (Leadersnet, 2 August 2022).
  • November 2026: the official site lists two addresses, Weihburggasse and Naschmarkt.

Sarah & Werner Gastronomiebetriebs GmbH, based at Wiedner Hauptstraße 130 in Vienna’s 5th district, is named as the operator in the site’s legal notice. The Praterstraße restaurant no longer appears on the official site. I could not confirm when or why it stopped trading.

Rinderwahn Vienna FAQ

Where is Rinderwahn in Vienna, and how many locations are there?

Rinderwahn has two locations in Vienna: the restaurant at Weihburggasse 3, 1010 Vienna, near St. Stephen’s Cathedral, and the takeaway kiosk at Naschmarkt 1, 1060 Vienna. These are the only two addresses on the official site as of 19 November 2026.

What are Rinderwahn’s opening hours, and is it open on Sunday?

Rinderwahn Weihburggasse is open Monday to Sunday from 12:00 to 22:30, including Sundays, according to the official site. The Naschmarkt kiosk is open Monday to Friday 11:30–21:00 and Saturday 08:00–21:00, with no Sunday hours published. Google shows slightly shorter evening hours for Weihburggasse, so check before a late visit.

How much does a burger cost at Rinderwahn in 2026?

A burger at Rinderwahn costs €17.90 to €29.90 in 2026: €17.90 for vegetarian burgers, €18.90 for the Rinderwahn Cheeseburger, €19.90 for most signature burgers and €29.90 for the Kobe Burger Deluxe. Fries cost extra, from €5.50.

What is the best burger to order at Rinderwahn?

The Rinderwahn Cheeseburger (€18.90) is the best first order, with a 180 g beef patty, cheddar and house mayo. For something more Austrian, try the Dicke Berta with rösti and Emmental or the Bella Luisa with mountain cheese and mushrooms, both €19.90.

Can you book a table at Rinderwahn Vienna?

Yes, you can book a table at Rinderwahn Weihburggasse online through the official website, rinderwahn.at, or by phone on +43 1 512 0996. The Naschmarkt kiosk is a takeaway stand and does not take bookings.

Does Rinderwahn have vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free burgers?

Yes. Rinderwahn has three vegetarian burgers at €17.90 (Veggie-Wahn, Heidi Burger and Ziegen Peter), Beyond Meat versions of its beef burgers at €18.90 to €19.90, and a gluten-free bun for €2.50 extra.

Does Rinderwahn deliver in Vienna?

Yes. Both Rinderwahn locations offer online ordering and delivery through Foodora, linked from the “Bestellen” button on the official website.

What is the Rinderwahn Dirty Lunch Deal?

The Dirty Lunch Deal is a €14.90 weekday lunch at Rinderwahn: a salad or soup plus one burger from a set list, served Monday to Friday from 12:00 to 15:00, except public holidays. Ask whether a drink is included, as Rinderwahn’s own pages disagree.

What is the difference between Rinderwahn Weihburggasse and Rinderwahn am Markt?

Weihburggasse is a full restaurant with table service, a garden terrace, online booking and seven-day opening. Rinderwahn am Markt, at the Naschmarkt, is a street-food kiosk for takeaway, open Monday to Saturday with no published Sunday hours.

Is the Rinderwahn on Praterstraße still open?

The Rinderwahn at Praterstraße 31, which opened in February 2019, no longer appears on the official website, which lists only Weihburggasse and the Naschmarkt. Its closing date and the reason have not been confirmed.

Does Rinderwahn have a restaurant in Graz or elsewhere in Austria?

No. The official site lists only the two Vienna addresses. If you are in Graz, read my guide to Le Burger Graz, with menu, prices and hours, and in Salzburg, my review of Ludwig’s organic beef burgers in Salzburg.
