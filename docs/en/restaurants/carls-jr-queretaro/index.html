Carl's Jr Queretaro: Locations, Hours and Prices

Carl's Jr. in Querétaro: 9 addresses with their sources, reported hours, Rappi peso prices and the one rating I could find, checked November 11, 2026.

By Carmen, Restaurants writer, MexicoPublished November 18, 2026

Querétaro had 14 Carl’s Jr. restaurants when the Centro Sur branch opened on January 22, 2026, according to the local outlet Al Diálogo. That newest branch, at Boulevard Bernardo Quintana 9790, opened with hours of 9 am to 11 pm every day. On Rappi, a Famous Star con Queso cost 149 MXN for delivery from the Galerías Querétaro branch when I checked on November 11, 2026. Hoping for a burger at 3 am? As of July 2025, no branch in the city was open 24 hours.

How I put this Carl’s Jr. Querétaro guide together

This guide draws on the official Carl’s Jr. México branch pages, local press, Rappi, 2 business directories and the chain’s press releases, all consulted on November 11, 2026. I did not eat at these branches for this piece. So you will find no tasting verdict here, and no rating from me.

Directory listings carry no date and may be stale. Whenever a fact rests on a directory alone, I say so.

Carl’s Jr. in Querétaro: a short history

Carl’s Jr. has been in Mexico since 1991, but Querétaro only shows up in its plans almost 2 decades later. The first Mexican restaurant opened on Avenida Humberto Lobo in San Pedro Garza García, Nuevo León, and Mexico was the chain’s first market outside the United States (Crónica).

In a release dated November 18, 2008, CKE wrote that its expansion plans for 2009 included new markets in Querétaro and Tampico (franchising.com). The same release announced Mexico’s 100th Carl’s Jr., in Aguascalientes. I could not confirm when the first Querétaro branch actually opened.

How big is the chain in Mexico today? The sources disagree. A December 3, 2024 release announced the 400th Mexican restaurant, in Tampico, and more than 200,000 burgers sold per day in the country (PR Newswire). Crónica gave 299 restaurants in September 2026, and InformaBTL gave 298 in July 2025. That is a gap of about 100 restaurants, and none of these sources explains it, so treat any national total with caution. Carl’s Jr. Centro Sur: the newest branch

Centro Sur is the newest Carl’s Jr. in Querétaro and the only one whose hours come from a dated source. It opened on January 22, 2026, at Boulevard Bernardo Quintana 9790, in the Centro Sur subdivision, as the city’s 14th branch (Al Diálogo).

The first 50 customers got a free Famous Star combo. At the launch, the brand called the Famous Star its most-requested burger and said it plans 2 to 3 openings a year in the state. Al Diálogo quoted Marco Martínez, CFO of Dexel Group, and Lisbeth Rodríguez, the branch manager. Dexel Group’s exact role in the franchise is not confirmed. I found no phone number for this branch.

Carl’s Jr. Galerías Querétaro

Galerías Querétaro has the most public data of any branch: an official page, a Rappi menu with prices and the only rating I could source. The official address is Av. 5 de Febrero 99, Los Virreyes, C.P. 76175, phone 442 193 5099 (carlsjr.com.mx). The official page gives no opening hours.

Carl’s Jr. Antea Querétaro

The Antea branch sits on the highway to San Luis Potosí, in the north-west of the city. The official address is Carretera San Luis Potosí–Querétaro 12401, Félix Osores Sotomayor, C.P. 76127, phone 442 688 2082 (carlsjr.com.mx). No hours are published.

Carl’s Jr. Aleda, on the Querétaro–Mexico City highway

Aleda is the stop for drivers on the highway to Mexico City, but it lies outside the city of Querétaro. It is at km 206+631 of the Autopista Querétaro–México, Col. Piamonte, in the municipality of El Marqués, C.P. 76246, phone 442 241 5105 (carlsjr.com.mx). Hours are not published, and I could not confirm a drive-thru.

Los Arcos and Milenio

Los Arcos and Milenio appear only in a business directory, which lists both as open from 9 am to 11 pm every day. Los Arcos is at Blvd. Bernardo Quintana 14, phone 442 193 8757. Milenio is at Camino Real de Carretas 366, phone 442 241 4789 (Sucursales24). The listings carry no date, so call ahead.

Is there a 24-hour Carl’s Jr. in Querétaro?

No. In July 2025, the few Carl’s Jr. restaurants open 24 hours in Mexico were in Nuevo León and Puebla, and none was in Querétaro. Most branches in the country close between 10 pm and 11 pm (InformaBTL).

What to order at Carl’s Jr. in Querétaro

On price, my pick is the Famous Star con Queso. At the Centro Sur launch the brand called the Famous Star its most-requested burger, and at 149 MXN on Rappi it costs just 3 pesos more than the cheapest burger on the list. The Double El Diablo costs 97 pesos more, about 65% extra. I have no tasting that justifies that gap. For a side, the poppers (cheese-filled jalapeños) are 89 MXN, only 6 pesos more than fries.

If you would rather make it at home, try our Carl’s Jr. Famous Star copycat recipe or the Western Bacon Cheeseburger copycat.

Is the Mexican menu different from the US menu?

The core burgers share their US names, often with con Queso added, as in the Famous Star con Queso. The Mexican menu on Rappi also lists a Teriyaki Burger, a Jalapeño Burger and the El Diablo burgers. I found no item exclusive to Querétaro: these belong to the national Mexican menu, not to a local specialty.

Vegetarian options at Carl’s Jr. in Querétaro

I found no vegetarian burger on the Querétaro menu. Nothing vegetarian appears on the Galerías Rappi listing, and I could not confirm a plant-based patty on the Mexican menu. Fries, CrissCut fries and onion rings are the meat-free sides listed. Ask staff how they are cooked.

Carl’s Jr Queretaro reviews

The only rating I could source for a Querétaro branch is 4.4/5 on Rappi, from about 130 reviews of the Galerías delivery listing. That score rates the whole delivery, courier included, not just the kitchen (Rappi).

I could not verify any Google or TripAdvisor rating for a Querétaro branch, and I found no relevant Yelp reviews. So I won’t summarize what diners praise or complain about. I would be guessing. Read the latest Google Maps reviews of your branch before you go.

Carl’s Jr. delivery in Querétaro

You can order Carl’s Jr. for delivery in Querétaro on Rappi. The Galerías listing showed a delivery window of 11 am to 9 pm when I checked. I could not verify Carl’s Jr. listings for Querétaro on Uber Eats or DiDi Food, or ordering through the Carl’s Jr. México app.

Services, payment and invoices

The official branch pages offer 2 things: Google Maps directions and an invoice request. Mexican customers who need a tax invoice (CFDI) can start from there. Drive-thru, payment methods, wheelchair access, parking and public transport are not confirmed for any branch, so ask the branch.

Carl’s Jr. promotions in Querétaro

Carl’s Jr. runs national promotions in Mexico, but I could not confirm which Querétaro branches take part. For the chain’s 35th anniversary in Mexico, the Famous Star con Queso sold on its own for 35 MXN on September 30, 2026. The deal excluded combos and airport branches, applied in participating restaurants only and ran while stocks lasted (Crónica). That one-day offer is over.

Which Carl’s Jr. should you choose in Querétaro?

Pick Centro Sur if you want the newest dining room and hours you can trust: 9 am to 11 pm daily, as reported at its January 2026 opening. Pick Galerías Querétaro if you plan to order in, since it has the Rappi menu and the rating. Driving to or from Mexico City? Aleda, at km 206 of the highway in El Marqués, is on your way.

My view: Carl’s Jr. is a known quantity, not a reason to come to Querétaro. If you are in the city to eat, start with our Querétaro burger guide before you settle on a chain.

Carl’s Jr Queretaro FAQ

How many Carl’s Jr. restaurants are there in Querétaro?

What time does Carl’s Jr. open and close in Querétaro?

Centro Sur opened with hours of 9 am to 11 pm every day, according to Al Diálogo in January 2026. A directory lists the same hours for Los Arcos and Milenio, without a date. Other branches publish no hours.

Is there a 24-hour Carl’s Jr. in Querétaro?

No. In July 2025, Mexico’s 24-hour Carl’s Jr. branches were in Nuevo León and Puebla only, according to InformaBTL. Most branches close between 10 pm and 11 pm.

How much is a Famous Star at Carl’s Jr. in Querétaro?

Does Carl’s Jr. deliver in Querétaro?

Yes, through Rappi. The Galerías listing showed delivery from 11 am to 9 pm and a 4.4/5 rating from about 130 reviews. Uber Eats and DiDi Food listings were not verified.

Is the Carl’s Jr. menu in Mexico different from the US?

Do Carl’s Jr. promotions apply in Querétaro?
