Le Burger Graz: Menu, Prices, Hours and Reviews

Le Burger Graz: autumn 2026 menu prices, daily opening hours, step-free access, reviews and how it compares with Burger Factory and Freigeist.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 18, 2026

Le Burger Graz is at Kaiserfeldgasse 1, 8010 Graz, by the Eisernes Tor and about 200 m from the Jakominiplatz tram stop. It opens every day at 11am. On the autumn 2026 menu, a build-your-own hamburger costs €10.50 and the signature burgers run from €11.90 to €16.00; I checked those prices on 16 November 2026. Searching for Rinderwahn in Graz? You will not find it. The Viennese chain lists 2 restaurants, both in Vienna, so in Graz the Austrian chain to try is Le Burger.

Le Burger Graz at a glance

Le Burger Graz serves Alpine beef, chicken, vegetarian and vegan burgers on 2 floors in Graz’s Innere Stadt, and has done since September 2021. These are the key facts, checked against the chain’s own page and Graz Tourismus on 16 November 2026.

Is there a Rinderwahn in Graz?

No: as of 16 November 2026, Rinderwahn has no restaurant in Graz. Its official restaurant page lists 2 addresses, both in Vienna: Weihburggasse 3, 1010 Wien, and Naschmarkt 1, 1060 Wien. The chain was founded by restaurateur Robert Huth. In February 2019 it also had a third Viennese address on Praterstraße (Hogapage), which has since disappeared from the official site. If Rinderwahn is what you are after, save it for a day in the capital: my guide to the best burgers in Vienna covers the city.

Where Le Burger comes from

Le Burger is an Austrian chain founded in Vienna in 2014 by Thomas Tauber and his son Lukas, starting with a small restaurant in the Donauzentrum. 10 years later, in September 2024, it had 18 restaurants in Austria, 1 in Munich, 1 in Dubai and 700 employees, and was aiming for around 50 new restaurants by 2028 (vienna.at, 11 September 2024). It has nothing to do with the Le Burger restaurant in Jakarta.

The menu spells out the sourcing promise. The beef is 100% from the Alpine region and never frozen. The buns come from the chain’s own baker, made with organic ingredients and enriched with butter. The sauces are made in the house sauce kitchen.

BBQ, chicken and steak burgers

The BBQ and chicken burgers cost €13.90 to €18.90. The new Albero (€18.90) comes with 160 g of BBQ brisket, and the Frisco IV costs €16.50. The chicken burgers are the Little Italy (€13.90), the Classic Chicken Burger (€13.90), the White City (€14.50) and the Kentucky (€16.50). At the top of the menu, the Steak Burger puts 200 g of rump steak in a bun for €26.90.

Seasonal Austrian specials

The most local items on the Graz menu are 3 autumn 2026 burgers built on Austrian classics. They leave when the autumn menu ends.

  • Schönbrunn, €14.90: pulled Tafelspitz (boiled beef) with Apfelkren, the apple-horseradish sauce Austrians serve with it.
  • Le Rösti Vol. 2, €15.90.
  • Brettljausn Burger, €13.90: a plant-based take on the Austrian cold-cut board.

If you eat here only once this season, order the Schönbrunn. Tafelspitz with Apfelkren is Sunday lunch in half the households of Austria, and no global chain will put it in a bun for you.

Le Burger Graz address and how to get there

Le Burger Graz is at Kaiserfeldgasse 1, 8010 Graz, about 200 m from Jakominiplatz, the city’s main tram and bus hub. From the stop, it is a short walk to the Eisernes Tor. Drivers will find a short-stay parking zone on Kaiserfeldgasse. 5 disabled parking bays sit 150 to 200 m away, at Schmiedgasse 31 and Kaiserfeldgasse 11, 12 and 13 (Graz Tourismus accessibility sheet).

Is Le Burger Graz wheelchair accessible?

Yes, but not through the front door, which has 3 uneven steps. The step-free entrance is through the Sparkasse bank foyer on the other corner of the building: a 3% slope and a 116 cm sliding door, which needs a bank card to open outside banking hours. The unisex accessible toilet is in the foyer; ask the staff for the key. It does not have a 150 cm turning circle. All of this comes from a 2023 Graz Tourismus sheet, so call +43 316 395200 to confirm before you go.

Reservations, delivery and takeaway

You can reserve a table, order delivery or pre-order for pick-up at Le Burger Graz. The official page lists all 3 and also takes event requests. Delivery runs every day from 11am to 9pm. The restaurant spreads over 2 floors, with “industrial chic” decor and a “Burger-Lift” that carries the food upstairs. Given what reviewers say about service at busy times (see below), I would book for a Friday or Saturday evening.

What diners like. In the reviews I could read, the same points keep coming back: tasty, generous burgers, a comfortable dining room and gluten-free buns (TripAdvisor).

What they criticise. Slow or disorganised service, a long wait to pay, and value for money that some find only average.

A Graz blogger reviewed the restaurant on opening day in September 2021 (zerokspot). The verdict: very comfortable seats, “perfectly crispy” fries, and friendly, fast service despite some teething problems. The meat came a notch below Freigeist, but so did the prices.

Le Burger vs Burger Factory and Freigeist: the best burger in Graz?

Burger Factory has the edge on critics’ scores in Graz, but Le Burger is the more practical pick in the old town. Falstaff’s Streetfood Guide 2025 gives Burger Factory 93 points and Le Burger 91. On lunchy.to, Burger Factory leads with 4.7 (2,390 reviews), ahead of Le Burger and Freigeist Burger at 4.5 each (2,185 reviews for Freigeist).

The second Graz-area restaurant: Shopping City Seiersberg

Le Burger also has a restaurant in Shopping City Seiersberg, just south of Graz in Seiersberg-Pirka, which the chain files under Graz. It suits shoppers and families with a car better than visitors staying in the old town.

  • Address: Haus 7, Ebene 2, Top 3/2/16, 8055 Seiersberg
  • Phone: +43 316 909090
  • Hours: Monday to Friday 11am–8pm, Saturday 11am–7pm, closed on Sundays and public holidays
  • Services: reservations, delivery and pick-up

It opened on 10 December 2021 as a franchise run by Klaus and Marianne Simon, and covers 380 m², with more than 148 seats and a 200 m² heated terrace (Hogapage; Le Burger Seiersberg). The practical difference is Sunday: Kaiserfeldgasse is open, Seiersberg is closed.

Is Le Burger Graz worth it?

Yes, if you want an Austrian-sourced burger in central Graz, and especially if you eat vegan or gluten-free or are curious about the seasonal Austrian specials. The beef is Alpine and never frozen, the menu is long and Falstaff gives it 91 points. The weak point is service at busy times, the complaint that comes up most often in reviews and probably weighs on the 3.1 on TripAdvisor. Book, or come mid-afternoon, and most of those complaints go away.

FAQ about Le Burger Graz

Does Rinderwahn have a restaurant in Graz?

No. As of 16 November 2026, the official Rinderwahn site lists only 2 restaurants, at Weihburggasse 3 and Naschmarkt 1 in Vienna. In Graz, Le Burger is the Austrian burger chain to visit.

Where is Le Burger in Graz and how do I get there by tram?

Le Burger Graz is at Kaiserfeldgasse 1, 8010 Graz, by the Eisernes Tor. Take any tram to Jakominiplatz: the restaurant is about 200 m away.

What are Le Burger Graz’s opening hours, including Sunday?

It opens at 11am every day. It closes at 10pm Sunday to Thursday and on public holidays, and at 11pm on Friday and Saturday. The kitchen stops 30 minutes before closing.

How much does a burger cost at Le Burger Graz?

On the autumn 2026 menu, a build-your-own hamburger costs €10.50 and the signature burgers €11.90 to €16.00. The Steak Burger is the most expensive at €26.90. Fries are €5.10.

Can I build my own burger at Le Burger?

Yes. Choose a hamburger (€10.50), cheeseburger (€11.50), chicken or veggie burger (€11.90), make it a double, and swap the bun for a Signature, vegan High Protein, gluten-free or rice-cake bun.

Does Le Burger Graz have vegan and gluten-free burgers?

Yes. There are vegan burgers from €13.50, vegan bowls, and a gluten-free bun for €3.00 extra. Gluten-free ingredients are marked on the menu.

Do I need to book at Le Burger Graz?

It is not required, but you can reserve a table. Booking makes sense on Friday and Saturday evenings, when reviewers report slow service.

Does Le Burger Graz deliver or do takeaway?

Yes. Delivery runs every day from 11am to 9pm, and you can pre-order online and pick up.

Is Le Burger Graz wheelchair accessible?

The main entrance has 3 steps. Step-free access is through the Sparkasse foyer on the other corner of the building, where the accessible toilet is also located. This information dates from 2023, so call +43 316 395200 to confirm.

What is the difference between Le Burger Kaiserfeldgasse and Le Burger Seiersberg?

Kaiserfeldgasse is in the old town and open daily until 10pm or 11pm. Seiersberg is in a shopping centre south of Graz, closes at 8pm (7pm on Saturday) and is closed on Sundays.

Le Burger or Burger Factory: where is the best burger in Graz?

Burger Factory has the higher Falstaff score (93 points against 91). Le Burger wins on location, hours and vegan and gluten-free choice.

Does Le Burger Graz have a kids’ menu?

Yes. Kids’ meals cost €8.90 to €9.90, with fries included.
