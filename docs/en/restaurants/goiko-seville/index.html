Goiko Seville: 4 Addresses, Hours, Menu, Reviews

All 4 Goiko restaurants in Seville compared: addresses, opening hours, the two Seville-only burgers and what diners say, checked on 22 October 2026.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished October 23, 2026

Goiko has 4 restaurants in Seville: Calle Albareda 14 in the historic centre, Avenida de la República Argentina 26 across the river, Avenida Eduardo Dato 79 in Nervión and the Lagoh shopping centre in the south of the city. For most visitors I’d pick Goiko Albareda 14. It’s the most central, and it’s the only one serving La Chicki. República Argentina 26 has the best rating of the 4: Gastroranking puts it 1st of Seville’s 39 burger restaurants. Three of the four open at 1 pm Monday to Wednesday and at noon Thursday to Sunday. They close at 11:30 pm early in the week and at midnight Thursday to Sunday. Lagoh opens at noon every day.

How I checked: every address, phone number and opening time below comes from Goiko’s own restaurant pages, read on 22 October 2026. For Lagoh, I also used the shopping centre’s page. Ratings come from Gastroranking, which combines Google, Just Eat and Tripadvisor scores, on the same date. The chain’s history comes from Goiko and the Spanish trade press. Where I couldn’t confirm something, I say so.

Goiko Albareda 14: the best Goiko for central Seville

Goiko Albareda, at Calle Albareda 14 (41001) in the Alfalfa part of the Casco Antiguo, is the address to choose if you’re staying in the historic centre. Of the four, it’s the closest to the cathedral area.

Calle Alfalfa in Seville’s Alfalfa quarter, home to Goiko Albareda 14, with its bars shuttered in the early evening (June 2015). Illustrative photo: the street shown is not Calle Albareda and the restaurant is not in it. (Photo: Pedro sevilla, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alfalfa.jpg)

Its exclusive is La Chicki: a beef patty with chicken sautéed with bacon, rosemary cheese, a fried egg and a chicken sauce. You can only order it here (official page). Beef and chicken in one bun is a lot. It’s a burger for the very hungry.

The official page lists free wifi, a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table, booking, delivery and takeaway. It says nothing about a terrace, step-free access or pets, so don’t count on them.

Gastroranking combines 8,567 reviews into a score of 8.9/10: 9.2 on Google (7,565 reviews), 7.2 on Just Eat (765) and 7.0 on Tripadvisor (241). That makes it 2nd of Seville’s burger restaurants and 41st of 4,567 restaurants in the city (Gastroranking). The weak point is the Just Eat score, which matches the complaints about late deliveries.

Goiko República Argentina 26: the best-rated Goiko in Seville

Goiko República Argentina, at Avenida de la República Argentina 26 (41011), is the highest-rated burger restaurant in Seville on Gastroranking. It sits on the west bank of the Guadalquivir, between Los Remedios and Triana.

Avenida de la República Argentina in Seville, the palm-lined avenue between Los Remedios and Triana where Goiko República Argentina 26, the city’s best-rated burger restaurant, is located. Photo from 2008; the restaurant itself is not visible. (Photo: Gregory Zeier, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Avenida\_de\_la\_Republica\_Argentina.JPG)

Its exclusive is La Macarena: wine mayonnaise, breaded goat’s cheese, tomato sautéed with garlic, serrano ham and lamb’s lettuce (official page). It’s named after Seville’s best-known Virgin and neighbourhood, and it’s built on goat’s cheese and serrano ham. Of the two Seville exclusives, it’s the one with a real local accent, and the one I’d cross the river for.

Services on the official page are free wifi, a kids’ menu, booking, delivery and takeaway. No terrace or accessibility details are listed.

It scores 9.0/10 across 9,324 reviews: 9.0 on Google (8,345), 9.2 on Just Eat (562) and 6.8 on Tripadvisor (420). It ranks 1st of 39 burger restaurants and 24th of 4,567 restaurants in Seville (Gastroranking).

Goiko Eduardo Dato 79: the Nervión address

If you’re staying east of the centre, Goiko Eduardo Dato, at Avenida Eduardo Dato 79 (41005) in La Buhaira, Nervión, is the most practical choice. Its official page lists no exclusive burger.

The official page lists free wifi, a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table and access for wheelchair users. Booking, delivery and takeaway aren’t on that page. Check the booking and delivery pages before you plan around them.

Gastroranking gives it 8.7/10 across 9,125 reviews: 9.2 on Google (8,104), 8.4 on Just Eat (897) and 4.8 on Tripadvisor (125). It ranks 3rd of the city’s burger restaurants and 119th of 4,567 restaurants (Gastroranking). That Tripadvisor score is the lowest of any established Seville Goiko, but it rests on only 125 reviews against more than 8,000 on Google.

Goiko Lagoh: the Goiko with a terrace

Goiko Lagoh, in the Lagoh shopping centre at Avenida de Palmas Altas 1 (41012), is the only Goiko in Seville whose official page lists a terrace. Dogs are welcome on it.

Lagoh is a 100,000 m² shopping centre built around a 6,000 m² artificial lake. It opened on 27 September 2019 (FashionUnited). The Goiko there keeps its own hours: noon to 11:30 pm on Monday and Tuesday, noon to midnight Wednesday to Sunday (Lagoh).

The official page also lists a kids’ menu, a baby-changing table and access for wheelchair users. There’s no exclusive burger.

Google is very generous here: 9.6/10 across 5,031 reviews. Gastroranking’s combined score is 8.7/10 across 5,091 reviews. The Tripadvisor score is 2/10, but it rests on only 3 reviews, so I wouldn’t read anything into it (Gastroranking).

Which Goiko in Seville should you choose?

Choose by where you’re staying:

  • Historic centre, first visit to Seville: Albareda 14, for La Chicki.
  • Triana, Los Remedios or the Feria grounds side of the river: República Argentina 26, the best-rated, for La Macarena.
  • Nervión or near the Sánchez-Pizjuán area: Eduardo Dato 79. It’s one of the two addresses that list access for wheelchair users.
  • With kids or a dog, or if you want to eat outside: Lagoh, the only one with a terrace.

Goiko Seville menu: what to order

Goiko’s Seville menu is the chain’s national menu, plus one exclusive burger at Albareda 14 and another at República Argentina 26. The official menu covers starters, burgers, smash burgers, Chicken Lovers, a kids’ menu, salads, desserts, Menús GOIKO set meals and drinks. There’s also an allergen page.

Goiko’s signature burgers

The permanent burgers are the Tres Razas, Kevin Truff, Kevin Beast, Kevin Bacon, Kevin Costner, M-30, Pigma, Retro, Moza (the menu of the day) and Veggie Mercury (goiko.com). The Kevin Bacon is the chain’s best-seller: bacon is minced into the beef patty, which is then topped with crispy onion and cheese (Tendance Hôtellerie). I recommended it for a first visit in my Goiko Valencia guide, and that holds in Seville. Unless you’re here for one of the two local exclusives, start with it.

Seville-only burgers

Two burgers are served only in Seville, each at a single restaurant.

Burger Restaurant Ingredients
La Chicki Albareda 14 Beef, chicken sautéed with bacon, rosemary cheese, fried egg, chicken sauce
La Macarena República Argentina 26 Wine mayonnaise, breaded goat’s cheese, garlic-sautéed tomato, serrano ham, lamb’s lettuce

Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options

Vegetarians get the Veggie Mercury, the only plant-based burger on the menu, and any burger can be ordered with a plant-based patty. I can’t confirm a fully vegan burger in Seville today: a 2025 review of Eduardo Dato 79 says there isn’t one. Goiko offers a gluten-free section only “in some restaurants”, and I couldn’t confirm which Seville addresses are included. Ask before you order.

Goiko Seville prices

Plan on under €20 per person at any of Goiko’s Seville restaurants, according to Gastroranking. Goiko’s online menu shows no prices. The per-burger prices I found on unofficial sites didn’t agree with each other, so I’m not quoting them. The ordering page shows each restaurant’s current prices.

Goiko Seville reviews: what diners say

Diners rate Goiko highly in Seville on Google, between 9.0 and 9.6 out of 10, and much lower on Tripadvisor. The 3 addresses that Gastroranking ranks among burger restaurants hold the top 3 places in the city.

Gastroranking’s review summaries for the 4 addresses keep coming back to the same points:

  • Praise: good ingredients, a fast kitchen and staff that diners mention by name. Albareda’s 2-for-1 deals and Eduardo Dato’s fast, hot deliveries also come up.
  • Complaints: late deliveries from Albareda, and at Lagoh one diner found the burgers dry and smaller than expected.

Why the gap between Google and Tripadvisor? Google reflects thousands of local regulars, while Tripadvisor has far fewer reviews, many of them from tourists. Lagoh’s 2/10 is based on 3 reviews. Review counts also change daily and vary between aggregators. Treat them as a snapshot taken on 22 October 2026.

Booking, takeaway and delivery at Goiko Seville

Albareda 14 and República Argentina 26 officially offer booking, delivery and takeaway. Goiko takes bookings at goiko.com/es/reservas and delivery orders at goiko.com/es/delivery. The official pages for Eduardo Dato 79 and Lagoh don’t list these services. For those two, check the booking and delivery pages directly.

Goiko’s story: from Madrid to Seville

Goiko is a Spanish burger chain founded in Madrid in 2013 by Andoni Goicoechea, a Venezuelan doctor of Basque descent. He started it with a €50,000 loan from his father and created the menu with his sister. Every restaurant is company-run, with no franchises (Goiko).

Seville came early. Alimarket reported that Goiko ended 2017 by opening its second restaurant in Seville, “in the centre of the city” (Alimarket). I couldn’t confirm the date or address of the first one. In June 2022 Goiko had 98 restaurants in Spain, 11 of them in Andalusia and 3 in Seville (Alimarket). By April 2023 it had 13 in Andalusia, still with 3 in Seville (Alimarket). Lagoh is the fourth. In October 2026 the official store locator lists 114 restaurants. Our Goiko brand profile has the full history and figures.

Is Goiko worth it in Seville?

Yes, if you want a reliable gourmet burger with long hours and online booking. Gastroranking ranks the 3 established addresses 1st, 2nd and 3rd among Seville’s burger restaurants, and two of them serve a burger you won’t find anywhere else. My advice: go to República Argentina 26 for La Macarena, or to Albareda 14 for La Chicki. Eat in rather than ordering delivery, because that’s where most of the complaints come from. FAQ: Goiko Seville

How many Goiko restaurants are there in Seville? Four, all with official pages on goiko.com on 22 October 2026: Albareda 14, República Argentina 26, Eduardo Dato 79 and the Lagoh shopping centre.

Which Goiko is closest to Seville Cathedral and the historic centre? Goiko Albareda, at Calle Albareda 14 (41001), in the Alfalfa part of the Casco Antiguo.

What are Goiko Seville’s opening hours? Albareda 14, República Argentina 26 and Eduardo Dato 79 open Monday to Wednesday 13:00–23:30 and Thursday to Sunday 12:00–00:00. Lagoh opens Monday and Tuesday 12:00–23:30, Wednesday to Sunday 12:00–00:00. Goiko notes that hours may vary.

Is Goiko open on Sundays and late at night in Seville? Yes. All 4 restaurants open on Sundays from noon to midnight, so a 10 pm summer dinner works at any of them. Early in the week, most close at 11:30 pm.

Do I need to book at Goiko Seville? Booking is optional. Albareda 14 and República Argentina 26 officially accept bookings, made at goiko.com/es/reservas.

How much does a meal at Goiko Seville cost? Under €20 per person at all 4 addresses, according to Gastroranking. Goiko’s online menu doesn’t show prices.

Which burgers are exclusive to Goiko in Seville? La Chicki, served only at Albareda 14, and La Macarena, served only at República Argentina 26.

What is Goiko’s most famous burger? The Kevin Bacon, the chain’s best-seller according to Tendance Hôtellerie: a beef patty with bacon minced into it, topped with crispy onion and cheese.

Does Goiko have vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free options in Seville? There’s a vegetarian burger, the Veggie Mercury, and a plant-based patty can replace the beef. A vegan burger isn’t confirmed. The gluten-free section is offered only in some restaurants, so ask on site.

Does Goiko deliver in Seville? Yes. Albareda 14 and República Argentina 26 list delivery, ordered through goiko.com/es/delivery. Reviews praise the fast deliveries from Eduardo Dato and complain about late ones from Albareda.

Which Goiko in Seville has a terrace? Only Goiko Lagoh lists a terrace on its official page, and dogs are allowed on it.

Is Goiko a Spanish chain? Yes. Goiko was founded in Madrid in 2013 by Andoni Goicoechea, and all its restaurants are company-run.
