Gburger Ottawa: Chef Matt Hinds, Hours and Menu

How a pandemic pop-up at Gitanes on Elgin Street grew into one of Ottawa's best burger spots, now in the old John's Diner on Wellington West and in Manotick.

By Olivia, Restaurants writer, Canada and the US NortheastPublished November 20, 2026

Gburger Ottawa (officially Gitanes Burger) is run by chef and co-owner Matt Hinds and co-owner Nader Salib, and it now has 2 locations. The first is 1365 Wellington St. W in Westboro, inside the former John’s Diner. The second is 1160 Beaverwood Rd. in Manotick. What sets it apart is the sourcing: the beef comes from 1 named farm, Enright Cattle Co. in Tweed, Ontario. And the signature Gitanes burger carries Époisses cheese and bordelaise sauce, 2 things you rarely meet at an order-at-the-counter place. I checked every address, hour and source below on October 29, 2026.

Who is behind Gburger: chef Matt Hinds and Nader Salib

Gburger has 2 co-owners: chef Matthew (Matt) Hinds, who runs the kitchen, and Nader Salib, managing director of Gitanes. They split the work cleanly. Hinds handles the food, and Salib handled the leases and the business side.

Matt Hinds, chef and co-owner

Matt Hinds is Gburger’s head chef and business partner, and he has been there since the first burger in 2020. Before that he was sous-chef at Gitanes, the Elgin Street restaurant where Gburger was born. He also cooked at Paisano’s in Manotick, and he comes from fine dining, according to the Ottawa Business Journal (Jordan Pizzuti, December 16, 2025) and Tastet. Neither source names the fine-dining kitchens he worked in, so I won’t guess.

Nader Salib, co-owner and Gitanes managing director

Nader Salib is Gburger’s co-owner and the managing director of Gitanes. He signed the original lease at 380 Elgin Street, Gburger’s home until June 2025 (OBJ, Kitchissippi Times).

A note on Steve Mitton

Steve Mitton is not the chef behind Gburger. No source I found ties him to it. Mitton co-founded Murray Street Kitchen Wine Charcuterie, which closed at the end of 2016, and later joined The Whalesbone Group. The Gburger chef is Matt Hinds.

From pandemic pop-up to neighbourhood diner: the Gburger story

Gburger started in 2020 as a takeout pop-up inside Gitanes on Elgin Street. In 2025 it became a counter-service diner on Wellington Street West. The pop-up was there to keep staff working and local farms paid during COVID, and the target was modest: 20 burgers a night (OBJ).

On Elgin, it first ran as a wine bar with high-end burgers. People came for the burgers and mostly skipped the wine. So the team rebuilt the model around what was selling: a quick-service “throwback diner with modern upgrades”, as OBJ put it in its profile of Hinds.

Why Gburger left Elgin Street

Gburger left 380 Elgin Street, at the corner of Gladstone Avenue, at the end of the week of June 20, 2025, because the lease was up. The building’s main staircase had also recently collapsed. “It’s been 10 years and it kind of just flew by,” Salib told OBJ. “The lease is up and we had to make a decision.” Those 10 years refer to Salib’s time on the lease, not to Gburger, which dates from 2020. Some listings, 613today among them, put the move in 2024 or say Gburger spent “over a decade” on Elgin. Neither is right.

What replaced John’s Diner on Wellington Street West

Gburger replaced John’s Diner at 1365 Wellington St. W. John’s Family Diner opened in 1974 and closed in April 2025 after 51 years (CTV News Ottawa). Gburger kept the original booths and the coffee machine, according to OBJ and Tastet. I think that was the smart call. Westboro regulars lost a 51-year-old diner, and they get to sit in the same booths, only with a chef’s burger on the plate.

You order at the counter and then sit down. There is a children’s area, and the room is set up for families. “It’ll be a little more of a traditional burger model,” Salib told OBJ before the move. “We’ll still have a strong beverage program, cocktails and wine, but everything will be in a takeaway format.” He meant the service style, counter orders and packaging. Both locations do list dine-in.

The Gitanes burger and the rest of the Gburger menu

The Gitanes burger is the one to order: a 6-oz patty cooked medium, Époisses cheese, a charred and lightly pickled red onion, and bordelaise sauce. The patty weight and doneness come from Tastet’s review. Époisses is a pungent washed-rind cheese from Burgundy. Bordelaise is the classic French red wine and shallot sauce. Most Ottawa burger counters would never stock either, and the pairing tells you where Hinds trained.

Tastet lists these other dishes on the menu:

  • L’Américaine: a double patty with cheese and bacon.
  • French onion rings, served with a dip in the style of French onion soup.
  • Poutines.
  • Rotating chef specials.
  • Kwinter’s all-beef hot dogs, split, griddled and served on soft potato rolls.

Gburger publishes no price list online. OpenTable puts it in its “CAN$30 and under” band, and 613today rates it “$$”. As everywhere in Ontario, the 13% HST is added at the till.

Vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options

Gburger has vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options, according to OpenTable and Find Me Gluten Free. Those are third-party listings. If you have an allergy or celiac disease, ask at the counter before you order.

Drinks: Ontario wine, local beer and kombucha

Gburger serves canned wine from small Ontario producers, beer (including non-alcoholic), cocktails, kombucha, tea and sodas. Tastet names local makers such as Dominion City, Beyond the Pale, Revel Cider, Artizen Kombucha and Zamalek.

Where Gburger gets its beef: Enright Cattle Co., Tweed

Gburger’s beef comes from Enright Cattle Co., a farm in Tweed, Ontario (OBJ, Tastet). It is a fourth-generation family farm just south of Tweed, run by Kara and Darold Enright. They raise purebred black and red Simmental cattle without artificial hormones and graze them rotationally (Canadian Cattlemen, Hastings County).

Illustrative photo, not taken at Enright Cattle Co.: a red-and-white Simmental cow grazing in Magog, Quebec. Enright raises purebred black and red Simmental cattle in Tweed, Ontario, for Gburger’s beef. (Photo: Hayden Soloviev, CC BY 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Simmental\_cattle\_in\_a\_field\_in\_Magog,\_Quebec.jpg (6000×4000))

About 95% of Enright’s beef goes to high-end restaurants in Toronto, Ottawa and Kingston. In other words, a Gburger patty comes from the same farm that supplies Ontario’s fine-dining kitchens. Ottawa Tourism adds that the beef is ground fresh daily. You may see “grass-fed” elsewhere, but no source says so, and Enright grows crops to feed its herd. I’m leaving that label off.

Is Gburger one of the best burgers in Ottawa?

Yes, on sourcing and on ambition. Ottawa Tourism includes Gburger (Gitanes Burger) in its list of Ottawa’s 8 best burger spots and describes an “upscale diner experience” with a chef-inspired menu. My reasoning is simpler. I know of few counters in this city that can name the farm their beef comes from, and fewer still that would put Époisses on a fast-casual burger. It is the opposite of a national chain like A&W at the Rideau Centre. This is a local kitchen with a chef’s name on the door.

Gburger Ottawa FAQ

Who owns Gburger in Ottawa?

Gburger is co-owned by chef Matt Hinds and Nader Salib, the managing director of Gitanes.

Who is the chef at Gburger (Gitanes Burger)?

Matt Hinds is the head chef. He was sous-chef at Gitanes, has been with Gburger since it started in 2020, and comes from fine dining.

Where is Gburger located now that it has left Elgin Street?

Gburger is at 1365 Wellington St. W in Westboro and at 1160 Beaverwood Rd., Building F, Unit 4A, in Manotick. It left 380 Elgin Street in June 2025.

What replaced John’s Diner on Wellington Street West?

Gburger took over the former John’s Family Diner at 1365 Wellington St. W after the diner closed in April 2025, and kept its original booths and coffee machine.

What is on the Gitanes burger?

A 6-oz patty cooked medium, Époisses cheese, a charred and lightly pickled red onion, and bordelaise sauce, according to Tastet.

Where does Gburger get its beef?

From Enright Cattle Co., a fourth-generation family farm in Tweed, Ontario, that raises Simmental cattle without artificial hormones.

Is Gburger the same restaurant as Gitanes?

No. Gburger began in 2020 as a pop-up inside Gitanes on Elgin Street and is now its own burger restaurant. Its official name is Gitanes Burger, and Nader Salib is involved in both.

Does Gburger have vegan or gluten-free burgers?

OpenTable and Find Me Gluten Free list vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options. Confirm with the restaurant if you have an allergy.

What are Gburger’s hours in Wellington West and Manotick?

Wellington West is open Sunday to Tuesday from 11:30 a.m. to 8 p.m. and Wednesday to Saturday from 11:30 a.m. to 9 p.m. Manotick is open Sunday to Thursday from 11:30 a.m. to 8 p.m. and Friday and Saturday from 11:30 a.m. to 9 p.m.

Does Gburger serve alcohol, take reservations or deliver?

Yes, it serves cocktails, canned Ontario wine and local beer. It offers dine-in, takeout and delivery, plus private bookings and catering through catering.gitanesburger.com. Ordering is at the counter.

What is the best burger in Ottawa?

Ottawa Tourism includes Gburger in its list of the city’s 8 best burger spots. Start with its Gitanes burger: Ontario beef, Époisses and bordelaise. Our Ottawa hub covers the rest of the city.
