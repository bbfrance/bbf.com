Best Burgers in Cape Town: 10 Top Spots for 2026

The 10 best burgers in Cape Town for 2026: Zuney, ranked world No. 2 by Time Out, R99 smash burgers at Le Pickle, rand prices and halaal picks.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished November 27, 2026

The best burger in Cape Town is at Zuney Wagyu Burgers, 69A Kloof Street, Gardens: Time Out ranked it the second-best burger in the world in May 2026. Brash in Sea Point comes next, as the highest African entry on the World’s 101 Best Burger Places 2026. For value, go to Le Pickle, where every burger costs R99. I built this top 10 from the two global burger rankings of 2026, Time Out Cape Town’s burger guide of 28 May 2026 and each restaurant’s own pages. I checked the prices and hours I could confirm on 20 November 2026. Where I couldn’t, the entry says so.

How I ranked Cape Town’s burger joints

This ranking is my own editorial call, built from published, dated sources. It is not a paid list, and it is not an average of star ratings. I gave the most weight to the two global rankings of 2026: Time Out’s World’s Best Burgers, published on 28 May 2026, and the World’s 101 Best Burger Places 2026. After that I used Richard Holmes’s The 24 Best Burgers in Cape Town for Time Out (updated 28 May 2026), local food press from 2024 to 2026 and each restaurant’s official site for addresses, hours and prices.

I have not yet recorded Google ratings or review counts. Where a fact rests on a single source, or I could not confirm it for November 2026, the entry says so, and where a restaurant does not publish a price, I don’t give one. Two picks, Sho Sho and The Dog’s Bollocks, are on the list because Time Out still rated them in May 2026. I could not confirm that either is trading this month.

1. Zuney Wagyu Burgers, Gardens: the world’s second-best burger

Zuney Wagyu Burgers, at 69A Kloof Street in Gardens, is the best burger in Cape Town: Time Out ranked it second in the world in May 2026, behind only Smash Things in Tokyo. Zuney also came 79th on the World’s 101 Best Burger Places 2026, which makes it the only Cape Town burger joint on both global lists (Time Out).

Zuney runs from farm to bun. Its founders, Henning Klopper and Devin Wells, are the sons of Henk Klopper and Denys Wells, who have raised pasture-fed Wagyu in the Zuney valley in the Eastern Cape since 2014. The brand sold its meat online before it opened on Kloof Street in late 2023 (Daily Maverick).

Chefs Pete Goffe-Wood and Ollie Swart advised on the recipe. They pushed it towards less ketchup and more mustard, so the beef leads. The bun is brioche, and the fries are cooked twice in Wagyu tallow. Brash beats Zuney on the 101 Best list, 51st to 79th. Zuney still tops mine, because it is the only Cape Town name on both global rankings and the only spot here that serves its founders’ own herd.

  • Address: 69A Kloof Street, Gardens, Cape Town
  • Prices (official site, checked 20 Nov 2026): Classic R120, Cheese R130, Wacon R180, Vegan R160
  • Hours: Sunday to Wednesday 11:00–22:00, Thursday to Saturday 11:00–01:00
  • Bookings: walk-in only, no reservations
  • Also at: Time Out Market Cape Town, V&A Waterfront (since 6 March 2026), and Nine Yards in Johannesburg
  • Website: zuneyburgers.com

Older guides list 69A Kloof Street as a Hudsons address. It is Zuney’s now. Hudsons Gardens is at 22 Kloof Street.

2. Brash, Sea Point: Africa’s top entry on the World’s 101 Best

Brash, at 90 Regent Road in Sea Point, is the best smash burger in Cape Town by global ranking: it placed 51st on the World’s 101 Best Burger Places 2026. Brash and Zuney are the only African entries on that list, and Brash is the higher of the two.

Brash started as a food truck run by two friends. They later took over the former Johnny Shawarma premises and turned it into a retro diner with red lino (Cape Town Magazine, 17 September 2024). The smash burgers come with American cheddar, onions, pickles, mustard and ketchup on a brioche bun. Order the Classic, or the Mack Daddy if one patty won’t do.

  • Address: 90 Regent Road, Sea Point, Cape Town
  • Phone: +27 21 002 8376
  • Prices: not published on the official site
  • Website: brashburger.com, Instagram @brashburger

3. Le Pickle: R99 smash burgers and halaal-friendly in three locations

Le Pickle is the best-value burger in Cape Town: every burger on the menu costs R99, and all three locations are halaal friendly. That flat price covers Le Classic, Le Onion, Le Godfather and the chicken, lamb and vegetarian burgers (Time Out, 30 September 2026).

Pieter Aucamp, Vincent Viviers, Trenton Devine and Enrico Ferigolli founded Le Pickle in March 2025. Buns, pickles and sauces are all made in-house. Claremont followed in July 2026, and Bree Street on 2 October 2026. For R99, with everything made on site, it is the best deal in this guide. Zuney’s cheapest burger costs R21 more.

  • De Waterkant: Shop 2, Cape Quarter Lifestyle Village, 8 Jarvis Street
  • City Centre: 215 Bree Street. Tuesday to Saturday 12:00–22:00, Sunday 12:00–21:00, closed Monday
  • Claremont: 13 Corwen Street, collection and delivery only
  • Website: lepickle.co.za, Instagram @lepickle_za

4. Sho Sho Burgers, Loop Street: the Springboks’ burger joint

Sho Sho Burgers, at 32 Loop Street in the City Centre, serves smash and bistro burgers from a team that includes the Springboks Cheslin Kolbe and Ox Nché. Their partners are Hanno Pienaar, of Cheese Louise Social Club in Pretoria, and Werner Neitz (Time Out, 2 October 2025).

The menu runs from the Truffle Berry Burger to a chicken burger with atchar. Time Out’s May 2026 guide lists a R79 combo, the cheapest burger meal in this guide.

  • Address: 32 Loop Street, City Centre, Cape Town
  • Hours (at launch): Monday 12:00–20:00, Tuesday to Thursday 12:00–23:30, Friday and Saturday 12:00–01:00, closed Sunday
  • Instagram: @shosho_burgers

Check before you go. As of 20 November 2026, Sho Sho’s official site lists only Durban and Pretoria, not Loop Street, and I could not confirm that the Loop Street branch is trading this month.

5. The Dog’s Bollocks at YARD, Gardens: 200 g burgers after dark

The Dog’s Bollocks is a night-only burger counter at 6 Roodehek Street in Gardens, near Buitenkant Street, serving 200 g burgers in a bare-bones room. By day the venue is The Bitch’s Tits, and from 17:00 it becomes The Dog’s Bollocks (Cape Town Magazine). Time Out ranked it fourth in Cape Town in May 2026.

  • Address: 6 Roodehek Street, Gardens, Cape Town
  • Hours: Monday to Saturday 17:00–22:00
  • Prices: R85–R140 (Cape Town Magazine and Time Out)

Check before you go. I found no source dated after May 2026, no official website and no verified Instagram account, so treat the hours and prices as unconfirmed.

6. Conscious Carnivores, V&A Waterfront: certified halaal Wagyu

Conscious Carnivores, at 8 Dock Road on the V&A Waterfront, is the best Wagyu burger in Cape Town for halaal diners: it is halaal certified and cooks only over charcoal. Mohamed Mohidien co-founded it at the Makers Landing food incubator, and it opened as its own venue in late 2024 (Time Out).

The OG Burger stacks a 200 g Wagyu patty, cheddar, maple-glazed “macon”, horseradish aioli and smoked paprika tomato sauce on brioche.

  • Address: 8 Dock Road, V&A Waterfront, Cape Town
  • Hours and prices: not verified as of 20 November 2026

7. Smasht., Belvedere Road: the Southern Suburbs smash burger

Smasht., at 54 Belvedere Road in the Southern Suburbs, makes a classic smash burger with 100 g grass-fed beef patties. Founder Tameem Jakoet started in Crawford before he moved to Belvedere Road (Cape Town ETC, 29 December 2024).

The Classic comes single, double or triple, with cheese, onions, pickles, mustard and ketchup on a brioche bun. Time Out lists the beef as halaal. It is the only source I found on halaal status, so ask in store.

  • Address: 54 Belvedere Road, Cape Town (Time Out places it in Claremont, Cape Town ETC in Rondebosch)
  • Prices: not published
  • Instagram: @smashtburgers

8. Aegir Project, Noordhoek: a burger with a sea view

Aegir Project, at 65 Beach Road in Noordhoek, is the pick for a burger with a sea view and a craft beer. Rory and Carey Lancellas opened the brewery in 2015 (Eat Out). The cheeseburger pairs grass-fed beef with aged cheddar on Hokkaido-style milk buns baked on site, and parents get a fenced play area for the children.

  • Address: 65 Beach Road, Noordhoek, Cape Town
  • Hours and prices: not verified for 2026. Confirm it is open before you make the drive.

9. Hudsons The Burger Joint: Cape Town’s burger institution since 2009

Hudsons The Burger Joint has been around longer than anyone else on this list. David Raad and Ross Collins opened it on Kloof Street in 2009, modelled on New York’s Burger Joint, and it now has four Cape Town locations open daily until 23:30 (Hudsons). It does not appear on either global list. Still, four branches open that late make it the most reliable fallback in this guide.

  • Gardens: 22 Kloof Street
  • Green Point: corner of Main Road and Upper Portswood Road
  • Claremont: 25 Protea Road
  • Muizenberg: 70 Beach Road, opens from 09:00
  • Hours: daily 12:00–23:30
  • Menu: the official site has a menu dated 25 May 2026

10. Clarke’s Bar & Dining Room, Bree Street: the bistro cheeseburger

Clarke’s, at 133 Bree Street, serves a bistro cheeseburger with grass-fed beef, local havarti and red onion jam. Lyndall Maunder opened it in 2011 and named it after her mother’s maiden name (Time Out). Get the house-made kimchi on the side.

  • Address: 133 Bree Street, City Centre, Cape Town
  • Hours and prices: not verified for 2026. Confirm it is open before you go.

Best smash burgers in Cape Town

The best smash burger in Cape Town is at Brash, ranked 51st in the world in 2026. Le Pickle gives the best value at R99, Smasht. is the pick in the Southern Suburbs, and Sho Sho is worth a try if Loop Street is still trading. Elsewhere, the style is done well at Honbo in Hong Kong and Uniburger in Montreal. To make one at home, try Alice’s Sonic All-American Smasher copycat recipe.

Halaal burgers in Cape Town

For halaal burgers in Cape Town, go to Conscious Carnivores, which is halaal certified, or Le Pickle, whose three locations are halaal friendly. Time Out also lists halaal beef at Smasht., but no second source confirms it.

Halaal burgers in Cape Town compared: Conscious Carnivores (8 Dock Road, V&A Waterfront) is halaal certified; Le Pickle’s three locations (De Waterkant, Bree Street, Claremont) are halaal friendly; Smasht. (54 Belvedere Road) is listed with halaal beef by Time Out only, so confirm in store. (Table: Burger Breton, sources Time Out Cape Town (28 May 2026 and 30 September 2026) and Le Pickle official site)

Wagyu burgers in Cape Town

The two Wagyu burgers to try in Cape Town are Zuney’s, made with beef from the founders’ own Eastern Cape herd, and the charcoal-grilled 200 g OG Burger at Conscious Carnivores. For a European take on a Wagyu burger, see my colleague Sofia’s review of Ron Gastrobar in Amsterdam.

Beyond the top 10: South African chains and nearby towns

South Africa’s own chains are worth knowing too. Start with the RocoMamas brand profile and its Stellenbosch branch. Out in the Winelands, De Vrije Burger in Stellenbosch also has a Cape Town branch at 7 Penarth Road, Sea Point. If you fly on to Gauteng, see my guides to Capital Craft in Pretoria and RocoMamas in Johannesburg.

If a pick above turns out to be closed, these are my reserves: Burger & Lobster (105 Bree Street), Iron Steak and Bar (114 Bree Street), Smokin Joe’s and Izzy Burger (both halaal), Jerry’s Burger Bar, and Redemption Burgers at the Old Biscuit Mill.

FAQ: burgers in Cape Town

Where can I eat the best burger in Cape Town?

Zuney Wagyu Burgers, at 69A Kloof Street in Gardens, serves the best burger in Cape Town. Time Out ranked it second in the world in May 2026. Brash in Sea Point (51st on the World’s 101 Best Burger Places 2026) and Le Pickle (R99 for any burger) complete the top three.

Which Cape Town burger was ranked the world’s second best in 2026?

Zuney Wagyu Burgers was ranked second in Time Out’s World’s Best Burgers on 28 May 2026, behind Smash Things in Tokyo. You can try it on Kloof Street in Gardens or at the Time Out Market Cape Town on the V&A Waterfront. Read the full ranking in our world’s best burgers 2026 roundup.

How much does a burger cost in Cape Town?

At the top spots in Cape Town, a burger costs R99 to R180 on the official menus I checked on 20 November 2026: every burger at Le Pickle is R99, and Zuney runs from R120 to R180. Time Out listed a R79 combo at Sho Sho in May 2026, and The Dog’s Bollocks is listed at R85–R140, but I could not confirm either price this month.

Where can I find halaal burgers in Cape Town?

Conscious Carnivores on the V&A Waterfront is halaal certified, and all three Le Pickle locations are halaal friendly. Time Out lists Smasht. on Belvedere Road with halaal beef, so confirm in store.

What burgers can I eat at the V&A Waterfront or Time Out Market Cape Town?

At the V&A Waterfront, Zuney has had a stand at the Time Out Market Cape Town since 6 March 2026. Conscious Carnivores, at 8 Dock Road, serves charcoal-grilled halaal Wagyu burgers.

Which Cape Town burger joints are open late?

Zuney on Kloof Street serves until 01:00 from Thursday to Saturday. At launch, Sho Sho listed hours until 01:00 on Friday and Saturday, and all four Hudsons locations open daily until 23:30.

Do I need to book at Cape Town burger restaurants?

Zuney on Kloof Street takes no reservations, so just walk in. None of my other sources gives a booking policy, so check with the restaurant.

Where can I eat a burger with a sea view in Cape Town?

Aegir Project, at 65 Beach Road in Noordhoek, pairs burgers and craft beer with a sea view. Hudsons Muizenberg, at 70 Beach Road, opens from 09:00, and Brash is in seaside Sea Point.
