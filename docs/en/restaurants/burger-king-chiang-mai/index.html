Burger King Chiang Mai: 24/7 Branch, Menu, Prices

Burger King Chiang Mai: the 24-hour branch at Tha Phae Gate, the airport outlet, Thai-only menu items and Whopper prices in baht, all sourced and dated.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished October 24, 2026

The Burger King in Chiang Mai that most travelers want is Burger King Thapae, at 2-6 Ratchadamnoen Rd, right by Tha Phae Gate on the east side of the Old City. It never closes: three listings agree it is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. A second branch serves the airport (CNX), on the 2nd floor of the domestic terminal, from 06:00 to 22:00 according to Wongnai. The last Thai prices I could verify (November 2024) put a Whopper at 189 THB. I checked every fact below on 24 October 2026.

Burger King Tha Phae Gate: address and how to get there

Burger King Thapae is at 2-6 Ratchadamnoen Rd, just inside Tha Phae Gate, at GPS 18.788007, 98.993098 according to Chiang Mai Locator. Its Thai listing name is เบอร์เกอร์คิง เชียงใหม่ ประตูท่าแพ. Show that to a driver and you will not need to explain.

Finding it is easy. Tha Phae Gate is the eastern entrance to the Old City, and the restaurant sits on the ground floor of the Hotel M building, next to The Coffee Club.

Tha Phae Gate, the brick east gate of Chiang Mai’s Old City. The yellow Hotel M sign rises behind the wall, and the building below that sign houses Burger King Thapae at 2-6 Ratchadamnoen Rd. (Photo: Jakub Hałun, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:20171105\_Tha\_Phae\_Gate\_Chiang\_Mai\_9784\_DxO.jpg)

  • Sunday evening: the Ratchadamnoen walking street starts at the gate, so expect crowds outside.
  • Night Bazaar: Chang Khlan Rd is about 1 km east.
  • Getting there: Chiang Mai has no metro. Use a red songthaew, a tuk-tuk or a ride-hailing app.

Burger King Chiang Mai opening hours: is Tha Phae really 24 hours?

Yes. Wanderlog, Wongnai and Restaurant Guru all list Burger King Thapae as open 24 hours a day, every day. In the Old City that is rare. When the bars close, this is often the only kitchen still working near the gate.

The airport branch is a different story. Wongnai gives 06:00–22:00 daily. Some directories claim 24 hours, but they seem to be mixing it up with the Central Chiang Mai Airport shopping mall, which is a separate place. Airport hours bend to the flight schedule, so do not plan a 23:00 dinner there.

Burger King Chiang Mai airport (CNX): eating before a flight

Burger King at Chiang Mai International Airport is on the 2nd floor of the domestic terminal, open 06:00–22:00 daily according to Wongnai. The same listing adds:

  • Phone: +66 53 922 422 (053-922-422 from a Thai number)
  • Seating: 11 to 40 seats
  • Payment: credit card and TrueMoney Wallet
  • Price range: 101 to 250 THB
  • Wongnai rating: 3.4/5 from 29 ratings

Thai-only items: pork, sticky rice and tom yum chicken

In Thailand, several burgers come with a choice of beef or pork patty, the Whopper Jr. included. The menu also lists sticky rice with grilled pork in the moo ping style, and fried chicken seasoned Hat Yai or tom yum style. These items are why I would walk in. A pork Whopper Jr. and a piece of tom yum chicken say more about how Thailand eats fast food than one more beef burger.

One caveat. These items are documented nationally, but I could not confirm which are on sale in Chiang Mai today, and some run as limited-time offers.

Vegetarian option: the Plant-based Whopper

Burger King Thailand launched the Plant-based Whopper on 1 March 2021, with a soy patty from v2food, in about 100 restaurants (Green Queen). Launch prices were 165 THB alone or 219 THB as a meal, and they are surely higher now. It comes with regular mayonnaise: vegetarian, not vegan. Wanderlog reviewers mention ordering it at Tha Phae.

What reviewers praise (Wanderlog):

  • the location by Tha Phae Gate and the 24-hour opening
  • friendly staff who speak English
  • a clean, well air-conditioned dining room and an outdoor terrace
  • cheap filter coffee
  • the Plant-based Whopper on the menu

What reviewers criticize (Wanderlog):

  • uneven cleanliness
  • burnt buns and cold patties
  • incomplete or wrong orders

My take: 4.1 from more than 1,300 Google reviews is about what a well-placed chain branch earns. Nobody crosses Chiang Mai for it. Plenty of people are glad it is there at 3 a.m. Wrong orders come up often enough that I would check the bag before leaving the counter.

Burger King Thapae services, delivery and payment

Restaurant Guru lists an outdoor terrace, Wi-Fi, wheelchair access and delivery at Burger King Thapae. I found nothing reliable on payment methods, parking or self-order kiosks there, so I make no claim. Card and TrueMoney Wallet are confirmed only at the airport.

For delivery, Minor Food retired its historic 1112 phone line in favor of online ordering at delivery.burgerking.co.th, run with Lalamove, and its 1112 Delivery app (Brand Inside, Minor Food). That is the national setup. Whether it, GrabFood or LINE MAN covers your address from Tha Phae is not confirmed. In the Old City, frankly, walking to the gate is usually faster.

Who runs Burger King in Thailand

Burger King in Thailand is operated by Minor Food, part of Bangkok-based Minor International, which brought the brand to the country in 2000. As of 31 December 2024, Minor Food ran 130 Burger King restaurants across Thailand, the Maldives and the Seychelles. When the first Chiang Mai branch opened, I could not find out.

The chain itself was born in 1953 in Jacksonville as Insta-Burger King, was relaunched in Miami by James McLamore and David Edgerton, and has belonged to Restaurant Brands International since 2014. Our Burger King brand profile covers the full history and figures.

Burger King or a local burger in Chiang Mai?

Burger King wins on hours and location; a local burger bar wins on the burger. At 2 a.m. near Tha Phae Gate, go to Burger King Thapae. With a free evening, I would eat elsewhere: start with my guide to The Duke’s, Chiang Mai’s burger favorite, then browse every pick on our Chiang Mai burger hub.

FAQ: Burger King Chiang Mai

Is Burger King at Tha Phae Gate really open 24 hours? Yes. Wanderlog, Wongnai and Restaurant Guru all list Burger King Thapae, 2-6 Ratchadamnoen Rd, as open 24/7 (checked 24 October 2026).

How many Burger King restaurants are there in Chiang Mai? Two are confirmed: Tha Phae Gate and the airport. Restaurant Guru mentions seven other branches without naming them, and listings for Chang Khlan Rd and an Esso drive-thru in Hang Dong could not be verified.

Is there a Burger King at Chiang Mai airport? Yes, on the 2nd floor of the domestic terminal. Wongnai lists it as open 06:00–22:00 daily; hours can shift with flights.

How much is a Whopper in Thailand? The last verified price was 189 THB for a Whopper (Thaiest, November 2024). The Double Whopper Cheese was 289 THB and the Whopper Jr. 149 THB.

Which Burger King items are only in Thailand? Pork patties on several burgers, sticky rice with moo ping-style grilled pork, Hat Yai and tom yum fried chicken, rice plates and breakfast congee. Availability in Chiang Mai is not confirmed item by item.

Does Burger King Chiang Mai have a vegetarian option? The Plant-based Whopper (soy patty by v2food) has been sold in Thailand since March 2021, and reviewers mention it at Tha Phae. It contains regular mayonnaise, so it is not vegan.

Can I get Burger King delivered in Chiang Mai? Burger King Thailand delivers through delivery.burgerking.co.th and Minor Food’s 1112 Delivery app, and Restaurant Guru lists delivery for Tha Phae. Check coverage for your address in the app.

What payment methods does Burger King Chiang Mai accept? Wongnai confirms credit cards and TrueMoney Wallet at the airport. Payment methods at Tha Phae are not documented, so carry some cash just in case.
