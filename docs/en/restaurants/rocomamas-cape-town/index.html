RocoMamas Cape Town: 14 Branches, Prices, Hours

Where to eat RocoMamas in Cape Town, which branch suits you, what to order and how much it costs, with every fact checked on 16 November 2026.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished November 27, 2026

RocoMamas has 14 restaurants in greater Cape Town, according to the brand’s official Western Cape list, which I checked on 16 November 2026. My advice is short. Staying in the City Bowl? Go to RocoMamas Long Street (Shop 3, 14 Long Street): it’s the only branch in the city centre, it’s halaal and it opens at 08:00 every day. Out shopping? RocoMamas Canal Walk in Century City serves until 23:00 on Fridays and Saturdays. On the national South African menu, burgers cost R69 to R165.

Which RocoMamas in Cape Town should you choose?

Pick the branch that fits where you are: Long Street for the city centre, Canal Walk for shopping, and Long Street, Kenilworth Centre, Vangate Mall or Westgate if you need a halaal kitchen.

  • Visitors in the city centre: Long Street. It’s the only RocoMamas in the CBD and it’s within walking distance of Cape Town station.
  • Shopping day: Canal Walk in Century City. Two MyCiTi stops, a train station and the mall car park make it easy to reach.
  • Halaal: Long Street, Kenilworth Centre, Vangate Mall (Bridgetown) and Westgate (Mitchells Plain). These are the four branches the brand labels ‘Halaal’.
  • Northern suburbs: Durbanville, Tableview, Cape Gate, Willowbridge, N1 City and Okavango Crossing.

If I had one RocoMamas meal in Cape Town, I’d have it at Long Street. It’s the newest branch, it scores half a point higher than Canal Walk on Google, and you can walk there from the station. Canal Walk is the better call on a Friday or Saturday night, when it keeps its kitchen open an hour later.

A warning for anyone using an old map: RocoMamas The Harrington, at 29 Buitenkant Street, has closed permanently. It used to be the city-centre favourite, with 4.2/5 from 1,760 Google reviews according to Wanderlog, and it’s gone from the brand’s official list. Some search results also show a ‘RocoMamas Green Point’. It isn’t on the official list either, so I don’t count it as open.

RocoMamas Long Street: address, opening hours and services

RocoMamas Long Street is at Shop 3, 14 Long Street, Cape Town City Centre, 8000. It’s open from 08:00 to 22:00 Monday to Saturday and from 08:00 to 21:00 on Sunday.

The brand’s official page and its local page agree on those hours. You can phone the branch on 021 023 4959 or email longstreet@rocomamas.com. Don’t use howzit@rocomamas.co.za for a branch question: that’s the brand’s general address.

Services at Long Street

Long Street offers dine-in, takeaway, delivery and online ordering, and it holds a liquor licence. The brand’s local page also lists contactless delivery and Wi-Fi. The official branch page confirms dine-in, takeaway, online ordering and the Halaal label.

A halaal branch that opened in August 2025

RocoMamas Long Street opened in early August 2025, set up from day one as a halaal restaurant. Bizcommunity’s 18 August 2025 report puts the opening on that month’s First Thursday, with a tasting for press and content creators, DJ sets and a ‘Make Your Own Smash Burger’ workshop. The halal directory Zabihah gives 7 August 2025 as the date. The press release only says ‘First Thursday’, so I’m not treating 7 August as confirmed. The brand labels the branch ‘Halaal’ without naming a certifying body, so I don’t name one either.

Getting to RocoMamas Long Street

You can walk to 14 Long Street from Cape Town station and the MyCiTi bus routes. The building is a grade-A office block with shops on the ground floor, near the bottom of Long Street in the CBD, according to its letting agent. I couldn’t find a reliable source on parking near the building. Come on foot or by bus.

RocoMamas Canal Walk: address, opening hours and how to get there

RocoMamas Canal Walk is at Shop 152B, Canal Walk, Century Boulevard, Century City, 7441. It’s open from 09:00 to 22:00 Monday to Thursday, 09:00 to 23:00 on Friday and Saturday, and 09:00 to 21:00 on Sunday.

It offers dine-in, takeaway and online ordering, and it holds a liquor licence. Phone it on 021 555 3192. Of the two branches covered in detail here, it’s the one for a late weekend dinner.

Getting to Canal Walk by MyCiTi, train or car

Two MyCiTi stops serve the mall: Canal Walk North and Canal Walk South. Century City train station and Golden Arrow buses are nearby too. Driving? A bridge leads straight to the upper levels of the mall’s car park. Sources: the Century City transport page and Canal Walk customer services, both checked on 16 November 2026.

RocoMamas Cape Town menu and prices in 2026

On the national South African menu, a RocoMamas burger costs R69 to R165, and the Old Skool costs R85 (prices checked on 16 November 2026). Every burger is built on a 150 g beef smash patty and the house RocoMayo sauce.

These prices come from the national menu at rocomamas.com/za/menu. The brand doesn’t say whether prices vary by branch, and I couldn’t check the separate halaal menu (a PDF). Use them as a budget guide, not a promise to the rand at Long Street or Canal Walk.

Sides, wings, ribs and G-Shakes

Sides and drinks start at R28 for water and R37 for regular shoestring fries.

  • Wings: 4 for R59, 8 for R115, 12 for R160.
  • Pork ribs: 250 g R130, 500 g R227, 750 g R320.
  • Fries: shoestring from R37 (regular) to R71 (sharing), crinkle-cut R45, cheese or chilli-cheese fries R57.
  • Other sides: Mac’n’Cheese R49, Nachos Classico R135 (half portion R79), chicken strips R85, Chicken Caesar Salad R120.
  • G-Shakes: R55, in Oreo, Butter Toffee, Espresso, Bubblegum, Strawberry, Blueberry, Belgian Chocolate and Peanut Butter.
  • Waffles: R52 to R65.
  • Drinks: water R28, Red Bull R46.
  • Kids: the Kid Rock menu (under 12) costs R59 to R82.

Prices rose between October and November 2026

RocoMamas raised several prices between 9 October and 16 November 2026. The Old Skool went from R79 to R85, the Classic Cheese from R99 to R105 and the Slacker from R159 to R165. Four wings went from R55 to R59, and a G-Shake from R50 to R55. In percentage terms the cheap items took the biggest hit: +10% on the G-Shake and about +8% on the Old Skool, against under 4% on the Slacker. If an unofficial menu site still shows R79 for the Old Skool, it’s quoting October prices.

What to order on a first visit

Order a single-patty burger and a G-Shake. That’s the cleanest way to judge the 150 g smash patty and the RocoMayo. At R85, the Old Skool is the one I’d pick: R16 more than the NuSkool, and R80 less than the Slacker. The Slacker (R165) stacks three 100 g patties, so it only makes sense if you’re very hungry. Feeding a picky eater? The ‘Make Your Own’ option starts from a 150 g beef or chicken smash.

Vegetarian, vegan and breakfast options

RocoMamas has no plant-based patty on its South African menu. The only dishes the menu confirms as vegetarian are the Chopped Green Salad (R40) and the Spicy Slaw (R45).

Bluntly, vegetarians will eat better elsewhere. Breakfast is a grey area. Several reviews mention breakfast at Long Street, which opens at 08:00, and ‘breakfast specials’ at Canal Walk. But the official menu has no breakfast section, so I can’t tell you what’s served or what it costs. Call the branch first.

Long Street reviews: 4.6/5 on Google

Long Street’s reviewers often name the staff who served them. They praise the burgers, the nachos, breakfast and the evening atmosphere. The complaints that come back are cold food and long waits (in February and March 2026), wrong orders, billing mistakes and loud music. Management replies to about 95% of reviews, which is rare for a chain branch. I found no TripAdvisor rating for this branch.

Canal Walk reviews: 4.1/5 on Google, 3.5/5 on TripAdvisor

Canal Walk’s fans mention juicy burgers, attentive service, clean tables, the waffles and the ginger beer G-Shake. Its critics mention the wait, service speed that changes from one visit to the next, burgers that sometimes arrive lukewarm, and value for money. On TripAdvisor it has 3.5/5 from 12 reviews. My reading: the complaints are about pace, not the food itself, so a weekday lunch is the safer bet.

Who owns RocoMamas? A short history

RocoMamas is a South African chain founded in Johannesburg. Since 2015 it has been majority-owned by Spur Corporation, a group based in Cape Town.

Brian Altriche and Paul Dempsey opened the first restaurant in July 2013 on Malibongwe Drive in Randburg, according to BusinessLIVE. Spur Corporation bought 51% of the chain in 2015 and now holds 70% of the franchise company. At 30 June 2026, RocoMamas had 115 restaurants, 92 of them in South Africa, according to Spur Corporation’s 2026 annual financial statements. For the full timeline, see our RocoMamas brand profile. For the chain’s home city, see my guide to RocoMamas in Johannesburg.

RocoMamas Cape Town FAQ

How many RocoMamas are there in Cape Town, and which is closest to the city centre?

The brand lists 14 RocoMamas restaurants in Cape Town as of 16 November 2026. The only one in the city centre is RocoMamas Long Street, at Shop 3, 14 Long Street. The Harrington branch on Buitenkant Street has closed permanently.

What are the opening hours of RocoMamas Long Street and Canal Walk?

Long Street is open from 08:00 to 22:00 Monday to Saturday and from 08:00 to 21:00 on Sunday. Canal Walk is open from 09:00 to 22:00 Monday to Thursday, 09:00 to 23:00 on Friday and Saturday, and 09:00 to 21:00 on Sunday.

Is RocoMamas halaal in Cape Town?

Four Cape Town branches carry the brand’s ‘Halaal’ label: Long Street, Kenilworth Centre, Vangate Mall and Westgate. The other ten don’t. The brand doesn’t name the certifying body, so ask the branch if that matters to you.

How much does a RocoMamas burger cost in South Africa in 2026?

On the national menu, checked on 16 November 2026, burgers cost R69 (NuSkool) to R165 (Slacker). The Old Skool costs R85 and the Classic Cheese R105. Prices may differ by branch, so treat these figures as a guide.

Does RocoMamas Cape Town deliver, and can I order online?

The official pages for Long Street and Canal Walk both offer online ordering. Only the brand’s local page for Long Street mentions delivery. I couldn’t confirm that any Cape Town branch is on Uber Eats or Mr D.

Can I book a table at RocoMamas in Cape Town?

No official source mentions table bookings at Long Street or Canal Walk. Phone the branch: 021 023 4959 for Long Street, 021 555 3192 for Canal Walk. Do the same if you need to check card payments or wheelchair access, which the official pages don’t cover either.

Does RocoMamas serve breakfast?

The official South African menu has no breakfast section. Some reviews mention breakfast at Long Street, which opens at 08:00, and breakfast specials at Canal Walk. Call ahead to confirm what’s available.

Are there vegetarian or vegan burgers at RocoMamas?

No. The South African menu has no plant-based patty. The only dishes the menu confirms as vegetarian are the Chopped Green Salad (R40) and the Spicy Slaw (R45).

Is RocoMamas a South African chain?

Yes. It was founded in Randburg, Johannesburg, in July 2013. Cape Town-based Spur Corporation bought a majority stake in 2015 and now holds 70% of the franchise company.

How do I get to RocoMamas Canal Walk by public transport?

Take MyCiTi to the Canal Walk North or Canal Walk South stop, or take the train to Century City station. Golden Arrow buses also serve the mall.
