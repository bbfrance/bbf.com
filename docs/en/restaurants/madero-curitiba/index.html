Madero Curitiba: Best Locations, Hours, Prices and Reviews

Madero was born in Curitiba in 2005 and the city has 24 locations. Which one to pick, opening hours, the R$54 cheeseburger and what diners say.

By AlexPublished October 9, 2026

Madero Curitiba is not one restaurant but 24 of them: that is how many Madero locations the chain’s official website lists, as of October 9, 2026, in the city where it began. My pick for a sit-down burger is Madero Steak House Batel (R. Bispo Dom José, 2377), rated 4.6/5 on Google from 4,675 reviews, the best score I could verify. For wine and steak, book Madero Prime Steak House in the historic center. With kids, go to Madero Steak House Comendador and its Maderoland play area. The signature cheeseburger has a national reference price of R$54, though what a given Curitiba location charges may differ.

Madero in Curitiba: the city where it all started

Curitiba is Madero’s home town. Chef Junior Durski opened the first Madero Steak House there on June 7, 2005, and the group is still based in Paraná (Grupo Madero corporate profile).

The first restaurant opened in Curitiba’s historic center, where Madero Prime Steak House stands today. Madero doesn’t say whether Prime occupies the original 2005 premises, and I couldn’t confirm it.

Then came the expansion. As of March 31, 2026, Grupo Madero ran 355 restaurants on 283 multi-brand sites across Brazil. Its brands are Madero (Steak House and Burger), Jeronimo (Smash Burger and Track) and the hybrid Madero & Jeronimo, which the group dates to 2024 (Grupo Madero).

Curitiba also launched the group’s second brand. Jeronimo, the fast-casual burger line, started in 2017, and its first restaurant opened at Shopping Estação in Curitiba (Grupo Madero, Guia da Semana).

Most of the food comes from one place. The group’s central kitchen in Ponta Grossa, about two hours west of Curitiba in Paraná, has run since 2015. It covers 32,408 m² (about 349,000 sq ft) in four production units: meat, sauces and desserts, charcuterie and smoked goods, and burgers and bakery. It makes or sources more than 96% of the food served in the restaurants and includes an organic farm.

Madero Prime Steak House (São Francisco)

Madero Prime Steak House is Madero’s upscale format, at Av. Jaime Reis, 262, in the São Francisco district of Curitiba’s historic center. It opens every day from 11:45 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. and takes reservations. The official page highlights a wine list from “one of the largest award-winning cellars in Brazil.” It offers dine-in with a digital menu and takes cards (official page).

Call the number on Madero’s site: +55 41 3017-6590. Some older third-party listings give a different number.

Madero Steak House Batel

Madero Steak House Batel is the best-rated Madero in Curitiba that I could verify: 4.6/5 on Google from 4,675 reviews and 4.5/5 on TripAdvisor from 694 reviews, as relayed by Wanderlog. It is at R. Bispo Dom José, 2377, in Batel, the city’s upscale dining district.

The dining room and takeout counter open Sunday to Thursday from 11:45 a.m. to 10:30 p.m., and Friday and Saturday until 11:00 p.m. Delivery runs Sunday to Thursday until 10:00 p.m., and Friday and Saturday until 10:30 p.m. You can reserve, but groups are only seated once at least half the party has arrived (official page).

Madero Steak House Comendador (Centro)

Madero Steak House Comendador is the family choice: it is downtown at R. Comendador Araújo, 152, and has the Maderoland kids’ area. Its hours match Batel’s, delivery included, and it takes reservations (official page).

Maderoland opens Monday to Thursday from 6:00 to 10:30 p.m., Friday from 6:00 to 11:00 p.m., and on weekends and public holidays from 11:45 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. The Brazilian travel blog Esse Mundo É Nosso describes the dining room as a recreated vila with a little street, terrace-style tables, a wine cellar and a play area (October 14, 2024 post).

Madero vs Jeronimo: what the hybrid format changes

Madero sells a thick, table-service cheeseburger. Jeronimo is the group’s fast-casual smash burger brand, and Madero & Jeronimo serves both under one roof. In June 2025 the group scaled the hybrid up, converting 14 restaurants in São Paulo state and Paraná, two of them in Curitiba. The menu combines Madero cheeseburgers and Jeronimo smash burgers with sides, salads, house-made desserts and shakes (Mercado & Consumo). Curitiba now has six Madero & Jeronimo locations on the official list.

Madero Curitiba menu and prices

The Madero cheeseburger is the dish to order, and its national reference price is R$54.00. That figure comes from a Forbes Brasil ranking built on data from ABF, the Brazilian Franchising Association, published March 6, 2026 (Portal Tela). It is not a price from a Curitiba menu, and the price you pay at a given location or on a delivery app may differ.

The basic Cheeseburger Madero has a 180 g (6.3 oz) patty, organic lettuce and tomato, cheddar-style cheese and house mayonnaise. It is the same burger I described in our guide to Madero in Fortaleza.

The Curitiba Steak House pages feature three signature dishes:

  • Cheeseburger Madero, the burger that made the chain’s name.
  • L’Entrecôte, the steak-house side of the menu.
  • Petit Gâteau de Doce de Leite, a molten cake made with Brazilian doce de leite, a caramelized milk spread.

Each format has its own menu. Madero publishes separate PDF menus for the Steakhouse, Container, Prime and Madero + Jeronimo formats in Portuguese and English, plus a nutrition and allergen chart (official menus). If you don’t read Portuguese, download the English PDF before you go.

Ignore old blog prices. One widely shared post, republished in 2024, still lists a 130 g cheeseburger at R$18.90. Those prices are clearly years out of date.

Is the Madero burger worth the trip?

Yes, if you want the burger that shaped Brazil’s premium chain market, in the city where it started. A 4.6/5 average held across more than 4,600 Google reviews is hard to fake. Don’t expect fast food, though. Slow table service is the complaint that keeps coming back at Batel, so skip it when you’re short on time and go to a Madero Burger counter instead. For a wider view of Brazil’s burger scene, compare it with our ranking of the best burgers in São Paulo.

Practical information: payment, reservations and delivery

Every Madero in Curitiba that I checked takes the major credit cards and Brazilian meal vouchers. Most Steak House locations take reservations, and the Burger format does not.

  • Payment: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Diners Club, Elo and Maestro, plus the Alelo, Sodexo, Ticket and VR meal vouchers. The list is the same on the Batel, Comendador, Pátio Batel, Shopping Estação and Burger Shopping Curitiba pages.
  • Reservations: possible at Prime, Batel, Comendador, Pátio Batel and Shopping Estação. Madero Burger Shopping Curitiba does not take bookings.
  • Delivery and takeout: offered by Batel, Comendador, Pátio Batel, Shopping Estação and Burger Shopping Curitiba. Delivery usually stops 15 to 30 minutes before the dining room closes; at Shopping Estação it ends at 9:30 p.m. every day.
  • Accessibility, parking and transit: Madero’s official pages don’t cover them. Call the location before you go.

FAQ: Madero Curitiba

How many Madero restaurants are there in Curitiba? Madero’s official website lists 24 Madero locations in Curitiba as of October 9, 2026: 1 Prime, 9 Steak House, 3 Burger, 1 Container, 6 Madero & Jeronimo and 4 Empório Madero.

Where was the first Madero, and can you still eat there? The first Madero Steak House opened in Curitiba on June 7, 2005, founded by chef Junior Durski. It opened in the historic center, where Madero Prime Steak House (Av. Jaime Reis, 262) operates today. Madero does not confirm that Prime occupies the same premises.

Which Madero should I choose in Curitiba? Pick Steak House Batel for the best-rated burger experience, Prime for wine and a special night out, Comendador for kids, and a Madero Burger location for a quicker meal without a booking. Madero & Jeronimo lets you try the Madero cheeseburger and a Jeronimo smash burger at the same table.

What are Madero Curitiba’s opening hours? Most Steak House locations open Sunday to Thursday from 11:45 a.m. to 10:30 p.m., and Friday and Saturday until 11:00 p.m. Prime opens daily from 11:45 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Of the locations I checked, Shopping Estação opens earliest, at 11:00 a.m. Check each location’s official page before you go.

How much does a burger cost at Madero? The Cheeseburger Madero costs R$54.00 at the national reference price published by Forbes Brasil on March 6, 2026. Prices at individual Curitiba locations and on delivery apps may differ.

Can I book a table at Madero in Curitiba? Yes, at the Prime and Steak House locations, including Batel, Comendador, Pátio Batel and Shopping Estação. At Batel, groups are seated once at least half the party has arrived. Madero Burger Shopping Curitiba is first come, first served.

Does Madero deliver in Curitiba? Yes. Batel, Comendador, Pátio Batel, Shopping Estação and Burger Shopping Curitiba all list delivery on their official pages. I could not verify current listings on iFood or Rappi.

Does Madero have vegetarian or vegan options? Madero’s national menu has a vegetarian and vegan section, but I could not confirm a specific meat-free burger, with its name and price, at the Curitiba locations. Check the allergen chart and the menu PDFs on Madero’s site.

Is Madero fast food or a sit-down restaurant? Madero Steak House and Prime are full-service restaurants with table service and reservations. Madero Burger and the Container unit are quicker, simpler formats. Jeronimo is the group’s fast-casual brand.

What is the difference between Madero and Jeronimo? Madero serves a thick cheeseburger with a 180 g patty, plus steaks, mostly in sit-down restaurants. Jeronimo, launched in 2017 at Shopping Estação in Curitiba, is a fast-casual smash burger brand. The Madero & Jeronimo hybrid serves both.

Is there a kids’ area at Madero in Curitiba? Yes. Madero Steak House Comendador has Maderoland. It is open Monday to Thursday from 6:00 to 10:30 p.m., Friday from 6:00 to 11:00 p.m., and on weekends and holidays from 11:45 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.

Is Madero really “the best burger in the world”? That is the chain’s own slogan, not an independent award. The evidence I can verify is strong: Batel holds 4.6/5 on Google from 4,675 reviews, and diners regularly call its burgers among Brazil’s best. Others point to slow service and a bun that crumbles.
