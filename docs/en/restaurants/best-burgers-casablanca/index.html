Best Burgers in Casablanca: 10 Top Spots for 2026

Pastrama's deli burgers, Medina Burger's Tanjia Burger, the best smash burgers: 10 Casablanca burger specialists ranked, with prices in MAD.

By Sam, Restaurants writer, Africa and the Middle EastPublished October 31, 2026

The best burgers in Casablanca are at Pastrama in Racine for craft deli burgers, at Blend Gourmet Burger in Gauthier for the city’s long-running gourmet classic, and at Medina Burger on boulevard d’Anfa for the Tanjia Burger, a Moroccan twist that costs 119 MAD. For smash burgers, go to Ladoze Street Burger or Easy Burger. Expect to pay about 50 to 200 MAD (Moroccan dirhams) for a burger.

If you only have room for one, make it the Tanjia Burger. It’s the only burger on this list you couldn’t order in Paris or London.

Restaurant Guru counts 477 restaurants serving burgers in Casablanca in its October 2026 ranking. Most online lists of the city’s best burgers pad themselves out with kebab shops, delis, Italian places and global chains. I kept only burger specialists. Every price is in Moroccan dirhams (MAD, also written DH) and was checked on 25 October 2026.

How I ranked Casablanca’s burger restaurants

This ranking covers dedicated burger restaurants only, chosen from current menus, local directories and 2026 review data. It’s a desk ranking: I haven’t eaten at these 10 for this edition, and I’ll add tasting notes as I visit.

  • Sources: the official menu when there is one (Pastrama), menu listings (Yumlo for Medina Burger), the July 2026 burger guide on guide-casa.ma, the Restaurant Guru October 2026 ranking, and directory pages on Wanderlog and Mapstr.
  • Open in 2026: every restaurant is listed as active in 2026 directories. Hours change, so check Google Maps before you go.

The 10 best burger restaurants in Casablanca

Pastrama tops the list and Texas Burger closes it. Each entry gives the full address, the hours and an official link where one exists.

1. Pastrama – Deli & Craft Burger (Racine)

Pastrama is the best gourmet burger in Casablanca right now: a deli and craft burger bar in Racine that puts its own pastrami on burgers. The Peper’n’pastrama and the Truffle Burger cost 129 MAD each, and the Tartare Burger and the Spice’n’Pepperoni Burger cost 115 MAD. Every burger comes with fries, coleslaw and a sauce. 1 order is dinner.

Why first? Pastrama is the only place on this list with a working website and a full priced menu, and it does the deli work in-house: the ribs, for instance, cook for 8 hours. That combination of transparency and craft is rare in this city.

  • Address: rue Oumayma Essayeh, Racine, Casablanca 20250
  • Hours: Saturday to Thursday 9:30 am–11:30 pm; Friday 2:30 pm–11:30 pm
  • Phone: +212 6 78 84 81 81
  • Website: pastrama.ma

2. Blend Gourmet Burger (Gauthier)

Blend Gourmet Burger is Casablanca’s established gourmet burger address, in the Gauthier district. The menu runs from a double cheeseburger and a truffle-mayo burger to poutine, cheesecakes and milkshakes. Guide-casa.ma puts the price range at 100 to 200 MAD.

It is older than some directories suggest. In September 2013, Médias24 reported that Victor Garnier, founder of the Paris restaurant Blend Hamburger Gourmet, accused the Casablanca Blend of copying his concept. So the Casablanca restaurant was already trading in 2013.

  • Address: 9 rue Théophile Gauthier, Gauthier, Casablanca
  • Hours: daily, 12 pm–11 pm
  • Instagram: @blendgourmetburger

3. Medina Burger – Anfa (boulevard d’Anfa)

Medina Burger serves the most Moroccan burger in Casablanca: the Tanjia Burger, at 119 MAD. The 100% beef patty is cooked tanjia-style, after Marrakech’s slow-cooked jar dish, then topped with mozzarella and a house sauce. Buns are made in-house. Coleslaw comes with every burger.

The rest of the menu is fairly priced. On Yumlo’s listing, the Classic is 69 MAD, the Chicken Chipotle 79 MAD and the Truffle Burger 109 MAD. Medina Burger also has a restaurant in Marrakech. The Anfa branch is the late-night option, open until 2 am.

  • Address: 45 boulevard d’Anfa, Casablanca
  • Hours: 12 pm–2 am
  • Instagram: @lemedinaburger
  • Menu source: Yumlo

Boulevard d’Anfa in Casablanca, seen from a rooftop in 2016. Medina Burger, home of the 119 MAD Tanjia Burger, is at no. 45 and stays open until 2 am (Photo: Salah Ghrissi, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Boulevard\_d%27Anfa.jpg)

4. Ladoze Street Burger (Oasis and boulevard de Nice)

Ladoze Street Burger is Casablanca’s go-to smash burger chain, with 2 branches and prices of 50 to 100 MAD per person. It’s priced for a weeknight, not an occasion. You can also order online through Klit.

  • Oasis branch: rue des Mésanges, Casablanca 20103. Open daily, 12 pm–11 pm
  • Boulevard de Nice branch: n°5, résidence Shems, corner of boulevard de Nice and avenue Mohamed Sijilmassi. Open daily, 12 pm–1 am. Phone +212 773-941140
  • Instagram: @ladozecasablanca
  • Online ordering: ladozeburger.com (redirects to Klit)

5. Easy Burger (rue Ennahas Annahoui)

Easy Burger is a small smash burger specialist whose reviews on Google and Tripadvisor are fewer than the big names’ but consistently strong. There is no website and no Instagram account that I could find. Call ahead.

  • Address: 04 rue Ennahas Annahoui, Casablanca 20330
  • Phone: +212 664-470771
  • Listing: Tripadvisor

6. Oncle Blend (rue Bab al Mendeb)

Oncle Blend is the burger restaurant for a long evening out in Casablanca. It sits halfway between a steakhouse and a French brasserie: burgers share the menu with entrecôtes and tartares, there’s a cocktail bar, and the dining room goes for an industrial, steampunk look.

  • Address: rue Bab al Mendeb, Casablanca 20250 (guide-casa.ma lists 6 rue Bab Ennasr, so check Google Maps before you go)
  • Hours: 12 pm–1 am, closed Tuesday
  • Phone: +212 6 67 75 50 62
  • Instagram: @oncle_blend

7. Burger Glory (rue Oussama Ibnou Zaid)

Burger Glory is a neighborhood burger joint, and its reviewers keep praising 1 thing: everything is homemade. Budget 50 to 100 MAD per person. It’s closed on Sundays.

  • Address: 44 rue Oussama Ibnou Zaid, Casablanca
  • Hours: Monday to Saturday 12:30 pm–11 pm, closed Sunday
  • Instagram: @burger_glory (the burgerglory.ma website was not resolving on 25 October 2026)

8. Nelly Burger (Les Princesses)

Nelly Burger is an evening-only burger spot in Les Princesses known for its brioche buns and house pickles, at 50 to 100 MAD. Cash only, so bring dirhams.

  • Address: rue Darkoutni, Les Princesses, Casablanca
  • Hours: Tuesday to Sunday 5 pm–12 am, closed Monday
  • Payment: cash only

9. La Table du Burger

La Table du Burger is one of the few Casablanca burger bars where the chicken burger, the Lowa Chicken Burger, is the signature. There’s also a double cheeseburger with fresh-cut fries. It’s open every day until midnight.

  • Address: directories disagree (rue Soltan Abdelhamid, 20040, or rue Ibnou Jahir, Bourgogne), so look it up on Google Maps before you set off
  • Hours: daily, 12 pm–12 am
  • Phone: +212 5 20 53 62 22

10. Texas Burger (avenue 2 Mars)

Texas Burger closes out the top 10 with American-style burgers, and the Hulk is its signature. It’s a small place with fewer reviews than the rest of this list. Don’t go on a Wednesday: it’s closed.

  • Address: résidence Al Wafa 1 n°26, corner of rue Stockholm and rue Bruxelles, avenue 2 Mars, Casablanca 20000
  • Hours: 12 pm–11 pm, closed Wednesday

Oh My Bun: Morocco’s smash burger pioneer

Oh My Bun bills itself as Morocco’s first smash burger chain, and it matters to the city’s burger story even though it isn’t in my top 10. Achraf Tahiri, from Meknès and a graduate of the American school in Ifrane, founded it in early 2021. By June 2022 it had opened its first franchise abroad, with 2 restaurants in Amsterdam (TelQuel, Femmes du Maroc).

The Gauthier branch is at the corner of rue Gauthier and rue El Bouhtouri. In the review data I checked, it scores well below every pick above. That’s why it gets a mention, not a ranking. On Glovo, the Dar Bouazza branch lists the Double Cheese Burger menu at 109 MAD and the Cheese Burger menu at 89 MAD. I couldn’t confirm Casablanca city prices or how many restaurants it runs today. There’s also a branch in Rabat.

Best smash burger in Casablanca

For a smash burger in Casablanca, go to Ladoze Street Burger for value and 2 branches, or Easy Burger for a small specialist with strong reviews. Oh My Bun, launched in 2021, is the local brand credited with bringing the style to Morocco.

A smash burger is a ball of beef pressed thin on a very hot griddle, so the edges caramelize and crisp. Our Smashburger brand profile traces how the style became a chain format in the United States. To make 1 at home, follow our copycat of Sonic’s All-American Bacon Smasher, which walks through the smash step by step.

Halal burgers in Casablanca

In Casablanca, halal is the norm: Morocco is a Muslim-majority country, and the beef and chicken in its burger restaurants are halal as a rule. Muslim travelers rarely need to hunt for a special halal burger. If it matters to you, ask the restaurant. The setting is what varies: Oncle Blend runs a cocktail bar, while most of the others are casual, family-friendly burger bars.

Best Casablanca neighborhoods for burgers

Gauthier and Racine are the best areas of Casablanca for gourmet burgers. Boulevard d’Anfa and Oasis are where you go for late-night and smash burgers.

  • Gauthier: Blend Gourmet Burger and Oh My Bun, a few streets apart.
  • Racine: Pastrama, the strongest craft burger menu in the city.
  • Anfa: Medina Burger at 45 boulevard d’Anfa, open until 2 am.
  • Oasis and boulevard de Nice: both Ladoze Street Burger branches, the original on rue des Mésanges.
  • Maârif: the district where Wayback Burgers, the Connecticut chain, opened Morocco’s first branch on 6 November 2017, at 10 bis rue Ibn Yala Al Ifrani (Fast Casual). I found no sign it is still trading in 2026.

Late-night burgers in Casablanca

Medina Burger on boulevard d’Anfa serves burgers later than anywhere else in Casablanca, until 2 am. Oncle Blend (closed Tuesday) and the boulevard de Nice branch of Ladoze serve until 1 am. La Table du Burger and Nelly Burger close at midnight.

Late-night burgers in Casablanca, by closing time: Medina Burger Anfa until 2 am, Oncle Blend and Ladoze on boulevard de Nice until 1 am, La Table du Burger and Nelly Burger until midnight, Pastrama until 11:30 pm and the rest until 11 pm (in this 24-hour scale, 25 = 1 am and 26 = 2 am) (Chart: Burger Breton, opening hours checked 25 October 2026 on official pages, guide-casa.ma, Wanderlog and Mapstr, rendered with QuickChart; CC BY 4.0)

FAQ: burgers in Casablanca

Where can I eat the best burger in Casablanca?

For the best burger in Casablanca, go to Pastrama in Racine for a craft burger, Blend Gourmet Burger in Gauthier for a gourmet classic, or Medina Burger on boulevard d’Anfa for the Tanjia Burger at 119 MAD.

Are burgers halal in Casablanca?

Yes, as a rule. Beef and chicken in Casablanca’s burger restaurants are halal as standard. Ask the restaurant if you want to be certain.

What is the best smash burger in Casablanca?

Ladoze Street Burger, with branches in Oasis and on boulevard de Nice, and Easy Burger on rue Ennahas Annahoui serve the best smash burgers in Casablanca. Oh My Bun, founded in 2021, bills itself as Morocco’s first smash burger chain.

How much does a burger cost in Casablanca?

Expect 59 MAD for a Big Mac, 65 MAD for a Whopper, 69 to 129 MAD for a signature burger in an independent restaurant, and up to 200 MAD in gourmet places.

Can I get a burger delivered in Casablanca?

Yes. Glovo, Yassir and Klit deliver burgers in Casablanca. Ladoze takes online orders through Klit, and Oh My Bun is listed on Glovo.

Are there Moroccan-style burgers in Casablanca?

Yes. Medina Burger’s Tanjia Burger uses beef cooked in the style of tanjia, the slow-cooked Marrakech dish, with mozzarella and a house sauce, for 119 MAD.

Where to eat in Casablanca for a casual dinner?

For a casual burger dinner in Casablanca, head to Gauthier or Racine, where Blend and Pastrama are a short ride apart. For a longer evening, Oncle Blend adds a steakhouse menu and a cocktail bar.
