Arcadas Coimbra: Chef Vítor Dias at Quinta das Lágrimas

Who really cooks at Coimbra's fine-dining benchmark, what the menu costs, and where to go if you came to Coimbra for a burger.

By AlexPublished October 9, 2026

Arcadas Coimbra is the fine-dining restaurant of the 5-star Hotel Quinta das Lágrimas, and its executive chef has been Vítor Dias since September 2015. It serves seasonal tasting menus built on produce from the region and the estate’s own garden. There is no burger on it: no menu I found from 2024 or 2025 lists one. If you came to Coimbra for a burger, skip to the burger section below or go straight to our Coimbra city page.

The Quinta das Lágrimas restaurant setting

The Quinta das Lágrimas restaurant occupies the palace’s former stables, and according to Frommer’s its arched windows open onto the gardens. Hence the name: Arcadas, the arcades. The hotel itself is an 18th-century palace in 12 hectares of gardens. It opened as a hotel in 1995, and in 2026 the estate marks 700 years of documented history.

A patio of the Quinta das Lágrimas estate, whose former palace stables now house the Arcadas restaurant, named after their arched windows. (Photo: Daderot, Wikimedia Commons, CC0 1.0 (5472×3648))

This is also where Portugal’s best-known love story is set, the legend of Pedro and Inês. The restaurant’s nod to it is a modest one: a house ‘Pedro & Inês’ wine on the list.

The Fonte das Lágrimas in the Quinta das Lágrimas gardens, linked to the legend of Pedro and Inês that gives its name to Arcadas’ house ‘Pedro & Inês’ wine. (Photo: Daderot, Wikimedia Commons, CC0 1.0 (5436×3624))

Vítor Dias, the chef behind Arcadas

Vítor Dias has run the Arcadas kitchen since September 2015, when he took over at 35. Both Frommer’s and food writer Nelson Carvalheiro (December 2016) give that start date. Ten years on he is still there: Lux and Estórias Magazine credit him with the 30th-anniversary Autumn-Winter menu of late 2025.

He cooks the region first. The hotel calls it ‘cuisine with a soul’, built on herbs, vegetables and fruit from the estate’s Chef’s Garden and orchard. Forbes Portugal (July 2024) fills in the rest of the supply chain: rice from the Mondego valley fields, fish landed at Figueira da Foz, produce from nearby farms.

Gardens of Quinta das Lágrimas: the estate’s grounds and orchard supply herbs, vegetables and fruit for Vítor Dias’s regional ‘cuisine with a soul’. (Photo: Pedro S Bello, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0 (4032×2268))

What a Vítor Dias menu looks like

The summer 2024 menu is the clearest picture of his style I found. Short names, local products and, to my reading, one sharp contrast per plate. Forbes Portugal described three of the dishes:

  • O Ovo: pea stew, pork cracklings and an organic egg.
  • A Gamba: prawn tartare, soft-shell crab, yuzu and Fundão cherry.
  • Herança: confit cod, Cadima lupin and coconut cream.

Fundão cherries and Cadima lupins both come from central Portugal. Yuzu and coconut are the outsiders. That balance, mostly the region with a few foreign accents, runs through every menu I read.

Arcadas then and now: a timeline

Arcadas went from a Michelin-starred table under Albano Lourenço to a regional tasting-menu restaurant under Vítor Dias. Several aggregators still list Lourenço as the current chef. They are about a decade out of date. Here is the sequence the sources support.

The Michelin star under Albano Lourenço

Arcadas held one Michelin star when Albano Lourenço was chef, with Joachim Koerper as consultant. It does not hold a star now. One blog dates the star to 2005–2013, and a Portuguese news snippet says it was lost in the 2013 guide. I could not confirm either against the Michelin Guide itself, so I give no years here.

The 30th-anniversary menu

For the hotel’s 30 years, Vítor Dias wrote an Autumn-Winter menu in tribute to Albano Lourenço and Joachim Koerper. It cost €95 per person without drinks, plus €35 for the wine pairing, and ran until March or April 2026, according to Lux and Estórias Magazine. Dishes included:

  • Fish soup with Algarve prawns.
  • ‘C.L.P.’: shrimp, lobster and turbot.
  • Slow-braised oxtail with truffle potato.
  • Burnt-milk cream with thyme ice cream.

A chef paying tribute to the predecessor everyone still confuses him with is a gracious move. It also says a lot about how much that star still weighs on the house.

Is Arcadas worth booking? Fine dining in Coimbra

Yes, if you want a regional tasting menu in a historic setting: Arcadas is the obvious fine-dining choice in Coimbra. The hotel says La Liste ranks it among the world’s 500 best restaurants, and Lifecooler repeats the claim. I could not find which La Liste edition that refers to, so read it as the hotel’s claim, not mine.

My view: book it for three things, the setting, the garden-driven cooking and a Dão and Bairrada list that is hard to drink this well outside Portugal. Don’t book it for a quick bite or a casual dinner, which is not what it does. And call before you go, because current 2026 prices and opening hours are not on the official site.

How I researched this profile

I built this profile from the hotel’s official Arcadas page, Portuguese food and lifestyle press, and travel guides, all checked on 8 October 2026. I did not eat at Arcadas for this piece, so there is no rating. Where sources disagreed or could not be confirmed, I said so or left the fact out. I also checked a lead naming a ‘João Alves’ as chef at Arcadas: no source supports it, and the only João Alves in Coimbra’s records is Bishop João Alves (1925–2013), Bishop of Coimbra from 1976 to 2001, who has no link to the restaurant.

Arcadas Coimbra FAQ

Who is the chef at Arcadas in Coimbra?

Vítor Dias is the executive chef at Arcadas and has been since September 2015. He was still in charge in late 2025, when he created the 30th-anniversary menu. Albano Lourenço, often still listed online, is the former chef.

Does Arcadas at Quinta das Lágrimas have a Michelin star?

No. Arcadas held one Michelin star when Albano Lourenço was chef, with Joachim Koerper as consultant, but it does not hold one today.

How much does dinner at Arcadas cost?

The last published price was €95 per person without drinks, plus €35 for wine pairing, for the 30th-anniversary menu that ended in March or April 2026. Current 2026 prices are not on the official site, so call +351 239 802 380 before you go.

Is Arcadas open for lunch, and which days is it closed?

I could not confirm opening hours, lunch service or closing days from an official source. Call the restaurant on +351 239 802 380 to check and book.

Is there a vegetarian or vegan menu at Arcadas?

Yes, as of 2024. Forbes Portugal listed a Garden menu, vegetarian and vegan, alongside the Tradition tasting menu and à la carte.

Does Arcadas serve burgers?

No. Arcadas is a seasonal tasting-menu restaurant and no burger appears on any menu I found from 2024 or 2025.

Where can you eat a good burger in Coimbra?

Local media point to Honorato on Rua João de Deus Ramos, Porreiro near Praça da República (takeaway and delivery only), Casa das Caldeiras on Rua Padre António Vieira and Hamburgueria de Coimbra in Celas. See our Coimbra city page for updates.
