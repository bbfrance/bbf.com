Five Guys Cambridge: Market St vs Leisure Park

Both Five Guys in Cambridge, England: Market Street or Leisure Park? Addresses, hours, phone numbers, kids' meal price and reviews, checked 31 Oct 2026.

By Emma, Restaurants writer, United KingdomPublished November 3, 2026

Cambridge, England, has two Five Guys, and they suit very different days out. Five Guys Cambridge Market Street is at 18 Market Street, CB2 3PA, a few steps from Market Square in the historic centre. Five Guys Cambridge Leisure Park is at Unit 14, Cambridge Leisure Park, Clifton Way, CB1 7DY, less than 10 minutes’ walk from Cambridge station. The Leisure Park opens at 11am daily and closes at 11pm Monday to Thursday, midnight on Fridays and Saturdays, and 10:30pm on Sundays. Market Street doesn’t publish its hours at all. Both score above 4/5 on Google. Prices? Five Guys UK publishes exactly one: the kids’ meal, from £8. I checked every fact below on 31 October 2026.

This guide covers Cambridge in Cambridgeshire, UK, not Cambridge, Massachusetts, which has its own Five Guys. For everything else we publish on burgers in the city, see our Cambridge burger page.

Five Guys Cambridge at a glance: two restaurants, two very different spots

Market Street is the one for the city centre, the colleges and Market Square. The Leisure Park is the one for the station, the cinema, parking and late-night food. The official Five Guys UK directory lists both among its 13 restaurants in the East of England (Five Guys UK, East of England).

Five Guys Cambridge Market Street: the city-centre branch

Five Guys Cambridge Market Street is at 18 Market Street, CB2 3PA, a short stroll from Market Square in the historic centre. It’s the branch for students, shoppers and anyone doing the colleges on foot. Call 01223 657021 for anything the website doesn’t answer.

Services and payment at Market Street

Market Street offers dine-in, takeaway, Click & Collect and delivery. It takes cash, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay. You can order ahead on the Five Guys UK ordering site, or get delivery through Just Eat, Deliveroo (Cambridge Centre zone) and Uber Eats.

Five Guys Cambridge Market Street opening hours

Market Street’s official page lists no opening hours, and the third-party sites can’t agree. Wanderlog says 10am–10:45pm every day. openingtimesin.uk says 11am–10:30pm Monday to Thursday and Sunday, and 11am–11pm on Fridays and Saturdays. A third listing simply copies the Leisure Park’s hours. None of them is reliable. Planning a late visit? Check the Five Guys app or ring 01223 657021 first.

Five Guys Cambridge Leisure Park: near the station, open late

Five Guys Cambridge Leisure Park is at Unit 14, Cambridge Leisure Park, Clifton Way, CB1 7DY. It’s the closest Five Guys to Cambridge station and the one that stays open latest: until midnight on Fridays and Saturdays. The phone number is 01223 657019.

Five Guys Cambridge Leisure Park opening hours

The Leisure Park opens at 11am every day. It closes at 11pm Monday to Thursday, midnight on Fridays and Saturdays, and 10:30pm on Sundays. The official page doesn’t show these hours, but Wanderlog and Family Friendly UK agree on them, and Tripaligner confirms the Saturday midnight close (checked 31 October 2026).

Services, families and parking at the Leisure Park

The Leisure Park branch offers dine-in, takeaway, Click & Collect, delivery and outdoor seating. It takes the same payment methods as Market Street and is part of the Five Guys Rewards loyalty scheme. Family Friendly UK lists high chairs, pushchair access and a kids’ menu. You can’t book: it’s walk-in only.

Beware of out-of-date Leisure Park details online

Some directories and older listings, SquareMeal among them, still show old details: the address ‘Unit G4, Cambridge Leisure Park, Clifton Road’, the phone number 01223 211130 and a noon opening on Sundays. None of these match the current official page. The right address is Unit 14, Clifton Way, and the number is 01223 657019. I also couldn’t confirm the 0800 083 3005 number that openingtimesin.uk gives for Market Street.

Market Street or Leisure Park: which Five Guys in Cambridge should you choose?

Go to Market Street if you’re already in the centre. Go to the Leisure Park if you’re arriving by train, driving, catching a film or eating late. On a day out in Cambridge, I’d pick Market Street: you eat in the middle of the city you came to see, with Great St Mary’s at the end of the street. For everything practical, the Leisure Park wins.

  • On foot or by bike in the centre: Market Street, right by Market Square.
  • Arriving by train: the Leisure Park, under 10 minutes’ walk from Cambridge station.
  • Driving: the Leisure Park, with its paid multi-storey and Blue Badge bays.
  • Late on a Friday or Saturday: the Leisure Park, open until midnight.
  • With small children: the Leisure Park, with high chairs, pushchair access and outdoor seating.
  • If you want hours you can trust: the Leisure Park, whose times match across sources. Market Street’s don’t.

Five Guys Cambridge menu: what you can order

Both Cambridge branches serve the standard Five Guys UK menu: burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches, breakfast, fries and milkshakes. According to the official UK menu, the line-up is:

  • Burgers, built your way, with the chain’s claim of more than 250,000 topping combinations.
  • Hot dogs.
  • Sandwiches, including a BLT and a grilled cheese.
  • Breakfast.
  • Boardwalk-style fries, cut in store every day and cooked in 100% refined peanut oil.
  • Milkshakes on a vanilla base with mix-ins, with the chain claiming 1,000 combinations.
  • Drinks from Coca-Cola and Dr Pepper.

The menu checked on 31 October 2026 also listed two limited editions: an M&M’S Shake and a coffee mix-in for shakes. On the pages for both Cambridge branches, Five Guys says its fries are cut fresh daily and that its kitchens have no freezers.

Vegetarian options, halal and allergies

For vegetarians, the menu has a grilled cheese and a veggie sandwich. I couldn’t confirm the halal status of either Cambridge branch, or their full vegan and allergen details. Check the official Five Guys UK Halal Info page and the nutrition and allergen PDF before you order. And remember the fries are cooked in peanut oil: tell the staff about any allergy.

Five Guys Cambridge prices: what a meal costs

The only price Five Guys UK publishes is the kids’ meal, from £8 on the official menu (a hamburger, hot dog or grilled cheese, with fries, a drink and a shake costing extra). That matches what I found for Five Guys Oxford. There’s no official price list for Cambridge. The lists on aggregator sites are neither official nor specific to the city, and delivery apps charge more than the counter. For an exact bill, open the Five Guys app and pick your branch before you go.

Five Guys Cambridge reviews: what customers say

Both Cambridge branches rate well on Google: 4.4/5 for the Leisure Park and 4.3/5 for Market Street. Tripadvisor is cooler, at 3.5/5 for both. These figures come from Wanderlog’s aggregates and Family Friendly UK, which shows 4.4 from about 2,060 Google reviews for the Leisure Park, so treat the counts as approximate (checked 31 October 2026).

  • What reviewers praise: burgers built exactly how you want them, big portions of crispy fries (the Cajun ones especially), free peanuts while you wait, soft drink refills, the milkshakes and friendly staff.
  • What reviewers criticise: high prices, fry portions that vary from visit to visit, and a dining room that can be noisy or sometimes untidy.

My take: you get the same build-your-own burger in Cambridge as anywhere else in the UK, so choose by location, not by food. I wouldn’t read much into Market Street’s 3.5 on Tripadvisor. About 27 reviews is too thin a sample. In the complaints, price comes up more often than the food. If you’d rather support an independent, our Cambridge burger page tracks what we publish on the city, and The Beefy Boys in Oxford shows what the independents are doing in the other big university city.

Five Guys: from Arlington to Cambridge

Five Guys was founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell and their sons. It opened its first restaurant outside North America in London on 4 July 2013, the branch I cover in my Five Guys London Covent Garden guide. By May 2026, the chain had 1,999 restaurants in 28 countries and territories. Read the full story and figures in our Five Guys brand profile, or compare Cambridge with the three Five Guys in Leeds.

Five Guys Cambridge FAQ

How many Five Guys are there in Cambridge?

There are two: Cambridge Market Street (18 Market Street, CB2 3PA) in the historic centre, and Cambridge Leisure Park (Unit 14, Clifton Way, CB1 7DY) near the station.

Which Five Guys in Cambridge should I go to?

Go to Market Street if you’re in the city centre. Go to the Leisure Park if you’re coming by train or car, heading to the cinema, or want food until midnight on a Friday or Saturday.

What are Five Guys Cambridge’s opening hours on Sunday?

The Leisure Park is open 11am–10:30pm on Sundays, according to Wanderlog and Family Friendly UK. Market Street’s Sunday hours vary between sources, so call 01223 657021 or check the app.

Is Five Guys Cambridge open late on Fridays and Saturdays?

Yes. The Leisure Park branch stays open until midnight on Fridays and Saturdays. Market Street’s late hours are not officially confirmed.

How much does a meal at Five Guys Cambridge cost?

The only official price is the kids’ meal, from £8 before extras. For adult burgers, fries and shakes, check the current prices in the Five Guys UK app for your branch. Delivery prices are higher.

Does Five Guys Cambridge deliver?

Yes. Both branches deliver, and Market Street lists Just Eat, Deliveroo and Uber Eats. You can also order for Click & Collect at order.fiveguys.co.uk.

Can I park near Five Guys Cambridge Leisure Park?

Yes. Cambridge Leisure Park has a paid multi-storey car park with Blue Badge bays on the ground floor.

Is Five Guys Cambridge near Cambridge station?

Yes. The Leisure Park branch is less than 10 minutes’ walk from Cambridge railway station.

Is Five Guys Cambridge halal?

I couldn’t confirm halal status for either Cambridge branch. Check the official Five Guys UK Halal Info page, or ask the restaurant.

Are there vegetarian options at Five Guys Cambridge?

Yes. The UK menu includes a veggie sandwich and a grilled cheese. Check the official allergen guide for vegan details.

Are Five Guys fries cooked in peanut oil?

Yes. Five Guys UK cooks its fries in 100% refined peanut oil. If you have a nut allergy, read the official allergen information and talk to the staff before ordering.

Do I need to book at Five Guys Cambridge?

No. Five Guys doesn’t take reservations, and the Leisure Park branch is walk-in only.
