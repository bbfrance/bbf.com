Five Guys Cleveland: Address, Hours, Menu, Prices

The only Five Guys in Cleveland is at Steelyard Commons. Address, daily hours, prices, reviews and the nine suburban locations, checked Nov. 15, 2026.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 26, 2026

There is exactly one Five Guys inside Cleveland’s city limits: Five Guys Steelyard Commons, 3273 Steelyard Drive, Cleveland, OH 44109 (216-398-2222). Directories list it as open 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day, with dine-in, takeout, curbside pickup, delivery and outdoor seating. Restaurantji rates it 4 out of 5 from 268 reviews. Staying in the suburbs? Nine more Five Guys cover Greater Cleveland, from Parma and Strongsville to Mayfield Heights and Westlake. I checked every fact below on November 15, 2026. I did not eat there for this guide, and I say so wherever it matters.

Five Guys Cleveland address: Steelyard Commons

Five Guys Cleveland sits in the Steelyard Commons shopping center at 3273 Steelyard Drive, Cleveland, OH 44109. It is the only Five Guys with a Cleveland address in the chain’s official Cleveland directory.

Steelyard Commons opened in 2007 in the Tremont area of Cleveland. It was built on the site of the former LTV Steel No. 2 plant, which closed in 2001 (Wikipedia). Walmart, Target and Home Depot anchor the center, so the burger stop pairs easily with a shopping run.

Watch out for stale listings. One old mall directory still gives a 2019 address at 3447 Steelyard Dr, with a different phone number. The official store page says 3273 Steelyard Drive and 216-398-2222. Trust that one.

How to get to Five Guys Steelyard Commons without a car

You can reach Five Guys Steelyard Commons by RTA bus, or on foot or by bike via the Towpath Trail. According to transit.wiki, RTA routes 2 (East 79), 16 (East 55) and 81 (Tremont-Storer) serve the shopping center (transit.wiki). That is a secondary source, so confirm your route on riderta.com before you leave.

Stage 2 of the Towpath Trail extension, a paved walking and cycling path, runs along the edge of Steelyard Commons (Canalway Partners). Restaurantji notes that dogs are welcome on the outdoor seating, handy if you come in off the trail with one.

Five Guys Cleveland opening hours

Five Guys Steelyard Commons is open from 11 a.m. to 10 p.m., Monday through Sunday, according to two directories that agree (Storeshours, Restaurantji). The official store page loads its hours with JavaScript, and it showed none when I checked.

I could not find holiday hours published anywhere. Before a late visit or a holiday trip, check the official store page or call 216-398-2222.

Five Guys Cleveland menu: what to order

The Five Guys Cleveland menu is the chain’s standard US menu: burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches, fries and milkshakes. I looked for a Cleveland-only item and found none.

The official store page claims more than 250,000 ways to customize a burger and more than 1,000 milkshake combinations. The beef is fresh, never frozen, and the fries are cooked twice in peanut oil (Five Guys).

Restaurantji reviewers keep naming the same dishes at Steelyard:

  • Bacon cheeseburger, the chain’s signature burger.
  • Double cheeseburger, two patties with the toppings of your choice.
  • Fries, regular or Cajun, served in generous portions.
  • Patty melt.
  • Grilled vegetables as toppings.

My advice on the fries: order one for two people. Reviewers call the portions generous, and at $7 to $11 for a single order, that matters.

Vegetarian, low-carb and allergy options

Restaurantji reviewers mention a lettuce wrap: any burger can come wrapped in lettuce instead of a bun. The press and price aggregators also list a Veggie Sandwich and a Grilled Cheese on the US menu. I could not confirm those two on the official menu page, so check the order app before you count on them.

If you have a peanut allergy, take it seriously here. Five Guys cooks its fries in peanut oil, and Restaurantji reviewers describe free peanuts handed out while you wait.

Five Guys Cleveland prices: what a meal costs

Expect roughly $9 to $17 for a burger or hot dog, $7 to $11 for fries and about $8 for a milkshake at Five Guys in the US, based on 2026 aggregator figures (The Pricer, checked November 15, 2026). These are national ranges, not Steelyard’s official prices.

Five Guys Cleveland prices, bar chart of 2026 US ranges: burgers and hot dogs $9–$17, fries $7–$11, milkshake about $8; national figures, not Steelyard prices. (Chart: Burger Breton, data from The Pricer (November 15, 2026))

Each franchisee sets its own prices, so the bill varies from one restaurant to the next. Aggregators quote about $11.99 for a cheeseburger, $12.99 to $13.69 for a bacon cheeseburger and about $8.25 for a large Cajun fries. None of these figures is specific to Cleveland.

For today’s exact prices, open the Five Guys app or order.fiveguys.com and pick Steelyard. For a burger, half of a shared fries and a shake, budget about $20 to $30 per person before tax and tip, based on the ranges above. That is sit-down money for counter service, and it is the one thing that would make me think twice.

Five Guys Cleveland reviews: is it worth it?

Yes, if you want a fresh, fully customized fast-casual burger and can accept a higher bill than at a classic fast-food chain. Five Guys Steelyard Commons holds 4/5 from 268 reviews on Restaurantji, with 4.3 for food, 4.1 for atmosphere and 4.1 for service (Restaurantji).

Five Guys Cleveland reviews, bar chart of Restaurantji ratings for Steelyard Commons: overall 4/5 from 268 reviews, food 4.3, atmosphere 4.1, service 4.1. (Chart: Burger Breton, data from Restaurantji (November 15, 2026))

What reviewers praise again and again:

  • Fresh ingredients and burgers they rate above regular fast food.
  • Generous, fully customizable toppings.
  • Free peanuts while you wait.
  • The lettuce wrap option for a bun-free burger.

What they criticize:

  • High prices for fast food.
  • Uneven service from one visit to the next.
  • Occasional waits.

My take: none of those complaints is specific to Steelyard. Price and uneven service are the usual charges against the chain, and a 4.3 for food says the kitchen here does its job. On these numbers, I would send a friend for the burger without hesitation, after warning them about the bill.

Delivery and online ordering at Five Guys Cleveland

Five Guys Steelyard Commons delivers, and you can order ahead for pickup or curbside on the chain’s official site. The “Order Now” link on the store page goes to order.fiveguys.com/location/steelyard/menu.

DoorDash lists Five Guys in Cleveland, but I could not confirm which third-party apps deliver from Steelyard. The official ordering site is the safest route.

Five Guys Parma, Ohio

Five Guys Parma opened on December 13, 2016, at The Shoppes at Parma, a 750,000-square-foot shopping center anchored by Walmart Supercenter and Dick’s Sporting Goods (FSR Magazine). Some delivery apps show a “Cleveland” postal address for it, but Parma is a separate city (official Parma page).

Which Five Guys to choose depending on where you stay

Downtown and near west side visitors should head to Steelyard Commons, the only Five Guys inside Cleveland. On the east side, Mayfield Heights and South Euclid are the obvious picks. West siders have North Olmsted, Fairview Park and Westlake. Parma and Strongsville cover the south.

Five Guys: a short history of the chain

Five Guys was founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell and their five sons, who gave the chain its name. Franchising started in 2003. The first international restaurant opened in Canada in 2010, followed by the UK in 2013. By its 40th anniversary in 2026, the chain counted close to 2,000 restaurants in more than 25 countries (Five Guys blog).

It crossed that line on September 25, 2026, when the 2,000th restaurant opened at Universal CityWalk in Orlando (QSR Magazine). Figures, markets and reviews for the whole chain are on our Five Guys brand profile, and the bigger story of the sandwich is in our history of the hamburger.

FAQ: Five Guys Cleveland

How many Five Guys are there in Cleveland?

One. The official directory lists only Steelyard Commons, 3273 Steelyard Drive, inside the city of Cleveland. Nine more operate in suburbs such as Parma, Strongsville and Westlake.

What is the address of Five Guys Cleveland?

Five Guys Steelyard Commons, 3273 Steelyard Drive, Cleveland, OH 44109. Phone: 216-398-2222.

What are the Five Guys Steelyard Commons hours?

Directories list 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day, Monday through Sunday. Holiday hours are not published, so check the official store page before you go.

How much does a meal at Five Guys Cleveland cost?

National 2026 ranges put a burger at about $9 to $17, fries at $7 to $11 and a shake near $8, so about $20 to $30 per person with shared fries, before tax and tip. Steelyard’s own prices are shown only in the app or at order.fiveguys.com.

Does Five Guys Cleveland deliver?

Yes. The Steelyard store offers delivery and curbside pickup, and you can order online at order.fiveguys.com. I could not confirm which third-party apps serve this location.

Is Five Guys safe with a peanut allergy?

Be cautious. Five Guys fries in peanut oil, and reviewers describe free peanuts handed out while customers wait.

Does Five Guys have vegetarian or bun-free options?

Reviewers mention a lettuce wrap in place of the bun. A Veggie Sandwich and a Grilled Cheese are reported on the US menu, but I could not confirm them on the official menu page.
