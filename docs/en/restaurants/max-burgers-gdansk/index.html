MAX Burgers Gdansk: 3 Restaurants, Hours, Prices

Bobby Burger has left Gdańsk. These are the city's three MAX Premium Burgers, with addresses, hours, złoty prices and ratings checked in November 2026.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 13, 2026

As far as I can find, Bobby Burger no longer has a single restaurant in Gdańsk. The Polish chain went into liquidation in December 2024, and its website lists no branches. If you want a chain burger in the city today, the name to know is MAX Premium Burgers, from Sweden. It has three restaurants here: MAX Gdańsk Forum next to the main station, MAX Osowa and MAX Kowale. The two suburban branches both have a drive-thru.

I checked every address, opening hour, price and rating below on 7 November 2026. When two sources disagree, I name both rather than quietly pick one. Prices are in Polish złoty (zł).

Is Bobby Burger still open in Gdansk?

No. I found no open Bobby Burger in Gdańsk, and the company that ran the chain is being wound up. Some travel guides and map listings still show a Gdańsk branch. They are out of date.

Bobby Burger began in 2012 as a food truck working events in Warsaw, founded by Krzysztof Kołaszewski and Bogumił Jankiewicz. The first permanent restaurant followed in Warsaw in 2013, and at its peak the chain ran nearly 50. It belonged to the Real Food group, set up in 2018 with Michał Kiciński, co-founder of CD Projekt (Franczyza w Polsce, Horecatrends).

In December 2024 the partners voted to dissolve the company and put it into liquidation. By February 2025 Bobby Burger was down to 13 restaurants (Foodservice24). The company is now registered as BOBBY BURGER SP. Z O.O. w likwidacji, KRS 0000447567 (Bizraport).

Gdańsk had already dropped off the map by then. In February 2025 the chain’s remaining cities were Warsaw, Kraków, Sopot, Bydgoszcz, Rzeszów, Toruń, Radom and Łódź. On 7 November 2026, bobbyburger.pl showed only one line, ‘Ta restauracja jest czasowo niedostępna’ (‘this restaurant is temporarily unavailable’), and its restaurant list page returned a 404 error.

What happened to Bobby Burger at Galeria Madison?

The Gdańsk branch I can document best was in the Galeria Madison shopping centre, ul. Rajska 10. It opened in the second half of July 2019 on 118.51 m², on the second floor next to the food court (e-restauracja, Eurobuild CEE). Its Uber Eats listing, ‘Bobby Burger - CH Madison’, shows it as closed since 3 February 2022. That is the only date I have: no primary source records the closure.

Some listings also put a Bobby Burger at ul. Długa 74. I found no opening date for it and nothing to suggest it is still there.

Beware the Sopot address listed as Gdansk

This one catches people out. Several aggregators list Bohaterów Monte Cassino 49, 81-767 as Bobby Burger Gdańsk, but that address is in Sopot, on the Monte Cassino promenade. That branch closed on 9 February 2025, and a Kebab King Premium has taken over the unit (eSopot).

MAX Gdańsk Forum: the closest MAX to the Old Town

MAX Gdańsk Forum is the one to pick if you are staying in the centre. It sits inside the Forum Gdańsk shopping centre at Targ Sienny 7, near Gdańsk Główny station and the Main Town (Główne Miasto).

Gdańsk Main Town (Główne Miasto), looking towards the Main Town Hall tower: from here MAX Gdańsk Forum is a short walk away at Targ Sienny 7 (Photo: Bukephalos, public domain, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gda%C5%84sk\_G%C5%82%C3%B3wne\_Miasto\_-\_D%C5%82ugi\_Targ\_(by\_Bukephalos).jpg)

  • Address: Targ Sienny 7, 80-806 Gdańsk
  • Phone: +48 58 732 11 21
  • Services: express till, breakfast, MAX delivery
  • Official page: maxpremiumburgers.pl, Gdańsk Forum

It opened in 2019, the second MAX in Gdańsk. Of the three, it gets the clearest gluten-free feedback: reviewers on Find Me Gluten Free praise being able to order any burger in a gluten-free bun.

MAX Osowa: drive-thru and the latest closing time

MAX Osowa is the late-night choice, with official closing times between midnight and 3am. It stands on the car park of the Osowa shopping centre, on the north-western side of the city.

  • Address: ul. Spacerowa 48 (48B in MAX’s press release), 80-299 Gdańsk
  • Phone: +48 58 501 26 50
  • Services: drive-thru, Wi-Fi, express till, breakfast
  • Official page: maxpremiumburgers.pl, MAX Gdańsk Osowa

It opened on 21 December 2021 as the third MAX in Gdańsk. At the time, MAX described a 130 m² dining room with 60 seats, a 50-seat garden and five self-order kiosks (MAX press release). Spinach adds card and mobile payment and a terrace, and labels it ‘very vegan-friendly’.

MAX Kowale: the first MAX in Gdańsk

MAX Kowale, at ul. Przywidzka 11, was the first MAX in Gdańsk and one of the first in Poland. Despite the name, the address is inside Gdańsk (postcode 80-174), on the edge of the neighbouring Kowale municipality.

  • Address: ul. Przywidzka 11, 80-174 Gdańsk
  • Services listed by Spinach: drive-thru, dining room, order kiosks, wheelchair access, terrace, card and mobile payment
  • Phone and official page: neither turned up in my search, so check the MAX app or call another branch before a detour

Eurobuild CEE announced the project in October 2017 as the second MAX in Poland: 385 m² on the ground floor, a drive-thru and 35 parking spaces, at the Przywidzka and Czermińskiego junction (Eurobuild CEE). It opened in 2018. Whether it still has 35 parking spaces, I cannot say.

MAX Gdansk menu and prices in złoty

An Original burger at MAX costs around 24.95 zł on its own and 38.95 zł as a meal, based on national prices listed in November 2026. A Double Original meal is 47.95 zł, and the cheapest burger in the same data, the Chili Cheese, is 17.45 zł. These prices come from Uber Eats and Pricelisto. I had no Gdańsk menu to read, so prices there may differ.

They also differ from what I reported for MAX Burgers in Krakow, where delivery platforms listed the Original at 27.95 zł. Delivery-app prices move, so check the kiosk before you order.

What is the MAX Rywal Chili Cheese?

The Rywal Chili Cheese is the burger MAX launched in every Polish restaurant on 5 November 2026, from 29.95 zł on its own or 39.95 zł as a meal. It comes in five versions: beef, beef and bacon, chicken, Plant Beef and Crispy Supreme.

Every version stacks a breaded chili cheese patty, jalapeños, cheddar, pickled red onion and Onion Mayonnaise sauce. The Combo Rywala costs 49 zł and adds four Cream Cheese Jalapeño Bites (Horecanet).

Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free burgers at MAX Gdansk

Of the chains in this guide, MAX makes meat-free burgers easiest to find in Gdańsk. Its Plant Beef patty goes into burgers such as the new Rywal Chili Cheese, and the whole Supreme Green range is plant-based.

Spinach labels MAX Osowa ‘very vegan-friendly’, and reviewers there mention the vegan options. At Kowale, some reviewers find the plant-based choice thin. Gluten-free diners should head to Forum, which gets good feedback for serving every burger in a gluten-free bun.

What reviewers say about MAX in Gdansk

Google ratings for MAX in Gdańsk are good, not outstanding: 4.1/5 at Kowale from 4,089 reviews and 4.0/5 at Osowa from 2,021 reviews. The figures come from Spinach, checked on 7 November 2026.

  • Kowale: reviewers praise friendly, fast service and a pleasant atmosphere, but mention waits at peak times.
  • Osowa: reviewers praise generous portions, good value, vegan options and sauces you can customise.
  • Forum: reviewers on Find Me Gluten Free single out the gluten-free buns. Its Google rating remains unverified.

Delivery and getting to MAX in Gdansk

MAX Gdańsk Forum offers MAX’s own delivery service, according to its official page. Whether the Gdańsk restaurants are currently on Uber Eats, Wolt or Glovo, I could not confirm.

Forum is the only branch you can walk to from the historic centre. Osowa and Kowale are built for drivers, with drive-thru lanes. I have not checked bus or tram routes to them, so use the local journey planner or take a taxi.

MAX in Gdańsk and in Poland: a short history

MAX is a family-owned Swedish chain founded in 1968 in Gällivare by Curt Bergfors and Britta Fredriksson. On 15 September 2025 it had 200 restaurants: 160 in Sweden, 26 in Poland, 8 in Norway and 6 in Denmark (MAX press release). Our MAX Burgers brand profile has the full history and the latest figures.

A MAX Burgers restaurant at dusk in Hammarby Sjöstad, Stockholm: the Swedish family chain founded in 1968 had 200 restaurants by September 2025, 26 of them in Poland (illustrative photo, not taken in Gdańsk) (Photo: Arild Vågen, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Max\_Hammarby\_sj%C3%B6stad\_2012.jpg)

The first Polish MAX opened on 1 September 2017 in Wrocław. Gdańsk came next with Kowale in 2018, then Forum in 2019 and Osowa in December 2021. When Osowa opened, Gdańsk was MAX’s second city in Poland after Warsaw.

In February 2023, Horecatrends counted 21 MAX restaurants in Poland, three of them in Gdańsk and none in Gdynia or Sopot (Horecatrends). In November 2026 I found no fourth Gdańsk branch. So for a MAX burger anywhere in the Tri-City, you still have to go to Gdańsk.

Other burger chains in Gdansk and the Tri-City

The homegrown alternative I would point you to is Pasibus, the Polish burger chain that started in Wrocław. Local portal trojmiasto.pl lists a Pasibus at ul. Stągiewna 27 on Granary Island (Wyspa Spichrzów), a few steps from the Main Town, and another in Gdynia at Centrum Riviera. I have not checked their current hours.

My verdict: which MAX in Gdansk should you choose?

For most visitors the answer is Forum: it is the only branch you can walk to from the Main Town, and its gluten-free buns are a real plus. Drive to Osowa if it is past 11pm or you want the drive-thru, but phone first, because its official hours do not add up. Kowale only makes sense if you are already on the southern edge of the city.

I have not rated these restaurants, because this guide is not a review of the food. If you came looking for Bobby Burger, MAX is the closest substitute in town: a fast-food chain, yes, but one with a broad plant-based range and late hours.

FAQ: MAX Burgers and Bobby Burger in Gdansk

Is Bobby Burger still open in Gdansk?

No. I found no open Bobby Burger in Gdańsk on 7 November 2026. The company went into liquidation in December 2024, Gdańsk was not among its remaining cities in February 2025, and its website lists no restaurants.

Where are the MAX Burgers restaurants in Gdansk?

There are three: MAX Gdańsk Forum at Targ Sienny 7 (Forum Gdańsk mall), MAX Osowa at ul. Spacerowa 48B, and MAX Kowale at ul. Przywidzka 11.

Which MAX is closest to Gdansk Old Town?

MAX Gdańsk Forum, at Targ Sienny 7 in the Forum Gdańsk shopping centre, near Gdańsk Główny station and the Main Town.

Does MAX Gdansk have a drive-thru?

Yes, at two branches: MAX Osowa (ul. Spacerowa 48B) and MAX Kowale (ul. Przywidzka 11). The Forum branch, inside a shopping centre, does not list one.

Is MAX in Gdansk open late at night?

Yes. On 7 November 2026, MAX Osowa’s official page listed a 1am close on most days, 3am on Tuesdays and midnight on Wednesdays. Kowale closes at 1am on Fridays and Saturdays, and Forum at midnight on Fridays and Saturdays.

How much does a burger cost at MAX in Poland?

In November 2026, an Original cost about 24.95 zł on its own and 38.95 zł as a meal, based on national delivery prices. The new Rywal Chili Cheese starts at 29.95 zł, or 39.95 zł as a meal.

Are there vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free burgers at MAX Gdansk?

Yes. MAX offers Plant Beef patties and a plant-based Supreme Green range, and reviewers at Forum praise the gluten-free buns available for every burger.

Does MAX deliver in Gdansk?

MAX Gdańsk Forum offers MAX’s own delivery service, according to its official page. I could not confirm the current Uber Eats, Wolt or Glovo listings.
