Five Guys Amsterdam: Why There Isn't One

Amsterdam refused Five Guys a licence in 2018 and the top court agreed in 2020. Why, the nearest Five Guys, and where to eat instead.

By Sofia, Restaurants writer, Italy, Switzerland and the BeneluxPublished November 27, 2026

There is no Five Guys in Amsterdam. As of 19 November 2026, the American chain has no restaurant anywhere in the Dutch capital. In March 2018 the mayor refused it a licence for Reguliersbreestraat 20, and on 25 March 2020 the Council of State (Raad van State) upheld that refusal. If you want a burger chain in Amsterdam, go to The Butcher: it was founded in the city in 2012, and its official site lists eight Amsterdam locations. The nearest Five Guys we could confirm is at Utrecht Central Station, about 30 minutes from Amsterdam Centraal by train.

Is there a Five Guys in Amsterdam?

No. Five Guys has no restaurant in Amsterdam, Netherlands, and none had been announced as of 19 November 2026. Amsterdam Lokaal, a Dutch local site, reached the same answer in October 2026 and sends its readers to branches in other Dutch cities.

Search results add to the confusion. Many “Five Guys Amsterdam” listings point to Amsterdam, New York, a small city upstate where Five Guys does trade, at 4855 State Highway 30. If a listing gives that address, it isn’t in the Netherlands.

Then there’s the irony. According to Wikipedia, Five Guys’ European companies (FGE International, FGO International BV and FG Coöperatief U.A.) are registered in Amsterdam. The chain runs its European business from a city where you can’t buy one of its burgers.

Why Five Guys couldn’t open in Amsterdam: the Reguliersbreestraat case

Five Guys couldn’t open in Amsterdam because the city classed it as fast food, and the site it wanted was zoned for restaurants only. Two courts agreed. The timeline below comes from the rulings and from Dutch news coverage at the time.

The 2018 licence refusal

On 26 March 2018, the mayor of Amsterdam refused FGO International B.V., the company behind Five Guys, an operating licence for Reguliersbreestraat 20. The zoning plan there allowed only category 4 hospitality (horeca 4): restaurants, lunchrooms, tea rooms and ice-cream parlours. Not fast food.

By May 2018 the city was calling Five Guys the “typical” kind of fast-food chain it wanted fewer of in the centre, according to DutchNews and NL Times. Five Guys argued that this restaurant would be different: more seats, table service instead of an ordering counter, a slower pace.

The court rulings in 2019 and 2020

On 7 June 2019 the Amsterdam district court ruled that Five Guys is fast food (horeca 1), even with reusable crockery and table service. Five Guys appealed. On 25 March 2020 the Raad van State, the Netherlands’ highest administrative court, dismissed the appeal (ECLI:NL:RVS:2020:873).

My read: the burgers were never the issue. The case was about how a place sells food, and the judges weren’t persuaded that real plates and a waiter turn a fast-food chain into a restaurant. For any chain eyeing central Amsterdam, that’s the precedent that counts.

Where is the nearest Five Guys to Amsterdam?

The nearest Five Guys we could confirm is in Utrecht, at Stationsplein 20, 3511 ED Utrecht, inside the Hoog Catharijne shopping centre that connects to Utrecht Central Station. Direct trains from Amsterdam Centraal take about 30 minutes.

Is it worth the trip? Not on its own, in my view. An hour on the train there and back is a lot for a chain burger. If you’re already changing trains at Utrecht Centraal, though, it’s a short walk through the mall.

The Butcher Amsterdam: the burger chain born in the city

The Butcher is the burger chain to try in Amsterdam. Yossi Eliyahoo of The Entourage Group founded it in 2012, and the first restaurant opened on Albert Cuypstraat in De Pijp. It serves Aberdeen Angus beef burgers in semi-brioche buns, a style it calls “haute fast food”, according to The Caterer and Franchising.eu.

It grew fast. In 2017 The Butcher took €12 million in revenue from four company-owned sites in Amsterdam and was aiming for 20 UK restaurants within five years, The Caterer reported in June 2018. Its official site also lists locations in London, The Hague, Manchester and Gibraltar.

The Butcher Amsterdam locations: which one to choose

The official site lists eight The Butcher locations in Amsterdam: Social Club, Albert Cuyp, Nine Streets, Damrak, Smashed Burger Albert Cuyp, Schiphol Airport, Foodhallen and On Wheels. For a first visit, I’d pick Albert Cuyp in De Pijp. It’s the original, it has the most complete details we could confirm, and the Albert Cuyp market outside is worth the walk on its own. The Butcher Albert Cuyp: address, phone and delivery

The Butcher Albert Cuyp is at Albert Cuypstraat 129, 1072 CS Amsterdam, in De Pijp, phone +31 20 470 7875. It delivers through Thuisbezorgd and Uber Eats. The head office answers on +31 20 811 3322 and at info@the-butcher.com.

We haven’t printed opening hours. Sources disagree: noon to 11pm, noon to midnight, or 11am to 1am. Check the location page on the-butcher.com or Google Maps before you go.

Is there a The Butcher at Schiphol Airport?

Yes. The Butcher lists a Schiphol Airport location on its official site. We couldn’t confirm the terminal zone or the hours, so check the chain’s Schiphol page before you fly.

The Butcher Amsterdam menu: burgers, prices and vegetarian options

The Butcher’s menu is built around Aberdeen Angus beef burgers cooked to order. There are also lamb, chicken, fish and vegetarian burgers, with toppings such as blue cheese, jalapeños and bacon, according to In Your Pocket. The kitchen is open to the dining room. The full menu is a PDF at the-butcher.com/menu.

How much does a burger cost at The Butcher Amsterdam?

We couldn’t confirm current prices. The only figure we found was “just under €10” for a burger, in an undated In Your Pocket listing that is probably out of date. Check the official PDF menu or a delivery app for current prices in euros.

Does The Butcher have vegetarian burgers?

Yes. The Butcher has a vegetarian burger alongside its beef, lamb, chicken and fish options. We couldn’t confirm vegan or halal options, so ask when you order.
