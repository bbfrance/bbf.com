4 Sobe Gospođe Safije in Sarajevo: Who Runs It

Dalila and Amer Sinanović's restaurant in a 1910 Sarajevo villa: modern Bosnian fine dining, not burgers. Owners, legend, dishes and opening hours.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 25, 2026

4 Sobe Gospođe Safije (“The Four Rooms of Mrs Safija”) is a fine-dining restaurant at Čekaluša 61 in Sarajevo, owned and run by the married couple Dalila and Amer Sinanović. It opened in July 2008 in a house built in 1910 and cooks modern dishes alongside traditional Bosnian ones. It is not a burger restaurant. Come here for a long dinner in an old villa. For a burger, go somewhere else in the city.

Who owns 4 Sobe Gospođe Safije?

Dalila and Amer Sinanović own and run 4 Sobe Gospođe Safije. They are married and describe themselves as equal partners. You will not read that in the English-language listings, which never name them. Their story is told in Bosnian: they gave it to Radio Sarajevo in 2015, and Bonjour.ba profiled the restaurant in 2016.

Dalila Sinanović, the visionary of the house

Dalila Sinanović, also known as Dalila Lakomica, is the restaurant’s legal proprietor. Local media call her its visionary. The akta.ba business register lists her as proprietor of “4 SOBE GOSPOĐE SAFIJE s.u.r. – konoba Sarajevo” at Čekaluša 61. In a February 15, 2024 interview with Bonjour.ba, she is presented as the owner and the mind behind the place.

From a 2004 idea to a 2008 opening

Four years passed between the idea and the first service. According to Bonjour.ba, work on the concept began in 2004, and the restaurant officially opened in July 2008. The same 2016 profile credits architect Nedžad Hrelja with the interior design and the reconstruction of the house. It is the only source that names him. The patience paid off where it counts: the 1910 house was restored, not gutted.

What does “4 Sobe Gospođe Safije” mean?

“4 Sobe Gospođe Safije” means “The Four Rooms of Mrs Safija”, a name taken from the love story the restaurant tells about its house. The venue is split into four spaces (restaurant, bar, lounge and garden), and that is the most natural reading of the “four rooms”. I found no statement from the owners that spells it out, though.

The legend of Safija and the Austrian count

According to the restaurant, Austrian Count Johann von Herberstein had the house on Čekaluša built for Safija, the daughter of a beg (an Ottoman-era notable), in the years when Bosnia passed from Ottoman to Austro-Hungarian rule. The official site tells it as a forbidden love and a gift. Bonjour.ba names Safija’s family as the Magbulija. It is a good story, and the restaurant tells it well. But Austro-Hungarian rule began in 1878, three decades before the house’s 1910 date, and I found no historical record of Safija or the count. Read it as the house legend, not as history.

What kind of food does 4 Sobe Gospođe Safije serve?

4 Sobe Gospođe Safije serves what it calls an “exotic fusion of modern cooking and traditional gems of BiH cuisine”, built mostly on local ingredients. The phrase says little on its own. The dishes the press has written about say more.

Dishes reported by the local press

These dishes come from interviews and reports published between 2015 and 2024. They show the kitchen’s style. They are not the current menu.

  • 2015 (Radio Sarajevo): tuna steak in a squid-ink crust with vegetable couscous; the tasting menu was the most popular order.
  • 2016 (Bonjour.ba): a fish fillet was the most-ordered dish.
  • 2024 (Bonjour.ba): Dalila recommended a celeriac steak in sesame with olive tapenade and celery sauce, paired with Mjesečar, a biodynamic wine from the Brkić winery in Čitluk, Herzegovina.

Read together, they describe a kitchen that leans on fish and vegetables as much as on the grilled meat Sarajevo is known for. When the owner’s own pick in 2024 is a celeriac steak, that tells you more about the place than its menu slogan does.

The 2017 guest dinner in Split

The team has cooked away from home. In April 2017 it served about 150 guests at Hotel Park in Split, Croatia, as Plava Kamenica reported. Chefs Emir Parovac, Nihad Mameledžija and Nikola Eterović sent out smoked Neretva trout, beef tartare with oxtail broth, polenta with a sous-vide egg and truffled dried beef, sous-vide lamb, and baklava with sweet kajmak. That is Bosnian and Herzegovinian produce through and through, cooked with modern technique. Those three chefs are documented for that event only, not as the restaurant’s current kitchen team.

Does 4 Sobe Gospođe Safije serve burgers?

No burger is verified on the menu of 4 Sobe Gospođe Safije. The restaurant’s December 2025 menu PDF could not be retrieved when I checked on November 13, 2026. The only trace I found is a single traveller review from October 2024 that mentions a pork burger, and I could not confirm it. Some listing sites claim a burger and a draught beer is “the standard order”. Nothing on the restaurant’s own pages supports that, so I left it out.

My advice is simple: do not plan a burger night around this address. If you are curious anyway, call ahead and ask whether one is on that evening.

Is 4 Sobe Gospođe Safije a good romantic restaurant in Sarajevo?

Yes, on its setting and its reputation. 4 Sobe Gospođe Safije is one of the Sarajevo restaurants most often listed for date nights and special occasions, and it also appears on the city’s fine-dining lists. A 1910 villa, a love story in the name, a tasting menu and a summer garden are made for a slow evening for two. I have not eaten there, so take this as a judgement on the setting and the format, not on the food.

Planning your visit

Book ahead and check the hours before you go. The official contact page gives Tuesday to Saturday, 15:00 to 23:00, closed Sunday and Monday (checked November 13, 2026), but hours may change with the season. Safija’s Garden opens in summer only. Reserve on +387 33 202 745, the number on the official site.

FAQ about 4 Sobe Gospođe Safije

Who owns 4 Sobe Gospođe Safije? Dalila Sinanović (née Lakomica) and her husband Amer Sinanović own and run the restaurant as equal partners. The business register lists Dalila as proprietor.

What does “4 Sobe Gospođe Safije” mean, and who was Mrs Safija? It means “The Four Rooms of Mrs Safija”. According to the restaurant, Safija was a beg’s daughter for whom the Austrian Count Johann von Herberstein built the house around the end of Ottoman rule. No historical record confirms the story, and the house itself dates from 1910.

When did 4 Sobe Gospođe Safije open? It officially opened in July 2008, after concept work that began in 2004. The house dates from 1910.

What kind of food does 4 Sobe Gospođe Safije serve? Modern cooking mixed with traditional Bosnian dishes, mostly from local ingredients. Dishes reported by the press include tuna steak in a squid-ink crust, a celeriac steak in sesame and a tasting menu.

Does 4 Sobe Gospođe Safije serve burgers? No burger is confirmed on its current menu. It is a fine-dining restaurant, not a burger restaurant.

What are the opening hours, and do I need a reservation? Tuesday to Saturday, 15:00 to 23:00, closed Sunday and Monday, per the official site on November 13, 2026. Book ahead on +387 33 202 745. Hours may change with the season.

How expensive is 4 Sobe Gospođe Safije? I could not verify current prices in convertible marks (KM), so I do not quote a range. Expect fine-dining prices, and check the menu or call before you go.
