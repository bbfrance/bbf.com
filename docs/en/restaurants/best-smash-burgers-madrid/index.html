Best Smash Burgers in Madrid: 10 Spots (2026)

Where to eat smash burgers in Madrid: the double that won Spain's burger championship, a €9.90 single, addresses and sourced prices.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished November 24, 2026

The best smash burgers in Madrid come from Junk Burger in Chamberí, whose Double Smashed won Spain’s national burger championship in 2022, and from Dalú Burger, which won The Champions Burger in 2026. For a classic beef, cheese and bun smash, go to La Prensa in Malasaña or Pacífico in Salesas. A simple smash costs €8 to €12; at the award-winning spots, budget €15 to €25 per person. All 10 addresses below were checked against their sources on November 14, 2026.

Most Madrid smash burger rankings I found while preparing this guide were dated 2024 or 2025, gave no prices and never said why one place beat another. This one does all 3. If you want the wider picture first, burgers of every style included, start with our Madrid burger guide.

How I picked and ranked Madrid’s smash burgers

I ranked the spots by public, checkable recognition: national competition wins first, then rankings by critics and food media, then how open each place is about its menu and prices. La Prensa sits at number 4 without a trophy because it is the place that brought the style to Madrid. This edition is built from sources, not from a tasting round, so there are no tasting notes yet. Every fact links to where it came from.

To qualify, a spot had to serve a real smash burger: a ball of beef pressed thin onto a very hot griddle. When a source gives only an average spend, the table says so. Pacífico’s and Basics by Goiko’s prices come from a delivery app and an aggregator, and dine-in prices can differ. I checked opening status against sources available on November 14, 2026, not by phone. Look at a spot’s Instagram or website before you go, especially La Prensa and Junk Burger.

What is a smash burger?

A smash burger is a ball of ground beef pressed hard onto a very hot griddle, which makes a thin patty with crispy, browned edges. The crust comes from the Maillard reaction, and cooks recommend beef with about 20% fat (80/20) (Hola, Directo al Paladar).

The style gave its name to Smashburger, the American chain, which doesn’t operate in Madrid. Every spot in this guide is a local business or a Spanish chain.

Pure smash vs. chef-driven smash

Madrid’s smash burger spots fall into 2 camps. Pure smash places like La Prensa, Pacífico and Junk stick to bun, beef and cheese. Chef-driven spots like Dalú, Kricky Pelton and The Hype pile on bacon jam, smoked cheese or house sauces. My bias is toward the first camp: the crust is the whole point of the style, and a heavy topping buries it.

1. Junk Burger: the Spanish championship winner

Junk Burger’s Double Smashed won the II Campeonato de España de Hamburguesas in March 2022, ahead of more than 240 competitors (Euro Weekly News). No other smash burger in Madrid has a bigger title that I could verify.

  • Address: Calle de José Abascal, 31, Chamberí (Ríos Rosas)
  • Signature: Double Smashed
  • Price: €15 to €20 average spend (IFEMA)

Junk sits at the pure end of the scale. I couldn’t confirm its current hours for this edition, so check before you cross town for it.

2. Dalú Burger: winner of The Champions Burger

Dalú Burger won The Champions Burger in 2026 with its Legend Winner, after the touring contest’s 18-day stop in Moratalaz (Time Out, June 1, 2026). That contest burger stacks a brioche bun, beef, smoked cheese, pulled ribs with bacon and crunchy onion.

  • Address: Calle de Hartzenbusch, 7, Chamberí
  • Signature on the menu: The Champion Smash, 2 100 g patties with cheddar, crispy onion and house bacon jam (The Sibarist)
  • Price: around €25 average spend (IFEMA)

3. Kricky Pelton: Uncle Jota’s, The Sibarist’s number 1

Kricky Pelton’s Uncle Jota’s topped The Sibarist’s ranking of Madrid smash burgers in May 2025 (The Sibarist). You choose the patty weight: 50, 100 or 150 g.

  • Address: Calle de Modesto Lafuente, 61, Chamberí
  • Signature: Uncle Jota’s
  • Price: around €18 average spend (Time Out)

The project belongs to Jota de Navasqüés-Pelton, singer of the band Mirrors Frequency, and draws on his mother Kricky Pelton’s cooking in Dallas (Neo2). Hence the Texan accent.

4. La Prensa: Madrid’s first smash burger

La Prensa in Malasaña is described as the first smash burger spot in Madrid, and it keeps things minimal: bun, beef and cheese (Madrid Secreto, IFEMA).

  • Address: Calle de Colón, 3, Malasaña
  • Signature: a double smash made with 2 70 g patties; Madrid Secreto says the cheese is made in-house
  • Price: not published by my sources

If you only have time for 1 smash burger in Madrid and you care about the crust, this is where I’d send you. Confirm it’s open first.

5. Pacífico Smash Burger: classic smash in Salesas

Pacífico serves a classic American-style smash burger from €11.00, and it holds a Guía Repsol Solete, the guide’s award for casual spots (Guía Repsol). Its prices are the clearest on this list.

  • Address: Calle del Conde de Xiquena, 2, Salesas / Chueca; other Madrid branches on Serrano and Don Pedro
  • Signature: Pacífico Smash Burger, €12.00 (beef, American cheddar, lettuce, onion, pickles, Pacífico sauce)
  • Also: Classic Smash, €11.00
  • Prices: listed on Uber Eats, checked November 14, 2026; dine-in prices may differ

6. The Hype: Samurai Shadow in Chamberí

The Hype’s Samurai Shadow is a double smash made with Galician beef aged 30 days, and IFEMA says it was named best burger of 2023 (IFEMA). It comes with double cheddar and American cheese, bacon, red onion pickled in lime and a house sauce.

  • Addresses: Calle del Cardenal Cisneros, 21 and Calle de Meléndez Valdés, 6, Chamberí
  • Signature: Samurai Shadow
  • Price: around €15 average spend (Time Out)

7. Milwaukee Burger: butter smash near Gran Vía

Milwaukee Burger is the easiest of these 10 to walk into without a plan. Its Infantas branch near Gran Vía takes no reservations and stays open all day, from 12:30 pm until past midnight (Time Out, January 8, 2025).

  • Addresses: Calle de las Infantas, 29 (Gran Vía); Calle de Narváez, 26 (Salamanca), home to the Billy Mitchell speakeasy, which takes reservations; Calle de Manuela Malasaña, 9; Avenida de América, 33
  • Signature: Wisconsin Butterburger, a double smash cooked with butter
  • Gluten-free: Milwaukee offers a gluten-free bun
  • Price: not published by my sources

8. Cesar’s Burgers: Campeona 2.0 in Arganzuela

Cesar’s Burgers is chef César Peña’s spot, and its Campeona 2.0 is one of the heaviest smash burgers in Madrid: 2 120 g Galician beef patties, 4 slices of American cheese, smoked bacon and XO sauce (Time Out).

  • Address: Calle de Méndez Álvaro, 74, 28045, Arganzuela (Madrid Secreto, Spotted by Locals)
  • Signature: Campeona 2.0
  • Price: around €15 per person

2 caveats. A 2024 IFEMA listing gives an older address, Calle San Lucas, 11 in Chueca, so the restaurant has probably moved; go by the 2025 sources. Madrid Secreto also credits Peña with Spain’s best burger of 2022 without naming the contest, so don’t mix it up with Junk Burger’s championship that year.

9. Muerdo: a short menu built around Hope

Muerdo keeps its menu to 5 burgers. The signature is Hope, made with La Finca beef, aged cheddar, candy bacon and a house sauce (Macarfi).

  • Address: Calle de Blasco de Garay, 96, 28003, Chamberí
  • Signature: Hope. Time Out calls it a double smash, Macarfi a triple, so ask at the counter
  • Price: around €15 average spend before drinks

10. Basics by Goiko: the budget smash chain

Basics by Goiko, the smash burger spin-off Goiko launched in 2019, is the cheapest reliable pick in this guide. A Single Smash costs €9.90 and comes with all-you-can-eat fries.

  • Addresses: Calle de la Princesa, 7 (Plaza de los Cubos); Calle de Hortaleza, 60 (Chueca); Avenida de Felipe II, 13 (Goya)
  • Prices: Single Smash €9.90, Basic Onion Smash €11.90, Classic Smash €12.40 (Gastroranking), checked November 14, 2026; confirm in the restaurant

It won’t beat Junk or La Prensa on character, but nothing else here gets you a smash and endless fries for under €10. Goiko’s main burgers are thicker and a different format: see all Goiko restaurants in Madrid for their addresses and hours, and our Goiko history and key figures page for the chain itself.

Other Madrid smash burgers worth knowing

2 more names come up often in Madrid smash burger coverage but missed the top 10 this time.

  • Maillard Smash Burger (Delicias and Ciudad Lineal) uses aged Galician beef. El Blog de GastroMadrid named its burger “burger of the week” on October 23, 2026 (source). My sources give no street addresses, so it stays off the list for now.
  • Manteca (Calle del Príncipe de Vergara, 56, Salamanca) was critic Alberto de Luna’s best smash in Madrid in 2023, ahead of Marlon’s and Boogie (Vozpópuli). I couldn’t confirm it’s still open in 2026.

Chamberí has more top smash burger spots than any other part of Madrid: 5 of the 10. Malasaña and Salesas are the best areas for a quick, simple smash near the center.

  • Chamberí: Junk Burger, Dalú, Kricky Pelton, The Hype, Muerdo
  • Malasaña: La Prensa, Milwaukee (Manuela Malasaña)
  • Salesas / Chueca: Pacífico, Basics by Goiko (Hortaleza)
  • Gran Vía / Plaza de España: Milwaukee (Infantas), Burger Jazz (Fomento), Basics by Goiko (Princesa, in Argüelles)
  • Salamanca: Milwaukee (Narváez), Pacífico (Serrano), Basics by Goiko (Goya)

FAQ: smash burgers in Madrid

Where is the best smash burger in Madrid? Junk Burger’s Double Smashed (Calle de José Abascal, 31) won the 2022 Spanish burger championship, and Dalú Burger (Calle de Hartzenbusch, 7) won The Champions Burger in 2026. For a classic beef-and-cheese smash, try La Prensa or Pacífico.

How much does a smash burger cost in Madrid? A simple smash burger costs €8 to €12, for example €9.90 for a Single Smash at Basics by Goiko or €11.00 for Pacífico’s Classic Smash. At the award-winning spots, expect to spend €15 to €25 per person.

Where can I get a cheap smash burger in Madrid? Burger Jazz’s simplest smash costs about €8, and Basics by Goiko’s Single Smash costs €9.90 with all-you-can-eat fries. Pacífico’s Classic Smash is €11.00 on delivery.

Which Madrid smash burgers have won awards? Junk Burger’s Double Smashed won the II Campeonato de España de Hamburguesas in March 2022. Dalú Burger won The Champions Burger in 2026 with its Legend Winner. Kricky Pelton’s Uncle Jota’s topped The Sibarist’s 2025 ranking.

Are there gluten-free smash burgers in Madrid? Yes. Milwaukee Burger offers a gluten-free bun at its Madrid branches. Elsewhere, ask staff before you order.

Do I need to book a smash burger restaurant in Madrid? Usually not for a quick burger. Milwaukee’s Infantas branch near Gran Vía takes no bookings and serves all day, while its Narváez branch takes reservations for the Billy Mitchell speakeasy. For the others, check the restaurant’s own channels.
