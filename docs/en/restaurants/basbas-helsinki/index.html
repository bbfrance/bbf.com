BasBas Helsinki: Address, Hours and Four Venues

BasBas Helsinki (Baskeri & Basso): address, hours and the four venues of Punavuori's natural-wine bistro, from Kulma to the new Sirkus. No burgers.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 11, 2026

BasBas Helsinki, full name Baskeri & Basso, is a natural-wine bistro in a courtyard at Tehtaankatu 27-29 in Punavuori, founded in 2015 by Nicolas “Niki” Thieulon and Kalle Kiukainen. It does not serve burgers. I went through every menu I could find for its four venues (Bistro, Kulma, Studio and the new Sirkus), and not one lists a burger. Come here for pasta, risotto, a chalkboard that changes often and a serious natural-wine list. For a burger, start from our Helsinki burger hub instead.

BasBas Helsinki at a glance

BasBas Bistro opens Tuesday to Friday from 16:00, in a courtyard off Tehtaankatu. It is closed at weekends, so on a Saturday go downstairs to Kulma, which opens at 14:00. I checked every line of this table against basbas.fi, the MyHelsinki listing and World of Mouth on 30 October 2026.

Who founded BasBas? Nicolas Thieulon and Kalle Kiukainen

Nicolas “Niki” Thieulon and Kalle Kiukainen founded BasBas. Three sources agree on that: a BasBas press release of 19 May 2026 distributed by STT, a 2024 profile in the Finnish trade magazine Avec Media and a 2017 report by Sparkling Travel Stories. Avec Media adds that Kiukainen writes the recipes of the upstairs bistro, with pasta and risotto at the centre.

Ownership has moved as the group grew. An STT release of 9 October 2026 names Noora Sipilä, Kalle Tanner and Nicolas Thieulon as shareholders. Is Kalle Tanner the same person as Kalle Kiukainen? I could not confirm it, so I name only the two founders the sources agree on.

Is Henri Alén involved in Baskeri & Basso?

No. Nothing I found ties Henri Alén to Baskeri & Basso. MyHelsinki, basbas.fi, both STT releases, Avec Media, World of Mouth and MTV Uutiset never name him as chef, owner or founder of BasBas.

The mix-up is easy to make, because Alén is a well-known Helsinki restaurateur too. He co-founded Muru-Dining Oy in 2010 with Timo Linnamäki and Samuil Angelov, according to MarketScreener. The company’s Ravintola Muru, at Fredrikinkatu 41, is a French bistro with an Italian touch. MyHelsinki and Yle report that it was named Helsinki’s restaurant of the year in 2012.

The BasBas concept: Paris bistro, San Francisco joint, Italian osteria

BasBas cooks simple, ingredient-driven food from a chalkboard menu that changes often, served with natural wines. MyHelsinki sums up the inspiration as Parisian bistros, casual San Francisco joints and small Italian osterias rolled into one.

That makes it the opposite of a burger bar, where the menu card barely moves from one year to the next. A 2018 visit written up by the Empty Plate blog lists whitefish carpaccio, scallops in ponzu, beef tartare, sage risotto and flank steak. Read that as a sample of the style. Today’s board will be different.

What does a meal at BasBas cost?

I could not confirm current BasBas prices. The only figures I found date from 2018, when the Empty Plate blog reported a “2+1+1” menu (starters, a pasta or risotto, a main) at €45 and wine at about €10 for a 16 cl glass. Eight years on, expect more; check the board or call the bistro before you go.

Why BasBas shows up in natural-wine guides

BasBas makes wine lists, not burger lists. World of Mouth, Decanter’s Helsinki guide for wine lovers and MyHelsinki all include it. In my view the wine is the reason to book here, and the food is built to go with it.

BasBas Bistro, Kulma, Studio and Sirkus: what is the difference?

BasBas is now a family of four venues: the original Bistro, Kulma downstairs, Studio on Pursimiehenkatu and Sirkus in Hakaniemi. Hours below come from basbas.fi, checked on 30 October 2026.

Venue Location What it is Hours

If you only have one slot in a short trip, my pick is Sirkus. It is the only BasBas venue open seven days a week, and from 07:30 on weekdays, so you can walk in for a bakery breakfast instead of planning a dinner around the bistro’s Tuesday-to-Friday schedule.

BasBas Kulma: the charcoal grill downstairs

BasBas Kulma opened in 2020 in the bistro’s building at Tehtaankatu 27-29. According to Avec Media, Veera Valtonen leads its kitchen and cooks over charcoal. It is also the only one of the Punavuori venues open on Saturdays.

BasBas Studio: a monthly themed menu

BasBas Studio, on Pursimiehenkatu in Punavuori, changes its themed menu every month, according to the May 2026 STT release and MyHelsinki. basbas.fi lists it from 16:00, Tuesday to Friday. I could not find its street number, so check basbas.fi for the exact door and the current theme before you go.

BasBas Sirkus: bakery, eatery and pasta factory in Hakaniemi

BasBas Sirkus opened on 15 October 2026 at Siltasaari 10 in Hakaniemi, according to a BasBas press release of 9 October 2026. Under one roof it puts a bakery and deli called the “Treatery”, a restaurant called the “Eatery” and an in-house pasta factory. Maija Ijäs runs the restaurant and Harri Nuora is head chef. The May release had promised September, so the opening slipped by about a month.

BasBas history: key dates

BasBas grew from one Punavuori bistro in 2015 to four venues in 2026. Each line names its source.

  • 2015: BasBas opens at Tehtaankatu 27-29 (Avec Media, Sparkling Travel Stories; the latter gives November 2015).
  • 2020: BasBas Kulma opens downstairs, with Veera Valtonen at the charcoal grill (Avec Media).
  • 19 May 2026: BasBas announces Sirkus in Hakaniemi (STT press release).
  • 15 October 2026: BasBas Sirkus opens at Siltasaari 10 (STT press release of 9 October 2026).

How I built this profile

This is a desk profile, not a review. I did not eat at BasBas for this piece, so it carries no rating. I checked the address, hours and phone on basbas.fi, MyHelsinki and World of Mouth on 30 October 2026, and I took the founders, venue openings and staff from the two STT press releases and Avec Media. Where a fact rests on one weak source, I left it out. Where I could not confirm something, the text says so.

BasBas Helsinki FAQ

Who owns BasBas in Helsinki?

Nicolas “Niki” Thieulon and Kalle Kiukainen founded BasBas. An October 2026 press release names Noora Sipilä, Kalle Tanner and Nicolas Thieulon as shareholders; I could not confirm whether Kalle Tanner and Kalle Kiukainen are the same person.

Is Henri Alén involved in Baskeri & Basso?

No. No source links Henri Alén to BasBas. He co-founded Muru-Dining Oy, the company behind Ravintola Muru at Fredrikinkatu 41.

What are BasBas Helsinki’s opening hours?

BasBas Bistro opens Tuesday to Thursday from 16:00 to 01:00 and Friday from 16:00 to 02:00, according to basbas.fi on 30 October 2026. Kulma, Studio and Sirkus keep their own hours (see the table above); Sirkus is the only venue open every day.

Do you need a reservation at BasBas Helsinki?

I could not confirm the booking policy. Call the bistro on +358 50 4673400 or check basbas.fi/bistro/ before you go.

Does BasBas serve burgers?

No. None of the sources I checked shows a burger on the menus of BasBas Bistro, Kulma, Studio or Sirkus. BasBas is a natural-wine bistro built around pasta, risotto and grill cooking.
