MAX Burgers Stockholm: Kungsgatan 4 Hours, Prices

Sweden's own burger chain now runs the address of Sweden's very first McDonald's, near Stureplan, and serves until 6 a.m. on Friday and Saturday nights.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 24, 2026

MAX Kungsgatan 4, at Kungsgatan 4A near Stureplan, is the MAX Burgers branch to head for in Stockholm. It opens at 10 a.m. every day and closes at 1 a.m. from Sunday to Tuesday, 5 a.m. on Wednesday and Thursday, and 6 a.m. on Friday and Saturday. Budget 100 to 200 SEK a person. You come for a late, cheap burger from Sweden’s own chain, and for the address itself: Sweden’s first McDonald’s opened here in 1973.

I cover Scandinavia for Burger Breton and have already followed MAX abroad, in Kraków and Gdańsk. Its home market is a different test. On 20 November 2026 I checked this branch’s hours, address and phone number against MAX’s official page. The history comes from press releases by MAX, McDonald’s Sweden and the landlord, Hufvudstaden. Ratings and prices come from the third-party platforms named in each section. Where I found no reliable source (metro exits, payment methods, a Kungsgatan price list), I say so rather than guess.

MAX Kungsgatan 4 address: where it is and how to find it

MAX Kungsgatan 4 is at Kungsgatan 4A, 111 43 Stockholm, at the Stureplan end of Kungsgatan, where Norrmalm meets Östermalm.

Stureplan is Stockholm’s nightlife hub, which explains the late hours. Search “MAX Kungsgatan” on a map and this is the branch that comes up. Delivery apps call it “MAX Sthlm – Kungsgatan”.

These are the hours on MAX’s official page on 20 November 2026, and they match the 2022 opening press release. Directories disagree, mostly about Sunday: 1 search snippet shows a 4 a.m. close, and Restaurant Guru contradicts itself. Go by the official page or the MAX app.

This matters if you are out late. At 5 a.m. on a Saturday or Sunday morning, Kungsgatan 4 is still serving, long after most kitchens in central Stockholm have closed.

Why MAX Kungsgatan 4 is famous: Sweden’s first McDonald’s

Kungsgatan 4 is where Sweden’s first McDonald’s opened, on 27 October 1973. It closed on Sunday 22 August 2021, and MAX moved in just over a year later.

The McDonald’s at Kungsgatan 4, Stockholm, in April 2017. Sweden’s first McDonald’s opened at this address on 27 October 1973 and closed on 22 August 2021, and MAX opened in the same space on 21 September 2022. (1218 × 952) (Photo: Anders Lindman, CC0 1.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stockholm\_City\_Kungsgatan\_McDonald%27s\_(33834108822).jpg)

MAX took over the lease on 1 December 2021: about 550 m² owned by the property company Hufvudstaden. It was one of 3 openings MAX planned for central Stockholm in 2022, with Odenplan and Sergels torg.

Richard Bergfors of MAX called it “a legendary address where the history of the burger in Sweden was written.” Big words, but fair. A Swedish chain taking over the American giant’s first Swedish address is a statement, and MAX clearly meant it as one.

The restaurant opened on 21 September 2022 (551 m², according to the opening release) with a burger sold only at this address, the Skrovmål Deluxe. I could not confirm whether it is still served, or at what price. Ask at the counter or check the app.

MAX, the Swedish fast food chain

MAX is Sweden’s homegrown burger chain, founded in 1968 in Gällivare, in Swedish Lapland, by Curt Bergfors and Britta Fredriksson.

The Bergfors family still owns it, and the head office is in Luleå. The name comes from Curt’s nickname, borrowed from the actor Max von Sydow.

MAX reached Stockholm in 1982 with a restaurant on Drottninggatan, and for years that was its only branch outside Norrland. I could not find out whether that first Stockholm restaurant still exists.

The 200th MAX opened on 15 September 2025 in Gislaved. At that point the chain had 160 restaurants in Sweden, 26 in Poland, 8 in Norway and 6 in Denmark. The full figures and history are on our MAX Burgers brand profile.

MAX Burgers vegan menu: plant-based and Impossible options

MAX has a real meat-free range, with Plant Beef, Impossible and Crispy Supreme versions of its burgers.

The labelling is clear, and HappyCow lists MAX Stockholm. The Grand Chili Cheese makes the point: the Plant Beef, Crispy Supreme and Impossible versions cost 97 SEK at Sköndal, the same as the beef Original. You pay nothing extra for going meat-free.

Reviewers at Kungsgatan also single out the vegan options. If you don’t eat meat, that is the strongest reason to choose MAX over the American chains in Stockholm.

MAX Burgers Stockholm reviews: what customers say

MAX Kungsgatan 4 has a Google rating of 3.9 out of 5 from 566 reviews, as relayed by Restaurant Guru on 20 November 2026.

I did not read that rating on Google Maps directly, and I could not get TripAdvisor or Yelp figures, so they are left out.

What reviewers praise at Kungsgatan:

  • Fast, friendly service.
  • Burgers they rate above the big American chains.
  • Vegan options.

What reviewers criticise at Kungsgatan:

  • Uneven quality from 1 visit to the next.
  • A harshly lit dining room with no outdoor seating.

Across the whole chain, the Trustpilot reviews summarised on our brand profile tell a similar story. Customers say the burgers have shrunk while prices have risen, and they report uneven cleanliness and service and missing items in delivery orders. On the plus side, they like the Swedish beef and the promotions.

Is MAX better than McDonald’s?

For a quick burger in Stockholm, I would pick MAX over McDonald’s. Reviewers at Kungsgatan rate its burgers higher, it puts Swedish beef forward and its plant-based range is clearly labelled.

Keep your expectations in check, though. A 3.9 rating and repeated complaints about consistency say this is still fast food, not a gourmet burger. MAX wins on its vegan range and on serving long after midnight. For a sit-down burger, book Mäster Anders or work through my best burgers in Stockholm.

Ordering, delivery and payment at MAX Stockholm

At MAX restaurants in Stockholm, you order at self-service kiosks, and you can get delivery through Wolt, Uber Eats and Foodora.

On Uber Eats, the flagship is listed as “MAX Sthlm – Kungsgatan”. Restaurant Guru lists dine-in, takeaway and delivery there.

Restaurant Guru also lists wheelchair access, but MAX’s own page does not confirm it, and it says nothing about payment methods, toilets or parking. Sweden runs mostly on cards, so bring one, and check the MAX app if you need to pay in cash.

What to order at MAX on your first visit

On a first visit, order a classic burger, then the Grand Chili Cheese while it lasts. It comes in beef, beef and bacon, chicken and 3 meat-free versions.

At Kungsgatan, ask whether the Skrovmål Deluxe, the 2022 opening exclusive, is still on the menu. If you don’t eat meat, go for the Plant Beef or the Impossible. They are the reason I would send a vegan friend to MAX rather than to the American chains.

FAQ: MAX Burgers Stockholm

Where is MAX Burgers in Stockholm? The flagship, MAX Kungsgatan 4, is at Kungsgatan 4A, 111 43 Stockholm, near Stureplan. Other central branches include Götgatan 41 (Medborgarplatsen), Sankt Eriksgatan 34–38 (Fridhemsplan) and Hagaesplanaden 46 (Torsplan).

Is MAX Kungsgatan open at night? Yes. It closes at 1 a.m. from Sunday to Tuesday, 5 a.m. on Wednesday and Thursday, and 6 a.m. on Friday and Saturday, according to MAX’s official page (checked 20 November 2026).

How much does a meal at MAX Stockholm cost? Plan on 100 to 200 SEK per person, according to Restaurant Guru. At MAX Sköndal, the limited-edition Grand Chili Cheese costs 97 SEK (103 SEK for the Beef ‘n’ Bacon), and meals cost 123 to 159 SEK.

Why is MAX Kungsgatan famous? It took over the address of Sweden’s first McDonald’s, which opened at Kungsgatan 4 on 27 October 1973 and closed on 22 August 2021. MAX opened there on 21 September 2022.

Does MAX have vegan burgers? Yes. MAX offers Plant Beef, Impossible and Crispy Supreme burgers, and HappyCow lists MAX Stockholm. The options are clearly labelled.

Can I get MAX delivered in Stockholm? Yes, through Wolt, Uber Eats and Foodora. In the restaurants, you order at self-service kiosks.

Can I pay cash at MAX in Sweden? I could not confirm accepted payment methods from an official MAX source. Bring a card, and check the MAX app or ask at the counter if you need to pay in cash.
