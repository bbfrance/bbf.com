Carl's Jr Cancun: Locations, Hours, Menu Prices and Reviews (2026)

Where to find Carl's Jr in Cancún, which branch to pick, what a burger costs in pesos and what diners say, all checked on October 7, 2026.

By AlexPublished October 7, 2026

Carl’s Jr in Cancun means one branch above all: the downtown restaurant on Avenida Yaxchilán, in El Centro, about 310 m from Parque de las Palapas. It is the only Cancún branch with a page on the official Carl’s Jr. México site and by far the most reviewed, at 4.2/5 on Google from about 3,486 reviews. Directories list it open every day from 8 am to 10 pm. Staying in the Hotel Zone? I found no confirmed branch there, so plan on a trip into town. Two more branches turn up in listings, at Las Plazas Outlet and Plaza Las Américas. A Famous Star con Queso on its own runs about 99 MXN, going by unofficial national menu prices.

What is Carl’s Jr.? The chain in Mexico

Carl’s Jr. is a Californian burger chain that has been in Mexico since 1991 and counts Mexico as its largest market outside the United States. Carl Karcher started the business in 1941 with hot dog stands in Los Angeles, and the first Carl’s Jr. restaurants opened in Anaheim, California, in 1956 (Milenio).

Carl Karcher, founder of Carl’s Jr., with a hot dog cart outside the company headquarters in Anaheim, California, in 1981. He started the business in 1941 with hot dog stands in Los Angeles. (Photo: Los Angeles Times, CC BY 4.0 (Wikimedia Commons))

The first Mexican restaurant opened in San Pedro Garza García, in Nuevo León, in 1991 (Milenio). The chain passed 200 Mexican restaurants in December 2015 (Franchising.com), and Milenio counted more than 300 in 2024. Carl’s Jr. belongs to CKE Restaurants Holdings, which also owns Hardee’s, the name many Americans know from the East and Midwest.

The brand turned 35 in Mexico in 2026. To mark it, on September 30, 2026, a Famous Star con Queso on its own sold for 35 MXN, excluding combos and airport restaurants (Crónica). It was a one-day deal. Don’t expect that price at the counter today.

Carl’s Jr. Yaxchilán: the downtown pick

Carl’s Jr. Yaxchilán is the branch to choose in Cancún: it is central, reviewed by thousands of diners and the only one with an official page. The chain lists it as “Carl’s Jr. Cancún”, Av. Yaxchilán 22, Cancún, Quintana Roo, C.P. 77500, phone 998 256 6397 (carlsjr.com.mx).

Avenida Yaxchilán at night in downtown Cancún, the street of the main Carl’s Jr. branch, about 310 m from Parque de las Palapas (photo from 2007). (Photo: Matthew T Rader, CC BY-SA 4.0 (Wikimedia Commons))

Directories and Wanderlog put the same branch at “Av. Yaxchilán 13 (Mz 12)”, with the same phone number (Sucursales24; Wanderlog). The “22” may be Supermanzana 22, the city block, rather than a street number. Nobody I could find settles it. My advice: search “Carl’s Jr Yaxchilán” in your maps app rather than typing a number.

The branch sits about 310 m from Parque de las Palapas, a short walk from the evening food carts. Competition is close: a Burger King about 350 m away, at Av. Yaxchilán and Av. Xcaret, and a Domino’s about 210 m away on Av. Cobá (Sucursales24).

Parque de las Palapas in downtown Cancún, a short walk from Carl’s Jr. Yaxchilán and the city’s evening food-cart hub. (Photo: City Foodsters, CC BY 2.0 (Wikimedia Commons))

Carl’s Jr. Las Plazas Outlet

Las Plazas Outlet Cancún appears in directories as a Carl’s Jr. branch, but no official source says it is open today. The listed address is Av. Quintana Roo, corner of Prolongación Kohunlich, s/n, C.P. 77500, with phone 998 848 3888 and hours of 8 am to 11 pm daily (Sucursales24; Sucursales y Horarios). Call before you make the trip.

Carl’s Jr. Plaza Las Américas

Plaza Las Américas has a Carl’s Jr. listed on Uber Eats as “Carl’s Jr. - Plaza Americas” (Uber Eats). The mall is on Av. Tulum Sur, SM 7, and is Cancún’s largest shopping center, open since 1998 (Wikipedia). Where the counter sits inside the mall, and when it opens, no source I found says.

Inside Plaza Las Américas on Av. Tulum Sur, Cancún’s largest mall, which has a Carl’s Jr. listed on Uber Eats (photo from 2012). (Photo: Eduardo P, CC BY-SA 3.0 (Wikimedia Commons))

Is there a Carl’s Jr. in the Cancún Hotel Zone?

No Carl’s Jr. branch is confirmed in the Zona Hotelera or at Cancún airport. From the beach strip, the nearest documented option is the downtown Yaxchilán branch. Our Cancún burger hub explains how the Hotel Zone and downtown differ.

What to order at Carl’s Jr. in Cancún

Diners at the Yaxchilán branch single out the Western Bacon, the Double Western Bacon and the Diablo burger, plus curly fries, Chili Cheese Fries and milkshakes made with real ice cream (Wanderlog). My one order would be the Western Bacon with curly fries: it is the burger reviewers name most often. Want heat? Go for the Diablo.

Carl’s Jr. Western Bacon Cheeseburger, with onion rings and BBQ sauce: the burger Cancún reviewers mention most (about 114 MXN in Mexico; photo taken in the US). (Photo: BrokenSphere, CC BY-SA 3.0 (Wikimedia Commons))

Is the Mexican menu different from the US menu?

The core burgers carry the same names as in the US, often with “con Queso” added, and I found no Cancún-only item. The Mexican site runs its own national promotions: “Martes Todos Comen Carl’s” on Tuesdays, the Happy Star Burger and Carl’s Packs, and it has a “Menú y Nutrición” section (carlsjr.com.mx).

Vegetarian options at Carl’s Jr. Mexico

No vegetarian or plant-based burger is confirmed at Carl’s Jr. in Cancún. A Beyond Famous Star has been sold in the US, but nothing shows it reached Mexico. Check the “Menú y Nutrición” section of the official site before you go.

Carl’s Jr Cancun reviews: what diners say

The Yaxchilán branch rates 4.2/5 on Google across about 3,486 reviews and 3.5/5 on TripAdvisor across 29 reviews, as relayed by Wanderlog. Trust the Google score; it rests on a large sample. The TripAdvisor one is too thin to weigh much.

Carl’s Jr Cancun reviews for the Yaxchilán branch: Google 4.2/5 (about 3,486 reviews) vs TripAdvisor 3.5/5 (29 reviews), via Wanderlog, October 7, 2026. (Chart: Burger Breton, data from Wanderlog (Google and TripAdvisor ratings))

What reviewers like

Reviewers praise the table service, the two-level dining room, the cleanliness and the music. On the food, praise centers on the Western Bacon burgers, the Diablo, curly fries, Chili Cheese Fries and the real ice cream shakes (Wanderlog).

What reviewers criticize

The recurring complaints: air conditioning that struggles on hot days, waits and disorganization at peak times, and order mistakes. Opinions on prices are split, and some diners say a tip is expected, but not consistently (Wanderlog). My advice in Cancún’s heat: go outside the lunch rush, and check your order before you dig in.

Carl’s Jr. delivery in Cancún

You can get Carl’s Jr. delivered in Cancún through Uber Eats, which lists the Plaza Las Américas branch. The only combo prices I found come from delivery platforms: 185 to 239 MXN for a Famous Star con Queso combo. Rappi and DiDi Food listings for Cancún did not show up in my checks.

Which Carl’s Jr. should you choose in Cancún?

Choose the Yaxchilán branch. It is the only one confirmed by the chain, the most reviewed, and it pairs easily with an evening at Parque de las Palapas. Plaza Las Américas makes sense if you are already shopping there or ordering in. Las Plazas Outlet? I would not travel there without calling first.

Carl’s Jr Cancun FAQ

How many Carl’s Jr. locations are there in Cancún?

One branch is confirmed by the chain, on Av. Yaxchilán downtown. Directories and delivery apps also list Las Plazas Outlet and Plaza Las Américas. I could not confirm a total count.

What are the hours of Carl’s Jr. on Avenida Yaxchilán?

Directories and Wanderlog report 8 am to 10 pm, every day. The chain does not publish these hours, so check Google Maps or call 998 256 6397 before you go.

How much is a burger at Carl’s Jr. in Cancún?

National reference prices from October 2026 put a Famous Star con Queso at 99 MXN and an Original Big Angus at 163 MXN. Combos range from 185 to 239 MXN on delivery platforms. These figures are unofficial and not confirmed for Cancún.

Can I get Carl’s Jr. delivered in Cancún?

Yes. Uber Eats lists the Plaza Las Américas branch. Rappi and DiDi Food listings were not confirmed.

Is there a Carl’s Jr. in the Cancún Hotel Zone?

No branch is confirmed in the Zona Hotelera or at Cancún airport. The nearest documented branch is on Av. Yaxchilán, downtown.

Is the Carl’s Jr. menu in Mexico different from the US?

The main burgers share their US names, such as the Famous Star and Western Bacon Cheeseburger, often with “con Queso” added. Mexico runs its own promotions, such as “Martes Todos Comen Carl’s”. I found no Cancún-only item.

Does Carl’s Jr. have vegetarian options in Mexico?

Not confirmed. A Beyond Famous Star has existed in the US, but I found no verified plant-based burger on the Cancún menu.

Can I pay in US dollars or by card at Carl’s Jr. in Cancún?

I found no reliable source on payment methods at the Cancún branches. Carry Mexican pesos as a backup.

Should I tip at Carl’s Jr. in Cancún?

The Yaxchilán branch offers table service, and reviewers report that a tip is sometimes expected, though not consistently. Tipping is your call; no official policy is published.
