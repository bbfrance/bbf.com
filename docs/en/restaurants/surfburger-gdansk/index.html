SurfBurger Gdańsk: Founder Story and 12 Addresses

How Łukasz Wingert turned an old kebab van into Gdańsk's own burger chain in 2013: the story, all 12 addresses and what to order.

By Alex, Restaurants editorPublished November 17, 2026

SurfBurger is Gdańsk’s home-grown burger chain, founded in 2013 by Łukasz Wingert and Wojciech Kamiński. It began as one food truck, a refurbished Peugeot that used to sell kebabs. By November 2026 the official site lists 12 restaurants across the Tricity, the rest of Pomerania and Elbląg, plus 4 food trucks. The branch closest to the historic centre is at ul. Garncarska 30 in Śródmieście. And the burger the brand pushes hardest today is a smash: the Big Tony SMASH.

I like this story for 1 reason: it is local. Swedish MAX Burgers now runs 3 restaurants in Gdańsk, but SurfBurger was built here, out of a van that was refused the beach pitch it was made for. The sections below trace the history, list every address, and show what the menu has looked like, from the 2020 burger topped with Norwegian brown cheese to today’s smash line.

SurfBurger Gdańsk at a glance

SurfBurger is a Gdańsk-born burger chain that grew from one food truck in 2013 into 12 restaurants and 4 trucks in 2026.

  • Founded: 2013, by Łukasz Wingert with co-founder Wojciech (Wojtek) Kamiński
  • First vehicle: a refurbished Peugeot food truck, almost 20 years old, that previously sold kebabs
  • Debut: Open’er Festival in Gdynia
  • First permanent site: Morena, Gdańsk
  • Today: 12 restaurants on the official list and 4 food trucks (surfburger.pl, checked 12 November 2026)
  • Closest to the Old Town: ul. Garncarska 30, Gdańsk Śródmieście, +48 58 526 06 06
  • Current focus: smash burgers (Big Tony SMASH) and a smash sub-brand, SMASSSH Grillin by SurfBurger

Who founded SurfBurger? The Łukasz Wingert story

Łukasz Wingert founded SurfBurger in 2013 with Wojciech Kamiński, and 2018 club announcements name him as the owner. The founding story comes from 2 Polish sources: a July 2020 feature in the regional magazine Prestiż Trójmiasto, and an interview with Wingert on trojmiasto.pl.

Wingert sums up his approach in 1 line, quoted by Prestiż: ‘Selling food, I give people tangible happiness’ (my translation from Polish). I could not find out what role Kamiński plays today, so he appears here as co-founder and nothing more.

A 20-year-old Peugeot that used to sell kebabs

The first SurfBurger was a second-hand Peugeot food truck, almost 20 years old, that had been a kebab van. The founders refurbished it and named the business after the plan: feeding surfers on the Hel Peninsula, the thin strip of sand across the bay from Gdańsk where kitesurfers and windsurfers gather every summer.

From Open’er Festival to a first restaurant in Morena

The surfers never got their burgers. According to Prestiż, the planned pitches on the Hel Peninsula and at Gdynia Marina were refused under local trading rules. So the truck made its debut at Open’er Festival in Gdynia, one of Poland’s biggest music festivals.

Festival-goers on the grass at Open’er Festival in Gdynia in July 2010, with the main stage in the distance. SurfBurger’s first food truck made its debut at this festival in 2013. This photo was taken 3 years earlier and does not show the truck. (Photo: Yves6, CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Open%27er\_Festival\_2010\_(04).jpg)

Then summer ended and the customers kept coming. Winter demand pushed the founders to open a first permanent site in Morena, a residential district of Gdańsk. A 2013 promo video called ‘Fala Tejstu’ (roughly ‘wave of taste’) helped the name spread, Prestiż reports.

Was SurfBurger the first food truck in Gdańsk?

Possibly. In their trojmiasto.pl interview, the founders say SurfBurger was the first food truck in Gdańsk and the second in the Tricity. I could not verify that independently, so treat it as their claim. What no source disputes is the order: truck first, restaurants second.

The sponsorships tell you more about this brand than any slogan. Between 2016 and 2018 SurfBurger backed 4 local clubs in 4 sports: MH Automatyka in hockey, Trefl in volleyball and in basketball, and Wybrzeże Gdańsk in speedway. A burger chain with its name on a speedway track is courting locals, not tourists.

The SurfBurger closest to Gdańsk Old Town: Garncarska 30

If you are staying in the historic centre, the nearest SurfBurger is at ul. Garncarska 30 in Śródmieście. In Your Pocket Gdańsk puts it ‘a little way outside of the old town’. Its phone number, +48 58 526 06 06, is the same on the official site and on In Your Pocket. Trojmiasto.pl has reported that this branch draws a late crowd of partygoers. That makes it the one to remember if you are still hungry at the end of a night out.

Opening hours and delivery

This guide gives no opening hours, because the official site did not let me confirm them for any branch. Check surfburger.pl or call before you go. For delivery, the smash sub-brand SMASSSH Grillin by SurfBurger is listed on Uber Eats and Glovo for Jaśkowa Dolina 101 and Elbląg. How those listings relate to the dine-in restaurants is not confirmed.

What to eat at SurfBurger: burgers, smash and sauces

SurfBurger describes its burgers as 100% beef on buns baked daily with natural sourdough, with fresh vegetables, house sauces and vegetarian options. That is the brand’s own wording, from its trojmiasto.pl listing. The current menu is only published as images and in the brand’s app, so I list no prices in złoty, no patty weights and no full builds.

Smash burgers at SurfBurger: Big Tony SMASH and SMASSSH

Yes, SurfBurger makes smash burgers, and they are now its headline product. The official site promotes the Big Tony SMASH, and the brand runs a smash line called SMASSSH Grillin by SurfBurger; Wanderlog even lists a ‘Surfburger Smasssh Garncarska’. I could not get the Big Tony’s build in text form. My read: smash is where the market went, and SurfBurger followed. The 2020 menu below, with Black Forest ham and Norwegian brown cheese, had more personality.

The 2020 menu: Szwarcmaliner, Amerykanusz and Norwegian caramel cheese

In July 2020, SurfBurger served 16 permanent burgers, 4 vegetarian burgers and a burger of the month, according to Prestiż. 3 named burgers stood out:

  • Szwarcmaliner: with Black Forest ham
  • Amerykanusz: with onion rings
  • Forfiter: a chicken burger

The house sauces were barbecue, lime and honey-mustard. The signature pairing was Norwegian caramel cheese, the sweet brown cheese Norwegians call brunost, on a beef burger. I cannot confirm that any of these burgers, or the cheese, is still on the 2026 menu. Read this as history, and ask at the counter.

Vegetarian burgers at SurfBurger

SurfBurger has vegetarian burgers. In 2020 there were 4 on the permanent menu, and the brand still mentions vegetarian options in its own description. I could not find the current vegetarian line-up in text form.

SurfBurger is the home-grown chain of Gdańsk’s burger scene, alongside smash specialists, a rival food truck and Old Town burger bars. Trojmiasto.pl, the main local news site, features these names in its ‘best burgers in Gdańsk’ coverage:

How I researched this guide

I built this guide from the brand’s official site, dated regional press and listings, and checked every source on 12 November 2026. I did not visit a branch for this piece, so SurfBurger gets no rating from me.

A correction on Tomasz Deker. This guide started as a profile of a chef called Tomasz Deker and a burger restaurant called Deker. That did not hold up. Tomasz Deker is a Gdańsk pastry chef and chocolatier, and his café-bakery at Obrońców Wybrzeża 15/8 sells pastries, cakes, ice cream, bread and coffee, but no burgers. I found no burger restaurant called Deker in the city, so I wrote about the founder who did build Gdańsk’s own burger chain.

SurfBurger Gdańsk FAQ

Who founded SurfBurger in Gdańsk, and when? Łukasz Wingert founded SurfBurger in 2013 with co-founder Wojciech (Wojtek) Kamiński. Wingert was named as the owner in 2018.

Was SurfBurger the first food truck in Gdańsk? The founders say so: first in Gdańsk and second in the Tricity, according to their trojmiasto.pl interview. It is their claim and has not been independently verified.

Where is the SurfBurger closest to Gdańsk Old Town? At ul. Garncarska 30 in Śródmieście, a short way outside the historic centre. Phone: +48 58 526 06 06.

Does SurfBurger make smash burgers? Yes. The official site promotes the Big Tony SMASH, and the brand runs a smash line called SMASSSH Grillin by SurfBurger.

Does SurfBurger have vegetarian burgers? Yes. It had 4 vegetarian burgers on the permanent menu in 2020 and still lists vegetarian options today.

What are SurfBurger’s opening hours, and does it deliver? I could not confirm current hours on the official site, so check surfburger.pl before you go. SMASSSH Grillin by SurfBurger is listed on Uber Eats and Glovo for Jaśkowa Dolina 101 and Elbląg.

What are the best burgers in Gdańsk? Trojmiasto.pl features Wu Tang Fries, Zebra Burger, SurfBurger, Original Burger and Burgermania. Our Gdańsk burger guide gathers the city’s verified addresses.

Is Tomasz Deker in Gdańsk a burger restaurant? No. Tomasz Deker is a pâtisserie, café and bakery at Obrońców Wybrzeża 15/8, 80-398 Gdańsk. It sells pastries, cakes, ice cream, bread and coffee, and no burgers. Tomasz Deker works as a pastry chef and chocolatier.
