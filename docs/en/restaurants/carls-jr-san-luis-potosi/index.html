Carl's Jr San Luis Potosí: Locations, Hours, Menu

Carl's Jr San Luis Potosí: 5 official branches with addresses and phones, 2 in Soledad, hours, Rappi menu prices from 160 MXN and Google reviews (Nov 2026).

By Carmen, Restaurants writer, MexicoPublished November 27, 2026

Carl’s Jr. has five official branches in the San Luis Potosí area, according to the chain’s Mexican site: Centro, Carranza, Carretera 57, Sendero and Andares. Rappi lists more delivery points, and next-door Soledad de Graciano Sánchez has two restaurants, the newer one opened on October 16, 2025. A Famous Star con Queso costs 160 MXN on Rappi. The most reviewed branch is Carranza, near the UASLP university area, with 4.3/5 on Google from 5,035 reviews. I checked every address, price and rating below on November 23, 2026. If you only try one thing, make it the Famous Star: it is the cheapest burger on the menu, and Carranza is the branch with the most reviews.

This guide sits under our San Luis Potosí burger guide hub. Would you rather eat local? Zato’s, the charcoal-grill burger house in my guide to the best burgers in San Luis Potosí, has been on the same Avenida Carranza since 1987.

How many Carl’s Jr locations are there in San Luis Potosi?

Carl’s Jr. lists five official branches in the San Luis Potosí area on carlsjr.com.mx, and Rappi shows seven delivery points in the city. The two lists overlap but do not match, so I keep them apart below.

Extra Carl’s Jr delivery points on Rappi

Rappi also lists three points that have no page on the official site: Chapultepec, Villa Magna and Palmas.

  • Chapultepec: Av. Chapultepec 1229, Fracc. El Pedregal.
  • Villa Magna: Blvd. Manuel Gómez Morín 565.
  • Palmas (also shown as Plaza Palmas or “San Francisco”): Lateral San Luis Potosí–Matehuala 690, Insurgentes.

Online directories also mention Blvd. Antonio Rocha Cordero 700 (Lomas del Tec) and Av. Sierra Vista Poniente at Plaza Lomas. No official source confirms either one, so I left them out of the table. One directory even files “Capitán Carlos León S/N” under San Luis Potosí. That is the address of Mexico City’s airport.

Carl’s Jr Soledad de Graciano Sánchez: the newest branch

Soledad de Graciano Sánchez, the city next door, has two Carl’s Jr. restaurants, and the second opened on October 16, 2025. It sits in colonia San Felipe, on Calle Acapulco, almost at the corner of Avenida México. Mayor Juan Manuel Navarro Muñiz opened it at around 11 a.m., according to Plano Informativo.

The launch drew a crowd. Posta reported that hundreds of people camped outside overnight and more than 150 were in line before 8 a.m. Why? The first 100 customers got a booklet good for one free burger combo a month for a year. I could not find the street number of this branch, nor the address of the first Soledad location.

Carl’s Jr San Luis Potosi reviews

The Carranza branch is the only one in the city with a Google rating I could confirm: 4.3 out of 5 from 5,035 reviews, as shown on Wanderlog on November 23, 2026. I found no TripAdvisor or Yelp rating for any Carl’s Jr. in San Luis Potosí.

  • What guests like: the Big Angus burgers, the kids’ play area, the family atmosphere, the combos and the milkshakes.
  • What guests criticize: a play area that is not always clean, wrong orders, cashiers short of change and uneven quality from one visit to the next.

Most of those complaints are standard fast-food grumbles. The one about change is worth acting on: bring small bills.

On Rappi, the Chapultepec branch scores 4.4 out of 5 from 64 reviews left over the past 90 days. That is a delivery score, so it says more about packing and speed than about the dining room.

Is there a pet-friendly Carl’s Jr in San Luis Potosi?

In April 2024, Posta described Carl’s Jr. “The Park” in the state of San Luis Potosí as the only Carl’s Jr. in Mexico with a pet-friendly area. It also listed a sauce bar, free refills, table service and food cooked to order.

I could not confirm the exact address of that branch or whether the pet area is still open in 2026. The Park shopping center is reported at Blvd. A. Rocha Cordero 135, but nothing official ties it to the restaurant. Call before you bring your dog.

Carl’s Jr in Mexico: a short history

Carl’s Jr. arrived in Mexico in 1991, its first market outside the United States, with a restaurant on Avenida Humberto Lobo in San Pedro Garza García, Nuevo León. The chain began in 1941, when Carl N. Karcher and his wife Margaret bought a hot dog cart in Los Angeles. The first Carl’s Jr. restaurants opened in 1956 in Anaheim and Brea.

Mexico’s 400th Carl’s Jr. opened in Tampico on December 3, 2024, and the chain sells more than 200,000 burgers a day in the country, according to CKE Restaurants Holdings. CKE, based in Franklin, Tennessee, runs Carl’s Jr. and Hardee’s and has been controlled by Roark Capital since 2013. For its 35th year in Mexico, the chain sold the Famous Star con Queso alone for 35 MXN on September 30, 2026, at participating restaurants. I could not confirm whether the San Luis Potosí branches took part. The full story is on our Carl’s Jr. brand page.

FAQ: Carl’s Jr San Luis Potosi

How many Carl’s Jr are there in San Luis Potosi?

The official site lists five branches in the area: Centro, Carranza, Carretera 57, Sendero and Andares. Rappi adds Chapultepec, Villa Magna and Palmas, and Soledad de Graciano Sánchez has two more.

What are Carl’s Jr’s hours in San Luis Potosi?

The Carranza branch shows 10 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily on Google. On Rappi, Chapultepec delivers from 11 a.m. to 9:30 p.m. (10 p.m. on Thursday and Friday) and Palmas from 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. No 24-hour branch is confirmed.

How much is a burger at Carl’s Jr in San Luis Potosi?

On Rappi, burgers run from 160 MXN for a Famous Star con Queso to 360 MXN for an Original Big Angus Burger, checked November 23, 2026. Counter prices may be lower; I could not check them.

Which Carl’s Jr is closest to the historic center of San Luis Potosi?

The Centro branch, at Damián Carmona 1 on the corner of Avenida Venustiano Carranza, is the official branch closest to the historic center.

Does Carl’s Jr deliver in San Luis Potosi?

Yes, Carl’s Jr. delivers through Rappi from at least seven points in the city. I could not confirm whether it is also on Didi Food or Uber Eats.

Does Carl’s Jr have a vegetarian burger in Mexico?

Yes, the Portobello Burger is listed at 185 MXN on Rappi in San Luis Potosí. Availability varies by branch.

Which Carl’s Jr in San Luis Potosi has a play area or a drive-thru?

Google reviews mention a kids’ play area at the Carranza branch. I found no confirmed information on drive-thrus, parking, Wi-Fi or wheelchair access at any branch.

Is there a Carl’s Jr in Soledad de Graciano Sánchez?

Yes, there are two. The newest opened on October 16, 2025, in colonia San Felipe, on Calle Acapulco near Avenida México.
