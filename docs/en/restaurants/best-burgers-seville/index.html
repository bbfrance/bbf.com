Best Burgers in Seville: 8 Burger Joints for 2026

Seville's 8 best hamburgueserías, ranked on awards, beef and reviews: addresses, neighbourhoods and euro prices, each with its source.

By Valentina, Restaurants writer, Spain, Portugal and ArgentinaPublished November 17, 2026

The best burgers in Seville are at Burger Food Porn, whose Doble Cheese Bacon was named Spain’s best burger at the 2023 Spanish Hamburger Championship, and at Bacanal Burger near Las Setas, winner of the 2023 Sevilla Burger League. Djanco, Street Food Burger and Atticus Finch complete my top 5. Budget €8 to €15 for a burger. I built this ranking on 17 November 2026 from competition results, the Guía Repsol, the local food press and Google review data; every pick comes with its address and neighbourhood, and with a price wherever one is published.

How I ranked Seville’s burger restaurants

I ranked each restaurant on 4 criteria: verified, dated awards; the quality and origin of the beef and bread; Google rating and review volume; and evidence that the place is still trading. I do not copy third-party scores into my own verdict, and I name the source of every price and award below.

The awards I counted are the Campeonato de España de Hamburguesas, The Champions Burger, Best Burger Spain, the Sevilla Burger League and the Guía Repsol’s Soletes. When sources disagreed (on a street number or a dry-ageing time), I say so rather than pick 1 at random.

Most Seville lists run to 10. Mine stops at 8, on purpose. Nickel Burger, a usual pick, had a temporarily closed sign at 1 branch in December 2025, and its old website now redirects to a casino page. The other contenders I looked at (Chechi’s, Gottan Grill, Toro Burger Lounge, Godeo 2) each had a single source. Not enough to rank them.

Why Seville’s burger scene changed in 2025

Seville’s burger boom hit a wall in 2025: the local food site CosasDeCome counted more than 20 closures in the province that year, with an average bill of €20 to €30 per person. Empire State Burger closed, and Nickel put up a temporarily closed sign (CosasDeCome, 18 December 2025).

The biggest name also changed hands. On 15 December 2025, the Valencian group Tagus Tao, owner of The Black Turtle, bought Burger Food Porn. Menu, staff and suppliers were kept, and founder Juan Higueras stays on as brand ambassador (CosasDeCome, Sivarious). The places that survived are the ones below.

The 8 best burger restaurants in Seville, Spain

1. Burger Food Porn (Los Bermejales and El Arenal)

Burger Food Porn makes Seville’s most decorated burger: its Doble Cheese Bacon won Mejor Hamburguesa de España at the third Campeonato de España de Hamburguesas in 2023, with the final held in A Coruña, and was also named best burger in Andalusia (Directo al Paladar, 7 March 2023).

The winning burger stacks dry-aged Galician blonde beef aged in the house’s own chambers, aged cheddar, crisp smoked bacon, a fried-egg emulsion and chistorra sausage. It sits in a brioche bun from Ángel Puchi, a baker in Coria del Río. Sources disagree on the ageing time, so I won’t quote a figure. Burger Food Porn also says it served Seville’s first smash burger.

Juan Higueras, who cooked in Michelin-starred kitchens such as Alejandro in Almería, founded it with his brother José Antonio. In 2023 burgers cost €12.90 to €15 (El Español, 8 March 2023). I could not confirm 2026 prices, so check the menu before you go.

  • Main address: Av. de Alemania, 19, 41012 Sevilla (Los Bermejales), awarded a Solete Repsol de Barrio in 2022 (Guía Repsol)
  • City-centre branch: C. Gamazo, 4, 41001 Sevilla (El Arenal). No reservations.
  • Website: burgerfoodporn.com, Instagram @burgerfoodprn

I list Los Bermejales first because the Arenal branch scores lower: 6.8/10 on Gastroranking. Keeping Burger Food Porn at number one is a call I’ll own. No Seville burger holds a bigger title, but since December 2025 the kitchen has new owners. Tagus Tao says the recipe and the suppliers stay. If that changes, this ranking changes with it.

2. Bacanal Burger (Las Setas, Casco Antiguo)

Near Las Setas, Bacanal Burger is the one to beat: its Sibarita 2.0 won the medallion category of the 2023 Sevilla Burger League, ahead of Djanco and Lou Burger (Sevilla Secreta, 18 December 2023).

The double patty is 2 × 110 g of Galician beef, dry-aged for 45 days and hand-minced every day, according to Bacanal’s own website. The Sibarita 2.0 costs €12.80, and the menu runs from €8.50 to €13.50 (official site, checked 17 November 2026). Bacanal also serves smash burgers.

Bacanal started in Tomares, where Estefanía and Skander’s original restaurant holds a Solete Repsol de los Cocineros (Guía Repsol).

  • Address: C. José Gestoso, 5, Sevilla, a few steps from the Setas de la Encarnación
  • Hours: 1:00–4:30 pm and 8:00 pm–midnight
  • Website: bacanalburguer.com, Instagram @bacanalburguer

3. Djanco Burger (El Porvenir)

Come to Djanco for aged-beef blends. Its Selecta was ranked the second-best burger in Andalusia at the fourth Campeonato de España de Hamburguesas in 2024 (Sevilla Secreta, 13 March 2024).

The Selecta blends Galician blonde picanha aged 20 to 30 days with Galician Friesian sirloin aged 70 to 90 days. It adds Iberian pork jowl (papada) and Havarti on a brioche bun. The Tartufa 2.0 is 200 g of aged Galician beef with Havarti, truffle mayonnaise and 2 truffle-and-cheese croquettes on top (El Español Sevilla, 12 November 2024).

  • Address: C. Ramón Carande, 19, 41013 Sevilla (El Porvenir, near Avenida Cardenal Bueno Monreal)
  • Phone and WhatsApp: +34 671 49 60 31

4. Street Food Burger (Los Bermejales)

Street Food Burger makes Seville’s most over-the-top competition burger: La Gamberra 3.0 won The Champions Burger Sevilla 2025 (Sevilla Negocios).

Picture a mozzarella-stuffed bun coated in Doritos, holding a thick smash patty, smoked beef rib, cheddar, house Gamberra sauce and caramelised bacon bits. Subtle it is not. The brand says its earlier Gamberra 2.0 placed second in Spain at The Champions Burger 2024 national final. It also claims the best burger in Andalusia at the fifth Campeonato de España de Hamburguesas in 2025 (streetfoodburger.com, the brand’s own claim).

Manuel Rodríguez’s company started in 2022 in Los Palacios y Villafranca, in Seville province. CosasDeCome reports it sold 500,000 burgers in 2025.

  • Address: Av. de Alemania, 5, 41012 Sevilla (Los Bermejales), per Gastroranking. Sevilla Secreta lists Av. de Alemania 19, local 9, so check the map pin before you go.
  • Phone: +34 679 13 19 49
  • Other Seville branches: Viapol and Sevilla Este
  • Website: streetfoodburger.com

5. Atticus Finch Burger (Mercado de Feria)

For value in the old town, Atticus Finch wins: a market-stall burger joint that earned a Solete Repsol in the spring 2026 edition (Guía Repsol).

Burgers are made every day with beef from the Valle de los Pedroches in Córdoba province, on artisan bread with homemade fries. Sevilla Secreta puts its burgers under €10. It is a short walk north of Las Setas, inside the Feria market.

  • Address: Mercado de Feria, C. Feria, 98, stall 45, 41003 Sevilla (Casco Antiguo, Feria)
  • Booking: CoverManager, Instagram @atticusfinchburger

6. Atípico (Triana)

Atípico is where I’d send anyone avoiding gluten or lactose: it is the one place on this list that advertises its whole menu in both versions at no extra cost. It is also among the cheapest, at €8 to €11.90 a burger (Directo al Paladar). CosasDeCome put them at €9.50 to €10 in December 2025, so expect the cheaper end to have moved up.

Rafael Trujillo and José Manuel López, known as Zipi, opened the first Atípico in Zahara de los Atunes on 1 July 2022. Their retinto beef comes from Rafa’s father’s farm, and the bread from Juanito Baker. Order La Trufada (retinto, truffle mayonnaise, cheddar, griddled egg) or La Típica (double smash, smoked cheddar, pickle, bacon, ketchup and house sauce).

  • Triana: C. Virtud, 2, Sevilla (open since September 2023)
  • Sevilla Este: Mercado de Abastos de Sevilla Este, unit 13, Av. del Deporte (open since 10 November 2022)

7. Lokal (Casco Antiguo)

Lokal is the old town’s crowd favourite on Google: 4.7/5 on 1,524 reviews, according to Solomeat. The brand started in Galicia in 2019, brings its beef from there and bakes its buns in-house. That is all I could verify, which is why strong reviews alone don’t lift it higher.

  • Address: C. Esperanza Elena Caro, 2, 41002 Sevilla (Casco Antiguo)

8. Monkey Food (Nervión)

Monkey Food has more Google reviews than any other Seville burger restaurant I found: 4.6/5 on 3,829 reviews, according to Solomeat. It placed third in the smash category of the 2023 Sevilla Burger League.

The chain started in Dos Hermanas. Meat comes from Cárnicas Desiderio and bread from La Esencia bakery (Sevilla Secreta, 23 January 2024). It is a handy stop before or after a match at the Sánchez-Pizjuán.

Smash burgers in Seville

For a smash burger in Seville, go to Burger Food Porn, which says it served the city’s first, or to Bacanal, Street Food Burger and Atípico, which all serve them. The 2023 Sevilla Burger League smash title went to Sofi Street’s La Bichota, ahead of Mundo Pizza and Monkey Food (Sevilla Secreta). If you’d rather make 1 at home, my colleague Alice’s Sonic bacon smash burger copycat shows the press-and-sear technique step by step.

Burgers near Seville Cathedral and Las Setas

The best burgers near Las Setas are at Bacanal Burger on C. José Gestoso, with Atticus Finch a short walk north in the Mercado de Feria and Lokal also in the Casco Antiguo. Near the Cathedral, Burger Food Porn’s Gamazo branch is in El Arenal. Milonguitas Grill & Burger, at C. Tomás de Ibarra, 2, is right by the Cathedral, but its reviews are mixed, so it stays off my list.

What beef do Seville’s best burger joints use?

Seville’s top hamburgueserías mostly use Galician beef, plus 2 Andalusian alternatives. The Andalusian options are retinto at Atípico and Valle de los Pedroches beef at Atticus Finch. Burger Food Porn, Bacanal and Djanco dry-age Galician beef, and Lokal brings its meat from Galicia. To see how breed, fat and ageing change a patty, read our guide to the best ground beef for burgers.

Bread matters just as much here: Burger Food Porn’s brioche comes from Ángel Puchi in Coria del Río, and Atípico’s comes from Juanito Baker. Our homemade burger buns recipe gets you close to that soft, rich crumb at home.

Around Seville: burgers worth the short trip

The best burger trip outside Seville is to Bacanal’s original restaurant in Tomares, at Plaza de la Cruz, 2, 41940 Tomares, a Solete Repsol de los Cocineros. Burger Food Porn also has branches in Bormujos and Mairena. Lou Burger, third in the 2023 Sevilla Burger League, and Lucky’s Burger are also in the province. I kept them off the city ranking so it stays useful to anyone staying in Seville.

FAQ: burgers in Seville

Where can I eat the best burger in Seville?

The best burger in Seville is the Doble Cheese Bacon at Burger Food Porn (Av. de Alemania, 19, Los Bermejales), named Spain’s best burger in 2023. In the old town, Bacanal Burger near Las Setas is the strongest pick, with its 2023 Sevilla Burger League-winning Sibarita 2.0 at €12.80.

How much does a burger cost in Seville?

A burger in Seville costs about €8 to €15. Atípico runs €8 to €11.90, Bacanal €8.50 to €13.50, and Burger Food Porn charged €12.90 to €15 in 2023. CosasDeCome puts the average bill at a Seville burger restaurant at €20 to €30.

How much a burger costs in Seville: Atípico €8–€11.90, Bacanal Burger €8.50–€13.50 (official menu, 17 November 2026) and Burger Food Porn €12.90–€15 (2023, the latest confirmed figure). Atticus Finch, under €10 according to Sevilla Secreta, is left out because it has no published range. Are there gluten-free burgers in Seville?

Yes: Atípico serves its whole menu gluten-free and lactose-free at the same price, at C. Virtud, 2 in Triana and in the Mercado de Abastos de Sevilla Este.

Do I need to book at Burger Food Porn?

You cannot book at Burger Food Porn’s city-centre branch on C. Gamazo, 4, according to its website; the other branches take reservations.

Is Burger Food Porn still owned by its founder?

No. The Valencian group Tagus Tao bought Burger Food Porn on 15 December 2025, keeping its menu, staff and suppliers, and founder Juan Higueras stays on as brand ambassador.

When is The Champions Burger in Seville?

The Champions Burger came to Seville in 2025, when Street Food Burger won with La Gamberra 3.0. I could not confirm dates for the next Seville stop, so check the event’s official channels before planning a trip around it.
