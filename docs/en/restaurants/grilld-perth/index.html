Grill'd Perth: Stores, Menu Prices and Hours

All 28 Grill'd stores in WA checked on official pages: the late-night CBD store, beach stores, WA's only drive-thru, prices in AUD and what diners say.

By Léo, Restaurants writer, Oceania and Southeast AsiaPublished November 6, 2026

Grill’d has 28 restaurants in Western Australia, most of them in greater Perth. The one to know is Grill’d William Street, at 140 William St in the CBD: its official page shows it open until 1am on Friday and Saturday. Heading for the coast? Pick Scarborough, Cottesloe or Fremantle. Want to stay in the car? Wanneroo has WA’s only Grill’d drive-thru. A Simply Grill’d costs about A$14.50 and a Wagyu Wunder about A$18.90, based on national indicative prices from August 2026. I checked every address, phone number and opening time below against Grill’d’s official store pages on 4 November 2026.

Grill’d in Perth at a glance

Grill’d is a Melbourne burger chain built on the promise of a healthier burger, and 28 of its stores are in Western Australia. Simon Crowe opened the first one at 754 Glenferrie Road, Hawthorn, on 2 March 2004. By 20 October 2026 the chain had 181 restaurants: 180 in Australia and one in Seminyak, Bali. The pitch hasn’t changed: grass-fed beef and lamb, RSPCA-approved chicken, no artificial additives. The full history and figures are on our Grill’d brand profile.

The official store list puts these 28 in WA: Applecross, Baldivis, Carousel, Claremont Quarter, Cockburn, Cottesloe, Currambine, Fremantle, Great Eastern Highway, Harrisdale, Hillarys, Innaloo, Joondalup, Karrinyup, Kingsway, Leederville, Mandurah, Midland, Mt Lawley, Ocean Keys, Rockingham, Scarborough, Subiaco, Victoria Park, Whitford City, William Street, Wanneroo and Waterford Plaza. The menu is national. I looked for a WA-only item and found none.

Which Grill’d Perth store to choose

The right Grill’d Perth store depends on where your day takes you: William Street for the CBD and late nights, Scarborough or Cottesloe for the beach, Fremantle for a day in Freo, Leederville, Mt Lawley, Subiaco or Victoria Park for an evening on a dining strip, and Wanneroo for the drive-thru. If you’re visiting and only have time for one, make it William Street. It’s central, it’s open latest at the weekend, and its page lists gluten-free, vegetarian and vegan options.

Grill’d William Street: the CBD store that opens late

Grill’d William Street is the Perth CBD store, and the one to remember when you want a burger after 11pm. It is at 140 William St, Perth WA 6000, phone +61 8 6158 4871. The official page gives Wednesday hours of 10:30am to 10:30pm and a 1am close on Friday and Saturday. Dine-in, takeaway, delivery, online ordering, catering and group bookings are all available, with gluten-free, vegetarian and vegan options. One warning: some listings put this store on Elizabeth Quay. It isn’t there. 140 William St is not on the quay.

Grill’d near the beach: Scarborough, Cottesloe and Fremantle

Three Grill’d stores sit close to the coast, and they don’t do the same job.

  • Scarborough: 188 The Esplanade, Scarborough WA, phone +61 8 9245 1055. Open Wednesday 10:30am–10pm. It is on the beachfront road itself, so it’s the obvious stop after a swim.
  • Cottesloe: 6 Napoleon St, Cottesloe WA 6011, phone +61 8 9383 4788. Open Wednesday 11am–10pm. It is on Cottesloe’s shopping street, a short walk inland from the beach.
  • Fremantle: corner of South Terrace and Collie Street, Fremantle WA 6160, phone +61 8 9335 3000. Open Wednesday 10:30am–11pm, the latest of the three. It takes table and group bookings and also does catering and delivery.

Grill’d in Perth’s dining strips

Four more stores sit on Perth’s eat-street strips. They’re handy before a film, a gig or a night out.

  • Leederville: 128 Oxford St, Leederville WA 6007, phone +61 8 9242 1084. Open Wednesday 10:30am–10pm. Pickup, delivery, catering and group bookings.
  • Mt Lawley: 669 Beaufort St, Mount Lawley WA 6050, phone +61 8 9271 9444. Open Wednesday 10:30am–11:30pm, the latest Wednesday close I found in Perth. On Beaufort Street midweek, this is the one still serving after 11pm.
  • Subiaco: Lot 54, 20-38 Subiaco Square, Subiaco WA 6008, phone +61 8 9380 9497. Open Wednesday 10:30am–10pm.
  • Victoria Park: 895 Albany Hwy, East Victoria Park WA 6101, phone +61 8 9355 1000. Open Wednesday 11am–10pm.

Grill’d Wanneroo drive-thru: the only one in WA

Grill’d Wanneroo is the only Grill’d drive-thru in Western Australia, and the fourth in the country after Brisbane, Geelong and Melbourne. It opened on Saturday 13 December 2025 by giving away its first 754 burgers, a nod to the address of the first store, 754 Glenferrie Road. The dining room and the drive-thru both serve the full menu of more than 40 options. The opening created about 50 jobs, according to the Buggybuddys opening report and Grill’d news.

The store is at Shop 9, 15 & 21 Herdsman Lane, Wanneroo WA 6065, phone +61 8 6402 5555. It opens Wednesday from 10am to 11pm, with dine-in and bookings, pickup, delivery and catering.

Gluten-free, vegetarian and vegan burgers at Grill’d Perth

Grill’d Perth offers gluten-free buns, vegetarian burgers and vegan burgers, and the William Street store lists all three on its official page. On the menu, that means the vegetarian Garden Goodness and the plant-based Beyond Simply Grill’d. If you have coeliac disease, say so clearly when you order: one William Street reviewer was served a burger as gluten-free that wasn’t.

No Grill’d store in WA is confirmed as halal. The only certified halal Grill’d I found is in Blacktown, NSW.

Grill’d Perth reviews: what diners say

Grill’d’s two most-reviewed Perth stores both score 4.1 out of 5 on Google, according to figures collected by Wanderlog. Diners praise the fresh ingredients and the friendly staff, and complain about wrong orders and peak-time waits. These aren’t my ratings. The figures come from Wanderlog’s William Street page and its Ocean Keys page, both checked on 4 November 2026, not from Google Maps directly.

  • William Street: 4.1/5 on Google (402 reviews). Reviewers like the fresh ingredients, the friendly staff, the fried chicken burgers, the zucchini chips and the BBQ wagyu burger. Complaints include a burger served as gluten-free that wasn’t, forgotten drinks and wrong information about the loyalty program.
  • Ocean Keys (Clarkson): 4.1/5 on Google (580 reviews) and 3.8/5 on TripAdvisor (30 reviews). Reviewers like the healthy options, the lamb and plant-based burgers, and the service. Complaints include waits at peak times, errors in online orders and missing ingredients.

My reading of these reviews: the food holds up across Perth, and the service slips when the queue builds. Go at a quiet hour if you can, and check your bag before you drive off.

Grill’d vs other burgers in Perth

Grill’d is a mid-range chain with a healthy angle. It’s a step up from fast food but less adventurous than Perth’s independent burger bars. For a quicker, cheaper fast-food burger, the Australian option is Hungry Jack’s. Grill’d Perth FAQ

How many Grill’d restaurants are there in Perth and Western Australia?

Grill’d has 28 restaurants in Western Australia on its official store list, checked on 4 November 2026. Most are in greater Perth, from Joondalup and Wanneroo in the north to Rockingham and Mandurah in the south.

Which Grill’d in Perth is open late?

Grill’d William Street in the Perth CBD stays open until 1am on Friday and Saturday, according to its official page. On a Wednesday, Mt Lawley closes latest, at 11:30pm.

Is there a Grill’d drive-thru in Perth?

Yes. Grill’d Wanneroo, at Shop 9, 15 & 21 Herdsman Lane, Wanneroo, is the only Grill’d drive-thru in WA. It opened on 13 December 2025 and serves the full menu at the drive-thru.

How much does a Grill’d burger cost in Australia?

A Grill’d burger costs about A$14.50 to A$18.90, according to national indicative prices from August 2026. Regular chips add about A$7.50.

Does Grill’d have gluten-free, vegetarian and vegan burgers?

Yes. Grill’d offers gluten-free buns, vegetarian burgers such as Garden Goodness and plant-based burgers such as Beyond Simply Grill’d. If you have coeliac disease, say so clearly when you order.

Can you book a table at Grill’d in Perth?

At some stores, yes. Fremantle and Wanneroo take table bookings, and William Street and Leederville take group bookings.

Is Grill’d halal in Perth?

No Grill’d store in Western Australia is confirmed as halal. The only certified halal Grill’d I found is Blacktown, in New South Wales.

Which Grill’d is best near the beach in Perth?

Scarborough, on The Esplanade, is the closest to the sand. Cottesloe is a short walk inland on Napoleon St. Fremantle stays open until 11pm on Wednesdays and suits a full day in Freo.
