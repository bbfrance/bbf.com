Five Guys Denver: 2 Locations, Prices and Reviews

Denver has 2 Five Guys, on South Colorado Blvd and in Northfield, plus 5 in Aurora: which to pick, how to get there, what it costs.

By Daniel, Restaurants writer, United StatesPublished November 18, 2026

Five Guys has exactly 2 restaurants inside Denver city limits: Five Guys University Park at 2021 South Colorado Blvd (CO 80222) and Five Guys Northfield at 8201 E. Northfield Blvd in Central Park (CO 80238). Both offer dine-in, curbside and delivery, according to the official Five Guys locator I checked on October 29, 2026. If you only go to one, make it Colorado Blvd. It is the more central of the two, it sits about 940 ft from RTD’s Colorado Station light rail stop, and it has by far the most reviews.

Five Guys Colorado Blvd (University Park): the one to pick

Five Guys on South Colorado Blvd is the easiest Five Guys in Denver to reach, by train or by car. Get off at Colorado Station and it is about a 4-minute walk. Drivers leave I-25 at exit 203 and are there in a minute. It also draws the most feedback online: 1,171 reviews aggregated on Birdeye.

Five Guys Northfield (Central Park): best for northeast Denver

Five Guys Northfield is the practical option if you are in Central Park, northeast Denver or heading toward the airport side of town. It sits in the Shops at Northfield retail area near I-270 and I-70. Without a car, it is a chore. Plan on a walk of about 1.1 miles from Central Park Station on the A Line, the train that links Union Station and Denver International Airport.

Is the Five Guys on Parker Road in Denver or Aurora?

The Five Guys at Promenade on Parker, 2300 S. Parker Rd, is officially in Aurora (CO 80231), not Denver, even though many directories file it under Denver. It sits near S Parker Rd and E Iliff Ave, and a secondary source lists its phone as 303-751-7449. If you are in southeast Denver, it is closer than either city location.

The other official Aurora locations are Gateway Point Plaza (3751 N. Tower Rd, CO 80011), Southlands (23963 E. Prospect Ave, CO 80016), Cornerstar (6710 S. Cornerstar Way Unit C, CO 80016) and Parkside (14535 E. Alameda Ave. Unit A, CO 80012). Across the metro area, Five Guys also lists restaurants in Arvada, Boulder, Broomfield, Englewood, Lakewood, Lone Tree, Thornton and Wheat Ridge, among other Colorado cities.

Five Guys Denver hours

I could not confirm official opening hours for either Denver location, so call before you go. The official restaurant pages showed no hours when I checked on October 29, 2026. Third-party directories contradict each other: one has Northfield closing early on Saturdays, another shows it open through the evening. Call University Park at 303-758-7007 or Northfield at 303-371-3483, or open the online ordering page, which shows whether the kitchen is taking orders.

Five Guys Denver menu: what you can order

The Five Guys menu in Denver is the standard national menu, with no Denver-only item: burgers, fries, shakes, hot dogs, sandwiches and drinks, plus free toppings. That is the official menu layout on the University Park page. Don’t come looking for a local special. The whole point here is building your own burger.

The chain’s promise, printed on its Denver restaurant pages, is specific: fresh ground beef with no freezers, fries cooked twice in 100% peanut oil, toppings prepped by hand every morning, more than 250,000 ways to build a burger and more than 1,000 milkshake combinations.

Five Guys Denver reviews: what diners say

Five Guys University Park holds a 4.1 out of 5 rating from 1,171 reviews on Birdeye, which aggregates Google reviews (checked October 29, 2026). Recent reviewers praise friendly staff who are patient with first-timers, and burgers that hold up well for takeout. 1 recent reviewer flagged a “no public bathrooms” sign on the door. That is a single customer report, not an official policy, so ask staff if it matters to you.

I could not verify a review rating for Northfield at the source, so I am not quoting one. Method note: I quote a rating only with its platform, its review count and the date I checked it, and I never average platforms.

How to order: dine-in, curbside, delivery and online

Both Denver Five Guys locations offer dine-in, curbside pickup and delivery, according to the official Five Guys locator. Online ordering runs through order.fiveguys.com, with 1 page per restaurant. The individual restaurant pages list takeout, dine-in, delivery and outdoor seating but say nothing about curbside, so confirm when you order. Which delivery apps serve each location is not listed either.

Five Guys history and its place in Denver

Five Guys was founded in 1986 in Arlington, Virginia, by Jerry and Janie Murrell and their sons, and started franchising in 2002. In May 2026 it had 1,999 restaurants in 28 countries and territories, 1,517 of them in the United States, according to the chain’s 2026 media fact sheet. Full history and figures are on our Five Guys brand profile.

In Colorado, the chain lives mostly in the suburbs: 19 Colorado cities have a Five Guys, and Aurora alone has more locations than Denver.

Want a local burger instead? Try Denver’s own

Honestly, if you have one burger to eat in Denver, skip the chain. A Five Guys burger tastes the same here as it does in Virginia. The Cherry Cricket, pouring beer at 2641 E 2nd Ave in Cherry Creek since 1945, is pure Denver. It is the top pick in my guide to the best burgers in Denver, and you will find every Denver guide on our Denver burger hub.

FAQ: Five Guys in Denver

How many Five Guys are there in Denver? There are 2 Five Guys in the city of Denver: University Park (2021 South Colorado Blvd) and Northfield (8201 E. Northfield Blvd). 5 more are in Aurora, just east of the city.

Which Five Guys in Denver should I choose? Choose University Park on South Colorado Blvd if you are in central or south Denver or using the light rail. Choose Northfield if you are in Central Park or northeast Denver.

Can I take the light rail to Five Guys on Colorado Blvd? Yes. Five Guys University Park is about 940 ft from RTD’s Colorado Station, roughly a 4-minute walk.

Is Five Guys Denver open on Sundays? Official Sunday hours were not published for either location when I checked. Call University Park at 303-758-7007 or Northfield at 303-371-3483 before you go.

How much does a burger and fries cost at Five Guys Denver? Based on indicative national prices from February 2026, a hamburger ($7.99) and regular fries ($5.19) come to about $13.18 before tax and drinks. Denver prices may differ; check the online ordering page.

Are Five Guys fries cooked in peanut oil? Yes. Five Guys says its fries are cooked twice in 100% peanut oil. If you have a peanut allergy, check the official allergen information on fiveguys.com and talk to staff before ordering.

Does Five Guys Denver have vegetarian or gluten-free options? The menu includes sandwiches and free toppings beyond burgers, but I could not confirm specific vegetarian or gluten-free items for the Denver locations. Check the official menu and allergen guide at fiveguys.com/menu.

Is the Five Guys on Parker Road in Denver? No. The Promenade on Parker location at 2300 S. Parker Rd is officially in Aurora, CO 80231, though many sites list it under Denver.
