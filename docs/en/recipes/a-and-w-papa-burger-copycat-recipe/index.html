A&W Papa Burger Copycat Recipe With Papa Sauce

Two thin beef patties, two slices of American cheese and a homemade Papa Sauce: the US A&W Papa Burger rebuilt at home in 25 minutes.

By Clara, Recipes editorPublished November 24, 2026

To make an A&W Papa Burger at home, sear four thin 75 g (2.7 oz) beef patties in a very hot cast-iron skillet. Put a slice of American cheese on each one, then stack two per lightly toasted bun with lettuce, tomato, onion and pickles. Finish with a homemade Papa Sauce of mayonnaise, sweet relish and a touch of ketchup. This copycat makes 2 burgers in about 25 minutes. It is my own version, not A&W’s official recipe.

One point first, because it trips up a lot of people. A&W Restaurants in the United States, headquartered in Lexington, Kentucky, and A&W Canada are two separate companies, and each sells its own burger called the Papa Burger. This recipe follows the US one, built from the menu at awrestaurants.com as I read it on November 12, 2026. The Canadian burger gets its own box further down.

What’s on an A&W Papa Burger?

A US A&W Papa Burger has two beef patties, American cheese, lettuce, tomato, onion and pickles on a lightly toasted bun, finished with A&W Papa Sauce. A&W’s menu page puts it this way: “Two juicy beef patties topped with gooey American cheese and stacked high on a lightly toasted bun with fresh lettuce, tomato, onion, and pickles. Finished with a drizzle of our signature A&W® Papa Sauce” (A&W Restaurants, checked November 12, 2026). The chain sells it as a single or a double.

There are two slices of cheese, one per patty. A&W’s press release of November 28, 2016 describes “two slices of melted American cheese” (Franchising.com), and FatSecret’s listing of the double gives the same build (FatSecret).

How much do the Papa Burger patties weigh?

The two patties weigh about 1/3 lb (151 g) raw in total, so about 75 g (2.7 oz) each, according to A&W in 2016. The figure comes from the chain’s November 28, 2016 release on the Spicy Papa Burger, which uses the Papa Burger as its base: “Two 100 percent U.S. beef patties totaling one-third pound” (Franchising.com). I found no current A&W page that confirms the weight for 2026. Treat it as the best figure available, not a guarantee.

The third-pound size has an awkward history at A&W. In the 1980s, under A. Alfred Taubman, the chain launched a third-pound burger to take on McDonald’s Quarter Pounder. It flopped: many customers thought 1/3 was smaller than 1/4 (Wikipedia; Food Republic, March 2, 2026). In 2021, A&W made fun of itself with a “3/9 lb” burger.

A&W Papa Sauce recipe

A&W Papa Sauce is creamy, sweet and tangy, with ketchup and pickle relish notes, and mayonnaise, sweet relish and a little ketchup get you close at home. Tasting Table calls it “equal parts sweet, tangy, and creamy” and compares it to In-N-Out’s spread (Megan Hageman, Tasting Table, July 31, 2026). The same ranking of 10 A&W items puts the Papa Burger at number 2.

An ingredient list A&W published in 2018 starts Papa Sauce with soybean oil, water, sugar and pickle relish. Read in that order, it is a mayonnaise-style base with real sweetness and plenty of relish. My first batch was mayo and relish alone, and it tasted like tartar sauce. A pinch of sugar and a few drops of white vinegar pulled it toward sweet and tangy; the teaspoon of ketchup adds the note Tasting Table picks up. These proportions are mine, not A&W’s formula. Make the sauce first, even an hour ahead: the relish needs time to flavor the mayo.

A&W also makes a Spicy Papa Sauce. It was first served with the Chicken Tenders, then went on the Spicy Papa Burger with jalapeños in November 2016. A&W brought back a Double Spicy Papa Burger on November 8, 2024 (A&W blog).

Ingredients for 2 Papa Burgers

You need about 2/3 lb of 80/20 ground beef, four slices of American cheese, two buns and the classic toppings. Quantities are for 2 double burgers.

Patties and assembly

  • 300 g (about 10.5 oz) ground beef, 20% fat (80/20)
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 2 hamburger buns
  • Iceberg lettuce, shredded or in leaves
  • 2 slices of tomato
  • White onion, thinly sliced
  • Dill pickle chips

Homemade Papa Sauce

  • About 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp sweet pickle relish
  • 1 tsp ketchup
  • 1 pinch of sugar
  • A few drops of white vinegar

Times: prep 15 minutes, cook 10 minutes, total about 25 minutes.

Step-by-step Papa Burger copycat

Mix the sauce, shape thin patties, toast the buns, sear hard, stack. Get everything ready before the skillet goes on: thin patties are done in minutes.

1. Mix the Papa Sauce

Stir the mayonnaise, sweet relish, ketchup, sugar and vinegar together in a small bowl. Taste, adjust the sugar or vinegar, and keep it in the fridge while you cook.

2. Shape four thin patties

Divide the beef into four 75 g (2.7 oz) portions. Press each one into a thin patty about 4.5 inches (11 to 12 cm) wide, a little wider than the bun. Mine went into the pan at about 11.5 cm and came out at about 10 cm, just the width of a standard bun.

3. Prepare the toppings

Shred or tear the lettuce, cut two tomato slices, slice the onion thinly and drain the pickle chips. Thin cuts keep the stack stable.

4. Toast the buns lightly

Toast the cut sides of the buns in a dry skillet until just golden, about 90 seconds in my pan. A&W says “lightly toasted”, so stop before the bread turns crisp.

5. Sear the patties and melt the cheese

Heat a cast-iron skillet over high heat, season the patties with salt and pepper and cook them 2 to 3 minutes per side, two at a time if your pan is small. Lay a slice of American cheese on each patty right after flipping, and cook until the center reaches 160°F (71°C).

6. Stack the burger

Put two cheesy patties on each bottom bun, then pickles, onion, tomato and lettuce. Drizzle Papa Sauce over the top and close with the top bun.

Double cheeseburger with American cheese: why one slice per patty

Put one slice of American cheese on each patty the moment you flip it. It melts on the second side and glues the two patties together, so the tall stack doesn’t slide when you bite. Skip the second slice and the burger comes apart; I tried it. Thin patties matter just as much. Two 75 g patties have far more seared surface than one thick 150 g patty, and that browned crust is where the fast-food flavor comes from.

Food safety sets the doneness, not the clock. Ground beef must reach 160°F (71°C) in the center, according to the USDA and Health Canada. With patties this thin, slide an instant-read thermometer in from the side.

Why this copycat matches A&W’s description

This copycat follows every part of the burger A&W publishes and guesses only where the chain stays silent: two patties at the 2016 weight, one slice of American cheese per patty, the four toppings from the menu page. The sauce follows the order of the 2018 ingredient list, oil and sugar first, then relish. The older copycats drop the second patty, the tomato and the onion. Fine burgers, but not this one.

My verdict: the sauce is the reason to make it. Without it, you have a decent double cheeseburger close to my A&W Cheeseburger copycat, which uses ketchup and mustard instead. With it, you get the sweet, tangy Papa Burger.

Where the Papa Burger comes from

The Papa Burger heads A&W’s Burger Family, launched in 1963, which also includes the Mama, Teen and Baby burgers. In 2004, the US chain made it its flagship again, “reinstating the Papa Burger as A&W’s flagship burger” (Wikipedia). Today A&W Restaurants is owned by its franchisees through A Great American Brand, LLC.

The Canadian Papa Burger is a different burger

A&W Canada’s Papa Burger uses ketchup, mustard and Teen Sauce, with no lettuce or tomato, so this recipe won’t copy it. Wikipedia lists “two regular beef patties (6 ounces (170 g) total), onion slice, pickles, ketchup, mustard, Teen Sauce, sesame seed bun, (optional) cheese slice” (Wikipedia). I could not read A&W Canada’s own product page on November 12, 2026, so the chain has not confirmed that list.

Papa Burger FAQ

What’s on a Papa Burger at A&W?

A US Papa Burger has two beef patties, two slices of American cheese, lettuce, tomato, onion and pickles on a lightly toasted bun, topped with Papa Sauce, according to A&W’s menu page in November 2026.

How much meat is in a Papa Burger?

About 1/3 lb (151 g) of raw beef in two patties, according to A&W’s 2016 press release on the Spicy Papa Burger. A&W has not confirmed that weight on a current page.

What cheese is on a Papa Burger?

American cheese, one slice on each of the two patties.

What does Papa Sauce taste like?

It is creamy, sweet and tangy, with ketchup and pickle relish notes. Tasting Table compares it to In-N-Out’s spread.

What is the difference between the Papa Burger and the Teen Burger?

Both belong to A&W’s Burger Family, launched in 1963. The Papa Burger is the two-patty member and the US chain’s flagship; the Teen Burger is built around Teen Sauce. My Teen Burger copycat recipe covers the A&W Canada version.

Is the US Papa Burger the same as in Canada?

No. A&W Restaurants (US) and A&W Canada are separate companies. The Canadian Papa Burger uses ketchup, mustard and Teen Sauce, with optional cheese and no lettuce or tomato.

How many calories are in a Papa Burger?

FatSecret lists the US double at 610 calories for a 291 g burger, with 33 g fat, 43 g carbohydrates and 38 g protein. Other sites give different figures, so check A&W’s official nutrition page for the current numbers.

A&W Papa Burger double (US) nutrition per FatSecret: 610 calories for a 291 g (10.3 oz) burger, with 33 g fat, 43 g carbohydrates and 38 g protein. Is there a single or a spicy Papa Burger?

Yes. A&W sells the Papa Burger as a single or a double. A Spicy Papa Burger with jalapeños and Spicy Papa Sauce launched in November 2016, and a Double Spicy Papa Burger came back on November 8, 2024.

What temperature should thin burger patties reach?

Ground beef must reach 160°F (71°C) in the center, according to the USDA and Health Canada. Thin patties get there in 2 to 3 minutes per side in a very hot skillet.
