Big Mac Copycat Recipe With the 2024 Updates

Two 1.6-ounce patties, a paprika-colored special sauce, rehydrated onions and a three-part sesame bun, with every estimate marked as one.

By Clara, Recipes editorPublished November 25, 2026

To make a Big Mac at home, cook 4 thin beef patties of 1.6 ounces (45 g) on a very hot griddle, salting and peppering them only once they’re on the heat. Stack them on a three-part sesame seed bun with special sauce, shredded lettuce, rehydrated dried onions, dill pickles and one slice of American cheese. The sauce is mayonnaise, sweet pickle relish, yellow mustard, a little white wine vinegar, garlic powder, onion powder and paprika. No ketchup. No French dressing. McDonald’s has confirmed those sauce ingredients but never the quantities, so every dose below is my own estimate, tested in my kitchen. This is a copycat, not McDonald’s official recipe.

What is in a Big Mac? The official ingredients

A Big Mac is 2 all-beef patties of 1.6 ounces (about 45 g) each, special sauce, lettuce, cheese, pickles and onions on a sesame seed bun cut into 3 parts. The middle slice is called the club. That is the build McDonald’s sang in its 1974 jingle: “Two all-beef patties, special sauce, lettuce, cheese, pickles, onions on a sesame seed bun” (Wikipedia). When I tried McDonald’s own product pages on November 12, 2026, they returned an error, so this list comes from Wikipedia and cross-checked product listings. Check it on mcdonalds.com before you treat it as official.

The patties are seasoned with salt and pepper only, on the grill, with no fillers or preservatives. The onions are dehydrated and rehydrated in water in the restaurant. Both details match what I found for my McDouble copycat recipe, which uses the same 1.6-ounce patty.

The 2023–2024 changes most Big Mac copycats miss

McDonald’s changed how it makes the Big Mac in 2023 and 2024: softer buns, more Big Mac sauce, onions added to the patties while they cook, a better sear and meltier cheese. The chain announced the changes in April 2023 (CNBC). It tested them on the West Coast first, in Los Angeles, Seattle, Las Vegas and Denver, then rolled them out across the US in 2024 (Fox 9).

Two details came out in the trade press. The sauce dose went from 0.3 oz (about 8.5 g) to 0.5 oz (about 14 g) (The Takeout). The Takeout also reports that patties now cook in batches of 6 instead of 8, and that the two-sided grill presses the meat less. I found both grill details in that one source only. Not one of the copycat recipes on the first page of Google that I read accounts for any of this. Most still call for fresh raw onion and a French dressing sauce. That’s why I wrote this one.

Big Mac sauce recipe

Big Mac sauce is mayonnaise, yellow mustard and sweet pickle relish, sharpened with white wine vinegar and seasoned with garlic powder, onion powder and paprika. That list comes from Dan Coudreaut, then McDonald’s executive chef, who made the sauce in a McDonald’s Canada YouTube video in July 2012 without giving any quantities (Christian Science Monitor, WTVR). The doses below are mine. They make enough for 2 burgers with some left over.

  • 1/4 cup (60 g) mayonnaise
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons (20 g) sweet pickle relish
  • 2 teaspoons (10 g) yellow mustard
  • 1 teaspoon (5 ml) white wine vinegar
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon sweet paprika

Whisk everything together, cover and chill for at least 1 hour so the dried spices soften into the mayonnaise. Taste it after resting, not before. Straight out of the bowl it tastes of raw mustard. An hour later it tastes of Big Mac.

Is there ketchup in Big Mac sauce?

No, Big Mac sauce contains no ketchup. Its pinkish-orange color comes from paprika, not ketchup, French dressing or Thousand Island, Coudreaut explained in 2012. The US industrial ingredient list confirms it: water, vegetable oil, pickles, vinegar, sugar, corn starch, corn syrup, egg yolks, mustard flour, spice extractives, salt, paprika extract and dehydrated garlic (The Daily Meal). Plenty of copycat recipes add ketchup or French dressing anyway, and you can taste it: the sauce turns sweeter and loses its pickle tang.

In 2018, McDonald’s also removed potassium sorbate, sodium benzoate and calcium disodium EDTA from the US sauce, according to Wikipedia. A homemade sauce never had them.

Homemade Big Mac ingredients (makes 2 burgers)

These amounts make 2 Big Macs. Weigh the beef to the gram: on a patty this thin, an extra 10 g changes both the cooking time and the bite. McDonald’s doesn’t publish how much lettuce, onion or pickle goes on, so those amounts are my estimates.

  • 6.4 oz (180 g) ground beef, about 80/20, for 4 patties of 1.6 oz (45 g)
  • 4 sesame seed hamburger buns (2 whole buns, plus 2 heels for the clubs)
  • 2 slices of American cheese
  • 1 batch of Big Mac sauce (above)
  • 2 tablespoons dried minced onion
  • Hot water, enough to cover the onions
  • 1 cup finely shredded lettuce (I use iceberg)
  • 4 dill pickle chips
  • Fine salt and ground black pepper

Equipment: kitchen scale, parchment paper, a flat griddle or cast-iron skillet, a stiff metal spatula, a serrated knife and an instant-read thermometer.

Times: prep 25 minutes, freezing 1 hour 30 minutes, cooking about 5 minutes, total about 2 hours. The sauce rests and the onions soak while the patties freeze.

How to make a Big Mac at home, step by step

Freeze the patties thin, make the sauce ahead, cut the club from an extra heel, then sear fast and stack in the right order. The beef method is the one from my McDouble recipe. This recipe adds the club, the sauce and the 2024 onion change.

1. Make the sauce

Whisk the mayonnaise, relish, mustard, vinegar, garlic powder, onion powder and paprika in a bowl. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.

2. Weigh, shape and freeze the patties

Divide the beef into 4 portions of exactly 1.6 oz (45 g). Press each one thin and flat, about 4 inches (10 cm) wide, between 2 sheets of parchment paper, with no salt or pepper in the meat. Freeze them flat for 1 hour 30 minutes so they hold their shape on the griddle.

3. Rehydrate the onions

Cover 2 tablespoons of dried minced onion with hot water and soak for 15 to 20 minutes. Drain well and press out the extra water so it doesn’t run into the bun.

A jar of dried onion: home-dehydrated yellow and red onion strips. Illustrative photo: the dried minced onion for a Big Mac comes in much finer pieces. Soak it in hot water for 15 to 20 minutes before use. Photo: Bradley Gordon (bradleygee), CC BY 2.0, https://www.flickr.com/photos/13951072@N00/16532675889 (Photo: Bradley Gordon (bradleygee), CC BY 2.0, https://www.flickr.com/photos/13951072@N00/16532675889)

4. Cut the club

Take the 2 extra bun heels and slice off their flat bottom crust with a serrated knife, leaving a slice about 1/2 inch (1.2 cm) thick with 2 cut faces. That slice is the club. If you bake your own, my homemade burger buns recipe works: roll a smaller, flatter piece of dough for each club and leave the sesame off it.

5. Heat the griddle and toast the bun

Heat a flat griddle or cast-iron skillet to 400 to 450°F (200 to 230°C). Toast the cut faces of the heels and crowns, and both sides of the clubs, for 20 to 30 seconds, then set them aside. McDonald’s doesn’t publish its grill temperature. I picked this range because a thin patty has to sear fast or it dries out before it browns.

6. Cook and season the patties

Lay the frozen patties on the griddle and press each one for 3 seconds. Salt and pepper the top on the heat. After about 1 minute, flip, scatter a pinch of rehydrated onions over each patty, as McDonald’s has done since the 2023–2024 update, and lay the cheese on 2 of the patties. Cook about 1 more minute, until the center reads 160°F (71°C).

7. Assemble the Big Mac

Bottom tier: heel, 1 tablespoon of sauce, onions, lettuce, then the cheese-topped patty. Middle tier: club, 1 tablespoon of sauce, onions, lettuce, 2 pickle chips, then the plain patty. Finish with the crown, press lightly for 2 seconds and eat it right away.

Two tablespoons per burger is about 30 g of sauce, roughly double the 0.5 oz McDonald’s now uses. I do this on purpose. At the restaurant dose, the homemade version tasted dry to me, because my lettuce isn’t as finely shredded and doesn’t hold the sauce the same way. If you want the exact restaurant dose, use 1/2 tablespoon per tier.

The Big Mac stacking order

A Big Mac stacks bottom to top as heel, sauce, onions, lettuce, cheese, patty, then club, sauce, onions, lettuce, pickles, patty, then crown. Most copycat recipes use this order, and so does the Spanish food site Directo al Paladar. McDonald’s hasn’t confirmed it on any page I could open, so treat it as the best available reconstruction. In my tests it gets one thing right: both tiers put sauce and lettuce between the bread and the beef, so every bite has the same balance and the club soaks up sauce without going soggy.

Why McDonald’s onions taste different

McDonald’s onions taste mild and slightly sweet because they are dehydrated onions rehydrated in water, not fresh ones. The Quarter Pounder is the exception, with fresh slivered onion (Food Republic, Tasting Table). Most copycat recipes call for fresh chopped onion, which gives a sharper, crunchier burger closer to a Quarter Pounder. A 15-to-20-minute soak in hot water gets you the right texture for pennies.

Why this recipe tastes like a Big Mac

It copies the 4 things that make a Big Mac recognizable: the 1.6-ounce salt-and-pepper patty, a sauce built on Coudreaut’s ingredient list with no ketchup, rehydrated onions, and a club that splits the burger into 2 sauced tiers. The club matters most. It puts bread in every bite and doubles the sauce and lettuce without adding beef. Salting on the griddle keeps the meat loose instead of sausage-firm. And putting the onions on the patties after the flip matches today’s US Big Mac, not the pre-2023 version that older recipes still describe.

Who invented the Big Mac?

Michael “Jim” Delligatti, a McDonald’s franchisee in the Pittsburgh area, invented the Big Mac. He first served it in 1967 in Uniontown, Pennsylvania, for 45 cents, and it went on McDonald’s national US menu in 1968 (Wikipedia, GBH News). Delligatti acknowledged that he borrowed the idea from the double-decker Big Boy had been selling since the 1940s. He died on November 28, 2016, at 98 (Global News, Fortune). For where the Big Mac fits in the longer story, read our history of the hamburger.

Is the Big Mac the same in the US and in France?

The build is the same, but the numbers differ. A US Big Mac has 580 calories, per Wikipedia. According to Open Food Facts, the French Big Mac weighs 219 g and has 231 kcal per 100 g, about 506 kcal per burger, with French-origin beef, 46 listed ingredients and a Nutri-Score of D. Open Food Facts is a collaborative database, not McDonald’s, so check mcdonalds.fr before relying on those figures. The 2023–2024 recipe changes were reported for the US.

Some markets sell their own versions: the Maharaja Mac in India uses chicken instead of beef, and the Mega Mac has 4 patties. In the EU, even the name was contested: in 2019 the EUIPO ruled for the Irish chain Supermac’s against McDonald’s over the “Big Mac” trademark, a decision that was appealed (Wikipedia). Big Mac vs McDouble vs Daily Double

The Big Mac is the only one of McDonald’s 2-patty burgers with a club, special sauce and lettuce. All 3 use 1.6-ounce patties. My McDouble copycat shares the patty, the griddle method and the rehydrated onions. The Daily Double copycat swaps in lettuce, tomato, fresh onion and mayonnaise.

McDouble 2 × 1.6 oz Plain, 2 parts Ketchup and mustard 1 slice of cheese, pickles, rehydrated onions
Daily Double 2 × 1.6 oz Plain, 2 parts Mayonnaise 1 slice of cheese, lettuce, tomato, fresh onion

Big Mac copycat FAQ

What are the ingredients in Big Mac sauce? Mayonnaise, yellow mustard, sweet pickle relish, a little white wine vinegar, garlic powder, onion powder and paprika, per McDonald’s chef Dan Coudreaut in 2012. McDonald’s has never published the quantities.

Is there ketchup in Big Mac sauce? No. The color comes from paprika. The US ingredient list has no ketchup and no tomato.

How much does a Big Mac patty weigh? 1.6 ounces, about 45 g, before cooking. A Big Mac has 2 of them, so 3.2 ounces (90 g) of raw beef.

What cheese is on a Big Mac? The Big Mac lists cheese, and in the US it is commonly a slice of American cheese on the bottom patty. I could not confirm the type or placement on an official McDonald’s page, so my recipe uses 1 slice of American cheese on the bottom patty.

How do you make the Big Mac’s three-part bun without a special bun? Use an extra bun heel as the club: slice off its bottom crust with a serrated knife to get a 1/2-inch (1.2 cm) slice with 2 cut faces, then toast both sides.

Why do Big Mac onions taste different? They are dehydrated onions rehydrated in water, milder and softer than fresh onion. Soak dried minced onion in hot water for 15 to 20 minutes to get the same result.

What is the order to assemble a Big Mac? Bottom to top: heel, sauce, onions, lettuce, cheese, patty, club, sauce, onions, lettuce, pickles, patty, crown. This is the order copycat recipes use; McDonald’s has not confirmed it publicly.

How much sauce goes on a Big Mac? About 0.5 oz (14 g) in the US since the 2023–2024 update, according to The Takeout. My recipe uses about twice that, 1 tablespoon per tier. Use 1/2 tablespoon per tier for the restaurant dose.

Can I make Big Mac sauce the day before? Yes. It needs at least 1 hour in the fridge for the dried spices to soften, and I make it the day before. Keep it covered in the fridge.

How many calories are in a Big Mac? 580 calories in the US, per Wikipedia, and about 506 kcal in France, per Open Food Facts (unofficial). I haven’t measured the homemade version: it depends on your beef, mayonnaise and buns.

Who invented the Big Mac, and when? Jim Delligatti, a McDonald’s franchisee in the Pittsburgh area. It was first served in Uniontown, Pennsylvania, in 1967 for 45 cents.
