Carl's Jr. Big Cheeseburger Copycat Recipe

Make Carl's Jr.'s Big Cheeseburger at home: a charred 4 oz patty, American cheese, pickles, onion, mustard and ketchup on a seeded bun.

By Alice, Recipes writerPublished November 18, 2026

To make a Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger at home, char a 4 oz (113 g) patty of 80/20 ground beef on a very hot grill or grill pan until it reads 160 °F (71 °C) in the center. Melt 1 slice of American cheese on top, then add dill pickles, a few slices of white onion, yellow mustard and ketchup, and serve it on a toasted seeded bun. Carl’s Jr. lists the US Big Cheeseburger at 540 calories. This is my homemade copycat, not the chain’s recipe: Carl’s Jr. names the components but publishes no patty weight, no seasoning and nothing on how the bun and sauces are made.

I built this Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger copycat from the official US menu page, which I checked on November 13, 2026, and cooked it at home for 2 burgers. Every component comes from Carl’s Jr. Every quantity is mine. My take: with no lettuce, no tomato and no special sauce, this burger has nowhere to hide, so the char on the beef does all the work. Skimp on the heat and you get a plain cheeseburger. For the chain’s history, figures and customer ratings, see our Carl’s Jr. brand profile.

What is in a Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger?

A Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger is a single charbroiled all-beef patty with melted American cheese, pickles, onions, mustard and ketchup on a seeded bun. Carl’s Jr. describes it on its US menu as follows: “This charbroiled all-beef patty is cooked fresh right after you order then topped with melted American cheese, pickles, onions, mustard and ketchup on a seeded bun” (Carl’s Jr., checked November 13, 2026).

The official page stops there. It gives no patty weight, no seasoning and no recipe for the bun or sauces (Carl’s Jr.). It also says “all-beef” and does not mention Angus, so I don’t call the US patty Angus.

Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger calories and nutrition

The Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger has 540 calories, according to the chain’s official US menu page checked on November 13, 2026. The Big Cheeseburger Combo, with a small drink and small fries, showed 850 to 1,540 calories on the same day (Carl’s Jr.). Older indexed versions of that page showed 780 to 1,500, so the combo range moves: check the page before you count on it.

For the rest of the nutrition panel, I only found an unofficial source. FatSecret lists a 219 g serving with 540 calories, 22 g of fat, 56 g of carbs and 28 g of protein (FatSecret). The calorie figure matches the official one, but treat the other numbers as a third-party estimate. Carl’s Jr.’s official US nutrition PDF is the document to check for fat and sodium.

Don’t confuse it with Hardee’s burger of the same name. Hardee’s current Big Cheeseburger is listed at 520 calories, and it’s a different build (see the comparison below).

Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger copycat ingredients (2 burgers)

You need 8 oz of ground beef, 2 slices of American cheese, 2 seeded buns, a small white onion, dill pickles, ketchup and yellow mustard. Carl’s Jr. names the components. The weights are my estimate of what gives the same bite.

  • 8 oz (227 g) ground beef, 80/20, shaped into 2 patties of 4 oz (113 g)
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 2 slices American cheese
  • 2 sesame seed burger buns
  • 6 to 8 dill pickle chips (3 or 4 per burger)
  • 1/4 small white onion, sliced very thin (about 2 tablespoons per burger)
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup (1 per burger)
  • 2 teaspoons yellow mustard (1 per burger)

Why 4 oz? Carl’s Jr. does not publish the patty weight. The 219 g total serving listed by FatSecret and the “Big” name both point to a quarter-pound patty, about 4 oz (113 g) raw. That’s an inference, not a confirmed spec. It’s also the weight I use in my Hardee’s Big Cheeseburger copycat recipe, so you can cook both side by side.

Why sesame? The menu only says “seeded bun”. Sesame is the obvious guess and what copycats of the Famous Star use, but Carl’s Jr. doesn’t confirm it for this burger. The onion cut isn’t published either. Hardee’s 2020 version of the same burger came with diced onions (The Fast Food Post). I slice mine paper-thin anyway, because thin slices sit flat on the pickles and don’t roll off when you bite. Diced onion is just as defensible if you prefer it.

How to make a Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger, step by step

Shape 2 4 oz patties, char them on a very hot grill to 160 °F (71 °C), melt the cheese on top during the last minute, then build on a toasted seeded bun with pickles, onion, mustard and ketchup. Count about 15 minutes of prep and 10 minutes of cooking.

1. Shape the patties

Divide the beef into 2 portions of 4 oz (113 g). Press each into a patty about 1/2 inch (1.3 cm) wider than your bun, with a shallow dimple in the center so it stays flat as it shrinks. Keep the patties in the fridge until the grill is ready.

2. Heat the grill or grill pan

Get a charcoal or gas grill, or a ridged cast-iron grill pan, as hot as it will go. You want dark grill marks and a little char, because “charbroiled” is the whole identity of this burger. Prep the onion and pickles now. Once the beef is on, there’s no time.

3. Season and grill the beef

Salt and pepper both sides just before cooking. Grill without pressing until the underside is well marked, flip once, and cook until an instant-read thermometer reads 160 °F (71 °C) in the center. On my grill pan that takes roughly 3 to 4 minutes a side, but the thermometer has the final word. Our burger patty internal temperature guide explains why 160 °F is the safe number for ground beef.

4. Melt the American cheese

Lay 1 slice of American cheese on each patty about 1 minute before it reaches 160 °F. Close the grill lid, or set a metal bowl over each patty in the pan, so the cheese melts and drapes over the edges while the beef finishes.

5. Toast the seeded buns

Toast the cut sides of the sesame buns on the cooler part of the grill or in a dry skillet until golden. A plain seeded bun is less fragile than brioche, so you can take it a shade darker for more crunch.

6. Assemble the burger

Spread 1 tablespoon of ketchup and 1 teaspoon of yellow mustard on the top bun. Set the cheesy patty on the bottom bun, then add 3 or 4 pickle chips and the sliced onion. Close it and eat it right away. Carl’s Jr. doesn’t publish its assembly order. I use this one because the pickles sit between the hot cheese and the onion, so the onion stays crisp.

How to get a charbroiled flavor without a grill

Heat a ridged cast-iron grill pan until it smokes and use 80/20 beef, so the fat sizzles on the ridges and gives you grill marks and a smoky edge. A flat skillet browns the meat well, but it tastes like a diner burger, not a charbroiled one. Open a window. The fat will smoke.

Why this recipe tastes like the original

My copycat gets close because it keeps the 4 things that define the Big Cheeseburger: a charred beef patty, American cheese, the ketchup-mustard-pickle trio with raw onion, and a plain seeded bun. Change one of them and you end up with a different burger.

  • The char. Flame or grill-pan ridges, not a flat griddle, give the smoky crust that “charbroiled” promises.
  • The sauce trio. Ketchup, yellow mustard and dill pickles: sweet, sharp and sour. No mayo, no lettuce, no tomato.
  • The onion. Raw white onion cuts through the soft, salty cheese.
  • The bun. A plain seeded bun stays neutral and lets the beef lead.

2 things I’d insist on. Keep the mustard at 1 teaspoon: any more and it drowns a single 4 oz patty. And don’t skip the onion. Together with the seeded bun, it’s what separates this burger from today’s Hardee’s version.

Carl’s Jr. vs Hardee’s Big Cheeseburger: what’s the difference?

The Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger keeps onions and a seeded bun, while Hardee’s current Big Cheeseburger uses a quarter-pound 100% Black Angus patty on a brioche-style bun, with no onions, for 520 calories. Both chains belong to CKE Restaurants Holdings, which explains the shared name.

Hardee’s launched its Big Cheeseburger in March 2020 in the Charbroiled Stars menu with nearly the same build that Carl’s Jr. sells today (diced onions on a seeded bun), also at 540 calories (The Fast Food Post, March 25, 2020). According to The Fast Food Post, the star logo on Hardee’s burgers came from Carl’s Jr. Hardee’s has since moved to Angus beef on brioche (Hardee’s). In practice, this Carl’s Jr. recipe is also the way to make Hardee’s original 2020 version at home: just dice the onion.

A double Big Cheeseburger at home

To make a double, grill 2 4 oz patties per burger, put a slice of American cheese on each, and keep the same toppings. I didn’t find a Double Big Cheeseburger on the Carl’s Jr. page I checked, so this is a home adaptation, not a menu item. For 2 doubles, buy 1 lb (454 g) of beef and add a pinch more onion so it doesn’t get lost.

A short history of Carl’s Jr. charbroiled burgers

Carl’s Jr. traces its start to July 17, 1941, when Carl and Margaret Karcher bought a hot dog cart in Los Angeles (Wikipedia; Mental Floss). Their Anaheim drive-in opened in 1945. Today the chain belongs to CKE Restaurants Holdings, which bought Hardee’s in 1997 for $327 million (Wikipedia). For how the burger moved from carts to drive-ins, read our history of the hamburger.

Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger FAQ

What are the ingredients in a Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger?

It’s a charbroiled all-beef patty, melted American cheese, pickles, onions, mustard and ketchup on a seeded bun. That’s the official US description on carlsjr.com, checked November 13, 2026.

How many calories are in a Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger?

It has 540 calories, according to Carl’s Jr.’s official US menu. As a combo with a small drink and small fries, it showed 850 to 1,540 calories on November 13, 2026. Hardee’s Big Cheeseburger is a different burger, at 520 calories.

How much beef is in a Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger?

Carl’s Jr. doesn’t publish the patty weight. A quarter pound, about 4 oz (113 g) raw, is a reasonable estimate based on the 219 g total serving reported by FatSecret, so that’s what I use. It isn’t a confirmed figure.

What’s the difference between the Big Cheeseburger and the Famous Star?

The Big Cheeseburger is a simple cheeseburger with pickles, onions, mustard and ketchup. The Famous Star adds lettuce, tomato, Special Sauce and mayonnaise. My Famous Star copycat recipe covers that build.

Is the Big Cheeseburger the same as the old Six Dollar Cheeseburger?

No. In 2009, Carl’s Jr. sold the Six Dollar Cheeseburger for $2.79. It had close to half a pound of Angus beef and 2 slices of American cheese, with onion, pickles, ketchup and mustard on a seeded bun (Nation’s Restaurant News). The toppings follow the same pattern, but it was a different, bigger burger.

Where can I eat the original outside the US?

Carl’s Jr. runs restaurants in Mexico, and our guides to Carl’s Jr. in Mexico City and Carl’s Jr. in Cancún list them. I couldn’t confirm that the Big Cheeseburger is on the menu there under that name.

Is the Big Cheeseburger sold at Carl’s Jr. in France?

I couldn’t confirm it either way. Carl’s Jr. has been in France since January 18, 2018, starting in La Garde near Toulon (Toute la Franchise). The French website advertises 100% Angus beef across its range (Carl’s Jr. France), but its online menu looked incomplete when I checked on November 13, 2026.

How much does a Carl’s Jr. Big Cheeseburger cost?

Carl’s Jr. doesn’t publish a national price, and prices vary from 1 restaurant to the next. The only figure I found had no official source, so I give none. Check the Carl’s Jr. app or your local restaurant.

Can I use something other than American cheese?

Yes. If you can’t find American cheese, use processed cheddar slices. They melt smoothly over the patty the same way. A block of aged cheddar tastes sharper and won’t drape as well.

Sesame or another seeded bun?

Sesame is the safest choice. Carl’s Jr. only says “seeded bun”, and sesame is what copycats of its other burgers use, but the chain doesn’t confirm it for the Big Cheeseburger.
