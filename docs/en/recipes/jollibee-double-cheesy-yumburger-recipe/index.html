Jollibee Double Cheesy Yumburger Copycat Recipe

Make Jollibee's Double Cheesy Yumburger at home in 25 minutes: two thin 50 g beef patties, American cheese and a sweet, creamy Yum sauce, Original or Special.

By Clara, Recipes editorPublished November 27, 2026

This Double Cheesy Yumburger copycat makes two of Jollibee’s double burgers at home in about 25 minutes. You need four thin 50 g (1.8 oz) beef patties, American cheese, soft unseeded buns and a quick sauce of mayo, ketchup and sweet relish. Jollibee lists what goes into the burger (beef patty, cheese, a “tangy, sweet, and creamy” dressing, soft bun) but has never published the formulas, so every quantity here is mine. My first try simply doubled the 90 g patty from my single Jollibee Yumburger copycat recipe. That came to 180 g of beef in a small soft bun, and the sauce disappeared under it. Halving each patty to 50 g fixed it.

What is in a Jollibee Double Cheesy Yumburger?

A Jollibee Double Cheesy Yumburger is two beef Yumburger patties with cheese and a dressing on a soft bun, sold in an Original and a Special version in the Philippines. Jollibee’s Philippine menu, checked on November 11, 2026, describes the Double Original Cheesy Yumburger as “the same cheesy, beefy langhap-sarap Cheesy Yumburger you love with double beef patty, cheese, and special burger dressing.” The Double Special Cheesy Yumburger comes with “two juicy, beefy langhap-sarap Yumburger patties, topped with cheese, dill pickle, chopped onions, and ketchup, sandwiched in between soft buns” (Jollibee Philippines menu).

The Double came first. Jollibee launched the Double Cheesy Yumburger in the Philippines in June 2024. At its BEST fest at TriNoma, Quezon City, on June 17, 2024, the chain named the P-pop group BINI as Cheesy Yumburger ambassadors, with a jingle set to BINI’s “Pantropiko” (Interaksyon). On October 1, 2025, Jollibee split the Cheesy Yumburger into an Original version (cheese and Yum sauce) and a Special version (cheese, ketchup, pickles and onions) (Flow Galindez).

The menu still lists a “Double Cheesy Yumburger w/ Drink”, with the same description as the Double Original, word for word. I read the 2024 Double as today’s Double Original. Jollibee has not said so.

If you only make one, make the Original. The Special is a good cheeseburger, but ketchup, pickle and onion taste like any American chain’s. The sweet, creamy Yum sauce is what makes it Jollibee.

What we know and what we assume

Jollibee confirms the components of the Yumburger but not the recipe behind them, so every quantity below is my estimate. Here is where the line falls.

  • Confirmed by Jollibee: a “100% pure beef patty—seasoned to suit the Pinoy palate”, a “special burger dressing that’s equally tangy, sweet, and creamy” and “soft burger buns” (Jollibee release relayed by Chasing Curious Alice, May 2019).
  • Confirmed by Jollibee: the two Double versions and their toppings, listed on the official Philippine menu.
  • Reported at launch: “double patties, double cheese, and double signature dressing” (Digital Filipina, July 2024). The current menu only says “cheese”, so I can’t tell whether today’s Double has one slice or two.
  • Not published: patty weight, patty seasoning, the dressing formula, the bun recipe, cooking temperatures and nutrition facts.
  • My estimates: 50 g patties, the seasoning blend, the sauce ratios and the pan temperatures in this recipe.

Double Cheesy Yumburger ingredients for 2 burgers

For two Double Cheesy Yumburgers you need 200 g (7 oz) of ground beef, two to four slices of American cheese, two soft buns and five sauce ingredients. Weigh the beef. Equal patties cook in the same time.

Times: prep 15 minutes, cook 10 minutes, total 25 minutes. Yield: 2 double burgers.

For the patties

  • 200 g (7 oz) ground beef, 80/20 (20% fat), well chilled
  • ½ tsp fine salt
  • ¼ tsp finely ground black pepper
  • ¼ tsp onion powder
  • ¼ tsp sugar (optional, for the sweet note)
  • 1 tsp neutral oil for the pan

For the Yum sauce (Original version)

  • 3 tbsp (40 g) mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp (15 g) banana ketchup, or regular tomato ketchup
  • 2 tsp (10 g) sweet pickle relish
  • ½ tsp sugar
  • 1 pinch white or black pepper

For assembly

  • 2 soft white burger buns, unseeded (potato rolls work well)
  • 2 to 4 slices American cheese (1 per burger for today’s menu description, 2 per burger for the 2024 “double cheese” launch version)
  • 1 tsp soft butter for the buns (optional)

For the Special version instead of the sauce

  • 2 tbsp (30 g) tomato ketchup
  • 8 thin dill pickle slices
  • 2 tbsp (20 g) finely chopped white onion

Double Cheesy Yumburger recipe step by step

Make the sauce first. Then cook four thin patties hard and fast, melt the cheese on them in the pan and build the burgers on warm buns. This is my home method: Jollibee does not publish how it cooks its patties.

1. Make the Yum sauce

Stir the mayonnaise, ketchup, sweet relish, sugar and pepper together until smooth. Chill it for at least 15 minutes so the sugar dissolves.

2. Portion and shape the patties

Divide the cold beef into four 50 g (1.8 oz) balls. Press each one between two sheets of parchment into a thin disc about 11 cm (4½ in) wide. Make it slightly wider than the bun, because it shrinks. Mix the salt, pepper, onion powder and sugar, and season both sides.

3. Toast the buns

Butter the cut sides lightly and toast them cut side down in a pan over medium heat for 30 to 45 seconds. Stop at pale gold. A crisp bun is the wrong bun here.

4. Sear the thin patties

Heat a cast-iron pan or griddle over medium-high heat, around 450 °F (230 °C) at the surface, and add the oil. Cook the patties for 1 minute 30 seconds without moving them, until the edges brown.

5. Melt the cheese

Flip the patties and lay the cheese on two of them right away, or on all four for the double-cheese version. Cover and cook 1 minute more so the cheese runs over the edges. Thin 50 g patties reach the USDA safe temperature for ground beef, 160 °F (71 °C), in that time. Our burger patty internal temperature guide explains how to check it.

6. Assemble the burger

For the Original, spread the sauce on both halves of the bun, then stack the two patties with the cheese between and on top. For the Special, spread ketchup on the bottom bun, stack the patties and cheese, then add four pickle slices and a tablespoon of chopped onion before the top bun. Wrap each burger in paper for one minute. The bun steams and softens, as it does in a fast-food wrapper.

Yum sauce recipe: sweet, tangy and creamy

My Yum sauce is three parts mayonnaise to one part ketchup, plus sweet relish and a pinch of sugar. That covers the three notes Jollibee names: creamy from the mayo, sweet from the sugar and ketchup, tangy from the relish. Wikipedia describes the original Yumburger as “a hamburger containing a beef patty topped with mayonnaise” (Wikipedia), which is why mayo leads.

Banana ketchup is my favorite swap. It is a common condiment in Filipino cooking, sweeter than tomato ketchup (Food Republic), and it pushes the sauce toward a Filipino sweet-savory balance. Jollibee does not say it uses banana ketchup in its dressing. That is my choice, not theirs.

For comparison, the popular copycat by Mae Serves You Food uses ½ cup mayo, ¼ cup ketchup, 2 tsp sweet relish and ½ tsp pepper. I use less ketchup and add a little sugar, so the mayo stays in front.

Why this copycat is built this way

Every choice below follows one of the three things Jollibee actually describes: thin beef patties, melted cheese and a sweet, tangy, creamy dressing on a soft bun.

  • Thin, light patties. The Yumburger is a small burger, and two 50 g patties keep the Double compact. The Mae Serves You Food copycat uses about 91 g per patty (454 g for five). That works for a single; doubled, it buries the sauce, as my first test showed.
  • No egg, no oyster sauce. Several online copycats bind the beef with egg and season it with oyster sauce. Jollibee calls its patty “100% pure beef”, so I keep the meat plain and season only the surface.
  • A touch of sugar and onion powder. My single Yumburger recipe sticks to salt and pepper. With two thin patties there is twice the crust and half the sauce per bite, so the Double needed help. A quarter teaspoon of sugar gave a sweet edge without tasting like dessert. Skip it if you prefer.
  • Processed American cheese. It melts smoothly in one minute, which matches the creamy texture Jollibee describes.
  • A soft, unseeded bun. Jollibee only says “soft bun”. Photos of the Cheesy Yumburger show a plain top, so no sesame.

Why does a Jollibee burger taste sweet?

A Jollibee burger tastes sweet because its dressing is officially “tangy, sweet, and creamy”, and because Filipino cooking readily mixes sweet and savory. Banana ketchup, sweet spaghetti sauces and sweet glazes are everyday flavors in the Philippines (Tasting Table). Jollibee’s slogan, “Langhap-Sarap”, roughly means “smells delicious”, and the menu still uses it in its burger descriptions. Tips for a better Double Cheesy Yumburger

Keep the beef cold, the patties thin and the bun soft. Those three choices matter more than any secret ingredient.

  • Shape the patties ahead and chill them on parchment for 10 minutes, so the fat stays in the meat.
  • Do not press the patties once they are in the pan, or the juices run out.
  • Four patties need room: cook in two batches if your pan is smaller than 28 cm (11 in), so they sear instead of steaming.
  • The sauce keeps three days in the fridge in a closed jar.
  • If you like double smashed patties with a crisper crust, my McDouble copycat recipe uses the same two-thin-patty build with a different sauce.

Double Cheesy Yumburger recipe FAQ

What is the difference between the Double Original and the Double Special Cheesy Yumburger?

The sauce and toppings. The Original has the creamy, sweet and tangy Yum dressing with no vegetables. The Special swaps the dressing for ketchup and adds dill pickle and chopped onions. Jollibee split the two versions on October 1, 2025.

How is the Double different from the regular Yumburger?

The Double has two beef patties instead of one, plus cheese. The plain Yumburger is one patty with Yum sauce on a soft bun, according to Jollibee’s menu. For the single version, see my Jollibee Yumburger copycat recipe.

How do you make Jollibee Yum sauce at home?

Mix 3 tbsp mayonnaise, 1 tbsp banana or tomato ketchup, 2 tsp sweet pickle relish, ½ tsp sugar and a pinch of pepper, then chill 15 minutes. This is a homemade approximation: Jollibee has never published its dressing recipe.

Is the Yumburger 100% beef?

Yes, according to Jollibee, which describes it as a “100% pure beef patty—seasoned to suit the Pinoy palate.” It does not list the seasoning.

Which cheese is closest to Jollibee’s?

Processed American cheese slices. Jollibee only says “cheese” and “creamy cheese”, and American slices melt smoothly in one minute on a hot patty.

Which bun should I use for a Jollibee-style burger?

A small, soft, unseeded white bun, such as a potato roll. Jollibee only says “soft bun”, and photos show a plain top.

Is the Double Cheesy Yumburger sold in the US or Europe?

Not that I could confirm. Jollibee’s US site listed the single Cheesy Yumburger at $4.99 but no Double when I checked on November 11, 2026. I have not checked availability in European branches.
