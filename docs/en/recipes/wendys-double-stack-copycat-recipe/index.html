Wendy's Double Stack Copycat Recipe (Makes 2)

Wendy's Double Stack copycat recipe: two square junior patties, one slice of American cheese between them, pickles and onion. Makes 2 in 45 minutes.

By Alice, Recipes writerPublished November 18, 2026

To make a Wendy’s Double Stack copycat at home, form two thin square patties of about 2.25 oz (64 g) each, freeze them firm, salt them in the pan and sear them hard in very hot cast iron. Stack them with one slice of American cheese in between. Then add ketchup, yellow mustard, chopped raw onion and crinkle-cut dill pickles on a toasted plain white bun. That’s the whole burger. This recipe makes 2 Double Stacks in about 45 minutes, most of it freezer time. It is not a smash burger, and it is my homemade reproduction, not Wendy’s official recipe.

What’s on a Wendy’s Double Stack?

A Wendy’s Double Stack is two junior beef patties, one slice of American cheese, ketchup, mustard, onion and pickles on a plain sandwich bun. That list goes back to the national launch in August 2008, as reported by QSR Web and Nation’s Restaurant News. NRN also placed the cheese: “two junior hamburger patties with a slice of cheese in between.”

Wendy’s own 2017 press release, published via Franchising.com, describes “more than a quarter pound” of fresh, never-frozen beef with American cheese, dill pickles, “fresh red onions,” ketchup and mustard on a “warm bun.” No lettuce, no tomato, no mayo: Tasting Table confirmed it in August 2026. My reference for this recipe is the US menu as of November 2026.

Wendy’s Double Stack copycat ingredients (makes 2 burgers)

These quantities give two burgers with just over a quarter pound of raw beef each, in line with what Wendy’s announced in 2017.

  • 8.8 oz (250 g) 80/20 ground beef, cold, divided into 4 portions of 2.1 to 2.25 oz (60 to 64 g)
  • Kosher salt, about 1/4 tsp in total
  • 2 slices yellow American cheese
  • 2 plain white hamburger buns (not brioche, not potato)
  • 2 tsp ketchup
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped raw red onion
  • 6 to 8 crinkle-cut dill pickle chips, fermented if you can find them

How to make a square Wendy’s-style patty

A Wendy’s patty is square so its corners stick out past a round bun. To keep those corners in the pan, shape it thin and get it cold. Wendy’s explains the square shape in its own fresh beef FAQ. Top Secret Recipes freezes its square junior patties before cooking in its Jr. Bacon Cheeseburger copycat. I borrowed that step after my first batch, cooked straight from the fridge, came out of the pan with rounded corners. The frozen batch kept them.

The square corners and the cheese between the patties match the Double Stack, but the lettuce and tomato do not (illustrative photo, not a Double Stack). I press each portion between two squares of parchment paper with the bottom of a flat pan, then square off the edges with a bench scraper until the patty measures 4 to 4.3 in on a side and about 1/4 in thick. A patty this thin cooks through in four minutes. Don’t press it again once it hits the heat.

Step-by-step: how to cook a Wendy’s Double Stack at home

Freeze the square patties, salt them in the pan, sear them hard, melt the cheese on one patty per burger, then assemble fast while everything is hot.

1. Shape the square patties

Divide the beef into 4 portions of 2.1 to 2.25 oz (60 to 64 g). Press each one between parchment into a square of 4 to 4.3 in (10 to 11 cm), 1/4 in (6 mm) thick, and square the edges with a bench scraper.

2. Chill the patties until firm

Lay the patties flat, still on their parchment, on a plate or sheet pan. Freeze them until firm, about 30 minutes in my freezer, so they hold their corners in the pan.

3. Prep the toppings and toast the buns

Finely chop the red onion and pat the pickle chips dry. Toast the cut sides of the buns in a dry pan over medium heat until lightly golden, about 1 minute, and set them aside.

4. Heat the cast-iron pan

Heat a cast-iron skillet or griddle over high heat until the surface reaches 450 to 480°F (230 to 250°C), about 5 minutes. An infrared thermometer helps. Without one, a drop of water should skitter and vanish at once.

5. Sear the patties and salt them

Peel off the parchment and lay the patties in the pan. Salt the top of each one right away, and cook for about 2 minutes, until the edges turn brown.

6. Flip and add the cheese

Flip the patties and cook for about 2 minutes more. Right after the flip, lay a slice of American cheese on two of the four patties, one per burger, so it melts while the second side cooks.

7. Stack and dress the burger

On each bottom bun, place a plain patty, then a cheese-topped patty, so the slice ends up between the two layers of beef. Add the pickles, the onion, 1 teaspoon of ketchup and 1/2 teaspoon of mustard, close with the top bun and serve at once.

Wendy’s has never published its build order. NRN’s 2008 description puts the cheese between the patties. The rest of my order is my own choice: the pickles and onion sit on the hot beef, and the sauces go just under the crown so they hit your tongue first.

Why this recipe tastes like the original Double Stack

This copycat works because it keeps the four things that define the Double Stack: thin square patties, salt-only beef, a single slice of cheese and a sharp, vinegary topping mix with no lettuce or mayo.

  • The beef is just beef and salt. Wendy’s ingredient statement, quoted by Fast Food Nutrition, lists “Ground Beef, Salt” for the patty. No pepper, no onion powder, no seasoning blend.
  • The patties are junior-size. Wendy’s announced 2.25 oz junior patties in a 2012 release on the Son of Baconator. Two of them make 4.5 oz of raw beef, which fits the “more than a quarter pound” Wendy’s claimed in 2017. Mashed reports 2 oz per patty instead, but two 2 oz patties would make exactly a quarter pound, not more. That’s why I never go below 2.1 oz (60 g).
  • One slice of cheese, not two. Tasting Table ranked the Double Stack 6th among fast-food double cheeseburgers in July 2026 and wrote that “relying on just one slice of cheese left the burger feeling a little unbalanced.” I disagree. With a second slice, the burger turns soft and the pickles get lost. One slice between the patties keeps it beefy, and I would not change it.
  • Fermented pickles and raw onion carry the flavor. Wendy’s switched to fermented “old-school” dill pickles in 2020, according to its own blog. With only ketchup and mustard as sauces, the pickles and onion do most of the work.
  • A plain bun. The Double Stack uses a standard white sandwich bun, the clearest physical difference from the potato bun of the Dave’s Double.

A short history of the Double Stack

The Double Stack went national in the US in August 2008 at 99 cents on Wendy’s Value Menu, where it replaced the Stack Attack. The burger had been tested in a few markets first, and Paul Kershisnik, then Wendy’s interim chief marketing officer, told QSR Web: “The Double Stack is a proven store traffic and sales driver.”

According to NRN, Wendy’s built it to fight McDonald’s $1 double cheeseburger. For the launch, the chain handed out 1 million coupons for a free Double Stack at 400 Florida restaurants on Labor Day 2008. The Stack Attack it replaced had launched in 2007 with ketchup and mayo, Mashed adds.

In January 2017, the Double Stack joined Wendy’s 4 for $4 meal deal. In 2026 it is one of the sandwiches you can pick in Wendy’s $6 Biggie Bag, sold since January 14. My colleague Noah puts that deal in context in his analysis of the 2026 burger value wars.

Wendy’s Double Stack FAQ

What’s on a Wendy’s Double Stack? Two junior beef patties, one slice of American cheese between them, ketchup, mustard, chopped raw onion and crinkle-cut dill pickles on a plain white bun. It has no lettuce, tomato or mayo.

What is the difference between the Double Stack and the Dave’s Double? The Double Stack uses two smaller junior patties and a plain sandwich bun, while the Dave’s Double uses larger patties on a potato bun, according to Tasting Table (August 2026). The Double Stack also skips the lettuce, tomato and mayo. Both share American cheese, ketchup, mustard, onion and crinkle-cut pickles. See my Dave’s Double copycat to compare.

What is the difference between the Double Stack and the Jr. Bacon Cheeseburger? Both are built on Wendy’s junior patty, but the Double Stack doubles the beef and has no bacon. The full build of the bacon version is in my Jr. Bacon Cheeseburger copycat.

How much does a Wendy’s junior patty weigh? Wendy’s announced junior patties of 2.25 oz (about 64 g) raw in 2012. Mashed and nutrition databases list 2 oz (about 57 g). Wendy’s does not publish a patty weight specific to the Double Stack in 2026.

What seasoning does Wendy’s put on its burger patties? Only salt. Wendy’s ingredient statement lists the patty as “Ground Beef, Salt,” and The Daily Meal reported in January 2025 that salt is the only seasoning on Wendy’s patties.

How many calories are in a Wendy’s Double Stack? It depends on the source: Mashed gives 410 calories and Fast Food Nutrition gives 440. Wendy’s own nutrition page for your market is the number to trust.

How much does a Double Stack cost in 2026? There is no single national price. It launched at 99 cents in 2008, and Tasting Table reported about $3.79 in August 2026, but Wendy’s prices vary by restaurant. It is also one of the sandwich choices in the $6 Biggie Bag sold since January 14, 2026.

Is there a Bacon Double Stack? Mashed reports that the Bacon Double Stack was taken off the menu for a time and then came back, without giving dates. I could not confirm that it is available in November 2026.

How do I keep square patties from shrinking into circles? Shape them thin (1/4 in), square the edges with a bench scraper and freeze them until firm before they hit a very hot pan. Do not press them once they are cooking.

Can I order a Double Stack outside the US? I could not verify the Double Stack’s availability or recipe in Canada, the UK or Mexico, where Wendy’s also operates. This recipe follows the US menu as of November 2026.
