Jalapeño & Cheese Whataburger Copycat Recipe

Jalapeño & Cheese Whataburger copycat recipe for 2: a thin 5-inch patty, American cheese, pickled jalapeños and yellow mustard, ready in 25 minutes.

By Julia, Recipes writerPublished November 27, 2026

A Jalapeño & Cheese Whataburger is a regular Whataburger with two additions: a slice of American cheese and pickled jalapeño slices laid right on it. To make it at home, toast a 5-inch bun and sear a thin beef patty seasoned with salt and pepper. Add the cheese and the jalapeños, then the standard toppings: yellow mustard, dill pickles, diced yellow onion, tomato and lettuce. The patty takes 3 to 4 minutes per side on a griddle or cast-iron pan over medium-high heat, until it reaches 160°F (71°C). This is my reproduction for two burgers, not Whataburger’s recipe. The chain doesn’t publish its bun, cheese or mustard formulas.

Of all the Whataburger builds I’ve copied for this site, this is the one I’d make again first. Mustard alone gives you tang. The jalapeño brine adds vinegar and heat on top of it, and the cheese keeps both in check.

What’s in the original Jalapeño & Cheese Whataburger?

The Jalapeño & Cheese Whataburger is a large 5-inch beef patty on a 5-inch bun, topped with American cheese, jalapeños, tomato, lettuce, pickles, diced onions and mustard. That is the build listed on a Whataburger restaurant menu (SinglePlatform, checked November 21, 2026). Chowhound describes it the same way: a standard Whataburger plus American cheese and jalapeños.

The chain’s ingredient list, as republished by FastFoodNutrition, gives a few more clues. I couldn’t check Whataburger’s own document, so treat these as third-party data:

  • Jalapeños: “whole jalapeño peppers, vinegar, water, salt and spices added”, so pickled peppers in brine, not fresh ones.
  • Onions: “diced, U.S. jumbo yellow onions”.
  • Patty: 100% USDA-inspected ground beef, seasoned with salt and pepper.
  • Allergens: wheat/gluten, milk and soy. No egg, fish, peanuts, shellfish or tree nuts.

Whataburger says its beef is 100% fresh, never frozen American beef, cooked to order, and that its cheese is made with milk from American dairy farms (Whataburger Stories, August 23, 2023). All of these details refer to the US menu as of November 2026.

Jalapeño & Cheese Whataburger copycat ingredients (2 burgers)

You need 8 oz of 80/20 ground beef, two 5-inch buns, two slices of American cheese, a jar of pickled jalapeños and the classic Whataburger toppings. Prep takes about 15 minutes and cooking about 10, so 25 minutes in total for 2 burgers. Weights are in ounces, with grams in parentheses.

  • 8 oz (226 g) ground beef, 80/20
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 white hamburger buns, 5 in (13 cm) across
  • About 1 tbsp (15 g) soft butter, for the buns
  • 2 slices American cheese
  • 16 to 20 pickled jalapeño slices from a jar, drained (8 to 10 per burger)
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard (1 tbsp per burger)
  • 8 dill pickle chips (4 per burger)
  • About 1.4 oz (40 g) yellow onion, finely diced
  • 2 to 4 tomato slices
  • A few leaves of iceberg lettuce, shredded or torn

The 4 oz patty is my estimate. Whataburger publishes only the 5-inch diameter. The quarter pound comes from the first Whataburger in 1950, and I use it here exactly as in my homemade Whataburger with Cheese copycat.

How to make a Jalapeño & Cheese Whataburger: step by step

You need one hot griddle and about 10 minutes at the stove. Toast the buns, sear the thin patties for 3 to 4 minutes per side and melt the cheese. Then stack the toppings, with the jalapeños directly on the cheese.

1. Prep the toppings

Drain the jalapeño slices on a paper towel so the brine doesn’t soak the bun. Dice the onion finely, slice the tomato and shred the lettuce. Get all of it ready before the beef goes in. The patties cook fast.

2. Shape the patties

Divide the beef into two 4 oz (113 g) balls. Press each one between two sheets of parchment into a thin disc about 5½ inches (14 cm) wide. It shrinks as it cooks and ends up close to the width of the 5-inch bun. Season both sides with salt and pepper just before cooking.

3. Toast the buns

Butter the cut sides of the buns and toast them cut-side down on the griddle over medium heat until golden. Whataburger’s menu doesn’t say whether stores butter their buns. I do it because a toasted, buttered crumb holds up to the mustard and the jalapeño brine.

4. Cook the patties to 160°F

Cook the patties on a griddle or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat for 3 to 4 minutes per side, until an instant-read thermometer shows 160°F (71°C) in the center. That is the USDA safe minimum for ground beef. In my 12-inch cast-iron skillet, both patties fit side by side and reached 160°F after about 3½ minutes per side. My burger patty internal temperature guide explains how to probe a patty this thin without going through it.

5. Melt the cheese

Lay a slice of American cheese on each patty right after the flip. If it hasn’t fully melted when the beef hits 160°F, cover the pan for 20 to 30 seconds. Don’t skip the melt: soft cheese is what holds the jalapeños in place.

6. Assemble the burger

Spread 1 tbsp of mustard on the bottom bun. Add 4 pickle chips, half the diced onion, the patty with its cheese, then 8 to 10 jalapeño slices on top of the cheese. Finish with tomato, lettuce and the top bun. On my first burger I tucked the jalapeños under the lettuce, and half of them slid out on the first bite. On the cheese, they stay put. Wrap the burger in parchment for a minute so the bun steams a little, the way a drive-thru burger arrives.

Fresh, pickled or grilled jalapeños?

Use pickled jalapeños from a jar. The chain’s ingredient list names peppers in vinegar, water, salt and spices, not fresh peppers. Chowhound’s reviewer still described them as fresh and crunchy, so pick a jar with firm slices and drain them well. Soft, waterlogged rings make the top of the burger mushy.

The number of slices per burger isn’t published anywhere. With 8 to 10 slices I get one even layer across a 5-inch patty; go up or down to taste. According to Mashed (May 21, 2026), grilled jalapeños are a separate customization Whataburger offers. They aren’t the standard topping on this burger.

Why this copycat comes close to the original

This copycat follows the published build component by component: a 5-inch bun, a thin salt-and-pepper patty, American cheese, vinegar-pickled jalapeños, yellow mustard and the same four vegetables. The brine and mustard supply the acidity and the cheese softens the heat. A thin patty means the beef doesn’t drown out the toppings.

Chowhound ranked the Jalapeño & Cheese No. 2 of 8 Whataburger burgers (September 29, 2025), writing that the peppers “complemented the American cheese, burger patty, lettuce, tomatoes, onions, and mustard so well, with just enough kick and sweetness to make their presence known.” I agree with that ranking, and that balance is what I cooked for.

My verdict: the homemade version gets the flavor right. The bun is harder. Whataburger doesn’t publish its bun, cheese or mustard formulas, and a supermarket bun is the part that gives the copy away. Deli American cheese and plain yellow mustard, on the other hand, are close enough that I stopped looking for better substitutes.

Variations and ordering tips

Mashed suggests ordering the Jalapeño & Cheese on a brioche bun, whose slight sweetness balances the heat. At home, a brioche bun about 5 inches across does the same job. Third-party menu aggregators also list a small 4-inch bun, Texas toast and no bun as options, but I couldn’t confirm these on an official menu.

Want a second jalapeño cheeseburger to compare? My colleague Alice rebuilt Sonic’s jalapeño double cheeseburger, a double-patty take on the same idea. For other Whataburger copycats, the Triple Meat Whataburger and the Sweet & Spicy Bacon Burger use the same patty method.

Jalapeño & Cheese Whataburger FAQ

What’s on a Jalapeño & Cheese Whataburger?

A 5-inch beef patty on a 5-inch bun with American cheese, jalapeños, tomato, lettuce, pickles, diced onions and mustard, as listed on a Whataburger US restaurant menu (November 2026).

Are Whataburger’s jalapeños fresh, pickled or grilled?

They’re pickled. A third-party site republishing the chain’s ingredient list names whole jalapeño peppers, vinegar, water, salt and spices. Grilled jalapeños are a separate add-on, not the standard topping.

Is the Jalapeño & Cheese Whataburger very spicy?

Not very. Chowhound found “just enough kick and sweetness” to notice the peppers without overwhelming the cheese and mustard. Pickled jalapeños are milder than fresh ones, and the cheese softens the heat further.

How many calories are in a Jalapeño & Cheese Whataburger?

About 680 calories on the 5-inch bun, with 32 g of fat, 34 g of protein and 1,800 mg of sodium, according to CalorieKing and Nutrola (US market, November 2026). Another third-party menu site lists 620 calories.

What’s the difference between the Jalapeño & Cheese Whataburger and the Bacon Blaze Jalapeño Double?

The Jalapeño & Cheese is a single patty with American cheese, pickled jalapeños and the classic toppings. The Bacon Blaze Jalapeño Double, a limited edition announced October 1, 2024, stacked two patties with Jalapeño Ranch, Jalapeño Cream Cheese, bacon, aged cheddar and jalapeño slices on two toasted 5-inch buns (PR Newswire).

Can I get it on a different bun?

Yes. Whataburger lets you customize every burger, and Mashed recommends brioche to balance the heat. Small buns, Texas toast and no bun also appear as options on third-party menu sites.

What temperature should a burger patty reach?

Ground beef should reach 160°F (71°C) in the center, the USDA’s safe minimum. A thin 5-inch patty gets there in 3 to 4 minutes per side over medium-high heat.
