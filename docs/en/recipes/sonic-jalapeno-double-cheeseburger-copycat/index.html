Sonic Jalapeno Double Cheeseburger Copycat Recipe

I tested a 2-burger copycat with smashed patties, pickled jalapeños and spicy mayo — inspired by Sonic, not its formula.

Sonic jalapeno double cheeseburger copycat ingredients for 2

These are my home quantities for 2 burgers, not Sonic’s formula. I prefer American cheddar here; pepper jack sounds fun but it bullies the beef.

  • 1.1 lb / 500 g ground beef 80/20, divided into 4 x 4 oz / 113 g balls
  • 1 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt, plus 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 soft white burger buns, about 4-inch / 10 cm, plus 1 Tbsp soft butter
  • 4 slices American cheddar (pepper jack works if you want more heat)
  • 32-48 pickled jalapeño rings, drained (about 1/3 cup / 80 ml)
  • 1 cup / 30 g shredded iceberg lettuce
  • 4 thin tomato slices
  • 8 thin red onion rings
  • 8 dill pickle chips
  • For the spicy mayo-style sauce: 1/3 cup / 75 g mayonnaise, 1 Tbsp hot sauce, 1 tsp jalapeño brine, 1/4 tsp garlic powder, 1/4 tsp smoked paprika

Step by step: Sonic-style jalapeño doubles at home

I smash thin patties hot and fast, melt the cheese on the griddle, and assemble right away. Soft bun, gooey cheese. Do not wait around.

1. Mix the spicy mayo-style sauce

This sauce is the creamy heat in my copycat; it is not Sonic’s official sauce. I whisk 1/3 cup mayonnaise with 1 Tbsp hot sauce, 1 tsp jalapeño brine, garlic powder, and smoked paprika. I chill it 15 minutes. It makes about 1/2 cup — enough for 2 burgers with extra for dipping.

2. Prep the jalapeños and vegetables

Pickled rings give steady heat and crunch with no extra grilling. I drain 32-48 rings and pat dry. I shred the lettuce, slice tomato and onion, and lay out the pickle chips. Everything stays cold until assembly.

Sliced pickled jalapenos showing heat level — 8 to 12 rings per copycat burger (Photo: bradleygee, CC BY 2.0 — https://www.flickr.com/photos/13951072@N00/15645680853)

3. Divide and season the beef

Thin 4-oz patties sear fast and stack like a drive-in double. I divide 1.1 lb / 500 g cold 80/20 beef into 4 loose balls of about 113 g. I do not overwork the meat. Salt and pepper go on just before the smash.

4. Toast the buns

Butter-toasted buns hold up to double patties and spicy mayo. I butter the cut sides with 1 Tbsp soft butter. I toast cut-side down in a skillet over medium heat for 1-2 minutes until golden, then set aside. For a softer crumb, use my soft homemade burger buns.

Butter-toasted soft white burger buns browning cut-side down for double cheeseburgers (Photo: jeffreyw, CC BY 2.0 — https://www.flickr.com/photos/7927684@N03/5436865232)

5. Smash-sear the patties

A hard smash on ripping-hot steel makes the crisp edges. I heat a cast-iron skillet or griddle over medium-high to 450°F / 230°C. I add 2 beef balls, smash firmly to 1/4-inch / 6 mm, and cook 90 seconds. I flip, cook 60-90 seconds more. Ground beef must reach 160°F / 71°C inside. Source: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/meat/ground-beef-and-food-safety. I cook in 2 batches.

Smash-seared patties with crisp edges on a hot griddle at 450F before cheese melt (Photo: Michael Rivera, CC BY-SA 4.0 — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?curid=54731043)

6. Melt the cheese

Cheese melted on the griddle actually sticks to the patty. I lay 1 slice American cheddar on each hot patty right after the flip. I add 1 Tbsp water at the edge and cover 30 seconds. Pepper jack melts slower, so give it 45 seconds.

Melting American cheddar draped over hot smashed patties for jalapeno double cheeseburger stack (Photo: jeffreyw, CC BY 2.0 — https://www.flickr.com/photos/7927684@N03/5065416449)

7. Assemble the double cheeseburgers

Hot patties go straight onto sauce so the cheese stays melted. For each burger I spread 1 Tbsp spicy sauce on bottom and top bun. I add pickles, onion, lettuce, tomato, then 2 cheesy patties. I top with 8-12 jalapeño rings and close. Rest 1 minute, then serve.

FAQ

What cheese for a Sonic jalapeño copycat: American, pepper jack, or both?

The official cheese is unconfirmed, so I use American cheddar for melt and keep pepper jack optional. I used 1 slice per patty in three tests. Honestly? American wins — it drapes, pepper jack splits and fights the jalapeños.

Spicy mayo, plain mayo, or chipotle sauce?

The official sauce is unconfirmed; my home hypothesis is spicy mayo. I mix mayo, hot sauce, jalapeño brine, garlic powder, and smoked paprika. Cream plus tang and heat, without burying the beef.

Fresh or pickled jalapeños, and how many for 2 burgers?

I use pickled rings for steady heat: 32-48 rings total for 2 burgers. Drain and pat them dry or the bun goes soggy. For fresh heat, add 1 thinly sliced fresh jalapeño per burger — greener, hotter, crunchier.

How many calories in a SuperSONIC Jalapeno Double Cheeseburger?

I do not cite a calorie number because I could not verify it from a primary source on October 2, 2026. Nutrition aggregators list estimates, but they vary and are not Sonic’s kitchen data. Weigh your own build if you track it.

How do I cook patties like Sonic at home?

I smash 4-oz / 113-g balls thin on a 450°F / 230°C griddle, season with salt and pepper, and cook to 160°F / 71°C. I tested this three times in my kitchen with a cast-iron skillet and instant-read thermometer. Work fast, ventilate well, and melt the cheese under a lid.
