Hardee's Bacon Cheeseburger Copycat Recipe

Make Hardee's Bacon Cheeseburger at home: a charbroiled quarter-pound Angus patty, crisp bacon, American cheese and mayo on brioche. Makes 2.

By Alice, Recipes writerPublished November 24, 2026

This Hardee’s Bacon Cheeseburger copycat recipe starts with a 4 oz (113 g) patty of 80/20 beef, Angus if you can find it, charred on a very hot grill or grill pan to 160 °F (71 °C) inside. Top it with melted American cheese and 2 strips of crisp bacon. Add lettuce, tomato and thin slices of onion, then put it on a toasted brioche-style bun spread with mayonnaise. Hardee’s names those parts on its menu. The quantities, the seasoning and the build order are my own, because the chain doesn’t publish them.

I built this copycat from Hardee’s official US menu page, checked on November 14, 2026, then cooked it at home on a ridged cast-iron grill pan. It makes 2 burgers. Where a detail is official, I link to it. Where it’s my estimate, I say so. 1 correction before we start: the official count is 640 calories, not the 740 that nutrition sites still show.

What is in a Hardee’s Bacon Cheeseburger?

A Hardee’s Bacon Cheeseburger is a quarter-pound charbroiled Angus patty with mayonnaise, bacon, melted American cheese, lettuce, tomato and sliced onions on a toasted brioche-style bun. Hardee’s describes it as “a Quarter Pound 100% Angus beef patty topped with mayonnaise, bacon, melted American cheese, lettuce, tomato, and sliced onions, all on a perfectly toasted Brioche style bun” (Hardee’s, checked November 14, 2026).

It sits in Hardee’s Charbroiled Burgers range, so the patty is cooked over a flame. The menu says “Angus”, not “Black Angus” as it does for the single Hardee’s Big Cheeseburger. That small wording gap is why I wouldn’t assume the beef is identical.

Hardee’s Bacon Cheeseburger calories: 640, not 740

A Hardee’s Bacon Cheeseburger has 640 calories, according to the chain’s official US menu page. Nutrition aggregators such as fatsecret still list a “Bacon Cheeseburger Single” at 740 calories and 281 g. Those figures have no date and don’t come from Hardee’s, so I don’t treat them as current.

Hardee’s also publishes a nutrition PDF, but its table is an image I couldn’t read reliably. So I give no fat, sodium or protein figures here. I’d rather leave them out than copy numbers I can’t check.

Hardee’s Bacon Cheeseburger copycat ingredients (2 burgers)

You need 8 oz of ground beef, 4 strips of bacon, 2 slices of American cheese, 2 brioche-style buns and fresh toppings. Only the quarter-pound patty comes from Hardee’s. The rest is my estimate.

  • 8 oz (227 g) ground beef, 80/20, Angus if you can find it, shaped into 2 4 oz (113 g) patties
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 4 strips thin-cut American-style bacon (2 per burger)
  • 2 slices American cheese
  • 2 brioche-style burger buns
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise (1 per burger)
  • 2 leaves green-leaf or iceberg lettuce
  • 2 to 4 tomato slices (1 or 2 per burger)
  • 4 to 6 thin rings of onion (2 or 3 per burger)

Buy ground chuck labeled 80/20, not lean beef. The fat drips and flares on the grill, and that gives a charbroiled burger its smoky edge. Thin-cut bacon gets crisp without taking over the bite. Thick-cut bacon makes a different burger.

Which bacon and cheese to use

Use thin-cut, regular smoked bacon and a deli or supermarket slice of American cheese. Hardee’s doesn’t publish its bacon’s weight, number of strips, thickness or wood smoke. 2 strips per burger is my estimate.

Hardee’s has named its bacon’s wood before: the 2016 Bacon 3-Way Thickburger used applewood-smoked bacon. That says nothing about today’s Bacon Cheeseburger, so pick the smoked bacon you like.

Hardee’s doesn’t say how many slices of American cheese go on the burger. I use 1 per patty. If you can’t find American cheese, a processed cheddar slice is the closest swap. It melts smooth instead of turning oily.

How to make a Hardee’s Bacon Cheeseburger, step by step

Crisp the bacon first. Then char 2 4 oz patties on a very hot grill to 160 °F (71 °C), melt the cheese on top and build the burger on a toasted brioche bun with mayo, lettuce, tomato and onion. Allow about 15 minutes of prep and 20 minutes of cooking.

1. Cook the bacon until crisp

Lay the strips in a cold skillet, then turn the heat to medium. Cook them, turning a few times, until they are crisp and flat, and drain them on paper towels. Starting cold renders the fat slowly, so the strips crisp evenly instead of burning at the edges.

2. Shape the patties

Divide the beef into 2 4 oz (113 g) portions and press each into a patty about 1/2 inch (1.3 cm) wider than your bun. Press a shallow dimple into the center with your thumb so it stays flat as it shrinks. Keep the patties cold until the grill is ready.

3. Heat the grill or grill pan

Get a charcoal or gas grill, or a ridged cast-iron grill pan, as hot as it will go. Dark grill marks and a slight char are what make a charbroiled burger different from a flat-top smash burger. If you’re using a grill pan, open a window: the fat will smoke.

4. Season and grill the beef

Salt and pepper both sides just before cooking. Grill without pressing until the underside is well marked, flip once, and cook until an instant-read thermometer reads 160 °F (71 °C) in the center. That’s the USDA safe minimum for ground beef (USDA FSIS), and my colleague Clara explains why in her burger patty internal temperature guide.

5. Melt the American cheese

Lay 1 slice of American cheese on each patty right after the flip. Cover the grill, or set a metal bowl over the patty in the pan, for the last minute so the cheese drapes over the edges.

6. Toast the brioche-style buns

Toast the cut sides of the buns on the cooler part of the grill or in a dry skillet until golden. Watch them closely. Brioche contains sugar and butter, so it burns much faster than a plain bun.

7. Assemble the burger

Spread 1 tablespoon of mayonnaise on the cut side of the top bun. On the bottom bun, stack the cheesy patty, 2 bacon strips laid side by side, the onion rings, the tomato and the lettuce. Close the burger and eat it while the cheese is still soft. Hardee’s doesn’t publish its build order. This is mine: the lettuce sits between the tomato and the mayo-spread crown, so tomato juice never reaches the brioche.

How to get a charbroiled bacon cheeseburger at home without a grill

Heat a ridged cast-iron grill pan until it smokes and use 80/20 beef so the fat sizzles on the ridges. You get real grill marks, and the burned fat brings the smoky, slightly bitter edge of flame-grilled beef. A flat skillet browns the meat well, but it tastes like a diner burger.

Hardee’s doesn’t say how its grills work: no temperature, no timing, no equipment. So I aimed for the result rather than the method: a firm crust with grill marks around a juicy center. Crisp the bacon in its own pan, not on the grill pan. I learned that one the hard way: bacon fat on hot ridges flares fast.

Why this recipe tastes like the original

It gets close because it keeps the 5 things that define the burger: a quarter-pound patty, a real char, crisp bacon over melted American cheese, the cool mayo-lettuce-tomato-onion layer, and a soft, slightly sweet bun. Change one and you have a different burger.

  • The weight. At 4 oz, the beef-to-bun ratio matches the official quarter pound.
  • The char. Flame or grill ridges, not a flat griddle, give the smoky crust the menu promises.
  • The bacon. Crisp and thin, so it adds salt and crunch without hiding the beef.
  • The fresh layer. Mayo, lettuce, tomato and onion, with no ketchup or mustard. That’s what separates it from the Big Cheeseburger’s ketchup, mustard and pickles.
  • The bun. A brioche-style bun adds a little sweetness that balances the bacon.

My tip: slice the onion as thin as you can. Thick rings turn this into an onion burger. The onion is also where I’m least sure. Hardee’s just says “sliced onions”, so red or white is your call. I like white, because it’s milder next to the bacon.

Hardee’s Double Bacon Cheeseburger at home

To make a Double Bacon Cheeseburger, grill 2 4 oz patties per burger and keep the same toppings. Hardee’s lists its Double Bacon Cheeseburger with 2 quarter-pound 100% Angus patties, mayonnaise, bacon, melted American cheese, lettuce, tomato, sliced onions and a toasted brioche-style bun, at 910 calories (Hardee’s, checked November 14, 2026).

For 2 doubles, buy 1 lb (454 g) of beef. I put a slice of cheese on each patty so the 2 layers stick together. Like the single, that’s my guess, not an official spec.

Is this the old 1/3 lb Bacon Cheese Thickburger?

Probably not the same burger, though it may be its successor. The old 1/3 lb Bacon Cheese Thickburger used a bigger Black Angus patty. No primary source I found dates its replacement or confirms that today’s quarter-pound Bacon Cheeseburger took its place. So I call it, at most, a likely ancestor.

Here are the dates I could confirm. Hardee’s launched its Thickburgers in 2003 and the Monster Thickburger on November 15, 2004 (Wikipedia; NBC News). On September 29, 2008, it added the 1/4 lb Little Thickburger: charbroiled Black Angus on a sesame bun with red onion, lettuce, tomato, pickles, cheese and condiments (Foodbeast).

Don’t confuse it with the Bacon 3-Way Thickburger either. Carl’s Jr. and Hardee’s launched that one in July 2016 with 1/3 lb of Black Angus, applewood-smoked bacon, bacon bits and bacon jam on a brioche-style bun (Fortune).

Hardee’s and Carl’s Jr.: 1 owner, 2 menus

Hardee’s has belonged to CKE Restaurants, the owner of Carl’s Jr., since 1997, but each chain keeps its own brand and menu (Wikipedia). So you won’t find this exact burger at Carl’s Jr. The sister chain’s bacon classic is the Western Bacon, and I rebuilt it in my Carl’s Jr. Western Bacon Cheeseburger copycat.

Hardee’s had 1,492 US restaurants in 2025, mostly in the South and Midwest (QSR 50, via Yahoo Finance). For figures, history and customer reviews, see our Hardee’s brand profile.

Hardee’s Bacon Cheeseburger FAQ

What are the ingredients in a Hardee’s Bacon Cheeseburger?

A quarter-pound 100% Angus beef patty, mayonnaise, bacon, melted American cheese, lettuce, tomato and sliced onions on a toasted brioche-style bun. That is the official US description on hardees.com, checked November 14, 2026.

How many calories are in a Hardee’s Bacon Cheeseburger: 640 or 740?

640 calories, according to Hardee’s official US menu page. The 740 calories shown on some nutrition sites has no date and doesn’t come from Hardee’s. The Double Bacon Cheeseburger has 910 calories.

How much beef is in a Hardee’s Bacon Cheeseburger?

A quarter pound, or about 4 oz (113 g) of raw beef, per Hardee’s menu. The Double has 2 quarter-pound patties.

How many strips of bacon should I use?

2 thin-cut strips per burger. That’s my estimate, not an official spec: Hardee’s doesn’t publish the number of strips, their weight or the bacon’s smoke.

What’s the difference between the Bacon Cheeseburger, the Big Cheeseburger and the Monster Burger?

The Bacon Cheeseburger (640 calories) adds bacon, mayo, lettuce, tomato and onion to a quarter-pound Angus patty. The Big Cheeseburger (520 calories) keeps it simple with ketchup, mustard and pickles. The Monster Burger is the heaviest in the range at 1,010 calories.

Is the Bacon Cheeseburger the old 1/3 lb Bacon Cheese Thickburger?

No primary source confirms it. The old Thickburger used a 1/3 lb Black Angus patty, and today’s burger uses a quarter pound of Angus. The Thickburger may be its ancestor, but I couldn’t find a date for the switch.

How do I get a flame-grilled taste without a barbecue?

Use a ridged cast-iron grill pan heated until it smokes, plus 80/20 beef. The ridges mark the meat and the fat burns on them, which gives you the smoky edge of a charbroiled burger.

Are Hardee’s and Carl’s Jr. the same chain?

They share an owner, CKE Restaurants, which has owned Hardee’s since 1997. But they are separate brands with separate menus, so the Bacon Cheeseburger isn’t a Carl’s Jr. item.

Where can I eat a Hardee’s Bacon Cheeseburger, and how much does it cost?

At Hardee’s restaurants in the US, mostly in the South and Midwest. Hardee’s doesn’t post a national price, so check the Hardee’s app or your local restaurant. In the Middle East, where Americana runs Hardee’s as a franchise with its own menu, I couldn’t confirm that this burger is sold.
