Bacon & Cheese Whataburger Copycat Recipe

Bacon & Cheese Whataburger copycat for 2: a thin 4 oz patty pressed to 5 inches, American cheese, 3 strips of crisp bacon and mustard. Ready in 35 min.

By Julia, Recipes writerPublished November 23, 2026

This Bacon & Cheese Whataburger copycat makes 2 burgers in about 35 minutes. Crisp 6 strips of smoked bacon first. Then griddle 2 4 oz (113 g) patties of 80/20 beef, pressed to 5 inches, in the bacon fat until they reach 160°F (71°C), and melt a slice of American cheese on each. Build on a toasted 5-inch bun with yellow mustard, dill pickles, diced onion, tomato and lettuce. No ketchup. No mayo. This is my home reproduction, not Whataburger’s recipe: the chain has never published one.

What’s on a Bacon & Cheese Whataburger?

A Bacon & Cheese Whataburger is a 5-inch bun, a 5-inch beef patty, American cheese, 3 slices of bacon, tomato, lettuce, pickles, diced onions and mustard. That build comes from the menu of a Whataburger in Austin, Texas, where it’s listed as the No. 5 (Allmenus, Austin menu, third-party, checked November 14, 2026). I couldn’t open the chain’s own product page, so read that list as the Austin menu’s, not an official 2026 spec. The menu number can change from 1 restaurant to the next, too.

Take away the cheese and bacon and you’re back to the classic Whataburger: same bun, same patty, same tomato, lettuce, pickles, diced onions and mustard (same Allmenus listing). So this is my Whataburger with Cheese copycat plus 3 strips of bacon. I kept every base quantity identical, so 1 shopping trip covers both.

Chowhound ranked it 6th of 8 Whataburger burgers on September 29, 2025. The reviewer found the bacon came in big shards rather than neat strips, with a strong smoky flavor, and credited the cheese with adding welcome moisture to a thin, well-done patty with little char (Chowhound). That’s what I cooked toward: smoke, mustard and soft cheese, not a thick, charred steakhouse burger. And I think the bacon earns its place. On a patty this thin, it’s the only thing with real crunch.

Bacon & Cheese Whataburger vs. Sweet & Spicy Bacon Burger

The Bacon & Cheese Whataburger has 1 patty, American cheese and mustard as its only sauce. The Sweet & Spicy Bacon Burger has 2 patties, grilled onions, Monterey Jack plus American cheese, mustard and a sweet-spicy pepper sauce. Online copycats mix the two up all the time (Front Range Fed). If you want the saucy one, I wrote a separate Whataburger Sweet and Spicy Bacon Burger copycat.

Ingredients for 2 homemade Bacon & Cheese Whataburgers

For 2 burgers you need 8 oz (226 g) of 80/20 ground beef, 6 strips of smoked bacon, 2 slices of American cheese, 2 large 5-inch buns, yellow mustard and the 4 classic toppings. The beef ratio, the patty weight and the bacon style are my calls; Whataburger confirms none of them.

  • 8 oz (226 g) ground beef, 80/20, split into 2 portions of 4 oz (113 g)
  • 6 strips smoked bacon (3 per burger)
  • 2 large burger buns, about 5 in (13 cm) wide
  • 1 tbsp (15 g) unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 slices American cheese
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 8 dill pickle chips (4 per burger)
  • 1/4 small white onion, finely diced (about 3 tbsp)
  • 2 large leaves iceberg lettuce, torn to fit the bun
  • 4 to 6 thin tomato slices (2 or 3 per burger)
  • Kosher salt, to season on the griddle

Which bacon for a Whataburger-style burger?

Use regular smoked American bacon and cook it until crisp; the restaurant’s exact bacon, thickness and brand aren’t published. Whataburger does sell its own hickory-smoked bacon in Texas grocery stores (H-E-B and Central Market). It launched on May 29, 2018, in 1 lb packs of 16 to 18 slices (Whataburger Stories). If you shop at H-E-B, I’d buy it for this burger. Just know the chain never says it’s the bacon its restaurants serve, so I won’t claim it is.

Which cheese and which bun?

Use American cheese, 1 full-size slice per patty, and a soft plain bun about 5 inches (13 cm) wide. Nobody confirms the slice size on this burger, so I use a full deli slice: anything smaller leaves a bare ring of beef around a 5-inch patty. Most supermarket buns are narrower than 5 inches. My colleague Clara’s homemade burger buns recipe works if you shape the dough into wide, flat rounds. For the beef, her guide to the best ground beef for burgers explains why 80/20 suits a thin griddled patty.

How to make a Bacon & Cheese Whataburger at home, step by step

A homemade Bacon & Cheese Whataburger takes 7 steps: cook the bacon, prep the toppings, press thin 5-inch patties, toast the buns, griddle the patties in the bacon fat, melt the cheese and build. Start with the bacon. It takes longest, and its fat seasons the patties.

1. Cook the bacon

Lay the 6 strips of bacon in a cold skillet and cook over medium heat for about 8 minutes, turning once, until crisp. Drain on paper towels. Pour off most of the fat but leave a thin film in the pan for the patties. Starting cold renders the fat slowly, so the strips crisp evenly instead of burning at the edges.

2. Prep the toppings

Dice the onion finely, slice the tomato thin and tear the lettuce to fit the bun. Count out 4 pickle chips per burger. Keep everything cold until the build: cold, crunchy toppings against hot beef and bacon are half the point.

3. Press thin 5-inch patties

Shape each 4 oz (113 g) portion into a loose ball, then press it between 2 sheets of parchment into a round about 5 inches (13 cm) wide. It will look far too thin. It isn’t: the patty shrinks as it cooks, and that wide, flat shape is the whole Whataburger look. Chill the patties while you toast the buns.

4. Toast the buns

Butter the cut sides of the buns. Toast them cut-side down on a griddle or cast-iron skillet over medium heat for 60 to 90 seconds, until golden. Set them aside cut-side up.

5. Griddle the patties

Heat the bacon skillet over medium-high heat. Lay the patties in, salt the top and cook 3 to 4 minutes per side, until the center reaches 160°F (71°C). Salt the second side after you flip. Don’t chase a heavy crust: the original is well done with little char. My colleague Clara’s burger patty internal temp guide explains why 160°F is the safe target for ground beef.

6. Melt the cheese

Lay 1 slice of American cheese on each patty for the last minute of cooking on the second side. If the edges haven’t softened, cover the pan for the final 30 seconds. Melted American cheese is what holds the bacon on the patty.

7. Build the burger

Spread 1 tablespoon of mustard on each bottom bun. Add the patty with its cheese, then 3 strips of bacon, the pickles, diced onion, tomato and lettuce, and close the bun. Whataburger doesn’t publish its build order, so this one is mine: bacon straight on the cheese so it sticks, wet toppings on top so the bacon stays crisp. Serve right away.

Recipe times and temperatures

The full recipe takes about 35 minutes for 2 burgers: about 15 minutes of prep and 18 minutes of cooking. These times come from my home method, not from Whataburger’s kitchens.

Stage Time Heat or target
Cook bacon from a cold pan About 8 min Medium, until crisp
Prep toppings and press patties 15 min —
Toast buttered buns 60–90 s Medium
Griddle patties in bacon fat 3–4 min per side Medium-high, center at 160°F (71°C)
Melt cheese 30–60 s Lid on, optional
Total About 35 min —

Why this recipe tastes like the original Bacon & Cheese Whataburger

This copycat gets close because it copies what defines the burger: a wide, thin, well-done patty on a 5-inch bun, mustard instead of ketchup and mayo, smoky crisp bacon, soft American cheese and cold toppings. The table separates what is reported from what I chose myself.

Element Bacon & Cheese Whataburger (reported) My homemade version
Bun 5” bun (Austin menu, third-party) Soft 5-inch (13 cm) bun, buttered and toasted 60–90 s
Patty 5” patty, 100% fresh, never-frozen American beef (official) 4 oz (113 g) 80/20 beef pressed to 5 in
Weight Quarter pound in 1950; current weight not published 4 oz (113 g)
Bacon 3 slices; type and brand not published 3 strips smoked bacon, cooked crisp
Cheese American cheese; slice size not confirmed 1 full-size slice
Sauce Mustard only 1 tbsp yellow mustard
Toppings Tomato, lettuce, pickles, diced onions Same, 4 pickles and 2–3 tomato slices
Build order Not published Mustard, patty and cheese, bacon, pickles, onion, tomato, lettuce

The beef. Whataburger says its patties use “100% fresh, never-frozen American beef” cooked when you order, and that its cheese is made with milk from American dairy farms (Whataburger Stories, Aug. 23, 2023). At home, that means fresh ground beef, never frozen patties.

The size. My 4 oz patty comes from history, not a current spec. The first Whataburger, sold for 25 cents on August 8, 1950, in Corpus Christi, Texas, by Harmon Dobson and Paul Burton, was a quarter-pound patty on a large bun with lettuce, 3 slices of tomato, 4 dill pickles, chopped onions, ketchup and mustard (Wikipedia). Ketchup has since left the standard build. No current official source gives the patty weight.

The bacon fat. Griddling the patties in the bacon fat is my addition, and the one I’d defend hardest. It pushes the smoke into the beef, which fits Chowhound’s point that smokiness drives this burger.

Bacon & Cheese Whataburger FAQ

What comes on a Bacon & Cheese Whataburger? A 5-inch bun, a 5-inch beef patty, American cheese, 3 slices of bacon, tomato, lettuce, pickles, diced onions and mustard, according to the menu of a Whataburger in Austin, Texas (Allmenus, third-party).

Does the Bacon & Cheese Whataburger have ketchup or mayo? No. It comes with mustard only. The 1950 original had ketchup and mustard, but today’s build lists mustard alone. You can ask for ketchup or mayo when you order.

What’s the difference between the Bacon & Cheese Whataburger and the Sweet & Spicy Bacon Burger? The Bacon & Cheese Whataburger has 1 patty, American cheese and mustard. The Sweet & Spicy Bacon Burger has 2 patties, grilled onions, Monterey Jack and American cheese, mustard and a sweet-spicy pepper sauce. See my Sweet and Spicy Bacon Burger copycat.

How many calories are in a Bacon & Cheese Whataburger? The No. 5 Bacon & Cheese Whataburger has 750 calories for 354 g, and the Jr. version has 400 calories, according to FatSecret, which republishes the chain’s data.

How much does a Bacon & Cheese Whataburger cost? On an Austin, Texas menu checked on November 14, 2026, it was $5.54 on its own and $9.24 as a Whatameal (Allmenus, third-party, update date unknown). Prices vary by location.

What bacon does Whataburger use? The chain doesn’t publish the bacon it serves in restaurants. It has sold its own hickory-smoked bacon at H-E-B and Central Market in Texas since 2018, but doesn’t say it’s the same product. Any crisp smoked American bacon works for this copycat.

What temperature should a burger patty reach? Cook ground beef patties to 160°F (71°C) in the center. A 5-inch, 4 oz patty gets there in 3 to 4 minutes per side over medium-high heat.

Where can I eat a real Whataburger? Whataburger has about 1,200 restaurants, all in the United States, according to an October 20, 2026 company press release, and its headquarters are in San Antonio, Texas (Wikipedia).
