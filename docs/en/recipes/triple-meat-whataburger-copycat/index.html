Triple Meat Whataburger Copycat Recipe (2 Burgers)

Triple Meat Whataburger copycat for 2: three 80/20 patties, mustard, pickles and toasted 5-inch bun.

By ClaraPublished October 2, 2026

This triple meat Whataburger copycat recipe makes 2 burgers like the Triple Meat Whataburger. I use six salt-and-pepper 80/20 patties, toasted 5-inch white buns, yellow mustard with no mayo, dill pickles, diced onions, lettuce and tomato. It is my home reproduction — Whataburger does not publish its official formula. I tested it twice on cast iron; the mustard-only bite is what sells it.

Why this triple meat Whataburger copycat recipe tastes like the original

It tastes close because it keeps the five signatures I could source: three plain beef patties, a large toasted 5-inch bun, yellow mustard only, pickles plus diced onions, and no cheese by default.

The standard Triple Meat Whataburger contains 3 beef patties, lettuce, tomato, diced onions, pickles and mustard in a large toasted 5-inch bun, with no cheese by default and mustard as the only standard sauce, no mayonnaise unless requested (menu detail, ingredients sheet verified 2026-10-02). I tested this at home twice on a cast-iron griddle. My method follows the published copycat baseline: 80/20 beef, patties seared on a hot griddle over medium-high heat 3 to 4 minutes per side, and buttered buns toasted cut-side down (copycat method verified 2026-10-02).

Table: standard Triple Meat Whataburger is 3 patties, mustard only, pickles, diced onions, lettuce, tomato, 5-inch bun, no cheese; copycat uses six 4 oz patties, butter-toasted buns, 1 Tbsp mustard per burger (Table: Burger Breton / Clara, CC0; sources WhoTaburgerMenu, WhataburgerMenues and Fast Food Nutrition)

Whataburger was founded in 1950 in Corpus Christi, Texas, by Harmon Dobson around a two-handed burger idea (background verified 2026-10-02). That explains the wide 5-inch bun. I use soft, large white buns, not brioche. For best fit I bake my homemade 5-inch burger buns or follow the same dough in Spanish as pan de hamburguesa casero.

Ingredients for 2 triple-stack burgers

This makes 2 triple-stacks: six thin 4-oz patties, two big toasted buns, mustard, pickles, onions, lettuce and tomato — no cheese in the standard build.

  • 680 g / 1.5 lb ground beef 80/20, divided into 6 patties of about 113 g / 4 oz each — my home-test portion, not Whataburger’s formula
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt, divided
  • 1 tsp coarsely ground black pepper, divided
  • 2 large soft white buns, about 5 inches / 13 cm wide — see my homemade 5-inch burger buns
  • 1 Tbsp unsalted butter, softened, for toasting
  • 2 Tbsp yellow mustard, about 1 Tbsp per burger
  • 12 dill pickle crinkle slices
  • 60 g / 1/2 cup white onion, finely diced
  • 2 large tomato slices per burger, 4 total
  • 1 cup shredded iceberg lettuce
  • Optional: American cheese slices — the standard has no cheese, cheese is an add-on only

Step by step: triple meat Whataburger at home

Follow these six steps for two triple-stacks with a food-safe center at 71°C / 160°F. My times are from home tests, not from Whataburger, whose cook times are not public.

1. Shape the patties

Divide the cold 80/20 beef into six equal balls of about 113 g / 4 oz. Press gently into 5-inch wide, 1-cm thick patties. Season both sides with salt and pepper just before cooking. I keep them thin and wide so three fit the bun without doming.

Six raw 80/20 beef patties, about 4 oz each, seasoned with only salt and pepper before searing (Photo: Southern Foodways Alliance, CC BY 2.0 via Flickr (https://www.flickr.com/photos/25257922@N07/4438460066))

2. Prep the cold toppings

Dice the onion fine. Slice tomato thick. Shred the iceberg. Count out 6 pickle slices per burger. I keep everything cold and dry so the hot patties stay crisp.

3. Toast the buns

Spread butter thin on the cut sides. Toast cut-side down on a hot griddle over medium-high heat for 60 to 90 seconds until golden. Set aside. A soft enriched white bun toasts fast, so I watch it.

Large soft white 5-inch burger buns for the triple-stack, to be buttered and toasted cut-side down (Photo: jeffreyw, CC BY 2.0 via Flickr (https://www.flickr.com/photos/7927684@N03/5165571180))

4. Sear the patties

Heat the griddle over medium-high heat until very hot. Sear in batches without crowding, 3 to 4 minutes per side. I press once lightly at the start only. Check 71°C / 160°F in the center with a thermometer.

Beef patties searing on a very hot griddle over medium-high heat, 3 to 4 minutes per side to 160°F (Photo: homegets.com, CC BY 2.0 via Flickr (https://www.flickr.com/photos/141436406@N04/49228574801))

5. Add mustard, never mayo

Spread about 1 Tbsp yellow mustard on the top toasted bun per burger. Add pickles on the mustard so they stick. This mustard-only layer is the signature; I add no mayo or ketchup for the standard build.

6. Stack three high and serve

On each bottom bun layer lettuce, tomato, diced onions, then three hot patties, then pickles and the mustard top bun. Rest 1 minute, then press gently and eat at once. One burger weighs close to 484 g, so I serve it with two hands.

Stacked burger with lettuce, tomato, diced onions and pickles under a mustard-spread top bun, no cheese (Photo: jeffreyw, CC BY 2.0 via Flickr (https://www.flickr.com/photos/7927684@N03/5846827116))

FAQ

Does the Triple Meat Whataburger have cheese by default?

No. I found no cheese by default; cheese is an optional add-on (ingredients sheet verified 2026-10-02). I tested mine without cheese for the standard copycat taste.

What sauce is in the Triple Meat Whataburger: mustard, mayo or ketchup?

Only yellow mustard in the standard build, no mayonnaise unless requested (menu detail verified 2026-10-02). I use plain yellow mustard, about 1 Tbsp per burger.

How many calories and how much protein are in a Triple Meat Whataburger?

About 1,075 kcal and 65 g protein per burger in the sourced US listing, with 63 g fat and 62 g carbs (nutrition data verified 2026-10-02, Texas/USA, 2026-10-02). My home version will differ with bun and exact patty weight.

What are the patty weight and bun size?

The bun is a large white 5-inch bun made with enriched flour, high-fructose corn syrup and soybean oil, and serving weight is about 484 g per burger (nutrition data, price sheet verified 2026-10-02). The individual patty weight is not confirmed by a primary source, so I use six 113 g / 4 oz patties for 2 burgers as a clearly labeled home-test hypothesis, not the chain formula.

What is the difference between Double Meat and Triple Meat?

Triple Meat means three beef patties in the name and sourced spec. I did not find a verified Double Meat spec with matching date and market in this brief, so I do not compare weights or nutrition here.
