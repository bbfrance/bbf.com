Carl's Jr. Super Star with Cheese Copycat Recipe

Make a Carl's Jr. Super Star with Cheese at home: 2 grilled patties, 2 slices of American cheese and a 3:1 ketchup-relish sauce. Makes 2 burgers.

By Alice, Recipes writerPublished November 21, 2026

To make a Carl’s Jr. Super Star copycat at home, grill 2 100–115 g (about 1/4 lb) beef patties per burger on a smoking-hot ridged cast-iron grill pan or a barbecue, melt a slice of American cheese on each, and stack them on a toasted sesame bun with lettuce, tomato, onion, dill pickles, a ketchup-and-sweet-relish special sauce and mayonnaise. 2 burgers take about an hour. Half of that is freezer time. The Super Star is a Famous Star with a second patty and a second slice of cheese, and in the US Carl’s Jr. now sells it as the Double Famous Star. Of the two, it’s the one I’d make. With a single patty, 2 sauces and a full set of toppings tend to bury the beef. The second patty puts the meat back in charge.

This is a homemade copy, not the official recipe. Carl’s Jr. publishes what goes on the Super Star with Cheese. It does not publish the patty weight, the build order or the Special Sauce formula. I worked from the official Carl’s Jr. Mexico description of the Super Star® with Cheese, checked on carlsjr.com.mx on November 15, 2026, and treated the US Double Famous Star as its equivalent. Hardee’s and Spain get their own notes below.

What’s in a Carl’s Jr. Super Star with Cheese?

A Super Star with Cheese is 2 grilled 100% beef patties with special sauce, American cheese, mayonnaise, lettuce, onion, tomato and pickles. Carl’s Jr. Mexico describes it as “Dos piezas de carne 100% de res asada a la parrilla, salsa especial, queso americano, mayonesa, lechuga, cebolla, tomate y pepinillos”. It lists the burger at 390 g and 920 kcal (Carl’s Jr. México).

A Mexican Rappi listing adds 2 details that the official page leaves out: 2 slices of American cheese and a sesame bun (“pan con ajonjolí”) (Rappi). The same listing has no onion. When the chain’s own page and a delivery app disagree, I trust the chain, so the onion stays.

How much does a Super Star with Cheese weigh?

In Mexico a Super Star with Cheese weighs 390 g. The one-patty Famous Star with Cheese weighs 284 g (Carl’s Jr. México). Both figures are for the whole burger, not the raw beef. The 106 g gap looks like 1 cooked patty plus 1 slice of cheese. That is my own deduction, though, not a published figure.

How much does each Super Star patty weigh?

Carl’s Jr. does not publish the weight of its patties. Hardee’s says its own Super Star uses “2 Quarter Pound 100% Angus beef patties”, which is about 113 g raw each (Hardee’s). I use that as my estimate for the Carl’s Jr. version: 100–115 g of raw beef per patty, the same as in my Famous Star copycat. Treat it as a copycat choice, not an official number.

Super Star vs Famous Star vs Double Famous Star

The Super Star is a Famous Star with 2 patties instead of one. In the US it is now called the Double Famous Star. The toppings, sauce and bun are the same. What changes is the second patty and, on Mexican listings, a second slice of cheese.

The US Carl’s Jr. menu no longer has anything called Super Star. Its Charbroiled Burgers section lists the Single Famous Star (670 cal), the Double Famous Star (920 cal) and the Triple Famous Star (1,190 cal) (Carl’s Jr. menu). The Double has “two all-beef patties—cooked on the grill right when you order— topped with melted American cheese, lettuce, tomato, sliced onions, dill pickles, Special Sauce, and mayonnaise on a seeded bun” (Carl’s Jr.). Chowhound sums it up: “the Double Famous Star at Carl’s Jr. is a Super Star at Hardee’s” (Chowhound). I found no reliable date for when the Super Star name left the US menu, so I give none.

Hardee’s Super Star recipe: the ketchup and brioche version

The Hardee’s Super Star is a different burger. It has ketchup instead of Special Sauce, a brioche-style bun and Angus beef. Today it is built from 2 quarter-pound 100% Angus patties, 2 slices of melted American cheese, lettuce, tomato, sliced onion, dill pickles, ketchup and mayonnaise on a toasted brioche-style bun, for 850 calories (Hardee’s). Carl’s Jr. started on the West Coast in 1941 and Hardee’s in the Southeast in 1960. Both have belonged to CKE Restaurants since 1997 (Chowhound).

To turn this recipe into a Hardee’s Super Star, make 3 changes. I’d only do it to compare the two. Without the relish, the Hardee’s version loses the sweet-sour note that sets a Star apart from every other fast-food double.

  • Use plain ketchup instead of the ketchup-relish special sauce.
  • Use brioche buns instead of sesame buns.
  • Use ground Angus beef, 113 g (1/4 lb) per patty.

It wasn’t always that way. On March 25, 2020, Hardee’s launched a limited-time Super Star with Cheese in its Charbroiled Stars menu. It had Special Sauce on a seeded bun, and it came in at 920 calories, 56 g of fat, 59 g of carbs and 48 g of protein (The Fast Food Post). That is the Carl’s Jr. build. The current Hardee’s version is 70 calories lighter. None of my sources explains why, so I won’t guess. The Hardee’s Big Cheeseburger copycat comes from that same 2020 menu. For the chain’s background, see the Hardee’s brand profile.

Carl’s Jr. special sauce: the ketchup-relish copycat

A good copycat of the Carl’s Jr. special sauce is about 3 parts ketchup to 1 part sweet pickle relish. Carl’s Jr. has never published the formula, so this is a copycat ratio, not the real recipe. Top Secret Recipes uses 1.5 tsp ketchup and 0.5 tsp sweet relish per burger, which is 3 to 1 (Top Secret Recipes). Food.com mixes closer to 4 to 1 (Food.com).

I use 3 to 1, the same as in my Famous Star copycat. For 2 Super Stars, stir 3 tbsp ketchup with 1 tbsp sweet pickle relish. Keep the mayonnaise separate, because Carl’s Jr. lists special sauce and mayonnaise as 2 ingredients. Don’t skip this step. With the red sauce on the bottom bun and the mayo on the top, each bite gets cold and creamy on 1 side and sweet and sharp on the other, instead of 1 pink dressing. If I had to drop everything else in this copycat, that contrast is what I’d keep.

Ingredients for 2 Super Star burgers

For 2 Super Star copycats you need 400–460 g of ground beef for 4 patties, 4 slices of American cheese, 2 sesame buns, a quick ketchup-relish sauce and the classic toppings. The weights are my choices, not Carl’s Jr. figures.

  • 400–460 g (14–16 oz) ground beef, split into 4 portions of 100–115 g
  • Fine salt and ground black pepper
  • Optional: 1 pinch each of garlic powder and onion powder (the Food.com seasoning)
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 2 sesame seed hamburger buns
  • 3 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp sweet pickle relish
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 6 dill pickle slices
  • 1 handful shredded iceberg lettuce (about 40 g / 1.5 oz)
  • 4 thin tomato slices
  • 2 thin white onion rings, cut in quarters

Equipment: a ridged cast-iron grill pan or a barbecue, parchment paper, a thin spatula and an instant-read thermometer. My homemade sesame burger buns recipe gives you the seeded bun, and it is sturdy enough to hold 2 patties.

Step by step: the Super Star with Cheese copycat recipe

Mix the sauce and chill the patties first. Then grill all 4 patties hard and fast, melt the cheese during the second side and stack everything while it is hot. Count about 1 hour in total, 30 minutes of it in the freezer.

1. Mix the special sauce

Stir 3 tbsp ketchup and 1 tbsp sweet pickle relish in a small bowl. Keep it in the fridge while you get everything else ready.

2. Shape and chill the patties

Divide the beef into 4 portions of 100–115 g. Press each one between sheets of parchment into a round about 12 cm (4.75 in) wide and 1 cm (3/8 in) thick. That is a little wider than the bun, because the patty shrinks on the grill. Freeze for 30 minutes. A cold, firm patty can stay on a very hot grill long enough to char without overcooking in the middle.

3. Prepare the toppings

Shred the lettuce, slice the tomato thin and cut 2 thin onion rings into quarters. Set out the pickles and the mayonnaise. With 4 patties on the grill, everything happens fast after this point.

4. Toast the buns

Toast the cut sides of the buns on the grill pan over medium heat for about 1 minute, until lightly golden. Set them aside. Do this before you crank up the heat, or the bread will burn.

5. Grill the patties

Heat the grill pan over high heat for at least 5 minutes, or light the barbecue for direct high heat. Season the cold patties with salt, pepper and, if you like, garlic and onion powder. Grill 3–4 minutes without moving them, flip once and grill 2–3 minutes more, until the center reaches 71 °C (160 °F). That is the safe minimum for ground beef (FoodSafety.gov). If all 4 patties don’t fit with a finger’s width between them, use the barbecue. Crowded patties steam instead of char.

6. Melt the cheese on both patties

Lay 1 slice of American cheese on each of the 4 patties right after you flip them. The cheese melts during the 2–3 minutes the second side needs anyway. If the corners stay stiff on a grill pan, cover the patties with a lid or a metal bowl for 20 seconds.

7. Stack and assemble the burger

Spread about 1 tbsp special sauce on the bottom bun. Add 3 pickles, the quartered onion, 1 cheesy patty and then the second cheesy patty. Top with 2 tomato slices and the shredded lettuce, spread 1 tbsp mayonnaise on the top bun and close. This puts 1 slice of cheese between the patties and 1 on top. Carl’s Jr. does not publish its build order, and copycat recipes don’t agree on one. I use the same build as my Famous Star copycat, with the wet sauce at the bottom and the mayo on top.

How to melt 2 slices of cheese on 2 patties without overcooking

Put the cheese on right after the flip, never after the patty is done. That way it melts during cooking time the beef needs anyway. Add it at the end and you end up leaving the patty on the heat until the cheese gives in, which dries out a thin patty. Stack the two patties straight from the grill. The heat of the top patty finishes melting the slice underneath it. Where the second slice goes is not published. I put it between the patties because it glues the stack together and keeps the top patty from sliding off when you bite.

How to get the charbroiled taste without a barbecue

For a charbroiled taste indoors, use a ridged cast-iron grill pan heated on high for at least 5 minutes, start with frozen-firm patties and leave them alone for 3–4 minutes on the first side. Grilling is the point of this burger. Carl’s Jr. Mexico says the beef is “asada a la parrilla”, and the US page says it is “cooked on the grill”. A flat skillet gives you a griddle burger. That can be a fine burger, but it is browned all over instead of striped and smoky, and it is no longer a Star. On a barbecue, Top Secret Recipes grills a quarter-pound patty for 2–3 minutes per side over high heat. Check for 71 °C (160 °F) in the center either way. My Famous Star copycat has the full grill-pan method.

Why this Super Star copycat stays close to the original

This copycat keeps the 5 things that define the Super Star with Cheese and labels every part that is a guess.

  • 2 grilled patties. The official description says “dos piezas de carne” cooked on the grill, so the second patty and the grill pan are both essential.
  • 2 sauces. Special sauce and mayonnaise are listed separately: 1 sweet and tangy, 1 creamy.
  • 2 slices of American cheese. This follows the Mexican listing and the Hardee’s builds. American cheese melts smoothly in a way cheddar doesn’t.
  • The full official topping list. Lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles. Nothing added and nothing missing.
  • A realistic patty weight. 100–115 g matches the quarter-pound figure Hardee’s gives for its Super Star, without claiming it is official for Carl’s Jr.

Super Star with Cheese calories and nutrition

A Carl’s Jr. Super Star with Cheese has 920 kcal in Mexico, with 48 g of protein, 56 g of fat, 59 g of carbohydrates and 1,540 mg of sodium for 390 g (Carl’s Jr. México). The US Double Famous Star also has 920 calories. The current Hardee’s Super Star has 850. These figures are for the chains’ burgers only. I give no nutrition for the homemade version, because it depends too much on your beef, bun and cheese.

Where to eat a real Super Star: Mexico, the US and Spain

The name Super Star with Cheese is still used at Carl’s Jr. in Mexico. You will also find a Super Star Burger at Carl’s Jr. in Spain and a Super Star at Hardee’s in the US. In the US, the Carl’s Jr. version is now the Double Famous Star.

  • Mexico. On Rappi, 1 Carl’s Jr. restaurant listed the Super Star con Queso at 166.00 MXN on its own and the Famous Star con Queso at 132.00 MXN. Other listings showed 169 MXN (checked November 15, 2026). These are delivery prices for 1 restaurant, and there is no official national price. Our city guides quote other figures: about 138 MXN in Carl’s Jr. in Cancún (mxmenu.org, unofficial, October 2026) and 179 MXN in Carl’s Jr. in Puebla. Treat every price as a rough guide. Addresses are in our guides to Carl’s Jr. in Mexico City and Carl’s Jr. in Mérida.
  • United States. Order the Double Famous Star at Carl’s Jr. The product page lists no price. At Hardee’s, the Super Star has ketchup and a brioche-style bun instead.
  • Spain. At Carl’s Jr. Barcelona on Glovo, the Super Star Burger is a double beef burger with cheddar, tomato, pickles, lettuce, onion, mayonnaise and special sauce. It costs €9.95 on its own and €15.45 as a meal with fries and a drink. The Famous Star Burger costs €8.95 and €12.45 (Glovo, checked November 15, 2026). Prices in the restaurant may be different.
  • France. I could not verify whether the current Carl’s Jr. menu in France has a Super Star.

Super Star FAQ

What’s in a Carl’s Jr. Super Star with Cheese? 2 grilled 100% beef patties, special sauce, American cheese, mayonnaise, lettuce, onion, tomato and pickles, according to the official Carl’s Jr. Mexico page checked on November 15, 2026.

What’s the difference between the Super Star and the Famous Star? The Super Star has 2 patties and, on Mexican listings, 2 slices of cheese. The Famous Star has one of each. The toppings, sauce and bun are the same. In Mexico the Super Star weighs 390 g and has 920 kcal, against 284 g and 670 kcal for the Famous Star.

Is the Super Star still on the Carl’s Jr. menu in the US? Not under that name. The US menu calls it the Double Famous Star: 2 patties, the full Famous Star toppings, 920 calories.

Is the Hardee’s Super Star the same as the Carl’s Jr. one? No. Hardee’s uses ketchup instead of Special Sauce, a brioche-style bun and 2 quarter-pound Angus patties, for 850 calories. The limited-time Hardee’s Super Star with Cheese from 2020 matched the Carl’s Jr. build.

How much does a Super Star with Cheese weigh, and how many calories does it have? In Mexico it weighs 390 g and has 920 kcal and 48 g of protein. The 390 g is the whole burger, not the raw beef.

What’s in Carl’s Jr. special sauce? Carl’s Jr. has never published it. Copycat recipes use ketchup and sweet pickle relish, mixed 3 to 1 or 4 to 1. I use 3 to 1.

How much does each Super Star patty weigh? Carl’s Jr. does not say. Hardee’s uses 2 quarter-pound patties (about 113 g raw each) in its Super Star, so I use 100–115 g per patty as an estimate.

How much does a Super Star cost in Mexico or Spain? As a rough guide, 166–169 MXN on Rappi in Mexico for 1 restaurant, and €9.95 on its own at Carl’s Jr. Barcelona on Glovo (both checked November 15, 2026). Neither chain publishes a national price.

Can I get a Super Star in France? I could not confirm that the current Carl’s Jr. menu in France has one.

Is this the official Carl’s Jr. recipe? No. It is a homemade copy. The ingredient list is official. The patty weight, sauce ratio, cooking times, cheese placement and build order are my choices or come from Top Secret Recipes and Food.com.
