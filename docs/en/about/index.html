About Burger Breton: who we are and why the name

Burger Breton is run by big burger lovers who share their passion. Meet the team and find out why the name: Brittany is everywhere around the world.

Updated October 1, 2026

Burger Breton is a burger magazine made by big burger lovers who share their passion, in English, French and Spanish.

Who we are

We are a small team of burger fans. We cook burgers at home, hunt them down wherever we travel and probably talk about them a little too much over dinner. Burger Breton started from a simple idea: share all of it with people who love burgers as much as we do.

The classic cheeseburger, where it all starts (Photo: jeffreyw, CC BY 2.0, via Flickr)

On the site, we publish three things:

  • Burger news: openings, trends and the people behind the grill.
  • Burger recipes tested in our own kitchen, with weights to the gram.
  • Restaurant guides city by city, with verified addresses and dated prices.

Why “Burger Breton”?

The name Burger Breton has two explanations: the best burger is Breton, and Brittany shows up all over the world.

Because the best burger is Breton. It is a scientific fact, established by us with complete objectivity. A touch of salted butter, a buckwheat galette instead of a bun… Fine, we may not be entirely impartial.

The Gwenn-ha-Du stuck in the bun: our ideal burger (Illustration: Burger Breton)

Because Brittany is everywhere. Brittany is the Celtic region at the western tip of France, and its black-and-white flag, the Gwenn-ha-Du designed in 1923, turns up at festivals and stadiums around the world. Breton crêperies are open from Tokyo to New York. And the fest-noz, the Breton dance gathering, has been on the UNESCO list of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity since 2012. Like Brittany, the burger has traveled the whole world, and we go looking for it wherever it is.

Our promise

Every recipe is tested before it goes live, every address is checked, and every article cites its sources and shows when it was published and updated. Burger Breton is published by BBF (Burger Breton France): see our legal notice for the details.
