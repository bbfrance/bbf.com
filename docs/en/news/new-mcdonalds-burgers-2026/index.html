New McDonald's Burgers 2026: Verified List by Market

No named 2026 burger is confirmed yet — here are the verified McDonald's menu moves in the US, Canada, UK and France as of October 2, 2026.

By Maria, News editorPublished October 4, 2026

Looking for new McDonald’s burgers 2026 in your country? I checked on October 2, 2026 — and I could not verify a single named new burger launched in 2026 in the US, UK, Canada or France against two sources including one official. My take: 2026 is a value-menu year, not a new-build year. The only sourced moves are McValue in the US and Canada, McDeal in France, and a pricing update in the UK.

No permanent, limited-edition, local-only or test burger for 2026 meets our verification bar yet. That is the short answer.

McDonald’s New Menu 2026 Burgers: What Changed in the US and Europe

The 2026 changes are about price architecture, not new builds.

  • In the US, McValue added 10+ items under $3 from April 21, 2026, plus $4 breakfast meals and $5/$6 Meal Deals in participating restaurants, delivery excluded.
  • In Canada, McDonald’s promised CA$5 McValue meals and a CA$1 small coffee before tax for one year in about 1,500 restaurants, delivery excluded.
  • In France, McDeal at €5 replaced McSmart on May 5, 2026 in 1,629 restaurants, with a double beef, chicken or fish burger plus sides and dessert or hot drink, guaranteed to Dec 31, 2026.
  • In France, the Best Of line moved to a €7.50 maximum recommended price, down from about €9, guaranteed to Dec 31, 2026.
  • In the UK and Ireland, the CEO announced a small increase on April 22, 2026 while Saver Menu and Meal Deal prices stayed flat.

Permanent, Limited-Edition, Local or Test: How I Classify It

I call a burger permanent only when the local McDonald’s site lists it with no end date.

I checked corporate.mcdonalds.com and national McDonald’s sites on October 2, 2026, plus our sourced value-war file. FAQ: New McDonald’s Burgers 2026

What new burgers did McDonald’s launch in the US in 2026?

None is verified to my standard as of October 2, 2026. The only sourced US move is the McValue expansion from April 21, 2026.

Which limited-edition McDonald’s burger is available now, and until when?

No 2026 limited-edition burger is verified with an end date. In France, the verified time-bound offers are McDeal at €5 and Best Of at €7.50 max, both guaranteed to December 31, 2026.

Is the 2026 McDonald’s new burger permanent or temporary?

It cannot be classified without a name, market and official listing. Permanent means no end date on the market site. Temporary means a stated end date. Local and test mean a limited restaurant list.

What is the price and build of the 2026 McDonald’s new burger?

No verified build exists for 2026. The only verified 2026 builds are France’s McDeal double (beef, chicken or fish) with small fries, small drink and dessert or hot drink, and the Best Of trio of McChicken, Filet-O-Fish and Double Cheese.
