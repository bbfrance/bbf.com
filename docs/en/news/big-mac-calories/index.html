Big Mac Calories: 580 in the US, 557 in Japan

A US Big Mac has 580 calories and 1,060 mg of sodium. Full nutrition facts, the meal total, the Whopper gap and why Australia's has 7 g less fat.

By Maria, News editorPublished November 24, 2026

A Big Mac has 580 calories in the United States. That’s for a 220 g (7.8 oz) burger with 34 g of fat, 45 g of carbs, 25 g of protein and 1,060 mg of sodium. Abroad the count shifts a little: 559 calories in Australia, 557 in Japan. I checked every figure here on November 12, 2026, and each one sits next to its source.

How many calories are in a Big Mac?

A US Big Mac contains 580 calories, 29% of the 2,000-calorie daily reference printed on US nutrition labels. The figure comes from Wikipedia’s Big Mac entry and matches what McDonald’s US product page shows in search results. You’ll still find 540, 550, 563 or 590 on other sites. Those come from older menus, or from aggregators that never say where or when they got the number.

Big Mac nutrition facts (US)

The US Big Mac weighs 220 g and provides 580 calories, 34 g of fat (11 g saturated), 45 g of carbohydrates, 25 g of protein and 1,060 mg of sodium, or about 264 calories per 100 g. The table sets each figure against the FDA’s Daily Values.

What’s inside a Big Mac

A Big Mac is 2 1.6 oz (45 g) beef patties, Big Mac sauce, shredded iceberg lettuce, a slice of American cheese, pickles and minced onions on a 3-part sesame seed bun. The middle bun is the signature. It’s also bread a regular double cheeseburger doesn’t carry, and its calories land on your tray all the same.

Big Mac protein

A Big Mac has 25 g of protein, most of it from the two beef patties. Respectable for 1 sandwich. But it arrives with 34 g of fat, so nobody should mistake it for a lean protein source.

Big Mac sodium

A US Big Mac has 1,060 mg of sodium, 46% of the FDA’s 2,300 mg Daily Value. That’s the number I’d watch, more than the calories. Nearly half a day’s sodium in one burger, before a single fry.

Big Mac meal calories

A medium Big Mac meal in the US, with medium fries and a medium Coca-Cola, has 1,170 calories according to McDonald’s US meal listing. The fries and the soda more than double the burger. Together they make up about 59% of a 2,000-calorie day.

Big Mac calories by country

The Big Mac doesn’t have the same calorie count everywhere: 580 in the US, 559 in Australia, 557 in Japan. The calories barely move. The fat does: 34 g in the US, 30.5 g in Japan, 26.9 g in Australia.

France, Spain and Mexico aren’t in the table yet. The figures circulating for those markets disagree with each other, and none of them cites McDonald’s own nutrition sheet. I won’t print a number I can’t trace back to the chain. Those rows go in once they’re confirmed.

Why the Big Mac changes from 1 country to another

The Big Mac’s calorie count varies by country because both the recipe and the labeling rules vary. Each market sets its own bun, sauce and cheese weights. And the labels aren’t built the same way. US labels give sodium in milligrams per serving. EU labels must give salt in grams and values per 100 g, under Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011. Put a European label next to an American one and they won’t line up at a glance, even when the burgers are close.

Big Mac vs Whopper calories

The Whopper has more calories than the Big Mac: 670 for a US Whopper against 580 for a US Big Mac. Add cheese and the Whopper reaches 740, which is 160 more.

Burger (US) Calories Total fat Saturated fat
McDonald’s Big Mac 580 34 g 11 g
Burger King Whopper 670 37 g 12 g
Burger King Whopper with Cheese 740 — —

Sources: Big Mac, Wikipedia; Whopper, FatSecret, both checked November 12, 2026. The Whopper figures still need confirming against bk.com. FatSecret also lists 1.5 g of trans fat for the Whopper.

The two burgers are built in opposite ways. The Big Mac stacks 2 thin patties between 3 pieces of bun. The Whopper puts 1 larger flame-grilled patty under fresh vegetables. If calories decide your order, the Big Mac wins. The gap is small, though, and the bigger choice is whether you add fries. Burger King’s build is broken down in our Whopper ingredients and copycat recipe.

Big Mac vs a homemade burger

A homemade burger can land above or below a Big Mac’s 580 calories. 3 things decide it: the patty weight, the fat percentage of the beef, and how much bun and sauce you use. A 150 g patty is already heavier than both Big Mac patties together (90 g), so homemade isn’t automatically lighter.

A short history of the Big Mac

The Big Mac was created by McDonald’s franchisee Jim Delligatti and launched on April 22, 1967, in Uniontown, about 45 miles south of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. A year later, in 1968, it was on every McDonald’s menu in the US. Our history of the hamburger tells the longer story. Big Mac calories FAQ

How many calories are in a Big Mac?

A US Big Mac has 580 calories for a 220 g burger, according to Wikipedia and McDonald’s US product listing, checked November 12, 2026. In Australia it has 559 calories, and in Japan 557.

Why doesn’t the Big Mac have the same calories in every country?

Each market uses its own recipe, with its own bun, sauce and cheese weights. Labeling rules differ too: the US gives sodium per serving, while the EU requires salt and values per 100 g under Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011.

How many calories are in a Big Mac meal?

A medium US Big Mac meal, with medium fries and a medium Coca-Cola, has 1,170 calories, about 59% of a 2,000-calorie day.

Which has more calories, a Big Mac or a Whopper?

The Whopper. A US Whopper has 670 calories and a US Big Mac 580, a 90-calorie gap. A Whopper with cheese reaches 740.

How much protein and sodium are in a Big Mac?

A US Big Mac has 25 g of protein and 1,060 mg of sodium. That sodium is 46% of the FDA’s 2,300 mg Daily Value.

Can you eat a Big Mac on a diet?

Yes, if you plan around it. On its own a Big Mac takes 29% of a 2,000-calorie day. The medium meal takes 59%. My advice: keep the burger, skip the fries and the soda. That’s where most of the meal’s calories are.
