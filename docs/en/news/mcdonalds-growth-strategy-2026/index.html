McDonald's Growth Strategy 2026, Market by Market

Every 2026 menu launch and test, sorted by country, date and status, and how each one fits the McDonald's > NEXT plan revealed in Las Vegas.

By Noah, Industry reporterPublished November 25, 2026

McDonald’s growth strategy for 2026 rests on four levers: bigger beef burgers, more chicken, a new specialty drinks platform and cheaper entry-level meals. Chicken, beef and beverages are the three priority categories of McDonald’s > NEXT, the plan unveiled in June 2026. In the US, that meant the Big Arch (March 3), an expanded McValue menu (April 21), new McCafé drinks (May 6), a Caesar menu (July 21) and holiday drinks (November 3). The home market has not followed yet. Global comparable sales rose 1.5% in the third quarter; US sales slipped 0.2%.

I went through the Q3 2026 earnings call of November 4, the trade press and every official announcement I could find, then dated each launch and gave it a status: national launch, limited time or test. One rule held throughout: a result stays tied to its market. A record week in Germany or a soft quarter in the US says nothing about the rest of the group.

What is the McDonald’s > NEXT plan?

McDonald’s > NEXT is the company’s new global growth plan, presented at its biennial worldwide convention in Las Vegas from June 1 to 4, 2026. It replaces Accelerating the Arches, the strategy McDonald’s had run since 2020 (Axios, TradingSat).

The plan names three menu categories to grow: chicken, beef and beverages. Two operating priorities sit around them, and both showed up in every market this year: value pricing to win back budget-conscious guests, and remodeled restaurants. Every launch below maps onto one of those levers.

Beef: the Big Arch is the flagship burger of 2026

The Big Arch is McDonald’s biggest beef bet of the year: a double quarter-pound burger that launched in the US, became permanent in the UK and went nationwide in Spain. If one product sums up the beef lever, it is this one.

Big Arch in the US: what’s in it and how long it stays

The Big Arch launched nationwide in the US on March 3, 2026, as a limited-time item. McDonald’s announced it on February 24 (Nation’s Restaurant News). It stacks two quarter-pound patties, three slices of white cheddar, crispy onions and slivered onions, lettuce, pickles and Big Arch sauce on a sesame and poppy seed bun.

It is a heavy sandwich: about 1,057 calories, 67 g of fat and 57 g of protein, according to Tasting Table. That is more than half of the 2,000-calorie daily reference printed on US nutrition labels, in one burger. Each restaurant sets its own price. I have not found a national price or an end date in a source I could verify, so I am quoting neither.

Big Arch in the UK and Spain

In the UK, the Big Arch moved to the permanent menu after strong sales, as NRN reported and the Q2 2026 results confirmed. In Spain, it was on sale nationwide by August 26, 2026, in restaurants, at McAuto drive-thrus and through MyMcDonald’s delivery, at €8 on its own and €10.50 as a meal (Moncloa.com). Its exact Spanish launch date and whether it is permanent there are not confirmed.

Chicken: hand-breaded tests and a Caesar menu

Chicken is the category where McDonald’s sees the most room to grow. In 2026 it made two moves: a limited-time Caesar menu in the US, and hand-breaded chicken tests in the US and Ireland.

The Caesar menu in the US

McDonald’s launched a limited-time Caesar menu nationwide on July 21, 2026. It brought the chain’s first Caesar sauce, the Bacon Caesar McCrispy, a Caesar Snack Wrap at a suggested $2.99, and McCrispy Strips with a new panko breading (QSR Magazine, Tasting Table).

McDonald’s hand-breaded chicken: where it’s being tested

McDonald’s is testing hand-breaded chicken in about ten restaurants in the US and Ireland, and plans 100 US test restaurants in early 2027. Management said on the Q3 call that the tests compare a two-item lineup (strips and a sandwich) with a three-item lineup that adds wings. Ireland is slated for a national rollout.

The Irish pilot runs for four months, from mid-October 2026 to February 2027, in a small group of Dublin restaurants. It covers McSpicy wings, McCrispy strips and hand-breaded McCrispy burgers. Tim Kenward, chief marketing officer of McDonald’s UK&I, announced it (Restaurant Online). It follows a similar US test near Chicago.

Beverages: why McDonald’s is betting on refreshers and energizers

McDonald’s is betting on drinks because they raise the check and bring guests in outside mealtimes. According to the Q3 call, checks that include a specialty drink run about 50% above the average check, and more than half of those visits happen after lunch. Of the four levers, this is the one with the clearest numbers behind it, and in my view the strongest: it lifts the check and fills the afternoon at the same time.

The new McCafé drinks platform

The new McCafé platform launched in the US on May 6, 2026, with a refreshed McCafé logo. It then rolled out to Canada, Germany, Australia and other markets through spring and summer (NRN). The US lineup: Strawberry Watermelon Refresher, Mango Pineapple Refresher, Blackberry Passion Fruit Refresher, Sprite Berry Blast, Orange Dream and Dirty Dr Pepper. It followed a test in more than 500 US restaurants. McDonald’s puts the global beverage opportunity at more than $100 billion (Nasdaq).

What the drinks delivered in Q3

About 18,000 restaurants now sell specialty drinks. The Red Bull Dragonberry Energizer, launched in August, is the platform’s best seller. McDonald’s plans to bring the drinks to more than 7,000 additional restaurants in Europe by mid-2027. It has not named the countries, so do not assume France or Spain are on the list.

For the holidays, participating US restaurants added the Frosted Cranberry Splash with Sprite, the Red Bull Frosted Cranberry Energizer and a Cookie Butter Frappé, Latte and Iced Coffee on November 3, 2026 (NRN).

Value: McValue in the US, the €5 McDeal in France

Value is the lever McDonald’s uses to win back low-income guests, and each market prices it its own way. In the US that means McValue and new bundles under test. In France it means the €5 McDeal.

McValue and the new US value bundles

On April 21, 2026, McDonald’s expanded McValue with about ten items under $3, such as a $1.50 Sausage McMuffin and a $2.50 McDouble. It also added a $4 Breakfast Meal Deal: Sausage McMuffin, hash brown and medium coffee (QSRweb, Fox Business). Prices vary by franchisee. If you’d rather make the burger at home, my colleague Clara rebuilds it in her McDouble copycat recipe.

In Q3, McDonald’s ran an eight-week test of two new all-day value bundles in about 1,900 US restaurants, with a national launch targeted for early 2027. About 40% of the US system already runs comparable entry-level deals (TradingView/Zacks).

The €5 McDeal in France

In France, the €5 McDeal replaced the McSmart on May 5, 2026, and runs until December 31, 2026. It includes a burger, small fries, a small drink and one extra item. Some Best Of meals (McChicken, Filet-O-Fish, Double Cheese) were capped at €7.50, and the Happy Meal stays at €4 (Journal du Geek). Of all the 2026 launches, this is the one a French customer is most likely to notice at the till. We compare these deals with Burger King’s and Wendy’s in the 2026 burger value wars.

Restaurants: NEXT remodels and the Archy ordering tool

The > NEXT plan also covers the restaurants themselves. The first NEXT restaurant outside the US opened in Poland, and Spain is next. McDonald’s targets more than 5,000 NEXT remodels a year worldwide from 2028. According to the Q3 call, its Archy ordering tool should be in most US restaurants by the end of 2027. Openings by other chains are covered in burger chain international expansion in 2026.

How McDonald’s sales performed in Q3 2026

McDonald’s global comparable sales grew 1.5% in the third quarter of 2026, reported on November 4. US sales dipped 0.2% for the quarter but are up 1.4% year to date. Adjusted EPS came in at $3.57 on revenue of about $7.2 billion (Motley Fool, MarketBeat).

Australia and Germany led the international operated markets. The Menu Heist campaign put signature items from other countries on local menus, and it lifted sales and traffic in Germany and Canada. Germany had one of the best sales weeks in its history. Japan posted its 44th straight quarter of growth. China’s comparable sales were negative.

France underperformed. Management blamed protests and a governance restructuring, and French franchisees voted to simplify that governance. McDonald’s did not publish a France-only sales figure, so I am not putting a number on it.

My take: a plan built on the US, tested one market at a time

McDonald’s launches in the US, proves a product in one market, then exports it. The Big Arch went from a US launch to the UK’s permanent menu and a nationwide run in Spain. The drinks went from a test in 500 US restaurants to about 18,000. Hand-breaded chicken is still a pilot. The method works abroad. The weak spot is the US itself, where comparable sales fell 0.2% in Q3 after a year full of launches. That is why I read the value bundles, not the burgers or the refreshers, as the real bet of > NEXT. If they do not bring US traffic back in early 2027, new menu items will not do it alone.

FAQ: McDonald’s in 2026

What are McDonald’s new menu items in 2026?

In the US, the 2026 launches are the Big Arch (March 3), the expanded McValue menu (April 21), the McCafé refreshers, dirty sodas and energizers (May 6), the Caesar menu (July 21) and holiday drinks (November 3). Outside the US, the Big Arch reached the UK and Spain, and France got the €5 McDeal.

Is the Big Arch permanent?

It depends on the country. The Big Arch is a limited-time item in the US and part of the permanent menu in the UK. Its status in Spain has not been confirmed.

How much is the Big Arch in the US?

McDonald’s has not set a national price. Each US restaurant prices the Big Arch itself. In Spain it costs €8 on its own and €10.50 as a meal.

What is McDonald’s > NEXT?

McDonald’s > NEXT is the global growth plan unveiled at the Las Vegas convention in June 2026. It succeeds Accelerating the Arches and targets growth in chicken, beef and beverages, backed by value pricing and restaurant remodels.

Will McDonald’s sell hand-breaded chicken nationwide in the US?

Not yet. Hand-breaded chicken is in about ten test restaurants in the US and Ireland, and McDonald’s plans 100 US test restaurants in early 2027. Ireland is the only market slated for a national rollout so far.

Will the new McCafé drinks come to Europe?

Yes, in part. Germany already has them, and McDonald’s plans to add more than 7,000 European restaurants by mid-2027. It has not said which other countries are included.

Why is McDonald’s investing so much in drinks?

Specialty drinks raise the check and bring guests in after lunch. According to the Q3 2026 call, checks with one of these drinks are about 50% higher than average, and more than half of those visits happen after lunch.
