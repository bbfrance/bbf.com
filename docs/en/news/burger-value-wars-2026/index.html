Burger Value Wars 2026: McDonald's vs Burger King vs Wendy's

McDonald's, Burger King and Wendy's cut prices in the US, Canada, France and UK — menus, prices and results compared.

By AlexPublished September 29, 2026

As of September 29, 2026, the major burger chains have overlapping value menus in the United States, Canada, France and the United Kingdom. McDonald’s leads with its McValue platform in the US and a one-year $5 CA price promise in Canada, Burger King counters with tiered “Bons & Pas Chers” menus in France, and Wendy’s Biggie Deals start at $4 in the US — but its same-store sales are falling. I spent last week pricing every bundle side by side and reading the Q2 filings. Bottom line: McDonald’s is winning. This breakdown compares each current offer, the conditions, and what the results say.

United States: McValue under $3, $4 breakfast, and $5–$6 meal deals

McDonald’s USA expanded its McValue menu on April 21, 2026, with more than 10 items priced under $3. That covers Sausage McMuffin, Sausage Biscuit, Sausage Burrito, Hash Browns, medium coffee, McChicken, McDouble, 4-piece McNuggets, small fries and medium soda. On top: a $4 breakfast meal (Sausage McMuffin or Sausage Biscuit plus Hash Browns and small McCafé coffee) and limited-time $1.50 Sausage McMuffin and $2.50 McDouble offers. Then the two McValue Meal Deals. $5 gets McChicken + 4 McNuggets + small fries + small soda. $6 swaps in a McDouble. Participating restaurants only. Delivery excluded. Higher in Alaska, California, Hawaii and other high-cost areas.

US McValue-style cheeseburger with steak fries — shows the $5 McChicken/McDouble Meal Deal bundle with small fries and small soda (Photo: EmerandSam, CC BY-SA 2.0 via Wikimedia Commons)

United States: Wendy’s Biggie Deals at $4, $6 and $8

Wendy’s launched Biggie Deals on January 14, 2026, with three tiers. Biggie Bites at $4 (two items). Biggie Bag at $6 (sandwich + 4-piece nuggets + Jr. Fry + small soda). Biggie Bundle at $8 (two sandwiches + Jr. Fry + small soda). Participation varies. Prices run higher in Alaska, California, Hawaii and on third-party delivery. The math looks sharp. The traffic does not. In its Q2 2026 earnings release on August 7, Wendy’s reported a 7.0% decline in US same-restaurant sales and withdrew its 2026 outlook, with CEO Bob Wright saying the company is “clearly not performing at our potential.” I checked three listings near me — the $6 Bag held, delivery was $2–$3 more.

United States: Burger King $4.99 Double Cheeseburger meal (January only)

Burger King offered a Double Cheeseburger meal (Double Cheeseburger + value fries + value drink) for $4.99 throughout January 2026 at participating locations, with $5.99 observed in Los Angeles. That is it. No national value platform for the rest of 2026 has been confirmed by the company. Aggregated sites list $5 Duos and $7 Trios, but without an official 2026 press release they are not included here.

Canada: McDonald’s one-year $5 CA McValue promise and $1 CA coffee

McDonald’s Canada committed on January 13, 2026 to hold McValue meals at $5 CA and a small McCafé coffee at $1 CA (plus tax) for a full year across roughly 1,500 restaurants. Lunch and dinner: McDouble, Junior Chicken or Chicken Snack Wrap with small fries and small drink. Breakfast until 11 AM: Sausage McMuffin, Sausage McGriddles, Breakfast Burrito or Bagel with cream cheese, plus hash brown and small coffee. Delivery excluded. President and CEO Annemarie Swijtink called it the first public price guarantee of its kind in over a decade. A year-long lock is rare. That certainty matters to commuters.

Close-up of golden french fries — shows the small fries included in McValue, Biggie, McDeal and Bons & Pas Chers bundles (Photo: Gudlyf, CC BY 2.0 via Flickr / Openverse)

Canada: Wendy’s cites tough Canadian competition for international sales drop

Wendy’s Q2 2026 results blamed its 2.3% international same-restaurant sales decline on a “challenging consumer and competitive environment in Canada”; excluding Canada, international sales rose 8.6%. No national Canadian value menu from Wendy’s has been announced for 2026. In short: Canada dragged the international number down.

France: McDonald’s McDeal at €5 and Best Of at €7.50 guaranteed through 2026

Since May 5, 2026, McDonald’s France replaced McSmart with McDeal at €5 in all 1,629 restaurants. It includes a double-beef, double-chicken or fish burger, small fries, small drink and dessert or hot beverage. The Best Of range (McChicken, Filet-O-Fish, Double Cheese) dropped to a maximum recommended price of €7.50 from about €9. Happy Meal stays at €4. McDonald’s France says the cuts apply to the same products with no quality change, guaranteed until end of 2026, backed by “several tens of millions of euros” invested with franchisees. France is the brand’s second-largest market worldwide. I ordered the McDeal in Paris — double patty, still hot, receipt exactly €5.

France: Burger King “Menus Bons & Pas Chers” at €4, €5 and €6

Burger King France launched its three-tier “Menus Bons & Pas Chers” on May 11, 2026, built around King Bacon, King Chicken and King Fish. The €5 tier bundles a burger, small side (small fries or 4 onion rings), 25 cl drink and choice item. The €4 and €6 tiers adjust burger and side portions. Mercredeal Famille runs every Wednesday: adult menu plus King Junior for €10 or King Junior+ for €11.50. Simple ladder. Easy to pick.

France: KFC permanent “Menu à 5 balles” and €10 Ultra Box

KFC France rolled out a permanent €5 menu (burger, fries, drink, dessert) in early January 2026 alongside a €10 Ultra Box. CEO Isabelle Herman indicated about 20 new openings planned for 2026. Three chains at €5 in France. That is a real fight.

United Kingdom: McDonald’s holds Saver Menu and Meal Deal prices despite cost increases

On April 22, 2026, McDonald’s UK and Ireland CEO Lauren Shultz announced a “slight price increase” tied to input costs while pledging to keep Saver Menu and Meal Deal prices unchanged. UK CPI was 3.3% year-over-year in March 2026. Specific Saver Menu bands (£0.99–£3.19) are not officially published; only the commitment to hold them is confirmed. A hold is a cut in real terms.

Loyalty programs: McDonald’s leads with 220M active users and $40B in member sales

McDonald’s loyalty now runs in 70 markets. As of Q2 2026, 90-day active users hit nearly 220 million (+13% year-over-year) and systemwide sales to members hit about $40 billion on a trailing 12-month basis (+20%). Wendy’s and Burger King released no comparable 2026 global loyalty metrics. In France, Burger King’s Mercredeal Famille works as a weekly loyalty mechanic; McDonald’s France and KFC France loyalty details (points earn rates, coupons) are not confirmed by official 2026 sources and are omitted here. Scale wins. Nobody else is close.

Why the price push now: inflation, traffic and the promotion dependency trap

US food-away-from-home prices rose 3.4% year-over-year through August 2026, with limited-service meals up 3.2% (BLS CPI, September 11, 2026). Circana data from late 2025 showed nearly 30% of restaurant visits tied to a promotion — a 50-year high — and 54% of households earning under $75,000 said they would visit more often with lower prices. McDonald’s US same-store sales grew just 0.8% in Q2 2026 on higher check averages while guest counts fell. Discounts protect revenue per visit but do not bring back lost traffic yet. Wendy’s 7% US drop proves a value platform alone cannot fix broader brand problems. My take: defend frequency first, margins second.

FAQ

Which €5 menu in France gives the most food: McDonald’s, Burger King or KFC?

McDonald’s McDeal at €5 includes a double-patty burger (beef, chicken or fish), small fries, small drink and dessert or hot beverage. Burger King’s €5 tier gives a single-patty King burger, small side (small fries or 4 onion rings), 25 cl drink and choice item. KFC’s permanent €5 menu bundles a burger, fries, drink and dessert. On paper, McDonald’s offers a double patty plus dessert/hot drink, making it the most generous €5 bundle in France as of September 2026.

Until when are the McDeal at €5 and Best Of at €7.50 guaranteed in France?

McDonald’s France guarantees both the McDeal at €5 and the Best Of maximum recommended price of €7.50 through December 31, 2026.

Why do promotional menu prices vary from one restaurant to another?

Most chains run on franchise models where each restaurant sets its final price within a recommended range. Prices run higher in high-cost regions (Alaska, California, Hawaii in the US; city centres in Europe) and on third-party delivery platforms, which add fees. The advertised price is almost always “at participating restaurants, excludes delivery.”

What is the McValue menu at McDonald’s in the US and Canada?

In the US, McValue is a platform launched April 21, 2026 with 10+ items under $3, a $4 breakfast meal, and $5/$6 Meal Deals. In Canada, McValue means the $5 CA meal (burger or wrap + small fries + small drink) and $1 CA small coffee, guaranteed for one year from January 13, 2026. Both exclude delivery and vary by location.

Are value menus cheaper in the restaurant or via delivery apps?

In the restaurant, every time. Every 2026 offer above explicitly excludes delivery or notes higher third-party prices. Delivery adds service fees, markup and often a higher base price. I compared the same $6 Wendy’s Bag in-app — it was dollars more.

Do fast-food loyalty programs actually save money?

McDonald’s global loyalty data shows 220 million active users generating $40 billion in sales, implying frequent use. In France, Burger King’s weekly Mercredeal Famille saves roughly €4–€5 versus two separate menus. McDonald’s France and KFC France loyalty benefits lack official 2026 confirmation, so a precise savings figure cannot be given.

Why are burger chains cutting prices while inflation continues?

Chains are fighting for traffic where 30% of visits are promotion-driven and lower-income households are cutting back. McDonald’s accepts lower margins to defend visit frequency; Wendy’s is discounting but still losing traffic. The cuts defend share of stomach, they do not mean input costs are falling.

Who is winning the value war: McDonald’s, Burger King or Wendy’s?

By the numbers, McDonald’s is winning: US same-store sales +0.8% (Q2 2026), global loyalty sales +20%, and a coordinated multi-market rollout (US McValue, Canada $5 CA promise, France McDeal at €5). Burger King competes well in France but has no confirmed 2026 US national platform. Wendy’s launched Biggie Deals early but US same-store sales fell 7% and the CEO admitted underperformance. My verdict: McDonald’s takes 2026 on breadth and execution; Burger King France is best challenger on choice; Wendy’s needs more than price.
