How we cover burgers

Our news covers openings, price wars, trends and the people behind the grill. Every story names its sources, from earnings releases to menus and local press, and says when the facts were checked.

Every recipe is tested several times before it goes live. Ingredients are weighed to the gram and oven temperatures are given in both °C and °F, so a bun or a patty turns out the same in any kitchen.

Our restaurant guides are built from menus, dated prices, local food press and review data. We check that each venue is still open and explain the method behind every ranking, so you know where each price and rating comes from.
